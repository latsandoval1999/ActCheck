TITLE: AN ACT CREATING A CONGRESSIONAL COMMISSION ON AGRICULTURE AND FISHERIES TO REVIEW, ASSESS, AND EVALUATE THE STATE OF PHILIPPINE AGRICULTURE AND RECOMMEND INNOVATIVE AND TARGETED POLICY REFORMS IN AGRICULTURE AND FISHERIES, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
[HB-20-2322:s1] Short Title: SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Congressional Commission on Agriculture and Fisheries (AGRICOM) Act"
[HB-20-2322:s2] Declaration of Policy: SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State shall reaffirm the promotion of a just and dynamic social order that will ensure the prosperity and independence of the nation that frees the people from poverty through policies that provide adequate social services, promote full employment, a rising standard of living, and an improved quality of life for all. The State shall affirm labor as a primary social economic force, shall develop a self-reliant and independent national economy effectively controlled by Filipinos, and shall promote comprehensive rural development and agrarian reform. To such ends, the State shall take the appropriate steps to ensure that the national agriculture and fisheries sector shall be a robust, dynamic and sustainable means to economic security on participants on the production side of the sector, and shall be a driver for human capital development, human liberation and development, and overall well-being and welfare on the part of Filipino consumers.
[HB-20-2322:s3] Objectives of AGRICOM: SECTION 3. [HB-20-2322:s4] Creation of AGRICOM: SECTION 4. Creation of AGRICOM. - There is hereby created a Congressional Agriculture and Fisheries Commission, hereinafter referred to as the Commission, to undertake a comprehensive national assessment and evaluation of the performance of the Philippine agriculture and fisheries sector for the purpose of recommending transformative, concrete and targeted reforms in the sector with the end of ensuring food security in the Philippines.
[HB-20-2322:s5] Composition of AGRICOM: SECTION 5. Composition of AGRICOM. - The Commission shall be composed of twelve (12) members, with six (6) members from the Senate and six (6) members from the House of Representatives: provided, that one of the six (6) members coming from each House shall represent the Minority as designated by the Minority Leader of each House. The Chairpersons of the Senate Committee on Agriculture, Food and Agrarian Reform, the Senate Committee on Economic Affairs, and the Senate Committee on Environment, Natural Resources and Climate Change shall be members of the Commission on the part of the Senate. The Chairpersons of the House Committee on Agriculture and Food, the House Committee on Aquaculture and Fisheries Resources, the House Committee on Agrarian Reform, and the House Committee on Climate Change shall be members of the Commission on the part of the House of Representatives. The other members from the Senate and the House of Representatives shall be designated by the Senate President and the Speaker of the House of Representatives, respectively. The members of the Commission shall receive no compensation, but travelling, communications, and other necessary expenses shall be allowed, subject to existing laws, rules and regulations.
[HB-20-2322:s6] Executive Director: SECTION 6. Executive Director. - The Executive Director (ED) to be appointed by the Commission must be a recognized expert in the fields of agriculture and fisheries. 5 The ED shall be a citizen of the Philippines with good moral standing and reputation, of recognized probity and independence, and have substantial experience and expertise in policymaking related to, or the practice of, agriculture development, aquaculture and fisheries development, economics, and sustainability. The ED shall work full-time and shall have the rank, privileges, and emoluments of an Executive Director IV (Salary Grade 29).
[HB-20-2322:s7] Technical Secretariat: SECTION 7. Technical Secretariat. - A Technical Secretariat, to be headed by the Executive Director, shall be organized to provide technical and administrative support to the Commission in the exercise of its powers and performance of its functions under Section 11 of this Act. The Executive Director shall organize its staff and technical panel, and hire and appoint such employees and personnel, whether temporary, contractual, or on consultancy, and determine their compensation subject to applicable civil service laws, rules and regulations, with a view to ensuring a competent and efficient secretariat. The Technical Secretariat shall co-exist with the Commission.
[HB-20-2322:s8] Philippine Institute for Development Studies as Research Arm: SECTION 8. Philippine Institute for Development Studies as Research Arm. - The PIDS shall serve as the research arm of the Commission. During the course of the Commission's existence, it shall produce data-based research and provide analysis to contribute to the formulation of agriculture and fisheries policy recommendations. It may coordinate with existing advisory and consultative bodies on agriculture and fisheries policies under the Department of Agriculture and the Department of Science and Technology.
[HB-20-2322:s9] Agriculture and Fisheries Legislation and Policy Advisory Council: SECTION 9. Agriculture and Fisheries Legislation and Policy Advisory Council. - An Agriculture and Fisheries Legislation and Policy Advisory Council is hereby created to provide the Commission with expert assistance and advice. The members of the Advisory Council shall always be present in all meetings of the Commission. The Senate President and the Speaker of the House of Representatives, upon the recommendation of the members of the Commission, shall choose recognized experts from the following sectors to form part of the Advisory Council: three (3) members from state university academia with equal representation from Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao, two (2) members from the business sector, two (2) members from government agriculture and fisheries agencies, two (2) members who are heads of LGUs, and two (2) members from civil society organizations and development partners engaged in agriculture and fisheries
[HB-20-2322:s10] Duration of AGRICOM: SECTION 10. Duration of AGRICOM. - The Commission shall accomplish its mandate within three (3) years from its organization. It shall organize itself and commence its mandate upon the effectivity of this Act.

Standing Committees. - The members of the Commission shall establish from among its members standing committees, to be chaired by such members or experts from 7 multisectoral groups, and engage resource persons from the public and private sectors as may be needed.
[HB-20-2322:s13] Appropriations: SECTION 13. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against available appropriations of the Senate and the House of Representatives.
