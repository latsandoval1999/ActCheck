    “GOVERNMENT ASSISTANCE TO STUDENTS AND TEACHERS IN PRIVATE

          Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
    Congress assembled:

1         SECTION 1. Short Title . – This Act shall be known as the “Private Basic
2   Education Vouchers Assistance Act”.
 1
 2           SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy . – It is the declared policy of the State to
 3   protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels, and take
 4   appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all. As part of its mandate, it
 5   is the State's policy to establish and maintain a system of scholarship grants, subsidies,
 6   and other incentives which shall be available to deserving students, as determined by
 7   the guidelines, in both public and private schools, especially to the underprivileged.
 8   Likewise, the State recognizes the complementary roles of public and private
 9   educational institutions in the educational system and the invaluable contribution that
10   the private schools have made and will make to education.
11
12          The State shall provide the mechanisms to augment the resources and enhance
13   the quality of private basic education, specifically promoting the complementary roles
14   of public and private schools and thus recognize the government's priority of providing
15   basic elementary and secondary education to all Filipino students.
16
17          SEC. 3. Coverage . – This Act shall cover all private basic education institutions
18   that receive government assistance in the form of vouchers for qualified students,
19   teachers and schools. This covers all the years of basic education.
20
21           The programs of assistance for students under this Act shall be extended only
22   to citizens of the Philippines.
23
24          SEC. 4. Criteria for Voucher Assistance. – The programs for voucher
25   assistance shall be based on a set of criteria which include among others, the following:
26
27         (a) Criteria for Students. – The voucher assistance shall be provided to
28   students from middle-income families, as determined by the Philippine Statistics
29   Authority (PSA), who attended public schools identified by the Department of
30   Education as congested or those living in areas with congested public schools or
31   without a nearby public school who wish to enroll in private basic education schools.
32
33         The priority students shall receive a higher voucher amount as determined by
34   the Basic Education Assistance Council (BEAC).
35
36          (b) Criteria for Schools. – The participating private basic education schools shall
37   receive assistance based on Quality Assurance under Section 6 of this Act, and the
38   geographic spread and size of student enrollment, as likewise determined by the BEAC,
39   except schools with gaps in resources and facilities which are located in geographically
40   isolated and disadvantaged and conflict-affected areas, or GIDCA schools. Private
41   schools shall undergo the process of recognition and periodic assessment to be able
42   to participate in the programs for voucher assistance.
43
44          SEC. 5. Em pow ering Student Choice. – The Department of Education
45   (DepEd) shall create a system that allows students and parents to select their
46   preferred schools from a registry of participating recognized schools, subject to
47   availability of slots.
48


                                               -2-
 1           The registry of participating recognized private basic educational institutions
 2   shall include their schools’ information, such as location, number of available slots,
 3   number of students per classroom and level, extra-curricular programs, current tuition
 4   fees, and top-up required in cases where the voucher amount is less than the tuition
 5   fee, among others.
 6
 7          SEC. 6. Quality Assurance . – The DepEd shall establish, operate and
 8   maintain a system and mechanism for monitoring program outcomes to ensure
 9   accountability of participating recognized private basic educational institutions. This
10   covers the following areas:
11
12           (a) Improved Student Performance – Appropriate performance indicators to
13   determine the extent and outcome of the government assistance and subsidies (GAS)
14   programs including youth literacy rates, national and international assessment scores,
15   among others. For this purpose, the National Achievement Tests and all national
16   assessment examinations generally given by the DepEd shall be administered to all
17   eligible students in all participating private basic education institutions;
18
19          (b) Improved Student Outcomes – Tracer system to collect and evaluate the
20   career and employment path of its graduates;
21
22         (c) School Performance – School performance data indicating the mean,
23   median and mode of the scores of the students in the national examinations, and such
24   other measures of quality education delivery; and
25
26           (d) Priority Subsidy for Underprivileged Students – Distribution of the GAS
27   programs while ensuring that funding is equitably allocated following the order of
28   priority groups.
29
30          SEC. 7. Other Form s of Assistance . – The following shall consist of
31   assistance to private basic education institutions and teachers participating in the
32   voucher program under this Act:
33
34          The forms of assistance granted to teachers in the participating recognized
35   private basic education schools include:
36
37         (a) In-service training fund for teachers in private basic education schools;
38         (b) Scholarships for qualified private basic education teachers enrolled in
39             quality graduate programs; and
40         (c) Participation in government-funded in-service training for basic education
41             teachers.
42
43           The DepEd may establish a program of assistance for the development of
44   qualified recognized private basic education schools to cover the following areas of
45   school operation:
46
47         (a) Institutional Development;
48         (b) Organizational Development;


                                              -3-
 1          (c) Human Resource Development;
 2          (d) Curriculum and Instructional Development;
 3          (e) Research and Planning; and
 4          (f) Facilities and School Improvement.
 5
 6          Consistent with Sec. 10 of Republic Act No. 10533, or the “Enhanced Basic
 7   Education Act of 2013”, the DepEd, in consultation with the Department of Budget
 8   and Management (DBM), may develop other forms of financial arrangements and
 9   engage the services of private basic education schools based on the complementary
10   roles of public and private institutions in the educational system.
11
12          SEC. 8. Basic Education Voucher Program. – Under the basic education
13   voucher program, kindergarten, elementary, and secondary students enrolled in
14   recognized private basic education schools charging school fees shall be provided by
15   the government with vouchers in such amount as may be determined by the DepEd:
16   Provided, That the amount shall not be less than the amount currently provided by
17   the DepEd: Provided, That the government shall reimburse the vouchers from the
18   schools concerned within sixty (60) days from the submission of complete documents,
19   as required by the BEAC, to the DepEd.
20
21          The budget for the basic education voucher program shall be equitably
22   allocated and distributed among all regions including the Bangsamoro Autonomous
23   Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) based on official data sources including, but not
24   limited to, congestion data and open seats, giving priority to underprivileged students.
25
26           Pursuant to Section 2(V), Article V of the Bangsamoro Organic Law, the
27   Bangsamoro Government shall exercise its authority over education for the areas
28   under the BARMM and will directly implement the same program to be charged against
29   their block grant. The DepEd shall provide technical assistance in the implementation
30   of the GAS Program, when necessary.
31
32          Schools participating in the basic education voucher program may increase
33   tuition on the condition that seventy percent (70%) of the amount of the tuition
34   increases shall go to the payment of salaries, wages, allowances, and other statutory
35   benefits of teaching and non-teaching personnel, except administrators who are
36   principal stockholders of the school, and may be used to cover salary increase as
37   provided for in the collective bargaining agreements existing or in force at the time
38   when this Act is approved and made effective. At least twenty percent (20%) of the
39   tuition increases shall go to the improvement or modernization of buildings,
40   equipment, libraries, laboratories, gymnasia and similar facilities and to the payment
41   of other costs of operation. For this purpose, schools shall maintain a separate record
42   of accounts for all assistance received from the government, any tuition increase, and
43   the detailed disposition and use thereof, which record shall be made available for

OBJECTIVES:
 •    To make quality basic education accessible by providing financial assistance to
      learners in public basic education schools who wish to study in private schools
 •    To expand the government assistance and subsidies of the law to cover
      kindergarten, elementary, junior high school, and senior high school learners
KEY PROVISIONS:
•   Expands the coverage of private basic education voucher beneficiaries to
    kindergarten, elementary, junior high school, and senior high school learners
•   Sets specific criteria for voucher assistance programs given to students and
    participating private schools
•   Empowers student choice through a system that allows students and parents to
    select their preferred schools from a registry of participating recognized schools,
    subject to availability of slots
•   Mandates the Department of Education (DepEd) to establish, operate and
    maintain a system and mechanism for monitoring program outcomes to ensure
    accountability of participating recognized private basic education institution,
    which covers the following area: (a) Improved student performance; (b) Improved
    student outcomes; (c) School performance; and (d) Priority subsidy for
    underprivileged students
•   Grants other forms of specific assistance to private basic education institutions
    and teachers participating in the voucher program
•   Mandates the DepEd to establish a program of assistance for the development
    of qualified recognized private basic education schools
•   Prescribes specific conditions in the implementation of the basic education
    voucher program, to wit:
    (a)   Equitable allocation of the voucher program among all regions, including
          the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM),
          based on official data sources including giving priority to underprivileged
          students;
    (b)   70% of the amount subsidized allotted for tuition or of the tuition increases
          shall go to payment of salaries, wages, allowances and other benefits of
          teaching and non-teaching personnel, except administrators who are
          principal stockholders of the school, and may be used to cover salary
          increase as provided for in the existing collective bargaining agreements;
    (c)   At least 20% of the voucher proceeds shall go to the improvement or
          modernization of buildings, equipment, libraries, laboratories, gymnasia
          and similar facilities and to the payment of other costs of operation
•   Establishes the following support mechanisms:
    (a)   In-service Training Fund or Inset Fund for the purpose of improving the
          quality of teaching in private basic education; and
    (b)   Teachers’ Salary Subsidy Fund to grant government subsidy to teachers in
          recognized private basic education schools participating in the voucher
          program, which shall form part of the exclusions from taxable gross income
•   Creates the Bureau of Private Education (BPE) under the DepEd to serve as the
    focal office for the administration, supervision, and regulation of all matters
    relating to private basic education institutions, including government assistance
    and subsidies, and provides for its specific powers and functions
•    Mandates the Basic Education Assistance Council to be responsible for policy
     guidance and direction, monitoring and evaluation of new and existing programs
     of assistance, to be headed by the Secretary of Education as Chairperson, with
     representatives from Department of Economy, Planning, and Development and
     Department of Budget and Management, as members
•    Mandates the Government Assistance and Subsidies Service under the BPE to
     be responsible for the day-to-day administration and program implementation on
     the programs of assistance to private basic education
 •   Allocates funds for the implementation of this Act to be included in the annual
     General Appropriations Act

 •   Penalizes a private school, after due process, for violating this Act
 •   Provides that the implementation of the provisions stipulated in this Act for the
     kindergarten and elementary levels shall gradually commence in school year
     2024-2025, as may be determined by the DepEd, subject to the availability of
     funds
 •   Repeals Sections 2, 3, 4(2), 4(3), 5, 6, 7, 12, 14, and 15 of Republic Act No. 8545
     pertaining to basic education
