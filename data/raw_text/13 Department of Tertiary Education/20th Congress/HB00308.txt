EXPLANATORY NOTE

The 1987 Constitution mandates the State to protect and promote the right of
all citizens to quality education at all levels and to take appropriate steps to
make such education accessible to all’. It further requires the State to establish
and maintain a complete, adequate, and integrated system of education that is
responsive to the needs of the people and society”.

At present, post-secondary education is administered separately by the
Commission on Higher Education and the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority. This bill seeks to establish the Department of Tertiary
Education to unify its functions under a single agency, addressing long-
standing issues of policy fragmentation, overlapping mandates, and the lack of
alignment between academic and technical pathways.

The proposed Department will serve as the central agency responsible for the
regulation, support, and coordination of all post-secondary education
pathways, including universities, colleges, technical-vocational institutions,
and non-traditional learning systems. It shall ensure unified standards,
promote inclusive access, and align education programs with national
workforce and development goals’.

By establishing a single authority for tertiary education, the State strengthens
quality assurance, institutional efficiency, and the relevance of education in
meeting the demands of a rapidly changing economy‘

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT CREATING THE DEPARTMENT OF TERTIARY EDUCATION,
DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, APPROPRIATING
FUNDS THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the “Department of
Tertiary Education of 2025”.

SEC 2. Declaration of Policy - It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels. The State shall
take appropriate measures to ensure that education, including tertiary
education, is accessible to all. Tertiary education aims to deepen knowledge,
develop specialized skills, foster critical thinking, and prepare individuals for
professional careers or advanced academic pursuits following the completion
of secondary education.

The State further commits to encouraging and supporting non-formal,
informal, and Indigenous learning systems, as well as_ self-learning,
independent, and out-of-school study programs, particularly those that
address community needs. To this end, the Department of Tertiary Education
(“DTE”) shall be established, integrating the functions of the Commission on
Higher Education (“CHED”) and the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (“TESDA”) into a single agency for efficient
governance, streamlined regulation, and industry-aligned workforce
development.

ARTICLE III
Transition and Funding

SEC. 8. Transition Provisions - All existing CHED and TESDA personnel
shall be absorbed into the DTE based on merit and qualifications. A Transition
Comunittee shall be created to facilitate the smooth integration of systems,
processes, and personnel.

SEC. 9. Appropriations - The initial funding for the Department shall be
sourced from the current appropriations of CHED and TESDA. Subsequent
funding shall be included in the General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations (“IRR”) - Within 90 days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Department Secretary, in consultation with
relevant stakeholders, shall formulate the IRR to operationalize the
Department.

SEC. 11. Separability Clause ~ If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall not be affected.

SEC. 12. Repealing Clause - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,

SEC. 13. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
