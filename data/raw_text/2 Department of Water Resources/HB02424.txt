EXPLANATORY NOTE

Water is a vital resource that is essential to life, health, food security, sanitation, disaster
resilience and overall national development. Given its critical importance, it is noted that the
Philippines lacks a centralized body that is responsible for the coordinated management,
development, and regulation of water resources of the Philippines.

At present, there are about 20 to 30 government agencies that have overlapping mandates
in various sectors such as water supply, irrigation, hydropower, flood control, sanitation, watershed
regulation and environmental protection.

In terms of water resources, the Philippines receives an estimated 2,300 mm to 4,000 mm
or rainfall annually. It also has 421 principal river basins and over 59 major rivers. Yet, the country
still experiences water shortages during the dry months. Moreover, some areas have water surplus
while others rely on groundwater- extraction that causes subsidence and salt water intrusion.

Pollution from domestic, industrial and agricultural sources affects several bodies of
water. Although generally we have abundant sources of water, sadly only a small portion of the

river systems yield clean water that is suitable for public water supply.

As such, this bill aims to create a central agency to manage, develop, and regulate water
resources, ensuring sustainable use and fair distribution for everyone.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Title. This Act shall be known as “An Act Creating the Department of Water
Resources, defining its powers and functions”.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy- It is hereby declared the policy of the State to ensure
protection, sustainability, equitable and efficient management and development of water resources
of the country. Cognizant to the importance of water in national development, ensuring food
security and public health, the State shall institutionalize a centralize system of water governance.

To this end, the State shall establish a Department of Water Resources that shall be
responsible in the coordination, protection, regulation and efficient management of all water-
related concerns aimed at achieving sustainability, efficiency and social equity.

SECTION 3. Definition of terms. - For purposes of this Act, the following terms are hereby
defined:

a. Department- shall refer to the Department of Water Resources.

SECTION 4. The Department of Water Resources- there is hereby created a Department

of Water Resources which shall be hereinafter referred to as the Department.

SECTION 5. Mandate of the Department- The Department shall be responsible in

determining and mapping the country’s water resources, and shall set policies and regulations on
how it is used, owned, appropriated equitably including its development and preservation.

It shall implement the provisions of PD 1067 or the Water Code of the Philippines.

It shall set regulations on septage system, pursuant to the provisions of Republic Act No.

9275 or the Clean Water Act of 2004. To this end, it shall engage national agencies, local
government units, civil society organizations, private organizations, individuals in formulating and
ensuring the implementation of a septage management plan across regions in the country.

SECTION 6. Objectives of the Department- The Department shall:

a) Develop a water demand management policy to ensure sustainable use of water
resources;

b) Promote water conservation, encourage reusing and recycling of water and treated
wastewater, set appropriate pricing to prevent wasteful use. Likewise, implement
system to harness, store and treat rainwater, floodwater and runoff as reserve supply
for the future;

c) Ensure the protection of water resources through coordination with the Local
Government Units to prevent pollution and focus on the restoration of rivers;

d) Develop standardized pricing structure for water use, septage and sewerage
services, consumer prices and/or rates.

e) Ensure the identification, mapping and monitoring of all water sources be it surface
water, groundwater, rainwater and even flood water for proper management.

Policymaking and Planning

Endorse the Integrated Water Resources Management as framework for water
management policymaking and planning;

Formulate policies to mitigate issues concerning water sanitation, food security,
environment, flood control as well as climate change;

Formulation of basin —wide plans based on surveyed and recorded river basins to
provide guidelines for the proper storage and management of water;

Determine priority infrastructure development for each river basin that would cover
areas such as water supply, sanitation, irrigation, flood control, urban drainage,
drought risk reduction and management of storm water;

Ensure that the most appropriate technology, quality and highest efficiency are
considered when planning for any water infrastructure.

Resource Allocation and Regulation

Manage water resources appropriately for optimal distribution and use. Improve and
maintain safe water quality, intensify conservation efforts for watersheds and address
water pollution concerns;

Enforce rules and regulations for the proper use, development and management of
water resource that would include setting clear water procedures, rules for operating
current and future water infrastructure, standards for data collection. Establish penalties
for violators.

Impose fees and charges relative to the development, conservation and protection of
water resources such as Polluter’s fee based on the net waste load as provided in Section
13 of the Republic Act No. 9275;

Coordination

a) Coordinate data gathering activities, researches and special studies with other
government agencies;

b) Coordinate with concerned government agencies involved in flood control, flood risk
management and drought risk management;

SECTION 9 Secretary of the Department of Water Resources- The Secretary of the
Department of Water Resources shall be appointed by the President. The Secretary of Water
Resources shall:

a) Establish policies and standards of operations based on the President’s guidelines;

b) Advise the President on the status of water supply and management in the country;

¢) Exercise provision and control over all bureaus and offices under the Department;

d) Represent the Department in all Contracts, awards and agreements;

e) Designate and appoint employees of the Department in accordance with the rules and
regulations of the civil service except the Undersecretaries, Assistant Secretaries, Regional
and Assistant Directors;

f) Perform such other duties and responsibilities as may be provided by law.

SECTION 10. Undersecretaries and Assistant Secretaries- The Secretary shall be assisted by
Three (3) Undersecretaries and three (3) Assistant Secretaries which shall be appointed by the
President upon the recommendation of the Secretary.

They shall have powers and functions as provided for in the Chapter 2, Book IV of the
Administrative Code of 1987.

SECTION 11 . Qualifications- appointed Secretary, Undersecretary and Assistant Secretary must
be a citizen of the Philippines, of good moral character, of proven integrity , competence and expertise in
water management.
