                               EXPLANATORY NOTE


       Water is one of the most important resources in the world. The Philippines, as
an archipelagic country, is gifted with this resource in abundance.              However,
mismanagement and unsustainable consumption of water continues to be a major
predicament of the country.


       In 2024, the Department of Environment and Natural Resources estimated
around 40 million Filipinos do not have access to a formal water supply. This leads to
various problems particularly on health and sanitation. An analysis conducted by the
Philippine Statistics Authority revealed that between the years 2010-2019, fourteen
(14) Filipinos die everyday because of water-borne diseases caused by contaminated
water such as cholera, typhoid, and leptospirosis.


      The government needs to immediately address these concerns by providing
comprehensive and long-term solutions.         Congruent with the international efforts
under the auspices of the WHO, the concerned government agencies should intensify
its efforts to develop and implement water safety planning, to strengthen systems and
capacity for drinking water quality surveillance and monitoring, and to improve water,
sanitation, and hygiene.


       Such endeavor is consistent with Sustainable Development Goal 6 which seeks
to provide clean water and sanitation for all. Among the targets under this SDG are
the achievement of universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking
water for all; improvement of water quality by reducing pollution, eliminating dumping
and minimizing release of hazardous chemicals and materials, halving the proportion
of untreated wastewater and substantially increasing recycling and safe reuse globally;
and implementation of integrated water resources management at all levels, including
through transboundary cooperation as appropriate.


       The "National Water Act' seeks to rationalize the management of water
resources, sewerage, and sanitation through the creation of the Department of Water
Resources.     The Department shall be the primary agency responsible for the
comprehensive and integrated identification and mapping of all water resources,
planning, policy formulation, and management of the ownership, appropriation,
utilization, exploitation, development, and protection of water resources in the
Philippines.


       The bill also establishes the Water Regulatory Commission which shall be placed
under the administrative supervision of the Department of Water Resources. To
ensure coherent and coordinated management of water resources, the economic
regulatory units and functions of the Metropolitan Waterworks and Sewerage System
(MWSS), National Water Resources Board (NWRB), and Local Water Utilities
Administration (LWUA) will be transferred to the Commission.
     In view of the foregoing, the swift passage of this measure is highly
recommended.

 5         Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. -
 6         (a) The State recognizes that water is fundamental to individual life, health, and
 7            dignity, and that every person has a right to access safe and clean water
 8            for personal and domestic use;
 9         (b)The State affirms that the right of all Filipinos to safe and clean drinking
10            water and sanitation is essential to the full enjoyment of life and all human
11            rights and hence, the attainment of a nationwide water supply, sanitation,
12            and sewerage services at reasonable rates must be pursued in an orderly,

15            economic, agricultural, and industrial activities, and is thus a scarce resource
 1              which must be carefully regulated to prevent resource depletion and to
 2              ensure its availability for future generations;
 3          (d)The State also maintains that water is indispensable to the existence and
 4              sustainability of natural ecosystems, and as such its use must neither
 5              endanger the balance and health of the environment nor compromise the

 8              climate, soil condition, land use and land cover, forest cover, and other
 9              natural resources. As such, the management of water will require inter-
10              sectoral and inter-agency collaboration as well as harmonization of laws,
11              policies, institutions, and stakeholders; and
12          (f) Finally, while the State maintains that all water resources remain under its
13              full ownership, control, and supervision, it emphasizes that it may enter into
14              arrangements with the private sector, as well as with civil society and
15              community-based organizations, on the basis of beneficial use, to ensure
16              the efficient and affordable delivery of water supply, sanitation, and septage
17              services to end-users.

20   resources in the country to ensure universal access to safe, adequate, and affordable

23                        DEPARTMENT OF WATER RESOURCES
24         Sec. 4. Establishment of the Department of Water Resources. - There is hereby
25   established the Department of Water Resources, hereafter referred to as the
26   Department.
27         Sec. 5. Mandate and Objectives. - The Department shall be the primary policy,
28   planning, coordinating, implementing, monitoring, and administrative entity of the
29   Executive Branch of the government responsible for the comprehensive, sustainable,
30   climate-resilient, and integrated development and management of the water resources
31   of the Philippines, and their optimal allocation and use for domestic and municipal
 1   water supply, sanitation, irrigation, hydropower, industry, navigation, flood
 2   management, and recreation, and water utilization aspects of fisheries or aquaculture.
 3          The Department shall also be the primary agency responsible for the planning
 4   and policy formulation towards the attainment of universal access to safe, adequate,
 5   affordable, and sustainable water supply, and improved sewerage and sanitation
 6   services for all Filipinos.

10          Sec. 43. Proceedings Before the Commission. - For the purpose of any
11   investigation, inquiry, or proceeding, the Commission shall:
12          (a) Issue subpoena duces tecum and subpoena ad testificandum;
13          (b) Appoint hearing officers to hear and receive evidence on behalf of the

15          (c) Cite any person or party for contempt for refusal to appear, testify or comply
16             with an order of the Commission on any matter that is the subject of
17             investigation, inquiry or proceeding before the Commission.
18          Sec. 44. Orders and Decisions of the Commission. - Any order, resolution, or
19   decision of the Commission shall be promulgated promptly, expeditiously, reasonably,
20   and in writing, and shall state clearly and distinctly the facts and law on which it is
21   based. The Commission shall publish and make available for public inspection, all
22   decisions and final orders in the adjudication of contested cases or applications.
23          Sec. 45. Appeais Procedure. - The orders, rulings, and decisions of the
24   Commission are final and executory unless appealed to the Court of Appeals within
25   fifteen (15) days from receipt of notice of such order, ruling or decision: Provided,
26   That orders, rulings, and decisions of the Commission approving tariffs, shall be
27   immediately executory and may be suspended only upon appeal and filing of a bond,
28   in an amount to be fixed by the Commission, to answer for damages occasioned by
29   the suspension or stay of execution of such orders, rulings, and decisions.
30         Sec. 46. Prohibition Against Injunctions. - Except for the Supreme Court, no
31   temporary restraining order, preliminary injunction, or preliminary mandatory
