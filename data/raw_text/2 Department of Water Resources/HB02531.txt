                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

        According to the Department of Environment and Natural Resources, about 40 million
Filipinos still lack access to formal sources of water which affect the health of families and
have implications on poverty in communities.1


        Between 2010 and 2019, the Philippines has recorded a troubling figure of
approximately 50,0002 cases of waterborne diseases - a direct consequence of poor water
management and the lack of adequate access to clean and safe water across the country.


        Despite the abundance of water resources in the country, the foregoing data reflects the
harsh reality faced by many Filipinos, especially in far-flung communities where water
infrastructure is severely underdeveloped or completely absent.


        The existence of overlapping policies, laws, and regulations concerning water
management such as the Presidential Decree (PD) No. 1067 or the Water Code of the
Philippines, Republic Act No. 9275 or the Clean Water Act of 2004, and Presidential Decree
No. 198 or the Provincial Water Utilities Act of 1973 shows the lack of a comprehensive,

       With this proposed bill, we seek to bridge a policy and institutional gap by creating the
Department of Water Resources (DWR) which will serve as the lead agency for all water-
related functions, ensuring centralized oversight, strong inter-agency coordination, and the
harmonization of government policies and programs related to water supply, sanitation,
conservation, and infrastructure.


       As water scarcity, pollution, and unequal access continue to threaten health, economic
growth, and national security, this bill becomes more urgent and indispensable.


       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

4            SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to recognize protect, realize
5    and enhance the right of all Filipinos to safe, clean, affordable, and accessible drinking water,
6    sanitation and irrigation as essential to the full enjoyment of life and the protection and
7    sustainability of the environment.

9            SEC. 3. The Department of Water Resources. - There is hereby created and established
10   a Department of Water Resources, herein referred to as the "Department" which shall be
11   organized within one hundred twenty (120) days alter the effectivity of this Act.

13           SEC. 4. Objectives, Duties and Powers of the Department of Water Resources. –
14
15      a. Formulate, recommend, and implement national policies, plans, programs, that will
16           establish systematic and efficient water management and ensure the availability of
17           water for specific purposes in the country;
18      b. Integrate the different agencies of the government that have water-related functions into
19           the Department of Water Resources;
1       c. Create and update a road map of river and water sources;
2       d. Conduct continuing surveys and studies of the country's renewable water supply with a
3           view towards formulating long-term policies to balance the sustainability and optimal
4           use of water resources, defining the hydrologic boundaries of basis of the existing water
5           supply sources, and create and implement countrywide master plans for water resources
6           management and development;
7       e. Regulate and control the utilization, exploitation, abstraction, diversion, and
8           development of water resources, taking into account is equitable distribution, and
9           determine the standards of beneficial and priority uses of water in times of crisis and
10          national emergencies;
11      f. Assist and provide the National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) with
12          required date and inputs from and for the water sector in the formulation of the country's
13          short-term and long-term strategic development plans and actions;
14      g. Require all private individuals, corporations and government agencies affecting water
15          resources and the construction of hydraulic facilities in watershed or basin areas of
16          water supply sources to obtain a permit to construct such facilities from the Department;
17      h. Conduct and continue studies pertaining to water use, exploitation, development,
18          conservation or protection of waters, water resources, and watershed or basin areas;
19      i. Enter any property of public dominion or any private land or building for the purpose
20          of conducting hydrologic surveys and investigations and to determine compliance with
21          water laws and standards;
22      j. Issue and promulgate rules, regulations, and guidelines as may be necessary to
23          implement and enforce its powers and functions under this Act;
24      k. Ensure and protect the rights and welfare of consumers;
25      l. Exercise such other acts as are incident to or appropriate and necessary in connection
26          with the creation of the Department.

29   coordinate with the different sector regulators as follows:
30
31      a. The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) shall continue to have
32          the primary authority and responsibility for protecting the environment and for the
33          conservation of water and related natural resources, including protecting water bodies
34          from waste and pollution and shall promulgate rules, regulations and standards in this
1            regard. The Department shall coordinate with the DENR with respect to the
2            conservation of water resources, protection from sewage and septage waste, and
3            restoration from environment damage;
4
5        b. The Department of Health (DOH) shall have the primary authority and responsibility
6            for determining and enforcing drinking water quality and sanitation standards. The
7            Department shall coordinate with the DOH to achieve an integrated approach for
8            sanitation, including toilets, collection, treatment, and evaluation of wastewater;
9
10       c. The Department shall subsume responsibility from the Department of Public Works
11           and Highways (DPWH) for wholly or primarily water-related public works projects
12           including flood control, water resources development, sewerage, sanitation, and the
13           harnessing and impounding of water. The Secretaries of each department shall
14           coordinate on the development of evaluation criteria as necessary, to determine proper
15           allocation of public works projects between the two departments; and
16
17       d. The Department shall absorb the responsibility from the Department of Interior and
18           Local Government (DILG) on the management and operation of water supply systems
19           of local government units.
