                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The recent woes showed that nearly half of the Philippines' 532 water districts
were wasting a third of their water supply, creating water systems losses. The
Chairperson of the Local Water Utilities Administration (LWUA), Ronnie Ong, sought
the assistance of the lower chamber in reducing, if not totally eliminating, the nearly
30% annual water system losses of almost half of the water districts in the country.1


        Based on LWUA data, the water districts, which serve areas outside Metro
Manila, yield an average nonrevenue water (NRW) rate of 29.34%, which translates
to an annual water loss of some 488 million cubic meters which Is over half the capacity
of Angat Dam that supplies water to Metro Manila and nearby provinces.


        Furthermore, the heat of the summer also contributed to the shrinking water
level of the dams, which even led water companies to conserve the water supply
meant for thousands of people in cities and villages. Warmer temperatures mean that

 heatwaves shall exacerbate drought conditions and contribute towards water

         With all the problems encountered, the country Is currently facing a water crisis.
President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., last March 2023, recently signed an executive order
creating a water resource management office (WRMO) as part of the effort to address
the water crisis threatening the country. In his latest SONA, the President even stated
that water security deserves our special focus and remains hopeful of the passage of
a bill creating the Department of Water.3


        Thus, this bill seeks to create the Department of Water Resources Management^
which ultimately aims to provide Filipinos with a better quality of life. In more ways
than one, the vision of such a life does and will always include clean and safe water
for everyone, including the generations still to come. An integrated water resources
management framework shall guide the Department, and they shall exercise the
powers and functions necessary for the review, revision, and Integration of all
regulations and policies pertinent to the conservation and effective management of
water resources and water supply. Under the Department, systems shall be developed
towards greater efficacy in optimizing the use of all the freshwater sources in the
country as well as in improving water conservation.


        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 5          Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It Is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 6   recognize, protect, realize, and enhance the right of all Filipinos to safe, clean,
 7   affordable, and accessible drinking water as essential to the full enjoyment of life and
 8   all human rights and the protection and sustainability of the environment.
 9          In pursuit of said policy, this Act shall have the following objectives:
10      a) To prioritize and pursue the immediate attainment of universal access to safe,
11         adequate and sustainable water supply for all Filipinos and adopt all necessary
12         measures directed towards the realization of the human right to water; and
13      b) To strengthen and coordinate water resource planning and policymaking to
14         ensure sustainable and fair allocation and efficient use of water resources
15         between competing water uses and users, including but not limited to domestic

11   hundred thousand (Php500,000) but not more than Two million pesos (Php2,000,000)
12   for every year of non-compliance.
13          Sec. 8. A person is guilty of unlawful defilement of water when he intentionally
14   or with reckless imprudence, introduces, places, or causes to be introduced or placed
15   into a water supply, a defiling agent with the intent to cause sickness, physical injury,
16   severe disfigurement or death of another human being, or with intent to cause
17   irreparable harm to such water supply or to disturb the public peace.
18          Violators of this provision shall be penalized with Imprisonment of not less than
19   five (5) years but not more than thirty (30) years. A fine of not less than Five hundred
20   thousand pesos (Php500,000) but not more than Ten million pesos (Phpl0,000,000)
21   shall also be imposed.

 9          approach for sanitation, including toilets, collection, treatment, and evaluation
