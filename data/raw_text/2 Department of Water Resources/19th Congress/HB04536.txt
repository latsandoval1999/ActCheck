AN ACT CREATING THE DEPARTMENT OF WATER RESOURCES,
DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, APPROPRIATING
FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This legislation seeks the creation of the Department of Water
Resources as the primary government agency for the management of

water utilities in the country.

When President Ferdinand R. Marcos, Jr. addressed this Congress
during his first State of the Nation Address, he stressed on the
importance of coordination and cooperation between government
agencies and private entities and stakeholders. Further, he instructed
that the organizational structure of the government must be inquired at

in order to guarantee its full efficiency.

Today, it appears however that several government agencies still
perform similar functions and responsibilities, resulting to the

unnecessary overlapping of jobs.

===== [page 2] =====
Looking into several agencies, units and entities that handle the
management of water utilities, the country has the Local Water Utilities
Administration, the National Irrigation Administration, the National
Water Resources Board, the Laguna Lake Development Authority, as
well as the DENR, the DPWH, the DOH, the DOST, and the DOE, to

name a few.

The obvious overlapping and duplication of functions on the
management of water utilities create a cloud of confusion as to which
department handles the multitude of concerns thereto. In effect, the
needed government action could not be expedited since we have to point

our fingers first.

In 2019, representatives filed 35 separate bills that would create
a “Department of Water Resources” (DWR), resulting in a consolidated
bill, the House Bill (HB) No. 4944, authored by Albay Rep. Jose Maria
Clemente “Joey” Salceda and was approved by the House committees
on government reorganization and public works and highways in

November 2019.

House Biil No. 4944 aspired to make the DWR as the primary
government agency to handle the management of water utilities. This
representation shares the same vision for a Department that will
oversee and streamline the management of water resources and utilities
in the country. In this proposed legislation, the Department will serve
as the unified agency that will absorb all the functions and duties
related to the management of water resources which are currently

scattered from among several government agencies and entities.

With this bill, significant improvements could be expected in
managing this important water sector, such as the reduction of costs

for both the government and regulated entities, the avoidance of

duplication of functions, and the reconciliation of differences in
agencies exercising related responsibilities. This Department aims to
create regulatory synergy rather than dysfunction - and this shall serve
as one of our responses to the call of the President for coordination and

cooperation between government agencies.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly
sought.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Department of Water

Resources Act".
SECTION. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State:

(a) To ensure that our country’s water supply is safe, affordable, sufficient,

and sustainable;

(b) To guarantee the protection and preservation of the country’s water

resources;

{c) To unify the administration and management of water utilities in the

country;

(d) To prevent the duplication of functions in the government through the

creation of a single agency;

{e) To oversee the participation of responsible private sectors regarding our

water resources.

CHAPTER II
THE DEPARTMENT OF WATER RESOURCES

SECTION 4. Creation of the Department of Water Resources. - There is hereby
created and established the Department of Water Resources, hereinafter referred
to as the “Department”. The Department shall have an organizational structure,

powers, functions, and duties mandated by the immediately preceding sections.

SECTION 5. Mandate. - The Department shall be the primary policymaking,
planning, coordinating, implementing, and administrative agency of the
Executive Branch of the government that will oversee and streamline the
management of water resources and utilities in the country. The Department will
serve as the unified agency and shall absorb all the functions and duties related
to the management of water resources presently distributed to several

government agencies and instrumentailities.

The Department shall adopt the Integrated Water Resource Management (IWRM),
which is a process promoting the coordinated development and management of
water, land and related resources, as the strategic framework for national water

management, policymaking and planning.

and stakeholders, the provisions set forth in this Act;

separated from service within six (6) months from the effectivity of this Act as a
