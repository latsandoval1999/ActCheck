                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

         There are around thirty (30) government agencies existing to regulate the water
resources of the country, including the Manila Bay Coordinating Office of the
Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), the water quality
management section of the Environmental Management Bureau of the DENR, the
water supply sanitation unit of the Department of the Interior and Local Government
(DILG), the flood management planning and sediment functions of the Department
of Public Works and Highways (DPWH),                   the Lagima Lake Development
Authority (LLDA), the Local Water Utilities Administration (LWUA), the National
Irrigation Administration (NIA), and the Metro Manila Waterworks and Sewerage
System (MWSS), among others.


         The overwhelming competition for jurisdiction over funding and water
management makes water resources vulnerable to gross mismanagement and
exploitation. As a result, the budget allocated for water management multiplies
uimecessarily, and jobs are created superfluously or redundantly. These issues
needlessly contribute to the growing water crisis in the country and add to the
challenge of achieving our UN sustainable development goal of providing universal
access to safe and sustainable water and sanitation to Filipinos by 2030.


       There is a need to streamline water resources management to strengthen the
government's role in regulating water supply and sanitation, reduce costs, and
improve the regulation of privatized water infrastructure.


       This bill seeks to establish the Department of Water Resources which shall be
responsible for the comprehensive and systematic management and development of
the country's water resources. Under the proposed measure, the Department shall
integrate the different government agencies with water-related functions into the
Department of Water Resources. National poHcies, plans, and programs focusing on
water sewerage and sanitation shall be formulated, recommended, and implemented
to ensure water availability for specific uses.


       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.
