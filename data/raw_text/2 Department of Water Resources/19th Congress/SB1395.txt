                THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Water is a basic human need and access to safe and clean drinking water is a
basic human right as recognized by the United Nations. As a national water crisis
looms according to a study by the Japan International Cooperation Agency and the
Philippines' National Water Resources Board and all major cities of the Philippines have
been foreseen to all suffer from water shortage by 2025, it is only imperative and
necessary to take a much needed affirmative action to prevent this catastrophe.

        The Philippines, although an archipelagic country blessed with an abundant
source of water including rivers, lakes, river basins, and groundwater reservoirs, has
already been experiencing severe water shortage ahead of the forecasted 2025 water
crisis due to the impact of rapid population growth, urbanization, pollution, excessive
and inefficient use of water, and extended period of dry season brought about by
climate change. The water shortage is further aggravated by the overlapping functions
of water agencies in the country, not to mention the mismanagement on the part of
both private and public regulators. The enactment of a feasible Jong-term solution
for the protection of water is necessary to combat the threat of possible water crisis
in the future. To ensure adequate availability of water in the future, an efficient and
effective management and protection of the country's water sources is a great
necessity.
      This bill, therefore, seeks to create the Department of Water Resources
Management, which shall be the primary agency responsible for the comprehensive
and integrated water resources development and management in the Philippines.
Guided by the integrated water resources management framework and taking into
account climate risks, the Department shall exercise such powers and functions
needed to review, revise, harmonize and integrate policies and regulations for
conservation of water resources and effective management of potable water supply.
In managing water resources, the          Department shall aim to improve water
conservation, increase system efficiencies, and optimize the use of all freshwater in
the country including ground water, surface water, rainwater, run-offs, floods, storm
water and urban drainage, and treated wastewater to meet various needs.
       Following the lead of other countries who have found it wise to create a special
department to manage this vital resource of water including Japan, Australia, China,
and India, the availability of potable water in the Philippines and the continuing water
supply for domestic, agricultural and industrial consumption may be ensured and the
perennial problem that seriously affects the lives of the people in terms of health,
sanitation, food security, and livelihood may be effectively addressed.
       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

1          Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Department of Water
2    Resources Management Act of 2022."

3           Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State
4    to recognize, protect, realize, and enhance the right of all Filipinos to safe, clean,
5    affordable, and accessible drinking water as essential to the full enjoyment of life and
6    all human rights and the protection and sustainability of the environment.

7           In pursuit of said policy, this Act shall have the following objectives:

 8          a) To prioritize and pursue the immediate attainment of universal access to
 9             safe, adequate and sustainable water supply for all Filipinos and adopt ail
10             necessary measures directed towards the realization of the human right to
11             water; and
12          b) To strengthen and coordinate water resource planning and policymaking to
13             ensure sustainable and fair allocation and efficient use of water resources
14             between competing water uses and users, including but not limited to
 1             domestic and municipal use, irrigation, sanitation, power generation,
 2             farming, fisheries, commercial, and industrial use.

3          Sec. 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act, the following terms
4    are hereby defined as follows:

 5      a) Department refers to the Department of Water Resources Management.
 6      b) Rainwater harvesting facility refers to a flood control structure such as a vertical
 7         detention tank, horizontal water tank, open retarding basin, and multi-use
 8         water catchment area, or an on-site regulation pond used to prevent or delay
 9         the release of rainwater into the public drainage system.
10      c) Sanitation refers to sewage, sanitation, and septage management.
11      d) Sewerage refers to any system or network of sanitary pipelines, ditches,
12         channels, or conduits including pumping stations, lift stations, and force mains,
13         service connections including other constructions, devices, or appliances
14         appurtenant thereof, which includes the collection, sanitary piping transport,
15         pumping and treatment of water-borne human or animal waste removed from
16         residences, buildings, institutions, and industrial and commercial
17         establishments to point of sewage treatment plant disposal.
18      e) Sewage refers to water-carried waste in solution or suspension, including
19         sanitary waste (waste products of normal living), commercial waste, industrial
20         waste, agricultural waste, and surface runoff, conveyed by a sewerage system
21         for treatment or disposal. For purposes of this Act. The definition of septage
22         shall be included in the use of sewage.
23      f) Septage refers to sludge produced on individual on-site wastewater disposal
24         systems, principally septic tanks and cesspools.

25          The terms infrastructure and public works as used in this Act should be ready
26   to apply only to wholly or primarily water-related projects and shall not be read to
27   incorporate projects which would not otherwise fall within the purview of the
28   objectives, powers, and duties of the Department.

16           Sec. 7. Rainwater Harvesting Facility Requirement - An owner or
17   developer of a new commercial, institutional, and residential development project
18   anywhere in the Philippines with a land area of at least two thousand (2,000) square
19   meters and requiring the issuance of a building permit, shall reserve, develop, and
20   maintain at least three percent of the total area, exclusive of roads, service streets,
21   and alleys as a rainwater harvesting facility. Department of Human Settlements and
22   Urban Development, other appropriate government agencies, and the LGUs shall
23   require and ensure compliance with this provision and shall deny requests for permits
24   concerning the construction project until this provision is complied with. The design
25   and specifications for the rainwater harvesting facility shall be prescribed by the
26   Department. Projects still under construction sixty (60) days following the effectivity
27   of this Act shall be covered by this provision and shall comply within three (3) years
28   from the effectivity of this Act.

29          Violators of this provision shall suffer the penalty of a fine of not less than Five
30   hundred thousand (Php500,000) but not more than Two million pesos (Php2,000,000)
31   for every year of non-compliance.

32          Sec. 8. Pena! Provisions. - A person is guilty of unlawful defilement of water
33   when he intentionally or with reckless imprudence, introduces, places, or causes to be
34   introduced or placed into a water supply, a defiling agent with the intent to cause
35   sickness, physical injury, severe disfigurement or death of another human being, or
36   with intent to cause irreparable harm to such water supply or to disturb the public
37   peace.

38           Violators of this provision shall be penalized with imprisonment of not less than
39   five (5) years but not more than thirty (30) years. A fine of not less than Five hundred
1    thousand pesos (Php500,000) but not more than Ten million pesos (Phpl0,000,000)
2    shall also be imposed.

3           Sec. 9. Collection of Fines. - Fines collected from violators of the two
4    preceding provisions shall be place in a special fund and allocated to the Department
5    for the implementation of Section 6 of this Act.

30        Sec. 22. Interface with Other Sector Regulators. — The Department is
31   mandated to coordinate with the different sector regulators, as follows:

32      a) The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) shall continue
33         to have the primary authority and responsibility for protecting the environment
34         and for the conservation of water and related natural resources, including
35         protecting water bodies from waste and pollution and shall promulgate rules,
36         regulates, and standards in this regard. The Department shall coordinate with


                                               13
 1         the DENR with respect to the conservation of water resources, protection from
 2         sewage and septage waste, and restoration from environment damage.
 3      b) The Department of Health (DOH) shall have primary authority and responsibility
 4         for determining and enforcing drinking water quality and sanitation standards.
 5         The Department shall coordinate with the DOH to achieve an integrated
 6         approach for sanitation, including toilets, collection, treatment, and evaluation
 7         of wastewater.
 8      c) The Department shall subsume responsibility from the Department of Public
 9         Works and Highways (DPWH) for wholly or primarily water related public works
10         projects including flood control, water resources development, sewerage,
11         sanitation, and the harnessing and impounding of water. The Secretaries of
12         each department shall coordinate on the development of evaluation criteria, as
13         necessary, to determine proper allocation of public works projects between the
14         two departments.
15      d) The Department shall absorb the responsibility from the Department of Interior
16         and Local Government (DILG) on the management and operation of water
17         supply systems of local government units.

18          Sec. 23. Transfer of Rights and Obligations. — The Department shall, by
19   virtue of this Act, be subrogated to all the rights and assume all the obligations of the
20   relevant parts of any government agency or unit whose powers and duties have been
21   transferred to the Department, including the DPWH, National Irrigation Administration
22   (NIA), National Water Resources Board (NWRB), Metropolitan Waterworks and
23   Sewerage System (MWSS), River Basin Control Office, Manila Bay Coordinating Office,
24   Local Water Utilities Administration (LWUA), Metro Manila Development Authority
25   (MMDA), Department of Environment and Natural Resources, the Department of
26   Interior and Local Government (DILG), Laguna Lake Development Authority, Pasig
27   River Rehabilitation Commission, the Water Resources Regional Offices, and all other
28   offices whose powers are transferred by this Act.

29          The transfer of powers and functions in Section 18 above, as applicable, and
30   agencies attached thereto, as herein provided for, shall take effect within one hundred
31   eighty (180) days after the effectivity of this Act. The foregoing transfer of powers
32   and functions shall include all applicable funds, personnel, records, property, and
33   equipment, as may be necessary. All offices shall continue to function under their
34   present mandates until transition is effected as provided for under this Act.

35          All rights and obligations of said government agencies are hereby transferred
36   to and assumed by the Department and shall be acted upon in accordance with the
37   rules and regulations of the Commission on Audit and other pertinent laws, rules, and
38   regulations.
