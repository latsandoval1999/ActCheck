the MWSS, the government was able to pass on all the financing burden to the private sector,
together with huge responsibilities for expanding and improving the system, while lowering
tariffs and significantly increasing government tax revenues through VAT and income tax.

The main difficulty in privatizing water utilities in a similar wal as the case of MWSS

vague as to whether jurisdiction lies with the LVWUA, NWRB oreven LGUs. Alen it requires a
differant set of skilis to properly regulate privatized water systems and we need to develop this

The main objectives of the proposed bill are to ensure universal access to water supply
and sanitation services, and to encourage responsible private sector participation, fostering
@nd prioritizing infrastructure and public works that adopt innovative solutions a nd international
best practices to address the challenges of climate change. The bill also creates a Department
of Water Resources ta be the primary agency responsible for the comprehensive and
