                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

7          The state is directly responsible for providing water security to all the citizens
8   and inhabitants of its territory. Water nurtures and sustains life. Civil societies
9   worldwide can neither endure nor survive without property managing their water
0   resources for the effective use of their people.

2           The philippines is a water-rich country. According to the world Bank (2015),
3   the annual available water volume per capita in the country is twice that of Asia and
4   six times the global scarcity threshold. Yet, many of our people are deprived of
5    efficient access to affordable, safe, and reliable potable water and to proper
6   sanitation services. This is because the governance and maintenance of our water
7   resources remains fragmented and uncoordinated. To date, there are over 30
8   government agencies at both the national and tocaJ teveJs inpJementing separate
9   and disharmonized policies, plans, and programs to develop and operate water
0   facttities and related infrastructure for various uses, and to regulate water use,
1    monitor water-related financial and technical parameters, and set water tariffs.

         To this end, this bill proposes to create the Department of Water to serve as
4   the primary agency responsible for national policy-making, coordination, and
5   management of all the water resources in the philippines. Under this Department, all
6   government agencies related to water shall be rationalized. Integrated Water
7   Resources Management (lwRM) shat+ be adopted as the strategie framewock for
8   water management policy-making and planning in the country. IWRM promotes the
9   coordinated development and management of water, land, and related resources in
0   order to maximize economic and social welfare in an equitable manner without
1   compromising the sustainability of vital ecosystems and the environment. This bill
2   also proposes to create an independent water Regulatory commission to ensure the
3   effective and adequate delivery of water and sanitation services throughout the
4   country under a credible, transparent, and accountable regulatory framework. This
5   Commission shall always protect the consumer welfare and reject abusive practices
6   in the water industry. Finally, this bill seeks to create the Nafronat water corporation
7    as the corporate arm of the Department of water to institutionalize the collaboration
8   between the government and the private sector for the provision of accessible,
9   reliable, and affordable water supply, distribution, and sanitation services throughout
 i   the country via established arrangements such as Joint ventures and public-Private
 2   Partnerships (PPPs).
