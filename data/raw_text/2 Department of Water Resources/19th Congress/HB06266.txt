                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

Water access is a fundamental human right. However, all water is the property
of the State, which will control how it is developed and used.

This bill creates the Department of Water Resources, which is mandated to
ensure the best possible use of water resources for domestic and municipal water
supply, sanitation, irrigation, hydropower, industry, navigation, flood
management, and recreation. Among other salient parts, the Department shall
be the primary agency responsible for comprehensive and integrated
identification and mapping of all water resources, planning, policy formulation,
and management of ownership, appropriation, utilization, exploitation,
development, and protection of water resources in the Philippines.

Likewise, the Department will also be the primary organization in charge of
developing plans and policies to ensure that every Filipino has access to safe,
sufficient, affordable, and sustainable water and better sanitation services.

The Department will perform the duties and exercise the authority formerly held
by the Pasig River Rehabilitation Commission. This measure will streamline the
processes that balance utilization and conservation of our precious water
resources.

Hence, the passage of this measure is sought.

 6         SEC. 6. Guiding Principles. –In the pursuit of its mandate, the Department
 7   shall be guided by the following key principles:
 8
 9     (a) The concept of good water governance for water security and sufficiency
10         shall be adopted across all levels of governance by stakeholders such as
11         the     national      government,   local    government,   non-government
12         organizations, private organizations, individuals, even global societal
13         actors, and in various sectors such as water supply, irrigation, fisheries,
14         hydropower, navigation, ecosystem services, flood management, climate
15         change, and recreation. It shall entail publicly transparent, socially
16         accountable, and multi-stakeholder participatory water policy planning,
17         management, and decision-making processes.
18      (b) Access to safe water supply and improved sanitation is a human right that
19         needs to be fulfilled and protected.
20      (c) Integrated Water Resource Management (IWRM) shall be adopted as the
21         basic framework which is the coordinated development and management
22         of water, land, and related resources within hydrological boundaries to
23         optimize economic and social welfare without compromising the
24         sustainability of vital ecosystems. The IWRM Framework shall include an
25         enabling environment that utilizes proper policies, legal instruments, and
26         institutional frameworks for effective implementation, participatory and
27         collaborative water policy and management decisions, and management
28         instruments for efficient use of updated and accurate data, science-based
29         decision support systems, proper information, education, communication
30         (IEC) programs, assessment and allocation tools, or regulatory functions.
31         It shall observe the mutual accountability mechanism among stakeholders
32         at the national, regional, and global levels reinforcing multi-stakeholder
33         decision-making following a framework with specific, measurable,
34         attainable, relevant, and timely actions.
35      (d) Each river basin has its unique characteristics relating to the physical,
36         socio cultural, and other aspects, and water management shall be
37         performed at river-basin level, involving all the stakeholders at that
38         particular river basin.

20      ensure that water is optimized, that water efficiency and conservation

30      people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with the rhythm and
31      harmony of nature.

33      planning and policy making to ensure sustainable and fair allocation and
34      efficient use of water resources among competing uses and users;
35   (d) Ensure that integrated water resources management is strongly
36      coordinated with land use and coastal and multi-hazard planning and
37      management;

 9         SEC. 30. Setting Tariffs, Rates, and Other Charges. – Regulatory units shall
10   establish tariffs, rates and other charges which are fair and reasonable, and
11   ensure economic viability and a fair return on investments.
12
13         Tariffs, rates and charges shall be based on and consistent with a rate-
14   setting methodology that the Commission shall, after due consultation, define
15   and publish, taking into account the following, among others:
16         (a) Reasonable and prudent capital and recurrent costs of providing the
17             service including a reasonable rate of return on capital;
18         (b) Efficiency of the service;
19         (c) Incentives for enhancement of efficiency;
20         (d) Willingness to pay of the customers/consumers;
21         (e) Equity considerations; and
22         (f) Administrative simplicity.
23
24         Tariffs, rates, and charges set by the regulatory units shall be presumed
25   valid and reasonable unless a protest or contest is filed with the Commission.
