                                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE



       Our country is one of the most disaster-prone countries in the world that
frequently experience natural calamities such as earthquakes swarms, phreatic
eruptions, and strong typhoons, among others.

      ThePhilippinesisgeographicallyloratedinthePacificRIngofFire,alsoknown
as the Circum-Pacific Belt, where majority of the world's volcanoes with active
volcanic and tectonic plates are situatedl. On a yearly average, the Philippine
Volcanology and Seismology (PIHLVOCS) reports around 100 to 150 earthquake
occurrences in the country, with an average magnitude of 4.0 and above2.

        Moreover, our country is likewise lox:ated in the Typhoon Belt - an area in the
western Pacific Ocean where nearly one-third of tropical cyclones form. This explains
the frequent occurrence of typhoons entering our territory - the Philippine Area of
Responsibility (PAR). Approximately, 19 typhoons pass into the country; around six
to nine of them make a landfall, annually3. With the effects of climate change,
particularlythewarmingofseasurfacetemperaturesandsea-levelrise,tropicalstorm
wind speeds are intensifying which have the potential of delivering more damage
should the same make a landfall4. In fact, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric

       It is therefore imperative for the State to formulate a proactive approach for the

       This bill proposes for the creation of the Department of Disaster Resilience
(DDR) as the principal agency responsible for leading, organizing, and managing the
national effort to reduce disaster risk, prepare for and respond to disaster, recover,
and rehabifitate, and re-build affected communities. While our established
government institution, specifically the National Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Council (NDRRMC), established through Republic Act No. 10121, has
been compliant to its mandate (to formulate and coordinate policies in anticipation of
and in response to disasters), there is a need, however, to estabfish an agency that whl
take the lead in the whole-of-government and whole-of-society approach - in
handling a comprehensive policy on disaster risk reduction and promoting resilience
amid the climate change impacts.

      The DDR is envisioned to oversee and coordinate the preparation,
implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of disaster and climate resilience plan,
programs, projects, and activities. It sham provide leadership in the continuous
developmentofstrategicandsystematicapproachestodisasterprevention,mitigation
preparedness response, rehabilitation, and recovery.

       Furthermore, as proposed, the DDR shall provide a clear and comprehensive
direction in the implementation of plans, programs, and projects to reduce the risk of
natural hazards, effects of climate change, and manage the impact of disasters. It shall
establish Regional Disaster Resilience Offices (RDROs) in each region and create
mechanisms and programs to ensure collaboration and coordination among different
RDROs.

SECTION 99. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be taken from the current fiscal year's appropriations of all agencies

necessary for the operation of the Department and the implementation of this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
