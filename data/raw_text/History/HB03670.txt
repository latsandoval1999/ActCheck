                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The State is responsible for providing water security to all citizens and inhabitants of
its country. The Philippines as a water-rich country needs to rationalize and streamline the
implementation of its existing and sometimes overlapping policies, laws and regulations
affecting its water resources including irrigation, sewage and sanitation.

      In President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.’s State of the Nation Address on 25 July 2022, the
President called on Congress to pave the way for the creation of a Department of Water
Resources to adopt an integrated water resource management and address the water
supply issues in the country especially in the urban areas.

        The bill proposes to create the Department of Water Resources to serve as the
primary agency responsible for national policy-making, coordination and management of
all the water resources in the Philippines. All government agencies related to water shall be
rationalized under this Department. Integrated Water Resources Management shall be
adopted as the strategic framework for water management policy-making and planning in
the country.

      The bill also proposes to create an independent Water Regulatory Commission to
ensure the effective and adequate delivery of water and sanitation services throughout the
country under a credible, transparent and accountable regulatory framework.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

218          across all levels of governance by stakeholders such as the national government, local

241      other aspects, and water management shall be performed at river-basin level, involving all the

243

249   (f) The development of water resources shall include strategies for the mitigation of water-

251

253      that water is fairly and sustainably managed.
