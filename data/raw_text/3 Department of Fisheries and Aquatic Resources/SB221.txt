                                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

          The Philippines, an archip>elagic nation, heavily relies on its fisheries sector for
food security and livelihood. In the first quarter of 2025, the country's fisheries
output reached 1.01 million metric tons contributing Php 55.10 Billion to the national
agriculture and fisheries value, a 1.5% increase in the previous year.1


          Projections from the UN's Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) anticipate
the Philippines remaining the 5th largest fish producer in Asia by 2032, with
production expected to increase by 12% to nearly 2.9 million metric tons (MT),
primarily driven by aquaculture.2


          Despite this vital role and growth potential, Filipino farmers (27.0%) and
fisherfolks (27.4%) consistently registered among the highest poverty incidences in

           The massive scope and unique challenges of the fisheries and aquaculture
sector, including issues like overfishing, illegal fishing, environmental degradation,
and limited access to resources, often exceed the capacity of the Bureau of Fisheries
and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), a bureau under the Department of Agriculture.


     To strategically develop and sustainably manage the country's vast fisheries and
aquatic resources, and to uplift the millions of flsherfolk from poverty, this bill
proposes the creation of a dedicated Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources
(DFAR). This new department will provide the focused attention and support
necessary for the comprehensive development, effective management, protection,
and conservation of marine wealth, ensuring the needs of fisherfolks are adequately
addressed and their welfare is prioritized.


          In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly
sought.

 3        Sec. 2. Declaration of Policies- It is hereby declared the policy of the State to:
 4        (a) promote the sustainable development and management of all fisheries,
 5            marine, and aquatic resources in Philippine waters, including its exclusive
 6            economic zones (EEZs), in the adjacent high seas and in coastal and inland
 7            aquatic resources consistent with the objective of maintaining         asound
 8            ecological    balance,   protecting and enhancing       the quality of     the
 9            environment;
10        (b) protect and conserve the nation's aquatic wealth in its archipelagic waters,
11            territorial seas, and EEZs and reserve its use and enjoyment exclusively to
12            Filipino citizens;
13        (c) safeguard the rights of small-scale fishermen in the preferential use of
14            communal marine, fisheries and aquatic resources;
 1        (d) ensure sustainable fisheries production in inland aquatic resources
 2            consistent with the objective of ensuring availability of food staples and
 3            achieving food security;
 4        (e) protect fisherfolk communities from the adverse effect of climate change
 5            and natural and man-made hazards;
 6        Sec. 3. The Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources- The Department
 7   of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources, hereinafter referred to as the "Department", is
 8   hereby created in accordance with the provisions of this Act.
 9        Sec. 4. Mandate- The Department shall have primary jurisdiction over the
10   management, conservation, development, protection, utilization, and disposition of
11   all fishery and aquatic resources of the country, including the habitats of fish and all
12   other marine life and other activities which impact these habitats, with the exception
13   of municipal waters, which shall remain under the jurisdiction of local government
14   units (LGUs): Provided, That LGUs shall manage their respective municipal waters in
15   accordance with national fishery policies, laws, rules, and regulations, and in a
16   manner consistent with the policies set forth herein.
17        The Department shall have the authority to supervise and regulate the
18   production and capture of fish and fishery products within its jurisdiction, as well as
19   the processing and marketing of all fisheries and aquatic products in the country.
20        The Department shall also have the responsibility of rationalizing all ocean
21   sector initiatives which impact on fish and marine resources or their habitat,
22   including, but not limited to, exploration and development of marine energy,
23   promotion and management of coastal and marine tourism, designation of shipping
24   lanes, and identification of and management of protected areas and marine industry
25   development areas.
26        Sec. 5. Powers and Functions- The Department shall exercise the following
27   powers and functions:
28        (a) Exercise authority and the responsibility over the utilization of the ocean,
29            including its fisheries and their habitat;
30        (b) Coordinate and monitor the implementation of fisheries and ocean sector,
31            statutes, policies, plans and programs for all maritime components;

14        Sec. 6. Composition- The Department shall be headed by a Secretary. The
15   Department proper shall be composed of the Office of the Secretary, the Offices of
16   the Undersecretaries and Assistant Secretaries.
17        Sec. 7. The Secretary of the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources-
18   The Secretary of the Department, hereinafter referred to as the Secretary, shall be
19   appointed by the President subject to the confirmation of the Commission on
20   Appointments, and shall exercise the following duties and functions:
21        (a) Exercise supervision and control over all bureaus, institutes, center and
22            offices under the Department;
23        (b) Exercise authority and assume responsibility for the performance of the
24            powers and functions of the Department;
25        (c) Provide advice to the President on the promulgation of policies, rules and
26            regulations, and other matters pertinent to the sustainable development,
27            utilization and management of fisheries and other aquatic resources;
28        (d) Formulate policies and standards for the efficient and effective operation of
29            the Department in accordance with the established plans, programs, and
30            standards of the Government;
31        (e) Promulgate rules and regulations, and other issuances to carry out the
32            mandates, objectives, plans, programs and projects of the Department;
1         (f) Perform such other functions as may be provided by law or assigned by the
2              President.
3         Sec. 8. Undersecretaries- The Secretary shall be assisted by three (3)
4    Undersecretaries,      who   shall     be    appointed    by     the   President   upon   the
5    recommendation of the Secretary: Provided, that two (2) of the Undersecretaries
6    shall be career officers.
 7        Sec. 9. Assistant Secretaries.- The Secretary shall be assisted by three (3)
 8   Assistant Secretaries who shall be appointed by the President                      upon the
 9   recommendation of the Secretary. Provided, that two (2) of the Assistant Secretaries
10   shall be career officers.
11        Sec. 10.      Regionai Offices.- The Department may establish, operate, and
12   maintain a regional office in the different administrative regions of the country as
13   may be necessary. Each regional office shall be headed by a Regional Director, who
14   may be assisted by one (1) Assistant RegionalDirector. The Regional Director and
15   Assistant Regional Director shall be appointed by the President upon the
16   recommendation of the Secretary The regional offices shall have support staffs as
17   may be necessary to perform the following duties and functions:
18        (a) Implement laws, policies, plans, projects, rules and regulations of the
19             Department;
20        (b) Provide efficient and effective frontline services to the fisheries sector,
21             particularly to municipal farmers;
22         (c) Coordinate with regional offices of other departments, offices, and
23             agencies, as well as with the LGUs within their respective jurisdictions; and
24        (d) Perform such other functions as may be provided by law or assigned by the
25             Secretary.
26        Sec.    11.    Qualifications.-    No     person    shall    be   appointed   Secretary,
27   Undersecretary, or Assistant Secretary of the Department unless the appointee is a
28   citizen and a resident of the Republic of the Philippines, of good moral character, of
29   proven integrity, and with at least seven (7) years of competence and expertise in
30   any of the following: biology, ecology, biogeochemistry, biotechnology, marine
31   science, limnology, oceanography, fisheries management or other related disciplines,
32   either in the private or public sector.

30        Sec. 13. Separation and Retirement from Service- Employees who are
31   separated from service within six (6) months from the effectivity of this Act as a
32   result of the consolidation and/or reorganization under the provisions of this Act
 1   shall receive separation benefits to which they may be entitled to under Executive
 2   Order No. 366, s. 2004: Provided, That those who are qualified to retire under
 3   existing retirement laws shall be allowed to retire and receive retirement benefits to
 4   which they may be entitled to under applicable laws and issuances.
 5         Sec. 14. Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern.- Subject to the approval
 6   of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Department shall
 7   determine its organizational structure and create new bureaus, divisions or units as
 8   it may deem necessary, and shall appoint officers and employees of the Department
 9   in accordance with the civil services law, rules and regulations.
10         Sec. 15.   Transition Period.- The transfer of functions, assets, funds,
11   equipment, properties, transactions and personnel of the affected agencies, and the
12   formulation of internal organic structure, staffing pattern, operating system, and
13   revised budget of the Department, shall be completed within six (6) months from
14   effectivity of this Act., during which time, the existing personnel shall continue to
15   assume their posts in hold over capacities until new appointments are issued:
16   Provided, That the Department, in coordination with the DBM, shall determine and
17   create new positions, the funding requirements of which shall not exceed the
18   equivalent cost of positions that may be abolished or reorganized.
19         Sec. 16. Appropriations.- The amount needed for the initial implementation of
20   this Act shall be taken from the current fiscal year's appropriation of the BEAR and
21   all agencies herein absorbed by or attached to the Department. Thereafter, the
22   amount needed for the operation and maintenance of the Department shall be
23   included in the General Appropriations Act.
24         Sec. 17. Implementing Rules and Regulations.- Within sixty (60) days from the
25   effectivity of this Act, the BEAR, Civil Service Commission (CSC) and DBM, in
26   coordination with the DA, PEDA, NERDI, NEARMC, and other government agencies
27   concerned, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to effectively
28   implement the provisions of this Act.
29         Sec. 18. Separability Clause. - If, for any reason, any provision of this Act or
