EXPLANATORY NOTE

Twenty-eight (28) years after the enactment of Republic Act No. 8435, otherwise
known as the Agriculture and Fisheries Modernization Act of 1997 (AFMA), the agriculture
and fisheries sector remains mired in persistent challenges. Despite multiple oversight bodies
like the Congressional Oversight Committee on Agricultural and Fisheries Modernization
(COCAFM) and the Congressional Oversight Committee on Anti-Agricultural Economic
Sabotage (COCAAES), fragmented policy implementation continues to undermine
modernization efforts. This measure therefore seeks to create a high-level, independent, and
time-bound Congressional Commission on Agriculture, Fisheries, and Food Self-Sufficiency
(AGRICOM), to harmonize mandates, conduct comprehensive review, and recommend
coherent reforms for national food security.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT
CREATING A CONGRESSIONAL COMMISSION ON AGRICULTURE,

FISHERIES, AND FOOD SELF-SUFFICIENCY (AGRICOM) TO REVIEW, ASSESS,
AND EVALUATE THE STATE OF PHILIPPINE AGRICULTURE AND
RECOMMEND REFORMS, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled,

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Congressional
Commission on Agriculture, Fisheries, and Food Self-Sufficiency (AGRICOM) Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote agricultural
modernization, food self-sufficiency, and rural industrialization by strengthening the welfare
of farmers, fisherfolk, cooperatives, and agribusiness stakeholders. The State shall enhance
institutional coordination among the Department of Agriculture (DA), Department of Agrarian
Reform (DAR), Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), Cooperative
Development Authority (CDA), and other concerned agencies.

SEC. 3. Objectives. - The AGRICOM shall: (a) conduct a comprehensive review of
all agriculture and fisheries programs; (b) identify policy gaps and redundancies; (c) harmonize
agency mandates; (d) promote innovation, climate resilience, and digital agriculture; (e)
strengthen market linkages and post-harvest logistics; (f) encourage public-private
partnerships; and (g) recommend targeted, measurable, and time-bound reforms.

SEC. 4. Creation of the Commission. — There is hereby created a Congressional
Commission on Agriculture, Fisheries, and Food Self-Sufficiency (AGRICOM) tasked to
review, assess, and recommend reforms to ensure a modern, efficient, sustainable, and food-
secure agricultural sector.

SEC. 5. Composition. — The Commission shall be composed of twelve (12) members:
six (6) from the Senate and six (6) from the House of Representatives, with at least one member
from the Minority of each chamber. The Chairpersons of the Senate Committee on Agriculture,
Food and Agrarian Reform, and the House Committee on Agriculture and Food shall serve as
Co-Chairpersons.
===== [page 3] =====
SEC. 6. Duration. — The AGRICOM shall accomplish its mandate within three (3) years
from organization, unless extended by Congress.

SEC. 7. Powers and Functions. — The AGRICOM shall have the power to: (a) review
and evaluate agriculture and fisheries bureaucracies and programs; (b) recommend structural
and policy reforms; (c) hold hearings and summon witnesses; (d) engage with stakeholders; (e)
commission studies; (f) recommend legislation; and (g) submit semi-annual and final reports
to Congress.

SEC. 8. Technical Secretariat. - A Technica! Secretariat, headed by an Executive
Director with the rank of Executive Director IV, shall be organized to provide administrative
and research support. The ED must be a recognized expert in agriculture and management.

SEC. 9. Research Arm. — The Philippine Institute for Development Studies (PIDS), in
coordination with State Universities and Colleges (SUCs) with agriculture and fisheries
curricula, shall serve as the official research arm of the AGRICOM.

SEC. 10. Advisory Council. — An Agriculture and Fisheries Advisory Council shall
assist the Commission. Its members shall come from academia, the business sector, LGUs,
government agencies, and civil society organizations.

SEC. 11. Standing Committees. — The AGRICOM may form standing committees
composed of members or experts from relevant sectors to facilitate consultations and technical
studies.

SEC. 12. Appropriations. — An initial amount of Two Hundred Million Pesos
(P200,000,000.00) shall be appropriated for the implementation of this Act, chargeable against
the current appropriations of Congress. Thereafter, necessary funds shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 13. Separability Clause. — \f any provision of this Act is declared invalid, the
remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 14. Repealing Clause. — All laws, rules, and regulations inconsistent with this
Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

SEC. 15. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
