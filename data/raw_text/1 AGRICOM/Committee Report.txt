SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Congressional
Commission on Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Security (AGRICOM) Act”.

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right of all farmers, farmworkers, and landowners, as well as
cooperatives, and other independent farmers' organizations. Further, the State shall
provide support to the agriculture sector through appropriate technology and
research, and adequate financial, production, marketing, and other support services.

SEC. 3. Creation of AGRICOM. — There is hereby created a Congressional
Commission on Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Security, hereinafter referred to as the
AGRICOM, to undertake a comprehensive national assessment and evaluation of the
performance of the Philippine agriculture and fisheries sector for the purpose of

recommending transformative, concrete and targeted reforms in the sector with the

SEC. 6. National Assessment and Evaluation. — Pursuant to Section 3 of

this Act, the national assessment and evaluation shall include the following:

(a) Review of the observance of the mandates of the laws that created the

DA, DAR, DENR and other government agencies in charge of agriculture;

(b) Determination of factors that have contributed to the continuing failure

in performance of the three (3) agencies in modernizing agriculture;

(c) Recommendation of specific, targeted and time-bound solutions to
enable the three (3) agencies to improve their performance vis-a-vis

measurable indicators;

(d) Identification of best practices of various national and international
agricultural institutions that can be adopted across the entire agricultural

system;

(e) Proposal for a monitoring and evaluation plan to ensure the timely

achievement of set targets; and

(f) Determination of the role of local government units (LGUs) in the delivery

of basic agricultural services.

SEC. 8. Technical Secretariat. — A Technical Secretariat, to be headed by the
Executive Director, shall be organized to provide technical and administrative support to
the AGRICOM in the exercise of its powers and performance of its functions under Section
5 of this Act.

The Executive Director shall organize its staff and technical panel, and hire and
appoint such employees and personnel, whether temporary, contractual, or on
consultancy, and determine their compensation subject to applicable civil service laws,
rules and regulations, with a view to ensuring a competent and efficient secretariat.

The Technical Secretariat shall co-exist with AGRICOM.

SEC. 9. Executive Director. - The Executive Director shall be appointed by the
AGRICOM and shall be a recognized expert in the fields of agriculture and fisheries.

The Executive Director shall work full-time and hold the rank, privileges, and
emoluments of an Executive Director IV (Salary Grade 29).

SEC. 10. Standing Committees. — The AGRICOM shall establish standing
committees, which shall each be chaired by a member. The Committees shall engage
experts from multisectoral groups and resource persons from the public and private
sectors in their work of providing recommendations for the Commission’s final output.

Council. — An Agriculture and Fisheries Legislation and Policy Advisory Council is
hereby created to provide AGRICOM with expert assistance and advice. The members
of the Advisory Council shall always be present in all meetings of AGRICOM. The

SEC. 12. Duration of AGRICOM. — The AGRICOM shall accomplish its
mandate within three (3) years from its organization. It shall organize itself and
commence its mandate upon the effectivity of this Act.

SEC. 13. Appropriations. — The amount of Two hundred million pesos
(P200,000,000.00) for the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against
available appropriations of the Senate and the House of Representatives. Thereafter,
the amount necessary for its continuous implementation shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act subject to existing budgeting, accounting and
auditing rules and regulations.

SEC. 14. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder thereof not otherwise affected shall remain in full force
and effect.

SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. — All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
letters of instruction, proclamations or administrative regulations that are inconsistent
with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 16. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days
following the completion of its publication in the Official Gazette, or in a newspaper of
general circulation.
