   CREATING A CONGRESSIONAL COMMISSION ON AGRICULTURE TO

                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article II, Section 1 of the 1987 Constitution states that "The State shall promote
industrialization and full employment based on sound agricultural development."

      From this constitutional provision, Congress enacted Republic Act No. 8435
otherwise known as the "Agriculture and Fisheries Modernization Act of 1997" (AFMA).
The AFMA emphasized the welfare of small farmers and fisherfolk (SFF) in its push
toward agriculture modernization. The AFMA aims to:
      i.     To modernize the agriculture and fisheries sectors by transforming these
             sectors from a resource-based to a technology-based industry;
      ii.    To enhance profits and incomes in the agriculture and fisheries sectors;
      iii.   To ensure the accessibility, availability, and stable supply of food at all
             times;
      IV.    To encourage horizontal and vertical integration, consolidation, and
             expansion of agriculture and fisheries activities;
             To promote people empowerment by strengthening people's
             organizations (POs), cooperatives, and nongovernment organizations
             (NGOs);
      VI.    To pursue a market-driven approach to enhance the comparative
             advantage of the agriculture and fisheries sectors in the world market;
      VII.   To induce the agriculture and fisheries sectors to ascend continuously on
             the value-added ladder;
      VIII. To adopt policies that will promote industry dispersal and rural
             industrialization;
       ix.   To provide social and economic adjustment measures that increase
             productivity and improve market efficiency while ensuring the protection
             and preservation of the environment and equity for SFF


       Twenty-eight years after its passage, the agricultural and fisheries sector
remains hampered by various challenges. According to the 2023 Family Income and
Expenditure Survey (FIES), farmers and fisherfolk continues to record among the
highest poverty rates on the 11 basic sectors identified under Republic Act No. 8425 or
the Social Reform and Poverty Alleviation Act, with poverty rates of 27.0% and 27.4%,
respectively, compared to the national rate of 15.5%. Rice importation continues to
increase year on year, with total rice imports reaching 4.68 million metric tons. The
share of agriculture in the GDP remains stagnant, with agricultural output contracting
by a record 2.2% in 2024.

      Given these various challenges, it is imperative that the country follows the
example of the Education Commission or EdCom and form a high-level Agriculture
Commission or AgriCom. This bill aims to create an Agriculture Commission to
thoroughly examine the agriculture bureaucracy and recommend institutional reforms
to enable the modernization of the sector.


      For the abovementioned reasons, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1       SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Congressional
 2   Commission on Agriculture (AGRICOM) Act".
 3
 4          SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the declared policy of the State to protect
 5   and promote the right of all farmers, farmworkers, and landowners, as well as
 6   cooperatives, and other independent farmers' organizations. Further,the Stateshall
 7   provide support to the agriculture sector through appropriate technology and research,
 8   and adequate financial, production, marketing, and other support services.
 9
10          SEC. 3. Objectives of the AGRICOM. - The AGRICOM shall have the following
11   objectives:
12         a) Develop and recommend policies that promote innovation, sustainability,
13            and competitiveness in agriculture;
14         b) Set specific, targeted, measurable and time-bound solutions that are
15            products of a comprehensive assessment and evaluation, effective planning,
16            and strategic investments in agriculture;
17         c) Develop medium and long-term development plans for the agriculture sector
18            with specific goals for productivity, export growth, irrigation, infrastructure,
19            market access, research and development, and rural development;
20         d) Promote agricultural research, innovation, and technology adoption, in
21            coordination with the Philippine Institute for Development Studies (PIDS),
22            to increase agricultural productivity and climate resilience;
  1          e) Develop and implement programs that shall further educate and train
 2               farmers, farmworkers, cooperatives, and agri-entrepreneurs in modern and
 3               sustainable practices to increase and improve production;
 4           f) Recommend the improvement in the harmonization of the policies and
 5               programs of the Department of Agriculture (DA), Department of Agrarian
 6               Reform (DAR), the Department of Environment and Natural Resources
 7               (DENR), the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA), and other
 8               government agencies in charge of agriculture;
 9           g) Identify domestic and international markets for the agricultural products in
10               order to support farmers and agri-businesses and assist them in complying
11               with global market standards;
12           h) Encourage and facilitate public-private partnerships (PPPs) to boost value-
13              adding, agro-industrialization, and export readiness;
14          i) Coordinate efforts and coordinate with the concerned departments and
15              agencies, local government units (LGUs), private sector, and other
16              stakeholders to implement national and local agricultural plans and
17              strategies;
18          j) Prioritize the adoption use of science, technology and innovation through
19              the promotion of digital farming;
20          k) Institutionalize policy and structural reforms necessary to meet the new
21              challenges to agriculture;
22          I) Recommend the adoption and institutionalization of relevant and meaningful
23              assessment tools, such as satellite information, yield analysis, sustainable
24              land management and other assessment tools, based on the best global
25              practices which shall be used by relevant agencies and institutions for their
26              continued monitoring, evaluation, and development.
27          m) Conduct multi-stakeholder consultations, using proper mechanisms and
28              guidance; and
29          n) Report to Congress its accomplishments on a quarterly basis, its findings
30              and recommendations on actions to be taken by Congress, the departments,
31              and other government agencies concerned with agriculture, and provide a
32              final report at the end of the existence of the AGRICOM;
33            SEC. 4. Creation of the AGRICOM. — There is hereby created a Congressional
34    Commission on Agriculture hereinafter referred to as the "AGRICOM", to undertake a
35    comprehensive national assessment and evaluation of the performance of the
36    Philippine agriculture sector for the purpose of recommending reforms in the sector
37    with the end in view of making the Philippines globally competitive in the agriculture
38    sector.
39
 1          SEC. 5. Composition of the AGRICOM. — The AGRICOM shall be composed of
 2   ten (10) members, with five (5) members from the Senate and five (5) members from
 3   the House of Representatives.
 4
 5   The Chairperson of the Senate Committee on Agriculture and the Chairperson of the
 6   House Committee on Agriculture shall serve as Co-Chairpersons of the Commission.
 7   The other four (4) members each from the Senate and the House of Representatives
 8   shall be designated by the Senate President and the Speaker of the House of
 9   Representatives, respectively.
10

11   The members of the Commission shall receive no compensation, but travelling,
12   communications, and other necessary expenses shall be allowed, subject to existing
13   laws, and rules and regulations.
14
15          SEC. 6. Duration of AGRICOM. — The AGRICOM shall accomplish its mandate
16   within three (3) years from its organization. It shall organize itself and commence its
17   mandate upon the effectivity of this Act.
18
19          SEC. 7. Powers and Functions of AGRICOM. — The AGRICOM shall have the
20   following powers and functions:
21
22      a) Review, assess, and evaluate the agriculture bureaucracy and recommended
23         institutional reforms;
24      b) Recommend the improvement in the harmonization of the policies and programs
25         of the DA, DAR, DENR and other government agencies in charge of agriculture;
26      c) Formulate and approve the workplan for the conduct of the national review,
27         assessment and harmonization tasks and the budget for the programs of the
28         government agencies concerned with agriculture and all disbursements
29         therefrom, including compensation of all necessary personnel. Provided that,
30         the AGRICOM may hire and appoint employees and personnel on temporary,
31         contractual, or on consultancy basis;
32      d) Formulate short-term and long-term policy and program recommendations that
33         consist of specific, targeted, measurable and time-bound solutions to include,
34         but not limited to, the following:
35             1) sectoral plans and targets;
36             2) value chain governance and management;
37             3) physical and digital infrastructure;
38             4) farmer and fisherfolk trainings and programs;
39             5) financing; and
40             6) coordination among departments and sectors concerned with human
41             resource management and development.

 3          consolidate coherent courses of action as remedies;
 4       f) Summon by subpoena any public official or private person to testify before it, or

 6          may be necessary;
 7       g) Secure from any department, bureau, office or instrumentality of the
 8          government such assistance, including technical information, preparation and

 16          SEC. 8. National Assessment and Evaluation. — Pursuant to Section 4 of this
 17   Act, the national assessment and evaluation shall include the following:
18
19           (a) Review of the observance of the mandates of the laws that created the DA,
20           DAR, DENR and other government agencies in charge of agriculture;
21           (b) Determination of factors that have contributed to the continuing failure in
22           performance of the three agencies in modernizing agriculture;
23               identified subject areas to meet the desired international and local standards;
24           (c) Recommendation of specific, targeted and time-bound solutions to enable
25           the three agencies to improve their performance vis-f-vis measurable indicators;
26           (d) Identification of best practices of various national and international
27           agricultural institutions that can be adopted across the entire agricultural
28           system;
29           (e) Proposal for a monitoring and evaluation plan to ensure the timely
30           achievement of set targets; and
31           (f) Determination of the role of local government units (LGUs) in the delivery of
32           basic agricultural services.
33

36    members or experts from multisectoral groups, and engage resource persons from the
37    public and private sectors as may be needed.

 8         SEC. 11. Executive Director. — The ED to be appointed by the AGRICOM must
 9    be a recognized expert with experience both in the fields of agriculture and
10    management. The ED shall work full-time and shall have the rank, privileges, and
11    emoluments of an Executive Director IV (Salary Grade 29).
12
13           SEC. 12. Philippine Institute for Development Studies (PIDS) as Research Arm
14    of the AGRICOM. — The PIDS shall serve as the research arm of the AGRICOM. During
15    the course of the AGRICOM's existence, it shall produce data-based research and
16    provide analysis to contribute to the formulation of policy recommendations that will
17    benefit the agriculture sector.
18
19           SEC. 13. Periodic Review.-The. Department of Agriculture (DA), in coordination
20    with the Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR), Department of Environment and
21    Natural Resources (DENR), Cooperative Development Authority (CDA), and the
22    Department of Economic Planning and Development (DEPDEV), shall conduct periodic
23    reviews every three (3) years or, as may be necessary, of the implementation and
24    accomplishments of the AGRICOM. Thereafter, after the conduct of such periodic
25    review, the DA shall submit to Congress a report containing their evaluation and
26    recommendation.
27
28           SEC.14. Agricuiture Advisory Council. — An Agriculture, Legislation and Policy
29    Advisory Council (Advisory Council) is hereby created to provide the AGRICOM with
30    expert assistance and advice. The members of the Advisory Council shall always be
31    present in all meetings of the AGRICOM. The Senate President and the Speaker of the
32    House of Representatives shall choose recognized experts from the following sectors
33    to form part of the Advisory Council: two (2) members from agriculture sector, two (2)
34    members from the business sector, two (2) members from the government agriculture
35    agencies, two (2) members who are heads of LGUs, and two (2) members from
36    organizations and development partners engaged in agriculture.
37

41    the Senate and seven (7) members from the House of Representatives, with the
42    chairperson of the Senate Committee on Agriculture, Food and Agrarian Reform and
43    the chairperson of the House of Representatives Committee on Agriculture and Food,
44    as joint chairpersons. The six (6) other members from the Senate shall be composed

 4   Agriculture and Food, and two (2) from the Committee on Environment, and one (1)
 5   from the minority, to be designated respectively by the President of the Senate and the
 6   Speaker of the House of Representatives.
 7
 8          SEC. 16. Appropriations. — The amount of two hundred million pesos (Php
 9   200,000,000.00) shall be appropriated for the initial implementation of this Act which
10   shall be charged against the available appropriations of the Senate and the House of
11   Representatives. Thereafter, the amount necessary for its continuous implementation,
12   including any deficiency in the current year, shall be included in the annual General
13   Appropriations Act subject to existing budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and
14   regulations.
