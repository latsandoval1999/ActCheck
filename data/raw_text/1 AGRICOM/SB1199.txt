                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE



      This legislative measure seeks to establish a Congressional Commission on
Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Security, or the AGRICOM, to address the long­
standing issues plaguing the Philippines' agricultural sector, which is hindered by a
fragmented and uncoordinated system despite numerous existing laws and policies.


       While there are various oversight committees such as the Congressional
Oversight Committee on Agricultural and Fisheries Modernization (COCAFM) and the
Congressional    Oversight Committee       on   Anti-Agricultural   Economic         Sabotage
(COCAAES), the core problem is the disjoined implementation approach. Government
agencies operate in "silos", leading to conflicting mandates, redundant programs, and
inefficient budget allocation.


       Despite a quarter of the country's workforce being in agriculture and fisheries,
labor continues to decline, and the sector's contribution to the Gross Domestic Product
(GDP) remains low. Our agricultural exports also lag behind our ASEAN neighbors. The
persistent issues of post-harvest losses, weak supply chain integration, limited access
to credit, and vulnerability to climate-related shocks further hinder the sector's
performance. Additionally, the overlapping responsibilities between national agencies
and local government units (LGUs), following the devolution of agricultural services,
require coordinated action for improvement.


        The AGRICOM is designed to provide a cohesive solution by acting as a central,
independent body that will: (1) Conduct a comprehensive review and assessment of
all existing agricultural and fisheries programs and policies, and projects across all
national agencies; (2) Identify policy conflicts and program redundancies to create a
more efficient and integrated framework; (3) Develop targeted, measurable, and time-
bound     policy   recommendations    aimed    at   increasing   productivity,   global
competitiveness, and capacity building for the sector; and (4) Report its findings and
recommendations to the public, fostering transparency and prompting swift action
from implementing agencies.


    The creation of the AGRICOM will help streamline government efforts, assist
agencies in balancing their mandates, and ensure that support for the agricultural
sector is targeted and effective. This will ultimately address the foundational issues
that have long hindered the sector's growth and stability.


    In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

 3            Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall reaffirm the promotion of a just
 4   and dynamic social order that will ensure the prosperity and independence of the
 5   nation that frees the people from poverty through policies that provide adequate social
 6   services, promote full employment, a rising standard of living, and an improved quality
 7   of life for all.
 8           The State shall affirm labor as a primary social economic force, shall develop a
 9   self-reliant and independent national economy effectively controlled by Filipinos, and
10   shall promote comprehensive rural development and agrarian reform.
11           To such ends, the State shall take the appropriate steps to ensure that the
12   national agriculture and fisheries sector shall be a robust, dynamic and sustainable
13   means to economic security on participants on the production side of the sector, and
14   shall be a driver for human capital development, human liberation and development,
15   and overall well-being and welfare on the part of Filipino consumers.
