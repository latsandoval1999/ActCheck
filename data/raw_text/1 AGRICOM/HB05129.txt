TWENTIETH CONGRESS
First Regular Session

HOUSE BILL NO. 9129

Introduced by Representative LEILA M. DE LIMA

AN ACT
CREATING A CONGRESSIONAL COMMISSION ON AGRICULTURE, FISHERIES,
AND FOOD SECURITY TO REVIEW, ASSESS, AND EVALUATE THE STATE OF
PHILIPPINE AGRICULTURE AND RECOMMEND INNOVATIVE AND TARGETED
POLICY REFORMS IN AGRICULTURE, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The agriculture and fisheries sector in the Philippines is going through a steady
decline, as exhibited by the sector's decreasing productivity and competitiveness and
diminishing GDP contribution. Current trends, such as the aging population of farmers
and fisherfolk due to reduced income and the country’s increased dependence on
importation due to lower agricultural yields, do not paint a positive picture for the future
of the country’s agricultural and fisheries sector.

Particularly concerning is the direct impact of a dying agricultural and fisheries
sector on the food security of the Filipino people. As such, timely, well-informed,
comprehensive, decisive, and innovative measures must be promptly taken to address
the challenges confronting the agricultural and fisheries sector and safeguard the
country’s food security now and in the future.

This bill proposes the creation of a Congressional Commission on Agriculture,
Fisheries, and Food Security, or the AGRICOM, to address the long-standing and
intersectional issues plaguing the country’s agricultural and fisheries sector. Among
these issues are the fragmented and uncoordinated system of supporting the
agriculture sector, the lack of adequate and institutionalized mechanisms of resource-
generation, and the disjoined implementation of agricultural laws and policies, thus
leading to conflicting mandates, redundant programs, and inefficient budget allocation.

===== [page 2] =====
The AGRICOM, acting as a central, independent body, shall provide cohesive
and comprehensive solutions to the sector's complex and intertwined problems
through the following:

Conducting a comprehensive review and assessment of all existing
agricultural and fisheries programs, policies, and projects across all
national agencies in the country;

Identifying policy conflicts and program redundancies to formulate a
more efficient and integrated framework;

Developing targeted, measurable, and_ time-bound policy
recommendations aimed at increasing the sector's productivity,
competitiveness, and capacity; and

Reporting its findings and recommendations to the public with the
purpose of fostering transparency and prompting swift action from
implementing agencies.

The AGRICOM is the government's way of creating a more comprehensive and
targeted response to the increasingly complex issues faced by the agriculture and
fisheries sector and the consequences of the sector’s decline on national food security,
employment, economy, and poverty. The urgency and necessity of taking decisive
measures to support the dying sector of agriculture and fisheries cannot be overstated.
This bill fully adopts Senator Francis Pangilinan’s SBN 1199 filed under the present
20* Congress.

Early passage of this bill is requested.

HOUSE BILL No. 9129

AN ACT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION. 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Congressional
Commission on Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Security” (AGRICOM) Act.

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall reaffirm the promotion of a just
and dynamic social order that will ensure the prosperity and independence of the
nation that frees the people from poverty through policies that provide adequate social
services and promote full employment, a rising standard of living, and an improved
quality of life for all.

The State shall affirm labor as a primary social economic force, shall develop a
self-reliant and independent national economy effectively controlled by Filipinos, and
shall promote comprehensive rural development and agrarian reform.

To such ends, the State shall take the appropriate steps to ensure that the
national agriculture and fisheries sector shall be a robust, dynamic, and sustainable
means to economic security on participants on the production side of the sector, and
shall be a driver for human capital development, human liberation and development,
and overall well-being and welfare on the part of Filipino consumers.

SEC. 3. Objectives of AGRICOM. — The Commission shall, among other things,
critically review and assess agriculture and fisheries policies, strategies, and programs
to attain the following objectives:

a. Re-examine public sector goals and objectives with respect to
agriculture and fisheries;

b. Modify agriculture and fisheries policies and public expenditures to
integrate modern technologies and climate change adaption with the
ultimate goal of raising the productivity and global competitiveness of our
local agriculture and fisheries subsectors, and to employ long-term
policy instruments for raising productivity, protecting the environment,
improving market efficiency, and ensuring equity and social justice,
particularly for the benefit of small farmers and families;

c. Refocus and re-prioritize support programs for agriculture and fisheries
to ensure food security in basic staples for all Filipino communities;

d. Restructure and streamline agriculture and __fisheries-related
bureaucracies to address their institutional weaknesses and
shortcomings, including the relationship between and among national,
provincial, and local stakeholders, in order to effectively implement
agriculture and fisheries policies and programs, including the transfer of
information and technology, particularly at the grassroots level; and

e. Encourage the active involvement of producers, cooperatives, media,
banks, financial institutions, and other members of the private sector in
the development of agri-business.

SEC. 5. Composition of AGRICOM. — The Commission shall be composed of
twelve (12) members, with six (6) members from the Senate and six (6) members from
the House of Representatives: Provided that one of the six (6) members from each
Chamber shall represent the Minority as designated by the Minority Leader of each
Chamber.

The Chairpersons of the Senate Committee on Agriculture, Food, and Agrarian
Reform, the Senate Committee on Economic Affairs, and the Senate Committee on
Environment, Natural Resources, and Climate Change shall be members of the
Commission on the part of the Senate.

The Chairpersons of the House Committee on Agriculture and Food, the House
Committee on Aquaculture and Fisheries Resources, the House Committee on
Agrarian Reform, and the House Committee on Climate Change shall be members of
the Commission on the part of the House of Representatives.

The members of the Commission shall receive no compensation, but travelling,
communications, and other necessary expenses shall be aliowed, subject to existing
jaws, rules, and regulations.

SEC. 6. Executive Director. - The Executive Director (ED) to be appointed by
the Commission must be a recognized expert in the fields of agriculture and fisheries.

The Executive Director shall work full-time and shall have the rank, privileges,
and emoluments of an Executive Director IV (Salary Grade 29).

SEC. 7. Technical Secretariat. ~- A Technical Secretariat, to be headed by the
Executive Director, shalt be organized to provide technical and administrative support
to the Commission in the exercise of its powers and performance of its functions under
Section 11 of this Act.

The Executive Director shall organize its staff and technical panel, and hire and
appoint such employees and personnel, whether temporary, contractual, or on
consultancy, and determine their compensation subject to applicable civil service laws,
rules, and regulations, with a view to ensuring a competent and efficient secretariat.

The Technical Secretariat shall co-exist with the Commission.

SEC. 8. Philippine Institute for Development Studies (PIDS) as Research Arm.
— The PIDS shall serve as the research arm of the Commission. During the course of
the Commission's existence, it shall produce data-based research and provide
analysis to contribute to the formulation of agriculture and fisheries policy
recommendations. It may coordinate with existing advisory and consultative bodies on
agriculture and fisheries policies under the Department of Agriculture and Department
of Science and Technology.

SEC. 9. Agriculture and Fisheries Legislation and Policy Advisory Council. — An
Agriculture and Fisheries Legislation and Policy Advisory Council is hereby created to
provide the Commission with expert assistance and advice. The members of the
Advisory Council shall always be present in all meetings of the Commission. The
Senate President and the Speaker of the House of Representatives, upon the
recommendation of the members of the Commission, shall choose recognized experts
from the following sectors to form part of the Advisory Council: three (3) members from
state university academia with equa! representation from Luzon, Visayas, and
Mindanao, two (2) members from the business sector, two (2) members from
government agriculture and fisheries agencies, two (2) members who are heads of
local government units (LGUs), and two (2) members from civil society organizations
and development partners engaged in agriculture and fisheries.

SEC. 10. Duration of AGRICOM. — The Commission shall accomplish its
mandate within three (3) years from its organization. It shall organize itself and
commence its mandate upon the effectivity of this Act.

SEC. 12. Standing Committees. — The members of the Commission shall
establish from among its members standing committees, to be chaired by such
members or experts from multisectoral groups, and engage resource persons from the
public and private sectors as may be needed.

SEC. 13. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against available appropriations of the Senate and the
House of Representatives. Thereafter, the amount necessary for its continuous
implementation, including any deficiency in the current year, shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and
auditing rules and regulations.

SEC. 14. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts not affected shail remain in full force
and effect.

SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, presidential decrees,
presidential proclamations, administrative orders, letters of instruction, rules and
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions
of this Act are hereby repealed, modified, or amended accordingly.

SEC. 16. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation in the
Philippines.
