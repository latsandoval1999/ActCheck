SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as
the “Congressional Commission on Agriculture, Fisheries,

and Food Security (AGRICOM) Act”.

SEC. 3. Creation of the AGRICOM. - There is hereby
created a Congressional Commission on Agriculture,
Fisheries, and Food Security, hereinafter referred to as the
AGRICOM, to undertake a comprehensive national
assessment and evaluation of the performance of the
Philippine agriculture and fisheries sector for the purpose
of recommending transformative, concrete, and targeted
reforms in order to make the Philippines globally

competitive in the agriculture and fisheries sector.

bureaucracy and recommend institutional reforms;

of the policies and programs of the DA, DAR, DENR, and

other government agencies in charge of agriculture;

(c) Formulate short-term and long-term policy and
program recommendations on agriculture and fisheries
consisting of specific, targeted, measurable, and time-

bound solutions to the following:
(1) Sectoral plan and targets;
(2) Physical and digital infrastructure;
(3) Trainings and programs;

SEC. 11. Agriculture and Fisheries Legislation and
Policy Advisory Council. — An Agriculture and Fisheries
Legislation and Policy Advisory Council is hereby created
to provide the AGRICOM with expert assistance and
advice. The members of the Advisory Council shall be
present in all meetings of the AGRICOM. The Senate
President and the Speaker of the House of
Representatives, upon the recommendation of the
AGRICOM members, shall choose recognized experts from
the following sectors to form part of the Advisory Council:
three (3) from state university academia with equal
representation from Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao, two (2)
from the business sector, two (2) from government
agriculture and fisheries agencies, two (2) heads of Local
Government Units, and two (2) from civil society
organizations and development partners engaged in

agriculture and fisheries.

SEC. 13. Appropriations. — The amount of Two
hundred million pesos (P200,000,000) for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against
available appropriations of the Senate and the House of
Representatives. Thereafter, the amount necessary for its
continuous implementation shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act subject to existing budgeting,

accounting, and auditing rules and regulations.

SEC. 14. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this
Act is declared unconstitutional, the remainder thereof not

otherwise affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. — All laws, presidential
decrees, executive orders, letters of instruction,
proclamations, or administrative regulations that are
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby

repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
