EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines continues to be heavily burdened by rabies, with studies
estimating that 300 to 400 Filipinos die annually and close to four (4) million animal
bite cases are reported yearly. Notably, from 2020 to 2024, there were 1,750
Filipino deaths from rabies. As emphasized by the Department of Health (DOH),
rabies is dangerous and fatal, capable of being transmitted through bites,
scratches, or saliva from a rabid animal. Tragic cases such as workers and
residents losing their lives due to delayed treatment, lack of proximity to health
units, or financial constraints in completing post-exposure prophylaxis, underscore
the urgent need for localized intervention.

While the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth),currently
provides an Animal Bite Treatment (ABT) benefit package worth P 5,850 across
more than 700 accredited providers nationwide, rabies-related mortalities still
persist. Rural Health Units (RHUs) serve as the primary healthcare contact points
in local communities and must be fully equipped to provide timely treatment.

To provide a holistic, multi-sectoral approach to public health and animal
safety, this measure works in close synergy with related legislative measures
authored by the same representative; (i) House Bill No. 2356 or the Animal Medical
Center Act, establishes public veterinary clinics across all local government units
to manage animal health, mass vaccinations, and zoonotic disease contro! at the
source; and (ii) House Bill No. 4870 or the Anti-Venom Distribution Act, which
ensures the expanded distribution and availability of life-saving anti-venom in
RHUs and infirmaries for snake and other venomous bites.

In line with the National Rabies Prevention and Control Program under the
Anti-Rabies Act of 2007, this measure seeks to address gaps in access by
establishing Animal Bite Centers (ABCs) in RHUs and mandating the provision of
free anti-rabies vaccinations and related medical interventions at the grassroots
level.

AN ACT STRENGTHENING THE NATIONAL ANTI-RABIES PROGRAM
THROUGH THE ESTABLISHMENT OF ANIMAL BITE CENTERS IN RURAL
HEALTH UNITS, THE PROVISION OF FREE ANTI-RABIES VACCINATIONS,
AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Free Anti-Rabies
Vaccination in Rural Health Units Act".

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the declared policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people. Towards this end, the State
shall adopt measures to strengthen public health services by ensuring accessible
and timely anti-rabies prevention and treatment services through the provision of
free anti-rabies vaccinations and related medical interventions in rural health units,
and by promoting greater access to anti-rabies services for all Filipinos.

SECTION 3. Establishment of Animal Bite Centers in Rural Health Units. —
The Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with local government units
(LGUs), shall ensure the establishment of Animal Bite Centers (ABCs) in Rural
Health Units (RHUs) nationwide for the prevention and management of rabies and
animal bite cases.

SECTION 4. Free Anti-Rabies Vaccination Program. — All RHUs nationwide
are hereby mandated to provide free anti-rabies post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP)
and, when necessary, pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP), in accordance with the
guidelines set by the DOH.

SECTION 5. Program Implementation. — The DOH shail lead the
implementation of the national free anti-rabies vaccination program. It shall
coordinate with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG),

SECTION 6. Public Information and Community Awareness. — The DOH, in

SECTION 7. Appropriations. — The amount necessary to carry out the

Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continuous implementation

relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to
effectively carry out the intent of this Act.

SECTION 9. Separability Clause. — Should any provision or part of this Act
be declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions and parts hereof,
insofar as they are separable from the invalid ones, shall remain in full force and
effect.

SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, issuances,
rules, and regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
