                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish a comprehensive framework for the titling,
protection, and management of public school sites under the Department of
Education (DepEd). This secures government ownership, prevents conflicting
claims, and supports the uninterrupted delivery of basic education services.

       As of March 2026, DepEd identified four thousand one hundred seventy eight
(44,178) school sites nationwide in need of titling, despite long-standing government
occupancy and use. Many of these properties are supported by deeds of donation,
deeds of sale, tax declarations, presidential proclamations, or other forms of
documentary evidence. Yet, it remains vulnerable to ownership disputes,
overlapping claims, and administrative delays.

       Legal ambiguity regarding land ownership directly impedes national and
private infrastructure development. This effectively blocks the construction of
essential classrooms, which is not helping the country’s classroom backlog problem
of approximately one hundred and sixty five thousand (165,000)1 units. Unresolved
property titles also expose critical school sites to protracted court litigations initiated
by the heirs of original land donors, which disrupts the continuous delivery of basic
education to students.

      Securing legal ownership over public school sites remains essential to sustain
educational infrastructure and protect lands devoted to learning for future
generations.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE TITLING, PROTECTION, AND MANAGEMENT OF
PUBLIC SCHOOL SITES UNDER THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION, AND
FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Public School Lands
Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby the declared policy of the State to
ensure the security, protection, and proper management of lands used for public
school purposes in order to support the delivery of basic education and promote an
environment conducive to learning. Towards this end, the State shall ensure the
systematic identification, registration, and titling of all public school sites in favor of
the Republic of the Philippines, represented by the Department of Education
(DepEd), and protect such lands from encroachment, unauthorized disposition, or
conflicting claims.

        SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

            a. Public school site refers to land actually, directly, and presently used
        for public school purposes by the DepEd, including but not limited to areas
        where there are DepEd Division Offices, DepEd offices, DepEd training
        facilities, teacher’s camp, hotels, inns, hostels, or commercial spaces where
        the income derived therefrom are used of the school’s benefit, or the like, and
        are necessary to deliver its mandate and functions, pursuant to Executive
        Order No. 292, otherwise known as the “Administrative Code of 1987”; and

            b. Qualified school site refers to land not occupied by DepEd at the time
        of the filing of the case for summary titling, but had been occupied as a public
        school site for at least five (5) years preceding the effectivity of this Act,
        whether or not the parcel of land is a property of the public domain, or it is

       owned by a person or entities other than the DepEd, whether the ownership
       thereof is registered or not.

        SEC. 4. Coverage. – This Act shall cover all lands registered in the name of
DepEd or its predecessor-in-interest, such as but not limited to “Ministry of
Education” and “Department of Education, Culture and Sports”, unregistered and
current public school sites under the administration or use of the DepEd, including
parcels of land not occupied by DepEd but which have been utilized as such for at
least five (5) years immediately preceding the effectivity of this Act.

       SEC. 5. Prima Facie Ownership by DepEd of Unregistered Land. – The DepEd shall
be the prima facie owner of all unregistered qualified school sites. The DepEd shall
acquire ownership of the unregistered school sites through the following:

         a.​ Filing of application for confirmation of title in the Regional Trial Court
       where the land is located;

          b.​ Filing of application for the issuance of special patent; or

          c.​ ﻿Filing of an application of the issuance of Presidential Proclamation for
       school sites situated in forest or timber land.

        SEC. 6. Summary Titling of Qualified School Sites on Unregistered Lots and Speedy
Hearing. – When the qualified school site had been surveyed and plotted, the DepEd
Secretary, or his or her duly authorized representative, through the Office of the
Solicitor General, shall institute original registration proceedings by filing the
necessary petition in the Regional Trial Court of the place where the land is situated
against any claimants, possessors, or occupants of such lands or any part thereof,
stating in substance that public interest requires that the title to such lands be settled
and adjudicated and praying that such titles be so settled and adjudicated in favor of
the Republic of the Philippines through the Department of Education.

       SEC. 7. Contents of the Petition. – The petition shall contain a description of the
land and shall be accompanied by an approved plan thereof, and may contain such
other data as may serve to furnish full notice to the occupants of the lands and to all
persons who may claim any right or interest therein.

       SEC. 8. Burden of Proof of DepEd. – The DepEd need only to establish that:

          a.​ ﻿The school site is not covered by a valid Torrens Certificate of Title;

          b.​ ﻿It has been used as a school site for at least five (5) years, or it has been
       used in furtherance of the functions or mandate of DepEd for at least five (5)
       years; and

          c.​ ﻿The land had been surveyed or plotted, its complete description, metes
       and bounds had been identified by a duly licensed surveyor or government
       surveyor and the approved survey is submitted to the Court.

       SEC. 9. Nature of Court Action. – All actions for the original registration of
qualified school sites under this Act shall be summary in nature. The action for
registration in this section shall comply with the notice requirements provided for in
Section 23, Presidential Decree (PD) No. 1529, entitled “Amending and Codifying the
Laws Relative to the Registration of Property and For Other Purposes”.

       SEC. 10. Disposal of Cases. – Consistent with Section 27 of PD No. 1529, the
Court, if it deems necessary, may refer the case or any part thereof to a
Commissioner who shall hear the parties and their evidence, and the Commissioner
shall submit his report thereon to the Court within fifteen days after the termination
of such hearing. Hearing before a referee may be held at any convenient place within
the province or city as may be fixed by him and after reasonable notice thereof shall
have been served to the parties concerned. The court may render judgment in
accordance with the report as though the facts have been found by the judge himself:
Provided, That the court may in its discretion accept the report, or set it aside in
whole or in part, or order the case to be recommitted for further proceedings. The
Regional Trial Court shall see to it that all registration-proceedings under this Act are
disposed of within one year from the date of filing.

       SEC. 11. Immediate Transfer of Qualified School Sites Owned by the National
Government and Local Government Units. – All qualified school sites established or
occupied by DepEd on property registered under the name of the Republic of the
Philippines or owned by any of its agencies or instrumentalities, as well as those
owned by the local government units (LGUs), shall be immediately transferred to
and titled in favor of DepEd. Such transfer shall include the buildings or other
immovable real properties thereto, without prejudice to the arrangements for
compensation, if any for government owned corporations, which shall be mutually
agreed upon between the DepEd and the agencies concerned and recommended to
the President for final approval.

       SEC. 12. Modes of Acquiring Qualified School Sites Not Occupied but
Privately-Owned, Owned by National Government, or Owned by LGUs. – For purposes of
expanding existing school sites, the DepEd may commence acquisition of the
following through donation, negotiated sale, or any other modes of acquisition as
provided by law:

          a.​ Privately-owned lots not occupied by DepEd;

           b.​ Lots owned      by   the   National    Government      or   any   of   its
       instrumentalities;

          c.​ and ﻿Lots owned by LGUs.

      SEC. 13. Unaccepted and Undocumented Donations. – All donated school sites
where proof of acceptance is insufficient, as well as undocumented donations of
property used as school sites, if the donor was alive during the occupation for use as
school site, said donations shall be deemed perfected by implied acceptance of the



                                           3
government from the time the property was actually utilized as a public school site,
or from the time that any acts of ownership thereon had been carried out, such as but
not limited to planting, fencing, occupation, possession, use, or the like. In donation
mortis causa, the acceptance is deemed made at the time of occupation thereof, which
is anytime subsequent to the death of the donor.

       SEC. 14. Guidelines for Negotiated Sale. – Should the DepEd and the owner of a
titled private property agree on a negotiated sale for the acquisition of a
privately-owned property to be established as a future school site, the standards
prescribed in the pertinent laws, rules and regulations, insofar as they are applicable,
shall be used to determine the fair market value of the property, subject to the review
and approval by the DepEd Secretary or his or her authorized representative.

        SEC. 15. Order of Registration Immediately Executory. – All judgements ordering
the registration of a qualified school site shall be immediately executory. The
Register of Deeds, upon receipt of such order, shall forthwith register and issue the
title and the corresponding certificate of title in favor of DepEd.

       SEC. 16. School Sites Privately-Owned under Contract of Usufruct. – To protect the
rights and interest of DepEd in its use and enjoyment of a school site that are
privately-owned under a Contract of Usufruct, the same shall be registered with the
Registry of Deeds concerned and annotated in the Certificate of Title of such real
property. All reasonable efforts should be exerted for the subsequent acquisition of
these properties in favor of DepEd.

       SEC. 17. School Sites Owned by Government Housing Agencies under Contract of
Usufruct. – All public school sites in use by DepEd within housing projects or
relocation sites established by housing agencies of the national government which
are covered by contracts of usufruct or similar instruments shall be titled in favor of
the DepEd in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

       SEC. 18. Titling and Registration of School Sites based on Tax Declarations registered
to DepEd. – Real properties with tax declarations registered in the name of the DepEd
which are being used as school sites shall be processed through the Department of
Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) for titling through the issuance of
Special Patents and Presidential Proclamations. The DENR shall facilitate the
immediate issuance of special patents to school sites through a Memorandum of
Agreement with the DepEd which must be consummated not more than one
hundred eighty (180) days after the effectivity of this Act.

       SEC. 19. Expeditious Registration of Land Titles for School Sites. – The Registry of
Deeds, Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR),
DENR, and LGUs concerned shall process the registration and titling of the
properties as provided herein in favor of DepEd in the most efficient and expeditious
manner, the consummation of which must be made not more than one hundred
eighty (180) days after the effectivity of this Act.

         SEC. 20. Exemption from Payment of Fees and Taxes for the registration of Land
titles for School Sites. – The DepEd shall be exempt from payment of all related costs,
fees, charges or all taxes to the National Government Agencies, including the BIR,
Land Registration Authority (LRA), Registry of Deeds, DENR, and LGUs, and other
government agencies involved in the registration and titling of school sites as
provided herein. The DepEd shall also enjoy all the other rights, exemptions and
privileges authorized by the law, in all its official transactions with National
Government Agencies and LGUs involving titling and registration of its current
school sites. Donations of land or other properties, real or personal for the benefit of
the DepEd shall also be exempt from donor's tax.

        SEC. 21. School Sites within Forest Land. – The DepEd shall cause the
application for the reclassification of land occupied by school sites, which are
classified as forest or timber, into alienable or disposable, to the Office of the
President through the DENR. Upon reclassification of school sites from forest land to
alienable and disposable, the Office of the President shall issue Presidential
Proclamation over such lands.

      SEC. 22. School Sites within Ancestral Domain. – The National Commission on
Indigenous Peoples shall assist the DepEd in securing Free, Prior and Informed
Consent (FPIC) from leaders or representatives of Indigenous Cultural Communities
(ICCs) for school sites within ancestral domain for the eventual grant of usufruct by
ICC’s to DepEd for the use of their lands. The requirement for FPIC, however, is
deemed waived if the ICCs have passed a resolution asking DepEd to establish and
construct a new school or repair and rehabilitate an existing one.

       SEC. 23. Ecological and Environmental Concerns. – In cases involving the
acquisition of private real property used as public school sites, the DepEd shall take
into account and consider environmental laws, land use ordinances and the
pertinent provisions of RA No. 7160, otherwise known as the “Local Government Code
of 1991”, as amended.

       SEC. 24. Titles of real properties registered under the name of DepEd. –

            a.​ ﻿Existing titles of real properties registered under the name of the
       DepEd, or its predecessors-in-interest, such as but not limited to Ministry of
       Education, Department of Education, Culture and Sports, or in the name of
       any person or private individual where the title is in the possession of the
       DepEd, whether the title is Spanish, American or Torrens Certificate of Title,
       shall be cancelled upon application of the DepEd for cancellation thereof at
       the Register of Deeds, free from all transfer taxes, charges and fees, and a new
       title will be issued to reflect the name “Republic of the Philippines,
       represented by the Department of Education”. The possession by DepEd of
       the original duplicate owner's copy of the title of a person or private
       individual over a property occupied as a school site shall be deemed prima
       facie evidence of a valid and accepted donation.

      DepEd stating that the area subject matter in the CLOA is an existing DepEd

      SEC. 25. Sites Titling and Management Office. – The existing Sites Titling Office
of the DepEd shall be reorganized and expanded to the Sites Titling and
Management Office, which shall, in addition to its current responsibilities, be
responsible for the following:

         a.​ Identification and acquisition of qualified school sites for future use of
      the DepEd;

        b.​ ﻿Maintaining all relevant records in connection to public school sites
      owned by the DepEd, or those considered for acquisition;

         c.​ ﻿Coordination with all relevant government agencies and private
      entities for the acquisition of school sites; and

         d.​ ﻿Any other task which is required for the full implementation of the
      provisions of this Act.

        SEC. 26. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The Department of Education, in
cooperation with all other relevant agencies, shall promulgate the rules and
regulations for the proper implementation of this Act within six (6) months from its
effectivity.

       SEC. 27. Appropriations. - Funds necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be included in the annual appropriations for the DepEd in the General
Appropriations Act.

subsequently declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof which are

       SEC. 29. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
