                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The 1987 Constitution provides for a party-list system to ensure proportional
representation in the House of Representatives and broaden the participation of
marginalized and underrepresented sectors in the legislative process.

        However, the implementation of Republic Act No. 7941, otherwise known as
the Party-List System Act, has exposed several institutional weaknesses, including the
proliferation of organizations without genuine sectoral representation, questionable
nomination practices, inadequate financial transparency, and the use of the system
for political convenience rather than meaningful representation.

       This measure seeks to reform the party-list system by establishing objective
accreditation requirements, ensuring genuine representation of sectors and
advocacies, strengthening internal party democracy, and promoting transparency in
the selection of nominees and allocation of seats.

       It likewise introduces safeguards against political dynasties and conflicts of
interest, while adopting a clear and proportional formula for the allocation of party-list
seats based on the votes received by participating organizations.

      Ultimately, this measure seeks to restore public confidence in the party-list
system and ensure that it remains a meaningful instrument of democratic
representation.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

                              AN ACT
     REFORMING THE PARTY-LIST SYSTEM, STRENGTHENING GENUINE
     SECTORAL    AND   ADVOCACY    REPRESENTATION,     PROMOTING
     TRANSPARENCY AND ACCOUNTABILITY IN PARTY-LIST ORGANIZATIONS,
     AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

 1   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
 2   Congress assembled:
 3
 4         SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Party-List System
 5   Reform Act."
 6
 7          SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
 8   strengthen the party-list system as a mechanism for proportional representation and
 9   democratic participation, ensuring that party-list organizations genuinely represent the
10   sectors, advocacies, and communities they seek to represent.
11
12           The State shall promote transparency, accountability, internal democracy, and
13   fair electoral competition among party-list organizations.
14
15           SECTION 3. Categories of Representation. – Party-list organizations may
16   participate under any of the following categories:
17           (a) Sectoral Representation, representing marginalized and underrepresented
18   sectors of society; and
19           (b) Advocacy Representation, representing organizations pursuing specific
20   civic, social, environmental, or public policy advocacies.
21
22         An organization shall register under only one (1) category and shall
23   demonstrate genuine affiliation with the sector, advocacy, or community it seeks to
24   represent.
25
 1          SECTION 4. Registration and Accreditation. – The Commission on
 2   Elections (COMELEC) shall establish objective and uniform requirements for the
 3   registration and accreditation of party-list organizations.
 4
 5          Applicants shall submit their organizational documents, constitution and by-
 6   laws, membership records, list of officers, organizational activities, and proof of
 7   continuing engagement with their intended constituency.
 8
 9           A party-list organization must have been in existence for at least one (1) year
10   prior to the filing of its application.
11
12          Registration and accreditation shall be conducted on a continuing basis,
13   provided that applications shall be resolved not later than one (1) year before the next
14   regular election.
15
16           The COMELEC shall maintain and publish an official registry of accredited party-
17   list organizations.
18
19          SECTION 5. Genuine Representation and Verification. – The COMELEC
20   shall verify the genuine representation of party-list organizations through their
21   membership base, organizational leadership, history of advocacy, and actual
22   engagement with the communities or sectors they claim to represent.
23
24         The COMELEC may conduct public hearings, require supporting documents, and
25   undertake verification proceedings.
26
27          Misrepresentation of sectoral affiliation, organizational activities, or
28   constituency representation shall constitute grounds for denial or cancellation of
29   registration, subject to due process.
30
31           SECTION 6. Nomination and Selection of Representatives. – Every
32   party-list organization shall submit a closed and ranked list of at least ten (10)
33   nominees to the COMELEC before the election.
34
35         The selection of nominees shall comply with the organization's constitution and
36   by-laws and shall be undertaken through transparent and democratic internal
37   procedures.
38
39         Nominees of sectoral organizations must belong to the sector they seek to
40   represent.
41
42         No person shall be nominated by more than one (1) party-list organization in
43   the same election.
44
45          SECTION 7. Qualifications and Disqualifications of Nominees. – In
46   addition to the qualifications prescribed by the Constitution and existing laws, no
47   person shall be nominated or assume a party-list seat if such person:
 1          (a) Is related within the second civil degree of consanguinity or affinity to an
 2   incumbent elective public official;
 3          (b) Is an incumbent elective public official at the time of nomination;
 4          (c) Has a direct financial interest in government contracts or transactions that
 5   may result in a conflict of interest; or
 6          (d) Has been convicted by final judgment of an offense involving moral
 7   turpitude.
 8
 9          Nothing in this section shall be construed as an additional qualification for
10   candidacy or a disqualification from election beyond what is constitutionally and legally
11   permissible. The restrictions shall apply to the assumption and holding of party-list
12   seats.
13
14           SECTION 8. Internal Party Democracy and Accountability. – Every
15   party-list organization shall adopt and maintain democratic internal governance
16   mechanisms, including:
17           (a) Regular meetings of its governing bodies;
18           (b) Transparent procedures for the selection of officers and nominees;
19           (c) Meaningful participation of members in organizational decisions;
20           (d) Mechanisms for consultation with represented constituencies; and
21           (e) Internal dispute resolution procedures.
22
23          SECTION 9. Financial Transparency. – Every accredited party-list
24   organization shall maintain complete records of its funds, contributions, donations,
25   grants, and expenditures.
26
27          It shall submit an annual financial report to the COMELEC, which shall be made
28   available for public inspection.
29
30         The COMELEC may conduct financial audits and impose appropriate
31   administrative sanctions for violations of this section.
32
33           SECTION 10. Party-List Seats and Proportional Representation. –
34   Party-list representatives shall constitute twenty percent (20%) of the total
35   membership of the House of Representatives, including those under the party-list
36   system, as provided under the Constitution.
37
38         Party-list seats shall be allocated among participating organizations in
39   proportion to the total number of valid votes received by each organization nationwide.
40
41           SECTION 11. Allocation of Party-List Seats. – The COMELEC shall allocate
42   party-list seats using the following procedure:
43           (a) Determine the total number of party-list seats available;
44           (b) Divide the total number of valid party-list votes nationwide by the total
45   number of available party-list seats to determine the seat value;
46           (c) Determine the initial seats of each party-list organization by dividing its total
47   votes by the seat value and counting the whole-number portion of the result; and
 1         (d) Distribute the remaining seats to organizations with the highest fractional
 2   remainders until all available seats are allocated.
 3
 4        No arbitrary seat cap shall be imposed on any party-list organization. The
 5   COMELEC shall publish the complete computation and final allocation of seats.
 6
 7           SECTION 12. Vacancies and Party Switching. – Any vacancy in a party-
 8   list seat shall be filled by the next qualified nominee in the order appearing in the
 9   submitted list of the same party-list organization.
10
11         A party-list representative who voluntarily resigns from, is expelled by, or
12   otherwise ceases to be a member of the organization under which such representative
13   was elected shall forfeit the party-list seat, subject to due process.
14
15          SECTION 13. Continuing Constituency Engagement. – Every party-list
16   organization shall maintain continuing programs and activities that advance the
17   interests of its represented sector, or advocacy.
18
19           Organizations shall submit a biennial report to the COMELEC detailing their
20   legislative initiatives, public service activities, consultations, and constituency
21   engagement.
22
23          SECTION 14. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The COMELEC
24   shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to implement this Act within
25   ninety (90) days from its effectivity.
26
27          SECTION 15. Repealing Clause. – Republic Act No. 7941, otherwise known
28   as the "Party-List System Act," and all other laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
29   regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended,
30   or modified accordingly.
31
32            SECTION 16. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
33   unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain
34   in full force and effect.
35
36           SECTION 17. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
37   its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
38   circulation.
