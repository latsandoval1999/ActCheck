                                       AN ACT
DECLARING THE KUTAWATO CAVES, LOCATED IN BARANGAY ROSARY HEIGHTS 1, CITY OF COTABATO,
AN ECOTOURISM SITE

                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to declare the Kutawato Caves, located at the base of Pedro Colina Hill (Tantawan Park)
along Don Teodoro V. Juliano Avenue in Barangay Rosartio Heights 1, City of Cotabato, as an eco-
tourism site.

Also known as Kutang Bato Caves, Kutawato Caves is a unique limestone cave system, the only one of
its kind in the Philippines, offering a distinct subterranean landscape amidst an urban environment.
The caves feature numerous hidden passageways, including a tunnel that connects directly to
Tamontaka Church, established in 1872.

The caves have been used historically by locals and guerrillas during the Spanish colonial era and World
War II, serving as a refuge and even as an armory. Today, the cave system is a popular tourist attraction,
offering world of wonders to history buffs and geological adventurers and explorers.

Declaring Kutawato Caves as an ecotourism site will result in its development and improvement as a
tourist destination, and more importantly, contribute to the overall economic progress of the local
community.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
Assembled:
SECTION 1. Kutawato Caves, located in Barangay Rosario Heights 1, City of Cotabato, is hereby declared
an ecotourism site. Pursuant thereto, its development shall be prioritized and supported by the
Department of Tourism (DOT), subject to the rules and regulations governing the development of
ecotourism sites.

SEC. 2. Within one (1) year after the approval of this Act, the local government of the City of Cotabato
shall, in coordination with the DOT, Department of Environment and Natural Resources , and
concerned agencies of the government, prepare a tourism development plan that shall include the
construction, installation, and maintenance of such appropriate infrastructure and facilities to enhance
tourism in the area: Provided, That said development plan shall ensure the preservation and
conservation of the natural beauty and resources of the area: Provided further, That the
implementation of the tourism development plan shall be included in the budget of the local
government of the City of Cotabato.

SEC. 3. The DOT shall assist the local government of the City of Cotabato in the implementation of the
tourism development plan by providing technical assistance in tourism capacity building. It shall also
include Kutawato Caves in its national and regional promotion programs.

SEC. 4. The funding for the implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual budget of the
local government of the City of Cotabato. The DOT may augment such allocation with internally-
generated funds.
SEC. 5. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the local government of the City of
Cotabato shall, in consultation with the DOT and concerned agencies of the government, promulgate
rules and regulations to implement this Act.
SEC. 6. All laws, rules, regulations, executive orders, proclamations, presidential decrees and other
issuances inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed repealed or amended
accordingly.
SEC. 7. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a
newspaper of general circulation.
