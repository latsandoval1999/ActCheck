                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Education remains one of the most effective means of promoting social
mobility, strengthening human capital, and ensuring national development.
While the State recognizes the complementary role of private educational
institutions in the delivery of quality basic education, many Filipino families
continue to struggle with the increasing cost of educational materials,
particularly textbooks and learning resources.
       Although tuition and miscellaneous expenses are often the primary
focus of educational assistance programs, the recurring cost of textbooks and
instructional materials imposes a significant financial burden on parents and
guardians, especially those belonging to low-income and middle-income
households. In many cases, students rely on outdated, borrowed, or
insufficient learning materials, adversely affecting learning outcomes and
academic performance.
       This measure seeks to establish a Textbook Assistance Voucher
Program that will provide qualified students enrolled in private elementary
and secondary schools with targeted financial support for the acquisition of
textbooks, workbooks, and other Department of Education-approved learning
materials. The proposed voucher mechanism aims to promote equitable access
to educational resources while supporting parental choice and the continued
participation of private schools in the national education system.

                              AN ACT
     ESTABLISHING A TEXTBOOK ASSISTANCE VOUCHER PROGRAM FOR
     STUDENTS ENROLLED IN PRIVATE BASIC EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS,
                AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR


     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
           Congress assembled:


 1             SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Private Basic
 2      Education Textbook Assistance Voucher Act”.

 3             SEC 2.    Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
 4      State to protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all
 5      levels and to take appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all. To
 6      this end, the State shall establish, among others, incentives and forms of assistance
 7      that shall be available to deserving students in both public and private educational
 8      institutions, especially those who are underprivileged, vulnerable, or otherwise
 9      disadvantaged.
10             The State recognizes that access to quality education necessarily includes
11      access to adequate, appropriate, and updated textbooks and other learning
12      materials. The recurring cost of such materials may impose a significant financial




                                                                                           1
 1   burden on parents and guardians, particularly those belonging to low-income and
 2   lower middle-income households.
 3          Toward this end, the State shall establish a Textbook Assistance Voucher
 4   Program that shall provide targeted financial assistance for the acquisition of
 5   textbooks and other Department of Education-approved learning materials by
 6   qualified students enrolled in private basic education institutions.
 7          SEC 3.     Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following
 8   terms are hereby defined:
 9          (a) Qualified Beneficiary refers to a student enrolled in a recognized private
10              basic education institution who meets the qualifications prescribed under
11              this Act and its implementing rules and regulations;
12          (b) Accredited Supplier refers to any publisher, bookstore, school
13              cooperative, educational supplier, or similar entity accredited by the
14              Department of Education to accept textbook vouchers under this Act;
15          (c) Learning Materials refer to textbooks, modules, workbooks, reference
16              materials, and other instructional resources approved by the Department
17              of Education.
18          SEC 4.     Textbook Assistance Voucher Program. – The Textbook
19   Assistance Voucher Program, hereinafter referred to as the “Program”, shall be
20   administered by the Department of Education in coordination with participating
21   private schools and accredited suppliers.
22          The Program shall provide annual textbook and learning material assistance
23   in the form of vouchers to qualified students enrolled in participating private basic
24   education institutions.
25          The Program shall be designed to reduce the financial burden of acquiring
26   essential learning materials and to promote equitable access to quality educational
27   resources among students, regardless of whether they attend public or private basic
28   institutions.

 1          The Program shall be implemented in a manner that complements existing
 2   government educational assistance programs and shall not diminish or otherwise
 3   impair existing benefits and assistance provided under law.
 4          SEC 5.     Coverage. – This Act shall cover qualified students enrolled in
 5   participating private basic education institutions nationwide, including all grade
 6   levels comprising basic education.
 7          The programs of assistance for students under this Act shall be extended
 8   only to citizens of the Philippines.
 9          SEC 6.     Eligibility and Priority Beneficiaries. – The Program shall cover
10   qualified students enrolled in private elementary and secondary schools
11   nationwide. Priority shall be given to:
12          (a) Students   belonging        to   low-income       and   lower    middle-income
13             households;
14          (b) Students who are beneficiaries of existing government educational
15             assistance programs;
16          (c) Students with disabilities;
17          (d) Indigenous Peoples learners; and
18          (e) Other disadvantaged or marginalized learners as may be identified by
19             the Department of Education.
20          The Department of Education may prescribe income thresholds,
21   documentary      requirements,     and      prioritization     guidelines    through   the
22   implementing rules and regulations.
23          SEC 7.     Amount and Form of Assistance. – Each qualified beneficiary
24   shall receive an annual textbook voucher in an amount to be determined by the
25   Department of Education, subject to the availability of funds and prevailing market
26   prices of educational materials.
27          The voucher may be issued in physical or digital form and shall be
28   redeemable only for approved learning materials from accredited suppliers.

 3          SEC 8.    Authorized Use of Voucher. – The voucher shall be used
 4   exclusively for the purchase of:
 5          (a) Textbooks;
 6          (b) Workbooks;
 7          (c) Learning Modules;
 8          (d) Reference Materials;
 9          (e) Laboratory manuals;
10          (f) Digital learning resources authorized by the Department of Education;
11             and
12          (g) Other educational materials as may be approved by the Department of
13             Education.
14          The use of vouchers for non-educational goods and services is strictly
15   prohibited.
16          SEC 9.    Accreditation of Suppliers. – The Department of Education shall
17   establish an accreditation system for suppliers authorized to participate in the
18   Program. To qualify for accreditation, suppliers must:
19          (a) Be duly registered with the appropriate government agencies;
20          (b) Demonstrate the capacity to provide legitimate and quality educational
21             materials;
22          (c) Comply with pricing transparency and reporting requirements; and
23          (d) Meet such other qualifications as may be determined by the Department
24             of Education.
25          The Department of Education may suspend or revoke the accreditation of
26   suppliers found violating this Act or its implementing rules.
27          SEC 10. Responsibilities of Participating Schools. – Private schools
28   participating in the Program shall:

 1          (a) Assist in the validation and submission of beneficiary information;
 2          (b) Provide students and parents with the official list of prescribed or
 3             recommended learning materials;
 4          (c) Coordinate with the Department of Education regarding program
 5             implementation and monitoring; and

10          SEC 11. Digital Monitoring and Transparency Mechanism. – The
11   Department of Education shall establish a digital monitoring and verification
12   system for the issuance, redemption, and liquidation of vouchers to ensure
13   transparency, accountability, and efficient program administration.
14          The system shall include safeguards against fraud, duplication, overpricing,
15   and unauthorized transactions.
16          SEC 12. Prohibited Acts. – The following acts are prohibited under this
17   Act:
18          (a) Selling, transferring, or converting vouchers into cash;
19          (b) Redemption of vouchers for non-approved goods or services;
20          (c) Submission of falsified documents or fraudulent claims;
21          (d) Overpricing or collusion by accredited suppliers;
22          (e) Unauthorized use of beneficiary information; and
23          (f) Any act intended to circumvent the provisions of this Act.
24          SEC 13. Penalties. – Any person or entity found guilty of violating the
25   provisions of this Act shall, upon conviction, suffer the following penalties:
26          (a) For individuals, a fine of not less than Fifty thousand pesos
27             (Php50,000.00) but not more than Five hundred thousand pesos
