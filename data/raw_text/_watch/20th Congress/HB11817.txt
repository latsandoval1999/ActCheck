                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

     Republic Act No. 10699, othenrvise known as the "National Athletes and
Coaches Benefits and lncentives Act," recognizes the contributions of Filipino athletes
who bring honor and recognition to the country through their achievements in
international sports competitions. Among its principal provisions is the grant of cash
incentives to athletes who win gold, silver, and bronze medals in recognized
international competitions.

       Despite this recognition and the honor that the Filipino athletes bring to our
nation through their achievements, Section 8 of the law prescribes different cash
incentives for medalists in the Olympic Games and the Paralympic Games, as well as
in other corresponding internationalsporting competitions. Underthe existing statutory
schedule, an Olympic gold medalist is entitled to Ten Million Pesos (F10,000,000.00),
while a Paralympic gold medalist is entitled to Five Million Pesos (P5,000,000.00).
Similar disparities apply to silver and bronze medalists and to athletes competing in
the Asian Games and Asian Para Games, and the Southeast Asian Games and
ASEAN Para Games.

       This distinction warrants legislative reconsideration. Olympic and Paralympic
athletes alike undergo rigorous training, demonstrate exceptional discipline and
athletic excellence, and compete at the highest levels of international sport while
representing the Republic of the Philippines. Their respective competitions demand
dedication, preparation, and performance worthy of national recognition.

        The State's obligation to promote sports development and protect the welfare
of national athletes should be consistent with the principles of equality, inclusivity, and
recognition of human dignity. This commitment necessarily includes ensuring that
persons with disabilities are afforded equal treatment and equal opportunities to
participate in sports, pursue athletic excellence, and receive recognition for their
achievements. The achievement of a medal for the country should be accorded equal
statutory recognition, regardless of whether it is earned in the Olympic Games or the
Paralympic Games. Such recognition affirms that disability should not be a basis for
diminished acknowledgment of an athlete's contribution to the nation.

       This proposed measure therefore seeks to amend Section I of Republic Act
No. 10699 by equalizing the cash incentives granted to medalists in the Olympic and
Paralympic Games, the Asian Games and Asian Para Games, and the Southeast
Asian Games and ASEAN Para Games- By establishing equality in the statutory
rewards for conesponding competitions, the measure seeks to ensure that all Filipino
athletes who bring honor to the country receive equal recognition for comparable
sporting achievements, regardless of their circumstances.

       ln view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.
