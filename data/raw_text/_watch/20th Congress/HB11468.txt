                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

            Oral health is an essential component of overall health and
      well-being. Access to basic dental services, however, remains a
      concern, particularly in rural and underserved communities where
      geographic, financial, and other barriers may limit access to regular
      dental care.

            The Rural Health Unit serves as an important primary health
      care facility within local communities. Integrating basic dental
      services into the Rural Health Unit will help bring preventive,
      promotive, and appropriate primary dental care closer to the people
      and facilitate the early detection and treatment of oral health
      conditions.

            This measure seeks to establish a Dental Unit in every Rural
      Health Unit, with at least one (1) public health dentist and one (1)
      trained dental aide, and to provide the Department of Health with
      the responsibility to establish appropriate standards, guidelines,
      and technical assistance for their operation.

             By strengthening dental services at the primary health care
      level, this Act will promote preventive oral health care, encourage
      early intervention, improve access to essential dental services, and
      strengthen the delivery of comprehensive primary health care in
      local communities.

           In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this
      measure is earnestly sought.

                          “AN ACT
      CREATING A DENTAL UNIT IN EVERY RURAL HEALTH UNIT
      AS PART OF THE PRIMARY HEALTH CARE APPROACH IN
      THE DELIVERY OF HEALTH SERVICES AND PROVIDING
      FUNDS THEREFOR”

       Be it enacted by the Senate          and   House   of   Representatives
of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Local Dental
Health Unit Act.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
pursue the Primary Health Care Approach in the delivery of health services,
undertake human resource development for this purpose, and promote
preventive and basic dental health care in rural and underserved areas as
part of the Primary Health Care strategy.

      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. -

      a) “Rural Health Unit” or “Main Health Center” refers to a primary health
         care facility operated or maintained by a local government unit for
         the delivery of basic preventive, promotive, and curative health
         services;

      b) “Dental Unit” refers to the dental service component of a Rural
         Health Unit, composed of at least one (1) public health dentist and
         one (1) trained dental aide; and

      c) “Dental Aide” refers to a Barangay Health Worker or other
         appropriately trained health worker designated to assist the public
         health dentist, subject to the scope of training and applicable laws,
         rules, and regulations.

      SECTION 4. Creation of a Dental Unit in every Rural Health Unit. -
As part of the Primary Health Care Approach, there is hereby created a Dental
Unit in every Rural Health Unit operated or maintained by a local government
unit.
      Each Dental Unit shall have at least one (1) public health dentist and
one (1) trained dental aide, subject to applicable civil service, staffing, and
budgetary rules and regulations.

      The Department of Health (DOH) shall provide the necessary standards,
guidelines, and technical assistance for the establishment and operation of
the Dental Units.

       SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DOH, in
coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local Government and
local government units, shall issue the necessary rules and regulations for
the effective implementation of this Act within six (6) months from its
effectivity.

      SECTION 6. Funding. - The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be funded from the current fiscal year's
appropriation for the DOH, subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and
auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

       Thereafter, such amount as may be necessary for the implementation
of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act and
the appropriate appropriations of the concerned local government units, as
applicable.

       SECTION 7. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act
shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, other provisions hereof which are
not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

      SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with
the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SECTION 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
