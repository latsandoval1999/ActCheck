                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to strengthen employment opportunities for qualified persons
with disability (PWDs). It further amends Section 5 of Republic Act (RA) No. 7277,
otherwise known as the “Magna Carta for Persons with Disability”, as amended by RA
No. 10524, to encourage smaller corporations with one hundred (100) or fewer
employees to also reserve positions for PWD.

       While RA No. 10524 required private corporations with more than one
hundred (100) employees to reserve at least one percent (1%) of their positions for
qualified PWDs, this proposed measure also allows a corporation to fill a reserved
position with another qualified applicant when no qualified PWD applies. It also
monitors private-sector compliance and provides tax incentives, in accordance with
existing tax laws and regulations.

       Employment provides PWDs an opportunity to use their skills, support their
families, and participate in the economic life of their communities, thereby
developing greater independence and trust in oneself. Ensuring their participation
in the workforce gives greater substance to the State’s commitment to equal
employment opportunity while giving employers a clear framework for compliance
and incentives.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
STRENGTHENING THE EMPLOYMENT OF QUALIFIED PERSONS WITH
DISABILITY, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “MAGNA CARTA FOR DISABLED PERSONS”,
AS AMENDED, PROVIDING INCENTIVES THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     Section 1. Section 5 of Republic Act (RA) No. 7277, otherwise known as the
“Magna Carta for Disabled Persons, as amended by RA No. 10524, is hereby further
amended to read as follows:

             “SEC. 5. Equal Opportunity for Employment. - x x x

              “At least one percent (1%) of all positions in all government
      agencies, offices or corporations shall be reserved for QUALIFIED
      persons with disability: Provided, That private corporations with more
      than one hundred (100) employees [are encouraged to] SHALL reserve
      at least one percent (1%) of all positions for QUALIFIED persons with
      disability: PROVIDED, FURTHER, THAT PERSONS WITH
      DISABILITY SHALL BE ASSESSED BASED ON THEIR ABILITY
      TO PERFORM THE ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS OF THE POSITION,
      WITH REASONABLE ACCOMMODATION, WHEN NECESSARY;
      PROVIDED, FINALLY, THAT WHERE NO QUALIFIED PERSON
      WITH DISABILITY APPLIES FOR THE RESERVED POSITION, OR
      WHERE NONE OF THE APPLICANTS MEETS THE
      QUALIFICATION STANDARDS AND REQUIREMENTS OF THE
      JOB, THE CORPORATION MAY FILL SUCH POSITION WITH
      ANOTHER QUALIFIED APPLICANT.

          “PRIVATE CORPORATIONS WITH ONE HUNDRED (100)
      OR FEWER EMPLOYEES ARE HIGHLY ENCOURAGED TO

        SEC. 2. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Labor and Employment, in coordination
with the National Council on Disability Affairs, Bureau of Internal Revenue, Civil
Service Commission, Department of Social Welfare and Development, Department
of Trade and Industry, Securities and Exchange Commission, Department of Health,
and other concerned agencies, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary
for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 3. Separability Clause. – In case any provision or any part thereof is
declared unconstitutional, the other provisions not so declared and affected shall
remain in force and effect.

       SEC. 4. Repealing Clause. – All decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations,
or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed
repealed, or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 5. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
