                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

The ongoing armed conflict in the Middle East has caused significant
economic disruptions in global energy markets, resulting in sharp increases
in oil prices and heightened inflationary pressures worldwide. The conflict
has disrupted critical oil supply routes such as the Strait of Hormuz through
which a substantial portion of the world’s energy supply passes, leading to
surges in crude oil prices exceeding $100 per barrel, a huge blow to
developing economies in Asia including the Philippines.

These developments have direct and indirect consequences on the
domestic economy. Higher fuel prices increase the cost of transportation,
electricity, and production, which in turn drive up the prices of basic
commodities, including food and essential goods. Oil price shocks fuel
inflation and reduce household purchasing power, most affecting the
poorest and vulnerable sectors of society.

Acting decisively, President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. issued Executive Order
No. 110, Series of 2026, Declaring a State of National Energy Emergency,
addressing the imminent danger resulting from the scarcity and instability of
the nation’s fuel supply.
In light of these extraordinary circumstances, there is a compelling need to
ensure that the conduct of the Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
elections remain orderly, credible, and responsive to prevailing
socio-economic realities of the people.

Thus, the resetting of the 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections to September 2027, or ten (10) months from the originally
scheduled date is hereby proposed as a measured and time-bound
response to the ongoing economic crisis. This measure ensures that the
will of the people to elect local officials is duly honored, while maintaining
the continuity of public service in local communities.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
RESETTING THE 2026 BARANGAY AND SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN
   ELECTIONS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 2 OF
 REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12232, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Amendment of Section 2 of Republic Act No. 12232. -
Section 2 of Republic Act No. 12232 is hereby amended to read as follows:

    “Sec. 2. Date of Election. - The next regular barangay and
    Sangguniang Kabataan elections shall be held on the first
    Monday of [November 2026] SEPTEMBER 2027 and every four
    (4) years thereafter.”

      SECTION 2. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the
implementation of this Act shall be taken from the appropriations of the
Commission on Elections (COMELEC) under the General Appropriations
Act and/or supplementary appropriation thereafter.
      SECTION 3. Separability Clause. - If any portion or provision of this
Act is declared unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision
not affected thereby shall remain in force and effect.

      SECTION 4 . Repealing Clause. - All other laws, acts, presidential
decrees, executive orders, issuances, presidential proclamations, rules and
regulations or parts thereof, which are contrary to and inconsistent with any
provision of this Act, are hereby repealed, amended, or modified
accordingly.

      SECTION 5 Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect immediately
following the completion of its publication either in the Official gazette or in
two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
