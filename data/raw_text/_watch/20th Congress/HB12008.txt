                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines is within Circum-Pacific Belt, better known as the Pacific Ring
of Fire. This horseshoe-shaped zone is the most active seismic area on Earth.
Our country is also in the path of the Pacific typhoon belt, also known as typhoon
alley, the most active tropical cyclone basin on Earth. Thus, it is no surprise that
the Philippines ranks among the most disaster-vulnerable countries in the world,
experiencing frequent and severe natural hazards such as typhoons, floods,
earthquakes, and volcanic eruptions.

At the forefront of preparedness, response, rescue and recovery operations are
the Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM) Officers and Personnel
who carry out these critical functions under challenging and high-risk conditions.
And yet, many DRRM personnel remain in non-permanent positions, with
limited access to benefits, inadequate training, and no clear career progression.

This proposed Magna Carta seeks to fill that gap and to secure the rights,
protection, and development of DRRM Officers and personnel across all levels of
government.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      AN ACT PROVIDING FOR A MAGNA CARTA OF DISASTER RISK
      REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT OFFICERS AND PERSONNEL

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Magna Carta for
Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Officers and Personnel.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to recognize the
critical role of Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM) Officers and
Personnel in saving lives, protecting communities, and mitigating the adverse
effects of disasters. In line with this, the State shall ensure the institutional
protection, welfare, capacity-building, and professionalization of DRRM workers
to enhance climate resilience at all levels of governance.

      SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – For the purposes of this Act, the following
terms are defined as follows:

      (a) DRRM Officers and Personnel – refers to individuals employed,
appointed, designated, or contracted in the public sector in a permanent,
temporary, contractual, or job order capacity to perform disaster risk reduction
and management functions, including prevention, mitigation, preparedness,
response, recovery, and rehabilitation.

      (b) DRRM Functions – includes risk assessment, planning, early warning,
emergency response, search and rescue, incident command, evacuation,
humanitarian logistics, disaster recovery, rehabilitation, preparedness programs,
and other activities aligned with the four thematic areas under R.A. No. 10121.
      (c) DRRM Office – refers to the local or national government unit, division,
or department responsible for implementing Republic Act No. 10121 (2010),
including the Local DRRM Office (“LDRRMO”) or National Disaster Risk
Reduction and Management Council (“NDRRMC”) Secretariat.

      SEC. 4. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all DRRM officers and personnel
engaged in DRRM-related functions, whether regular, permanent, contractual,
or designated, in national government agencies, Local Government Units (LGU),
state universities and colleges, and government-owned and controlled
corporations.

       SEC. 5. Rights and Benefits. – DRRM Officers and Personnel shall be
entitled to the following:

       (a) Security of tenure and protection against unjust termination or political
interference;

      (b) Hazard pay not less than twenty percent (20%) of their monthly basic
salary when exposed to hazardous conditions during operations;

      (c) Subsistence    allowance and other risk-related compensation in
accordance with law;

      (d) Legal assistance and indemnification for acts done in the lawful
performance of duty;

      (e) Continuing education, specialized training, and access to scholarship
and certification programs;

     (f) Access to psychosocial services, mental health programs, and wellness
support;

      (g) Inclusion in government insurance, medical assistance, death and
disability benefits;

      (h) Opportunities for promotion and merit-based career advancement;

    (i) Right to self-organization, collective negotiation, and participation in
DRRM policy-making processes;
      (j) Appropriate work-hour policies, compensatory time-off, and rest after
prolonged deployment.

      SEC. 6. Standards and Qualifications. – The Civil Service Commission
(CSC), in coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local Government
(DILG), the Office of Civil Defense (OCD), and the Department of Budget and
Management (DBM), shall:

       (a) Establish qualification standards, competency benchmarks, and
plantilla positions for DRRM officers and personnel;

      (b) Institutionalize a competency-based framework to guide recruitment,
training, and promotion;

      (c) Ensure DRRM personnel are designated or hired in all LGUs in
accordance with the provisions of R.A. No. 10121.

      SEC. 7. Professional Development. – DRRM Officers and Personnel shall
undergo free mandatory training in accordance with standards prescribed by the
OCD, the Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), and other
relevant agencies. They shall also have free re-training, certification, and
capacity-building programs. The OCD, in partnership with academic institutions
and training providers, shall develop a standardized national training
curriculum.

      SEC. 8. Institutional Mechanisms.

      (a) LGUs and national government agencies shall ensure the proper
functioning of their DRRM offices and provide sufficient resources for DRRM
personnel;

      (b) LGUs shall utilize their Local DRRM Fund (LDRRMF) to support the
welfare, training, and logistical needs of DRRM personnel;

       (c) Agencies shall provide DRRM personnel with protective equipment,
communications tools, mobility, and other logistical support necessary for
effective and safe performance of duties;

       (d) National DRRM agencies shall integrate personnel welfare programs in
their institutional plans.

      SEC. 9. Insurance and Benefits. – All DRRM Officers and Personnel shall
be entitled to government service insurance coverage, including:
      (a) Death and permanent disability benefits for duty-related injury or
death;

      (b) Medical treatment and hospitalization;

      (c) Access to mental health support and post-trauma interventions; and

      (d) Accident insurance coverage during deployment or training.

       SEC. 10. Health, Wellness, and Safety Programs. – Employing agencies
shall institute programs that promote physical, mental, and emotional well-being,
including mandatory health assessments, psychological debriefing post-
deployment, and wellness leave.

      SEC. 11. Database and Monitoring. – The DILG and OCD shall establish
and maintain a national registry of all DRRM officers and personnel, monitor
compliance with this Act, and submit an annual report to Congress on the
implementation of the Magna Carta and the status of DRRM human resources
nationwide.

      SEC. 12. Funding. – The amount necessary for the implementation of this
Act shall be charged against current appropriations and shall thereafter be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act. Local government units shall
likewise allocate funds to support DRRM personnel under their jurisdiction.

      SEC. 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations ("IRR"). – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the CSC, in coordination with the DBM, DILG,
OCD, and other relevant agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations to implement this Act.

     SEC. 14. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

      SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, and rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed
or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 16. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
