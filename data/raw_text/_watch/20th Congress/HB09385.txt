                                       Explanatory Note


This legislative measure seeks to renew the legislative franchise of the PRIMAX
BROADCASTING NETWORK, INC. to construct, install, establish, operate and maintain
radio and television broadcasting stations throughout the Philippines.

        The Primax Broadcasting Network, Inc. (Primax, for brevity) was incorporated and
registered at the Securities and Exchange Commission with a primary purpose of establishing
radio and television broadcasting stations in the Philippines for a commercial purpose and in
the public interest and to the extent that it may be authorized by law. It is guided by the tenet
that broadcasting is a public trust and the network is mindful of its duty to share in the task of
nation-building. With its twenty-five years of existence, it strives to serve more audiences in
more areas possible.

        The Primax will be embarking to set the pace by establishing stations in areas that are
underserved, if not served at all, by free-to-air broadcasting. Hence, there is an urgent need to
seek a legislative authorization for its continued operations.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

10   SECTION 1. Nature and Scope of Franchise. - Subject to the provisions of the 1987
11   Philippine Constitution and applicable laws, rules and regulations, the franchise
12   granted to the Primax Broadcasting Network, Inc. under Republic Act No. 8134
13   entitled “An Act Granting Primax Broadcasting Network, Inc. a Franchise to
14   Construct, Install, Operate and Maintain Radio and Television Broadcasting Stations
15   in the Philippines”, hereunder referred to as the grantee, its successors or assigns, to
16   construct, install, establish, operate, and maintain for commercial purposes and in the
17   public interest, radio and/or television broadcasting stations, including digital
18   television system, through microwave, satellite or whatever means, including the use of
19   any new technologies in television and radio systems, with the corresponding

20   technological auxiliaries and facilities, special broadcast and other program and
21   distribution services and relay stations in the Philippines is hereby renewed for another
22   twenty-five (25) years from the effectivity of this Act.


23   SEC. 2. Manner of Operation of Stations or Facilities. - The stations or facilities of
24   the grantee shall be constructed and operated in a manner as will, at most, result only
25   in the minimum interference on the wavelengths or frequencies of existing stations or
26   other stations which may be established by law, without in any way diminishing its
27   own privilege to use its assigned wavelengths or frequencies and the quality of
28   transmission or reception thereon as should maximize rendition of the grantee's
29   services and/or availability thereof.


30   SEC. 3. Prior Approval of the National Telecommunications Commission. - The
31   grantee shall secure from the National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) the
32   appropriate permits and licenses for the construction and operation of its stations and
33   facilities and shall not use any frequency in the radio/television spectrum without
34   authorization from the NTC. The NTC, however, shall not unreasonably withhold or
35   delay the grant of any such authority.


36   SEC. 4. Responsibility to the Public. - The grantee shall provide adequate public
37   service time to enable the government, through the said broadcasting stations or
38   facilities, to reach the population on important public issues; provide at all times sound
39   and balanced programming; assist in the functions of public information and
40   education; conform to the ethics of honest enterprise; and not use its stations and
41   facilities for the broadcasting of obscene and indecent language, speech, act or scene,
42   or for the dissemination of deliberately false information or willful misrepresentation,
43   to the detriment of the public interest, or to incite, encourage or assist in subversive
44   or treasonable acts.


45   SEC. 5. Right of Government. - A special right is hereby reserved to the President of
46   the Philippines, in times of war, rebellion, public peril, calamity, emergency, disaster
47   or disturbance of peace and order: to temporarily take over and operate the stations or

48   facilities of the grantee; to temporarily suspend the operation of any station or facility
49   in the interest of public safety, security and public welfare; or to authorize the
50   temporary use and operation thereof by any agency of the government, upon due
51   compensation to the grantee, for the use of said stations or facilities during the period
52   when these shall be so operated.


53      The radio spectrum is a finite resource that is part of the national patrimony and
54   the use thereof is a privilege conferred upon the grantee by the State and may be
55   withdrawn any time after due process.


56   SEC. 6. Term of Franchise. - This franchise shall be in effect for a period of twenty-
57   five (25) years, unless sooner revoked or cancelled. This franchise shall be deemed
58   ipso facto revoked in the event the grantee fails to operate continuously for two (2)
59   years.


60   SEC. 7. Acceptance and Compliance. - Acceptance of this new franchise shall be given
61   in writing to the Congress of the Philippines, through the Committee on Legislative
62   Franchises of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Public Services of
63   the Senate, within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act. Upon giving such
64   acceptance, the grantee shall exercise the privileges granted under this Act.
65   Nonacceptance shall render the franchise void.


66   SEC. 8. Self-regulation by and Undertaking of Grantee. - The grantee shall not require
67   any previous censorship of any speech, play, act or scene, or other matter to be
68   broadcast from its stations: Provided, That the grantee, during any broadcast, shall cut
69   off from the air the speech, play, act or scene, or other matter being broadcast if the
70   tendency thereof is to propose and/or incite treason, rebellion or sedition; or the
71   language used therein or the theme thereof is indecent or immoral: Provided, further,
72   That willful failure to do so shall constitute a valid cause for the cancellation of this
73   franchise.

74   SEC. 9. Warranty in Favor of National and Local Governments. - The grantee shall
75   hold the national, provincial, city and municipal governments of the Philippines free
76   from all claims, accounts, demands or actions arising out of accidents or injuries,
77   whether to property or to persons, caused by the construction or operation of the
78   stations of the grantee.


79   SEC. 10. Sale, Lease, Transfer, Grant of Usufruct, or Assignment of Franchise. - The
80   grantee shall not lease, transfer, grant the usufruct of, sell nor assign this franchise or
81   the rights and privileges acquired thereunder to any person, firm, company,
82   corporation or other commercial or legal entity, nor merge with any other corporation
83   or entity, nor shall the controlling interest of the grantee be transferred, whether as a
84   whole or in parts, and whether simultaneously or contemporaneously, to any such
85   person, firm, company, corporation or entity without the prior approval of the
86   Congress of the Philippines: Provided, That Congress shall be informed of any lease,
87   transfer, granting the usufruct of, sale or assignment of franchise or the rights and
88   privileges acquired thereunder, or of the merger, or transfer of controlling interest of
89   the grantee, within sixty (60) days after the completion of said transaction: Provided,
90   further, That failure to report to Congress such change of ownership shall render the
91   franchise ipso facto revoked: Provided, finally, That any person or entity to which this
92   franchise is sold, transferred or assigned shall be subject to the same conditions, terms,
93   restrictions and limitations of this Act.


94   SEC. 11. General Broadcast Policy Law. - The grantee shall comply with and be
95   subject to the provisions of a general broadcast policy law, which Congress may
96   hereafter enact.


97   SEC. 12. Reportorial Requirement. - The grantee shall submit an annual report to the
98   Congress of the Philippines, through the Committee on Legislative Franchises of the
99   House of Representatives and the Committee on Public Services of the Senate, on its
100 compliance with the terms and conditions of the franchise and on its operations on or
101 before April 30 of every year during the term of the franchise.

102 SEC. 13. Penalty Clause. - Failure to submit the annual report to Congress will be
103 penalized with a fine of five hundred pesos (Php500.00) per working day of
104 noncompliance. The fine shall be collected by the NTC from the delinquent franchise
105 grantee separate from the reportorial penalties imposed by the NTC. The collected
106 funds shall accrue to the monitoring fund of the NTC in line with its supervisory and
107 regulatory functions. The reportorial compliance certificate issued by Congress shall
108 be required before any application for permit or certificate is accepted by the NTC.


109 SEC. 14. Equality Clause. - Any advantage, favor, privilege, exemption, or immunity
110 granted under existing franchises, or which may hereafter be granted for radio and/or
111 television broadcasting, upon prior review and approval of Congress, shall become
112 part of this franchise and shall be accorded immediately and unconditionally to the
113 herein grantee: Provided, however, That the foregoing shall neither apply to nor affect
114 provisions of broadcasting franchises concerning territory covered by the franchise,
115 the life span of the franchise, or the type of service authorized by the franchise:
116 Provided, further, That the foregoing shall not apply to sale, lease, transfer, or grant of
117 usufruct of legislative franchise with prior congressional approval.


118 SEC. 15. Separability Clause. - If any of the sections or provisions of this Act is held
119 invalid, all other provisions not affected thereby shall remain valid.


120 SEC. 16. Repealability and Nonexclusivity Clause. - This franchise shall be subject to
121 amendment, alteration, or repeal by the Congress of the Philippines when the public
122 interest so requires and shall not be interpreted as an exclusive grant of the privileges
123 herein provided for.


124 SEC. 17. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
125 publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
