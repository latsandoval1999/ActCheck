                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

       As enshrined in the 1987 Constitution, “[t]he State shall adopt an
integrated and comprehensive approach to health development which shall
endeavor to make essential goods, health and other social services available
to all the people at affordable costs. There shall be priority for the needs of
the underprivileged, sick, elderly, disabled, women and children.”1

     “The family has the duty to care for its elderly members, but the State
may also do so through just programs of social security.”2

       The issues concerning rising incidents of abandonment of the elderly
have been raised during this Representation’s consultation with the senior
citizen sector. The problem is often attributed to the migration of young family
workers, as well as the costs for elderly care being unaffordable and
inaccessible to low-income households.

       Recognizing the rights of the elderly to life and protection, this bill seeks
to establish a nursing home for the abandoned and neglected senior citizens
in the Fifth District of Quezon City.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Novaliches
Home for the Elderly Act”.

SECTION 2. Nursing Home for the Elderly – There is hereby established
a nursing home for the abandoned and neglected senior citizens in the Fifth
District of Quezon City.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act, the following terms
shall mean:

   (a) Senior Citizen – refers to any resident citizen of the Philippines at
       least sixty (60) years of age;
   (b) Abandoned and Neglected Senior Citizen – refers to a senior
       citizen who has been deserted, permanently or temporarily, by
       their relatives or persons who have general or legal responsibility
       over the senior citizen, and/or from whom all basic care has been
       withdrawn, whether willfully or not;
   (c) Homeless Senior Citizen – refers to a senior citizen who lacks a
       fixed, regular residence, or whose current residence is unfit for
       human habitation, or lacks resources to obtain proper housing;
   (d) Nursing Home – refers to a home for the aged/elderly, established
       and maintained under this Act by each barangay which provides
       living accommodations, food and clothing, medical and health
       care, and recreational and social interaction activities for
       abandoned senior citizens or homeless senior citizens.
SECTION 4. Administration. – The Nursing Home established by this Act
shall be built, operated, and maintained by the Department of Social Welfare
and Development (DSWD) in coordination with the concerned local
government units.

SECTION 5. Functions of Nursing Homes. – The nursing homes shall
provide homeless senior citizens or abandoned senior citizens within its
territorial jurisdiction an institutionalized residential care program and
services such as but not limited to:

   (a) Comfortable living quarters;
   (b) Adequate food and clothing;
   (c) Medical consultation or treatment;
   (d) Health care;
   (e) Occupational opportunities;
   (f) Recreational and social interaction activities;
   (g) Counseling
   (h) Outreach to existing family for reintegration;
   (i) Literacy education;
   (j) Productivity development; and.
   (k) Religious, cultural, and vocational activities.

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DSWD, in
coordination with the Department of Interior and Local Government,
Department of Health, National Commission of Senior Citizens, the concerned
local government units, and other civil society organizations advancing the
welfare of senior citizens to be identified by the DSWD, shall hereby
promulgate the rules and regulations to carry out the provisions of this Act
not later than ninety (90) days after the effectivity of this Act.

SECTION 7. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of
the year following its enactment into law and every year thereafter.

SECTION 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or provision not otherwise affected
shall remain valid and subsisting.

SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule, or regulation
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is hereby repealed,
modified, or amended accordingly.

SECTION 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in at least two (2) newspaper of general circulation.
