                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

         The Barangay, as the frontline institutions of public service, play critical roles in
implementing various government programs, maintaining peace and order, resolving
disputes, responding to disasters and calamities, and delivering basic services at the
community level. As such, the effective delivery of these essential public services
depends significantly on the stability and capacity of barangay governments and their
officials.

         Consistent with Article X, Section 8 of the 1987 Constitution, which authorizes
Congress to determine by law the term of office of barangay officials, this counterpart
measure also filed in the Senate likewise seeks to enhance the stability and consistency
of public service at the barangay level by providing for a five (5)-year term for barangay
officials and members of the Sangguniang Kabataan. The proposed extension would
promote continuity in governance, provide sufficient time for the planning and
implementation of long-term community development initiatives, reduce disruptions
caused by frequent electoral exercises, and enable local officials to focus more
effectively on public service rather th an on electoral cycles.

      Ultimately, this measure aims to strengthen governance in the barangay level
and enable them to fully realize their mandates under the Local Government Code of
1991 as the primary planning and implementing units for government policies, plans,
programs, projects, and activities in the community.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly requested.
