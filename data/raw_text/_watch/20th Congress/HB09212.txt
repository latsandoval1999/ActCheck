                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This proposed measure seeks to professionalize the public basic education
sector by institutionalizing a dedicated Supervision Career Line within the Department
of Education's (DepEd) expanded career progression system.

       Republic Act No. 12288, otherwise known as the “Career Progression System
for Public School Teachers and School Leaders Act” was a landmark legislation that
marked a significant milestone for teacher welfare. The law was enacted in 2025 in
order to establish classroom teaching and school administration career lines with a
ladder for professional advancement. However, this still leaves a policy gap that needs
to be filled as more technical and pedagogical leadership is still required to sustain
such career growth in the teaching profession.

       This piece of legislation shall serve as the critical pillar in transforming the
teaching profession into a career that is growth-driven. It recognizes that instructional
leadership, in the form of mentoring teachers and managing curriculum, is a distinct
professional skill set that goes beyond classroom instruction and facility management.
While the existing law successfully allowed teachers to reach higher salary grades,
such as Master Teacher V and VI, without being forced to leave the classroom, without
this proposed measure, these teachers often lack the specialized career scaffolding
needed to meet increasingly complex professional standards. This bill ensures that
Master Teachers who possess a genius for pedagogical leadership can transition into
the Supervision Line as Education Supervisors or Senior Education Program Specialists.

       Under this expanded framework, the Supervision Career Line offers distinct
tracks that cater to the evolving needs of the education system. Educators may
advance through the Senior Education Program Specialist track (SG 19) or progress
through the Education Supervisor I to IV levels (SG 20 to SG 23), culminating in the
Chief Education Supervisor position (SG 24). These tracks are designed to ensure that
technical assistance and pedagogical guidance are available at every level of
governance, from the individual school to the regional office.

       The Supervision Career Line Act ensures that our educators are not just
promoted, but are actively supported by a professionalized supervisory system. We
are also hereby ensuring that the ladder provided by RA 12288 is held steady by a
competent supervision line.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                 AN ACT
     PROVIDING EQUITABLE CAREER PATHWAYS FOR PUBLIC SCHOOL
     TEACHERS AND LEADERS BY ESTABLISHING A SUPERVISION CAREER LINE
     AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

     in Congress assembled:

 1          SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Supervision Career
 2   Line Act.”
 3
 4          Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the declared policy of the State to uphold
 5   and enhance the right of all public school teachers and school leaders to professional
 6   advancement, recognizing that a motivated and highly competent teaching force is
 7   essential to attracting and retaining the best available talents through competitive
 8   remuneration and job fulfillment.
 9
10          Towards this end, the State shall implement just and equitable measures to
11   provide more defined career pathways and advancement opportunities, as well as
12   quality professional development programs, not only for public school teachers and
13   school heads, but also for supervisors that shall advance the right of each Filipino to
14   quality education.

35          Sec. 4. Institutionalizing a Supervision Career Line. – The expanded career
36   progression system for public school teachers and school leaders shall be strengthened
37   by institutionalizing a supervision career line, which will further promote their career
38   advancement and professional development and define their career lines within the
39   public school system, specifically in the elementary and secondary levels, and the
40   teacher career progression framework of DepEd.

46   confirmed the achievement of the professional standards' indicators relating to the
47   career stage of the position being applied for.

11          Sec. 8. Switching Career Lines. – Switching to and from the Supervision Career
12   Line to and from other career lines shall be allowed; Provided, That the aspirant
13   possesses all the required qualifications and set competencies of the position being
14   applied for in another career line, subject to the necessary assessment and other
15   applicable staffing standards and deployment parameters for the classroom teaching,
16   school administration and supervisory positions.
17
18        Sec. 9. Non-Diminution in the Salaries, Benefits and Other Privileges of
19   Incumbent Supervisors. – In no case shall incumbent supervisors’ ranks be demoted
20   or their salaries, benefits and other privileges, granted under existing laws, decrees
21   and others or stipulated in any policy, contract or agreement, between them and
22   DepEd, be diminished upon the effectivity of this Act.
23
24         Sec. 10. Promotion of Supervisors. – Supervisors may be promoted through
25   natural vacancies or reclassification of positions.
26
27         Applicants for promotion to any of the supervisory positions shall be evaluated
28   based on organizational and managerial effectiveness, in terms of productivity,
29   performance, utility, efficiency and other requirements related to professional
30   standards.
31
32         DepEd shall formulate clear guidelines for assessing supervisors seeking
33   promotion. A standards-based assessment shall be implemented and the assessment
34   process, criteria and point system shall be made accessible to the public.

11          Sec. 12. Support Program for Supervisors. – Public school teachers and school
12   heads aspiring to become supervisors, as well as the supervisors aspiring to advance
13   in the supervision career line but who do not pass the professional standards-based
14   assessment for two (2) consecutive times, shall be required to undergo a professional
15   development program. This support program shall be developed by the NEAP and shall
16   be offered free of charge.

18          TEC shall review, align and continuously monitor the roles of NEAP and the

21   constitutional right of teachers and school leaders to professional advancement.
22
23           Sec. 13. Role of Government Agencies. – In support of this Act, the CSC, DepEd,
24   TEC, NEAP and the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) shall harmonize the

26   administration and supervision career lines in the public school system.
27
28          The Department of Budget and Management (DBM) shall create the new
29   position titles of Education Supervisor I, Education Supervisor II, Supervisor III and
30   Education Supervisor IV and include the same in the Index of Occupational Services,
31   Occupational Groups, Classes and Salary Grades.
32
33           Sec. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
34   the effectivity of this Act, the CSC, DBM, DepEd, PRC and TEC, in consultation with

36   jointly issue the necessary rules and regulations (IRR) for its effective implementation.

38   circulation.
