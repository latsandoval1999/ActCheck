                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Oslob National High School, located in Barangay Lagunde, Municipality of
Oslob, Province of Cebu, was established in 1990 and has been in continuous operation
since. The campus occupies a 7,472-square-meter lot directly donated to the
Department of Education – Province of Cebu. It is strategically located to Oslob’s
Población and currently houses several buildings, a school canteen, and a school clinic.
As of School Year 2021-2022, the school serves 1,710 students in both junior and senior
high school, supported by 46 teachers and 2 non-teaching personnel.

       Despite its long-standing record of delivering quality education and its proven
capacity to effectively manage its own operations, Oslob National High School remains
an extension campus of Santander National High School which is located around 18
km away.

        Given the school’s proven capability growing student population, and the
increasing demand for quality education, it is both necessary and timely to convert the
Oslob National High School into an independent institution and separate it from the
Santander National High School. This will enhance administrative efficiency and allow
the school to better respond to the needs of its teachers, students, staff, and other
stakeholders.

        In 2021, the Sangguniang Bayan of Oslob expressed full support for the school’s
separation through Sangguniang Bayan Resolution No. 169: S-2021 dated November
10, 2021. Similar resolutions were also passed by the Parent-Teacher Association
(PTA) and the Barangay Council of Brgy. Lagunde,

       The Schools Division Superintendent, Ms. Marilyn S. Andales, EdD, CESO V,
has requested the separation of the Oslob National High School as it had met the
requirements set under DepEd Order No. 40 s. 2014 on the establishment, merging, and
conversion of public schools and separation of publicschool annexes in basic education.

       Unfortunately, despite the broad support of the stakeholders and the school’s
compliance with all necessary requirements, Oslob National High School remains an
annex.

         AN ACT SEPARATING THE OSLOB NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL
        LOCATED IN BARANGAY LAGUNDE, MUNICIPALITY OF OSLOB,
       PROVINCE OF CEBU, FROM ITS MOTHER UNIT, THE SANTANDER
      NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, CONVERTING IT INTO AN INDEPENDENT
      NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

            Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
     in Congress assembled:

 1        SECTION 1. Separation and Conversion into a National High School. – The
 2   Oslob National High School, an extension campus of the Santander National High
 3   School, located in Barangay Lagunde, Municipality of Oslob, Province of Cebu, and
 4   converted into an independent national high school.
 5
 6          SECTION 2. Transfer of Assets and Liabilities. – All personnel, assets,
 7   liabilities, and records pertaining to Oslob National High School, previously an
 8   extension campus of the Santander National High School, are hereby transferred to and
 9   absorbed by the independent Oslob National High School.
10
11         SECTION 3. Appropriations. – The Secretary of Education shall immediately
12   include in the Department of Education’s program the operationalization of the newly
13   independent Oslob National High School, the initial funding of which shall be charged
14   against the current year’s appropriations of the Oslob National High School, formerly
15   an extension campus of Santander National High School, and thereafter it shall be
16   included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
17
18         SECTION 4. Implementation. – The Secretary of Education shall issue rules and
19   regulations as may be necessary to effectively carry out the purpose of this Act.
20
21          SECTION 5. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
22   after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
