                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Republic Act No. 6975, otherwise known as the DILG Act of 1990, laid the
foundation for a more organized public safety and peace and order system in the
Philippines. Among the agencies established under the law are the Bureau of Fire
Protection (BFP) and the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP)---agencies
which have particularly important responsibilities in highly urbanized and densely
populated cities. In communities where large numbers of people live and work within
limited spaces, the risk of fires, emergencies, overcrowding, and other public safety
concerns is greater. Hence, the effective performance of their respective mandates
directly contributes to public safety, security, and the maintenance of peace and order.

       Given the increasingly complex demands placed upon these agencies, there is a
need to strengthen their organizational structures and professional standards. In 2018,
Republic Act No. 11200 was enacted to update the rank classifications of the BFP and
BJMP. These reforms were intended to establish clearer lines of authority and
responsibility, improve organizational efficiency, and address ambiguities in the existing
rank structure.

       However, organizational reforms must be complemented by measures that
strengthen the qualifications and capabilities of those entrusted with leadership and
supervisory responsibilities. For this reason, this proposed measure seeks to prescribe
higher educational qualifications for certain positions in the BFP and BJMP. Higher
educational standards are expected to enhance the capacity of personnel to manage their

responsibilities, exercise sound judgment, and effectively lead uniformed personnel in the
performance of their duties.

        The proposed measure recognizes that the BFP and BJMP are essential
components of the country's public safety and peace and order framework. Strengthening
their leadership and professional standards is particularly important in densely populated
urban areas, where the demands on these agencies are greater and where effective
coordination, supervision, and decision-making can have a direct impact on the safety
and welfare of the public.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is respectfully and fervently
sought.

SECTION 1. Section 3 of Republic Act No. 9263, as amended, is hereby amended
to read as follows:
       "SEC. 3. Organization and Key Positions of the BFP and the BJMP. -
The BFP and the BJMP shall be respectively headed by a Chief who shall be
assisted by two (2) Deputy Chiefs, one (1) for Administration and one (1) for
Operations, all of whom shall be appointed by the President upon
recommendation of the Secretary of the DILG from among the qualified officers
with at least the rank of senior superintendent FIRE/JAIL BRIGADIER GENERAL
in the service: Provided, That in no case shall any officer who has retired or is
retirable within six (6) months from his/her compulsory retirement age be
appointed as Chief of the Fire Bureau or Chief of the Jail Bureau, as the case may
be: Provided, further, That the Chief of the Bureau and Chief of the Jail Bureau
shall serve a tour of duty not to exceed four (4) years: Provided, however, that in
times of war or other national emergency declared by Congress, the President
may extend such tour of duty.

      The Heads of the BFP and the BJMP with the rank of director FIRE/JAIL
GENERAL shall have the position title of Chief of the Fire Bureau and the Chief
of the Jail Bureau, respectively. The second officers in command of the BFP and

the BJMP with the rank of chief superintendent FIRE/JAIL LIEUTENANT
GENERAL shall have the position title of Deputy Chief for Administration of the
Fire Bureau and Deputy Chief for Administration of the Jail Bureau, respectively.
The third officers in command of the BFP and the BJMP with the rank of chief
superintendent FIRE/JAIL LIEUTENANT GENERAL shall have the position title
of Deputy Chief for Operations of the Bureau and Deputy Chief for Operations of
the Jail Bureau, respectively. The fourth officers in command of the BFP and the
BJMP with the rank of chief superintendent FIRE/JAIL LIEUTENANT GENERAL
shall have the respective position title of Chief of Directorial Staff of the Fire Bureau
and Chief of Directorial Staff of the Jail Bureau, who shall be assisted by the
Directors of the Directorates in the respective National Headquarters Office with
at least the rank of chief superintendent FIRE/JAIL MAJOR GENERAL.

       The BFP and the BJMP shall CONSTRUCT, establish, operate and
maintain their respective regional offices in each of the administrative regions of
the country which shall be respectively headed by a Regional Director for Fire
Protection and a Regional Director of Jail Management and Penology with the
rank of chief superintendent FIRE/JAIL BRIGADIER GENERAL. He/she shall be
assisted by the following officers with the rank of FIRE/JAIL COLONEL: Assistant
Regional Director for Administration, Assistant Regional Director for Operations,
and Regional Chief of Directorial Staff: PROVIDED, THAT IN THE NATIONAL
CAPITAL REGION, THE AFOREMENTIONED OFFICERS SHALL HAVE THE
RANK OF FIRE/JAIL BRIGADIER GENERAL.

SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DILG in coordination with the
BFP and the BJMP, the CSC, the Department of Budget and Management, and
the Department of Finance, shall within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this
Act, promulgate the rules and regulations necessary to implement the provision of
this Act.

SEC. 6. Separability Clause. - If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity and effectivity of the other
provisions not affected thereby.

SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. - The renaming provisions of Republic Act No. 6975,
as amended by Republic Act No. 9263 that have reference to the rank and
classification of the uniformed personnel of the BFP and BJMP, and all laws,
decrees, orders, rules and regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof,
which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are hereby deemed
repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SEC. 8. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication In the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation, whichever comes earlier.
