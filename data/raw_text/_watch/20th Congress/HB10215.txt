  AN ACT TO KEEP INCOMES TAX-RELIEVED AND AUGMENTED (KITA) BY
 AMENDING SECTIONS 24, 32, 33, 34, 53, 57, 109, 123, AND 236 OF THE NATIONAL
   INTERNAL REVENUE CODE, AS AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

Filipino workers are burdened by prices that rise faster than wages, and a tax schedule that has not
moved since 2018. The result is “bracket creep,” where salary adjustments push workers to higher
statutory tax brackets, increasing their tax burden and eroding their increase in purchasing power.

To provide equitable support to Filipino workers, who pay taxes for social assistance programs but
rarely qualify for them, this “Keeping Incomes Tax-relieved and Augmented” (KITA) Act
proposes several measures to maximize their net take-home pay:
    1. Increase the annual income tax exemption from ₱250,000 to ₱650,000 of taxable income
       and the tax-free ceiling on bonuses to ₱200,000, and expand exemptions to holiday pay,
       overtime pay, night shift differential, hazard pay, service charges, and compensation for
       election service, so those with up to ₱57,000 a month in basic pay incur no income tax.
    2. Indexes brackets and ceilings to the Consumer Price Index, so relief granted toward is not
       lost to inflation tomorrow, shifts the taxation of fringe benefits from a flat rate to the
       progressive schedule, and relieves employer-provided insurance premiums.
    3. Raises the VAT-exempt threshold to ₱4 million, simplifying tax obligations for micro-
       taxpayers, allowing establishments to claim mandated discounts for senior citizens, persons
       with disabilities, and solo parents as direct tax credits, and providing administrative relief
       by automating filing extensions during calamities and removing unnecessary requirements.

By alleviating these burdens, the KITA Act recognizes workers as essential to our economy, and
lives up to the Constitution’s mandate for Congress to “evolve a progressive system of taxation”.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Keeping Incomes Tax-relieved
and Augmented” Act or the “KITA” Act.

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to:

   (a) Enhance the progressivity of the tax system through the rationalization of the Philippine
       internal revenue tax system, thereby promoting sustainable and inclusive economic
       growth;

   (b) Preserve the integrity of a progressive tax system and safeguard taxpayers' purchasing
       power by implementing an automatic tax indexation mechanism that adjusts income tax
       brackets for inflation, thereby preventing the financial erosion caused by tax bracket creep;
       and

   (c) Provide, as much as possible, an equitable relief to a greater number of taxpayers and their
       families in order to improve levels of disposable income and increase economic activity.
