                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to strengthen transparency and accountability in public service by
requiring government officials and employees to voluntarily waive bank-secrecy
protections provided under Republic Act (RA) No. 1405, otherwise known as the “Secrecy of
Bank Deposits Law”, and authorize the Office of the Ombudsman to examine all their
deposits and investments, both domestic and foreign.

        According to Transparency International’s 2021 Corruption Perceptions Index, the
Philippines ranked 114th out of 180 countries, scoring thirty-three (33) out of a hundred
(100)1. This underscores the urgent need for stronger mechanisms to ensure financial
disclosure, and protect the public interest.

        To address this, this proposed measure mandates the waiver of bank secrecy, and
grants the Ombudsman direct access to the financial holdings of public officials, enabling
the government to detect, prevent, and penalize illicit transactions, money laundering, and
concealment of assets.

        This proposal complements RA No. 3019, otherwise known as the “Anti‑Graft and
Corrupt Practices Act”, and RA No. 6713, otherwise known as the “Code of Conduct and
Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees” by closing a critical gap in transparency
and enforcement. The timely submission of the written waiver and the accountability it
imposes create a strong deterrent against hidden financial misconduct among those
entrusted with serving the people.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
MANDATING GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS AND EMPLOYEES TO EXECUTE
AND SUBMIT A WRITTEN PERMISSION TO EXAMINE, INQUIRE OR LOOK
INTO ALL THEIR DEPOSITS AND INVESTMENTS THEREBY WAIVING THE
BANK SECRECY LAW AS PROVIDED FOR UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERS
1405 AND 6426

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bank Secrecy Waiver Act”.

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote transparency, accountability, and integrity in public service. All public officials
and employees shall be open and transparent in all their financial transactions and
dealings.

      SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all public officials and employees as
defined under Section 3(a) and (b) of Republic Act No. 6713, otherwise known as the
“Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees”, except those
who serve in an honorary capacity.

       SEC. 4. Submission of Written Waiver. – All public officials and employees covered
by this Act shall, within thirty (30) days from assumption of office, submit to the Office
of the Ombudsman a written waiver expressly authorizing the Ombudsman to
examine, inquire into, and look into all their deposits and investments, including
foreign currency deposits, in banks and other financial institutions, both within and
outside the Philippines, as well as investment bonds issued by the government of the
Philippines, its political subdivisions, and instrumentalities.

       Incumbent public officials and employees shall submit their written waiver
within thirty (30) days from the effectivity of the implementing rules and regulations of
this Act.




                                            1
       SEC. 5. Failure or Refusal to Submit Bank Secrecy Waiver. – Any public official or
employee who fails or refuses to submit the required written waiver as mandated by
this Act shall not be allowed to enter upon, or continue exercising the functions of, his
or her office, and shall be subject to administrative and/or criminal liability as may be
provided by law.

       SEC. 6. Use and Confidentiality of Information. – The written waiver and any
information or data obtained by virtue thereof shall be exclusively used by the Office of
the Ombudsman for the purpose of investigating a duly verified complaint or
prosecuting a case before the courts. The Ombudsman shall ensure the confidentiality
of such information, except as may be necessary for the performance of its official
functions.

      SEC. 7. Lead Agency. – The Office of the Ombudsman shall have the primary
responsibility to implement this Act: Provided, That it shall enlist the advice of the
Department of Justice (DOJ) and the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) in the drafting of
the written permission or waiver as provided in this Act.

      SEC. 8. Penalties. – Any person who unlawfully discloses or misused information
obtained under this Act shall be penalized in accordance with existing laws, without
prejudice to administrative or criminal liability.

      SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Office of the Ombudsman shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this
Act within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

       SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are hereby repealed
or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.

       Approved,
