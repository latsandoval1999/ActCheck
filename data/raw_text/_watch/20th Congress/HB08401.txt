                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to terminate the collection of toll fees along the portion of the
Benguet Road (Kennon Road) from Klondyke’s Spring to Camp Six, and to repeal
Executive Order (EO) No. 34, Series of 1954, as amended, which provides the legal
basis for such toll collection.

        The Baguio–Bontoc–Halsema Highway–Kennon National Toll Road currently
includes three toll gates situated in Camp 1, Camp 6, and Acop, Tublay, Benguet.
The toll collection at Camp Six has been implemented pursuant to EO 34, issued by
President Ramon Magsaysay on June 1, 1954. This order authorized the imposition
of toll fees along specified sections of the Benguet Road to support its maintenance
at that time.

       However, the enactment of Republic Act (RA) No. 8794 on June 27, 2000,
entitled “An Act Imposing a Motor Vehicle User's Charge on Owners of all Types of Motor
Vehicles and for Other Purposes”, fundamentally changed the framework for funding
road maintenance nationwide. Under RA 8794, all motor vehicle user’s charges are
collected and deposited in special trust accounts in the National Treasury, including
the Special Road Support Fund and the Special Local Road Fund. The Department of
Public Works and Highways utilizes these funds for the repair, maintenance, and
improvement of national and local roads across the country.

      Given this statutory scheme, continued toll collection at Camp Six results in
double charging of road users, once through the Motor Vehicle User's Charge
mandated by RA 8794, and again through the tolls authorized under EO 34. This
redundancy places an undue burden on thousands of motorists who traverse
Kennon Road daily, many of whom are farmers from Benguet, Mountain Province,
and nearby provinces transporting their agricultural produce to the La Trinidad
Vegetable Trading Post or to various markets in Metro Manila.

      Maintaining this duplicative toll system is inequitable and inconsistent with
the current national policy on road user fees. Repealing EO 34 and terminating the
Camp Six toll collection will promote fairness, ease the financial load on farmers and
local transporters, and align existing regulations with the comprehensive road
funding structure established under RA 8794.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is respectfully sought.

                            AN ACT
TERMINATING THE COLLECTION OF TOLL FEES ALONG THE PORTION
OF THE BENGUET ROAD (KENNON ROAD) FROM KLONDYKE'S SPRING
TO CAMP SIX, REPEALING FOR THE PURPOSE EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 34,
SERIES OF 1954, AS AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. The collection of toll fees along the portion of the Benguet Road
(Kennon Road) from Klondyke's Spring in Camp Six within the Mountain Province
is hereby terminated.

       SEC. 2. Executive Order No. 34, Series of 1954, as amended, is hereby
repealed. All other laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations, and other issuances,
or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are repealed or modified
accordingly.

       SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
