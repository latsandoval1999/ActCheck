                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to establish a Shari’ah-Compliant Financing Facility under
the Social Security System (SSS), further amending for the purpose Republic
Act (RA) No. 1161, as amended, otherwise known as the “Social Security
Law.”

       The 1987 Philippine Constitution recognizes the State’s duty to promote
social justice, afford full protection to labor, and ensure equal protection of the
laws. It likewise guarantees the free exercise of religion. These constitutional
principles call for a social security system that is responsive to the diverse needs
and circumstances of its members, including those who observe Islamic
financial principles.

      The SSS, pursuant to RA 1161, as amended, provides social security
protection against disability, sickness, maternity, old age, death, and other
contingencies resulting in loss of income or financial burden. In addition to the
payment of social security benefits, the law expressly authorizes the investment
of SSS reserve funds in various forms of member financing, including salary,
educational, livelihood, calamity, emergency, housing, and pensioner loans.

       The existing financing programs of the SSS, however, are generally
structured on conventional interest-bearing arrangements. For members who
observe Islamic financial principles, particularly the prohibition against riba or
interest, such arrangements may not provide an appropriate financing option
consistent with their religious beliefs and practices.
       RA 11439, otherwise known as the “Islamic Banking Act,” recognizes
the role of Islamic finance in promoting financial inclusion and provides for
various Shari’ah-compliant financing structures, including Murabahah, Ijarah,
Musharakah, Mudarabah, decreasing participation, and Istisna’a. These
structures provide alternative means of financing based on principles such as
asset-backed transactions, leasing, partnership, profit-sharing, and other
arrangements consistent with Shari’ah principles.

       In recognition of these principles, this measure seeks to establish a
Shari’ah-Compliant Financing Facility under the SSS, providing qualified
members with an alternative means of accessing SSS financing without
requiring the payment or receipt of interest prohibited under Shari’ah
principles. The Facility shall not constitute a separate social security benefit,
provident savings scheme, or banking operation. Rather, it shall serve as an
alternative financing modality within the existing financing authority of the SSS
and provide Shari’ah-compliant financing programs and products
corresponding to financing activities already authorized under RA 11199.

      The measure further ensures that the Facility shall remain subject to the
actuarial, financial, investment, liquidity, safety and security safeguards
applicable to the SSS. Participation in the Facility shall be voluntary and open
to all qualified SSS members, regardless of religious affiliation, while
responding to the financing needs for Muslim Filipinos who observe Shari’ah
principles.

      To ensure the integrity and proper implementation of the Facility, the
measure likewise establishes a Shari’ah Advisory Council that shall advise the
Commission on the Shari’ah compliance of proposed financing products,
contracts, structures, and policies. The Council shall also exercise an advisory
and oversight function on Shari’ah compliance without interfering with the
ordinary administration and management, and operational functions of the SSS.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.
