                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article II, Section 13 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that the State recognizes the vital
role of the youth in nation-building and shall promote and protect their physical, moral, spiritual,
intellectual, and social well-being. Furthermore, Article XV, Section 3 guarantees the right of
children to assistance, including proper care and nutrition, and special protection from all forms of
neglect, abuse, cruelty, exploitation, and other conditions prejudicial to their development.

Schools are meant to be safe havens where young learners acquire knowledge, foster friendships,
and build the foundation for their futures. However, public schools across the Philippines face
growing vulnerabilities to security threats, unauthorized intrusions, theft of government equipment,
vandalism, and physical violence. Elementary and high school students, owing to their tender age,
are particularly vulnerable and require proactive protection.

At present, many public schools lack dedicated, regular security personnel due to budgetary
constraints or reliance on short-term contractual hiring and voluntary community assistance. This
leaves basic educational facilities unprotected, placing both students and educators at risk.

This bill seeks to address this critical gap by creating permanent, regular position titles for School
Security Officers (SSOs) across all public schools nationwide. By establishing plantilla positions
under the Department of Education (DepEd), the government ensures professionalized, well-
trained, and stable security coverage in our basic education system.

Additionally, this legislation mandates a Comprehensive School Safety and Security Program
applicable to both public and private elementary schools. This program includes physical security
enhancements, compulsory emergency drills, mandatory visitor screening protocols, anti-bullying
initiatives, and close coordination with local law enforcement and disaster risk management units.

In view of the paramount importance of safeguarding our children and ensuring a conducive
learning environment, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
 CREATING PERMANENT SCHOOL SECURITY OFFICER POSITIONS IN ALL
     PUBLIC ELEMENTARY AND HIGH SCHOOLS NATIONWIDE AND
  ESTABLISHING A COMPREHENSIVE SCHOOL SAFETY AND SECURITY
              PROGRAM, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the "Schools Safety Officer Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and promote
the physical, moral, and intellectual well-being of the youth. The State recognizes
that a safe, secure, and peaceful learning environment is essential to quality basic
education. To this end, the State shall mandate adequate security infrastructure,
professional personnel, and emergency preparedness protocols in all elementary
schools nationwide.

SEC. 3. Creation of Permanent School Security Officer (SSO) Positions. —

   1. There are hereby created permanent plantilla positions for School Security
      Officers (SSOs) under the Department of Education (DepEd).

   2. Every public elementary and high school in the Philippines shall be
      allocated at least one (1) regular SSO position, with additional personnel
      assigned based on student population, physical campus size, and localized
      risk assessment, subject to the guidelines set by DepEd and the
      Department of Budget and Management (DBM).
   3. The qualification standards, salary grades, and duties for SSOs shall be
      determined by DepEd in coordination with the Civil Service Commission
      (CSC).

SEC. 4. Qualifications and Training of School Security Officers. —

   1. SSOs must be Filipino citizens of good moral character, possessing the
      requisite security clearance and civil service eligibility as determined by
      the CSC.

   2. Prior to deployment, all appointed SSOs shall undergo a compulsory
      comprehensive training program conducted by DepEd in partnership with
      the Philippine National Police (PNP). The training curriculum shall
      include, but not be limited to:

         o   Child protection policies and child-sensitive handling techniques;
         o   Campus access control and visitor verification protocols;
         o   De-escalation and non-violent conflict resolution;
         o   Basic first aid and emergency response; and
         o   Threat identification and physical security management.

SEC. 5. Comprehensive School Safety and Security Program. — All public and
private schools shall establish and implement a Comprehensive School Safety
and Security Program, which shall include the following minimum components:

   1. Access Control: Mandatory perimeter fencing, functional gate locks, single-
      point entry protocols during school hours, and visitor logging mechanisms.

   2. Emergency Preparedness: Regular, age-appropriate drills for earthquakes,
      fires, lockdown scenarios, and active security threats, conducted at least
      once per quarter.

   3. Child Protection and Anti-Violence: Strict enforcement of anti-bullying
      regulations, abuse prevention measures, and clear reporting procedures for
      threats or incidents inside school premises.

   4. Law Enforcement Coordination: Formal operational linkages between each
      school and its nearest PNP station or local Barangay Health and Safety
      Task Force for rapid response during emergencies.

   5. Physical and Electronic Surveillance: Standard installation of adequate
      lighting and, subject to available funding and privacy guidelines, security
      monitoring systems in key public areas of the campus.

SEC. 6. Private School Compliance. — Private schools shall develop and enforce
their own internal safety and security programs conforming to the standards set
forth in Section 5 of this Act as a requirement for the issuance and renewal of
their permits to operate by DepEd.

SEC. 7. Inter-Agency Task Force on Campus Security. — An Inter-Agency Task
Force chaired by the Secretary of the Department of Education, with
representatives from the Department of the Interior and Local Government
(DILG), the Philippine National Police (PNP), and the National Disaster Risk
Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC), is hereby created to monitor
compliance, conduct annual security audits, and recommend policy
improvements.

SEC. 8. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act, including the creation and filling of regular SSO positions in public
elementary schools, shall be charged against the current appropriations of the
Department of Education. Thereafter, such sums as may be needed for its
continued implementation shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act (GAA).

SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Education, in consultation with the
DBM, CSC, DILG, and PNP, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary
to carry out the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 10. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, administrative orders,
rules, and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act
are hereby repealed, modified, or amended accordingly.

SEC. 12. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
