                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Republic Act No. 9136, otherwise known as the “Electric Power Industry Reform
Act” (EPIRA), heralded the transition from a public sector vertically-integrated monopoly
to a private sector-led competitive but regulated industry. One of the declared policies of
the EPIRA is to “establish a strong and purely independent regulatory body and system to
ensure consumer protection and enhance the competitive operation of the electricity
market.”1 This regulatory body is the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC). It is
responsible for setting performance standards and retail rates, allowing recovery of just and
reasonable costs as well as reasonable returns, and ensuring consumer protection.2 These
duties result in a delicate balancing act of the regulator in deciding its cases.

      However, the lack of representation for consumers is glaring in cases before the ERC.

      Hence this measure. This bill creates an Energy Consumer Advocate Office (ECAO)
led by the Energy Consumer Advocate. The ECAO acts as the independent consumer
representative in all rate-setting, rule-making, and other energy-related cases and
proceedings before government agencies, including judicial and quasi-judicial bodies. The
creation of the ECAO aims to attain what jurisdictions with an institutionalized consumer
advocate have, such as the postponement of utilities’ rate increase applications, a decrease
in the income of utilities, and lower electricity retail prices.3

      Hence, the passage of this measure is sought.

                            AN ACT
     CREATING THE ENERGY CONSUMER ADVOCATE OFFICE,
     PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

1         SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be referred to as the “Energy
2    Consumer Advocate Act.”
3

4         SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
5    to recognize the right of energy consumers to reasonable rates, adequate and reliable
6    services, and to transparent processes involving these rates and services. Towards
7    this end, there is a need for institutionalized and independent consumer
8    representation in rate-setting, rule-making, and other energy related cases and
9    proceedings before government agencies including judicial and quasi-judicial
10   bodies.
11

12        SEC. 3. The Energy Consumer Advocate Office (ECAO). – The ECAO is
13   hereby created which shall be an independent and autonomous office attached to the
14   Department of Justice (DOJ) in accordance with Section 38 (3), Chapter 7, Book IV
15   of Executive Order No. 292, otherwise known as the Administrative Code of 1987,
16   as amended.
17
1        SEC. 4. Powers and Functions. - The ECAO shall be the representative of all
2    consumers in energy sector matters affecting the public interest before any
3    department, commission, agency, or court. It shall independently discharge its
4    mandate towards the best interest of all consumers, ensuring least cost without
5    compromising safety, reliability, and sufficient service. Towards this end, the
6    ECAO shall:
7

8        (1) Initiate, intervene, and represent all consumers, in rate-setting, rule-
9             making, and other energy related cases, complaints, proceedings, and
10            consultations before the Department of Energy (DOE), Energy Regulatory
11            Commission (ERC), National Electrification Administration (NEA),
12            Philippine Competition Commission (PCC), and other judicial and quasi-
13            judicial bodies;
14       (2) Initiate, intervene, and represent all consumers in actions before any trial
15            or appellate court, including the Supreme Court, which involve the
16            validity of any decision, memoranda, rule, regulation, circular, or order of
17            the DOE, ERC, NEA, PCC, and other departments, commissions, and
18            agencies insofar as these decisions and policy issuances affect energy rates
19            and services;
20       (3) Monitor all issuances of the DOE, ERC, NEA, PCC, and other
21            departments, commissions, and agencies insofar as these affect energy
22            rates and services;
23       (4) Engage in regular information, education, and communication programs
24            for all consumers on laws, rules, regulations, decisions, circulars, and
25            orders that affect energy rates and services, including consumer rights and
26            remedies;
27       (5) Be furnished or served, as the case may be, by departments, commissions,
28            and agencies with all documents, notices, petitions, applications,
29            complaints, answers, motions, and other pleadings in all energy related
30            cases, complaints, proceedings, and consultations;
31       (6) Have the right to access, free of charge, all files, records, and documents
32            in the DOE, ERC, NEA, PCC and other departments, commissions, and
1              agencies pertaining to energy related cases, complaints, proceedings, and
2              consultations: Provided, That the files, records, and documents do not
3              pertain to the following:

4              (a) Personnel information;
5              (b) Confidential communications between commissioners, judges, or
6                    justices and their general counsels on records relating to proceedings
7                    in which the ECAO is a party to; or
8              (c) Confidential information declared as such by the ERC; and
9

10        (7) Be free of charge in its use of ordinary mail, registered mail, or courier
11             service for the transmittal of official communications and papers directly
12             connected with the conduct of its powers, functions, or exercise of
13             administrative supervision over its personnel.
14

15      ECAO lawyers shall have the authority to administer oaths in connection with
16   the performance of their duty.
17

18        SEC. 5. The Energy Consumer Advocate and Deputy Energy Consumer
19   Advocate. – The Energy Consumer Advocate (ECA) shall head the ECAO, shall
20   have the primary authority and responsibility for the effective exercise of its mandate
21   and the discharge of its powers and functions, and shall be assisted by a Deputy
22   ECA. In case of temporary absence of the ECA, the Deputy ECA shall be designated
23   as the Officer-in-Charge of the ECAO.
24

25        The ECA and Deputy ECA shall be natural-born citizens of the Philippines, at
26   least forty (40) years of age, with at least fifteen (15) years of practice in law, and
27   with proven competence, integrity, probity, and independence: Provided, That the
28   ECA shall have at least six (6) years of practice in the field of energy, and the Deputy
29   ECA shall have at least four (4) years of practice in the field of energy. Both of them
30   shall not be affiliated with any energy company, as member of the board, officer,
31   employee, or consultant, at least one (1) year before their respective appointments.
32
1         SEC. 6. Appointment, Term, and Rank. – The ECA and Deputy ECA shall be
2    appointed by the President: Provided, That the appointment of the Deputy ECA shall
3    be upon the recommendation of the ECA. They shall both serve for a term of six (6)
4    years and may be reappointed: Provided, That for the first appointees, the ECA and
5    Deputy ECA shall hold office until the end of term of the appointing President.

6

7         The ECA and Deputy ECA shall have the same rank, salaries, allowances, and
8    retirement benefits as the Prosecutor General and Senior Deputy State Prosecutor of
9    the National Prosecution Service respectively: Provided, That their retirement
10   benefits shall be prorated in accordance with Section 21 of Republic Act No. 10071,
11   otherwise known as the Prosecution Services Act.

12

13        SEC. 7. Prohibitions. – The ECA and Deputy ECA shall be prohibited from:
14

15        (1) Serving as a member of the board, officer, employee, or consultant of any
16             energy company in the course of their respective terms of office: Provided,
17             That this prohibition shall also apply to their family members up to the
18             fourth degree of consanguinity or affinity;
19        (2) Having any interest, ownership, or stake in any energy company in the
20             course of their respective terms of office: Provided, That this prohibition
21             shall also apply to their family members up to the fourth degree of
22             consanguinity or affinity;
23        (3) Representing any government agency, government –owned or –controlled
24             corporation, or private company in any energy related case, proceeding,
25             or consultation before any department, commission, agency, or court, in
26             the course of one (1) year after the end of their respective terms of office;
27             and
28        (4) Being members of a political party and committee, or engaging in any
29             partisan political activity in the course of their respective terms of office.
30
1           SEC. 8. Organizational Structure and Plantilla Positions. – The ECAO, upon
2    approval of the DOJ, shall submit its organizational structure and plantilla positions
3    to the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) not later than six (6) months
4    from the effectivity of this Act: Provided, That the DBM shall approve the final
5    organizational structure and plantilla positions of the ECAO not later than three (3)
6    months from submission.
7

8           The ECAO, upon approval of the DOJ, may request the DBM for any change
9    in the organizational structure and plantilla positions after a comprehensive review
10   resulting to a finding that any alteration will improve the discharge of the ECAO’s
11   powers and functions: Provided, That the DBM shall render a decision on such
12   request within three (3) months from submission.
13

14          The ECAO shall have legal, technical, and administrative personnel in its
15   organizational structure and staffing complement, and may hire consultants as expert
16   witnesses or technical advisors as it finds necessary for the full and efficient
17   discharge of its powers and functions. All employees and consultants shall be subject
18   to civil service laws, rules, and regulations: Provided, That all employees shall be
19   subject to Republic Act No. 6758, otherwise known as the Salary Standardization
20   Act.
21

22          SEC. 9. Removal and Vacancy. – The ECA and Deputy ECA shall not be
23   removed or suspended, except for cause provided by law. The President may remove
24   the ECA or the Deputy ECA for malfeasance, misfeasance, or nonfeasance in office,
25   or for any cause which renders any of them ineligible for appointment, or incapable,
26   or unfit to discharge the mandate, powers, and functions of the ECAO.
27

28          In case of death, permanent incapacity, removal, or resignation of the
29   incumbent ECA or Deputy ECA, the President may appoint or designate one, as the
30   case may be, in an acting capacity until a new ECA or Deputy ECA has been
31   appointed: Provided, That the new appointment shall only be for the unexpired term
32   of the predecessor.

2           SEC. 10. Exemption from Fees and Costs of Suits. – The ECAO shall be exempt
3    from payment of docket and other fees incidental to instituting an action in court and
4    other quasi-judicial bodies, as an original proceeding or on appeal.
5

6           SEC. 11. Costs of Suit. – The costs of the suit, attorney’s fees, and contingent
7    fees imposed upon adversarial parties of the ECAO after a successful litigation shall
8    be deposited in the National Treasury.
9

10          SEC. 12. Grant of Special Allowances. – The ECA, Deputy ECA, service or
11   division heads, and lawyers shall be granted special allowances in amounts to be
12   determined by the DBM: Provided, That the grant of said special allowances shall
13   not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the basic salary of the ECAO officials
14   and lawyers: Provided further, That special allowances shall be paid from costs of
15   suit, attorney’s fees and contingent fees deposited in the National Treasury pursuant
16   to Section 11 of this Act: Provided finally, That whenever the funding source is
17   insufficient, the special allowance shall be paid through the General Appropriations
18   Act.
19

20          SEC. 13. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
21   implementation of this Act in the amount of two hundred million pesos
22   (P200,000,000.00) shall be taken from the budget of the DOJ. Thereafter, the amount
23   of two hundred million pesos (P200,000,000.00) shall be automatically appropriated
24   and included in the annual General Appropriations Act every year which may be
25   increased as may be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act.
