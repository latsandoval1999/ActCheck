                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to declare the second Thursday of March of every year as
a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Mankayan, Province of
Benguet, in commemoration of its Foundation Day. The measure aims to give
due recognition to the municipality’s historical evolution, cultural heritage,
and enduring contributions to the province.

       The Municipality of Mankayan traces its beginnings to the year 1913,
when it was established as a municipal district annexed to the then
Sub-Province of Benguet. This development marked the formal recognition of
its local government officials, following a period under the jurisdiction of the
Lepanto–Bontoc Province since 1903. Mankayan’s status as a full-fledged
municipality was later affirmed on June 6, 1955 through the enactment of
Republic Act No. 1302 entitled “An Act to Convert the Municipal District of
Mankayan, Sub-Province of Benguet, Mountain Province, into a Municipality”,
thereby establishing its present political and administrative identity.

       Today, Mankayan stands as one of Benguet’s most historically and
economically significant municipalities. Widely regarded as a mining town, it
hosts major mining operations, including those of the Lepanto Consolidated
Mining Company. The municipality, classified as second class, comprises
twelve (12) barangays: Balili, Bedbed, Bulalacao, Cabiten, Colalo, Guinaoang,
Paco, Palasaan, Poblacion, Sapid, Tabio, and Taneg. Celebrated as the “Bosom
of Gold and Copper of Benguet,” Mankayan is richly endowed with mineral
resources, mountains shaped by centuries of mining, and a vibrant tapestry of
traditions and collective memory.

      The people of Mankayan commemorate their municipality’s founding
through the annual celebration of Foundation Day, every second Thursday of
March, as provided in Municipal Ordinance No. 2004-01 enacted by the
Sangguniang Bayan on January 26, 2004. Declaring this day as a special
nonworking holiday will institutionalize this important local tradition,
encourage broader community participation, and foster deeper appreciation
of Mankayan’s history and identity.

     In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                        AN ACT
DECLARING THE SECOND THURSDAY OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL
NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MANKAYAN,
PROVINCE OF BENGUET IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING
ANNIVERSARY, TO BE KNOWN AS “MANKAYAN DAY”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Second Thursday of March of every year is hereby declared a
special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Mankayan, Province of
Benguet, to be known as “Mankayan Day”, in commemoration of its Founding
Anniversary.

      SEC. 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
