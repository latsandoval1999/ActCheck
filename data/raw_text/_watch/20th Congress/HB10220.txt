                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The coconut industry remains one of the cornerstones of Philippine agriculture,
providing livelihood to millions of Filipino farmers and contributing significantly to rural
development, food security, and export earnings. Recognizing the historical injustice
suffered by coconut farmers, Congress enacted Republic Act No. 11524, or the
Coconut Farmers and Industry Trust Fund Act, to ensure that the recovered coconut
levy assets are utilized exclusively for the benefit of coconut farmers and the long-term
rehabilitation and modernization of the coconut industry.

      While the enactment of Republic Act No. 11524 marked a significant milestone,
experience in its implementation has revealed operational, administrative, and
governance challenges that limit the timely and effective utilization of the Trust Fund.
Delays in project implementation, limited absorptive capacity, fragmented coordination
among implementing agencies, outdated beneficiary records, and the absence of
measurable performance targets have hindered the realization of the law's intended
benefits. There likewise remains a need to strengthen transparency, improve
accountability mechanisms, and ensure that Trust Fund resources directly translate
into higher productivity, increased farmer incomes, and long-term resilience of the
coconut sector.

      This bill seeks to strengthen the implementation of Republic Act No. 11524 by
enhancing the governance framework of the Coconut Farmers and Industry Trust
Fund. It authorizes the Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA) to adopt more responsive
implementation strategies, establish measurable annual performance targets,
periodically update the Coconut Farmers and Industry Development Plan, digitize and
continuously validate the Coconut Farmers Registry, and create a dedicated Program
Management Office to improve project delivery and monitoring. The measure also
expands support programs for coconut farmers, including productivity enhancement,
comprehensive insurance, research and innovation, enterprise development,
infrastructure, social protection, and disaster assistance.

     The proposed amendments further strengthen transparency and accountability
by requiring the publication of annual implementation reports, beneficiary information

                                             1
consistent with data privacy laws, audit findings, and project accomplishments. It
likewise refines the management and disposition of coconut levy assets to ensure that
decisions are guided by the long-term interests of coconut farmers and that public
assets are utilized in a manner that generates measurable economic benefits for the
industry. The bill also enhances farmer participation in governance by extending the
tenure of farmer representatives in the PCA Board and institutionalizing regular
consultations with stakeholders.

      By modernizing the governance structure of the Coconut Farmers and Industry
Trust Fund and ensuring more efficient, transparent, and accountable implementation,
this measure seeks to maximize the developmental impact of recovered coconut levy
assets and secure sustainable economic opportunities for present and future
generations of coconut farmers.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
