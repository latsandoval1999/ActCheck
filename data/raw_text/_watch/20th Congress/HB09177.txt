                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

     Late 2025, this Representation was approached by members of the Philippine National
Police – Batangas City Police Office who were seeking medical assistance for their three
comrades-in-arms wounded during a shootout and were confined in a private hospital in
Batangas City.

     The hospital bill for each policeman ranged from half a million to almost a million pesos.
The combined amount was so large that their co-workers were “passing the hat” in order to
raise the funds needed. It took the donations from private individuals as well as numerous
DOH Guarantee Letters to finally settle the bill.

     This incident made this Representation realize that our Military and Uniformed Personnel
(MUP), the very people charged with securing the peace and order of our country and
protecting our sovereignty lack the State’s support when their bodies are injured in the course
of doing their duty as they lay their very lives on the line for our country. This incident should
not be repeated ever again. It is for this reason that this measure seeking to provide members
of the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP), the Philippine National Police (PNP), the Bureau
of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP), and the Philippine Coast Guard (PCG) with full
coverage medical assistance for injuries incurred in the line of duty is being filed.

    In view of the foregoing, the expeditious passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
PROVIDING FULL COVERAGE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE TO ANY OFFICER OR
 UNIFORMED PERSONNEL OF THE ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES,
THE BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION, THE BUREAU OF JAIL MANAGEMENT
 AND PENOLOGY, THE PHILIPPINE COAST GUARD, AND THE PHILIPPINE
      NATIONAL POLICE, FOR INJURIES SUSTAINED DURING THE
   PERFORMANCE OF OFFICIAL DUTIES, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
                          THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Full Coverage Medical Assistance
for Military and Uniformed Personnel Act”.

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the declared policy of the State to protect the dignity and
rights of its citizens, including those responsible for upholding the Constitution, defending the
sovereignty of the country and its territory against all enemies, enforcing the laws of the land,
preventing crimes, and maintaining peace and order. To this end, the State shall provide full
coverage medical assistance to military and uniformed personnel (MUP), including officers
and uniformed personnel of the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP), the Bureau of Fire
Protection (BFP), the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP), the Philippine Coast
Guard (PCG), and the Philippine National Police (PNP), for injuries sustained during the
performance of official duties.

SEC. 3. Full Coverage Medical Assistance – Any officer or uniformed personnel of the AFP,
BFP, BJMP, PCG, or PNP sustaining injuries during the performance of official duties, shall
be entitled to full coverage medical assistance chargeable against the Department of Health’s
(DOH) Medical Assistance to Indigent and Financially Incapacitated Patients Program
(MAIFIPP) fund, subject to said fund’s guidelines.

Officers or uniformed personnel of the AFP, BFP, BJMP, PCG, or PNP who have been granted
medical assistance shall continue to enjoy such assistance until injury has been fully treated.

SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the Secretaries of the Department of Budget and Management, Department of
Defense, Department of the Interior and Local Government, and Department of Transportation
and the Department of Health, in coordination with Chief of Staff of the AFP, the Chief of the
BFP, the Chief of the BJMP, the Commandant of the PCG, and the Chief of the PNP, shall
issue the necessary rules and regulations to implement this Act.

SEC. 5. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – Should any provision of this Act be declared invalid, the
remaining provisions shall continue to be in force and effect.

SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules, regulations or parts thereof
inconsistent with this Act are hereby amended, repealed, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
