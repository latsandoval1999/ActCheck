                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to convert the city road of F. Dulalia St., Lingunan in the
City of Valenzuela, Metro Manila into a national road.

      At present, this roadway serves as a vital thoroughfare, accommodating
heavy daily traffic from both local residents and travelers from neighboring
municipalities. Given its strategic importance, it is imperative to ensure that the
road is maintained to the highest standards for the safety, convenience, and
mobility of the commuting public.

       Placing F. Dulalia St., Lingunan under the jurisdiction of the national
government will allow for better allocation of resources and more efficient
maintenance, as well as ensure its integration into the broader national
infrastructure development plan. This conversion will not only enhance road
quality and traffic management but will also contribute significantly to the
long-term effort of alleviating the traffic congestion that continues to burden
Metro Manila.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                             AN ACT
    PROVIDING FOR THE CONVERSION OF F. DULALIA ST., LINGUNAN, IN
    THE CITY OF VALENZUELA, METRO MANILA, INTO A NATIONAL
    ROAD, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
          Congress assembled:


1             SECTION 1. The F. Dulalia St., Lingunan, in the City of Valenzuela, Metro
2      Manila, is hereby converted into a National Road.

3             SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
4      Department’s program the improvement, repair, and maintenance of said road, the
5      funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
6             SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
7      at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
