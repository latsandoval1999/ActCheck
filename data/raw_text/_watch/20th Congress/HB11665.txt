                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Santa Maria–San Jose Road, located in the
Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Bulacan, into a National Road under the
jurisdiction and maintenance of the Department of Public Works and Highways
(DPWH).

Spanning approximately 8.96 kilometers, the Santa Maria–San Jose Road serves
as a vital arterial route connecting key barangays within Santa Maria and
providing strategic linkages to neighboring municipalities in Bulacan. It functions
as a heavily traversed corridor for the transport of agricultural produce,
commercial goods, industrial materials, and daily commuters traveling toward
regional economic hubs and major expressways.

Despite its critical economic and social function, the continuous maintenance of
this 8.96-kilometer stretch pose a significant financial burden on the local
government unit (LGU) of Santa Maria. Local budget limitations restrict the
municipal government's capacity to execute essential structural reinforcements,
drainage system improvements, and routine maintenance necessary to support
growing traffic volumes and severe weather conditions.

Converting the Santa Maria–San Jose Road into a national road will transfer its
supervision, maintenance, and rehabilitation to the DPWH. This will ensure
regular funding from the national budget, raise safety and engineering standards,
reduce traffic congestion, and unlock further economic potential for Santa Maria
and the Province of Bulacan as a whole.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT CONVERTING THE SANTA MARIA–SAN JOSE ROAD IN THE
    MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A
       NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The Santa Maria–San Jose Road, an 8.96-kilometer local road
traversing the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Bulacan, is hereby
converted into a National Road under the jurisdiction and supervision of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department’s program the improvement, repair, maintenance, and rehabilitation
of the Santa Maria–San Jose Road, the funding of which shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

SEC. 3. The DPWH shall issue the necessary rules, regulations, and directives to
implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved
