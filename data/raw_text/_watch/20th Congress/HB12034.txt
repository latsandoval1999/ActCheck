                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to accelerate the implementation and completion of
government infrastructure projects through round-the-clock operations. It
establishes a minimum three (3)-shift work schedule for covered projects and
provides a framework for efficient use of manpower, equipment, and other project
resources.

        Government infrastructure projects directly affect mobility, connectivity, and
access to public services, making its timely implementation an important
consideration in the use of public funds. In the 2026 national budget, the
Department of Budget and Management has emphasized the need to expedite
infrastructure and keep projects on schedule in support of the allocated One trillion
Five hundred fifty six billion pesos (Php 1.556 Trillion) to the Build Better More
Infrastructure Program1. A commitment of this scale highlights the urgency of
efficient execution so vital infrastructure projects reach the public without delay.

       The bill addresses the need for a more continuous work schedule through
continuous three (3)-shift operations for covered projects. It also requires
appropriate worker protections, community and environmental safeguards, and the
inclusion of related costs in approved project budgets.

        Faster infrastructure delivery benefits communities that rely on public
facilities for daily activities and essential services. Continuous operations shorten
the time construction sites occupy public spaces, ease traffic disruptions, and restore
full access sooner—turning public investment into completed projects for the people
they serve.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
STREAMLINING      THE   IMPLEMENTATION OF   GOVERNMENT
INFRASTRUCTURE      PROJECTS   THROUGH  ROUND-THE-CLOCK
OPERATIONS TO ACCELERATE PROJECT COMPLETION AND IMPROVE
PUBLIC SERVICE DELIVERY

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Accelerated
Infrastructure Development Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
accelerate the implementation and completion of government infrastructure
projects, particularly roads, bridges, and other essential public infrastructure,
through the efficient utilization of available manpower, equipment, and resources,
while protecting the safety, health, and welfare of workers and affected
communities.

       SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all government infrastructure
projects funded by the national government, local government units (LGUs), and
government-owned or controlled corporations (GOCCs), including projects
involving the construction, rehabilitation, repair, improvement, and maintenance of
roads, bridges, buildings, and other public infrastructure.

       SEC. 4. Round-the-Clock Operations. – Covered government infrastructure
projects shall be implemented on a round-the-clock basis through a minimum of
three (3) shifts per day, subject to the nature, scope, location, technical requirements,
and actual conditions of the project.

       The implementing agency shall, in coordination with the contractor, as
applicable, establish an appropriate shift schedule to maximize the utilization of
manpower, equipment, and other project resources.

       Where operational, technical, environmental, safety, or other legitimate
conditions prevent the implementation of three (3) shifts per day, the implementing
agency may authorize an alternative work schedule, subject to the submission of a
written justification and compliance with the implementing rules and regulations of
this Act.

        SEC. 5. Workforce, Health and Safety. – The implementing agency and the
contractor, as applicable, shall provide workers engaged in round-the-clock
operations with appropriate working conditions, rest periods, compensation,
protective equipment, lighting, sanitation, emergency response measures, and other
facilities necessary for their safety, health, and welfare, in accordance with existing
labor and occupational safety and health laws, rules, and regulations.

      The implementing agency shall coordinate with the Department of Labor and
Employment (DOLE) and other concerned agencies, as necessary, to address
workforce and occupational safety and health requirements arising from
round-the-clock operations.

       SEC. 6. Community and Environmental Safeguards. – Round-the-clock
operations shall be implemented in accordance with applicable environmental laws,
permits, standards, and requirements, and with due regard to the safety and
convenience of affected communities. The implementing agency and contractor, as
applicable, shall adopt reasonable measures to minimize excessive noise, traffic
disruption, obstruction, dust, lighting impacts, and other adverse effects associated
with nighttime construction activities.

      SEC. 7. Project Scheduling and Monitoring. – The implementing agency shall
incorporate the round-the-clock work schedule into the approved project
implementation plan and shall regularly monitor project progress against the
approved schedule. Any deviation from the required three (3)-shift schedule shall be
documented and supported by the appropriate justification in accordance with the
implementing rules and regulations.

       SEC. 8. Budgetary Requirements. – The funding requirements necessary for the
implementation of round-the-clock operations, including additional labor costs,
overtime pay, safety measures, lighting, equipment operation, security, and other
related expenses, shall be included in the approved project cost, subject to existing
budgeting, procurement, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

        SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Public Works and Highways, Department
of the Interior and Local Government, DOLE, Department of Environment and
Natural Resources, Department of Budget and Management, Government
Procurement Policy Board, and other concerned agencies shall promulgate the rules
and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

       The implementing rules and regulations shall prescribe the criteria for
projects subject to round-the-clock operations, allowable exceptions, minimum

       SEC. 10. Compliance with Existing Laws. – Nothing in this Act shall be
construed as exempting any government infrastructure project, implementing
agency, contractor, or worker from compliance with existing laws, rules, regulations,
permits, technical standards, labor standards, occupational safety and health
requirements, environmental safeguards, procurement laws, and other applicable
requirements.

       SEC. 11. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force
and effect.

       SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
