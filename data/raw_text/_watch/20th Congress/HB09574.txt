CODE," AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The registration of motor vehicles serves as a fundamental mechanism through
which the State regulates the operation of vehicles on public roads, promotes road
safety, ensures accountability among vehicle owners, and facilitates the collection of
lawful government revenues. However, the current system requiring frequent
registration renewals imposes recurring financial and administrative burdens upon
motorists and contributes to congestion in Land Transportation Office (LTO) offices
nationwide.

       With the continuous advancement of digital governance, automated monitoring
systems, centralized databases, and electronic payment platforms, there is an
opportunity to streamline vehicle registration procedures without compromising public
safety and regulatory oversight. Many vehicle owners spend considerable time and
resources complying with annual registration requirements despite maintaining valid
insurance coverage, roadworthy vehicles, and updated ownership records.

       This measure seeks to extend the validity of motor vehicle registration to five
(5) years while preserving the government's authority to conduct periodic inspections,
enforce roadworthiness standards, verify insurance compliance, monitor
environmental requirements, and ensure the accuracy of ownership records. The bill
likewise establishes a modernized registration management framework that utilizes
digital technologies to improve efficiency, reduce administrative costs, curb
opportunities for corruption, and enhance public service delivery.

      By reducing unnecessary regulatory burdens while strengthening compliance
mechanisms, this measure promotes a more efficient, citizen-centered, and
technologically advanced transportation system that benefits both motorists and the
government.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 4          SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Five-Year Motor
 5   Vehicle Registration Act of 2026."
 6
 7           SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the
 8   State to promote efficiency, accessibility, and convenience in the delivery of
 9   government services by reducing unnecessary administrative and financial burdens
10   imposed upon motor vehicle owners. Towards this end, the State shall ensure that all
11   motor vehicles operating on public roads remain roadworthy, environmentally
12   compliant, and properly insured, while harnessing modern information and
13   communications technology to improve the administration of transportation services.
14   The State shall likewise strengthen transparency and accountability in the registration
15   and monitoring of motor vehicles and foster public trust through the provision of
16   efficient, responsive, and citizen-centered regulatory processes.
17
18         SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:
19               (a) Motor Vehicle refers to any vehicle propelled by any power other
20         than muscular power using public highways, as defined under Republic Act No.
21         4136.

 2               (b) Registered Owner refers to the person, natural or juridical, whose
 3          name appears in the Certificate of Registration.
 4
 5                 (c) Roadworthiness Inspection refers to an examination conducted
 6          to determine whether a vehicle complies with safety standards prescribed by
 7          law and regulation.
 8
 9                (d) Digital Registration Certificate refers to an electronically
10          generated registration record issued by the LTO.
11
12                 (e) Periodic Compliance Verification refers to the process of
13          validating insurance coverage, emission compliance, ownership information,
14          and roadworthiness requirements during the five-year registration period.

16          SECTION 4. Five-Year Registration Period. - All privately owned motor
17   vehicles shall be issued a Certificate of Registration valid for five (5) years from the
18   date of issuance or renewal, subject to compliance with the provisions of this Act and
19   the rules and regulations promulgated hereunder.

32          LTO.

 4         SECTION 7. Initial Registration of Brand-New Vehicles. - Brand-new
 5   motor vehicles shall automatically be entitled to five (5) years of registration validity
 6   upon initial registration with the LTO.
 7
 8         Manufacturers, distributors, and dealerships shall facilitate the registration
 9   process in accordance with LTO regulations.

11          SECTION 8. Mandatory Insurance Coverage. - Every registered motor
12   vehicle shall maintain valid Compulsory Third-Party Liability (CTPL) insurance
13   throughout the five-year registration period.
14          The lapse or cancellation of insurance coverage shall constitute grounds for
15   administrative sanctions under this Act.
16
17          SECTION 9. Periodic Roadworthiness Inspection. - Notwithstanding the
18   five-year registration validity, vehicle owners shall undergo periodic roadworthiness
19   inspections at intervals prescribed by the LTO.
20
21          Inspection results shall be electronically transmitted to the LTO database.
22
23          SECTION 10. Emission Compliance. - Motor vehicles shall comply with all
24   emission standards established under existing environmental laws.
25          The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), in coordination
26   with the LTO, shall establish procedures for integrating emission compliance records
27   into the national motor vehicle registration database.
28
29          SECTION 11. Updating of Ownership Records. - Any transfer of
30   ownership shall be reported to the LTO within thirty (30) days from the date of
31   transfer.
32
33         Failure to update ownership information shall subject the parties concerned to
34   administrative penalties.
