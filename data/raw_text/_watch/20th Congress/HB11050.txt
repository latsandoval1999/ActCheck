                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The 1987 Constitution provides that the State shall give priority to
education and science and technology to foster patriotism and nationalism,
accelerate social progress, and promote total human liberation and
development.1 The Constitution also states that science and technology are
essential for national development and progress.2 Furthermore, the Constitution
also provides that the State shall establish and maintain a system of scholarship
grants, student loan programs, subsidies, and other incentives which shall be
available to deserving students in both public and private schools, especially to
the underprivileged.3

       The country is visited by an average of twenty (20) typhoons annually.
Typhoons naturally have destructive capabilities as a result of the strong winds
they generate, the intense rainfall they bring, and the major floodings that they
can produce. The effects range from structural damages both to man-made and
natural structures in the surroundings as well as economic impacts on human
life and livelihood.

        Especially now, as the globe grapples with the undeniable effects of climate
change, the poly-crisis it brings, and its awe-inspiring negative impact on ways-
of-life, livelihoods, safety, and societal peace overall, the country must increase
its capacity to mitigate such negative effects by training professionals in the
meteorological sciences.

     In order to mitigate the consequences brought by typhoons, it is important
that government will continue to strengthen its weather forecasting and

      In light of the foregoing, this bill proposes establishing a National
Meteorology Scholarship Program to encourage the youth to pursue and gain
expertise in the field of Meteorology. These deserving students who will benefit
from the proposed scholarship program will be great assets to the country in the
government's efforts in providing information to the public and organizations
during typhoons, protecting life and property during weather disturbances,
reducing weather-related economic losses, and promoting national safety and
prosperity.

      Based on the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL METEOROLOGY SCHOLARSHIP
               PROGRAM AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National
Meteorology Scholarship Act of 2026”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to utilize scientific and technical knowledge and information as an effective
instrument to ensure national safety, security, and development through various
applications of meteorology and its allied sciences. It is also hereby declared a
policy of the State to maintain a competent and capable human resource tasked
to provide atmospheric, geophysical, astronomical, and other weather and
climate-related services and information to the public.

      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following
terms shall mean:

      (a) Meteorology – the branch of science that studies the atmosphere and
          its phenomena, including the weather and climate.

      SECTION 4. National Meteorology Scholarship Program. – A National
Meteorology Scholarship Program is hereby established to provide educational
assistance to qualified Filipino citizens and to promote the study and encourage
the pursuit of careers in Meteorology and its allied sciences.

      SECTION 5. Coverage. – The National Meteorology Scholarship Program
established under this Act shall be made available to deserving Filipino students
who want to pursue a degree in Meteorology in state universities and colleges
(SUC) or private higher education institutions (PHEIs) in the country. The
Program does not negate, but rather, can run concurrently with other such
scholarship programs.

      SECTION 6. Qualifications. – An applicant to the National Meteorology
Scholarship Program must possess the following minimum requirements:

     (a) Must be a Filipino citizen residing in the Philippines;
     (b) Must have passed the entrance examinations and other related
         requirements for admission of the university where the student intends
         to attend.

       SECTION 7. Scholarship Benefits. – The scholarship program shall cover
the following:

     (a) Free tuition and other school fees;
     (b) Allowance for prescribed books, supplies, and equipment;
     (c) Monthly basic and meal allowance which shall not be less than twenty
         thousand pesos (Php 20,000.00) per month during enrolled semesters,
         subject to periodic review of the DOST;
     (d) Clothing or uniform allowance;
     (e) Allowance for dormitory or boarding house accommodation, if
         necessary;
     (f) Transportation allowance;
     (g) Training fees, if necessary;
     (h) Professional licensure examination fees, if applicable in the future;
     (i) Other education-related expenses.

      SECTION 8. Conditions for the Grant of Scholarship and Return Service
Obligation. – Deserving students accepted to the National Meteorology
Scholarship Program shall be subject to the following conditions:

     (a) Must sign an agreement stating the terms and conditions of the
         scholarship;
     (b) Must carry the full load of subjects prescribed per semester by the SUC
         or PHEI, and shall, under no circumstance, drop a course that will
         result in underloading;
     (c) Must finish the entire program in the prescribed time frame in the SUC
         or PHEI where the scholar is enrolled, subject to the retention policies
         of the SUC or PHEI;
     (d) Upon graduation, the scholar must render service in the country for a
         period equivalent to the number of years the scholar enjoyed the
         benefits of the scholarship. The scholar shall work in any of the
         following, in decreasing priority:
             a. Philippine Atmospheric Geophysical and Astronomical Services
                Administration (PAGASA);
             b. Any office or project under the DOST;
             c. The Philippine Space Agency (PhilSA);
             d. In the absence of the former, upon certification of the Secretary
                of Science and Technology and/or the head of the Human
                Resources Management Office of DOST that no vacant positions
                are available within six (6) months from the graduation of the
                scholar, in any office or project under any branch, agency or
                instrumentality of the government or in the private sector in the
                Philippines;
      (e) The scholar may be allowed, for valid reasons preventing the
          enrollment, to defer availing of the scholarship and file a leave of
          absence for a period not exceeding one (1) school year, subject to the
          guidelines and policies of the concerned SUC and PHEI;
      (f) Must take the professional licensure examination, if becomes
          applicable in the future, within a maximum period of 1 year after
          graduation;

      SECTION 9. Disqualifications. – The scholarship shall be terminated in
case of the following circumstances:
      (a) If the scholar accepts another scholarship from other government or
          private agencies or entities while enjoying the benefits under this Act;
      (b) If the scholar fails in forty percent (40%) of the subjects or fails to
          meet the academic requirements of the SUCs or PHEIs or to complete
          the course within the prescribed period without valid cause as may be
          determined by the SUC or PHEI, or the DOST such as due to absence
          without notice, reasons of willful neglect or other causes within the
          control of the scholar;
      (c) If the scholar commits behavioral misconduct in a manner that would
          bring significant damage to the SUC or PHEI, government institution
          concern, persons, and the community.

       SECTION 10. Post Scholarship Incentives. – In order to encourage
graduates of the scholarship program under this Act to serve in fulfillment of
their service obligation and entice them to continue to stay and serve in the
country even beyond their service obligation, they shall be entitled to the
following incentives:

      (a) Priority for job placement in the Government or in the private sector in
          positions appropriate to their area of education or training;
      (b) Grants-in-aid and access to government research facilities in the
          conduct of research and development projects and other services and
          technology activities;
      (c) Access to credit with liberal terms from government banks and financial
          institutions;
      (d) Payment of hardship and/or hazard allowance to those who are
          engaged in research and other meteorology-related activities that pose
          danger and difficulties to their well-being; and
      (e) Travel and accident insurance coverage.

      SECTION 11. Implementing Body. – The National Meteorology Scholarship
Program shall be directly implemented by the DOST through the Science
Education Institute.

      SECTION 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of
Science and Technology in coordination with relevant government agencies shall
promulgate the implementing rules and regulations of this Act within sixty (60)
days from its effectivity.

      SECTION 13. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act, or part
hereof, is held invalid or unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or provision
not otherwise affected shall remain valid and subsisting.

      SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. – All provisions of laws, presidential
decrees, letters of instruction, and other presidential issuances that are
incompatible or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed
amended or repealed.

      SECTION 15. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
