                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Public plumbing provides a clean and safe water supply while ensuring proper
wastewater management and drainage systems that protect public health and safety.
Our systems of plumbing are concealed beneath the concrete slabs of contemporary
buildings and are essential to maintaining modern society's sanitation and safety. This
makes plumbing design and installation important and must be carried out by
individuals with sufficient technical and academic training in compliance with widely
recognized engineering standards and procedures.

        Unfortunately, the law regulating the profession has remained the same despite
the major developments in plumbing systems, particularly in the materials used and
technologies implemented. Despite the enactment of Republic Act No. 1378, otherwise
known as the Plumbing Law in 1955, the legal framework governing the practice of
master plumbing has remained unchanged for the past 7 decades. One clear proof of
this obsolescence is that the existing law still considers plumbing a "trade" despite the
current era of high-rise buildings and rapid urbanization. If remained out-of-date, this
will result in an unconscious threat to public health, proper hygiene, and the overall
quality of life in many communities.

      To address these challenges, this measure seeks to modernize the regulation
of the plumbing profession by updating the legal framework to reflect current
engineering practices, technological advancements, and internationally recognized
standards. It strengthens professional qualifications, promotes continuing
competence, and recognizes the increasingly specialized and engineering-oriented
nature of plumbing practice.

      Through these reforms, the bill aims to ensure that the profession remains
capable of meeting the country's growing infrastructure needs while upholding the
highest standards of public health, safety, and environmental sustainability.

      In light of the foregoing, support for this bill, which is a counterpart measure
of Senate Bill 494, is earnestly sought.

 3         SECTION. 1. Title. This Act shall be known as the "Philippine Plumbing
 4   Engineering Act".

 6          SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. The State hereby recognizes the importance
 7   of plumbing engineering in maintaining public health, sanitation, and safety. Towards
 8   this end, and in recognition of the need to train and develop competent and globally-
 9   competitive professionals, the State shall implement regulatory measures, programs
10   and activities which shall promote the sustained development of a reservoir of
11   plumbing professionals whose competence has been determined by honest and
12   credible board examinations, maintain internationally-recognized standards of
13   professional service and practice, and foster professional growth and career
14   advancement in the field of plumbing engineering.
15
16          SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. For the purposes of this Act, the following terms
17   are hereby defined as follows:

18         (a) Master Plumber refers to a person who holding a valid certificate of
19         registration or professional license and professional identification card as
20         master plumber;

21         (b) Practice of Plumbing Engineering refers to the professional and organized
22         act of applying scientific principle and technique for the design, estimate,
23         installation and operation of plumbing systems involving hot and cold-water
24         supply installation to fixtures, sanitary drainage, vent system, storm drainage,
25         gas piping, swimming pools, water feature, fire protection system, irrigation

                                              1
 1          system, water refilling stations, private water system, private sewage disposal
 2          system within or adjacent to any buildings and other structures. It shall also
 3          include, but not be limited to, the following services and activities:

 4                 (1) Consultation, design and preparation of plans, specifications and
 5                 estimates; and erection, installation and supervision of plumbing works
 6                 including the inspection and acceptance of materials used therein;

 7                 (2) Extension, alteration and modifications of all piping for fixtures,
 8                 equipment, appliance, and appurtenance in connection with any of the
 9                 above scope;

10                 (3) Engagement in the transfer of the knowledge and technology of
11                 plumbing engineering in any institution of learning; and

12                 (4) Appointment in any regular, job order, or contract-of-service position
13                 in any government agency or instrumentality that requires professional
14                 knowledge in plumbing engineering:

15          Provided, That the enumeration of services and activities in this subsection shall
16   not be construed to exclude any other work requiring plumbing knowledge and
17   application; and

18          (c) Plumbing Engineer refers to a person holding a valid certificate of
19          registration or professional license and professional identification card issued
20          by the Board of Plumbing Engineering and the Professional Regulation
21          Commission and who is legally qualified to practice plumbing engineering in the
22          Philippines pursuant to this Act.

 4          SEC. 16. Rating in the Board Examination. To pass the board examination
 5   for Plumbing Engineers, a candidate must obtain a general weighted average of
 6   seventy percent (70%) with no grade lower than fifty percent (50%) in any given
 7   subject.

 9          SEC. 17. Report of Ratings. The Board shall submit to the Commission the
10   rating obtained by each candidate within twenty (20) calendar days after the

13   the later's given address using the self-addressed mailing envelope submitted during
14   the examination.

16           SEC. 18. Re-examination. An applicant who fails to pass the examination for
17   the third time shall be allowed to take another examination only after the lapse of one
18   year.

20          SEC. 19. Oath. All successful candidates in the examinations and all those
21   who have qualified for registration without an examination as provided under Section
22   32(c) of this Act shall be required to take an oath of profession before the Board or
23   any person authorized by the Commission to administer oaths, prior to entering into
24   the practice of the Plumbing Engineering profession.

26           SEC. 20. Certificate of Registration and Professional License. A

14                                       ARTICLE IV
15                           PRACTICE OF PLUMBING ENGINEERING

16           SEC. 24. Practice of Plumbing Engineering. Except as may be provided in
17   this Act, no person shall use the title "Registered Plumbing Engineer", or display or
18   use any title, sign card, advertisement, device or implement to indicate such person
19   practices or offers to practice plumbing engineering, unless the person shall have
20   received from the Board a certificate of registration and issued a professional
21   identification card by the PRC and the Board: Provided, That Section 3 (b) of this Act
22   shall not be construed to exclude other services and activities that may require
23   plumbing engineering knowledge and application.
24
25         The practice of Plumbing Engineering is a professional service, admission to
26   which shall be determined upon the basis of an individual's personal qualifications, for
27   which the Plumbing Engineer is responsible for the correctness of the plumbing work.
28
29          No firm, partnership, corporation or association may be licensed and registered
30   the practice of plumbing engineering: Provided, however. That duly licensed and
31   registered plumbing engineers may, among themselves or with a person or persons
32   properly registered and licensed as Plumbing Engineer, form partnerships, firms and
33   associations and use the title "Plumbing Engineers" or "Engineers": Provided, That no
34   person shall be made partner, owner, or member of such firm, partnership or
35   association unless he or she is a duly registered and licensed Plumbing Engineer.
36
37          SEC. 25. Roster of Plumbing Engineers. The Commission shall maintain a
38   roster of duly licensed and registered plumbing engineers, stating their names,
39   registration numbers, and places of business or employment. The Commission shall
40   regularly update such roster and make the same available to the public.
41
42          SEC. 26. Foreign Reciprocity. No foreign plumbing engineer shall be
43   authorized to take the board examination, given a certificate of registration, issued a
44   temporary license to practice plumbing engineering, engage in covered activities and
45   services, or be entitled to any of the rights and privileges under this act of unless the
46   country of which he is a subject or citizen specifically permits Filipino Plumbing

                                                9
 1   Engineers to practice within its territorial limits on the same basis as the subjects or
 2   citizens or such foreign state or country.

 5   engineer shall be required to indicate the certificate of registration, professional
 6   identification card number, date of issuance and the duration of validity, including the

 8   issues in connection with the practice of the plumbing engineering.

17
18          SEC. 29. Grounds for Suspension and Revocation of License,

21   or to cancel a temporary / special permit for any cause specified in the proceeding
22   section, including but not limited to:

23          (a) The use or perpetration of any fraud deceit in obtaining a certificate of
24          registration;

25          (b) For incompetence, negligence, or for abatement of the illegal practice of

36          SEC. 30. Prohibited Acts. The following acts shall be prohibited:
37          (a) No person shall perform any of the following acts:

38                 (1) Submit fraudulent evidence of any kind to the Board or to any
39                 member thereof to obtain a certificate of registration of professional
40                 license;

41                 (2) Use the title of "Registered Plumbing Engineer", or make use of
42                 any means to display, advertise or otherwise convey the impression


                                                10
 1                 that such person is a plumbing engineer without holding a valid
 2                 certificate or license to practice plumbing engineering;

 3                 (3) Practice or offer to practice plumbing engineering in the Philippines
 4                 without a valid certificate of registration and professional license, or
 5                 temporary/special permit from the Board of Plumbing Engineering;

 6                 (4) Stamp or seal any document with the seal of a plumbing engineer
 7                 after his or her professional license has expired or lost its validity, unless
 8                 he has been reinstated at the practice and/or unless his license has been
 9                 renewed;

10                 (5) Sign his or her name, affix his or her seal or use any other method
11                 of signature on pians, specifications or other documents made by or
12                 under supervision of another plumbing engineer, unless the same is
13                 made in such manner as to clearly indicate the part of such work that
14                 the plumbing engineer concerned has actually performed; or

15                 (6) Sign for any branch of work or any function of Plumbing Engineering
16                 practice that he or she has not actually performed, unless he or she is
17                 the Plumbing Engineer in-charge for all plans, specifications and other
18                 documents issued under his seal or authorized signature.

19          (b) No officer or employee of any government agency or instrumentality,
20          including government-owned or -controlled corporations, state universities and
21          colleges, and local government units, shall perform the following acts:

22                 (1) Accept or endorse any plumbing proposal, design, specifications,
23                 working drawing, program, or report which have not been prepared and
24                 submitted in full accord with the provisions of this Act; or

25                 (2) Approve or cause any payment for any work, the plans and
26                 documents of which have not been prepared, signed and sealed by a
27                 duly licensed Plumbing Engineer with the seal and registration number
28                 affixed thereto.

29          SEC. 31. Penalty. In addition to the administrative sanctions that may be
30   imposed under this Act, any person or firm, partnership or association violating any
31   provisions of this Act, and its rules and regulations shall, upon conviction, be penalized
32   by a fine of not less than One Hundred Thousand Pesos (Php 100,000.00) nor more
33   than Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (Php 500,000.00), or imprisonment of not less
34   than six (6) months, nor more than three (3) years, or both, at the discretion of the
35   Court.

33           SEC. 34. Funding. The amount necessary for the immediate implementation
