                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) make invaluable contributions to the
Philippine economy through their hard work and sacrifice abroad. In recognition
of their vital role in nation-building, it is imperative that the government ensure
accessible, affordable, and quality healthcare services for OFWs and their
families.

      While the establishment of the OFW Hospital in San Fernando, Pampanga
marked a significant step in addressing the healthcare needs of migrant workers,
there remains a need to expand access to specialized healthcare services in other
parts of the country and to institutionalize financial protection for OFWs seeking
medical treatment.

      This measure strengthens the OFW Hospital, authorizes the establishment
or designation of Regional OFW Hospitals to bring healthcare services closer to
OFWs and their dependents, and institutionalizes a zero-billing policy to ensure
that qualified OFWs and their dependents receive medically necessary treatment
without financial burden, subject to existing laws and available government
assistance programs.

      By enhancing healthcare access and support mechanisms for OFWs and
their families, this bill affirms the State’s commitment to safeguarding the
welfare of the nation’s modern-day heroes.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                             “AN ACT
        ENHANCING THE MEDICAL ACCESS OF OVERSEAS FILIPINO
        WORKERS (OFWs) AND THEIR FAMILIES BY STRENGTHENING
        THE  OFW   HOSPITAL    AND   APPROPRIATING   FUNDS
        THEREFOR’”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


        SECTION 1. Short Title- This act shall be known as the “OFW Hospital
Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy- It is the policy of the State to adopt
an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development which shall
endeavor to make the essential goods, health, and other social services available
to all the people and affordable cost. To this end, the State shall protect the
interest and promote the well-being of Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) through
the provision of social welfare services in recognition of their importance and
contribution to economic development and nation-building.

        SECTION 3. Definition of Terms- As used in this Act:

          a) Dependents to refers of the following:

          1) The legal spouse entitled by law to receive support from the OFW;
          2) The legitimate, legitimated, legally adopted, or illegitimate child who
             is unmarried, not gainfully employed, and has not reached twenty-
             one (21) years of age, or if over twenty-one (21) years of age, is
             congenitally incapacitated or while still a minor has been
             permanently incapacitated and incapable of self-support, physically
             or mentally; or
          3) The parents who receive regular support from the OFW; and
         b) Overseas Filipino Worker (OFW) or Migrant Worker refers to a
            Filipino who has been engaged, is engaged, or is to be engaged in a
            remunerated activity in a country of which she said person is not an
            immigrant citizen, or a permanent resident of, or is not awaiting
            naturalization, recognition, or admission, whether land-based or
            sea-based regardless of status; excluding a Filipino engaged under
            a government-recognized exchange visitor program for cultural and
            educational purposes. For purposes of this Act, a person engaged in
            overseas employment but has yet to leave the Philippines, regardless
            of status, and includes “Overseas Contract Workers.”

       SECTION 4. OFW Hospital and Diagnostic Center- The “Overseas
Filipino Worker Hospital and Diagnostic Center” (OFW Hospital) established in
the City of San Fernando, province of Pampanga shall primarily cater to OFWs
and their dependents. It shall be a government a government instrumentality
with corporate powers, managed and supervised by the Board of Trustees
chaired by the Secretary of the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW), being the
primary national agency with the mandate of ensuring the welfare of the OFWs
and their dependents. Provided, that nothing herein shall prevent the OFW
Hospital from accommodating patients other than OFWs or their qualified
dependents.

       SECTION 4-A. Regional OFW Hospitals. - To expand access to
specialized healthcare services for Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and their
qualified dependents, the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW), in
coordination with the Department of Health (DOH), may establish Regional OFW
Hospitals or designate existing government hospitals, medical centers, and
specialty hospitals as Regional OFW Hospitals in areas with a significant
concentration of OFWs and their families, subject to the availability of funds and
compliance with existing laws, rules, and regulations.

      The Regional OFW Hospitals shall provide services consistent with the
purposes and objectives of this Act and shall coordinate with the OFW Hospital
in the City of San Fernando, Pampanga to ensure an integrated referral and
healthcare delivery system for OFWs and their qualified dependents.

      SECTION 5. Purposes and Objectives – The OFW Hospital shall:

      a.) Establish, operate and maintain medical facilities, including a medical
          and diagnostic center to provide comprehensive and people center
          health care services to OFWs and their qualified dependents and the
          general public.

      b.) Serve as a national referral facility for repatriated OFWs needing
          medical assistance and support while ensuring its participation in the
          provincial and inter-regional healthcare provider networks to allow for
        a seamless and coordinated referral system for the medical repatriation
        program in accordance with Republic Act No. 11223 or the “Universal
        Health Care Act.”

     c.) Promote, encourage, engage in, and publish scientific research on the
         prevention, care, and treatment of occupational diseases to OFWs,
         including the underwriting of research on biological, demographic,
         social, economic, eugenics, physiological aspects of occupational
         disorders of OFWs, and conduct relevant congresses, conventions,
         seminars and conferences.

     d.) Develop training programs for medical and allied medical professionals
         while shall focus on strengthening occupational and migrant health
         services in the country and the creation of specialized services.

     e.) Provide telehealth services to migrant workers and their dependents for
         follow up consultations, and to assist in the medical assessment and
         management of distresses OFWs while awaiting repatriation, which
         may be extended at the Philippine foreign posts or migrant workers
         offices:

     f.) Conduct pre-employment medical examinations of OFW applicants,
         subject to pertinent rules and regulations;

     g.) Strengthen health surveillance through the provision of a post-
         employment or post-arrival medical examination for OFWs, in
         consonance with Republic Act No. 8042 or the “Migrant Workers and
         Overseas Filipinos Act of 1995” as amended, and the Philippine Migrant
         Health Program of the Department of Health (DOH);

     h.) Raise public awareness and conduct a massive information drive on the
         prevention, treatment, and control of diseases common to OFWs,
         occupational health and safety, and physical fitness; and

     i.) Assist and support universities, research institutions, and other
         hospitals or medical facilities in their studies on health-related
         concerns of OFWs.

      SECTION 6. Powers of the OFW Hospital. –The OFW Hospital shall have
the powers to:

     a.) Acquire and hold, in any manner, property of whatever nature or
         description, and to dispose of such property under any mode of
         encumbrance or conveyance;

     b.) Contract and be contracted with;
     c.) Levy, assess and collect such fees, charges, and assessments as may
         be necessary or proper to support, finance, and maintain its operation;

     d.) Mortgage, lease, sell, transfer, convey, or otherwise dispose of its
         properties;

     e.) Invest in, and purchase or otherwise acquire, own, hold, use, lease
         grant, mortgage, sell, transfer or otherwise deal with, real and/or
         personal properties of every kind and nature, including shares of stock,
         bonds, notes and other evidence of indebtedness or obligations of other
         corporations whether government or private;

     f.) Solicit and receive donations, endowments, and funds in the forms of
         contributions, whether in cash or in kind, from both the public and
         private sectors subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing
         rules and regulations;

     g.) Open accounts in banks and other financial institutions, and to
         disburse funds or invest the same as the DMW Secretary may direct to
         accomplish or advance the purposes or interest of the hospital;

     h.) Invite foreign specialists and experts in the various medical fields not
         available in the country to train the personnel, trainees, and residents;

     i.) Send the personnel to research institutes, medical institutes, or
         universities for advanced training or observation and to attend
         international or regional conventions, conferences, congresses, and
         seminars as the Medical Center Chief may deem necessary to
         accomplish the purposes and objectives of OFW Hospital, subject to the
         approval of the DMW Secretary; and

     j.) Exercise such other powers and functions as may be necessary or
         incidental for the accomplishment of the purposes and objectives of the
         OFW Hospital.

      SECTION 7. Creation of the OFW Hospital Board of Trustees. – There
is hereby created an OFW Hospital Trust, for the benefit of OFWs, to be
administered by a Board of Trustees, composed of seven (7) members. Three (3)
members of the Board shall constitute a quorum to transact business and any
decision of the three (3) members on any matter shall constitute a valid and
binding act of the OFW Hospital.

     The Composition of the OFW Hospital Board shall be as follows;

     a.) Chairperson: Secretary, DMW;
     b.) Vice-Chairperson: Secretary, Department of Health (DOH); and
      c.) Members:

      1.) Secretary, Department of Labor and Employment;
      2.) Administrator, Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA);
      3.) Secretary, Department of Social Welfare and Development;
      4.) Land-based OFWs Representative; and
      5.) Sea-based OFWs Representative

       The representatives of land-based and sea-based OFWs shall be appointed
by the President from a list of nominees of their OFW organizations as endorsed
by the Chairperson to the President. The nominees shall at least be forty (40)
years of age, and an OFW with at least five (5) years of work experience abroad
at the time of his or her nomination. The members of the Board may elect an
alternate, whose name shall be submitted to the President for appointment. In
case of vacancy, the bread of agency may nominate ab alternate, subject to
official appointment by the President of the Philippines.

      The following shall be the powers and functions of the Board of Trustees:

      a.) Approve and promulgate policies, guidelines, programs, and plans for
          the sound administration, maintenance, and operations of the OFW
          Hospital for the Implementation of the purposes and objectives of this
          Act;

      b.) Approve the organizational structure and staffing pattern of the OFW
          Hospital in accordance with the revised compensation and position
          classification system subject to the evaluation and approval of the DBM
          and in compliance with civil service and other pertinent laws, rules and
          regulations;

      c.) Appoint, remove, suspend or otherwise discipline the officers of OFW
          Hospital occupying executive and senior management positions subject
          to civil service and other applicable laws, rules and regulations;

      d.) Adopt the annual and supplemental budget of the OFW Hospital; and

      e.) Exercise all the general powers necessary or proper for the attainment
          of the purpose and objectives of this Act.

       SECTION 8. Medical Center Chief. – The Medical Center Chief shall be
appointed by the DMW Secretary. The Medical Center Chief shall have the
following duties and responsibilities:

      a.) Provide overall direction and strategic leadership to the OFW Hospital;
          toward the provision of high-quality health services;
b.) Oversee all managerial and administrative processes and operations of
    all departments of the OFW Hospital;

c.) Supervise the daily activities of functional units;

d.) Ensure high standards in clinical training for medical and allied
    medical personnel;

e.) Execute, on behalf of the OFW Hospital, all contracts and agreements
    which it may enter into and accomplish and deliver any and all
    documents relative to such contracts and agreements, subject to the
    approval of the DMW Secretary;

f.) Represent the OFW Hospital in all dealings with other offices, agencies
    and instrumentalities of the government and all other persons or
    entities, domestic or foreign and government or private;

g.) Submit, for the consideration and approval of the Board, the proposed
    development plans, programs, measures, policies, and guidelines
    necessary for the effective implementation of the purposes and
    objectives of this ACT;

h.) Appoint, promote, transfer, remove, suspend, or otherwise discipline
    the officers and employees of the OFW Hospital, except those appointed
    by the Board;

i.) Exercise such other powers and perform such duties as may be vested
    by the DMW Secretary.

SECTION 9. Duties and Responsibilities of the DMW. – The DMW shall:

a.) Establish a focal office for migration and health in the DMW to ensure
    that the country’s migrant health program is mainstreamed in the
    plans and programs of the OFW Hospital which shall include:

1.) Assistance in identifying, documenting, processing, and approving
    applications for benefits granted to OFWs and their qualified
    dependents;

2.) Establishments of a referral system within the OFW Hospital to ensure
    efficient delivery of medical and health care services to OFWs and their
    qualified dependents;

3.) Strengthening of the existing health benefits and medical assistance
    programs, through OWWA, such as subsidies on hospitalization and
         medical procedures for OFWs and their qualified dependents, subject
         to the availability of funds and resources; and

      4.) Active engagement in the Philippine Migrant Health Network, an inter-
          agency, multi-stakeholder network for migrant health, composed of
          members from other government, non-governmental agencies,
          academe, civil society organizations, private sector, and international
          organizations, to advocate and lobby support for migrant health
          programs.

      b.) Create a consultative committee and other necessary mechanisms for
          the effective participation of land-based and sea-based accredited OFW
          organizations in the formulation of policies for the effective
          implementation of programs and measures to attain the objectives of
          the OFW Hospital; and

      c.) Perform such other functions that are necessary for the attainment of
          the purposes and objectives of this Act.

      SECTION 10. Duties and Responsibilities of the DOH. – The DOH shall:

      a.) Ensure that the necessary permits and licenses to operate the OFW
          Hospital are obtained, consistent with relevant issuances, rules and
          regulations;

      b.) Endorse and encourage medical specialists in other government
          hospitals such as the Philippines Heart Center, National Kidney and
          Transplant Institute, Lung Center of the Philippines, National
          Children’s Hospital, and the Philippine General Hospital, to hold clinics
          in the OFW Hospitals;

      c.) Support the OFW Hospital through the provision of technical
          assistance, extending capital outlay, and monitoring the hospital’s
          compliance with standards to deliver quality healthcare; and

      d.) Perform such other functions that are necessary for the attainment of
          the purposes and objectives of this Act.

      SECTION 11. Support from other Government Agencies. – The OWWA
shall ensure that existing health benefits and medical assistance programs for
the OFWs and their qualified dependents are strengthened: Provided, that
nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish or reduce the existing benefits
of OFWs and their dependents provided under R.A No. 11223

      All other agencies, departments, bureaus, offices, or instrumentalities of
the government, including government-owned or controlled corporations, shall
render assistance to the OFW Hospital for the effective implementation of this
Act.

      SECTION 11-A. Zero-Billing Policy for Qualified OFWs and Their
Dependents. The OFW Hospital and any Regional OFW Hospital established or
designated under this Act shall implement a zero-billing policy for qualified
Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and their dependents, subject to existing laws
and the availability of funds.

      For purposes of this Act, "zero-billing" shall mean that no qualified OFW
or dependent shall be charged or billed for medically necessary services covered
by the hospital, after application of applicable benefits from the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), Overseas Workers Welfare Administration
(OWWA), and other available government medical assistance programs.

      The Department of Migrant Workers (DMW), the Department of Health
(DOH), the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA), PhilHealth, and
other concerned agencies shall coordinate to ensure the effective implementation
and sustainability of the zero-billing policy established under this Act.

       SECTION 12. – Tax Exemption. – All grants, bequests, endowments,
donations, and contributions made to the OFW Hospital to be used actually,
directly, and exclusively by the OFW Hospital shall be exempt from donor’s tax
and the same shall be considered as allowable deduction from the gross income
of the donor, in accordance with the provisions of the National Internal Revenue
Code of 1997, as amended. The importation of equipment, machinery and spare
parts used actually, directly, and exclusively for the operation of the OFW
Hospital and are not available locally shall be exempt from value-added tax and
customs duty.

      SECTION 13. Funding for the OFW Hospital. – The amount necessary
for the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current
year's appropriations of the DMW. Thereafter, such amounts as may be
necessary for the continued operation, upgrading, expansion, and maintenance
of the OFW Hospital, the establishment and operation of Regional OFW
Hospitals, and the implementation of the zero-billing policy for qualified OFWs
and their dependents shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

      SECTION 14. – Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty
(60) days from the approval of this Act, the DMW shall, in consultation with the
DOH, the OWWA, and other stakeholders, promulgate the rules and regulations
to carry out the provisions of this Act.

      SECTION 15. – Separability Clause. – If any of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

      SECTION 16. – Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees,
executive orders, letters of instruction, proclamations, and rules and regulations
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 17. – Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15)
days following its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.

      Approved.
