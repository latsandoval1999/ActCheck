                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE


       This measure seeks to convert and declare seven (7) key road networks within the Fourth
District of the Province of Leyte into national roads. By elevating these routes to national status,
the responsibility for their improvement, repair, and maintenance will be transferred to the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), with funding integrated into the annual
General Appropriations Act.
       The Fourth District of Leyte is one that is characterized by its vast agricultural potential
and burgeoning local commerce. However, the full economic realization of these sectors is often
hindered by the limitations of local infrastructure. The conversion of these specific roads is vital
for the following reasons:
1. Improving Transportation for Agricultural and Commercial Industries
The San Isidro-Guiwan-Central-Hinablayan Road and the Liloan-RM Tan-San Sebastian
Road serve as direct link between agricultural production areas and major trade centers like
Palompon and Ormoc City, hence their proposed conversion.
   •   Reduced Transport Costs: Improved road quality ensures that farmers can transport crops
       and livestock more efficiently, reducing spoilage and logistical expenses.
   •   Market Accessibility: Seamless connection between the Libungao-Matag-ob-Palompon
       Road and the Palompon-Isabel-Merida-Ormoc Road will facilitate a more robust flow of
       goods and services across the district.




                                                 1
2. Boosting Tourism and Social Connectivity
       The Fourth District is home to significant natural landmarks, most notably Lake Danao.
The inclusion of the San Pablo-Lake Danao-Cabintan-Rubas Road and the Lake Danao-Gaas-
Liberty Road is essential for:
   •   Tourism Development: Providing safe and reliable access for tourists traveling from the
       Palo-Carigara-Ormoc Road to the district's highland attractions.
   •   Social Services: Enhancing the mobility of residents in traversing barangays ensures faster
       access to healthcare, education, and government services in urban centers.
3. Strategic Infrastructure Integration
By converting these routes—including the Bantigue-Sumangga-San Pablo Road , the San Juan-
Lao-Naungan Road, and the Curva-Domonar-Liberty Road —we create a cohesive, high-
standard internal network. This integration ensures that no barangay is left isolated and that the
infrastructure can withstand the demands of modern traffic and climate challenges.


       In view of the significant impact this will have on the quality of life and economic resilience
of the people of the Fourth District of Leyte, the early approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
CONVERTING AND DECLARING CERTAIN ROADS NAMED HEREUNDER IN THE
  FOURTH DISTRICT, PROVINCE OF LEYTE INTO NATIONAL ROADS AND
                   PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

     SECTION 1. The following roads in the Fourth District of the Province of Leyte are hereby
converted into national roads:
         a) Bantigue- Sumangga- Patag- Donghol- Biliboy- San Pablo Road, described as the
             road commencing at the Ormoc Baybay- Southern Leyte Boundary Road in
             Barangay Bantigue and traversing Barangays Sumangga, Patag, Donghol, Biliboy
             and San Pablo up to the junction at the Palo-Carigara-Ormoc Road in Ormoc City;
         b) San Isidro- Guiwan 1- Guiwan 2- Central 1- Central 2- Hinablayan Poblacion,
             Pinagdait Poblacion - Pinaghi-usa Poblacion Road, described as the road
             commencing at Libungao- Matag-ob- Palompon Road in Barangay San Isidro and
             traversing Barangays Guiwan 1, Guiwan 2, Central 1, Central 2, Hinablayan
             Poblacion, Pinagdait Poblacion, and Panaghi-usa Poblacion up to the junction at the
             Palompon-Isabel-Merida-Ormoc Road in Palompon, Leyte;
         c) San Juan- Lao- Naungan Road, described as the road commencing at Palompon-
             Isabel- Merida- Ormoc Road in Barangay San Juan and traversing Barangays Lao,

             Naungan, Linao, and Alegria up to the junction at the Ormoc-Baybay-Southern Leyte
             Boundary Road in Ormoc City;
          d) Curva - Domonar- Mabini- Liberty Road, described as the road commencing at
             Palompon- Isabel- Merida- Ormoc Road in Barangay Curva and traversing
             Barangays Domonar, Mabini, and Liberty up to the junction at the Libungao- Matag-
             ob-Palompon Road in Ormoc City and Palompon, Leyte;
          e) San Pablo- Lake Danao- Cabintan- Rubas Road, described as the road commencing
             at Palo-Carigara- Ormoc Road in Barangay San Pablo and traversing Barangays
             Luna, Dolores, Milagro, Lake Danao, Cabintan up to Barangay Rubas, Jaro Road in
             Ormoc City and Jaro, Leyte;
          f) Liloan - RM Tan- Nueva Sociedad- Leondoni- Manlilinao- San Sebastian Road,
             described as the road commencing at Palompon-Isabel-Merida- Ormoc Road in
             Barangay Liloan and traversing Barangays RM Tan, Nueva Sociedad, Leondoni,
             Manlilinao, and San Sebastian up to the junction of Libungao- Matag-ob- Palompon
             Road in Ormoc City and Matag-ob, Leyte; and
          g) Lake Danao- Gaas- Liberty Road, described as the road commencing at Palo-
             Carigara- Ormoc Road, in Barangay San Pablo and traversing Barangays Luna,
             Dolores, Milagro, Lake Danao, Gaas, and Liberty up to the junction of Albuera-
             Burauen Road in Ormoc City.

      SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the department's
program the improvement, maintenance and repair of the said roads, the funding of which shall
be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect sixty (60) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
