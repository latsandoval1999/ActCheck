                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

Reliable internet and telecommunications are vital to daily commerce, education, and
public communication. However, subscribers frequently face prolonged service
interruptions without receiving compensation or bill adjustments. This bill amends
Republic Act No. 7925 to protect consumers by mandating automatic billing adjustments
and refunds for significant network downtime.

Key Provisions include automatic pro-rated refunds, enforcement and penalties, and
revocation and consumer relief.

To ensure fair business practices, hold telecom providers accountable, and safeguard
consumer rights, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7925, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
    “PUBLIC TELECOMMUNICATIONS POLICY ACT OF THE PHILIPPINES”, BY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled.

Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Refund for Internet and
Telecommunications Service Outages and Disruptions Act”.

Section 2. A new subsection under Section 3 of Republic Act No. 7925, otherwise known
as the “Public Telecommunications Policy Act of the Philippines” to read as follows:

   (A) XXX
   (B) XXX
   […]
   (L) INTERNET SERVICE OUTAGE IS THE COMPLETE OR PARTIAL FAILURE OF
   INTERNET SERVICES
   (M) FORTUITOUS EVENT MAY EITHER BE AN “ACT OF GOD,” OR NATURAL
   OCCURENCES SUCH AS FLOODS OR TYPHOONS, STORMS, EARTHQUAKES, OR
   OTHER CATACLYSMIC EVENTS; OR AN “ACT OF MAN”, SUCH AS RIOTS, STRIKES,
   WARS, GOVERNMENTAL PROHIBITIONS, ROBBERY, ETC.

Section 3. A new Section 20-A is hereby added to Republic Act No. 7925, otherwise
known as the "Public Telecommunications Policy Act of the Philippines", to read as
follows:

            "SEC. 20-A. REFUNDS FOR INTERNET AND TELECOMMUNICATIONS
       SERVICE    OUTAGES    AND    INTERRUPTIONS.   –  (A)  PUBLIC
       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ENTITIES, INCLUDING INTERNET SERVICE
                                                                                          Page 2 of 3
       PROVIDERS (ISPS), SHALL, ON A PRO-RATED BASIS, PROVIDE A REFUND
       CREDIT TO A CUSTOMER, OR ADJUST A CUSTOMER'S BILL, WHO HAS
       EXPERIENCED A SERVICE OUTAGE OR DISRUPTION FOR AN AGGREGATE
       PERIOD OF TWENTY-FOUR (24) HOURS OR MORE – WITHIN A MONTH.
            THE ISPS AND PUBLIC TELECOMMUNICATIONS ENTITIES CONCERNED
       SHALL NOT REQUIRE THE CUSTOMER TO TAKE ANY ACTION IN ORDER TO
       RECEIVE A REFUND CREDIT OR BILL ADJUSTMENT UNDER THIS SECTION;
            (B) THE REFUND CREDIT GRANTED UNDER THE CIRCUMSTANCES
       MENTIONED ABOVE SHALL LIKEWISE BE GRANTED TO CUSTOMERS
       SUBSCRIBING TO SERVICE ON A PRE-PAID BASIS
            (C) THE NATIONAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS MAY, MOTU PROPIO OR
       UPON COMPLAINT OF ANY INTERESTED PARTY, IN THE APPROPRIATE
       ADMINISTRATIVE AND/OR QUASI-JUDICIAL PROCESS, IN ACCORDANCE
       WITH DUE PROCESS, SHALL:
                   (1) IMPOSE FINE OF NOT LESS THAN TWO HUNDRED
                   THOUSAND PESOS (PHP200,000.00) BUT NOT MORE THAN
                   TWO MILLION PESOS (PHP2,000,000.00) FOR EACH
                   VIOLATION. PROVIDED, THAT IF THE SERVICE PROVIDER HAS
                   A GROSS ANNUAL INCOME NOT EXCEEDING TEN MILLION
                   PESOS (PHP 10,000,000.00), THE PENALTY SHALL BE
                   EQUIVALENT TO ONE PERCENT (1%) TO TWO PERCENT (2%)
                   OF ITS GROSS ANNUAL INCOME; AND
                   (2) FOR REPEATED VIOLATIONS, REVOKE THE PTES
                   PROVISIONAL AUTHORITY OR CERTIFICATE PUBLIC
                   CONVENIENCE     AND   NECESSITY,   OR    CANCEL   THE
                   REGISTRATION OF AN ISP, WHICHEVER APPLIES, INCLUDING
                   THE WAVING OF ANY PRE-TERMINATION FEES OF AFFECTED
                   SUBSCRIBERS AND TIMELY DISBURSEMENT OF ANY
                   REMAINING CREDITS FROM EXCESSIVE DOWNTIME."

Section 4. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of the Act is declared void
or unconstitutional, the remaining portions or provisions hereof shall not be affected by
such declaration.

Section 5. Repealing Clause. – Any provision of law or regulations inconsistent herewith
is hereby repealed, modified or amended accordingly.

Section 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
