                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Rising global oil prices place a heavy burden on Filipino households by
increasing transportation costs and driving up the prices of food and other basic
commodities. As fuel costs rise, these are inevitably passed on to commuters and
consumers, worsening inflationary pressures and reducing purchasing power.

       Toll fees, while essential for infrastructure development, add to the cost of
transport and logistics. During periods of high oil prices, their continued
collection further compounds the financial strain on the public.

       This bill seeks to provide immediate and automatic relief by suspending
toll fees whenever global oil prices reach a specified threshold. By reducing
transport costs, the measure aims to ease the burden on motorists, support
public transport and delivery services, and help stabilize the prices of essential
goods.

     At the same time, the bill ensures that toll operators are fairly
compensated, maintaining the safety and sustainability of tollway operations.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                             “AN ACT
      ESTABLISHING A MECHANISM FOR THE TEMPORARY
      SUSPENSION OF TOLL FEES DURING PERIODS OF HIGH
      GLOBAL OIL PRICES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Toll Relief Act.”

        SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to protect
consumers, stabilize the prices of goods and services, and provide immediate
relief to the public during periods of significant increases in global oil prices.

      The State recognizes that high fuel costs increase transport expenses,
which in turn affect the prices of food, basic commodities, and other essential
goods.

       SECTION 3. Automatic Suspension of Toll Fees. When the average
monthly price of Dubai crude oil, or any other internationally recognized
benchmark crude oil, reaches or exceeds Eighty United States Dollars (US$80)
per barrel, as officially reported by the Department of Energy (DOE), the
imposition and collection of toll fees on all toll expressways in the Philippines
shall be automatically suspended.

     The suspension shall take effect on the day immediately following the
DOE’s official confirmation that the threshold has been met.

      SECTION 4. Duration of Suspension. The suspension of toll fees shall
remain in effect for as long as the average monthly crude oil price remains at or
above US$80 per barrel.

       Once the average monthly price falls below US$80 per barrel, toll collection
shall automatically resume on the first day of the succeeding month.
     SECTION 5. Coverage. The suspension shall apply to all vehicles
authorized to use toll expressways.

       Provided, That Congress may, upon recommendation of the Department of
Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev), limit the suspension to public
utility vehicles (PUVs), delivery trucks carrying basic goods, and other priority
sectors, if fiscal conditions so require.

      SECTION 6. Compensation Mechanism. To ensure the continued
maintenance and safe operation of toll facilities, the National Government shall
provide just compensation to affected toll operators during the period of
suspension.

       Funds for this purpose may be sourced from:

       (a) savings from the national budget;
       (b) contingent funds for economic stabilization; or
       (c) such other sources as may be identified in the General Appropriations
           Act (GAA).

      SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within thirty (30)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOE, the Toll Regulatory Board (TRB),
the Department of Transportation (DOTr), and the Department of Finance (DOF)
shall jointly issue the necessary rules and regulations for its proper
implementation.

     SECTION 8. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
and regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

      SECTION 10. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
