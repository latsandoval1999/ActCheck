                                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to decongest existing national penitentiaries and expand
appropriate facilities for the safekeeping and reformation of persons deprived of
liberty (PDLs). It establishes regional penitentiary facilities in Regions I, II, III, V, VI,
VIII, IX, X, XII, XIII, and the Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR).

       The need for additional and geographically distributed correctional capacity
remains evident despite ongoing decongestion efforts. As of December 31, 2025, the
Bureau of Corrections (BuCor) recorded twenty thousand and nine hundred
ninety-nine (20,999) PDLs at the New Bilibid Prison against a capacity of seven
thousand and ninety-eight (7,098). This yielded to a two hundred and ninety-six
percent (296%) occupancy rate and a one hundred and ninety-six (196%) congestion
rate1.

      This proposed measure establishes regional penal farms under the BuCor
with uniform infrastructure plans. Each facility shall provide custodial, health,
administrative, educational, livelihood, recreational, and visitation facilities to
support orderly operations and reformation programs

       A modern correctional system must protect public safety while upholding
PDL’s dignity and basic human rights. Pushing facilities beyond its capacity
undermines humane conditions, security, and rehabilitation. Regional penitentiaries
ease pressure on overcrowded prisons, strengthen custodial oversight, and create
conditions suited to reformation and reintegration.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                              AN ACT
ESTABLISHING PENITENTIARY SYSTEMS IN REGIONS I, II, III, V, VI, VIII,
IX, X, XII, XIII, AND THE CORDILLERA ADMINISTRATIVE REGION TO
DECONGEST THE NEW BILIBID PRISON AND OTHER PENAL FARMS AND
PROVINCES, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Regional Penitentiaries
Act”.

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – Section 5 of Article II of the 1987 Philippine
Constitution states that:

                 “Section 5. The maintenance of peace and order, the protection of life,
        liberty, and property, and promotion of the general welfare are essential for the
        enjoyment by all the people of the blessings of democracy.”

       It is hereby declared the policy of the State to value human dignity and
guarantee full respect for human rights regardless of any wrongful act or omission
done. The State likewise adopts the mandate under Article 5 of the Universal
Declaration of Human Rights that “no one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel,
inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment”. Among others, an adequate standard
of living for our prisoners, including decent accommodations, is considered a
human right.

        Towards this end, the State shall ensure the equitable distribution of persons
deprived of liberty (PDLs) among correctional facilities, taking into consideration
their security classification, rehabilitation requirements, and proximity to their
families and communities. It shall likewise provide adequate infrastructure,
personnel, healthcare, livelihood, educational, and rehabilitation services to support
their safekeeping and eventual reintegration into society.

       SEC. 3. Establishment of Regional Penitentiaries. – There are hereby established
additional regional penitentiaries in Regions I, II, III, V, VI, VIII, IX, X, XII, XIII, and
the Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR), which shall be operated by the Bureau
of Corrections (BuCor) under the administrative supervision of the Department of
Justice (DOJ).

       The regional penitentiaries shall serve as additional correctional facilities for
the decentralized safekeeping and reformation of PDLs, with priority given to the
decongestion of existing national correctional facilities and penal farms. The BuCor
shall determine the appropriate locations, land requirements, security classifications,
and maximum holding capacities of the regional penitentiaries.

        SEC. 4. Facilities. – The regional penitentiaries established under this Act shall
be provided with the facilities and infrastructure prescribed under Section 7 of
Republic Act (RA) No. 10575, otherwise known as “The Bureau of Corrections Act of
2013”, and such other facilities as may be necessary to ensure the safe, humane, and
efficient operation of the regional penitentiaries.

        BuCor shall ensure that the design, construction, and maintenance of these
facilities comply with national building, fire safety, environmental, accessibility, and
other relevant standards, as well as the minimum requirements for the humane
treatment of persons deprived of liberty.
        ​
​       SEC. 5. Roles and Functions of the Bureau of Corrections. – The BuCor is hereby
mandated to:

          a.​ Create an Infrastructure Plan which shall begin not later than six (6)
       months from the effectivity of this Act, to be completed not later than five (5)
       years from the effectivity of this Act;

          b.​ Coordinate the construction of regional penitentiaries, whenever
       necessary, with the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), the
       Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Department of
       Environment and Natural Resources, local government units concerned, and
       such other concerned government agencies;

          c.​ Carry out the decentralization and transfer of prisoners incarcerated in
       the national penitentiaries to regional penal farms, including the performance
       and coordination of all tasks and activities, pursuant to this Act; and

           d.​ Ensure the implementation of the provisions stipulated in this Act.

       SEC. 6. New Bilibid Prison Decongestion and Transfer Program. – The BuCor
shall establish and implement a comprehensive New Bilibid Prison (NBP)
Decongestion and Transfer Program to facilitate the systematic and orderly transfer
of PDLs from the NBP to the regional penitentiaries established under this Act and
other BuCor facilities. The Program shall include:




                                             2
         a.​ A phased transfer schedule based on the operational readiness and
      capacity of receiving facilities;

         b.​ Individual PDL assessment and classification considering security risk,
      sentence length, medical condition, rehabilitation needs, and institutional
      conduct;

         c.​ Prioritization of transfers to relieve NBP congestion without
      overcrowding other institutions;

         d.​ Measures ensuring the continuity of medical care, rehabilitation, and
      education during and after transfer;

         e.​ Timely notification of PDLs and their immediate families or
      representatives, subject to security requirements; and

         f.​ A monitoring and evaluation mechanism to track population and
      congestion impacts.

       No PDL shall be transferred unless the receiving facility is certified by the
BuCor as fully operational and capable of providing adequate accommodation,
security, healthcare, and reformation services.

        SEC. 7. Determination of Budgetary Requirements. – Within six (6) months from
the effectivity of this Act, the BuCor, in coordination with the DOJ, DBM, and
DPWH, shall determine the budgetary requirements for the establishment and
operationalization of the regional penitentiaries, indicating the estimated capital and
operating expenditures, implementation schedules, funding requirements, and
measures to ensure the efficient utilization of government resources.

      The BuCor and DOJ shall submit such requirements to the appropriate
committees of both Houses of Congress and to the DBM for consideration in
preparation of the budget.

       SEC. 8. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the BuCor and
other concerned agencies. Thereafter, such amounts as may be necessary for the
continued implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DOJ, through the BuCor, in consultation with the DBM,
DPWH, concerned LGUs, and other relevant agencies, shall promulgate the rules
and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
force and effect.
