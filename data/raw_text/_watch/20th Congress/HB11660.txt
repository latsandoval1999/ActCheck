                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Santa Maria in the Province of Bulacan serves as a major
industrial, commercial, and agricultural hub in Central Luzon. Due to its
strategic location and proximity to Metro Manila, the municipality experiences
high volumes of daily vehicular traffic, ranging from light passenger vehicles to
heavy cargo trucks transporting agricultural and manufactured goods.

To alleviate severe traffic congestion along major thoroughfares, particularly
within the urban center and major arterial roads, the local government and the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) undertook the construction
and development of the New Bagbaguin Bypass Road. Stretching approximately
two (2) kilometers, this bypass road provides a critical alternative route, diverting
heavy traffic away from congested residential and commercial centers, reducing
travel time, and improving overall logistical efficiency in the province.

While the New Bagbaguin Bypass Road serves a vital regional function in
supporting interstate trade and transport, its maintenance, repair, and future
improvement currently rest on local government funds. Given the heavy volume
and high axle loads of commercial traffic utilizing this bypass, local funding
constraints hamper continuous, optimal maintenance and necessary upgrades.

Converting the New Bagbaguin Bypass Road into a National Road will transfer its
administration, maintenance, and further development under the responsibility
of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH). This will guarantee
standard quality maintenance, enhance road safety, and ensure long-term
sustainability to keep up with regional economic growth.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT CONVERTING THE NEW BAGBAGUIN BYPASS ROAD IN THE
    MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A
        NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The New Bagbaguin Bypass Road, extending approximately two (2)
kilometers in length, located in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of
Bulacan, is hereby converted into a national road under the jurisdiction and
supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department's program the operationalization, maintenance, and improvement of
the New Bagbaguin Bypass Road, the funding of which shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary
rules, regulations, and directives to implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
