                           EXPLANATORY NOTE


This bill seeks to institutionalize a day of great historical and spiritual
significance for the people of Santa Maria, Bulacan. The "Return of the
Virgin" commemorates the recovery of the town's patroness, an event that
remains a cornerstone of local identity and communal unity.

By declaring this a special non-working holiday, we provide the citizens of
Santa Maria the opportunity to fully participate in the commemorative rites,
foster local tourism, and preserve the rich cultural heritage of the province.

The provision regarding February 2 ensures that this local celebration does
not overlap with the existing Feast of the Candles, allowing for an orderly
and respectful observance of both traditions.

In view of the cultural importance of this event to the municipality of Santa
Maria, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING THE FIRST THURSDAY OF FEBRUARY OF EVERY
YEAR, UNLESS IT FALLS ON FEBRUARY 2, A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
 HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF
BULACAN, IN COMMEMORATION OF THE RETURN OF THE IMAGE OF
                  THE BLESSED VIRGIN MARY


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of
the Philippines in Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. The first Thursday of February of every year is hereby
declared a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Santa Maria,
Province of Bulacan, to commemorate the historic return of the image of the
Blessed Virgin Mary (La Purisima Concepcion de Santa Maria) to the
municipality.
      SEC. 2. In the event that the first Thursday of February falls on
February 2, which coincides with the Feast of Our Lady of the Candles
(Candelaria), the holiday shall be observed on the immediately following day
to ensure the distinct solemnity of both religious and local historical events.
       SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
