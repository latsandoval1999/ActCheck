                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to institutionalize the Shared Services Facilities (SSF)
Project initiated by the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), within the legal
framework of the Magna Carta for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises
(MSMEs)(Republic Act No. 6977, as amended by Republic Act No. 9501).
Specifically, the bill seeks to recognize key aspects of the SSF Program:
(a)Fabrication Laboratories, consisting in high-end equipment and computer-
controlled tools, (b) Geographic Equity and Coverage, to establish Fabrication
Laboratories in every province, prioritizing provinces without existing
Fabrication Laboratories, and (c) Educational Partnerships for Sustainability, to
ensure monitoring and maintenance, the Fabrication Laboratories will be
partnered with State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), or where SUCs are
absent, they shall be attached to Local Universities and Colleges (LUCs), as
determined by the DTI in coordination with the Department of Interior and Local
Government (DILG).

       The bill seeks to address gaps in the production value chain, enabling
MSMEs to work on a reliable fund source existing within the DTI budget that is
already included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The bill seeks to
better respond to sustainability hurdles for SSFs especially in rural areas.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.
