                           AN ACT

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Education is the foundation of a strong, progressive, and inclusive nation. At
the heart of our public education system are the teachers and employees who dedicate
their knowledge, skills, time, and energy to shaping the future of our children and
strengthening the human capital of our country.

       Every day, public school teachers and education personnel perform
responsibilities that go far beyond the traditional boundaries of their jobs. Teachers
prepare lessons, assess learners, provide individualized support, address learning
gaps, manage classrooms, and respond to the diverse needs of students. Non-
teaching personnel, meanwhile, provide essential administrative, technical, and
support services that ensure the continuous and effective delivery of basic education.

       Despite the vital nature of their work, many public school teachers and
employees continue to face increasing financial pressures brought about by the rising
cost of food, transportation, housing, utilities, healthcare, and other basic necessities.
The demands of the profession have likewise continued to grow, with education
personnel being called upon to perform increasingly complex responsibilities in an
evolving educational environment.

       The State recognizes the invaluable contribution of teachers and education
personnel to national development. The 1987 Constitution declares that the State shall
assign the highest budgetary priority to education and shall ensure that teaching will
attract and retain its rightful share of the best available talents through adequate
remuneration and other means of job satisfaction and fulfillment.
      Providing adequate and competitive compensation is not merely an issue of
employee welfare. It is an investment in the quality and stability of our public education
system. Fair and meaningful increases in salaries can help improve the economic well-
being of education personnel, strengthen employee morale, promote retention, and
encourage qualified and competent individuals to remain in or enter the public
education service.

       In recognition of their indispensable contribution to the Filipino people, this
measure seeks to grant an across-the-board increase of Fifteen Thousand Pesos
(₱15,000.00) in the basic monthly salary of teachers and employees in public
basic education. To ensure fiscal sustainability and facilitate the orderly
implementation of the measure, the proposed increase shall be implemented in three
tranches: ₱6,000.00 in the first year, ₱5,000.00 in the second year, and ₱4,000.00
in the third year.

        The proposed salary increase shall be granted in addition to existing salary
adjustments under applicable salary standardization laws and shall not prejudice any
succeeding salary increases that may be granted to government employees pursuant
to law.

       The measure further recognizes that the welfare of teachers and education
personnel is directly connected to the quality of education that our children receive. By
providing them with better compensation and stronger economic security, the
government can help create a more motivated, committed, and resilient public
education workforce capable of responding to the changing needs of Filipino learners.

      Our teachers and education personnel are not merely government employees.
They are builders of communities, shapers of minds, and partners in nation-building.
Their work creates opportunities, transforms lives, and lays the foundation for the
country's future.

       It is therefore imperative that the State provide them with compensation
commensurate with the importance of their work and the responsibilities they carry.
This measure seeks to reaffirm our commitment to the men and women who serve at
the frontlines of public education and to ensure that their dedication and sacrifices are
met with meaningful support from the government.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

Be It enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the “Dagdag Sahod for
Public Basic Education Teachers and Employees Act”.
       Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy - It is the policy of the State to promote and
improve the economic status and well-being of teachers and employees in the
education sector. Towards this end, the State shall provide the highest budgetary
priority to education and to provide adequate remuneration of teachers and employees
in the public education system.
       Sec. 3. Coverage - This Act shall cover all public school teachers and
employees directly engaged in the delivery of basic education services in line with the
curriculum and learning modalities recognized by the Department of Education.
       Sec. 4. Increase in Basic Monthly Salary - All teachers and employees in public
basic education, regardless of their length of service and employment status are
entitled to an across-the-board increase of Fifteen Thousand Pesos (PhP 15,000.00)
to their basic monthly salary.

Provided, that this increase is to be implemented in addition to the existing Salary
Standardization Law and shall not be construed as an exemption from succeeding
salary increases for government employees.
       Sec. 5. Salary Increases Schedule - The salary increases shall be given in three
tranches under the following schedule:
                                    Year 1 - 6,000.00
                                    Year 2 - 5,000.00
                                    Year 3 - 4,000.00
Provided that the salary schedule shall be implemented starting January 1 of the year
after the enactment of this Act, and every January 1 of the year thereafter.
       Sec. 6. Appropriations. - The amount necessary to implement the provisions of
this Act shall be provided in the General Appropriation Act.
       Sec. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The Department of Budget and
Management (DBM), in consultation with the Department of Education (DepEd) and
teachers’ unions and organizations, shall promulgate within sixty (60) days after the
effectivity of this Act the necessary rules and regulations for its effective and immediate
implementation.
       Sec. 8. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended or modified accordingly.
       Sec. 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in newspapers of general circulation.
