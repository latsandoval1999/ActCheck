                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       A public school cannot deliver quality education without teachers who are
physically and mentally capable of performing their duties. Teachers do more
than teach in the classroom. They also monitor student progress, provide
feedback, prepare reports, participate in school activities, and respond to the
different needs of their learners.


       Public school teachers therefore require a dependable health-care
arrangement that complements existing government benefits. Access to a
Healthcare Maintenance Organization can help teachers obtain preventive,
rehabilitative, and other necessary medical services while reducing delays and
personal expenses. Moreover, an HMO incentive has higher benefit limits,
consultations, hospital coverage for pre-existing conditions, and more access to
accredited hospitals nationwide.


       This bill seeks to establish a health-care program for all public school
teachers under the Department of Education. It directs the Department to
procure, through competitive bidding, a contract with a duly recognized
Healthcare Maintenance Organization accepted by hospitals throughout the
country.


      The bill further provides that the amount necessary for its implementation
shall be included in the General Appropriations Act for the year following its
approval.


      By protecting the health of teachers, the State also protects the continuity
and quality of public education.

                     AN ACT
PROVIDING A HEALTH CARE PROGRAM FOR PUBLIC
SCHOOL TEACHERS AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Health Care for
Public School Teachers Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policies. The State shall uphold the right of
all citizens to accessible, affordable, and quality health care. Accordingly, public
school teachers shall be provided with comprehensive, cost-effective, preventive,
rehabilitative, and other appropriate health services..

       SECTION 3. Coverage. This Act shall cover all public school teachers
under the Department of Education.

       SECTION 4. Healthcare Maintenance. The Department of Education
shall, through a competitive bidding process conducted at the national or


                                                                                    3
regional level, procure healthcare maintenance coverage for public school
teachers from a licensed and accredited health maintenance organization with an
established network of hospitals and healthcare providers nationwide.

       The healthcare maintenance coverage in this provision shall be in addition
to, and shall not replace or reduce, any existing medical allowance or benefit
currently granted to public school teachers by the Department of Education.

       The HMO package shall include, but not be limited to:

          a. Outpatient services: Consultations, laboratory tests, and diagnostic
              procedures;
          b. Inpatient services: Hospital rooms, physician’s fees, and surgical
              requirements;
          c. Emergency care: Full coverage on emergency room fees and other
              medically performed treatments;
          d. Annual physical examination (APE): Comprehensive annual
              check-ups; and
          e. Mental Health Support: Access to psychiatric or psychological
              consultations and therapy.

       SECTION 5. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the immediate
implementation of the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General
Appropriations Act for the year following the approval of this Act.

       SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. The Secretary of the
Department of Education shall formulate the Implementing Rules and
Regulations (IRR) for the effective implementation of this Act within thirty (30)
days from the effectivity of this Act.

       SECTION 7. Separability Clause. If any portion or provision of this Act
shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected
thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.
