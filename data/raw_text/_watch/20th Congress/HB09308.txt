    AN ACT STRENGTHENING PUBLIC OFFICE ACCOUNTABILITY AND

         NET WORTH, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        “Public office is a public trust. Public officers and employees must at all times
       be accountable to the people, serve them with utmost responsibility, integrity,
       loyalty, and efficiency, act with patriotism and justice, and lead modest lives.”

        Nowhere can a clearer mandate for transparency and accountability be found other than
in Article XI, SECTION 1 of the 1987 Constitution.

        The filing of the Statement of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth (SALN) has long been
enshrined in Philippine law as a cornerstone of public accountability. Under existing rules,
government officials and employees are required to annually disclose their financial standing
to deter illicit enrichment and promote transparency in public service.

        SALN declarations, however, remain unverified unless acted upon in the course of an
investigation, falling short of its promise to promote greater transparency within the
government.

       Under this proposed legislation, the digitalization of SALN filing and publication shall
be mandatory, and cross-agency verification mechanisms shall be established to ensure that
high officials cannot hide behind any veil of discretion.

        By deploying artificial intelligence-assisted analytics and a flagging system, the SALN
transforms from a passive disclosure requirement into an active instrument of detection and
accountability. Equally important, the bill requires interim disclosure of high-value transactions
occurring between annual filing periods, closing a loophole long exploited to obscure the
movement of assets.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “SALN Digitalization and
Verification Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The regular filing of Statement of Assets, Liabilities, and Net
Worth (SALN) of all public officials and employees has produced limited effectivity in
promoting transparency and accountability.

It shall be the policy of the State to subject SALNs to digitalization and verification of
government agencies capable of verifying and cross-checking SALN declarations. Upon
finding of the existence of inconsistencies in the declaration upon verification, verifying
agencies shall be given the power to proactively flag and report to the Civil Service
Commission and/or the Ombudsman for further investigation and action.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – The following terms used herein this Act shall be defined as
follows:

       a) “Digitalization” refers to the use of digital technologies to convert processes,
       records, or services into digital form and improve them through digital systems.

       a) “Politically Exposed Person” refers to a natural person who is or has been entrusted
       with prominent public positions in the Philippines, whether in the national or local
       government or as executives of government or state owned or controlled corporations.

SEC. 4. Scope. – The provisions of this Act shall only apply to all government officials and
employees classified as politically exposed persons.

SEC. 5. Digitalization of SALN filing. – The filing of SALNs shall be made through digital
means. The Civil Service Commission (CSC) shall provide for the guidelines in relation to the
template, format, and procedures to be uniformly adopted across all government agencies and
instrumentalities and. In consultation with the Department of Information and Communications
Technology (DICT), the CSC shall ensure that the digital document or data reflecting the SALN
shall be interoperable across all relevant agencies mentioned in this Act herein.

SEC. 6. Mandatory Digital Publication of SALNs. – SALNs disclosing the financial status,
including assets, liabilities and business interests shall be posted in the website of the Civil
Service Commission and across all websites of the government and its instrumentalities within
ten (10) working days from the time of filing within their respective repository agencies.

Personal information such as but not limited to home addresses, signatures, children’s details
and government IDs shall not be disclosed pursuant to Republic Act No. 10173 or the Data
Privacy Act.

SEC. 7. Verification of SALN Declaration. – All declarations made under the SALN shall be
verified as truthful and factual by the following agencies:

      a) For real properties – Land Registration Authority (LRA);

      b) For vehicle assets – Land Transportation Office (LTO), Civil Aviation Authority of the
         Philippines (CAAP), and the Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA);

      c) For business and financial interests – Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
         and the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR); and

      d) For declared cash-in bank – Respective depository bank.

Verification for the purposes of this Act shall not be construed as applicable only to the
information declared in the SALN, but shall be performed as a cross-checking mechanism to
detect any under- or non-declaration of any assets possessed by the politically exposed person.

For the purposes of the scope of this Section, all assets or accounts belonging to the subject
politically exposed person, owned through sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporate
ownership, including ownership as the ultimate beneficial owner under SEC guidelines, shall
be assessed.

SEC. 8. Declaration of Select Interim Assets and Liabilities . – Assets and liabilities acquired,
disposed, or incurred in the interim period or the period between the filing of the SALN at the
end of each year shall be declared and annexed to the latest SALN filed within thirty (30) days
of such transaction, and shall be reflected within the same period as provided under Section 6
of this Act: Provided, That the covered transactions shall be limited to the following:

       a) Acquisition and/or disposal of real property and vehicles;

       b) Business and financial interests, including but not limited to shareholdings,
          acquisitions, mergers, and dissolution; and

       c) Acquisition and disposal of other high-value assets exceeding One million pesos
          (P1,000,000.00).
Verification of interim assets and liabilities under (a) and (b) of this Section shall likewise be
conducted.

SEC. 9. SALN Analytics and Flagging System . – The Ombudsman shall, in coordination with
the DICT and the CSC, develop and maintain an interoperable and artificial intelligence-
assisted digital system with the agencies and entities enumerated under Section 7 of this Act,
subject to the secure data-sharing protocols, which shall automatically flag the following:

       a) Sudden net worth increases;

       b) Under- or non-declaration of real properties or other personal properties;

       c) Non-declaration of business and financial interests; and

       d) Inconsistencies between declared sources of income vis-à-vis declared net worth.

Provided, That the data sharing between the Ombudsman and the other agencies and
government entities shall be limited to data or information germane to the respective agency’s
verification processes: Provided, further, That data sharing shall also be enabled between the
digital system established under this Act and Philippine Government Electronic Procurement
System under Republic Act No. 12009, for the purpose of flagging any potential conflict of
interest.

SEC. 10. Flagged Activities or Transactions. – Flagged activities or transactions shall be
automatically subjected to an investigation by the Civil Service Commission and the
Ombudsman while ensuring that due process is accorded to flagged individuals. Should prima
facie evidence with reasonable certainty of conviction be found in relation to any violation of
prevailing laws, the Ombudsman shall within a period of not more than sixty (60) days file the
necessary and relevant charges for prosecution, without prejudice to other pertinent
administrative charges that may be filed.

SEC. 11. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 12. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional, the
remainder not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations or parts thereof
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 14. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
