                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

Public service is sustained by the dedication and labor of government employees, many of
whom perform physically demanding, emotionally taxing, and care-intensive work for
decades. Women comprise a significant portion of the public sector workforce, particularly in
education, health, social services, and local government—sectors where prolonged exposure to
stress, long working hours, and dual roles in paid work and unpaid care responsibilities are
common. As workers age, these realities increasingly affect their health, productivity, and
overall well-being.

Republic Act No. 8291, otherwise known as the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS)
Act of 1997, presently allows optional retirement at sixty (60) years of age, subject to at least
fifteen (15) years of government service. While this framework has provided stability, it does
not sufficiently respond to the differentiated impacts of aging, gender roles, and labor
conditions on government workers—particularly women and those in frontline and caregiving
roles.

This bill seeks to strengthen labor protection and promote gender responsiveness in the public
sector by lowering the optional retirement age from sixty (60) years to fifty-six (56) years,
while retaining the minimum requirement of fifteen (15) years of credited service. By
amending Section 13-A of Republic Act No. 8291, the measure provides qualified government
employees with the option—not the obligation—to retire earlier, allowing them to make
retirement decisions that take into account their health, family responsibilities, and life-course
realities.

Early optional retirement recognizes that workers do not experience aging uniformly. For many
women, years of balancing paid employment with unpaid domestic and caregiving labor result
in earlier physical and emotional exhaustion. Providing an earlier retirement option affirms the
State’s commitment to gender equality, decent work, and the humane treatment of public
servants, consistent with constitutional principles and international labor standards.
From a labor perspective, the measure supports occupational health and safety by preventing
prolonged exposure to work conditions that may no longer be sustainable for aging employees.
It likewise facilitates workforce renewal, enables knowledge transfer, and opens opportunities
for younger workers—particularly women and professionals from underrepresented groups—
to enter government service.

To ensure that expanded retirement options do not compromise the long-term viability of the
Government Service Insurance System, the bill provides for a one-time offset fund in the
amount of Two Hundred Million Pesos (₱200,000,000). This allocation is intended to cushion
any potential actuarial impact on the GSIS Social Insurance Fund arising from earlier
retirements, thereby balancing enhanced social protection with fiscal prudence.

Notably, this measure is a refiled bill that was approved on Third Reading during the
Nineteenth Congress, reflecting prior legislative recognition of the need for a more flexible,
equitable, and worker-centered retirement framework.

In advancing this reform, the State affirms that retirement policy is not merely a matter of age
and service length, but a critical component of labor protection and gender-responsive
governance. By providing government workers with greater choice and dignity at the latter
stages of their careers, this bill contributes to a more inclusive, humane, and sustainable public
service system. The passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
LOWERING THE OPTIONAL RETIREMENT AGE OF GOVERNMENT WORKERS
FROM SIXTY (60) YEARS TO FIFTY-SIX (56) YEARS, AMENDING FOR THE
PURPOSE SECTION 13-A OF REPUBLIC ACT 8291, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS
“THE GOVERNMENT SERVICE INSURANCE SYSTEM ACT OF 1997”


Be it enacted in the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Section 13-A of Republic Act 8291, otherwise known as “The
Government Service Insurance System Act of 1997” is hereby amended to read as
follows:

      SEC. 13-A. Conditions for Entitlement. – A member who retires from the service
      shall be entitled to the retirement benefits enumerated in paragraph (a) of
      Section 13 hereof: Provided, That SUCH MEMBER:

      (1) [he] has rendered at least fifteen (15) years of service;

      (2) [he] is at least [sixty (60)] FIFTY-SIX (56) years of age at the time of
      retirement; and

      (3) [he] is not receiving a monthly pension benefit [from] DUE TO permanent
      total disability.

SEC. 2. One-time Offset Fund. To mitigate any adverse impact on the actuarial life
of the Social Insurance Fund of the GSIS arising from the lowering of the optional
retirement age, there is hereby appropriated the amount of Two Hundred Million Pesos
(₱200,000,000) as a one-time offset fund.

SEC. 3. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations, or other
issuances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 4. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
