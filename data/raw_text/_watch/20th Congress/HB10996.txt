                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE


        This bill seeks to strengthen public health measures against diet-related diseases while
generating sustainable funding for critical national health and nutrition initiatives. The primary
policy objective of this legislation is to curb the rising prevalence of lifestyle illnesses, such as
diabetes and obesity, by using fiscal policy to encourage healthier consumer choices and
incentivize manufacturers to reformulate their products with lower sugar content.

        To achieve this health deterrent, the measure updates tax rates on sweetened beverages,
establishing higher rates for drinks containing high fructose corn syrup to disincentivize its
widespread industrial use. The policy scope is also broadened to encompass commercial
sweetened milk and coffee products, ensuring a comprehensive framework across the beverage
market. At the same time, the bill safeguards essential public nutrition by strictly exempting
staple items such as plain unsweetened milk, infant formula, and standard unflavored coffee
products.

        Beyond deterrence, a core outcome of this legislation is establishing a dedicated legal
mechanism for statutory revenue earmarking. The bill guarantees that revenues collected are
funneled directly into essential public services. To address rooted health disparities, forty percent
of revenues are ring-fenced specifically for local government support, focusing on targeted
nutrition programs for pregnant mothers and children in their first five years of life, alongside
vital water, sanitation, and hygiene infrastructure.

         Finally, the bill incorporates an annual tax indexation mechanism so that the health
deterrent maintains its real value over time without being eroded by economic changes. Coupled
with expanded legislative oversight to strictly track the distribution and use of funds at the local
level, the ultimate outcome of this measure is a self-sustaining cycle where taxes collected from
sugary beverage consumption are directly transformed into long-term investments in maternal
care, child nutrition, and accessible healthcare for all Filipinos.
In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
