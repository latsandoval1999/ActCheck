2    DECLARING AUGUST 14 OF EVERY YEAR AS" ARAW NG MAWAB" AND A SPECIAL
3    NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MAWAB, PROVINCE OF
4    DAVAO DE ORO, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

6    Be enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:
7
8    SECTION 1. August 14 of every year is hereby declared as "Araw ng Mawab" and a special
 9   non-working holiday in the Municipality of Mawab, Province of Davao de Oro, in
10   commemoration of its founding anniversary.
11
12   SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
13   Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

15   Approved.
