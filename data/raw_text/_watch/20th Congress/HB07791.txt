                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Pamplona-San Fernando Bypass Road in the Province of
Camarines Sur, into a national road.

Roads are considered as the backbone of one’s province’s infrastructure as it connects people
to goods and services, access to healthcare, social activities, and employment opportunities,
and efficient and faster travel to one’s workplace and school. In fact, roads is one of the major
dictations of a developing city or municipality or province as it promotes their overall
development due to their efficiencies on how they reduce transportation costs and travel time.

The Pamplona-San Fernando Bypass Road is a road that connects two (2) barangays in the
Municipality of Pamplona and three (3) barangays in the Municipality of San Fernando that
stretches for seven kilometers and sixty-two meters, which is beneficial to its constituents for
the delivery of goods and services to and from the said barangays.

However, just like any other infrastructure, roads are prone to wear and tear that are in need
of either maintenance or immediate repair. As much as local government units acknowledge
their significance within their jurisdictions for efficient and effective flow of goods and services,
and resources, their budgets are limited and need the assistance of the national government
to both maintain and repair such roads.

Therefore, in order to ensure the San Juan-San Lorenzo-Antipolo Road’s orderly function in
the province of Camarines Sur, the conversion of the said road into a national road is essential.

  AN ACT CONVERTING THE PAMPLONA-SAN FERNANDO BYPASS ROAD INTO A
                           NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. – The Pamplona-San Fernando Bypass Road connecting Barangay San Gabriel
in the Municipality of Pamplona to Barangay Del Pilar in the Municipality of San Fernando in
the Province of Camarines Sur, consisting of Seven Kilomters and Sixty-Two Meters (7.62km),
more or less, is hereby converted into a national road.

SECTION 2. – The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue
the necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 3. – This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
