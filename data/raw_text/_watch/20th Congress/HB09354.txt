                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The State recognizes that forward-looking infrastructure planning is
essential to managing urban growth, sustaining economic activity, and
ensuring long-term mobility. Bypass roads are critical components of such
planning, as they redirect through-traffic away from congested urban
centers, improve traffic flow, and support the efficient movement of goods
and services.

       The Iligan City Coastal Bypass Road is envisioned as a strategic coastal
corridor that will serve as an alternate route to the City’s existing road
network. A feasibility study has already been conducted, affirming its
viability and importance to the city’s long-term transport and infrastructure
development. Following its original alignment from St. Peter’s in Barangay
Sta. Filomena to Palm Court in Barangay Tomas Cabili, the road is designed
to ease congestion along primary urban corridors and enhance connectivity
across key coastal areas.

      While portions of the alignment have been constructed, the road
remains incomplete and is not yet open to traffic. This condition continues
to constrain its intended function as a bypass route and limits its
contribution to overall traffic decongestion and network efficiency.

      Establishing the Iligan City Coastal Bypass Road as a national road will
ensure sustained funding, coordinated implementation, and adherence to
national standards. This will enable the Department of Public Works and
Highways to undertake its phased construction and completion as a vital
component of the City’s transport network.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
       DECLARING THE ILIGAN CITY COASTAL BYPASS ROAD,
 FROM ST. PETER’S IN BARANGAY STA. FELOMINA TO PALM COURT
   IN BARANGAY TOMAS CABILI, ILIGAN CITY A NATIONAL ROAD,
PROVIDING FOR ITS CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT AND MAINTENANCE,
            AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Iligan City
Coastal Bypass National Road Development Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration. — The Iligan City Coastal Bypass Road, from
St. Peter’s in Barangay Sta. Filomena to Palm Court in Barangay Tomas Cabili,
Iligan City, shall be declared a national road under the supervision and
administration of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

      SECTION 3. Development and Maintenance. — The DPWH shall
undertake the construction, completion, improvement, and maintenance of
the road in accordance with national standards and specifications.

      SECTION 4. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the
continued construction, improvement, and maintenance of the road, as well
as other activities necessary for its development as a national road, shall be
included in the General Appropriations Act (GAA). Thereafter, the Secretary
of Public Works and Highways shall include the funding requirements
therefor in the Department’s program for inclusion in the annual GAA.
       SECTION 5. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
