EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to establish a segregated Shari’ah-compliant provident savings and
housing finance window under the Pag-IBIG Fund, amending for the purpose Republic Act
(RA) No. 9679, otherwise known as the “Home Development Mutual Fund Law of 2009.”

While the 1987 Constitution guarantees access to decent, affordable housing and
promotes broad-based economic participation, structural gaps in the existing financial
framework continue to limit access to provident savings and housing finance for members who
adhere to non-interest-based financial principles. Under RA No. 9679, Pag-IBIG’s existing
savings and financing mechanisms are principally structured around conventional, interest-
bearing arrangements. Providing an alternative mechanism within the country’s principal
public provident savings and housing finance institution can help address this gap and broaden
financial inclusion.

Republic Act No. 11439, or the “Islamic Banking Act,” recognizes Islamic banking and
establishes a policy of tax neutrality between Islamic banking transactions and _ their
conventional equivalents. However, RA No. 11439 principally governs banks and Islamic
banking units, while Pag-IBIG is a government provident fund and housing finance institution.
Express authority under RA No. 9679 is therefore necessary for the Fund to establish and
sustainably operate a Shari ‘ah-compliant window suited to its mandate.

To bridge this gap, this measure establishes a segregated operational and accounting
window within Pag-IBIG. It authorizes the use of recognized, non-interest-based and asset-
backed structures such as Mudarabah (profit-sharing), Murabahah (cost-plus sale), [jarah
Muntahiyah bi al-Tamlik (lease-to-own), Musharakah Mutanagqisah (diminishing partnership),
and Istisna (construction financing). The Window will be available to all qualified Pag-IBIG
members on a voluntary basis, regardless of religion, while existing conventional savings and

housing programs remain available and unaffected.

Because certain Shari’ah-compliant structures may require the Fund to acquire, hold,
lease, co-own, sell, or transfer property, the measure provides the incidental authority necessary
to implement such transactions. It likewise requires appropriate segregation of assets,
liabilities, income, and expenses, prudent standards for investment, liquidity, reserves, risk
management, valuation, accounting, and internal controls, and policies that facilitate access by
lower-income members while preserving the actuarial soundness and solvency of the Fund.

To ensure Shari’ah compliance and protect members, the bill establishes a Shari’ah
Advisory Committee to certify products, contracts, operational policies, and investments and
to conduct compliance reviews. It also provides for appropriate disclosures, transparent pricing,
fair treatment in matters such as default and early settlement, and accessible complaints
mechanisms. The implementing rules will provide the detailed procedures for enrollment,
participation, fund management, accounting, standard contracts. disclosures, compliance, and
other operational matters.

The measure likewise adopts the principle of tax neutrality so that additional transfers
or documents required solely by an approved Shari ’ah-compliant financing structure do not
result in more burdensome tax treatment than a functionally equivalent conventional
transaction. This is intended to prevent structural double taxation while preserving existing tax
laws and exemptions applicable to the Fund.

By modernizing Pag-IBIG’s framework to accommodate voluntary, inclusive financing
options, this bill expands member choice, strengthens financial inclusion and savings
mobilization, and makes state-backed housing programs more accessible while safeguarding
the provident character, financial stability, and public accountability of the Fund.

For these reasons, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, Bureau of Internal Revenue, Commission on Audit, National

and stakeholders, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.

The implementing rules and regulations shall, among others, provide for the operational
and accounting segregation of the Window; eligibility, enrollment, and participation;
applicable Shari’ah-compliant savings and financing structures; management of investments,
liquidity, reserves, and risks; allocation of income and expenses; disclosure and member
protection, Shari’ah compliance and certification; treatment of investment gains and losses;
transfer and withdrawal procedures as may be authorized by law; and such other matters as
may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.

The implementing rules and regulations shall be consistent with Republic Act No. 9679,
as amended, the National Internal Revenue Code, as amended, applicable auditing and
procurement laws, and other relevant laws and regulations.

SEC. 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, administrative orders. rules,
and regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.

SEC. 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
