                           AN ACT
FURTHER STRENGTHENING THE RIGHTS, PRIVILEGES, BENEFITS, AND
IDENTIFICATION SYSTEM OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES, AMENDING
FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
“MAGNA CARTA FOR DISABLED PERSONS,” AS AMENDED, AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES


                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The State recognizes its constitutional duty to protect and promote the rights
and welfare of persons with disabilities (PWDs) and to ensure their full participation
in society. Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the “Magna Carta for Persons
with Disability,” as amended, has long served as the principal law safeguarding the
rights, privileges, and welfare of PWDs in the Philippines. Despite existing legal
protections, many PWDs continue to face barriers in accessing government services,
employment opportunities, mobility assistance, social protection, and essential
support systems.

       One recurring concern raised by persons with disabilities, particularly those with
permanent disabilities and elderly PWDs, is the burdensome process of repeatedly
renewing identification cards despite the permanent nature of their conditions. Many
are compelled to endure travel difficulties, inaccessible government facilities, long
queues, and repetitive documentary requirements merely to maintain access to
benefits already guaranteed by law. This situation imposes unnecessary hardship on
a sector that the State is duty-bound to protect.
        This measure seeks to strengthen and modernize the Magna Carta for Persons
with Disability by institutionalizing reforms that improve accessibility, promote
inclusion, and prevent abuse of the privileges granted to PWDs. It establishes
standardized validity periods for PWD identification cards, including lifetime validity for
persons with permanent disabilities and elderly PWDs, while ensuring safeguards
against fraudulent use. It further enhances accountability through stronger penalties
against the falsification, misuse, or illegal issuance of PWD identification cards.

        The bill likewise expands support mechanisms for PWDs by recognizing
assistive devices and assistive technology services as essential tools for independent
living and social participation. It provides free assistive devices and services for
marginalized PWDs, grants additional social protection measures such as monthly
stipends for indigent persons with severe disabilities, mandates express lanes in
establishments, and exempts qualified PWDs from certain travel-related government
fees and charges.

        In addition, the measure strengthens employment incentives for private entities
hiring PWDs and promotes more meaningful compliance with employment
opportunities reserved for them. It also mandates inter-agency coordination in
establishing a unified and reliable database of persons with disabilities to improve
policy implementation and service delivery nationwide.

        Through these reforms, the bill seeks to uphold the dignity of persons with
disabilities, eliminate unnecessary barriers to their participation in society, and ensure
that government policies are truly inclusive, responsive, and accessible.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
