                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Dipolog Airport serves as the primary aviation gateway of the Province of
Zamboanga del Norte, connecting the province to major domestic hubs such as Manila
and Cebu and supporting the movement of passengers, goods, and services across the
Zamboanga Peninsula. Located in Minaog, Dipolog City and operating under the IATA
code DPL, the airport currently functions solely as a domestic facility despite the
province’s growing tourism, commerce, and regional mobility needs.

       In recent years, passenger volumes have steadily grown and have nearly returned
to pre-pandemic levels, reflecting rising demand for air transport services in the region.
This growth underscores the limitations of the airport’s existing facilities and highlights
the need to expand its operational capacity to accommodate future aviation demand

       At present, international air connectivity in Mindanao remains concentrated in
only a few locations, particularly Davao and General Santos. As a result, travelers from
the northern and western parts of Mindanao must undertake long land journeys to
reach international gateways. Converting Dipolog City Airport into an international
airport will address this gap by providing a more accessible gateway for residents,
tourists, overseas Filipino workers, and businesses in the region.

       The conversion of Dipolog Airport into an international airport is expected to
generate significant economic and social benefits, including increased tourism,
improved trade and investment opportunities, enhanced regional connectivity, and new
employment for local communities. It will also support the national government’s
objective of strengthening regional infrastructure and promoting balanced economic
development in Mindanao.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
     CONVERTING THE DIPOLOG AIRPORT IN THE PROVINCE OF
  ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE, INTO AN INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT TO BE
      KNOWN AS THE DIPOLOG INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT, AND
              APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Dipolog International
Airport Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to provide an efficient
infrastructure system for transport and logistics to promote sustainable and inclusive
economic growth. To support the achievement of the long-term development vision for
the Philippines, the State shall establish strategic national transport networks,
including international gateways, to facilitate mobility and connectivity.

SEC. 3. Conversion. — The existing Dipolog Airport located in the City of Dipolog,
Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby converted into an international airport to
be known as the Dipolog International Airport.

SEC. 4. Scope of Development. — The conversion of the Dipolog Airport into an
international airport shall include, but shall not be limited to, the expansion and
modernization of airport facilities necessary to accommodate international flights,
including runway extension and strengthening, construction of an international
passenger terminal building, installation of immigration and customs facilities, security
and safety infrastructure, air navigation systems, cargo handling facilities, and other
essential airport support services consistent with international aviation standards.

SEC. 5. Implementing Agency. — The Department of Transportation (DOTr), in
coordination with the Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP), the Bureau of
Immigration, the Bureau of Customs, the Department of Tourism, and other concerned
government agencies, shall be responsible for the implementation of this Act and the
development of the Dipolog International Airport.

SEC. 6. Development Plan. — Within one (1) year from the effectivity of this Act, the
DOTR, through the CAAP, shall formulate and implement a comprehensive
development master plan for the Dipolog International Airport to ensure that its
facilities comply with international aviation standards and are capable of supporting
both passenger and cargo international operations.

SEC. 7. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The DOTr and the CAAP
may also explore public-private partnerships and other financing mechanisms for the
development, operation, and maintenance of the Dipolog International Airport.

SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DOTr, in coordination with the CAAP and other concerned
agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

SEC. 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.

SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
