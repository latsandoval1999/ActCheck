                           AN ACT
 PROVIDING MANDATORY INSURANCE COVERAGE AND OTHER BENEFITS
                   FOR ALL LINE WORKERS

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The vitality of our national economy and the uninterrupted continuity of modern
life are inextricably linked to the stability of our energy sector and each power grid, of
which stand the line workers, the courageous individuals tasked with the construction,
installation, and maintenance of our intricate electrical transmission and distribution
systems. Despite the indispensable nature of their service, these workers operate
under conditions of extreme peril, routinely exposed to high-voltage electricity and
hazardous environments to ensure that homes and industries remain energized. It is
a profound systemic oversight that many of these frontliners continue to perform their
duties bereft of adequate protection against death, debilitating injury, or chronic
illness sustained in the line of duty, leaving them and their families vulnerable to the
very risks they manage on behalf of the public.

        To address this inequity, this bill mandates that private distribution utilities,
electric cooperatives, and transmission grid operators provide compulsory insurance
coverage and comprehensive benefits to all their respective line workers. Drawing
from the legislative precedent of House Bill No. 7561 approved during the Nineteenth
Congress, this measure seeks to institutionalize a safety net comprising disability and
retirement benefits, death and burial assistance, and medical expense
reimbursements.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 1         Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Line Workers
 2   Insurance and Benefits Act”.
 3
 4          Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the role of the electrical
 5   line workers in the quest for sustainable economic development through the
 6   continuous and reliable supply of electricity, not only in residential dwellings, but most
 7   importantly, in commercial and industrial establishments, specifically in the
 8   countryside and rural areas, as envisioned by the Rural Electrification Program, and
 9   the risk they encounter in the performance of their duties and responsibilities. As such,
10   it shall provide measures that recognize the contributions of line workers to nation-
11   building particularly in the transmission and distribution sectors of the power industry
12   by ensuring that their employers are providing them insurance coverage and other
13   benefits in addition to what is provided for regularly by existing labor laws.

14          Sec. 3. Definition of Line Worker. – For the purposes of this Act, line workers
15   shall refer to persons, including crews, drivers and helpers, who are directly
16   responsible    for   the   construction,   installation,   maintenance,   reconstruction,
17   rehabilitation and repair of electrical transmission and distribution systems, including
18   underground cables, electrical substations and other related electrical facilities.

                                                 1
 1          Sec. 4. Coverage. – This Act shall cover all line workers in the power industry
 2   sector as defined in Section 3 hereof, including those working in private distribution
 3   utilities, electric cooperatives and transmission or grid operators.

 4          Sec. 5. Mandatory Insurance Coverage for Line Workers. – All covered line
 5   workers shall be entitled to life, accident or disability insurance benefits in accordance
 6   with the following minimum insurance coverage schedule:

 7          (a) For electric cooperatives, the minimum insurance coverage shall be as
 8                follows:

 9                   Classification          Minimum Insurance Coverage

10                       Small               Two Hundred Thousand Pesos (P200,000.00)

11                       Medium              Four Hundred Thousand Pesos (P400,000.00)

12                       Large               Six Hundred Thousand Pesos (P600,000.00)

13                       Extra Large         Eight Hundred Thousand Pesos (P800,000.00)

14                       Mega Large          One Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00)

15          (b) For private distribution utilities, the minimum insurance coverage shall be
16                One Million Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (P1,500,000.00); and
17          (c)   For transmission or grid operators, the minimum insurance coverage shall
18                be Two Million Pesos (P2,000,000.00).

19          The coverage shall be effective upon the commencement of their employment
20   until their separation from the company, either by resignation, termination, or
21   retirement. The insurance premium payments shall be shouldered entirely by the
22   employer.

23          Employers who are already providing insurance coverage to their line workers
24   equal to or more than the minimum requirement as prescribed herein are no longer
25   required to make adjustments to their insurance coverage. Otherwise, they should
26   augment the amount of coverage to comply with this Act: Provided, That in no way
27   shall this Act be construed to diminish or reduce any insurance coverage which the
28   line workers are already receiving or any entitlement under existing laws, company

 1   policy, or employment contracts or collective bargaining agreements between the
 2   employer and the employees.

 3          Sec. 6. Mandatory Benefits. – In addition to the mandated insurance coverage
 4   as provided in Section 5 of this Act, the employers of line workers shall also ensure
 5   the provision of the following mandatory benefits:

 6          (a) Retirement benefits;
 7          (b) Death benefits and burial assistance;
 8          (c)   Disability benefits;
 9          (d) Reimbursement of actual medical expenses in case of death, accident,
10                sickness, disability or injury sustained by the line worker in the line of
11                duty; and
12          (e) Other benefits and entitlements as may be identified by the Department
13                of Labor and Employment (DOLE) in consultation with the stakeholders
14                concerned:

15   Provided, That in cases when a line worker participates in power restoration and
16   rehabilitation efforts in calamity-stricken areas or similar engagements hosted by an
17   entity other than the employer of the line worker, such host entity shall provide the
18   line worker with benefits similar to those provided under this Section and as may be

19   allowed by its financial capacity to cover death, accident, sickness, disability or injury
20   sustained by the line worker during such engagement.

21          Sec. 7. Disqualifications from Benefits. – No benefit shall be extended to line
22   workers or their beneficiaries in cases where the death, accident, sickness, disability,
23   or injury suffered by the line worker happened during an unauthorized absence or
24   abandonment of office, or if the same was caused by any of the following:

25          (a) Willful intention to injure or kill oneself;
26          (b) Gross and habitual negligence;
27          (c)   Failure to observe safety protocols or exercise reasonable care for one's
28                own safety; and
29          (d) Commission of a crime by the line worker.
