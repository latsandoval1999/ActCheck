                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act No. 7277 (1992), otherwise known as the Magna Carta for Persons
with Disability, recognizes the rights of Persons with Disabilities (“PWDs”) and
guarantees their access to programs, services, and privileges that promote their welfare,
inclusion, and participation in society. These benefits are largely accessed through the
PWD Identification Card (“IDC”) issued by local government units. However, under
existing practice, PWD-IDCs are subject to periodic renewal regardless of whether the
disability is temporary or permanent. Currently, the PWD-IDC issued to the PWD shall
be valid for five (5) years.1 For persons with permanent disabilities, this requirement often
results in unnecessary expenses, repeated medical certification, and additional
administrative procedures despite the lifelong and irreversible nature of their condition.
The burden is particularly felt by individuals with mobility limitations, those residing in
remote areas, and indigent PWDs who rely on uninterrupted access to statutory benefits
and assistance programs.

       This bill seeks to establish a clear statutory definition of “permanent disability”
and provide lifetime validity for PWD-IDCs issued to qualified individuals. The measure
requires that permanent disability be certified by the municipal or city health office and
verified by the Local Social Welfare and Development Office (LSWDO) to ensure the
integrity of the system. By eliminating repetitive renewal requirements for conditions
that are medically determined to be irreversible, the bill reduces bureaucratic barriers
while preserving appropriate safeguards against misuse.

       The measure likewise promotes more efficient public service delivery by allowing
local government units to focus their resources on identifying, registering, and assisting
unserved and underserved members of the PWD sector rather than processing repetitive
renewals. As of 2025, the National Council for Disability Affairs (NCDA) reported more
than 2.5 million Filipinos who are registered as PWDs nationwide,2 a figure that remains

AN ACT FURTHER AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277, OTHERWISE KNOWN
     AS THE “MAGNA CARTA FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITY,” AS
     AMENDED, TO PROVIDE FOR THE LIFETIME VALIDITY OF
     IDENTIFICATION CARDS ISSUED TO PERSONS WITH PERMANENT
     DISABILITY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Section 4 of the Republic Act No. 7277, as amended, otherwise known
as the “Magna Carta for Persons with Disability is hereby amended to read as follows:


       “SECTION 4. Definition of Terms: For purposes of this Act, these terms are
       defined as follows:
                                          xxx


       (c) Disability shall mean (1) a physical or mental impairment that
       substantially limits one or more psychological, physiological, or anatomical
       functions of an individual or activities of such individual; (2) a record of
       such an impairment; or (3) being regarded as having such an impairment;


       (c-1) Permanent disability refers to a physical, mental, intellectual, or
       sensory impairment that (1) substantially limits one or more major bodily
       functions or activities of an individual; and (2) is medically determined to
       be irreversible, non-transitory, or otherwise expected to last for the lifetime
       of the individual, as certified by a licensed physician of the municipal or
       city health office and verified by the Local Social Welfare and Development
       Office (LSWDO). x x x”

      SEC. 2. Section 32 of the same Act, as amended, is hereby amended to read as
follows:


      “SEC. 32. Persons with disability shall be entitled to the following:


                                        xxx


      The abovementioned privileges are available only to persons with disability
      who are Filipino citizens upon submission of any of the following as proof
      of his/her entitlement thereto:


      (I) An identification card issued by the city or municipal mayor or the
      barangay captain of the place where the persons with disability resides; OR


      (II) The passport of the persons with disability concerned; of


      [(III) Transportation discount fare Identification Card (ID) issued by the
      Nationat Council for the Welfare of Disabled Persons (NCWDP).]


      PROVIDED, THAT IDENTIFICATION CARDS ISSUED TO PERSONS
      WITH PERMANENT DISABILITY SHALL BE FREE OF CHARGE AND
      SHALL BE VALID DURING THE LIFETIME OF THE CARDHOLDER.


      PROVIDED, FURTHER, THAT SUCH PERMANENT DISABILITY IS
      CERTIFIED BY THE MUNICIPAL OR CITY HEALTH OFFICE AND
      VERIFIED BY THE LOCAL SOCIAL WELFARE DEVELOPMENT
      OFFICE (LSWDO).


      PROVIDED, FURTHERMORE, THAT IN CASE OF LOSS OR
      DAMAGE,        THE      CARDHOLDER           MAY       RENEW       HIS/HER
      IDENTIFICATION CARD.”
