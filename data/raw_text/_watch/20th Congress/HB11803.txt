EXPLANATORY NOTE

Finding employment remains a challenge for Filipinos living in geographically isolated
and disadvantaged areas (GIDA). Limited transportation, unreliable internet connectivity, and the
distance from major employment centers often make it difficult for jobseekers to pursue
opportunities outside their communities. While online job platforms have made vacancies easier
to find, attending interviews and completing recruitment assessments can still require considerable
travel and expenses, particularly for young and first-time jobseekers.

In Barangay Sagrada, Palawan, Joshua Alarcon, a young student featured in a 2026 account
by Dualtech Training Center, reportedly walked nearly 1.5 kilometers each day just to find a
mobile signal and check updates on his technical training application. Although his experience
involved access to training, it reflects the difficulties that residents of remote communities face in
pursuing opportunities that others can access more easily.!

Republic Act No. 8759, or the Public Employment Service Office Act of 1999, as amended
by Republic Act No. 10691, established Public Employment Service Offices (PESOs) to provide
employment information, job matching, referrals, and other employment assistance services.
While the law authorizes the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) to develop additional
employment assistance programs, it does not expressly recognize remote recruitment facilitation
for jobseekers in GIDA communities as a PESO service or require specific service standards for
its implementation.

This bill seeks to address that gap by expressly including remote recruitment facilitation
among the services provided through the PESO system and requiring DOLE to establish minimum
service standards for its implementation. Where practicable, PESOs shall facilitate the
participation of jobseekers in remote preliminary interviews, screening activities, and recruitment
assessments, in coordination with willing employers and using available facilities and resources.

The measure does not require employers to conduct recruitment activities remotely or
establish a new government program or office. Rather, it seeks to make existing public
employment services more accessible to communities where distance and connectivity remain
significant barriers to employment. A qualified applicant should not lose the chance to pursue a
job simply because of the distance between home and the nearest employment center.

AN ACT
STRENGTHENING EMPLOYMENT ACCESS FOR JOBSEEKERS IN
GEOGRAPHICALLY ISOLATED AND DISADVANTAGED AREAS THROUGH
REMOTE RECRUITMENT FACILITATION, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8759, AS AMENDED, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "PUBLIC
EMPLOYMENT SERVICE OFFICE ACT OF 1999"

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “G/DA Remote Recruitment
Access Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote full employment, equality of employment opportunities, and equitable access to decent
and productive work.

Toward this end, the State shall strengthen existing public employment services to address
geographic and logistical barriers that limit the ability of jobseekers residing in geographically
isolated and disadvantaged areas to participate in recruitment and employment selection activities.

SECTION 3. Amendment to Section 6 of Republic Act No. 8759, as Amended. — Section
6 of Republic Act No. 8759, as amended by Republic Act No. 10691, is hereby amended by adding
a new paragraph at the end thereof, to read as follows:

"Public Employment Service Offices (PESOs) shall, where practicable and using available
facilities and resources, facilitate the participation of jobseekers residing in geographically isolated
and disadvantaged areas (GIDA) in remote preliminary job interviews, screening activities, and
recruitment assessments, in coordination with willing employers.

"Such assistance may include scheduling, access to available communication facilities, and
referral follow-up, with priority given to young, first-time, and low-income jobseekers.
"The Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) shall establish minimum service standards
for remote recruitment facilitation under the PESO system, taking into account local connectivity
conditions, available resources, and the needs of GIDA communities."

SECTION 4. Employer Participation and Coordination. — The DOLE, through existing
employment facilitation mechanisms, shall encourage employers participating in PESO referrals
and government-supported recruitment activities to provide appropriate remote arrangements for
preliminary interviews and assessments involving qualified jobseekers from GIDA communities.
Employer participation shall be voluntary. Nothing in this Act shall restrict the authority of
employers to prescribe lawful job qualifications and selection procedures or to require in-person
assessments where reasonably necessary.

SECTION 5. Accessibility and Alternative Arrangements. — In implementing remote
recruitment facilitation, PESOs shall consider limitations in internet connectivity, transportation
access, and available communication facilities in GIDA communities.

Where video-based recruitment activities are not feasible, PESOs may, with the agreement
of participating employers, facilitate alternative arrangements such as telephone interviews,
electronic submission of preliminary requirements, or access to existing government or partner
facilities.

No jobseeker shall be denied access to other available PESO employment services solely
because remote recruitment arrangements cannot be provided.

SECTION 6. Implementation Through Existing Resources. — This Act shall be
implemented through the existing functions, personnel, facilities, and employment facilitation
mechanisms of the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Public Employment Service
Offices (PESOs), and concerned local government units.

No separate recruitment office, additional plantilla positions, or parallel employment
registry shall be established solely for the implementation of this Act.

SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DOLE, in coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local
Government, concerned local government units, and other appropriate government agencies, shall
promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for its effective implementation.

The implementing rules and regulations shall prescribe the minimum service standards,
appropriate GIDA identification criteria, employer coordination procedures, applicant referral
mechanisms, and monitoring arrangements contemplated under this Act.

SECTION 8. Appropriations. — The implementation of this Act shall be charged against
the existing appropriations of the Department of Labor and Employment and concerned local
government units, subject to the availability of funds and applicable budgeting, accounting, and
auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

SECTION 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force
and effect.

SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,

amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
