                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

         The Department of Science and Technology (DOST) has long been at the forefront of
fostering technological innovation and scientific advancement in the Philippines. A crucial part
of DOST’s strategy in fulfilling its mandate is through the implementation of various programs
aimed at empowering local industries, improving the lives of Filipino communities, and
contributing to national progress. These programs, such as the Small Enterprise Technology
Upgrading Program (SET-UP), Community Empowerment through Science and Technology
(CEST), OneLab, and Grants-in-Aid for Research and Innovation on Development (GRIND),
play a pivotal role in addressing the needs of local businesses, communities, and scientific
institutions.
         To ensure the effective and seamless implementation of these programs and meet the
increasing demands for science and technology interventions, it is crucial to strengthen the
capacities of the DOST Regional Offices that would enhance collaboration with local
government units (LGUs), stakeholders, and regional industries, contributing to the overall
advancement of science and technology initiatives across the country. This will allow DOST
to expand its reach, improve program delivery, and provide more responsive, localized
services to various sectors across the country.
         Strengthening the DOST Regional Offices to better implement and manage these key
programs is essential in addressing the evolving needs of the local communities, industries,
and research sectors. With enhanced capabilities at the regional level, DOST will be able to
ensure more efficient program delivery, facilitate the adoption of science and technology
innovations, and contribute to the overall progress of the country.
         Finally, the proposed strengthening of the regional offices will empower DOST to more
effectively carry out its mandate of fostering inclusive and sustainable growth through science,
technology, and innovation across the Philippines.
         In view of the foregoing, immediate approval of this measure is earnestly requested.

                              AN ACT
FURTHER STRENGTHENING THE REGIONAL OFFICES OF THE DEPARTMENT OF
SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Title. - This Act shall be known as the “DOST Regional Office Act”.

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared that the policy of the State to take
account regional and sectoral needs and conditions to strengthen Local Government Units
(LGUs) therein and to accelerate the economic and social growth and development in every
part of the country. The State also recognizes the role of science, technology and innovation
(STI) for national benefit as such there is a need to modernize the existing Regional Offices
of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST) in the generation, transfer, promotion
and regulation of STI-based knowledge and application; and to encourage the widest
participation of all Filipinos in these endeavors.

SEC. 3. Objectives. The following are the objectives of this Act:

   (a) To strengthen the operational capacity of the DOST Regional Offices.
   (b) To enhance the implementation of science and technology programs at the regional
       level.
   (c) To integrate the OneLab Program into regional operations to enhance local access to
       laboratory services.
   (d) To improve the delivery of the Community Empowerment Through Science and
       Technology (CEST) Program to empower local communities through scientific
       innovation.
   (e) To establish a unified network that fosters collaboration between local governments,
       businesses, academic institutions, and DOST.

SEC. 4. Functions of the Regional Office. – The Regional Offices shall have the following
primary functions being the focal points for the planning and implementation of Science,
Technology and Innovations (STI) programs and projects in the regions. Specifically, each
regional office shall assume the following functions:
    a) Identify the needs and opportunities in STI in the region;
    b) Formulate and implement a regional STI plan consistent with and supportive of
       regional development thrusts;
    c) Provide STI interventions to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME’s) in the
       regions to improve their productivity and competitiveness in both local and international
       markets through the Small Enterprises Technology Upgrading Program (SET-UP), the
       banner program of DOST for technology transfer and commercialization, CEST,
       GRIND, iHub and OneLab.

SEC. 5. Organizational Restructure. - The Department of Science and Technology Regional
Offices shall be restructured to enhance operational effectiveness and improve coordination
with local government units (LGUs), academic institutions, and businesses.
        The Regional Office shall be headed by a Regional Director, who shall be assisted by
at least two (2) Assistant Regional Directors. The designated Chief Science Research
Specialist shall be reclassified as Director III to serve as ARD of the RO.
        The DOST shall likewise determine the organization structure and staffing pattern of
the Regional Offices including its Provincial Science and Technology Offices under RA 11914,
in accordance with civil service laws, rules and regulations, subject to the review and approval
of the DBM. In line with this, the existing position of Chief Science Research Specialist which
is currently held by the head of the PSTOs shall be reclassified as Director III.

SEC. 6. Functions and Responsibilities of the Assistant Regional Directors. – The
Assistant Regional Director/s shall have the following functions and responsibilities:
   1. Program Management and Implementation. –
       a) Assist the Regional Director in overseeing the implementation of science and
           technology programs in the region.
       b) Ensure the timely and efficient delivery of DOST services, including research and
           development (R&D), technology transfer, innovation support, and training
           programs.
   2. Coordination with Local Government. –
       a) Facilitate close coordination with LGUs to ensure that DOST programs and
           projects are aligned with local development priorities.
       b) Provide technical assistance to LGUs in implementing regional S&T policies and
           programs.
   3. Resource Mobilization and Partnership Building. –
       a) Collaborate with stakeholders, including the private sector, academic institutions,
           and non-governmental organizations, to secure additional resources for regional
           S&T projects.
       b) Forge partnerships to promote science and technology initiatives and create
           opportunities for innovation-driven regional development.
   4. Monitoring and Evaluation. –
       a) Assist in the monitoring, evaluation, and assessment of the effectiveness and
           impact of DOST programs and projects in the region.
       b) Provide recommendations for improving program performance and ensuring that
           targets are met.
   5. Capacity Building and Technical Assistance. –
       a) Oversee the training and capacity-building activities for local stakeholders,
           including government officials, community leaders, and entrepreneurs, to
           strengthen regional innovation ecosystems.
       b) Provide technical assistance to local industries and communities to ensure
           effective adoption of technologies and innovation.

SEC. 7. Qualifications of Assistant Regional Director/s. – The Assistant Regional
Director/s shall be appointed by the Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology,
based on the following minimum qualifications:
        a) A bachelor’s degree in science, engineering, technology management, or a related
            field; a master’s degree in a relevant area is preferred;
        b) At least five (5) years of experience in program management, preferably in the
            science and technology sector, with significant exposure to regional development
            initiatives;
        c) Strong leadership, management, communication, and coordination skills;
            demonstrated ability to work with diverse stakeholders at the local and national
            levels.

SEC. 8. Establishment of OneLab Services in Regional Offices. - The DOST Regional
Offices (ROs) shall establish or upgrade the existing regional OneLab centers to provide Micro
Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), local government units, and the general public with
easier access to laboratory services, including testing, calibration, and product certification.
        The DOST ROs shall likewise partner with local laboratories, universities, and other
entities to extend laboratory services and testing capabilities to underserved areas. Services
provided through OneLab shall be priced reasonably to ensure accessibility to all sectors,
especially MSMEs, start-ups, and local entrepreneurs.

SEC. 9. Strengthening the Community Empowerment Through Science and Technology
(CEST) Program. The CEST Program shall be expanded to include additional communities
across all regions, focusing on areas in need of scientific assistance to address local
challenges.
        The CEST Program shall focus on providing scientific solutions but not limited to the
following sectors: a) Agriculture; b) Health and nutrition; c) Education; d) Livelihood and
entrepreneurship; and e) Environmental sustainability
        The program shall likewise facilitate the transfer of appropriate technologies to
communities, enhancing productivity and livelihoods.
        Each DOST Regional Office shall engage local communities to assess needs, provide
training, and deliver science-based solutions.
        In line with this, an efficient monitoring and evaluation system will be developed to
measure the effectiveness of the CEST Program and ensure its sustainability.

SEC. 10. Establishment of the Grassroots Innovation for Inclusive Development
Program (GRIND). - The Department of Science and Technology (DOST) shall establish and
implement, through the Regional Offices, the Grassroots Innovation for Inclusive Development
(GRIND) Program to support, promote, and scale grassroots innovations. The program shall
be inclusive and accessible to all Filipino citizens, with particular focus on individuals and
communities in marginalized, rural, or underserved areas. It shall address the following key
areas:
   a) Technology and Product Development. – Providing support for the development of
       locally relevant and culturally appropriate innovations.
   b) Market Access. – Helping grassroots innovators access markets, networks, and
       commercial opportunities.
   c) Capacity Building. – Providing training, mentorship, and resources to enable
       innovators to refine their products, services, and business models.
   d) Sustainability and Scalability. – Supporting efforts to scale successful innovations
       and integrate them into local economies in a sustainable manner.

SEC. 11. Creation of the Regional Science and Technology Innovation Hub.
Each DOST Regional Office shall create a Regional Science and Technology Innovation Hub,
which will serve as the focal point for the following activities:

   a) Research and Development – Support research and development initiatives to
      address regional issues, such as agricultural productivity, public health, environmental
      protection, and sustainable energy.
   b) Capacity Building – Offer programs that equip local communities, students, and

   c) Partnerships – Foster partnerships between local government units, businesses, and

SEC. 12. Role of Local Government Units. – Local government units (LGUs) shall play an
active role in the implementation of the CEST Program within their respective areas.
        In cooperation with the DOST, the LGUs shall develop and implement a Community
Science and Technology Development Plan (CSTDP), which shall be integrated into their local
development plans.
        Further, this Act empowers LGUs to:

        (a)    Establish a City/Municipality Science and Technology Office (CMSTO)
responsible for coordinating and implementing science and technology-related programs,
projects, and activities within their respective jurisdictions to enhance the local implementation
of science and technology initiatives.

         (b)    Collaborate with the DOST to identify the needs of their communities and
prioritize technology interventions.

        (c)     Provide logistical support, facilitate the integration of S&T solutions into local
policies, and allocate funds to complement the national government’s efforts.

       (d)      Support local S&T innovations by facilitating market access, distribution, and
sustainability.


SEC. 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). The DOST through the Office of the
Undersecretary for Regional Operations in consultation with relevant stakeholders shall
promulgate the implementing rules and regulations of this Act within ninety (90) days after the
effectivity thereof.

SEC. 14. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall remain in full force and
effect.

SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, proclamations, rules and
regulations, and issuances, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

SEC. 16. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in one (1) newspaper of general circulation.
