                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

Many DOH-retained hospitals are severely overcrowded. They often operate far beyond their
authorized bed capacity, forcing patients to stay in hallways, corridors, and makeshift areas.
This situation creates heavy strain on doctors, nurses, and other health workers and
compromises the quality of patient care.

At present, any increase in bed capacity or upgrade in service capability of a DOH hospital
requires the passage of a separate law by Congress. This legislative process is slow and lengthy,
often taking years before approval. As a result, hospitals cannot respond quickly to growing
patient demands, population changes, or health emergencies.

This bill solves the problem by giving the DOH the authority to set and approve bed capacities
and service capabilities through administrative action. The Department can now make timely
decisions based on actual needs, disease trends, and available resources, while still submitting
annual reports to Congress.

By removing this unnecessary legislative bottleneck, the bill will reduce overcrowding,
improve access to hospital care, and allow DOH hospitals to serve more Filipinos more
efficiently.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
AUTHORIZING THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH (DOH) TO SET AND APPROVE THE
 BED CAPACITY AND SERVIСЕ САРABILITY OF ALL DOH HOSPITALS SECTION

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "DOH Hospital Bed Capacity and Service
Capability Rationalization Act".

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall protect and promote the right to health of the people
and achieve universal healthcare by strengthening the public healthcare delivery system and ensuring
timely and equitable access to quality, essential health and social services for all. To this end, the State
shall empower the Department of Health (DOH) to efficiently and responsively determine and
rationalize the authorized bed capacities and service capabilities of hospitals under its supervision and
control, consistent with national health facility planning standards and evolving population health
needs.

SEC. 3. Purpose, Scope, and Coverage. - This Act shall streamline and institutionalize the process
for determining, setting, adjusting, and approving changes in the authorized bed capacities and
corresponding service capabilities of all hospitals under the supervision and control of the DOH.

SEC. 4. Authority to Determine and Approve Authorized Bed Capacity and Service Capability. - The
DOH is hereby authorized to determine, set, adjust, and approve the authorized bed capacities and
corresponding service capabilities of hospitals under its supervision and control.

In the exercise of this authority, the DOH shall consider the Philippine Health Facility Development
Plan (PHFDP), hospital development and modernization plans, catchment population and projected
demographic trends, epidemiological data and burden of disease, health human resource requirements
and staffing standards, existing and planned healthcare provider networks, and other nationally
recognized planning and licensing standards.

When adjusting authorized bed capacity and service capability, the DOH shall undertake mandatory
consultations with the healthcare provider networks to ensure alignment with the province-wide and
city-wide health systems established under Republic Act No. 11223 or the Universal Health Care Act:
Provided, That such adjustments shall not result in the creation of a new hospital, the abolition of an
existing hospital, or the transfer of hospital ownership or management without an enabling law enacted
by Congress.
SEC. 5. Philippine Health Facility Development Plan. - The DOH shall continue to update, develop,
and implement the PHFDP to guide the development, modernization, expansion, and rational
distribution of government health facilities nationwide. The PHFDP shall be the primary basis for
determining funding requirements for adjustments in the bed capacity and service capability of DOH
hospitals and facilitating access to available capital outlay allocations and other government funding
mechanisms. Nothing in this Act shall be interpreted as giving preference to hospital investments over
investments in primary care, preventive services, and community-based health systems pursuant to
Republic Act No. 11223.

SEC. 6. Conversion, Upgrading, and Modernization of DOH Hospitals. - The DOH shall upgrade
and modernize the existing equipment and facilities of DOH hospitals, consistent with their authorized
bed capacities and service capabilities, to ensure responsiveness to the health needs of the population.

SEC. 7. Funding Requirements. - The funding requirements for the adjustment of authorized bed
capacities and service capabilities shall be included in the DOH program in accordance with the
PHFDP and the respective hospital modernization and development plans. Such funding shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act, subject to the regular budget process and
authorization of Congress.

SEC. 8. Annual Report to Congress and Public Disclosure. – The DOH shall submit to Congress,
through the Committee on Health of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Health and
Demography of the Senate, an annual report detailing the approved bed capacity adjustments;
corresponding changes in service capability; budgetary implications; status of implementation; and
alignment with the PHFDP and Universal Health Care objectives. The DOH shall publish on its official
website all approved adjustments in authorized bed capacity and service capability, including the basis
and justification for such approvals, to ensure transparency and public accountability.

SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). - The DOH shall formulate the IRR of this Act
within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

SEC. 10. Separability Clause. - If any provision or part of this Act is declared unconstitutional, the
remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, or parts
thereof that are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 12. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its publication
in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
