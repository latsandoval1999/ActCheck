EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to establish a Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA)
Extension Office in Tagbilaran City, Province of Bohol.

The maritime sector remains indispensable to the economic, social, and
transport connectivity of the Province of Bohol. As an island province
strategically located in Central Visayas, Bohol relies heavily on maritime
transportation for the movement of people, goods, services, and tourism
activities.

Created under Presidential Decree No. 474, the MARINA is the
government's principal maritime administration tasked to promote the integrated
development of the maritime industry and to ensure the safe, adequate, and
efficient transport of people, and goods throughout the archipelago. MARINA
likewise exercises regulatory and supervisory functions over domestic shipping,
ship registration and licensing, maritime safety, maritime manpower
development, and related maritime activities.

At present, Bohol remains under the jurisdiction of MARINA Regional
Office VII based in Cebu Province. While MARINA has established extension
and field operations in selected localities, the growing maritime demands of
Bohol underscore the need for a stronger, more permanent, and institutionally
equipped MARINA presence within the province itself.

Bohol has approximately 1.4 million residents and 654 kilometers of
coastline, 6,245 square kilometers of municipal waters, 304 coastal barangays,

and 72 islands and islets, making it one of the most maritime-dependent provinces

Also, the presence of major ports and maritime gateways throughout the
province highlights Bohol’s maritime significance. The Philippine Ports
Authority identifies the Port of Tagbilaran as the Baseport of Bohol,
complemented by major terminal ports in Jagna, Tubigon, Ubay, Talibon, Getafe,
and Loon.

The province is likewise home to MARINA-recognized maritime higher
education institutions and a MARINA-accredited maritime training institution
that develops Filipino seafarers and maritime professionals. This growing
maritime workforce requires accessible certification, licensing, documentary, and
regulatory services that are currently concentrated outside the province.

Existing records further demonstrate the substantial volume of maritime
regulatory work being undertaken in Bohol. As reported by MARINA Regional
Office VII, there are 951 registered motorbancas and recreational boats, 547
vessel registration transactions processed in 2026, 629 vessel imspections
conducted from January to August 2026, and 819 mobile maritime service
activities. These figures reflect the increasing demand for maritime services and
the necessity of expanding MARINA's operational presence in Bohol.

The establishment of a MARINA Extension Office in Tagbilaran City will
significantly improve the delivery of public services by bringing maritime
services closer to seafarers, vessel owners, other stakeholders, and coastal
communities. It will reduce travel costs and administrative burdens for clients
who would otherwise need to transact with offices outside the province, while
strengthening maritime safety oversight, vessel compliance monitoring, and
coordination with local government units, the Philippine Coast Guard, the
Philippine Ports Authority, and other government agencies.

Moreover, establishing the extension office in Tagbilaran City will provide
a strategic and accessible center for maritime governance and public service
delivery, supporting MARINA's mandate to foster a safe, secure, efficient, and
sustainable maritime industry and to bring government services closer to the
people.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly

sought. .

AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A MARITIME INDUSTRY AUTHORITY EXTENSION
OFFICE IN THE CITY OF TAGBILARAN, PROVINCE OF BOHOL,
AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. There shall be established an extension office of the Maritime
Industry Authority (MARINA) in the City of Tagbilaran, Province of Bohol, to
be known as the Tagbilaran City MARINA Extension Office, under the
supervision of the Maritime Industry Authority Regional Office VII.

SEC. 2. The Tagbilaran City MARINA Extension Office shall perform all
functions, powers, and duties delegated by the MARINA, which shall include,

the following:

===== [page 4] =====
(a) Registration, safety inspection, licensing, and documentation of
domestic vessels, watercraft, and motorboats operating within the
jurisdiction of the Province of Bohol;

(b) Issuance, verification, and renewal of seafarer identification and record
books (SIRB), certificates, credentials, and other related services for
local seafarers and maritime personnel;

(c) Monitoring, supervision, and enforcement of safety standards, rules,
and maritime safety regulations;

(d) Administration and assistance in maritime industry development
programs, eco-tourism transport support, and training in coordination
with local government units (LGUs);

(e) Provision of general frontline maritime assistance to ship-owners,
operators, seafarers, fisherfolk, and the public;

(f) Coordination with the Philippine Ports Authority, Philippine Coast
Guard, and other government agencies; and

(g) Such other functions as may be delegated by the MARINA pursuant to
law.

SEC. 3. The Administrator of MARINA, in consultation and coordination

with the Department of Transportation and the Department of Budget and

Management, shall determine the organizational structure and create the

necessary positions for the staffing pattern of the Tagbilaran City MARINA

SEC. 4. The Administrator of the MARINA shali immediately include in
the Agency’s programs the operationalization of the Tagbilaran City MARINA

SEC. 5. Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the

SEC. 6. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
