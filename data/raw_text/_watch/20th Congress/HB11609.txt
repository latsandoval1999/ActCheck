                            EXPLANATORY NOTE


Motorcycle use in the Philippines has increased because it provides affordable
and convenient transportation. At the same time, delivery riders have become
essential in bringing goods, food, and services to the public.

However, many motorcycle riders and delivery personnel have difficulty finding
proper parking. Some establishments do not have designated motorcycle
parking, while others charge fees even when delivery riders stay only briefly to
pick up or deliver orders.

This proposed law requires commercial, office, and public establishments to
provide designated parking spaces for motorcycles. It also grants delivery riders
a reasonable grace period during which they may wait or complete their
transactions without being charged parking fees or penalties.

This measure promotes fair access to parking facilities and recognizes the
important role of delivery riders in the Philippine economy.

 AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE MANDATORY ALLOCATION OF PARKING
  SPACES FOR MOTORCYCLES AND A GRACE PERIOD FOR DELIVERY
 SERVICE VEHICLES IN ALL PUBLIC AND PRIVATE ESTABLISHMENTS,
                  AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Motorcycle Parking
Accessibility and Delivery Service Regulation Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to protect the rights of motorists, promote the welfare of delivery service
personnel, and ensure orderly traffic management. The State recognizes that
providing accessible parking and reasonable accommodations for motorcycle
users is essential to urban mobility and the sustainability of the gig economy.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For the purposes of this Act:

   1. Establishment refers to any commercial, industrial, institutional, or
      government building or complex, including malls, offices, hospitals, and
      schools, that provides parking facilities to the public.

   2. Motorcycle refers to any two-wheeled motor vehicle as defined under
      existing traffic laws.

   3. Designated Parking Area refers to a specific, marked, and secure portion
      of a parking facility allocated exclusively for motorcycles.

   4. Delivery Service Vehicle refers to any motorcycle utilized by a registered
      delivery service provider for the purpose of picking up and delivering
      goods, food, or documents.

   5. Grace Period refers to the reasonable duration of time allowed for delivery
      service vehicles to wait at an establishment for pick-up or drop-off
      without incurring parking fees or penalties.
SECTION 4. Mandatory Motorcycle Parking. – All establishments providing
parking facilities shall allocate at least Twenty percent (20%) of their total
parking capacity, or a reasonable number of slots for the exclusive use of
motorcycles. These areas must be clearly marked, safely situated, and
accessible.

SECTION 5. Delivery Service Grace Period. – Establishments shall grant a
mandatory grace period of at least Thirty (30) minutes for delivery service
vehicles. During this period, the vehicle shall be allowed to park in designated
areas or specifically allocated drop-off zones without the imposition of any
parking fee.

SECTION 6. Penalties. – Any establishment found in violation of the provisions
of this Act shall be subject to the following penalties:

   1. First Offense: A fine of Ten thousand pesos (P10,000.00) and a written
      reprimand.

   2. Second Offense: A fine of Thirty thousand pesos (P30,000.00).

   3. Third and Subsequent Offenses: A fine of Fifty Thousand pesos
      (P50,000.00) and the suspension or revocation of the establishment's
      business permit/license to operate, as determined by the concerned LGU.

SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – The Department of
Transportation (DOTr), in consultation with the Department of Trade and
Industry (DTI) and the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG),
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to implement this Act
within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

SECTION 8. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other portions or provisions hereof
which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with or contrary to the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
