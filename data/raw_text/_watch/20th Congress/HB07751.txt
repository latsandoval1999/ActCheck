                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The “Atomic Energy Regulatory and Liability Act of 1968” or Republic
Act No. 5207 (RA No. 5207) was enacted more than five decades ago in support
of the advancement and utilization of nuclear energy for all peaceful purposes.
With the passage of time and the advancement of modern technology, its
realignment with the current principles and modern standards in the field of
nuclear liability is clearly required.

        The Philippines has been a signatory to various international conventions,
namely - the 1963 Vienna Convention on Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage
(the “1963 Vienna Convention”); the 1988 Joint Protocol Relating to the
Application of the Vienna Convention and the Paris Convention ( the “1988
Joint Protocol”); the 1994 Convention on Nuclear Safety (the “1994 CNS”); the
1997 Protocol to Amend the Vienna Convention on Civil Liability for Nuclear
Damage ( the “1997 Vienna Convention”); the 1997 Joint Convention on the
Safety of Spent Fuel Management and the safety of Radioactive Waste
Management (the “1997 Joint Convention”); and the Convention on
Supplementary Compensation for Nuclear Damage (the “1997 CSC”)1.
However, only the 1963 Vienna Convention has been ratified and the rest are
still pending up to this day.

       This bill seeks to update RA No. 5207 and align its provisions with the
modern principles and standards of nuclear liability. It includes key provisions
on strict liability of the nuclear power plant operators, liability caps, availability
of compensation for nuclear damage, establishment of a Claims Handling
Tribunal, and having exclusive jurisdiction for claim resolutions.

      Moreover, the bill aims for a three-tier funding system—operator funds,
CSC joint public funds, and the Philippine Government appropriated funds, to
ensure adequate compensation, to the extent necessary, for nuclear damage.

       Through a clear and comprehensive nuclear liability policy in place, the
Philippines can strengthen its commitment in complying with relevant
international conventions on nuclear safety and liability thereby leading to a
responsible advancement of the country’s nuclear energy program.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

4           Sec. 5. The Liable Operator. – The operator of a nuclear installation shall
5    be liable for nuclear damage upon proof that such damage has been caused by a
6    nuclear incident:
7         a) in such nuclear installation; or
8         b) involving nuclear material coming from or originating in such nuclear
9            installation, and occurring:
10             i.   before liability with regard to nuclear incidents involving the
11                  nuclear material has been assumed, pursuant to the express terms
12                  of a contract in writing, by the operator of another nuclear
13                  installation;
14            ii.   in the absence of such express terms, before the operator of another
15                  nuclear installation has taken charge of the nuclear material; or
16           iii.   where the nuclear material is intended to be used in a nuclear
17                  reactor with which a means of transport is equipped for use as a
18                  source of power, whether for propulsion thereof or for any other
19                  purpose, before the person duly authorized to operate such reactor
20                  has taken charge of the nuclear material; but
21           iv.    where the nuclear material has been sent to a person within the
22                  territory of a non-Contracting State, before it has been unloaded
23                  from the means of transport by which it has arrived in the territory
24                  of that non-Contracting State;
25

26        c) involving nuclear material sent to such nuclear installation, and
27           occurring:
28             i.   after liability with regard to nuclear incidents involving the nuclear
29                  material has been assumed by the operator pursuant to the express
30                  terms of a contract in writing, from the operator of another nuclear
31                  installation;
32            ii.   in the absence of such express terms, after the operator has taken
33                  charge of the nuclear material; or

3                   is equipped for use as a source of power, whether for propulsion

5            iv.    where the nuclear material has, with the written consent of the

16          Sec. 6. Carrier as Operator. – Upon the request of a carrier of nuclear
17   material or a person handling radioactive waste and with the consent of the

26          Sec. 7. Liability of More than One Operator. – Where nuclear damage
27   engages the liability of more than one operator, the operators involved shall, in
28   so far as the damage attributable to each operator is not reasonably separable, be
29   jointly and severally liable. The amount of public funds made available per
30   incident shall be limited to the difference, if any, between the amounts hereby
31   established and the amount established pursuant to Section 16 hereof.

36   nuclear damage which engages the liability of more than one operator, the total
37   liability shall not exceed the highest amount applicable with respect to any one
1    of them pursuant to Section 16 hereof.

3          In neither of the cases referred to in this Section shall the liability of any
4    one operator exceed the amount applicable with respect to such operator pursuant
5    to Section 16 hereof.

7           Sec. 8. Liability of One Operator for Several Nuclear Installations. –
8    Subject to the provisions of Section 7, where several nuclear installations of one

10   liable in respect of each nuclear installation involved up to the amount applicable
11   with respect to such operator pursuant to Section 16 hereof. The amount of public
12   funds made available per incident shall be limited to the difference, if any,
13   between the amounts hereby established and the amount established pursuant to
14   Section 16.
15
16         Sec. 9. Absolute and Exclusive Liability of Operator. – The liability of the
17   operator for nuclear damage shall be absolute.
18
19         Except as otherwise provided for in this Act, no person other than the
20   operator shall be liable for nuclear damage.
21
22         Sec. 10. No Relief of Operator Liability. – The operator shall be liable for
23   nuclear damage caused by a nuclear incident caused directly due to a grave
24   natural disaster of an exceptional character.
25
26         Sec. 11. Exclusions from Operator Liability. – No liability shall attach to
27   an operator for nuclear damage caused by a nuclear incident directly due to an act
28   of armed conflict, hostilities, civil war, or insurrection.
29
30         Sec. 12. Operator Relief from Compensation Obligation. – The competent
31   court may relieve the operator wholly or partly from the obligation to pay
32   compensation for nuclear damage suffered by a person if the operator proves the
33   nuclear damage resulted wholly or partly from the gross negligence of that person
34   or from an act or omission of that person done with intent to cause damage.
35
36        Sec. 13. Damage Deemed Nuclear Damage. – Whenever both nuclear
37   damage and damage other than nuclear damage have been caused by a nuclear
1    incident or jointly by a nuclear incident and one or more other occurrences, such
2    other damage shall, to the extent that it is not reasonably separable from the
3    nuclear damage, be deemed, for the purposes of this Act, to be nuclear damage
4    caused by that nuclear incident. Where, however, damage is caused jointly by a
5    nuclear incident covered by the provisions of this Act and by an emission of
6    ionizing radiation not covered by it, nothing in this Act shall limit or otherwise
7    affect the liability, either as regards any person suffering nuclear damage or by
8    way of recourse or contribution, of any person who may be held liable in
9    connection with that emission of ionizing radiation.
10
11         Sec. 14. Operator Not Liable. – The operator shall not be liable under this
12   Act for nuclear damage:
13        a) to the nuclear installation itself and any other nuclear installation,
14           including a nuclear installation under construction, on the site where that
15           installation is located;
16        b) to any property on that same site which is used or to be used in
17           connection with any such installation.
18
19         The operator shall incur no liability for damage caused by a nuclear
20   incident outside the provisions of this Act.
21
22         Sec. 15. Exercise of Right to Compensation. – Subject to the provisions of
23   Sections 32, 33, 35 and 36, any person may bring a claim for compensation for
24   nuclear damage.
25
26         The right to compensation for nuclear damage may be exercised only
27   against the operator liable or person furnishing financial security pursuant to this
28   Act.

33         Sec. 16. Operator Liability Limit. – The liability of the operator for any one
34   nuclear incident is limited to an amount in Philippine pesos equivalent to Three
35   hundred million (300,000,000) SDRs.
36
37         Notwithstanding paragraph 1 of this Section, the Authority, having regard
1    to the nature of the nuclear installation or the nuclear substances involved and to
2    the likely consequences of an incident originating therefrom, may establish a
3    lower limit of liability of the operator: Provided, That:
4         a) in no event shall any amount so established be less than an amount in
5            Philippine pesos equivalent to Five million (5,000,000) SDRs; and
6         b) in such a case Section 19 applies.
7
8          The amounts established by this Section shall apply wherever the nuclear
9    incident occurs.
10
11         The amounts established by this Section are exclusive of any interest or
12   costs which may be awarded by the competent court.
13
14         Sec. 17. Minimum Financial Security. – The operator shall be required to
15   maintain insurance or other financial security covering its liability for nuclear
16   damage in the amount established by Section 16.
17
18         Nothing in this Act shall be construed to require the National Government
19   or any government agency or instrumentality operating a nuclear installation to
20   maintain insurance or other financial security to cover their liability as operators.
21
22          Sec. 18. Type and Terms of Financial Security. – The Authority shall
23   prescribe by regulation or order the type and terms of the required financial
24   security: Provided, That in so prescribing, the Authority shall be guided by the
25   objectives of assuring adequate and effective compensation to victims of a
26   nuclear incident without imposing unreasonable burden on the operator
27
28         Sec. 19. Government Satisfaction of Claims. – The Government shall
29   ensure the payment of claims for compensation for nuclear damage which have
30   been established against the operator:
31        a) to the extent that the yield of financial security is inadequate to satisfy
32           such claims;
33        b) if the Authority has established a lower limit of the operator’s liability
34           in accordance with paragraph 2 of Section 16; and
35        c) if the Government or any Government agency or instrumentality is the
1             operator, in all cases, up to the limit provided for in paragraph 1 of
2             Section 16.
3
4           Sec. 20. Equitable Distribution. – Compensation for nuclear damage under
5    Section 16, including any Government satisfaction of claims under Section 19,
6    shall be distributed equitably without discrimination on the basis of nationality,
7    domicile, or residence: Provided, That the Authority may limit such funds
8    available to compensate nuclear damage occurring in a non-Contracting State that
9    has a nuclear installation on its territory at the time of the nuclear incident and
10   does not afford equivalent reciprocal benefits: Provided further, That such limit
11   is an amount not lower than the Philippine pesos equivalent to One hundred fifty
12   million (150,000,000) million SDRs.
13
14         Sec. 21. Exclusive Use of Funds. – The funds provided by insurance, by
15   other financial security pursuant to Section 17 and 18, or by the Government

32        Sec. 28. Period of Extinction. – Rights of compensation for all nuclear
33   damage under this Act shall be extinguished if an action is not brought within:
34        a) ten (10) years from the date of the nuclear incident; and
35        b) three (3) years from the date on which the person suffering nuclear
36           damage had knowledge or ought reasonably to have had knowledge of
1              the damage and of the operator liable for the damage, provided that the
2              period established in paragraph 1(a) of this section is not exceeded.
3
4           Notwithstanding paragraph 1(a) of this Section, the Authority may by
5    regulation or order determine that rights of compensation with respect to loss of
6    life and personal injury only shall be extinguished if an action is not brought
7    within thirty (30) years from the date of the nuclear incident. Provided, That the
8    period established in paragraph 1(b) of this Section is not exceeded.
9
10         Where nuclear damage is caused by a nuclear incident involving nuclear
11   material which at the time of the nuclear incident was stolen, lost, jettisoned, or
12   abandoned, the period established pursuant to paragraph 1 shall be computed
13   from the date of that nuclear incident, but the period shall in no case exceed a
14   period of twenty (20) years from the date of the theft, loss, jettison, or
15   abandonment.
16
17          Any person who claims to have suffered nuclear damage and who has
18   brought an action for compensation within the period established in paragraph 1
19   of this Section may amend the claim to take account of any aggravation of the
20   damage, even after the expiry of such period. Provided, That final judgment has
21   not been entered.

29        a) if this is expressly provided for by a contract in writing; or

11         Sec. 30. Additional Provisions. – The provisions of the Vienna Convention
12   and the Convention on Supplementary Compensation shall apply to cases not
13   provided for in this Act or in regulations or orders issued by the Authority.

15          Sec. 31. Applicable Law. – The nature, form, extent, and equitable
16   distribution of compensation for nuclear damage caused by a nuclear incident in
17   the territory of the Republic of the Philippines or where the liable operator is

20   extent such Convention is in force for the Republic of the Philippines) and the
21   applicable law of the Republic of the Philippines.
22
23         Sec. 32. Dose Determination by the Authority. – For the purposes of this
24   Act, the Authority shall determine if a release of radiation is in excess of the
25   authorized radiation dose limits in the Republic of the Philippines.
26
27          Sec. 33. Claims Handling Panel. – Within fifteen (15) days of a
28   determination by the Authority under Section 32 that a release of radiation is in
29   excess of the authorized radiation dose limits in the Republic of the Philippines,
30   a claims handling panel, referred to as the Nuclear Claims Panel, shall be
31   established by the Department of Justice and shall have exclusive jurisdiction

34          The Nuclear Claims Panel shall be comprised of three (3) retired Regional
35   Trial Court judges or experts in civil law, in good standing.

37         Sec. 34. Investigation for Nuclear Claims. – With respect to claims brought
1    to the Nuclear Claims Panel, the Authority shall:
2          (a) investigate the purported nuclear incident;
3          (b) form an opinion on the radiation dose associated with the claims and
4              the potential for such dose limit to cause nuclear damage; and
5          (c) supply evidence-based information on radiation doses that may
6              definitively cause human harm, and such findings and opinion shall be
7              provided to and considered by the Nuclear Claims Panel in its
8              determinations.
9
10          Sec. 35. Compensable Claims. – For the purposes of the Nuclear Claims
11   Panel understanding relative risk and harm, such Panel shall obtain expert
12   opinions on the relative harm caused by the relevant radiation dose, compared to
13   other common hazards such as smoking, airborne particulate pollution, common
14   low-level chemical exposure, and exposure to elevated natural background
15   radiation occurring in notable locations in the world, including in Ramsar in Iran,
16   Kerala State in India, and Guarapari Beach in Brazil.
17
18          The Nuclear Claims Panel may refuse to hear any claim that it considers to
19   be frivolous or vexatious.
20
21        The Nuclear Claims Panel shall be entitled to establish priorities for
22   compensating classes of nuclear damage.
23
24         The Nuclear Claims Panel shall determine that a claim is eligible for
25   compensation under this Act only if the radiation dose associated with the claim
26   is in excess of the authorized radiation dose limits in the Republic of the
27   Philippines established in accordance with the Appendix to this Act.
28
29          Sec. 36. Prohibition to Double Compensation. – A claimant shall not be
30   entitled to compensation under this Act if such claimant has already received or
31   will receive adequate compensation pursuant to public health insurance, social
32   insurance, social security, workers’ compensation or occupational disease
33   compensation systems.
34
35          The Nuclear Claims Panel shall seek the assistance of the Authority on any
36   other technical matters where appropriate.

2           Sec. 37. Review of Judgement. – Appeals from the Nuclear Claims Panel
3    shall be heard by the Court of Appeals.
4
5          Sec. 38. Recognition of Foreign Judgments. – A judgment which is
6    recognized under the Vienna Convention or the Convention on Supplementary
7    Compensation shall be enforceable.
8
9           Sec 39. Authority. – The Authority, as defined in Section 3(a) of this Act,
10   shall be responsible for the issuance of regulations necessary to implement the
11   Act and other matters as provided for in the Act.
12
13         Sec. 40. Implementing Regulations. – The Authority shall issue the
14   regulations necessary to implement this Act within one hundred and eighty (180)
15   days from the effective date of this Act.
16
17         Sec. 41. Separability. – If a provision of this Act is declared
18   unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions shall not be affected and shall
19   remain in full force and effect.
20
21         Sec. 42. Repeal. – All Acts, executive orders, administrative orders,
22   proclamations, rules and regulations inconsistent with any provisions of this Act,
23   including Sections 37 to 63 of Part VII, Liability for Nuclear Damage, under
24   Republic Act No. 5207, are repealed or modified accordingly.
25
26         Sec. 43. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
27   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
