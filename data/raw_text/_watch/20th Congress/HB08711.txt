                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The national government operates thousands of offices, schools, hospitals,
and public facilities across the country, all of which require substantial and
continuous electricity to deliver essential services to the Filipino people. As
electricity and fuel prices remain volatile and subject to global market
fluctuations, the cost of powering these facilities continues to place an
increasing burden on public funds.

       In recent years, rising electricity costs –largely influenced by the fluctuating
prices of fossil fuels, have significantly affected government operating expenses.
These recurring expenditures reduce the resources that could otherwise be directed
toward vital public programs and services.

       The Philippines, however, is endowed with abundant solar energy throughout
the year. Harnessing this renewable resource provides the government with a
practical and sustainable opportunity to reduce electricity consumption from
conventional sources, and stabilize long-term energy costs.

        This measure mandates the integration and use of solar energy systems
in government infrastructure, buildings, and offices nationwide. By gradually
shifting government facilities toward solar power, the State can significantly reduce
electricity expenditures and ensure more efficient use of public funds. Through this
initiative, government facilities can also serve as leading examples in the adoption
of renewable energy technologies while strengthening the country’s efforts toward a
more sustainable and resilient energy future.

         In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT MANDATING THE USE OF SOLAR ENERGY IN ALL
GOVERNMENT INFRASTRUCTURE, BUILDINGS, AND OFFICES IN
THE PHILIPPINES AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Solar Government
Act”.

        Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the urgent need to
transition toward renewable and clean energy sources in order to ensure energy
security, reduce dependence on fossil fuels, lower electricity costs, and mitigate the
adverse effects of climate change.

Towards this end, the State shall promote the transition to clean energy by mandating
the adoption and utilization of solar energy systems in government infrastructure,
buildings, and offices.

   Section 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all government infrastructure,
buildings, and offices in the Philippines, including:

   1. Public Schools, hospitals, and libraries;
   2. Sports facilities, parks, and museums;
   3. Airports, docks, and port areas; and
   4. All other properties owned and utilized by the National Government, and
      Government-Owned and Controlled Corporations (GOCC).
Provided, That properties owned by the government that are scheduled for
disposition or sale shall be excluded from this Act.

       Section 4. Solar Government Plan. – Within one (1) year from the effectivity
of this Act, the Department of Energy (DOE), in coordination with the Department
of Budget and Management (DBM) and the Department of Public Works and
Highways (DPWH), shall formulate and adopt a Solar Government Plan for the
phased installation and integration of solar energy systems in government
infrastructure, buildings, and offices covered by this Act.

The Solar Government Plan shall include, but not be limited to:

   a. A comprehensive inventory of government infrastructure, buildings, and
      offices covered by this Act;

   b. Technical and feasibility assessments to determine the suitability of solar
      energy systems in such facilities;

   c. A phased implementation schedule prioritizing high-energy-consuming
      government facilities;

   d. Technical standards and guidelines for the installation, operation, and
      maintenance of solar energy systems; and

   e. Estimated funding requirements and recommended financing mechanisms for
      the implementation of this Act.

Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOE shall promulgate
the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.

       Section 5. Mandatory Installation of Solar Energy Systems. – All
government infrastructure, buildings, and offices covered by this Act shall, where
technically feasible and practicable, install and operationalize solar energy systems
in accordance with the Solar Government Plan established under this Act.

Such solar energy systems shall be capable of supplying at least thirty percent (30%)
of the building’s average daily electricity consumption.

All newly constructed government infrastructure, buildings, and offices shall be
designed and constructed to accommodate and integrate solar energy systems as part
of their standard energy infrastructure.

The installation and operation of solar energy systems shall comply with the
technical standards and guidelines prescribed by the Department of Energy (DOE)
and other relevant government agencies.

       Section 6. Operation and Oversight. – The Department of Energy (DOE)
shall have overall supervision in the installation and operation of the solar energy
system. Upon implementation of this Act, the DOE shall submit an annual report to
Congress for assessment and review.

       Section 7. Privately-Owned Properties Used by the Government. – Privately-
owned properties used or occupied by the government through lease, usufruct, or
similar arrangements may be covered by this Act, subject to the mutual agreement
of the government and the property owner.

The installation of solar energy systems in such properties shall be undertaken only
when the remaining term of the lease, usufruct, or similar agreement is sufficient to
justify the cost of installation and operation of the system, as may be determined
under the Solar Government Plan.

Cost-sharing arrangements between the government and the property owner for the
procurement, installation, and maintenance of solar energy systems may be agreed
upon by the parties, subject to existing laws, rules, and regulations.

      Section 8. Support for Local Government Units. – Local government units
(LGUs) are encouraged to adopt and install solar energy systems in their respective
government infrastructure, buildings, and offices.

Subject to the availability of funds, the Department of Energy (DOE), in
coordination with the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), may provide
financial assistance or subsidies to LGUs for up to ten percent (10%) of the
procurement and installation costs of solar energy systems.

The DOE shall establish guidelines for the availment and distribution of such
assistance, with priority given to LGUs with limited financial capacity and those
implementing large-scale solar energy adoption in public facilities.

       Section 9. Bidding and Procurement Process – All bidding and procurement
done for the purpose of this Act shall be in accordance with the law and existing
rules and regulations. The same shall be made in public with all pertinent documents
available.

     Section 10. Appropriations. – An initial amount of One Billion Pesos
(P1,000,000,000.00) shall be appropriated for the implementation of this Act.
Thereafter, such amounts necessary for the completion and implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       Section 11. Separability Clause – If any provision or part of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected
shall continue to be in full force and effect.
      Section 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, and
administrative orders or parts thereof inconsistent with any provision of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.
        Section 13. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
