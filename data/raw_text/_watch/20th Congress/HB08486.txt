                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XII, Section 11 of the 1987 Constitution mandates that the State shall
adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development which shall
endeavor to make essential goods, health and other social services available to all
the people at affordable cost. This includes the elderly or senior citizens in the
country. However, the number of abandoned or neglected senior citizens in the
Philippines has been rising, with reports indicating an increase in the number of
elderly individuals who are homeless or in need of care.

       The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) provides a range
of residential facilities and programs intended to support and care for elderly
individuals, including, but not limited to, GRACES, the Home for the Elderly, and
Kanlungan ni Maria. However, the availability of these services remains
geographically limited, as such facilities operate only in selected locations across the
country.

      This proposed measure aims to require the creation of at least one public
nursing home in each city and municipality nationwide, thereby guaranteeing
accessible and comprehensive care services for elderly persons in all communities.

      For these reasons, the swift passage of the foregoing measure is earnestly
sought.

       AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NURSING HOME FOR QUALIFIED HOMELESS
 AND ABANDONED SENIOR CITIZENS IN EVERY CITY OR MUNICIPALITY IN THE
PHILIPPINES, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Homes for
Abandoned Seniors Act.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
value the dignity of every human person, guarantee full respect for human rights,
extend assistance to its elderly citizens through programs of social security, and
promote the right to health and safety of senior citizens. Towards this end, the State
shall extend assistance to abandoned and homeless senior citizens through a social
care and security program.

      SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — As used in this Act:

        (a) “Abandoned Senior Citizen” refers to a senior citizen who has been
deserted, permanently or temporarily, by his relatives or persons who have general
or legal responsibility over the senior citizen, and/or from whom all basic care has
been withdrawn, whether willfully or not;

       (b) “Homeless Senior Citizen” refers to a senior who lacks a fixed, regular
residence, or whose current residence is unfit for human habitation, or lacks
resources to obtain proper housing;
       (c) “Nursing Home” refers to a home for the aged, established and maintained
under this Act by a city or municipality which provides living accommodations, food
and clothing, medical and health care, recreational and social interaction activities
for abandoned senior citizens or homeless senior citizens;

        (d) “Senior Citizen” refers to any resident citizen of the Philippines with at
least sixty (60) years of age; and

       SEC. 4. Establishment of Nursing Homes. — There shall be established in
every municipality and city a nursing home to serve qualified abandoned and
homeless senior citizens (beneficiaries), to be built by the local government units
(LGUs) concerned and shall be operated and maintained by the Department of Social
Welfare and Development (DSWD) in coordination with the LGUs concerned. To
qualify for availment, the beneficiary must have neither the means nor the ability to
care for him/herself.

      SEC. 5. Functions of Nursing Homes. — The nursing homes shall provide the
beneficiaries within its territorial jurisdiction institutionalized residential care
programs and services such as but not limited to:

       (a) Comfortable living quarters;

       (b) Adequate food and clothing;

       (c) Medical consultation or treatment;

       (d) Health care;

       (e) Recreational and social interaction activities;

       (f) Counseling;

       (g) Case conference;

      (h) Family dialogue;

       (i) Outreach to the existing family for reintegration;

       (j) Literacy education;

      (k) Productivity development; and

       (l) Religious, cultural and vocational activities.
       SEC. 6. Voluntary Exit from the Home. — Any beneficiary may opt to leave the
nursing home provided that the DSWD deems them able to care for themselves or
that they are endorsed to their family or guardians who will take responsibility for
their care. Beneficiaries who have recovered from the impairment which prevents
them to take care of themselves may be endorsed to the Department of Labor and
Employment or the DSWD Livelihood program for employment or livelihood
opportunities and the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development
(DHSUD).

      SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — The DSWD, in
coordination with the Department of Interior and Local Government, Department of
Health and the Federation of Senior Citizens Associations of the Philippines, and
Confederation of Older Persons Associations of the Philippines shall hereby
promulgate the rules and regulations to carry out the provisions of this Act not later
than ninety (90) days after its effectivity.

       SEC. 8. Exemptions of the Nursing Home. — The established nursing home
shall be exempted from the payment of customs duties, taxes, and tariffs on the
importation of equipment and supplies used actually, directly, and exclusively by the
nursing home pursuant to this Act, including those donated to the Nursing Home.

       SEC. 9. Appropriations. — The amount necessary to carry out the provisions
of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following
its enactment into law and every year thereafter.

      SEC. 10. Separability Cause. — If, for any reason, any section, subsection,
clause or term of this Act is held invalid or unconstitutional, such parts not affected
by such declaration shall remain in full force and effect.

      SEC. 11. Repealing Cause. — All existing laws, orders, decrees, rules and
regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
amended, modified, or repealed accordingly.

      SEC. 12. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
