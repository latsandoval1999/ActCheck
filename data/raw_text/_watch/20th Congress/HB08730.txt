                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Ang akses sa maaasahang kuryente ay pundasyon ng kaunlaran sa mga kanayunan.
 Nagsisilbi itong susi sa pag-angat ng lokal na ekonomiya, serbisyong panlipunan, at
 pangkalahatang kalidad ng pamumuhay. Mahalaga rin ito para sa agrikultura—mula
 irigasyon hanggang post-harvest—na direktang nakatutulong sa kabuhayan ng ating mga
 magbubukid.

        Isinusulong ng panukalang batas na ito ang pagbibigay ng prangkisa sa Capiz
 Electric Cooperative, Inc. (CAPELCO) upang maghatid ng kuryente sa Roxas City at mga
 bayan ng Capiz. Itinatakda rin ang mga pamantayan para sa maayos na pakikipagtulungan
 sa pamahalaan tungo sa ganap na elektripikasyon o kuryente para sa lahat.

       Ang CAPELCO ay isang non-stock, non-profit na kooperatiba na itinatag noong
 1971. Ito ay pinagkalooban ng 50-taong prangkisa ng National Electrification
 Administration (NEA) noong 1978, na magtatapos sa 2028.

        Sa kasalukuyan, pinaglilingkuran ng CAPELCO ang 16 na bayan at 1 lungsod, na
 sumasaklaw sa 475 barangay at mahigit 180,000 koneksyon. Napatunayan na nito ang
 kanilang kakayahan sa pagbibigay ng maaasahang serbisyo sa kuryente, kabilang ang
 pagbawas ng system loss at patuloy na pakikiisa sa Sitio Electrification Program.

        Para sa pagpapaunlad ng mga kanayunan at masuportahan ang sektor ng
 agrikultura, ang panukalang ito ay titiyak ng sapat, maaasahan, at abot-kayang kuryente
 para sa ating mga kababayan sa Capiz.

       Dahil dito, hinihiling ang agarang pag-apruba ng panukalang batas na ito.

                                   AN ACT
         GRANTING THE CAPIZ ELECTRIC COOPERATIVE, INC. (CAPELCO) A
        FRANCHISE TO CONSTRUCT, INSTALL, ESTABLISH, OPERATE, OWN,
            MANAGE AND MAINTAIN DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS FOR THE
       CONVEYANCE OF ELECTRIC POWER TO THE END-USERS IN THE CITY
         OF ROXAS AND MUNICIPALITIES OF CUARTERO, DAO, DUMARAO,
           DUMALAG, IVISAN, JAMINDAN, MAAYON, MAMBUSAO, PANAY,
       PANITAN, PILAR, PONTEVEDRA, PRESIDENT ROXAS, SAPIAN, SIGMA,
                  AND TAPAZ, ALL IN THE PROVINCE OF CAPIZ

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Republic of
      the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 1           SECTION 1. Nature and Scope of Franchise. – Subject to the
 2    provisions of the Constitution and applicable laws, rules and regulations, there
 3    is hereby granted to the Capiz Electric Cooperative, Inc. (CAPELCO) hereunder
 4    referred to as the Grantee, its successors or assigns a franchise to construct,
 5    install, establish, operate, own, manage and maintain in the public interest
 6    and commercial purposes, a distribution system for the conveyance of electric
 7    power to the end-users in the City of Roxas and Municipalities of Cuartero,
 8    Dao, Dumarao, Dumalag, Ivisan, Jamindan, Maayon, Mambusao, Panay,
 9    Panitan, Pilar, Pontevedra, President Roxas, Sapian, Sigma, and Tapaz, all in
10    the Province of Capiz.

11           As used in this Act, distribution system refers to the system of wires and
12    associated facilities including sub-transmission lines belonging to a franchised
13    distribution utility extending between the delivery point on the national
14    transmission system or generating facility and the metering point/facility of
15    the end-users.

16            SEC. 2. Manner of Operation of Facilities. – All electric distribution
17    facilities, lines and systems for electric services installed, owned, maintained,
18    operated, managed by the Grantee, its successors or assigns shall be operated
                                                                                      2
 1   or maintained at all times in a superior manner, and it shall be the duty of the
 2   Grantee, its successors or assigns, whenever required to do so by the Energy
 3   Regulatory Commission (ERC), or its legal successor, or the Department of
 4   Energy (DOE), or its legal successor, or any other government agency
 5   concerned to modify, improve and change such facilities or systems in such

16          SEC. 3. Authority of the ERC and NEA. – The Grantee shall secure
17   from the ERC or NEA any government agency having jurisdiction over their
18   operations, the Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity and any other
19   license, permit or authority indispensable for the construction and operation
20   of the electric power distribution system.

21          SEC. 4. Excavation and Restoration Works. – For the purpose of
22   erecting and maintaining the poles or other supports for said facilities, wires
23   or other conductors or for the purpose of laying and maintaining said facilities,
24   wires, cables or other conductors, it shall be lawful for the Grantee, its
25   successors, or assignees, in coordination with the Department of Public Works
26   and Highways (DPWH) or the local government unit (LGU) concerned, as may
27   be appropriate, to make excavations or lay conduits in any of the public places,
28   highways, road, streets, lanes, alleys, avenues, sidewalks, or bridges of the
29   province, cities or municipalities: Provided, however, That a public place,
30   highway, road, street, lane, alley, avenue, sidewalk, or bridge disturbed,
31   altered, or changed by reason of erection of poles or other supports or the
32   underground laying of wires, other conductors or conduits, shall be repaired
33   and replaced in workmanlike manner by the Grantee, its successors or
34   assignees, in accordance with the standards set by the DPWH or the local
35   government unit concerned. Should the Grantee, its successors or assignees,
36   after the ten (10)-day notice from the grant of authority, fail, refuse or neglect
37   to repair or replace any part of public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley,
38   avenue, sidewalk, or bridge altered, changed or disturbed by the said Grantee,
39   its successors or assignees, then the DPWH or the local government unit
40   concerned shall have the right to have the same repaired and replaced in good
41   order and condition and charge the Grantee, its successors or assignees,
42   double the amount of the cost and expenses for such repair or replacement.

43          SEC. 5. Responsibility to the Public. – The Grantee shall supply
44   electricity to its captive market in the least costly manner. In the interest of
45   the public good and as far as feasible and whenever required by the ERC, the
46   Grantee shall modify, improve, or change its facilities, poles, lines, systems
                                                                                     3
 1   and equipment for the purpose of providing efficient and reliable service and
 2   reduced electricity costs. The Grantee shall charge reasonable and just power
 3   rates for its services to all types of consumers within its franchised area in
 4   order that business and industries shall be able to compete.

 5          The Grantee shall have the obligation to provide open and
 6   nondiscriminatory access to its distribution system and services for any end-
 7   user within its franchise area consistent with Republic Act (RA) No. 9136,
 8   otherwise known as the “Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001”. The
 9   Grantee shall not engage in any activity that will constitute an abuse of market
10   power such as unfair trade practices, monopolistic schemes, and other
11   activities that will hinder competitiveness of business and industries.

12          SEC. 6. Rates for Services. – The retail rates and charges for the
13   distribution of electric power by the Grantee to its end-users shall be regulated
14   by and subject to the approval of the ERC or its legal successor.

15         The Grantee shall identify and segregate in its electricity bill to the end-
16   users the components of the retail rate pursuant to RA 9136, unless otherwise
17   amended. Such rates charged by the Grantee to the end-users shall be made
18   public and transparent. The Grantee shall implement lifeline rate to
19   marginalized end-users as mandated under RA 9136.

20         SEC. 7. Promotion of Consumer Interests. – The Grantee herein shall
21   establish a consumer desk that will handle consumer complaints and ensure
22   adequate promotion of consumer interests. The Grantee shall act with
23   dispatch on all complaints brought before it.

24          SEC. 8. Commitment to Provide and Promote the Creation of
25   Employment Opportunities. – The Grantee shall create employment
26   opportunities and shall allow on-the-job training in its franchise operation:
27   Provided, That priority shall be accorded to the residents where their principal
28   office is located: Provided, further, That the Grantee shall comply with the
29   applicable labor standards and allowance entitlement under existing labor
30   laws, rules and regulations, and similar issuances.

31         SEC. 9. Right of the Government. – A special right is hereby reserved
32   to the President of the Philippines, in times of war, rebellion, public peril,
33   calamity, emergency, disaster or disturbance of peace and order: to
34   temporarily take over and operate the stations or facilities of the Grantee; to
35   temporarily suspend the operation of any station or facility in the interest of
36   public safety, security, and public welfare; or to authorize the temporary use
37   and operation thereof by any agency of the government, upon due
38   compensation to the Grantee, for the use of the stations or facilities during the
39   period when these shall be so operated.

40         SEC. 10. Right of Eminent Domain. – Subject to the limitations and
41   procedures prescribed by law, the Grantee is authorized to exercise the right
42   of eminent domain insofar as it may be reasonably necessary for the efficient
43   maintenance and operation of services. The Grantee is authorized to install
44   and maintain its poles, wires, and other facilities over and across public
                                                                                 4
 1   property, including streets, highways, forest reserves, and other similar
 2   property of the Government of the Philippines, its branches, or any of its
 3   instrumentalities. The Grantee may acquire such private property as is
 4   actually necessary for the realization of the purposes for which this franchise
 5   is granted: Provided, That proper expropriation proceedings shall have been

 7          SEC. 11. Term of Franchise. – Unless sooner cancelled, the franchise
 8   shall be in effect for a period of twenty-five (25) years from the date of effectivity
 9   of this Act. This franchise shall be deemed ipso facto revoked in the event the
10   Grantee fails to operate continuously for two (2) years.

11        SEC. 12. Warranty in Favor of the National and Local
12   Governments. – The Grantee shall hold the national, provincial, city, and
13   municipal governments of the Philippines free from all claims, accounts,
14   demands, or actions arising from accidents causing injury to persons or
15   damage to properties, during the construction, installation, operation, and
16   maintenance of the distribution system of the Grantee.

17         SEC. 13. Liability for Damages. – The Grantee shall be liable for any
18   injury to persons and damage to properties arising from accidents by reason
19   of any defective construction under this franchise or of any neglect or omission
20   to keep its poles and wires in safe condition.

21          SEC. 14. Sale, Lease, Transfer, Usufruct, or Assignment of
22   Franchise. – The Grantee shall not sell, lease, transfer, grant the usufruct, or
23   assign this franchise or the rights and privileges acquired thereunder to any
24   person, firm, company, corporation, or other commercial or legal entity, or
25   merge with any other corporation, or entity, or shall transfer the controlling
26   interest of the Grantee, whether as a whole or in parts, and whether
27   simultaneously or contemporaneously, to any such person, firm, company,
28   corporation, or entity without the prior approval of the Congress: Provided,
29   That Congress shall be informed of any sale, lease, transfer, grant of usufruct,
30   sale, or assignment of franchise or the rights and privileges acquired
31   thereunder, or of the merger, or sale of the controlling interest within sixty (60)
32   days after the completion of said transaction: Provided, further, That any such
33   transfer, sale, or assignment is in accordance with the constitutional
34   limitations: Provided, furthermore, That failure to report to Congress such
35   change of ownership shall render the franchise ipso facto revoked: Provided,
36   finally, That any person or entity to which this franchise is sold, transferred,
37   or assigned, shall be subject to the same conditions, terms, restrictions, and
38   limitations of this Act.

39          SEC. 15. Reportorial Requirement. – The Grantee shall submit an
40   annual report to Congress, through the Committee on Legislative Franchises
41   of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Public Services of the
42   Senate, on its compliance with the terms and conditions of the franchise and
43   on its operations on or before April 30 of every year during the term of the
44   franchise. The reportorial compliance certificate issued by Congress shall be
45   required before any application for permit or certificate is accepted by the ERC.

 6         SEC. 17. Equality Clause. – Any advantage, favor, privilege, exemption,
 7   or immunity granted under existing franchises, or which may hereafter be
 8   granted, upon prior review and approval of Congress, shall become part of this
 9   franchise and shall be accorded immediately and unconditionally to the herein
10   Grantee: Provided, however, That the foregoing shall neither apply to nor affect
11   provisions concerning territory covered by the franchise, the life span of the
12   franchise or the type of service authorized by the franchise: Provided, further,
13   That the foregoing shall not apply to the sale, lease, transfer, grant of usufruct,
14   or assignment of legislative franchises with prior congressional approval.

15           SEC. 18. Applicability of Existing Laws. – The Grantee shall comply
16   with and be subject to the provisions of Commonwealth Act No. 146 or the
17   “Public Service Act”, as amended, RA 9136, and RA 10531 or the “National
18   Electrification Administration Reform Act of 2013.”

19          SEC. 19. Existing Powers. – Existing mandates, powers, functions, and
20   privileges granted to electric cooperatives under existing laws, including
21   Section 10 of RA 10531 shall remain valid and effective, unless expressly
22   repealed by succeeding laws.

23          SEC. 20. Repealability and Non-Exclusivity Clause. – This franchise
24   shall be subject to amendment, alteration, or repeal by Congress when the
25   public interest so requires and shall not be interpreted as an exclusive grant
26   of the privileges herein provided for.

27          SEC. 21. Separability Clause. – If any of the sections or provisions of
28   this Act is held invalid, all other provisions not affected thereby shall remain
29   valid.

30         SEC. 22. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
31   orders, letters of instruction, administrative rules and regulations or parts
32   thereof which is are contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act
33   are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

34         SEC. 23. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect upon the
35   expiration of the Grantee’s franchise or fifteen (15) days after its publication
36   in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation, whichever
37   comes later.
