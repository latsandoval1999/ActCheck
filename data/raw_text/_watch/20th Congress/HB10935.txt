                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Filipino workers are the backbone of our nation's progress. Every day, they work
tirelessly to provide for their families and contribute to the country's economic
growth, often sacri icing time for rest, health, and loved ones. Yet despite the
increasing demands of the modern workplace, the law continues to grant only ive (5)
days of Service Incentive Leave (SIL), a bene it that has remained unchanged for
decades. At a time when work-related stress and burnout have become increasingly
common, it is only itting that our labor standards evolve to better protect the well-
being of Filipino workers.

Service Incentive Leave allows employees to recover from illness, attend to family
responsibilities, manage personal matters, and take the rest they need to remain
healthy and productive. This bill seeks to increase the annual Service Incentive Leave
from ive (5) days to ten (10) days. To encourage employees to actually use their leave
for rest and recovery, only up to ive (5) unused leave days may be converted into cash,
while the remaining ive (5) days are intended to be enjoyed as paid leave. The
measure likewise protects workers by ensuring that existing leave bene its that are
more favorable than those provided by law will not be diminished.

By giving workers more time to rest, recharge, and care for themselves and their
families, this measure promotes a healthier, more productive, and more motivated

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Article 95 of Presidential Decree No. 442, as amended, otherwise
known as the “Labor Code of the Philippines,” is hereby amended to read as
follows:

     “Article 95. Right to Service Incentive Leave. —

     (a) Every employee who has rendered at least one (1) year of service shall be
         entitled to a yearly service incentive of ive TEN days with pay, PROVIDED,
         THAT THE EMPLOYEE SHALL BE ENTITLED TO CONVERT ONLY FIVE
         DAYS OF LEAVE TO ITS MONEY EQUIVALENT IF NOT USED OR
         EXHAUSTED AT THE END OF THE YEAR.

     (b) This provision shall not apply to those who are already enjoying the bene it
         herein provided, those enjoying vacation leave with pay of at least ive TEN
         days, and those employed in establishments regularly employing less than
         ten employees or in establishments exempted from granting the bene it by
         the Secretary of Labor and Employment after considering the viability or
          inancial condition of such establishment.

     (c) The grant of bene it in excess of that provided herein shall not be made a
         subject of arbitration or any court or administrative action.”

SECTION 2. Applicability to Existing Employees. — Employees who have already
completed at least one (1) year of service on the date of effectivity of this Act shall be
entitled to the increased service incentive leave beginning with the service year
immediately following such effectivity.

Employees who complete one (1) year of service after the effectivity of this Act shall
be entitled to the bene it provided herein upon completion of the required period of
service.

Nothing in this Section shall authorize the diminution, cancellation, or forfeiture of
leave bene its already earned before the effectivity of this Act.

SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Labor and Employment and
other government agencies charged with the administration and enforcement of the
Labor Code of the Philippines or any of its parts, in consultation with relevant
stakeholders, shall issue the implementing rules and regulations (IRR) for the
effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 4. Construction. — Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish any
right, bene it, or privilege granted under the Labor Code, as amended, or under any
other applicable law, company policy, collective bargaining agreement, employment
contract, or established employer practice.

SECTION 5. Separability Clause. — If any part, section or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such
declaration shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
