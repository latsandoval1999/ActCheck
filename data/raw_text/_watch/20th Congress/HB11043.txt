                              AN ACT
STRENGTHENING THE TECHNICAL-VOCATIONAL-LIVELIHOOD CURRICULUM IN
SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL, INSTITUTIONALIZING INDUSTRY-RESPONSIVE
TRAINING AND CERTIFICATION, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippine economy needs workers who have not only academic knowledge, but
also practical and industry-recognized skills. The Technical-Vocational-Livelihood
(TVL) track in senior high school was created to help students prepare for work,
entrepreneurship, or further education.

However, outdated equipment, limited access to quali ied trainers, and weak links
with industries can prevent students from gaining the skills employers need. We must
make sure that every TVL student is given a real chance to succeed after graduation.

This measure strengthens the TVL track by bringing government, schools, local
governments, and industries together. It creates a TVL Coordinating Council to ensure
that training remains relevant to actual jobs and industry needs, while improving
access to competency assessment and certi ication. DepEd will continue to oversee
the academic curriculum, while TESDA will remain responsible for technical skills,
assessment, and certi ication. DOLE and DTI will help connect schools and learners
with labor-market information, employers, work immersion, and entrepreneurship
opportunities.

The bill also encourages stronger partnerships with industries through work
immersion, mentoring, teacher and trainer exposure, equipment support, job fairs,

and employment facilitation. These partnerships will help students learn skills that
are actually used in the workplace, while ensuring that work-based learning is safe,
supervised, educational, and free from exploitation. It also promotes support for
quali ied donations and existing tax incentives allowed under the National Internal
Revenue Code, helping schools gain access to needed resources without creating new
tax exemptions.

Education should open doors, not leave young Filipinos wondering what comes next
after graduation. A strong TVL program can give students practical skills, con idence,
and a pathway to decent and productive work even without a college degree. By
making TVL more accessible, industry-responsive, and trusted by employers, we
invest not only in our students but also in the future workforce and economy of the
Philippines.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

                              AN ACT
STRENGTHENING THE TECHNICAL-VOCATIONAL-LIVELIHOOD CURRICULUM IN

TRAINING AND CERTIFICATION, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Strengthened TVL
Curriculum Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right of all citizens to quality education and to establish, maintain, and
support a complete, adequate, and integrated system of education responsive to the
needs of the people, the country, and society.

Consistent with the objectives of enhanced basic education, the State shall strengthen
the Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) track in senior high school by providing
learners with relevant academic knowledge, industry-recognized technical skills,
career guidance, work-based learning opportunities, and pathways to employment,
entrepreneurship, further education, and lifelong learning.

The State shall promote an education and training system aligned with national
development, industrialization, regional economic priorities, and the evolving
requirements of industry. It shall ensure that TVL graduates are equipped to obtain


                                                                           3|Page
appropriate national certi ications and to secure decent, productive, and
compensated work, whether or not they pursue a college degree.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish the authority of the Department of
Education (DepEd), the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority
(TESDA), or other agencies under existing laws.

SECTION 3. Objectives. —

This Act shall pursue the following objectives:

   (a) Strengthen the quality, relevance, accessibility, and industry responsiveness of
       the TVL track in senior high school;

   (b) Align TVL programs with the Philippine Quali ications Framework, applicable
       TESDA training regulations, national certi ication requirements, and current
       and emerging industry needs;

   (c) Enable quali ied TVL learners to acquire the competencies necessary to obtain
       appropriate National Certi icates (NCs) or Certi icates of Competency (COCs),
       subject to existing TESDA assessment and certi ication rules;

   (d) Provide learners with meaningful work-based learning, including work
       immersion, apprenticeship, school-enterprise cooperation, and other
       comparable arrangements, subject to applicable labor, safety, child protection,
       and education laws;

   (e) Improve the transition of TVL graduates to employment, entrepreneurship,
       further education, and other livelihood opportunities; and

   (f) Establish a coordinated system for curriculum development, teacher and
       trainer quali ication, assessment, certi ication, career guidance, employment
       facilitation, and industry participation.

SECTION 4. Coverage. — This Act shall apply to public and private senior high
schools offering the TVL track, including schools, institutions, and training providers
duly authorized or recognized by the appropriate government agency.

The implementation of this Act shall respect the authority of DepEd over the basic
education curriculum and the authority of TESDA over technical education and skills

                                                                           4|Page
development, including the promulgation of training regulations and the conduct of
competency assessment and certi ication.

SECTION 5. TVL Coordinating Council. — A TVL Coordinating Council, hereinafter
referred to as the Council, is hereby created to coordinate the implementation of this
Act.

The Council shall be attached to DepEd for policy coordination and administrative
support and shall be composed of the following:

   (a) The Secretary of Education, as Chairperson;

   (b) The Director General of TESDA, as Vice-Chairperson;

   (c) The Secretary of Labor and Employment;

   (d) The Secretary of Trade and Industry;

   (e) The Secretary of Social Welfare and Development, or the Secretary of
       Information and Communications Technology, as may be designated by the
       President;

   (f) A representative of the Philippine Chamber of Commerce and Industry (PCCI);
       and

   (g) A representative of the Employers Confederation of the Philippines (ECOP).

The members representing PCCI and ECOP shall be appointed by the President from
nominees submitted by their respective organizations. They shall serve for a term of
three (3) years and may be reappointed for one additional term.

The Council may invite representatives from local government units, industry
associations, labor organizations, professional organizations, schools, higher
education institutions, civil society organizations, and other relevant stakeholders
whenever their participation is necessary.

Members of the Council shall serve without additional compensation. They may,
however, be reimbursed for actual and reasonable expenses incurred in the
performance of their of icial functions, subject to existing accounting and auditing
laws, rules, and regulations.

                                                                          5|Page
A member may designate a permanent representative who shall have a rank not lower
than an Undersecretary, Assistant Secretary, or equivalent, as applicable. Such
representative shall be authorized to participate and vote in meetings of the Council.

The Council shall meet at least once every quarter. The Chairperson or a majority of
its members may call a special meeting when necessary. Meetings may be conducted
through videoconferencing or other electronic means when an in-person meeting is
impracticable, provided that all members are able to participate effectively, and the
integrity of the proceedings is maintained.

SECTION 6. Powers and Functions of the Council. —

The Council shall:

   (a) Develop a national policy and coordination framework for the implementation
       and continuous improvement of the TVL track;

   (b) Facilitate periodic consultations with industries, employers, labor
       organizations, schools, learners, local government units, and other
       stakeholders to identify current and emerging skills requirements;

   (c) Recommend the review and updating of TVL programs, specializations,
       learning competencies, equipment standards, work-based learning
       arrangements, and assessment pathways;

   (d) Coordinate the integration, where appropriate and legally permissible, of
       TESDA competency standards and certi ication requirements into the TVL
       curriculum;

   (e) Ensure that TVL programs are aligned with the Philippine Quali ications
       Framework and relevant national, regional, and local development priorities;

   (f) Promote the availability of competency assessment and certi ication
       opportunities for quali ied learners and graduates, without compromising
       TESDA’s authority over assessment and certi ication;

   (g) Develop programs that facilitate the employment, entrepreneurship, further
       education, and lifelong learning of TVL graduates;


                                                                          6|Page
   (h) Promote career guidance, labor-market information, job fairs, placement
       assistance, and recognition of TVL quali ications by employers;

   (i) Encourage local government units, local school boards, and other public and
       private institutions to coordinate their plans, resources, and programs in
       support of TVL schools;

   (j) Establish a monitoring and evaluation system based on, among others,
       participation, completion, certi ication, employment, entrepreneurship,
       further education, employer satisfaction, and graduate outcomes; and

   (k) Perform such other acts as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this
       Act.

The Council shall not exercise powers vested by law in DepEd, TESDA, DOLE, DTI, or
any other government agency.

SECTION 7. Responsibilities of DepEd. —

DepEd shall:

   (a) Develop, prescribe, and periodically review the academic component of the
       TVL curriculum in accordance with the enhanced basic education program
       and applicable laws;

   (b) Coordinate with TESDA to ensure that the academic and technical components
       of the TVL curriculum are properly integrated without reducing the required
       learning competencies for senior high school;

   (c) Ensure that learners acquire foundational knowledge, communication skills,
       numeracy, digital literacy, entrepreneurship competencies, civic values, and
       other competencies necessary for employment, further education, and lifelong
       learning;

   (d) Recruit, designate, train, and provide continuing professional development for
       teachers assigned to the TVL track, subject to civil service, quali ication, and
       budgetary laws;




                                                                           7|Page
   (e) Ensure that public and private schools offering the TVL track comply with
       applicable requirements on facilities, equipment, learner safety, child
       protection, accessibility, and quality assurance;

   (f) Maintain a system for monitoring the performance and outcomes of TVL
       programs; and

   (g) Coordinate with TESDA and other agencies to expand access to competency
       assessment and certi ication for quali ied learners.

SECTION 8. Responsibilities of TESDA. —

TESDA shall:

   (a) Develop, promulgate, and periodically update the applicable training
       regulations, competency standards, assessment arrangements, and
       certi ication pathways for TVL specializations under its jurisdiction;

   (b) Assist DepEd in aligning the technical component of the TVL curriculum with
       TESDA training regulations and the Philippine Quali ications Framework;

   (c) Establish or authorize appropriate assessment and certi ication arrangements
       for quali ied learners and graduates;

   (d) Prescribe the quali ication standards for trainers, assessors, and other
       technical personnel involved in the delivery and assessment of TVL
       competencies;

   (e) Provide technical assistance, capacity-building programs, and, where
       authorized by law and subject to available appropriations, access to training
       facilities and equipment;

   (f) Coordinate with DepEd on the recognition of relevant prior learning and
       competencies, subject to existing rules; and

   (g) Submit to the Council periodic reports on certi ication outcomes, assessment
       capacity, and emerging skills requirements.




                                                                         8|Page
SECTION 9. Responsibilities of DOLE. —

DOLE shall:

   (a) Provide labor-market information and employment projections relevant to
       the design and review of TVL programs;

   (b) Assist in formulating guidelines for work immersion and other work-based
       learning arrangements, in consultation with DepEd, TESDA, employers, labor
       organizations, and other stakeholders;

   (c) Ensure that work immersion and other work-based learning arrangements
       comply with applicable labor, occupational safety and health, child protection,
       anti-discrimination, and social protection laws;

   (d) Facilitate employment referral, career guidance, job matching, and placement
       services for quali ied TVL graduates; and

   (e) Provide the Council with information on employment outcomes, skills gaps,
       labor demand, and emerging occupations.

SECTION 10. Responsibilities of DTI and Other Government Agencies. —

DTI shall:

   (a) Provide information on industry needs, priority sectors, entrepreneurship
       opportunities, and local value chains;

   (b) Facilitate linkages among TVL schools, micro, small, and medium enterprises,
       industry associations, and other private-sector partners; and

   (c) Promote entrepreneurship, product development, mentoring, and market
       access programs for TVL learners and graduates.

Other government agencies may, consistent with their respective mandates and
subject to applicable laws, rules, regulations, and appropriations:

   (a) Provide technical assistance, facilities, equipment, materials, data, and other
       resources for TVL programs;


                                                                          9|Page
   (b) Enter into agreements with DepEd, TESDA, local government units, and
       quali ied private-sector partners; and

   (c) Temporarily detail quali ied personnel or make available government
       property when the head of the agency determines that doing so will not
       prejudice the public service.

No transfer, loan, or disposal of government property shall be made except in
accordance with applicable laws, accounting rules, and auditing regulations.

SECTION 11. Industry and Private-Sector Partnerships. —

DepEd and TESDA shall establish and maintain partnerships with quali ied
employers, industry associations, chambers of commerce, labor organizations, social
enterprises, and other private-sector institutions to support the implementation of
the TVL track.

Partnerships may include:

   (a) Curriculum consultation and validation;

   (b) Donation or loan of equipment, tools, materials, and other learning resources;

   (c) Teacher and trainer immersion and industry exposure;

   (d) Work immersion, apprenticeship, mentoring, and other work-based learning
       opportunities;

   (e) Joint skills competitions, career activities, and job fairs;

   (f) Assessment and certi ication support, subject to TESDA rules; and

   (g) Employment, entrepreneurship, and placement programs.

All work-based learning arrangements shall be covered by a written agreement and
shall provide for learner safety, supervision, learning objectives, grievance
procedures, con identiality, and compliance with applicable laws. No learner shall be
required to perform work that is hazardous, exploitative, discriminatory, or unrelated
to the approved learning objectives.

SECTION 12. Regional and Local TVL Industry Councils. —

DepEd and TESDA may organize regional, provincial, city, or municipal TVL industry
councils or similar coordinating mechanisms to identify local skills requirements,
support school-industry partnerships, and monitor graduate outcomes.

Such councils shall not be treated as separate government agencies and shall be
constituted and operated in accordance with rules issued under this Act.

SECTION 13. Support for Assessment and Certi ication. — Subject to applicable
laws, rules, regulations, and appropriations, DepEd and TESDA shall provide quali ied
learners reasonable access to competency assessment and certi ication.

The inclusion of competencies in the TVL curriculum shall not be deemed to
guarantee the issuance of a National Certi icate or Certi icate of Competency.
Certi ication shall remain subject to the applicable TESDA competency standards,
assessment requirements, and certi ication procedures.

The assessment and certi ication fees of quali ied learners may be subsidized from
appropriations speci ically provided for the purpose, subject to eligibility
requirements and applicable government accounting and auditing rules.

SECTION 14. Incentives for Donations and Contributions. — Donations, grants,
bequests, endowments, and contributions to DepEd, TESDA, or duly accredited
educational institutions or partner organizations for the implementation of this Act
may be entitled to the tax treatment and deductions allowed under the National
Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended, and other applicable laws, subject to the
requirements prescribed by the Department of Finance and the Bureau of Internal
Revenue.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed as granting a tax exemption or deduction
beyond what is authorized under existing tax laws.

SECTION 15. Information and Transparency. — DepEd and TESDA shall maintain
publicly accessible information, subject to the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and other
applicable laws, on:

   (a) TVL programs and specializations offered;

   (b) Applicable quali ication and certi ication pathways;

                                                                        11 | P a g e
   (c) Available assessment and training facilities;

   (d) Industry and work-based learning partnerships; and

   (e) Aggregated data on completion, certi ication, employment, entrepreneurship,
       and further education outcomes.

SECTION 16. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of
DepEd, TESDA, DOLE, DTI, and other concerned agencies, subject to existing laws,
rules, and regulations.

Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall
be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

No provision of this Act shall be construed to authorize the creation of permanent
positions, of ices, or staf ing complements without the corresponding appropriation
and authorization required by law.

SECTION 17. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, DepEd and TESDA, in consultation with DOLE, DTI, the
Council, local government units, schools, industry associations, labor organizations,
learners, and other stakeholders, shall promulgate the rules and regulations
necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

The implementing rules and regulations shall include, among others:

   (a) Standards for curriculum alignment and periodic review;

   (b) Procedures for industry consultation and partnership accreditation;

   (c) Standards for work immersion and other work-based learning arrangements;

   (d) Teacher, trainer, assessor, and facility quali ication requirements;

   (e) Procedures for assessment and certi ication support;

   (f) Monitoring, evaluation, reporting, and public disclosure mechanisms; and


                                                                              12 | P a g e
   (g) Safeguards for learner welfare, safety, privacy, and protection from
       discrimination and exploitation.

SECTION 18. Congressional Oversight. — DepEd and TESDA shall submit to
Congress, through the appropriate committees, an annual report on the
implementation of this Act, including the status of curriculum alignment, industry
partnerships, assessment and certi ication, budget utilization, and graduate
outcomes.

SECTION 19. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such declaration shall remain
in full force and effect.

SECTION 20. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 21. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
