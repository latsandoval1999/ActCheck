                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Province of Batangas is endowed with abundant natural
resources and ecologically significant areas that possess immense
potential for sustainable tourism development. Among these is the Sabang
River located in Barangay Wawa, City of Tanauan, a scenic and
environmentally important river system that serves as a source of
livelihood, recreation, and ecological balance for the surrounding
communities.

       The Sabang River is characterized by its natural beauty, rich
biodiversity, and tranquil environment, making it an ideal destination for
eco-tourism activities such as river trekking, nature appreciation,
educational tours, and other environmentally sustainable recreational
activities. Its preservation and responsible development can contribute
significantly to environmental awareness while generating economic
opportunities for local residents.

      Recognizing the growing importance of eco-tourism as a driver of
inclusive local economic growth, it is imperative for the government to
promote and develop environmentally sustainable tourism destinations that
balance conservation with community development. Declaring the Sabang
River as an eco-tourism site will encourage the establishment of
appropriate infrastructure and tourism facilities while ensuring the
protection and preservation of its ecological integrity.

      By enacting this measure, tourism in Tanauan will be showcased,
thereby ensuring the environmental conservation of our natural heritage,
and generating livelihood and employment opportunities for our local
communities in Batangas.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
earnestly sought.

                        AN ACT​
  DECLARING THE SABANG RIVER IN BARANGAY WAWA, CITY OF
  TANAUAN, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS AS AN ECOTOURISM SITE

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as “Sabang River
Eco-tourism Site Act.”

       SEC. 2. Tourism Site. - The Sabang River in Barangay Wawa, City
of Tanauan, Province of Batangas is hereby declared as an eco-tourism
site. As such, it shall be accorded priority development by the Department
of Tourism (DOT), subject to the rules and regulations governing the
development of tourism sites.

       SEC. 3. Development Plan. - The DOT, coordination with the
Tourism Infrastructure and Enterprise Zone Authority (TIEZA) and other
concerned agencies of government shall, within one (1) year from the
approval of this Act, prepare a development plan involving construction,
installation and maintenance of such appropriate facilities and infrastructure
as shall enhance tourism in said are, provided, that said development plan
shall ensure the preservation of the natural beauty and recreational
significance of the area.

      SEC. 4. Implementing Agency. - The DOT shall take immediate
steps to implement said development plan with the Municipal Government
of Cuenca and shall incorporate the same in the Department’s overall
tourism development program for the year following the approval of this
Act.

      SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOT, in consultation with relevant
local government units, shall issue the necessary rules and regulations to
implement this Act.

      SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, and
issuances, or portions thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act, are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 7. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
