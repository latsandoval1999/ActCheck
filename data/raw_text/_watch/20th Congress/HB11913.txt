                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The 1987 Constitution recognizes the sovereign right of the people to directly
participate in governance through the exercise of initiative and referendum. Aligned
with this constitutional mandate, Republic Act No. 6735, otherwise known as the
           n    n            n   m was enacted to provide the citizens with a
mechanism to directly propose, amend, or reject laws and local ordinances.

      More than three decades after its enactment, however, the processes
prescribed under Republic Act No. 6735 remain largely dependent on manual and
paper-based methods of signature collection and verification. While these

opportunities to make democratic participation more accessible, efficient, and

 use of digital technologies in the collection, submission, and validation of signatures
 for initiative and referendum proceedings.

        This measure seeks to modernize the existing law by authorizing the use of
 secure digital platforms for signature collection and verification while maintaining
 the integrity, transparency, and credibility of the initiative and referendum process.
 The bill provides safeguards through voter authentication mechanisms,
 cybersecurity standards, independent audits, data privacy protections, and penalties
 for fraud and unauthorized access. At the same time, it preserves traditional paper-
 based methods to ensure that citizens who may have limited access to digital
 technologies are not disenfranchised.

        By leveraging secure digital tools, this measure aims to reduce administrative
 burdens, improve accessibility, encourage broader civic participation, and
 strengthen the exercise of direct democracy without compromising electoral
 integrity. In doing so, it advances the constitutional principle that sovereignty resides
 in the people and that all government authority emanates from them.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

       SEC 6. Authority of the Commission on Elections (COMELEC).                                   The
COMELEC shall develop, establish, maintain, or accredit secure digital petition
platforms and digital verification systems for initiative and referendum proceedings.
The agency shall promulgate rules and regulations governing digital signature
collection, voter authentication, submission procedures, cybersecurity standards,
data retention, audit requirements, and other matters necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.


       SEC 7. Authentication and Verification Requirements.                                All digitally
submitted signatures shall undergo verification through mechanisms established by
the COMELEC, which may include voter registration database matching, multi-
factor authentication, digital certificates, biometric verification where legally
permissible, or other comparable technologies. No signature shall be counted unless
the identity and eligibility of the signer have been successfully verified in accordance
with COMELEC regulations.


       SEC 8. Cybersecurity and Data Protection Safeguards. ± The COMELEC
shall ensure that all digital petition platforms comply with nationally recognized

cybersecurity standards and applicable data protection laws. All collected
information shall be protected against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or
destruction. The COMELEC shall implement encryption, access controls, audit
trails, intrusion detection systems, and other appropriate safeguards to preserve the
integrity of the initiative and referendum process.


      SEC 9. Accessibility and Non-Exclusivity.         The use of digital signature
collection shall supplement, and not replace, traditional methods of signature

      The COMELEC shall ensure that citizens who lack internet access, digital
literacy, or necessary technological resources are not deprived of their constitutional
right to participate in initiative and referendum processes.


      SEC 10. Transparency and Public Accountability.           The COMELEC shall
publish guidelines, audit procedures, validation methodologies, aggregate signature
counts, and relevant reports necessary to promote public confidence in the integrity
of digital initiative and referendum proceedings. Provided, That no personally
identifiable information protected under applicable laws shall be disclosed.


      SEC 11. Independent Audit and Oversight.          All accredited digital petition
platforms shall be subject to periodic independent security audits conducted by
qualified third-party auditors accredited by the Department of Information and
Communications Technology (DICT).
      The Commission on Audit (COA), the National Privacy Commission (NPC),
and other relevant government agencies may conduct oversight reviews within their
respective mandates.

                                                                                     6
      SEC 12. Prohibited Acts.       The following acts are prohibited:
      (a) Knowingly submitting false, fictitious, fraudulent, or unauthorized digital
      signatures;
      (b) Impersonating a registered voter for purposes of digitally signing a
      petition;
      (c) Unauthorized access to, interference with, or manipulation of digital
      petition platforms or verification systems;
      (d) Selling, transferring, or unlawfully disclosing personal information
      obtained through initiative or referendum proceedings;
      (e) Using automated systems, bots, artificial intelligence tools, or similar
      technologies to fraudulently generate or submit signatures; and
      (f) Any other act intended to compromise the integrity, security, or legitimacy
      of digital initiative and referendum processes.
