                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Section 16, Article II of the 1987 Constitution declares that the State shall
protect and advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology
in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature. In the face of rapid
urbanization, climate change, deforestation, and environmental degradation,
there is a growing need to expand and preserve green spaces within local
communities.

      Trees and urban green spaces play a vital role in improving air quality,
reducing heat, mitigating flooding, conserving biodiversity, and promoting the
physical and mental well-being of the public. However, many municipalities
across the country continue to experience limited access to sustainable and
protected public green areas.

      This bill seeks to establish at least one (1) Tree Park in every municipality
in the Philippines to serve as a living repository of native flora, a center for
environmental awareness and education, and a public space for recreation and
ecological conservation. The proposed measure likewise aims to strengthen local
climate resilience and support national reforestation and environmental
sustainability efforts through active participation of local government units and
communities.

       By institutionalizing municipal tree parks nationwide, this measure
promotes environmental stewardship, biodiversity protection, and sustainable
local development for the benefit of present and future generations.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            “AN ACT
      ESTABLISHING A TREE PARK IN EVERY MUNICIPALITY IN THE
      PHILIPPINES AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR
      OTHER PURPOSES”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Municipal Tree
Park Act of 2026."

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to promote ecological balance, environmental sustainability,
biodiversity conservation, and public health through the conservation,
protection, and propagation of trees and other forms of vegetation. Toward this
end, the State shall ensure that every municipality establishes, develops, and
maintains a designated tree park for the benefit of its constituents and future
generations.

      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. For purposes of this Act:

            (a) Tree Park – refers to an identified and designated public area
                planted with various species of trees, shrubs, and other
                vegetation, serving as a green space for recreation, biodiversity
                preservation,    environmental    protection,  and     ecological
                education.

            (b) Municipality – refers to local government units classified as
                municipalities under existing laws.

      SECTION 4. Establishment of Tree Parks. - Every municipality in the
Philippines shall establish and maintain at least one (1) Tree Park, with an area
not less than one (1) hectare, which shall be located in a suitable public land
owned, donated, or acquired by the municipality.
       SECTION 5. Purpose and Objectives. - The Tree Park shall serve the
following purposes:

         (a) To serve as a living repository of local and native flora;

         (b) To promote public awareness and appreciation of environmental
             conservation;

         (c) To provide a public green space for leisure, recreation, and
             outdoor activities;

         (d) To serve as a venue for research on arboriculture and urban
             forestry;

         (e) To contribute to the carbon sequestration and climate resilience
             efforts of local government units; and

         (f) To enhance the natural aesthetic and environmental value of the
             locality.

      SECTION 6. Implementation -

       a) The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), in
coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG),
shall formulate the implementing rules and regulations (IRR) within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act.

      b) The concerned Local Government Units (LGUs) shall be primarily
responsible for the establishment, development, operation and maintenance of
the Tree Park.

      c) The DENR shall provide technical assistance on tree species selection,
landscaping, biodiversity management, and sustainable park development.

      SECTION 6. Funding. - The amount necessary to implement the
provisions of this Act shall be charged against the annual budgets of the
concerned municipality and may be supplemented by funds from the DENR's
reforestation and greening programs.

      LGUs are likewise encouraged to engage in partnerships with private
organizations, non-governmental organizations NGOs), and civic groups to
support the establishment and maintenance of Tree Parks.

     SECTION 7. Incentives. - Municipalities that successfully establish and
maintain Tree Parks in accordance with standards to be prescribed by the DENR
shall be given priority in the grant of environmental assistance, greening
programs, technical support, and related government projects.

     SECTION 9. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

       SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules, and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.

      Approved.
