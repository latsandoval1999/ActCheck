                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The timely issuance of academic records and credentials is essential to the
educational, professional, and economic advancement of students and graduates.
Documents such as transcripts of records, diplomas, certificates of graduation,
certifications of enrollment, and other school records serve as indispensable
requirements for employment, professional licensure examinations, scholarship
applications, transfer to other educational institutions, overseas opportunities, and
various government transactions.

       Despite advances in digital technology and records management systems,
many educational institutions continue to subject students and graduates to prolonged
and often unnecessary waiting periods before releasing these documents. While some
schools are capable of processing requests within a matter of days, others require
several weeks or even months before the requested records are made available. Such
delays have become a recurring concern among students, graduates, and employers
alike.

        In an era where information technology enables the rapid retrieval, verification,
and transmission of records, lengthy processing periods can no longer be justified as
a matter of routine administrative practice. The continued reliance on outdated
procedures unnecessarily burdens students and graduates who are often faced with
strict deadlines for employment, admissions, scholarships, board examinations, and
other opportunities. In many instances, delayed issuance of academic documents has
resulted in missed job opportunities, forfeited scholarships, deferred enrollment, and
other avoidable setbacks.

       The growing digitalization of educational records, coupled with the
government's ongoing efforts to improve bureaucratic efficiency and public service
delivery, makes it both reasonable and necessary to establish a uniform standard for
the processing of school documents. Educational institutions must be encouraged to
modernize their records management systems and adopt procedures that prioritize
efficiency, transparency, and responsiveness to the needs of students and graduates.
This measure seeks to require all educational institutions to process and release
academic documents within three (3) working days from receipt of a complete request,
with a target of two (2) working days for institutions possessing digitized records. It
further promotes online processing, transparency in service standards, accountability
mechanisms, and appropriate sanctions for unreasonable delays.

       By ensuring prompt access to academic credentials, this bill upholds the rights
of students and graduates, strengthens public trust in educational institutions, and
aligns the education sector with modern standards of efficient public service.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                 AN ACT
     REQUIRING ALL EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS TO PROCESS AND RELEASE
     SCHOOL    RECORDS,   TRANSCRIPTS    OF  RECORDS,    DIPLOMAS,
     CERTIFICATIONS, AND OTHER ACADEMIC DOCUMENTS WITHIN THREE (3)
     WORKING DAYS FROM RECEIPT OF A COMPLETE REQUEST, PROVIDING
     PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

 1   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
 2   Congress assembled:
 3
 4        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Academic
 5   Records Processing Efficiency Act."
 6
 7          SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the
 8   State to promote accessible, efficient, transparent, and responsive delivery of
 9   educational services. The State recognizes the right of students, graduates, and
10   former students to timely access their academic records and credentials, which are
11   often necessary for employment, professional licensure examinations, scholarship
12   applications, transfer to other educational institutions, immigration requirements, and
13   other lawful purposes.
14
15          The State further recognizes that unreasonable delays in the processing and
16   release of school documents impose unnecessary burdens upon students and
17   graduates and may result in lost educational, employment, and economic
18   opportunities. Towards this end, all educational institutions shall adopt efficient
19   systems and procedures to ensure the prompt processing and release of academic
20   records.
21
22         SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:
23
 1         (a) Academic Documents refer to all records and certifications maintained
 2         by an educational institution relating to a student’s academic standing,
 3         enrollment, graduation, or scholastic achievements.
 4
 5         (b) Complete Request refers to a request accompanied by all documentary
 6         requirements and payment of applicable fees, if any, as prescribed by the
 7         educational institution.
 8
 9         (c) Processing Period refers to the period within which an educational
10         institution must evaluate, prepare, approve, and release the requested
11         document.
12
13         (d) Working Days refer to days when the institution's administrative offices
14         are open for business, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and declared holidays.
15
16          SECTION 4. Scope and Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all public and
17   private educational institutions in the Philippines, including:
18
19         (a) Elementary schools;
20
21         (b) Secondary schools;
22
23         (c) Technical-vocational institutions;
24
25         (d) Colleges and universities;
26
27         (e) Graduate and post-graduate institutions; and
28
29         (f) Other educational institutions regulated by the government.
30
31           SECTION 5. Mandatory Processing and Release Period. - All educational
32   institutions shall process and release requested academic documents within three (3)
33   working days from receipt of a complete request.
34
35          For institutions possessing fully digitized student records, the processing and
36   release shall, whenever practicable, be completed within two (2) working days.
37
38         The prescribed period shall apply to the following documents:
39
40         (a) Transcript of Records (TOR);
41
42         (b) Diploma or certified copy thereof;
43
44         (c) Form 137, Form 138, or equivalent student records;
45
46         (d) Certificates of Enrollment;
47
48         (e) Certificates of Graduation;

 2         (f) Certificates of Good Moral Character;

 4         (g) Certifications of Units Earned;
 5
 6         (h) Certifications of Academic Standing;
 7
 8         (i) Transfer Credentials;
 9
10         (j) Authentication of school records; and
11
12         (k) Other academic documents and certifications customarily issued by
13         educational institutions.
14
15          SECTION 6. Digital Submission and Processing. - Educational institutions
16   shall establish systems allowing requests for academic documents through electronic
17   means, including online portals, electronic mail, or other digital platforms.
18
19          Whenever feasible, institutions shall provide electronically signed or digitally
20   authenticated copies of academic documents consistent with existing laws, rules, and
21   regulations.
22
23         SECTION 7. Exceptional Circumstances. - The processing period may be
24   extended only under the following circumstances:
25
26         (a) Force majeure events;
27
28         (b) Natural disasters;
29
30         (c) Fire, system failures, cyberattacks, or other extraordinary events affecting
31         institutional operations;
32
33         (d) Retrieval of archived records that are more than ten (10) years old and are
34         not yet digitized; or
35
36         (e) Verification of records involving legal disputes, fraud investigations, or court
37         proceedings.
38
39   In no case shall the extension exceed an additional five (5) working days. The
40   requesting party shall be notified in writing or electronically of the reason for the
41   extension and the expected release date.
42
43         SECTION 8. Prohibition Against Unreasonable Delays. - It shall be
44   unlawful for any educational institution to:
45
46         (a) Require processing periods beyond those provided in this Act without lawful
47         justification;
48
 1         (b) Impose arbitrary administrative requirements not reasonably necessary for
 2         the issuance of requested documents;
 3
 4         (c) Refuse to receive requests without legal basis;
 5
 6         (d) Delay the release of documents due to administrative inefficiency;
 7
 8         (e) Fail to inform the requesting party of deficiencies in submitted requirements
 9         within one (1) working day from receipt of the request; or
10
11         (f) Maintain procedures that unnecessarily prolong the processing and release
12         of academic documents.
13
14          SECTION 9. Student Accounts and Outstanding Obligations. - Nothing
15   in this Act shall prevent educational institutions from enforcing lawful financial
16   obligations consistent with existing laws and regulations.
17
18           However, educational institutions shall comply with all laws, regulations, and
19   policies governing the release of academic records and credentials and shall not use
20   administrative delays as a means to compel payment or settlement of obligations
21   beyond what is allowed by law.
22
