                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Section 15 of Article II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution declares that
the State shall protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill
health consciousness among them. Complementing this constitutional mandate,
Section 11 of Article XIII directs the State to adopt an integrated and
comprehensive approach to health development and endeavor to make essential
goods, health, and other social services available to all the people at affordable
cost, with priority given to the needs of the underprivileged, the sick, the elderly,
persons with disabilities, women, and children. In fulfillment of these
constitutional mandates, this measure seeks to require the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) to establish a dedicated Home-Based
Healthcare Benefit Package covering medically necessary healthcare services
provided in the homes of eligible PhilHealth members. The proposed measure
recognizes that achieving Universal Health Care requires healthcare systems that
not only expand insurance coverage but also improve the accessibility, continuity,
and affordability of healthcare services for patients who are unable to regularly
access hospitals and other institutional healthcare facilities.
       Despite the enactment of Republic Act No. 11223 or the Universal Health
Care Act, significant challenges remain in ensuring equitable access to healthcare
services, particularly for elderly persons, persons with disabilities, bedridden
patients, and individuals living with chronic or terminal illnesses. The
Department of Health's National Objectives for Health 2023–2028 identifies
integrated, people-centered, and community-based healthcare as a strategic
priority for improving service delivery and reducing health inequities.1 Likewise,
the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation recognizes that financial risk
protection remains central to achieving Universal Health Care, yet many Filipinos
continue to incur substantial out-of-pocket expenditures for healthcare services
that are not fully covered under existing PhilHealth benefit packages.2 While
current laws authorize PhilHealth to develop health benefit packages, no statute
specifically mandates the establishment of a dedicated reimbursement package
for medically necessary home-based healthcare services.

       International studies consistently demonstrate the effectiveness of home-
based healthcare as a safe, efficient, and patient-centered model of care. A 2024
Cochrane systematic review found that hospital-at-home programs produce
health outcomes comparable to inpatient hospitalization while improving patient
satisfaction, reducing institutionalization, and lowering healthcare costs for
appropriately selected patients.3 Similarly, a 2021 Cochrane review on home-
based end-of-life care concluded that patients receiving home-based healthcare
were more likely to receive care in their preferred setting, experienced improved
satisfaction, and reduced unnecessary hospital utilization without compromising
the quality of care.4 These findings support the growing international recognition
that expanding professionally supervised home-based healthcare strengthens
health system efficiency while improving patient outcomes.

       Recognizing these policy gaps and supported by both local policy priorities
and international evidence, this proposed legislation institutionalizes a dedicated
PhilHealth Home-Based Healthcare Benefit Package covering physician home
visits, skilled nursing care, home rehabilitation, palliative care, and other
medically necessary healthcare services delivered in patients' homes. The
measure further establishes beneficiary eligibility, provider accreditation,

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:
      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "PhilHealth
Home-Based Healthcare Benefit Act."

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – Pursuant to Article II, Section 15 and
Article XIII, Section 11 of the 1987 Constitution, it is hereby declared the policy
of the State to protect and promote the right to health of the people by ensuring
equitable, accessible, and affordable healthcare services for all Filipinos.
Consistent with Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise known as the Universal
Health Care Act, and Republic Act No. 7875, as amended, otherwise known as
the National Health Insurance Act, the State shall require the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) to establish and provide a dedicated health
insurance benefit package covering medically necessary home-based healthcare
services for eligible members requiring continued treatment outside hospitals or
other institutional healthcare facilities.

       Toward this end, the State shall strengthen financial risk protection,
improve access to quality healthcare, reduce unnecessary hospital admissions,
promote continuity of care, and support patient-centered healthcare delivery
through an effective home-based healthcare benefit package.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

       (a) Home-Based Healthcare Services refer to medically necessary
       individual-based health services provided at the residence of an eligible
       member, whether as an alternative to, or continuation of, healthcare
       provided in a health facility, including physician, nursing, rehabilitative,
       palliative, diagnostic monitoring, and other appropriate healthcare
       services, consistent with Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise known as the
       “Universal Health Care Act,” and other applicable laws, rules, and
       regulations;

       (b) Eligible Members refer to Filipino members of the National Health
       Insurance Program and their qualified dependents who, in accordance with
       applicable laws, rules, regulations, and PhilHealth policies, are medically
       assessed and certified as requiring medically necessary home-based
       healthcare services due to illness, chronic disease, disability, advanced age,
       terminal illness, or other medical conditions requiring continued healthcare
       outside a health facility;

       (c) Home Healthcare Provider refers to a health care provider, health
       facility, health care professional, or other duly authorized healthcare entity
       that is licensed, registered, or otherwise authorized under existing laws,
       rules, and regulations to provide the applicable home-based healthcare

      services and is accredited or contracted by PhilHealth for reimbursement
      under this Act;

      (d) PhilHealth Accreditation refers to the accreditation or contracting
      authority granted by PhilHealth, pursuant to applicable laws, rules,
      regulations, and PhilHealth policies, for purposes of reimbursement under
      this Act. Such accreditation shall not be construed as a substitute for any
      license, permit, registration, or other authority required under existing laws

      SEC. 4. Creation of the PhilHealth Home-Based Healthcare Benefit
Package. – PhilHealth is hereby mandated to develop, establish, implement,
maintain, and periodically review a dedicated Home-Based Healthcare Benefit
Package exclusively covering medically necessary home-based healthcare
services for eligible members.

      The benefit package shall supplement existing PhilHealth benefits under
Republic Act No. 11223 and shall not diminish any benefits otherwise provided
under existing laws.

      SEC. 5. Covered Home-Based Healthcare Services. – The Home-Based
Healthcare Benefit Package shall cover medically necessary services, including
but not limited to:

      (a) Physician home visits;

      (b) Skilled nursing care;

      (c) Home-based physical, occupational, speech, and other medically
      necessary rehabilitation services;

      (d) Home-based palliative and supportive care;


                                                                                  3
      (e) Essential diagnostic monitoring and follow-up services conducted at
      home; and

      (f) Such other medically necessary home-based healthcare services as may
      be determined by PhilHealth in accordance with applicable medical
      standards and evidence-based practice.

      SEC. 6. Eligibility and Covered Beneficiaries. - The benefit package
established under this Act shall be made available to all eligible PhilHealth
members and qualified dependents who have been certified by an attending
physician to require medically necessary home-based healthcare services.

      Priority shall be given to senior citizens, persons with disabilities,
bedridden patients, chronically ill patients, terminally ill patients, and other
individuals who are medically unable to regularly access hospital-based
healthcare services.

      SEC. 7. Accreditation of Home Healthcare Providers. - PhilHealth shall
establish an accreditation framework solely for reimbursement purposes covering
qualified healthcare professionals, healthcare institutions, and home healthcare
organizations authorized to deliver services under this Act.

Such accreditation shall be without prejudice to the licensing, regulatory, and
supervisory powers of the Department of Health, the Professional Regulation
Commission, and other appropriate government agencies under existing laws.

      Only services rendered by PhilHealth-accredited providers shall be eligible
for reimbursement under this Act.

      SEC. 8. Reimbursement Mechanisms and Benefit Payment. – PhilHealth
shall establish standardized reimbursement mechanisms, benefit payment



                                                                               4
systems, claims processing procedures, and payment schedules covering the
services provided under the Home-Based Healthcare Benefit Package.

PhilHealth shall ensure that reimbursement rates are periodically reviewed to
maintain fairness, financial sustainability, and adequacy of benefit coverage.

      SEC. 9. Interaction with Existing Statutory Privileges. – The benefits
provided under this Act shall be coordinated with, and shall be without prejudice
to, the statutory privileges and benefits granted under Republic Act No. 9994,
otherwise known as the “Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010,” Republic Act
No. 10754, otherwise known as the “Act Expanding the Benefits and Privileges
of Persons with Disability,” Republic Act No. 11228, otherwise known as the
“Mandatory PhilHealth Coverage for Persons with Disability Act,” and other
applicable laws.

      The availment, coordination, and computation of the benefits under this
Act and the statutory privileges referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be
undertaken in accordance with existing laws, rules, regulations, and applicable
PhilHealth and Department of Health issuances.

      In the case of a qualified person with disability, the benefits under this Act
shall likewise be coordinated with the mandatory PhilHealth coverage under
Republic Act No. 11228 and the applicable medical-service privileges under
Republic Act No. 10754.

      Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish, repeal, restrict, or
otherwise reduce any health benefit, discount, exemption, or other privilege
already granted under existing laws.

      SEC. 10. Exclusions and Limitations. – Nothing in this Act shall be
construed as regulating or amending existing laws governing:

                                                                                  5
      (a) The licensing of home healthcare agencies or healthcare institutions;

      (b) The professional practice and regulation of healthcare professionals;

      (c) The licensing, accreditation, regulation, or standards governing
      hospitals, infirmaries, nursing homes, or long-term care facilities;

      (d) General home care, custodial care, caregiving services, or social
      welfare services not constituting medically necessary healthcare; and

      (e) Other matters outside the specific establishment and administration of
      the PhilHealth Home-Based Healthcare Benefit Package.

      SEC. 11. Monitoring and Implementation. – PhilHealth, in coordination
with the Department of Health, shall establish an oversight, monitoring, and
evaluation mechanism to ensure the effective implementation of this Act.

      Such mechanisms shall include measures for quality assurance, utilization
review, fraud prevention, claims validation, financial accountability, and periodic
assessment of the adequacy and effectiveness of the benefit package.

      PhilHealth shall submit an annual implementation report to the Office of
the President and Congress containing the utilization, financial performance,
coverage, and recommendations for further improvement of the benefit package.

      SEC. 12. Appropriations. – The amounts necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current funds of the
Philippine Health Insurance Corporation.

      Thereafter, such amounts as may be necessary for the continued
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act, subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and
regulations.

      SEC. 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the PhilHealth Board, in coordination with the
Department of Health and other relevant government agencies and stakeholders,
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

      SEC. 14. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in full force and effect.

      SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, administrative
issuances, rules, and regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions
of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 16. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
