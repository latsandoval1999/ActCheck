                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The chronically overworked state of public school teachers in the
Philippines is well-known. The workload of public school teachers is not only
limited to teaching but also to other non-teaching tasks. Teachers are likewise
expected to participate in the implementation of various government programs,
such as mass immunizations, community mapping, conditional cash transfer,
deworming, feeding, population census, antidrug, election, among others, as the
Philippine Institute for Development Studies documented in 2019. With their
attention divided on a lot of things in their work, they have to think also about
the situation of their children. Because of this, they often forget about their own
career growth and development.

       It is the responsibility of the State to provide means to alleviate the
situation of our respectable teachers. Through this bill, the State aims to provide
welfare to our teachers through the provision of scholarships for them to pursue
career growth and development. Furthermore, it also seeks to extend support to
their children in their education through educational grants. Through these
means, the State hopes to capacitate the teachers to redirect some of their
attention and focus further on teaching the minds of what Dr. Jose Rizal has
termed in his poem A la Juventud Filipina (To the Filipino Youth) the “fair hope
of our fatherland” — the youth.

      The gap this bill fills is plain. Republic Act No. 12288 now builds a
teacher’s career on professional growth, yet the free tuition under Republic Act
No. 10931 reaches undergraduate students only. A teacher who wants a master’s
degree still pays for it out of her own pocket.

     Furthermore, this bill aims to address the need for teachers in the public
education sector. In 2025, the Department of Education reported that public
schools were still short of around 30,000 teachers. By providing other forms of
incentives for teachers, the State will promote public service among teachers.

      This bill is crucial in uplifting the state of public education and the welfare
of one of the most underpaid, yet prolific, workers in our society. Teachers
contribute a lot not only to the future of our country but also to other aspects of
our society. They have undertaken a lot for the benefit of our nation and it is
time to give back to their contributions.

     In view of the foregoing, the urgent passage of this bill is highly
encouraged.

                           AN ACT
   INSTITUTIONALIZING SCHOLARSHIP GRANTS TO PUBLIC SCHOOL
TEACHERS AND EDUCATIONAL CASH ASSISTANCE TO THEIR CHILDREN
  IN TERTIARY EDUCATION, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Scholarship
Grants for Public School Teachers Act”.

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It shall be the policy of the State to
provide access to free education to public school teachers and their children by
providing for them institutionalized scholarship grants and educational cash
assistance, respectively. The scholarship grants to public school teachers will
give them high morale and self-esteem; providing children of the public school
teachers with educational cash assistance will lessen their social and economic
burden and encourage them to focus on their studies.

      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For the purpose of this Act, the term:

      1.    “Children of public school teachers” shall refer to the legitimate,
            illegitimate, and legally adopted children of public school teachers;
      2.    “Educational cash assistance” shall refer to cash allowance provided
            to the children of public school teachers in tertiary education; and
      3.    “Public school teachers” shall refer to all persons engaged in
            classroom teaching, at any level of institution, on a full-time basis
            or part-time basis, including guidance counselors, school librarians,
            industrial arts or vocational instructors, and all other persons
            performing supervisory and/or administrative functions in all
            schools, colleges, and universities operated by the government or its
             political subdivisions, but shall not include school nurses, school
             physicians, school dentists, and other school employees.

      SECTION 4. Scholarship Grant and Educational Cash Assistance. – The
scholarship grants for public school teachers shall cover graduate/post-
graduate education, including tuition and other school fees and such allowances
as the implementing rules and regulations may provide, while the educational
cash assistance covers their children in tertiary education, including technical-
vocational education and training.

       SECTION 5. Eligibility of Public School Teachers. – Public school teachers
who are in active service shall be eligible to avail of the scholarship grants to
enroll in any graduate or postgraduate courses provided that the public school
teacher shall have been in active duty for at least two (2) consecutive years
immediately prior to the date of application for the grant. Priority shall be given
to teachers assigned in rural, remote, and underserved areas.

      SECTION 6. Eligibility of Children of Public School Teachers. – The children
of public school teachers who are in active service shall be eligible to avail of the
educational cash assistance. The children of public school teachers who died or
were permanently disabled while in active service shall likewise be eligible.

      SECTION 7. Eligibility of Retired Public School Teachers. – Retired public
school teachers may avail of the educational cash assistance for their children.
However, their children shall be entitled to only a proportional amount of
educational cash assistance, as may be determined in the implementing rules
and regulations.

       SECTION 8. Schools Where Scholarship Grants are Applicable. – The
scholarship grant shall be applicable in all state universities and colleges and
local universities and colleges throughout the country, and in such private
higher education institutions as the Commission on Higher Education (CHED)
may recognize for this purpose. The children of public school teachers shall be
qualified to avail of the educational cash assistance only upon passing all the
requirements set by the respective schools.

       SECTION 9. Forfeiture of Grant. – Public school teachers who have been
found guilty, by final decision and after due process, of professional misconduct
shall forfeit their eligibility to avail of the grant. They shall also forfeit the
eligibility to avail of the grant for their children: Provided, That children already
receiving educational cash assistance shall continue to receive it until the end of
the current academic year.

      SECTION 10. Implementing Agency. – The Department of Education
(DepEd) and the CHED shall be the implementing agencies for this Act. Within
ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the above agencies shall formulate
the implementing rules and regulations for this Act, in consultation with the
Department of Budget and Management, the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority, teachers’ organizations, and other stakeholders.

       SECTION 11. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations
of the DepEd and the CHED. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its
continued implementation shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 12. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions or parts unaffected
shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules, and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 14. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
