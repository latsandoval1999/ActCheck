                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       In light of the ongoing economic challenges faced by Filipinos and the pressing
need for fiscal reforms that prioritize the welfare of the people, the reduction of the
personal income tax minimum threshold to P350,000 is proposed. This initiative aims
to alleviate the financial burdens on low- and middle-income earners, fostering
economic resilience and promoting equitable taxation.


       By allowing individuals earning up to P350,000 annually to retain their income
without the burden of income tax, we empower them to increase their consumption.
This, in turn, will invigorate local businesses and stimulate economic activity across
various sectors.


       The proposed measure also recognizes that the rising cost of essential goods
and services has continued to erode the purchasing power of the Filipino households.
Providing tax relief to low- and middle-income earners will enable them to better meet
their daily needs, improve the quality of life, and build greater financial security.


       Moreover, increasing the disposable income of working Filipinos encourages
greater household spending, which serves as a key driver of the country's economic
growth.
