                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Good governance must be measured not only by the programs implemented, funds spent, or
awards received by a local government unit, but by the actual improvements it brings to the lives of
its people.

        For many Filipinos, the true measure of government performance is simple: whether children
receive quality education, families have access to adequate healthcare, communities have better
livelihood opportunities, and people enjoy safe, peaceful, and dignified lives.

       Republic Act No. 11292, otherwise known as the "Seal of Good Local Governance Act of
2019," provides an important framework for recognizing local government units that demonstrate
good governance and effective public service. However, as communities continue to face changing
economic, social, and development challenges, there is a need to strengthen the existing system by
measuring not only institutional compliance but also actual human development outcomes.

        This proposed measure seeks to consolidate and strengthen the performance measurement and
incentive systems for local government units by institutionalizing a Human Development
Meritocracy framework. It introduces measurable criteria in health, education, standard of living, and
inflation, alongside existing governance standards, to ensure that local government performance is
evaluated based on its tangible impact on people's lives.

        The measure also strengthens coordination among national government agencies, improves
data collection and sharing, provides technical assistance to local governments, and establishes a
fairer system of assessment that considers the individual circumstances and historical performance of
each locality.

       Furthermore, it seeks to provide meaningful incentives to well-performing local government
units while ensuring that those experiencing development setbacks are assessed fairly, taking into
account circumstances beyond their control.
       Ultimately, the success of local governance should not be determined merely by the
recognition received by an institution, but by the lives improved, opportunities created, and
communities uplifted through effective public service.

         A Seal of Good Local Governance must represent more than compliance with standards. It
must represent a government that delivers, a community that progresses, and a people whose quality
of life continues to improve.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                                AN ACT
 CONSOLIDATING ALL PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT TOOLS AND REWARDS
   SYSTEMS FOR ALL LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS BY INSTITUTIONALIZING
  MERITOCRACY IN THE PHILIPPINES, AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS OF
 REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11292, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "SEAL OF GOOD LOCAL
                        GOVERNANCE ACT OF 2019"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "SGLG Reform Act."

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to recognize the
good performance of local government units (LGUs) in transparency and accountability in the use of
public funds, preparedness for challenges posed by disasters, sensitivity to the needs of vulnerable and
marginalized sectors of society, implementation of health programs, investment and employment
promotion, protection of constituency from threats to life and damage to property, and safeguarding
the integrity of the environment. In such recognition the State hopes to encourage all LGUs to take on
greater challenges, encourage outcome-based performance, and [to] reward local governments for their
pursuing [the] POLICIES THAT PROMOTE the general welfare of their constituency and in enforcing
existing laws.

TO THIS END, A UNIFIED, PRACTICAL, AND REAL-TIME PERFORMANCE
MEASUREMENT TOOL SHALL BE INSTITUTIONALIZED TO GAUGE THE
EFFECTIVENESS OF LGUS IN ACHIEVING THEIR RESPECTIVE LOCAL DEVELOPMENT
OUTCOMES IN THE AREAS OF HEALTH, EDUCATION, STANDARDS OF LIVING, AND
PEACE AND ORDER. LGUS UNDER THIS ACT SHALL INCLUDE PROVINCES, CITIES,
MUNICIPALITIES, AND BARANGAYS.

THE STATE FURTHER RECOGNIZES THAT GOOD GOVERNANCE MUST BE MEASURED
NOT ONLY BY INSTITUTIONAL COMPLIANCE BUT ALSO BY ITS TANGIBLE IMPACT ON
THE LIVES OF CONSTITUENTS. ACCORDINGLY, THE PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT
SYSTEM SHALL ADOPT HUMAN DEVELOPMENT INDICATORS THAT REFLECT
IMPROVEMENTS IN THE QUALITY OF LIFE, ACCESS TO ESSENTIAL SERVICES, AND
SOCIOECONOMIC OPPORTUNITIES OF THE PEOPLE.

SEC. 6. Section 7 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

"SEC. 7. Criteria. – For the purpose of this Act, TWO CRITERIA GOVERNING THE
SEAL SHALL BE APPLIED ON DIFFERENT ASPECTS OF LOCAL
GOVERNANCE: (1) 'GOVERNANCE CRITERIA' WHICH EMPHASIZES
GOVERNANCE AND INSTITUTIONAL STRENGTHENING, AND (2) 'HUMAN
DEVELOPMENT MERITOCRACY CRITERIA' WHICH FOCUSES ON GAUGING
DEVELOPMENT OUTCOMES THAT AN LGU IS EXPECTED TO ACHIEVE. The
dimensions in each criteria category shall be understood and interpreted as follows:

(1) GOVERNANCE CRITERIA

The existing governance criteria under Republic Act No. 11292 and its implementing
rules and regulations shall continue to apply, subject to the review and updating of the
performance indicators by the Council.

(2) HUMAN DEVELOPMENT MERITOCRACY CRITERIA

(a) HEALTH AND SOCIAL DIMENSION – THIS DIMENSION MEASURES THE
OVERALL QUALITY OF HEALTH WITHIN A LOCALITY THROUGH THE
LONGEVITY OF LIFE OF ITS CONSTITUENTS.

(b) EDUCATION – THIS DIMENSION MEASURES THE ACCESS OF A
LOCALITY'S CONSTITUENTS TO QUALITY EDUCATION.

(c) STANDARD OF LIVING – THIS DIMENSION MEASURES THE LEVEL OF
INCOME OF CONSTITUENTS AND ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITIES WITHIN A
LOCALITY.

(d) INFLATION – THIS DIMENSION MEASURES INFLATION AND ITS EFFECT
ON A LOCALITY'S CONSTITUENTS.

Each dimension under the Human Development Meritocracy Criteria shall be measured
using a set of indicators to be formulated and determined by the concerned committee
and approved by the Council based on acceptable national or international standards,
together with the data in the Philippine Human Development Report.

In determining such, the Council shall prioritize indicators that can be generated from
national and local government sources without the need for primary data-gathering
methodologies such as statistical surveys, census, and registers.
     The set of indicators for each dimension shall be used to compute the qualifications of
     each LGU and assess based on their respective historical growth or performance so that
     each level of LGU competes against itself and not as against other LGUs.

     The Council shall continuously explore alternative statistical indicators for the Human
     Development Meritocracy Criteria as may be necessary and appropriate for purposes of
     faster data collection and processing. These indicators shall be updated annually by the
     concerned agency and shared with the concerned LGUs to ensure that the criteria
     effectively reflect the key dimensions of the Human Development Meritocracy Criteria
     as stipulated under this Act and are more readily transparent and accessible for efficient
     collection and processing of the LGUs and implementing agencies.

     The Council shall ensure that performance assessments are context-sensitive and take into
     account the fiscal capacity, geographic conditions, population characteristics, and
     socioeconomic circumstances of each LGU. No LGU shall be unfairly disadvantaged
     solely because of factors beyond its reasonable control."

        SEC. 16. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is held unconstitutional or
