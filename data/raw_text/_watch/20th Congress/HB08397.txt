                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

     This bill seeks to amend Republic Act No. 11371, otherwise known as the
Murang Kuryente Act, to further reduce electricity rates by optimizing the use of the
Malampaya Fund and minimizing the universal charges imposed on consumers.

        Despite the enactment of RA 11371, Filipino consumers continue to bear high
electricity costs, partly due to universal charges for stranded contract costs and
stranded debts assumed by the Power Sector Assets and Liabilities Management
Corporation (PSALM) under the Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001. These
charges, which are passed on to consumers, contribute significantly to overall power
rates.

       To provide more immediate and substantial relief, this measure allocates PhP
208,000,000,000 from the net national government share of the Malampaya Fund to
cover PSALM’s stranded contract costs, stranded debts, and anticipated shortfalls.
By directly funding these obligations, the bill reduces the need to collect universal
charges from electricity consumers, thereby lowering power rates nationwide.

        The measure further mandates that twenty percent (20%) of the annual
approved Universal Charges for Missionary Electrification (UCME) be sourced from
the Malampaya Fund, subject to amended implementing rules and regulations, until
the exhaustion of the fund. This ensures continued support for missionary
electrification while easing the financial burden on consumers.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
FURTHER DECREASING THE PRICES OF ELECTRICITY IN THE COUNTRY,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11371, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS “THE MURANG KURYENTE ACT”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Mas Murang Kuryente
Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the state to
protect public interest by ensuring the provision of reliable, secure, and affordable
supply of electric power to consumers. Towards this end, the state shall implement
policies and programs to ensure transparent and reasonable prices of electricity to
consumers by minimizing the universal charges for stranded contract costs and
stranded debts.

      SEC. 3. Section 4 of Republic Act No. 11371, otherwise known as the “Murang
Kuryente Act”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

              “Section 4. Use of Malampaya Fund.— x x x

             “IN ADDITION, TWENTY PERCENT (20%) OF THE
        ANNUAL    APPROVED     UNIVERSAL   CHARGES   FOR
        MISSIONARY   ELECTRIFICATION   (UCME)  SHALL  BE
        COLLECTED FROM THE MALAMPAYA FUND, UNTIL THE
        EXHAUSTION OF SAID FUND AND SUBJECT TO THE
        AMENDED IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS OF
        THIS ACT.

            “THE COMMISSION ON AUDIT (COA) SHALL STRICTLY
        MONITOR THE UTILIZATION OF SUCH FUNDS TO ENSURE
        TRANSPARENCY,  ACCOUNTABILITY,   AND   EXCLUSIVE



                                           1
       APPLICATION TO MISSIONARY ELECTRIFICATION PROJECTS
       AND ACTIVITIES”

       SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DOE and DOF, in consultation with the DBM, the
Bureau of the Treasury, and the PSALM shall amend the Implementing Rules and
Regulations of Republic Act No. 11371.

        SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
