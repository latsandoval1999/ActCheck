                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to provide financial assistance and other incentives to indigent
individuals who intend to earn a livelihood as public utility vehicle (PUV) drivers. It
provides subsidies for licensing-related expenses and transportation assistance.

       In June 2026, the Department of Social Welfare and Development reported that it
had distributed cash assistance to over one point eight (1.8) million beneficiaries from
various sectors of the public transportation industry nationwide1. In the Cordillera
alone, more than six thousand (6,000) PUV drivers were included in the government’s
Fuel Discount Program in June 2026, which reflects the continuing need for assistance
among transport workers2. Existing government programs provide reprieve for fuel
costs but do not address other expenses that an indigent individual incurs before
becoming a PUV driver. This may include training, medical certification, clearances,
examinations, and other licensing-related expenses.

        The proposed measure grants a twenty percent (20%) discount in the availment
of government-issued documents that the Land Transportation Office may require in
obtaining a valid driver’s license. It also gives qualified applicants priority access to
available free driving courses and provides a daily transportation allowance for
required training attendance. These forms of assistance reduce both the direct costs of
licensing and the incidental expenses that applicants incur while completing the
requirements.

      No qualified citizen should have to abandon a lawful opportunity to work
simply because the initial costs of meeting government requirements are beyond reach.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
PROVIDING SUBSIDIES AND OTHER INCENTIVES FOR INDIGENT PUBLIC
UTILITY DRIVERS IN THE PAYMENT OF FEES AND CHARGES FOR
CERTAIN CERTIFICATES AND CLEARANCES REQUIRED BY THE LAND
TRANSPORTATION OFFICE IN APPLYING FOR A PROFESSIONAL DRIVER’S
LICENSE

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Indigent Public Utility
Driver’s License Application Subsidy Act”.

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to provide accessible
government services and appropriate assistance to qualified individuals who seek
employment or livelihood opportunities as drivers of public utility vehicles.

       Towards this end, the State shall provide financial assistance and other
incentives to indigent applicants for a Professional Driver’s License to reduce the
cost of required certificates, clearances, training, examinations, and other related
requirements, without compromising the qualifications and standards for
professional drivers.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

          a. Applicant refers to an individual seeking to apply for a license to
       operate a public utility vehicle as a means of livelihood. Qualified applicants
       under this Act are individuals whose per capita income falls below the official
       poverty threshold as defined by the government as identified and certified by
       the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) based on the criteria set under
       Republic Act No. 11315, otherwise known as the “Community-Based
       Monitoring System (CBMS) Act”; and

          b. Public Utility Vehicle (PUV) refers to a motor vehicle considered as a
       public transport conveyance or common carrier duly registered with the Land


                                            1
       Transportation Office (LTO) and granted a franchise by the Land
       Transportation Franchising Regulatory Board (LTFRB).

       SEC. 4. Subsidies and Other Incentives for Qualified Applicants. – Under this Act,
qualified applicants shall be granted a twenty percent (20%) discount in the
payment of the following clearances, certificates, and fees required by the LTO in
applying for a Professional Driver’s License:

          a.​ Birth certificate from the PSA;

          b.​ Medical certificate from an LTO-accredited clinic or physician;

          c.​ Clearance from the National Bureau of Investigation (NBI);

          d.​ Clearance from the Philippine National Police (PNP);

          e.​ Enrollment fees for Theoretical and Practical Driving Courses given by
       LTO-accredited and Technical Education and Skills Development Authority
       (TESDA)-certified driving schools;

          f.​ Examination Fees; and

          g.​ Other government-issued documents and fees that the LTO may
       require.

       Priority registration shall be given to applicants in cases where Theoretical
and Practical Driving Courses in LTO-accredited and TESDA-certified driving
schools can be enrolled free of charge. Furthermore, applicants shall be given a fixed
daily allowance for expenses incurred in the commute to and from their chosen
driving school.

        SEC. 5. Penalties. – Any public officer or employee who refuses or fails to
provide the benefits granted to qualified applicants outlined in Section 4 of this Act
shall, upon conviction, be subject to a fine of not less than Five thousand pesos (Php
5,000.00) but not more than Twenty thousand pesos (Php 20,000.00). Prosecution for
an offense outlined in this Act shall be without prejudice to any liability for violation
of any other existing laws, including civil service law, rules and regulations.

         SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within six (6) months from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Transportation (DOTr), in consultation with
the PSA, Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), NBI, PNP,
Department of Justice (DOJ), Department of Health (DOH), the TESDA, and the
National Anti-Poverty Commission (NAPC) shall issue the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

        SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any section or provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof shall not be
affected and thereby remain in full force and effect.
