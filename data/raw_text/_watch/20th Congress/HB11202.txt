                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE


The Sierra Madre Mountain Range, known as the "Backbone of Luzon," rising to 6,283 feet and
spanning over 540 kilometers, serves as the country’s longest mountain range and a natural shield
against typhoons entering the Pacific side. Aside from its role as a natural typhoon barrier, the
Sierra Madre Mountain Range is home to around 40 percent of the Philippines’ forest cover and
supports rich biodiversity, including approximately 3,500 plant species, 58 percent of which are
endemic and serves as a crucial carbon sink, with forested areas covering approximately 1.4 million
hectares. 1


Despite its ecological importance, the Sierra Madre Mountain Range is threatened by illegal
logging, mining, unregulated land conversion, and deforestation. Existing conservation efforts
remain disconnected due to jurisdictional overlaps among local government units (LGUs) and
various national agencies.


This bill seeks to create the Sierra Madre Conservation and Development Authority (SMCDA) to
establish a unified, centralized, and dedicated governing body tasked with the holistic management,
protection, restoration, and sustainable development of the Sierra Madre region.


Specifically, the creation of the Sierra Madre Conservation and Development Authority (SMCDA),
an administrative body, shall formulate, coordinate, and execute a comprehensive conservation

                           AN ACT
 ENHANCING THE PROTECTION AND DEVELOPMENT OF THE SIERRA
MADRE RANGE IN THE ISLAND OF LUZON, CREATING FOR THE PURPOSE
THE SIERRA MADRE CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY,
  DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, AND PROVIDING FUNDS
                         THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:


        SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Sierra Madre Conservation and
Development Authority Act”


        SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.- It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect, conserve, restore, and sustainably manage the Sierra Madre Mountain Range and its
ecosystems, forests, watersheds, biodiversity, and other natural resources, recognizing their vital
role in climate resilience, disaster risk reduction, water security, food security, and the socio-
economic development of communities within and adjoining the Sierra Madre Region.


        SECTION 3. Creation of the Sierra Madre Conservation and Development Authority-
A government corporation named the Sierra Madre Conservation and Development Authority
(Authority) is hereby created, to be organized within ninety (90) days after the approval of this Act.
The Authority’s jurisdiction covers the Sierra Madre Range and its surrounding areas,
encompassing the provinces of Cagayan, Isabela, Nueva Vizcaya, Quirino, Nueva Ecija, Aurora,
Bulacan, Rizal, Laguna, and Quezon.

                                                                                                              3
       SECTION 4. Purposes and Functions of the Authority- The Authority shall perform
the following functions:
           a) Establish the boundary and conduct a comprehensive survey of the physical,
               natural, and socio-economic resources of the Sierra Madre Region. Based on this,
               the Authority shall draft a comprehensive, detailed plan to conserve and utilize
               resources, promoting the rapid social and economic development of the area,
               subject to the approval of the Department of Economy, Planning, and
               Development (DEPDev). The management of protected areas shall remain under
               existing laws, subject to the monitoring and supervision of the Authority.
           b) Provide necessary planning, management, and technical assistance to prospective
               and existing investors in the Region.
           c) Make recommendations to appropriate agencies on providing financial, technical,
               or physical support and assistance to agricultural, industrial, and commercial
               projects, generally according to the level of priority accorded by the Authority.
           d) Pass upon and approve or disapprove all plans, programs, and projects proposed
               by local government offices, public corporations, private persons, or enterprises
               within the Region that are related to the Authority’s vision. The Authority must
               issue clearance for approved plans within thirty (30) days.
           e) Engage in agriculture, industrial, or other activities contributing directly to the
               socio-economic development of the Region, including organizing, financing, or
               investing with private persons or entities, but excluding activities that engage the
               public interest unless specified by law.
           f) Plan, program, finance, or undertake infrastructure projects like river control, flood
               control, waterworks, sewerage works, and resettlement of populations within the
               Region. The Authority may collect reasonable fees for projects, subject to
               DEPDev approval.
           g) Lend or facilitate financial assistance or act as surety/guarantor to agricultural,
               industrial, and commercial enterprises.
           h) Require cities and municipalities in the Region to pass appropriate zoning
               ordinances and other regulatory measures necessary to carry out the Authority's
               objectives.
           i) Carry out the Authority’s projects and programs, notwithstanding existing water
               rights over public waters within the Region.

            j) Undertake studies on the conservation, preservation, exploration, development,
               and maintenance of the Sierra Madre Region, subject to DEPDev approval, with
               support from national and local government units.


      SECTION. 5. Regulatory Fees. – The Authority is empowered to collect fees for the
beneficial use of all resources, including recreational use, resource extraction, energy
development, and waste disposal. These fees shall be used for the management and development
of the Region, and the rates must be approved by the President of the Philippines.


      SECTION. 6. Corporate Powers. – The Authority shall have the power to:
            a) Succeed on its corporate name;
            b) Sue and be sued;
            c) Adopt, alter, and use a corporate seal;
            d) Adopt, amend, and repeal its by-laws;
            e) Enter into contracts;
            f) Acquire, buy, purchase, lease, sell, or otherwise dispose of real and personal
               property;
            g) Exercise the right of eminent domain necessary to achieve its objectives;
            h) Borrow funds from local or foreign financial institutions;
            i) Purchase, hold, or dispose of shares of capital stock or any bond, securities, or
               other evidence of indebtedness, provided it does not invest its funds in any highly
               risky debt instruments, commercial banks, or highly speculative stocks;
            j) Carry on its business;
            k) Issue rules and regulations to implement the powers and purposes of the Authority,
               subject to DEPDev approval and subsequent publication.


      SECTION 7. Capitalization and Financing.- The Authority shall have an authorized
capital of One billion pesos (P1,000,000,000.00). The initial operating capital is set at One
hundred million pesos (P100,000,000.00), authorized to be appropriated out of the National
Treasury.


      SECTION. 8. Operating Expenses. – For operating expenses, One hundred million
pesos (P100,000,000.00) is appropriated annually for five (5) years from the general fund, unless
the DEPDev recommends a higher amount.


                                                                                                5
      SECTION. 9. Tax Subsidy. – The Authority may avail of the tax expenditure subsidy
administered by the Fiscal Incentives Review Board (FIRB).


      SECTION 10. Guarantee by the Government. The Republic of the Philippines guarantees
the payment by the Authority of the principal and interest of bonds, debentures, collateral, notes,
or other obligations. Should the Authority fail to pay, the Secretary of Finance shall pay the
amount and recoup it from the Authority.


      SECTION 11. Subsidiaries. - The Authority has the power to form, establish, organize,
and maintain subsidiary corporations. The Authority shall initially own fifty-one percent (51%)
of the capital stock of a subsidiary and shall hold the majority seats on the subsidiary’s Board of
Directors.


      SECTION 12. Board of Directors: Composition. – The corporate powers are vested in a
Board of Directors composed of nine (9) members: the Executive Secretary, the Secretaries of
DEPDev, DENR, DTI, and DOE, the Chairperson of the NCIP, a Provincial Governor, a city
or Municipal Mayor, and the General Manager. All members are appointed by the President of
the Philippines.


      SECTION 13. Board Meetings. – The Board shall meet at least once a month.


      SECTION 14. Quorum; Effect of Vacancies. – A majority of members constitutes a
quorum. Vacancies do not impair the powers of the Board as long as a quorum exists.


      SECTION 15. Per Diems and Allowances. – Members shall receive per diem for every
meeting attended, not to exceed fifty percent (50%) of their basic monthly pay.


      SECTION 16. Power and Functions of the Board of Directors. – The Board shall
formulate rules and regulations, appoint and fix compensation for officials, suspend or remove
members, approve the annual or supplemental budgets, and perform other necessary functions.




                                                                                                 6
      SECTION 17. Power and Functions of the General Manager. – The General Manager,
who is the Chief Executive Officer, shall submit policies for Board consideration, execute and
administer approved policies and projects, and supervise the internal operation of the Authority.
      SECTION 18. Residence. – The General Manager shall establish residence within the
Sierra Madre Region and shall not engage in any business or profession that conflicts with official
duties.


      SECTION 19. Activities of the Authority: Key Officials. – The General Manager shall be
assisted by a Senior Deputy General Manager. The Authority shall have departments covering
Administrative, Legal, Finance, Project Management, Planning and Project Development, and
Engineering and Technical Services.


      SECTION 20. Audit. – The Board shall appoint an Internal Auditor. The Commission
on Audit (COA) shall be the full-time auditor of the Authority and its subsidiaries.


      SECTION 21. Compensation. – All personnel benefits and compensation shall be subject
to the rules of the Civil Service Commission.


      SECTION 22. Authority of the Governance Commission on GOCCs. – The Authority
shall be under the general supervision of the Governance Commission on GOCCs (GCG).


      SECTION 23. Legal Counsel. – The Government Corporate Counsel shall serve as the
ex-officio legal counsel of the Authority.


      SECTION 24. Supervision. – The Authority shall be under the direct control and
supervision of the President of the Philippines.


      SECTION 25. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Board shall promulgate the
necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act within ninety (90)
days of approval and publish them in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation.


      SECTION 26. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held unconstitutional
or invalid, the other unaffected provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 27. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive issuances, or parts thereof that are
inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.


      SECTION 28. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.




      Approved.
