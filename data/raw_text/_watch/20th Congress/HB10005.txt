                             AN ACT
PROHIBITING THE IMPOSITION OR COLLECTION OF PARTICIPATION FEES IN
 MOTOR VEHICLE COMPREHENSIVE INSURANCE CLAIMS, AND FOR OTHER
                            PURPOSES

                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

        A motor vehicle comprehensive insurance policy protects vehicle owners against financial
loss from damage to or loss of the insured vehicle due to accidents, theft, fire, and natural disasters,
subject to the terms and conditions of the policy.
        In practice, many motor vehicle comprehensive insurance policies require the insured to
pay a “participation fee” or “deductible” for every claim, which may range from Two Thousand
Pesos (P2,000.00) to Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) or higher depending on the policy. While
risk-sharing mechanisms may be used in insurance pricing, participation fees may operate to
unduly reduce the actual benefit received by the insured, particularly where the loss was caused
by another motor vehicle.
        This bill seeks to protect consumers by prohibiting the imposition and collection of
participation fees (deductibles) in motor vehicle comprehensive insurance, ensuring transparency,
fairness, and the availability of prompt indemnification, while recognizing the regulatory authority
of the Insurance Commissioner.
       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

 2          Section 1. Insertion of a New Section in the Insurance Code. –
 3
 4          A new section is hereby inserted in Republic Act No. 10607, otherwise known as “The
 5          Insurance Code,” to be numbered as Section 391-A, immediately after Section 391 thereof,
 6          to read as follows:
 7
 8                         “SEC. 391-A. Prohibition on Participation Fee in Motor
 9                 Vehicle Comprehensive Insurance. — The owner of a damaged motor
10                 vehicle covered by motor vehicle comprehensive insurance shall not be
11                 required to pay any participation fee (deductible) for the cost of repair
12                 or the replacement of parts thereof.
13
14                         Any claim for damage sustained by a motor vehicle covered by
15                 motor vehicle comprehensive insurance shall be charged against the
16                 insurer, and shall be paid without requiring the insured to pay,
17                 advance, or shoulder any participation fee, and without deducting any
18                 participation fee from the proceeds of the claim, without prejudice to
 1                  the right of the party paying the claim to recover against the owner or
 2                  insurer of the motor vehicle responsible for the accident
 3
 4          For purposes of this section, “participation fee” or “deductible” refers to any
 5          amount required to be paid, advanced, shouldered, or deducted from the proceeds
 6          of the claim by the insured as a condition for the processing, approval, or payment
 7          of a claim for repair or replacement of parts.
