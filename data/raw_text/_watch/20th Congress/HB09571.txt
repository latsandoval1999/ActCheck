                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Constitution of the Republic of the Philippines recognizes the
indispensable role of women in nation-building and mandates the State to ensure
substantive equality between women and men. In furtherance of this constitutional
policy, the government continues to strengthen programs that empower women
through education, livelihood, and employment opportunities.

         Despite existing laws promoting gender equality and women’s welfare, many
women in the Province of Tarlac—particularly those from rural communities, low-
income households, solo-parent families, indigenous groups, and displaced
workers—continue to face limited access to technical-vocational training and
sustainable sources of livelihood. Economic barriers, lack of accessible training
facilities, and insufficient industry-linked programs hinder many Tarlaqueñas from
fully participating in the local economy.

        Tarlac is strategically positioned as a growing center for agriculture, commerce,
logistics, tourism, and emerging industries in Central Luzon. However, there remains
a need for a dedicated institution that will equip women with specialized technical
skills responsive to the present and future demands of industry and entrepreneurship
within the province.

      This proposed measure seeks to establish a TESDA Women’s Center in the
Province of Tarlac to serve as a specialized training and development hub focused on
women empowerment, skills enhancement, and economic inclusion. The Center shall
provide accessible and gender-responsive technical-vocational education and training
programs designed to improve employability, encourage entrepreneurship, and
expand livelihood opportunities for women across the province.

       The proposed TESDA Women’s Center shall prioritize programs in areas such
as digital technology, food processing, agri-based enterprises, tourism services,
creative industries, renewable energy, dressmaking, wellness services, and other in-
demand sectors identified by local labor market studies. It shall likewise provide
entrepreneurship training, financial literacy seminars, career counseling, and
employment facilitation services in coordination with local government units,
educational institutions, and private sector partners.

      Moreover, the Center shall create a safe and inclusive learning environment for
women by integrating support mechanisms, including gender-sensitive policies,
psychosocial support, and community-based outreach programs, particularly for
disadvantaged groups.

       By investing in the skills and capabilities of women in Tarlac, this measure aims
to strengthen local economic development, reduce unemployment and
underemployment, and promote greater participation of women in productive and
leadership roles within their communities.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
        ESTABLISHING THE TESDA WOMEN’S TRAINING AND
        DEVELOPMENT CENTER IN THE PROVINCE OF TARLAC,
     PROVIDING FOR ITS FUNCTIONS, APPROPRIATIONS, AND FOR
                        OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1   SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Tarlac TESDA Women’s
 2   Center Act.”

 3   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 4   uphold the rights and welfare of women by promoting equal access to education, skills
 5   development, employment, and livelihood opportunities.

 6   Toward this end, the State shall support programs that strengthen the economic
 7   participation of women, particularly those belonging to disadvantaged and
 8   marginalized sectors, through accessible and gender-responsive technical-vocational
 9   education and training.

10   The State likewise recognizes the important role of local development initiatives in
11   empowering communities and improving the quality of life of women and their
12   families in the Province of Tarlac.
 1   SECTION 3. Objectives. — The TESDA Women’s Training and Development Center
 2   in Tarlac shall pursue the following objectives:

 3      a. Provide accessible technical-vocational education and specialized skills
 4         training programs for women in the Province of Tarlac;
 5      b. Enhance women’s employability, productivity, and entrepreneurial capability
 6         through competency-based training;
 7      c. Encourage women’s participation in emerging industries, digital technology,
 8         agribusiness, tourism, manufacturing, and other high-growth sectors;
 9      d. Promote gender-sensitive and safe learning environments for women trainees;
10      e. Develop community-based livelihood opportunities and sustainable income-
11         generating activities; and
12      f. Strengthen partnerships among TESDA, local government units, educational
13         institutions, industries, and other stakeholders for employment generation and
14         enterprise development.

15   SECTION 4. Establishment of the TESDA Women’s Training and Development
16   Center. — There shall be established in the Province of Tarlac a TESDA Women’s
17   Training and Development Center, hereinafter referred to as the “Center,” which shall
18   serve as the primary technical-vocational training institution dedicated to the
19   education, empowerment, and economic advancement of women in the province.

20   The Center shall be established in coordination with the Provincial Government of
21   Tarlac and concerned local government units.

22   SECTION 5. Functions of the Center. — The Center shall perform the following
23   functions:

24      a. Conduct technical-vocational education and training programs consistent with
25         TESDA competency standards and local industry requirements;
26      b. Offer skills upgrading, retraining, entrepreneurship, and livelihood
27         development programs for women;
28      c. Provide competency assessment and certification services for graduates and
29         trainees;
30      d. Facilitate job placement, employment referrals, and career development
31         assistance;
32      e. Establish linkages with private enterprises, cooperatives, and government
33         agencies for apprenticeship and employment opportunities;
34      f. Implement gender-responsive programs and support services, including
35         counseling and community outreach activities;
36      g. Maintain records and databases of trainees and graduates for monitoring and
37         employment tracking purposes; and
 1      h. Perform such other functions necessary to achieve the objectives of this Act.

 2   SECTION 6. Administration and Management. — The Technical Education and Skills
 3   Development Authority (TESDA) shall serve as the lead implementing agency
 4   responsible for the supervision, administration, and operation of the Center.

 5   TESDA shall coordinate with the Provincial Government of Tarlac, local government
 6   units, state universities and colleges, and other concerned agencies in the
 7   implementation of programs and services under this Act.

 8   SECTION 7. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation
 9   of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of TESDA. Thereafter,
10   such amount as may be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall
11   be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

12   SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60) days from the
13   effectivity of this Act, TESDA, in consultation with the Philippine Commission on
14   Women (PCW), Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Department of Trade
15   and Industry (DTI), Department of Social Work and Development (DSWD), local
16   government unit (LGUs), and relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary
17   rules and regulations to effectively implement the provisions of this Act.

18   SECTION 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
19   unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
20   full force and effect.

21   SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
22   regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

23   SECTION 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
24   publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
25   circulation.
