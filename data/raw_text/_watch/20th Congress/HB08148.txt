                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       At present, Republic Act 9064, known as the Sports Incentives Act, confines the
awarding of cash bonuses to medal winners in the Olympics, World Championships, Asian
Games and SEA Games. Similarly, coaches and trainers of athletes involved in these
competitions are the only ones entitled to cash bonuses. Athletes and coaches of athletes who
won medals in international competitions outside of these tournaments are not entitled to
bonuses from the government.

        It is high time that athletes representing the country in other international competitions
similar to the four competitions mentioned above also be recognized and be given bonuses
for bringing pride and honor to the Philippines. Athletes like the Philippine Dragon Boat team
who continuously win in various international tournaments like the Asian Beach Games.

      Further, athletes competing in the Paralympic Games should also be included in the
coverage of Republic Act 9064.

         Also, athletes participating in sports that are excluded in the Olympic program like
billiards, bowling and chess should also be given recognition upon bringing home medals from
international tournaments.

        Past achievements in these non-Olympic sports omitted. There should be no
discrimination in sports and equality must be applied to our deserving athletes, whose victories
in international competitions and championships inspire and unify our people.

       In view of the foregoing, immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

SEC 3. Section 10 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:
              Section 10. Incentives for Coaches and Trainers – Coaches and
              trainers shall also be entitled to cash awards if they have personally
              trained and rendered service to the athletes or teams WHO WIN IN
              INTERNATIONAL COMPETITIONS at least one (1) year prior to
              the international competition. Certification to this effect by the
              individual athlete or team captain shall be in writing, DULY
              ATTESTED BY THEIR RESPECTIVE NATIONAL SPORTS
              ASSOCIATIONS (NSAs).

               The incentives for the coaches and trainers shall be equivalent to
               fifty percent (50%) of the cash awards for gold, silver and bronze
               medallists.

               There is hereby created a body to be known as the Philippine
               Coaches Accreditation Council that shall accredit and recognize
               coaches and trainers. It shall be composed, among others, of
               representatives from the PSC, POC and the academic community,
               preferably a professor in physical education or sports science.
