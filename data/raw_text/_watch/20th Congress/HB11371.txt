                              EXPLANATORY NOTE


      Infrastructure directly drives economic growth by boosting connectivity,
market access, higher productivity and efficiency, and sustained prosperity. A long-
term and coordinated infrastructure strategy is essential to ensure that government
investments are aligned with the country's development goals, address regional
disparities, and promote sustainable and resilient communities.


      This proposed measure to be known as the “Masterplan for Infrastructure and
National Development (MIND) Act” aims to create a 30-year Comprehensive
Infrastructure Development Masterplan (CIDMP) that would serve as long-term
blueprint for infrastructure projects, ensuring they are coordinated and aligned with
the country’s economic and social development goals. This shall cover agri-fisheries
and food logistics, energy, information and communications technology, social
infrastructure, transport and logistics, and water resources. Moreover, this bill

10         SEC. 5. Formulation o/a Comprehensive Infrastructure Development Master
11   Plan (CIDMP). – There shall be formulated a 30-year Comprehensive Infrastructure
12   Development Masterplan (CIDMP) which shall set-forth the country's long-term
13   vision, goals, and strategy for infrastructure development. The CIDMP shall be the
14   framework for forward planning, prioritization, and development of infrastructure
15   investment and programs, taking into consideration the country's long-term national
16   development plans, including the medium-term Philippine Development Plan. It shall
17   serve as the integrated framework of infrastructure programs and projects of the
18   government enterprise architecture.
19
20         The CIDMP shall be reviewed and updated every 10 years or earlier as the need
21   arises, in anticipation of disruptions, emergencies, crises, and new and emerging
22   technologies and trends; Provided, That such early review and update be consistent
23   with long-term national development vision and goals.
24
25         In order to effectively implement this Act, a whole-of-government approach
26   shall be adopted for the formulation and adoption of the CIDMP. This approach shall
27   facilitate engagement with government agencies, instrumentalities, government-
28   owned and -controlled corporations (GOCCs), local government units (LGUs),
29   regional development councils (RDCs), ICT Councils, technical and standards
30   organizations, and other relevant stakeholders towards ensuring the full and effective
31   implementation of this Act.

 2         SEC. 6. Infrastructure Policies and Strategies. – The CIDMP shall pursue the
 3   following national development policies and strategies of the Government:
 4
 5            a. Establishment of a whole-of-government strategy to coordinate
 6                infrastructure investment planning and implementation that promote
 7                collaboration among key actors, with the Department of Economy,
 8                Planning and Development as the lead coordinator;
 9            b. Prioritization of projects of national significance which are consistent
10                with the approved national and physical framework plans, as well as
11                with national, regional, local, and sectoral development plans,
12                roadmaps, and master plans;
13            c. Observance of the following principles in the determination of priorities:
14                (l) effectiveness in meeting national development objectives; (2)
15                economic feasibility and impact; (3) poverty alleviation and social
16                inclusion; (4) environmental sustainability and climate resilience; (5)
17                safety; (6) security; (7) affordability; (8) public access; (9) technical
18                readiness for implementation; and (10) financial viability and value for
19                money;
20            d. Harmonization and coordination of national, regional, and local
21                infrastructure development goals;
22            e. Promotion of public consultation and feedback mechanisms on
23                infrastructure investment priorities and projects at the national and local
24                levels;
25            f. Maximization of private sector participation in the planning,
26                development, financing, design, construction, and operation and
27                maintenance of infrastructure;
28            g. Utilization of any of the following modes in infrastructure
29                implementation: (a) by the concerned national government agencies and
30                GOCCs as provided under their respective charters; (b) under Public-
31                Private Partnerships (PPP) arrangements; or (c) in partnership with
 1        LGUs to complement public finance as well as promote technology and
 2        knowledge transfers;
 3   h. Implementation of adequate infrastructure asset preservation and
 4        maintenance strategies;
 5   i. Development of an efficient, safe, economical, accessible, affordable,
 6        reliable, integrated, seamless,   convenient,   user-oriented,   disaster-
 7        resilient, and environmentally sustainable national infrastructure
 8        system consistent with the latest industry standards;
 9   j.   Establishment of a robust national geospatial infrastructure that will
10        provide location-specific data to support evidence-based planning and
11        implementation of infrastructure projects;
12   k. Establishment of a strategic national transport network consisting of
13        complementary roads, railways, ports, and airports that serve medium
14        and long-distance high-density traffic between key cities and
15        municipalities, economic hubs, international gateways, and major
16        corridors in urban centers;
17   l. Establishment of food security infrastructure that will ensure the
18        smooth flow of cargo across the archipelago shall be emphasized;
19   m. Prioritization of the development of mass public transportation to
20        reduce dependence on private vehicles:
21   n. Adoption of inclusive mobility designs through the provision of a
22        network of protected bicycle lanes, sidewalks, at-grade and elevated
23        walkways, and green and open spaces;
24   o. Incorporation of appropriate infrastructure risk management measures,
25        including risk identification, allocation, and mitigation, in project
26        development and management;
27   p. Adoption of green and sustainable design, climate change adaptation
28        and disaster resilience measures, as well as updated strength, safety,
29        health, and environmental standards in the design and construction of
30        infrastructure projects;

27         estimated funding requirements, and financing modalities for each of
28         the infrastructure sub-sectors.

27                 network that shall link production areas with processing, warehousing,
28                 transport and transshipment hubs, and markets, and ensure unimpeded flow
29                 of people, goods, services, disaster response equipment, relief goods, and basic
30                 commodities in times of emergencies;
