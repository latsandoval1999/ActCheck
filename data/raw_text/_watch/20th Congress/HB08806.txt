                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to institutionalize monthly maintenance medication support for
senior citizens to ensure that they can sustain access to essential medicines needed to
manage chronic conditions and preserve their quality of life.

       The Philippines continues to experience a steady demographic shift toward an
aging population. The Philippine Statistics Authority reported that there were 10.98
million Filipinos aged sixty (60) years and older in 2025, which comprises 9.6% of the
country’s total population. This number continues to grow due to improvements in life
expectancy and healthcare access. However, longevity without adequate health support
exposes elderly Filipinos to prolonged vulnerability, particularly in managing
age-related illnesses that require continuous treatment and medication.

       A study published by the Philippine Academy of Family Physicians reveals that
more than sixty percent (60%) of our senior citizens suffer from non-communicable
diseases such as cardiovascular diseases, cancers, chronic respiratory diseases,
hypertension, diabetes, and arthritis1, among others. These conditions require consistent
medication to prevent complications that may eventually lead to premature death.

        While Republic Act (RA) No. 9994, otherwise known as the “Expanded Senior
Citizens Act of 2010”, provides senior citizens a twenty percent (20%) discount and tax
exemptions on essential goods and services which includes medicines, the out-of-pocket
costs for maintenance medicines still range from one thousand pesos (P1,000.00) to five
thousand pesos (P5,000.00) monthly, sometimes even more, depending on the quantity
and type of prescription medicine. This prohibits those without active income and
family support from getting the medication they need.

       This proposed measure provides a monthly financial support of one thousand
pesos (P1,000.00) to senior citizens requiring maintenance medications to augment
benefits provided under RA 9994. It ensures that our elderly can adhere to their
prescribed medications without sacrificing other basic needs like food and shelter.

                            AN ACT
PROVIDING MONTHLY MAINTENANCE MEDICATION SUPPORT FOR SENIOR
CITIZENS, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES THEREFOR

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Monthly Maintenance
Medication Support for Senior Citizens Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the health and well-being of elderly citizens for their continued
meaningful contribution and participation in the society pursuant to Article XIII, Sec. 11
of the 1987 Philippine Constitution. It provides that the State shall adopt an integrated
and comprehensive approach to health and other social services available to all people
at affordable cost. To this end, this Act shall provide monthly financial assistance to
senior citizens, enabling them to access and afford necessary maintenance medications,
thereby enhancing their quality of life and health outcomes.

        SEC. 3. Monthly Maintenance Medication Support. – The government shall provide
monthly financial support amounting to One thousand pesos (P1,000.00) to all senior
citizens, hereinafter referred to as “beneficiaries”, who are qualified and registered as
such under Republic Act No. 9994, otherwise known as the “Expanded Senior Citizens Act
of 2010.”

      SEC. 4. Eligibility and Registration. – To qualify for the Monthly Maintenance
Medication Support, a senior citizen must meet the following criteria:

          a. Be a Filipino citizen and a registered senior citizen as defined in Republic
       Act No. 9994;

          b. Submit an application to the Department of Health (DOH) or its
       designated agency, accompanied by a valid prescription for maintenance
       medications issued by a licensed medical practitioner within the last six (6)
       months from the date of application; and

         c.​ Once approved, beneficiaries shall be issued a Monthly Maintenance
       Medication Support Card.

       SEC. 5. Use of the Monthly Support. – The Monthly Maintenance Medication
Support shall be used exclusively to purchase prescribed maintenance medications,
including but not limited to medicines for chronic illnesses, vitamins, and supplements,
from all public or private pharmaceutical establishments upon presentment of a
prescription validly issued within the last six (6) months from the date of purchase. The
Monthly Maintenance Medication Support shall not be transferrable to any person other
than the person to which the same is provided as named in the Monthly Maintenance
Medication Support Card. Any unused portion of the support shall not be convertible to
cash.

       SEC. 6. Implementation and Coordination. – The Department of Health (DOH) shall
be responsible for the implementation of this Act and shall coordinate with other
relevant agencies, including the Department of Social Welfare and Development
(DSWD), to ensure the efficient distribution of the Monthly Maintenance Medication
Support.

       SEC. 7. Monitoring and Reporting. – The DOH shall establish a monitoring system
to track the utilization of the Monthly Maintenance Medication Support and shall
submit an annual report to Congress detailing the program’s effectiveness and impact
on senior citizens’ health and well-being.

        SEC. 8. Penalties. – Any person who commits fraud, misrepresentation, or any
form of abuse in the application or use of the Monthly Maintenance Medication Support
shall be subject to penalties as prescribed by law.

        SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DOH, in coordination with the
DSWD and other relevant agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations
to effectively implement this Act within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

      SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not so declared shall remain in force and effect.

       SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders and rules, and regulations
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
amended accordingly.

       SEC. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation.
