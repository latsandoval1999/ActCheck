AN ACT
PENALIZING SELECTIVE, DISCRETIONARY, AND DISCRIMINATORY ACTS IN THE
DELIVERY OF SOCIAL PROTECTION PROGRAMS

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 1, Article XI of the 1987 Constitution states that “[p]ublic office is a public
trust. Every government worker is expected to serve the Filipino people with utmost
integrity, efficiency, and fairness.

However, the persistent gap between this ideal situation and the reality on the
ground is apparent. Across the country, access to social assistance, from emergency relief
and medical subsidies to educational aid and cash transfers, is too often derailed by
favoritism, politics, and local bias. During times of crisis, the local distribution of aid
frequently devolves into a system of "palakasan." Some barangays are selective in their
distribution of relief goods, requiring the presentation of voter’s IDs or prioritizing their
friends and relatives.’ Deserving, low-income families get left off the master lists simply
because they lack the right connections or fall on the wrong side of local political lines.
Meanwhile, those with close ties to officials skip the line, turning basic public services
into political favors.

This bill seeks to tackle the issue of discrepancy head-on by creating clear,
enforceable penalties for any government worker who uses discretion to favor, exclude,
or discriminate against beneficiaries. By removing personal bias from the distribution
process and enforcing strict accountability, this measure ensures that government aid
finally reaches every qualified Filipino fairly, impartially, and without condition.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Anti-Discrimination in
the Delivery of Social Protection Programs Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — it is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote a just and dynamic social order through policies that provide adequate social
services, promote full employment, a rising standard of living, and an improved quality
of life for all.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — As used in this Act:

(a) Discrimination refers to any distinction, exclusion, restriction or preference
which has the effect of nullifying or impairing the access to social protection
programs; and

(b) Social Protection Programs refers to various government programs aimed at
ensuring that marginalized and vulnerable groups have access to essential
services and opportunities, which shall include but not limited to financial
assistance, medical assistance, livelihood programs, and other relief
assistance programs.

SEC. 4. Coverage. — This Act shall apply to all government employees, whether
acting alone or in conspiracy with private persons, in the performance of or in connection
to the delivery of cash grants, food stubs, livelihood or relief packages, and/or other social
protection programs of the government.

SEC. 5. Prohibition on Selective, Discretionary, or Discriminatory Acts in the Delivery
of Social Protection Programs. — It shall be unlawful for any government employee,
whether acting alone or in conspiracy with private persons, who by act or omission,
directly or indirectly:

===== [page 3] =====
(a) Distributes or assists in the distribution of cash grants, food stubs, livelihood
or relief packages, or other assistance programs to persons other than the
qualified beneficiaries;

(b) Gives priority to or directs another to prioritize their relatives by affinity or
consanguinity within the third degree, or other qualified beneficiaries; or

(c) Excludes qualified beneficiaries to receive assistance programs.

SEC. 6. Penalties. — Any violation of this Act shall be punished with imprisonment
of not less than one (1) year but not more than six (6) years and perpetual disqualification
from holding public office.

SEC. 7. Separability Clause. — Should any provision or part of this Act be declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions and parts hereof, insofar as they are
separable from the invalid ones, shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, issuances, rules and
regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

SEC. 9. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,
