      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representativ es of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the "Abot-Kayang Solar Energy for
Every Filipino Home Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to ensure affordable, reliable,
and clean energy for all Filipinos. Recognizing the heavy financial burden of high
electricity costs and the urgent need to mitigate climate change, the State shall actively
promote the adoption of renewable energy at the household level. To achieve this, the
government shall provide accessible financing, tax incentives, and streamlined
administrative processes to make residential solar energy systems affordable for average
and low-income families.
