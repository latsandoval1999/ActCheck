                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Electricity rates in the Philippines remain among the highest in Southeast
Asia, imposing a substantial financial burden on Filipino households. Apart from
the cost of electricity actually consumed, consumers are billed for various
regulated charges, including the system loss charge, which represents the cost
of recovering electricity lost during transmission and distribution due to
technical losses and other causes recognized under Republic Act No. 9136,
otherwise known as the "Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001."

      Although system loss is a regulatory cost recovery mechanism authorized
under existing law, it does not represent electricity actually consumed by
residential customers. Nevertheless, the amount collected for system loss forms
part of the electricity bill and is presently subjected to the Value-Added Tax
(VAT), thereby increasing the financial burden borne by consumers.

      It is the policy of the State to protect consumers and promote equitable
taxation. While VAT is generally imposed on the sale of goods and services,
Congress may, in the exercise of its constitutional power to grant tax exemptions,
exempt specific transactions or components thereof whenever public interest so
requires. Considering the continuing increase in electricity costs, it is imperative
to provide targeted tax relief to residential household consumers by exempting
the system loss component from VAT.

       This measure likewise ensures that the tax relief granted under this Act
directly benefits consumers by requiring the Energy Regulatory Commission
(ERC) to prevent distribution utilities and electric cooperatives from recovering
the exempted VAT through other regulated charges or billing adjustments. To
promote transparency and accountability, electricity bills shall separately reflect
the system loss charge and the corresponding VAT savings resulting from this
Act.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                             “AN ACT
      EXEMPTING THE SYSTEM LOSS COMPONENT OF ELECTRICITY
      BILLED TO RESIDENTIAL HOUSEHOLD CONSUMERS FROM
      THE VALUE-ADDED TAX, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
      SECTION 109 OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE
      OF 1997, AS AMENDED, PROVIDING SAFEGUARDS TO ENSURE
      THAT THE BENEFITS THEREOF ARE FULLY PASSED ON TO
      CONSUMERS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "VAT Exemption
on Systems Loss Charges for Residential Household Consumers Act."

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to protect the welfare of electricity consumers by promoting
affordability, transparency, and fairness in electricity pricing while ensuring that
tax measures intended to reduce electricity costs directly benefit residential
household consumers.

       Toward this end, the State shall exempt the system loss component from
the Value-Added Tax (VAT) in the sale of electricity to residential household
consumers and ensure that the resulting savings are fully reflected in their
electricity bills.

       SECTION 3. Section 109 of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997,
as amended, is hereby further amended by inserting a new subsection to read
as follows:


     “SEC. 109. Exempt Transactions. – (1) Subject to the provisions of
subsection (2) hereof, the following transactions shall be exempt from the
Value-added tax:

      XXX
"(EE) THE SYSTEM LOSS COMPONENT IN THE SALE OF
ELECTRICITY BY DISTRIBUTION UTILITIES INCLUDING PRIVATE
DISTRIBUTION UTILITIES AND ELECTRIC COOPERATIVES TO
RESIDENTIAL HOUSEHOLD CONSUMERS SHALL BE EXEMPT
FROM THE VALUE-ADDED TAX.

FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SUBSECTION, THE SYSTEM LOSS
COMPONENT SHALL REFER TO THE COST RECOVERY
REPRESENTING THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE ELECTRIC
ENERGY DELIVERED TO THE DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM (ENERGY
INPUT) AND THE ELECTRIC ENERGY DELIVERED TO END-USERS
(ENERGY OUTPUT), AS DETERMINED UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO.
9136, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE 'ELECTRIC POWER INDUSTRY
REFORM ACT OF 2001,' AND THE PERTINENT RULES AND
REGULATIONS OF THE ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION
(ERC).

THE EXEMPTION PROVIDED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION SHALL
APPLY EXCLUSIVELY TO THE SYSTEM LOSS COMPONENT AND
SHALL NOT BE CONSTRUED TO EXEMPT THE ACTUAL SALE OF
ELECTRICITY OR ANY OTHER CHARGE THAT CONSTITUTES
CONSIDERATION FOR THE GENERATION, TRANSMISSION,
DISTRIBUTION, OR SUPPLY OF ELECTRICITY.

THE ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION SHALL ENSURE THAT
THE FULL BENEFIT OF THIS VAT EXEMPTION IS DIRECTLY
PASSED ON TO RESIDENTIAL HOUSEHOLD CONSUMERS AND
SHALL NOT BE OFFSET, RECOVERED, OR RECOUPED THROUGH
DISTRIBUTION   WHEELING    CHARGES,    PASS-THROUGH
CHARGES, RATE ADJUSTMENTS, OR ANY OTHER REGULATED
BILLING COMPONENT.

EVERY DISTRIBUTION UTILITY AND ELECTRIC COOPERATIVE
SHALL SEPARATELY INDICATE IN ITS MONTHLY BILLING
STATEMENT THE SYSTEM LOSS CHARGE, THE VAT EXEMPTION
APPLIED THERETO, AND THE CORRESPONDING AMOUNT OF
SAVINGS ACCRUING TO THE RESIDENTIAL HOUSEHOLD
CONSUMER, IN SUCH FORM AS MAY BE PRESCRIBED BY THE
ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION."
      SECTION 4. Non-Circumvention. - No distribution utility, electric
cooperative, or any other entity engaged in the distribution of electricity shall
directly or indirectly recover the value of the VAT exemption granted under this
Act through increases in distribution wheeling charges, pass-through charges,
administrative fees, or any other regulated billing component.

      The Energy Regulatory Commission shall exercise its regulatory authority
under Republic Act No. 9136 to ensure compliance with this provision and may
impose the appropriate administrative sanctions in accordance with existing
laws, rules, and regulations.

       SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance (DOF) through
the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), and the Energy Regulatory Commission
(ERC) shall jointly promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act after consultation with distribution utilities,
electric cooperatives, consumer groups, and other relevant stakeholders.

       SECTION 6. Reportorial Requirement. - Within one (1) year from the
effectivity of this Act, and annually thereafter, the ERC shall submit to Congress
a report on the implementation of this Act, including the aggregate amount of
VAT savings realized by residential household consumers, the compliance of
distribution utilities and electric cooperatives with this Act, and any
recommendations for further legislative or regulatory action.

     SECTION 7. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
