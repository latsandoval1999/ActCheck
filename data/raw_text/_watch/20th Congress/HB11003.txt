                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to replace the Motor Vehicle User’s Charge (MVUC) established under
Republic Act (RA) No. 8794 entitled “An Act Imposing a Motor Vehicle User’s Charge on
Owners of All Types of Motor Vehicles and For Other Purposes”, as amended, and replace it with
a Motor Vehicle Road User’s Tax (MVRUT). It aims to direct a portion of additional revenues
toward public transportation and road safety.

        Since the enactment of RA No. 8794 in 2000, the number of motor vehicles has
continued to increase and placed greater pressure on an already congested road network.
Construction, maintenance, and rehabilitation costs have likewise increased, yet still the
country continues to face persistent challenges in public transportation and road safety.
Motor vehicles also vary in type, weight, capacity, use, and their impact on public roads
likewise differs. Despite this, the existing rates do not provide a sufficiently responsive basis
for determining the cost of road use.

         The proposed measure introduces a revised tax structure based on vehicle
classification and gross vehicle weight, and shall cover registered private and government
motor vehicles. It shall also provide a reduced rate for for-hire vehicles in recognition of
their role in public transportation and commercial activity. It likewise recognizes that
improving the country’s transportation system requires more than maintaining roads. Fifty
percent (50%) of the incremental revenues will be directed to public utility vehicle
modernization, programs for the prevention of road deaths, and assistance to road accident
victims. This ensures that the additional tax placed on vehicle owners benefits not only
them, but also the millions of Filipinos who depend on the country’s roads everyday.

        A road user tax should reflect the realities of road use today and contribute to
addressing the transportation challenges that come with it. The modernization of the
existing tax system for motor vehicle users provides a manageable transition and makes the
country’s roads more sustainable, its public transportation more efficient and reliable, and
its communities safer.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DOTr, in coordination with
the DOF, the DOH, and the LTFRB, shall promulgate the rules and regulations to
implement this Act within thirty (30) days from its effectivity.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. - All laws, orders, issuances, circulars, rules and
regulations or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act,
are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof not affected thereby
shall continue to be in full force and effect.

       SEC. 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least one (1) newspaper of general
circulation.
