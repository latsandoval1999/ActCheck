                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Norzagaray–San Jose Road, located in the
Municipality of Norzagaray, Province of Bulacan, into a national road under the
jurisdiction and supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways
(DPWH).

Spanning approximately 11.46 kilometers, the Norzagaray–San Jose Road serves
as a vital arterial route within Bulacan. It facilitates the movement of people,
agricultural produce, industrial supplies, and essential goods between local
communities and neighboring commercial centers. Norzagaray is host to vital
national infrastructure, including major water reservoirs and quarrying/cement
manufacturing hubs, making its road network integral not only to municipal
commerce but to regional trade and economic activity.

Despite its strategic importance, the continuous maintenance and upgrade of this
11.46-kilometer stretch place a significant financial burden on the local
government unit (LGU) of Norzagaray. Municipal funding limitations often restrict
the LGU's capacity to undertake comprehensive road widenings, structural
reinforcements, and routine rehabilitation necessary to support heavy vehicular
traffic and adverse weather conditions.

Converting the Norzagaray–San Jose Road into a national road will transfer its
control, maintenance, and improvement to the DPWH. This ensures regular
budget allocation under the General Appropriations Act (GAA), bringing the road
up to national standards, enhancing road safety, and boosting regional socio-
economic growth.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT CONVERTING THE NORZAGARAY–SAN JOSE ROAD IN THE
    MUNICIPALITY OF NORZAGARAY, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A
       NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The Norzagaray–San Jose Road, consisting of approximately eleven
point forty-six (11.46) kilometers in the Municipality of Norzagaray, Province of
Bulacan, is hereby converted into a national road.

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department’s program the improvement, repair, and maintenance of the said
road, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary
rules, regulations, and directives to implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved
