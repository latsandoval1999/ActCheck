                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Negros Oriental II Electric Cooperative (NORECO II) has long served as a
vital distribution utility in the province. Incorporated with the National
Electrification Administration on April 2, 1977, and registered with the
Cooperative Development Authority on February 27, 1996, NORECO II has
been engaged in the retail distribution of electricity within its franchise area
for decades. Its existing franchise, which began on February 9, 1983 for a
period of fifty (50) years, has enabled the cooperative to construct, operate,
and maintain the distribution systems necessary to bring electricity to its
member-consumer-owners.

NORECO II’s service area covers the Cities of Dumaguete, Tanjay, and
Bayawan, and the Municipalities of Pamplona, Amlan, San Jose, Sibulan,
Valencia, Bacong, Dauin, Zamboanguita, Siaton, Santa Catalina, and Basay,
all in the Province of Negros Oriental. Across this coverage area, the
cooperative has energized 272 out of 272 barangays, reflecting the full reach
of its electrification efforts and its important role in sustaining access to
electricity for households, institutions, and enterprises.

As of December 2025, NORECO II serves a total membership of 183,456,
composed of 180,635 regular members and 2,821 associate members. It
operates with 339 employees and maintains several sub-offices, collection
centers, and substations strategically located across its franchise area. These
facilities allow the cooperative to respond to consumer concerns, manage
distribution operations, collect payments, and maintain service reliability
throughout its coverage area.

The scale of NORECO II’s operations further demonstrates its importance to
the local economy. It recorded total energy purchases of 470,767,434
kilowatt-hours, an average peak demand of 91.08 megawatts, total revenue
of ₱4,702,134,648.00, and total assets amounting to ₱3,475,000,219.00.
These figures show not only the magnitude of the cooperative’s responsibility,
but also the continuing growth in electricity demand within its franchise area.

The renewal and grant of legislative franchise to NORECO II is therefore
necessary to ensure the uninterrupted distribution of electricity in its service
areas. Without a valid franchise, the cooperative’s authority to construct,
install, establish, operate, own, manage, and maintain distribution systems
may be placed in uncertainty, to the prejudice of its member-consumer-
owners and the communities dependent on its services.

This bill seeks to grant NORECO II a franchise to continue providing electric
distribution service to the Cities of Dumaguete, Tanjay, and Bayawan, and
the Municipalities of Pamplona, Amlan, San Jose, Sibulan, Valencia, Bacong,
Dauin, Zamboanguita, Siaton, Santa Catalina, and Basay, Province of Negros
Oriental. It recognizes the cooperative’s established infrastructure,
institutional capacity, and continuing public-service mandate in advancing
rural electrification and supporting local development.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                 AN ACT
        GRANTING THE NEGROS ORIENTAL II ELECTRIC COOPERATIVE
       (NORECO II) A FRANCHISE TO CONSTRUCT, INSTALL, ESTABLISH,
      OPERATE, OWN, MANAGE, AND MAINTAIN DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS
     FOR THE CONVEYANCE OF ELECTRIC POWER TO THE END-USERS IN
         THE CITIES OF DUMAGUETE, TANJAY AND BAYAWAN AND THE
         MUNICIPALITIES OF PAMPLONA, AMLAN, SAN JOSE, SIBULAN,
     VALENCIA, BACONG, DAUIN, ZAMBOANGUITA, SIATON, STA. CATALINA
                AND BASAY, PROVINCE OF NEGROS ORIENTAL

     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Republic
     of the Philippines in Congress assembled:


 1         SECTION 1. Nature and Scope of Franchise. – Subject to the
 2   provisions of the Constitution and applicable laws, rules and regulations,
 3   there is hereby granted to the Negros Oriental II Electric Cooperative
 4   (NORECO II) hereunder referred to as the Grantee, its successors or assigns
 5   a franchise to construct, install, establish, operate, own, manage and
 6   maintain in the public interest and commercial purposes, a distribution
 7   system for the conveyance of electric power to the end-users in the Cities of
 8   Dumaguete, Tanjay, and Bayawan, and the Municipalities of Pamplona,
 9   Amlan, San Jose, Sibulan, Valencia, Bacong, Dauin, Zamboanguita, Siaton,
10   Santa Catalina, and Basay, all in the Province of Negros Oriental.
 1           As used in this Act, distribution system refers to the system of wires
 2   and associated facilities including sub-transmission lines belonging to a
 3   franchised distribution utility extending between the delivery point on the
 4   national transmission system or generating facility and the metering point or
 5   facility of the end-users.

 6           SEC. 2. Manner of Operation of Facilities. – All electric distribution
 7   facilities, lines and systems for electric services installed, owned, maintained,
 8   operated, managed by the Grantee, its successors or assigns shall be operated
 9   or maintained at all times in a superior manner, and it shall be the duty of
10   the Grantee, its successors or assigns, whenever required to do so by the
11   Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), or its legal successor, or the
12   Department of Energy (DOE), or its legal successor, or any other government
13   agency concerned to modify, improve and change such facilities or systems in
14   such manner and to such extent as the progress in science or technology and
15   improvements or innovations in the electric power services may render
16   reasonable and proper.

17         Whenever practicable, and for purposes of maintaining order, safety
18   and aesthetics along the highways, roads, streets, alleys or right-of-way, the
19   Grantee may allow the use of free spaces in its poles, facilities, or right-of-way
20   by interested parties upon reasonable compensation to the Grantee
21   considering the costs incurred to accommodate and administer the use of the
22   Grantee’s facilities by such parties. The ERC shall decide in case of dispute
23   or disagreement between the parties.

24          SEC. 3. Authority of the ERC and NEA. – The Grantee shall secure
25   from the ERC, NEA, or any government agency having jurisdiction over their
26   operations, the Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity and any other
27   license, permit or authority indispensable for the construction and operation
28   of the electric power distribution system.

29           SEC. 4. Excavation and Restoration Works. – For the purpose of
30   erecting and maintaining the poles or other supports for said facilities, wires
31   or other conductors or for the purpose of laying and maintaining said
32   facilities, wires, cables or other conductors, it shall be lawful for the Grantee,
33   its successors, or assignees, in coordination with the Department of Public
34   Works and Highways (DPWH) or the local government unit (LGU) concerned,
35   as may be appropriate, to make excavations or lay conduits in any of the
36   public places, highways, road, streets, lanes, alleys, avenues, sidewalks, or
37   bridges of the province, cities or municipalities: Provided, however, That a
38   public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley, avenue, sidewalk, or bridge
39   disturbed, altered, or changed by reason of erection of poles or other supports
40   or the underground laying of wires, other conductors or conduits, shall be
 1   repaired and replaced in workmanlike manner by the Grantee, its successors
 2   or assignees, in accordance with the standards set by the DPWH or the local
 3   government unit concerned. Should the Grantee, its successors or assignees,
 4   after the ten (10)-day notice from the grant of authority, fail, refuse or neglect
 5   to repair or replace any part of public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley,
 6   avenue, sidewalk, or bridge altered, changed or disturbed by the said Grantee,
 7   its successors or assignees, then the DPWH or the local government unit
 8   concerned shall have the right to have the same repaired and replaced in good
 9   order and condition and charge the Grantee, its successors or assignees,
10   double the amount of the cost and expenses for such repair or replacement.

11          SEC. 5. Responsibility to the Public. – The Grantee shall supply
12   electricity to its captive market in the least costly manner. In the interest of
13   the public good and as far as feasible and whenever required by the ERC, the
14   Grantee shall modify, improve, or change its facilities, poles, lines, systems
15   and equipment for the purpose of providing efficient and reliable service and
16   reduced electricity costs. The Grantee shall charge reasonable and just power
17   rates for its services to all types of consumers within its franchised area in
18   order that business and industries shall be able to compete.

19         The Grantee shall have the obligation to provide open and
20   nondiscriminatory access to its distribution system and services for any end-
21   user within its franchise area consistent with Republic Act (RA) No. 9136,
22   otherwise known as the “Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001”. The
23   Grantee shall not engage in any activity that will constitute an abuse of
24   market power such as unfair trade practices, monopolistic schemes, and
25   other activities that will hinder competitiveness of business and industries.

26          SEC. 6. Rates for Services. – The retail rates and charges for the
27   distribution of electric power by the Grantee to its end-users shall be
28   regulated by and subject to the approval of the ERC or its legal successor.

29         The Grantee shall identify and segregate in its electricity bill to the end-
30   users the components of the retail rate pursuant to RA 9136, unless otherwise
31   amended. Such rates charged by the Grantee to the end-users shall be made
32   public and transparent. The Grantee shall implement lifeline rate to
33   marginalized end-users as mandated under RA 9136.

34         SEC. 7. Promotion of Consumer Interests. – The Grantee herein shall
35   establish a consumer desk that will handle consumer complaints and ensure
36   adequate promotion of consumer interests. The Grantee shall act with
37   dispatch on all complaints brought before it.

38         SEC. 8. Commitment to Provide and Promote the Creation of
 1   Employment Opportunities. – The Grantee shall create employment
 2   opportunities and shall allow on-the-job training in its franchise operation:
 3   Provided, That priority shall be accorded to the residents where their principal
 4   office is located: Provided, further, That the Grantee shall comply with the
 5   applicable labor standards and allowance entitlement under existing labor
 6   laws, rules and regulations, and similar issuances.

 7         SEC. 9. Right of the Government. – A special right is hereby reserved
 8   to the President of the Philippines, in times of war, rebellion, public peril,
 9   calamity, emergency, disaster or disturbance of peace and order: to
10   temporarily take over and operate the stations or facilities of the Grantee; to
11   temporarily suspend the operation of any station or facility in the interest of
12   public safety, security, and public welfare; or to authorize the temporary use
13   and operation thereof by any agency of the government, upon due
14   compensation to the Grantee, for the use of the stations or facilities during
15   the period when these shall be so operated.

16          SEC. 10. Right of Eminent Domain. – Subject to the limitations and
17   procedures prescribed by law, the Grantee is authorized to exercise the right
18   of eminent domain insofar as it may be reasonably necessary for the efficient
19   maintenance and operation of services. The Grantee is authorized to install
20   and maintain its poles, wires, and other facilities over and across public
21   property, including streets, highways, forest reserves, and other similar
22   property of the Government of the Philippines, its branches, or any of its
23   instrumentalities. The Grantee may acquire such private property as is
24   actually necessary for the realization of the purposes for which this franchise
25   is granted: Provided, That proper expropriation proceedings shall have been
26   instituted and just compensation paid.

27          SEC. 11. Term of Franchise. – This franchise shall be for a term of
28   twenty-five (25) years that shall commence from the date of expiration of
29   electric cooperative’s existing franchise, unless sooner cancelled. This
30   franchise shall be deemed ipso facto revoked in the event that the Grantee
31   fails to operate continuously for two (2) years.

32        SEC. 12. Warranty in Favor of the National and Local
33   Governments. – The Grantee shall hold the national, provincial, city, and
34   municipal governments of the Philippines free from all claims, accounts,
35   demands, or actions arising from accidents causing injury to persons or
36   damage to properties, during the construction, installation, operation, and
37   maintenance of the distribution system of the Grantee.

38         SEC. 13. Liability for Damages. – The Grantee shall be liable for any
39   injury to persons and damage to properties arising from accidents by reason
 1   of any defective construction under this franchise or of any neglect or
 2   omission to keep its poles and wires in safe condition.

 3          SEC. 14. Sale, Lease, Transfer, Usufruct, or Assignment of
 4   Franchise. – The Grantee shall not sell, lease, transfer, grant the usufruct,
 5   or assign this franchise or the rights and privileges acquired thereunder to
 6   any person, firm, company, corporation, or other commercial or legal entity,
 7   or merge with any other corporation, or entity, or shall transfer the controlling
 8   interest of the Grantee, whether as a whole or in parts, and whether
 9   simultaneously or contemporaneously, to any such person, firm, company,
10   corporation, or entity without the prior approval of the Congress: Provided,
11   That Congress shall be informed of any sale, lease, transfer, grant of usufruct,
12   sale, or assignment of franchise or the rights and privileges acquired
13   thereunder, or of the merger, or sale of the controlling interest within sixty
14   (60) days after the completion of said transaction: Provided, further, That any
15   such transfer, sale, or assignment is in accordance with the constitutional
16   limitations: Provided, furthermore, That failure to report to Congress such
17   change of ownership shall render the franchise ipso facto revoked: Provided,
18   finally, That any person or entity to which this franchise is sold, transferred,
19   or assigned, shall be subject to the same conditions, terms, restrictions, and
20   limitations of this Act.

21          SEC. 15. Reportorial Requirement. – The Grantee shall submit an
22   annual report to Congress, through the Committee on Legislative Franchises
23   of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Public Services of the
24   Senate, on its compliance with the terms and conditions of the franchise and
25   on its operations on or before April 30 of every year during the term of the
26   franchise. The reportorial compliance certificate issued by Congress shall be
27   required before any application for permit or certificate is accepted by the
28   ERC.

29          SEC. 16. Fine. – The failure of the Grantee to submit the requisite
30   annual report to Congress shall be penalized by a fine in the amount of Five
31   hundred pesos (P500.00) per working day of noncompliance which shall be
32   collected by the ERC. The fine shall be collected separately from the
33   reportorial penalties imposed by the ERC and shall be remitted to the Bureau
34   of Treasury.

35          SEC. 17. Equality Clause. – Any advantage, favor, privilege,
36   exemption, or immunity granted under existing franchises, or which may
37   hereafter be granted, upon prior review and approval of Congress, shall
38   become part of this franchise and shall be accorded immediately and
39   unconditionally to the herein Grantee: Provided, however, That the foregoing
40   shall neither apply to nor affect provisions concerning territory covered by the
 1   franchise, the life span of the franchise or the type of service authorized by
 2   the franchise: Provided, further, That the foregoing shall not apply to the sale,
 3   lease, transfer, grant of usufruct, or assignment of legislative franchises with
 4   prior congressional approval.

 5          SEC. 18. Applicability of Existing Laws. – The Grantee shall comply
 6   with and be subject to the provisions of Commonwealth Act No. 146 or the
 7   “Public Service Act”, as amended, RA 9136, and RA 10531 or the “National
 8   Electrification Administration Reform Act of 2013.”

 9         SEC. 19. Existing Powers. – Existing mandates, powers, functions,
10   and privileges granted to electric cooperatives under existing laws, including
11   Section 10 of RA 10531 shall remain valid and effective, unless expressly
12   repealed by succeeding laws.

13          SEC. 20. Repealability and Non-Exclusivity Clause. – This franchise
14   shall be subject to amendment, alteration, or repeal by Congress when the
15   public interest so requires and shall not be interpreted as an exclusive grant
16   of the privileges herein provided for.

17          SEC. 21. Separability Clause. – If any of the sections or provisions of
18   this Act is held invalid, all other provisions not affected thereby shall remain
19   valid.

20         SEC. 22. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
21   orders, letters of instruction, administrative rules and regulations or parts
22   thereof which is are contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act
23   are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

24         SEC. 23. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect upon the
25   expiration of the electric cooperative’s franchise or fifteen (15) days after its
26   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation,
27   whichever comes later.
