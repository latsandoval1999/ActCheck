                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Filipino family has long been regarded as the first source of care and support
for its elderly members. This duty is expressly recognized in Section 4, Article XV, of the
1987 Constitution: “The family has the duty to care for its elderly members but the State
may also do so through just programs of social security.”

       The Constitution likewise directs the State to make essential health and social
services available at affordable cost, with priority given to the elderly, among other
vulnerable sectors (Article XIII, Section 11 of the 1987 Constitution).

       Congress has acted on these constitutional commitments through Republic Act No.
7432, as amended by Republic Act Nos. 9257, 9994, and 11916. These laws established
and expanded various benefits and forms of government assistance for senior citizens. In
particular, Republic Act No. 9994 institutionalized the Social Pension Program for indigent
senior citizens, while Republic Act No. 11916 subsequently increased the monthly social
pension to One Thousand Pesos (Php 1,000.00).

       The existing program, however, remains principally directed toward indigent senior
citizens. This measure seeks to take a further step in strengthening social protection for the
country's aging population by extending a monthly social pension to all senior citizens who
are sixty-five (65) years old and above, regardless of their economic status. It likewise
maintains assistance for indigent senior citizens aged sixty (60) to sixty-four (64), who may
face financial hardship even before reaching the age covered by the proposed universal
benefit.

       The proposed social pension is intended to serve as a modest and continuing source
of support for ordinary expenses, particularly food, medicine, and other medical and
subsistence needs. The amount provided under this measure is not intended to replace
employment income, retirement benefits, or pensions received under existing systems such
as the Social Security System or Government Service Insurance System. Rather, it is
conceived as a basic form of social protection that may supplement whatever resources a
senior citizen may already have.

        Ultimately, this measure recognizes that the protection of older Filipinos should not
depend solely on whether they have been classified as indigent. Reaching old age is a stage
of life in which many citizens experience increased medical expenses, reduced earning
capacity, and greater dependence on family or other forms of assistance. A universal social
pension for senior citizens aged sixty-five (65) years and above provides a straightforward
form of government support while preserving additional assistance for younger senior
citizens who are already experiencing serious economic vulnerability.

       The State has a continuing responsibility to ensure that its senior citizens are able to
live their later years with dignity and a reasonable measure of financial security. By
expanding the social pension, this measure seeks to make that responsibility more
responsive to the realities faced by an aging Filipino population.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
EXPANDING THE COVERAGE OF THE SOCIAL PENSION PROGRAM BY
GRANTING SOCIAL PENSION TO ALL SENIOR CITIZENS AGED 65 YEARS
OLD AND ABOVE, AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO.
7432, AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9257, REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9994,
AND REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11916, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Social Pension of
Senior Citizens Act.”


       SEC. 2. Section 2 of Republic Act No. 7432, as amended by Republic Act No.
9257, further amended by Republic Act No. 9994, otherwise known as the “Expanded
Senior Citizen Act of 2010”, is hereby further amended to read as follows:
       “Sec. 2. Definition of Terms. - For this Act, these terms are defined as follows:

              xxx                  xxx                   xxx

       “(i) SOCIAL PENSION REFERS TO THE MONTHLY STIPEND
MONETARY GRANT FROM THE GOVERNMENT TO AUGMENT THE
DAILY SUBSISTENCE AND OTHER MEDICAL NEEDS OF SENIOR
CITIZENS, WHICH SHALL NOT BE LESS THAN ONE THOUSAND PESOS
(Php 1000.00) PER MONTH.

        SEC. 3. Section 5 of the same Act, as amended, is hereby further amended to read
as follows:

              xxx                  xxx                   xxx

     “(h) Additional Government Assistance
     “(1) Social Pension
     “[Indigent] ALL senior citizens 65 YEARS OLD AND ABOVE SHALL BE
ENTITLED TO A MONTHLY SOCIAL PENSION OF AT LEAST ONE
THOUSAND PESOS (Php 1,000.00) to augment the daily subsistence and other
medical needs of senior citizens.

      INDIGENT SENIOR CITIZENS AGED SIXTY (60) YEARS OLD TO
SIXTY-FOUR (64) YEARS OLD SHALL BE ENTITLED TO A MONTHLY
SOCIAL PENSION OF AT LEAST ONE THOUSAND PESOS (PHP1,000.00) to
augment the daily subsistence and other medical needs of senior citizens

        The amount of social pension SHALL BE REVIEWED AND, when
necessary, ADJUSTED OR INCREASED BY THE DSWD every two years after
the effectivity of this act, in consultation with the DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET
AND MANAGEMENT (DBM) AND OTHER RELEVANT STAKEHOLDERS,
TAKING INTO ACCOUNT THE VARIOUS NEEDS OF SENIOR CITIZENS,
THE NATIONAL INFLATION RATE, AND OTHER RELEVANT ECONOMIC
INDICATORS, as reported and published by the pertinent government agencies or
authorities: PROVIDED, THAT THE SOCIAL PENSION SHALL NOT BE LESS
THAN THE AMOUNT INITIALLY SPECIFIED IN THIS ACT.

      FIVE YEARS AFTER THE EFFECTIVITY OF THIS ACT, ALL SENIOR
CITIZENS SHALL BE ENTITLED TO A MONTHLY SOCIAL PENSION AT
THE PREVAILING RATE, WHICH SHALL NOT BE LESS THAN ONE
THOUSAND PESOS (PHPI,000.00) to augment the daily subsistence and other
medical needs of senior citizens.

               XXX                    XXX                   XXX

        SEC. 4. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions, which are not affected, shall continue to be in
full force and effect.

        SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, or rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed
or modified accordingly.

        SEC. 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
