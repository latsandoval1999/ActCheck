                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to provide a long-term framework to upgrade court
infrastructure, modernize judicial technology and equipment, and strengthen the
development, welfare, and protection of judicial personnel to improve the
administration of justice in the country.

       Article VIII, Section 1 of the 1987 Constitution vests judicial power in one
Supreme Court and in such lower courts as may be established by law. Article III,
Section 16 likewise guarantees every person’s right to the speedy disposition of their
cases before all judicial, quasi-judicial, and administrative bodies. The effective
discharge of these constitutional mandates requires a Judiciary equipped with
adequate facilities, reliable technology, modern equipment, and sufficient human
resources. However, realities on the ground paint a different picture otherwise.
Many courts continue to operate with outdated infrastructure, fragmented
information systems, and inadequate personnel.

       This proposed measure institutionalizes a Judiciary modernization program
that supports the construction and rehabilitation of court facilities, the deployment
of secure information technology systems, the acquisition of modern equipment, and
the continuous training, welfare, and protection of judicial personnel.

        A modern and capable Judiciary promotes the timely administration of
justice, strengthens public confidence in the rule of law, improves access to judicial
services, and enables the courts to exercise the judicial power vested in them by the
Constitution.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE MODERNIZATION OF THE JUDICIARY,
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Judiciary Modernization
Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
implement a modernization program for the Judiciary to develop a more efficient,
reliable, competent, technologically adaptive, and modern judicial department. Such
program shall enhance ease of access, enhance the security of judicial transactions,
and improve the institution, litigation, and resolution of civil, criminal, and special
proceedings through the adoption of modern technologies and administrative
processes. Towards this end, the State shall promote the swifter and more accurate
determination of genuine conflicts in the interpretation of the law and enable the
Judiciary to exercise the powers vested in it by the Constitution in a more responsive
and effective manner.

     SEC. 3. Components of the Judiciary Modernization Program. – The Judiciary
Modernization Program shall consist of the following components:

           a. Infrastructure Development and Facility Modernization – The
        objectives of this component are:

                  i.  Acquisition, titling, and management of land and other real
               estate by the national government dedicated exclusively for the
               construction of structures wherein each branch of the Regional Trial
               Court, Metropolitan Trial Courts, Municipal Trial Courts, and
               Municipal Circuit Trial Courts are to be individually situated,
               eliminating the need for shared salas, courtrooms, and other physical
               spaces between independent branches;

              ii.​ Upgrading, modernizing, and streamlining of facilities
             dedicated to the support and execution of judicial functions such as -
             but not limited to - offices of the court staff, offices of the clerks of
             court, offices of the sheriff, offices of the judicial marshals, and private
             chambers of judges and executive judges; and

              iii.​ Development, procurement, and management of secure,
             reliable, and cost-efficient information technology systems that
             promote ease of transaction, allow for automation, and ensure
             integration of existing systems for case management and document
             retrieval essential to or related to the conduct of judicial functions.

          b.​ Equipment Upgrading, Acquisition, and Maintenance – The objectives
      of this component are:

               i.​ Provision to all courts of the most reliable and technologically
             modern internet and telephone connectivity to allow for attendance in
             remote hearings by parties who are determined to participate in court
             proceedings by the rules set forth by the Supreme Court;

              ii.​ Acquisition and provision of modern computer or computer
             systems to allow for faster execution of judicial functions to all relevant
             personnel of the Judiciary; and

              iii.​ Acquisition of data and information technology systems
             necessary to allow submission of documents through means of internet
             connectivity; Provided, That the data and information technology
             systems ensure the integrity of data upon submission and reliability of
             ascertaining the fact of receipt by all parties concerned in the
             transaction; and

          c.​ Personnel Development, Welfare, and Protection – The objectives of
      this component are the hiring of additional qualified personnel, the
      continuous education of all qualified employees, and the assurance of
      security for all members of the Judiciary whose lives and physical well-being
      are at risk by malefactors seeking to unjustly harm personnel in the course of
      performing their duties.

        SEC. 4. Congressional Judiciary Modernization Oversight Committee. – There is
hereby created the Judiciary Modernization Oversight Committee, hereinafter
referred to as the “Committee”, to monitor and oversee the implementation of the
provisions of this Act. The Committee shall be composed of six (6) members from
the House of Representatives and six (6) members from the Senate with the
Chairpersons of the Committee on Justice of both Houses as Joint Chairpersons. The
five (5) other members from each chamber shall be designated by the Speaker of the
House and the Senate President, respectively, with the minority having at least two
(2) representatives from both Chambers.

        SEC. 5. Submission of the Judiciary Modernization Program. – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Court Administrator, upon approval by the
Chief Justice, and with notice to the Department of Justice (DOJ), after consultation
with the executive judges, presiding justices, the Department of Budget and
Management (DBM), and the Department of Finance (DOF), shall submit the
Judiciary Modernization Program to the Committee for its consideration, which
shall indicate the following:

          a.​ The number of personnel, inventory, valuation, and useful life of
      facilities and equipment currently in use and owned by the Judiciary in the
      exercise of their functions;

         b.​ The modernization projects and activities under the program to be
      undertaken and the corresponding component and objectives to which the
      projects are directed to fulfill;

          c.​ The order of priority, the schedule of implementation, and the
      corresponding phases of implementation of the modernization projects and
      activities; and

         d.​ The estimated cost of each modernization project or activity to be
      undertaken.

       SEC. 6. Means of Procurement. – All other laws governing government
procurement notwithstanding, the Judiciary shall procure the necessary property
taking into consideration the following policies and principles:​

          a.​ The Judiciary shall give preference to domestic entities that are
      qualified to participate in the procurement activities to be undertaken under
      this Act;​

          b.​ The Office of the Court Administrator (OCA) shall determine the
      specific minimum standards for items, equipment, technology, and
      information systems to be submitted for approval of the Chief Justice. The
      OCA shall be mandated to ensure that the minimum standards set are
      responsive to the needs of the Judiciary and shall ensure the usability of all
      procured items for a period of more than ten (10) years;​

          c.​ ﻿The Court Administrator, in the implementation of any and all of the
      provisions of this Act, with the approval of the Chief Justice, may enter into
      multi-year contracts and other contractual arrangements: Provided, That none
      of the multi-year contracts and other contractual arrangements shall be the
      most favorable arrangement for the government;​

          d.​ ﻿Congress shall, upon the issuance of a multi-year obligation authority
      by the DBM and a certification by the President, make the corresponding
      appropriation for the ensuing fiscal year. The OCA, upon approval of the
      Chief Justice, shall issue implementing guidelines that ensure consistency

        SEC. 7. Fund Augmentation from the Provincial, City, and Municipal Governments
for Halls of Justice and Other Structures Dedicated to the Execution of the Duty of the
Judiciary. – It shall be mandatory for the provincial, city, and municipal governments
to automatically allocate at least five percent (5%) of its annual appropriations
earmarked for local development projects, sourced from its internal revenue
allocation, as its annual fund augmentation to support priority projects and activities
of Halls of Justices, and other structures dedicated to the execution of the duty of the
Judiciary embodied in their territorial jurisdiction.

      SEC. 8. Period of Implementation. – The Judiciary Modernization Program shall
be implemented over a period of ten (10) years. The payment for amortization of
outstanding multi-year contract obligations incurred under this Act may extend
beyond such period.

        SEC. 9. Review. – Within five (5) years after the effectivity of this Act, the
Committee shall conduct a review or evaluation of the accomplishments and effect
of the Judiciary Modernization Program for the purpose of determining remedial
legislation.

      SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The OCA, upon approval of the
Chief of Justice, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations within one
hundred twenty (120) days from the approval of this Act for its effective
implementation.

        SEC. 11. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
