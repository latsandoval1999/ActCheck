                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

Traffic congestion and road accidents remain significant concerns in both
urban and rural areas. Motorists and pedestrians often experience uncertainty
when approaching intersections because they cannot determine how much
time remains before a traffic or pedestrian signal changes. This may result in
sudden stops, rushed crossings, traffic violations, and avoidable accidents.

The installation of countdown timers on traffic lights and pedestrian lights will
provide motorists and pedestrians with clear and timely information regarding
the remaining duration of each signal. By displaying the time left before a
signal changes, timers can promote safer decision-making, improve compliance
with traffic rules, reduce sudden movements at intersections, and contribute to
the smoother flow of traffic.

Through this measure, the State can promote safer roads, improve traffic
management, protect pedestrians and motorists, and reduce congestion
through the use of simple and effective technology.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT MANDATING THE INSTALLATION OF TIMERS IN TRAFFIC LIGHTS
                   AND PEDESTRIAN LIGHTS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the " Traffic and
Pedestrian Lights Timer Act".

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State recognizes that efficient and safe
road networks are essential to economic growth, a better quality of life, and the
reduction of traffic congestion. Accordingly, it shall promote road safety and
ease traffic through innovative technologies, including the installation of
countdown timers on traffic lights.

SECTION 3. Definition. - For the purpose of this Act, the following terms shall
mean:

       a. "Pedestrian Light" refers to a signal device that indicates when
          pedestrians may cross a road;
       b. "Traffic Light" refers to a signal device that regulates the movement of
          vehicles at a road intersection;
       c. "Timer" refers to a device that measures and displays the remaining
          time before a signal changes.

SECTION 4. Installation of Timers in Traffic and Pedestrian Lights. — Within
one (1) year from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Transportation
shall require the installation of countdown timers on all traffic signals and
pedestrian lights in urban and rural areas. These timers shall clearly display
the remaining time before the signal changes and shall be visible to both
motorists and pedestrians.

SECTION 5. Maintenance of Timers. — The DOTr and LGUs shall ensure that
the timers are properly synchronized, regularly inspected, maintained, repaired,
and replaced when necessary. Intersections with heavy traffic, many
pedestrians, or frequent accidents shall be given priority.

SECTION 6. Penalty for Non-Compliance. —Failure to comply with the
provisions of this Act will result to a fine of not less than Ten Thousand Pesos
(Php 10,000.00) but not more than Twenty Thousand Pesos (Php 20,000.00) for
each traffic light that does not have a functioning timer.
SECTION 7. Appropriations. -The amount necessary to implement the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The Department of
Transportation, Department of Interior and Local Government, and Local
Government Units, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for
the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 9. Separability Cause. - If any provisions of this Act are declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions thereof shall not be
affected.

SECTION 10. Repealing Cause. - All laws inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
