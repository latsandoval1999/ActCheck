                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

In the vibrant and rapidly growing Municipality of Santa Maria, local
infrastructure serves as the essential pulse of everyday commerce and
community life. Spanning approximately 1.60 kilometers, the Barangay Tumana
– Macaiban Road in Barangay Poblacion is far more than a simple strip of
concrete; it is a vital artery connecting fundamental residential, agricultural, and
economic hubs within the town.

Every single day, hundreds of motorists, public transport drivers, small business
owners, and commuters traverse this thoroughfare. It bears a volume of heavy
everyday traffic far beyond what a standard municipal road was ever originally
designed to handle.

Under our current decentralized framework, local government units bear the
primary responsibility for municipal road maintenance. However, as Santa Maria
continues its rapid urbanization, the toll on this 1.60-kilometer stretch has grown
increasingly severe. Potholes, surface deterioration, and drainage limitations are
frequent occurrences that demand continuous, capital-intensive intervention.

While our Local Government Unit in Santa Maria works tirelessly to serve its
constituents, municipal resources are finite. Every peso spent on emergency road
repairs is a peso pulled away from other crucial frontline services—whether in
public health, education, social welfare, or local disaster risk reduction.

Passing the responsibility of this critical road to the national government through
the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) is both a logical and
necessary legislative step.
By converting the Barangay Tumana – Macaiban Road into a National Road, it
integrates the road's maintenance, rehabilitation, and long-term expansion
directly into the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA). It ensures that road
widening, flood mitigation systems, standard pavement markings, and structural
reinforcements meet rigid national DPWH engineering standards.

Our local communities cannot shoulder the weight of regional growth alone. It is
our duty as legislators to provide the structural support our towns need to thrive.
Converting this key municipal road into a national thoroughfare is a tangible,
high-impact step toward ensuring the safety, convenience, and economic vitality
of the residents of Santa Maria and the motorists plying this route.

For these reasons, I earnestly ask for the passage of this bill.

   AN ACT CONVERTING THE MUNICIPAL ROAD IN BRGY. TUMANA –
 MACAIBAN, BRGY. POBLACION IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA,
    BULACAN INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
                          THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. The Brgy. Tumana – Macaiban, Brgy. Poblacion Road in the
municipality of Santa Maria, province of Bulacan is hereby converted into a
National Road.


      SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) shall include
in the Department’s program the improvement, repair, and maintenance of the
said road, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act (GAA).


      SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
