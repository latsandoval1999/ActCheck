                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to rename the Polis Barrio School located in Sitio Polis,
Barangay Poblacion, Municipality of Kibungan, Province of Benguet, to Poldo
Babsa-ay Elementary School in recognition of the invaluable contribution of the late
Poldo Babsa-ay, who generously donated the land on which the present school site
stands.

       Established in 1948 and presently operated and maintained by the
Department of Education (DepEd), Polis Barrio School has long served as an
important institution providing basic education to the children of Barangay
Poblacion and nearby communities. Over the decades, the school has played a vital
role in advancing literacy, nurturing young learners, and contributing to the overall
development of the municipality.

       The initiative to rename the school originated from the Parents and Teachers
Association (PTA) of Polis Barrio School, which formally requested the Municipal
School Board and the Sangguniang Bayan of Kibungan to honor the memory and
generosity of the late Poldo Babsa-ay. The Municipal School Board subsequently
issued a resolution recommending the renaming of the school, consistent with
Sections 13(e) and 99(d) of the Local Government Code of 1991, which authorize the
Sangguniang Bayan to change the name of a public school upon the
recommendation of the Municipal School Board.

       Acting upon this recommendation, the Sangguniang Bayan of Kibungan
enacted Municipal Ordinance No. 135, Series of 2025, formally approving the
renaming of Polis Barrio School to Poldo Babsa-ay Elementary School. The renaming
recognizes the enduring legacy of the late donor and expresses the community’s
gratitude for his generosity in providing the land that continues to serve as a center
of learning for present and future generations.

       In compliance with the established guidelines on the naming and renaming of
public institutions, the National Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP)
reviewed the relevant documents and issued a certification stating that it does not
object to the proposed renaming. The NHCP likewise recommended that the former
name of the school be indicated beneath the new name, “Formerly Polis Barrio
School,” to preserve its historical identity and assist in public recognition and postal
communication.

      Given the strong support of the local community, the endorsement of the
Municipal School Board and the Sangguniang Bayan, and the non-objection of the
NHCP, the renaming of the school is both appropriate and well-justified. This
measure honors a benefactor whose generosity made possible the establishment and
continued operation of a vital educational institution in Kibungan, Benguet.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is respectfully sought.

                          AN ACT
RENAMING POLIS BARRIO SCHOOL IN BARANGAY POBLACION,
MUNICIPALITY OF KIBUNGAN, PROVINCE OF BENGUET, TO POLDO
BABSA-AY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Renaming of Polis Barrio School. - The Polis Barrio School, located in
Sitio Polis, Barangay Poblacion, Municipality of Kibungan, Province of Benguet, is
hereby renamed POLDO BABSA-AY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

       SEC. 2. Historical Reference. - In order to preserve the historical identity of the
institution and facilitate public recognition and postal communication, the previous
name of the school shall be indicated beneath the new name in official signage and
documents as follows:

                        POLDO BABSA-AY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
                             (Formerly Polis Barrio School)

        SEC. 3. Implementation. - The Department of Education (DepEd) shall take the
necessary steps to implement the provisions of this Act, including the updating of
official records, school registries, maps, signages, and other relevant documents
reflecting the new name of the school.

       SEC. 4. Compliance with Existing Guidelines. – The renaming of the school shall
be consistent with the Revised Guidelines on the Naming and Renaming of Streets,
Public Schools, Plazas, Buildings, Bridges, and other Public Structures issued by the
National Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP).

       SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, rules and regulations, or
parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

       SEC. 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
