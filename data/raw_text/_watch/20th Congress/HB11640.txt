AN ACT DECLARING JUNE 24 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN
THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAGWAIT, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL SUR, IN CELEBRATION
OF ITS KALIGUAN FESTIVAL

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Cagwait, Province of Surigao del Sur, celebrates the Kaliguan Festival
every June 24 in connection with the feast of St. John the Baptist. The celebration is a
cultural and tourism event that showcases the municipality's natural resources, particularly
its white sand beaches, while bringing together the local government, national government
agencies, schools, community organizations, and other sectors of the local community.

The declaration of June 24 as a special non-working holiday will provide residents, visitors,
workers, students, and the various sectors of the community sufficient opportunity to
participate in the festivities and will promote the preservation of the municipality's cultural
identity, traditions, and tourism potential.

This measure likewise recognizes the important role of the Local Government Unit of
Cagwait in organizing and supporting the Kaliguan Festival. For this purpose, the
Municipality may provide for and appropriate funds in its annual budget, or utilize legally
available local appropriations, subject to existing budgeting, accounting, auditing,
procurement, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL NO.

Introduced by HON. ROMEO S. MOMO, SR.

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE 24 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN
THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAGWAIT, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL SUR, IN CELEBRATION
OF ITS KALIGUAN FESTIVAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Special Non-Working Holiday. June 24 of every year is hereby declared
a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Cagwait, Province of Surigao del Sur, in
celebration of its Kaliguan Festival.

SEC. 2. Kaliguan Festival. The Municipality of Cagwait shall recognize and promote
the Kaliguan Festival as an annual cultural and tourism celebration of the municipality, including
activities connected with the feast of St. John the Baptist, consistent with applicable national
and local laws.

SEC. 3. Appropriation and Use of Local Funds. The Local Government Unit of
Cagwait shall, subject to the availability of funds and in accordance with its approved locai
development and investment programs and annual budget, appropriate funds or utilize legally
available local appropriations for the planning, organization, implementation, promotion, safety,
cultural activities, and other legitimate expenses related to the Kaliguan Festival. All
expenditures shall comply with existing budgeting, accounting, auditing, procurement, and other
applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

SEC. 4. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, regulations, and
other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 6. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation, whichever comes later.
