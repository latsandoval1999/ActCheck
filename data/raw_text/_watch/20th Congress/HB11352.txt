                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Republic Act No. 7699, otherwise known as the “Limited Portability Law,” allows
workers who have rendered service or made contributions under both the Social Security
System (SSS) and the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) to totalize their creditable
periods for purposes of qualifying for benefits. This recognizes the contributions and years of
service of workers who have moved between the private and public sectors.

      Despite this recognition, pensioners who qualify under the portability scheme have not
always received benefits on the same basis as similarly situated old-age pensioners of the
respective systems, particularly with respect to supplemental pension benefits and pension
increases.

      This measure seeks to ensure fair and equal treatment by granting qualified pensioners
under Republic Act No. 7699 the same annual Christmas cash gift, thirteenth-month
pension, and pension increases granted to similarly situated pensioners of the SSS and GSIS.
A pensioner receiving pensions from both systems shall be entitled to the applicable benefits
from both systems, based on the pension payable by each.

      The measure further seeks to protect existing benefits and ensure that the
implementation of the law shall not result in the diminution or reduction of benefits already
enjoyed by portability pensioners.

      The passage of this measure will uphold the principle that workers who have
contributed to both social insurance systems should not be placed at a disadvantage merely
because their pension entitlement was established through the portability scheme.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                   “AN ACT
      PROVIDING FOR FAIR TREATMENT OF CERTAIN SENIOR CITIZEN
      PENSIONERS BY GRANTING ANNUAL CHRISTMAS CASH GIFT,
      THIRTEENTH-MONTH      PENSION,   AND   PENSION  INCREASES   TO
      PENSIONERS OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY SYSTEM AND GOVERNMENT
      SERVICE INSURANCE SYSTEM WHO ARE BENEFICIARIES OF REPUBLIC
      ACT NO. 7699, “AN ACT INSTITUTING LIMITED PORTABILITY SCHEME IN
      THE SOCIAL SECURITY INSURANCE SYSTEMS BY TOTALIZING THE
      WORKERS’ CREDITABLE SERVICES OR CONTRIBUTIONS IN EACH OF THE
      SYSTEMS”

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       SECTION 1. — Notwithstanding any provision of law, rules, regulations, or issuances
to the contrary, pensioners who are beneficiaries of Republic Act No. 7699, “An Act Instituting
Limited Portability Scheme in the Social Security Insurance Systems by Totalizing the
Workers’ Creditable Services or Contributions in Each of the Systems,” shall be entitled to
receive the same benefits granted to similarly situated old-age pensioners of the respective
systems, including an annual Christmas cash gift from the Government Service Insurance
System (GSIS), a thirteenth-month pension from the Social Security System (SSS), and
pension increases granted by the SSS and GSIS.

       A pensioner receiving pensions from both the SSS and GSIS pursuant to Republic Act
No. 7699 shall be entitled to receive the annual Christmas cash gift from the GSIS, the
thirteenth-month pension from the SSS, and the pension increases granted by both the SSS
and GSIS, in accordance with the pension payable by each System.

      SECTION 2. — Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the SSS and GSIS,
in consultation with the Department of Finance and duly recognized organizations of SSS
and GSIS pensioners, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary to implement
this Act: Provided, that any conflict in the interpretation of this Act and the implementing
rules and regulations shall be resolved in favor of the pensioners.

      SECTION 3. — Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish or reduce the benefits
being enjoyed by pensioners under Republic Act No. 7699 arising from existing laws,
issuances, or SSS and GSIS Board Resolutions.
      SECTION 4. — All laws, presidential decrees or issuances, executive orders, letters of
instruction, administrative orders, SSS and GSIS Board resolutions, and rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
modified, or amended accordingly.

       SECTION 5. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
