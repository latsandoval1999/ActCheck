                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish the National Cancer Center of the Philippines
under the supervision of the Department of Health. It creates a dedicated
sanctuary that will provide accessible, specialized, and integrated services for
cancer prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation, while ensuring that
Filipinos receive compassionate and patient-centered care while battling this
disease.

       The Philippine Cancer Society reported that more than one hundred fifty
thousand (150,000) new cancer cases are recorded annually in the Philippines,
with Cancer now ranking as the second (2nd) leading cause of death in the
country1. Many Filipino patients face late diagnosis, fragmented referral systems,
and limited access to specialized treatment facilities. These drive high
out-of-pocket expenditures, which often result in financial toxicity, treatment
delays, and preventable deaths. Families bear not only the economic burden but
also the emotional and psychological strain that accompanies prolonged illness.

       Despite existing efforts, the country lacks a centralized institution that
integrates advanced clinical care, research, training, and data systems for cancer
control. The National Cancer Center of the Philippines shall serve as the
country’s premier hub for oncological care, research, and capacity building. It
shall provide comprehensive services that cover the full continuum of care, from
early detection to survivorship and palliative support.

       This vital piece of legislation reaffirms the State's commitment to protect
and promote the right to health, and to uphold the dignity of every Filipino
battling this disease. It recognizes that equitable access to quality cancer care is
not only a health imperative but also a matter of social justice.

                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL CANCER CENTER TO BE KNOWN AS THE
NATIONAL CANCER CENTER OF THE PHILIPPINES, AND APPROPRIATING
FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Creation of the Philippine National Cancer Center. — There is hereby
created, under the supervision of the Department of Health (DOH), the National
Cancer Center of the Philippines.

       SEC. 2. Board of Trustees. — The National Cancer Center of the Philippines
shall be governed and its activities and properties be directed, controlled and
managed, by a Board of Trustees, which shall be composed of a Chairman and six (6)
members, all of whom to be appointed by the President of the Philippines upon the
recommendation of the DOH.

        The members of the Board of Trustees shall serve for a period of four (4) years
and until their successors shall have been appointed to fill the vacancy shall hold
office for the unexpired term of the member. Whenever a vacancy shall occur in the
Board, the person appointed to fill the vacancy shall hold office for the unexpired
term of the member.

       The Board shall elect a Chairman from among themselves, who shall be
presiding officer of the Board of Trustees. The Medical Director of the Philippine
National Cancer Center shall serve as Vice-Chairman of the Board.

       Four (4) members of the Board of Trustees shall constitute a quorum to
transact business and any decision of such four (4) members on any matter duly
presented to the Board of Trustees shall constitute a valid and binding act of the
National Cancer Center of the Philippines.

       SEC. 3. Government Contribution. — The Government shall contribute the
necessary land, building, equipment and facilities to the National Cancer Center of
the Philippines.

        SEC. 4. Property of the Center. — The property of the National Cancer Center of
the Philippines shall consist of such real, personal, mixed, and other type of
property, now owned or reserved for, or may hereafter be given, donated, acquired,
transferred or conveyed to the National Cancer Center of the Philippines by the
government, its branches and instrumentalities, any foreign government, as well as
by trust, foundations, corporations, or persons, alien or domestic, in order to carry
out its purposes and objectives as set forth herein.

       SEC. 5. Purposes and Objectives. — The National Cancer Center of the
Philippines shall have the following purposes and objectives:

          a. To construct, establish, equip, maintain, administer and operate a
      specialized hospital for diagnosis, treatment, care, rehabilitation, and/or relief
      of cancer patients;

         b. To promote medical research relative to the prevention and treatment of
      cancer including sponsorship and conduct of relevant congresses, convention,
      seminars and conferences;

         c. To create awareness on cancer prevention, treatment and control
      through public information dissemination;

         d. To encourage the training physicians, nurses, medical technicians,
      health officers and social workers on the practical and scientific conduct and
      implementation of services, and related activities;

         e. To assist universities, colleges, hospitals and research institutions in
      their studies of cancer and encourage advanced training and specialization on
      matters of, or affecting cancer;

         f. To provide psychosocial support services and counseling to the patient
      and family members; and

         g. To consolidate the information from other related organizations for the
      purpose of aiding government's efforts, policies and programs relative to the
      purposes and objectives enumerated herein.

       SEC. 6. Powers. — For the attainment and/or furtherance of the above
enumerated purposes and objectives, the National Cancer Center of the Philippines,
acting through its Board of Trustees, shall have the following orders and functions,
among other things:

          a. To contract and be contracted with;




                                           2
          b. To adopt by-laws, rules and regulations not contrary to law to govern
      the administration and operation of the affairs of the National Cancer Center
      of the Philippines;

         c. To purchase, receive, take or grant, hold, convey, sell, lease, pledge,
      mortgage and otherwise deal with such property of whatever nature the
      transaction of the lawful business of the National Cancer Center of the
      Philippines may reasonably and necessarily require subject to the limitations
      prescribed by law;

        d. To invite foreign cancer specialists and similar experts in the various
      medical fields to train the personnel or trainees or residents of the National
      Cancer Center of the Philippines;

         e. To advance the training of personnel of the Philippine conventions,
      conferences, congresses, seminars to accomplish the purpose and objectives of
      the National Cancer Center of the Philippines;

         f. To exercise such other powers and functions as may be necessary to
      carry out the purposes of the National Cancer Center of the Philippines.

      SEC. 7. Tax Exemption on Donations. — All donations, contributions or
endowment which may be made by entities or persons to the National Cancer
Center of the Philippines shall be exempt from income and gift taxes.

      The National Cancer Center of the Philippines shall be exempt from the
payment of all taxes, charges and fees imposed by the government or any political
subdivision or instrumentality thereof.

       SEC. 8. Assistance from Government Offices. — The National Cancer Center of
the Philippines may call upon any department, bureau, offices, and agency or
instrumentality of the Government, including government-owned or controlled
corporations, for such assistance as it may need in the pursuit of purposes and
objectives.

       SEC. 9. Appropriations. — The amount of One billion pesos (P1,000,000,000.00)
is hereby appropriated for the initial operation of the National Cancer Center of the
Philippines which shall be charged to the appropriations of the DOH under the
current General Appropriations Act. Thereafter, the necessary amount to support the
continued operation and maintenance of the National Cancer Center of the
Philippines shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 10. Auditing. — The books of accounts of the National Cancer Center of
the Philippines shall be subject to periodic auditing by the Commission on Audit.

      SEC. 11. Annual Report.- The National Cancer Center of the Philippines shall
render to the DOH an annual report on its operations and accomplishments.

       SEC. 12. Effect of Dissolution. — If for any reason, the National Cancer Center
of the Philippines is dissolved, its properties and assets remaining at the time of
such dissolution shall revert to the Philippine Government for disposition in
accordance with law.

      SEC. 13. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is held invalid or
unconstitutional the other provisions not so declared shall remain in force and effect.

       SEC. 14. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders and rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 15. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
