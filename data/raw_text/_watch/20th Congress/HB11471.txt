                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Entering the workforce after graduation is an important transition for
young Filipinos, but it often comes with immediate expenses. Job applications,
transportation, documentation, medical examinations, work attire, and other
requirements can impose a financial burden at a time when many fresh
graduates have yet to receive their first income from employment.

      For many young Filipinos, the lack of readily available funds can make the
transition from education to employment more difficult. A modest and timely
form of financial assistance can help bridge this gap and enable graduates to
pursue employment opportunities without having to rely immediately on family
members or incur debt for basic employment-related expenses.

      This measure seeks to provide qualified fresh Filipino graduates of duly
recognized or authorized higher education institutions or duly registered
technical-vocational programs in the Philippines with a one-time cash grant of
Five Thousand Pesos (₱5,000.00).

      The proposed grant is not conditioned on unemployment or household
income. Its purpose is to provide basic transition assistance to qualified
graduates as they seek and commence employment. The measure likewise limits
availment to one time per beneficiary and requires application within one year
from graduation or completion of the program.

      The Commission on Higher Education (CHED) is designated as the lead
implementing agency, in coordination with the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (TESDA) for the verification and processing of
applications of graduates and completers of technical-vocational programs. The
measure further provides for electronic verification of graduation records and
safeguards against duplicate availment, while requiring compliance with existing
data privacy laws.

     The amount necessary for implementation shall be provided through the
annual General Appropriations Act.
      The enactment of this measure will provide practical and immediate
assistance to young Filipinos as they take their first steps toward employment
and productive participation in the economy.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                           “AN ACT
      PROVIDING FRESH FILIPINO GRADUATES OF PHILIPPINE
      HIGHER    EDUCATION    AND    TECHNICAL-VOCATIONAL
      INSTITUTIONS AND PROGRAMS WITH A ONE-TIME CASH
      GRANT OF FIVE THOUSAND PESOS (₱5,000.00)”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Fresh Graduates
One-Time Cash Grant Act of 2026.”

      Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the declared policy of the State to
recognize the vital role of the youth in nation-building. The State shall promote
and protect the welfare of the youth and facilitate their access to employment
and livelihood opportunities.

      To this end, the State shall establish measures to assist fresh Filipino
graduates in addressing the initial financial burden associated with seeking and
obtaining employment, including expenses for job applications, transportation,
documentation, medical examinations, work attire, and other reasonable pre-
employment and employment-related expenses.

       Section 3. Coverage of Cash Grant to Fresh Graduates. - All qualified
Fresh Filipino Graduates of duly recognized or authorized higher education
institutions or duly registered technical-vocational programs in the Philippines
shall be entitled to a one-time cash grant of Five Thousand Pesos (₱5,000.00) to
cover the pre-employment, onboarding, and other initial employment-related
expenses

       For purposes of this Act, a “Fresh Filipino Graduate” refers to a Filipino
citizen who has successfully graduated from a duly recognized or authorized
higher education institution or completed a duly registered technical-vocational
program in the Philippines, and who applies for the cash grant within one (1)
year from the date of graduation or completion of the program.
     No qualified beneficiary shall be entitled to receive the cash grant more
than once, regardless of the number of degrees, certificates, or programs
completed.

       Section 4. Proof of Graduation or Completion. – To avail of the one-time
cash grant under this Act, the Fresh Filipino Graduate shall provide proof of
successful graduation or completion of his or her degree, diploma, certificate, or
program through documentary submission or electronic verification of official
records, which may include a diploma, certificate of graduation or completion,
certification, or other official school or training records issued by the institution
from which he or she graduated or completed the program.

       The diploma, certification, or other official school or training document
shall, whenever applicable, indicate the date of graduation or completion and the
degree, diploma, certificate, course, or training program completed, and shall be
duly signed or authenticated by the head or authorized representative of the
concerned institution.

       The implementing agencies shall establish mechanisms for the electronic
verification of graduation or completion records to facilitate the efficient
processing of applications and, where practicable, dispense with the submission
of physical documentary proof.

     Section 5. Implementing Agencies and Inter-Agency Coordination. -
The Commission on Higher Education (CHED) shall be the lead agency in the
implementation of this Act.

       The CHED shall, in coordination with the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (TESDA), establish the mechanisms for the application,
verification, approval, and disbursement of the cash grant.

      The CHED shall administer the grant for qualified graduates of higher
education institutions, while TESDA shall administer the grant for qualified
graduates or completers of technical-vocational education and training programs
within its mandate, in accordance with the mechanisms established jointly by
CHED and TESDA under this Act.

       The CHED and TESDA shall establish appropriate mechanisms for the
verification of graduation or completion records and the prevention of duplicate
availment of the cash grant.

      The concerned agencies may coordinate with other government agencies
and local government units for the efficient implementation of this Act.

      The processing and implementation of the cash grant shall comply with
Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the “Data Privacy Act of 2012,” and
other applicable laws, rules, and regulations on data privacy and security.
      Section 6. Appropriations. - The amount necessary to implement this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act under the
appropriations of the CHED and the TESDA, subject to the usual budgetary,
accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

      Section 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the CHED and the TESDA, in coordination
with other concerned government agencies, shall jointly promulgate the
necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

      The implementing rules and regulations shall include, among others, the
application and verification procedures, documentary requirements,
mechanisms for the disbursement of the cash grant, measures to prevent
duplicate availment, and appropriate mechanisms for electronic verification of
graduation or completion records.

      Section 8. Separability Clause. - If any section or provision of this Act is
held unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions not affected
thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

      Section 9. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

       Section 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
following its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers
of general circulation.
