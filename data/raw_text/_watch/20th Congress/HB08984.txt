                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

     “A false witness will not go unpunished, and he who breathes out lies will
     perish.” — Proverbs 19:9

      Truthfulness in sworn statements and judicial proceedings is a fundamental pillar
of the justice system. The integrity of affidavits, testimonies, and criminal complaints
is essential to the fair administration of justice, the protection of individual rights, and
the preservation of public trust in legal institutions. When individuals deliberately make
false statements under oath, particularly through fabricated criminal complaints, they
do not merely commit perjury—they weaponize the legal system against innocent
persons.

      Under existing law, perjury is penalized; however, the current penalties may not
sufficiently address the gravity of cases where false statements are used to maliciously
impute serious crimes upon another person. Fabricated criminal complaints can lead
to wrongful arrests, prolonged detention, reputational damage, financial loss, and
severe emotional and psychological harm to the accused. In some instances,
individuals may suffer consequences equivalent to those of actual offenders, despite
being entirely innocent.

      This proposed measure seeks to strengthen accountability by imposing a penalty
commensurate to the seriousness of the false accusation. Where perjury is committed
through the execution of a fabricated criminal complaint, affidavit, or sworn statement
falsely imputing the commission of a crime, and where the penalty for such falsely
imputed offense is higher than the existing penalty for perjury, the law shall impose a
penalty equivalent to that prescribed for the offense falsely alleged.

      By aligning the penalty with the gravity of the harm caused, the measure aims to
deter the misuse of legal processes, uphold the sanctity of sworn declarations, and
protect individuals from malicious and baseless accusations. It reinforces the principle
that the justice system must not be exploited as a tool for harassment, coercion, or
personal vendetta.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
