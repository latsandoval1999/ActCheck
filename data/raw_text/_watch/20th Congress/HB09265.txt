                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Dentists in the public sector play a vital role in promoting oral health,
preventing dental diseases, and delivering essential healthcare services to
Filipinos, especially in underserved and vulnerable communities. They serve
in government hospitals, rural health units, public schools, state universities
and colleges, and other public health institutions where access to dental care
is often limited.

       Despite the importance of their profession and the increasing demands
of public healthcare service, government dentists continue to receive
compensation that is not commensurate with their duties, responsibilities,
qualifications, and professional expertise. The current salary structure has
contributed to low morale, difficulty in retaining qualified personnel in
government service, and the migration of dental professionals to the private
sector or overseas employment opportunities.

       Republic Act No. 7305, otherwise known as the “Magna Carta of Public
Health Workers,” recognizes the State policy of promoting and improving the
social and economic well-being of health workers and encouraging competent
professionals to remain in government service. In line with this policy, there
is a pressing need to upgrade the salary grades of dentists in the public sector
to ensure fair compensation, strengthen the delivery of oral healthcare
services, and enhance the government’s capacity to attract and retain
competent dental professionals.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                         AN ACT
      MANDATING THE UPGRADE OF THE SALARY GRADES OF
      DENTISTS IN THE PUBLIC SECTOR, APPROPRIATING
      FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

        Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
 Philippines in Congress Assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Public
Dentists’ Position Reclassification and Compensation Act of 2026.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - Section 2 of Republic Act No.
7305, otherwise known as the “Magna Carta of Public Health Workers,”
provides that the State shall promote and improve the social and economic
well-being of health workers, their living and working conditions, and terms
of employment; develop their skills and capabilities so that they may become
more responsive and better equipped to deliver government health programs
and projects; and encourage those with proper qualifications and excellent
abilities to join and remain in government service.

       SECTION 3. Coverage. - This Act shall cover all dentists, regardless of
employment status, in all public health institutions, whether national or
locally funded, national government agencies including state universities and
colleges, government owned and controlled corporations, and local
government units.

      SECTION 4. Salary Grade Upgrading. - The salary grades of dentists
in government service are hereby upgraded to the following salary grades:

             Position Title            New Salary Grade
             Dentist I                       17
             Dentist Il                      20
             Dentist Ill                     23
             Dentist IV                      25
             Dentist V                       27
      SECTION 5. Appropriations. - The Department of Budget and
Management shall appropriate such amounts as may be necessary to carry
out the objectives of this Act. Funds required for the implementation of this
Act upon its effectivity shall be sourced from savings and other sources as
determined by their respective offices. Subsequent allocations shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 6. Rules and Regulations. - The Secretary of the
Department of Budget and Management after consultation with the
appropriate agencies of government as well as the Philippine Dental
Association, shall formulate and prepare the necessary rules and regulations
to implement the provisions of this Act within thirty (30) days from its
effectivity.

       SECTION7. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders,
administrative orders, rules, regulations, decrees, and other issuances or
parts thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
revoked, repealed, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in operation and remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after completion of its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2)
newspapers of general circulation.

      Approved.
