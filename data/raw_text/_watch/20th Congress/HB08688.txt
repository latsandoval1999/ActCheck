                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This proposed measure aims to institutionalize a long-term, strategic
approach to infrastructure planning and development through the creation of
a Comprehensive Infrastructure Development Masterplan. This Masterplan
will be fully integrated into all stages of the planning, programming, and
budgeting cycle. To be spearheaded by the Masterplan for Infrastructure and
National Development (MIND) Council, the initiative seeks to advance the
country’s long-term development vision of becoming a prosperous middle-
class society. It is designed to rationalize public and private infrastructure
investments, promote policy continuity, and clearly delineate the roles and
responsibilities of government agencies and instrumentalities involved in the
implementation of critical infrastructure projects. The Masterplan will adopt a
holistic framework encompassing key infrastructure subsectors, including
land, air, and maritime transportation and logistics; energy; water resources;
information and communications technology; social infrastructure such as
housing, health, and education; agri-fisheries modernization and food logistics
systems; as well as asset preservation and maintenance.

      The MIND Council shall be chaired by the President of the Philippines,
with the Secretary of Economy, Planning, and Development serving as Vice-
Chair. Its membership shall include the heads of major infrastructure-related
agencies, thereby strengthening inter-agency coordination in the formulation,




                                                                               1
implementation, and monitoring of infrastructure policies and projects.

       This legislation is timely, given the sustained increase in national
infrastructure spending. From 2022 to 2025, budgetary allocations for
infrastructure development grew by an annual average of 7.9 percent. As
infrastructure expenditures continue to expand, the need for sound long-term
planning becomes imperative to maximize returns on investment and to avoid
the inclusion of redundant or low-impact projects. With infrastructure
commanding a larger share of public resources, it is essential to ensure that
taxpayers’ funds are directed toward high-impact initiatives that deliver
meaningful benefits to the public. It is envisioned that this measure will enable
the country to address long-standing infrastructure gaps and propel the
Philippines toward higher levels of economic growth.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 3         SEC. 5. Formulation of the Comprehensive Infrastructure Development
 4   Master Plan (CIDMP). – There shall be formulated a 30-year Comprehensive
 5   Infrastructure Development Master Plan (CIDMP) which shall set forth the
 6   country’s long-term vision, goals, and strategy for infrastructure development. The
 7   CIDMP shall be the framework for forward planning, prioritization, and
 8   development of infrastructure investment and programs, taking into consideration
 9   the country’s long-term national development plans, including the medium-term
10   Philippine Development Plan. It shall serve as the integrated framework of
11   infrastructure programs and projects of the government enterprise architecture.
12         The CIDMP shall be reviewed and updated every 10 years or earlier as the
13   needs arises, in anticipation of disruptions, emergencies, crises, and new and
14   emerging technologies and trends: Provided, That such early review and update be
15   consistent with long-term national development vision and goals.
16         In order to effectively implement this Act, a whole-of-government approach
17   shall be adopted for the formulation and adopted of the CIDMP. This approach
18   shall facilitate engagement    with   government      agencies,    instrumentalities,
19   government-owned and -controlled corporations (GOCCs), local government units
20   (LGUs), regional development councils (RDCs), ICT councils, technical and
21   standards organization and other relevant stakeholders towards ensuring the full and
22   effect implementation of this Act.
