                               EXPLANATORY NOTE


      Road safety remains a critical concern in the Philippines, with a significant
number of accidents attributed to mechanical failures of buses, trucks, and other
heavy vehicles. These vehicles, by their very nature, carry large numbers of
passengers or heavy loads, and when they malfunction on the road, the
consequences are often severe and far-reaching.

       This proposed measure seeks to mandate monthly preventive maintenance
and quarterly inspections of buses, trucks, and other heavy vehicles. The objective is
to institutionalize regular and systematic checks to detect mechanical issues early,
thereby reducing the risk of accidents caused by vehicle failure. Such inspections will
not only protect passengers and drivers but also safeguard other motorists and
pedestrians who share the road.

       By requiring transport operators to comply with strict maintenance and
inspection schedules, the State affirms its commitment to ensuring public safety,
promoting accountability in the transport sector, and fostering a culture of
responsibility in road use. This measure complements existing traffic regulations by
addressing the root cause of many accidents -- mechanical neglect -- thus
strengthening the overall framework for road safety.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACТ
MANDATING MONTHLY PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE AND QUARTERLY
INSPECTIONS OF BUSES, TRUCKS, AND OTHER HEAVY VEHICLES TO
PREVENT ROAD ACCIDENTS CAUSED BY MECHANICAL FAILURE AND TO
ENSURE ROAD SAFETY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “HEAVY VEHICLE
PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE AND INSPECTION ACT.”


      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to ensure public safety on roads by preventing road accidents caused by
mechanical failures of heavy vehicles. Towards this end, the State shall require the
regular maintenance and inspection of all buses, trucks, and other heavy vehicles
operating within the country.


      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:


      a)     Heavy Vehicles refer to buses, trucks, and other motor vehicles with
gross ​vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of more than 4,500 kilograms;




                                         2
      b)​      Preventive Maintenance refers to scheduled inspections, servicing and
​     repairs intended to prevent mechanical failures and ensure optimal vehicle
​     performance.


​     SECTION 4. Monthly Preventive Maintenance. - All heavy vehicles
operating within the Philippines shall be required to undergo monthly preventive
maintenance to ensure their safe operation: Provided, that such maintenance shall
include, but not limited to, through checks of the engine and break systems, tire
condition and alignment, steering and suspension systems, lighting and signaling
devices, fluid levels, leak detection, and emission levels: Provided, further, that the
preventive maintenance must be performed by a Land Transportation Office
(LTO)-accredited service center: Provided, finally, that a Monthly Preventive
Maintenance Report shall be issued upon completion of the monthly preventive
maintenance.


      SECTION 5. Quarterly Inspection by the Land Transportation Office
(LTO). - All heavy vehicles shall be required to undergo quarterly inspection by the
LTO or an LTO-accredited Motor Vehicle Inspection Center (MVIC). A Certificate of
Roadworthiness shall be issued upon successful completion of the inspection.


      SECTION 6. Maintenance and Inspection Records. - All registered
owners and/or operators of heavy vehicles shall maintain and keep a copy of the
following records onboard each vehicle at all times:


      a)​      Monthly Preventive Maintenance Report issued by the LTO-accredited
​     service center; and


      b) Certificate of Roadworthiness issued by the LTO or an accredited Motor ​
Vehicle Inspection Center (MVIC).


These documents shall be kept for a minimum period of two (2) years and shall be
readily available for inspection by the Land Transportation Office (LTO), Philippine

National Police-Highway Patrol Group (PNP-HPG), or any law enforcement officer,
upon request.


Failure to maintain or present these documents upon lawful inspection shall give rise
to the presumption that the vehicle has not complied with the mandatory monthly
preventive maintenance or quarterly inspection requirements under this Act. Such
failure shall subject the registered owner or operator to the penalties provided in
Section 7 of this Act.


       SECTION 7. Penalties. – Any owner or operator who fails to comply with
the provisions of this Act shall be subject to the following penalties:


       a)​    First Offense - Fine of Twenty Thousand Pesos (P20,000.00);


       b)​    Second Offense - Fine of Fifty Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00) and ​
suspension of vehicle registration for two (2) months;


       c)​    Third and Subsequent Offenses - Fine of One Hundred Thousand Pesos
​      ​      (P100,000.00) and suspension of vehicle registration for six (6)
months.


       SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Land Transportation Office (LTO), the
Department of Transportation (DOTr), and the Land Transportation Franchising and
Regulatory Board (LTFRB), in consultation with the appropriate government agencies
and other stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to
implement this Act.


       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. - If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not affected
thereby shall remain in force and effect.
