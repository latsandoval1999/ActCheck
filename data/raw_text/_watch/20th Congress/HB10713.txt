                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Barangay governments, as the frontline units of government, play a vital role
in implementing national and local programs, maintaining peace and order, resolving
community disputes, responding to disasters and emergencies, and delivering basic
services at the grassroots. The effective discharge of these responsibilities depends
largely on the stability, continuity, and institutional capacity of barangay governments
and their officials.

       This measure seeks to strengthen governance at the barangay level by
extending the term of office of barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan officials from four
(4) years to five (5) years and by resetting the next Barangay and Sangguniang
Kabataan Elections from the first Monday of November 2026 to the first Monday of
November 2028.

        The proposed five-year term will provide barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
officials with adequate time to plan, implement, and complete development programs
and projects, ensure continuity in governance, reduce disruptions caused by frequent
electoral exercises, and allow local officials to focus more on public service than on
recurring election cycles.

       Correspondingly, postponing the next Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections to November 2028 is necessary to harmonize the election schedule with the
proposed five-year term of office and to ensure an orderly transition to the new
electoral cycle. This will promote stability in local governance, avoid disruptions in
the implementation of ongoing community programs, and enable barangay
governments to sustain development initiatives that require longer periods for
planning and execution.

       Ultimately, this measure reinforces the role of barangays, as envisioned under
the Local Government Code of 1991, as the primary planning and implementing units
of government policies, plans, programs, projects, and activities within their
communities, as well as forums where the collective views of the people are expressed,
consensus is built, and local disputes are amicably resolved.

       By providing greater continuity in leadership and a more stable governance
framework, this measure seeks to strengthen grassroots institutions and improve the
delivery of public services to every Filipino community.
       In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

AN ACT FIXING THE TERM OF OFFICE OF BARANGAY OFFICIALS AND MEMBERS
 OF THE SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN TO FIVE (5) YEARS, AMENDING REPUBLIC
ACT NO. 12232 OR “AN ACT SETTING THE TERM OF OFFICE OF THE BARANGAY
OFFICIALS AND MEMBERS OF THE SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN, AND FOR OTHER
                              PURPOSES”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


        Section 1. Section 1 of Republic Act No. 12232 or “An Act Setting the Term of

Office of Barangay Officials and Members of the Sangguniang Kabataan, and for Other

Purposes” is hereby amended to read as follows:

        “SECTION 1. Term of Office. – The term of office of all elected barangay and

Sangguniang Kabataan officials shall be [four (4)] FIVE (5) years. No elective

barangay official shall serve for more than three (3) consecutive terms in the same

position. No elective Sangguniang Kabataan official shall serve for more than one (1)

term in the same positions. Voluntary renunciation of office for any length of time

shall not be considered as an interruption in the continuity of service for the full term

for which the elective official was elected.”

        Sec. 2. Section 2 of Republic Act No. 12232 is hereby amended to read as

follows:
       “SEC. 2. Date of Election. – The next regular barangay and Sangguniang

Kabataan elections shall be held on the first Monday of November [2026] 2028 and

every [four (4)] FIVE (5) years thereafter.”

       Sec. 3. Section 5 of Republic Act No. 12232 is hereby amended to read as

follows:

       “SEC. 5. Transitory Provision. – Incumbent elective barangay officials SHALL BE

REGARDED AS HAVING COMPLETED ONE TERM IN THEIR RESPECTIVE

POSITIONS, CONSIDERING THE EXTENSION OF THEIR TERMS BY TWO (2)

YEARS. INCUMBENT OFFICIALS serving their third consecutive term in the same

position shall [not be eligible] BE INELIGIBLE to run for the same position in the

November [2026] 2028 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections.”

       Sec. 4. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to implement the provisions

of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act.

       Sec. 5. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part hereof is declared

unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not affected thereby shall

remain in full force and effect.

       Sec. 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, acts, decrees, executive orders, issuances,

and rules and regulations or parts thereof which are contrary to and inconsistent with

this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

       Sec. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its

publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
