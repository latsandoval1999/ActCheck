                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The 1987 Constitution, recognizes the value of local governments. The Barangay
and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections (BSKE) play a vital role in ensuring
grassroots democracy and local governance.

However, numerous challenges have arisen that affect the effective conduct of
these elections. These include the need for continuity in local programs, the
efficient allocation of public funds, and the prioritization of national recovery
efforts in light of economic pressures.

Furthermore, the current global climate, marked by ongoing armed conflicts and
geopolitical tensions, has underscored the urgent need for governments to
exercise prudence in fiscal management. The Philippines must ensure the proper
allocation of its fiscal resources to safeguard national interests, mitigate the
economic impact of war-related disruptions, and strengthen resilience against
external shocks. This election will also place a significant financial burden on the
government, diverting resources that could otherwise be used for essential public
services and development programs.

This proposed measure seeks to postpone the Barangay and Sangguniang
Kabataan Elections originally scheduled for 2026 to 2029. The extension of the
term of incumbent barangay and SK officials will allow for the uninterrupted
implementation of ongoing projects, programs, and reforms at the community
level.

Moreover, it aims to afford the government ample time to introduce and
implement necessary electoral reforms that will strengthen the integrity,
transparency, and efficiency of future barangay elections.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Section 1 of Republic Act No. 9164, as amended, is hereby further
amended to read as follows:

     “Section 1. Date of Election. - There shall be synchronized barangay and
sangguniang kabataan elections, which shall be held on the last Monday of
October 2029 and every three (3) years thereafter.”

SEC. 2. Hold-Over. - Until their successors have been duly elected and qualified,
all incumbent barangay and sangguniang kabataan officials shall remain in office,
unless sooner removed or suspended for cause: Provided, That barangay and
sangguniang kabataan officials who are ex officio members of the sangguniang
bayan, sangguniang panlungsod, or sangguniang panlalawigan, as the case may
be, shall continue to serve as such members in the sanggunian concerned until
the next barangay and sangguniang kabataan elections unless removed in
accordance with their existing rules or for cause.

SEC. 3. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the implementation of this
Act shall be taken from the appropriations of the Commission on Elections
(COMELEC) under the General Appropriations Act and/or supplementary
appropriation thereafter.

SEC. 4. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act shall be held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not otherwise affected shall
remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. - All laws, acts, presidential decrees, executive orders,
proclamations, rules and regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof which
are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SEC. 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
