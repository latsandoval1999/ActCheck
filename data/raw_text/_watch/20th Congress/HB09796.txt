                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to provide a five-year validity period for all Professional
Identification Cards (PICs) issued by the Professional Regulation Commission
(PRC), subject to compliance with all legal and mandatory requirements for renewal,
including the Continuing Professional Development (CPD) requirements under
Republic Act No. 10912.

       At present, professionals are required to renew their PRC identification cards
every three years, resulting in additional financial costs, administrative burdens, and
inconvenience for millions of Filipino professionals. Frequent renewals often lead to
long queues, processing delays, scheduling difficulties, and unnecessary expenses,
particularly for professionals residing in far-flung areas or those working overseas.

       This proposed measure seeks to address these concerns by extending the
validity of Professional Identification Cards from three years to five years while
retaining all existing legal and regulatory safeguards for renewal. The bill likewise
directs the PRC to strengthen its digital systems and administrative processes to
ensure more efficient and accessible public service delivery.

       Providing a longer validity period for PRC identification cards promotes
convenience, efficiency, and ease of doing business for Filipino professionals without
compromising regulatory standards. At a time when the country continues to
pursue digitalization and streamlined government services, the State must adopt
practical and responsive policies that reduce unnecessary burdens on the public.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR A FIVE-YEAR VALIDITY PERIOD OF THE PROFESSIONAL
IDENTIFICATION CARDS OF ALL PROFESSIONAL UNDER THE
PROFESSIONAL REGULATION COMMISSION

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. All Professional Identification Cards (PICs) issued by the
Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) shall be valid for five (5) years, subject
to the compliance with all the legal, procedural and mandatory requirements for its
renewal such as those indicated under Republic Act No. 109212, otherwise known as
the Continuing Professional Development (CPD) Act of 2016.

       SEC. 2. In order to operationalize the provisions of this Act, the Information
and Communication Technology Service (ICTS) and other concerned
offices/divisions of the PRC shall complete the necessary systems enhancement
(Licensure Examination Registration Information System or the LERIS) and other
administrative processes, including the finalization of the revised assessment of
annual registration fees within six (6) months from the effectivity of this Act.

       SEC 3. If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected are in full force
and effect.

       SEC 4. All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, and rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are amended,
repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.
