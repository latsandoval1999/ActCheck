                               EXPLANATORY NOTE


       Special Education (SPED) teachers perform highly specialized functions that go
beyond conventional classroom instruction. They are required to address diverse
learning needs, manage individualized education plans, and provide targeted
interventions for learners with disabilities, all of which demand advanced training,
specialized competencies, and sustained emotional and professional commitment.

       Despite the critical nature of their role in delivering inclusive and equitable
education, the current compensation framework for SPED teachers does not
adequately reflect the complexity, intensity, and expertise required in their work. This
disparity has contributed to challenges in recruitment, retention, and professional
development within the SPED sector, ultimately affecting the quality and accessibility
of services for learners with special needs.

        This bill seeks to upgrade the minimum salary grade level of SPED teachers
from Salary Grade 14 to Salary Grade 22 in recognition of their specialized
qualifications and responsibilities. The proposed adjustment aligns compensation
with the level of technical expertise required, while providing a meaningful incentive
to attract and retain qualified educators in the field of special education.

       Moreover, strengthening support for SPED teachers is integral to the State’s
commitment to inclusive education and equal access to learning opportunities for all
Filipinos. A well-supported SPED workforce ensures that learners with disabilities
receive appropriate interventions and are meaningfully integrated into the education
system. By providing just and commensurate compensation, this measure aims to
enhance the delivery of SPED services, improve educational outcomes for learners
with special needs, and uphold the State’s obligation to promote inclusive and quality
education.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                         AN ACT
UPGRADING THE MINIMUM SALARY GRADE LEVEL OF SPED TEACHERS
                FROM SALARY GRADE 14 TO 22

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:


SECTION 1. Increase in the Minimum Salary Grade Level. - The present minimum
salary grade level of public-school Special Education (SPED) teachers in the
elementary and secondary schools shall be upgraded from Grade 14 to Grade 22:
Provided, however, That the salary upgrading shall be differentiated in accordance
with the 5 qualifications and length of service rendered by teachers and shall not be
prejudiced by across-the-board salary adjustments.


SECTION 2. Priority in Budget Allocation. - The National Government shall
appropriate such amount, as may be necessary to carry out the objectives of this Act:
Provided, That the salary increase of public-school SPED teachers shall take priority
over other non-educational and non-agricultural budgetary allocations.

SECTION 3. Appropriations. - The Department of Education (DepEd) shall come up
with a specific programmed budget needed to cover the expenses for the upgrading
in salary levels for all the corresponding SPED teacher plantilla positions for a period
of at least five (5) years to allow the Department of Budget and Management (DBM)
to make the necessary budgetary adjustments to facilitate the smooth implementation
of this Act.
The amount necessary to implement the provisions of this Act shall be included in the
General Appropriations Act for the year following the approval of this Act.


SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DepEd and the DBM shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this
Act.


SECTION S. Repealing Clause. - All provisions of laws, orders, decrees, including
rules and regulations inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed and/or modified
accordingly.


SECTION 6. Separability Clause. - If any part or provision of this Act shall be held
unconstitutional or invalid, other provisions hereof which are not affected thereby
shall continue to be in full force and effect.


SECTION 7. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
