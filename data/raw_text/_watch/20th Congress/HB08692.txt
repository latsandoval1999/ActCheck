                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Barangays, as the smallest political units, serve as the primary planning
and implementing arm of the government and the most accessible level of
governance for the Filipino people. Under the Local Government Code of 1991,
barangays were envisioned to exercise local autonomy and deliver basic
services at the grassroots level. However, despite this mandate, many
barangays continue to face structural limitations, including inadequate
resources, limited institutional support, and insufficient compensation for
barangay officials.


      This bill seeks to establish a Magna Carta for Barangays to complement
and strengthen the existing framework under the Local Government Code. It
aims to professionalize barangay governance by recognizing barangay officials
as regular government personnel entitled to appropriate compensation and
benefits, while also ensuring the provision of essential services such as potable
water, transportation access, education, and primary healthcare in every
community.


     Furthermore, the measure reinforces the principle of local autonomy by
guaranteeing a more equitable and predictable share in national and local
revenues, ensuring the timely release of funds, and empowering barangays to

manage resources for their own development. It also promotes
community-based initiatives such as scholarship programs, employment
prioritization, and cooperative enterprise development to stimulate inclusive
growth at the grassroots level.


      By strengthening institutional capacity and aligning with the
decentralization framework established under the Local Government Code,
this proposed Magna Carta aims to enable barangays to more effectively
perform their vital role in governance and public service delivery.


     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                                  AN ACT

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
           Congress assembled:


         I. TITLE, DECLARATION OF POLICY, AND GENERAL PRINCIPLES

 1             SECTION 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as “The Magna Carta for
 2      Barangays.”

 3             SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to promote,
 4      develop and improve the general welfare of barangay residents, raise the economic
 5      and social status of barangay officials, grant every barangay the basic facilities for
 6      decent, healthy and comfortable living therein and provide new measures that will
 7      inspire its enjoyment of local autonomy to ensure the effective performance of its
 8      role as the primary planning and implementing unit of government programs,
 9      projects and activities, and as a forum in which the collective views of the people in
10      the community may be crystallized and considered.
11             SEC. 3. General Principles. – The following are the general principles of
12      this Act:

 5                    III.   BASIC PRIORITIES IN THE BARANGAY

 6          SEC. 6. Drinking Water for Every Barangay. – It is the right of every
 7   barangay to have a regular supply of clean and potable drinking water. To attain this
 8   goal, every city and municipality, as the case may be, is hereby required to construct
 9   and/or maintain at least one deep well with pumping device for drawing drinking
10   water to supply the needs of every one thousand residents for each barangay within
11   its jurisdiction.
12          SEC. 7. Transportation for Every Barangay. – It is also the right of every
13   barangay to have public transportation available at least once a day. For this purpose,
14   every municipality or city, as the case may be, should make such necessary
15   representations before the appropriate government agencies to require public utility
16   companies operating within its jurisdiction to provide the minimum means of
17   transportation in every barangay.
18          SEC. 8. Schools, Health Centers, and Barangay Halls for the Barangays.–
19   Every barangay is entitled to have at least one elementary school: Provided, that
20   there shall be at least one high school for every five (5) kilometers from the barangay
21   center. It shall also be the right of every barangay to have one health center and one
22   barangay hall.

 4          SEC. 13. Scholarship Grant. – Every barangay shall sponsor at least one
 5   scholarship grant every year leading to any bachelor degree to be awarded by a
 6   committee of five persons appointed by the Sangguniang Barangay with the
 7   approval of the Punong Barangay after competitive examinations are given to
 8   applicants who should be bona fide residents of the barangay. The grant shall include
 9   tuition fees and all other school fees, reasonable allowances for schoolbooks,
10   supplies and transportation and other expenses as the barangay may deem
11   appropriate.
12          SEC. 14. Priority in Employment. – Residents in every barangay shall have
13   the priority in the hiring of workers and laborers to be needed in any government
14   construction or development projects within the barangay.
15          SEC. 15. Cooperative Enterprise. – Cooperative enterprises in the barangay
16   shall be encouraged and every barangay is directed to give full assistance for the
17   establishment and organization of said enterprises and to take every possible effort
18   to make cooperative undertakings a way of life in the barangay.
