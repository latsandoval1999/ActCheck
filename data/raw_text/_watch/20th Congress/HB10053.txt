                           AN ACT
          DECLARING EVERY THIRD WEEK OF NOVEMBER AS
              “NATIONAL ENTREPRENEURSHIP WEEK”

                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Section 14, Article XII of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that
the State shall promote the sustained development of a reservoir of Filipino
entrepreneurs and managers.

      The Philippines remains a highly entrepreneurial economy, with micro,
small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) continuing to serve as the backbone of
national development. According to the latest available data from the
Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), based on the 2023 List of
Establishments of the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), there are
approximately 1.2 million business enterprises operating in the country. Of
these, 99.6% are MSMEs, while only about 0.4% are large enterprises. Micro
enterprises account for roughly 89% of total MSMEs, followed by small
enterprises at around 10%, and medium enterprises at less than 1% (DTI, 2024;
PSA, 2023).1

       MSMEs play a critical role in employment generation and economic
activity, contributing approximately 63% of total employment in the Philippines

to modern business practices.

      One of the most pressing constraints encountered by current and aspiring
entrepreneurs is the lack of adequate knowledge in business development,
innovation, and financial management, particularly at the early stages of
enterprise formation. Strengthening entrepreneurial education and cultivating
an entrepreneurial mindset among the youth are therefore crucial to sustaining
inclusive economic growth.

      The institutionalization of a week-long national celebration on
entrepreneurship seeks to address these gaps by encouraging the youth to
become self-starters, innovators, and job creators. Such a celebration will expose
students and aspiring entrepreneurs to the vast opportunities in enterprise
development, while providing a platform for successful Filipino entrepreneurs to
share their experiences, insights, and best practices through forums, workshops,
and other learning activities.

       It is imperative for the government to take proactive steps in fostering a
culture of entrepreneurship by promoting awareness, enhancing skills
development, and equipping the youth with fundamental business
competencies. In doing so, the State strengthens the foundation for a more
resilient, innovative, and inclusive Philippine economy.

                       AN ACT
      DECLARING EVERY THIRD WEEK OF NOVEMBER AS
          “NATIONAL ENTREPRENEURSHIP WEEK”


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as
“The National Entrepreneurship Week Act”.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared a policy
of the State that the sustained development of a reservoir of national
talents consisting of Filipino entrepreneurs and managers in order to
maintain the global standards of high-level technical manpower.

The State shall also develop a self-reliant and independent national
economy effectively controlled by Filipinos and ensure that all sectors of
the economy and all regions of the country shall be given optimum
opportunity to develop.

The State likewise recognizes the importance of educational institutions
as catalysts of entrepreneurial development with participation of the
youth, families, educators and the private sector, thereby emphasizing
personal interaction and facilitating the exchange of ideas throughout
the country.
SECTION 3. National Entrepreneurship Week. – Every third week
of November of each year is hereby declared as National
Entrepreneurship Week.

SECTION 4. Implementation. – The Department of Education and
the Commission on Higher Education, in coordination with the
Department of Trade and Industry, shall plan and implement activities
for the observance of “National Entrepreneurship Week”, such as
school trade fairs and seminar workshops on entrepreneurship. Other
government     agencies,   local  government    units,   and   other
instrumentalities of the government are encouraged to join the week-
long celebration by way of public exhibits, display of tarpaulins,
among other.

SECTION 5. Separability Clause. If any provision, or part hereof, is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the
provision shall remain valid and subsisting.

SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. Any law, presidential decree or
issuance, executive order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule
or regulation contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is
hereby repealed, modified or amended accordingly.

SECTION 7. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
