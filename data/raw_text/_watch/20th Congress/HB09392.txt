                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Barangay officials, barangay tanods, and members of the Lupong
Tagapamayapa serve as the frontline providers of government services and
play a crucial role in maintaining peace and order, implementing community
programs, and responding to the immediate needs of the people at the
grassroots level. Despite their vital contribution to local governance and
nation-building, many continue to receive inadequate compensation, limited
benefits, and insufficient social protection.

      This measure seeks to recognize their dedication, sacrifices, and years
of public service by enhancing their benefits and providing additional forms
of assistance and protection. The bill likewise grants retirement gratuity pay
and other non-monetary benefits aimed at promoting their welfare, morale,
and economic security.

      Recognizing the fiscal implications of the proposed measure, the bill
mandates coordination among the Department of the Interior and Local
Government (DILG), the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the
Department of Finance (DOF), and other concerned agencies to ensure fiscal
sustainability and effective implementation.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

        SEC. 2. Barangay Retirement Gratuity Fund (BRGF)
Created. - There is hereby created a Barangay Retirement Gratuity
Fund (BRGF) which shall be used to fund the retirement gratuity
of officials, including barangay tanods and members of the Lupong
Tagapamayapa, as mentioned in this Act. An amount equivalent
to one percent (1%) of the National Tax Allocation shares of local
government units under Section 284 of Republic Act No. 7160, as
amended, shall be appropriated for this purpose subject to existing
budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

       The release and disbursement of funds shall be subject to
existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and
regulations.

      The Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG)
shall administer and ensure the appropriate disbursement of the
Fund.

       SEC. 3. Coverage. – The benefits granted under this Act
shall cover all barangay officials, barangay tanods, and members
of the lupong tagapamayapa including those serving in the
Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM).

      SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – the
Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), in
coordination with the Department of Budget and Management
(DBM), Department of Finance and Department of Transportation
(DOTr) shall issue the necessary rules and regulations to
implement this Act.

      SEC. 5. Repealing Clause — All laws, executive orders,
issuances, rules and regulations inconsistent with this Act are
hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 6. Effectivity – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after completion of its publication in the Official Gazette or in
(2) newspapers of general circulation.
