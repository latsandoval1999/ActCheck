                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

    The public demands fast and efficient service not only from the private sector, but most
importantly from government. In fact, people have higher expectations from government with
regard to providing services. It is for this reason that Republic Act No. 11032 or the Ease of Doing
Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018 was enacted into law. Clearly,
streamlining government processes is a priority of any administration.

    With the developments in the field of information technology, government processes can
now easily be improved with appropriate legislation, strict implementation, and proper
technology on information sharing. However, Filipinos in need of government aid still encounter
delays in receiving assistance due to redundant or excessive documentary requirements, tedious
procedures, long processing times and even the lack of availability of funds.

     To further remove administrative roadblocks from our countrymen in need of aid, this bill
seeks to streamline the process / procedures and reduce the excessive requirements needed to
avail of all applicable government aid or assistance. Moreover, this bill seeks to utilize digital
systems to facilitate requests and processes for government aid or assistance.

    By adopting simplified and standardized procedures and requirements, the process of
securing government aid need not be stressful.

    Hence, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
     SIMPLIFYING PROCEDURES AND REDUCING DOCUMENTARY
REQUIREMENTS FOR ACCESSING GOVERNMENT AID OR ASSISTANCE AND
                     FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as "Simplified Access to Aid Act".

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the declared policy that the State shall prioritize the needs
of the underprivileged and ensure that social services are accessible and responsive. Moreover, the
state shall ensure that individuals and families in difficult situations are provided with timely and
accessible assistance.

To this end, the State shall adopt measures to streamline procedures and remove excessive
documentary requirements for availing aid and assistance from government agencies. This affirms
the mandate of the State to service with utmost responsibility and efficiency.

Section. 3. Scope and Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all national government agencies and
instrumentalities that has social amelioration programs, including but not limited to the
Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of Health (DOH),
Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Technical Education and Skills Development
Authority (TESDA), Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), and Philippine Amusement
and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR).

Section. 4. Streamlined Procedures and Standardized Requirements – All government agencies
with social amelioration programs shall further streamline the procedures and minimize
documentary requirements in order to facilitate the speedy processing and release of requested
assistance. Moreover, all implementing agencies shall coordinate with the Anti-Red Tape
Authority (ARTA) to enhance compliance with Republic Act No. 9485, as amended by Republic
Act No. 11032 or the "Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of
2018" and shall likewise coordinate and cooperate with ARTA to address complaints involving
undue delays, excessive documentary requirements, or other forms of non-compliance with the
provisions of this Act. Lastly, the Commission on Audit (COA) shall provide updated audit
guidelines based on the least or minimum documentation needed for compliance vis-à-vis release
or granting of aid or assistance.

All concerned government agencies shall process requests for assistance immediately: Provided,
That complete documentary requirements are submitted during official working hours. Requests
submitted on weekends, holidays, or outside official working hours shall be processed on the next
working day.

Section. 5. Digital Requests for Government Aid - The Department of Information and
Communications Technology (DICT) shall be primarily tasked to develop the appropriate digital
systems or platforms as may be prescribed in the implementing rules and regulations, as a
mechanism for Filipinos to avail aid from government agencies, either in cash or non-cash format.

Section. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA) in coordination with DSWD, DOH, DOLE, TESDA,
PCSO, PAGCOR, and the Department of the Interior and Local Government, shall promulgate the
implementing rules and regulations.

Section. 7. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions and parts hereof not otherwise affected shall remain in full
force and effect.

Section. 8. Repealing Clause. -All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations, rules and regulations, or
parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified
accordingly.

Section. 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its publication
in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
