EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Cagwait, Province of Surigao del Sur, was created into an independent
municipality on January 20, 1953 by virtue of Executive Order No. 559, Series of 1953,
issued by then President Elpidio Quirino, organizing a portion of the Municipality of Tago,
Province of Surigao, into a new independent municipality named Cagwait.

January 20 is annually observed by the Municipality of Cagwait as its Foundation Day, or
“Adlaw Nan Cagwait,” which commemorates its establishment and highlights the
municipality's historical significance while strengthening the people's sense of identity,
pride, and belongingness.

Declaring January 20 of every year as a special non-working holiday in Cagwait will
provide the residents, government personnel, schools, businesses, and other sectors of
the community an opportunity to participate fully in the activities commemorating the
municipality's founding anniversary.

This measure seeks to institutionalize the annual observance of the founding anniversary
of Cagwait and recognize its importance to the cultural and historical life of the
municipality.

in view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Introduced by HON. ROMEO S. MOMO, SR.

AN ACT DECLARING JANUARY 20 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAGWAIT, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL SUR, IN
COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Special Non-Working Holiday. January 20 of every year is hereby
declared a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Cagwait, Province of Surigao del
Sur, in commemoration of its founding anniversary.

SEC. 2. Observance of the Founding Anniversary. The Municipality of Cagwait shall
celebrate and commemorate January 20 of every year as its Foundation Day, also known as
“Adlaw Nan Cagwait,” through appropriate commemorative, cultural, civic, educational, and
community activities, consistent with existing laws, rules, and regulations.

SEC. 3. Support for the Celebration. The Local Government Unit of Cagwait may
appropriate funds in its annual budget, or utilize legally available and authorized local
appropriations, for the planning, organization, implementation, promotion, and other legitimate
expenses necessary for the celebration of its founding anniversary, subject to existing
budgeting, accounting, auditing, procurement, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

SEC. 4. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, regulations, and
other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 6. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation, whichever comes later.
