                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippine National Police (PNP) personnel dedicate their lives to protecting the public,
often at the risk of their own health and safety. Some are compelled to retire under Total
Permanent Physical Disability (TPPD) after rendering twenty (20) years or more of faithful
government service due to injuries or illnesses sustained in the performance of their duties.

Under the existing provisions of Section 73 of Republic Act No. 6975, as amended by Section
35 of Republic Act No. 8551, the survivorship pension of a TPPD retiree is limited to the
remainder of the five-year guaranteed period. Consequently, upon the death of the retiree
beyond said period, the surviving spouse loses the pension benefit, despite the deceased
member's long years of service and sacrifice.

This measure seeks to address this inequity by granting the surviving lawful spouse of a PNP
member retired under TPPD, who had rendered at least twenty (20) years of active government
service prior to retirement, a lifetime survivorship pension equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of
the deceased retiree's monthly pension, regardless of the number of years that elapsed between
retirement and death. Recognizing the sacrifices of our police personnel and providing financial
security and social protection to their surviving spouses.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT AMENDING SECTION 73 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6975, AS AMENDED
BY SECTION 35 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8551, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE REFORM AND REORGANIZATION ACT OF
1998, TO GRANT LIFETIME SURVIVORSHIP PENSION BENEFITS TO THE
SURVIVING LEGAL SPOUSE OF A PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE MEMBER
RETIRED UNDER TOTAL PERMANENT PHYSICAL DISABILITY WHO HAD
RENDERED AT LEAST TWENTY YEARS OF ACTIVE GOVERNMENT SERVICE,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Section 73 of Republic Act No. 6975, as amended by Section 35 of Republic
Act No. 8551, is hereby further amended by revising its second paragraph to read as follows:


       SEC. 73. Permanent Physical Disability. – An officer or non-officer who is permanently
       and totally disabled as a result of injuries suffered or sickness contracted in the
       performance of his duty as duly certified by the National Police Commission, upon
       finding and certification by the appropriate medical officer, that the extent of the
       disability or sickness renders such member unfit or unable to further perform the duties
       of his position, shall be entitled to one year's salary and to lifetime pension equivalent
       to eighty percent (80%) of his last salary, in addition to other benefits as provided under
       existing laws.

       Should such member who has been retired under permanent total disability under this
       section die within five (5) years from his retirement, his surviving legal spouse or if
       there be none, the surviving dependent legitimate children shall be entitled to the
       pension for the remainder of the five (5) years guaranteed period.

       Provided, further, that if the deceased PNP member retired under Total Permanent
       Physical Disability (TPPD) and had accumulated at least twenty (20) years of active
       government service prior to such disability retirement, the surviving legal spouse shall,
       upon the death of the said TPPD retiree, continue to receive a lifetime survivorship
       pension equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of the deceased retiree’s monthly pension,
       irrespective of the officer’s retirement to the date of death.”
        SECTION 2. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 3. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
