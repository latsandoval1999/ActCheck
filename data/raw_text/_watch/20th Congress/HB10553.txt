12        PROVIDING FOR THE MAGNA CARTA FOR PUBLIC DISASTER RISK REDUCTION
13          AND MANAGEMENT WORKERS AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
14
15   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
16   assembled:

17   Section 1. Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Magna Carta for Public Disaster

18   Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM) Workers".


19   Section 2. Statement of Policy. – It shall be the policy of the State to:


20   a)      Uphold the people's constitutional rights to life and property by addressing the

21           root causes of vulnerabilities to disasters, strengthening the country's

22           institutional capacity for disaster risk reduction and management, and building

23           the resilience of local communities to disasters including climate change

24           impacts;


25   b)      Recognize and strengthen the capacities of Local Government Units (LGUs)

26           and communities in mitigating, preparing for, responding to, and recovering

27           from the impact of hazards and disasters; and
