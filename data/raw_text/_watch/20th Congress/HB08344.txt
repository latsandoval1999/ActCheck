                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Agriculture and inland fisheries remain vital sources of food and
livelihood for upland communities in Iligan City. In Barangay Rogongon,
where many families rely on small-scale farming and aquaculture, access to
quality fingerlings and reliable production inputs remains limited due to
distance and logistical constraints.

       Following a pre-feasibility site assessment conducted on 28 November
2025, the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) Region X
identified Sitio Hanginan in Barangay Rogongon as a technically viable site
for the establishment of a multi-species freshwater fish hatchery. The area
has firm and generally plain soil conditions, is surrounded by upland
vegetation, and is situated away from industrial and other pollution sources.
It also possesses a sufficient and good-quality freshwater supply from a
spring located within the proposed site area, which is advantageous for
hatchery operations.

      While the site presents accessibility and infrastructure challenges due
to its distance from major roads and service centers, these conditions
likewise underscore the need for government investment in localized
production facilities. At present, fish growers in the area depend on distant
sources of spawners and fingerlings, increasing costs and limiting
productivity. Establishing a hatchery within the community will improve
access to quality inputs and create sustainable livelihood opportunities for
small-scale producers.
      The local community has expressed support for the proposed project,
recognizing its anticipated socio-economic benefits. With appropriate
infrastructure development and institutional coordination, the proposed
hatchery can contribute meaningfully to agricultural productivity and rural
development in Iligan’s hinterland barangays.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
  ESTABLISHING A MULTI-SPECIES FRESHWATER FISH HATCHERY
     IN SITIO HANGINAN, BARANGAY ROGONGON, ILIGAN CITY,
 TO STRENGTHEN INLAND AQUACULTURE AND RURAL LIVELIHOOD,
              AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:


     SECTION 1. Establishment of a Multi-Species Freshwater Fish Hatchery.
— There shall be established, under the supervision and control of the
Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), a multi-species
freshwater fish hatchery in Sitio Hanginan, Barangay Rogongon, Iligan City,
to support inland aquaculture development and enhance local fish
production.

     SECTION 2. Establishment and Operationalization. — The BFAR shall
be responsible for the planning, construction, establishment, and initial
operation of the hatchery.

       BFAR shall ensure that the facility is equipped with the necessary
infrastructure, equipment, and technical support for the production and
distribution of quality freshwater fish fingerlings to local growers and
beneficiaries.

       In the implementation of this Act, BFAR may coordinate with the local
government of Iligan City and other concerned agencies for technical and
logistical support.
       SECTION 3. Feasibility Study and Management Transfer. — Consistent
with the site assessment conducted by the BFAR, the agency shall undertake
a full feasibility study and prepare the necessary technical and financial
requirements for the establishment of the multi-species freshwater fish
hatchery, in accordance with applicable rules of BFAR, the Department of
Budget and Management, and other relevant agencies.

      Within two (2) years from the completion of construction, BFAR may,
through a Memorandum of Agreement, transfer the management and
operation of the hatchery to the local government of Iligan City. Prior to such
transfer, BFAR shall implement a capacity-building and phased transition
program to ensure that local personnel are adequately trained in hatchery
management and operations.

      SECTION 4. Research and Technical Support. — BFAR shall undertake
continuing technical support, performance monitoring, and, where
necessary, adaptive research to enhance breeding practices, improve
survival rates, and ensure the sustainable production of freshwater fish
fingerlings suited to local conditions.

      SECTION 5. Funding Support. — The Secretary of Agriculture shall
include in the Department’s programs the funding requirements for the
establishment and initial operation of the multi-species freshwater fish
hatchery in Sitio Hanginan, Barangay Rogongon, Iligan City, subject to the
usual budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and regulations.

       The local government of Iligan City may allocate funds from any
available local revenue to support the operationalization and sustainability
of the hatchery.

      SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Agriculture shall, in
coordination with the BFAR and the local government of Iligan City, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

       SECTION 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
