                                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish the National Nutrition Commission, thereby
repealing Presidential Decree No. 491, otherwise known as the “Nutrition Act of the
Philippines,” which created the National Nutrition Council. It strengthens the
Commission’s powers and functions as the country’s primary policymaking,
coordinating, and monitoring body on nutrition and food security.

       The 2025 Updating Survey released by the Department of Science and
Technology Food and Nutrition Research Institute (DOST-FNRI) reported that more
than twenty five percent (25.3%) of Filipino children under five (5) years of age are
stunted. This represents a 1.7% increase from 2023.1 At the same time, the country
faces a growing burden of overweight and obesity among both children and adults,
highlighting the need to address the double burden of malnutrition through
stronger nutrition governance and coordinated interventions.2

      This measure mandates the formulation of a ten-year Philippine Plan of
Action for Nutrition aligned with the Philippine Development Plan and reinforces a
whole-of-government and -society approach to addressing malnutrition. It provides
the Commission an enhanced authority to formulate national nutrition policies,
coordinate government interventions, strengthen nutrition surveillance, mobilize
resources, and monitor the implementation of nutrition programs.

       Good nutrition is fundamental to improving public health, educational
attainment, workforce productivity, and national development. Strengthening the
country’s nutrition governance is an investment in the health and future of every
Filipino and a crucial step toward building a healthier, more productive, and
food-secure nation.

                         AN ACT
ESTABLISHING THE NATIONAL NUTRITION COMMISSION, REPEALING
FOR THE PURPOSE PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 491, ENTITLED
“CREATING A NATIONAL NUTRITION COUNCIL AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Nutrition Act of the
Philippines”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote and promote the right of every Filipino to health through adequate,
accessible, affordable, safe, and nutritious food, and to adopt a comprehensive and
integrated approach to nutrition and food security as essential components of
national development.

       Towards this end, the State shall ensure the formulation of evidence-based
policies, the efficient coordination of national and local nutrition programs, and the
prevention and elimination of all forms of malnutrition. Further, the State shall be
guided by the principles embodied in the Constitution, and the Philippines’
international commitments, including the International Covenant on Economic,
Social and Cultural Rights, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, and the
Convention on the Eradication of All Forms of Discrimination against Women.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

          a. ﻿Food Security refers to an incidence where people, at all times, have
       physical and economic access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food that
       meets their dietary needs and food preferences for an active and healthy life;

          b. Hunger refers to a status of an individual whose food intake regularly
       provides less than their minimum energy requirements;



                                            1
          c.​ Malnutrition refers to the lack of proper nutrition, caused by not
       having enough to eat, not eating enough of the right things, or being unable
       to use the food that one does at;

          d.​ Non-Government Organizations refer to non-stock, non-profit
       organizations duly registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission,
       focusing on the upliftment of the basic or disadvantaged sectors of society by
       providing advocacy, training, community organizing, research, access to
       resources, and other similar activities, as defined in Republic Act (RA) No.
       8425, otherwise known as the “Social Reform and Poverty Alleviation Act”;

          e.​ Nutrition refers to the process of providing or obtaining the food
       necessary for health and growth; and​

          f.​ ﻿Urban Poor refers to individuals or families in urban areas with
       incomes falling below the poverty line as defined by the National Statistical
       Coordination Board.​

       SEC. 4. Renaming of the National Nutrition Council. – The nomenclature of the
National Nutrition Council (NNC) which was established pursuant to Presidential
Decree (PD) No. 491, as amended, is hereby changed to the “National Nutrition
Commission”, hereinafter referred to as the “Commission”, which shall be attached to
the Office of the President for policy and program coordination.​

        SEC. 5. Composition of the Commission. – The Commission shall be headed by a
Chairperson with the rank of Cabinet Secretary and two (2) Commissioners with the
rank of Undersecretary. The Chairperson and the Commissioners shall be citizens
and residents of the Philippines, of good moral character, of recognized probity and
independence, and must have distinguished themselves professionally in public,
civic, or academic service in the fields of health and nutrition. They must have been
in the active practice of their professions for at least ten (10) years.

        SEC. 6. Terms of Office. – The term of office of the Chairperson and the
Commissioners shall be six (6) years without reappointment. Of the first set of
appointees, the Chairperson and first commissioner appointed shall hold office for
six (6) years and the second commissioner appointed shall serve for a term of four
(4) years. In case a vacancy occurs before the expiration of the term of office, the
appointment to such vacancy shall only be for the unexpired term of the
predecessor. The Chairperson and the Commissioners shall enjoy the security of
tenure and shall not be suspended or removed from office, except for just cause as
provided by law. At the expiration of the term of the Chairperson or in case of
vacancy, the most senior of the Commissioners shall temporarily assume and
perform the duties and functions of the Chairperson until a permanent Chairperson
is appointed by the President.

       SEC. 7. Powers, Functions, and Responsibilities of the Commission. – The powers,
functions, and responsibilities of the Commission are as follows:

         a.​ ﻿To formulate an integrated and multi-sectoral national policy,

      nutrition, eliminate hunger, and food security;​

          b.​ ﻿To supervise, coordinate, monitor, and evaluate the implementation of

         e.​ ﻿To create and maintain a system for nutrition surveillance with a

         f.​ ﻿To coordinate the release of public funds for nutrition and food in

         g.​ ﻿To coordinate all requests for loans and grants by the government

         h.​ ﻿To provide monetary and non-monetary incentives to nutrition
      champions by establishing a rewards and recognition system; and

         i.​ ﻿To call on any department, bureau, agency, and other instrumentalities
      of the government for assistance in the form of personnel, resources, and
      facilities as the need arises.

       SEC. 9. Philippine Plan of Action for Nutrition. – A ten-year multi-sectoral and
integrated national nutrition plan of action shall be developed and formulated by
the Commission in coordination with NGOs and relevant government agencies,
such as the DOH, DSWD, and DepEd, among others. The PPAN should be aligned
with the Philippine Development Plan (PDP).

        In developing the PPAN, the policies, principles, and programs embodied in
the PDP (Ambisyon 2040); RA No. 8976, otherwise known as the “Philippine Food
Fortification Act of 2000”; RA No. 8172, otherwise known as the “An Act for Salt
lodization Nationwide (ASIN)”; RA No. 10862, otherwise known as the “Nutrition and
Dietetics Law of 2016”; RA No. 11037, otherwise known as the “Masustansyang
Pagkain Para sa Batang Pilipino Act”, RA No. 11148, otherwise known as the
“Kalusugan at Nutrisyon ng Mag-Nanay Act”, and all relevant laws on the nutrition
and food security to be enacted hereafter must be considered.

       The said plan must have measurable targets for ending malnutrition and
undernutrition after ten (10) years of its implementation. The PPAN must be
concurred in by its provisional members. The PPAN must be submitted to the Office
of the President for direction and coordination.

       SEC. 10. Nutrition Month. – The month of July shall be designated as
“Nutrition Month” for the purpose of creating greater awareness among our people
on the importance of nutrition. The Commission shall formulate and approve
programs and activities for Nutrition Month which should be coordinated across all
sectors, whether public or private.

       SEC. 11. Donations. – The Commission shall be authorized to receive
donations, grants, or gifts in whatever form and from whatever sources: Provided,
That said grants, gifts, or donations shall be administered, obligated, and disbursed
in accordance with the terms of the grant or donation and in such manner as the
Commission may in its discretion determine.

        SEC. 12. Retention of Personnel; Security of Tenure; Upgrading of Positions and
Salaries. – All existing permanent personnel of the Commission shall not be
adversely affected by this Act. They shall continue in office and shall not be removed
or separated from the service, except for cause as provided by existing laws. Further,
the present positions and salaries of personnel shall be upgraded to the level of their
counterparts in a department set-up. The Commission shall hire such other
personnel as may be necessary for the performance of its functions.

      SEC. 13. Transitory Provisions. – In consonance with the above provision, the
incumbent Executive Director and Deputy Executive Directors shall serve as the first
Chairperson and Commissioners upon the effectivity of this Act.

       SEC. 14. Appropriations. – The State shall hereby provide the initial amount

       SEC. 15. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is

force and effect.

       SEC. 16. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 17. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
