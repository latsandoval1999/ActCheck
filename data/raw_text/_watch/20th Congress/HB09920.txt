                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to institutionalize local clean-up teams in every city and
municipality in the country to immediately respond to incidents of illegal dumping
and littering. It strengthens Republic Act No. 9003, otherwise known as the
“Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000”, and also increases the fines and
penalties imposed under the Act.

       Despite the enactment of the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act,
improper waste disposal and littering continue to degrade waterways, public
spaces, and communities. Rapid shifts in lifestyle and heightened consumerism have
led to an increase in plastic wastes and vastly outpaces garbage collection capacities.
This has led to clogged drainage systems, polluted rivers and esteros, and the
accumulation of solid waste which contribute to flooding and public health risks.

      This measure establishes local clean-up teams that shall undertake waste
removal, site rehabilitation, and preventive interventions in coordination with local
solid waste management authorities. It likewise allows offenders sentenced to
community service to participate in clean-up activities under the supervision of local
government units, reinforcing accountability, and encouraging reform and civic
responsibility.

        Effective solid waste management depends not only on regulation and
enforcement but also on active community participation. This measure promotes the
spirit of volunteerism and reinforces the Filipino value of kapwa through collective
action in maintaining clean surroundings, protecting public health, and promoting
environmental stewardship at the local level.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
ESTABLISHING LOCAL CLEAN-UP TEAMS AND INCREASING THE FINES
AND PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9003, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “ECOLOGICAL
SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT ACT OF 2000”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. A new Section 11- A is hereby inserted after Section 11 of Republic
Act No. 9003, otherwise known as the “Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of
2000”, to read as follows:

          “SEC. 11-A. CREATION OF LOCAL CLEAN-UP TEAMS. —
      EACH CITY AND MUNICIPALITY SHALL ESTABLISH A
      CLEAN-UP TEAM, COMPOSED OF PERSONNEL FROM THE
      LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT, BARANGAY OFFICIALS, AND
      ACCREDITED COMMUNITY VOLUNTEERS, TASKED TO
      IMMEDIATELY RESPOND TO INCIDENTS OF ILLEGAL
      DUMPING, LITTERING, OR OTHER VIOLATIONS OF THIS ACT.
      THE CLEAN-UP TEAM SHALL CONDUCT WASTE REMOVAL,
      SITE RESTORATION, AND IMPLEMENT PREVENTIVE
      MEASURES, IN COORDINATION WITH THE SOLID WASTE
      MANAGEMENT BOARD.

            “PERSONS PENALIZED UNDER THIS ACT WITH
      COMMUNITY SERVICE, PARTICULARLY UNDER SECTION 49
      (A), MAY BE ASSIGNED AS PART OF THE LOCAL CLEAN-UP
      TEAMS FOR THE DURATION OF THEIR SERVICE, UNDER THE
      SUPERVISION OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT.”
