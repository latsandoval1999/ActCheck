                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      There has been an alarming surge in road rage incidents across the
country in recent years. Reports of motorists engaging in hostile behavior,
including shouting, cursing, using foul or degrading language, issuing threats
or engaging in intimidation, have become all too common. In many instances,
road rage has escalated into physical assaults or attempts to inflict harm.
Some cases have even resulted in serious injuries or fatalities due to the use of
firearms and other weapons.

       The increasing frequency and severity of road rage incidents demand
decisive government intervention. At present, there is a glaring lack of specific
legislation that categorically defines and penalizes road rage as a distinct and
punishable offense. This legislative gap leaves road users vulnerable to the
hostile and often violent actions of aggressive drivers.

        This proposed bill seeks to define and penalize acts of road rage to
safeguard all road users from harassment, intimidation, physical harm, or loss
of life. Beyond punitive measures, this bill also emphasizes the importance of
prevention and education. It mandates the implementation of programs that
promote responsible road behavior and foster a culture of respect and
accountability among motorists.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT PENALIZING ACTS OF ROAD RAGE AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “ANTI-ROAD RAGE
ACT.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to maintain peace, order, and discipline on public roads and highways.
The State condemns all forms of aggressive and violent behavior arising from
road-related conflicts, collectively known as road rage, which threaten public
safety and disrupt public order.

towards this end, the State shall adopt a zero-tolerance approach toward road
rage and shall ensure the protection of all road users from harassment,
intimidation, and physical harm. The State shall likewise implement preventive,
educational, and punitive measures to promote responsible road behavior and
a culture of respect and accountability among motorists.

SECTION 3. Definition of Road Rage. – “road rage,” as used in this Act,
refers to any aggressive, hostile, or violent behavior by a motorist in traffic or
on the road, including, but not limited to, excessive or threatening gestures
toward another person; physical assault or an attempted assault; reckless
driving; threats or intimidation; the use of force against another person; and
other analogous acts.

Provided, that mere verbal disagreements, gestures, or minor traffic violations,
in the absence of overtly dangerous acts, shall not be considered road rage
under this Act.

SECTION 4. Penalties. – Any person is found guilty of road rage as defined
under Section 3 shall be penalized as follows:

         a) When no injury or death occurred, imprisonment of not less than
            six (6) months but not more than one (1) year and/or a fine of not
            less than One hundred thousand pesos (P100,000.00);
         b) In cases resulting to physical injury, regardless of the degree,
            imprisonment of two (2) years to four (4) years and/or a fine of not
            less than Two hundred fifty thousand pesos (P250,000.00); and

         c) In cases resulting to death of a party, imprisonment of six (6) years
            to twelve (12) years and/or a fine of not less than Five hundred
            thousand pesos (P500,000.00);

SECTION 5. Revocation of Driver’s License. – A person found guilty of this
Act shall have their driver’s license revoked and prohibited from renewing it for
a period of five (5) years after the incident.

SECTION 6. Independent Action. – Nothing in this Act shall prevent a victim
of road rage from bringing a separate action for damages or other appropriate
affirmative relief.

SECTION 7. Aggravating Circumstance. – If a more serious crime is
committed during a road rage, the latter shall be considered an aggravating
circumstance as provided under Article 14, Section 1 of the Revised Penal Code.

SECTION 8. Prohibited Defense. – Being under the influence of alcohol, illegal
drugs, or any other mind-altering substance shall not be accepted as a defense
under this Act. Instead, it shall be considered an aggravating circumstance.

Section 9. Compulsory Anger Management Program.- In addition to the
penalties stated above, individuals found guilty under this Act shall be required
to complete a government-approved anger management program to address
aggressive behavior and help prevent road rage incidents.

SECTION 10. Public Education and Awareness. – The Department of
Transportation, together with the LTO and other agencies, shall launch a
nationwide campaign promoting safe, respectful, and responsible driving.
Driver education and licensing requirements shall also include road safety and
ways to prevent and manage road conflicts.

SECTION 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Justice (DOJ), the
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), and the Department
of Transportation (DOTr), through the Land Transportation Office (LTO), shall
promulgate the rules and regulations necessary to implement this Act.

SECTION 12. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not
affected thereby shall remain in force and effect.

SECTION 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, rules and regulations and other issuances, or any part thereof,
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, modified, or amended
accordingly.

SECTION 14. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of
general circulation.
