and traceability of land records. The measure further ensures that digitization shall

existing land-registration laws. Paper-based registration and title services shall

       Section 4. Nationwide Digital Land Registration System - The Land
Registration Authority (LRA) shall establish, operate, and maintain a secure
nationwide online system for the registration, annotation, and issuance of digital land
titles. Paper-based services shall still remain available to persons who prefer or
require them.
      Section 5. Mandatory Digitization of Land Records. The Land Registration
Authority (LRA), including all Registries of Deeds, the Department of Environment
and Natural Resources (DENR), Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR), National
Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP), Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR),
local government units (LGUs), and other concerned government agencies shall
digitize all existing land-related records under their custody and ensure the electronic
recording of future records.


      Section 6. System Synchronization.        The Department of Environment and
Natural Resources (DENR), Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR), National
Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP), Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR),
local government units (LGUs), and other concerned government agencies shall
securely synchronize their system and records with the Land Registration Authority
(LRA).


      Section 7. Use of Blockchain Technology. The Land Registration Authority
(LRA) is hereby authorized to use blockchain or other secure and tamper-evident
technology to protect the integrity, authenticity, and traceability of records
maintained under the Nationwide Digital Land Registration System.


      Section 8. Acceptance of Electronic Documents.           The Land Registration
Authority (LRA), including all Registries of Deeds, the Department of Environment
and Natural Resources (DENR), Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR), National
Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP), Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR),
local government units (LGUs), and other concerned government agencies shall
accept digital documents and those electronically notarized under the Rules on
Electronic Notarization for registration, annotation, issuance of digital land titles,
and for other official purposes


      Section 9. Legal Validity and Evidentiary Value. Digital land titles and land
records registered, annotated, or issued pursuant to this Act shall have the same legal
validity, force, effect, and evidentiary value as their paper counterparts. They shall
not be denied admissibility in any judicial, quasi-judicial, or administrative
proceeding solely because they are in digital form, subject to applicable laws and
rules on authentication and evidence.


      Section 10. Appropriations. The amounts necessary for the implementation
of this Act shall be included in the respective budgets of the concerned national
government agencies and local government units.


      Section 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within (180) days from
the approval of this Act, the Land Registration Authority shall promulgate the
Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) to carry out the provisions of this Act,
which is to shift to fully digitalization of the registration, annotation, issuance of
land titles and to streamline its process.


      Section 12. Separability Clause.       If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full
force and effect.
      Section 13. Repealing Clause.        All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, presidential proclamations, rules and regulations or parts thereof contrary to
or inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


      Section 14. Effectivity.   This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
