                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
       The Land Transportation Office (LTO) District Office in the Municipality
of Bayombong, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, is presently classified as a Class “C”
district office. Situated in the provincial capital, it delivers the core services of
the Office within its jurisdiction, including motor vehicle registration, the
issuance of driver’s licenses and permits, and the enforcement of land
transportation laws, rules and regulations, to the municipalities of Bayombong,
Bagabag, Diadi, Villaverde, Solano, Quezon and Ambaguio.
       The sustained growth in motor vehicle registrations and licensing
transactions within this area of responsibility has correspondingly increased
the responsibilities and workload of the office. The present Class “C”
classification no longer reflects the expanding functions and service demands
of the office. Its existing organizational structure and resource allocation limit
its capacity to efficiently serve the increasing number of clients while
maintaining the quality of public service.
       Upgrading the Bayombong District Office to Class “A” will provide the
appropriate organizational structure, additional personnel, and operational
resources needed to effectively carry out its mandated functions. It will likewise
enhance service efficiency, strengthen enforcement activities, and improve the
delivery of LTO services to the communities within its jurisdiction.
     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

    AN ACT UPGRADING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO)
DISTRICT OFFICE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BAYOMBONG, PROVINCE OF
NUEVA VIZCAYA, FROM CLASS “C” TO CLASS “A,” AND APPROPRIATING
                        FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. The district office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in
the Municipality of Bayombong, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, is hereby upgraded
from Class “C” to Class “A” district office.
       SEC. 2. The Secretary of the Department of Transportation shall
immediately include in the Department’s program the upgrading of the LTO
district office in the Municipality of Bayombong, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, the
initial funding of which shall be charged against the current year’s
appropriation of the existing LTO district office in the Municipality of
Bayombong, Province of Nueva Vizcaya. Thereafter, the funding of which shall
be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
      SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
      Approved.
