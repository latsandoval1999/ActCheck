                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

     This bill seeks to establish a public elementary school in Barangay Matalapitap,
Municipality of Paniqui, Province of Tarlac.

       As provided in Resolution No. 093-s2026 of the Sangguniang Bayan of the
Municipality of Paniqui, statistics show that the local government of Paniqui, Tarlac is
experiencing a steady increase in population - leading to a rise in the number of
school-aged children and students within Barangay Matalapitap, Municipality of
Paniqui, Province of Tarlac. Moreover, the nearest existing public elementary school in
the same municipality is located approximately one (1) kilometer away from Barangay
Matalapitap which exposes the students of tender age to safety hazards as they travel to
the existing public elementary school.

       Section 1, Article XIV of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that “The
State shall protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels,
and shall take appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all.” Furthermore,
paragraph 2, Section 2 of the same article mandates the State to “Establish, maintain,
and support a complete, adequate, and integrated system of education relevant to the
needs of the people and society.”

       Establishing and constructing a dedicated public school within Barangay
Matalapitap will significantly alleviate classroom congestion in neighboring schools,
reduce the financial burden on local families, and ensure a safer and more conducive
learning environment for the youth of the Province of Tarlac.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A PUBLIC ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY
MATALAPITAP, MUNICIPALITY OF PANIQUI, PROVINCE OF TARLAC TO BE
KNOWN AS MATALAPITAP PUBLIC ELEMENTARY SCHOOL AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled

      SECTION 1. There is hereby established, under the supervision of the
Department of Education, a public elementary school in Barangay Matalapitap,
Municipality of Paniqui, Province of Tarlac to be known as Matalapitap Public
Elementary School.

      SECTION 2. The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of the Matalapitap Public Elementary
School, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

       SECTION 3. Within ninety (90) days from the approval of this Act, the Secretary
of Education shall formulate the rules and regulations implementing the provisions of
this Act. Such rules and regulations shall provide for the school’s organization,
administration, operations and management, in accordance with Republic Act No. 9155
or the “Governance of Basic Education Act of 2001”, Republic Act No. 10533 or the
“Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013”, and other basic education-related laws.

       SECTION 4. This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.

Approved.
