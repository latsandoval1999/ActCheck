                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to mandate the inclusion of Basic Farming and Agricultural
Awareness in the curricula of all public and private elementary schools throughout
the Philippines in order to instill in the youth a deep appreciation of agriculture and
its vital role in national development. It aims to equip learners at an early age with
fundamental knowledge and practical skills in farming, thereby strengthening food
security, promoting sustainable practices, and nurturing a generation that values
and supports the agricultural sector.

       The Philippine agriculture sector continues to face persistent challenges such
as declining interest among the youth, an aging farmer population, food supply
vulnerabilities, and increasing dependence on imported agricultural products.
Despite being an agricultural country, many young Filipinos grow up with limited
understanding of where food comes from and how it is produced. This disconnect
contributes to the declining number of individuals entering the agricultural
workforce, threatening the long-term sustainability of the sector.

       This measure responds to these concerns by integrating basic farming
concepts and agricultural awareness into elementary education. It empowers
learners with essential knowledge on crop production, environmental stewardship,
and the socio-economic importance of agriculture. Embedding these principles in
early education will promote a culture of self-sufficiency, responsibility, and
innovation among the youth.

       The bill further directs the Department of Education (DepEd), in coordination
with the Department of Agriculture (DA), the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (TESDA), and the Agricultural Training Institute (ATI), to
develop age-appropriate, science-based, and practical learning modules. It also
ensures that teachers receive adequate training and resources to effectively deliver
the subject matter.

      Moreover, this proposed measure promotes holistic education while
addressing long-term national priorities. It strengthens rural development, supports
food security initiatives, and inspires future generations to actively participate in
building a resilient and progressive agricultural sector.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
REQUIRING THE MANDATORY INCLUSION OF BASIC FARMING AND
AGRICULTURAL AWARENESS IN THE CURRICULA OF ALL PUBLIC AND
PRIVATE ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS IN THE PHILIPPINES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Basic Farming and
Agricultural Awareness for the Youth Act”.

        SEC 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the vital role of the youth in
nation-building and shall promote and protect their physical, moral, spiritual,
intellectual, and social well-being. It shall inculcate in the youth patriotism and
nationalism, and encourage their involvement in public and civic affairs.

       The State shall give priority to education, science and technology, arts,
culture, and sports to foster patriotism and nationalism, accelerate social progress,
and promote total human liberation and development. The State further affirms its
commitment to comprehensive rural development and agrarian reform as
foundations of national growth and equity.

      Towards this end, the State shall ensure that basic farming and agricultural
awareness are integrated into the curricula of all public and private elementary
schools. For this purpose, it shall establish, maintain, and support a complete,
adequate, and sustainable system of education that equips the youth with
fundamental knowledge and appreciation of agriculture, and fosters their role in
advancing food security and rural development.

       SEC 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

           a. Basic Farming refers to the fundamentals of the practice of cultivating
       land for crops, plants, fruits, fruit trees, vegetation, fungi, and other life forms
       for food, fiber, and other products used to sustain life, as well as cultivation of
       animals and raising stock; and


                                            1
         b.​ Agricultural Awareness refers to basic information or knowledge of
      the fundamental principles of agriculture, its scientific, social, economic, and
      industrial implications in the community and the country as a whole.

       SEC. 4. Mandatory inclusion of Basic Farming and Agricultural Awareness in
School Curricula. – Basic Farming and Agricultural Awareness shall now be
mandatorily included as required subjects in the academic curricula of all public and
private elementary schools throughout the Philippines.

       The Department of Education (DepEd) shall ensure that a scientifically
supported program be integrated to existing school curricula in order to cover the
areas of Basic Farming and Agricultural Awareness. The DepEd shall institutionalize
high standards of learning and teaching methods to ensure credibility and academic
integrity of said subjects.

       Within one (1) year from the issuance of the Implementing Rules and
Regulations (IRR) of this Act, Basic Farming and Agricultural Awareness shall be
included in the curricula of all public and private elementary schools throughout the
Philippines.

       SEC. 5. Implementing Agency. – The Department of Education (DepEd)
through the Bureau of Curriculum Development, shall develop and maintain new
programs to be integrated with existing similar programs or subjects, creating and
including Basic Farming and Agriculture Awareness in the school curricula of all
public and private elementary schools.

      SEC. 6. Skills Training and Development. – The Department of Education
(DepEd), Department of Agriculture (DA), Technical Education Skills Development
Authority (TESDA), and the Agricultural Training Institute (ATI), and other related
agencies shall continuously provide scientific and technical training and provisions
for materials and equipment necessary for faculty members for teaching and
dissemination of Basic Farming and Agricultural Awareness to the students.

       SEC 7. Appropriations. – The Secretary of Education shall immediately include
in the Department’s program the development and maintenance of Basic Farming
and Agricultural Awareness, the funding of which shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days after the
effectivity of this Act, DepEd, in consultation with and support of the DA, TESDA,
ATI, and other related agencies, shall formulate the rules and regulations necessary
for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall
remain in full force and effect.
