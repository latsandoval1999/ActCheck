                            AN ACT
REQUIRING THE CONDUCT OF PERIODIC INSPECTION AND MAINTENANCE
    OF NATIONAL GOVERNMENT INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS, AND
ALLOWING THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS ACCESS
              TO THE SITES OR LOCATIONS THEREOF

                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

A well-planned and well-built infrastructure system is vital for national development
and economic transformation.1 Every year, the government allocates funding for the
construction of roads, bridges, public buildings, and other vital structures. These
projects are intended to provide high-quality, safe, reliable, and resilient
infrastructure facilities and services that are directly responsive to the everyday
needs of the Filipino people.

However, construction is only the first step. The true value of any infrastructure
project is found in its longevity and usability. It is necessary to ensure that these
public investments do not prematurely deteriorate, and instead remain as long-term
assets. One strategy to optimize the use of public funds in relation to these national
infrastructure projects is for the government to analyze the life cycle and utilization of
said assets.2 Central to this strategy is the implementation of systematic inspections
to accurately identify which projects require maintenance, preventive repairs, or
more extensive rehabilitation.

On the ground, however, a critical issue hinders these essential upkeep efforts. The
Department of Public Works and Highways face difficulties when trying to access the

While Republic Act No. 12289 or the Accelerated and Reformed Right-of-Way
(ARROW) Act was recently enacted to hasten the acquisition of properties for
national government infrastructure projects, this does not answer the issue of access
to existing infrastructure projects and their rehabilitation or maintenance.

This measure bridges this gap by requiring the Department of Public Works and
Highways to conduct regular and periodic inspections of all national government
infrastructure projects. The inspections shall be conducted every five to ten years
after completion, as determined by the DPWH, based on the project type. Based on
the inspection findings, the DPWH shall undertake the necessary maintenance,
repair, or rehabilitation measures for these projects. In line with this, the owner or
lawful possessor of the site or location of the national government infrastructure
projects is mandated to allow access to the national government for the conduct of
the regular and periodic inspections and the maintenance, repair, and rehabilitation
of said government projects.

By institutionalizing this framework, we elevate the standard for national
infrastructure projects and ensure that we do not treat these as disposable
commodities, but as long-term investments for our nation’s development. Ultimately,
we want to safeguard billions in public investments so that taxpayers get their full
money’s worth out of these projects for years to come.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “National Infrastructure
Inspection and Maintenance Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
ensure the proper implementation, continuous maintenance, and upkeep of national
government infrastructure projects to guarantee their long-term benefit to the Filipino
people.

SEC. 3. Regular and Periodic Inspection and Maintenance. – The Department of
Public Works and Highways (DPWH) shall conduct regular and periodic inspections
of all national government infrastructure projects. The inspections shall be conducted
every five to ten years after completion, as determined by the DPWH, based on the
project type. The results of the inspection shall be detailed in a written report made
available to the public.

Based on the inspection findings, the DPWH shall undertake the necessary
maintenance, repair, or rehabilitation measures for these projects.

SEC. 4. Grant of Access to the National Government. – The owner or lawful
possessor of the site or location of the national government infrastructure projects


                                          3
shall allow access to the National Government, through the DPWH and other
relevant government agencies, for the conduct of the regular and periodic
inspections and the maintenance, repair, and rehabilitation of said government
projects.

SEC. 5. Penalties. Unjustified refusal to allow access and impede the conduct of
regular and periodic inspections and maintenance of all national government
infrastructure projects shall be punishable by a fine of not more than Fifty Thousand
Pesos (P50,000.00).

SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DPWH shall promulgate the
implementing rules and regulations within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this
Act.

SEC. 7. Separability Clause. - If any part or provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected
thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, rules and regulations or other
issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provision of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SEC. 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
