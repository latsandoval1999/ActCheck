                                                     AN ACT
                  PROVIDING ONLINE VOLUNTEERISM OPPORTUNITIES
                          FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES

                                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Volunteerism is one of the ways citizens can participate actively in nation
building. It encourages the involvement of the public in important social and civil
affairs. Opportunities for volunteerism activities, however, mostly require service
delivery in physical or face-to-face set-ups.

      While the State provides for the inclusion and integration of Persons with
Disabilities into the mainstream of society, physical disability may limit a
person’s capacity to participate in volunteer work.

      In the Philippines, about 2.5 million Filipinos are registered persons with
disability1. To uphold the rights and welfare of persons with disabilities, Republic
Act No. 7277 or the Magna Carta for Disabled Persons was enacted in 1992.

      Throughout the years, RA 7277 has had its fair share of amendments to
adapt to the times. Despite this, there remains a gap in making volunteerism
opportunities inclusive to persons with disabilities.

      Fortunately, the digital age has brought forth great opportunities for
persons with disabilities to further participate in social activities by breaking

       Given the promise of the digital age, this bill seeks to provide persons with
disabilities with opportunities to engage in worthy humanitarian activities and
overcome barriers to inclusion through online volunteerism. These online
opportunities will enable them to develop their skills and become part of
communities that advance different advocacies and human development, as they
participate in society on an equal basis with everyone else while helping their
fellow people who face different challenges.

       Online volunteerism will serve as a platform for our siblings with
disabilities to share their strengths, abilities, and skills from the comfort of their
own homes while making a tremendous impact on the lives of others as well as
their own. It is the hope of this measure to showcase not disabilities, but people’s
different abilities with the end goal of achieving a more inclusive Filipino society.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                                      AN ACT
           PROVIDING ONLINE VOLUNTEERISM OPPORTUNITIES
                   FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - The Act shall be known as the “Online Volunteerism
for Persons with Disabilities Act”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State recognizes the role of persons
with disabilities in nation-building and community development. Thus, it is
hereby declared the policy of the State to encourage and promote volunteerism
while ensuring the inclusivity of persons with disabilities in nation-building.

SECTION 3. Definitions - For the purposes of this Act, the following terms are
defined as follows:

   a) ‘Governmental entity’ shall refer to any branch, office, or instrumentality
      of government;

   b) ‘Non-profit organization’ shall refer to any organization, which is organized
      and conducted for public benefit, operated primarily for charitable,
      religious, educational, professional, cultural, fraternal, literary, scientific,
      social welfare, civic service, or similar purposes, and not for profit;
   c) ‘Online volunteerism’ shall refer to advocacy groups established online with
      an agenda to commit their time and energy for the well-being of others
      with an extraordinary opportunity to put their skills to serve via the
      internet;

   d) ‘Persons with disabilities’ shall be defined as someone who has a physical
      or mental impairment that has a substantial and long-term adverse effect
      on his or her ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities;

   e) ‘Volunteer’ shall refer to an Individual performing volunteer services who
      does not receive compensation or any other thing in lieu of compensation,
      other than reasonable reimbursement or allowance for expenses actually
      incurred; and

   f) ‘Volunteer services’ shall refer to services rendered by a volunteer for a
      governmental entity and non-profit organization.

SECTION 4. Online Volunteerism Program for Persons with Disabilities. –
The National Council for Disability Affairs, in cooperation with the Philippine
National Volunteer Service Coordinating Agency, shall establish an Online
Volunteerism Program for Persons with Disabilities where both government
entities and non-government organizations can provide opportunities and
humanitarian activities for the capacity development of differently-abled persons
through registered web platforms.

SECTION 5. Selection and Supervision of Online Volunteers with
Disabilities — The NCDA, in cooperation with its partner government entities
and non-government organizations, shall exercise due diligence in the selection
and supervision, including training of online volunteers with disabilities to
perform their volunteer tasks and services.

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations – The NCDA, the Philippine
National Volunteer Service Coordinating Agency, in consultation with relevant
governmental entities, non-government organizations and stakeholders, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to effectively implement the
provisions of this Act within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.
SECTION 7. Non-Impairment Clause. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed
as to diminish, impair, or repeal rights recognized, granted, or made available to
disadvantaged, marginalized, or specific sectors under existing laws.

SECTION 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall continue to
be in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
