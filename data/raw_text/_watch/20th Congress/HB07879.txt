                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Section 47(f) of Republic Act No. 9136,1 otherwise known as the
Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001 (EPIRA), excluded the Agus and
Pulangi Hydroelectric Power Complexes from the initial privatization
program.

       However, the same provision authorizes the Power Sector Assets and
Liabilities Management (PSALM) Corporation to determine any future
privatization, subject only to consultation with Congress. Under this
framework, the disposition of these hydroelectric complexes may proceed
without the need for express legislative approval.

      Consultation does not require concurrence. It does not require an
affirmative act of Congress prior to the transfer of ownership or control.

      The Agus and Pulangi complexes are strategic national assets. They
were developed through substantial public investment and were intended
to advance long-term energy security and regional development in
Mindanao. Their continued operation directly affects power stability,
electricity pricing, industrial growth, and economic resilience in the region.

      Given their scale and strategic importance, decisions concerning their
privatization or transfer of control should not rest on administrative

      This measure amends Section 47(f) of EPIRA to provide that any
privatization, sale, concession, joint venture, or similar transfer of
ownership or control of the Agus and Pulangi Hydroelectric Power
Complexes shall require a joint resolution of Congress expressly authorizing
such disposition. Express approval of Congress is necessary to ensure that
any proposed privatization or disposition is aligned with the State’s energy
policy, mindful of Mindanao’s developmental imperatives, and grounded in
the collective judgment of the people’s representatives.

      The bill thereby restores full legislative oversight over the future of
these critical energy assets and ensures that any change in ownership occurs
only through deliberate legislative action.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
      AMENDING SECTION 47(f) OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9136,
 OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE ELECTRIC POWER INDUSTRY REFORM
  ACT OF 2001, TO REQUIRE AN ACT OF CONGRESS PRIOR TO ANY
    PRIVATIZATION OR TRANSFER OF THE AGUS AND PULANGI
             HYDROELECTRIC POWER COMPLEXES

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

      SECTION 1. Amendment to Section 47(f) of Republic Act No. 9136 —
Section 47(f) of Republic Act No. 9136, as amended, is hereby further
amended to read as follows:

            “(F) THE AGUS AND PULANGI HYDROELECTRIC POWER
      COMPLEXES IN MINDANAO SHALL REMAIN EXCLUDED FROM THE
      INITIAL PRIVATIZATION PROGRAM UNDER THIS ACT. ANY
      SUBSEQUENT PRIVATIZATION OR DISPOSITION THEREOF SHALL BE
      UNDERTAKEN BY THE POWER SECTOR ASSETS AND LIABILITIES
      MANAGEMENT (PSALM) CORPORATION ONLY AFTER PRIOR
      CONSULTATION WITH CONGRESS, WHICH SHALL INCLUDE THE
      FORMAL SUBMISSION OF THE PROPOSED TERMS AND
      JUSTIFICATION OF THE DISPOSITION, AND UPON THE APPROVAL OF
      A JOINT RESOLUTION OF CONGRESS.”

      SECTION 2. Repealing Clause — All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.
       SECTION 3. Effectivity — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
