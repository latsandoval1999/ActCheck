11                                                EXPLANATORY NOTE
12
13           The right to travel is a fundamental liberty guaranteed by the Constitution. Article III,
14   Section 6 of the 1987 Constitution provides that the right to travel shall not be impaired except
15   in the interest of national security, public safety, or public health, as may be provided by law.
16
17          The issuance of a Philippine passport facilitates the exercise of this constitutional right
18   by enabling Filipino citizens to travel internationally for lawful purposes, including
19   employment, education, business, family reunification, and tourism. Under the New Philippine
20   Passport Act (2024), the State shall protect the people’s constitutional right to travel, prescribe
21   only minimum requirements, and ensure expeditious action on applications for and the issuance
22   of passports and other travel documents.1
23
24            Further, the DFA may provide mobile passport services and consular outreach missions
25   to Filipinos in areas outside its offices to make passport services accessible.2 This is particularly
26   relevant given the continuing volume of Filipinos travelling abroad.
27
28          According to the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) 2025 Philippine Statistical
29   Yearbook, there were 3,890,671 outbound Philippine residents in 2023 and 6,853,204 in 2024,
30   representing a 76.14 percent increase.3 In addition, the DFA reported that it issued 2,615,953
31   passports in 2024. Of this number, 1,474,317 passports were issued through Regional Consular
32   Offices, while 161,384 passports were issued through Temporary Offsite Passport Services
33   (TOPS).4 These growing figures demonstrate the growing importance of establishing timely
34   and accessible passport services.

 1           In this regard, the establishment of a DFA Passport Satellite Office in the Municipality
 2   of Liloan, Cebu is both timely and necessary. Liloan is a growing and strategically located
 3   municipality in northern Cebu, with a population of 158,387 based on the 2024 Census of
 4   Population of the Philippine Statistics Authority.5 Despite the presence of an existing DFA
 5   Passport Satellite Office in nearby Consolacion, residents of Liloan must still travel outside the
 6   municipality to access passport services. For applicants, particularly workers, students, senior
 7   citizens, and persons with disabilities (PWDs), traveling to another municipality entails
 8   additional transportation expenses, travel time, and loss of productive hours.
 9
10            Therefore, establishing a satellite office in Liloan would bring passport services closer
11   to the residents and the office can help distribute passport applications among existing
12   facilities and contribute to the decongestion of heavily utilized passport service centers.
13   Ultimately, the establishment of the DFA Passport Satellite Office in Liloan will improve
14   access to essential travel documents, and support the constitutional right to travel by bringing
15   a vital national government service close to the Filipino people.
16
17             In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

11                                 AN ACT
12         ESTABLISHING A DEPARTMENT OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS PASSPORT
13       SATELLITE OFFICE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LILOAN, PROVINCE OF
14                CEBU, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
15
16         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
17   assembled:
18
19          SEC. 1. Establishment of DFA Passport Satellite Office in the Municipality of
20   Liloan. – There shall be established, under the supervision and administrative control of the
21   Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA), a Passport Satellite Office in the Municipality of Liloan,
22   Fifth District, Province of Cebu. The Satellite Office shall provide passport application
23   processing, passport renewal, authentication, and other consular services to residents of the
24   Municipality of Liloan, Province of Cebu, and adjacent cities and municipalities.
25
26          The Office shall provide passport-related services, including, but not limited to:
27          (a) Acceptance of applications for Philippine passports;
28          (b) Processing of passport renewal applications;
29          (c) Biometric enrollment and collection of applicant information;
30          (d) Releasing of passports, subject to applicable DFA rules;
31          (e) Assistance and information services concerning passport applications; and
32          (f) Such other passport-related services as may be authorized by the DFA.
33
34         SEC. 2. Purpose. – The establishment of a DFA Passport Satellite Office in the
35   Municipality of Liloan, Province of Cebu, aims to:
36         (a) Enhance public access to passport and related consular services in Northern Cebu;
37         (b) Reduce the travel time, transportation costs, and other burdens of applicants from
38             geographically distant municipalities;
39         (c) Decongest existing DFA consular offices in the Province of Cebu; and




                                                                                            Page 3 of 4
 1          (d) Support the growing demand for passport, travel, overseas employment, and related
 2              consular services among the constituents of the Fifth District of Cebu and
 3              neighboring areas.
 4
 5          SEC. 3. Staffing and Operations. – The DFA shall assign adequate personnel,
 6   equipment, and resources to ensure the effective and efficient operation of the Satellite Office.
 7   The Office shall adopt the standard operating procedures, security protocols, and digital
 8   systems used in all DFA consular offices.
 9
10          SEC. 4. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial establishment and
11   operationalization of the DFA Passport Satellite Office in the Municipality of Liloan, Province
12   of Cebu, shall be charged against the current appropriations of the Department of Foreign
13   Affairs (DFA), to the extent available. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the
14   continued operation and maintenance of the Office shall be included in the annual General
15   Appropriations Act.
16
17           SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
18   effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Foreign Affairs, in coordination with the Department
19   of Budget and Management (DBM) and other concerned agencies, shall promulgate the rules
20   and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act. The implementing rules
21   and regulations shall include, among others, the organizational structure, staffing requirements,
22   location and facility standards, security requirements, service capacity, appointment
23   procedures, and operating guidelines of the Liloan Passport Satellite Office.
24
25           SEC. 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
26   the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
27
