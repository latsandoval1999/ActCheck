                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MARAMAG, PROVINCE OF
BUKIDNON, A SOUTH SCHOOLS DMSION OFFICE AND APPROPRIATING
FUNDS THEREFOR

                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE


The 1987 Constitution states that the State shall "Establish, maintain and support a complete,
adequate and integrated system ofeducation relevant to the needs of the people and society."


In line with this mandate, this measure seeks the establishment of a South School Division
Office in the Province of Bukidnon to support the existing operations of its School Division
currently handling 20 municipalities. Bukidnon is considered as one of the largest provinces
in Mindanao with its vast land area of approximately 10,498.59 square kilometers. It also has
one of the largest schools division in the entire Region X with a teacher population of
approximately 11,000 and jurisdiction spanning to 20 municipalities. To effectively perform
their mandate and its students, this proposed measure seeks to divide the municipalities to the
current school division and the South School Division.


The partition of the School Division in the province into two will provide, not only additional
manpower, but will greatly improve the competence and capacities of the teachers and school
administrators of the two schools division that will redound to excellence in academic
achievement of schools in the province.


In view of the foregoing, I earnestly seek the approval of this measure.
