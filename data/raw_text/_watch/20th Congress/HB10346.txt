                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       In light of President Marcos Jr.'s State of the Nation Address (SONA), the
Minimum Corporate Income Tax (MCIT) bill reflects the administration's commitment
to fostering a fair and equitable tax system for micro and small enterprises. This bill
seeks to advance the administration's commitment to a fair, equitable, and efficient
tax system that promotes fiscal responsibility while encouraging a competitive
business environment for micro and small enterprises.


       The National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC) imposes a minimum corporate
income tax of two percent (2%) on gross income, aimed at ensuring that all
corporations contribute to national revenue, particularly as the government seeks to
enhance funding for public services and infrastructure.


       The NIRC also authorizes the Secretary of Finance to suspend the tax for
companies facing significant financial challenges, thereby promoting economic
resilience. This flexibility ensures that the MCIT does not impose an undue burden on
legitimately distressed enterprises, while allowing otherwise viable businesses to
recover, preserve employment, and continue their operations.


      This legislation aligns with the administration's broader economic goals,
emphasizing support for businesses while ensuring fiscal responsibility and sustainable
growth.
