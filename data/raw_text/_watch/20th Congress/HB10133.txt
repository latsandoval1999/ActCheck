                                        Explanatory Note

The Philippines is entering what many describe as its "Silver Horizon," characterized by the
steady growth of its senior citizen population brought about by increasing life expectancy and
demographic transition. Today, many Filipinos between the ages of sixty-six (66) and seventy-
five (75) remain healthy, skilled, and fully capable of contributing meaningfully to nation-building.
Despite this, opportunities for continued participation in the workforce remain limited, resulting in
the underutilization of invaluable knowledge, experience, and professional expertise.
At the same time, the country's youth continue to face significant challenges in securing
meaningful employment, acquiring practical workplace skills, and accessing experienced mentors
who can help bridge the gap between education and employment. While the youth possess
innovation, technological proficiency, and fresh perspectives, many lack opportunities for guided
professional development.
This measure seeks to institutionalize the Senior and Youth Employment Tandem Program, a
framework that promotes intergenerational collaboration through short-duty employment,
consultancy engagements, mentoring arrangements, and knowledge-sharing opportunities. By
pairing senior citizens with young workers, the program harnesses the complementary strengths
of both generations, creating a more productive, inclusive, and resilient workforce.
Recognizing the unique needs of senior citizens, the bill likewise provides health and
transportation support, flexible work arrangements, and other enabling mechanisms to ensure
that their continued participation in the workforce remains dignified, accessible, safe, and
sustainable.
The proposed measure is expected to generate significant economic benefits, including:
   •   Strengthening national productivity by combining the experience, institutional
       knowledge, and leadership of senior citizens with the innovation, adaptability, and digital
       competencies of younger workers.
   •   Expanding labor force participation through flexible employment and consultancy
       opportunities, thereby increasing household income and contributing to sustained
       economic growth.
   •   Supporting micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), cooperatives, and local
       industries by providing access to seasoned professionals who can offer mentorship,
       technical expertise, and business guidance.
   •   Reducing economic dependency by enabling senior citizens to supplement their
       retirement income without jeopardizing existing pension benefits, thereby easing financial
       pressures on both families and government support systems.
Beyond its economic advantages, the measure also provides meaningful social benefits.
For senior citizens, the program:
   •   promotes continued productivity and financial independence;
   •   combats social isolation and encourages active community engagement;
   •   recognizes and values their lifelong experience, skills, and contributions to national
       development.
For the youth, the program:
   •   provides direct mentorship from seasoned professionals and community leaders;
   •   enhances employability through practical work experience, skills development, and
       professional guidance;
   •   cultivates discipline, leadership, and a deeper appreciation of Filipino values and
       traditions.
More importantly, this bill serves as a significant investment in strengthening Filipino culture and
social cohesion. By fostering meaningful collaboration between generations, it promotes mutual
respect, understanding, and solidarity while challenging stereotypes that portray senior citizens
as merely dependent members of society or young people as inexperienced and incapable.
Through continuous interaction, mentorship, and shared responsibility, both generations become
active partners in preserving the nation's cultural heritage, traditional knowledge, and community
wisdom.
The proposed tandem employment system likewise reinforces enduring Filipino values such as
pakikipagkapwa, bayanihan, respect for elders, volunteerism, and collective responsibility—
principles that remain essential in building stronger families, more resilient communities, and a
more inclusive nation.
Ultimately, the proposed Senior and Youth Employment Tandem Program Act of 2026 is more
than an employment initiative. It is a strategic investment in human capital, intergenerational
cooperation, economic resilience, and cultural continuity. It empowers senior citizens to remain
productive and financially secure, equips young Filipinos with practical skills and lifelong mentors,
and strengthens the bonds that unite generations in the shared pursuit of national development.
In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                    AN ACT
     PROMOTING THE RE-EMPLOYMENT OF SENIOR RETIREES AGED SIXTY-SIX (66)
      TO SEVENTY-FIVE (75) YEARS OLD THROUGH CONSULTANCY, SHORT-DUTY
       ENGAGEMENTS, AND YOUTH–SENIOR TANDEM EMPLOYMENT, PROVIDING
      HEALTH AND TRANSPORTATION INCENTIVES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as “the Senior and Youth Tandem
 2   Employment Program”.

 3   This Act shall be known as the “Senior Employment and Youth–Senior Tandem Act of
 4   2026.”

 5        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to promote productive
 6   ageing, intergenerational learning, and inclusive employment opportunities for senior
 7   retirees aged sixty-six (66) to seventy-five (75). The State shall ensure that seniors remain
 8   active contributors to society while receiving adequate health and transportation support.

 9        The Youth as the future of the country be given opportunities to partner with
10   experienced and learned senior individuals in employment activities that will mold their
11   values further and allow both individuals a new synergy of sharing knowledge and new
12   information in the work place.

13        SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – The following terms are defined, as follows:

14      a. Senior Retiree – Filipino citizen aged 66–75, retired but physically and mentally
15            fit for work.
16      b. Youth–Senior Tandem – A structured employment arrangement pairing a senior
17            retiree with a youth worker (18–30 years old) for mutual skills transfer and shared
18            duties.
19      c. Short-Duty Employment – Project-based or part-time work not exceeding one
20            hundred twenty (120) days per year.
 1      d. Consultancy Engagement – Advisory or technical service contracts not
 2          exceeding twelve (12) months.


 3        SEC. 4. Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all government agencies including Local
 4   Government Units (LGUs), Government-Owned and Controlled Corporations (GOCCs),
 5   and private enterprises operating in the Philippines.

 6        SEC. 5. Employment Modalities. – The modalities that are applicable under this
 7   Act are:

 8      1. Consultancy Contracts – Seniors may be hired for advisory and technical roles.
 9      2. Short-Duty Assignments – Seniors may serve as mentors, trainers, or project
10          specialists.
11      3. Youth–Senior Tandem Employment – Seniors paired with youth workers for joint
12          contracts, ensuring intergenerational learning.
13              ● Seniors transfer traditional skills and experience.

14              ● Youth provide digital literacy, modern tools, and physical support.

15        SEC. 6. Program Standards and Safeguards. - All employment, consultancy,
16   short-duty, and youth-senior tandem arrangements under this Act shall be voluntary,
17   decent, safe, and suited to the age, health, capacity, and skills of the senior retiree and
18   the youth participant however during the implementation of this program, no participants
19   shall be considered and hired as a regular employee.

20        The Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) in
21   coordination with the National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC) and National
22   Youth Commission (NYC) shall formulate a new short skills training program or the
23   participants under this Act. The duration of the skills training shall not be more than fifteen
24   (15) hours including but not limited to values formation, social conduct, digital literacy,
25   entrepreneurship, and community service skills.

26        All participating employers whether from the public or private sector shall comply
27   with the existing laws and policies on occupational safety and health standards.

28        Participation employers shall adopt a tandem work and learning plan arrangement
29   with clear stipulations as to type of work and working hours.

30        Any arising issues and complaints in the implementation of this Act shall be
31   addressed by NCSC, NYC, and DOLE.

32        SEC. 7. Accreditation of Private Employers. - The DOLE, in coordination with the
33   NCSC, National Youth Commission (NYC), Technical Education and Skills Development
34   Authority (TESDA), and LGUs, shall establish simple accreditation guidelines for private
35   employers participating in the Program.
 1        Accreditation shall require proof that the employer can provide safe work conditions,
 2   reasonable working hours, mentoring arrangements, and basic health and accessibility
 3   support for the program participants.

 4        SEC. 8. Incentives for Employers. - Participating employers from the private sector
 5   shall receive a five percent (5%) tax credits in their annual income tax returns. In addition,
 6   employers providing health and transportation benefits shall be eligible for priority
 7   accreditation under Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) and National
 8   Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC) programs.

 9        Seniors under this Act and not yet enrolled in the Philhealth System shall be enrolled
10   automatically to ensure that they can avail of its benefits.

11        Employers from both Private and Public Sectors shall cover the annual medical
12   check-ups of participating seniors and shall provide transportation allowance equivalent
13   to Php150.00 per day of actual service.

14        SEC. 9. Government Participation. - All national agencies and LGUs shall allocate
15   a reasonable percentage of consultancy and short-duty positions to qualified senior
16   retirees, with priority given to tandem employment arrangements.

17        For job matching and implementation of this program LGUs through their Public
18   Employment Service Offices, Offices for Senior Citizens Affairs, Local Social Welfare
19   Development Offices, Youth Affairs Office, and such other appropriate offices, shall assist
20   in identifying qualified senior retirees and youth participants for the Program.

21        The DOLE, NCSC, NYC, TESDA, and LGUs shall develop a simple referral and job-
22   matching system to match senior retirees and youth participants with suitable
23   consultancy, short-duty, training, mentoring, and tandem employment opportunities.

24        Priority shall be given to indigent senior citizens who are still fit and. For the youth
25   sector participants, priority shall be given to out-of-school youth, unemployed, persons
26   with disabilities, youth from low-income households who has signified to participate in this
27   Program.

28        SEC. 10. Funding. – Funding requirement for this Program shall be incorporated in
29   the annual budget of the NCSC, NYC, and TESDA.

30        SEC. 11. Monitoring and Evaluation. - The NCSC shall submit an annual report to
31   the Speaker of the House of Representatives, the Chairperson of the Committee on
32   Appropriations, the Senate of the Philippines, the Chairperson of the Committee on
33   Finance, and to the Office of the President, with the following data:

34      ● Number of seniors employed,
35      ● Youth–Senior tandem contracts executed,
 1      ● Health and transport benefits provided,
 2      ● Economic and social impact.


 3        SEC.12. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). - Within sixty (60) days from
 4   effectivity, the NCSC, NYC, DOLE, and TESDA shall issue IRR for the effective
 5   implementation of this Act.

 6        SEC.13. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
 7   unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

 8        SEC. 14. Repealing Clause. - All laws inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed
 9   or modified accordingly.

10        SEC. 15. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication in
11   the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

12        Approved.
