EXPLANATORY NOTE

Over twenty-three million students are enrolled in basic education,
from elementary to senior high schools, in the Philippines‘. This translates to
vast amounts of academic and learner information across thousands of
schools nationwide. Yet, many schools, particularly public schools, rely on
paper-based records, resulting in significant administrative work for teachers
and school personnel in the preparation, filing, retrieval, verification, and
transfer of student records.

Given the delays and additional administrative work associated with
these processes, there is a need to maximize the use of technology in the
education system. The digitalization of academic records presents an
opportunity to modernize and streamline these processes. As we reduce the
administrative workload of school personnel, we also provide students more
time to focus on learning.

This measure seeks to modernize the management of academic records
through the establishment of a National Digital Student Records System
(NDSRS). Through this system, the government can reduce administrative
burdens, improve the efficiency and accuracy of record management, and
facilitate the timely retrieval and transfer of learner information.

This proposed measure likewise recognizes the importance of
interoperability across the broader basic education sector. While the
digitalization of records in public elementary and secondary schools shall be
mandated, private basic education institutions shall be allowed to voluntarily
participate in the NDSRS. Their inclusion can promote greater continuity and
efficiency in the movement and verification of learner records across public
and private educational institutions.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

AN ACT
MANDATING THE DIGITALIZATION OF STUDENT ACADEMIC
RECORDS IN ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY SCHOOLS,
ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL DIGITAL STUDENT RECORDS SYSTEM,
AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Digitalization

of Basic Education Academic Records Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. ~ It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to:

(a) Modernize the administration of the public basic education system
through the use of digital technologies;

(b) Ensure the efficient, secure, and accurate management of student
academic records;

(c) Promote ease of access to academic records by learners, parents,
guardians, schools, and authorized government agencies;

(d) Facilitate the transfer, verification, and retrieval of learner

information across educational institutions, and

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:

(a) Academic Records refer to all official educational records
maintained by a school, including report cards, permanent records,
learner profiles, certificates, transcripts, enrollment records,
attendance records, and other documents related to a learner's
academic history;

(b) Digital Academic Record refers to an electronic version of a
student's academic record maintained in a secure and verifiable
format;

(c) National Digital Student Records System (NDSRS) refers to the
centralized digital platform established under this Act for the
storage, management, processing, and verification of student
academic records;

(d) Learner refers to any student enrolled in a public basic education
institution;

(e) Authorized User refers to school officials, teachers, learners, parents
or guardians, and government agencies granted lawful access to
records under this Act.

The Department of Education shall establish and maintain a National Digital

(a) Learner enrollment records;

(b) Report cards and academic performance records;

(e) Attendance records;

(f) Learner profiles and demographic information,

(g) Special education records, where applicable; and

(h) Other records as may be determined by the Department of

Education, in coordination with schools and local government units where
appropriate, shall undertake the gradual digitization of existing student records.

SEC. 7. Access and Retrieval. — Learners, parents, or legal guardians shall
be entitled to request and receive certified copies of academic records through
electronic or physical means.

The Department of Education shall establish procedures for:

(a) Online requests and verification;

(b) Electronic issuance of records;

(c) Digital authentication mechanisms;

(d) Protection against unauthorized access; and
(e) Timely processing of requests.

SEC. 8. Data Privacy and Security. — The collection, storage, processing,
sharing, and retrieval of academic records under this Act shall comply with the
provisions of Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act
of 2012, and other applicable laws.

The Department of Education shall implement appropriate cybersecurity
measures, including:

(a) Encryption;

(b) Access controls;

(c) Audit trails;

(d) Backup and disaster recovery systems; and
(e) Periodic security assessments.

SEC. 9. Responsibilities of the Department of Education. — The
Department of Education shall:

(a) Develop and maintain the NDSRS;

SEC. 10. Responsibility of Public Schools. — Ail public elementary and
secondary schools shall:

(a) Digitize and maintain learner records in accordance with standards

(c) Designate responsible personnel for records management;

(d) Protect learner information from unauthorized disclosure, and

(e) Comply with reporting and implementation requirements.

SEC. 11. Voluntary Participation of Private Basic Education Institutions. —
Private elementary and secondary schools may enroll in and utilize the National
Digital Student Records System on a voluntary basis, subject to the implementing
guidelines issued by the Department of Education. Participating institutions shall
comply with the technical, security, interoperability, and data privacy standards
established under this Act and shall have access to the system's digital verification,
records transfer, and authentication services.

SEC. 12. Capacity Building and Technical Support. - The Department of
Education shall provide training programs for school heads, teachers, registrars,
and administrative personnel to ensure the effective implementation of this Act.
