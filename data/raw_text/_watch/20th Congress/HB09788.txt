                           AN ACT
EXPANDING THE OFFERING OF MEDICAL AND ALLIED HEALTH COURSES
IN STATE UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES TO STRENGTHEN THE
NATIONAL HEALTH WORKFORCE

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Health Courses Expansion
Act”.

     SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people and to ensure the availability of
competent health professionals across the country. Towards this end, the State shall
strengthen and expand access to quality medical and allied health education,
especially in underserved and geographically isolated areas, by expanding the
capacity of State Universities and Colleges (SUCs).

       SEC. 3. Expansion of Medical and Allied Health Programs in SUCs. – Qualified
SUCs, subject to evaluation and approval by the Commission on Higher Education
(CHED), are hereby encouraged to establish and offer degree programs in the
following fields:

            a.                                                                                   B
         achelor of Science in Nursing;

            b.                                                                                   B
         achelor of Science in Medical Technology;

            c.                                                                                   B
         achelor of Science in Dentistry;
         d.                                                                             B
      achelor of Science in Pharmacy; and

           e.                                                                         O
       ther relevant allied health programs.
       In approving such programs, CHED shall ensure that SUCs meet the minimum
standards for faculty qualifications, facilities, laboratories, and clinical training
requirements.

      SEC. 4. Health Workforce Needs Assessment. – CHED, in coordination with the
Department of Health (DOH) and other relevant agencies, shall conduct periodic
assessments of national and regional health workforce needs to guide the strategic
expansion of medical and allied health programs in SUCs, with priority given to
underserved and health workforce-deficient areas.

     SEC. 5. Institutional Support and Capacity Building. – CHED, DOH, and other
concerned agencies shall provide support through:

         a.
      faculty development and training programs;

         b.                                                                             i
      nfrastructure and laboratory development assistance;

         c.                                                                             a
      cademic and institutional partnerships; and

         d.                                                                          r
      ecognition or incentive programs for SUCs that significantly contribute to the
      development of the health workforce, particularly in underserved regions.

     SEC. 6. Implementing Agencies. – CHED shall serve as the lead implementing
agency of this Act. CHED shall coordinate with the DOH, SUCs, and other relevant
government agencies to ensure the effective implementation of this Act.

      SEC. 7. Funding. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of CHED and concerned
SUCs. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued implementation
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

      SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, CHED, in coordination with the DOH, SUCs, and other relevant

     SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – Should any provision or part of the Act be declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions and parts hereof, insofar as they are
separable from the invalid ones, shall remain in full force and effect.

     SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations, rules, and
regulations, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

     SEC. 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.
