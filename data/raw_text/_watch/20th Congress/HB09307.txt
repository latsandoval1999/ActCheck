   INTRODUCED BY REPRESENTATIVE WALFREDO R. DIMAGUILA

The Land Transportation Office (LTO) plays a vital role in ensuring efficient land transpor-
tation regulation, motor vehicle registration, driver licensing, and enforcement of transporta-
tion laws in the country. As urban centers continue to expand and economic activities inten-
sify, the demand for accessible and efficient public services from the LTO correspondingly
increases.

The City of Cabuyao in the Province of Laguna has experienced rapid urbanization, industri-
al growth, and population expansion over the years. Being one of the major economic hubs
in CALABARZON, Cabuyao hosts numerous industrial parks, commercial establishments,
and residential communities, resulting in a significant increase in the number of registered
motor vehicles and licensed drivers within its jurisdiction.

Despite the growing volume of transactions and clientele being served by the LTO Cabuyao
District Office, its present classification as a Class “B” district office limits its operational
capacity, personnel complement, and service efficiency. Consequently, residents, motorists,
and business stakeholders experience congestion, longer processing times, and limited acces-
sibility to essential LTO services.

Upgrading the LTO Cabuyao District Office from a Class “B” to a Class “A” district office
will enable the government to enhance its operational capability, improve public service de-
livery, augment personnel and resources, and accommodate the increasing demands of the
riding and motoring public in Cabuyao and nearby localities. This measure will likewise
support the continued economic development of the area by providing more responsive and
efficient transportation-related services.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT UPGRADING THE CLASSIFICATION OF CABUYAO DISTRICT
OFFICE OF THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) IN CABUYAO,
LAGUNA FROM CLASS “B” TO CLASS “A” DISTRICT OFFICE, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Creation. – Cabuyao District Office of the Land Transportation Office is
hereby upgraded from Claa”B” to Class”A” district office in the Municipality of Cabuyao,
Laguna.

      Section 2. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Assistant Secretary of the Land
Transportation Office, in consultation with the Department of Transportation (DOTr), shall
formulate the necessary rules and regulations to implement this Act.

      Section 3. Appropriations. – The Secretary of the Department of Transportation
(DOTr) shall immediately include in the Department’s program the operationalization of
LTO Cabuyao in the Municipality of Cabuyao, Laguna, the funding of which shall be in-
cluded in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       Section 4. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the online version of the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation or with established internet website presence.

      Approved.
