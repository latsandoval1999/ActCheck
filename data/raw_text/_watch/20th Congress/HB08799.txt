                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to institutionalize the provision of a health maintenance
organization (HMO) to all public school teachers under the Department of
Education (DepEd). It ensures that teachers are afforded adequate health protection
by granting them access to essential healthcare services and maintain their overall
well-being. This promotes their overall well-being and enables them to perform their
duties with competence and dignity.

        Public school teachers perform a critical role in nation-building as they shape
the intellectual capacity and values of the youth. The demands of their profession
require not only subject matter expertise, but also sustained physical and mental
fitness. However, the nature of their work, which oftentimes involves long hours,
heavy workloads, and administrative responsibilities, exposes them to various
health risks that necessitate accessible and reliable healthcare support.

        Despite the enactment of Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise known as the
“Universal Health Care Act”, many public school teachers continue to bear
out-of-pocket healthcare expenses that further strain their limited financial sources.
This affects not only their personal well-being but also their capacity to perform
their functions at an optimal level.

       The provision of HMO coverage to our educators is a strategic and necessary
investment in the country’s education system, affirming the State’s commitment to
protecting the welfare of educators. A healthy teaching workforce leads to improved
productivity, reduced absenteeism, and better learning outcomes for the future of
the nation.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR A HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATION FOR
PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Health Maintenance
Organization for Public School Teachers Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the vital role of public
sector employees in nation building. As an important component of the public
sector, the State shall ensure that public school teachers have access to
comprehensive, quality, cost-effective, preventive, and rehabilitative health services.

      SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall cover all public teachers under the
Department of Education (DepEd).

      SEC. 4. Selection of Health Maintenance Organization. – The DepEd shall, in
accordance with the provisions of Republic Act No. 9184, otherwise known as the
Government Procurement Reform Act, as amended, procure a contract with a Health
Maintenance Organization that shall be able to provide healthcare services to its
employees.

       For the purposes of this Act, a Health Maintenance Organization (HMO)
refers to a health insurance organization which provides health insurance coverage
for a monthly or annual fee charged to one’s employer and covers the fundamental
services needed by individuals to maintain their health and well-being such as
in-patient and out-patient care, basic surgeries, and other ancillary services like
laboratory testing and medication.

       SEC. 5. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the immediate
implementation of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act for
the year following its approval.

       SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Education,

        SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held invalid or

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders and rules, and regulations
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
amended accordingly.

      SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation.
