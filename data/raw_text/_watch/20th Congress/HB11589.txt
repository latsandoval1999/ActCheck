EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines ranks among the world's major seaweed producers, and the industry
provides livelihoods and incomes for thousands of coastal families, farmers, fisherfolk, processors,
traders, and other stakeholders. Beyond its economic significance, seaweed is a primafy raw
material for carrageenan and is used in diverse food, pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, agricultural,
cosmetic, and industrial applications.

Despite considerable potential, the Philippine seaweed and carrageenan sectors continue to
encounter challenges related to productivity, climate change, disease management, post-harvest
losses, technology adoption, product quality, value addition, research and development, and access
to both domestic and international markets.

A coordinated, science-based approach is required to strengthen the seaweed and
carrageenan value chain in the Philippines.

The proposed measure aims to establish a National Seaweed and Carrageenan Scienee and
Innovation Program under the leadership of the Department of Science and Technology. This
Program will provide a comprehensive framework for research, technology development,
innovation, technology transfer, extension services, commercialization, and market development.

A key provision of the bill is the establishment or designation of Centers of Excellence,
which will serve as regional hubs for research and development, technology demonsffation,
product development, training, testing, technology transfer, and collaboration among government,
academia, industry, farmers, fisherfolk, and communities.

The bill also establishes Seaweed and Carrageenan Funds to provide sustained fimancial
support for research and innovation, technology development, training, technology transfer,
commercialization, product development, and market promotion.

Recognizing that scientific discoveries and technologies have limited impact unless
effectively disseminated, the measure provides for strengthened technology transfer and extension
services. These services will enable farmers, fisherfolk, processors, cooperatives, and enterprises
to access and adopt appropriate technologies that can increase productivity, improve quality,
reduce losses, enhance sustainability, and increase income.

The bill further advances market development initiatives to strengthen the position of
Philippine seaweed and carrageenan products in both domestic and international markets. Support
in product development, certification, branding, standards compliance, trade promotion, and

===== [page 2] =====
export development will enable Philippine producers and enterprises to compete more effeetively
in the global marketplace. An Inter-Agency Committee on Seaweed and Carrageenan Science and
Innovation will be established to ensure coordination among government agencies and
stakeholders, and to prevent duplication of programs and resources. This measure will strengthen
the scientific and technological foundations of the seaweed and carrageenan industries, while
promoting environmental sustainability, inclusive development, innovation, and global
competitiveness.

The passage of this measure is expected to result in higher productivity and inéomes,
stronger rural and coastal economies, increased value addition, expanded markets, amd the
development of a more sustainable and globally competitive Philippine seaweed and carrageenan
industry.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

KATRINA REIKO CHUA-TAI

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Seaweed and Carrageenan

Science and Innovation Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to pfomote
the sustainable development, scientific advancement, technological innovation, productivity, and

global competitiveness of the Philippine seaweed and carrageenan industries.

The State recognizes seaweed as an important marine resource and carrageenan as @ high-
value seaweed-derived product with significant potential for food, pharmaceutical, nutraceutical,

agricultural, cosmetic, industrial, and other applications.

The State shall therefore encourage and support research and development, innovation,
technology transfer, sustainable production, value addition, enterprise development, market

expansion, and human resource development for the benefit of seaweed farmers, fishierfolk,

SEC. 3. Lead Agency. — The Department of Science and Technology (DOST) shal! be the

lead agency in the implementation of this Act.

The DOST shall, in coordination with the Department of Agriculture (DA), Bureau of

other concerned stakeholders, formulate and implement programs consistent with the objectives

of this Act.

The DOST shall ensure that government research and innovation programs relating to
seaweed and carrageenan are properly coordinated and responsive to the needs of producers,

processors, enterprises, consumers, and markets.

processing, carrageenan extraction, refinement, and product development;

(b) Develop sustainable, climate-resilient, and environmentally responsible seaweed

production and processing technologies;

SEC. 8. Market Development and Promotion. — The DTI, in coordination with the
