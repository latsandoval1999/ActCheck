LAW

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The constitutional mandate of the Office of the Ombudsman as an independent
guardian of public accountability is gravely impaired by persistent fiscal limitations.
When measured against the resources provided to comparable anti-corruption
institutions in other jurisdictions, the present appropriations for the Office remain
manifestly insufficient.

       To fortify the state's anti-corruption efforts without imposing new burdens on
public taxpayers, this measure introduces a self-funding model for accountability. By
establishing a statutory claim on state-recovered assets seized under Republic Act No.
1379, the legislation mandates that exactly 30% of all judicially confiscated properties
be diverted directly into the operational budget of the Office of the Ombudsman. This
dedicated revenue stream provides the fiscal stability necessary to sustain complex,
long-term litigations and clear backlogs of public misconduct cases.

       The passage of this bill therefore is earnestly sought.
