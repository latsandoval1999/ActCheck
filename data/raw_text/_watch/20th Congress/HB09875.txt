                           AN ACT
 ESTABLISHING A COMMUNITY TOOLBOX IN BARANGAY NAGKAISANG
  NAYON, DISTRICT V OF QUEZON CITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
                          THEREFOR

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Barangay Nagkaisang Nayon, located in District V of Quezon City, is a
densely populated and rapidly developing community where many
households rely on incremental home improvement, informal repairs, and
small-scale livelihood activities to sustain their daily needs. Despite this,
access to basic tools for carpentry, plumbing, sewing, and minor construction
remains limited due to financial constraints.

      For many residents, the cost of purchasing tools competes with
essential household expenditures such as food, education, and healthcare. At
the same time, a significant number of residents possess the skills necessary
for basic repairs and productive activities, but are constrained by the lack of
access to appropriate equipment.

    This measure seeks to establish a Community Toolbox in Barangay
Nagkaisang Nayon, which will serve as a shared facility where residents may
borrow essential tools for home maintenance, small construction, and
livelihood-related activities. By institutionalizing a tool-sharing system at the
barangay level, the measure promotes:

   1. Economic Relief – reducing the need for households to purchase costly
      tools for occasional use;
   2. Livelihood Support – enabling residents to engage in small-scale repair
      services and income-generating activities;
   3. Disaster Preparedness and Resilience – ensuring communities have
      access to the tools necessary for immediate repairs during emergencies;
   4. Environmental Sustainability – encouraging shared use of resources and
      reducing unnecessary consumption; and
   5. Community Cohesion – fostering cooperation and shared responsibility
      among residents.

    The establishment of a Community Toolbox aligns with the State’s policy
to empower local government units and strengthen community-based
initiatives that improve quality of life at the grassroots level.

   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Community
Toolbox of Barangay Nagkaisang Nayon”.

SECTION 2. Definition. – For purposes of this Act, “Community Toolbox”
refers to a barangay-managed facility that provides residents access to shared
tools and equipment, including but not limited to those used for carpentry,
plumbing, electrical work, sewing, and minor construction, which may be
borrowed subject to guidelines issued by the barangay.

SECTION 3. Establishment of Barangay Nagkaisang Nayon Community
Toolbox.– There is hereby established a Community Toolbox in Barangay
Nagkaisang Nayon, District V of Quezon City, which shall be housed in the
Barangay Hall or in any suitable facility designated by the barangay.

SECTION 4. Management and Operations. The Barangay Local
Government Unit of Nagkaisang Nayon shall be responsible for the
management, maintenance, and operation of the Community Toolbox,
including the formulation of policies on borrowing, returns, maintenance, and
accountability of tools.

SECTION 5. Funding. – The initial funding for the establishment of the
Community Toolbox shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act. Thereafter, the maintenance and operation of the facility shall be funded
through the barangay’s annual budget, subject to existing budgeting,
accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations. The barangay may also
receive grants, donations, or assistance from public or private entities.

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. –Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the Department of the Interior
and Local Government (DILG), in coordination with the Quezon City Local
Government Unit and Barangay Nagkaisang Nayon, shall promulgate the
necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 7. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any provision of this
Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, parts or provisions of this Act
which are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
and regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general
circulation.
