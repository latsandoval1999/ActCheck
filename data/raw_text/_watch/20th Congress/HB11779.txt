 IMPLEMENTATION SUPPORT FUND FOR BASIC EDUCATION, PROVIDING

                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Department of Education (DepEd), through DepEd Order No. 003, s.
2026, or the Foundational Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Basic
Education, has established a national framework for the safe, responsible, and
meaningful use of artificial intelligence in basic education. The Order provides
guidance on the appropriate use of AI while emphasizing human oversight,
learner protection, data privacy, academic integrity, and the responsible
adoption of AI technologies in schools.

      The effective implementation of these policies, however, requires adequate
and equitable resources. Schools differ significantly in their access to trained
teachers, digital learning resources, devices, connectivity, technical assistance,
and other infrastructure necessary to support the responsible use of AI. Without
sustained support, schools with limited resources may face greater difficulty in
implementing the standards and safeguards established under DepEd Order No.
003, s. 2026.

       This measure therefore seeks to establish a National Artificial Intelligence
Education Support Fund to provide the financial and technical resources
necessary to support the implementation of DepEd's existing and subsequent
policies on artificial intelligence in basic education. The Fund shall support
teacher training and professional development, curriculum-aligned learning
resources, necessary digital infrastructure and connectivity, technical
assistance, data privacy and cybersecurity measures, and targeted assistance to
schools with limited resources and technical capacity.

       The proposed measure does not seek to replace or duplicate the
substantive policies of DepEd Order No. 003, s. 2026. Rather, it provides a
statutory and sustainable funding mechanism to help DepEd translate these
policies into effective and equitable school-level implementation, consistent with
school readiness, available resources, and applicable laws and regulations.

       By providing dedicated and targeted support, the measure seeks to ensure
that the benefits of responsible AI use in education are not limited to schools
and communities that already possess greater technological resources. It aims
to strengthen teacher capacity, improve access to appropriate educational
resources and technology, and enable schools to adopt AI-related initiatives in a
safe, responsible, and sustainable manner.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “AI Education
Implementation Support Fund Act”.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to promote the
safe, responsible, equitable, and meaningful use of artificial intelligence (AI) in
basic education, consistent with the constitutional mandate to protect and
promote the right of all citizens to quality education.

The State recognizes DepEd Order No. 003, s. 2026, or the Foundational
Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence in Basic Education, as the existing policy
framework governing the responsible use of AI in basic education. The State shall
provide appropriate and equitable support for its effective implementation,
particularly in schools and communities with limited resources and technical
capacity.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed as amending, replacing, or diminishing
the authority of the Department of Education (DepEd) to formulate and
implement policies, standards, curricula, and guidelines on the use of AI in basic
education.
SECTION 3. National Artificial Intelligence Education Implementation
Support Program. There is hereby established a National Artificial Intelligence
Education Implementation Support Fund, hereinafter referred to as the Fund, to
be administered by the Department of Education.

The Fund shall provide targeted financial and technical support for the
implementation of DepEd policies on AI in basic education, including DepEd
Order No. 003, s. 2026 and subsequent issuances consistent therewith.

The Fund shall complement, and shall not duplicate or replace, existing
appropriations, programs, projects, and partnerships of DepEd supporting
digitalization, teacher development, learning resources, and AI-related
initiatives.

SECTION 4. Uses of the Fund. - The Fund shall be used for AI-specific
implementation requirements, including:
      (a) AI-specific teacher capacity-building and professional development,
      including responsible AI use, AI literacy, data privacy, cybersecurity,
      academic integrity, and appropriate pedagogical applications;

      (b) Development, adaptation, and dissemination of AI-related learning
      resources that are curriculum-aligned, age-appropriate, accessible, and
      consistent with DepEd policies;

      (c) Technical assistance and implementation support for schools adopting
      AI-enabled educational initiatives consistent with DepEd policies;

      (d) Measures for responsible and safe AI use, including support for data
      privacy, cybersecurity, child protection, academic integrity, accessibility,
      and other applicable safeguards;

      (e) Pilot programs, research, monitoring, evaluation, and technical support
      for AI initiatives in basic education;

      (f) Targeted support to geographically isolated and disadvantaged schools,
      last-mile schools, and other schools with demonstrated gaps in AI
      readiness and technical capacity; and

      (g) AI-specific devices, connectivity, digital platforms, and related
      infrastructure, when necessary for implementation and when such
      requirements are not adequately supported by existing DepEd programs
      and appropriations.
SECTION 5. Readiness-Based and Equitable Allocation. — DepEd shall
allocate the Fund based on school readiness, teacher capacity, infrastructure
and connectivity gaps, learner needs, accessibility requirements, and other
relevant indicators of capacity to implement AI-related policies and initiatives.

Priority shall be given to schools and communities with limited access to
technology, technical assistance, and AI-related capacity-building.

The Fund shall not be used to require the uniform adoption of any particular AI
tool, platform, or system among schools.

SECTION 6. Coordination and Non-Duplication of Funding. — DepEd shall
coordinate the use of the Fund with its existing appropriations, programs,
projects, and partnerships, including those supporting ICT, digitalization,
teacher professional development, learning resources, and AI-related initiatives.

The Fund shall primarily address AI-specific implementation requirements that
are not adequately supported by existing resources. DepEd shall establish
appropriate mechanisms to prevent duplication and ensure the efficient and
equitable use of public funds.

SECTION 7. Annual Artificial Intelligence Education Implementation and
Funding Plan. — DepEd shall prepare an annual Artificial Intelligence
Education Implementation and Funding Plan identifying:

      (a) priority AI-related activities and initiatives;

      (b) target schools and beneficiaries;

      (c) teacher capacity-building and technical assistance requirements;

      (d) AI-related learning resource and infrastructure requirements;

      (e) measures for responsible and safe AI implementation; and

      (f) estimated funding requirements and expected outcomes.

The Plan shall guide the allocation and utilization of the Fund and shall be
aligned with DepEd's existing AI policies and applicable national education
plans.
SECTION 8. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations
of the Department of Education, subject to existing budgeting, accounting,
auditing, procurement, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued implementation of this Act
shall be included as a distinct and identifiable item in the annual General
Appropriations Act, subject to the usual budget authorization and appropriation
process.

The Fund shall be used in accordance with the Annual Artificial Intelligence
Education Implementation and Funding Plan under Section 7 of this Act.

SECTION 9. Partnerships. DepEd may enter into partnerships with relevant
national government agencies, local government units, educational institutions,
civil society organizations, development partners, and qualified private entities
to support the objectives of this Act, subject to existing laws, rules, and
regulations.

All partnerships shall comply with applicable laws on public procurement, data
privacy, child protection, cybersecurity, intellectual property, and government
auditing.

No partnership shall authorize the unauthorized commercial use, disclosure, or
exploitation of learner or teacher data.

SECTION 8. Monitoring and Annual Report. -- The Secretary of Education
shall submit an annual report to Congress on the implementation of this Act,
including:

      (a) Fund allocation and utilization;

      (b) participating and assisted schools;

      (c) teacher capacity-building activities;

      (d) learning resources and technical assistance provided;

      (e) implementation in geographically isolated and disadvantaged and other
      priority schools;

      (f) measures undertaken to ensure responsible and safe AI use; and
      (g) program outcomes, challenges, and recommendations.

SECTION 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within one hundred
twenty (120) days from the effectivity of this Act, DepEd shall promulgate the
rules and regulations necessary for its implementation.

The implementing rules and regulations shall be consistent with DepEd Order
No. 003, s. 2026 and subsequent policies on AI in basic education, and shall be
developed in consultation with relevant government agencies and stakeholders.

SECTION 10. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 11. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly

SECTION 12. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
