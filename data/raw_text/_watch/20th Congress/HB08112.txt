                             AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A ONE-STOP-SHOP SERVICE CENTER IN THE MUNICIPALITY
    OF SOGOD, SECOND LEGISLATIVE DISTRICT OF SOUTHERN LEYTE,
  INTEGRATING FRONTLINE SERVICES OF KEY NATIONAL GOVERNMENT
          AGENCIES, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

Access to essential government services remains a continuing challenge for many
Filipinos living in provincial and geographically dispersed areas. In the Province of
Southern Leyte, particularly in the municipalities comprising the Second Legislative
District, residents often travel long distances to transact with various national
government agencies for basic requirements such as civil registry documents, social
security services, health insurance concerns, tax-related transactions, and housing
program applications. These logistical and financial burdens result in delayed access to
government assistance and reduced efficiency in public service delivery.

The Municipality of Sogod serves as the primary commercial and transportation hub of
the Second District of Southern Leyte, making it a strategic and accessible location for the
establishment of an integrated government service facility. Creating a centralized One-
Stop-Shop Government Service Center in Sogod will significantly reduce travel time,
transportation costs, and administrative inconvenience for constituents who would
otherwise need to visit multiple offices located in different cities or provinces.

This proposed measure seeks to institutionalize a coordinated and citizen-centered
approach to public service delivery by consolidating frontline services of key national
government agencies, namely the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), Social Security System (SSS), Bureau of Internal
Revenue (BIR), and the Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund), within a single
accessible location. By integrating these high-demand services, the government can
streamline transactions, improve inter-agency coordination, and enhance the overall
efficiency and responsiveness of public service.
The bill likewise supports the national government’s ongoing efforts to modernize
bureaucracy, reduce red tape, and promote inclusive access to government programs
through localized service hubs complemented by digital assistance and inter-agency
cooperation. Establishing a district-level service center will not only improve
convenience for citizens but also strengthen government presence and responsiveness in
underserved communities.

Ultimately, this measure aims to bring government closer to the people by ensuring that
essential public services are accessible, efficient, and responsive to the everyday needs of
constituents in Southern Leyte.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
                                       assembled:

Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Southern Leyte Second District
One-Stop-Shop Government Service Center Act."

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to bring
government services closer to the people by ensuring accessible, efficient, and integrated
delivery of frontline public services, particularly in provinces where residents must travel
long distances to transact with national government agencies.

Consistent with efforts to reduce bureaucratic red tape and improve citizen access to
services, the State shall establish localized service hubs that consolidate multiple national
government functions in a single location. Such one-stop-shop service mechanisms have
been adopted nationwide to streamline transactions and improve public service delivery.

Section 3. Establishment of the One-Stop-Shop Government Service Center. – A
National Government One-Stop-Shop Service Center is established in the Municipality of
Sogod, Province of Southern Leyte, which shall primarily serve the residents of the
Second Legislative District.

The Center shall function as an integrated facility where citizens may access multiple
national government services through a single physical location.

The Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) and other
relevant agencies are hereby mandated to provide technical assistance to ensure the
expeditious issuance or release of any such requirement and subsequent clearance,
permit, and application.

Section 4. Participating Agencies – The following agencies shall assign personnel,
establish satellite desks, or provide regular on-site services within the Center:
   (a) Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA);
   (b) Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth);
   (c) Social Security System (SSS);
   (d) Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR);
   (e) Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund);
   (f) Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth)

Section 6. Functions of the Center. – The One-Stop-Shop Service Center shall:
   (a) Provide integrated access to national government programs and assistance;
   (b) Reduce travel time and cost for constituents seeking government services;
   (c) Facilitate inter-agency coordination and faster processing of applications;
   (d) Support digital and online government platforms through assisted processing;
       and
   (e) Serve as a district-level hub for government outreach activities.

Section 7. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act and may be supplemented
by grants, partnerships, or other lawful funding sources.

Section 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DICT, in consultation with concerned agencies and stakeholders,
shall promulgate the implementing rules and regulations.

Section 9. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act shall
be declared as unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected
shall remain in full force and effect.

Section 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders and rules
and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

Section 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
