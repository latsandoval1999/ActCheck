               Introduced by REP. CLAUDINE DIANA D. BAUTISTA-LIM


AN ACT MANDATING THE ADOPTION OF A STANDARDIZED TRICYCLE
PASSENGER CABIN DESIGN TO ENHANCE PASSENGER SAFETY,
ACCESSIBILITY, COMFORT, AND ROADWORTHINESS, PROVIDING GUIDELINES
FOR ITS IMPLEMENTATION, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Standard Tricycle
Passenger Cabin Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to promote safe, reliable,
accessible, and environmentally sustainable public transportation. Recognizing that
tricycles remain one of the primary modes of public transport in cities and municipalities
throughout the Philippines, the State shall ensure that tricycle passenger cabins meet
minimum standards of safety, structural integrity, accessibility, and passenger comfort
while preserving the affordability of public transportation and protecting the livelihood of
tricycle operators and drivers.

SECTION 3. Objectives. - This Act aims to:

(a) Establish a national standard for the construction and design of tricycle passenger
cabins;

(b) Improve the safety and protection of passengers and drivers during road operations;

(c) Promote accessibility for senior citizens, persons with disabilities (PWDs), pregnant
women, and children;

(d) Ensure adequate passenger space, ventilation, and weather protection;

(e) Encourage the use of durable and locally manufactured materials; and

(f) Improve the overall quality and roadworthiness of tricycles operating as public utility
vehicles.
SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

(a) Tricycle refers to a motorcycle with an attached sidecar used for public transportation
and duly registered with the Land Transportation Office (LTO) and authorized by the
concerned Local Government Unit (LGU).

(b) Passenger Cabin refers to the sidecar or passenger compartment attached to a
motorcycle for the purpose of transporting passengers.

(c) Standard Passenger Cabin refers to a passenger cabin that complies with the
technical specifications prescribed under this Act and its Implementing Rules and
Regulations (IRR).

SECTION 5. Adoption of National Standards. - Within one hundred eighty (180) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Transportation (DOTr), through the Land
Transportation Office (LTO), in coordination with the Department of Trade and Industry
(DTI), the Department of Science and Technology (DOST), the Technical Education and
Skills Development Authority (TESDA), the League of Cities of the Philippines,
the League of Municipalities of the Philippines, accredited tricycle manufacturers,
transport cooperatives, and transport organizations, shall formulate and adopt
the National Standard Tricycle Passenger Cabin Design.

The standards shall include, but shall not be limited to:

   1. Minimum structural strength and crash resistance;
   2. Standard dimensions ensuring adequate passenger legroom and headroom;
   3. Ergonomic seating configuration;
   4. Non-slip flooring;
   5. Safety handrails and grab handles;
   6. Proper entry and exit clearances;
   7. Weather-resistant roofing and side protection;
   8. Adequate ventilation;
   9. Standard lighting and reflectors;
   10. Emergency exit provisions where applicable;
   11. Fire-resistant or fire-retardant construction materials;
   12. Maximum passenger capacity;
   13. Provisions to improve accessibility for senior citizens and PWDs; and
   14. Other safety features deemed necessary by the DOTr.

SECTION 6. Compliance. - All newly manufactured tricycle passenger cabins intended
for public transport shall comply with the national standards established under this Act.

Existing tricycles shall be given a transition period of not less than five (5) years to comply
with the prescribed standards, subject to the implementing guidelines.
SECTION 7. Financial Assistance and Incentives. - To minimize the financial burden
on tricycle operators and drivers, the national government, subject to the availability of
funds, may provide:

(a) Low-interest financing programs;

(b) Credit facilities;

(c) Modernization assistance;

(d) Technical support for local fabricators; and

(e) Other incentives necessary to facilitate compliance with this Act.

Priority shall be given to low-income operators and members of accredited transport
cooperatives and associations.

SECTION 8. Local Fabrication. - The DTI and DOST shall encourage the development
of standardized passenger cabins utilizing locally sourced materials and manufactured by
accredited local fabricators, provided that such products comply with national safety
standards.

SECTION 9. Inspection and Certification. - No tricycle passenger cabin shall be
authorized for public transport unless certified by the Local Government Units concerned
as compliant with the standards prescribed under this Act.

Periodic inspections shall likewise be conducted to ensure continued compliance.

SECTION 10. Role of Local Government Units. - Cities and municipalities shall
incorporate compliance with the national standard passenger cabin as one of the
requirements in the issuance or renewal of tricycle franchises, permits, or licenses.

SECTION 11. Penalties. - Any manufacturer, fabricator, or operator who knowingly
manufactures, sells, installs, or operates a non-compliant passenger cabin after the
expiration of the prescribed transition period shall be subject to:

(a) A fine of not less than Twenty Thousand Pesos (₱20,000.00) but not more than One
Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱100,000.00);

(b) Suspension or revocation of accreditation or permit; and/or

(c) Other administrative sanctions as may be provided under the IRR.

SECTION 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within one hundred eighty (180)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOTr, in coordination with the LTO, DTI, DOST,
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), TESDA, LGUs, and relevant
stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

SECTION 13. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the implementing agencies.
Thereafter, the necessary funds shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

SECTION 14. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 15. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.

SECTION 16. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
