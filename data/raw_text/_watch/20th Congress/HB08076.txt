                                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XII, Section 1 of the 1987 Constitution states that “[t]he goals of the
national economy are a more equitable distribution of opportunities, income, and
wealth; a sustained increase in the amount of goods and services produced by the
nation for the benefit of the people; and an expanding productivity as the key raising
the quality of life for all, especially the underprivileged”.

       The Pili tree (Canarium ovatum) is steadily emerging as a true “tree of life,”
next to the coconut in terms of its wide-ranging benefits and economic value. Its well-
known kernel continues to be a prized product, while its pulp and sap contribute to
the pharmaceutical and cosmetic industries. At the same time, its wood and shells are
transformed into furniture and fashion accessories. In essence, the Pili tree represents
food security, livelihood generation, and sustainable enterprise, making it a valuable
asset for our communities and our economy.

As the only country in the world devoted to large-scale commercial Pili production, the
Philippines holds a unique global competitive advantage that must be institutionalized
through strategic branding and legislative support. At the heart of this industry lies
the Province of Sorsogon, the nation’s leading Pili producer. According to the
Commodity Fact Sheet from the Philippine Statistics Authority, Sorsogon contributes
approximately 55% of the total production in the Bicol Region. Since 2014, Sorsogon
has consistently been the leading pili nut producer among all provinces in Region V,
yielding 4,004.18 metric tons in 2014 and 2,804.61 metric tons in 2023. Although
production has decreased slightly over the years, Sorsogon’s annual output over the
last decade continues to surpass that of other provinces.1

brittle, measuring 144.16 square meters. This agricultural excellence provides a
sustainable livelihood for thousands of Bicolano farmers who depend on the cultivation
and processing of this "green gold."

      This bill was previously filed during the 18th Congress and underwent approval
by the Committee on Agriculture and Food, signaling its long-standing merit and
urgency. By institutionalizing the creation of a Pili Subsidy Fund and a Pili Research
and Development Center, this bill will strengthen agricultural branding, fuel MSMEs,
and honor the tireless dedication of Bicolano farmers.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

 1         SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Pili Industry
 2   Development Act.”
 3
 4          SEC. 2. Declaration of Policies. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
 5   to foster the growth and development of a globally competitive and sustainable Pili
 6   industry, recognizing its strategic importance to the national economy and the Province
 7   of Sorsogon as its primary production hub. To this end, the State shall implement
 8   integrated measures to protect the industry’s viability, ensure the welfare of farmers
 9   and stakeholders, and catalyze rural development through consistent employment
10   generation and livelihood security.
11
12          SEC. 3. Creation of the Pili Research and Development Center. – Within
13   a year from the approval of this Act, there shall be created the Pili Research and
14   Development Center in Sorsogon which shall be attached to the Sorsogon State College
15   in Sorsogon City, and to the Central Bicol State University of Agriculture in Camarines
16   Sur.
17
18           SEC. 4. Functions of the Pili Research and Development Center. – The
19   Pili Research and Development Center shall perform the following functions and duties:
20                 a. Undertake and conduct researches and technical studies on the
21                 development, production, management, and marketing of pili nuts and its
22                 by products;
23
24                b. Provide technical assistance and support to pili farmers and others
25                stakeholders in the Bicol Region;

18         SEC. 5. Pili Subsidy Fund –The Pili Subsidy Fund is hereby created, which shall
19   be directly and exclusively used to support the sustainable development and
20   competitiveness of the pili industry. The Fund shall cover the following:
21         a. Pili Tree Planting and Cultivation- to support the propagation, establishment,
22         and maintenance of pili trees, including the provision of quality seedlings, farm
23         inputs, technical assistance, and adoption of climate-resilient and
24         environmentally sustainable farming practices;
25
26         b. Skills Training and Capital Development- to provide capacity-building
27         programs, entrepreneurial and technical skills training, and financial or capital
28         assistance to farmers, cooperatives, and stakeholders engaged in the pili value
29         chain, including processing, product development, marketing, and other value-
30         adding activities;
31
32         c. Research, Innovation, and Technology Support- to fund research initiatives,
33         development of improved pili varieties, post-harvest technologies, and innovation
34         that enhance productivity, product quality, and market competitiveness; and
35
36         d. Promotion and Market Development- to support marketing, branding, trade
37         fairs, and other activities that increase domestic and international demand for pili
38         products. The Fund shall be sourced from appropriations in the General
39         Appropriations Act, grants, donations, or other lawful sources, and shall be
40         administered by the appropriate agency indicated herein. All expenditures shall
41         be made in a transparent and accountable manner to ensure that resources are
42         efficiently used for the sustainable growth of the pili industry, promotion of rural
43         livelihoods, and inclusive economic development.
44
45         SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations – The DA in collaboration
46   with DTI, DOST, DOT and the Sorsogon State University, shall issue and promulgate the
47   necessary rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this act.
