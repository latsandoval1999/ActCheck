15                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE
16
17          This bill seeks to convert Nisa Road in Iloilo City, presently classified as local
18   city road, into a national road.
19
20          The road spans a total length of 521 lineal meters and is located at the City
21   Proper District of Iloilo City. The Nisa Road is an important transportation artery in
22   Iloilo City, as it is used by motorists to reach Guimaras Ferry Terminal, which is the
23   main port used to reach Guimaras Island from Panay Island. The road is also used to
24   access the Iloilo City Hall, Camp Delgado, San Jose Church and the City Mall.
25   Declared heritage sites such as Plaza Libertad and Calle Real are also reached by
26   motorist through Nisa Road. The conversion of Nisa Road into a national road will
27   definitely benefit the local tourism industry and the numerous people accessing the
28   Guimaras Ferry Terminal on a daily basis.
29
30         I therefor highly recommend the approval of this measure.

56     “AN ACT CONVERTING NISA ROAD IN CITY PROPER DISTRICT,
57   ILOILO CITY INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
58                           THEREFOR”
59
60       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
61   Congress assembled:
62
63         SECTION 1. Nisa Road in City Proper District, Iloilo City is hereby converted into
64   a national road under the supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways.
65
66        SECTION 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include the
67   Department’s program the improvement, repair and maintenance of the said road, the
68   funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
69
70         SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
71   Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
