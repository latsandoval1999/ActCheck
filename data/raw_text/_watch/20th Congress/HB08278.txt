                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to recognize the importance of the Municipality of Jaro’s founding anniversary
by declaring December 10 of every year a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of
Jaro, Province of Leyte.

The Municipality of Jaro, Province of Leyte, holds a significant place in the history of the
province. As one of the established and culturally vibrant municipalities in Leyte, Jaro has
contributed meaningfully to the region’s development through its heritage, local traditions, and
the resilience of its people.

The founding anniversary of Jaro has long been recognized and celebrated by the local
community as an important historical milestone. The annual commemoration showcases not
only its cultural identity but also the unity of its residents in honoring their shared past and
aspirations for continued progress.

This bill seeks to institutionalize the founding anniversary of Jaro as a special non-working
holiday, enabling the community to celebrate fully, participate in activities, and strengthen
municipal pride. The formal declaration of this holiday will also support the promotion of
cultural programs and potential tourism-related initiatives that contribute to local development.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING DECEMBER 10 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-
WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF JARO, PROVINCE OF LEYTE,
IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. December 10 of every year is hereby declared as a special non-working
holiday in the Municipality of Jaro, Province of Leyte.

SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
