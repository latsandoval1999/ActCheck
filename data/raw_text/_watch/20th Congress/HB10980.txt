                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Philippine passport is a primary document that confirms an individual’s citizenship
and identity. As such, it is critical to ensure that only genuine Filipino citizens are granted the
privilege of holding a Philippine passport. However, the increasing cases of foreigners
fraudulently acquiring Philippine passports through fake birth certificates or improperly
documented Delayed Registration of Live Birth highlight a loophole in the current system.

        The Philippine Passport Law, as it stands, provides insufficient safeguards against the
fraudulent acquisition of passports by individuals who exploit the less stringent requirements
for applicants using Delayed Registration of Live Birth. This issue compromises the integrity
of our passport system and tarnishes the reputation of the Philippine government in the
international community.

       To address this concern, the proposed legislation seeks to amend the existing Philippine
Passport Law by mandating additional documentary requirements for applicants using Delayed
Registration of Live Birth.

        These additional safeguards aim to uphold the integrity of the Philippine passport and
ensure that it is issued only to bona fide Filipino citizens. By requiring independent attestations
and clearances from various agencies, this measure provides a robust mechanism to prevent
the misuse of delayed birth registrations for fraudulent purposes.

        Furthermore, this legislation is a proactive response to the growing global security
concerns and the need for stronger border control mechanisms. It aligns with the government’s
commitment to protecting the sanctity of Philippine citizenship and safeguarding the trust
placed in our passport by international authorities.

       In light of these considerations, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

  AN ACT AMENDING CERTAIN SECTIONS OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11982, AS
   AMENDED, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “NEW PHILIPPINE PASSPORT ACT”

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Section 5 of Republic Act No. 11983, otherwise known as “New Philippine
Passport Act”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

     “Section 5. Requirements for the Application and Issuance of a Passport. - The
     DFA Secretary, or a duly authorized consular official, shall issue a passport to an
     applicant who is a Filipino citizen and who has complied with the following
     requirements:
        (a) Personal appearance for biometric and biographic data capturing;
        (b) A duly accomplished application form;
        (c) Proof of citizenship as prescribed by relevant laws regarding the acquisition
        of Philippine citizenship which includes, but it not limited to:
           (1) For natural-born citizens, Certificate of Live Birth or Report of Birth, or
           Certificate of Foundling, whichever is applicable, authenticated by the
           Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA); PROVIDED, HOWEVER, THAT
           ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR THE FIRST-TIME
           APPLICANTS PRESENTING A LATE REGISTRATION OF THEIR
           CERTIFICATE OF LIVE BIRTH TO INCLUDE THE
           FOLLOWING:
              (i)      AFFIDAVIT OF TWO DISINTERESTED PERSONS
                       IDENTIFYING THE APPLICANT AS FILIPINO;
              (ii)     NBI CLEARANCE;
              (iii)    POLICE CLEARANCE; AND
              (iv)     BARANGAY CERTIFICATE.
           (2) For naturalized citizens, Certificate of Naturalization, Identification
           Certificate, Court Order granting naturalization, Decree of Naturalization,
           or copy of the law granting legislative naturalization;
           (3) XXX XXX XXX
           (4) XXX XXX XXX
        (d) Valid and sufficient proof of identity, foremost of which is the applicant's
        PhilID issued pursuant to Republic Act No. 11055 or the "Philippine
        Identification System Act", or competent proof of identity;
              XXX XXX XXX”
