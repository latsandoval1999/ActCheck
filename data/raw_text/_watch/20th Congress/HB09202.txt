                                  AN ACT
      DEFINING AND PENALIZING ESPIONAGE AND OTHER OFFENSES AGAINST
                            NATIONAL SECURITY


     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:


1           Section 1. This Act shall be known as the “New Anti-Espionage Act.”
2
3           Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is a declared policy of the State that paramount
4    consideration shall be given to its national sovereignty, territorial integrity, national interest,
5    and the right to self-determination. The State recognizes that espionage is inimical to its
6    sovereignty and democracy. Further, the fight against espionage, clandestine acquisition of
7    sensitive information and unlawful obtaining of classified information that affect the national
8    security or national defense requires a comprehensive approach. To this end, the State shall
9    adopt and implement effective measures to combat and prevent espionage to protect and
10   promote national security and interests. In the implementation of the policy stated above, the
11   State shall uphold the basic rights and fundamental liberties of the people as enshrined in the
12   Constitution.
13
14          Sec 3. Definition of Terms. — As used in this Act:
15              a) Classified matter refers to information or materials in whatever nature or form,
16                    that is designated as classified for national security purposes. It includes
17                    everything classified under any issuance or legislation concerning the security
