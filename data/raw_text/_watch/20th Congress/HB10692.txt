                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act No. 12290, signed into law on September 12, 2025, created
the Virology and Vaccine Institute of the Philippines (VIP) to serve as the
country's lead institution for virology research, vaccine development, and
diagnostic innovation. It was, and remains, a necessary response to the
inadequacies during the COVID-19 pandemic, which exposed the country's
ability to detect and understand emerging pathogens. However, the Institute's
promise will only be felt nationwide if its capacity reaches beyond the capital
region, and as presently structured, there is no guarantee that it will.

       The pandemic made painfully clear what provincial and rural communities
have long known: that the country's scientific infrastructure for pathogen
detection has always been concentrated in Metro Manila. Genome sequencing
capacity, in particular, was housed almost exclusively at the Philippine Genome
Center in UP Diliman and the Research Institute for Tropical Medicine in
Muntinlupa, forcing provinces to ship samples to the capital and absorb the
resulting delays before results could inform local response.

       The Department of Health itself acknowledged this bottleneck, moving in
2021 and 2022 to establish satellite genome sequencing laboratories in the
Visayas and Mindanao to ease the transportation burden on regional health
workers. That expansion was a step forward, but it remains partial. Many
provinces, particularly those in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas,
still have no meaningful local access to genome sequencing or virology diagnostic
capacity, and must rely on a small number of hub facilities far from where
outbreaks actually begin.

      The VIP, as currently mandated, does not explicitly address this
imbalance. Its flagship facility, still under construction, is planned for a five-
hectare site in New Clark City, Capas, Tarlac. While the site sits outside Metro
Manila proper, it remains firmly within the Luzon corridor already best served
by existing scientific infrastructure, offering little direct benefit to provinces in
the Visayas, Mindanao, and other far-flung regions that were hit hardest by
diagnostic delays during the pandemic. Left as is, the country risks building a
second generation of centralized capacity that repeats the very gap it was created
to close.

       This bill seeks to correct that by amending Republic Act No. 12290 to
explicitly mandate the VIP to establish regional and provincial satellite
laboratories and outreach programs, prioritizing regions and provinces currently
underserved by existing virology and genomic surveillance infrastructure. It
directs the Institute to partner with state universities and colleges, provincial
and regional hospitals, and local government units outside Metro Manila to build
local diagnostic and research capacity, train local scientists and health workers,
and ensure that when the next outbreak begins in a rural municipality rather
than the capital, the country's response does not depend on how quickly a
sample can reach Manila.

     It is for the foregoing premises that the approval of this Bill is earnestly
sought.

 AN ACT STRENGTHENING THE VIROLOGY AND VACCINE INSTITUTE OF
 THE PHILIPPINES BY MANDATING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF REGIONAL
     AND PROVINCIAL SATELLITE LABORATORIES AND OUTREACH
      PROGRAMS TO EXPAND ACCESS TO VIROLOGY RESEARCH,
   DIAGNOSTIC, AND GENOME SEQUENCING CAPACITY IN RURAL AND
 PROVINCIAL AREAS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO.
           12290, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Provincial VIPs
Act of 2026.”

      SECTION. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to protect
and promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness
among them. The State recognizes that science and technology is essential for
national development and progress.

      To this end, the State shall formulate and implement policies that shall
address current health issues, such as the quality and adequacy of health and
medical human resources, health institutions, and delivery of health services,
and shall give priority to research, the development and promotion of innovations
in both technology and services, the use of safe and indigenous health-promoting
practices, and their appropriate utilization by the health sector.

      In this regard, the State shall prioritize the establishment of an institute
that will conduct in-depth studies on viruses and their potential disease-causing
agents that affect people, plants, and animals, in order to provide the scientific
bases for the treatment of viral diseases, and for the formulation of policies that
shall prevent the transmission of these diseases within the community and
national context.

     SECTION. 3. Section 3 of Republic Act No. 12290 is hereby amended by
adding a new paragraph (i) to read as follows:

       "SEC. 3. Objectives. - This Act seeks to establish a Virology and Vaccine
Institute that shall focus on research and development to address pathogens and
potential disease-causing agents affecting plants, animals, and humans. Its
mandate shall include, but not limited to, the following:

      (a) to (h) [No change.]

      (I) Expanding access to virology research, diagnostic, and genome
sequencing capacity beyond Metro Manila and its adjacent provinces, by
establishing regional and provincial satellite laboratories and outreach programs
that prioritize regions, provinces, and geographically isolated and disadvantaged
areas (GIDAs) currently underserved by existing virology and genomic
surveillance infrastructure."

     SECTION. 4. Section 6 of Republic Act No. 12290 is hereby amended by
adding a new paragraph (r) to read as follows:

      "SEC. 6. Powers, Functions and Duties. - To accomplish its mandate, the
VIP shall have the following powers, functions, and duties:

      (a) to (q) [No change.]

      (r) establish, operate, and maintain regional and provincial satellite
laboratories and outreach programs for virology research, diagnostics, and
genome sequencing, in coordination with the Department of Health, the
Research Institute for Tropical Medicine, local government units, and state
universities and colleges outside Metro Manila, giving priority to regions and
provinces with limited or no existing access to such facilities."

  SECTION 5. A new Section 19-A is hereby inserted into Republic Act No.
12290, to read as follows:

    "SEC. 19-A. Regional and Provincial Satellite Laboratories and Outreach
Program. - In addition to its official site under Section 19 of this Act, the VIP
shall establish, within three (3) years from the effectivity of this amendatory Act,
at least one (1) satellite laboratory in each of the following island groups: Luzon
(outside the National Capital Region and Central Luzon), Visayas, and Mindanao,
with priority given to regions and provinces identified by the Department of
Health and the VIP Board as having limited or no existing access to virology
diagnostic or genome sequencing capacity.
   Satellite laboratories established under this Section shall, at minimum, be
capable of pathogen diagnostics and genome sequencing, and shall serve as
regional hubs to reduce sample transport time and turnaround delays for local
government units, provincial and regional hospitals, and rural health units
within their catchment areas.

   The VIP shall enter into partnerships with state universities and colleges,
provincial and regional hospitals, and local government units in the host regions
for the operation of these satellite laboratories, including the training of local
scientists, medical technologists, and health workers, and shall prioritize the
hiring of qualified local personnel in staffing these facilities.

   The VIP shall submit a report on the establishment, staffing, and operational
status of these satellite laboratories as part of its annual report under Section
24 of this Act."

      SECTION. 5. Section 16 of Republic Act No. 12290 is hereby amended to
read as follows:

       "SEC. 16. Promotion of Knowledge-transfer and Research in the Academe.
- The VIP shall promote the creation of hubs and programs for virology research
and development in the academe, particularly in state universities and colleges
(SUCs), giving priority to sucs located outside metro manila and in regions hosting
the satellite laboratories established under section 19-a of this act. To this end,
the VIP shall assist SUCs in the creation of virology courses and programs, open
internships, and scholarships for qualified students for higher education
institutions (HEIs), and collaborate with HEIs in the creation of virology research
centers.

      The CHED shall collaborate with the DOST in the promotion of HEI-VIP
partnerships."

      SECTION. 6. Section 26 of Republic Act No. 12290 is hereby amended to
read as follows:

       "SEC. 26. Appropriations. - The amount necessary to carry out the initial
implementation of this Act, including the establishment of regional and provincial
satellite laboratories under section 19-a of this act, shall be charged against the
current year's appropriations of the DOST. Thereafter, such amount needed for
its continued implementation shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act."

      SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DOST Secretary, in consultation with the
DOH, RITM, DA, DBM, CSC, and other relevant government agencies, shall
amend the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 12290 to
incorporate the provisions of this amendatory Act.

       SECTION 8. Separability Clause. - If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
force and effect.

       SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and
regulations or other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions
of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
