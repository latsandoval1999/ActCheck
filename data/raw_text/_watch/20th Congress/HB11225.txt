                            EXPLANATORY NOTE
       This Bill seeks the official statutory creation and recognition of Barangay
Pagalungan in the Municipality of Polomolok, Province of South Cotabato, so that
it may receive its lawful share in the National Tax Allotment, formerly referred to as
the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA). Similar measures were filed in previous
Congresses.
       Barangay Pagalungan came into existence through Sangguniang Panlalawigan
Ordinance No. 02, Series of 1995, as amended by Sangguniang Panlalawigan
Ordinance No. 04, Series of 1995. Its creation was ratified in a plebiscite conducted
by the Commission on Elections on 29 July 1995. It has since functioned as a regular
local government unit, serving its constituents, electing its officials, implementing
public service programs, and administering its assets and facilities.
       Because Barangay Pagalungan was created by a local government unit after
the effectivity of the Local Government Code of 1991 and was not created by
statute, its financial requirements have historically been borne by the local
government units concerned pursuant to the final proviso of Section 285 of
Republic Act No. 7160.
        Official recognition by Congress will place Barangay Pagalungan on equal
fiscal footing with other barangays and enable it to receive its just share of national
revenues. A regular National Tax Allotment will strengthen its capacity to provide
basic services, undertake development projects, and respond directly to the needs of
its residents.
      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this Bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:
        SECTION 1. Official Creation of Barangay Pagalungan. - Barangay
Pagalungan in the Municipality of Polomolok, Province of South Cotabato, is hereby
officially created as a barangay and shall retain its identity and existing territorial
boundaries. Its metes and bounds may be surveyed and specifically determined by
the appropriate government agencies, in coordination with the local government
units concerned.
       SEC. 2. Public Infrastructure, Assets, and Facilities. - All public
infrastructure, assets, and facilities owned by or belonging to Barangay Pagalungan,
Municipality of Polomolok, Province of South Cotabato, shall be retained by it.
      SEC. 3. Incumbent Barangay Officials. - The incumbent barangay officials
of Barangay Pagalungan shall continue to hold office until such time that their
successors have been duly elected and qualified.
       SEC. 4. National Tax Allotment Share. - Barangay Pagalungan shall be
entitled to its corresponding share in the National Tax Allotment, formerly referred
to as the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA), in accordance with Section 285 of
Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, and other applicable laws, rules, and
regulations.
    SEC. 5. Plebiscite. - The plebiscite conducted and supervised by the
Commission on Elections (COMELEC) on 29 July 1995, pursuant to Provincial
Ordinance No. 02, Series of 1995, as amended by Provincial Ordinance No. 04,
Series of 1995, which ratified the creation of Barangay Pagalungan, shall serve as
substantial compliance with the plebiscite requirement required by law.
      SEC. 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
      Approved.
