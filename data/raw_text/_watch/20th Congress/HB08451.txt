                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE
The Municipality of Sta. Ignacia in the Province of Tarlac marks its foundation anniversary every
January 27. Historically, the town was established as a civil municipality through a superior decree
dated May 6, 1874, which was later approved by a royal decree on August 13, 1880. After a period
of annexation to the town of Camiling in 1903, the people successfully lobbied for independence,
leading to the inauguration of its first municipal council on January 1, 1914.


Today, the Local Government Unit of Santa Ignacia and its citizens observe late January as a
period of significant historical and cultural importance, with the 112th Founding Anniversary most
recently celebrated in 2026.


The proposed bill seeks to:
Formalize the Commemoration: Declare January 27 as a special non-working holiday to allow
residents to participate in ceremonies and activities honoring their town's heritage.


Foster Local Pride: Provide a dedicated day for the community to reflect on the historical journey
and "intrepid" spirit of the early settlers who fought for the town's autonomy.


Promote Cultural Tourism: Support the municipality’s annual festivities, including the "Festival
of Lights," which showcases its agricultural achievements and community spirit.
By institutionalizing this holiday, we ensure that the history and distinct identity of Sta. Ignacia
are preserved and celebrated by future generations of Kailyans.


In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
