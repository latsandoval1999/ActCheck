                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The rising cost of living in the Philippines is no longer a statistic metric. It
is a daily reality for millions of Filipinos. Accordingly, inflation has shown a
persistent upward trend wherein housing, utilities, good, and services reach
exacerbating levels. Our inflation rate rose to 2.0% in January 2026, up from
1.8% in December 2025.1 The continuing costs of services and household
maintenance are constantly increasing due to global supply chain pressures and
high energy costs. Further, recent data provided by the Bangko Sentral ng
Pilipinas (BSP) showed that the overall consumer price index (CPI) as of January

        For ordinary Filipinos, the substantial reduction of the purchasing power
of the peso equates to reduced take-home pay. As restricted by the current
threshold in the TRAIN Law, such circumstances put the public in worrisome
financial burden. Therefore, it is only apt to provide financial relief for working
Filipinos.


        This measure seeks to amend Sections 24 and 32 of Republic Act No. 8424,
otherwise known as the Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended by Republic
Act No. 10963, or the TRAIN Law. It shall increase the annual income tax
exemption from the current P250,000.00 to P400,000.00, as well as increasing
the cap of the tax-free bonuses from P90,000.00 to P150,000.00.


        In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.
