                          AN ACT
    PROVIDING FOR NURSES' APPRECIATION THROUGH RAISED
  SALARIES (NARS) BY UPGRADING THE MINIMUM SALARY GRADE
 LEVEL OF ALL PUBLIC NURSES FROM SALARY GRADE 15 TO 16 IN
 ALL PUBLIC HOSPITALS AND OTHER GOVERNMENT INSTITUTIONS,
AND INCREASING THE SALARY RATE OF ALL PRIVATE NURSES IN ALL
        PRIVATE HOSPITALS AND HEALTH INSTITUTIONS

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Philippines is one of the largest migrant-sending countries in the
world and among the world’s largest suppliers of nurses. Over the years,
Filipino nurses have continuously migrated in search of greener pastures
overseas, while those who remain in the country often opt to bring their
services to other relatively lucrative sectors which do not involve the practice
of their profession.

      Republic Act No. 9173, otherwise known as the Philippine Nursing Act
of 2002, established Salary Grade 15 as the minimum base pay for nurses in
public health institutions. However, in light of rising living costs and the
increasing demands of the nursing profession, there is a need to further
improve their compensation by upgrading the minimum salary grade to Salary
Grade 16.

      The country's continuing nursing workforce challenges underscore the
urgency of this measure. According to the Second Congressional Commission
on Education (EDCOM II), the Philippines needs an additional 196,000 nurses
to meet its healthcare workforce requirements. Meanwhile, a 2023
Department of Health survey revealed that 48.3 percent of surveyed
healthcare workers had considered leaving their respective facilities,
highlighting concerns over      compensation,    working   conditions,   and
professional opportunities.

      The proposed measure seeks to upgrade the minimum salary grade of
nurses in public hospitals and other government institutions from Salary
Grade 15 to Salary Grade 16, while providing for increased salary rates for
nurses employed in private hospitals and healthcare institutions. Through
more competitive and equitable compensation, the measure aims to recognize
the contributions of nurses, strengthen workforce retention, and encourage
more nursing professionals to serve in the country.

      Investing in the welfare of nurses is an investment in the strength and
sustainability of the Philippine healthcare system.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
    PROVIDING FOR NURSES' APPRECIATION THROUGH RAISED
  SALARIES (NARS) BY UPGRADING THE MINIMUM SALARY GRADE
 LEVEL OF ALL PUBLIC NURSES FROM SALARY GRADE 15 TO 16 IN

AND INCREASING THE SALARY RATE OF ALL PRIVATE NURSES IN ALL
        PRIVATE HOSPITALS AND HEALTH INSTITUTIONS

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as “Nurses Appreciation
through Raised Salaries (NARS) Act”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the Government’s concern and
responsibility to provide adequate remuneration and benefits of health
workers to enhance the general welfare and to attract and retain its rightful
share of talents with the end in view of fulfilling the state policy of providing
essential health services to all Filipinos.

SECTION 3. Coverage. – This Act shall cover all government-compensated
nurses working in public hospitals and other government institutions, and all
private nurses in all private health facilities and institutions.

SECTION 4. Upgrading the Minimum Salary Grade Level for all Public
Nurses. – The present minimum salary grade level of a public nurse in
hospitals and medical facilities shall be upgraded from Grade 15 to Grade 16;
Provided, however, that the salary upgrading shall be differentiated in
accordance with the qualifications and length of service rendered by public
nurses and shall not be prejudiced by across-the-board salary adjustments.

SECTION 5. Increasing the Salary Rate of Private Nurses. – The present
minimum monthly salary rate for private nurses shall be upgraded to Thirty
Thousand Pesos (Php 30,000). Salaries of those occupying higher positions
shall be adjusted accordingly.

SECTION 6. Budget Required. – The Department of Health (DOH) shall come
up with a specific programmed budget needed to cover the expenses for the
upgrading in salary levels for all the corresponding nurses for a period of at
least five years to allow the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) to
make the necessary budgetary adjustments to facilitate the smooth
implementation of this Act.

SECTION 7. Appropriation. – The amount necessary to implement the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act
(GAA) for the year following the approval of this Act. Initial funds required for
the implementation of this Act shall be sourced from the General
Appropriations Act Miscellaneous Personnel Benefit Fund and other sources
from the GAA Miscellaneous Personnel Benefit Fund and other possible
sources that may be determined by the Office of the President. The funds for
private health facilities shall be included in the corporate budget.

SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of
Health and the Department of Budget and Management shall promulgate the
necessary rules and regulations for the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 9. Penalty. – A fine of not less than Five Hundred Thousand Pesos
(P500,000) and/or imprisonment of not less than one (1) year nor more than
two (2) years shall be imposed upon any person violating any provision of this
Act.

Provided That, for government agencies, juridical persons, including local
government units or any other entity, the head of the agency and Board of
Directors, or executive officials of the agency, shall assume full responsibility
for violations of this Act.

SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. – All provisions of laws, orders, decrees,
including rules and regulations inconsistent herewith, are hereby repealed
and/or modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act shall
be held unconstitutional or invalid, other provisions hereof which are not
affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
