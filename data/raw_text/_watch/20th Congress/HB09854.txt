                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

The right to health is a fundamental constitutional guarantee. Article II, Section
15 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution explicitly declares that the State shall
protect and promote the people's right to health and instill health consciousness
among them. This mandate was significantly advanced with the enactment of
Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise known as the Universal Health Care (UHC)
Act, which aims to realize universal health coverage for all Filipinos progressively.

The UHC Act's full implementation is hindered by a fragmented health
information infrastructure. Patient records are often filed in various hospitals and
health units, forcing patients to carry physical copies of their medical history. In
emergencies, the lack of immediate access to this information can result in
diagnostic errors, delayed treatment, and preventable complications.

Furthermore, the absence of a unified system results in redundant medical tests,
bureaucratic inefficiencies, and a lack of real-time, accurate public health data
necessary for proactive government epidemiologic interventions.
This bill seeks to address these gaps by establishing the National Electronic
Health Record System (NEHRS).

The NEHRS will serve as a secure, centralized, and interoperable digital platform
that aggregates a patient’s medical history across all healthcare providers
nationwide.

The modernization of our healthcare system through digital integration is no
longer a luxury; it is an urgent public health necessity.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORD
      SYSTEM TO ENSURE CONTINUITY OF CARE, IMPROVE MEDICAL
    EFFICIENCY, AND PROMOTE UNIVERSAL HEALTH ACCESS FOR ALL
                                   FILIPINOS
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the This Act shall be
known as the "National Electronic Health Record System (NEHRS) Act."

       Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right to health of all Filipinos. Towards this end, the State shall adopt
a digital-first approach to healthcare administration by establishing a unified,
secure, and interoperable National Electronic Health Record System (NEHRS) to
streamline health services, optimize clinical outcomes, reduce healthcare costs,
and guarantee the seamless continuity of patient care nationwide.

      Sec. 3. Objectives – This Act aims to achieve the following:

   (a) Establish a single, secure electronic registry for the health records of all
       Philippine citizens;

   (b) Mandate interoperability standards among all public and private
       healthcare facilities;

   (c) Protect patient data privacy while allowing seamless, authorized clinical
       data sharing; and

   (d) Provide real-time, aggregated data to health authorities for public health
       monitoring and syndromic surveillance.
      Sec. 4. Definition of Terms - As used in this Act:

   (a) Electronic Health Record (EHR) refers to a digital version of a patient’s
      medical history, maintained by the provider over time, including clinical
      data, progress notes, problems, medications, vital signs, past medical
      history, immunizations, laboratory data, and radiology reports.

   (b) Interoperabilityrefers to the ability of different information systems,
      devices, and software applications to access, exchange, integrate, and
      cooperatively use data in a coordinated manner.

   (c) Healthcare Provider refers to any health facility, hospital, clinic, rural
      health unit (RHU), barangay health station, sanitarium, or healthcare
      professional licensed to provide medical services.


      Sec. 5. Creation of the National Electronic Health Record System (NEHRS). –
There is hereby established a National Electronic Health Record System (NEHRS),
which shall serve as the primary centralized digital infrastructure for managing
patient health information in the Philippines.

      The Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with the Department of
Information and Communications Technology (DICT) and the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), shall design, implement, maintain, and
oversee the NEHRS architecture.

      Sec. 6. Mandatory Participation and Interoperability.- All public and private
healthcare providers operating within the Philippines shall implement electronic
medical record systems that are fully compatible and interoperable with the
NEHRS platform.

   (a) All government-owned hospitals, rural health units (RHUs), and primary
       care facilities shall be migrated to the NEHRS within three (3) years from
       the effectivity of this Act.

   (b) Private healthcare providers shall fully integrate their systems with the
       NEHRS within five (5) years from the effectivity of this Act.

      Compliance with the NEHRS integration shall be a mandatory prerequisite
for the renewal of Department of Health (DOH) facility licenses and PhilHealth
accreditations.

      Sec. 7. Data Privacy and Security Guarantees.— The NEHRS shall strictly
comply with the provisions of Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the
"Data Privacy Act of 2012."
   •   Patient Ownership: Patients shall own their electronic health records. Their
       explicit consent shall be required for the sharing of records, except in life-
       threatening emergencies where consent cannot be practically obtained.

   •   Access Control: Only licensed and authorized healthcare practitioners
       directly involved in the patient's care shall have access to specific clinical
       records.

   •   Cybersecurity Standard: The DICT and DOH shall employ state-of-the-art
       encryption protocols, multi-factor authentication, and blockchain or
       equivalent immutable ledger systems to prevent unauthorized data
       breaches.

       Sec. 8. Public Health Surveillance and Anonymization. - The DOH shall
utilize aggregated, fully anonymized data from the NEHRS for public health
planning, epidemiological research, and emergency response management.
Under no circumstances shall personally identifiable information (PII) be released
for research or statistical purposes without explicit patient consent.

       Sec. 9. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to cover the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s
appropriations of the DOH and DICT. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary
for its continued operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act (GAA)

      Sec. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DOH, in joint consultation with the DICT,
PhilHealth, the National Privacy Commission (NPC), and relevant stakeholders
from the public and private medical sectors, shall promulgate the necessary rules
and regulations for its effective implementation.

       Sec. 11. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not
affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

      Sec. 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders,
presidential issuances, and other administrative rules and regulations or parts
thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.

      Sec. 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
