                          AN ACT
GRANTING HAZARD PAY TO LOCAL GOVERNMENT ENVIRONMENT AND
NATURAL RESOURCES PERSONNEL EXPOSED TO OCCUPATIONAL HAZARDS
IN THE PERFORMANCE OF THEIR DUTIES, AND PROVIDING FUNDS
THEREFOR
                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE
       Local government environment and natural resources personnel serve at the frontlines
of environmental protection, conservation, solid waste management, pollution control, forest
and watershed protection, environmental enforcement, and other environmental programs of
local government units.

       The nature of their work regularly exposes some of these personnel to occupational
hazards, including extreme weather, contaminated waste, polluted environments, dangerous
terrain, poisonous plants, venomous animals, and other conditions that may endanger their
health and safety.

       Recognizing these risks, the Philippine League of Local Environment and Natural
Resources Officers, Inc. (PLLENRO-SOC), South Cotabato Chapter, through Resolution No.
01, Series of 2026, approved on July 26, 2026, requested the Representatives of the Province
of South Cotabato to sponsor legislation granting hazard pay to qualified environmental
workers regularly exposed to occupational hazards in the performance of their official duties.

      While existing government compensation rules provide hazard duty pay under particular
circumstances, there is a need for a specific statutory benefit for local environmental personnel
who are actually exposed to occupational hazards in the performance of their duties.

       This measure therefore seeks to recognize the risks borne by local environmental
personnel by granting hazard pay when they are actually exposed to occupational hazards in
the performance of their official duties.
In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Local Environmental
Workers Hazard Pay Act.”

       SEC. 2. Coverage. — All personnel employed or engaged by provincial, city, or
municipal governments whose principal duties involve environmental and natural resources
protection, conservation, management, regulation, enforcement, or related field operations
shall be entitled to hazard pay when actually exposed to occupational hazards in the
performance of their official duties.

      For purposes of this Act, occupational hazards shall include exposure to contaminated
or hazardous waste, polluted or disease-prone environments, dangerous terrain, extreme
weather conditions, hazardous substances, poisonous plants, venomous or dangerous animals,
and other comparable risks to health or safety.

       SEC. 3. Hazard Pay. — Covered personnel shall be entitled to hazard pay at such rates
as may be determined by the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), in coordination
with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) and the Department of
Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), taking into consideration the nature, duration,
and degree of occupational exposure.

       SEC. 4. Prohibition Against Double Payment. — Personnel already receiving hazard
pay or an equivalent allowance under another law, rule, or regulation for the same occupational
hazard and for the same period shall not receive hazard pay under this Act for such hazard and
period.
       SEC. 5. Funding. — The amounts necessary for the implementation of this Act shall
be charged against the respective funds of the local government units concerned, subject to
applicable budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

       SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DBM, in coordination with the DILG and DENR, shall issue the
rules and regulations necessary for its implementation.

       SEC. 7. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. — All laws, rules, regulations, and issuances inconsistent
with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 9. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
