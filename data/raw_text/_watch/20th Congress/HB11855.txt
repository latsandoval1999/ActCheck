EXPLANATORY NOTE

The tricycle is one of the primary means of transportation in the country, providing
efficient and affordable transportation services, especially in rural areas and in
communities not reached by larger public utility vehicles. Beyond its role in mobility, it
has become a cultural icon and a source of livelihood for many Filipino families.

According to the 2022 Annual Report of the Land Transportation Office (LTO),
there are more than 1.5 million registered tricycles in the country.! Among all regions,
Central Luzon (Region III) accounted for the highest number of registered tricycles as of
December 2022 at around 283,000 units.2 Within the region, the City of Cabanatuan in
Nueva Ecija stands out, with around 30,000 tricycles currently operating within its
jurisdiction.’ Tricycle operation in the city has become the source of livelihood for around
10,000 families, which is why the city has long been popularly known as the “Tricycle
Capital of the Philippines.”

This bill seeks to formally declare the City of Cabanatuan as the “Tricycle Capital
of the Philippines” in recognition of the city’s distinct identity and the vital contribution
of its tricycle drivers and operators to local transportation and the economy. It also
mandates the Department of Tourism to feature and promote the city as such in its
regional and national promotion programs, which will help attract visitors and boost local
tourism and commerce.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate adoption of this measure is earnestly

requested.

AN ACT
DECLARING THE CITY OF CABANATUAN IN THE PROVINCE OF
NUEVA ECIJA AS THE “TRICYCLE CAPITAL OF THE PHILIPPINES”

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

Section 1. The City of Cabanatuan in the Province of Nueva Ecija is hereby
declared the “Tricycle Capital of the Philippines” in recognition of its significant
contribution to the local transportation sector of the country and to the livelihood
of thousands of Filipino families who depend on the tricycle industry.

Sec. 2. The Department of Tourism shall feature and promote the City of
Cabanatuan as the “Tricycle Capital of the Philippines” in all its regional and
national promotion programs.

Sec. 3. All laws, rules, regulations, presidential decrees, and other
issuances inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed
repealed or amended accordingly.

Sec. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
