                         AN ACT
EXTENDING THE VALIDITY OF THE AUTHORIZATION TO
SOLEMNIZE MARRIAGE ISSUED TO PRIESTS, MINISTERS, IMAMS,
OR RABBIS, FROM A PERIOD OF THREE (3) YEARS TO FIVE (5)
YEARS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6514, AND
FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This proposed measure aims to 1) extend the validity of the authorization to
solemnize marriages from three (3) years to five (5) years; 2) ensure the inclusion of
imams among the religious leaders duly authorized to perform marriage rites; and 3)
institutionalize a seminar requirement for priests, ministers, imams, and rabbis as a
prerequisite to the renewal of their authorization.

       Marriage is a vital social institution that carries profound legal, moral, and
cultural significance. The authority to solemnize marriages granted to priests,
ministers, and rabbis ensures that this institution is respected, regulated, and properly
recorded under the law.

       Under Republic Act No. 6514, the authorization granted to priests, ministers,
rabbis, and other religious officers to solemnize marriages is valid for a period of
three (3) years. After the expiration of this period, they are required to renew their

                                                                                          1
 authorization with the appropriate government authority. However, the limited
 validity period poses administrative challenges, resulting in frequent renewals,
 additional documentary requirements, and undue burden both on the religious
 sectors and on the concerned government agencies.

        By extending the period of validity, the measure promotes administrative
 efficiency and reduces bureaucratic workload. Meanwhile, the inclusion of imams
 affirms the State’s recognition of the diverse religious practices in the Philippines
 and ensures inclusivity in the exercise of religious functions. Finally, the
 requirement to undergo a seminar prior to renewal reinforces accountability and
 ensures that solemnizing officers remain updated with existing civil laws and
 regulations relating to marriage, family relations, and the registration of vital events.

       The passage of this bill strikes a vital balance between enhancing
 administrative efficiency and upholding the professional and ethical standards of
 solemnizing officers, while upholding the constitutional guarantee of equal respect
 and recognition for all religions.

                         AN ACT

OR RABBIS, FROM A PERIOD OF THREE (3) YEARS TO FIVE (5)
YEARS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6514, AND
FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. Section 1 of Republic Act No. 6514 is hereby amended to read
as follows:


      "Section 1. Article 95 of Republic Act Numbered Three Hundred and eighty-
      six is hereby amended to read as follows:"



                                                                                1
"Art 95. The public official in charge of registration of priests, ministers,
IMAMS or rabbis with the approval of the proper head of department, is
hereby authorized to prepare the necessary forms and to promulgate rules and
regulations for the purpose of enforcing the provisions of this Title: provided,
that the authorization to solemnize marriages shall be valid for a period of
[three] FIVE years, shall expire on the thirty-first day of December of every
[third] FIFTH year, and shall be renewable within the said month of
December: provided, further, that the authorizations to solemnize marriage
issued prior to and valid on the date of the approval of this amendatory Act
shall continue to be valid until the thirty-first day of December of the year
when this amendatory Act takes effect, any regulation to the contrary
notwithstanding: provided, finally, that the words "my authority to solemnize
marriage expires on December 31, 19_" shall be indicated in all marriage
contracts just below the title of the person so authorized to solemnize
marriages."


"Priest or ministers, IMAMS, or rabbis belonging to one diocese, religious
order, congregation, or sect, may apply for authorization, through their bishop,
head of religious order or congregation chief of ministers, or duly authorized
representative."


“PRIOR TO THE EXPIRATION OF THEIR AUTHORITY TO
SOLEMNIZE MARRIAGES, PRIESTS, MINISTERS, IMAMS, AND
RABBIS SHALL FIRST ATTEND A SEMINAR AS A REQUIREMENT
FOR THE RENEWAL OF THEIR AUTHORIZATION.”




                                                                              2
      "The public official who is in charge of registration may also by regulation fix
      and collect fees for the authorization to solemnize marriage."


      SECTION 2. Separability Clause. – Should any provision of this Act be
declared invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue to be valid and subsisting.


      SECTION 3. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees, issuances, rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


      SECTION 4. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days
following its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
