                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Atherosclerosis is a silent, preventable disease that is quietly killing more
Filipinos than any other condition in the country.

       This bill declares the third week of February of every year as “National
Atherosclerosis Awareness Week.” It aligns the observance with global initiatives led
by the American Heart Association and local initiatives by the University of the
Philippines National Institutes of Health.

       Heart disease remains the leading cause of death in the Philippines. Ischaemic
heart disease, a direct consequence of atherosclerosis, caused twenty-three thousand
four hundred forty-eight (23,448) deaths in the first quarter of 2026 alone. This figure
represents twenty-one point two percent (21.2%) of all deaths recorded nationwide
during that period, according to provisional data from the Philippine Statistics
Authority. Atherosclerosis has held this position among the country’s top causes of
death since at least 2013.1

       Public awareness has not kept pace with this burden. Nearly half of Filipino
adults, forty-seven point two percent (47.2%), have borderline to high total
cholesterol levels. A similarly high share, forty-seven point five percent (47.5%),
carry borderline to high levels of LDL or “bad” cholesterol. Medical experts note that
blood tests remain the only reliable way to detect these unhealthy cholesterol levels,
yet routine screening is not standard practice for most Filipino adults. Left
undetected, elevated bad cholesterol clogs the arteries and leads to severe
complications, including stroke and heart attack.2

       The bill mandates a public education and awareness campaign on lipid
disorders and atherosclerosis. It encourages routine cholesterol screening, and it
reinforces early intervention before atherosclerosis progresses to a cardiac event. The

                          AN ACT
DECLARING THE THIRD WEEK OF FEBRUARY OF EVERY YEAR AS
“NATIONAL ATHEROSCLEROSIS AWARENESS WEEK”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National Atherosclerosis
Awareness Week”.

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote the prevention, management, and control of lipid disorders and
atherosclerosis. Toward this end, the State shall endeavor to reduce the incidence of
cardiovascular diseases through sustained public education and awareness
programs.

        SEC. 3. National Atherosclerosis Awareness Week. – The third week of February
of every year is hereby declared as “National Atherosclerosis Awareness Week” to
align with global initiatives led by the American Heart Association and local
initiatives by the University of the Philippines National Institutes of Health in
promoting heart health and cholesterol awareness.

       SEC. 4. Public Education and Awareness Program. – To ensure the meaningful
observance of the National Atherosclerosis Awareness Week, a comprehensive
public education and awareness campaign shall be implemented. This program shall
focus on disseminating information on lipid disorders and atherosclerosis, and their
impact on public health and well-being.

        SEC. 5. Implementing Agency – The Philippine Lipid and Atherosclerosis
Society, Inc. (PLAS), in coordination with relevant non-governmental organizations
and patient advocacy groups, shall serve as the lead agency in the implementation
of this Act.
        SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
