                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to strengthen the lateral entry program of the Philippine
National Police (PNP). It amends Section 33 of Republic Act (RA) No. 6975,
otherwise known as the “Department of the Interior and Local Government Act of 1990”,
and expands the professional qualifications eligible for appointment to line and
technical services, and establishes appropriate entry ranks.

       RA No. 6975 only identifies certain professions for lateral entry into the PNP
technical services despite the growing operational requirements of a modern police
organization. This bill includes professionals in fields such as physical and sports
therapy, psychology, nutrition and dietetics, and other technical professions. It also
recognizes qualified PNP personnel and creates a more structured pathway for
career advancement and organizational development.

       A modern police organization requires more than traditional law enforcement
capabilities. It demands specialized knowledge and diverse expertise to effectively
respond to emerging threats and increasingly complex public safety concerns. A
broader and more clearly defined lateral entry system shall enable the PNP to draw
from a wider pool of qualified professionals. This shall maximize the expertise of its
existing personnel and strengthens its capacity to provide an avenue for professional
development within the service.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
TO INCLUDE OTHER BACCALAUREATE DEGREES TO THE LATERAL
ENTRY PROGRAM FOR LINE AND TECHNICAL OFFICERS, AMENDING
FOR THIS PURPOSE SECTION 33 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6975, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS THE “DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL
GOVERNMENT ACT OF 1990”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Section 33 of Republic Act (RA) No. 6975, otherwise known as the
“Department of the Interior and Local Government Act of 1990”, is hereby amended to
read as follows:

             “Section 33. Lateral Entry of Officers into the PNP. – In general, all
      original appointments of commissioned officers in the PNP shall
      commence with the rank of [inspector] POLICE LIEUTENANT, to
      include all those with highly technical qualifications applying for the
      PNP technical services, such as, BUT NOT LIMITED TO: dentists,
      optometrists, PHYSICAL THERAPISTS, SPORTS THERAPISTS,
      PSYCHOLOGISTS,                PSYCHOMETRICIANS,                     nurses,
      NUTRITIONISTS              AND           DIETITIANS,             MEDICAL

      ENGINEERS, COMPUTER ENGINEERS, CRIMINOLOGISTS,
      DATA ACTUARIALS, ELECTRICAL ENGINEERS, ELECTRONICS
      ENGINEERS, MECHANICAL ENGINEERS, METALLURGICAL
      ENGINEERS, GUIDANCE AND COUNSELING, SOCIAL
      WORKERS,        STATISTICIANS,           VETERINARY          MEDICINES,
      INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY PROFESSIONALS OTHER
      TECHNICAL PROFESSIONS, and graduates of forensic sciences.

          “THE ABOVEMENTIONED PROFESSIONALS SHALL BE
