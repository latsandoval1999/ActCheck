14                                         EXPLANATORY NOTE
15
16   Western Visayas (Region VI) serves as one of the primary agricultural and food baskets of the
17   Philippines, producing substantial yields of rice, sugar, seafood, livestock, poultry, and high-value
18   crops across its six provinces—Aklan, Antique, Capiz, Guimaras, Iloilo, and Negros Occidental.
19   However, the region faces recurring challenges in post-harvest handling, supply chain inefficiencies,
20   market volatility, and inadequate cold-chain logistics, which result in substantial post-harvest losses
21   and depressed incomes for small farmers and fisherfolk.
22
23   Iloilo City, strategically positioned as the economic, administrative, and logistical hub of Region VI,
24   possesses the necessary infrastructure, port facilities, and inter-island connectivity to host a
25   centralized agri-fishery distribution system. Establishing a modern Regional Food Hub in Iloilo
26   City will consolidate agricultural produce from across Western Visayas, streamline farm-to-market
27   trade, reduce reliance on unnecessary intermediaries, and ensure stable, affordable food supplies for
28   consumers.
29
30   Furthermore, this initiative aligns with national programs led by the Department of Agriculture
31   (DA) to deploy strategic mega food hubs and strengthen cold storage, processing, and distribution
32   networks across key regions.
33
34   In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 74       AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE WESTERN VISAYAS REGIONAL FOOD HUB IN
 75        ILOILO CITY, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, PROVIDING AN
 76       IMPLEMENTING FRAMEWORK, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
 77
 78         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
 79   assembled:
 80
 81   SECTION 1. Short Title.
 82
 83          This Act shall be known as the "Western Visayas Regional Food Hub Act."
 84
 85   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.
 86
 87           It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote agricultural productivity, ensure
 88   regional food security, increase the income of agricultural workers, and modernize the post-harvest
 89   supply chain. To achieve these ends, the State shall establish state-of-the-art regional food hubs that
 90   integrate cold-chain infrastructure, modern logistics, trading platforms, quality control, and direct
 91   producer-to-market channels.
 92
 93   SECTION 3. Creation and Establishment of the Regional Food Hub.
 94
 95           There is hereby established a modern agro-industrial and logistical complex to be known as
 96   the Western Visayas Regional Food Hub (WVRFH), located in a strategically designated area
 97   within Iloilo City.
 98
 99           The WVRFH shall serve as the central aggregation, processing, cold storage, warehousing,
100   testing, and wholesale trade terminal for agricultural, aquatic, livestock, and processed food
101   products sourced from across Region VI.
102
103   SECTION 4. Objectives and Functions of the WVRFH.
104
105          The WVRFH shall fulfill the following core objectives and operational functions:
106
107      1. Aggregation & Logistics: Provide a centralized hub for small farmers, fisherfolk, agri-
108         cooperatives, and commercial producers to aggregate produce efficiently.
109
110      2. Cold-Chain & Post-Harvest Preservation: Equip the hub with cold storage units, blast
111         freezers, ambient warehouses, and modern packing facilities to minimize post-harvest losses.
112
113      3. Market Access & Price Stabilization: Facilitate direct trading between agricultural
114         producers and institutional buyers, exporters, retail networks, and commercial
115         establishments, minimizing multi-tiered middleman markups.
116
117      4. Quality Control & Standard Compliance: Establish testing and inspection facilities in
118         coordination with the Bureau of Plant Industry (BPI), Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic
119         Resources (BFAR), Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI), and the Food and Drug
120         Administration (FDA) to enforce food safety and quality standards.
121
122   SECTION 5. Appropriations.
123
124          The amount necessary for the initial construction, land acquisition, facility development,
125   and operationalization of the Western Visayas Regional Food Hub shall be included in the annual
126   General Appropriations Act (GAA) under the budget of the Department of Agriculture.
127
128          Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued operation, maintenance, and
129   expansion shall be included in the annual GAA.
130
131   SECTION 6. Operational Guidelines and Incentives.
132
133      1. Registered agricultural cooperatives and agrarian reform beneficiaries' organizations
134         (ARBOs) participating in the WVRFH shall be entitled to preferential stall fees, logistics
135         subsidies, and technical support services.
136
137      2. The DA, in coordination with DTI and TESDA, shall conduct capacity-building programs
138         for accredited farmers and fisherfolk on post-harvest handling, food safety compliance, and
139         financial management.
140
141   SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).
142
143          Within ninety (90) days from the approval of this Act, the Department of Agriculture, in
144   consultation with the City Government of Iloilo, DTI, DOST, and relevant stakeholder groups, shall
145   formulate and issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.
146
147   SECTION 8. Separability Clause.
148
149          If any provision or part of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining
150   provisions or parts not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.
151
152   SECTION 9. Repealing Clause.
153
154          All laws, executive orders, administrative orders, rules, or regulations inconsistent with the
155   provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
156
157   SECTION 10. Effectivity.
158
159         This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a
160   newspaper of general circulation.
