                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to expand the coverage and duration of paternity leave
from seven (7) to thirty (30) working days. It amends Republic Act (RA) No.
8187, otherwise known as the “Paternity Leave Act of 1996”, extends the
eligibility to all male employees regardless of marital status, and recognizes
partners in cohabiting arrangements.

      Despite the enactment of RA No. 8187, the existing seven-day leave
period is often insufficient for fathers and their partners to provide
meaningful support during childbirth and postpartum recovery. Many fathers
are compelled to immediately return to work despite the physical, emotional,
and financial demands faced by mothers and newborn children during the
crucial early stages of recovery and caregiving.

      According to the United Nations Children’s Fund (UNICEF), fathers
who actively participate in early caregiving help reduce maternal stress and
improve child well-being1. Likewise, the International Labour Organization
(ILO) emphasized that paternity leave policies promote gender equality in
caregiving responsibilities and create more family-supportive workplaces2.

      This proposed measure provides a more responsive, inclusive, and
progressive paternity leave policy that promotes shared parenting
responsibilities and supports the welfare of Filipino families. It recognizes the
important role of fathers and partners in ensuring a safe, healthy, and
supportive environment for both mother and child.

                          AN ACT
EXPANDING THE SCOPE OF AND PROVIDING FOR EXTENDED
PATERNITY LEAVE, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT
NO. 8187, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “PATERNITY LEAVE ACT OF
1996”, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Section 2 of Republic Act No. 8187, otherwise known as the
“Paternity Leave Act of 1996”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

            “Section 4. PATERNITY LEAVE. – Notwithstanding any
      law, rules, and regulations to the contrary, every [married] male
      employee in the private and public sectors, REGARDLESS OF
      EMPLOYMENT STATUS, shall be entitled to a paternity leave of
      [seven (7) days] THIRTY (30) WORKING DAYS with full pay
      for the first four (4) deliveries of [the legitimate spouse] HIS
      PARTNER with whom he is cohabiting. The male employee
      applying for paternity leave shall notify his employer of the
      pregnancy of his [legitimate spouse] PARTNER and the expected
      date of such delivery. For purposes of this Act, delivery shall
      include childbirth, EMERGENCY TERMINATION OF
      PREGNANCY, or any miscarriage.”

      SEC. 2. Section 3 of the same Act is hereby removed.

       SEC. 3. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in
full force and effect.
