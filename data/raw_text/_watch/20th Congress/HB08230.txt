                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to establish that identification cards (IDs) issued to persons with
permanent disabilities shall have lifetime validity, while IDs for those with temporary disabilities
shall be valid for five (5) years and be subject to renewal after medical reassessment. It also
centralizes the issuance process through the Persons with Disability Affairs Offices (PDAO) to
ensure standardization across all local government units.

         This measure recognizes the growing population of persons with disabilities (PWDs) and
the systemic barriers they continue to face in accessing social services and economic
opportunities. As of June 20251, the National Council for Disability Affairs (NCDA) reported
more than 2.5 Million registered PWDs in the Philippines who rely on accessible government
programs and benefits. Streamlining the identification system is therefore essential in ensuring
that eligible individuals can readily avail themselves of the support mechanisms provided by law.

        The bill further aligns with the Philippines’ commitments under the United Nations
Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD), which calls on State Parties to
simplify administrative procedures to facilitate the full enjoyment of rights and privileges without
undue hardship2. Removing the cost of PWD ID issuance ensures that government assistance
programs remain accessible to all qualified individuals regardless of financial capacity.

        In addition, the measure also expands the eligibility of PWD IDs to include refugees and
stateless persons. This ensures that the Philippines adheres to its international obligations
regarding humanitarian protection while guaranteeing that the most vulnerable citizens of the
country can access essential statutory benefits.

        In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR VALIDITY PERIODS OF IDENTIFICATION CARDS ISSUED
TO PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “MAGNA CARTA FOR
DISABLED PERSONS”, AS AMENDED, AND PRESCRIBING PENALTIES FOR
VIOLATIONS THEREOF

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Chapter 8, Section 32 of Republic Act (RA) No. 7277, otherwise known as
the “Magna Carta for Disabled Persons”, as amended by RA No. 9442, is hereby further
amended to read as follows:

               “SEC. 32. Persons with [disability] DISABILITIES shall be entitled
       to the following:

              xxx

               The abovementioned privileges are available only to persons with
       [disability] PERMANENT OR TEMPORARY DISABILITIES who are
       Filipino citizens, REFUGEES, OR STATELESS PERSONS upon
       submission of [any of the following as proof of his/her entitlement thereto:]

               [(I)] An identification card issued by the [city or municipal mayor or
       the barangay captain] PERSONS WITH DISABILITY AFFAIRS OFFICE
       (PDAO) OR A DESIGNATED FOCAL PERSON PERFORMING PDAO
       FUNCTIONS, DULY SIGNED BY THE CITY OF MUNICIPAL
       MAYOR of the place where the persons with [disability] DISABILITIES
       reside[s], AS PROOF OF THEIR ENTITLEMENT THERETO.[;

              (II) The passport of the persons with disability concerned; or

              (III) Transportation discount fare Identification Card (ID) issued by
       the National Council for the Welfare of Disabled Persons (NCWDP).]

       LOSS OR DAMAGE.”

       SEC. 3. The NCDA and the Department of Social Welfare and Development
(DSWD) shall, in coordination with the Philippine Statistics Authority, the Department of
Health (DOH), the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), the
Professional Regulation Commission, and the Department of Information and
Communications Technology, strengthen a unified and reliable database or registry of
persons with disabilities, including a Community-Based Monitoring System.

        SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within six (6) months from the
effectivity of this Act, the NCDA and the DSWD shall, in coordination with the DILG, the
DOH, other government agencies and stakeholders, issue the necessary rules and regulations
for the effective implementation of this Act.

        SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is subsequently
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof which are not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

        SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive
order, letter of instruction, rule or regulation inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

        SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette and at least two (2) newspapers of national circulation.
