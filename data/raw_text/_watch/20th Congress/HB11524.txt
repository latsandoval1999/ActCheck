                            AN ACT
  MANDATING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITY
   AFFAIRS OFFICES (PDAOs) IN ALL LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS,
AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10070, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ACT
    ESTABLISHING INSTITUTIONAL MECHANISM TO ENSURE THE
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROGRAMS AND SERVICES FOR PERSONS WITH
    DISABILITIES IN EVERY PROVINCE, CITY, AND MUNICIPALITY

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The objective of Republic Act No. 10070, which establishes an
institutional mechanism to ensure the implementation of programs and
services for Persons with Disabilities in every province, city, and municipality,
is to ensure that relevant and sustainable programs for Persons with
Disabilities are implemented at the local level. Under this law, specific roles
ascribed to Local Government Units (LGUs) are initially delineated and
coordinated within the operational framework of the Persons with Disability
Affairs Office (PDAO). For provinces, cities, and 1st to 3rd class municipalities,
PDAO shall be established through a Local Ordinance. In 4th to 6th class
municipalities, a Focal Person (FP) performs the functions of PDAO, which is
mandated through an Executive Order.

       However, thirteen years after RA No. 10070 was passed, over 50% of
LGUs still have yet to establish their respective PDAO. Based on the recent
record of the National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA), there are 739
PDAOs, or 43% compliance, as of February 2022. One of the primary reasons
for non-compliance is funding constraints. Since the establishment of an
office is subject to the availability of funds, it leaves discretion to LGUs to
create their respective PDAO. Not to mention that the success of data
gathering, a comprehensive database, further capacity building, information
dissemination, and public awareness campaigns all remain to be seen.
      In addition, there is still a lack of updated and reliable official data on
the actual number of people in the disability sector, which has a strong
correlation to delays in policy implementation. Due to a lack of capacity and
funding sources, many LGUs do not have a properly maintained database.
This highlights the pressing need to capacitate and establish PDAOs in all
LGUs tasked with having a uniform and standardized local database linked
to a national Philippine Registry for Persons with Disability (PRPWD) system.

      In light of the implementation of the Mandanas ruling and in the
continuous and overdue quest for improving local service delivery for the
disability sector, the need to insist on the mandatory nature of the creation of
PDAOs and appropriate funding source for the establishment and operations
of PDAOs is timely and imperative.

      This measure proposes to amend RA No. 10070 specifically to: (1)
ensure the mandatory creation and operation of PDAOs in all Local
Government Units (LGUs); (2) provide a clear budget allocation for their
establishment; (3) further strengthen their functions; (4) include a uniform
and standardized local database linked to a national PRPWD system; (5)
ensure mechanisms for data management, capacity building, and information
dissemination about PDAOs; and (6) refine its fragmented institutional
framework.

       After more than a decade since the enactment of RA No. 10070, policy
interventions should be made to fully strengthen the implementation of RA
No. 7277 and to ensure relevant and sustainable programs as well as active
participation and empowerment of the disability sector in various
undertakings and projects of LGUs.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
  MANDATING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITY
   AFFAIRS OFFICES (PDAOs) IN ALL LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS,
AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10070, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ACT
    ESTABLISHING INSTITUTIONAL MECHANISM TO ENSURE THE
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROGRAMS AND SERVICES FOR PERSONS WITH
    DISABILITIES IN EVERY PROVINCE, CITY, AND MUNICIPALITY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Persons with
Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) in All Local Government Units Act".

SECTION 2. Section 1 of Republic Act No. 10070, amending Section 40 of
Republic Act No. 7277, is hereby further amended to read as follows and shall
renumber the sub-sections therein accordingly:

    Sec. 40 of Republic Act No. 7277, as amended, is hereby further
    amended to read as follows AND SHALL RENUMBER THE SUB-
    SECTIONS THEREIN ACCORDINGLY:

    Sec. 40. Role of National Agencies and Local Government Units.

    (a) [Local government units shall promote the establishment of
        organizations of persons with disabilities (PWDs) in their
        respective territorial jurisdictions.] ALL LOCAL GOVERNMENT
        UNITS (LGUs) SHALL PROMOTE AND INCLUDE IN THEIR
        LOCAL PRIORITY PROGRAMS THE ESTABLISHMENT OF
        PERSONS WITH DISABILITY AFFAIRS OFFICE (PDAO) IN
        THEIR RESPECTIVE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTIONS. National
        agencies and local government units may enter into joint
        ventures with PDAO AND OTHER organizations or associations
   of [PWDs] PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES to explore livelihood
   opportunities and other undertakings that shall enhance the
   OVERALL QUALITY OF LIFE, health and physical fitness,
   COGNITIVE,       economic,     social,   MENTAL,       AND
   PSYCHOLOGICAL well-being of [PWDs] PERSONS WITH
   DISABILITIES.

(b) Local government units THROUGH THEIR LOCAL CHIEF
EXECUTIVE shall organize and establish the following:

(1) Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO)
PDAO shall be created in every province, city and municipality. ALL
PROVINCES, CITIES, AND MUNICIPALITIES ARE MANDATED TO
ESTABLISH THEIR RESPECTIVE PERSONS WITH DISABILITY
AFFAIRS OFFICE (PDAO) DIRECTLY UNDER THE OFFICE OF
THE LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE (OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR,
CITY OR MUNICIPAL MAYOR, RESPECTIVELY). The local chief
executive shall appoint a PDAO HEAD who shall manage and
oversee the EFFICIENT operations of the office, pursuant to its
mandate under this Act. Priority shall be given to qualified [PWDs]
PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES to head the said office in carrying
out the following functions:

  (i) Formulate and implement policies, plans and programs for the
  promotion of EMPOWERMENT, RIGHTS, AND [the] welfare of
  [PWDs] PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES in coordination with
  concerned national and local government agencies;

  (ii) Coordinate the implementation of the provisions of this Act,
  Batas Pambansa Blg. 344, otherwise known as the Accessibility
  Law, REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10754, and ALL other relevant
  DISABILITY-RELATED laws at the local level;

  (iii) Represent [PWDs] PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES in
  meetings of local development councils and other special bodies;

  (iv) Recommend and enjoin the participation of non-government
  organizations (NGOs) and people’s organizations (POs) in the
  implementation of all disability-related laws and policies;

  (v) Gather and compile relevant data on [PWDs] PERSONS WITH
  DISABILITIES in their localities;

  (VI) MAINTAIN A UNIFORM AND STANDARDIZED LOCAL
  DATABASE LINKED TO A NATIONAL PHILIPPINE REGISTRY
  FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES (PRPWD) SYSTEM;

  (vii) Disseminate information including, but not limited to,
  programs and activities for [PWDs] PERSONS WITH
      DISABILITIES, statistics on [PWDs] PERSONS WITH
      DISABILITIES WITH CATEGORIES BY ASSIGNED SEX AT
      BIRTH, GENDER, TYPE OF DISABILITY, PER BARANGAY,
      EDUCATIONAL ATTAINMENT, EMPLOYMENT STATUS, AND
      OTHER REQUIRED INFORMATION, including children with
      disability, and training and employment opportunities for [PWDs]
      PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES;

      (viii) Submit reports to the office of the local chief executive on the
      implementation of programs and services for the promotion of
      EMPOWERMENT, RIGHTS, AND welfare of [PWDs] PERSONS
      WITH DISABILITIES in their respective areas of jurisdiction;

      (ix) Ensure that the policies, plans and programs for the
      promotion of EMPOWERMENT, RIGHTS, AND welfare of [PWDs]
      PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES are funded by both the national
      and local government;

      (x) Monitor fundraising activities being conducted for the benefit
      of [PWDs] PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES;

      (xi) Seek donations in cash or in kind from local or foreign donors
      to implement an approved work plan for [PWDs] PERSONS WITH
      DISABILITIES, in accordance with existing laws and regulations;
      and

      (xii) Perform such other functions as may be necessary for the
      promotion of EMPOWERMENT, RIGHTS, AND welfare of [PWDs]
      PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES.

    (2) Focal Person

    In consideration of budget restraints, local chief executives of fourth
    (4th), fifth (5th), and sixth (6th) class municipalities may, in lieu of
    the creation of a PDAO, designate a focal person who shall perform
    the functions of the PDAO. Priority in appointment should be given
    to a [PWDs] PERSON WITH DISABILITIES with experience in
    providing services to THE DISABILITY SECTOR.

    The establishment of a PDAO or the appointment of a focal person,
    as the case may be, should be done in consultation and coordination
    with the DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL
    GOVERNMENT, National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA),
    NGOs and POs.

    EMPLOYMENT       STATUS,   AND     OTHER     REQUIRED
    INFORMATION.
SECTION 6. Section 4 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows
and re-numbered accordingly:

    SEC. 5. THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL
    GOVERNMENT (DILG) IS MANDATED TO ENSURE THAT THE
    ANNUAL BUDGET OF EVERY PROVINCE, CITY AND
    MUNICIPALITY INCLUDES PROVISIONS TO FUND THE
    ESTABLISHMENT OF A PERSONS WITH DISABILITY OFFICE
    (PDAO) IN THEIR RESPECTIVE JURISDICTIONS.

    IN ORDER TO EFFECTIVELY CARRY OUT THE OBJECTIVES OF
    REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10070, EVERY PROVINCE, CITY AND
    MUNICIPALITY ARE HEREBY MANDATED TO ALLOCATE AT
    LEAST 1 % FROM THEIR GENERAL FUND FOR THE
    ESTABLISHMENT AND OPERATION OF A PERSONS WITH
    DISABILITY  OFFICE   (PDAO)  IN  THEIR  RESPECTIVE
    JURISDICTIONS AND THAT THE SAME BE INCLUDED IN THE
    LGUs' ANNUAL PLANS AND BUDGETS.
    LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS (LGUs) ARE LIKEWISE MANDATED
    TO ENSURE THAT THE NECESSARY FUNDS FROM LOCAL
    REVENUES ARE ALLOCATED FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF
    THIS ACT FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE DISABILITY SECTOR IN
    THEIR JURISDICTIONS.

SECTION 7. A new section 6 is hereby inserted to read as follows:

    SEC. 6. APPROPRIATIONS. THE AMOUNT NECESSARY TO
    CARRY OUT THE PROVISIONS OF THIS ACT SHALL BE
    INCLUDED IN THE ANNUAL GENERAL APPROPRIATIONS ACT
    (GAA).


SECTION 8. A new section 7 is hereby inserted to read as follows:

    SECTION 7. CREATION OF THE BUREAU OF DISABILITY
    AFFAIRS. - THE BUREAU OF DISABILITY AFFAIRS (BDA),
    HEREINAFTER REFERRED TO AS THE BUREAU, IS HEREBY
    CREATED TO SUPERVISE, OVERSEE AND MONITOR THE
    IMPLEMENTATION AND ESTABLISHMENT OF PDAOS IN ALL
    LGUS. THE BUREAU SHALL FUNCTION AS A REGULAR BUREAU
    OF THE DILG, UNDER THE SUPERVISION OF THE
    DEPARTMENT SECRETARY AND TO BE HEADED BY A BUREAU
    DIRECTOR AND AN ASSISTANT BUREAU DIRECTOR.

    THE BUREAU SHALL:

       (a) ENSURE    THE   CREATION,    COMPLIANCE   AND
       IMPLEMENTATION OF ALL LGUs IN THE ESTABLISHMENT OF
       PDAO IN THEIR RESPECTIVE JURISDICTIONS;

       (b) PROVIDE TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE TO LGUS SUCH AS,
       BUT NOT LIMITED TO, CAPACITY BUILDING AND
       FACILITATING A SMOOTH TRANSITION FROM SWDO TO
       PDAO;

       (c) INCORPORATE THE PRESENCE OF PDAO AND ITS
       PROGRAMS AS PART OF THE INDICATORS BEING
       MONITORED THROUGH LGPMS AND SGLG;

       (d) ASSESS AND MONITOR THE FUNCTIONALITY OF PDAOS;
       (e) COORDINATE WITH OTHER NATIONAL AGENCIES
       REGARDING CONCERNS OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES;
       AND

       (f) INITIATE AND CONDUCT IMMEDIATE INVESTIGATION
       IN NON-COMPLYING LGUS AND RECOMMEND APPROPRIATE
       ACTION WITHOUT DELAY.

SECTION 10. Non-Impairment Clause. – Nothing in this Act shall be
construed as to diminish, impair, or repeal rights recognized, granted, or
made available to disadvantaged, marginalized, or specific sectors under
existing laws.

SECTION 11. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act
is declared unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provisions not
affected thereby shall remain in force and effect.

SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, rule or regulation inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act is hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days
following its complete publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2)
newspapers of general circulation, whichever comes first.
