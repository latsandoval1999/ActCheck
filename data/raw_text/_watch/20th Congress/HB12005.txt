                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Siaton, located at the southernmost tip of Negros Oriental, is rich
in marine biodiversity. Its coastal waters are among the richest fishing grounds in
Negros Oriental, providing not only a sanctuary for diverse marine life but also
livelihood and food security to its residents.

Consistent with Section 7, Article XIII of the 1987 Philippine Constitution, which
provides that "The State shall protect the rights of subsistence fishermen, especially of
local communities, to the preferential use of local marine and fishing resources, both
inland and offshore," this bill proposes the establishment of a multi-species marine
hatchery in the Municipality of Siaton, in the third district of the province of Negros
Oriental. The establishment of a multi-species hatchery will greatly benefit the people
of Siaton and neighboring communities by providing a stable source of employment
for local fisherfolk, ensuring a reliable supply of high-value marine products, and
promoting sustainability and food security.

The hatchery will likewise serve as a research and training center to improve breeding
techniques and adapt them to needs of the community.

The establishment of this facility will not only boost local fisheries production but
also contribute to the overall economic growth of Siaton and its adjacent communities.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A MULTI-SPECIES HATCHERY IN THE MUNICIPALITY
OF SIATON, PROVINCE OF NEGROS ORIENTAL, AND APPROPRIATING
                     FUNDS THEREFOR


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

Section 1. There shall be established, under the supervision of the Bureau of Fisheries
and Aquatic Resources of the Department of Agriculture (DA-BFAR), a multi-species
hatchery in the Municipality of Siaton, in the third district of the Province of Negros
Oriental.

Section 2. Prior to the establishment of the multi-species hatchery, the DA-BFAR
shall conduct a full-scale feasibility study in support of its construction to comply
with the requirements of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM).

The DA-BFAR, shall transfer the management of the multi-species hatchery to the
local government of Siaton, in the third district of the province of Negros Oriental
within two (2) years after its construction through a memorandum of agreement. It
shall implement a training and phasing-in program for local government personnel on
the management and operation of the hatchery.

Section 3. The DA-BFAR shall conduct continuing research and experimentation on
the breeding and production of marine species, especially as they apply to local
conditions, to discover new methods and technology that shall benefit the fisheries
industry.

Section 4. The Secretary of Agriculture shall include in the Department’s programs
the conduct of the feasibility study and the consequent establishment of a multi-
species hatchery in the Municipality of Siaton, in the third district of the Province of
Negros Oriental, the funding of which shall be included in the General Appropriations
Act. The local government unit concerned shall set aside funds from any available
local revenue in an amount deemed appropriate for the operationalization of the
hatchery.

Section 5. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of
Agriculture shall issue the rules and regulations on the supervision, operation,
monitoring, and the eventual transfer of the management of the multi-species hatchery
to the local government of Siaton, in the third district of Negros Oriental.

Section 6. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
