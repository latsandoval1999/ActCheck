                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE
       This bill seeks to create three (3) Municipal Trial Courts (MTCs) in the Province of South
Cotabato, one each for the municipalities of Sto. Niño, Lake Sebu, and T’boli.

       For this purpose, the existing Municipal Circuit Trial Court (MCTC) for Norala-T’boli-
Sto. Niño shall be decircuitized and hereinafter denominated as MTC in the Municipality of
Norala. Similarly, the existing Municipal Circuit Trial Court (MCTC) for Surallah-Lake Sebu shall
be decircuitized and hereinafter denominated as MTC in the Municipality of Surallah.

       The creation of an MTC for T’boli is justified by the large population of Indigenous
Peoples (IPs) in the municipality and the fact that T’boli is very far from Norala where the existing
MCTC is situated. Although accessible, the transportation cost is burdensome for most litigants
because one needs to cross over Surallah and Sto. Niño in going to Norala. The driving distance
from T’boli to Norala is approximately 40.6 kilometers.

        The same goes for Lake Sebu which while accessible to Surallah, where the present MCTC
is located, would still require a separate MTC for the former in view of the significant IP
population. The distance of 22.5 kilometers (14 miles) from Lake Sebu to Surallah would also be
challenging for most IPs.

        If we are indeed going to transform our country into an oasis of freedom and justice, we
need to ensure that “those who have less in life should have more in law.” Those who are in the
margins of our society, which counts our indigenous peoples, need greater access to the pathways
of justice for the redress of their grievances.

        For these and other similar compelling reasons, the early approval of this measure is
earnestly sought.

                              AN ACT
CREATING THREE (3) ADDITIONAL MUNICIPAL TRIAL COURTS (MTCs), ONE (1)
MUNICIPAL TRIAL COURT FOR STO. NIÑO, ONE (1) MUNICIPAL TRIAL COURT
FOR LAKE SEBU AND ONE (1) MUNICIPAL TRIAL COURT FOR T’BOLI, ALL
SITUATED IN THE PROVINCE OF SOUTH COTABATO, IN THE ELEVENTH
JUDICIAL REGION, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 30 OF BATAS
PAMBANSA BLG. 129, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “JUDICIARY
REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1980”, AS AMENDED, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR


SECTION 1. Three (3) additional Municipal Trial Courts (MTCs), in the Eleventh Judicial Region
are hereby created and stationed in the Province of South Cotabato as follows:

       (a) One (1) MTC in the Municipality of Sto. Niño;
       (b) One (1) MTC in the Municipality of Lake Sebu; and
       (c) One (1) MTC in the Municipality of T’boli.

       The existing Municipal Circuit Trial Court (MCTC) of Norala-T’boli-Sto. Niño stationed
in Norala shall be decircuitized and hereinafter denominated as Municipal Trial Court (MTC) in
the Municipality of Norala.

       The existing Municipal Circuit Trial Court (MCTC) of Surallah-Lake Sebu stationed in
Surallah shall be decircuitized and hereinafter denominated as Municipal Trial Court (MTC) in the
Municipality of Surallah.

SEC. 2. The Chief Justice of the Supreme Court, in coordination with the Secretary of the
Department of Justice, shall immediately include in the Court’s program the operationalization of
the three (3) additional MTCs in the municipalities of Sto. Niño, Lake Sebu and T’boli in the
Province of South Cotabato. The Supreme Court shall issue the necessary rules and regulations for
the effective implementation of this Act.
SEC. 3. The funds necessary for the implementation of this Act, including appropriations for
personnel services such as salaries, benefits and emoluments for judges and court personnel, court
operations under maintenance and other operating expenses (MOOE), and provisions for Halls of
Justice or courtrooms to accommodate judges and court staff, as well as funds necessary for the
implementation of automatically-created prosecutor positions pursuant to Section 13 of Republic
Act No. 10071 or the “Prosecution Service Act of 2010” and appointment of public attorneys
pursuant to Section 7 of Republic Act No. 9406 or the Act Reorganizing and Strengthening the
Public Attorney’s Office (PAO), resulting from the creation of these courts, including
appropriations for personnel services such as salaries, benefits, and emoluments for prosecutors
and public attorneys, shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       The funds necessary for the operation of the courts herein created shall be appropriated and
released only upon the actual organization of the court and the appointment of its personnel.

SEC. 4. Section 30 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 129, otherwise known as “The Judiciary
Reorganization Act 1980”, as amended, and all other laws, rules and regulations which are
inconsistent with this Act are hereby amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or
in a newspaper of general circulation.
