                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Philippines’ commitment to inclusivity and human dignity is
enshrined in Article II, Section 11: “The State values the dignity of every human
person and guarantees full respect for human rights.” This commitment is
embedded in how our laws would uphold and protect the rights and welfare of
Filipinos who are marginalized.

       Among those who continue to face stigma and discrimination are persons
of short stature or little people. A little person is one of the approximately 400
types of dwarfism1. Dwarfism is a medical or genetic condition that usually
results in an adult height of 4’10” or shorter, although in some cases, the average
height of an adult with dwarfism is 4’0, but typical heights range from 2’8” to
4’8”2.

      Since dwarfism can be caused by so many different genetic or medical
conditions, it is difficult to get an accurate number of individuals affected. The
most recognizable and most common form of dwarfism in little people is
achondroplasia, which accounts for 70% of dwarfism cases, and occurs in

        Despite the validity of their existence and their contributions to society,
little people continue to face challenges in accessing equal opportunities for all,
are stigmatized, and are discriminated against due to being different.

      This bill seeks to declare the month of October of every year as the
“National Dwarfism Awareness Month”, to instill public awareness of the human
rights situation of the Filipino Little People. Likewise, October twenty-fifth of
every year shall be declared the “National Dwarfism Awareness Day” for the
purposes of promoting recognition and acceptance of persons with dwarfism, in
line with the annual celebration of Dwarfism Awareness Month and Dwarfism
Awareness Day by the Little People of America, one of the early organizations
that provides support, resources, and information to individuals with dwarfism
and their families.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "National Dwarfism
Awareness Month Act".

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State values the dignity of every
human person and guarantees full respect for human rights. The State shall also
take all appropriate measures to ensure that the Filipino Little People can
exercise their right to equal opportunities and protection from harassment,
discrimination, and violence.

The State likewise recognizes the importance of promoting awareness of
dwarfism and Little People to eliminate barriers to inclusivity and to lay the
foundations for the integration of Filipinos living with dwarfism into mainstream
society.

SECTION 3. National Dwarfism Awareness Month. – Every month of October
of each year is hereby declared as the National Dwarfism Awareness Month for
the purposes of instilling public awareness on the plight of the Little People.

SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Commission on
Human Rights, all education agencies and their attached agencies, the
Department of Health, the Department of Social Welfare and Development, the
National Council on Disability Affairs, and the Presidential Communications
Operations Office, together with the Philippine Society for Orphan Disorders,
shall issue rules and regulations to effectively implement the provisions of this
Act.

SECTION 6. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provisions not
affected thereby shall remain in force and effect.

SECTION 7. Non-Impairment Clause. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed
as to diminish, impair, or repeal rights recognized, granted, or made available to
disadvantaged, marginalized, or specific sectors under existing laws.

SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree, or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, rule, or regulation inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act is hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days
following its complete publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2)
newspapers of general circulation.
