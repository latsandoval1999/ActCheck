AN ACT
STRENGTHENING CAMPUS SECURITY IN EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS
THROUGH THE INSTALLATION OF CLOSED-CIRCUIT TELEVISION (CCTV)
SYSTEMS IN STRATEGIC AREAS AND THE ADOPTION OF CAMPUS
SECURITY PLANS, PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The proposed measure seeks to strengthen campus security in educational
institutions through the installation of Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) systems
in strategic areas and the adoption of Campus Security Plans.

Schools must provide safe and secure environments for students,
teachers, non-teaching personnel, and visitors. However, violence, bullying, and
other security incidents continue to threaten the safety and well-being of
members of the school community. The 2024 Philippines Global School-based
Student Health Survey found that 35.3 percent of students aged 13 to 17 had
been involved in a physical fight during the preceding twelve months, while 30.2
percent reported having been bullied on school property.!

Recent incidents of violence in schools further underscore the need to
reinforce preventive security measures, prompt reporting, effective emergency
response, and coordination with appropriate authorities. CCTV systems in
strategic areas may help deter wrongdoing, monitor security risks, preserve

The bill requires educational institutions to adopt Campus Security Plans

To protect the rights of students, personnel, and visitors, the bill mandates
compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, or the “Data Privacy Act of 2012,” and
relevant issuances of the National Privacy Commission governing the collection,
use, storage, access, disclosure, retention, and disposal of CCTV footage.

House Bill No. 11575

INTRODUCED BY REP. PATRICK MICHAEL “PM” D. VARGAS

AN ACT STRENGTHENING CAMPUS SECURITY IN EDUCATIONAL
INSTITUTIONS THROUGH THE INSTALLATION OF CLOSED-CIRCUIT
TELEVISION (CCTV) SYSTEMS IN STRATEGIC AREAS AND THE ADOPTION
OF CAMPUS SECURITY PLANS, PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Campus Security
Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to ensure a

safe, secure, and conducive learning environment for students, teachers, non-
teaching personnel, and visitors in educational institutions.

To this end, the State shall strengthen campus security by installing and using
closed-circuit television (CCTV) systems in strategic areas and adopting campus

security plans, consistent with the right to privacy and other applicable laws,
rules, and regulations.

SECTION 3. Coverage. — This Act shall apply to all public and private basic
education institutions and higher education institutions in the Philippines.
SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. — As used in this Act:

(a) Campus refers to the buildings, grounds, facilities, and other areas

under the control or administration of an educational institution and used
for educational purposes;

(b) Campus Security Plan refers to the written plan of an educational
institution that identifies campus security risks and provides measures

===== [page 4] =====
for the prevention of and response to crimes, violence, emergencies, and
other security incidents;

(c) Closed-Circuit Television or CCTV System refers to a system of cameras
and related equipment used to capture, transmit, record, store, retrieve,
and view video images for legitimate campus security purposes;

(d) Educational Institution refers to a public or private institution offering
basic education or higher education and recognized, authorized, or

permitted to operate by the Department of Education or the Commission
on Higher Education, as the case may be; and

(e) Strategic Areas refer to entrances, exits, perimeter areas, corridors,
parking areas, common areas, and other locations identified in the

Campus Security Plan where CCTV coverage is necessary and
proportionate to address a security risk.

SECTION 5. Installation of CCTV Systems in Strategic Areas. — Every
educational institution shall install, operate, and maintain CCTV systems in
strategic areas within its campus, as identified in its Campus Security Plan.

CCTV systems shall not be installed in toilets, washrooms, changing rooms,

sleeping quarters, clinics, counseling rooms, or other areas where persons have
a reasonable expectation of privacy.

The installation, operation, access, Storage, use, disclosure, retention, and
disposal of CCTV footage shall comply with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise
known as the “Data Privacy Act of 2012,” its implementing rules and regulations,
and relevant issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

SECTION 6. Campus Security Plan. - Every educational institution shall
prepare, adopt, implement, and periodically update a Campus Security Plan.

The Campus Security Plan shall include, at a minimum:
(a) Identification and assessment of campus security risks;

(b) Identification of strategic areas for CCTV coverage;

(c) Measures on access control, visitor management, and coordination with
law enforcement, local government units, and other relevant agencies;

(d) Procedures for reporting and responding to crimes, violence,
emergencies, and other security incidents;

(ec) Measures for the protection of students, teachers, non-teaching
personnel, visitors, and other persons within the campus; and

===== [page 5] =====
(f} Measures to ensure compliance with data privacy and information-

security requirements.

The Campus Security Plan shall be reviewed and updated at least once every
three (3) years, or earlier when necessary because of an incident, material change
in campus conditions, or identified security risk.

SECTION 7. Data Privacy and Access to Footage. — Educational institutions
shall adopt reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards to
protect CCTV footage against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss,
or destruction.

Access to CCTV footage shall be limited to authorized personnel and may be
disclosed only when necessary for a legitimate security purpose, compliance with

law, investigation of an incident, or pursuant to a lawful order of a competent
authority.

Educational institutions shall post clear and visible notices in areas under CCTV

coverage and shall adopt a written policy on the operation and use of CCTV
systems.

SECTION 8. Funding. —- The amount necessary to implement this Act in public
educational institutions shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act. Private educational institutions shall allocate the necessary funds for
compliance with this Act, subject to such phased implementation and assistance
programs as may be provided under the implementing rules and regulations.

SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within one hundred
eighty (180) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Education
and the Commission on Higher Education, in coordination with the Department
of Budget and Management, the Department of the Interior and Local
Government, the Department of Information and Communications Technology,
and the National Privacy Commission, shall promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 10. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 11. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 12. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
