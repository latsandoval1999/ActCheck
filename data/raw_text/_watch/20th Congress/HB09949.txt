                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Many Filipinos render services beyond the regular eight-hour workday, sometimes on
holidays in hazardous and strenuous circumstances. This reflects both their dedication and hard
work to their jobs and the need for additional compensation to meet daily necessities.

        However, under the current income tax system, overtime pay, hazard pay and night-shift
differential pay, generally form part of an employee’s gross income and are subject to income
taxation, except where the law expressly provides otherwise. Notably, the National Internal
Revenue Code (NIRC), as amended, already exempts minimum wage earners (MWEs) from
income tax and likewise exempts their holiday pay, overtime pay, night shift differential pay, and
hazard pay.

        This measure seeks to extend calibrated tax relief to compensation earners regardless of
wage level by excluding from gross income certain overtime pay, night shift differential pay, and
hazard pay that are mandated under applicable labor laws and regulations, subject to statutory
limitations. To avoid diminishing the benefits already granted by law to MWEs, this measure
likewise provides that nothing herein shall be construed to reduce or limit the existing tax
exemptions of MWEs under the NIRC.
       This proposed amendment is intended to increase employees’ take-home pay for additional
work rendered and hazardous assignments undertaken, strengthen household purchasing power,
and support broader economic activity.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.
