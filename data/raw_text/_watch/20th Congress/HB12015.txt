2​​SECTION​​1.​​TITLE.​​–​​The​​short​​title​​of​​this​​Act​​shall​​be:​​the​​‘Revised​​PAGCOR​
3​​Charter of 2027’​

4​​SECTION​ ​2.​ ​DECLARATION​ ​OF​ ​POLICY.​ ​–​ ​It​ ​is​ ​hereby​ ​declared​ ​to​ ​be​ ​the​
5​​policy​​of​​the​​State​​to​​centralize​​and​​integrate​​into​​one​​government​​corporate​​entity​
6​​the​ ​regulation,​ ​supervision​ ​and​ ​licensing​ ​of​ ​all​ ​games​ ​of​ ​chance​ ​not​ ​otherwise​
7​​authorized​ ​by​ ​existing​ ​franchises​ ​or​ ​permitted​ ​by​ ​law,​ ​or​ ​regulated​ ​by​ ​other​
8​​government bodies, in pursuit of the following objectives:​

9​         ​(a)To​ ​establish​ ​the​ ​Philippines​ ​as​ ​a​ ​world-​class​ ​gaming​ ​destination​ ​and
10               ​gaming hub;​

11         ​(b)To​ ​prevent​ ​money​ ​laundering,​ ​counter​ ​terrorism​ ​financing,​ ​and​ ​counter
12              ​proliferation financing in the gaming industry;​

13         ​(c)To​ ​promote​ ​the​ ​development​ ​of​ ​the​ ​Philippines​ ​as​ ​a​ ​center​ ​of​ ​excellence
14               ​and expertise for gaming-​related competencies and skills;​
