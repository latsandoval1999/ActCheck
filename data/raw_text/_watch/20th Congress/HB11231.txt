                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The University of Antique (UA), established under Republic Act No. 9746, has long
served as a principal institution of higher learning in the Province of Antique and a key
contributor to human resource development, research, extension services, and socio-economic
progress in Western Visayas.

        As the demands of higher education continue to evolve, there is a need to strengthen the
University’s institutional framework and expand its capacity to respond to emerging academic,
technological, and development priorities. This bill seeks to revise the Charter of the University
of Antique by broadening its academic mandate, strengthening its governance and administrative
structures, enhancing institutional autonomy, and improving access to quality higher education
throughout the province.

        The measure expands the University’s curricular offerings to include undergraduate and
graduate programs, advanced professional and technical instruction, and training in fields such as
law, medicine, nursing, maritime education, engineering, information and communications
technology, and other disciplines responsive to the needs of Antique and Western Visayas. It
likewise strengthens UA’s functions in research, extension, innovation, technology transfer, and
entrepreneurship.

        The bill also strengthens UA as a multi-campus state university. It recognizes its existing
regular campuses in Sibalom, Tibiao, and Hamtic; converts the extension campuses in Caluya
and Libertad into regular campuses; and mandates the creation of new regular campuses in
Bugasong, Tobias Fornier, and San Remigio. It further authorizes the establishment of additional


                                                1
branches or extension campuses as may be necessary to improve access to quality education and
advance the University’s research and academic capabilities.

        Taken together, these reforms will provide the University of Antique with a stronger and
more responsive institutional framework to deliver accessible, relevant, and globally competitive
higher education, while further advancing research, innovation, public service, and sustainable
regional development.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Revised University of Antique
   Charter”.

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State shall promote, foster, nurture, and protect the
   right of all citizens to accessible quality education. It shall establish, maintain, and support a
   complete, adequate, and integrated system of education towards globalization that is relevant
   to the needs of the people and society.

        To this end, the University of Antique (UA) and its constituent units shall be
   strengthened as a state university to enhance its capability to provide accessible quality
   education. It shall also be further strengthened by expanding its curricular offerings and the
   composition and powers of its governing board, developing the competencies and
   encouraging the specialization of its constituent units, engaging in conventional or alternative
   instructional techniques, methodologies and strategies, and fostering a unique and distinctive
   academic leadership towards the improvement of UA as an academic institution and the
   delivery of higher education services in the Province of Antique and the Western Visayas
   Region.

        SEC. 3. University Campuses and Other Units. – The UA shall be composed of the
   following existing regular campuses: UA-Main Campus in the Municipality of Sibalom;


                                                        1
UA-Tario Lim Memorial Campus in the Municipality of Tibiao; and UA-Hamtic Campus in
the Municipality of Hamtic, all in the Province of Antique.

    The UA shall also be composed of the following extension campuses, which shall be
converted into regular campuses upon the effectivity of this Act: the UA-Caluya Campus in
the Municipality of Caluya and the UA- Libertad Campus in the Municipality of Libertad,
both in the Province of Antique.

    The UA is also mandated to create new regular campuses in the Municipalities of
Bugasong, Tobias Fornier and San Remigio, all in the Province of Antique, to be known as
the UA-Bugasong Campus, UA-Tobias Fornier Campus, and UA-San Remigio Campus,
respectively.

     The UA may open new branches or extension campuses in consortium with other
academic institutions, as it may deem necessary, to improve access to quality education,
promote innovation and progress in the academe, and advance its research capabilities,
subject to applicable memoranda with other institutions: Provided, that the UA shall comply
with the policies, standards, and guidelines of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED)
for the operation of regular or extension campuses.

    The existing reasonably-sized laboratory junior and senior high school shall continue to
operate under the supervision of the College of Education of the UA to serve the in-campus
requirements of its students.

     SEC. 4. General Mandate. – The UA shall primarily provide advanced education, higher
technological, professional courses and training programs in the arts, culture, agriculture,
forestry, fisheries, education, law, medicine and allied health sciences, maritime education,
social and natural sciences and technology, and other relevant fields of study. It shall also
undertake research and extension services and production activities, in support of the
socio-economic development of the Province of Antique and the Western Visayas Region.

     SEC. 5. Curricular Offerings. – The UA shall primarily offer undergraduate and
graduate courses, advanced professional and technical instruction and training within its areas
of specialization in the fields of arts, culture, agriculture, forestry, fishery, education, law,
medicine, nursing, maritime education, ecology, engineering, information and
communications technology, other social and natural sciences and technology, and other
relevant fields of study within its areas of specialization and according to its capabilities, as the
Board of Regents may deem necessary for the accomplishment of its objectives, and those
which respond to the human resource development needs of the Province of Antique and the
Western Visayas Region: Provided, That the UA shall comply with the policies, standards, and
guidelines of the CHED for the offering of courses and the introduction of new or revised
programs.

    SEC. 6. Sports, Culture, and the Arts. – The UA shall undertake and support
comprehensive sports programs, promote physical education, uphold excellence, and
encourage competitive participation in sports activities. It shall instill school identity and
                                                2
solidarity and cultivate pride, self-discipline, and teamwork. The UA shall strengthen culture
and the arts and strategic national resources and promote its importance to our identity, social
well-being, and in the preservation of the cultural heritage of the country.

     SEC. 7. Administration. – The UA shall have the general powers of a corporation set
forth in Republic Act (RA) No. 11232, otherwise known as the “Revised Corporation Code
of the Philippines”. The exercise of its corporate powers shall be vested exclusively in the
Board of Regents, hereinafter referred to as the Board, and the President of the UA.

     SEC. 8. The Governing Board. – The governing board of the UA shall be the Board of
Regents, hereinafter referred to as the Board. The Chairperson of the Board shall be the
Chairperson of the CHED and the Vice-Chairperson shall be the President of the UA. The
following shall be the members:

        a.​ Chairperson of the Committee on Higher, Technical and Vocational Education
            of the Senate;
        b.​ Chairperson of the Committee on Higher and Technical Education of the House
            of Representatives;
        c.​ Regional Director of the National Economic and Development Authority
            (NEDA);
        d.​ Regional Director of the Department of Agriculture;
        e.​ Regional Director of the Department of Science and Technology;
        f.​ Regional Director of the CHED;
        g.​ President of the federation of faculty associations of the UA;
        h.​ President of the federation of student councils of the UA;
        i.​ President of the federation of alumni associations of the UA;
        j.​ President of the nonteaching personnel association of the UA; and
        k.​ Two (2) prominent citizens from the private sector who have distinguished
            themselves in their professions or fields of specialization.

        The Board shall appoint two (2) prominent citizens who shall be selected from a list
of at least five (5) qualified persons from the Province of Antique as recommended by a
Search Committee constituted by the President of the UA, in consultation with the
Chairperson of the CHED and other members of the Board, based on the guidelines,
qualifications and standards for the position set by the Board. They shall serve for a term of
two (2) years from the date of appointment.

        The terms of office of the Presidents of the federation of faculty associations,
federation of student councils, federation of alumni associations, and the representative of the
nonteaching personnel association of the UA shall be coterminous with their terms of office,
as provided under their respective constitutions and bylaws.

     SEC. 9. Powers and Duties of the Board. – The administration of the UA and the
exercise of its corporate powers are vested in the Board, which shall have the following
specific powers and duties in addition to its general powers of administration and the exercise
of all the powers granted to the board of directors of a corporation under Section 35 of RA
                                             3
No. 11232, the Board shall have the following specific powers and duties:

        a.​ Promulgate and implement policies in accordance with the declared State policies
            on education, other pertinent provisions of the Constitution on education,
            agriculture, science and technology, and the policies, standards and thrusts of the
            CHED under RA No. 7722, otherwise known as the "Higher Education Act of
            1994";

        b.​ Approve the curricula, instructional programs, and rules on student discipline
            drawn by the Administrative and Academic Councils, as herein provided;

        c.​ Appoint, upon the recommendation of the President of the UA, the vice
            presidents, deans, directors, heads of the campuses, faculty members, and other
            officials of the UA;

        d.​ Prescribe the salaries of faculty members and administrative officials, and
            employees, subject to the provisions of RA No. 11466, otherwise known as the
            “Salary Standardization Law of 2019”, and other laws amendatory thereto, civil
            service rules and regulations governing hours of service and such other
            requirements and conditions, other pertinent laws, rules and regulations;

        e.​ Fix the tuition fees and other necessary school charges, such as matriculation
            fees, graduation fees, and laboratory fees, as the Board may deem proper to
            impose, after due consultation with the concerned sectors. The fees and charges,
            including government subsidies and other income generated by the UA, shall
            constitute its funds to be used exclusively in the pursuit of its mandate as a state
            university, and shall be deposited in any authorized government depository bank.
            All interests that shall accrue therefrom shall form part of the same funds for the
            use of the UA;

            The fees and charges collected by the UA, including government subsidies and
            proceeds from other income sources, as well as interests that would accrue
            therefrom, shall be retained by the University: Provided, That in no case shall the
            said income be used for the payment of allowances and other benefits not
            authorized by law: Provided, further, That all fiduciary fees shall be disbursed for
            the specific purpose for which they were collected;

            If the UA, for reasons beyond its control, would not be able to pursue any
            project for which funds have been appropriated and allocated under its approved
            program of expenditures, the Board may authorize the use of said fund for any
            reasonable purpose for which it deems necessary and urgent for the attainment
            of the objectives of the University: Provided, That funds collected from students
            for specific purposes shall not be realigned or reprogramed to other
            expenditures;

        f.​ Adopt and implement a socialized scheme of tuition and other school fees for

   or the “Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act”;

g.​ Establish professorial chairs in the UA and provide fellowships for qualified
    faculty members, and academic and athletic scholarships to deserving students;

h.​ Receive and appropriate sums as may be provided by law for the support of the
    UA, and all other sums in the manner it may, in its discretion, determine to carry
    out the purposes and functions of the UA;
i.​ Set the policies on admission and graduation of students;

j.​ Award honorary degrees upon persons in recognition of their outstanding
    contributions in the fields of education, public service, arts, science and
    technology, sports and human kinetics or any field of specialization within the
    academic competency of the UA, and authorize the awarding of certificates of
    completion of nondegree and nontraditional courses;

k.​ Establish research and extension centers to promote the development of the UA;

l.​ Develop academic arrangements for institutional capability building with
    appropriate institutions and agencies, whether public, private, local or foreign,
    and appoint experts or specialists as consultants, part-time, visiting or exchange
    professors, scholars or researchers, as the case may be;

m.​ Adopt modern and innovative modes of transferring knowledge for the
    promotion of greater access to education such as the use of information
    technology, the dual training system, open distance learning, and community

n.​ Collaborate with the governing bodies of other state universities and colleges

o.​ Absorb non-chartered tertiary education institutions within the Province of
    Antique and the Western Visayas Region as branches, extension campuses, and
    external centers, in coordination with the CHED and in consultation with the
    DBM, and offer therein programs or courses to carry out the constitutional
    mandate of providing equal access to educational opportunities;

p.​ Authorize an external management audit of the UA, to be financed by the
    CHED, subject to the rules and regulations of the Commission on Audit, and
    institute reforms, including academic and structural changes, on the basis of the

        s.​ Enter into joint ventures with business and industry, after consultation with the

        t.​ Receive, in trust, all legacies, gifts, and donations of real and personal properties

        u.​ Extend the term of the President of the UA beyond the age of retirement, but

        y.​ Authorize the construction or repair of its buildings, machinery, equipment and
            other facilities, and the purchase and acquisition of real property, including the

        z.​ Promulgate rules and regulations, as may be necessary, to carry out the purposes

    SEC. 10. Meetings of the Board. – The Board shall regularly convene once every three
(3) months. The Chairperson of the Board may, however, call a special meeting whenever
                                             6
necessary: Provided, That members are notified in writing at least three (3) days prior to the
conduct of said meeting.

        A quorum of the Board shall consist of a majority of all its members holding office at
the time of the meeting: Provided, however, That the Chairperson of the Board or the President
of the UA is among those present in the meeting.

         In case the Chairperson of the CHED cannot attend a meeting of the Board, a
CHED Commissioner duly designated by Chairperson of the CHED as representative to the
meeting, shall have all the rights and responsibilities of a regular member: Provided, That
during the meeting, the President of the UA, as Vice Chairperson, shall be the presiding
officer: Provided, further, That this provision notwithstanding, the Chairperson of the CHED
may designate a CHED Commissioner as the regular Chairperson of the Board, in which
case, the said CHED Commissioner shall act as the presiding officer.

        In case the Chairpersons of the Senate Committee on Higher, Technical and
Vocational Education and the House Committee on Higher and Technical Education cannot
attend the Board meeting, they may designate their respective representatives, who shall have
the same rights and responsibilities as regular members of the Board.

        The members of the Board shall not receive any salary, but shall be entitled to
reimbursements for actual and necessary expenses incurred, either for their attendance to the
meetings of the Board or in connection with other official business authorized by resolutions
of the Board, subject to existing laws and regulations.

        SEC. 11. The University President. – The UA shall be headed by a President who
shall be appointed by the Board, subject to its guidelines, qualification and standards, and
based on the recommendation of a Search Committee duly constituted for the purpose of
selecting the President.

         The UA President shall render full-time service for a term of four (4) years, and may
be reappointed to another term: Provided, That in order to ensure the attainment of effective
implementation of this Act, the incumbent President of the UA shall serve a new term as the
first President of the UA and shall be eligible for reappointment to another term.

       Within six (6) months before the expiration of the term of office of the President of
the UA, the Board shall constitute the Search Committee for the Presidency, and shall ensure
the appointment or reappointment of a president on or before the expiration of the term.

        The powers and duties of the President of the UA, in addition to those specifically
provided for in this Act, shall include those usually pertaining to the Office of the President
of similar state universities and colleges and those delegated by the Board.

        The salary of the President of the UA shall be in accordance with RA No. 11466, and
such other laws amendatory thereto, which may hereinafter be enacted, and on the joint
circular for state universities and colleges’ leveling instruments, and guidelines of the CHED
                                             7
and the DBM.

      In case of a vacancy in the Office of the President by reason of death, compulsory
retirement, resignation, removal for cause, or incapacity of the President to perform the
functions of the office, the Board shall designate an Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of the UA,
pending the appointment of a new President: Provided, That the OIC shall serve only during
the unexpired portion of the term.

     The President of the UA shall be assisted by the following officials: Vice President for
Academic Affairs; Vice President for Administration, Planning, and Finance; Vice President
for Research, Development, and Extension; Vice President for Linkages, Production, and
Research Generation; and Vice President for Quality Assurance. They shall all be appointed
by the Board upon the recommendation of the President of the UA.

     SEC. 12. The Secretary. – The Board shall appoint a Secretary who shall serve as such
for both the Board and the UA and shall keep all records and proceedings of meetings of the
Board. The Secretary of the UA shall serve upon each member of the Board the appropriate
notice of board meetings.

    SEC. 13. The Treasurer. – The Treasurer of the Philippines shall be the ex officio
Treasurer of the UA.

     SEC. 14. The Administrative Council. – There shall be an Administrative Council
consisting of the President of the UA as Chairperson, and the vice presidents, deans,
directors, and other officials of equal rank, as members. The Administrative Council shall
review and recommend to the Board the policies that shall govern the administration,
management, and development planning of the UA.

    SEC. 15. The Academic Council. – There shall be an Academic Council, which shall
formulate academic policies. It shall be composed of the President of the UA, who shall act as
Chairperson, and all academic staff with the rank of at least an assistant professor, as
members.

     The Academic Council shall review and recommend the curricular offerings and rules on
student discipline, subject to the approval of the Board. It shall fix the requirements for the
admission of students, as well as for their graduation and conferment of degrees, subject to
the review and approval of the Board through the President of the UA.

     It shall exercise disciplinary power over the students of the UA and formulate rules and
regulations on student discipline, subject to the approval of the Board.

   ​ SEC. 16. Heads of University Campuses. - The UA campuses shall be headed by a
Campus Director who shall render full-time service for a term of two (2) years and may be
reappointed to another term.

​   The Campus Director shall be appointed or designated by the Board, upon the
                                             8
recommendation of the Search Committee and the UA President, subject to the guidelines,
qualifications and standards set by the Board: Provided, That upon the effectivity of this Act,
the incumbent Campus Directors of the existing campuses of the UA shall continue to serve
as such.

      SEC. 17. The Faculty. - No political belief, sex, gender preference, cultural or community
affiliation, ethnic origin, or religious opinion or affiliation shall be a matter of inquiry in the
appointment of faculty members of the UA: Provided, That said appointment shall be subject
to the guidelines, qualifications and standards set by the Board: Provided, further, That no
member of the faculty shall teach for or against any particular religious denomination.

    SEC. 18. Academic Freedom. – The UA shall enjoy academic freedom pursuant to
Paragraph 2, Section 5 of Article XIV of the Constitution.

     SEC. 19. Scholarship Programs and Admission. – The UA shall provide scholarship
programs and other affirmative action programs to assist poor but deserving students who
qualify for admission to the UA.

     No student shall be denied admission to the UA by reason of sex, gender, religion,
cultural community affiliation, and ethnic origin, or any other forms of discrimination.

     SEC. 20. Loan or Transfer of Supplies, Apparatuses, or Equipment, and Detail of
Personnel. – Heads of national government agencies and instrumentalities may, upon the
request of the Board, through the President of the UA, loan or transfer of supplies,
apparatuses, or equipment, and detail personnel to the UA: Provided, That such loan, transfer
or detail shall not be detrimental to public service.

     The personnel to be detailed to the UA shall perform such duties as may be deemed
required by the President of the UA and the time spent for such duties shall be counted as
part of the detailed personnel’s regular service.

      SEC. 21. Assets, Liabilities and Personnel. – All assets, both real and personal,
personnel and records of the UA, as well as its liabilities or obligations, shall remain under the
name of the UA: Provided, That the positions, rights, and security of tenure of faculty
members and personnel therein employed under existing laws shall be respected: Provided,
further, That the incumbents occupying the positions shall remain in the same status until
otherwise provided for by the Board: Provided, finally, That the UA shall also accordingly
upgrade or increase its human resource component as may be deemed appropriate by the
Board.

     All parcels of land belonging to the government that are occupied by the UA and all its
campuses are declared as property of the UA and shall be titled under its name: Provided, That
should the UA cease to exist or be abolished or should such aforementioned parcels of land
be no longer needed by the UA, the same shall revert to the concerned local government
units or to the Republic of the Philippines, as the case may be.


                                               9
     SEC. 22. Duty and Tax Exemptions. - The importation of books or publications,
instruments, equipment and apparatuses which are for economic, technical, vocational,
scientific, medical, philosophical, historical, legal or cultural purposes made by the UA, upon
certification by the CHED, shall be exempt from customs duties in accordance with the
provisions of RA 10863, otherwise known as the “Customs Modernization and Tariff Act
(CMTA)”.
     All grants, bequests, endowments, donations, and contributions made to and used
actually, directly, and exclusively for educational purposes by the UA, shall be exempt from
donor’s tax and the same shall be considered as allowable deductions from gross income for
purposes of computing the taxable income of the donor in accordance with the provisions of
the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended.

     SEC. 23. Development Plan, Management Audit, and Organizational, Administrative,
and Academic Structure. – Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the approval of this
Act, the UA shall accomplish the following:
        a.​ Submit a five (5)-year development plan, including its program budget to the
            CHED for recommendation to the DBM;

       b.​ Set up its organizational, administrative, and academic structure, including the
           appointment of key officials of the UA; and

       c.​ Undergo a management audit in coordination with the CHED.

    SEC. 24. Filing of Report. – On or before the fifteenth (15th) day of the second (2nd)
month after the opening of regular classes each year, the Board shall file a detailed report on
the progress, condition, and needs of the UA with the Office of the President of the
Philippines and both Houses of Congress, through the Chairperson of the CHED.

    SEC. 25. Suppletory Application. – The provisions of RA No. 8292, otherwise known as
the “Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997”, shall form part of this Act.

   SEC. 26. Transitory Provision. – Upon the effectivity of this Act, all employees of the
UA shall be retained in their present positions and no one shall be adversely affected by the
approval of the amendments to the original charter.

    SEC. 27. Parity Clause. – All other powers, functions, privileges, responsibilities, and
limitations under existing laws shall be deemed granted to or imposed upon the UA and its
officials, whenever appropriate.

    SEC. 28. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s appropriation of the UA. Thereafter,
such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall be included
in the annual General Appropriations Act.

    SEC. 29. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Board shall, in consultation with the CHED, formulate rules and
                                            10
regulations to implement its provisions.

    SEC. 30. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected are in full
force and effect.

    SEC. 31. Repealing Clause. – RA No. 9746, entitled “An Act Converting the Polytechnic
State College of Antique in the Municipality of Sibalom into a State University to be Known
as the University of Antique, Including its Extension Campuses in the Municipalities of
Hamtic and Tibiao, All Located in the Province of Antique and Appropriating Funds
Therefor”, and other laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, and rules and regulations
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are amended, repealed, or modified
accordingly.

    SEC. 32. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

       Approved,
