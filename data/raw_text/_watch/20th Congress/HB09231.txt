                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Section 284 of Republic Act (RA) No. 7160, otherwise known as the “Local
Government Code of 1991”, provides that local government units (LGUs) shall receive 40%
share from the national taxes based on the collection of the third fiscal year preceding the
current year. Subsequently, Republic Act No. 7924 was enacted, creating the Metropolitan
Manila Development Authority (MMDA) and providing among others that it shall continue to
receive the Internal Revenue Allotment (now National Tax Allotment [NTA]) allocated to the
now defunct Metro Manila Authority. In the present allocation of the NTA, the MMDA is
considered as a province.

         Apart from its allocation from the NTA, MMDA also receives: (1) annual
appropriations from the General Appropriations Act; (2) income from fines, fees and charges
that it is authorized to impose and collect; and more importantly, (3) Five percent (5%) of the
total annual gross revenue of the preceding year, net of NTA, of each LGU in the Metropolitan
Manila.

       Hence, as the annual gross revenues of all the cities and municipality in the
Metropolitan Manila exponentially increase due to dynamic economic activities, the respective
shares of the MMDA proportionately increase with them. In fact, the total shares received by
MMDA from constituent cities and municipality stood at P4.533-billion in 2025, which is
substantially larger than its share from the NTA amounting to P0.817-billion in the same year
or 18.02%.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared a policy of the State to support
the economic growth and development of its regions through the equitable provision of
resources to be administered by local government units. Towards this end, more resources shall
be made available to provinces outside self-sustaining and urbanized areas to ensure equitable
progress and prosperity among Filipinos.

       SEC 2. Section 10 of Republic Act No. 7924 is hereby amended to read as follows:

       “Section 10. Sources of Funds and the Operating Budget of MMDA:

               (a)     To carry out the purposes of this Act, the amount of One billion pesos
                       (P1,000,000,000) is hereby authorized to be appropriated for the initial
                       operation of the MMDA. Thereafter, the annual expenditures including
                       capital outlays of the MMDA shall be provided in the General
                       Appropriations Act.

               (b)     The MMDA shall continue to receive the Internal Revenue Allotment
                       (IRA) currently allocated to the present MMA.

               (c)[B] The MMDA is likewise empowered by levy fines, and impose fees and
                      charges for various services rendered.
               (d) [C] Five percent (5%) of the total annual gross revenue of the preceding
                       year, net of the internal revenue allotment, of each local government unit
                       mentioned in Section 2 hereof, shall accrue and become payable
                       monthly to the MMDA by each city or municipality. In case of failure
                       to remit the said fixed contribution, the DBM shall cause the
                       disbursement of the same to MMDA chargeable against the IRA
                       allotment of the city or municipality concerned, the provisions of
                       Section 286 of RA 7160 to the contrary notwithstanding.”

        SEC. 3. Transitory Provision. – The updated NTA of provinces shall be adjusted in the
fiscal year immediately following the enactment of this Act.

        SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Budget and
Management shall, in coordination with other relevant government agencies, formulate the
rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act within thirty (30)
days after its approval.

        SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any section, clause or term of this Act
is held to be illegal, invalid, or unconstitutional, such parts not affected by such declaration
shall remain in full force and effect.

        SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, issuances, executive orders,
letters of instruction, rules, and regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 7. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
