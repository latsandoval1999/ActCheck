                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Constitution mandates that the State shall protect and promote the
right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them
(Article II, Section 15) and to undertake appropriate health, manpower
development and research responsive to the country’s health needs and
problems. (Article XIII, Section 12).

       In a report by the World Health Organization, dental care is among one
of the most neglected health aspects of an individual that has affected one’s
overall health, well-being, and quality life. The Department of Health’s
National Monitoring and Evaluation Dental Survey states that about 83
million Filipinos suffer from tooth decay and that seven out of ten Filipinos
have never sought the services of a dentist.

       With only less than 1% of Filipinos seeking treatment for dental care1,
oral disease remains to be a serious public health problem in the country
affecting the overall work performance, nutrition, social interactions, and self-
esteem of the people.

       To address the gaps and to improve the oral care and hygiene of the
Filipinos, this bill seeks to create a dental unit each of the rural health units
(RHUS) listed under the Department of Health.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Local Dental
Health Unit Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to pursue
the Primary Health Care Approach in the delivery of health services and to
undertake human resource development for this policy and to promote
preventive dental healthcare in the rural areas as part of the Primary Health
Care strategy.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. –

         a) “Rural Health Unit" - also known as Main Health Center. It is
            composed of a doctor, a nurse, and a midwife. Its function to
            carry out the public health functions of the Department of Health
            which includes both preventive and curative health care.

         b) “Dental Unit” - composed of a public health dentist and a trained
            Barangay Health Worker as a dental aide.
SECTION 4. Creation of a Dental Unit in every Rural Health Unit. – As
part of the Primary Health Care Approach, there is hereby created a dental
unit in every rural health unit under the supervision of the Department of
Health.

SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations – The Department of
Health, in coordination with local government units, shall issue the necessary
rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act within six (6)
months of the effectivity hereof.

SECTION 6. Funding. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be funded from the current fiscal year’s appropriation for the
Department of Health. Thereafter, such amount as may be necessary for the
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

SECTION 7. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act shall
be held unconstitutional or invalid, other provisions hereof which are not
affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
