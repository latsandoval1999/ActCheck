      AN ACT POSTPONING THE NOVEMBER 2026 BARANGAY AND
  SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN ELECTIONS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
                    REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12232


                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Republic Act No. 12232 fixed the term of office of barangay officials and members of
the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) at four (4) years to promote stability and continuity in
grassroots governance. The 1987 Constitution grants Congress the sole authority to define the
term duration for barangay officials under Article X, Section 8. While Congress is vested with
legislative authority over the scheduling of barangay and SK elections, such authority is not
absolute and must be exercised in a manner consistent with the Constitution, particularly the
people’s right of suffrage and the periodicity of elections.
        Jurisprudence, including Abas v. COMELEC and Macalintal v. COMELEC, clarifies
that any postponement of elections must be justified by genuine, compelling, and sufficiently
weighty state interests, and must not unduly infringe upon the constitutional mandate of regular
and periodic elections.
        Taking cognizance of the state of national energy emergency declared by the President
of the Philippines due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, there is a need to postpone
the forthcoming Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections. The prevailing socio-
economic challenges that have significantly impacted energy supply, inflation, and the cost of
essential goods, these conditions strain both government resources and Filipino households.
The conduct of the November 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections under such
conditions may impose substantial fiscal pressures on the government and the electorate and
may affect the efficient and orderly administration of the electoral process.
        This bill, therefore, seeks to postpone the November 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang
Kabataan Elections to May 2027 only as an exceptional and temporary measure, and subject to
the standards laid down by the Supreme Court, while ensuring that any extension of terms
through hold-over is strictly limited, reasonable, and consistent with constitutional principles.
       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:
SECTION 1. Declaration of Policies. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to ensure
the orderly, credible, and meaningful conduct of elections, consistent with the constitutional
guarantee of suffrage and the promotion of the general welfare.

While the scheduling of Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan elections promotes stability in
local governance pursuant to Republic Act No. 12232, the State recognizes that postponement
thereof may be justified under extraordinary circumstances such as energy crisis to protect
public welfare and preserve the integrity of the electoral process.

In view of the state of national energy emergency declared by the President of the Philippines
due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, which has resulted in significant increases in
fuel prices, energy costs, and basic commodities, the State adopts the policy of postponing the
2026 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections as a necessary and reasonable measure
to ensure economic stability, uninterrupted delivery of basic services at the barangay level, and
the conduct of elections under conditions conducive to genuine democratic participation;
Provided, That such postponement shall be temporary and the elections shall be held as
provided in this Act.

SEC. 2. – Section 2 of Republic Act No. 12232, is hereby amended to read as follows:

       “SEC. 2. Date of Election. – The next regular Barangay and Sangguniang
       Kabataan elections shall be held on the first Monday of [November 2026] MAY
       2027.

       SUBSEQUENT ELECTIONS SHALL BE HELD EVERY FOUR (4)
       YEARS THEREAFTER.”

SEC. 3. – Section 3 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

       “SEC. 3. Assumption of Office. – The term of office of barangay officials and
       members of the Sangguniang Kabataan elected after the MAY 2027 election upon
       effectivity of this Act shall commence on the first day of [December] JUNE next
       following their election.

       THE TERMS OF OFFICE OF BARANGAY OFFICIALS AND MEMBERS
       OF THE SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN OF SUBSEQUENT ELECTIONS
       THEREAFTER SHALL COMMENCE ON THE FIRST DAY OF JUNE
       FOLLOWING THEIR ELECTION.”

SEC. 3. Section 5 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

       “SEC. 5. Transitory Provision. – Incumbent elective barangay officials serving
       their third consecutive term in the same position shall not be eligible to run for the
       same position in the [November 2026] MAY 2027 Barangay and Sangguniang
       Kabataan Elections.”

SEC. 4. Hold-Over. – All incumbent barangay officials and members of the Sangguniang
Kabataan shall remain in office unless sooner removed or suspended for cause until their
successors shall have been elected and qualified.

SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity
of this Act, the Commission on Elections shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations
for the implementation of this Act.

SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not affected thereby shall remain
in force and effect.

SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, administrative
orders, and rules and regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.

SEC. 8. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect upon publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
