                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Death is inevitable. Yet, for many Filipino families, the burden of laying a
loved one to rest is compounded by financial constraints. In moments of grief,
families are often compelled to make urgent decisions on funeral arrangements
despite limited resources.

       The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) reported that
approximately ₱1.7 billion in burial assistance was provided to 165,907
beneficiaries under the Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)
program from January to September 2024, highlighting the number of Filipino
families who face difficulty in meeting funeral and burial costs.

       Funeral services including caskets, embalming, transportation of remains,
and related arrangements remain costly and often beyond the means of indigent
households. While existing mechanisms such as the DSWD’s AICS program and
local government unit (LGU) assistance provide relief, these are often limited,
discretionary, and insufficient to address prevailing market costs.

       Consistent with the State’s policy to promote consumer protection and
ensure access to essential services, this measure strengthens the regulatory role
of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) in monitoring funeral service
pricing and mandates all funeral establishments to offer affordable service
packages. It likewise reinforces the role of LGUs and the DSWD in identifying
qualified beneficiaries and facilitating access to available assistance, consistent
with existing social protection frameworks.

       This measure seeks to ensure the availability of affordable funeral services
for indigent and underprivileged families by regulating costs and promoting
accessible service packages. While not entirely free, it aims to prevent grieving
families from incurring undue financial burden or debt.


      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
   INSTITUTIONALIZING AFFORDABLE FUNERAL SERVICES FOR
   INDIGENT FAMILIES, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as “Affordable Funeral
Service Act”.

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
promote a just and dynamic social order that will ensure the prosperity and
independence of the nation and free the people from poverty through policies
that provide adequate social services, promote full employment, a rising
standard of living, and an improved quality of life for all. Pursuant thereto,
the State shall ensure that affordable funeral services are made available to
grief-stricken families, especially those classified as indigent and extremely
poor.

      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – The following terms as used in
this Act shall mean the following:

      (a) Funeral Services refers to a package of goods and services offered by a
          funeral establishment comprised of basic embalming or mortuary
          services, transportation of remains, provision of a casket or coffin, wake
          venue and equipment, funeral hearse and transport to burial or
          cremation sites;

      (b) Indigent Family refers to a family whose monthly income is equivalent
          to the minimum wage set in the region where they reside;

      (c) Extremely Poor Family refers to a family whose income falls below the
          subsistence threshold as defined by the Philippine Statistics Authority
          (PSA);

      (d) Implementing Agencies refer to the Department of Social Welfare and
          Development (DSWD) and the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI);
          and
      (e) Funeral Establishment includes funeral parlors, funeral chapels and
          any similar place used in the preparation, storage and care of the body
          of a deceased person for burial or cremation.

         SECTION 4. Availability of Affordable Funeral Services in All
Funeral Establishments. All funeral establishments shall make available at all
times a funeral service package for indigent families at a price not exceeding
Twenty Thousand Pesos (₱20,000.00).

      The package may be unbundled into individual component services;
Provided, That the total cost of the selected components shall not exceed the
prescribed amount, thereby allowing indigent families flexibility in choosing
services.

       In the event that a required service is unavailable, the funeral
establishment shall provide a comparable or higher value service at no additional
cost to the beneficiary.

      The price ceiling shall be adjusted annually in accordance with the
Consumer Price Index (CPI) as published by the Philippine Statistics Authority
(PSA). The DTI shall conduct a comprehensive review every two (2) years to
ensure that the price remains fair, reasonable, and responsive to prevailing
economic conditions.

      SECTION 5. Availment of Affordable Funeral Services. – The deceased
indigent’s family must present a Certificate of Indigency and a corresponding
case study proving their indigent status from either the medical social service of
a hospital, the local social welfare office or the DSWD.

      SECTION 6. Penalties. – Any violation of this Act shall be penalized with
a fine not exceeding Two Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱200,000.00) and
suspension of the license to operate of the offending funeral establishment for a
period not exceeding six (6) months.

     Subsequent violations shall be penalized with a fine not exceeding Four
Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱400,000.00) and revocation of the license to operate.

      SECTION 7. Implementing Agencies. – The DTI shall monitor and
regulate funeral service pricing and enforce compliance with this Act.
      The DTI shall promulgate the implementing rules and regulations (IRR)
within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, in coordination with the
DSWD and other relevant agencies.

      The DSWD shall continue to provide financial assistance, where
applicable, under the AICS program and shall assist in identifying qualified
beneficiaries and facilitating access to services.

     SECTION 8. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s
appropriations of the departments and agencies concerned. Thereafter, such
amount shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act
is declared void or unconstitutional, the remaining portions or provisions hereof
shall not be affected by such declaration.

      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. – Any provision of law or regulation
inconsistent herewith is hereby repealed, revoked, or modified accordingly.

       SECTION 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
