                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Masbate Electric Cooperative, Inc. (MASELCO) is the primary electric distribution
utility in the Province of Masbate, serving consumers across its three major islands — Masbate,
Ticao, and Burias. For decades, MASELCO has played a critical role in ensuring that the
people of Masbate have access to electricity, which is indispensable for economic
growth, social development, and the improvement of quality of life.

However, MASELCO continues to face significant financial and operational challenges. The
cooperative has accumulated overdue obligations to the National Electrification
Administration (NEA) in the amount of P66,422,575.75 and the National Power
Corporation (NPC) in the amount of P140,230,959.27, including unpaid interests, penalties,
surcharges, and unremitted universal charges. These obligations have grown over time due
to factors beyond MASELCO’s immediate control, such as high systems losses, aging
infrastructure, natural calamities affecting power lines, and the rising costs of energy supply
and operations.

The financial distress of MASELCO has not only hindered its ability to settle its debts but has
also constrained its capacity to rehabilitate its distribution facilities, improve power reliability,
and expand electrification coverage to unserved and underserved communities in the
province. This proposed measure seeks to grant condonation of all unpaid interests,
penalties, surcharges, and unremitted universal charges on the overdue obligations of
the Masbate Electric Cooperative, Inc. to the National Electrification Administration
and the National Power Corporation. The intent is to allow MASELCO to recover
financially, restore operational stability, and reallocate its limited resources toward the
rehabilitation and modernization of its power distribution system.

By granting this relief, the government reaffirms its commitment to supporting the
sustainability of rural electric cooperatives, consistent with the objectives of the Rural
Electrification Program under Presidential Decree No. 269, as amended. The condonation
will not only ensure the continued viability of MASELCO but will also guarantee that the
people of Masbate—especially those in remote and island barangays—continue to receive
affordable, reliable, and uninterrupted electric service.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT FOR THE CONDONATION OF UNPAID INTERESTS, PENALTIES,
SURCHARGES, AND UNREMITTED UNIVERSAL CHARGES ON THE OVERDUE
OBLIGATONS OF THE MASBATE ELECTRIC COOPERATIVE, INC. TO THE
NATIONAL ELECTRIFICATION ADMINISTRATION (NEA) AND NATIONAL
POWER CORPORATION (NAPOCOR).

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. All accumulated interests, surcharges, penalties and universal charges on the
principal obligation incurred by the Masbate Electric Cooperative, Inc., to the National
Electrification Administration (NEA) and National Power Corporation (NAPOCOR), as of
December 31, 2025 are hereby condoned, thereby perpetually relieving MASELCO from the
payment of said interests, surcharges, penalties and universal charges. Moreover, the amount
paid by MASELCO of such accumulated interests, surcharges, and penalties, shall be applied
to and deducted from the principal obligation of MASELCO both to NEA and NAPOCOR.

SECTION 2. A one-time restructuring program is hereby authorized to be adopted by the NEA
and NAPOCOR in favor of MASELCO over their unpaid principal obligations; provided, that
once a staggered payment scheme shall have been adopted between MASELCO and the NEA
and NAPOCOR on the principal obligations, MASELCO shall regularly submit to NEA and
NAPOCOR their performance improvement programs and the status of their implementations.

SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity
of this Act, The National Electrification Administration and the National Power Corporation
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this
Act. Taking into account the legislative intent to authorize the condonation of interests,
surcharges, penalties and universal charges and the one-time restructuring program, the said
rules and regulations shall not entail additional requirements that shall negate the purpose of
this Act; Provided, that subject to MASELCO’s circumstances, NEA and NAPOCOR shall be
empowered to determine the guidelines on which MASELCO is eligible to request for
condonation and shall further require MASELCO to submit a Performance Improvement Plan
to ensure payment compliance; Provided further, that the failure of the NEA and NAPOCOR
to promulgate the rules and regulations shall not prevent the implementation of this Act sixty
days after its effectivity.
SECTION 4. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the other parts or provision hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be
in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders and issuances, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

SECTION 6. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following the completion
of its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulations.
