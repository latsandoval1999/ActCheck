                        THEREFOR


                           EXPLANATORY NOTE


Public utility jeepney, tricycle, and bus drivers absorb the full shock of
global oil price increases, often being forced to choose between raising
fares — which burdens commuters — or absorbing losses that push them
deeper into debt. This bill institutionalizes a fuel subsidy program that
activates automatically once pump prices breach a defined threshold,
rather than depending on ad hoc, one-time cash aid announced after
public protests.

It builds on the experience of prior fuel subsidy programs by making the
trigger, amount, and distribution mechanism permanent and predictable.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Public Transport Fuel
Relief Act."
      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State recognizes the vital
role of public utility vehicle (PUV) drivers and operators in national
transportation and shall protect them from the volatility of global fuel
prices.
      SECTION 3. Automatic Fuel Subsidy. — Whenever the prevailing
pump price of diesel or gasoline increases by fifteen percent (15%) or more
within a ninety (90)-day period, as monitored by the Department of Energy,
a fuel subsidy shall automatically be released to registered PUV drivers
and operators through the Land Transportation Franchising and
Regulatory Board (LTFRB) registry.
      SECTION 4. Coverage. — The subsidy shall cover operators and
drivers of jeepneys, UV Express units, buses, taxis, and tricycles duly
registered with the LTFRB or the concerned local government unit.
      SECTION 5. Fuel Subsidy Fund. — There is hereby created a
standing Fuel Subsidy Fund under the Department of Energy, to be
replenished annually through the General Appropriations Act and
augmented by a portion of excise tax collections on petroleum products
during periods of sustained high prices.
      SECTION 6. Distribution Mechanism. — Subsidies shall be
distributed through fuel discount cards, direct fuel vouchers, or electronic
cash transfer, at the option of the driver or operator, to minimize leakage
and delay.

     SECTION 7. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year's
appropriations of the Department of Energy, and thereafter included in the
annual General Appropriations Act.
      SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The
Department of Energy shall issue implementing rules within sixty (60)
days from effectivity.
      SECTION 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is
declared invalid, the other provisions not affected shall remain in force.
     SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive
orders, and issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.
      SECTION 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
