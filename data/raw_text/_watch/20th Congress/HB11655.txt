                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Philippines has an obligation to protect and promote the right to nutrition
and adequate food under relevant domestic and international laws: 1987 Philippine
Constitution; Universal Declaration of Human Rights; International Covenant on
Economic, Social, and Cultural Rights; Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of
Discrimination Against Women; Convention on the Rights of the Child, as well as the
United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.

        In line with this, several offices of government have taken on the task of
directly or indirectly protecting and promoting the right to nutrition and adequate
food, namely: The National Nutrition Council, created through Presidential Decree
(PD) No. 491, s. 1974; National Food Authority, established through PD No. 4, s.
1972; Anti-Hunger Task Force, created through Executive Order (EO) No. 616, s.
2007; Inter-Agency Task Force on Hunger, established through EO No. 101, s. 2020;
Presidential Assistant for Food Security and Agricultural Modernization under the
Office of the President, established through Memorandum Order No. 70, s. 2014;
Food and Nutrition Research Institute, which is one of the research and development
institutes of the Department of Science and Technology; and, Department of
Agriculture, specifically through its Food Staples Sufficiency Program.

       While the government has committed to AmBisyon Natin 2040, which
promises a strongly rooted, comfortable, and secure life for every Filipino, where “no
one is poor, no one is ever hungry,” undernutrition and malnutrition remain as
perennial problems besetting the country. In fact, the Philippines missed its
Millennium Development Goal of lowering the undernutrition and malnutrition rate to
15% by 2015, and the problem appears to be getting worse. The World Bank reports

                                          1
that as of 2019, one in every three Filipino children younger than five years old suffer
from stunting, resulting in the country being ranked fifth among countries in the East
Asia and Pacific Region with the highest number of stunted children.

      The effect of COVID 19 on the economy has made hunger, undernutrition and
malnutrition even worse in the country. The Social Weather Stations reported an
alarming 31% of families experiencing hunger, and 9% suffering severe hunger in
September 2020, seven months after the enforcement community quarantine—in
both cases, the highest levels recorded in more than 20 years.

        In this regard, this Bill seeks to create a National Nutrition Commission to
serve as the overarching agency responsible for establishing a unified policy
direction and a comprehensive approach towards food accessibility and security,
improved nutrition and overall health development of the Filipino people. This shall
replace the existing National Nutrition Council, as well as streamline the nutrition
functions rendered by other offices of government. Furthermore, nutrition programs
at sub-national levels shall be guaranteed with manpower complement, as a way of
improving nutrition performance.

       Immediate passage of this bill is hereby earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
CREATING THE NATIONAL NUTRITION COMMISSION, REPEALING FOR THE
PURPOSE PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 491, ENTITLED “CREATING A
NATIONAL NUTRITION COUNCIL AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

    Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “National Nutrition
Commission Act.”

      Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State recognizes nutrition as a basic
human right and guarantees the right to adequate food, which should be
implemented by the government in an integrated manner.

       Section 3. The National Nutrition Commission. - There is hereby created a
National Nutrition Commission (“Commission”), which will be attached to the Office
of the President. The Commission shall be the primary policy-making, coordinating,
implementing and advisory body on nutrition and food concerns.

      Section 4. Powers and Functions of the Commission. - The Commission shall
have the following powers and functions:




                                        3
      1) Formulate national food and nutrition policies and strategies, and serve as
         the policy, coordinating and advisory body of food, nutrition and health
         concerns;

      2) Monitor and evaluate the implementation of its programs, projects, and
         activities undertaken by all agencies and instrumentalities of both the
         government and private sector concerning nutrition and food;

      3) Recommend to Congress measures to effectively promote the full
         realization of the right to nutrition and adequate food, and the
         harmonization of existing laws and issuances addressing food, nutrition
         and health concerns through the review of relevant administrative and
         legislative framework;

      4) Conduct investigations upon all matters relating to the enforcement of the
         right to nutrition, health, and adequate food, and assist individuals and
         group of individuals to have access to legal assistance to better assert the
         progressive realization of their abovementioned rights;

      5) Call on any department, bureau, office, agency and other instrumentalities
         of the government for assistance in the form of personnel, facilities and
         resources as the need arises;


      6) Strengthen competencies and capabilities of stakeholders through public
         education, capacity building, and skills development;

      7) Coordinate the release of funds, loans, and grants from government
         organizations and non-government organizations;


      8) Provide monetary and non-monetary incentives to nutrition champions by
         establishing rewards and recognition system;


      9) Establish a continuing program for information dissemination and
         awareness; and

      10) Exercise such other powers and functions as are necessary for the
          implementation of this Act.

      Section 5. Composition of the Commission. - The Commission shall be
composed of a Chairperson and two (2) Commissioners, all of whom shall be
appointed by the President for a term of four (4) years, without prejudice to one
reappointment. They shall continue to hold office until their successors have been


                                         4
appointed. Any Commissioner who shall be appointed to fill a vacancy shall serve
only the unexpired portion of the term of the predecessor.

       Section 6. The Chairperson and the Commissioners. - The Chairperson and
the Commissioners of the Commission must be citizens of the Philippines; with at
least ten (10) years of experience in the fields of food security, good nutrition
promotion, and mitigation of hunger, and malnutrition; at least thirty (35) years of
age; and, of good moral character. At least one (1) of them must be duly licensed to
practice Medicine in the Philippines.

       The Chairperson and Commissioners shall receive annual compensation and
shall be entitled to other benefits and privileges provided under existing laws for a
Department Undersecretary and Department Assistant Secretaries, respectively.
They shall not, during their tenure, hold any other office or employment, or in the
active management or control of any business which, in any way, may affect the
functions of their office.

      Section 7. Advisory Board. - There shall be constituted an Advisory Board
which shall meet with the Commission at least once a year to assist it with its policies
and plans.

       The Board shall be composed of the following offices and agencies:

       1) Department of Agriculture
       2) Department of Agrarian Reform
       3) Department of Education
       4) Department of Health
       5) Department of the Interior and Local Government
       6) Department of Social Welfare and Development
       7) Department of Budget and Management
       8) Department of Science and Technology
       9) Department of Trade and Industry
       10)Commission on Human Rights
       11) National Anti-Poverty Commission
       12) Council for the Welfare of Children
       13)National Economic and Development Authority
       14)National Food Authority
       15) Philippine Statistics Authority
       16) League of Provinces of the Philippines
       17) League of Cities of the Philippines
       18) League of Municipalities of the Philippines
       19)Nutrition Foundation of the Philippines
       20)Nutrition Center of the Philippines

                                           5
      The members of the Advisory Board shall receive per diem for actual
attendance in board meetings and reimbursement for actual and reasonable
expenses and incentives as authorized by the Commission.

       Section 8. Composition of the Secretariat. - The Commission shall establish a
secretariat to be headed by an Executive Director, who must be a natural born
citizen of the Philippines; at least thirty-five (35) years of age on the day of the
appointment; holder of a college degree; with at least five (5) years executive and
management experience; with considerable exposure to food security, good nutrition
promotion, and mitigation of hunger and malnutrition; and, a person of good moral
character: Provided, That he or she shall be a career officer.

      The Commission shall hire such other personnel as may be necessary for the
performance of its basic functions.

        Section 9. Nutrition Office. – For the effective implementation of nutrition
programs at sub-national levels, every province and city/municipality shall have a
nutrition office. The Provincial Nutrition Office shall be headed by a Provincial
Nutrition Action Officer, supported by a Provincial Nutrition Coordinator and other
adequate staff complement. The City/Municipal Nutrition Office shall be headed by a
City/Municipal Nutrition Action Officer, supported by other adequate staff
complement.

       Plantilla positions for the staff composing the said offices shall be determined
by the Commission and created by local government units, in consultation with the
Civil Service Commission and the Department of Budget and Management.

        Section 10. Research and Policy Development Support. - The Food and
Nutrition Research Institute of the Department of Science and Technology, as well as
other agencies of government and private entities which are performing nutrition
research, shall cooperate with the Commission for research and policy development.

       Section 11. Donations. – The Commission shall be authorized to receive
donations, contributions, grants, or gifts in furtherance of the objectives of this Act:
Provided, That said donations, grants, or gifts shall be administered, obligated and
disbursed in accordance with the terms thereto and in such manner as the
Commission may in its discretion determine.

       Any donation, contribution, grant, or gift which may be made to the
Commission shall constitute as allowable deduction from the income of the donor for
income tax purposes and shall be exempt from donor’s tax, subject to such
conditions as provided under the National Internal Revenue Code, as amended.

                                           6
       Section 12. Transitory Provisions. - The National Nutrition Council (“Council”)
is reconstituted as the National Nutrition Commission. Such personnel, properties,
assets and liabilities, functions and responsibilities of the Council are hereby
transferred to or absorbed by the Commission. Incumbent officials and employees
shall continue to exercise their respective functions and responsibilities with the
corresponding benefits and privileges.

      The incumbent Executive Director and Deputy Executive Directors of the
Council shall serve as Chairperson and Commissioners, respectively, until formally
appointed by the President in accordance with Section 5. Thereafter, the
Chairperson shall appoint the Commission’s new Executive Director.

        Section 13. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the
provisions of this Act, with the exception of Section 9, shall be charged to the current
fiscal year appropriations of the National Nutrition Council. Thereafter, such sum as
may be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall be included in
the annual General Appropriations Act.

       Section 14. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days
from the appointment of the Chairperson and Commissioners, the Commission, in
coordination with the members of the Advisory Board, shall promulgate the
necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

       Section 15. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part hereof is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or the provision or part not
otherwise affected shall remain valid and subsisting.

      Section 16. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees or issuances,
executive orders, rules and regulations and other issuances contrary to or
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, modified, or
amended accordingly.

       Section 17. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

       Approved,
