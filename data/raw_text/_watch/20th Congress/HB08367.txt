                             AN ACT
  DECLARING THE MONTH OF APRIL OF EVERY YEAR AS “BAWAL BASTOS
 AWARENESS MONTH” AND MANDATING A NATIONAL CAMPAIGN AGAINST
 GENDER-BASED SEXUAL HARASSMENT IN ACCORDANCE WITH REPUBLIC
    ACT NO. 11313, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “SAFE SPACES ACT”

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Republic Act No. 11313, otherwise known as the Safe Spaces Act, affirms the State’s
commitment to ensure that all individuals are protected from gender-based sexual
harassment in streets, public spaces, workplaces, educational institutions, and online
platforms. Despite the enactment of this landmark legislation, many incidents of gender-
based harassment continue to be underreported, while awareness of the rights and
protections guaranteed under the law remains limited among the general public.

Gender-based sexual harassment—commonly referred to as bastos behavior in public
spaces—undermines the dignity, safety, and equality of individuals, particularly women
and members of vulnerable sectors. Catcalling, unwanted sexual remarks, stalking,
online harassment, and other forms of gender-based misconduct create environments of
fear and intimidation that restrict the freedom of individuals to move, work, study, and
participate fully in society.

While laws exist to penalize such acts, prevention through education, public awareness,
and cultural change remains crucial. A sustained nationwide campaign can help cultivate
a culture of respect, accountability, and gender sensitivity while encouraging victims and
witnesses to report violations and seek redress.

This bill therefore seeks to declare the month of April of every year as Bawal Bastos
Awareness Month, which is aligned with the enactment of the Safe Spaces Act on April
17, 2019. The annual observance will provide a dedicated platform for government
agencies, local government units, educational institutions, and private organizations to
conduct information campaigns, seminars, trainings, and other activities aimed at
promoting the provisions of the Safe Spaces Act and preventing gender-based sexual
harassment.

Through a coordinated national campaign led by the Philippine Commission on Women
and other relevant agencies, the observance will strengthen public awareness, reinforce
reporting and accountability mechanisms, and encourage institutions to adopt proactive
measures that ensure safe and respectful spaces for all.

This bill will help deepen public understanding of gender-based violence, promote
respect in both physical and digital spaces, and strengthen the implementation of
existing laws protecting the dignity and rights of every person.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                    AN ACT
       DECLARING THE MONTH OF APRIL OF EVERY YEAR AS “BAWAL BASTOS
      AWARENESS MONTH” AND MANDATING A NATIONAL CAMPAIGN AGAINST
      GENDER-BASED SEXUAL HARASSMENT IN ACCORDANCE WITH REPUBLIC
         ACT NO. 11313, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “SAFE SPACES ACT”

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1   SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bawal Bastos Awareness
 2   Month Act.”
 3
 4   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 5   recognize the role of women in nation-building and ensure the fundamental equality
 6   before the law of women and men. The State shall likewise guarantee the protection of
 7   all persons, especially women, from all forms of gender-based violence and harassment
 8   in streets, public spaces, workplaces, educational institutions, and online platforms.
 9
10   Pursuant to the provisions of Republic Act No. 9710, otherwise known as the “Magna
11   Carta of Women,” and Republic Act No. 11313, otherwise known as the “Safe Spaces Act,”
12   the State shall promote awareness and strengthen mechanisms to prevent and respond
13   to gender-based sexual harassment.
14
15   SECTION 3. Bawal Bastos Awareness Month. – The month of April of every year is
16   hereby declared as “Bawal Bastos Awareness Month.” During this period, a nationwide
17   campaign shall be conducted to raise public awareness on the prevention of gender-based
18   sexual harassment in streets, public spaces, workplaces, educational institutions, and
19   online platforms.
20
21   SECTION 4. Implementing Agencies. – The Philippine Commission on Women (PCW)
22   shall lead the observance of Bawal Bastos Awareness Month in coordination with the
23   Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), the Department of Information
24   and Communications Technology (DICT), the Department of Education (DepEd), the
25   Commission on Higher Education (CHED), the Technical Education and Skills Development
26   Authority (TESDA), and other relevant government agencies.
27
28   Local Government Units (LGUs), government agencies, educational institutions, and
29   private establishments are hereby encouraged to participate in the observance through
30   information campaigns, seminars, trainings, and other appropriate activities aimed at
31   preventing gender-based sexual harassment.
32
33   SECTION 5. Compliance Audit. – The Philippine Commission on Women (PCW), in
34   coordination with relevant agencies, shall conduct periodic compliance audits of
35   government agencies, local government units, schools, and private establishments with
36   respect to the provisions of Republic Act No. 11313, including but not limited to:
37
38      a. Installation of clearly visible warning signs against gender-based sexual
39         harassment in public establishments;
40      b. Establishment of mechanisms for reporting and addressing complaints of sexual
41         harassment;
42      c. Conduct of gender sensitivity seminars and awareness campaigns; and
43      d. Implementation of educational modules and workplace policies addressing gender-
44         based sexual harassment.
45
46   SECTION 6. Participation of Educational Institutions. – All public and private educational
47   institutions shall support the annual observance of Bawal Bastos Awareness Month by
48   conducting age-appropriate educational programs, discussions, and activities that
49   promote respect, gender sensitivity, and awareness of the Safe Spaces Act.
50
51   SECTION 7. Appropriations. – The funding necessary for the implementation of this Act
52   shall be charged against the existing appropriations of the implementing agencies.
53   Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be included in the annual General
54   Appropriations Act.
55
56   SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
57   effectivity of this Act, the Philippine Commission on Women (PCW), in coordination with
58   concerned agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
59   implementation of this Act.
60
61   SECTION 9. Separability Clause. - If any clause, sentence, paragraph or part of this Act
62   shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, such judgment shall not affect, invalidate or
63   impact any other part of this Act.
64
65   SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. - Any provision of laws, orders, agreements, rules or
66   regulations contrary to and inconsistent with this Act is hereby repealed, amended or
67   modified accordingly.
68
69   SECTION 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
70   in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
