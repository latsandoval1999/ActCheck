                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

     Teachers play a vital role in shaping the minds of the youth and in
promoting national development through quality education. Their dedication,
commitment, and sacrifices deserve meaningful recognition and continued
support from the State.

      In recognition of the invaluable contribution of teachers, Congress enacted
Republic Act No. 10743, declaring October 5 of every year as National Teachers’
Day, in line with the observance of World Teachers’ Day established by the
United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO).

      Since 2019, public school teachers have been granted a World Teachers’
Day Incentive Benefit (WTDIB) through the annual General Appropriations Act
in the amount of One Thousand Pesos (₱1,000). However, the grant remains
dependent on yearly appropriations and has not been adjusted despite
increasing economic challenges and the rising cost of living.

       This bill seeks to institutionalize the annual grant of the World Teachers’
Day Incentive Benefit and increase the amount to Three Thousand Pesos (₱3,000)
for every public school teacher. The measure aims to ensure the continuous and
consistent release of the incentive while providing greater recognition and
support to teachers for their indispensable service to the nation.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
      INSTITUTIONALIZING THE ANNUAL GRANT OF THE WORLD
      TEACHERS’ DAY INCENTIVE BENEFIT FOR PUBLIC SCHOOL
      TEACHERS AND INCREASING THE AMOUNT OF THE
      INCENTIVE TO THREE THOUSAND PESOS

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Annual World
Teachers’ Day Incentive Benefit Act.”

     SECTION 2. Declaration of Principles. - It is the policy of the State to
promote the welfare of public school teachers and provide meaningful recognition
and support for their invaluable contribution to nation building.

      SECTION 3. Annual World Teachers’ Day Incentive Benefit. - There is
hereby established an Annual World Teachers’ Day Incentive Benefit in the
amount of Three Thousand Pesos (₱3,000), which shall be given to each public
school teacher not earlier than October 5 of every year, subject to guidelines to
be issued by the Department of Education.

      SECTION 4. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to implement this
Act shall be included in the Annual General Appropriations Act.

      SECTION 5. Effectivity. – This act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in the newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
