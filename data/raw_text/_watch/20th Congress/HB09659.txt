                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Metro Manila has long been recognized as one of the world’s megacities,
with severe traffic congestion and consistently ranking among the most
congested urban areas globally. Recent data from the TomTom Traffic Index
(2024) continues to place Metro Manila among the slowest-moving cities in
Asia, with average travel times significantly exceeding pre-pandemic levels.
While temporary relief was observed during the height of COVID-19
lockdowns, congestion has since rebounded sharply with the economy's full
reopening.

        The Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) estimates that
traffic congestion in Metro Manila costs the Philippine economy approximately
Php 3.5 billion daily, and, without substantial intervention, this figure is
projected to increase to Php 5.4 billion per day by 2035 (JICA, “Roadmap for
Transport Infrastructure Development for Metro Manila and Its Surrounding
Areas,” 2014). More recent assessments suggest that actual losses may
already be higher due to inflation and prolonged commute times, underscoring
the need for practical and sustainable interventions.

      As a measure to help ease traffic congestion in Metro Manila—
particularly in the Fifth District of Quezon City—this bill proposes the
establishment of bicycle-friendly community lanes in Barangay North
Fairview, Quezon City. The measure aims to promote cycling as a safe, viable,
and sustainable alternative mode of transportation, while improving road
safety and accessibility at the barangay level.

      Among the various modes of transport, cycling is widely recognized as
environmentally sustainable, producing zero emissions and minimal noise. It
also provides health benefits, serving as a form of regular physical activity that
helps reduce stress and improve overall well-being (World Health
Organization, 2020). Thus, promoting bike-friendly communities not only
addresses economic losses from traffic congestion but also contributes to
environmental protection and public health.

      With the implementation of this measure, the barangay is positioned to
move toward a safer, cost-efficient, and sustainable transport system for its
residents.

      In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is urgently sought.

                          AN ACT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bike-friendly
Barangay North Fairview Act”.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to pursue economic growth consistent with its obligation to promote and
protect the global environment to attain sustainable development.

Pursuant thereto, it shall endeavor to promote and encourage among its
citizens an alternative mode of transportation mechanism that shall have a
long-term positive environmental and health impact.

SECTION 3. Definition. – As used in this Act, the following terms are defined
as:
    a. Bike or Bicycle -a non-motorized vehicle consisting of a light frame
       mounted on two wire-spoked wheels, one behind the other, and having
       a seat, handlebars for steering, brakes, and two pedals.
    b. Cyclist – a person who rides a bicycle
    c. Bike lanes – a portion of the roadway, designated by striping and
       pavement markings, designated for the exclusive use of cyclists
    d. Safety gear -protective clothing and equipment designed to ensure
       cyclists are safe from potential injury while riding. This can include a
      helmet, gloves, kneecaps, protective glasses, reflectors, and a bell on
      the bike.

SECTION 4. Bicycle-Friendly Community Program. The Barangay shall
establish a bike program to promote safe biking on all barangay roads of
Barangay North Fairview, Quezon City. The program shall include the
construction and maintenance of bicycle lanes, parking, and support
facilities. The Barangay shall also create an educational and awareness
program on traffic safety.

SECTION 5. Bicycle Lanes – There shall be designated bicycle lanes in all
barangay roads of Barangay North Fairview, Quezon City that would serve as
exclusive access for bicycle riders. All motorized vehicles shall be prohibited
from being driven or parked on any bicycle lane.

The bicycle lanes shall be separated by a physical barrier and shall be clearly
identified with signs and/or pavement markings. In cases when the
installation of a physical barrier is not feasible, the lane for bicycles shall be
identified through reflectorized yellow painted lines. Bicycle lanes must never
compromise the mobility and safety of pedestrians.

The bicycle lanes should traverse across all roads of the barangay and connect
to all major roads of the city.

SECTION 6. Bicycle Parking. All public places, government offices, schools,
major business establishments, including malls, banks, restaurants,
hospitals, and the like, are hereby required to provide bicycle racks for bicycle
parking.

Bicycle parking should be secure, visible, accessible, and not in the way of
pedestrians or motor vehicles.

SECTION 7. Bicycle Infrastructure. – In addition to the bicycle lanes and
bicycle parking areas, support facilities shall be established to ensure that
cyclists and their bicycles can travel safely and to lessen the possibility of
collisions with pedestrians. These facilities shall include, but are not limited
to, the following:

   a. Sidewalk improvements;
   b. Traffic and speed reduction improvements;
   c. Pedestrian and bicycle crossing improvements;
   d. Traffic signage pertaining to bicycles;
   e. Off-street pedestrian and bicycle facilities; and
   f. Traffic diversion improvements.

SECTION 8. Educational Program. The Barangay shall establish an
educational program to increase awareness of bike safety and road sharing.
The program shall be designed for traffic enforcers, motorists, cyclists, and
the commuting public. The program shall include:

   a. Traffic education and enforcement;
   b. Motorists' awareness and road-sharing; and
   c. Bicycle and pedestrian safety.

SECTION 9. Incentives. The Barangay shall create an incentive mechanism
for all private and public establishments that will encourage their employees
to ride bicycles by providing for bicycle facilities within their establishments.

SECTION 10. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the National Tax
Allotment (NTA) of the Quezon City LGU, specifically for Barangay North
Fairview.

SECTION 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within one hundred
eighty (180) days after the approval of this Act, the local government unit
shall, in consultation with the Department of Public Works and Highways, the
Department of Transportation, and such other relevant government agencies
and stakeholders, prepare and issue the necessary rules and regulations for
the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 12. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act
is declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain
in full force and effect.

SECTION 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, or issuances or any part thereof which are inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 14. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general
circulation.
