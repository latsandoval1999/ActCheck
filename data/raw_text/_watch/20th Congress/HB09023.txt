                             AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A UNIFIED TRANSPORT REGISTRY SYSTEM FOR PUBLIC

ELIGIBILITY, STREAMLINED VERIFICATION, AND REGULAR UPDATING OF
BENEFICIARY DATABASES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The public transport sector remains a critical pillar of the Philippine economy,
ensuring mobility for millions of Filipinos daily. However, the effective delivery of
government assistance to transport workers has long been hindered by fragmented,
outdated, and difficult-to-access verification systems. These inefficiencies have
resulted in delays, exclusion of legitimate beneficiaries, and administrative burdens
that undermine the intent of social protection programs.

       For instance, the Fuel Subsidy Program recently rolled out by the government
targeted about 1.43 million beneficiaries from the transport sector. However, only
about 201,000 or 14.1% of the public transport workers were able to receive the said
government subsidy . 1

        A significant portion of transport workers operate within the informal sector,
often lacking complete or updated documentation required under traditional
verification processes. As a result, many qualified drivers and operators are unable to
access timely assistance, particularly during periods of crisis such as fuel price surges
or national emergencies. At the same time, overly complex systems increase the risk
of duplication, inaccurate records, and the inclusion of ineligible beneficiaries.

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the Unified Transport
Registry System Act.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to promote equitable access to government assistance and social protection programs
to marginalized sectors, particularly public transport workers. Toward this this, the
government shall ensure the efficient, accurate, and inclusive identification of public
transport workers and establish a reliable, interoperable, and transparent database for
the transport sector to prevent duplication, exclusion, and delays in the delivery of
government services.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following terms
shall be defined as follows:
        (a) Unified Transport Registry System (UTRS) – a centralized and
            integrated digital database of all public transport workers and operators
            nationwide.
        (b) Public Transport Workers – include drivers, operators, conductors,
            dispatchers, and other personnel engaged in public land transportation
            services.
        (c) Transport Groups or Cooperatives – duly registered associations,
            corporations, or cooperatives of public transport workers and operators.
        (d) Member Verification – the process of confirming the identity and eligibility
            of individuals listed in the registry.
       SEC. 4. Establishment of the Unified Transport Registry System. – There
is hereby established a Unified Transport Registry System (UTRS), which shall serve
as the primary and authoritative database of all public transport workers in the country.

       The UTRS shall be the primary basis for:
       1. Distribution of fuel subsidies, financial assistance, and other government
          support;
       2. Policy planning and program implementation; and
       3. Emergency response measures affecting the transport sector.


       SEC. 5. Lead Implementing Agency. – The Department of Transportation
(DOTr) shall establish and manage the UTRS, in coordination with the Land
Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB), the Department of Social
Welfare and Development (DSWD), the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), the
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG); and the local government
units (LGUs).

     SEC. 6. Integration of Existing Databases. – The UTRS shall integrate and
harmonize data from:
      1. The DSWD national household and beneficiary databases;
      2. LTFRB records of franchise holders and drivers;
      3. PSA database;
      4. Databases of recognized transport groups and cooperatives; and
      5. Registries maintained by LGUs.

       All concerned agencies and entities shall ensure data sharing, subject to
applicable data privacy laws.

    SEC. 7. Inclusive Eligibility Criteria. – The UTRS shall adopt inclusive and
flexible eligibility criteria, ensuring that both formal and informal transport workers are
covered. Provided that, drivers and operators without complete documentation may
be provisionally registered, subject to validation. Provided, further, that membership in
transport groups or cooperatives shall be recognized as supporting proof of eligibility.
Provided, finally, that no qualified beneficiary shall be excluded due to technical or
documentary barriers.

       SEC. 8. Simplified Verification of Membership. – For faster delivery of social
assistance, the DOTr shall establish a simplified verification system by accepting the
following as proof of membership and eligibility:
        1. Certification by duly recognized transport groups or cooperatives;
        2. Endorsement by LGUs; and/or
        3. Cross-referencing with government databases.
The DOTr and LTFRB shall develop a standardized and digital verification system to
minimize processing time and administrative burden.

       SEC. 9. Registration and Updating. – Registration in the UTRS shall be
continuous and accessible and shall be available through online platforms, LGU
offices and authorized transport cooperatives and organizations.

      The database shall be updated regularly and validated every two (2) years. The
DOTr shall provide for mechanisms for real-time updating of status changes.

       SEC. 10. Data Privacy and Protection. – All data collected and maintained
under this Act shall be protected in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

        Appropriate safeguards shall be implemented to ensure confidentiality,
integrity, and proper use of personal information.

       SEC. 11. Funding. – The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be charged against appropriations under the General Appropriations Act, and
such other sources as may be identified by the government.

       SEC. 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DOTr, in coordination with concerned agencies, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations.

      SEC. 13. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder shall remain in full force and effect.

      SEC. 14. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 15. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
