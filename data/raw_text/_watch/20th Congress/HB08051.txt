                 PARA SA BATANG PILIPINO ACT”

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

The State recognizes the vital role of the youth in nation-building and shall promote
and protect their physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual, and social well-being according
to Section 13, Article II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution.

In line with this, Republic Act No. 11037 or the “Masustansyang Pagkain Para sa Bawat
Batang Pilipino Act” is enacted into law which aims to institutionalize a national feeding
program for undernourished children in day care centers and in public schools from
Kindergarten to Grade 6.

However, the prevalence of malnutrition among Filipino children is still rampant at an
alarming rate. According to the Year 3 Report of the Congressional Commission on
Education (EDCOM 2), 23.6% of children aged under five (5) years old are stunted
and 5.6% are wasted, impairing cognitive development. In addition, the prevalence
of stunting among poorest households reaches 42%. Further, more than 140,000
nutritionally-at-risk pregnant adolescents begin motherhood already facing lifelong
disadvantages. Meanwhile, around 200,000 malnourished children aged 2-4 are not
served by DSWD’s Supplemental Feeding Program, due to funding that still falls short
of what is required to address persistent child malnutrition.

Hence, this bill is filed to amend Republic Act No. 11037 to expand coverage to Filipino
children aged 2 years old in the DSWD Supplementary Feeding Program (SFP), and
nutritionally-at-risk pregnant adolescents in the DepEd School-Based Feeding Program
(SBFP). The said measure is also included in the Legislative-Executive Development
Advisory Council (LEDAC) Common Legislative Agenda (CLA) priority bills for passage.

In the view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.
