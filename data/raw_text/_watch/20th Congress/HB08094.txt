  IN EVERY BARANGAY OF THE FIFTH DISTRICT OF QUEZON CITY,

    INCENTIVES FOR LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS AND PRIVATE
          STAKEHOLDERS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The second Congressional Commission on Education (EDCOM II)
identified a severe foundational learning crisis in the Philippines, particularly in
early-grade literacy. A significant number of Filipino learners in Kindergarten to
Grade 3 are unable to read at grade level, undermining long-term educational
outcomes, workforce readiness, and national development.

       EDCOMII emphasized that schools alone cannot resolve the literacy crisis.
It called for stronger community-based interventions, expanded local
government unit (LGU) participation, and whole-of-society approaches to
foundational learning. Barangays, as the most basic political units, are uniquely
positioned to provide accessible and localized reading support that complements
schooling.

      This measure seeks to institutionalize Barangay Community Reading
Center in every barangay within the Fifth District of Quezon City, as non-formal,
supplementary learning hubs focused on foundational literacy. These Centers
are envisioned to support early-grade learners and identified non-readers
through structured reading mediation, enrichment activities, and community-
led literacy   programs   aligned   with   existing   Department   of   Education
frameworks.

       By adopting a results-oriented, incentive-based approach, this measure
enables the barangays of the Fifth District of Quezon City to pilot a sustainable
model for literacy support that complements formal schooling, promotes civic
participation, and strengthens partnerships among local government units,
schools, civil society, and the private sector.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “District V
Community Reading Centers and Foundational Literacy Support Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to prioritize foundational literacy as a cornerstone of quality education. The State
shall promote community-based, inclusive, and collaborative approaches to
improve reading proficiency, and collaborative approaches to improve reading
proficiency among learners and non-readers through localized interventions that
complement formal schooling.

SECTION 3. Coverage. This Act shall apply to all fourteen (14) barangays
presently comprising the Fifth District of Quezon City, namely: Bagbag, Capri,
Fairview, Gulod, Greater Lagro, Kaligayahan, Nagkaisang Nayon, North Fairview,
Novaliches Proper, Pasong Putik, San Agustin, San Bartolome, Sta. Lucia, and
Sta. Monica.

SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:
   1. Community Reading Center (CRC) - refers to a barangay-based, non-
      formal learning facility that provides structured reading support,
      remediation, and literacy activities for Kindergarten to Grade 3 learners
      and other identified non-readers;

   2. Foundational Literacy – refers to the ability to read with understanding
      and fluency appropriate to early grade levels;

   3. Reading Facilitator – refers to a trained individual who conducts reading
      sessions and literacy interventions in a Community Reading Center;

   4. Private Supporting Establishments - refer to private entities, including
      corporations, partnerships, cooperatives, and sole proprietorships, that
      provide financial, material, or technical assistance to Community Reading
      Centers.

SECTION 5. Establishment of Community Reading Centers. Community
Reading Center shall be established in every barangay within the Fifth District
of Quezon City, subject to the availability of suitable space and resources.

       Community Reading Centers may be housed in existing facilities such as
barangay halls, day care centers, public or private schools after class hours, or
other suitable community spaces. No new building shall be required unless
existing facilities are unavailable.

SECTION 6. Functions of Community Reading Centers. – Community
Reading Centers shall:

   1. Provide reading remediation and enrichment programs aligned with
      DepEd-approved literacy frameworks, including existing programs such as
      Early Language, Literacy, and Numeracy (ELLN) and similar initiatives;

   2. Support learners in Kindergarten to Grade 3 and other identified
      struggling or non-readers;

   3. Conduct or support regular reading assessments in coordination with
      partner schools;

   4. Engage parents, guardians, and community volunteers in literacy
      activities;

   5. Serve as venues for reading camps, storytelling sessions, and community-
      based literacy programs.
SECTION 7. Reading Facilitators and Capacity Building. Reading activities
shall be conducted by trained Reading Facilitators, who may include Alternative
Learning System (ALS) teachers, public school teachers on voluntary
assignment, barangay child development workers, or accredited volunteers.

      Relevant training and capacity-building programs for Reading Facilitators
shall be conducted in coordination with the Schools Division Office of Quezon
City and concerned agencies.

SECTION 8. Integration with Public and Private Schools. Community
Reading Centers shall complement and support existing public and private
school literacy programs and shall not replace formal instruction.

      Schools are encouraged to partner with Community Reading Centers for
learner referral, reading diagnostics, remediation, and progress monitoring.

SECTION 9. Roles of Local Government Units. Barangays within the Fifth
District shall serve as primary implementers of Community Reading Centers.
       The Quezon City Government shall provide technical, administrative, and
logistical support to barangays, including access to training, learning materials,
and coordination with schools and relevant national government agencies.
SECTION 10. Incentives for Barangays. Barangays with operational and
compliant     Community   Reading   Centers      demonstrating       measurable
improvements in reading outcomes shall be eligible for incentives, including but
not limited to:

      a. Priority access to literacy-related training programs and educational
         assistance;
      b. Public recognition through awards or certification programs
         administered by the City Government; and
      c. Access to grants or support from partner institutions.

SECTION 11. Incentives for Private Supporting Establishments. Private
Supporting Establishments that donate books, learning materials, equipment,
facilities, or financial support to accredited Community Reading Centers shall be
entitled to tax incentives in accordance with existing provisions of the National
Internal Revenue Code, as amended.

SECTION 11. Funding. Funding for Community Reading Centers shall be
sourced from the Special Education Fund (SEF) of Quezon City, subject to
existing budgeting, accounting, and autditing rules and regulations, as well as
from from grants or donations form private and civil society partners.
       Barangays shall not be required to fund Community Reading Centers
solely from their regular funds.


SECTION 13. Monitoring, Evaluation, and Congressional Oversight. The
Quezon City Government, in coordination with the Schools Division Office of
Quezon City, shall establish a monitoring and evaluation system focused on
literacy outcomes, learner participation, and program effectiveness.
SECTION 14. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DepEd, in coordination with the Quezon City
Government and other concerned agencies, shall promulgate the necessary
implementing rules and regulations.
SECTION 15. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act shall be
declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not otherwise affected
shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, orders, rules, and regulations, other
issuances, or any part thereof, inconsistent with any provision of this Act, are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 14. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
