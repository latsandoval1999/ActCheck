AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE CYBERSECURITY
      OPERATIONS CENTER FOR THE PROTECTION OF PNP CRITICAL
 INFORMATION INFRASTRUCTURE, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS,
     APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Philippine National Police (PNP) increasingly relies on digital systems,
interconnected networks, electronic databases, and online frontline services in performing its
law-enforcement and public-safety functions. While these technologies have improved the
delivery of police services, they have also exposed the organization to cyberattacks, unauthorized
access, data breaches, malware, service disruptions, and other forms of cyber intrusion.

       A successful attack against PNP systems may interrupt essential police operations,
compromise sensitive and special personal information, impair the delivery of public services,
and weaken public confidence in law-enforcement institutions. The reported breach involving the
PNP Firearms and Explosives Office illustrates the serious consequences that may arise when
systems containing sensitive government and citizen information are not adequately protected.

        Cybersecurity within the PNP can no longer be treated solely as a routine information
technology function. It is an essential component of operational readiness, data protection, public
safety, and national security. The protection of PNP Critical Information Infrastructure must
therefore be supported by a permanent, specialized, and coordinated institutional capability.

       This measure seeks to establish the PNP Cybersecurity Operations Center, or PNP
CSOC, as the primary operational and technical unit of the PNP responsible for cybersecurity
operations, cyber threat intelligence, and incident response for the protection of PNP Critical
Information Infrastructure. It is consistent with the declared policy of the State to strengthen the
cybersecurity posture of law-enforcement agencies and ensure the security, integrity,
confidentiality, and availability of PNP systems and online frontline services.

        The PNP CSOC will provide a centralized mechanism for continuously monitoring cyber
threats, identifying vulnerabilities and emerging attack methods, analyzing cybercrime trends
that may affect PNP systems, and coordinating timely responses to significant cybersecurity
incidents. Instead of relying on fragmented or purely reactive measures, the Center will allow the
PNP to adopt a unified and proactive approach to the protection of its digital assets and critical
systems.

        The Center will also provide specialized technical and digital forensic support to PNP
units investigating cybercrime offenses that directly affect, or originate from attacks against,
PNP Critical Information Infrastructure. This includes the proper identification, collection,
examination, preservation, and handling of digital evidence necessary to support investigations
and prosecutions.
        In addition, the PNP CSOC will gather, analyze, and disseminate actionable cyber threat
intelligence; develop cybersecurity, incident-response, and digital-forensics training programs for
PNP personnel; conduct research and development; recommend appropriate cybersecurity
policies to the Chief PNP; and contribute to public-awareness initiatives on the protection of
personal information when using PNP online services. It will likewise coordinate with the
Department of Information and Communications Technology and other relevant national and
international government and law-enforcement institutions involved in cybersecurity and the
protection of government-owned critical infrastructure.

        The mandate of the PNP CSOC is deliberately focused on the protection of PNP Critical
Information Infrastructure. It is not intended to replace or duplicate the general mandates of
existing cybersecurity and cybercrime agencies. Rather, it will strengthen the PNP’s internal
capability while enabling more effective coordination with other government institutions
whenever a cyber incident affects PNP systems, networks, databases, or digital services.

        To ensure effective leadership and accountability, the Center will be headed by a Director
with the rank of Police Brigadier General or higher and will be composed of highly skilled and
specialized personnel, including cybersecurity analysts, cybercrime investigators, and digital
forensic examiners. Its detailed organizational structure, staffing pattern, and regional presence
will be determined by the Chief PNP, in consultation with the National Police Commission and
subject to the availability of funds.

       The measure further directs the PNP to provide the Center with the technologies, tools,
secure infrastructure, digital forensic laboratories, and incident-response capabilities necessary to
perform its functions. Because the Center will handle sensitive cybersecurity and investigative
information, the bill requires the strictest confidentiality and the adoption of safeguards against
unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or compromise.

        Funding for the establishment, continuous operation, and enhancement of the PNP CSOC
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act and the regular budget of the PNP.
The Chief PNP, in coordination with the National Police Commission and the Department of
Information and Communications Technology, shall promulgate the necessary implementing
rules and regulations within ninety days from the effectivity of the proposed law.

        The establishment of the PNP Cybersecurity Operations Center will institutionalize a
dedicated, professional, and unified cybersecurity capability within the Philippine National
Police. It will strengthen the protection of PNP Critical Information Infrastructure, safeguard
sensitive government and citizen data, improve the resilience and continuity of police operations,
and enhance the PNP’s capacity to prevent, detect, investigate, and respond to cyber threats.

      As law enforcement becomes increasingly dependent on digital technologies, the PNP
must be equipped not only to police the physical domain but also to protect the digital systems
upon which its operations and public services depend.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE CYBERSECURITY
      OPERATIONS CENTER FOR THE PROTECTION OF PNP CRITICAL

     APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:


      SEC. 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as the "PNP Critical Information Infrastructure
Cybersecurity Act."

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to strengthen
the cybersecurity resilience of the Philippine National Police (PNP) and ensure the protection of
its Critical Information Infrastructure (CII), which is essential to the effective delivery of law
enforcement services, the preservation of public trust, and the safeguarding of national security
interests. The State recognizes that the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of
PNP information systems, digital platforms, databases, communications networks, and online
frontline services are indispensable to the maintenance of peace and order and the protection of
sensitive government and citizen information.

        Toward this end, the State shall establish a dedicated and specialized cybersecurity
capability within the PNP to provide continuous cyber threat monitoring, cyber threat
intelligence, incident detection and response, digital forensics, and cybersecurity risk
management for PNP Critical Information Infrastructure. Such capability shall enable the PNP to
anticipate, prevent, detect, investigate, and respond to evolving cyber threats, cyberattacks,
unauthorized access, data breaches, and other malicious activities that may compromise its
operations and information assets.

        The State further recognizes that emerging cyber threats increasingly transcend the digital
domain and may have direct implications for public safety, including the misuse of online
platforms to facilitate malicious activities, disseminate threats, target vulnerable sectors, and
influence harmful real-world conduct. Accordingly, the PNP shall be equipped with the
necessary institutional, technical, and operational capabilities to address these evolving risks
while ensuring the protection of personal data, digital evidence, and other sensitive information
entrusted to the government.

       In pursuing these objectives, the State shall promote inter-agency cooperation, capacity
development, technological innovation, and international collaboration to enhance the PNP's
cybersecurity posture and ensure the continued security, reliability, and resilience of its critical
information infrastructure.
        SEC. 3. Establishment of the PNP Cybersecurity Operations Center. – There is hereby
established the Philippine National Police Cybersecurity Operations Center (PNP CSOC), which
shall serve as the PNP's central cybersecurity command, operations, and coordination hub for the
protection of PNP Critical Information Infrastructure (CII).

        The PNP CSOC shall be the primary operational and technical unit responsible for
safeguarding PNP information systems, digital platforms, databases, communications networks,
digital assets, and other critical cyber resources against cyber threats, cyberattacks, unauthorized
access, data breaches, malicious online activities, and other cybersecurity risks. It shall maintain
a continuous capability to monitor, detect, analyze, prevent, mitigate, and respond to incidents
that may compromise the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of PNP
information infrastructure.

        As the focal point for cybersecurity operations within the PNP, the PNP CSOC shall
integrate cybersecurity monitoring, cyber threat intelligence, incident response, digital forensics,
vulnerability management, risk assessment, and cyber defense operations into a unified
framework for the protection of PNP Critical Information Infrastructure.

        The PNP CSOC shall serve as the principal center for the collection, processing, analysis,
and dissemination of cyber threat intelligence relevant to the PNP. It shall identify emerging
cyber threats, threat actors, attack methodologies, vulnerabilities, indicators of compromise,
online extremist activities, and other cyber-enabled risks that may affect PNP operations, public
safety, or national security. Through strategic, operational, and tactical threat intelligence, the
Center shall provide timely warnings, risk assessments, and actionable recommendations to
support informed decision-making, incident prevention, cybersecurity planning, and operational
readiness across all PNP units.

        The Center shall likewise establish mechanisms for information sharing and threat
intelligence exchange with relevant government agencies, the cybersecurity and law enforcement
community, critical infrastructure operators, academic institutions, private sector partners, and
international counterparts, subject to applicable laws, regulations, and security protocols. Such
collaboration shall enhance the PNP's ability to anticipate evolving threats, strengthen cyber
resilience, and support whole-of-government efforts to address cyber-enabled crimes and
emerging digital risks.

        The PNP CSOC shall operate as a specialized and proactive capability designed to
strengthen the PNP's cyber defense posture, protect sensitive government and citizen
information, preserve public trust in digital services, support law enforcement operations, and
contribute to the maintenance of public safety and national security in an increasingly
interconnected and technology-driven environment.

        SEC. 4. Mandate and Functions. – The PNP Cybersecurity Operations Center (PNP
CSOC) shall serve as the Philippine National Police's central cybersecurity operations, threat
intelligence, and cyber defense capability for the protection of PNP Critical Information
Infrastructure (CII). In pursuit of its mandate, it shall perform the following functions:

       (a)​ Security Operations and Continuous Monitoring – Establish and maintain a
            centralized capability for the continuous monitoring, detection, analysis, and
            management of cybersecurity events affecting PNP information systems, networks,
            digital platforms, databases, communications infrastructure, and other critical cyber
            assets. The Center shall identify vulnerabilities, indicators of compromise, malicious
            activities, and emerging attack vectors to enable timely preventive and corrective
            actions.

       (b)​Cyber Threat Intelligence – Collect, process, analyze, produce, and disseminate
           strategic, operational, and tactical cyber threat intelligence relevant to the PNP. The
           Center shall monitor emerging threats, threat actors, cybercriminal networks,
   malicious online activities, extremist and radicalization trends in digital spaces,
   vulnerabilities, and cyber-enabled risks that may affect law enforcement operations,
   public safety, or national security. It shall provide actionable intelligence, early
   warning advisories, threat assessments, and cybersecurity recommendations to
   support decision-making, operational readiness, and risk mitigation across all PNP
   units.

(c)​ Cybersecurity Incident Response and Recovery – Serve as the PNP's lead operational
     unit for the management, containment, investigation, remediation, and recovery of
     cybersecurity incidents affecting PNP Critical Information Infrastructure. The Center
     shall coordinate with relevant government agencies, service providers, and
     stakeholders to ensure a timely, effective, and coordinated response to cyber incidents
     and security breaches.


(d)​Digital Forensics and Cyber Investigation Support – Provide specialized technical
    assistance, cyber forensic capabilities, malware analysis, digital evidence collection,
    preservation, examination, and related investigative support to PNP units in
    connection with cybercrime investigations, cyber-enabled offenses, and incidents
    affecting PNP systems and information assets.


(e)​ Vulnerability Assessment and Cyber Risk Management – Conduct cybersecurity
     assessments, penetration testing, security audits, vulnerability analyses, and risk
     assessments of PNP information systems and critical infrastructure. The Center shall
     develop and recommend appropriate safeguards, controls, and mitigation measures to
     strengthen cyber resilience and reduce risk exposure.


(f)​ Cyber Defense and Infrastructure Protection – Develop, implement, and oversee
     technical and operational measures necessary to protect PNP Critical Information
     Infrastructure from cyberattacks, unauthorized access, data loss, disruption, sabotage,
     and other threats that may compromise the confidentiality, integrity, availability, or
     resilience of PNP systems and services.


(g)​Information Sharing and Inter-Agency Coordination – Establish and maintain
    cybersecurity cooperation, threat intelligence-sharing arrangements, and operational
    coordination with the Department of Information and Communications Technology
    (DICT), the Cybercrime Investigation and Coordinating Center (CICC), other
    government agencies, law enforcement organizations, critical infrastructure operators,
    private sector partners, academic institutions, and international counterparts, subject
    to applicable laws, regulations, and security protocols.


(h)​Capacity Building and Professional Development – Develop and implement training,
    certification, simulation, and professional development programs to enhance the
    cybersecurity competencies, cyber investigation capabilities, digital forensic
    expertise, and incident response readiness of PNP personnel.


(i)​ Research, Innovation, and Technology Development – Conduct and support research,
     innovation, and technology development initiatives relating to cybersecurity, cyber
     defense, digital forensics, artificial intelligence, threat detection, and other emerging
     technologies to strengthen the operational effectiveness of the PNP's cybersecurity
     capabilities.
       (j)​ Policy Development and Strategic Advisory Services – Provide strategic advice and
            recommendations to the Chief, PNP on cybersecurity policies, standards, governance
            frameworks, operational procedures, and long-term cyber resilience strategies
            necessary to protect PNP Critical Information Infrastructure.


       (k)​Cybersecurity Awareness and Stakeholder Engagement – Support public awareness,
           cyber hygiene education, and stakeholder engagement initiatives aimed at promoting
           responsible use of digital technologies, protecting personal information, combating
           cyber-enabled threats, and enhancing public confidence in PNP digital services.


       (l)​ Such Other Functions – Perform such other cybersecurity-related functions as may be
            assigned by the Chief, PNP or provided by law, consistent with the mandate of
            protecting PNP Critical Information Infrastructure and strengthening the cyber
            resilience of the Philippine National Police.

        SEC. 5. Organizational Structure. – The PNP Cybersecurity Operations Center (PNP
CSOC) shall be headed by a Director, with the rank of Police Brigadier General or higher, who
shall be appointed by the Chief, Philippine National Police (PNP), and who shall possess
demonstrated competence, experience, and training in cybersecurity, information and
communications technology, intelligence, cybercrime investigation, digital forensics, or related
fields.

       The PNP CSOC shall be organized as a specialized, multidisciplinary, and
mission-oriented unit composed of uniformed and non-uniformed personnel with expertise in
cybersecurity operations, cyber threat intelligence, digital forensics, cyber defense, incident
response, cybercrime investigation, network security, data analytics, information technology, risk
management, research and development, and other relevant disciplines necessary to fulfill its
mandate.

        To ensure continuous and effective cyber defense operations, the PNP CSOC shall
establish and maintain functional divisions, units, or teams responsible for, among others:

   a.​ Security Operations and Continuous Monitoring;
   b.​ Cyber Threat Intelligence and Analysis;
   c.​ Cybersecurity Incident Response and Recovery;
   d.​ Digital Forensics and Cyber Investigation Support;
   e.​ Vulnerability Assessment and Cyber Risk Management;
   f.​ Cyber Defense and Infrastructure Protection;
   g.​ Research, Innovation, and Emerging Technologies;
   h.​ Training, Capacity Building, and Cybersecurity Awareness; and
   i.​ Strategic Planning, Policy Development, and Inter-Agency Coordination.

       The PNP CSOC shall operate on a continuous basis and maintain the necessary
personnel, facilities, technologies, and operational capabilities to provide real-time cyber
monitoring, threat detection, intelligence analysis, incident response, and cyber defense support
for PNP Critical Information Infrastructure.

       Subject to the availability of funds and the operational requirements of the PNP, the
Chief, PNP, in consultation with the National Police Commission (NAPOLCOM), may establish
regional, provincial, or other field-based cybersecurity units or satellite operations centers to
enhance the nationwide cyber resilience, threat visibility, and incident response capabilities of
the PNP.

       The Chief, PNP, in consultation with NAPOLCOM and other relevant government
agencies, shall determine the detailed organizational structure, staffing pattern, qualification
standards, and operational framework of the PNP CSOC, taking into consideration international
best practices, evolving cyber threats, and the long-term cybersecurity requirements of the
Philippine National Police.
        This provision shall be interpreted to ensure that the PNP CSOC develops into a
professional, intelligence-driven, and technology-enabled organization capable of proactively
protecting PNP Critical Information Infrastructure and supporting the broader objectives of
public safety, law enforcement effectiveness, and national security in the digital age.

       SEC. 6. Powers of the Director. – The Director of the PNP Cybersecurity Operations
Center (PNP CSOC) shall exercise leadership, supervision, and operational control over the
Center and shall be responsible for the effective implementation of its mandate. For this purpose,
the Director shall have the authority to:

   a.​ Provide strategic direction, oversight, and management of the PNP CSOC and ensure the
       continuous and effective performance of its cybersecurity operations, cyber defense,
       threat intelligence, incident response, and critical infrastructure protection functions;
   b.​ Formulate, issue, implement, and enforce policies, standards, guidelines, operational
       procedures, and cybersecurity frameworks necessary to strengthen the security, resilience,
       and protection of PNP Critical Information Infrastructure, subject to existing laws,
       regulations, and approval requirements;
   c.​ Direct and coordinate the conduct of cybersecurity monitoring, cyber threat intelligence
       activities, risk assessments, vulnerability management, digital forensics operations, and
       incident response measures to address actual or emerging threats affecting PNP
       information systems and digital assets;
   d.​ Establish and maintain mechanisms for information sharing, threat intelligence exchange,
       operational coordination, and collaborative cybersecurity initiatives with government
       agencies, law enforcement organizations, critical infrastructure operators, academic
       institutions, private sector partners, and international counterparts, subject to existing
       laws and with the approval of the Chief, PNP, where required;
   e.​ Recommend, develop, and implement programs, projects, technologies, and
       capability-enhancement initiatives necessary to improve the cyber resilience, operational
       readiness, and technological capabilities of the PNP;
   f.​ Administer, allocate, and manage the personnel, financial, technological, and other
       resources of the PNP CSOC in accordance with existing laws, government accounting
       and auditing rules, and established PNP policies;
   g.​ Recommend the appointment, assignment, promotion, training, professional
       development, recognition, and disciplinary action of personnel assigned to the PNP
       CSOC, in accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations;
   h.​ Represent the PNP on matters relating to cybersecurity operations, cyber defense, cyber
       threat intelligence, and critical information infrastructure protection before national,
       regional, and international forums, subject to existing laws and policies;
   i.​ Submit periodic reports and strategic assessments to the Chief, PNP on the cybersecurity
       posture of the organization, significant cyber incidents, emerging threats, operational
       performance, capability requirements, and other matters relevant to the protection of PNP
       Critical Information Infrastructure; and
   j.​ Exercise such other powers and perform such other functions as may be necessary,
       incidental, or related to the attainment of the objectives of this Act, or as may be assigned
       by the Chief, PNP, consistent with law.

       The Director shall ensure that the PNP CSOC operates as an intelligence-driven,
technology-enabled, and proactive cybersecurity organization capable of anticipating,
preventing, detecting, and responding to evolving cyber threats in support of public safety, law
enforcement effectiveness, and national security.


        SEC. 7. Technical Expertise and Equipment. – The PNP shall ensure that the PNP CSOC
is equipped with state-of-the-art technologies, tools, and infrastructure necessary to carry out its
functions effectively. This includes, but is not limited to, advanced digital forensic laboratories,
secure network infrastructure, and incident response tools specifically for the protection of
PNP-CII.

        SEC. 8. Confidentiality of Information. – All information obtained by the PNP CSOC in
the course of its duties, particularly sensitive data related to the cybersecurity of PNP-CII and
investigations thereof, shall be treated with the strictest confidentiality and shall not be disclosed
to unauthorized persons or entities, except as may be required by law or lawful court order.
Appropriate safeguards shall be put in place to protect such information.

       SEC. 9. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. Thereafter, such sums as may be
necessary for the continuous operation and enhancement of the PNP CSOC shall be included in
the annual budget of the Philippine National Police.

        SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Chief PNP, in coordination with the National Police Commission
(NAPOLCOM), the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Department of Interior
and Local Government (DILG), and the Department of Information and Communications
Technology (DICT), shall promulgate the necessary implementing rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act.

        SEC. 11. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations,
and other issuances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

       SEC. 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation


       Approved.
