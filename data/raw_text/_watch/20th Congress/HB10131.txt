                                     Explanatory Note


The Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) provides retirement benefits to
government employees under Republic Act No. 8291. The GSIS pensions do not have
an automatic escalation mechanism. Pension amounts remain fixed unless Congress
enacts a new law or the GSIS Board authorizes special adjustments.

This situation has created inequity between public and private sector retirees. While SSS
retirees now enjoy predictable annual increases under the Pension Reform Program
(PRP) beginning in 2025, GSIS retirees are left vulnerable to inflation and rising costs of
living.

Government retirees, many of whom dedicated decades of service to the nation, deserve
protection against the erosion or decline of their pensions’ real value. This bill seeks to
establish an automatic annual adjustment mechanism for GSIS pensions, either
pegged to inflation or set at a minimum of five percent (5%) per year, with safeguards to
ensure fund sustainability.

By institutionalizing pension escalation, the measure promotes fairness, strengthens
social protection, and upholds the dignity of government retirees.

Approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                  AN ACT
        ESTABLISHING AN AUTOMATIC PENSION ESCALATION MECHANISM FOR
        RETIREES OF THE GOVERNMENT SERVICE INSURANCE SYSTEM (GSIS),
             PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Automatic Pension
 2   Escalation Act for GSIS Retirees.”

 3        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 4   protect the welfare of government retirees by ensuring that their pensions remain
 5   adequate and responsive to inflationary pressures, and to promote parity between GSIS
 6   and SSS retirees.

 7        SEC. 3. Annual Pension Adjustment. - All GSIS old-age and disability pensioners
 8   shall receive an automatic annual increase in their monthly pension.

 9        SEC. 4. Basis and Timing of Pension Adjustment. - The annual pension
10   adjustment under this Act shall be based on the official Consumer Price Index or inflation
11   data published by the Philippine Statistics Authority, whichever is higher.

12        The annual pension escalation under this Act shall be based on the official
13   Consumer Price Index or inflation data published by the Philippine Statistics Authority,
14   whichever is higher. Those receiving death and survivor pensions shall also receive an
15   annual increase of five percent (5%) or equivalent to the Consumer Price Index (CPI)
16   inflation, whichever is higher.
17
18        SEC. 5. Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all GSIS old-age and disability
19   pensioners except pensioners who opted for lump-sum retirement benefits.

20        SEC. 6. Funding Source. - The annual pension escalation shall be funded from the
21   GSIS investment earnings and other sources as may be determined by the GSIS Board
22   of Trustees.
 1        SEC. 7. Annual Actuarial Review. - The GSIS shall conduct an annual actuarial
 2   study to assess and determine the sustainability of the pension fund and submit a report
 3   to Speaker of the House of Representatives, the Chairperson of the Committee on
 4   Appropriations, the Senate President, the Chairperson of the Committee on Finance, and
 5   the Department of Finance.

 6        SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
 7   effectivity of this Act, the GSIS, in coordination with the Governance Commission for
 8   GOCCs and other stakeholders shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to
 9   implement the provisions of this Act.

10        SEC. 9. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
11   unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

12        SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
13   regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

14        SEC. 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
15   in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

16   Republic of the Philippines.

17          Approved.
