EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Batalay-Bote-Carorian-Cagraray-Buenavista Provincial
Road, situated along the Bato Tourism Circuit in the Municipality of Bato, Catanduanes, into
a national road, placing its supervision, improvement, repair, and maintenance under the
Department of Public Works and Highways.

The Municipality of Bato is home to various historical and natural attractions that
contribute to the tourism potential and local economy of Catanduanes. Among these are the
Batalay Shrine, Batalay Mangrove Eco-Park, Bote Lighthouse, and the natural attractions of
Carorian. The subject road serves as a vital link connecting these destinations and nearby
communities, facilitating tourism, transportation, and the movement of goods and essential
services.

Given its importance, the conversion of the road into a national road will facilitate the
allocation of appropriate national funding for its improvement, rehabilitation, and long-term
maintenance, while easing the financial burden on the provincial government. This will help
ensure safer, more accessible, and disaster-resilient road infrastructure, further promoting
tourism, connectivity, and socioeconomic development in Bato and Catanduanes.

This proposal is anchored on DPWH Department Order No. 112, s. 2024, which sets
the standards for road conversion. The road proposed is eligible for conversion as it meets the
standard of directly connecting accredited primary tourism enterprises to national roads. As
part of the Bato Tourism Circuit, the subject road provides access to various tourism
destinations and surrounding communities, supporting the tourism connectivity envisioned
under the Department Order, subject to verification of the prescribed requirements.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this proposed measure is earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL NO.

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

AN ACT CONVERTING THE BATALAY-BOTE-CARORIAN-CAGRARAY-
BUENAVISTA PROVINCIAL ROAD, SITUATED ALONG THE BATO TOURISM
CIRCUIT IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BATO, CATANDUANES, INTO A
NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. It is hereby declared that the Batalay-Bote-Carorian-Cagraray-Buenvista
Provincial Road, situated along the Bato Tourism Circuit in the Municipality of Bato,
Catanduanes, is converted into a national road under the jurisdiction and supervision of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DP WH).

SECTION 2. The Secretary of the DPWH shall include in its program the expansion,
improvement, rehabilitation, maintenance, and repair of the said road, the funding of which

shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
