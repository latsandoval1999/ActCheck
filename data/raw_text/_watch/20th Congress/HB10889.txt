                                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

     Finding a job requires persistence, preparation, and patience. It should not,
however, require a tour of government offices.

        Before employment begins, a jobseeker may need to secure clearances,
registrations, certifications, and other government documents from different agencies.
When these transactions require separate systems, repeated submissions, multiple
appointments, and unnecessary travel, routine compliance can become another barrier to
work.

       This matters especially for young workers entering the labor market. In June 2026,
the Philippine Statistics Authority reported a youth employment rate of 86.5 percent,
while around 743,000 employed youth were underemployed.1

       Congress already reduced one barrier through Republic Act No. 11261 (First Time
Jobseekers Assistance Act), which waives covered government fees for qualified first-time
jobseekers and directs Public Employment Service Offices to assist them in securing pre-
employment documents. But cost is only one part of the problem, and first-time jobseekers
are only one part of the workforce. The need for simpler government transactions also
arises when workers change jobs, reenter the workforce, seek employment after
displacement, or otherwise pursue new employment opportunities.

       The proposed Streamlined Transactions for Employment Processing (STEP) Act
addresses this broader administrative challenge. It establishes the STEP Service Network,
an integrated service framework using existing digital government platforms, Public
Employment Service Offices, participating agencies, and assisted-access points, to make
employment-related government transactions easier to access and complete.

           The measure builds on Republic Act No. 12254 (E-Governance Act), which

mandates interoperable, citizen-centered government services and integration of frontline
transactions involving agencies such as the Philippine Statistics Authority, National
Bureau of Investigation, Bureau of Internal Revenue, Social Security System, Pag-IBIG
Fund, and Philippine Health Insurance Corporation.

       It seeks to provide jobseekers with a common point of access to participating
services; facilitate electronic applications, appointments, payments, and transaction
tracking where legally and technically feasible; provide assisted access through PESOs;
and reduce unnecessary repetitive submissions through secure government-to-
government verification.

       A citizen who is ready to work has already taken an important step toward
contributing to the economy. Government should help make the next step simpler, faster,
and more economical.

        The passage of the STEP Act is therefore respectfully urged for the jobseeker who
is taking their initial step into employment, pursuing a better opportunity, rebuilding
after displacement, or simply trying to earn a living without first having to navigate a
maze of government transactions.

                                   AN ACT
           INSTITUTIONALIZING AN INTEGRATED SERVICE NETWORK FOR
             GOVERNMENT TRANSACTIONS RELATED TO EMPLOYMENT,
         STREAMLINING ACCESS THERETO FOR JOBSEEKERS, AND FOR OTHER
                                  PURPOSES

            Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1         Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Streamlined Transactions
 2   for Employment Processing (STEP) Act.”
 3
 4          Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 5   promote full, productive, and freely chosen employment and facilitate the transition of
 6   Filipinos from education, training, or unemployment to decent work.
 7
 8          Towards this end, the State shall simplify access to government documents and
 9   services commonly required when applying for or commencing employment to reduce
10   unnecessary travel, repetitive submissions, waiting time, and administrative costs. The
11   State shall likewise maximize existing government facilities and digital infrastructure,
12   provide assisted access to persons who need it, and ensure that government systems are
13   interoperable, secure, and inclusive.
14
15          Section 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:
16
17          (a) First-time jobseeker refers to a person qualified to avail of the benefits under
18              Republic Act No. 11261, otherwise known as the “First Time Jobseekers
19              Assistance Act”;
20
21          (b) Jobseeker refers to refers to a person seeking employment, whether for local or
22              overseas employment;

 2              owned or -controlled corporation, local government unit, or other government
 3              entity whose employment-related document, registration, credential,
 4              clearance, certification, or service is accessible through the STEP Service
 5              Network; and

11              track transactions, receive assistance, and, when legally and technically

13              credentials, certifications, and other services related to employment.
14
15           Section 4. Establishment of the STEP Service Network. – The Department of Labor
16   and Employment (DOLE), in coordination with the Department of Information and
17   Communications Technology (DICT), Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA), Department of
18   the Interior and Local Government (DILG), Department of Migrant Workers (DMW), and
19   participating agencies, shall institutionalize the STEP Service Network by integrating
20   employment-related government services with existing digital government systems and
21   providing assisted access through existing government facilities.
22
23          The STEP Service Network shall provide integrated access to employment-related
24   government services for all jobseekers, without prejudice to the exemptions, benefits, and
25   assistance granted to qualified first-time jobseekers under Republic Act No. 11261.
26
27           The STEP Service Network shall, to the extent applicable, operate through the
28   Citizen Frontline Delivery Service Platforms and other E-Government Programs
29   established under Republic Act No. 12254, otherwise known as the “E-Governance Act,”
30   and through PESOs. Job placement offices in educational institutions may serve as
31   additional assisted-access points in accordance with Republic Act No. 8759, otherwise
32   known as the “Public Employment Service Office Act of 1999,” as amended, and
33   applicable agreements with the DOLE.
34
35          Implementation of the STEP Service Network shall not require the establishment
36   of a duplicative stand-alone digital portal when the relevant service may be integrated
37   into or accessed through existing E-Government Programs. Neither shall it require the
38   construction of a separate building, establishment of a separate office, or creation of a
39   separate organizational unit solely for the implementation of the STEP Service Network.
40
41           The DOLE shall lead employment service coordination, while the DICT shall lead
42   digital integration, interoperability, and the adoption of applicable technical standards.
43   With respect to services specific to overseas employment, the DMW shall exercise its
44   powers and functions under Republic Act No. 11641, otherwise known as the

 2   authority over, and responsibility for, the evaluation, approval, denial, issuance, validity,
 3   and custody of documents and information under its mandate.
 4
 5         Section 5. Covered Services. – The STEP Service Network shall facilitate access to
 6   government documents, registrations, credentials, and services commonly required in
 7   connection with the application for or commencement of employment.
 8
 9          Such services may include:
10          (a) Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) registration or verification through the
11              Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR);
12          (b) Clearance issued by the National Bureau of Investigation (NBI);
13          (c) Police clearance issued through the Philippine National Police (PNP);
14          (d) Registration and membership services of the Social Security System (SSS),
15          (e) Registration and membership services of the Philippine Health Insurance
16              Corporation (PhilHealth);
17          (f) Registration and membership services of the Home Development Mutual Fund
18              (Pag-IBIG Fund);
19          (g) Civil registry documents issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA);
20          (h) Barangay or local government certifications or clearances lawfully required in
21              connection with employment;
22          (i) Medical certificates issued by public hospitals or other government health
23              facilities, when applicable; and
24          (j) Such other employment-related government documents or services as may be
25              identified by the DOLE in consultation with the concerned participating
26              agency.
27
28          Inclusion of a document or service in the STEP Service Network shall not, by itself,
29   constitute authority for an employer to require such document or service from a job
30   applicant or employee.
31
32          Where personal appearance, biometric capture, examination, medical procedure,
33   interview, or other in-person process is required by law or by the nature of the transaction,
34   the STEP Service Network may facilitate the filing of applications in advance,
35   appointment scheduling, payment when applicable, and transaction tracking, but shall
36   not dispense with the required process.
37
38          Section 6. Integrated and Simplified Processing. – Subject to applicable laws and
39   technical feasibility, the STEP Service Network shall provide:
40          (a) Consolidated and regularly updated information on government documents
41              and services related to employment;
42          (b) Common point of access to participating services;
43          (c) Electronic submission or retrieval of information and supporting documents;
44          (d) Appointment scheduling;

 1          (e) Electronic payment of lawful fees and charges, when applicable;
 2          (f) Transaction tracking and notifications; and
 3          (g) Issuance or retrieval of electronic documents or credentials when permitted by
 4              law.
 5
 6          The DOLE, DICT, ARTA, and participating agencies shall coordinate the
 7   reengineering and harmonization of procedures to eliminate unnecessary duplication,
 8   reduce processing time, and improve accessibility in accordance with Republic Act No.
 9   11032, otherwise known as the “Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service
10   Delivery Act of 2018.”
11
12          No additional documentary requirement shall be imposed solely because a
13   transaction is coursed through the STEP Service Network.
14
15          Section 7. Reuse and Verification of Government Data. – Where authorized by law
16   and technically feasible, a jobseeker shall not be required to resubmit information or a
17   government-issued document that has already been verified by a participating agency
18   and may be lawfully and securely retrieved or verified from the government entity
19   responsible for such information or document, provided that the information or
20   document remains valid and applicable to the transaction.
21
22         The government entity legally responsible for the information shall retain custody
23   and control of such information and responsibility for its maintenance and protection in
24   accordance with applicable law. Data sharing and verification shall, as far as practicable,
25   be undertaken through secure and interoperable mechanisms without creating an
26   unnecessary duplicate database solely for purposes of the STEP Service Network.
27
28          Section 8. Assisted Access Through Public Employment Service Offices. – Without
29   prejudice to the assistance provided to first-time jobseekers under Republic Act No. 11261,
30   PESOs shall serve as assisted-access points of the STEP Service Network for all jobseekers
31   and shall, as part of their employment-facilitation functions, provide reasonable
32   assistance in accessing or completing transactions through the Network.
33
34          Such assistance may include:
35          (a) Identifying applicable government documents and services applicable to the
36              jobseeker’s intended employment or transaction;
37          (b) Accessing participating digital services;
38          (c) Assisting in the completion and submission of applications;
39          (d) Scheduling appointments;
40          (e) Monitoring transaction status;
41          (f) Retrieving authorized electronic documents; and
42          (g) Referring jobseekers to the appropriate government agency or employment
43              program, or other available service.

30          Section 13. Phased Implementation. – The DOLE and DICT, in coordination with
31   participating agencies, shall implement the STEP Service Network in phases based on
32   transaction volume, relevance to employment, availability through existing digital
33   systems, technical readiness, interoperability, accessibility, data protection requirements,
34   cybersecurity risks, and cost-effectiveness.
35
36           Within one (1) year from the effectivity of the implementing rules and regulations,
37   the DOLE and DICT shall implement the initial phase covering priority employment-
38   related services that are already digitally available or may reasonably be integrated
39   without major redevelopment of existing government systems. Priority shall be given to
40   employment-related services commonly accessed by jobseekers, including relevant
41   services covered by Republic Act No. 11261, and to services already included or capable
42   of integration under Republic Act No. 12254.

 4         Section 14. Monitoring and Evaluation. – The DOLE, in coordination with the
 5   DICT, ARTA, and participating agencies, shall annually evaluate the implementation and
 6   performance of the STEP Service Network.
 7
 8          The evaluation shall consider the following:
 9          (a) Number of jobseekers assisted;
10          (b) Transaction volume and completion rates;
11          (c) Processing and referral times;
12          (d) Utilization by different categories of jobseekers, including first-time jobseekers;
13          (e) Accessibility and gaps in service delivery gaps;
14          (f) User complaints and feedback;
15          (g) System reliability; and
16          (h) Aggregate information on material cybersecurity or data protection incidents
17              affecting service delivery, subject to applicable confidentiality and security
18              requirements.
19
20         A non-sensitive summary of the evaluation shall be submitted annually to the
21   appropriate committees of Congress and published on the official websites of the DOLE
22   and DICT.
23
24          Section 15. Limitations. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed to:
25          (a) Create a new substantive qualification or documentary requirement for
26              employment;
27          (b) Authorize an employer to require a document merely because it is included in
28              the STEP Service Network;
29          (c) Authorize the collection of a fee or charge not otherwise allowed by law;
30          (d) Modify the statutory authority of a participating agency;
31          (e) Alter the validity period of a government-issued document or credential;
32          (f) Waive or dispense with any verification, investigation, examination, biometric
33              capture, medical procedure, personal appearance, or other process required by
34              law; or
35          (g) Expand, restrict, or modify the persons entitled to exemptions or benefits under
36              Republic Act No. 11261 or any other law.
37
38           Section 16. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation

41   available for employment facilitation, digital government, and related purposes.

43   Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
