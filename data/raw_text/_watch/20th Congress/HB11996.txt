                            AN ACT
   CLARIFYING THE STATUS OF THE PAMANTASAN NG LUNGSOD NG
   MAYNILA AS A STATE UNIVERSITY, AMENDING, FOR THE PURPOSE,
               REPUBLIC ACT NO. 4196, AS AMENDED

                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

          Equal protection demands equality among equals;1 students of a congressionally
chartered university deserve the same State support accorded to their peers in all other State
                                       Universities.

      This measure amends Republic Act No. 4196, the charter of the Pamantasan ng
Lungsod ng Maynila (PLM), to expressly declare PLM a State University within the
contemplation of Republic Act Nos. 7722, 8292, 10931, and all other applicable laws.

       This addresses the root cause of PLM’s exclusion from recognition by the
Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and from national student financial
assistance programs under the Unified Student Financial Assistance System for
Tertiary Education (UniFAST). PLM was validly created by an Act of Congress in the
exercise of national legislative power, yet it has been denied classification as a State
University or College (SUC) solely because its charter assigns the primary funding
obligation to the City of Manila, a circumstance that CHED has treated as converting
the institution into a Local University or College (LUC).

       This misclassification overlooks the settled legal principle confirmed by the
Supreme Court in Mandaue City College v. CHED2 that an institution’s legal status is
determined by the authority that created it, not by the source of its funding; LUCs are
established by local ordinance, while PLM was created by Republic Act, placing it by
origin within the category of SUCs. The resulting exclusion denies PLM students the
benefits of free tuition and other fees, as well as the Tertiary Education Subsidy under
Republic Act No. 10931, violating the constitutional guarantee of equal protection by

mandated by law itself.

       Corrective legislation is necessary not because Republic Act No. 10931 is
defective, but because PLM's original charter did not explicitly use the terminology
now used in prevailing statutes, leaving an ambiguity that has been interpreted to the
institution’s disadvantage.

        This measure does not alter the existing governance structure or relieve the City
of Manila of its statutory duty to fund and maintain the university; rather, it clarifies
status so national support supplements, rather than replaces, local obligations,
ensuring no disruption to operations or unfunded mandates. By aligning PLM’s legal
classification with its congressional origin, this amendment resolves a long-standing
administrative uncertainty, removes barriers to lawful benefits due to qualified
students, and upholds both the legislative intent and the national policy of universal
access to quality tertiary education, without restructuring the higher education system
or creating unwarranted precedents.


      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

               REPUBLIC ACT NO. 4196, AS AMENDED

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

    SECTION 1. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to ensure universal
access to quality tertiary education, rationalize the development of public higher
education institutions, and uphold the rule of law that the manner of creation, not the
source of funding, determines the legal classification of a public higher education
institution.

   SEC. 2. Section 1 of Republic Act No. 4196 is hereby amended to read as follows:

           "SECTION 1. THERE IS HEREBY CREATED AND ESTABLISHED IN
        THE CITY OF MANILA A PUBLIC HIGHER EDUCATION
        INSTITUTION TO BE KNOWN AS THE PAMANTASAN NG LUNGSOD
        NG MAYNILA (PLM). IT IS A STATE UNIVERSITY WITHIN THE
        MEANING OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7722, REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8292,
        REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10931, AND ALL OTHER APPLICABLE LAWS. IT
        SHALL HAVE ITS PRINCIPAL SEAT IN THE CITY OF MANILA AND
        SHALL OPERATE AS A BODY CORPORATE WITH THE POWERS AND
        ATTRIBUTES OF A STATE UNIVERSITY."

    SEC. 3. Status and Eligibility. — The Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila, created
by an Act of Congress, shall be classified as a State University and shall be entitled to
all rights, privileges, funding, benefits, and coverage extended by law to State
Universities and Colleges (SUCs), including, but not limited to, free tuition and other
fees, the Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES), and inclusion in all programs of the
Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Unified Student Financial
Assistance System for Tertiary Education (UniFAST): Provided, That nothing in this
Act shall be construed to relieve the City Government of Manila of its statutory
obligation to provide funds for the operation and maintenance of the University under

                                           Page 3 of 4
Section 15 of Republic Act No. 4196: Provided, further, That the University's inclusion
in national funding programs shall be complementary to, and not in substitution for,
the financial support from the City of Manila: Provided, finally, That the University's
institutional autonomy and academic freedom under the Constitution and applicable
laws shall in no way be impaired.

    SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Commission on Higher Education, in coordination with the
UniFAST Board, the Department of Budget and Management, the City Government
of Manila, and the Board of Regents of the Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila, shall
issue the necessary rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this Act.

   SEC. 5. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, such declaration shall not affect the validity of the
remaining provisions hereof.

   SEC 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

   SEC. 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.


   Approved.
