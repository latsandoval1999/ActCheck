                         AN ACT
EXPANDING THE COVERAGE OF COMPULSORY INSURANCE OF
OVERSEAS FILIPINO WORKERS, FURTHER AMENDING REPUBLIC
ACT NO. 8042, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE MIGRANT WORKERS
AND OVERSEAS FILIPINOS ACT OF 1995, AS AMENDED


                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) remain among the country’s most vital
contributors to national development, serving as a major source of foreign
exchange and providing economic stability to millions of Filipino families
through their remittances. Despite their invaluable contributions, OFWs continue
to face significant risks while working abroad, including illness, injury, disability,
displacement arising from crises, and even death. These vulnerabilities
underscore the critical need for a comprehensive and responsive compulsory
insurance system that ensures adequate protection throughout the duration of their
employment overseas.

      While existing laws mandate insurance coverage for OFWs, evolving
global conditions — including public health emergencies, armed conflicts, civil
unrest, and natural or man-made disasters — have exposed gaps in the scope of
protection. Strengthening and expanding compulsory insurance coverage is
therefore essential to provide more responsive, inclusive, and timely assistance to
migrant workers and their families in times of distress.
         This proposed measure seeks to enhance the current compulsory insurance
  framework by introducing additional benefits and improving claims mechanisms.
  Among its salient features are the provision of temporary partial disability
  benefits, expanded repatriation coverage arising from epidemic, pandemic, war,
  civil unrest, and other calamities, and the adoption of a single-entry approach for
  money claims under this measure. The bill likewise imposes upon recruitment
  and manning agencies the responsibility to assist migrant workers in the filing
  and processing of insurance claims.

         Through these reforms, the proposed legislation aims to strengthen the
  State’s protective mantle over OFWs, ensuring that those who sustain the nation’s
  economy are afforded adequate security and support in the face of unforeseen
  risks abroad.

        In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.
