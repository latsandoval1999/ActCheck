                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       At present, increases in the bed capacity and service capability of Department of
Health (DOH) hospitals generally require the enactment of a separate law by Congress.
While legislative oversight remains important, this process can delay much-needed
improvements in government hospitals despite clear and urgent healthcare needs.

       The Department of Health is in the best position to determine when hospitals
need additional beds or upgraded services based on population growth, disease patterns,
hospital occupancy, and other public health considerations. Empowering the DOH to
make these administrative determinations will enable government hospitals to respond
more quickly and efficiently to the healthcare needs of the Filipino people.

       This measure authorizes the DOH to determine and approve the bed capacity and
service capability of all DOH hospitals, subject to appropriate standards and existing
budgetary laws. By streamlining the process while preserving Congress' power over
appropriations, the bill promotes a more responsive and effective public healthcare
system.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN   ACT AUTHORIZING     THE   DEPARTMENT    OF  HEALTH    TO
DETERMINE, SET, AND APPROVE THE BED CAPACITY AND SERVICE
CAPABILITY OF ALL DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH HOSPITALS, ELIMINATING
THE REQUIREMENT OF A SEPARATE LAW FOR FUTURE ADJUSTMENTS, AND
FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "DOH Hospital Capacity
Rationalization Act."

        Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to protect and promote
the right to health of the people by ensuring that government hospitals are adequately equipped
and capable of responding to the evolving healthcare needs of the population. Towards this
end, the State shall strengthen the capacity of Department of Health (DOH) hospitals through
an efficient, responsive, and evidence-based mechanism for determining and approving their
authorized bed capacity and service capability.

       Section 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:

        (a) Bed Capacity refers to the authorized number of hospital beds approved for
operation by a DOH hospital.

        (b) Service Capability refers to the classification of a hospital based on the range of
clinical, diagnostic, therapeutic, emergency, specialty, and ancillary services it is authorized to
provide.

      (c) DOH Hospital refers to any hospital owned, operated, or directly supervised by the
Department of Health.

       Section 4. Authority of the Department of Health. — Notwithstanding any provision
of law to the contrary, the Department of Health shall have the authority to determine, set,
increase, decrease, or otherwise modify the authorized bed capacity and service capability of
all DOH hospitals through the issuance of an administrative order, subject to the standards and
requirements provided under this Act.
       Any future adjustment in the authorized bed capacity or service capability of a DOH
hospital shall no longer require legislative enactment and may be approved by the Department
of Health in accordance with this Act and its implementing rules and regulations.

       Such authority shall include the approval of:

       (a) Increase or decrease in authorized bed capacity;

       (b) Upgrading or downgrading of service capability;

       (c) Conversion or expansion of hospital services necessary to meet public health
       demands; and

       (d) Other operational adjustments necessary to improve healthcare delivery.

        Section 5. Basis for Approval. – In determining the appropriate bed capacity and
service capability of a DOH hospital, the Department shall consider, among others:

       (a) Population served and projected population growth;

       (b) Hospital occupancy rate and patient utilization;

       (c) Disease burden and epidemiological trends;

       (d) Geographic accessibility and referral network;

       (e) Availability of healthcare professionals;

       (f) Existing infrastructure and equipment;

       (g) Financial sustainability and operational efficiency; and

       (h) National and regional health priorities.

       Section 6. Periodic Review. – The Department of Health shall conduct a
comprehensive review of the bed capacity and service capability of every DOH hospital at least
once every five (5) years, or earlier when public health emergencies, disasters, significant
demographic changes, or other compelling circumstances warrant.

        Section 7. Effect of Approval. – Any adjustment in the authorized bed capacity or
service capability approved by the Department of Health pursuant to this Act shall take effect
upon the issuance of the corresponding administrative order and shall not require the enactment
of a separate law.

        The Department shall ensure that any approved increase is accompanied by the gradual
provision of the necessary personnel, equipment, infrastructure, and operational support,
subject to existing budgeting, procurement, civil service laws, rules and regulations, and the
availability of appropriations.
        Section 8. Budgetary Requirements. – The Department of Health shall incorporate
the funding requirements necessary to implement approved adjustments in bed capacity and
service capability into its annual budget proposal.

       Nothing in this Act shall be construed to automatically appropriate funds for any
approved increase. The implementation of such adjustments shall remain subject to the
availability of appropriations as provided in the General Appropriations Act and other
applicable laws.

        Section 9. Coordination with Other Agencies. – The Department of Health shall
coordinate with the Department of Budget and Management, the Civil Service Commission,
the Department of Public Works and Highways, and other concerned agencies, whenever
necessary, to facilitate the implementation of approved increases in hospital capacity and
service capability.

        Section 10. Transparency and Reporting. – The Department of Health shall maintain
and publish an updated registry of all DOH hospitals indicating their authorized bed capacity,
service capability, occupancy rates, and any approved modifications.

       The Department shall likewise submit an annual report to Congress detailing all
adjustments made pursuant to this Act and the corresponding status of implementation.

        Section 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for its effective implementation.

         Section 12. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be
in full force and effect.

        Section 13. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

        Section 14. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
