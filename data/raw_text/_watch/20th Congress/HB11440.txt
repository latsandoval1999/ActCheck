                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 16, Article XIII of the 1987 Philippine Constitution guarantees the right
of the people and their organizations to effective and reasonable participation
at all levels of social, political, and economic decision-making. Furthermore,
Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of
1991, directs local government units (LGUs) to promote the establishment and
operation of people's and non-governmental organizations to become active
partners in the pursuit of local autonomy.

Despite these constitutional and statutory mandates, civil society participation
in many local government units remains consultative, informal, or merely
symbolic. Citizens and sectoral organizations often lack a formal,
institutionalized mechanism to directly participate in policy formulation,
budget deliberation, program monitoring, and local governance.

This bill seeks to mandate and institutionalize the establishment of a People's
Council in every city and municipality across the Philippines. Inspired by
successful local participatory models, such as the Naga City People's Council,
this measure empowers accredited civil society organizations (CSOs), non-
governmental organizations (NGOs), and sectoral groups to sit in local special
bodies, attend legislative sessions, propose local measures, and participate
directly in local decision-making processes.

By providing a structured framework for civil society engagement, this
legislation aims to deepen local democracy, ensure transparency, foster
accountability, and guarantee that public policies genuinely reflect the needs
of the community.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT MANDATING AND INSTITUTIONALIZING THE ESTABLISHMENT
  OF A PEOPLE'S COUNCIL IN EVERY CITY AND MUNICIPALITY IN THE
  PHILIPPINES, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, PROVIDING
 MECHANISMS FOR CITIZEN PARTICIPATION IN LOCAL GOVERNANCE,
                        AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Institutionalized People's
Council Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote and
defend the right of the people to effective and reasonable participation at all
levels of decision-making. The State recognizes non-governmental,
community-based, and sectoral organizations as essential partners in local
development, transparent governance, and public accountability.

SEC. 3. Scope and Coverage. — This Act shall apply to all cities and
municipalities nationwide.

SEC. 4. Establishment of a People's Council. — There shall be established in
every city and municipality a People's Council, which shall serve as the
umbrella organization and primary mechanism for the direct participation of
civil society organizations (CSOs), non-governmental organizations (NGOs),
people's organizations (POs), and sectoral groups in local governance.

SEC. 5. Composition and Accreditation. —

   1. The People's Council shall be composed of authorized representatives
      from accredited CSOs, POs, NGOs, professional associations, academic
      institutions, and sectoral representatives (including women, youth,
      farmers, fisherfolk, labor, persons with disabilities, senior citizens,
      indigenous peoples, and the urban poor) operating within the city or
      municipality.
   2. The Local Sanggunian (Sangguniang Panlungsod or Sangguniang
      Bayan) shall establish a simplified, transparent, and non-
      discriminatory process for the accreditation of CSOs and member-
      organizations within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act.
   3. The People's Council shall elect its own officers, adopt its internal rules
      of procedure, and determine its leadership structure independent of
      local government interference.

SEC. 6. Powers, Rights, and Functions. — The People's Council shall have the
following powers and functions:

   1. Representation in Local Special Bodies: Elect civil society
      representatives to sit as voting members in all Local Special Bodies
      (LSBs), including but not limited to the Local Development Council
      (LDC), Local Health Board, Local School Board, Local Pre-qualification,
      Bids and Awards Committee (PBAC), and Local Peace and Order
      Council.
   2. Legislative Participation: Sit in all committee hearings and regular
      sessions of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or Sangguniang Bayan as
      non-voting participants, with the right to participate in deliberations,
      voice sectoral concerns, and submit proposed ordinances or
      resolutions.
   3. Budget and Policy Review: Participate actively in local budget
      preparation, public hearings, local development planning, and sectoral
      policy formulation.
   4. Monitoring and Evaluation: Monitor, evaluate, and conduct citizen
      audits on the implementation of government projects, public
      infrastructure, service delivery, and local procurement.
   5. Transparency and Access to Information: Access public records, local
      financial reports, project disclosure documents, and council minutes
      in accordance with existing laws and executive orders on freedom of
      information.

SEC. 7. Independence of the People's Council. — The People's Council shall
remain an independent, non-partisan entity. No elective or appointive local
government official, or their relatives within the fourth civil degree of
consanguinity or affinity, shall serve as an officer or voting member of the
People's Council.

SEC. 8. Support and Funding. —

   1. LGUs shall provide reasonable office space or facilities for the
      operations of the People's Council within or adjacent to the city hall or
      municipal hall.
   2. LGUs shall appropriate an annual operational budget for the People's
      Council, derived from the LGU's general fund or local development
      fund, to support capacity building, public consultations,
      administrative costs, and monitoring activities.
   3. Under no circumstance shall the allocation or release of funds be used
      by the local government unit to exert control, political influence, or
      pressure over the decisions or operations of the People's Council.

SEC. 9. Sanctions and Penalties. — Any local chief executive, member of the
local sanggunian, or local official who willfully refuses, delays, or obstructs
the establishment, accreditation, or operations of the People's Council, or
denies its representatives their lawful seats in local special bodies, shall be
subject to administrative liability under Section 60 of Republic Act No. 7160
(Local Government Code of 1991) and other applicable civil service laws.

SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90)
days from the approval of this Act, the Department of the Interior and Local
Government (DILG), in full consultation with representative civil society
organizations and national leagues of local government units, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

SEC. 11. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, decrees,
administrative orders, rules, and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with
the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified
accordingly.

SEC. 13. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
