                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to mandate the free issuance of essential employment
documents and the digitalization of academic records systems in all higher
education institutions (HEIs). It aims to facilitate a seamless transition for Filipino
graduates from college to the workforce and remove unnecessary financial and
administrative barriers that delay employment opportunities for newly graduated
Filipinos.

       For many fresh graduates, the path to employment is stalled at the very
beginning due to high costs of documentation and processing charged by HEIs.
While Republic Act No. 11261, otherwise known as the “First Time Jobseekers
Assistance Act”, covers for the waiving of fees for government clearances, it does not
comprehensively cover the costs and processing challenges associated with
academic records required by employers. As labor market conditions become
increasingly competitive, there is a need to ensure that fresh graduates can promptly
access the documents necessary to pursue employment opportunities.

      This measure mandates all HEIs, both public and private, to provide fresh
graduates with free copies of their transcripts, diplomas, and certificates of
graduation. It likewise establishes a Digital Academic Records System to facilitate a
more efficient and secure processing of academic records.

       The youth represents one of the nation’s greatest assets and a driving force of
economic growth and national development. It is a constitutional imperative for the
State to protect their social well-being and promote full employment. Through the
provisions stipulated in this Act, the State helps unlock opportunities, strengthen
productivity, and support inclusive economic progress.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                         AN ACT
MANDATING THE UNIVERSAL DIGITALIZATION OF ACADEMIC
RECORDS AND PROVIDING FREE ACCESS TO EMPLOYMENT
DOCUMENTS, STRENGTHENING THE TRANSITION OF FRESH
GRADUATES ENTERING THE WORKFORCE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Fresh Graduate
Workforce Transition Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the vital role of the youth
in nation-building and shall adopt measures that promote gainful employment,
equal access to opportunities, and a smooth transition from education to productive
participation in the workforce. To this end, the State shall remove financial and
administrative barriers, such as high document costs and manual processing delays,
that hinder fresh graduates from entering the labor market.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:​

          a. Fresh Graduate refers to a Filipino citizen who has successfully
       completed a degree or vocational program from a Higher Education
       Institution (HEI) or training institution within twelve (12) months of availing
       the benefits of this Act;​

          b. Higher Education Institution (HEI) refers to all public and private
       universities, colleges, and learning institutions offering post-secondary
       degree programs; and​

          c. Digital Academic Records System refers to a secure electronic system
       for the storage, issuance, and verification of academic records, including
       transcripts, diplomas, and certifications.



                                            1
       SEC. 4. Universal Access to Free Employment Documents. – All HEIs shall
provide fresh graduates with their employment-related academic documents free of
charge. This entitlement shall expand upon the benefits provided under Republic
Act (RA) No. 11261, otherwise known as the “First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act”,
and shall apply to all public and private HEIs. Every fresh graduate shall be entitled
to receive, free of any charge, up to five (5) certified true copies each of the
following:

           a.​ Transcript of Records (TOR), or a Certificate of Graduation where the
       or is not yet available; and

          b.​ Diploma.

       Within twelve (12) months from the date of graduation, a fresh graduate may
request and receive an additional five (5) certified true copies of the documents
specified in this Section without the payment of any fee or charge.

      Fresh graduates may likewise opt to receive electronic copies of such
documents. Electronic copies bearing digital signatures or other authentication
mechanisms recognized under RA No. 8792, otherwise known as the “Electronic
Commerce Act of 2000”, shall have the same validity and evidentiary value as
physical copies.

      HEIs shall ensure that the issuance of such documents is carried out not later
than three (3) working days from receipt of request. Nothing in this Act shall
preclude HEIs from charging the applicable fees and charges for requests exceeding
the number of copies granted hereunder.

​     SEC. 5. Integration with National Digital Infrastructure. – All HEIs shall establish
and maintain a Digital Academic Records System that enables graduates to securely
submit requests for academic records through electronic or online platforms to
promote timely and efficient issuance. ​

       The Commission on Higher Education (CHED), in coordination with the
Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), shall issue the
necessary guidelines and standards to ensure the effective implementation of this
section and may provide technical assistance to HEIs in establishing and
maintaining their respective digital academic records systems.

      SEC. 6. Data Privacy and Security. – All HEIs shall ensure that their Digital
Academic Records Systems comply with the provisions of RA No. 10173, otherwise
known as the “Data Privacy Act of 2012”, and shall implement appropriate technical,
organizational, and physical safeguards to protect academic records against
unauthorized access, disclosure, identity theft, alteration, loss, and other form

      SEC. 7. Responsibilities of Higher Education Institutions. – For the effective
implementation of this Act, all HEIs shall undertake the following core
responsibilities:

          a.​ Ensure the timely issuance of employment application documents to
      fresh graduates in accordance with Section 4 of this Act;​

         b.​ Establish and maintain a Digital Academic Records System that allows
      for the electronic submission, processing, storage, and verification of
      documentary requirements set forth in Section 5 of this Act, in accordance
      with the guidelines prescribed by CHED and DICT;

          c.​ Designate the appropriate office or authorized personnel responsible
      for the processing, certification, and issuance of academic records covered by
      this Act;​

         d.​ Maintain accurate, secure, and complete academic records of students
      and graduates to ensure the integrity and authenticity of documents issued
      by the institution;​

         e.​ Comply with such additional standards and procedures as may be
      prescribed by CHED for the effective implementation of this Act; and​

          f.​ Submit periodic compliance reports to CHED in such form and
      frequency as may be determined in the implementing rules and regulations.

      SEC. 8. Oversight by the Commission on Higher Education. – CHED shall exercise
comprehensive regulatory oversight over the implementation of this Act. For this
purpose, CHED may require HEIs to submit reports, records and all other relevant
documents, conduct compliance reviews and inspections, and issue directives
necessary to ensure compliance with the provisions of this Act.

       SEC. 9. Administrative Sanctions. – Any HEI that fails to comply with the
provisions of this Act or its implementing rules and regulations shall, after due
notice and hearing, be subject to appropriate administrative sanctions to be imposed
by CHED in accordance with its existing powers.

        SEC. 10. Complaints and Redress Mechanism. – CHED shall establish a
mechanism through which fresh graduates may file complaints regarding delays in
the issuance of documents, unlawful fees or charges, refusal to issue records, or
other violations of this Act. CHED shall promptly act upon such complaints and
shall issue appropriate directives to ensure compliance.

        SEC. 11. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of CHED and the
DICT. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation
of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, CHED, in coordination with the DICT, shall promulgate

       SEC. 13. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
