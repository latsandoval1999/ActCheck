          RESEARCH CENTER ON OBESITY PREVENTION

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The 2019 National Nutrition Survey of the Food and Nutrition Research
Institute (FNRI) reported that there are about 27 million Filipinos who are
obese and overweight. Furthermore, the Philippines is said to have “very rapid
growth” in adult obesity over the last two decades and is mostly attributed to
poor diet and nutrition.

      Numerous studies have indicated that obesity increases risks in an
individual’s health, thereby also reducing a person’s life expectancy.
Potentially fatal conditions associated with obesity include Type 2 diabetes,
coronary heart disease, cancers, and gall bladder disease.

       As a State policy “to protect and promote the right to health of the people
and instill health consciousness among them” (Article II, 1987 Philippine
Constitution), this bill seeks to curb obesity in the country and protect
Filipinos from this epidemic by establishing a research center on obesity
prevention.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
     MANDATING THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH TO ESTABLISH A
          RESEARCH CENTER ON OBESITY PREVENTION

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Obesity Prevention
Act.”

SECTION 2. Overweight and Obesity Defined. – As used in this Act, unless
the context otherwise requires, the terms overweight and obesity are defined
as the abnormal or excessive fat accumulation that presents a risk to health.
A crude population measure of obesity is the body mass index (BMI), a
person’s weight in kilograms divided by the square of the individual’s height
in meters. An overweight and obese person also suffers from one or more of
the following conditions or diseases: (1) Type II Diabetes; (2) Impaired glucose
tolerance; (3) Hyperinsulinemia; (4) Dyslipidemia; (5) Hypertension; (6)
Cardiovascular disease; (7) Cerebrovascular disease; (8) Osteoarthritis of the
hips or knees; (9) Sleep apnea; (10) Gastric reflux disease; or (11) Gall bladder
disease.

SECTION 3. Establishment of Research Center by the Department of
Health. – The Obesity Prevention Research Center to be established by the
Department of Health (DOH) is authorized to sample and collect data on
individual cases where obesity is being actively treated and to analyze such
data in order to evaluate the impact of treating obesity. Such data collection
and analysis shall include the following:

      a. The epidemiology, risk factors, screening and diagnosis,
         complications, and control of obesity in Filipino adults, adolescents,
         and children;
      b. The effectiveness of existing methods for treating or preventing
         obesity;

      c. The effectiveness of alternate methods for treating or preventing
         obesity;
      d. The fiscal impact of treating or preventing obesity;
      e. The compliance and cooperation of patients with various methods of
         treating or preventing obesity; or
      f. The reduction in serious medical problems associated with diabetes
         that results from treating or preventing obesity.

SECTION 4. Funding. – The DOH is authorized to fund the research center
authorized in this Act from gifts, grants, and donations from individuals,
private organizations, foundations, or any governmental unit; except that no
gift, grant, or donation may be accepted by the DOH if it is subject to
conditions that are inconsistent with this Act or any other laws of this State.
The DOH shall have the power to direct the disposition of any such gift, grant,
or donation for the purposes of this Act.

After completion of the research, the DOH shall submit a report and
supporting materials to the legislature by June 1 of the following year.

SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DOH shall be
primarily responsible for implementing the provisions of this Act. The DOH,
in consultation with relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary
implementing rules and regulations within thirty (30) days upon the effectivity
of this Act.

SECTION 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part hereof is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not
otherwise affected shall remain valid and subsisting.

SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is hereby repealed,
modified, or amended accordingly.

SECTION 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days
following its publication in the Official Gazette and/or in newspapers of
general circulation in the Philippines.
