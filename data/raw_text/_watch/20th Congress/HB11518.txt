                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Fifth District of Quezon City, or the Novaliches District, is one of
the fastest developing areas in Quezon City. It has a size of 5,246.72 hectares,
comprises 14 barangays, and its population has already reached the half-
million mark. Located at the northernmost part of the city, Novaliches serves
as the gateway to the metropolis of the big province of Bulacan and is also the
geographical center of Quezon City, San Jose, and Caloocan City. The District
also houses the La Mesa Dam Watershed, which supplies water to over 20
million residents of Metro Manila, and the La Mesa Eco-Park, an ecological
nature reserve site that has become a favorite destination of city dwellers.

      Apart from its development potential, the Novaliches District has also
been a witness to the Philippines’ rich history.

      The District is the home of the Katipunan Tree, which was a staging
area of the Katipuneros during the Philippine Revolution. Under this old
duhat tree, the Katipuneros held meetings and treated their wounded1.

      Additionally, it is in one of the barangays in Novaliches where Melchora
Aquino, more popularly known as Tandang Sora, last stood her ground and
was eventually arrested by the Guardia Civil on August 29, 1896, prior to her

       On July 2, 2012, a separate legislative district of Novaliches was
reapportioned and created in Quezon City by virtue of Republic Act No. 10170.
It is on this day that the newly established Fifth District of Quezon City was
founded and continued to flourish and thrive with Hon. Alfred Vargas as its
first-ever district representative in the Philippine Congress.

       This bill seeks to commemorate this historic event and declare July 2
of every year as “Araw ng Novaliches”, a special nonworking holiday in the
Fifth District of Quezon City. It is the hope of this measure to instill a sense
of pride and consciousness in all Novaleños.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Araw ng
Novaliches Act".

SECTION 2. Araw ng Novaliches. – The second day of July of every year is
hereby declared as “Araw ng Novaliches”, a special nonworking holiday to be
observed in the Fifth District of Quezon City.

SECTION 3. Implementation. – To ensure meaningful observance of the
“Araw ng Novaliches”, all district barangay government officials, officers of the
public and private sectors shall encourage and afford sufficient time and
opportunities for their employees and students to engage and participate in
any activity conducted within the premises of their respective offices,
establishments, or schools to celebrate the “Araw ng Novaliches”, such as but
not limited to parades, fairs, sportsfest, and the like.

SECTION 4. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, all other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain valid.

SECTION 5. Effectivity. – This Act takes effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
