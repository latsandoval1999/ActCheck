AN ACT
DECLARING BUBULUDTUA FALLS, LOCATED IN BARANGAY NABALAWAG, MUNICIPALITY OF BARIRA,
PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO DEL NORTE, AN ECOTOURISM SITE

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to declare Bubuludtua Falls, located in Barangay Nabalawag within the Municipality of
Barira, as an eco-tourism site.

This 200-foot waterfall was named after the term “Bubuludtua”, an Iranon word meaning “rainbow”;
such a name describes the waterfall’s signature attraction: sunlight hitting the water spray, creating
rainbow-like shimmers, making it a popular subject for loca! travel and landscape photography.

It is one of the taller waterfalls in the region, surrounded by dense forests, cool mists, and a dramatic
vertical drop, documented by local travelers since 2018, and is now included in commercial travel
listings.

The area is ideal for quiet, off-the-beaten-path adventures, hiking, trekking, camping, nature, wildlife,
and bird photography.

As one of the many tourism havens that has the potential to attract even international visitors,
established nature and bird photography enthusiasts, and mountaineers, declaring Bubuludtua Falls
an ecotourism site will result in its development and improvement as a tourist destination, and more
importantly, the economic progress of the local community.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

AN ACT
DECLARING BUBULUDTUA FALLS, LOCATED IN BARANGAY NABALAWAG, MUNICIPALITY OF BARIRA,
PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO DEL NORTE, AN ECOTOURISM SITE

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
Assembled:

SECTION 1. The Bubuludtua Falls located in Barangay Nabalawag, Municipality of Barira, Province of
Maguindanao del Norte, is hereby declared an ecotourism site. Pursuant thereto, its development shall
be prioritized and supported by the Department of Tourism (DOT), subject to the rules and regulations
governing the development of ecotourism sites.

SEC. 2. Within one (1) year after the approval of this Act, the local government of the Municipality of
Barira shall, in coordination with the DOT, Department of Environment and Natural Resources, and
concerned agencies of the government, prepare a tourism development plan that shall include the
construction, installation, and maintenance of such appropriate infrastructure and facilities to enhance
tourism in the area: Provided, That said development plan shall ensure the preservation and
conservation of the natural beauty and resources of the area: Provided further, That the
implementation of the tourism development plan shall be included in the budget of the local
government of the Municipality of Barira.

SEC. 3. The DOT shall assist the local government of the Municipality of Barira in the implementation
of the tourism development plan by providing technical assistance in tourism capacity building. It shall
also include the Bubuludtua Falls in its international, national, and regional promotion programs.

SEC. 4. The funding for the implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual budget of the
tocal government of the Municipality of Barira. The DOT may augment such allocation with internally-
generated funds.

SEC. 5. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the local government of the Municipality
of Barira shall, in consultation with the DOT and concerned agencies of the government, promulgate
rules and regulations to implement this Act.

SEC. 6. All laws, rules, regulations, executive orders, proclamations, presidential decrees and other
issuances inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed repealed or amended
accordingly.

SEC. 7. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a
newspaper of general circulation.
