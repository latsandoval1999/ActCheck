                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

     Presidential Decree No. 1271, as amended, was enacted to provide a
mechanism for the validation of certain land titles issued covering lands within
the Baguio Townsite Reservation. It recognized that, despite the nullification of
decrees of registration and certificates of titles issued pursuant to Republic Act.
No. 931, as amended, equity and fairness demanded that titles over lands which
are alienable and disposable and which satisfied the requirements of the said law
be afforded an opportunity for validation.
      The reglementary period of availing validation started in March 1978. It was
extended by Presidential Decree No. 1651 and Presidential Decree No. 2034 and
eventually expired in February 1987. Numerous petitions for validation were filed
within the reglementary period, of which not less than two thousand six hundred
thirteen (2,613) have not been acted upon and remain unresolved to this day. As
a result, many petitioners and their successors-in -interest continue to suffer from
uncertainty regarding the status of their properties, adversely affecting the
exercise of property rights, the transfer and development of land, and the orderly
administration of land ownership within the Baguio Townsite Reservation.
     One of the factors contributing to the delay is the present composition of the
Validation Committee, which composed of the Secretary of Justice, the Solicitor
General, and the Director of the Land Management Bureau, coupled with the
absence of a specific budgetary allocation for the Committee to support the
discharge of its functions. There is no question as to the competence, expertise,
and dedication of these officials in the discharge of their respective duties,
however, the Department of Justice, the Office of the Solicitor General, and the
Land Management Bureau under the Department of Environment and Natural
Resources, are each entrusted with broad and demanding national mandates and
are continuously confronted with numerous and important governmental
responsibilities requiring their immediate attention. Consequently, the members
of the present Validation Committee are necessarily called upon to attend to
numerous other official functions in addition to their responsibilities under PD
1271. The situation makes it difficult for them to devote sufficient time and
attention to the substantial number of petitions for validation pending before the
Committee. While the existing composition may have been appropriate at the
time the Decree was issued, the experience of its implementation demonstrates
the need for a more accessible and responsive mechanism that can devote focused
attention to the resolution of these long-pending petitions.
      The proposed localization of the Validation Committee is therefore not
intended to diminish or question the competence of its present members. Rather,
it seeks to complement their expertise by entrusting the validation function to
local officials who are physically and institutionally closer to the properties,
records, petitioners, and other stakeholders directly affected by the proceedings.
      Considering that the subject properties, records, and government offices
directly involved in the validation process are principally located in Baguio City,
it is both practical and efficient to constitute a localized Validation Committee
composed, among others, of the Regional Director of the Department of
Environment and Natural Resources- Cordillera Administrative Region, The City
Mayor of Baguio City, the Community Environment and Natural Resources
Officer (CENRO), the Baguio City Legal Officer, together with other appropriate
members whose functions and expertise are relevant to the validation of the
subject land titles.
     A localized Committee will be in a better position to undertake the necessary
evaluation of petitions, coordinate with concerned government agencies, examine
records and technical documents, and conduct such proceedings as may be
necessary for the proper disposition of the petitions. More importantly, it will
provide a mechanism through which long-pending petitions may be addressed
with greater focus, accessibility, and administrative efficiency.
     This proposed measure therefore seeks to amend Section 6 of Presidential
Decree No. 1271, as amended, by reconstituting and localizing the Validation
Committee. The passage of this measure will help bring long-overdue resolution
to the numerous petitions for validation that have remained pending for many
years, promote the orderly and efficient administration of lands within the Baguio
Townsite Reservation, and strengthen the government’s commitment to ensuring
a fair, accessible, and expeditious validation process.
    In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

 AN ACT AMENDING SECTION 6 OF PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO.
 1271, AS AMENDED, ENTITLED “AN ACT NULLIFYING DECREES
  OF REGISTRATION AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE COVERING
LANDS WITHIN THE BAGUIO TOWNSITE RESERVATION ISSUED
   IN CIVIL RESERVATION CASE NO. 1, GLRO RECORD NO. 211
    PURSUANT TO REPUBLIC ACT NO. 913, AS AMENDED, BUT
CONSIDERING AS VALID CERTAIN TITLES OF SUCH LANDS THAT
       ARE ALIENABLE AND DISPOSABLE UNDER CERTAIN
 CONDITIONS AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”, TO LOCALIZE THE
        COMPOSITION OF THE VALIDATION COMMITTEE


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:
SECTION 1. Section 6 of Presidential Decree No. 1271, as amended, is hereby
amended to read as follows:
      “Section 6. There is hereby constituted a Baguio Townsite Validation
Committee which shall evaluate and determine all petitions for validation filed
pursuant to this Decree.
      The Committee shall be composed of the following:
       a. The Regional Executive Director of the Department of Environment and
Natural Resources- Cordillera Administrative Region (DENR-CAR), as
chairperson;
      b. The City Mayor of Baguio City, as vice-chairperson;
      c. The Community Environment and Natural Resources Officer (CENRO)
of Baguio City, as member
      d. The City Assessor of Baguio City, as member;
      e. The City Planning Officer of Baguio City, as member;
      f. The City Legal Officer of Baguio City, as member; and
      g. The City Prosecutor of Baguio City, as member, and
       h. The Chairman of the Committee on Urban Planning, Lands, and Housing
of the Baguio City Council.
    The Committee shall adopt its own rules of procedure consistent with this
Decree and shall decide petitions for validation by a majority vote of all its
members.
SEC. 2. All petitions for validation pending before the Committee at the time of
the effectivity of this Act shall continue to be heard and decided by the
reconstituted Committee created under this Act without prejudice to any
proceedings already undertaken.
SEC. 3. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, or parts thereof
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.
SEC. 4. If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the
remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SEC. 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
