                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental condition that affects
social communication, behavior, and sensory processing. Individuals on the autism
spectrum possess diverse abilities, talents, and potentials, yet many continue to face
barriers to early diagnosis, healthcare, education, employment, and full participation
in society. Families and caregivers likewise carry significant responsibilities in ensuring
the well-being and development of persons with ASD, often without adequate
institutional support.

     In the Philippines, services related to autism remain unevenly distributed and
frequently inaccessible, particularly in underserved communities. Long waiting periods
for assessment, limited numbers of trained specialists, insufficient intervention
services, and gaps in inclusive education and employment support create substantial
obstacles for individuals with ASD and their families. While existing laws such as the
Magna Carta for Persons with Disability and the Universal Health Care Act provide
general protections, a comprehensive and coordinated national framework specifically
addressing Autism Spectrum Disorder remains necessary.

      This proposed measure seeks to establish a National Plan of Action for Autism
Spectrum Disorder, ensuring coordinated policies, programs, and services across
government agencies and sectors. The bill aims to strengthen systems for early
detection, screening, and diagnosis; expand access to evidence-based health and
intervention services; promote inclusive education and skills development; support
employment opportunities; and enhance social protection and community-based
support for persons with ASD and their families.

      The measure likewise emphasizes multi-sectoral collaboration among national
government agencies, local government units, academic institutions, civil society
organizations, and autism self-advocates to ensure evidence-based policymaking and
effective service delivery. It also underscores the importance of public awareness and
advocacy in reducing stigma and fostering a society that values neurodiversity and
inclusion.

     Ultimately, this bill recognizes that persons with Autism Spectrum Disorder are
integral members of our society whose dignity, rights, and potential must be protected
and nurtured. By strengthening institutional support and promoting inclusive
environments, the State affirms its commitment to ensuring that persons with ASD are
empowered to live meaningful, productive, and fulfilling lives.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                  AN ACT
     INSTITUTING A NATIONAL PLAN OF ACTION FOR THE CARE, SUPPORT AND
     INCLUSION OF PERSONS WITH AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER AND
     APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1       SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "National Autism Care,
 2   Support, and Inclusion Act."
 3
 4         SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy - the State recognizes persons with Autism
 5   Spectrum Disorder (ASD) as persons with disability, protects and upholds their rights,
 6   dignity, and inherent worth, and promotes their full inclusion, participation, and well-
 7   being in society.
 8
 9          To this end, the State shall pursue responsive, inclusive, and coordinated
10   policies, programs, and services that ensure access to early detection and screening,
11   diagnosis, timely and evidence-based intervention, quality healthcare, inclusive
12   education, employment opportunities, and appropriate social support for persons with
13   ASD and their families in accordance with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) for
14   good health, quality education, decent work, and reduced inequalities. These policies,
15   programs, and services shall contribute to sustainable development by building
16   inclusive societies that leave no one behind, ensuring long-term equity, empowerment,
17   and advancement for persons with ASD.
18
19           The State shall foster an environment that is supportive and free from stigma
20   and discrimination, enabling persons with ASD to achieve holistic development,
21   independence, self-reliance, and productive participation in community life, consistent
22   with their individual capacities and aspirations. The State shall strengthen institutional
23   and community-based mechanisms, promote multi-sectoral collaboration and
24   encourage civil society and private sector participation to complement government
25   efforts, in accordance with this Act and existing laws.

 2         SEC. 3. Definition of Terms used in this Act -
 3
 4              a)  Autism or Autism Spectrum Disorder ASD refer to a
 5                  neurodevelopmental condition characterized by persistent differences
 6                  in social communication and interaction, and restricted or repetitive
 7                  patterns of behavior interest, or activity), as recognized in the
 8                  Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders          (DSM)
 9                  and the World Health Organization (WHO) International Classification
10                  of Diseases (ICD-11), where ASD is classified under
11                  neurodevelopmental disorders;
12              b) Early Detection and Screening refer to community or facility-based
13                  activities undertaken by trained health. personnel to identify early
14                  signs of ASD and facilitate timely referral for appropriate assessment;
15              c) Inclusive Education refers to the process of addressing and
16                 responding to the diverse needs of all learners by ensuring full
17                 participation, presence, and achievement in learning environments,
18                 consistent with Republic Act No. 11650 or the "Instituting a Policy of
19                 Inclusion and Services for Learners with Disabilities in Support of
20                 Inclusive Education Act, including the provision of reasonable
21                 accommodation and learning support aides and the implementation of
22                 the Child Find System;
23              d) Intervention Services refer to evidence-based medical, therapeutic,
24                 educational, habilitative, and other support services intended to
25                 improve functioning. development, and quality of life of persons with
26                 ASD;
27              e) Neurodivergent refers to individuals whose neurological development
28                 or functioning differs from what is typically expected, including but not
29                 limited to persons with ASD and other neurodevelopmental conditions
30                 recognized in the DSM or the WHO ICD-11. for purposes of
31                 determining appropriate support and reasonable accommodation
32                 under this Act;
33              f) Persons with ASD refer to individuals clinically diagnosed with ASD.
34                 References in this Act to "Persons with Autism", "Persons with Autism
35                 Spectrum Disorder", or "Autistic Persons" shall be understood as
36                 equivalent and non-hierarchical, and shall not be construed to diminish
37                 the dignity, identity, or self-identification of individuals on the autism
38                 spectrum, and;
39              g) Universal Design refers to the design of products, environments,
40                 programs, and services to be usable by all people, to the greatest
41                 extent possible, without the need for adaptation or specialized design,
42                 consistent with internationally recognized disability frameworks and
43                 existing Philippine accessibility laws. Universal design may include
44                 sensory-inclusive design approaches that accommodate differences in
45                 sensory processing, cognition, and regulation, particularly in settings
46                 intended for individuals on the autism spectrum;

48         SEC. 4. National Plan of Action for Autism Spectrum Disorder. - A National
49   Plan of Action for Autism Spectrum Disorder shall be formulated. It shall consist of
50   coordinated policies, programs, projects, services, and activities to ensure access to

 1   early detection and screening, diagnosis, timely and evidence-based intervention,
 2   quality healthcare, inclusive education, employment opportunities, and appropriate
 3   social support for persons with ASD and their families.
 4
 5           The Plan shall provide for a baseline package of services accessible to persons
 6   with ASD and a framework for needs-based service intensification guided by objective
 7   criteria and rights-based safeguards, to ensure equitable prioritization where service
 8   capacity is limited. It shall be subject to regular review and updating to address
 9   emerging needs and developments, and its actions shall be progressively
10   mainstreamed and integrated into the plans programs, and budgets of relevant
11   government agencies and sectors.
12
13            Facilities, programs, and services established or supported under this Act shall
14   be guided by accessibility and universal design principles, consistent with existing
15   disability and accessibility laws, to promote inclusive and usable environments. The
16   Plan shall include measurable targets and service capacity benchmarks for early
17   detection, screening. diagnosis, and intervention services, including, as applicable,
18   required geographic coverage, referral network standards, and time-bound access
19   indicators such as maximum acceptable waiting periods from screening to
20   assessment and initiation of intervention, with due consideration for Geographically
21   Isolated and Disadvantage Areas (GIDAs).
22
23         The National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA), in coordination with the
24   Department of Health (DOH) and concerned agencies, shall submit an annual
25   implementation report to Congress detailing progress against these targets, service
26   coverage gaps, and proposed corrective measures.
27
28        SEC. 5. Inter-Agency and Multi-Sectoral Coordination for Planning and
29   Implementation, - The NCDA shall lead in the formulation of the National Plan of
30   Action for Autism Spectrum Disorder, in coordination with the DOH. Department of
31   Education (DepEd), Commission on Higher Education (CHED), Technical Education
32   and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), Department of Labor and Employment
33   (DOLE), Civil Service Commission (CSC). Department of the Interior and Local
34   Government (DILG), Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), other
35   concerned national government agencies, local government units (LGUs), and in
36   consultation with relevant civil society organizations, autism self-advocates. parent
37   groups, and other stakeholders.
38
39           The NCDA, in coordination with the DOH. shall strengthen collaboration with
40   academic and research institutions, including the University of the Philippines National
41   Institutes of Health (UP-NIH), the Jose R. Reyes Memorial Medical Center (JRRMMC),
42   the National Center for Mental Health (NCMH), and professional organizations
43   specializing in neurodevelopmental disorder, to support autism research, surveillance,
44   data collection, and evidence-based policymaking. Such collaboration shall include,
45   but not limited to the establishment of research partnerships, data-sharing
46   mechanisms, and the integration of research findings into the Plan. It shall also
47   advocate, coordinate, oversee, and assess the implementation of this Act and the Plan,
48   through existing inter-agency mechanisms. The NCDA shall, through its Executive
49   Director. provide secretariat support and assistance to the planning. implementation,
50   monitoring, and evaluation of the Plan.

37   employment, livelihood, social protection, and participation in decisions affecting their
38   welfare.
