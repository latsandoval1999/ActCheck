                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to expand mental health coverage under the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) by establishing a Mental Health Voucher System
(MHVS). It institutes tiered benefit packages calibrated to the severity of a
beneficiary's condition and establishes a dedicated Mental Health Fund Pool to
ensure sustainable financing.

       Mental health remains one of the most underfunded and inaccessible areas of
healthcare in the Philippines. Despite the enactment of Republic Act No. 11036,
otherwise known as the “Mental Health Act”, which legally recognized mental health
care as a fundamental right, substantial barriers to treatment persist. High costs for
psychiatric consultations, therapy, and essential medications remain a major
obstacle, leaving many Filipinos unable to maintain the continuity of care necessary
for recovery. This current gap in financing prevents timely interventions and
undermines the effectiveness of the national mental health strategy.

       This bill creates a dedicated, needs-based financing mechanism for mental
health care. In fulfillment of this goal, it promotes early diagnosis, continuity of
treatment, and improved health outcomes for Filipinos living with mental health
conditions.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                         AN ACT
EXPANDING MENTAL HEALTH COVERAGE UNDER THE PHILIPPINE
HEALTH INSURANCE CORPORATION BY ESTABLISHING A MENTAL
HEALTH VOUCHER SYSTEM, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Mental Health Voucher
Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people, including the right to mental
health. It shall also ensure that essential health services are accessible, affordable,
and equitable for all.

      Towards this end, the State shall strengthen mental health care financing
under the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation, in support of Republic Act (RA)
No. 11036, otherwise known as the “Mental Health Act”. It shall expand benefit
coverage and introduce a Mental Health Voucher System to promote early diagnosis,
continuity of care, and improved health outcomes.

       SEC. 3. Mental Health Voucher System. – A Mental Health Voucher System
(MHVS), which shall serve as a financing mechanism to provide eligible members
and their qualified dependents with access to mental health services and
medications through pre–approved financial subsidies, is established under the
Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth).

       Under the MHVS, PhilHealth shall issue mental health vouchers to qualified
beneficiaries. Each voucher represents a pre–approved financial subsidy that must
be used exclusively for mental health benefit packages and services covered under
this Act. The voucher is redeemable only through accredited mental health service
providers.

       PhilHealth shall ensure that the MHVS is implemented in a manner that
promotes accessibility, continuity of care, and the efficient delivery of mental health
services.

       SEC. 4. Qualifications. – Any duly registered member of PhilHealth, including
qualified dependents, who has been clinically diagnosed with a mental health
condition by a licensed physician, is eligible to apply for a mental health voucher.

        Eligibility for the issuance of a voucher is determined based on a duly
accomplished mental health assessment certificate issued and signed by a licensed
physician, preferably a psychiatrist, and affiliated with an accredited government
hospital under the Department of Health (DOH). The certificate shall indicate the
clinical diagnosis of the patient, the recommended treatment plan, and the required
services and prescribed medications.

       SEC. 5. Application and Evaluation. – The applicant shall submit the mental
health assessment certificate, together with such other documents as may be
required, through a prescribed application process. PhilHealth shall establish a
streamlined and accessible system for the receipt, verification, and evaluation of
applications, which may include digital or electronic platforms, consistent with the
principles of ease of access and administrative efficiency.

       SEC. 6. Issuance and Validity. – Upon verification of eligibility and
completeness of requirements, PhilHealth shall issue a non–transferable mental
health voucher in the name of the qualified beneficiary, indicating the applicable
benefit package, authorized services, and period of validity.

       The voucher shall be valid for a period of not less than six (6) but not more
than twelve (12) months, or such other period as may be determined by PhilHealth,
consistent with the prescribed period and based on clinical assessment and
treatment requirements. The voucher is renewable annually, subject to re–evaluation
and the submission of an updated mental health assessment certificate.

        PhilHealth shall adopt appropriate safeguards to ensure the authenticity of
certifications, prevent fraudulent claims, and be used solely for their intended
purpose, without prejudice to the imposition of administrative, civil, or criminal
liability under existing laws.

       SEC. 7. Mental Health Packages. – PhilHealth shall develop, adopt, and
periodically review tiered mental health benefit packages, which shall be responsive
to the varying levels of severity, clinical needs, and continuity of care requirements
of beneficiaries.

      The benefit packages shall, at a minimum, include the following:

         a.​ Basic package for outpatient and early–stage interventions, which
      entitles the beneficiary to not less than six (6) to ten (10) psychiatric
      consultations per year, at least six (6) therapy sessions, and a corresponding

      PhilHealth shall, in coordination with DOH, determine the specific scope,
frequency, and monetary value of services covered in each package in accordance
with prevailing clinical standards, cost assessments, and actuarial studies. Further,
PhilHealth shall structure the benefit packages to promote early diagnosis, ensure
continuity of care, and prevent the progression of mental health conditions, without
prejudice to the grant of additional benefits as may be warranted.

       SEC. 8. Review and Adjustment. – PhilHealth is authorized to revise, expand, or
restructure the benefit packages, including the addition of services and adjustments
to coverage limits, as may be necessary to respond to emerging mental health needs,
advances in treatment, and financial sustainability of the program: Provided, That
such revisions shall not diminish the minimum entitlements established under this
Act.

       SEC. 9. Establishment of the Mental Health Fund Pool. – To ensure the
sustainability and efficient administration of the program, there is hereby
established within PhilHealth, a dedicated Mental Health Fund Pool, which shall be
used exclusively for the financing of benefits, services, and administrative
requirements under the MHVS.

       PhilHealth shall ensure that the allocation and utilization of the Mental
Health Fund Pool are consistent with actuarial soundness, fiscal sustainability, and
the timely delivery of benefits to qualified members and their dependents. For this
purpose, PhilHealth shall incorporate the funding requirements of the MHVS in its
annual budget proposals, and shall adopt appropriate financial management and
accountability mechanisms.

      The DOH and the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) shall, in
coordination with PhilHealth, ensure the adequate and continuing funding of the
program, consistent with national health priorities and the objectives of this Act.

       The implementation of the MHVS under this Act shall be funded from the
available funds of PhilHealth, such sums as may be appropriated annually under
the General Appropriations Act, and such other sources as may be identified or

authorized by law, including allocations from sin taxes and other earmarked health
revenues.

      SEC. 10. Reportorial Requirements. – PhilHealth shall, in coordination with the
DOH, submit to the Congress of the Philippines an annual report on the
implementation of the MHVS within ninety (90) days from the end of each fiscal
year.

        The report shall include, among others, the number of beneficiaries served,
utilization rates of each benefit package, total expenditures, geographic distribution
of services, and such other relevant information as may be necessary to assess the
effectiveness, efficiency, and financial sustainability of the program.

      PhilHealth shall likewise publish a summary of the report in its official
website to ensure transparency and public access to information, subject to existing
laws on data privacy and confidentiality.

      SEC. 11. Implementing Agencies. – PhilHealth shall serve as the lead
implementing agency for the MHVS established under this Act and shall be
primarily responsible for its administration, financing, and overall management.

       The DOH shall, in coordination with PhilHealth, establish and periodically
update the clinical, diagnostic, and treatment standards applicable for the
implementation of this Act, ensure the quality and appropriateness of mental health
services rendered by accredited providers, and assist in the formulation of policies
and guidelines.

       The National Mental Health Council (NMHC) shall act as a coordinating and
advisory body in the implementation of this Act, particularly in ensuring alignment
with the National Mental Health Program, promoting inter–agency coordination,
and supporting the development of policies and strategies relating to mental health
care delivery, data management, and program evaluation.

       PhilHealth, DOH and NMHC shall ensure close coordination in the
development of benefit packages, accreditation of mental health service providers,
integration of mental health data systems, monitoring of service delivery, and
evaluation of program outcomes, with due consideration to accessibility, quality of
care, and continuity of treatment.

      SEC. 12. Monitoring and Data Management. – PhilHealth shall establish and
maintain a secure and integrated digital monitoring and data management system to
ensure the efficient processing of applications, accurate tracking of voucher issuance
and utilization, and the continuity of care for beneficiaries across accredited service
providers.

       The system shall, at a minimum, maintain a centralized database of
beneficiaries and services availed, generate relevant data to support policy
development, budget planning, and program evaluation, and incorporate

safeguards to detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and overutilization of
benefits.

       PhilHealth shall coordinate with the DOH and NMHC to promote
interoperability and alignment with existing national mental health information
systems and data governance frameworks.

        SEC. 13. Data Privacy. – PhilHealth shall ensure that all data collected,
processed, and stored under this system are protected in accordance with RA No.
10173, otherwise known as the “Data Privacy Act of 2012”, and other applicable laws,
rules, and regulations. Likewise, PhilHealth shall adopt appropriate administrative,
technical, and organizational safeguards to preserve the confidentiality, integrity,
and security of personal and health information.

       SEC. 14. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current operating budget of PhilHealth.
Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 15. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the President and Chief Executive Officer of PhilHealth, in
coordination with the Secretary of Health and other relevant agencies, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of
this Act.

       SEC. 16. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
force and effect.

       SEC. 17. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 18. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
