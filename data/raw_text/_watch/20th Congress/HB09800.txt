                            AN ACT
RATIONALIZING THE BARANGAY NEWLY-ELECTED OFFICIALS (BNEO)
PROGRAM BY LIMITING MANDATORY PARTICIPATION TO FIRST-TIME ELECTED
AND NEWLY APPOINTED BARANGAY OFFICIALS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

The barangay is the government unit closest to the people and serves as the primary
frontline provider of public services in every community. To perform their duties
effectively, barangay of icials must be equipped with the necessary knowledge and
skills in governance, planning, inancial management, and accountability. For this
purpose, the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) requires
participation in the Barangay Newly-Elected Of icials (BNEO) Program, which
provides basic orientation and training for barangay of icials.

While the BNEO Program remains an important tool for capacity-building, requiring
all elected of icials to repeatedly attend the same basic orientation, even those who
have previously served and completed similar trainings, results in unnecessary and
duplicative expenditures. These costs, which are borne by barangay funds, could
otherwise be used to support essential programs, projects, and services that directly
bene it local communities.

This bill seeks to rationalize the implementation of the BNEO Program by limiting
mandatory participation in the Basic Orientation Course to irst-time elected
barangay of icials and newly appointed of icials who have not previously served in an
elective barangay position. It likewise directs the DILG to recognize prior completion

of substantially similar training programs to prevent repetitive training requirements,
unless additional or updated learning is necessary.

By focusing resources on of icials who genuinely need foundational training, this
measure promotes more ef icient use of public funds, avoids unnecessary expenses,
and strengthens the professionalization of barangay governance.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “BNEO Rationalization
Act of 2026.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote ef icient, economical, and effective local governance by ensuring that
capacity development programs for barangay of icials are targeted to those who
need foundational orientation, while avoiding unnecessary or duplicative training
expenses chargeable to barangay funds, consistent with the Local Government Code
of 1991.

SECTION 3. De inition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act, the following terms
shall mean:

   (a) BNEO Program — the Barangay Newly-Elected Of icials program or its
       successor capacity development program for barangay of icials as designed
       and implemented by the Department of the Interior and Local Government
       (DILG), through the Local Government Academy (LGA) and other partner
       agencies;

                                                                           3|Page
   (b) First-time elected barangay of icial — a person who is elected for the irst
       time to any of the following barangay elective positions: Punong Barangay or
       Sangguniang Barangay Member;

   (c) Newly appointed barangay of icial — a person who is appointed to ill a
       vacancy in an elective barangay position, who has not previously served in any
       elective barangay position;

   (d) Returning barangay of icial — a person who has previously served as an
       elective barangay of icial, whether immediately preceding the current term or
       in any prior term, and is elected again to any elective barangay position;

   (e) Basic Orientation Course — the foundational module(s) of the BNEO
       Program intended to orient of icials on barangay governance, roles, and core
       legal/administrative responsibilities.

SECTION 4. Mandatory Coverage; Limitation to First-Timers. —

   (a) Mandatory participation in the Basic Orientation Course of the BNEO
       Program shall apply only to:

      1. First-time elected barangay of icials; and
      2. Newly appointed barangay of icials.

   (b) Returning barangay of icials shall not be required to undergo the Basic
       Orientation Course as a condition for the performance of their functions,
       access to barangay funds, or eligibility for any bene it or recognition program
       of DILG or any national government agency.

   (c) Nothing in this Act shall be construed to prohibit returning barangay of icials
       from voluntarily enrolling in the Basic Orientation Course or other BNEO
       modules, subject to Section 5.

SECTION 5. Rules on Funding and Chargeability of Expenses. —

   (a) Expenses for the conduct of the BNEO Basic Orientation Course for covered
       of icials under Section 4(a) may be charged against:



                                                                          4|Page
       1. Appropriate barangay funds, subject to applicable budgeting and auditing
          rules; and/or
       2. Augmentation support from the city/municipal government or other
          lawful sources, consistent with DILG guidance on on-site conduct and cost
          ef iciency.

   (b) For returning barangay of icials who voluntarily enroll, expenses chargeable
       against barangay funds shall be limited to actual, necessary, and reasonable
       costs, and shall be subject to the prioritization of basic services and programs
       under existing law.

   (c) The DILG shall ensure that the Basic Orientation Course is designed and
       delivered in a manner that minimizes costs, including, where practicable:

       1. Conducting trainings on-site within the city/municipality; and

       2. Allowing blended, modular, or other cost-reducing delivery modes
          consistent with training effectiveness.

SECTION 6. Standardized Equivalency and Recognition of Prior Training. —

   (a) The DILG shall establish an equivalency mechanism recognizing prior
       completion of any substantially similar barangay governance orientation
       course administered or accredited by the DILG/LGA, for purposes of
       compliance with Section 4(a).

   (b) A barangay of icial who has previously completed the Basic Orientation Course
       (or its equivalent) shall not be required to repeat the same course, unless the
       DILG determines that substantial program changes justify a short bridging
       module.

SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DILG, through the LGA, in coordination with the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM) and other concerned agencies, shall
promulgate the implementing rules and regulations (IRR) to carry out the provisions
of this Act.

SECTION 8. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.
