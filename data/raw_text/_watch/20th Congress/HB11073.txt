ACTIVITIES IN PUBLIC PARKS, STRENGTHENING SPORTS
DEVELOPMENT AMONG YOUTH AND COMMUNITIES, AND
PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR


                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Sports and recreational activities are essential components of youth
development, physical health, mental well-being, and nation-building. They foster
discipline, teamwork, resilience, and social cohesion among individuals and
communities.

       Despite the constitutional mandate recognizing the role of sports in fostering
self-reliance and national development, access to safe and organized recreational
spaces remains limited in many communities. Public parks, which are intended as
communal spaces for recreation and social engagement, are often underutilized or
lack structured programs that encourage active participation in sports and physical
activities.



                                                                                     1
        This bill seeks to institutionalize the “Laro’t Saya sa Parke (LSP)” Program,
 that was created under the Memorandum Circular No. 49 issued by the Office of the
 Presidential, as a national initiative that promotes organized sports and recreational
 activities in public parks during weekends. It aims to encourage active lifestyles,
 strengthen community engagement, and provide accessible sports opportunities for
 children, youth, and families.

        By leveraging public parks as inclusive spaces for recreation and sports
 development, this measure seeks to address sedentary lifestyles, promote mental and
 physical well-being, and cultivate a culture of sportsmanship and community
 participation among Filipinos.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                   AN ACT
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE “LARO’T SAYA SA PARKE” PROGRAM,
PROMOTING WEEKEND COMMUNITY SPORTS AND RECREATIONAL
ACTIVITIES IN PUBLIC PARKS, STRENGTHENING SPORTS
DEVELOPMENT AMONG YOUTH AND COMMUNITIES, AND
PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR
     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Laro’t Saya sa
Parke Act.”


      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to promote the development of sports and physical fitness as an integral


                                                                              1
component of youth development, public health, and nation-building. The State
recognizes the importance of accessible recreational spaces in fostering active,
healthy, and socially engaged communities.


      The State shall institutionalize community-based sports and recreational
programs in public parks and open spaces, particularly during weekends, to
encourage widespread participation in physical activities and to strengthen social
cohesion among individuals and families.


      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:


      (a). Laro’t Saya sa Parke Program refers to a national community-based
      initiative that promotes organized sports, games, and recreational activities
      in public parks during weekends;


      (b). Public Parks refers to open and publicly accessible spaces designated
      for recreation, sports, and leisure activities under the management of Local
      Government Units (LGUs);


      (c). Participants refers to the individuals, including children, youth, adults,
      and senior citizens, who engage in program activities;


      SECTION 4. Institutionalization of the Laro’t Saya sa Parke Program. --
Thereby institutionalized the Laro’t Saya sa Parke Program, a nationwide initiative
to be implemented primarily during weekends in designated public parks and open
community spaces.


                                                                                        2
      The program shall be jointly implemented by the Department of the Interior
and Local Government (DILG), Department of Education (DepEd), Philippine
Sports Commission (PSC), Local Government Units (LGUs), and the Sangguniang
Kabataan (SK), in coordination with other relevant agencies and stakeholders.


      SECTION 5. Objectives. - The Laro’t Saya sa Parke Program shall have the
following objectives:


      (a) To promote sports and physical fitness as a vital component of youth and
      community development;
      (b) To encourage active and healthy lifestyles among Filipinos through
      accessible recreational activities;
      (c) To utilize public parks as inclusive spaces for sports, recreation, and social
      interaction;
      (d) To strengthen community engagement and social cohesion through
      organized weekend activities;
      (e) To develop sports interest and talent at the grassroots level;
      (f) To promote mental well-being and reduce sedentary lifestyles among
      youth and families.


      SECTION 6. Implementation in Public Parks. – All Local Government
Units shall designate suitable public parks and open spaces for the regular conduct
of Laro’t Saya sa Parke activities every weekend.




                                                                                      3
      LGUs shall ensure that designated areas are safe, accessible, and equipped
with basic facilities necessary for sports and recreational activities, including but not
limited to open fields, courts, and multipurpose spaces.

      SECTION 7. Program Activities – The Laro’t Saya sa Parke Program shall
consist of structured, inclusive, and age-appropriate sports, fitness, and recreational
activities to be conducted regularly, primarily during weekends, in designated public
parks and open spaces.


      Such activities shall include, but shall not be limited to, traditional Filipino
games (larong pinoy), team sports such as basketball, volleyball, and football,
individual and group fitness activities, youth sports clinics, recreational
competitions, family-oriented games, and community-based wellness programs.


      The Philippine Sports Commission (PSC), in coordination with the
Department of Education (DepEd) and Local Government Units (LGUs), shall
develop program guidelines to ensure that activities are development-oriented,
culturally relevant, safe, and responsive to the needs of various age groups and
sectors. Program activities shall be designed to promote physical fitness, teamwork,
discipline, sportsmanship, and mental well-being, and may include talent
identification and grassroots sports development components.


      SECTION 8. Role of Local Government Units. — Local Government Units
(LGUs) shall serve as the primary implementing bodies of the Laro’t Saya sa Parke
Program within their respective jurisdictions and shall ensure the effective, regular,
and sustainable conduct of program activities.



                                                                                       4
      (a). Designate and maintain suitable public parks and open spaces as program
      venues, ensuring that such areas are safe, accessible, and adequately equipped
      for sports and recreational use;
      (b). Allocate sufficient funds and resources for the implementation,
      maintenance, and improvement of program facilities, equipment, and
      logistics;
      (c). Appoint a Local Program Head, who shall oversee the overall planning,
      coordination, implementation, and supervision of program activities, and who
      shall work in close coordination with the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK),
      barangay officials, and relevant stakeholders;
      (d). Develop and implement a regular schedule of weekend activities, taking
      into consideration community needs, participation levels, and inclusivity;
      (e). Ensure the availability of trained facilitators, coaches, volunteers, and
      support personnel;
      (f). Maintain peace and order, and ensure safety and security in all program
      venues; and
      (g). Coordinate with schools, community organizations, civil society groups,
      and private sector partners to promote participation and support program
      implementation.


      SECTION 9: Designation of Local Program Head. –                   Each Local
Government Unit (LGU) shall designate a Local Program Head for the Laro’t Saya
sa Parke Program, who shall be responsible for the overall planning, coordination,
implementation, and supervision of program activities within their jurisdiction.




                                                                                   5
      The Local Program Head shall be appointed by the Local Chief Executive and
may be drawn from the local sports office, youth development office, or any
appropriate unit of the LGU. The designated official shall closely coordinate with
the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK), barangay officials, and relevant community
stakeholders to ensure the effective conduct of weekly program activities.


      The Local Program Head shall have the following functions:


      (a) Develop and oversee the weekly schedule of sports and recreational
         activities in designated public parks;
      (b) Ensure the availability of facilitators, volunteers, and necessary resources
         for program implementation;
      (c) Coordinate with schools, youth organizations, and community groups to
         encourage participation;
      (d) Ensure compliance with safety, inclusivity, and child protection standards;
      (e) Submit periodic reports on program implementation, participation rates,
         and challenges encountered to the Local Government Unit and relevant
         national agencies.


      The Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) shall serve as a key partner in mobilizing
youth participation, assisting in program facilitation, and promoting community
engagement in all activities under this Act.




                                                                                    6
      SECTION 10. Role of National Agencies. — Relevant national government
agencies shall coordinate to provide technical assistance, program support, and
policy alignment in furtherance of the objectives of this Act;


      (a) The Philippine Sports Commission (PSC) shall serve as the lead technical
          agency and shall provide guidance on program design, sports development,
          training modules, and capacity-building for local implementers;
      (b) The Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) shall
          oversee and monitor LGU compliance with this Act and shall issue policy
          directives to ensure effective local implementation; and
      (c) The Department of Education (DepEd) shall promote student participation
          and integrate awareness of the program into school-based physical
          education and youth development initiatives.


      SECTION 11. Private Sector and Community Participation. – The State
shall encourage the active participation of private entities, sports organizations, non-
government organizations, academic institutions, and community groups in the
implementation of the Laro’t Saya sa Parke Program. Such participation may include
the provision of financial support, sponsorships, sports equipment, technical
expertise, volunteer services, and other forms of assistance, subject to applicable
laws, rules, and regulations.


      Local Government Units may enter into partnerships or agreements with
private and civil society partners to enhance program delivery, provided that such
partnerships uphold the public interest and ensure transparency and accountability.




                                                                                      7
      SECTION 12. Safety and Child Protection Measures. – All activities
conducted under this Act shall adhere to established standards on safety, health, and
child protection.


      Local Government Units shall ensure that:


      (a) Adequate safety measures, including the presence of first aid kits, trained
          first responders, and emergency response protocols, are in place during all
          program activities;
      (b) Facilities and equipment used are safe, well-maintained, and appropriate
          for participants;
      (c) Activities are supervised by qualified personnel to prevent accidents,
          injuries, or misconduct;
      (d) Child protection policies are strictly observed, including safeguarding
          mechanisms against abuse, exploitation, and discrimination; and
      (e) Health protocols, as may be prescribed by relevant authorities, are
          complied with to protect participants from health risks.


      SECTION 13. Inclusion and Accessibility. – The Laro’t Saya sa Parke
Program shall be inclusive and accessible to all sectors of society, without
discrimination based on age, gender, disability, socioeconomic status, or other
similar grounds.




      Local Government Units shall ensure that program activities are designed to
accommodate the participation of children, youth, women, senior citizens, and


                                                                                   8
persons with disabilities, including the provision of reasonable accommodations and
adapted activities where necessary.


      SECTION 14. Funding. – The funding requirements for the implementation
of this Act shall be included in the annual budgets of LGUs and participating national
government agencies. Additional funding may be sourced from partnerships,
donations, and sponsorships, subject to applicable laws and regulations.


      SECTION 15. Monitoring and Evaluation. – The DILG, in coordination
with PSC and LGUs, shall establish a monitoring and evaluation system to assess
program implementation, participation rates, and community impact. An annual
report shall be submitted to Congress.


      SECTION 16. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within ninety
(90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DILG, PSC, and concerned agencies
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations.


      SECTION 17. Separability Clause – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.


      SECTION 18. Repealing Clause – All laws, decrees, and regulations
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


      SECTION 19. Effectivity – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
