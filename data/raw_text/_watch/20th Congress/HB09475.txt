                           AN ACT

IN THE PERSONNEL ECONOMIC RELIEF ALLOWANCE (PERA) GRANTED TO
                   GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Personnel Economic Relief Allowance (PERA) was introduced more than
thirty (30) years ago through Budget Circular No. 4 (1991), which granted a ₱500.00
allowance to government employees to help cushion the adverse effects of inflation
and economic instability in the country. In 2009, an additional ₱1,500.00 was
authorized through Joint Resolution No. 4, s. 2009, in recognition of the rising cost of
living.

       However, the PERA has remained fixed at ₱2,000.00 per month for the past
sixteen (16) years. During this period, the Philippines experienced multiple economic
crises, public emergencies, global supply disruptions, and sustained increases in the
prices of basic commodities, transportation, fuel, electricity, healthcare, and housing.
While essential commodities continued to rise, the value of the PERA steadily
declined.

       To restore its purpose as a genuine economic relief measure, this bill seeks to
increase the PERA to ₱5,000.00. It also mandates a periodic review to ensure that
future adjustments remain reasonable and aligned with the government’s financial
capacity.

      The urgency of this measure has become even more apparent in recent years.
Despite rising daily expenses, public sector workers continue to deliver essential

services during disasters and public emergencies. Teachers continue educating
despite increasing classroom and transportation costs. Health workers, emergency
responders, and maintenance personnel remain on duty even as inflation steadily
diminishes the real value of their salaries and allowances. By providing a more realistic
economic relief allowance, this measure helps ensure that government employees can
continue serving the public while still being able to provide a dignified living for
themselves and their families.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

Section 1. Personnel Economic Relief Allowance. – The Personnel Economic
Relief Allowance (PERA) being granted to employees of the government shall be
subject to an automatic yearly adjustment equivalent to any increase of the annual
inflation rate in the country. Concomitantly, upon the effectivity of this Act, the current
PERA in the amount of Two Thousand Pesos (P2,000) per month is hereby adjusted
to Five Thousand (P5,000) per month.

SEC. 2. Coverage. - The PERA shall be granted to civilian government personnel
whether employed by the national or local governments, appointive or elective, and
whether occupying regular, contractual, or casual positions, whose positions are
covered by Republic Act No. 6758, otherwise known as the "Compensation and
Position Classification Act of 1989," as amended, as well as military and uniformed
personnel: Provided, That government personnel stationed abroad already receiving
overseas allowances shall not be entitled to the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 3. Periodic Review of PERA. - The Department of Budget and Management
(DBM), in coordination with the Civil Service Commission (CSC), the Department of
Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev), and other relevant agencies, shall
conduct a review of the adequacy of the Personnel Economic Relief Allowance



                                            3
(PERA) every three (3) years, taking into account prevailing inflation rates, cost-of-
living indicators, and fiscal sustainability.

SEC.4. Appropriations.– For the first year of the implementation of this Act, funds
necessary for the PERA of national and local government agencies shall be charged
against savings, which for this purpose includes unreleased appropriations and other
programmed appropriations. Thereafter, the amount necessary shall be provided in
the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, rules and regulations,
and other issuances or parts thereof, which are contrary or inconsistent with this Act
are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
