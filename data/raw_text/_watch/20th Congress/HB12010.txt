                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article II, Section 4 of the 1987 Constitution explicitly provides that the prime
duty of the Government is to serve and protect the people, and that the
Government may call upon the people to defend the State. Citizens may be
required, under conditions provided by law, to render military or civil service.

The Citizen Armed Forces, or the Reserve Force of the Armed Forces of the
Philippines (AFP), plays a crucial role in our national security system. They are
our ready base for expansion in the event of war, invasion, or rebellion.
Furthermore, they are indispensable during times of national emergencies,
calamities, and disaster response operations.

Despite their invaluable contribution and the sacrifices they endure, many
members of the Reserve Force face discrimination and job insecurity in the
civilian workforce. Reports of reservists being denied employment, passed over
for promotions, or even terminated due to their military training schedules or
sudden mobilization orders remain a pressing concern. It is inherently unjust
that the men and women who volunteer to serve the country are penalized in their
civilian livelihood.

This bill, the "Reservists Employment Rights Act," seeks to institutionalize the
protection of the employment rights of our citizen soldiers. It guarantees their
security of tenure, prohibits discrimination on the basis of their military service,
and ensures their seamless reintegration into their civilian jobs following
deployment or training. By protecting their livelihood, we do not only honor their
service but also encourage more Filipinos to participate in the Reserve Force.

In view of the foregoing, the swift passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT STRENGTHENING THE EMPLOYMENT RIGHTS OF MEMBERS OF
THE CITIZEN ARMED FORCES OR THE RESERVE FORCE OF THE ARMED
      FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the "Reservists Employment
Rights Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to maintain a strong
and highly responsive Citizen Armed Force. To this end, the State shall protect
the employment rights of the members of the Reserve Force of the Armed Forces
of the Philippines (AFP) and ensure that their civilian livelihood is not prejudiced
by their military service, training, or mobilization.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

(a) Reserve Force refers to the Citizen Armed Forces of the AFP as defined under
Republic Act No. 7077, otherwise known as the "Citizen Armed Forces of the
Philippines Reservist Act."

(b) Reservist refers to a person incorporated into the Reserve Force.

(c) Employer refers to any person, natural or juridical, including the National
Government and its political subdivisions, employing the services of a reservist.

(d) Military Service refers to any active duty training, active duty tour, or
mobilization in times of war, national emergency, or local calamities as directed
by the AFP.
SEC. 4. Protection Against Discrimination. – It shall be unlawful for any employer
to deny employment, reemployment, retention in employment, promotion, or any
benefit of employment to a person on the basis of that person's membership,
application for membership, performance of service, application for service, or
obligation in the Reserve Force.

SEC. 5. Security of Tenure and Military Leave of Absence. – A reservist who is
called to military service shall be entitled to a military leave of absence. During
this period, the reservist shall not be considered separated from employment but
merely on leave. The military leave of absence may be without pay from the
employer, provided that the reservist shall receive the corresponding military pay
and allowances from the AFP during the period of service. Employers are not
precluded from voluntarily paying the reservist’s civilian salary, in whole or in
part, during the leave of absence.

SEC. 6. Reintegration to Civilian Employment. – Upon completion of the military
service, the reservist shall be entitled to reinstatement to their former position
without loss of seniority rights, or to a substantially equivalent position if the
former position no longer exists. The reservist must notify the employer of their
intent to return to work within fifteen (15) days after completion of the military
service or release from hospitalization incidental to the service.

SEC. 7. Exemption from Probationary Periods. – The period during which a
reservist is engaged in military service shall be credited in the computation of
their probationary period for regular employment and in the determination of
their seniority and other employment benefits.

SEC. 8. Penalties. – Any employer who violates any provision of this Act shall be
punished with a fine of not less than Fifty Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00) but not
more than Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (P500,000.00), or imprisonment of not
less than three (3) months but not more than two (2) years, or both, at the
discretion of the court. If the offender is a corporation, trust, firm, partnership,
or association, the penalty shall be imposed upon the guilty officer or officers.

SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) and the
Civil Service Commission (CSC), in coordination with the Department of National
Defense (DND), shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act.

SEC. 10. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, administrative orders,
rules, and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
SEC. 12. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
