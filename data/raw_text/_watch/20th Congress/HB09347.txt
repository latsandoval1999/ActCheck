15                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

16   Section 11, Article XIII of the 1987 Constitution mandates the State to adopt an
17   integrated and comprehensive approach to health development and make essential social
18   services available to all people at affordable cost, with priority for the elderly, among
19   others.

20   Likewise, Republic Act No. 7876 declares it a policy of the State to provide adequate
21   social services and improve the quality of life of senior citizens. The law further provides
22   for the establishment of senior citizens centers to promote the welfare and well-being of
23   elderly persons.

24   Despite existing laws, many senior citizens remain abandoned, neglected, homeless, or
25   without adequate family support. A significant number of elderly Filipinos suffer from
26   lack of shelter, medical care, nutrition, and emotional support, thereby exposing them to
27   abuse, exploitation, and serious health risks.

28   This proposed measure seeks to establish at least one Senior Citizen Residential Care
29   Home in every region of the country to serve as a safe haven for indigent, abandoned,
30   neglected, homeless, and vulnerable senior citizens. These centers shall provide
31   residential care, medical and psychosocial services, nutrition, recreation, and other
32   interventions necessary to ensure a dignified and humane quality of life for the elderly.

33   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

63   AN ACT ESTABLISHING SENIOR CITIZEN RESIDENTIAL CARE HOMES IN
64       EVERY REGION OF THE PHILIPPINES, PROVIDING FOR THEIR
65            FUNCTIONS, FUNDING, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

66   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
67   Congress assembled:

68   Section 1. Short Title.

69   This Act shall be known as the “Senior Citizen Residential Care Homes Act.”

70   Section 2. Declaration of Policy.

71   It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect and promote the welfare, dignity,
72   and well-being of senior citizens and ensure that abandoned, neglected, homeless,
73   indigent, and vulnerable elderly persons are provided with adequate shelter, care,
74   protection, medical attention, and social services.

75   Towards this end, the State shall establish accessible and properly managed residential
76   care homes for senior citizens in every region of the country.

77   Section 3. Definition of Terms.

78   For purposes of this Act:

79      1. Senior Citizen refers to any resident citizen of the Philippines aged sixty (60)
80         years or older;
81      2. Residential Care Home refers to a government-operated or government-
82         accredited residential facility that provides temporary or permanent shelter, health
83         care, nutrition, psychosocial services, and daily living assistance to senior citizens;
84      3. Indigent Senior Citizen refers to a senior citizen who has no regular source of
85         income or financial support sufficient for subsistence;
86      4. Abandoned or Neglected Senior Citizen refers to a senior citizen who has been
87         deserted, left without proper care, or deprived of basic needs by persons
88         responsible for his or her care.

89   Section 4. Establishment of Senior Citizen Residential Care Homes.

90   There shall be established at least one (1) Senior Citizen Residential Care Home in
91   every administrative region of the Philippines.
92    The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), in coordination with the
93    Department of Health (DOH), local government units (LGUs), and concerned agencies,
94    shall determine the appropriate location, capacity, and operational requirements of each
95    care home.

96    Section 5. Coverage and Beneficiaries.

97    The Residential Care Homes established under this Act shall primarily serve:

 98      1. Abandoned senior citizens;
 99      2. Homeless senior citizens;
100      3. Neglected or abused senior citizens;
101      4. Indigent senior citizens without family support; and
102      5. Senior citizens requiring temporary protective custody or residential care.

103   Section 6. Services and Facilities.

104   The Residential Care Homes shall provide senior citizens with appropriate shelter, food,
105   medical and psychosocial care, recreational and wellness activities, rehabilitation services,
106   and other support necessary for their protection, health, and well-being.

107   All facilities established under this Act shall be safe, sanitary, accessible, and compliant
108   with standards prescribed by applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

109   Section 7. Administration and Management.

110   The DSWD shall be the lead agency responsible for the administration, supervision, and
111   management of the Residential Care Homes established under this Act.

112   Section 8. Admission and Referral System.

113   The DSWD shall formulate guidelines for the admission, referral, transfer, discharge, and
114   monitoring of beneficiaries under this Act.

115   Section 9. Appropriations.

116   The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against
117   the current appropriations of the DSWD. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for
118   the continued implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General
119   Appropriations Act.

120   Section 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations.

121   Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DSWD, in coordination with
122   the DOH, DILG, DBM, National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC), and relevant
123   stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
124   implementation of this Act.

125   Section 11. Separability Clause.

126   If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining
127   provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

128   Section 12. Repealing Clause.
129   All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent
130   with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

131   Section 13. Effectivity.

132   This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or
133   in a newspaper of general circulation.
