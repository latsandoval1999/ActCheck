 AN ACT PROVIDING FREE HEMODIALYSIS, PERITONEAL DIALYSIS, AND
   OTHER DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH APPROVED DIALYSIS PROCEDURES
                              TO ALL SENIOR CITIZENS


                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE


The vision of this proposed legislation comes from Article XIII, Section 11 of the 1987
Constitution which seeks to make essential goods and services available and
affordable to Filipinos. This state guarantee finds relevance in healthcare especially
in providing treatment for marginalized sectors, such as senior citizens. With heart
diseases, high blood pressure, and diabetes, chronic kidney disease (CKD) becomes
prevalent within the elderly population. In 2017, it resulted in 1.2 million deaths.


This should not have been the case. CKD is largely preventable and treatable through
dialysis procedures such as hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis. However, in
countries where the right to health comes with a cost, such as the Philippines,
universal healthcare is still limited for CKD.


Per Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (Philhealth) Cirular No. 22-2015, only 90
out of 144 dialysis sessions needed in a year of treatment are being subsidized. With
a case rate of Php 2,500.00 per session of dialysis, lifesaving treatment for the
remaining 54 sessions costs Php 135,000.00. Senior citizens are vulnerable to CKD
and most of them only rely on their measly monthly pension.


In order to honor our elderly and give them essential goods and services pursuant to
their right to health, this bill seeks to provide free hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis,
and other Department of Health (DOH) approved dialysis procedures to senior
citizens.


In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:


 1          SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Free Dialysis for
 2   Senior Citizens Act."
 3
 4          Sec. 2. Free Dialysis for All Senior Citizens. – The cost of all the needed
 5   hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and other Department of Health ('DOH") approved
 6   dialysis procedure shall be fully reimbursed by the Philippines Health Insurance
 7   Corporation (PHIC), provided such treatment is availed of at PHIC accredited hospitals
 8   or free-standing dialysis centers. Provided further, that only those procedures that use
 9   the dialysis solutions included in the latest edition of the Philippine National Drug
10   Formulary ("PNDF") shall be reimbursed by the Philhealth. Provided finally, that only
11   laboratory procedures and supplies considered necessary shall be covered by the
12   Philhealth. Philhealth shall, as soon as possible, reimburse all official receipts for drugs,
13   supplies, and laboratory procedures dated thirty (30) days prior to the date of the
14   claimed session.
 1
 2          Sec. 3. Appropriations. – The amount needed for the implementation of this
 3   Act shall be charged against the appropriations of the DOH and the PHIC in the current
 4   and subsequent General Appropriations Act and their own respective funds.
 5   Thereafter, the amount needed for the continued implementation of this Act shall be
 6   included In the budget of the concerned departments In the annual General
 7   Appropriations.
 8
 9          Sec. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within thirty (30) days from
10   the approval of this Act, the DOH, the PHIC, and the National Commission on Senior
11   Citizens (NCSC), in coordination with other implementing agencies, shall formulate
12   rules and guidelines as may be necessary for the proper implementation and
13   enforcement of this Act.
14
15          Sec. 5. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act or the application
16   thereof to any person or circumstance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other
17   provisions or sections of this Act and the application of such provision or section to
18   other persons or circumstances, not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force
19   and effect.
20
21          Sec. 6. Repealing Clause. –       All laws, presidential decrees or issuances,
22   executive orders, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules or regulations
23   contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, modified
24   or amended accordingly.
25
26          Sec. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
27   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
