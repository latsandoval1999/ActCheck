                           AN ACT
SUSPENDING FOR TWO (2) YEARS THE IMPLEMENTATION OF CERTAIN
PROVISIONS OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12001, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
“REAL PROPERTY VALUATION AND ASSESSMENT REFORM ACT,”
PROVIDING FOR THE CONTINUED APPLICATION OF EXISTING
SCHEDULES OF MARKET VALUES DURING THE PERIOD OF SUSPENSION,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

                  Republic Act No. 12001, otherwise known as the “Real Property Valuation and
            Assessment Reform Act,” introduced far-reaching reforms intended to establish a uniform,
            transparent, and internationally aligned system for the valuation of real property in the
            Philippines. The objectives of the law remain laudable and necessary. However, reforms
            must also be responsive to the economic circumstances prevailing at the time of their
            implementation. What may constitute sound policy under normal conditions may impose
            unintended hardship when introduced during a period of extraordinary economic and natural
            disruption.

                         The Philippine economy is presently confronting precisely such circumstances.

                   According to the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), the Philippine economy grew
            by only 2.3 percent in the second quarter of 2026. This represents a significant slowdown
            from the rates of economic expansion to which the country had become accustomed in
            recent years. At the same time, Filipino households continue to contend with elevated
            prices. Headline inflation stood at 6.2 percent in July 2026, following 6.4 percent in June.

                   These economic difficulties have been aggravated by circumstances that are, to a
            significant degree, beyond the control of the Philippine Government. The continuing
            conflict in the Middle East has disrupted global energy markets and contributed to elevated
            fuel prices and supply-side pressure which are external uncertainties weighing on the
            Philippine economy.
_________ NOVFFFFFFF




                    Our country has likewise suffered successive natural calamities. On June 8, 2026, a
            magnitude 7.8 earthquake struck off Sarangani, affecting communities across Mindanao and
            damaging homes, schools, bridges, and other infrastructure. And there were other strong
            earthquakes during the previous year as well, with the local governments, and constituents,
            still in the process of recovering from the effects of the natural disaster.

                   The Government has responded—and continues to respond—to these challenges.
            Nevertheless, public policy must recognize that the burden of global conflict, natural
            disasters, elevated inflation, and slower economic growth ultimately falls upon ordinary
            Filipino households and businesses. This is therefore not the appropriate time to introduce
            another potential source of financial pressure upon them, hence the move to suspend the
            reform legislation for a period of two (2) years.

                   Additionally, without the suspension, the effects extend beyond the real property
            taxes imposed by local government units. Under Republic Act No. 12001, the approved
            Schedule of Market Values may likewise affect local transfer taxes and other property-
            related taxes, fees, and charges. It may also be used in determining the tax base for internal
            revenue taxes involving real property transactions.

                  Thus, an abrupt adjustment in government property valuations at this particular time
            may increase the cost of owning, transferring, inheriting, donating, developing, and
            conducting business upon real property precisely when households and enterprises are
            already adjusting to higher prices and weaker economic conditions.

                  This measure proposes a two-year suspension of the implementation of the provisions
            of Republic Act No. 12001 relating to the adoption and use of new or updated Schedules of
            Market Values and the general revision of real property assessments based thereon.

                  Fiscal reform and taxpayer protection are not incompatible. Republic Act No. 12001
            can achieve its long-term objectives without requiring its full financial consequences to be
            imposed upon taxpayers at the height of extraordinary economic difficulties. A temporary
            suspension is therefore not an abandonment of reform. It is a measured and compassionate
            adjustment in its timing.

                       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
SUSPENDING FOR TWO (2) YEARS THE IMPLEMENTATION OF CERTAIN
PROVISIONS OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12001, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
“REAL PROPERTY VALUATION AND ASSESSMENT REFORM ACT,”
PROVIDING FOR THE CONTINUED APPLICATION OF EXISTING
SCHEDULES OF MARKET VALUES DURING THE PERIOD OF SUSPENSION,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Real Property
Valuation and Assessment Reform Implementation Suspension Act.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
pursue a just, equitable, transparent, and nationally consistent system of real
property valuation while ensuring that reforms in property valuation and taxation
are implemented in a manner that does not impose sudden, excessive, or
unreasonable financial burdens upon property owners, taxpayers, businesses, and
local communities.

        Towards this end, the State shall provide sufficient time for the National
Government, local government units, taxpayers, and other affected stakeholders to
assess the fiscal and economic impact of the implementation of Republic Act No.
12001 and to adopt appropriate safeguards against abrupt and substantial increases
in real property valuations and the taxes, fees, and charges based thereon.

     SEC. 3. Suspension of Implementation. — Notwithstanding any provision of
Republic Act No. 12001, its implementing rules and regulations, or any other law,
_________ NOVFFFFFFF




rule, regulation, circular, or issuance to the contrary, the implementation and
application of Sections 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19 of Republic Act No. 12001, insofar as
these provisions result in the adoption, approval, effectivity, or use of new or
updated Schedules of Market Values, and the general revision of real property
assessments and classifications based thereon, are hereby suspended for a period of
two (2) years from the effectivity of this Act.

                       During the period of suspension:

                       (a) No new or updated Schedule of Market Values prepared, reviewed,
                           certified, approved, or otherwise issued pursuant to Republic Act No.
                           12001 shall take effect or be used as basis for the imposition, computation,
                           or collection of any national or local tax, fee, or charge;

                       (b) No general revision of real property assessments and classifications based
                           upon a new or updated Schedule of Market Values prepared pursuant to
                           Republic Act No. 12001 shall take effect;

                       (c) No new or updated Schedule of Market Values shall be used by the
                           Bureau of Internal Revenue in computing internal revenue taxes involving
                           real property transactions;

                       (d) No taxpayer shall be required to pay any increase in a national or local tax,
                           fee, or charge arising solely from the application of a new or updated
                           Schedule of Market Values prepared pursuant to Republic Act No. 12001;
                           and

                       (e) The preparation, collection of data, consultation, review, capacity-
                           building, establishment of information systems, and other preparatory
                           and administrative activities authorized under Republic Act No. 12001
                           may continue: Provided, That no new or updated Schedule of Market
                           Values resulting therefrom shall become effective or be used for taxation
                           during the period of suspension.

       SEC. 4. Continued Application of Existing Schedule of Market Values. — During
the period of suspension provided under Section 3 of this Act, the Schedule of
Market Values, zonal values, assessed values, and other valuation bases lawfully in
force immediately prior to the implementation or application of the corresponding
new or updated valuation under Republic Act No. 12001 shall continue to be used
for purposes of national and local taxation, assessment, fees, charges, and other
government transactions.

      No provision of Republic Act No. 12001 repealing, amending, superseding, or
modifying any prior valuation mechanism shall be construed to prevent the
continued application of such valuation mechanism during the period of suspension
provided under this Act.
_________ NOVFFFFFFF




      For avoidance of doubt, the legal and administrative framework necessary to
maintain the valuation bases existing immediately prior to their replacement under
Republic Act No. 12001 shall remain operative during the period of suspension.

       SEC. 5. Schedules of Market Values Already Prepared, Certified, or Approved. —
Any Schedule of Market Values prepared, submitted, reviewed, certified, or
approved pursuant to Republic Act No. 12001 prior to the effectivity of this Act shall
not be invalidated solely by reason of this Act.

      However, its implementation and use for taxation, assessment, or the
imposition of fees and charges shall be suspended for the duration of the period
provided under Section 3 hereof.

      Upon expiration of the suspension period, the Department of Finance,
through the Bureau of Local Government Finance, shall determine whether such
Schedule of Market Values remains reflective of prevailing market conditions and
complies with the Philippine Valuation Standards before the same may be
implemented.

       SEC. 6. Taxpayer Protection and Impact Assessment. — Within one (1) year from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance, in coordination with the
Bureau of Local Government Finance, Bureau of Internal Revenue, Department of
the Interior and Local Government, local government units, and other relevant
agencies and stakeholders, shall conduct a comprehensive assessment of the
projected fiscal, economic, and social impact of the implementation of the updated
Schedules of Market Values under Republic Act No. 12001.

                       The assessment shall include, among others:

                       (a) The projected increase or decrease in real property taxes by property
                           classification and locality;

                       (b) The projected impact upon residential, agricultural, commercial, and
                           industrial property owners;

                       (c) The impact upon housing affordability, rental rates, business costs,
                           agricultural production, and investment;

                       (d) The impact upon estate taxes, donor’s taxes, capital gains taxes,
                           documentary stamp taxes, and other national taxes involving real
                           property;

                       (e) The fiscal impact upon local government units; and
_________ NOVFFFFFFF




                       (f) Appropriate measures to prevent sudden, excessive, or confiscatory
                           increases in the tax liabilities of affected taxpayers.

       The Department of Finance shall submit its report and recommendations to
the President of the Philippines, the Senate President, and the Speaker of the House
of Representatives not later than eighteen (18) months from the effectivity of this
Act.

        SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within thirty (30) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance, in coordination with the Bureau of
Local Government Finance, Bureau of Internal Revenue, Department of the Interior
and Local Government, and representatives of local government units, shall
promulgate such rules and regulations as may be necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.

       No implementing rule or regulation shall authorize the collection of any tax,
fee, or charge based upon a new or updated Schedule of Market Values whose
implementation is suspended under this Act.

      SEC. 8. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions or parts thereof not affected
thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, administrative issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

        The repeal or amendment under Section 38 of Republic Act No. 12001 of
provisions of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the “Local Government
Code of 1991,” Republic Act No. 8424, otherwise known as the “National Internal
Revenue Code of 1997,” as amended, Presidential Decree No. 921, and other laws
shall, insofar as necessary to give effect to the suspension and continued application
of existing valuation bases provided under this Act, be deemed temporarily
inoperative during the suspension period.

      SEC. 10. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect immediately after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

                       Approved.
