                        AN ACT
INCREASING THE ALLOWANCES OF BARANGAY OFFICIALS,
GRANTING HONORARIA AND OTHER BENEFITS TO THE
BARANGAY WORKERS WHO HAVE SERVED FOR AT LEAST ONE
(1) YEAR, AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AMENDING FOR
THE PURPOSE, THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991

                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Barangays serve as the primary frontliners of governance and public
service delivery in communities. Barangay officials and workers play a vital role
in maintaining peace and order, implementing national and local government
programs, responding to disasters, mediating disputes through the Katarungang
Pambarangay system, and ensuring the effective delivery of basic services at the
grassroots level.

      However, under Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local
Government Code of 1991, as amended, the honoraria of barangay officials
remain pegged at a minimum of ₱1,000 per month for the Punong Barangay and
₱600 per month for each Sangguniang Barangay member, Barangay Treasurer,
and Barangay Secretary. These amounts were set more than three decades ago
and are no longer commensurate with the responsibilities and workload expected
from barangay officials.

      Moreover, barangay tanods, who perform essential functions in
maintaining community peace and order and assisting in public safety and
  emergency response, are not consistently provided with honoraria and benefits
  despite the critical services they render.

        This measure seeks to update and rationalize the compensation structure of
  barangay officials by increasing their minimum honoraria, extending
  compensation and benefits to barangay workers who have rendered at least one
  year of service, and recognizing the contributions of barangay tanods through the
  grant of honoraria and other benefits.

         By providing a more responsive and reasonable compensation framework,
  the State acknowledges the indispensable role of barangay officials and workers
  in grassroots governance while encouraging professionalism, accountability, and
  sustained public service in local communities.

        This measure has been filed since the 11th Congress and has been
  repeatedly referred to the Committee on Local Government.

        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Section 393 of the Republic Act numbered 7160 is hereby
amended to read as follows:


            "SEC. 393. Benefits of Barangay Officials - (a) Barangay officials,
      including barangay tanods and members of Lupong Tagapamayapa shall
      receive honoraria, allowances and such other emoluments as may be
      authorized by law or barangay, municipal, or city ordinance in accordance
      with the provisions of this Code, but in no case shall it be less than [One
      thousand pesos (P1,000)] THREE THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED
      PESOS (₱3,500) per month for the Punong Barangay and [Six hundred
      pesos (P600)] TWO THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS (₱2,500)
      per month for the sangguniang barangay members, barangay treasurer,
      [and] barangay secretary, AND BARANGAY TANODS: Provided,
      however, That the annual appropriations for personal services shall be
      subject to the budgetary limitations prescribed under Title Five, Book I of
      this Code.
               "(b). The Punong Barangay, the sangguniang barangay members,
      the barangay treasurer, [and] the barangay secretary, AND THE
      BARANGAY TANODS shall also:
               "XXX,
               [(d). All duly appointed members of the barangay tanod brigades, or
      their equivalent, which shall number not more than twenty (20) in each
      barangay, shall be granted insurance or other benefits during their
      incumbency, chargeable to the barangay or city or municipal government
      to which the barangay belongs.]


      Section 2. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged to the appropriations of the
Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG) under the current General
Appropriations Act. Thereafter, such amount as may be necessary for the
continuous implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual budget of
the city or municipality concerned.


      Section 3. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
