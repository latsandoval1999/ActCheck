                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Along with emerging and fast-growing economy of Cagayan de Oro City, unfortunately,
the traffic situation in the City is worsening. At present, traffic in the central business districts
of Cagayan de Oro is at its all time worse. This is not only inconvenient but also costs many
businessmen.

       One possible solution is to improve and add additional roads in the City. As a result of
consultations, it was agreed upon that a skyway in Cagayan de Oro City will be a big help in
decongesting the streets of the City.

       This proposed skyway will be a 2.4 km skyway estimated to cost around P2.6 billion
to be known as the JR Borja-Carmen-Patag Skyway.

        The 1st approach of the 1st section comprising of 1.6 kms will start from JR Borja
Extension near the Maria Reyna Hospital passing beside the Cogon Market towards the JR
Borja Bridge. The 2nd approach of the 1st section will follow the existing Max Suniel St.
intersecting Villarin St. comprising 1.26 kms which existing road will be upgraded. The 2nd
section will start from Villarin St. near Country Village to Patag after the 4 th ID Road. This will
total 780 meters.

       With this skyway, traffic in the City will ease up with the end goal of improving the flow
of goods and services which will be beneficial for the economy of the City.

        In view of the foregoing, immediate approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE CAGAYAN DE ORO (J.R. BORJA-
CARMEN-PATAG) SKYWAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. There shall be constructed a skyway in Cagayan de Oro City to be known as the
Cagayan de Oro (J.R. Borja-Carmen-Patag) Skyway.

SEC. 2. The Skyway shall consist of two sections. The first section will start from JR Borja
Extension near the Maria Reyna Hospital passing beside the Cogon Market towards the JR
Borja Bridge 1st approach at Burgos St., 1600 meters. From the 2nd approach of J.R. Borja
Bridge, it will follow the existing Max Suniel St. intersecting Villarin St. The road will be
upgraded, 1,260 meters.
        The 2nd section of the skyway will start from Villarin St. near Country Village to Patag
after the 4th ID Road. This will total 780 meters.

SEC 3. The Department of Public Works and Highways is hereby directed to implement the
planning and construction of the Cagayan de Oro (J.R. Borja-Carmen-Patag) Skyway.

SEC 4. The amount of Two Billion Six Hundred Million Pesos (P2,600,000,000) necessary for
the construction of the Cagayan de Oro (J.R. Borja-Carmen-Patag) Skyway shall be included
in the budget of the Department of Public Works and Highways in the General Appropriations
Act.

SEC 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general
circulation.
