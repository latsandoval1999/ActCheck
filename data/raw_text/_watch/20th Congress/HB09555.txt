                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Calamba District Office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in Calamba City,
Laguna, serves as a vital frontline government office catering not only to the residents of
Calamba City but also to motorists, transport operators, and vehicle owners from neighboring
cities and municipalities in Laguna and nearby provinces. With the rapid urbanization,
industrial expansion, and sustained economic growth of the area, the demand for LTO services
has significantly increased over the years.

        Pursuant to LTO Memorandum Circular No. VDM-2024-2693, the classification of
LTO District Offices is determined primarily by their average revenue collections for the last
three fiscal years, among other operational indicators. The Memorandum Circular provides that
District Offices with average revenue collections exceeding One Hundred Million Pesos
(₱100,000,000.00) qualify for classification as a Class “A” District Office.

       Records show that the Calamba District Office generated revenues amounting to One
Hundred Seventy-Seven Million Eight Hundred Seventy-Two Thousand Three Hundred Forty-
Three Pesos and Sixty-Five Centavos (₱177,872,343.65) in 2023, One Hundred Ninety-Two
Million One Hundred Seventy-Eight Thousand Four Hundred Seventy-Four Pesos and Sixty-
Five Centavos (₱192,178,474.65) in 2024, and One Hundred Ninety-Five Million Nine
Hundred Forty-Six Thousand Two Hundred Fifty-Six Pesos and Fifty-One Centavos
(₱195,946,256.51) in 2025. These collections yield a three-year average revenue of
approximately One Hundred Eighty-Eight Million Six Hundred Sixty-Five Thousand Six
Hundred Ninety-One Pesos and Sixty Cents (₱188,665,691.60), substantially exceeding the
minimum threshold prescribed for a Class “A” District Office.

        Moreover, the Calamba District Office has consistently recorded a high volume of
transactions, averaging approximately Three Hundred Fifty-Seven Thousand Two Hundred
Fifty (357,250) transactions annually over the last three years. This volume reflects the growing
demand for vehicle registration, driver's licensing, permit issuance, enforcement-related
services, and other LTO transactions within its area of jurisdiction.
        Despite its current classification as a Class “C” District Office, the office already
performs functions and handles workloads comparable to those of higher-classified district
offices. The existing classification no longer adequately reflects the operational realities,
revenue-generating capacity, and service demands faced by the Calamba District Office.

       The proposed upgrading of the Calamba District Office from Class “C” to Class “A”
will enable the expansion of its organizational structure, augmentation of personnel
complement, enhancement of facilities, and improvement of service delivery. Ultimately, this
measure will strengthen the government's capacity to provide efficient, accessible, and
responsive transportation and regulatory services to the motoring public in Calamba City and
surrounding areas.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
UPGRADING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION (LTO) DISTRICT OFFICE IN THE CITY
  OF CALAMBA, PROVINCE OF LAGUNA, FROM CLASS “C” TO CLASS “A”, AND
                  APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

           SECTION 1. The district office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the City of
Calamba, Province of Laguna, is hereby upgraded from Class "C" to Class "A" district office.

            SEC. 2. The Secretary of the Department of Transportation shall immediately include in
the Department's program the upgrading of the LTO district office in the City of Calamba, Province
of Laguna, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current year's appropriation of the
existing LTO district office in the City of Batac, Province of llocos Norte. Thereafter, the funding of
which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

            SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
