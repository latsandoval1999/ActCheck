                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

            Persons with disabilities (PWDs) continue to encounter difficulties
      in accessing public and private establishments, particularly when parking
      spaces designated for their exclusive use are occupied or obstructed by
      unauthorized vehicles. Such practices undermine their mobility,
      independence, and equal access to public facilities and services.

           While Batas Pambansa Blg. 344, otherwise known as the
      Accessibility Law, requires the provision of accessible facilities, there is a
      need to strengthen its provisions by expressly prohibiting the
      unauthorized use of parking spaces reserved for PWDs and imposing
      appropriate penalties against violators and establishments that fail to
      comply with the law.

           This measure seeks to ensure the provision and maintenance of
      exclusive PWD parking spaces, penalize their unauthorized use or
      obstruction, and strengthen enforcement and accountability among
      government and private establishments.

           In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
      earnestly sought.

                              AN ACT
      MANDATING THE PROVISION OF EXCLUSIVE PARKING SPACES
      FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES AND PENALIZING THE
      UNAUTHORIZED USE THEREOF, AMENDING FOR THE
      PURPOSE BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 344, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS
      THE “ACCESSIBILITY LAW”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Exclusive
Parking Space for Persons with Disabilities Act.”

      SECTION 2. Amendment of Section 1 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344. -
Section 1 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 is hereby amended to read as follows:

                “SECTION 1. In order to promote the realization of
          the rights of persons with disabilities to participate
          fully in the social life and development of the societies
          in which they live and to enjoy the opportunities
          available to other citizens, no license or permit for the
          construction, repair, or renovation of public and private
          buildings for public use, educational institutions,
          airports, sports and recreation centers and complexes,
          shopping centers or establishments, public parking
          places, workplaces, and public utilities shall be granted
          or issued unless the owner or operator thereof shall
          install and incorporate in such building, establishment,
          institution, or public utility such architectural facilities
          or structural features as shall reasonably enhance the
          mobility of persons with disabilities, such as sidewalks,
          ramps, railings, and the like.

                 If  feasible,  all   such   existing    buildings,
          institutions, establishments, or public utilities may be
          renovated or altered to enable persons with disabilities
          to have access to them: Provided, however, That
          buildings, institutions, establishments, or public
          utilities to be constructed or established for which
          licenses or permits had already been issued may comply
          with the requirements of this Act: Provided, further,
          That in the case of government buildings, streets, and
          highways, the Secretary of Public Works and Highways
          shall see to it that the same shall be provided with
          architectural facilities or structural features for
          persons with disabilities.

                 In the case of parking areas of any of the foregoing
          institutions, buildings, establishments, or public
          utilities, the owner or operator thereof shall reserve
          sufficient and suitable parking spaces for the
          EXCLUSIVE use of persons with disabilities. Such
          parking spaces shall be properly identified, marked,
          and provided with appropriate signage, and shall
          comply with the prescribed dimensions, location, and
          accessibility requirements under the implementing rules
          and regulations of this Act.”

      SECTION 3. Insertion of a New Section 2-A to Batas Pambansa Blg.
344. – A new Section 2-A is hereby inserted after Section 2 of Batas Pambansa
Blg. 344, to read as follows:

                    “SEC. 2-A. PROHIBITION ON UNAUTHORIZED
                PARKING. – It shall be unlawful:

                     (a) For any person without disability to use,
                         occupy, block, or otherwise obstruct parking
                         spaces exclusively reserved for persons with
                         disabilities in any government or private
                         establishment,    building,   or   structure:
                         Provided, That a vehicle operated by a
                         person without disability, including a driver
                         or caregiver assisting a person with
                         disability, may use such reserved parking
                         space when the vehicle is being used to
                         transport or assist a person with disability
                         to or from the establishment, building, or
                         structure;

                     (b) For any owner, operator, administrator,
                         manager, or authorized personnel of any
                         government    or    private    establishment,
                         building,  or    structure    to    knowingly
                         authorize,   tolerate,     or    permit   the
                         unauthorized use, occupation, or obstruction
                         of parking spaces exclusively reserved for
                         persons with disabilities.

                     The use of parking spaces reserved for persons
                with disabilities shall be subject to the presentation
                or display of a valid identification card issued to the
                person with disability and such parking permit or
                authorization as may be prescribed under the
                implementing rules and regulations of this Act.”
      SECTION 4. Amendment of Section 4 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344. -
Section 4 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 is hereby amended to read as follows:

                      “SEC. 4. PENALTIES. -

                        (a)   Any person who violates any of the provisions
                              of this Act, including the unauthorized use,
                              occupation, blocking, or obstruction of parking
                              spaces exclusively reserved for persons with
                              disabilities, shall, upon conviction by a court of
                              competent jurisdiction, pay a fine of not less
                              than TEN THOUSAND PESOS (PHP10,000.00)
                              but not more than FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS
                              (PHP50,000.00).

                        (b)   Any owner or operator of a government or
                              private establishment, building, or structure
                              covered under Section 1 of this Act who fails to
                              provide and maintain the required parking
                              spaces exclusively reserved for persons with
                              disabilities shall, upon conviction by a court of
                              competent jurisdiction, pay a fine of not less
                              than FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS (PHP50,000.00)
                              but not more than ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND
                              PESOS (PHP100,000.00).

                        (c)   In the case of corporations, partnerships,
                              cooperatives, or associations, the responsible
                              president, director, manager, administrator, or
                              officer who participated in, authorized, or
                              knowingly permitted the commission of the
                              violation shall be held criminally liable under
                              this Act.

                             The penalties imposed under this Act shall be
                        without prejudice to the imposition of applicable
                        administrative sanctions under existing laws, rules,
                        and regulations.”

      SECTION 5. Amendment of Terminology. - All references in Batas
Pambansa Blg. 344 to “disabled persons” shall henceforth be amended to read
as “persons with disabilities.”

      SECTION 6. Enforcement. - The Philippine National Police, traffic
enforcement units of local government units, and other duly authorized
enforcement personnel shall enforce the provisions of this Act and may issue
appropriate citation tickets against persons violating Section 2-A hereof, subject
to applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

      The removal or towing of vehicles illegally parked in spaces exclusively
reserved for persons with disabilities may be undertaken in accordance with
applicable laws, ordinances, and regulations.
       SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The Secretary of
Public Works and Highways and the Secretary of Transportation, in coordination
with the Executive Director of the National Council on Disability Affairs and the
Secretary of the Interior and Local Government, shall promulgate and issue the
necessary implementing rules and regulations within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act.

      The implementing rules and regulations shall include the standards for
the number, dimensions, location, identification, and maintenance of parking
spaces exclusively reserved for persons with disabilities, as well as the
procedures for monitoring, reporting, and enforcement of violations.

      SECTION 8. Non-Diminution of Rights. - Nothing in this Act shall be
construed to diminish, impair, or repeal any rights, benefits, privileges, or
protections granted to persons with disabilities or other disadvantaged and
marginalized sectors under existing laws.

       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. –-If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall continue to
be in full force and effect.

      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees, presidential proclamations, letters of instruction, rules and regulations,
or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed
or modified accordingly.

       SECTION 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
following its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation in the Philippines.
