EXPLANATORY NOTE

For many senior citizens and indigent Filipinos with disabilities, access to health care remains
difficult even when government health services are available.

Age, illness, disability, limited mobility, transportation costs, and financial hardship can
prevent a person from regularly visiting a health center or hospital. This is particularly difficult
for senior citizens who live alone, persons who are bedridden or have difficulty walking, and
families who cannot afford repeated transportation and other expenses associated with seeking
medical attention.

In these situations, government health services should not always require the patient to come
to government. Where circumstances require, government must also be capable of bringing
basic health services closer to the patient.

This bill seeks to establish a Health Outreach for Seniors and Indigents Program that will
provide appropriate health services to qualified senior citizens and indigent persons with
functional disabilities through coordinated national and local health outreach.

The Program shall strengthen home and community-based health services, including basic
health assessment, monitoring, appropriate medical consultation, access to medicines and basic
diagnostic services, referral to hospitals and specialists when necessary, and health education
for patients and their families. It shall likewise provide qualified beneficiaries with appropriate
assistive devices and basic hygiene and oral-care supplies according to their individual needs.

The measure also recognizes the important role of local government units, barangay health
workers, public health facilities, and other frontline health workers. By strengthening
coordination among these existing institutions, government can identify persons who have
difficulty accessing health facilities and connect them with the services they need without
unnecessarily creating another layer of bureaucracy.

A person's age, disability, inability to travel, or lack of financial resources should not become
a barrier to receiving basic health care. Bringing appropriate health services closer to those who
need them most is a practical way of making our health care system more accessible, responsive,
and humane.

HOUSE BILL NO. 11625

AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE OUTREACH PROGRAM FOR
SENIOR CITIZENS AND INDIGENT PERSONS WITH FUNCTIONAL
DISABILITIES, PROVIDING FOR INTER-AGENCY AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT
COORDINATION, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SEC. 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Health Outreach for Seniors and
Indigents Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. \t is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right to health of all Filipinos, particularly senior citizens, indigent persons, and
persons with disabilities who experience difficulty in accessing health facilities and services.

Towards this end, the State shall strengthen community and home-based health services and
bring appropriate health care closer to persons whose age, disability, health condition, mobility
limitation, financial circumstances, or other similar conditions substantially hinder their access
to health facilities.

SEC. 3. Health Outreach for Seniors and Indigents Program. The Department of Health
(DOH), in coordination with local government units (LGUs) and other concerned government
agencies, shall establish and implement a Health Outreach for Seniors and Indigents Program,
hereinafter referred to as the Program.

The Program shall provide appropriate health outreach services to qualified beneficiaries who
have difficulty accessing health facilities due to age, functional disability, mobility limitation,
health condition, financial hardship, or other circumstances as may be determined by the DOH.

The Program shall utilize and strengthen existing national and local health systems and shall,
as far as practicable, be integrated into primary care provider networks under the Universal
Health Care system.

The DOH, in coordination with LGUs, shall establish an appropriate system for the
identification, profiling, and registration of qualified beneficiaries. Such profiling shall include
an assessment of the health, functional, mobility, and other relevant needs of the beneficiary
for purposes of determining the appropriate services and frequency of outreach visits under the
Program.

SEC. 4. Qualified Beneficiaries. The Program shall primarily cover:

a) Senior citizens who, by reason of age, illness, disability, mobility limitation, or other
health condition, have substantial difficulty accessing health facilities;

b) Indigent persons with functional disabilities or mobility limitations that substantially
restrict their ability to access health facilities; and

c) Such other similarly situated persons as may be determined by the DOH pursuant to the
implementing rules and regulations of this Act.

Priority shall be given to persons who are bedridden, homebound, living alone without
adequate assistance, financially incapacitated, or otherwise unable to regularly access
appropriate health services.

SEC. 5. Health Outreach Services. Subject to the individual needs of the beneficiary and the
professional judgment of qualified health personnel, services under the Program may include:

a) Basic health assessment and monitoring;

b) Appropriate medical consultation;

c) Monitoring of chronic illnesses and existing health conditions;

d) Assistance in obtaining prescribed maintenance medicines and other medicines
available under government health programs;

e) Basic laboratory or diagnostic services that can safely and practicably be provided
through community or mobile health services;

f) Referral to appropriate primary care facilities, hospitals, specialists, rehabilitation
services, or other health facilities when necessary;

h) Follow-up care after hospitalization or medical treatment, when appropriate; and

i) Provision or facilitation of access to appropriate assistive devices, including

Nothing in this Act shall authorize health personnel to perform procedures outside their lawful
scope of practice.

SEC. 6. Home and Community-Based Health Services. Where the condition of the beneficiary
makes travel to a health facility difficult or impracticable, appropriate services under the
Program may be provided at the beneficiary's residence or at an accessible community-based
location.

The DOH and LGUs may utilize existing health centers, barangay health stations. mobile health
clinics, community health teams, and other appropriate facilities and mechanisms in
implementing this Section.

Home and community visits shall be undertaken periodically based on the assessed health and
functional needs and circumstances of each beneficiary. The DOH shall prescribe minimum
standards on the frequency of such visits, taking into consideration the condition of the
beneficiary, the need for continuing monitoring, and the availability of qualified health
personnel and resources. Beneficiaries requiring closer monitoring may be visited more
frequently as may be medically or functionally necessary.

SEC. 7. Role of Local Government Units. LGUs shall coordinate with the DOH in
implementing the Program within their respective jurisdictions.

Consistent with the Universal Health Care Act and the Local Government Code, LGUs shall
endeavor to:

a) Identify, profile, and assist in the registration of qualified beneficiaries within their
communities;

b) Coordinate outreach schedules and activities;

c) Facilitate referrals to appropriate health facilities;

d) Maintain appropriate records necessary for continuity of care;

e) Assist beneficiaries in accessing medicines, diagnostic services, medical assistance, and
other existing government health programs; and

f) Perform such other functions necessary for effective implementation of the Program.

SEC. 8. Participation of Barangay Health Workers and Frontline Health Personnel.
Barangay health workers, barangay nutrition scholars, nurses, midwives, social workers, and
other appropriate frontline personnel may assist in identifying beneficiaries, conducting health
education, monitoring patients, facilitating referrals, coordinating health visits, and performing
other functions consistent with their respective training and lawful scope of practice.

The DOH shall provide or facilitate appropriate training and guidance for personnel
participating in the Program.

SEC. 9. Referral and Continuity of Care. The Program shall establish an appropriate referral
mechanism to ensure that beneficiaries requiring services beyond the capacity of an outreach
team are connected to the appropriate health facility or health care provider.

Whenever practicable, referrals shall be coordinated through the applicable health care
provider network to ensure continuity of care.

Beneficiaries discharged from hospitals who require appropriate follow-up care may likewise
be referred to the Program when their circumstances warrant home or community-based
monitoring.

Section 10. Mobile Health Services. The DOH and LGUs may utilize and strengthen existing
mobile health clinics and similar facilities to deliver services under the Program, particularly
in communities with limited access to health facilities.

Mobile health services may provide appropriate consultations, basic diagnostic procedures,
medicines available through government programs, health education, and referrals, subject to
applicable standards and the availability of qualified personnel and resources.

SEC. 11. Coordination with Existing Health and Social Protection Programs. The DOH shall
coordinate with the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), Department of
Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA),
National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC), LGUs, and other concerned agencies to
ensure that qualified beneficiaries are connected, when appropriate, to existing health, medical
assistance, social protection, and other government programs.

Implementation of this Act shall complement, rather than unnecessarily duplicate, existing
government programs and services.

SEC. 13. Public Information. The DOH, DILG, LGUs, NCSC, NCDA, and other concerned
agencies shall disseminate information regarding the Program, its qualified beneficiaries,
available services, and procedures for accessing such services.

Information shall, as far as practicable, be made available through barangay health centers,
senior citizens’ offices, Persons with Disability Affairs Offices, local social welfare offices, and
other accessible government facilities.

SEC. 14. Monitoring and Evaluation. The DOH shall establish appropriate mechanisms for
monitoring and evaluating the implementation of the Program.

Such evaluation shall include, among others, the number and profile of beneficiaries served,
services provided, referrals completed, geographic coverage, and other indicators necessary to
determine whether the Program is improving access to health services among qualified
beneficiaries.

SEC. 15. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity
of this Act, the DOH, in coordination with the DILG, DSWD, PhilHealth, NCSC, NCDA,
representatives of LGUs, and other concerned agencies and stakeholders, shall promulgate the
rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

SEC. 18. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall
be charged against the current appropriations of the DOH and other concerned agencies.
Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall
be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

LGUs may likewise appropriate funds for the implementation of the Program within their
respective jurisdictions, subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and
regulations.

SEC. 19. Separability Clause. If any provision or part of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions or parts not affected thereby shall remain in full force and
effect.
