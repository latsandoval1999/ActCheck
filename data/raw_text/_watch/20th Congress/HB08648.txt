                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to introduce stronger regulatory safeguards that protect
Filipino consumers from arbitrary and disproportionate increases in the prices
of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), while preserving fair competition and
ensuring transparency in the downstream oil industry. It amends Republic
Act (RA) No. 8479, otherwise known as the “Downstream Oil Industry
Deregulation Act of 1998”.

       Ongoing tensions in the Middle East, particularly disruptions along the
Strait of Hormuz, have weaponized the global energy supply chain, pushing
Brent crude toward a hundred dollars ($100) per barrel breaking point. This is
projected to be part of a potential prolonged period of energy insecurity that
directly transmits inflationary pressures to import-dependent countries like
the Philippines. In such conditions, the absence of responsive domestic
safeguards allows the full burden of global volatility to fall on Filipino
households, especially those who rely on LPG for daily cooking and basic
needs.

       At present, LPG pricing in the Philippines remains heavily influenced
by international contract prices. Some market players immediately impose
full price increases at the start of the month, yet delay price reductions when
global prices decline. This raises concerns on fairness and transparency in
price-setting mechanisms.

      This bill strengthens the powers of the Department of Energy (DOE) to
require disclosure of cost components, mandates timely investigation of
unreasonable price increases, and designates the Energy Regulatory
Commission (ERC) to determine the validity of such increases within a
defined period.
      These interventions prevent predatory inventory-tax profiteering where
oil majors justify new price hikes using old, lower-cost stock. It provides the
necessary structural armor necessary to ensure that energy remains accessible
during a global war of attrition.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.
