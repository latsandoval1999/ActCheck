                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XIV, Section 19 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that:

                       The State shall promote physical education and
                       encourage sports programs, league competitions, and
                       amateur sports, including training for international
                       competitions, to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and
                       excellence for the development of a healthy and alert
                       citizenry

       Involvement in sports is very important for everyone specially the children. It helps in
strengthening and toning the muscles and bones in the body. It teaches kids the importance
of team works and they learn to work in groups at an early age and hopefully they can use it
when they actually start working and start to build relationships.

       Also, sports make people mentally strong because it teaches about success and failure
which is an important life lesson. It build confidence and enhances the decision making
process of individuals.

        Aside from these, participating in sports is a very constructive use of time instead of
just lounging around which could sometimes leads children and even adults to the use of
prohibited drugs.

        As such, it is important for a city to have a sports complex where its citizens can actively
participate in different sports programs and where they can spend their free time constructively
by working out, building relationships and improving one’s personal health.

       In view of the foregoing, immediate approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

                               AN ACT
AUTHORIZING THE CONSTRUCTION OF A SPORTS COMPLEX IN CAGAYAN DE ORO
CITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Declaration of Policy – It is the policy of the State to promote physical education
and encourage sports programs, league competitions, and amateur sports, including training
for international competitions, to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for the
development of a healthy and alert citizenry

SEC. 2. The Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) is hereby authorized to
construct a sports complex in Cagayan de Oro City, consisting of an air-conditioned
gymnasium, a basketball court, and training centers for various sports. The sports complex
shall have a seating capacity of 10,000 persons

SEC. 3. The amount of Five Hundred Million Pesos (Php 500,000,000) is hereby appropriated
from the annual budget of the DPWH as provided by the General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette or
in any two (2) national newspapers of general circulation.
