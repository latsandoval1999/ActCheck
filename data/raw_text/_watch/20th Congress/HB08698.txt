                        AN ACT
CONVERTING THE SATELLITE OFFICE OF THE MARITIME

EXTENSION OFFICE AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill proposes the conversion of the Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA)
satellite office in Guiuan, Eastern Samar into a regular extension office. This
expansion aims to enhance the Maritime Industry Authority's capacity to oversee
and regulate maritime activities in the region more effectively, ensuring better
service delivery, regulatory compliance, and support for local maritime
stakeholders.

The Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA) is the national government agency
responsible for the development, promotion, and regulation of the maritime
industry in the Philippines. It plays a critical role in ensuring the safety and
security of maritime operations, protecting the marine environment, and
enhancing the global competitiveness of the Filipino maritime workforce.
Currently, MARINA operates a satellite office in Guiuan, Eastern Samar, which
provides essential services and regulatory functions to the local maritime
community.

The proposed conversion is a strategic move to strengthen MARINA's presence
and effectiveness in Eastern Samar, reflecting the agency's commitment to
advancing the maritime industry and supporting local maritime interests. By
upgrading the satellite office to an extension office, MARINA will be better
positioned to fulfill its mandate, ensuring safer, more secure, and environmentally
responsible maritime operations while enhancing the global standing of the
Filipino maritime workforce.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The existing Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA) Satellite
Office in the Municipality of Guiuan, Province of Eastern Samar is hereby
converted into a regular MARINA Extension Office. The extension office shall
provide services for the residents of the Province of Eastern Samar.

SEC 2. The Administrator of MARINA shall immediately include in the
Agency’s programs and the operationalization of the MARINA Extension Office
in the Municipality of Guiuan, Province of Eastern Samar, the funding of which
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC 3. The Secretary of Transportation, thru the Administrator of MARINA, are
hereby authorized to reorganize the offices, personnel, funds, and properties of
the MARINA within Region VIII towards the effective and efficient delivery of
services for the residents within the islands of Samar and Leyte. The Secretary of
Transportation and the MARINA shall submit the consolidated reorganization
plan and any amendments thereof to the President of the Philippines for his
approval.

SEC 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.


Approved.
