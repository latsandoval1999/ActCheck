                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

On March 24, 2026, President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. issued Executive Order (E.O.) No. 110
declaring a State of National Energy Emergency following major global oil supply disruptions
caused by the war in Iran and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. The resulting energy
constraints have created significant operational and logistical challenges nationwide.

The conduct of the Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections requires a stable and
reliable power supply to ensure orderly and credible proceedings. Holding the elections as
scheduled on November 6, 2026 risks operational disruptions, undermining election integrity,
and placing additional strain on limited energy resources needed for essential services,
including hospitals, water systems, public safety, and economic activity.

To allow the government to focus on stabilizing the energy situation and to safeguard the
integrity of the elections, it is prudent to postpone the Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections for six (6) months.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      AN ACT POSTPONING THE NEXT REGULAR BARANGAY AND
  SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN ELECTIONS SCHEDULED UNDER REPUBLIC
 ACT NO. 12232 ON NOVEMBER 2, 2026 TO MAY 3, 2027 (OR A PERIOD OF SIX
                (6) MONTHS), AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Postponement of the Next Regular Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections. –The Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections scheduled on November 2,
2026 are hereby postponed the first (1st) Monday of May 2027 (or for a period of six (6)
months).

SECTION 2. Hold-Over Provision. – All incumbent barangay officials shall remain in office
in a hold-over capacity until their successors shall have been duly elected and qualified, unless
sooner removed or suspended for cause in accordance with existing laws.

SECTION 3. Implementing Authority. – The Commission on Elections (COMELEC) shall
issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act and shall
ensure that adequate contingency measures are in place to address energy-related concerns
during the rescheduled elections.

SECTION 4. Appropriations. – Funds previously appropriated for the conduct of the
Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections shall remain available for the rescheduled
elections and may be used by the COMELEC in accordance with existing laws and regulations.

SECTION 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations,
or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

APPROVED.
