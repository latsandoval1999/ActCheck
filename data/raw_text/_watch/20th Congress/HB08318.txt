                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Under Sec. 7, Article XIII of the Constitution, the State is mandated to
“protect the right of subsistence fishermen, especially of local communities, to
the preferential use of the communal marine and fishing resources, both
inland and offshore.” Furthermore, the State shall “provide support to such
fishermen through appropriate technology and research, adequate financial,
production and marketing assistance, and other services.”

      This constitutional mandate is far from realized, as there is a glaring
inadequacy in the number of our municipal fish landing centers, the
designated places where our subsistence fisherfolk unload and store their fish
catch. In the 2021 Listing of Marine Fish Landing Centers of the Philippine
Statistics Authority, there are only 12,773 municipal fish landing centers in
the whole Philippines, of which 12,366 are considered “traditional” or without
any managing administration.

      Another problem that our fisherfolk face is fish spoilage. The Bureau of
Fisheries and Aquatic Resources has reported that the current fish spoilage
across the country is between 25 to 40 percent because of the shortage in
post-harvest equipment, like blast freezers, ice-making machines, cold storage
warehouses, and fish landing sites.

       Thus, this measure seeks to establish a fish port with ice plant and cold
storage facility in Barangay La Libertad, Municipality of Gutalac, Province of
Zamboanga del Norte. Establishing the fish port with ice plant and cold
storage facility in La Libertad, Gutalac is feasible, considering that the area
boasts of high-valued marine resources from the Sulu Sea. It will also help the
residents who are mostly subsistence fisherfolk, as they will be provided with
the necessary facilities where they can unload and store the fish that they
catch.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

       AN ACT ESTABLISHING A FISH PORT WITH ICE PLANT
    AND COLD STORAGE FACILITY IN BARANGAY LA LIBERTAD,
 MUNICIPALITY OF GUTALAC, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE,
             AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. There shall be established, under the supervision of the
Department of Agriculture, through the Philippine Fisheries Development
Authority (PFDA), a fish port with ice plant and cold storage facility in
Barangay La Libertad, Municipality of Gutalac, Province of Zamboanga del
Norte.

SEC. 2. Within two (2) years after the construction of the fish port with ice
plant and cold storage facility, the PFDA, through a memorandum of
agreement, shall transfer its management to the local government unit (LGU)
concerned. It shall implement a training and phasing-in program for the local
government personnel on the management and operation of the fish port with
ice plant and cold storage facility.

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Agriculture shall include in the Department’s
programs the establishment of the fish port with ice plant and cold storage
facility, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act. The LGU concerned shall set aside funds from any
available local revenue the amount necessary for the operationalization of the
fish port with ice plant and cold storage facility.

SEC. 4. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of
Agriculture shall issue the rules and regulations on the supervision,
monitoring, and transfer of the said fish port with ice plant and cold storage
facility by the PFDA to the LGU concerned.

SEC. 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
