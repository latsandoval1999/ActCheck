                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines is becoming an aging society. According to the Philippine Statistics
Authority (PSA), there were over 9.2 million senior citizens (60 years old and above) in
2020, comprising 8.5% of the total population, and this number is projected to further
increase in the coming decades.¹ As our population ages, the burden of chronic and
degenerative diseases such as hypertension, diabetes, cardiovascular illnesses, dementia,
and cancer continues to rise.

Under Republic Act No. 11223 or the Universal Health Care Act, the State guarantees
equitable access to quality and affordable healthcare services.² Meanwhile, Republic Act
No. 11350 created the National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC) to promote the
welfare of older persons.³ However, despite these important laws, the country still lacks
a dedicated, comprehensive, and specialized national referral center solely focused on
geriatric health.

Bilang isang NANAY, hindi natin maaaring pabayaan ang ating mga lolo at lola. They are the
pillars of our families and the keepers of our history. They deserve not only respect, but
accessible and specialized medical care responsive to their unique needs.

This measure seeks to establish the Philippine Geriatric Center (PGC) as a national end-
referral, training, and research institution for geriatric care, and to ensure the
establishment of regional geriatric specialty centers nationwide, fully integrated within
the Universal Health Care framework.

Through this bill, we affirm that caring for our elderly is not charity… it is a responsibility
of a grateful nation.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE PHILIPPINE GERIATRIC CENTER UNDER THE
   DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:
SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Geriatric Center
(PGC) Act.”
SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right to health of older persons and the aging population by ensuring
accessible, affordable, and quality health services responsive to their unique medical,
psychological, and social needs. Towards this end, the State shall adopt an integrated and
comprehensive approach to geriatric health development, and ensure that specialized
services are integrated within the Universal Health Care (UHC) Framework.
SEC. 3. Philippine Geriatric Center. – The National Center for Geriatric Health–Jose R.
Reyes Memorial Medical Center (NCGH-JRRMMC), located in San Miguel, Manila, is
hereby renamed as the Philippine Geriatric Center (PGC) and established as a tertiary
specialty hospital under the direct control and supervision of the Department of Health
(DOH) to ensure the healthcare and quality of life of older persons and aging population:
Provided, That the existing hospital development plan, current bed capacity, and service
capabilities, including organizational structure and staffing requirements, shall be
updated and upgraded consistent with the expanded mandate under this Act: Provided,
further, That the DOH shall formulate a National Geriatric Health Service Delivery Plan,
which shall include the establishment and upgrading of at least one (1) regional geriatric
specialty center per region within five (5) years from the effectivity of this Act to ensure
equitable access to geriatric care, in coordination with local government units (LGUs) and
existing DOH Hospitals.
SEC. 4. Purposes and Objectives. – The PGC shall have the following purposes and
objectives:
       (a) Equip, maintain, administer, and operate an integrated medical institution
           which shall specialize in geriatric health services;
       (b) Promote medical services to the elderly and scientific research relative to the
           prevention, diagnosis, treatment, care, rehabilitation, and relief of diseases of
           older people and the aging population,
       (c) Establish and maintain a National Geriatric Health Registry, which shall collect
           and update data on the health status, care needs, and service utilization of the
           elderly population;
       (d) Provide education and training for physicians, nurses, social workers,
           barangay health workers, and other allied medical and technical personnel in
           the practice and scientific implementation of health services to older people
           and the aging population;
       (e) Coordinate the various efforts and activities of other government agencies and
           LGUs towards a comprehensive and integrated approach to geriatric health
           and healthcare service delivery;
       (f) Develop and implement a Geriatric Human Resource Development Program
           that will provide scholarships, incentives, and continuing professional
           development for healthcare workers specializing in geriatrics, in coordination
           with the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Professional
           Regulation Commission (PRC), including bonding requirements or service
           commitments as may be necessary;
       (g) Ensure the integration of geriatric services within the UHC framework,
           including PhilHealth coverage for age-related conditions, long-term care, and
           preventive health services; and
       (h) Coordinate with the National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC) to align
           geriatric health programs with social protection, welfare, and community-
           based support services for older persons.

SEC. 5. Referral System. – The PGC shall be the National End-Referral and Training
Center for complicated geriatric cases and shall provide technical assistance to regional
and local hospitals in developing geriatric health services.
SEC. 6. Appropriations. – An initial amount of Five Hundred Million Pesos (PhP
500,000,000) shall be appropriated for the upgrading, capacity-building, and initial
operations of the PGC: Provided, That said funding shall be released in phases over a
three-year period, subject to periodic performance evaluation and progress reports
submitted to Congress: Provided, further, That the Secretary of Health shall immediately
include in the Department’s program the implementation of this Act, the funding of
which shall be included in the subsequent Annual General Appropriations Act.
Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued operation of the hospital, including
its upgrading and expansion of services, shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.
SEC. 7. Joint Congressional Oversight Committee. – There is hereby created a Joint
Congressional Oversight Committee on Geriatric Health Services to oversee, monitor,
and evaluate the implementation of the provisions of this Act.
It shall be composed of ten (10) members: five (5) members from the Senate, to be
appointed by the Senate President, at least one (1) of whom shall be from the minority,
and must include the Chairpersons of the Committees on Health and Demography;
Finance; and Social Justice, Welfare and Rural Development; and five (5) members from
the House of Representatives, to be appointed by the Speaker of the House, at least one
(1) of whom shall be from the minority, and must include the Chairpersons of the
Committees on Health; Appropriations; and Senior Citizens.
The Joint Congressional Oversight Committee shall have the following powers and
functions:
       (a) Review and evaluate the implementation of this Act, including compliance
           with its mandates, utilization of funds, the results of the periodic review
           conducted by the DOH under Section 8 hereof, and the performance of the
           Philippine Geriatric Center;
       (b) Conduct hearings, inspections, and consultations with the DOH, NCSC, LGUs,
           medical professionals, and other stakeholders;
       (c) Require the DOH and PGC to submit periodic reports, including an annual
           performance report and audited financial statements; and
       (d) Recommend remedial legislation, budgetary support, or policy actions to
           strengthen the delivery of geriatric health services nationwide.

SEC. 8. Periodic Review. – Within five (5) years from the effectivity of this Act, and every
five (5) years thereafter, the DOH shall conduct a comprehensive review of the
implementation of this Act and submit a report to Congress, including recommendations
for legislative or policy amendments, which shall be transmitted and referred to the Joint
Congressional Oversight Committee on Geriatric Health Services for appropriate action.
SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Health shall, in
consultation and coordination with appropriate government agencies such as the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Civil Service Commission (CSC), NCSC,
Civil Society Organizations (CSOs), Non-Government Organizations (NGOs), and other
stakeholders, within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, promulgate the
Implementing Rules and Regulations for the effective and efficient implementation of the
provisions of this Act.
SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder thereof not otherwise affected shall remain in
full force and effect.
SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and regulations,
or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly: Provided, That nothing in this act shall be construed to repeal or
diminish the mandates of Republic Act No. 11223, or the Universal Health Care Act, and
Republic Act No. 11350, or the National Commission of Senior Citizens Act.
SEC. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following the completion
of its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
