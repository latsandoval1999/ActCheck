                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE
       The exponential rise of the digital economy has transformed retail and consumer
behavior across the Philippines. While e-commerce platforms and digital marketplaces
have provided unparalleled convenience and expanded market access for thousands of
businesses, this shift has also resulted in a dramatic surge in single-use plastic waste.
Daily parcel deliveries generate vast amounts of secondary and tertiary packaging
materials such as bubble wrap, plastic mailers, courier pouches, and adhesive tape. The
use of such secondary and tertiary packaging materials created a severe burden on local
solid waste management systems and marine ecosystems.
       In 2022, Republic Act No. 11898 amended Republic Act No. 9003 (the “Ecological
Solid Waste Management Act of 2000”) by institutionalizing Extended Producer
Responsibility (EPR). This framework mandates large product producers to recover and
manage their post-consumer plastic footprint. However, a critical regulatory gap persists
regarding online retail logistics: the majority of e-commerce parcel packaging bypasses
the EPR regime because individual micro, small, and medium enterprise (MSME) sellers
do not qualify as "obliged enterprises," leaving the platform-facilitated plastic footprint
largely unaccounted for.
       This bill seeks to address that loophole by extending EPR obligations to large-scale
operators of e-commerce platforms and digital marketplaces. Specifically, this measure
proposes to: a) explicitly define platform operators as product producers responsible for
the aggregate plastic packaging generated through their networks; b) establish criteria
for "obliged enterprises" in the e-commerce sector based on total assets, revenue, and
logistics infrastructure (e.g., fulfillment centers, sorting hubs, and warehouses; and c)
mandate a centralized digital tracking mechanism requiring platforms to monitor, account
for, and compute the total platform-facilitated plastic footprint generated by transactions
and third-party merchant deliveries.
       By requiring digital marketplace giants to take full institutional responsibility for the
plastic waste generated through their ecosystems, this bill ensures a level playing field,
supports circular economy initiatives, and protects our environment from mounting
packaging waste. Further, the mechanisms that will be created in this proposed legislation
will ensure that the endeavours to minimize plastic waste will come from all fronts, making
a more wholistic effort to protect the environment.
      For the Filipino people, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress
Assembled:

    AN ACT FURTHER AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9003, OTHERWISE
      KNOWN AS THE “ECOLOGICAL SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT ACT
   OF 2000,” AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11898, BY MANDATING
   E-COMMERCE PLATFORMS AND DIGITAL MARKETPLACES TO COMPLY
  WITH EXTENDED PRODUCER RESPONSIBILITY FOR PLASTIC PACKAGING
                  WASTE, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     Section 1. Section 3 of Republic Act No. 9003, as amended, is hereby further
amended by inserting additional definitions to read as follows:

            “SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — For the purposes of this Act:

            xxx

            (w-2) Product producer shall refer to any of the following persons:

            xxx

                 (3) OPERATORS OF E-COMMERCE PLATFORMS OR DIGITAL
            MARKETPLACES THAT PROVIDE A PLATFORM FOR MERCHANTS AND
            THIRD-PARTY VENDORS TO SELL OR DISTRIBUTE GOODS TO
            CONSUMERS WITHIN THE PHILIPPINES.

            xxx
            (M-2) E-COMMERCE PLATFORM OR DIGITAL MARKETPLACE SHALL REFER
      TO A DIGITAL SERVICE, WEBSITE, ELECTRONIC PORTAL, OR APPLICATION
      OPERATED BY A THIRD PARTY THAT FACILITATES COMMERCIAL TRANSACTIONS
      BY ALLOWING MERCHANTS AND THIRD-PARTY VENDORS TO ADVERTISE,
      DISPLAY, SELL, OR DISTRIBUTE GOODS, INCLUDING CONSUMER PRODUCTS
      PACKAGED IN PLASTICS, DIRECTLY TO END-USERS WITHIN THE PHILIPPINES.

            (V-4) PLATFORM-FACILITATED PLASTIC FOOTPRINT SHALL REFER TO
      THE TOTAL AGGREGATE WEIGHT OF PLASTIC PACKAGING MATERIALS—
      INCLUDING SECONDARY AND TERTIARY PACKAGING SUCH AS PLASTIC
      MAILERS, BUBBLE WRAPS, COURIER POUCHES, AND ADHESIVE TAPES—
      UTILIZED BY THIRD-PARTY MERCHANTS OR THE PLATFORM’S OWN LOGISTICS
      AND FULFILLMENT NETWORKS TO PACK AND DELIVER GOODS SOLD THROUGH
      THE E-COMMERCE PLATFORM.”

      Sec. 2. Section 44-B of Republic Act No. 9003, as added by Section 4 of Republic
Act No. 11898, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

             “Sec. 44-B. Obliged Enterprises Under This Article. — Product producers
      obliged to implement EPR under this Article shall refer to large enterprises,
      INCLUDING THOSE OPERATING AS E-COMMERCE PLATFORMS OR DIGITAL
      MARKETPLACES, that generate or are responsible for plastic packaging waste
      introduced into the Philippine market: Provided, however, That micro, small and
      medium enterprises defined under Republic Act No. 9501 shall not be covered:
      Provided, further, That in case the total value of assets of all enterprises carrying
      the same brand, label or trademark exceeds that of medium enterprises stated
      under Republic Act No. 9501, these enterprises shall be deemed obliged
      enterprises: PROVIDED, FURTHERMORE, THAT FOR PURPOSES OF DETERMINING
      WHETHER AN E-COMMERCE PLATFORM OR DIGITAL MARKETPLACE OPERATOR
      QUALIFIES AS A LARGE ENTERPRISE, ITS TOTAL ASSETS, CAPITAL
      INVESTMENTS, ANNUAL REVENUE, AND THE SCALE OF ITS OPERATIONAL
      INFRASTRUCTURE, INCLUDING WAREHOUSES, SORTING HUBS, FULFILLMENT
      CENTERS, AND LOGISTICS NETWORKS, SHALL BE TAKEN INTO ACCOUNT, AS
      REFLECTED IN ITS LATEST AVAILABLE AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND
      OTHER RELEVANT BUSINESS RECORDS.

      xxx

      Sec. 3. A new section on e-commerce platform obligations under Chapter III of
RA 9003, as amended is hereby added
      “SEC. 44-C-1. PLASTIC PACKAGING OF E-COMMERCE PLATFORMS AND DIGITAL
      MARKETPLACES. — THE PLASTIC PACKAGING COVERED UNDER SECTION 44-C
      AND ASSOCIATED WITH TRANSACTIONS FACILITATED THROUGH E-COMMERCE
      PLATFORMS OR DIGITAL MARKETPLACES SHALL BE ACCOUNTED FOR BASED ON
      THE AGGREGATE WEIGHT OF THE PLATFORM-FACILITATED PLASTIC
      FOOTPRINTS DERIVED FROM THE PLATFORM AND MERCHANT TRANSACTION
      DATA AND FULFILLMENT LOGS. IT SHALL BE THE RESPONSIBILITY OF THE
      PLATFORM OPERATORS TO ESTABLISH AND MAINTAIN A CENTRALIZED DIGITAL
      TRACKING MECHANISM TO MONITOR AND COMPUTE THE AGGREGATE WEIGHT
      OF SUCH PLATFORM-FACILITATED PLASTIC FOOTPRINT, AND TO REQUIRE
      MERCHANTS AND THIRD-PARTY VENDORS USING THEIR PLATFORMS TO
      PROVIDE COMPLETE, ACCURATE, AND VERIFIABLE DATA ON THE PLASTIC
      PACKAGING USED IN CONNECTION WITH THEIR TRANSACTIONS.

         Sec. 4. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be
in full force and effect.

       Sec. 5. Repealing Clause - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

       Sec. 6. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
