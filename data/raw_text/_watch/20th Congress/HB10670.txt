                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Effective education delivery is crucial across all schools in the Philippines, as it
equips students who will contribute to the country’s growth and development. Since
the enactment of Republic Act No. 9155, or the Governance of Basic Education Act of
2001, the realities faced by the Department of Education (DepEd) in decentralizing
the administration of the country’s basic education system have significantly changed.
Learner populations have grown, the number of schools has expanded, and the
complexity of delivering quality education across diverse geographic contexts has
increased and there is a need for innovative reforms and adaptive strategies to keep
up with the evolving needs of schools and students across the country.

        According to the Second Congressional Commission on Education (EDCOM II),
several Schools Division Offices (SDOs) now manage more than 1,300 schools, making
it increasingly difficult for division offices to effectively oversee schools, support
educators, and implement initiatives at the local level. Moreover, the disparity in
workload among SDOs remains one of the most pressing concerns. Under these
conditions, the management of divisions face increasing administrative burdens, which
may undermine DepEd’s capacity to provide timely and effective support to schools.

       This bill seeks to address these gaps by authorizing the rationalization and
creation of Schools Division Offices to ensure that the delivery of basic education
services remains responsive and equitable, as strengthening these Schools Division
Offices is important to ensure that national policies provide meaningful support to
schools and educators.




                                            1
       By allowing school divisions to have a more organized and flexible structure,
this measure aims to provide administrative, technical, and management support
closer to our educational institutions. Moreover, the bill authorizes the Secretary of
Education, in coordination with the Department of Budget and Management (DBM),
to create additional school divisions and reorganize, combine, adjust, or divide existing
divisions and districts, when necessary, based on the number of learners, schools
served, geographic conditions, workload requirements, or other factors affecting the
delivery of education. This new system and scheme also ensure that every province
and city will have a minimum of one school division, giving DepEd the freedom to
modify organizational structures in accordance with objective standards and changes
in needs.

       Improving learning outcomes for Filipino students is ultimately the goal of
better division-level governance. Students gain the most when teachers are better
prepared, school administrators receive more support, and educational services are
provided more effectively. Most importantly, these improvements can lead to better
learning outcomes, ensuring that every Filipino student receives quality education
through a more responsive educational structure.

       In light of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
