                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) remain one of the strongest pillars of the
Philippine economy. In 2025 alone, remittances reached approximately US$35.63
billion, accounting for about 7.3 percent of the country’s gross domestic product
(GDP). Beyond these numbers are millions of Filipino families whose daily
sustenance, education, and future depend on the sacrifices of their loved ones working
abroad. It is therefore the duty of the State to ensure that government services for
OFWs are accessible, responsive, and within reach of the communities that need them
most.

        Data consistently show that Region IV-A (CALABARZON) hosts the largest
concentration of OFWs in the country. Cavite, in particular, remains a major source of
migrant workers and seafarers. The City of Bacoor is a prime example as there are
over 60,000 OFWs claiming Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA)
membership status – which represents over 17 percent of the total OWWA
membership in the province.

        Bacoor also sits at a critical transportation crossroads connecting Cavite to
Metro Manila and the rest of the region. With major road networks and transport
infrastructure projects underway, the city offers an accessible and practical location
for a provincial DMW office that can efficiently serve Caviteño OFWs and nearby
communities.

        Despite this high concentration and prime location, many OFWs and their
families still need to travel significant distances to access services of the Department

      Establishing a DMW Cavite Provincial Office in Bacoor City will bring essential
frontline services closer to migrant workers and their families—from documentation
and welfare assistance to reintegration support and emergency response. More
importantly, it will strengthen the government’s on-the-ground presence in one of the
country’s largest OFW-sending regions, ensuring faster, more coordinated, and more
compassionate service delivery.

      At a time when the nation continues to rely heavily on the contributions of its
modern-day heroes, it is both timely and necessary to expand institutional support
where it is most needed. This measure affirms the State’s continuing commitment to
protect and serve OFWs throughout the entire migration cycle.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A PROVINCIAL OFFICE OF THE DEPARTMENT OF MIGRANT
WORKERS IN BACOOR CITY, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, TO BE KNOWN AS THE
   “DMW CAVITE PROVINCIAL OFFICE”, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
                           THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. There shall be established a provincial office of the Department of
Migrant Workers (DMW) to be located in Bacoor City, Province of Cavite, to be known
as “DMW Cavite Provincial Office”, which shall exercise jurisdiction over the Province
of Cavite and shall be under the direct supervision of the DMW.

      SEC. 2. The DMW Secretary shall include in the Department’s program the
operationalization of a provincial office in Bacoor City, Province of Cavite, and shall
transfer all records, property, equipment, supplies, and materials pertaining to the
jurisdiction and administration of the new provincial office and shall provide all the
necessary assistance in its establishment in accordance with existing laws and
regulations.

      SEC. 3. The DMW Secretary shall include in the Department’s program the
operationalization of the DMW Cavite Provincial Office in Bacoor City, Province of
Cavite, the funding of which shall be included in the General Appropriations Act.
