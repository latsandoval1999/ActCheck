  AN ACT AMENDING ARTICLES 333 AND 344, AND REPEALING ARTICLE
   334 OF THE REVISED PENAL CODE, TO REMOVE THE DISTINCTION
      BETWEEN ADULTERY AND CONCUBINAGE AND ESTABLISH A
          GENDER‑FAIR DEFINITION OF MARITAL INFIDELITY

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Revised Penal Code provides for a comprehensive legal framework that
penalizes unfaithfulness and infidelity to the sanctity of marriage. It classifies adultery
and concubinage as crimes against chastity, while the Family Code refers to them as
sexual infidelity, or more generally, marital infidelity. Albeit both defined as marital
infidelity, the existing legal frameworks penalizes them differently, effectively placing
a greater burden on women or wives, than on men or husbands.

       Without a doubt, marital infidelity of varying degrees knows no gender or sex.
A marriage in which a wife sows doubt, infidelity, and deceit is no different from a
marriage in which a husband commits the same acts. The harm inflicted upon the
marital union, the erosion of trust, and the betrayal of the obligations of marriage
remain the same regardless of whether the offending party is the husband or the wife.

       The 1987 Philippine Constitution expressly provides that all Filipinos are
afforded the same and equal protection under the law, regardless of gender or sex.
Maintaining different standards for adultery and concubinage is inconsistent with the
Constitution's guarantee of equal protection under the law. It therefore seeks to
ensure that the law treats marital infidelity with the same measure of accountability,
regardless of the gender of the offending spouse. The law must protect the institution
of marriage without compromising the constitutional guarantee of equality before the
law.

      This measure likewise gives effect to Section 12 of Republic Act No. 9710,
otherwise known as the Magna Carta of Women, which guarantees women equal
treatment before the law and mandates the review and amendment of laws that
discriminate against women. The existing provisions under the Revised Penal Code
that codify concubinage and adultery continue to prescribe different elements,
standards of proof, and penalties for the same act of marital infidelity. The promise of
equality before the law cannot be fulfilled if marital infidelity continues to be penalized
differently based solely on the sex of the offending spouse. This bill therefore seeks
to remove these distinctions and establish a single, gender-fair standard of marital
infidelity consistent with the Constitution and existing law.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

       AN ACT AMENDING ARTICLES 333 AND 344, AND REPEALING ARTICLE
        334 OF ACT NO. 3815, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “REVISED PENAL
         CODE”, TO REMOVE THE DISTINCTION BETWEEN ADULTERY AND
          CONCUBINAGE AND ESTABLISH A GENDER‑FAIR DEFINITION OF
                              MARITAL INFIDELITY

     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1   SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Marital Infidelity Act”.
 2
 3   SEC. 2. Article 333 of Act No. 3815 is hereby amended to read as follows:
 4
 5   “ARTICLE 333. MARITAL INFIDELITY [Who are guilty of adultery.] – MARITAL
 6   INFIDELITY [Adultery] is committed by any married PERSON [woman] who shall
 7   have sexual RELATIONS [intercourse] with a PERSON [man] OTHER THAN HIS
 8   OR her SPOUSE [husband], and by the SEXUAL PARTNER [man] of the MARRIED
 9   PERSON, knowing the OTHER [her] to be married.
10
11   MARITAL INFIDELITY [adultery] shall be punished by DESTIERRO IN ITS
12   MAXIMUM PERIOD.
13
14   ANY PERSON ACCUSED OF MARITAL INFIDELITY SHALL BE EXEMPT FROM
15   CRIMINAL LIABILITY, IF THE MARRIAGE BE SUBSEQUENTLY DECLARED
16   VOID, OR, IF THE OFFENSE WAS COMMITTED AFTER THE ISSUANCE OF A
17   FINAL DECREE OF LEGAL SEPARATION BY A COMPETENT COURT.
18   HOWEVER, IF THE OFFENSE WAS COMMITTED AFTER THE ACCUSED
19   SPOUSE HAD BEEN ABANDONED FOR FOUR (4) CONSECUTIVE YEARS, OR
20   THE   OFFENDED     PARTY   HAD  PREVIOUSLY    BEEN   DECLARED
 1   PRESUMPTIVELY DEAD BY THE COURTS [(If the person guilty of adultery
 2   committed this offense while): abandoned without justification] OR SUBJECTED TO
 3   PHYSICAL VIOLENCE OR ABUSIVE CONDUCT by the offended spouse, the
 4   penalty next lower in degree provided in the [next] preceding paragraph shall be
 5   imposed, AND THE SAME PENALTY SHALL BE IMPOSED ON THE CO-ACCUSED
 6   IN THE MARITAL INFIDELITY CASE.”
 7
 8   SEC. 3. Article 334 of Act No. 3815 is hereby repealed.
 9
10   SEC. 4. Article 344 of Act No. 3815 is hereby amended to read as follows:
11
12   “ARTICLE 344. Prosecution of the Crimes of MARITAL INFIDELITY [Adultery,
13   Concubinage,] Seduction, Abduction, Rape and Acts of Lasciviousness. — The crimes
14   of MARITAL INFIDELITY [adultery and concubinage] shall not be prosecuted
15   except upon a complaint filed by the offended spouse.
16
17   HOWEVER, IF, IN THE COURSE OF THE PRELIMINARY INVESTIGATION, THE
18   FILING OF A MARITAL INFIDELITY CASE IS FOUND TO HAVE BEEN
19   INITIATED TO HARASS OR THREATEN THE OTHER SPOUSE, OR IF THE
20   OFFENDED SPOUSE IS LIKEWISE FOUND TO HAVE SEXUAL RELATIONS
21   WITH A PERSON OTHER THAN HIS OR HER SPOUSE, THE CASE SHALL BE
22   DISMISSED.
23
24   THE DISMISSAL SHALL OPERATE AS A BAR TO THE FILING OF ANY
25   SUBSEQUENT MARITAL INFIDELITY CASES AGAINST THE ACCUSED
26   SPOUSE.
27
28   The offended party cannot institute criminal prosecution without including both the
29   guilty parties, if they are both alive, nor, in any case, if he shall have consented or
30   pardoned the offenders.
31
32   The offenses of seduction, abduction, rape or acts of lasciviousness, shall not be
33   prosecuted except upon a complaint filed by the offended party or her parents,
34   grandparents, or guardian, nor, in any case, if the offender has been expressly
35   pardoned by the above-named persons, as the case may be.
36
37   [In cases of seduction, abduction, acts of lasciviousness and rape, the marriage of the
38   offender with the offended party shall extinguish the criminal action or remit the
39   penalty already imposed upon him.] The provisions of this paragraph shall also be
40   applicable to the co-principals, accomplices and accessories after the fact of the
41   abovementioned crimes.”
