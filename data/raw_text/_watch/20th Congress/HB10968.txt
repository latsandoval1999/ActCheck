                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Misamis Oriental II Electric Service Cooperative, Inc. (MORESCO
II) has been serving the eastern portion of the Province of Misamis Oriental
for several decades. Incorporated and registered on October 1, 1976,
MORESCO II has grown from its early rural electrification operations into
a major distribution utility serving a geographically extensive and
economically diverse area.

      This bill seeks to grant MORESCO II a franchise to construct, install,
establish, operate, own, manage, and maintain a distribution system for
the conveyance of electric power to end-users within the City of Gingoog
and the Municipalities of Magsaysay, Medina, Talisayan, Balingoan,
Kinoguitan, Sugbongcogon, Binuangan, Salay, Lagonglong, Balingasag,
and Claveria, all in the Province of Misamis Oriental.

      MORESCO II presently serves a franchise area extending over
approximately 1,800 square kilometers. Its service territory encompasses
both urbanizing population centers and remote communities where access
to electricity remains an important component of local development. The
cooperative has approximately 123,699 registered member-consumers,
with around 110,892 service connections, representing approximately 93%
of potential household connections within its coverage area.
           The cooperative has also consistently attained a “Triple A” rating
    from the National Electrification Administration (NEA) and is classified as
    a Mega Large Electric Cooperative, reflecting its operational performance
    and institutional capability. Its continued efforts toward total
    electrification, modernization of its distribution system, improvement of
    consumer services, and strengthening of its financial and operational
    performance further demonstrate its continuing role in the development of
    the communities under its care.

           Granting MORESCO II a congressional franchise will provide the
    necessary statutory authority and institutional certainty for the
    cooperative to continue serving these communities, invest in the expansion
    and modernization of its distribution network, respond to increasing
    electricity demand, improve system reliability and resiliency, and pursue
    the electrification of remaining underserved and unserved areas.

          More importantly, the grant of this franchise recognizes that the
    provision of electricity is not merely a commercial undertaking but a public
    service imbued with public interest. MORESCO II is called upon to ensure
    that the benefits of electrification reach communities throughout its
    franchise area while remaining accountable to its member-consumer-
    owners and compliant with applicable laws, regulations, technical
    standards, and the requirements of government regulatory agencies.

         In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
    earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
         GRANTING THE MISAMIS ORIENTAL II ELECTRIC SERVICE
      COOPERATIVE, INC. (MORESCO II), A FRANCHISE TO CONSTRUCT,
       INSTALL, ESTABLISH, OPERATE, OWN, MANAGE AND MAINTAIN
       DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS FOR THE CONVEYANCE OF ELECTRIC
       POWER TO THE END-USERS IN THE CITY OF GINGOOG AND THE
     MUNICIPALITIES OF MAGSAYSAY, MEDINA, TALISAYAN, BALINGOAN,
     KINOGUITAN, SUGBONGCOGON, BINUANGAN, SALAY, LAGONGLONG,
      BALINGASAG, AND CLAVERIA, ALL IN THE PROVINCE OF MISAMIS
                              ORIENTAL

    Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Republic
    of the Philippines in Congress assembled:


1          SECTION 1. Nature and Scope of Franchise. – Subject to the
2   provisions of the Constitution and applicable laws, rules and regulations,
3   there is hereby granted to the Misamis Oriental II Electric Service Cooperative,
4   Inc. (MORESCO II), hereunder referred to as the Grantee, its successors or
5   assigns a franchise to construct, install, establish, operate, own, manage and
6   maintain in the public interest and commercial purposes, a distribution
 1   system for the conveyance of electric power to the end-users in the City of
 2   Gingoog and the Municipalities of Magsaysay, Medina, Talisayan, Balingoan,
 3   Kinoguitan, Sugbongcogon, Binuangan, Salay, Lagonglong, Balingasag, and
 4   Claveria, all in the Province of Misamis Oriental.

 5           As used in this Act, distribution system refers to the system of wires
 6   and associated facilities including sub-transmission lines belonging to a
 7   franchised distribution utility extending between the delivery point on the
 8   national transmission system or generating facility and the metering point or
 9   facility of the end-users.

10           SEC. 2. Manner of Operation of Facilities. – All electric distribution
11   facilities, lines and systems for electric services installed, owned, maintained,
12   operated, managed by the Grantee, its successors or assigns shall be operated
13   or maintained at all times in a superior manner, and it shall be the duty of
14   the Grantee, its successors or assigns, whenever required to do so by the
15   Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), or its legal successor, or the
16   Department of Energy (DOE), or its legal successor, or any other government
17   agency concerned to modify, improve and change such facilities or systems in
18   such manner and to such extent as the progress in science or technology and
19   improvements or innovations in the electric power services may render
20   reasonable and proper.

21         Whenever practicable, and for purposes of maintaining order, safety
22   and aesthetics along the highways, roads, streets, alleys or right-of-way, the
23   Grantee may allow the use of free spaces in its poles, facilities, or right-of-way
24   by interested parties upon reasonable compensation to the Grantee
25   considering the costs incurred to accommodate and administer the use of the
26   Grantee’s facilities by such parties. The ERC shall decide in case of dispute
27   or disagreement between the parties.

28          SEC. 3. Authority of the ERC and NEA. – The Grantee shall secure
29   from the ERC or NEA any government agency having jurisdiction over their
30   operations, the Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity and any other
31   license, permit or authority indispensable for the construction and operation
32   of the electric power distribution system.

33           SEC. 4. Excavation and Restoration Works. – For the purpose of
34   erecting and maintaining the poles or other supports for said facilities, wires
35   or other conductors or for the purpose of laying and maintaining said
36   facilities, wires, cables or other conductors, it shall be lawful for the Grantee,
37   its successors, or assignees, in coordination with the Department of Public
38   Works and Highways (DPWH) or the local government unit (LGU) concerned,
39   as may be appropriate, to make excavations or lay conduits in any of the
 1   public places, highways, road, streets, lanes, alleys, avenues, sidewalks, or
 2   bridges of the province, cities or municipalities: Provided, however, That a
 3   public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley, avenue, sidewalk, or bridge
 4   disturbed, altered, or changed by reason of erection of poles or other supports
 5   or the underground laying of wires, other conductors or conduits, shall be
 6   repaired and replaced in workmanlike manner by the Grantee, its successors
 7   or assignees, in accordance with the standards set by the DPWH or the local
 8   government unit concerned. Should the Grantee, its successors or assignees,
 9   after the ten (10)-day notice from the grant of authority, fail, refuse or neglect
10   to repair or replace any part of public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley,
11   avenue, sidewalk, or bridge altered, changed or disturbed by the said Grantee,
12   its successors or assignees, then the DPWH or the local government unit
13   concerned shall have the right to have the same repaired and replaced in good
14   order and condition and charge the Grantee, its successors or assignees,
15   double the amount of the cost and expenses for such repair or replacement.

16          SEC. 5. Responsibility to the Public. – The Grantee shall supply
17   electricity to its captive market in the least costly manner. In the interest of
18   the public good and as far as feasible and whenever required by the ERC, the
19   Grantee shall modify, improve, or change its facilities, poles, lines, systems
20   and equipment for the purpose of providing efficient and reliable service and
21   reduced electricity costs. The Grantee shall charge reasonable and just power
22   rates for its services to all types of consumers within its franchised area in
23   order that business and industries shall be able to compete.

24         The Grantee shall have the obligation to provide open and
25   nondiscriminatory access to its distribution system and services for any end-
26   user within its franchise area consistent with Republic Act (RA) No. 9136,
27   otherwise known as the “Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001”. The
28   Grantee shall not engage in any activity that will constitute an abuse of
29   market power such as unfair trade practices, monopolistic schemes, and
30   other activities that will hinder competitiveness of business and industries.

31          SEC. 6. Rates for Services. – The retail rates and charges for the
32   distribution of electric power by the Grantee to its end-users shall be
33   regulated by and subject to the approval of the ERC or its legal successor.

34         The Grantee shall identify and segregate in its electricity bill to the end-
35   users the components of the retail rate pursuant to RA 9136, unless otherwise
36   amended. Such rates charged by the Grantee to the end-users shall be made
37   public and transparent. The Grantee shall implement lifeline rate to
38   marginalized end-users as mandated under RA 9136.

39         SEC. 7. Promotion of Consumer Interests. – The Grantee herein shall
 1   establish a consumer desk that will handle consumer complaints and ensure
 2   adequate promotion of consumer interests. The Grantee shall act with
 3   dispatch on all complaints brought before it.

 4          SEC. 8. Commitment to Provide and Promote the Creation of
 5   Employment Opportunities. – The Grantee shall create employment
 6   opportunities and shall allow on-the-job training in its franchise operation:
 7   Provided, That priority shall be accorded to the residents where their principal
 8   office is located: Provided, further, That the Grantee shall comply with the
 9   applicable labor standards and allowance entitlement under existing labor
10   laws, rules and regulations, and similar issuances.

11         SEC. 9. Right of the Government. – A special right is hereby reserved
12   to the President of the Philippines, in times of war, rebellion, public peril,
13   calamity, emergency, disaster or disturbance of peace and order: to
14   temporarily take over and operate the stations or facilities of the Grantee; to
15   temporarily suspend the operation of any station or facility in the interest of
16   public safety, security, and public welfare; or to authorize the temporary use
17   and operation thereof by any agency of the government, upon due
18   compensation to the Grantee, for the use of the stations or facilities during
19   the period when these shall be so operated.

20          SEC. 10. Right of Eminent Domain. – Subject to the limitations and
21   procedures prescribed by law, the Grantee is authorized to exercise the right
22   of eminent domain insofar as it may be reasonably necessary for the efficient
23   maintenance and operation of services. The Grantee is authorized to install
24   and maintain its poles, wires, and other facilities over and across public
25   property, including streets, highways, forest reserves, and other similar
26   property of the Government of the Philippines, its branches, or any of its
27   instrumentalities. The Grantee may acquire such private property as is
28   actually necessary for the realization of the purposes for which this franchise
29   is granted: Provided, That proper expropriation proceedings shall have been
30   instituted and just compensation paid.

31          SEC. 11. Term of Franchise. – Unless sooner cancelled, the franchise
32   shall be in effect for a period of twenty-five (25) years commencing upon the
33   expiration of the existing franchise. This franchise shall be deemed ipso facto
34   revoked in the event the Grantee fails to operate continuously for two (2)
35   years.

36        SEC. 12. Warranty in Favor of the National and Local
37   Governments. – The Grantee shall hold the national, provincial, city, and
38   municipal governments of the Philippines free from all claims, accounts,
39   demands, or actions arising from accidents causing injury to persons or
 1   damage to properties, during the construction, installation, operation, and
 2   maintenance of the distribution system of the Grantee.

 3         SEC. 13. Liability for Damages. – The Grantee shall be liable for any
 4   injury to persons and damage to properties arising from accidents by reason
 5   of any defective construction under this franchise or of any neglect or
 6   omission to keep its poles and wires in safe condition.

 7          SEC. 14. Sale, Lease, Transfer, Usufruct, or Assignment of
 8   Franchise. – The Grantee shall not sell, lease, transfer, grant the usufruct,
 9   or assign this franchise or the rights and privileges acquired thereunder to
10   any person, firm, company, corporation, or other commercial or legal entity,
11   or merge with any other corporation, or entity, or shall transfer the controlling
12   interest of the Grantee, whether as a whole or in parts, and whether
13   simultaneously or contemporaneously, to any such person, firm, company,
14   corporation, or entity without the prior approval of the Congress: Provided,
15   That Congress shall be informed of any sale, lease, transfer, grant of usufruct,
16   sale, or assignment of franchise or the rights and privileges acquired
17   thereunder, or of the merger, or sale of the controlling interest within sixty
18   (60) days after the completion of said transaction: Provided, further, That any
19   such transfer, sale, or assignment is in accordance with the constitutional
20   limitations: Provided, furthermore, That failure to report to Congress such
21   change of ownership shall render the franchise ipso facto revoked: Provided,
22   finally, That any person or entity to which this franchise is sold, transferred,
23   or assigned, shall be subject to the same conditions, terms, restrictions, and
24   limitations of this Act.

25          SEC. 15. Reportorial Requirement. – The Grantee shall submit an
26   annual report to Congress, through the Committee on Legislative Franchises
27   of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Public Services of the
28   Senate, on its compliance with the terms and conditions of the franchise and
29   on its operations on or before April 30 of every year during the term of the
30   franchise. The reportorial compliance certificate issued by Congress shall be
31   required before any application for permit or certificate is accepted by the
32   ERC.

33          SEC. 16. Fine. – The failure of the Grantee to submit the requisite
34   annual report to Congress shall be penalized by a fine in the amount of Five
35   hundred pesos (P500.00) per working day of noncompliance which shall be
36   collected by the ERC. The fine shall be collected separately from the
37   reportorial penalties imposed by the ERC and shall be remitted to the Bureau
38   of Treasury.

39         SEC. 17. Equality Clause. – Any advantage, favor, privilege,
 1   exemption, or immunity granted under existing franchises, or which may
 2   hereafter be granted, upon prior review and approval of Congress, shall
 3   become part of this franchise and shall be accorded immediately and
 4   unconditionally to the herein Grantee: Provided, however, That the foregoing
 5   shall neither apply to nor affect provisions concerning territory covered by the
 6   franchise, the life span of the franchise or the type of service authorized by
 7   the franchise: Provided, further, That the foregoing shall not apply to the sale,
 8   lease, transfer, grant of usufruct, or assignment of legislative franchises with
 9   prior congressional approval.

10           SEC. 18. Applicability of Existing Laws. – The Grantee shall comply
11   with and be subject to the provisions of Commonwealth Act No. 146 or the
12   “Public Service Act”, as amended, RA 9136, and RA 10531 or the “National
13   Electrification Administration Reform Act of 2013.”

14         SEC. 19. Existing Powers. – Existing mandates, powers, functions,
15   and privileges granted to electric cooperatives under existing laws, including
16   Section 10 of RA 10531 shall remain valid and effective, unless expressly
17   repealed by succeeding laws.

18          SEC. 20. Repealability and Non-Exclusivity Clause. – This franchise
19   shall be subject to amendment, alteration, or repeal by Congress when the
20   public interest so requires and shall not be interpreted as an exclusive grant
21   of the privileges herein provided for.

22          SEC. 21. Separability Clause. – If any of the sections or provisions of
23   this Act is held invalid, all other provisions not affected thereby shall remain
24   valid.

25         SEC. 22. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
26   orders, letters of instruction, administrative rules and regulations or parts
27   thereof which is are contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act
28   are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

29         SEC. 23. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
30   days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
31   circulation.
