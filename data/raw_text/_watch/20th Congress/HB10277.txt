 AN ACT INSTITUTING AN AUTOMATIC REGIONAL MINIMUM WAGE
    ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
     PERTINENT PROVISIONS OF THE LABOR CODE OF THE
                        PHILIPPINES

                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

Minimum wage earners across the country continue to lose purchasing
power every time inflation outpaces the slow, petition-based wage-setting
process of the Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Boards. Workers
and their families often wait a year or longer for a wage order, by which
time the increase granted has already been eroded by rising prices. This
bill proposes an automatic, formula-based adjustment of the regional
minimum wage tied to the official inflation rate, removing the need for a
new petition each time prices rise. The mechanism retains the tripartite
boards' authority to set the base wage and to adjust the formula for
extraordinary circumstances, but ensures that workers are not left waiting
indefinitely for relief that is already overdue.

This measure is intended to protect the take-home pay of minimum wage
workers without waiting for Congress or the boards to act on every price
spike.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:


     SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Automatic Minimum
Wage Adjustment Act."
      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State affirms its policy to
afford full protection to labor and to ensure that wages keep pace with the
cost of living, consistent with the principles of social justice enshrined in
the Constitution.
      SECTION 3. Automatic Adjustment Mechanism. — Beginning the
year following the effectivity of this Act, the daily minimum wage rates set
by the Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Boards shall be
automatically adjusted every six (6) months based on the movement of the
regional Consumer Price Index (CPI) as officially released by the Philippine
Statistics Authority, without need of a new wage petition: Provided, That
the adjustment shall not exceed the actual percentage change in the CPI
for the relevant period.
      SECTION 4. Retention of Board Authority. — Nothing in this Act
shall diminish the authority of the Regional Tripartite Wages and
Productivity Boards to conduct wage reviews, issue wage orders
addressing structural or extraordinary conditions, and set the base
minimum wage from which future automatic adjustments shall be
computed.

       SECTION 5. Non-Diminution of Benefits. — No wage, benefit, or
privilege currently enjoyed by workers shall be reduced, diminished, or
discontinued by reason of this Act.
     SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The
Department of Labor and Employment, in coordination with the National
Wages and Productivity Commission and the Philippine Statistics
Authority, shall issue the implementing rules and regulations within
ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.
      SECTION 7. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is
declared invalid, the other provisions not affected shall remain in force.
     SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders,
and issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.
      SECTION 9. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
