                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to convert the Osmena Extension Street to the CDO Port Road in
Cagayan de Oro City to a national road.

        The road is presently in poor condition and in need of immediate repair and
rehabilitation. City and provincial funds are not available for its maintenance and/or upgrading.
Hence, the need to use other sources.

        With the proposed improvements to the Osmena Extension Street to the CDO Port
Road, easier access to both points would be ensured and the potentials for commercial and
industrial development would be realized.

       With its conversion into a national road it would receive much needed funding and
assistance it deserves.

       Passage of this measure is earnestly requested.

                                AN ACT
CONVERTING THE OSMENA EXTENSION STREET TO THE CDO PORT ROAD IN
CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
Assembled:

        Section 1. The Osmena Extension Street to the CDO Port Road in Cagayan de Oro
City is hereby converted into a National road.

       Section 2. The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue
the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

      Section 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a
newspaper of general circulation.
