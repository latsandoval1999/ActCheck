House BILL No.11639

Introduced by HON. ROMEO S. MOMO, SR.

AN ACT DECLARING JULY 16 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN
THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARRASCAL, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL SUR, IN
CELEBRATION OF ITS ANNUAL TOWN FIESTA AND THE PAHINUNGOD FESTIVAL

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Carrascal, Province of Surigao del Sur, celebrates its annual town
fiesta in honor of Our Lady of Mount Carmel, with the Pahinungod Festival serving as a
distinctive cultural and religious celebration of the municipality. The Provincial Government
of Surigao del Sur identifies the Pahinungod Festival as a major event of Carrascal, with
festival activities traditionally held in July and centered on devotion, community
participation, cultural expression, and thanksgiving.

Declaring July 16 of every year a special non-working holiday in Carrascal will enable
residents, visitors, workers, and families to participate meaningfully in the celebration and
will help preserve and strengthen the municipality's cultural and religious heritage.

This measure likewise recognizes the responsibility of the Local Government Unit of
Carrascal to plan and support the orderly conduct of the Pahinungod Festival. For this
purpose, the Municipality may appropriate funds in its annual budget, or utilize available
and legally authorized local appropriations, subject to existing budgeting, accounting,
auditing, procurement, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

House BILL No. 116339

Introduced by HON. ROMEO S. MOMO, SR.

AN ACT DECLARING JULY 16 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN
THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARRASCAL, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL SUR, IN CELEBRATION
OF ITS ANNUAL TOWN FIESTA AND THE PAHINUNGOD FESTIVAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Special Non-Working Holiday. July 16 of every year is hereby declared a
special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Carrascal, Province of Surigao del Sur, in
celebration of its annual town fiesta and the Pahinungod Festival.

SEC. 2. Pahinungod Festival. The Municipality of Carrascal shall recognize and promote
the Pahinungod Festival as an integral part of its annual town fiesta, particularly its cultural,
religious, historical, and community-building activities, consistent with applicable national and local
laws.

SEC. 3. Appropriation and Use of Local Funds. The Local Government Unit of Carrascal
shall, subject to the availability of funds and consistent with the approved local development and
investment programs and annual budget, appropriate funds or utilize legally available local
appropriations for the planning, organization, implementation, promotion, safety, cultural activities,
and other legitimate expenses related to the Pahinungod Festival. All expenditures shall comply
with existing budgeting, accounting, auditing, procurement, and other applicable laws, rules, and
regulations.

SEC. 4. Separabllity Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. Aji laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, regulations, and
other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 6. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation, whichever comes later.
