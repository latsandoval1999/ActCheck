                       AN ACT
FIXING THE TERM OF OFFICE OF BARANGAY AND
SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN OFFICIALS TO FIVE (5) YEARS
AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12232 OR “AN
ACT SETTING THE TERM OF OFFICE OF THE BARANGAY
OFFICIALS AND MEMBERS OF THE SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

The barangay, as the basic political unit of the Philippines, plays a vital role in
delivering essential government services, implementing community development
programs, and ensuring effective grassroots governance. As frontline public
servants, barangay officials require sufficient time to fully implement their
programs and address the evolving needs of their constituents.

The existing four-year (4) term of office of barangay officials may not always
provide adequate time to complete long-term projects and sustain development
initiatives. Extending the term of office of barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
officials to five (5) years will provide greater continuity, stability, and
effectiveness in local governance while maintaining democratic accountability
through regular elections.

To facilitate an orderly transition to a five-year electoral cycle, this measure seeks
to postpone the 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections to
November 2028 as a one-time transitional measure. This will allow the electoral
calendar to be aligned with the proposed five-year term of office and ensure
uninterrupted delivery of public services through a holdover arrangement for
incumbent officials until their successors are elected and qualified.

Recognizing the need to balance continuity of leadership with accountability and
leadership renewal, this measure likewise provides for nationwide consultations
among barangay officials, local government units, government agencies, and
other stakeholders to establish an appropriate framework on term limitations for
elective barangay officials.

Through this reform, the State strengthens grassroots governance by empowering
barangay leaders with sufficient time to pursue sustainable community
development while preserving the democratic principles of accountability,
participation, and responsive leadership.

                       AN ACT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Barangay Governance Reform
Act of 2026."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to strengthen
grassroots governance by providing barangay officials with sufficient time to effectively
implement development programs, ensure continuity in the delivery of basic services, and
promote long-term community development, while preserving democratic accountability
through periodic elections.

To achieve an orderly transition to a five-year electoral cycle, the 2026 Barangay and
Sangguniang Kabataan Elections shall be postponed as a one-time transitional measure.

SEC. 3. Amendment of Section 3 of Republic Act No. 12232. - Section 3 of Republic Act
No. 12232 is hereby amended to read as follows:

           "Section 3. Term of Office. – The term of office of all elected barangay
           officials shall be five (5) years, commencing at noon of the first day of
           December following their election.
            The term of office of Sangguniang Kabataan officials shall likewise be
            five (5) years, subject to the qualifications and limitations provided
            under Republic Act No. 10742, as amended.

            Congress shall, after conducting nationwide consultations with
            barangay officials and other stakeholders, enact the appropriate
            provisions governing the maximum number of consecutive terms that
            may be served by elective barangay officials, taking into consideration
            the principles of continuity in governance, democratic accountability,
            and leadership renewal."

SEC. 4. One-Time Postponement of the 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections. - Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, the Barangay and
Sangguniang Kabataan Elections scheduled for the first Monday of November 2026 are hereby
postponed to the first Monday of November 2028.

The incumbent barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan officials shall continue to serve in a
holdover capacity until their successors shall have been duly elected and qualified.

SEC. 5. Subsequent Elections. - Beginning in 2028, Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections shall be held every five (5) years on the first Monday of November.

SEC. 6. Nationwide Consultation. - Within one (1) year from the effectivity of this Act, the
House Committee on Local Government and the Senate Committee on Local Government shall
jointly conduct nationwide public consultations with barangay officials, local government
units, the Liga ng mga Barangay, the Department of the Interior and Local Government, the
Commission on Elections, civil society organizations, and other stakeholders for the purpose
of determining the appropriate term-limit framework for elective barangay officials.

The recommendations arising from such consultations shall serve as the basis for subsequent
legislation prescribing the maximum number of consecutive terms for elective barangay
officials.

SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, or parts
thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional, the
remaining provisions shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SEC. 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
