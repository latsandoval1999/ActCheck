                              AN ACT
MANDATING THE FREE USE OF GOVERNMENT-DONATED FARM MACHINERY
AND EQUIPMENT, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 18 OF REPUBLIC ACT
NO. 7607, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “MAGNA CARTA OF SMALL FARMERS”

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Filipino farmers play a vital role in ensuring the country's food security, yet many
continue to struggle with rising production costs and limited access to affordable farm
equipment. Although the government has invested billions of pesos in tractors and
other agricultural machinery to help increase farm productivity, many small farmers
are still required to pay service charges for using equipment that was purchased with
public funds. As a result, the very assistance intended to ease their burden often
remains beyond their inancial reach, leaving many farmers with little choice but to
rely on expensive private services or continue using labor-intensive methods.

This bill seeks to ensure that government assistance truly bene its the farmers it is
meant to serve. It requires that government-donated farm machinery and equipment
be made available to eligible small farmers without service charges, subject only to
the cost of fuel and the availability of the equipment. The measure covers the irst
three hectares cultivated by each eligible farmer and may be availed of twice each
year, allowing farmers to reduce production costs, cultivate more land, improve
productivity, and invest more in their crops instead of spending scarce resources on
machinery fees.

This measure reaf irms the State's commitment to supporting Filipino farmers and
strengthening the agricultural sector. By making government-provided farm

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Amendment of Section 18 of Republic Act No. 7607. — The second
paragraph of Section 18 of Republic Act No. 7607, otherwise known as the “Magna
Carta of Small Farmers”, is hereby amended to read as:

     “Sec. 18. Availability of Farm Machinery and Equipment.
     — XXX

     “The Government shall support the farmers in acquiring their inventory of farm
     equipment. With the use of grants-in-aid, as well as other domestic and foreign
     funds, the Government shall acquire and distribute to farmers’ organization
     farm equipment and machinery so as to increase their productive capabilities[.]:
     PROVIDED, THAT IN THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT OR DEED OF
     DONATION TO THE FARMER’S ORGANIZATION, THE DEPARTMENT OF
     AGRICULTURE SHALL REQUIRE THAT THE USE OF SUCH FARM MACHINERY
     AND EQUIPMENT FOR THE PRODUCTION OF RICE, CORN, AND OTHER
     GRAINS, INCLUDING LAND PREPARATION FOR THE DEVELOPMENT AND
     MAINTENANCE OR ORCHARDS AND OTHER HIGH-VALUE CROPS SHALL BE
     PROVIDED FREE, EXCEPT COST OF FUEL, FOR THE FIRST THREE (3)

                                                                           3|Page
     HECTARES OF FARMLAND TILLED BY EACH FARMER AND SHALL BE
     EXTENDED TWICE A YEAR. The funding requirement for this undertaking shall
     be included in the annual budget of the Department of Agriculture.”

SECTION 2. Funding. — The Department of Agriculture shall include in its annual
budget the amount necessary to support the implementation of this Act, including
expenses for monitoring, training, inspection, and, when authorized under the
General Appropriations Act, the repair and maintenance of government-donated farm
machinery and equipment.

The implementation of this Act shall be complementary and supplementary to, and
shall not replace, existing programs for agricultural mechanization and assistance.

SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Agriculture shall promulgate the
necessary rules and regulations to implement this Act.

SECTION 4. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force
and effect.

SECTION 5. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 6. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
