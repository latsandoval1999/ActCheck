EXPLANATORY NOTE

The international best practices for an effective deposit insurance system continue to evolve, and the
Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation (PDIC) must be equipped with the authorities necessary to meet
global standards in depositor protection.

The proposed amendments to the PDIC Charter include (i) the expansion of insurance coverage to financial
products offered by non-bank financial institutions deemed as deposits by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas
(BSP) and the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) as the respective supervising agencies; (ii) the
implementation of differential coverage that will provide insurance coverage higher than the Maximum
Deposit Insurance Coverage of P1 million per depositor for deposit accounts with significant social or
economic impact; (iii) the adoption of a unique identifier system, under which deposit-taking institutions
assign a distinct identifier to distinguish the account of a person or entity from all others within the system,
and the implementation of the Single Customer View framework to facilitate risk-based assessment, improve
the determination of the Deposit Insurance Fund target level, and enable the prompt settlement of deposit
insurance claims while safeguarding depositor confidentiality; (iv) the exclusive use of the Deposit
Insurance Fund (DIF), as a special fund, solely for the payment of deposit insurance claims and financial
assistance to distressed banks pursuant to international best practices; (v) temporary blanket coverage in
cases of systemic risk that threaten monetary and financial stability and prevent broader contagion, with a
mechanism allowing PDIC to recover the cost through a special assessment on covered institutions; (vi) the
enhancement of the liquidation process through the creation of a wholly-owned subsidiary to facilitate asset
disposal and accelerate recoveries for creditors of closed banks; and (vii) exemption from compensation
standardization laws to enable PDIC to maintain a compensation framework responsive to its specialized
mandate and operational requirements.

SEC. 28. Repealing Clause. - All acts or parts of acts and executive orders, administrative orders, or parts
thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.

SEC. 29. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following the completion of its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
