                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


The Meycauayan-Santa Maria Bypass Road, stretching approximately 4.53
kilometers within the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Bulacan, serves as
a vital transportation artery connecting key municipalities in eastern Bulacan to
major thoroughfares, including the North Luzon Expressway (NLEX).

As the Municipality of Santa Maria and surrounding areas continue to experience
rapid economic growth, industrial expansion, and population increases, local
traffic congestion has intensified. The bypass road was constructed to relieve
traffic along inner municipal roads, facilitate the seamless movement of goods
and agricultural products, and improve travel time for commuter and commercial
traffic across the province.

However, the maintenance, repair, and upgrade requirements of a heavy-traffic
arterial road pose a continuous financial burden on the local government unit
(LGU). Converting the Meycauayan-Santa Maria Bypass Road into a National
Road will transfer its maintenance, repair, and future improvements under the
supervision and funding of the Department of Public Works and Highways
(DPWH). This shift ensures that the road complies with national safety standards,
receives dedicated annual budget allocations, and continues to sustain regional
economic growth without overburdening local municipal funds.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT CONVERTING THE MEYCAUAYAN-SANTA MARIA BYPASS ROAD IN
 THE MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF BULACAN INTO A
      NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. The Meycauayan-Santa Maria Bypass Road, spanning four point
fifty-three (4.53) kilometers located in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province
of Bulacan, is hereby converted into a National Road under the jurisdiction and
supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department's program the improvement, maintenance, and repair of the
Meycauayan-Santa Maria Bypass Road, the funding of which shall be included
in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary
rules, regulations, and directives to implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved
