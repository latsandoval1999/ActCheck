                             EXPLANATORY NOTE
      The Land Transportation Office (LTO) is mandated to register motor
vehicles, issue and renew driver's licenses, and enforce land transportation
laws throughout the country. The accessibility of these services is a basic
expectation of local government units and their constituents, particularly in
provinces where distances between municipalities are considerable and public
transportation is limited.​
      At present, the entire Province of Nueva Vizcaya, comprising fifteen (15)
municipalities, is served by a single LTO District Office located at the Capitol
Compound, Municipality of Bayombong. Residents of the southern
municipalities of the province — particularly Bambang, Aritao, Kasibu, Kayapa,
Dupax Del Norte, Dupax Del Sur, and Sta. Fe — must travel a considerable
distance, averaging approximately twenty-nine (29) kilometers from Aritao
alone, just to transact with the LTO. This does not yet account for residents
from farther upland municipalities such as Kayapa, who must traverse
mountainous terrain to reach Bayombong.​
      The Municipality of Aritao is a second-class municipality with twenty-two
(22) barangays and a population of forty-five thousand (45,000) as of the 2024
Census, making it one of the most populous municipalities in the southern half
of the province. It sits along the Maharlika Highway (AH26) and serves as the
eastern terminus of the Benguet–Nueva Vizcaya Road connecting Nueva
Vizcaya to Baguio City, making it a natural transport and trade hub for the
neighboring municipalities of Kayapa, Santa Fe, Dupax del Sur, and Bambang,
as well as the boundary municipality of Carranglan in the neighboring province
of Nueva Ecija.
      The volume of vehicle registration, driver's license applications and
renewals, and other LTO transactions in this part of the province has
continued to grow alongside the area's population and economic activity,
particularly in agriculture and trade along the Maharlika Highway corridor. The
absence of a local LTO facility compels residents to shoulder additional
transportation costs and lost productive time, and contributes to congestion at
the Bayombong District Office. This inconvenience also creates conditions that
make residents vulnerable to fixers and unauthorized transaction facilitators.
      This bill seeks to establish an LTO Extension Office in the Municipality of
Aritao, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, to bring vehicle registration, licensing, and
related transportation services closer to the residents of Aritao and its
neighboring municipalities. This measure is consistent with the State's policy
of ensuring accessible, efficient, and citizen-centered delivery of public services,
particularly to communities located far from provincial and regional centers.​
      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

       AN ACT ESTABLISHING AN EXTENSION OFFICE OF THE LAND
  TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ARITAO,
PROVINCE OF NUEVA VIZCAYA, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:​
       SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “LTO Aritao
Extension Office Act.”
       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to promote a
comprehensive, efficient, and dependable transportation system that is
accessible to the public at reasonable cost, and to ensure the delivery of basic
government services to the citizenry with the least inconvenience, particularly
in geographically disadvantaged and underserved areas. Toward this end, the
State shall bring the services of the Land Transportation Office closer to
communities that are distant from existing district offices.
       SEC. 3. Establishment. There is hereby established, under the
supervision of the Department of Transportation (DOTr), through the Land
Transportation Office (LTO), an extension office of the LTO in the Municipality
of Aritao, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, hereinafter referred to as the “LTO Aritao
Extension Office.”
       SEC. 4. Coverage. The LTO Aritao Extension Office shall cater to the
transportation-related transactions of the residents of all barangays of the
Municipality of Aritao, and shall likewise serve the residents of the neighboring
municipalities of Kayapa, Santa Fe, Dupax del Sur, and Bambang, and other
nearby localities within the Province of Nueva Vizcaya, without prejudice to
their option to transact with any other LTO office.
       SEC. 5. Powers and Functions. The LTO Aritao Extension Office shall
exercise such powers and perform such functions as may be delegated to it by
the LTO Central Office and the LTO Regional Office having jurisdiction over the
Province of Nueva Vizcaya, including but not limited to the registration of
motor vehicles, the issuance and renewal of driver's licenses and permits, and
the collection of fees and charges incidental thereto, in accordance with
Republic Act No. 4136, otherwise known as the “Land Transportation and
Traffic Code,” as amended, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.
       SEC. 6. Personnel and Facilities. The LTO, in coordination with the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM), shall provide the LTO Aritao
Extension Office with adequate personnel, equipment, and facilities necessary
for its efficient operation, including the option to enter into a memorandum of
agreement with the Local Government Unit of Aritao for the use of a suitable
government-owned site or building, subject to existing government accounting
and auditing rules and regulations.
       SEC. 7. Implementation. The Secretary of Transportation shall
immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the
LTO Aritao Extension Office, and shall prioritize its establishment within one
(1) year from the effectivity of this Act.
       SEC. 8. Appropriations. The initial amount necessary for the
establishment and operationalization of the LTO Aritao Extension Office shall
be charged against the current year's appropriations of the DOTr and the LTO.
Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued operation and
maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).
       SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Transportation, through the
LTO, shall issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.
       SEC. 10. Separability Clause. If, for any reason, any provision of this Act
is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected
thereby shall remain in full force and effect.
       SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, rules and regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, which are
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.
       SEC. 12. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.​
