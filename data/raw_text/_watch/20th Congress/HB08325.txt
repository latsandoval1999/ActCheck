                           AN ACT
    MANDATING THE INSTALLATION OF FIREHOSE BOXES IN EVERY
   150SQM OF AREAS WITHIN BARANGAY STA. LUCIA OF DISTRICT V,
       QUEZON CITY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR


                             EXPLANATORY NOTE
       The State provides that it shall ensure public safety, promote economic
development through the prevention and suppression of all kinds of destructive
fires as well as enforce all laws, rules, and regulations to ensure adherence to
standard fire prevention and safety measures, and promote accountability in the
fire protection and prevention service.
      As such, the Fire Code of the Philippines, otherwise known as Republic
Act (RA) 9514, was enacted, as well as RA 11589, or the ‘Bureau of Fire
Protection Modernization Act”, to ensure that the public is protected from fire-
related incidents.
       In 2022, however, the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) reported over 13,000
fire incidents for the year, with instances notably high during the Christmas
season due to fires caused by fireworks and firecrackers.
       A consultation with the BFP National Capital Region Director and the
District V Fire Bureau officers revealed that many fire occurrences are not
immediately stopped because fire trucks frequently cannot fit in narrow alleys in
numerous densely inhabited areas of the Barangays. Meanwhile, these areas are
usually rather prone to fires due to the use of lightweight and easily combustible
building materials.
      To address the District firefighters’ predicament, this measure proposes
the establishment of firehose boxes within 150sqm distance across Barangay
Sta. Lucia, Quezon City. The firehose boxes in the barangay are a means of
providing a quicker response in the event of fire incidents, particularly in
neighborhoods with narrow alleys.
      The proposed measure not only intends to improve public safety and
disaster response at the barangay level but also to ultimately save precious lives.
     In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly
sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Fire-Proof Barangay
Sta. Lucia Act”.
SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to ensure public
safety, promote economic development through the prevention and suppression
of all kinds, of destructive fires, and promote the professionalization of the fire
service as a profession. Towards this end, the State shall enforce all laws, rules,
and regulations to ensure adherence to standard fire prevention and safety
measures and promote accountability in the fire protection and prevention
service.
SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. As used in this Act, the following words and
phrases shall be defined as follows:
   a. Firehose- a specialized high-pressure hose that carries water or other fire
      retardants (such as foam) to a fire to extinguish it and is normally attached
      to either a fire engine, fire hydrant, or portable fire pump;

   b. Firehose box- a box that shall contain a firehose and shall be installed at
      a conspicuous area within the barangays’ neighborhood;
SECTION 4. Establishment of Firehose Boxes in Barangay Sta. Lucia,
Quezon City. There shall be established a firehose box in every 150 square meter
area in Barangay Sta. Lucia, Quezon City, that shall be installed in conspicuous
areas such as walls of dwellings or concrete walls of buildings. These firehoses
shall serve as water pipelines for fire control and safety.
SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within six (6) months from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), in
coordination with the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) and other relevant agencies, and
with the participation of the Sangguniang Barangay of Sta. Lucia, Quezon City, shall
promulgate rules and regulations to carry out the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 6. Funding. The amount needed for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be charged against the current year’s available appropriation of DILG.
Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation of
this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
SECTION 7. Separability Clause. If any portion of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the portions or provisions that are not affected shall
continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations or parts thereof which are contrary to, or inconsistent with, this Act
are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
SECTION 9. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) national newspapers of
general circulation.
