20                                                EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   The 1987 Constitution enshrines a myriad of policies that guide the State in promoting
23   the welfare and development of the Filipino people. Among these are the duties of the
24   State to inculcate in the youth patriotism and nationalism, encourage their involvement
25   in public and civic affairs, foster appreciation of the nation's history and cultural heritage,
26   preserve and enrich the Filipino national culture, and promote the nation’s historical and
27   cultural heritage and resources as well as artistic creations.
28
29   Consistent with these constitutional mandates, educational institutions have long
30   utilized off-campus educational activities as an important complement to classroom
31   instruction.
32
33   However, recent accidents and the tragic loss of young and promising lives during off-
34   campus non-curricular activities remind us that no educational opportunity should come
35   at the cost of a learner's life. These unfortunate incidents have exposed the limitations
36   of existing guidelines, which remain largely administrative in nature, and clearly show
37   the lack of a comprehensive, uniform, and enforceable framework for risk assessment,
38   supervision, and accountability applicable to all off-campus educational activities.
39
40   This proposed measure seeks to establish a risk-based framework governing off-
41   campus educational activities by public and private educational institutions. It
42   introduces the need for accreditation for service or venue providers, mandatory risk
43   assessments, minimum supervisor-to-learner ratios, comprehensive parental
44   disclosure requirements, weather-related restrictions, and the imposition of criminal
45   liability for supervisors and other heavier penalties for responsible entities who
46   disregard learner safety.
47
48   Through these safeguards, the bill seeks to strike a balance between promoting
49   meaningful off-classroom learning and ensuring that every reasonable precaution is
50   taken to protect the life and welfare of learners.
1   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

21                                AN ACT
22      PROVIDING FOR THE REGULATION AND SAFETY STANDARDS OF LOCAL
23        OFF-CAMPUS EDUCATIONAL ACTIVITIES OF PUBLIC AND PRIVATE
24           EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
25
26   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
27   Congress assembled:
28
29   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Local Off-Campus Activities
30   Safety Act”.
31
32   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is declared the policy of the State to establish,
33   maintain and support a complete, adequate, and integrated system of education
34   relevant to the needs of the people. It is likewise the policy of the State to protect, foster
35   and promote the right of all citizens to affordable quality education at all levels.
36   Consistent with these policies, the State recognizes the authority of basic and higher
37   education institutions to adopt learning delivery strategies that integrate relevant off-
38   campus educational activities with regular classroom instructional programs to broaden
39   and supplement the learning opportunity.

40   Learning outside the confines of the classroom diminishes the security provided by its
41   walls and subjects learners to safety concerns inherent in an exposed learning
42   environment. The State shall therefore prescribe the framework within which these off-
43   campus educational activities may be undertaken to ensure a more meaningful learning
44   experience in an ecosystem that advances the utmost safety and well-being of learners.
45
46   SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. - As used in this Act:
 1           (a) Curricular activities - off-campus educational activities that are an integral
 2   part of the instructional program.

 3          (b) Institution - any public or private school offering basic education program or
 4   higher or technical education;

 5           (c) Non-curricular, co-curricular or extra-curricular activities - local off-campus
 6   activities that may or may not be anchored on standard curriculum and are considered
 7   non-program based activities but are deemed relevant and desirable to broaden the
 8   opportunity for learning;

 9         (d) Off-campus educational activities - curricular, non-curricular, co-curricular,
10   and extra-curricular activities undertaken outside the premises of the institution; and

11          (e) Service providers - educational tour and/or transport operators offering their
12   services for a consideration.
13
14   SEC. 4. Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all local off-campus educational activities,
15   whether curricular, non-curricular, co-curricular, or extra-curricular, undertaken by
16   public and private institutions under the supervision of the Department of Education
17   (DepEd), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), and the Technical Education
18   and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), including technical-vocational education
19   and training (TVET) programs.
20
21   SEC. 5. Conditions for Local Off-Campus Activities. - No off-campus non-curricular,
22   co-curricular, and extra-curricular educational activities shall be undertaken unless they
23   are:

24          (a) relevant and aligned with the educational competencies of the curriculum;

25          or

26          (b) necessary to the acquisition of relevant knowledge, skills, and values of the
27   learners.

28           Participation in any off-campus non-curricular, co-curricular or extra-curricular
29   activities shall be voluntary and in no case shall the inability to participate in the off-
30   campus activity constitute a failure to complete any requirement or in any way put the
31   learner at a disadvantage.
32
33   SEC. 6. Classifications of Off-Campus Educational Activities. - For purposes of
34   ensuring learner safety and proportional regulatory requirements, all off-campus
35   educational activities shall be classified accordingly.

36   DepEd, CHED, and TESDA shall prescribe detailed standards for each risk category in
37   their respective implementing rules and regulations, subject to the following minimum
38   classifications:
 1          (a.)   Category I – Low-Risk Activities. - These refer to activities conducted
 2          within a fifty-kilometer (50-km) radius from the educational institution and
 3          undertaken in controlled or supervised environments involving minimal
 4          foreseeable risk, including but not limited to:

 5                 i.     Visits to museums, science centers, libraries, cultural centers,
 6                 exhibitions, and similar educational facilities;

 7                 ii.    Historical and cultural site visits;

 8                 iii.   Visits to government institutions; and

 9                 iv.  Other activities determined by DepEd, CHED, or TESDA to involve
10                 minimal risk.

11   Category I activities shall not involve overnight stays.

12          (b.)   Category II – Moderate-Risk Activities. - These refer to activities
13          involving extended travel, overnight stay, large gatherings, fieldwork, immersion,
14          or other circumstances requiring enhanced supervision, including but not limited
15          to:

16                 i.    Any educational tours under Category I conducted beyond a fifty-
17                 kilometer (50-km) radius from the educational institution;

18                 ii.    Inter-provincial educational activities;

19                 iii.   Overnight camps, leadership programs, and similar activities;

20                 iv.    Academic competitions requiring travel;

21                 v.     Industry immersion visits;

22                 vi.   Research, fieldwork, and similar activities conducted outside
23                 school premises; and

24                 vii. Other activities determined by DepEd, CHED, or TESDA to involve
25                 moderate risk.

26   The foregoing activities shall be classified as Category II regardless of distance
27   whenever the nature, duration, or circumstances of the activity require enhanced
28   supervision.

29          (c.)    Category III – High-Risk Activities. - These refer to activities involving
30          significant physical, environmental, transportation, operational, or safety
31          hazards, including but not limited to:

32                 i.     Mountain climbing, trekking, hiking, and wilderness expeditions;
 1                 ii.    Water-based activities;

 2                 iii.     Activities involving heights, specialized equipment, adventure
 3                 facilities, or comparable attractions;

 4                 iv.    Visits to industrial plants, construction sites, mines, laboratories,
 5                 ports, airports, power generation facilities, or other hazardous workplaces
 6                 or operational environments;

 7                 v.    Activities conducted in geographically isolated, remote, disaster-
 8                 prone, or otherwise high-risk areas; and

 9                 vi.   Other activities determined by DepEd, CHED, or TESDA to involve
10                 substantial risk.

11   Any off-campus educational activity shall be classified as a Category III activity
12   whenever any component, segment, portion, or reasonably foreseeable incident of the
13   itinerary involves a Category III activity, regardless of the distance to the destination,
14   duration of the activity, or whether such activity constitutes the principal purpose of the
15   program.
16
17   SEC. 7. Minimum Requirements for Off-Campus Educational Activities. - No off-
18   campus educational activity shall be conducted unless the following minimum
19   requirements are complied with:

20          (a.)   Prior approval from the educational institution in accordance with the
21          requirements prescribed by DepEd, CHED, or TESDA;

22          (b.)    Prior written informed consent from the parent or legal guardian of the
23          participating learner, regardless of age;

24          (c.)   Disclosure to the parent or legal guardian of the objectives, itinerary,
25          destinations, transportation arrangements, accommodations, planned activities,
26          and any reasonably foreseeable incidental or ancillary activities;

27          (d.)   Preparation and disclosure to the parent or legal guardian, prior to
28          obtaining their consent, of a safety and security plan, including emergency
29          response procedures, communication protocols, and emergency contact
30          information;

31          (e.)  Supervision by qualified school personnel or other authorized persons
32          designated by the educational institution throughout the duration of the activity;

33          (f.)  Insurance coverage for participating learners, in accordance with
34          standards prescribed by DepEd, CHED, or TESDA; and

35          (g.)  Engagement only of duly accredited or authorized service providers,
36          where transportation, accommodation, tour, or other services are outsourced.
 1   SEC. 8. Additional Requirements for Category II and Category III Activities. - In
 2   addition to the requirements applicable to all off-campus educational activities,
 3   Category II and Category III activities shall be subject to the following:
 4          (a.)    Risk Assessment. - Prior to approval, the educational institution shall
 5          conduct a risk assessment of the proposed activity. For this purpose, the
 6          educational institution shall constitute a Risk Assessment Team which shall
 7          conduct such inspections, validations, and evaluations as may be necessary to
 8          determine the safety and suitability of the activity. The composition,
 9          qualifications, functions, and procedures of the Risk Assessment Team shall be
10          prescribed by DepEd, CHED, or TESDA.

11         (b.)  Medical Screening. - Participating learners shall undergo such medical
12         screening or health assessment to determine suitability to participate in the off-
13         campus educational activities.

14         (c.)  Pre-Activity Orientation. - The educational institution shall conduct a
15         mandatory orientation for participating learners at least 2 days before the
16         scheduled departure. Such orientation shall include the itinerary, safety
17         protocols, emergency procedures, and other matters necessary to ensure
18         informed participation.

19         (d.)    Specialized Personnel. - Category III activities shall be accompanied by
20         qualified personnel possessing certifications, training, licenses, or experience
21         appropriate to the nature of the activity and the risks involved, in accordance with
22         standards prescribed by DepEd, CHED, or TESDA.
23
24   SEC. 9. Supervision Requirements. - Off-campus educational activities shall be
25   supervised by qualified school personnel or other authorized persons designated by the
26   educational institution.

27   The minimum ratio of supervisors to participating learners shall be as follows:

28         (a.) Category I Activities — one (1) supervisor for every twenty-five (25)
29         learners or fraction thereof;

30         (b.) Category II Activities — one (1) supervisor for every fifteen (15) learners
31         or fraction thereof; and

32         (c.)   Category III Activities — one (1) supervisor for every ten (10) learners or
33         fraction thereof.

34   DepEd, CHED, and TESDA may prescribe stricter supervision requirements based on
35   the age of the learners, duration of the activity, number of participants, presence of
36   learners with disabilities or special needs, nature of the activity, and level of risk
37   involved.
 1   The experience, training, competence, age, academic standing, or prior participation of
 2   the learners shall not be a ground for reducing the minimum supervision requirements
 3   prescribed under this Act.

 4   SEC. 10. Duties of Supervisors. - Every supervisor designated by the educational
 5   institution for an off-campus educational activity shall:

 6          (a.) Exercise reasonable care and supervision over learners assigned under
 7          their case throughout the duration of the activity;

 8          (b.) Ensure compliance with the approved safety and security plan and
 9          emergency response protocols;

10          (c.)  Ensure that learners participate only in activities authorized under the
11          approved program and for which the necessary consent have been obtained;

12          (d.) Immediately report any accident or injury to the educational institution and
13          other appropriate authorities; and

14          (e.) Perform such other duties as may be prescribed by DepEd, CHED, or
15          TESDA.
16
17   SEC. 11. Weather-Related Suspension of Activities. - Educational institutions shall
18   monitor official weather forecasts, advisories, warnings, and bulletins issued by
19   PAGASA.

20          (a.) Category I activities may proceed during a Yellow Rainfall Warning,
21          provided that all destinations, routes, and transit points remain under a Yellow
22          Rainfall Warning or lower advisory and the activity may be conducted safely.

23          (b.) Category II and Category III activities shall not commence and shall be
24          suspended upon the issuance of any rainfall warning, Tropical Cyclone Wind
25          Signal, Gale Warning, or other weather advisory indicating unsafe conditions.

26   DepEd, CHED, and TESDA shall prescribe the applicable weather thresholds,
27   restrictions, and suspension protocols for each category of activity.
28
29   SEC. 12. Accreditation of Venues and Destinations. - The DepEd, CHED, and
30   TESDA, in consultation with the Department of Tourism, National Museum of the
31   Philippines, National Historical Commission of the Philippines, National Commission for
32   Culture and the Arts, the local government units (LGUs) and other relevant government
33   agencies, shall separately accredit the venues, destinations, and activities where the
34   educational institutions under their respective jurisdictions may conduct, hold, or
35   participate in off-campus non-curricular, co-curricular or extra-curricular activities. In
36   accrediting off-campus venues, destinations and activities, the respective agencies
37   shall consider the following:
 1      (a.) Suitability of the venue, place or activity in relation to the program requirement
 2      and learning objectives;

 3      (b.) Carrying capacity of the venue or place to ensure the accommodation of
 4      learners, optimize the learning experience, and minimize adverse social and
 5      environmental impact;

 6      (c.) Sufficiency of amenities and other facilities for the comfort and convenience of
 7      the learners; and

 8      (d.) Adequacy of protective protocols to ensure the security and safety of the
 9      learners.

10   The list of accredited venues and destinations shall be published in the website of the
11   DepEd, CHED, and TESDA, and shall be updated as the DepEd, CHED, and TESDA
12   consider necessary and appropriate.

13   Only venues, destinations, and activities accredited pursuant to this section may be
14   considered by educational institutions in planning for and in implementing off-campus
15   non-curricular, co-curricular or extra-curricular educational activities.
16
17   SEC. 13. Outsourcing of Services. - The conduct of off-campus educational activities
18   shall primarily utilize the resources and transport equipment of the educational
19   institutions concerned. In case of absence or inadequacy of such resources and
20   equipment, educational institutions may outsource their needs from accredited service
21   providers: Provided, That the overall responsibility for the off-campus activity shall
22   remain with the educational institution: Provided, further, That the service provider shall
23   not be relieved of any responsibility in case of failure to exercise the degree of care and
24   vigilance required under the circumstances: Provided, finally, That in no case shall the
25   off-campus activity be left to the control and management of the service provider.

26   The DepEd, CHED, and TESDA shall, in consultation with the Department of Tourism,
27   Department of Transportation, Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board,
28   and other relevant government agencies, accredit service providers as defined under
29   Section 3(e) of this Act under such terms and conditions as they shall separately
30   prescribe pursuant to this section, including the following:

31          (a.) Technical and financial capability;

32          (b.) Experience and track record; and

33          (c.) Road, sea or airworthiness of transport equipment; Provided, That in case of
34   land transportation, the vehicle shall not be more ten (10) years old from date of
35   manufacture.

36   No educational institution shall engage a service provider unless the same is duly
37   accredited by the DepEd, CHED, or TESDA, as the case may be.
 1   The expenses to be incurred in the conduct of off-campus educational activities shall
 2   primarily be borne by the learners, subject to consultation with the parents or guardians:
 3   Provided, That assistance from LGUs, institutional sponsors, and other well-meaning
 4   benefactors may be solicited and accepted by the educational institutions.
 5
 6   SEC. 14. Responsibility and Liability of Educational Institutions. - The educational
 7   institution shall have primary responsibility for the safety, security, health, and welfare
 8   of participating learners. The engagement of service/venue providers shall not relieve
 9   the educational institution or its responsible officials and personnel of liability under this
10   Act, without prejudice to their right to seek reimbursement, indemnification, or other
11   remedies against the service provider or any person responsible for the loss, damage,
12   injury, or death.
13
14   SEC. 15. Prohibited Acts. - The following acts are prohibited:

15          (a.) Conducting or authorizing an off-campus educational activity without the
16          approvals required under this Act;

17          (b.) Knowingly falsifying, concealing, altering, or misrepresenting any risk
18          assessment, safety plan, weather advisory, medical clearance, permit,
19          accreditation, or other document required under this Act;

20          (c.)   Knowingly failing to disclose a material activity, destination, hazard, or risk
21          required to be disclosed;

22          (d.) Knowingly permitting, continuing, or resuming an off-campus educational
23          activity despite the existence of conditions requiring its suspension;

24          (e.) Knowingly classifying an activity under a lower category, or otherwise
25          misrepresenting the nature of an activity;

26          (f.)   Conducting an activity without the minimum supervision requirements;

27          (g.) Knowingly engaging or utilizing a service provider, venue, transport
28          provider, facilitator, guide, contractor, or other person lacking the accreditation
29          or qualifications;

30          (h.) Knowingly permitting, allowing, inducing, coercing, or facilitating the
31          participation of a learner without the written informed consent required, or after
32          such consent has been expressly withheld, denied, or withdrawn;

33          (i.)   Obstructing, interfering with, or refusing lawful inspection, investigation,
34          or enforcement actions conducted; and

35          (j.)  Retaliating against any learner, parent, guardian, employee, or other
36          person for reporting violations of this Act or cooperating in an investigation.
37
 1   SEC. 16. Penalties. - Any educational institution or responsible person found to have
 2   committed any of the acts under Section 15 shall be subject to the following penalties:
 3         (a.) For the first offense, a fine of not less than One Million Pesos
 4              (₱1,000,000.00) but not more than Two Million Pesos (₱2,000,000.00) and
 5              suspension of authority, accreditation, or eligibility to conduct, organize,
 6              facilitate, or participate in off-campus educational activities for a period of
 7              1 year;
 8         (b.) For the second offense, a fine of not less than Two Million Pesos
 9              (₱2,000,000.00) but not more than Three Million Pesos (₱3,000,000.00),
10              and suspension of authority, accreditation, or eligibility to conduct,
11              organize, facilitate, or participate in off-campus educational activities for a
12              period of 2 years; and
13         (c.) For the third and subsequent offenses, a fine of not less than Three Million
14              Pesos (₱3,000,000.00), and revocation of accreditation.

15   Where the offender is a public educational institution, the penalties shall be imposed
16   upon the school head, principal, president, dean, faculty adviser, organizer, supervisor,
17   or other official directly responsible for the violation.

18   This shall be without prejudice to other liabilities under other applicable laws, rules, and
19   regulations.

20   No waiver of action or liability executed by the learners, their parents or guardians prior
21   to the activity shall operate to exonerate any educational institution, service provider, or
22   their officials and employees from a cause of action arising from the conduct of off-
23   campus activity.
24
25   SEC. 17. Offenses by Supervisors and Specialized Personnel. - Any supervisor or
26   other specialized personnel designated pursuant to this Act who knowingly:

27          (a.)   fails to exercise the supervision and care required under this Act;

28          (b.) fails to comply with the approved safety and security plan or emergency
29          response protocols;

30          (c.)   permits the conduct or continuation of an activity despite the existence of
31          conditions requiring its suspension, postponement, modification, rerouting, or
32          termination;

33          (d.) permits participation in an activity beyond the scope of the learner's
34          parental consent, medical clearance, or authorized participation; or

35          (e.) deliberately disregards a mandatory safety requirement prescribed under
36          this Act, shall suffer the penalty of prisión correccional in its minimum and
37          medium periods.
38
39   SEC. 18. Liability of Service Providers and Venue Operators During Dangerous
40   Weather Conditions. - Any service provider, venue operator, transport provider, tour
41   guide, facilitator, or other person exercising control or supervision over an off-campus
 1   educational activity who knowingly allows the activity to proceed despite adverse
 2   weather conditions shall be liable.

 3   For the first offense, the offender shall be fined Five Hundred Thousand Pesos
 4   (₱500,000.00);

 5   For the second offense, the offender shall be fined One Million Pesos (₱1,000,000.00)
 6   and shall suffer suspension of accreditation, permit, license, or authority to operate for
 7   a period of not less than one (1) year; and

 8   For the third and subsequent offenses, the offender shall be fined Two Million Pesos
 9   (₱2,000,000.00) and shall suffer revocation of accreditation, permit, license, or authority
10   to operate.

11   The foregoing penalties shall apply regardless of whether injury, damage, or loss has
12   occurred and shall be without prejudice to any civil, criminal, or administrative liability
13   under other laws. Liability shall not be negated by the existence of protective equipment
14   or similar protocols.
15
16   SEC. 19. Liability for School-Related Activities. - Where an activity is participated in
17   primarily by learners of the same educational institution, or is represented, promoted,
18   organized, or reasonably perceived as being connected with a school event, sportsfest,
19   competition, celebration, party, retreat, training program, recognition activity, or similar
20   undertaking, but is not formally authorized by the educational institution, the facilitator,
21   organizer, coordinator, or person who initiated, promoted, managed, or exercised
22   control over the activity shall be deemed the principal responsible person for purposes
23   of liability under this Act.
24
25   Service providers, venue operators, transport providers, guides, contractors, and other
26   persons exercising control or supervision over the activity shall likewise be liable for
27   violations of this Act.
28
29   An educational institution may likewise be held liable where it knew or reasonably
30   should have known that such activity was being planned, organized, promoted, or
31   conducted by its learners, employees, or representatives, and failed to take reasonable
32   measures to prevent the activity, disassociate itself therefrom, or comply with the duties
33   and obligations imposed under this Act.
34
35   The absence of formal authorization from the educational institution shall not exempt
36   any person from liability under this Act.
37
38   SEC. 20. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DepEd, CHED, and TESDA
39   shall, in consultation and coordination with appropriate national government agencies,
40   LGUs, and other stakeholders, promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
41   effective implementation of this Act, within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.
