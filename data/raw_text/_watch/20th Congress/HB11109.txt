                           AN ACT
  MANDATING THE PROVISION OF EXCLUSIVE PARKING SPACE FOR
 PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES AND PENALIZING THE UNAUTHORIZED
USE THEREOF, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE BATAS PAMBANSA BLG.
      344, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE ACCESSIBILITY LAW

                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The State recognizes persons with disabilities and their remarkable
contributions to our development as a nation. In line with this, the country
has enacted Batas Pambansa (B.P.) Blg. 344, otherwise known as the
“Accessibility Law” in 1983. This piece of legislation has initially facilitated the
inclusion of persons with disabilities into the mainstream of Philippine
society.

      However, after almost 40 years of its enactment, it has been observed
that the Accessibility Law does not fully cater to the accessibility needs of
persons with disabilities. For one, B.P. 344 does not provide for sanctions for
the unauthorized use and obstruction of parking spaces reserved for persons
with disabilities. As such, these parking spaces are frequently used by
persons without disabilities, thereby preventing persons with disabilities from
being able to access essential services.

      Similarly, Republic Act No. 7277, or the Magna Carta for Disabled
Persons, simply states, in Section 25 thereof, that the State “shall ensure the
attainment of a barrier-free environment for disabled persons in accordance
with the Accessibility Law.”

      With the absence of clear penalties for the unauthorized use and
obstruction of such designated spaces, persons with disabilities are further
robbed of opportunities and spaces that ought to be for them. Thus, there
remains a need to improve our existing laws for the welfare of Filipino persons
with disabilities.
       This bill seeks to address this limitation of the Accessibility Law by
mandating the provision of exclusive parking for persons with disabilities,
defining the prohibited acts, and providing the penalties in cases of violation.
It is the hope of this measure to strengthen the Accessibility Law and
complement existing laws that protect and uphold the rights of persons with
disabilities to attain an inclusive and barrier-free environment

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
  MANDATING THE PROVISION OF EXCLUSIVE PARKING SPACE FOR
 PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES AND PENALIZING THE UNAUTHORIZED
USE THEREOF, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE BATAS PAMBANSA BLG.
      344, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE ACCESSIBILITY LAW

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Exclusive Parking
Space for Persons with Disabilities Act.”


SECTION 2. Section 1 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 is hereby amended to read
as follows:
               “SECTION 1. In order to promote the realization of the rights
       of [disabled] persons WITH DISABILITY to participate fully in the
       social life and the development of the societies in which they live
       and the enjoyment of the opportunities available to other citizens,
       no license or permit for the construction, repair, or renovation of
       public and private buildings for public use. Educational
       institutions, airports, sports, and recreation centers and
       complexes, shopping centers or establishments, public parking
       places, work-places, public utilities, shall be granted or issued
       unless the owner or operator thereof shall install and incorporate
       in such building, establishment, institution or public utility, such
       architectural facilities or structural features as shall reasonable
       enhance the mobility of [disabled] persons WITH DISABILITY
       such as sidewalks, ramps, railings, and the like. If feasible, all
       such existing buildings, institutions, establishments, or public
       utilities, may be renovated or altered to enable the [disabled]
       persons WITH DISABILITY to have access to them: Provided,
       however, That buildings, institutions, establishments, or public
       utilities to be constructed or established for which licenses or
       permits had already been issued may comply with the
     requirements of this law: Provided, further, That in case of
     government buildings, street and highways, the [Ministry]
     SECRETARY of Public Works and Highways shall see to it that
     the same shall be provided with architectural facilities or
     structural features for [disabled] persons WITH DISABILITY.
            In the case of the parking place of any of the above
     institutions, buildings, or establishments, or public utilities, the
     owner or operator shall reserve sufficient and suitable space for
     the EXCLUSIVE use of [disabled] persons WITH DISABILITY.”

SECTION 3. A new Section 2A is hereby added to Batas Pambansa Blg. 344,
to read as follows:

           “SEC. 2A. PROHIBITION ON PARKING. – IT IS UNLAWFUL:

           a) FOR ANY PERSON WITHOUT DISABILITY TO USE,
              BLOCK OR OTHERWISE OBSTRUCT THE PARKING
              SPACES IN ANY GOVERNMENT AND PRIVATE
              ESTABLISHMENTS, BUILDINGS AND STRUCTURES
              EXCLUSIVELY RESERVED FOR PERSONS WITH
              DISABILITY.

           b) FOR ANY PERSON IN THE EMPLOY OF GOVERNMENT
              ESTABLISHMENTS, BUILDINGS AND STRUCTURES
              TO TOLERATE OR ALLOW THE USE OF THESE
              RESERVED PARKING SPACES BY PERSONS WITHOUT
              DISABILITY.

          ANY VEHICLE OPERATOR WHO IS WITHOUT DISABILITY
     SHALL NOT USE THE RESERVED EXCLUSIVE PARKING SPACES
     FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITY UNLESS A PERSON WITH
     DISABILITY IS A PASSENGER OF THE VEHICLE.”

SECTION 4. Section 4 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 is hereby amended to read
as follows:

           [SEC. 4. Any person violating any provision of this Act or of
     the rules and regulations promulgated hereunder shall, upon
     conviction by a court of competent jurisdiction, suffer the penalty
     of imprisonment of not less than one month but not more than
     one year or a fine of two thousand pesos (Php2,000.00) to five
     thousand pesos (Php5,000.00) or both, at the discretion of the
     court: Provided, That in the case of corporations, partnerships,
     cooperatives or associations, the president, manager or
     administrator, or the person who has charge of the construction,
     repair or renovation of the building, space or utilities shall be
     criminally responsible for any violation of this Act and/or rules
     and regulations promulgated pursuant thereto.]
     SEC. 4. PENALTY CLAUSE. –
             (1) ANY PERSON WHO VIOLATES ANY OF THE
                 PROVISIONS OF THIS ACT SHALL PAY A FINE OF NOT
                 LESS THAN TEN THOUSAND PESOS (PHP10,000.00)
                 BUT NOT MORE THAN FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS
                 (PHP50,000.00).

             (2) ANY ESTABLISHMENT COVERED UNDER SECTION 3
                 OF THIS ACT THAT FAILS TO PROVIDE THE
                 REQUIRED PARKING SPACE FOR PERSONS WITH
                 DISABILITIES SHALL PAY A FINE OF NOT LESS THAN
                 FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS (PHP50,000.00) BUT NOT
                 MORE THAN ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (PHP
                 100,000.00)

SECTION 5. The title of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 is hereby amended to read
as “An Act to Enhance the Mobility of Persons with Disability by Requiring
Certain Buildings, Institutions, Establishments, and Public Utilities to Install
Facilities and Other Devices,” and all references in the said law to “disabled
persons” shall likewise be amended to read as “persons with disability.”

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Secretary of
Public Works and Highways and the Secretary of Transportation, in
coordination with the Executive Director of the National Council on Disability
Affairs, shall promulgate and issue the implementing rules and regulations
within sixty (60) days upon approval of this Act.

SECTION 7. Non-Impairment Clause. – Nothing in this Act shall be
construed as to diminish, impair, or repeal rights recognized, granted, or
made available to disadvantaged, marginalized, or specific sectors under
existing laws.

SECTION 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees, presidential proclamations, letters of instruction, rules and
regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect within fifteen (15) days
following its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of
general circulation in the Philippines.
