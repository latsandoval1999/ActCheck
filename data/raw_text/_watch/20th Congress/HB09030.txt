              AN ACT AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9513,
       OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE RENEWABLE ENERGY ACT OF 2008

                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE:


The Philippines continues to face persistent energy challenges marked by high electricity
costs, supply tightness during peak demand periods, exposure to volatile global fuel prices,
and increasing dependence on imported fossil fuels. The depletion of indigenous energy
resources, coupled with growing demand driven by economic expansion, has further strained
the country‟s ability to produce a stable, affordable, and secure energy supply. These
conditions underscore the urgency of accelerating the development of reliable, indigenous,
and sustainable energy sources.

Republic Act No. 9513, or the Renewable Energy Act of 2008, was enacted to address these
concerns by promoting the development and utilization of renewable energy resources. While
the law has facilitated the entry of renewable energy projects, structural gaps and evolving
market conditions have limited its full potential to respond to the present energy crisis. There
is therefore a need to strengthen the legal and regulatory framework to unlock investments,
improve system reliability, and reduce long-term power costs.

This bill seeks to amend the Renewable Energy Act to directly address these challenges.

   1. Update on key definitions to recognize emerging technologies such as energy storage
      systems and their integration with renewable energy facilities. This is critical in
      addressing intermittency issues associated with renewable energy and ensuring a more
      stable and reliable power supply, particularly during peak demand periods.
   2. This measure strengthens the authority of the Department of Energy as the lead
      agency and streamlines access to land for renewable energy development. By
      reducing administrative barriers and clarifying access rights, the bill facilitates faster
      project development timelines, which is essential in addressing immediate supply
      constraints.
   3. This bill modernizes the Feed-in-Tariff system by introducing competitive auction
      mechanisms. This reform promotes efficiency and price discovery, ensuring that
      renewable energy projects are developed at least cost, thereby contributing to the
      reduction of electricity prices for consumers over time.
   4. Enhances the Renewable Energy Market framework and strengthens the issuance of
      Renewable Energy Certificates.
   5. Improves the fiscal regime for renewable energy projects by rationalizing incentives,
      including duty-free importation and real property tax assessment levels. These
      measures are intended to attract more investments, accelerate capacity additions, and
      help address supply shortages that contribute to high power costs.
   6. Provides clarity and predictability in the government share from renewable energy
      projects, thereby enhancing investor confidence while ensuring that the State
      continues to benefit from the development of its energy resources.
   7. Removes legal uncertainties by clarifying that renewable energy resources are not
      subject to constitutional restrictions applicable to natural resources, including
      nationality requirements and term limits. This is expected to unlock significant
      foreign and domestic capital, which is critical in scaling up investments needed to
      address the country‟s energy supply gap.

In sum, the proposed amendments are responsive to the country‟s current energy crisis. By
accelerating renewable energy development, improving system reliability, reducing
dependence on imported fuels, and promoting competitive electricity pricing, the bill
provides a comprehensive policy response to ensure long-term energy security and
sustainability.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

SECTION 1. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to further
encourage private sector participation in the exploration, development and utilization of
renewable energy resources to achieve energy security and self-reliance in a manner that
balances the goals of economic growth and development with the protection of health and the
environment.
