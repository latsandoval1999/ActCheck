EXPLANATORY NOTE

The City of Naga, Province of Cebu, celebrates its city fiesta every 10" of
October giving honor to its patron saint, St Francis of Assisi. This celebration
is deeply rooted in tradition, faith and community spirit.

Declaring the date of the city fiesta a special non-working holiday
will allow greater unity and participation among the constituents. A blended
celebration to honor its socio-cultural, historical and religious traditions. Naga
considered as one of the Cebu’s most livable city couple with increasing of
various industrial developments and tourism is a huge attraction to both local
and international visitors.

In view of the foregoing, passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

REP. RH GULLAS

AN ACT
DECLARING OCTOBER 10 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF NAGA, PROVINCE OF CEBU, IN CELEBRATION
OF ITS ANNUAL CITY FIESTA

Be enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. October 10 of every year, is hereby declared a special
non-working holiday in the City of Naga, Province of Cebu, in celebration of its
annual city fiesta in honor of St Francis of Assisi, it’s patron saint.

SEC. 2. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulations in
the Philippines.

Approved.
