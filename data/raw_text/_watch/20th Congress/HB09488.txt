                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

        More than three (3) decades after the enactment of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise
known as the “Local Government Code of 1991,” local government units (LGUs) continue
to bear primary responsibility for delivering essential public services and implementing local
development programs. The Mandanas-Garcia ruling affirmed that LGUs are entitled to their
just share in all national taxes, thereby increasing the overall National Tax Allotment (NTA).
However, it did not fully address the structural inequities embedded in the distribution of
national transfers.

        Municipalities remain particularly disadvantaged. Despite serving around sixty
percent (60%) of the country’s population and delivering critical services such as health care,
agriculture, local infrastructure, disaster response, social welfare, environmental protection,
and community development, municipalities receive only thirty-four percent (34%) of the
NTA. In 2023, cities generated and spent approximately Ten Thousand Nine Hundred Sixty-
Six Pesos (P10,966.00) per capita on average, compared to only Three Thousand One
Hundred Eighty-Six Pesos (P3,186.00) per capita for municipalities, reflecting a substantial
disparity in fiscal capacity.

        Fiscal inequality likewise exists within LGU categories. Among cities, the proportion
with Annual Regular Income per capita below the national average increased from forty-five
and sixty-five hundredths percent (45.65%) in 2015 to fifty-nine and seventy-three
hundredths percent (59.73%) in 2024, indicating that revenue growth has become
increasingly concentrated among a smaller number of fiscally stronger cities. Similar
disparities are evident among provinces and other LGUs.

        This bill seeks to strengthen local fiscal autonomy by granting LGUs an additional
five percent (5%) share in all national taxes, to be phased in at one percentage point (1%)
annually. Recognizing the fiscal constraints faced by municipalities, seventy percent (70%)
of the additional allocation shall be distributed to municipalities, while ten percent (10%)
each shall accrue to provinces, cities, and barangays.
        The preference for municipalities is based on substantial distinctions. Municipalities
generally have narrower revenue bases, fewer opportunities to capture economic growth,
more limited administrative capacity, and significant responsibility for frontline services,
especially in geographically dispersed and less urbanized communities. The measure
therefore uses the additional share, not the existing NTA, to correct fiscal imbalance without
reducing the current shares of provinces, cities, municipalities, or barangays.

        The bill further introduces an Income Classification Equalization Factor to provide
greater support to lower-income LGUs. For provinces, cities, and municipalities, this factor
shall be based on the income classification system under Republic Act No. 11964, otherwise
known as the “Automatic Income Classification of Local Government Units Act.” For
barangays, the equalization factor shall be based on the income classification of the city or
municipality where the barangay is located, unless a separate official barangay income
classification system is subsequently provided by law.

        The bill also addresses practical implementation concerns by providing clear rules
on computation, automatic release, official data sources, treatment of newly created or
converted LGUs, correction of data errors, transparency reporting, and fiscal impact
monitoring. These safeguards are intended to make the measure technically sound,
politically supportable, and administratively implementable.

        By enhancing local fiscal space, especially for municipalities and fiscally weaker
LGUs, this measure is expected to improve local development outcomes, strengthen
decentralization, reduce overreliance on congressional insertions and discretionary national
assistance for basic infrastructure and services, and enable LGUs to better fulfill their
devolved functions under the Local Government Code and the Mandanas-Garcia framework.

       Immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
    STRENGTHENING LOCAL FISCAL AUTONOMY BY PROVIDING AN
  ADDITIONAL SHARE IN NATIONAL TAXES FOR LOCAL GOVERNMENT
     UNITS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160,
  OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991, AS
               AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Local Fiscal Autonomy
Enhancement Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to ensure the
genuine and meaningful local autonomy of local government units (LGUs) by providing
them with adequate, equitable, predictable, and automatically released fiscal resources.

       The State recognizes that LGUs serve as frontline providers of essential public
services, including health care, agriculture, social welfare, disaster risk reduction and
management, environmental protection, local infrastructure, and community development.

       The State further recognizes that municipalities generally have narrower local
revenue bases, fewer opportunities to capture economic growth, and more limited fiscal and
administrative capacity, despite serving a significant portion of the country’s population and
performing extensive devolved functions. The allocation of a larger portion of the Additional
Share created under this Act to municipalities is therefore based on substantial distinctions
germane to the purpose of strengthening local fiscal autonomy, correcting fiscal imbalance,
and promoting equitable local development.

        The State likewise recognizes that fiscal inequality exists within LGU categories.
Accordingly, the State shall use an Income Classification Equalization Factor to provide
greater relative support to lower-income LGUs.
       Nothing in this Act shall be construed to reduce, impair, or diminish the existing
National Tax Allotment (NTA) shares or other statutory fiscal entitlements of any LGU.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:

   (a) Additional Share refers to the additional share of LGUs in all national taxes provided
       under this Act, separate and distinct from the existing forty percent (40%) NTA under
       Section 284 of Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, and applicable jurisprudence;

   (b) Local Government Unit or LGU refers to a province, city, municipality, or barangay
       covered by Republic Act No. 7160, as amended;

   (c) National Tax Allotment or NTA refers to the just share of LGUs in national taxes
       under Section 284 of Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, and applicable
       jurisprudence;

   (d) National Taxes refers to all national taxes used as the base for computing the NTA
       of LGUs under Section 284 of Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, and applicable
       jurisprudence;

   (e) Income Classification Equalization Factor refers to the weighted allocation factor
       under this Act that gives greater relative weight to lower-income LGUs in the
       distribution of the Additional Share;

   (f) Income Classification refers to the official income classification of provinces, cities,
        and municipalities under Republic Act No. 11964, otherwise known as the
        “Automatic Income Classification of Local Government Units Act,” and its
        implementing rules and regulations. For barangays, the income classification of the
        city or municipality where the barangay is located shall be used, unless a separate
        official barangay income classification system is subsequently provided by law; and

   (g) Official Data refers to population, land area, income classification, and other data
       issued, certified, or recognized by the appropriate national government agency for
       purposes of computing fiscal transfers to LGUs.

SEC. 4. Insertion of a New Section 284-A in Republic Act No. 7160. – A new Section 284-
A is hereby inserted after Section 284 of Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, to read as
follows:

       SEC. 284-A. Additional Share of Local Government Units in National Taxes.
       – In addition to the share of local government units in national taxes provided
       under Section 284 of this Code, local government units shall be entitled to an
       Additional Share equivalent to five percent (5%) of all national taxes.

              The Additional Share shall be computed based on the collections of
       national taxes in the third fiscal year preceding the current fiscal year, following
       the same reference-year principle used in the computation of the National Tax
       Allotment.
              The Additional Share shall be automatically released to the local
       government units concerned in the same manner, schedule, and procedure as
       the National Tax Allotment. The release of the Additional Share shall not be
       subject to any lien, holdback, retention, or withholding, except as may be
       expressly authorized by law.

               Nothing in this section shall be construed to reduce, impair, or diminish
       the existing National Tax Allotment shares or any other statutory fiscal
       entitlement of local government units.

SEC. 5. Phased Implementation of the Additional Share. – The Additional Share shall be
implemented gradually at the rate of one percentage point (1%) per fiscal year, commencing
in the first fiscal year following the effectivity of this Act for which the amount may be
included in the National Expenditure Program and the General Appropriations Act, until the
full additional five percent (5%) share is attained, as follows:

   (a) First year – one percent (1%) of all national taxes;

   (b) Second year – two percent (2%) of all national taxes;

   (c) Third year – three percent (3%) of all national taxes;

   (d) Fourth year – four percent (4%) of all national taxes; and

   (e) Fifth year and every year thereafter – five percent (5%) of all national taxes.

       The phased implementation under this section shall not authorize the reduction,
suspension, deferment, or withholding of the Additional Share once the applicable annual
percentage has taken effect, except pursuant to a subsequent law.

SEC. 6. Allocation Among Local Government Unit Categories. – The Additional Share
provided under this Act shall be allocated among LGU categories as follows:

   (a) Provinces – ten percent (10%);

   (b) Cities – ten percent (10%);

   (c) Municipalities – seventy percent (70%); and

   (d) Barangays – ten percent (10%).

SEC. 7. Formula for Distribution Within Each Local Government Unit Category. – The
share of each province, city, municipality, and barangay from the allocation corresponding
to its respective LGU category under Section 6 of this Act shall be determined according to
the following formula:

   (a) Population – twenty-five percent (25%);

   (b) Land area – twenty-five percent (25%); and
   (c) Income Classification Equalization Factor – fifty percent (50%).

       The population component shall be distributed within each LGU category in
proportion to the population of each LGU relative to the total population of all LGUs within
the same category.

       The land area component shall be distributed within each LGU category in
proportion to the land area of each LGU relative to the total land area of all LGUs within the
same category.

      The Income Classification Equalization Factor component shall be distributed within
each LGU category based on weighted population, giving greater relative weight to lower-
income LGUs.

       For this purpose, each province, city, municipality, or barangay shall be assigned the
following income classification weight:

   (a) First Class – 1.00;

   (b) Second Class – 1.10;

   (c) Third Class – 1.25;

   (d) Fourth Class – 1.40; and

   (e) Fifth Class or any lower income classification recognized by law – 1.60.

      For provinces, cities, and municipalities, the income classification weight shall be
based on their official income classification under Republic Act No. 11964 and its
implementing rules and regulations.

        For barangays, the income classification weight shall be based on the income
classification of the city or municipality where the barangay is located, unless a separate
official barangay income classification system is subsequently provided by law.

      The weighted population of each province, city, municipality, or barangay shall be
computed as follows:

       Weighted Population = Population × Income Classification Weight

        The share of each province, city, municipality, or barangay under the Income
Classification Equalization Factor component shall be computed as follows:

       Income Classification Equalization Share = (Weighted Population of the Local
       Government Unit ÷ Total Weighted Population of All Local Government Units
       Within the Same Category) × Fifty percent (50%) of the Allocation for the
       Corresponding Local Government Unit Category.
SEC. 8. Official Data Sources and Data Cut-Off. – For purposes of computing the
Additional Share:

   (a) Population shall be based on the latest official census of population issued by the
       Philippine Statistics Authority;

   (b) Land area shall be based on the latest official land area data issued or recognized by
       the appropriate national government agency, including the Philippine Statistics
       Authority, the National Mapping and Resource Information Authority, or such other
       agency officially designated by law or regulation;

   (c) Income classification for provinces, cities, and municipalities shall be based on the
       latest official income classification issued by the Department of Finance pursuant to
       Republic Act No. 11964 and its implementing rules and regulations; and

   (d) For barangays, the income classification of the city or municipality where the
       barangay is located shall be used, unless a separate official barangay income
       classification system is subsequently provided by law.

        The Department of Budget and Management, in coordination with the Department
of Finance, Department of the Interior and Local Government, Philippine Statistics
Authority, and other concerned agencies, shall prescribe a uniform annual data cut-off date
for purposes of computing the Additional Share.

      Data changes after the applicable cut-off date shall take effect in the computation of
the Additional Share for the succeeding fiscal year, unless otherwise provided in the
implementing rules and regulations.

SEC. 9. Treatment of Newly Created, Converted, Merged, Divided, or Abolished Local
Government Units. – A newly created, converted, merged, divided, or abolished LGU shall
be treated for purposes of computing and releasing the Additional Share in accordance with
the following rules:

   (a) A newly created LGU shall be entitled to receive its corresponding Additional Share
       beginning in the fiscal year immediately following the effectivity of its creation,
       conversion, merger, or division, subject to the availability of official data necessary
       for computation;

   (b) If complete official data is not yet available, the Department of Budget and
       Management, in coordination with the Department of Finance, Department of the
       Interior and Local Government, Philippine Statistics Authority, and other concerned
       agencies, shall prescribe an interim computation method based on the best available
       official data;

   (c) A municipality converted into a city shall continue to be treated under its previous
       LGU category until the next fiscal year for which official computation can be made,
       unless otherwise provided by law;
   (d) In case of merger, division, abolition, or boundary adjustment, the affected LGUs
       shall receive their adjusted shares beginning in the fiscal year following the issuance
       or recognition of the applicable official data; and

   (e) No LGU shall be deprived of its Additional Share solely by reason of delay in the
       issuance of official data, without prejudice to adjustment in the succeeding fiscal
       year.

SEC. 10. Data Correction and Administrative Review. – An LGU may request correction
of clerical, mathematical, or data-based errors in the computation of its Additional Share.

        The request shall be filed with the Department of Budget and Management within
the period and in the manner prescribed in the implementing rules and regulations. The
Department of Budget and Management shall resolve the request in coordination with the
Department of Finance, Department of the Interior and Local Government, Philippine
Statistics Authority, and other concerned agencies, as may be necessary.

       The filing or pendency of a request for correction shall not suspend, delay, or
withhold the release of the undisputed portion of the Additional Share. Any correction found
to be meritorious shall be reflected as an adjustment in the next practicable release or in the
succeeding fiscal year.

SEC. 11. Lead Agencies and Administrative Responsibilities. – The Department of Budget
and Management shall be the lead agency for the computation, programming, and release of
the Additional Share, in coordination with the Department of Finance, Department of the
Interior and Local Government, Philippine Statistics Authority, and other concerned
agencies.

        The Department of Finance, through the Bureau of Local Government Finance, shall
provide the official income classification data necessary for the application of the Income
Classification Equalization Factor.

      The Department of the Interior and Local Government shall assist in the validation
of LGU data, including barangay-level data, and in coordinating with LGUs for the effective
implementation of this Act.

       The Philippine Statistics Authority shall provide or certify official population data
and such other statistical data as may be necessary for the implementation of this Act.

SEC. 12. Budgetary Programming and Automatic Release. – The amounts necessary to
implement this Act shall be included annually in the National Expenditure Program and the
General Appropriations Act as part of the statutory fiscal transfers to LGUs.

        The Additional Share shall be released automatically and directly to the LGUs
concerned in accordance with the schedule, manner, and procedure applicable to the NTA,
subject only to such modifications as may be necessary to implement this Act.
        The inclusion of the Additional Share in the annual General Appropriations Act shall
not be construed as converting the Additional Share into a discretionary appropriation or as
authorizing its withholding, retention, or reduction, except pursuant to law.

SEC. 13. Use, Accountability, and Local Fiscal Autonomy. – The Additional Share shall
form part of the general funds of the recipient LGUs and shall be used in accordance with
Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, applicable budgeting, accounting, auditing, and
procurement laws, and duly approved local development plans, annual investment programs,
and budgets.

       Recipient LGUs are encouraged to prioritize essential devolved services, including
health care, agriculture, social welfare, local infrastructure, disaster risk reduction and
management, environmental protection, and community development.

       Nothing in this Act shall be construed to authorize any national government agency
to impose spending conditions that would unduly impair local fiscal autonomy or delay the
automatic release of the Additional Share.

SEC. 14. Transparency and Public Reporting. – The Department of Budget and
Management shall publish annually, in an accessible and machine-readable format, the
computation of the Additional Share for each LGU, including the applicable population, land
area, income classification, weights, formula components, adjustments, and total amount
released.

        Each recipient LGU shall include the receipt and utilization of the Additional Share
in its existing local budget documents, financial reports, and full disclosure postings, in
accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

       The reporting requirements under this section shall promote transparency and
accountability, but shall not constitute a condition precedent to the automatic release of the
Additional Share.

SEC. 15. Capacity Development Support. – The Department of the Interior and Local
Government, Department of Budget and Management, Department of Finance, and other
concerned agencies shall provide technical assistance and capacity development support to
LGUs, especially municipalities and lower-income LGUs, on local planning, budgeting,
revenue generation, procurement, project implementation, monitoring, and financial
management.

        The provision of capacity development support under this section shall not be used
as a ground to delay, reduce, or withhold the Additional Share.

SEC. 16. Fiscal Impact Review. – Within three (3) years from the initial implementation of
this Act, and every three (3) years thereafter, the Department of Budget and Management
and Department of Finance shall jointly submit to Congress a fiscal impact report on the
implementation of this Act.
       The fiscal impact report shall include:

   (a) The total amount released as Additional Share;

   (b) The distribution of the Additional Share by LGU category, income classification, and
       region;

   (c) The impact of the Additional Share on the fiscal position of the National Government;

   (d) The impact of the Additional Share on local fiscal capacity, service delivery, and
       development outcomes, to the extent measurable using available data;

   (e) Implementation issues, data gaps, and recommended administrative or legislative
       improvements; and

   (f) Measures to improve fiscal sustainability, transparency, and accountability.

The fiscal impact review shall not suspend, defer, reduce, or otherwise impair the automatic
release of the Additional Share, unless otherwise provided by a subsequent law.

SEC. 17. Application to Special Fiscal Regimes. – This Act shall apply to LGUs covered
by Republic Act No. 7160, as amended.

       The application of this Act to LGUs within autonomous regions, special
administrative regions, freeport areas, economic zones, or other areas governed by special
laws shall be without prejudice to the Constitution, applicable organic laws,
intergovernmental fiscal arrangements, and special laws governing such areas.

SEC. 18. No Diminution of Existing Shares. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed to
reduce, impair, diminish, or otherwise prejudice the existing NTA shares of LGUs under
Section 284 of Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, applicable jurisprudence, or any other
fiscal entitlement provided by law.

SEC. 19. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within one hundred ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Budget and Management, Department of
Finance, Department of the Interior and Local Government, Philippine Statistics Authority,
and other concerned agencies shall jointly promulgate the implementing rules and
regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.

       The implementing rules and regulations shall include, among others:

   (a) The detailed computation methodology for the Additional Share;

   (b) The schedule and procedure for automatic release;

   (c) The official data sources and data cut-off dates for population, land area, and income
       classification;

   (d) The application of the Income Classification Equalization Factor;
   (e) The treatment of barangays for purposes of income classification equalization;

   (f) The treatment of newly created, converted, merged, divided, or abolished LGUs;

   (g) Procedures for resolving data conflicts, data gaps, and correction requests;

   (h) Transparency and public reporting standards;

   (i) The fiscal impact review process; and

   (j) Such other mechanisms as may be necessary to ensure transparent, predictable,
       equitable, and administratively feasible implementation.

SEC. 20. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 21. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations,
issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.

SEC. 22. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

       Approved.
