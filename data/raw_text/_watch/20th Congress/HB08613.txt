     OF THE PHILIPPINES, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Millions of Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) endure the physical, emotional,
and financial burdens of working abroad, often navigating complex and exhausting
travel arrangements in the process. Despite their vital role, many OFWs face long
layovers, delayed flights, and inadequate airport facilities upon departure from or
arrival in the country. This bill seeks to address these gaps by institutionalizing a
system of care that reflects the government’s commitment to uphold their welfare and
dignity.

        This measure mandates the establishment of OFW Airport Rest and Transit
Facilities in all international airports across the country. These facilities are envisioned
as safe, accessible, and dignified spaces where OFWs can rest and recover while
awaiting flights or completing travel procedures. By mandating the provision of
essential amenities—such as comfortable seating, charging stations, shower facilities,
real-time flight information systems, and dedicated help desks—the bill ensures that
OFWs are afforded not only convenience but also humane and respectful treatment.

       The bill further strengthens its responsiveness by requiring the establishment
of dedicated sleeping quarters at the Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA),
particularly for OFWs coming from provinces who must endure overnight stays due to
connecting travel. These accommodations are designed to provide secure and

Ultimately, more than a matter of convenience, this measure affirms the nation’s
gratitude by guaranteeing that every OFW is treated with the proper care they rightfully
deserve.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be referred to as the "OFW Transit Care Act".

SEC 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to bolster
the importance of the Overseas Filipino Workers as a driving force in the nation's
economy and nation building. Towards this end, the State shall adopt measures that
would protect the well-being of the Overseas Filipino Workers by providing adequate
and timely services that would safeguard their rights and promote their welfare.

SEC. 3. Establishment of OFW Airport Rest and Transit Facilities. — In
recognition of the sacrifices of Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and the logistical
challenges they face in international travel, the National Government, through the
Department of Migrant Workers (DMW), in coordination with the Overseas Workers
Welfare Administration (OWWA), the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA), the
Department of Transportation (DOTr), the Manila International Airport Authority
(MIAA), and other concerned airport authorities, shall establish, operate, and maintain
OFW Airport Rest and Transit Facilities in all airports chartering international flights in
the country.

These facilities shall serve as safe, accessible, and dignified spaces where OFWs
may rest while awaiting departure, arrival processing, or connecting flights.


                                            3
   (a) OFW Resting Lounges. — All international airports shall provide a designated
       OFW Resting Lounge open twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a
       week, free of charge, which shall include, at a minimum:
         (i) Comfortable seating areas;
        (ii) Gadget charging stations;
       (iii) Shower and sanitary facilities;
       (iv) Monitors and/or speakers to track boarding and departure times; and
        (v)  Assistance or help desks for OFW-related concerns.

   (b) Dedicated Sleeping Quarters at NAIA. — In addition to the general resting
       lounges, the DMW shall establish, operate, and maintain dedicated sleeping
       quarters at the Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA), which shall primarily
       cater to OFWs residing outside Metro Manila who are required to stay overnight
       or for extended waiting periods due to flight schedules, domestic connecting
       travel, or similar circumstances.The sleeping quarters shall:
          (i) Be open twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week;
         (ii) Be made available free of charge or at a highly subsidized rate, as may
               be provided in the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR);
        (iii)  Provide safe, secure, gender-responsive, and clean sleeping
               accommodations;
        (iv) Include secure luggage storage, potable water, sanitation facilities, and
               basic personal care amenities; and
         (v)   Adopt reasonable verification procedures to ensure that only qualified
               OFWs may avail of the facility.

The DMW shall ensure that all Airport Rest and Transit Facilities are compliant with
airport security protocols and standards.

SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The DMW shall, together with the
OWWA and the National Reintegration Center for OFWs (NRCO), promulgate the
necessary implementing rules and regulations within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act. The IRR shall provide detailed guidelines on eligibility, duration
of stay, facility management, inter-agency coordination, and future expansion of
similar sleeping quarters to other international gateways as may be necessary.

The promulgation shall be made in coordination with the key government agencies
mentioned in this Act, and in consultation with concerned GOCCs, especially those
giving grants, extending loans, and providing technical assistance to MSMEs, and
other relevant stakeholders in the private sector and NGOs.

SEC. 5. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of the departments

and agencies concerned. Thereafter, such amount shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 6. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part hereof is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provisions not otherwise affected shall
remain valid and subsisting.

SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. — Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive
order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation contrary to or
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is hereby repealed, modified, or amended
accordingly.

SEC. 8. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
