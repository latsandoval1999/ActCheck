AN ACT RECLASSIFYING AND CONVERTING PARCELS OF TIMBERLAND WITH
 A TOTAL AREA OF SEVENTY-SIX THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED NINETY-FIVE
    SQUARE METERS LOCATED IN POBLACION, BACACAY, ALBAY INTO
                ALIENABLE AND DISPOSABLE LANDS


                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE


Land is highly valued not only because it is the people’s shared heritage, but also because it is an
essential factor of production. Land is a witness to the struggles, dreams and aspirations of those
who labor hard on it as well as those who unconscionably live off some poor tillers’ backs.

Consistent with the constitutionally upheld principle that the use of property bears a social
function, the use and ownership of lands are subject to reasonable restrictions by the State in
order to promote distributive justice and optimized production.

It is in the aforementioned context that this bill seeks to reclassify and convert parcels of
timberland in Poblacion, Bacacay, Albay into alienable and disposable lands. The proposed
disposable timberland covers a total of seventy-six thousand one hundred ninety-five (76,195)
square meters which is a portion of the three hundred fifty-five thousand nine hundred
forty-three (355,943) square meters timberland fringing the coastal areas of Bacacay.

The area recommended for alienation and disposition had been thoroughly subjected to
evaluation by the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR). The Mines and
Geosciences Bureau (MGB) initially expressed reservations against the reclassification as the
identified assessed areas are prone to natural hazards like rain-induced landslides and floods.
Further assessment, however, reconsidered that the 76,195 square meters portion of the current
timberland is a built-up area, already inhabited and developed with concrete structures. Thus,
reclassification and conversion will enhance the integration into the local government unit’s
development plan of: 1) sustainable land use, 2) human settlements, 3) food production, 4)
environmental protection, 5) infrastructure requirements, and 6) disaster risk reduction.
Additionally, the legitimization of economic enterprises established in the unconverted
timberland would increase the LGU’s revenues.

To allay the apprehensions relative to susceptibility to hazards, the bill provides for health and
safety and environmental safeguards that include: 1) declaring areas within the high hazard zones
not suitable for commercial, industrial, residential and institutional development, and for
evacuation centers; 2) construction of appropriate high integrity flood control mitigation
structures, and 3) strict implementation of the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (R.A.
9003).

In view of the foregoing, approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

SECTION 1.​ Declaration of policy. - The 1987 Constitution declares that all lands of the public
domain and all other natural resources are owned by the State. Considering, however, that lands
are not only part of the people’s heritage that should be preserved, but an essential factor of
production, agricultural lands are subject to alienation. Verily, it is declared the policy of the
State to reclassify and convert portions of agricultural lands into alienable and disposable lands
to help pursue the goals of the national economy. The use of these lands shall seek equitable
distribution of opportunities, income and wealth. Moreover, the reclassification and conversion
shall take into account the requirements of conservation of natural resources, environmental
protection and ecological balance, and sustainable national development.

SEC 3. Disposition of Lands. - The lands herein reclassified and converted shall be disposed of
in accordance with Republic Act No. 10023, Commonwealth Act 141 as amended, and other
applicable laws, rules and regulations.

Qualified bona fide settlers and occupants who have been cultivating or residing in the
reclassified area for at least ten (10) years prior to the effectivity of this Act shall be prioritized in
the issuance of land titles or residential patents.

The settlers occupying the portion of the mangrove experimental forest and the
easement/salvaged zone shall be offered relocation to resettlement areas in accordance with the
Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) of the local government unit (LGU). Whereas those with
highly concrete and developed structures within the mangrove areas shall be advised to apply for
or enter into a Forest Land Use Agreement.

SEC 4. Ecological Balance and Environmental Sustainability. - After reclassification and
conversion of the 7.6195 hectares into alienable and disposable, the remaining 28.53 hectares of
the timberland specially the Bacacay Mangrove Experimental Forest shall be retained and
maintained for preservation and conservation to protect the existing rich biodiversity of the
subject area. No tenurial instruments shall be issued to occupants within the forest lands. A
municipal ordinance shall be passed and strictly implemented to prevent further encroachment of
the timberland area. There shall be strict observance of the 20-meter easement which shall be
declared by the Bacacay Municipal Government as “No Build Zone” pursuant to PD 1067
(Water Code) and PD 705 (Revised Forestry Code). Settlers or occupants who refuse to vacate
the timberland shall be indicted for illegal occupation.

SEC 5. Health and Safety and Environmental Safeguards. - a) Areas within high hazard zones
shall be declared not suitable for commercial, industrial, residential and institutional
development. Evacuation centers shall not be established in these zones. During impending
floods, no persons except disaster response personnel shall be allowed to enter and stay in the
subject zones; b) Appropriate flood control mitigation structures of high integrity shall therein be
erected forthwith; c) The Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (RA 9003) shall be strictly
implemented to avert improper waste disposal or solid waste mismanagement.
SEC 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the Secretary of the DENR in consultation with the Municipality of Bacacay and
representatives of the settlers or occupants of the reclassified and converted lands shall
promulgate the rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

SEC 7. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid,
other provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC 8. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
