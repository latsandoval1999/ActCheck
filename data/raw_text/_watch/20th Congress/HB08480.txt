                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Launched in 2008 and institutionalized under Republic Act No. 11310 in
2019, otherwise known as the “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) Act,” the
4Ps serves as the government’s primary poverty alleviation and human capital
development initiative. The Program grants conditional cash assistance to eligible
low-income households for a period not exceeding seven (7) years, with the
objective of enhancing beneficiaries’ access to essential health, nutrition, and
educational services.

      This program has significantly impacted 5,220,953 poor families in its 16
years of implementation1 and has been one of the contributing factors to the decline
of poverty in the Philippines. However, poverty remains one of the persistent and
enduring challenges confronting the country. As noted in the Commission on Audit’s
Performance Audit Report, approximately ninety percent (90%) of program
beneficiaries continue to live below the established poverty threshold. 2

       The proposed measure seeks to amend Republic Act No. 11310 to strengthen
and expand support mechanisms for adult beneficiaries of the Pantawid Pamilyang
Pilipino Program (4Ps) by promoting access to inclusive education,
entrepreneurship, and employment opportunities. It recognizes the necessity of

     [G] AT LEAST ONE (1) ADULT BENEFICIARY MUST JOIN AND COMPLETE
ANY OF THE FOLLOWING TRACKS OF ADULT EDUCATION:

     “(1)NON-FORMAL EDUCATION THROUGH THE ALTERNATIVE LEARNING
SYSTEM (ALS) TRACK OF THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION (DEPED). THE ALS
TRACK REFERS TO A PARALLEL LEARNING SYSTEM THAT PROVIDES A VIABLE
ALTERNATIVE TO THE EXISTING FORMAL EDUCATION INSTRUCTION AND
ENCOMPASSES BOTH NON-FORMAL AND INFORMAL SOURCES OF KNOWLEDGE
AND SKILLS;

     “(2) ENTREPRENEURSHIP TRACK OF THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND
INDUSTRY (DTI) AND DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT
(DSWD). THIS REFERS TO INTERVENTIONS WHICH FOCUS ON THE
ESTABLISHMENT OF MICRO-ENTERPRISE THROUGH ENHANCEMENT OF
SKILLS, PROVISION OF FINANCIAL CAPITAL AND BUILDING OR RE-BUILDING
PHYSICAL AND NATURAL ASSETS, AND WHICH COVERS PRODUCT
DEVELOPMENT, MARKETING, AND BASIC BUSINESS FINANCE; OR

     “(3) EMPLOYMENT TRACK OF THE TECHNICAL EDUCATION AND SKILLS
DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (TESDA) OR THEIR ACCREDITED PRIVATE SECTOR
AND CIVIL SOCIETY ORGANIZATIONS. THIS REFERS TO PROGRAMS THROUGH
WHICH LEARNERS AND WORKERS ARE PROVIDED WITH LEARNING
OPPORTUNITIES TO ACQUIRE, UPGRADE, OR BOTH, THEIR ABILITY,
KNOWLEDGE AND BEHAVIOR PATTERN REQUIRED AS QUALIFICATIONS FOR A
JOB OR RANGE OF JOBS IN A GIVEN OCCUPATIONAL AREA.

     “THE AVAILMENT OF ADULT EDUCATION SHALL COMMENCE ON THE
SECOND YEAR OF ENROLLMENT IN THE 4PS. AS USED IN THIS ACT, ADULT
EDUCATION REFERS TO INTERVENTIONS THAT IMPROVE TECHNICAL OR
PROFESSIONAL QUALIFICATIONS, FURTHER DEVELOP ABILITIES, ACQUIRE OR
ENRICH KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND COMPETENCIES IN A NEW FIELD THROUGH
ALTERNATIVE LEARNING SYSTEM TRACK, ENTREPRENEURSHIP TRACK OR
EMPLOYMENT TRACK.

      [The household beneficiary shall also be given priority in the availment of the
