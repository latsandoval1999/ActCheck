20                                                EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   A person with disability should not have to live a life defined by dependence or the
23   constant fear of the next financial emergency.
24
25   For many PWDs, disability comes with constant expenses for medicines, healthcare,
26   assistive devices, support, and upgraded transportation, not to mention that barriers to
27   employment and livelihood can make it more difficult to secure a stable income.
28
29   These are burdens that no person should have to carry alone, particularly when what
30   they seek is simply the opportunity to live with dignity and independence.
31
32   This proposed measure therefore seeks to establish a social pension for registered
33   PWDs to help ease the financial burdens that come with their disability. At the same
34   time, it seeks to promote access to microfinance, microinsurance, and microenterprise
35   opportunities, so that our brothers and sisters with disabilities are not left behind when
36   it comes to opportunities to build livelihoods and achieve financial independence.
37
38   The State should not merely be present when a PWD is in crisis. Rather, it should be a
39   continuing source of support as they strive to build a secure and independent life.
40
41   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

21     AN ACТ INSTITUTIONALIZING THE PROVISION OF FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE,
22      SOCIAL PENSION, AND FINANCIAL PROTECTION FOR FILIPINO PERSONS
23               WITH DISABILITIES, AND FOR RELATED PURPOSES
24
25   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
26   Congress assembled:
27
28   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Persons with Disabilities
29   Financial Resilience Act".
30
31   SEC. 2. National PWD Registry - The Department of Social Welfare and Development,
32   National Council on Disability Affairs, Philippine Health Insurance Corporation, National
33   Privacy Commission, and the Philippine Statistics Authority shall establish a National
34   Persons With Disability (PWD) Registry, a verified, secured, and shared access
35   consolidated national database on all Filipino PWDs based on a detailed national
36   household inventory. The Registry data shall serve as the bases for the special PWD
37   classification and marking by the Philippine Identification System for the issuance of the
38   PhilSys National ID and consequent availment for benefits due as PWD.

39   SEC. 3. Microfinance, Microinsurance, and Microenterprise - The Department of
40   Finance, Department of Trade and Industry, Small Business Corporation, and Bangko
41   Sentral ng Pilipinas shall coordinatively undertake the inclusive access to and availment
42   of PWDs of their respective microfinance, microinsurance, and microenterprise
43   programs and projects with lower than prevailing interest rates and with added
44   emphasis on the application of new technologies, specific processes for persons of
45   different types of disabilities, and active involvement of private enterprises engaged in
46   the provision of microfinance, microinsurance, and microenterprise access and
47   services.
 1   SEC. 4. Monthly Social Pension - The Department of Social Welfare and
 2   Development shall establish and implement a Social Pension Program for all registered
 3   PWDs. The social pension shall be in the amount of one thousand pesos (P1,000.00)
 4   per month which may be disbursed on a quarterly, semi-annual, or annual basis as may
 5   be deemed necessary and 2 appropriate by the DSWD. The DSWD PWD Social
 6   Pension shall be separate from the national Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)
 7   and stipend programs of local government units sourcing their available local funds and
 8   from their share in the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA). Senior citizens already
 9   enrolled and availing of DSWD social pension are excluded from the PWD social
10   pension.
11
12   SEC. 5. Protection from Loan Sharks and Financial Scams - The Department of
13   Social Welfare and Development, Securities and Exchange Commission, Department
14   of Trade and Industry, Department of Interior and Local Government, and Department
15   of Justice shall establish and implement special protection policies and measures for
16   PWDs against loan sharks, investment scammers, estafa, and all other forms of
17   financial fraud. The PWD social pension and funds received from availment of
18   microfinance, microinsurance, and microenterprise programs are prohibited from being
19   used as collateral or be subjected to any deductions of any kind at any time for the
20   availment of any loans issued by private entities and individuals.

21   SEC. 6. Funding - Funding for this Act shall come from the annual appropriations of
22   the concerned implementing agencies.

23   SEC. 7. Phased Implementation - Cognizant of the availability of funds, the PWD
24   Social Pension may be implemented in phases with urgent priority to PWDs residing in
25   municipalities classified as 3rd, 4th, 5th, and 6th class according to their income level
26   as ascertained by the Bureau of Local Government Finance of the Department of
27   Finance.

28   SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations - All implementing rules and
29   regulations necessary to enforce this Act must be issued and in effect not later than
30   ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.
31
32   SEC. 9. Effectivity - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
33   the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
