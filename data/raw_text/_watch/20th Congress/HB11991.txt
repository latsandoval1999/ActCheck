                                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XIII, Section 9 of the 1987 Constitution mandates the State, in
cooperation with the private sector, to undertake a continuing program of urban land
reform and housing that makes decent housing and basic services available at
affordable cost to underprivileged and homeless citizens. Despite this constitutional
mandate and existing government housing programs, access to decent and affordable
housing remains a significant challenge for many Filipino families, particularly low- and
middle-income earners. According to the Philippine Statistics Authority's 2026
estimates, the country faces approximately 3.77 million housing units in accumulated
and projected housing needs from 2023 to 2028.1

       Despite existing housing programs, high borrowing costs remain a significant
barrier to homeownership, especially for low- and middle-income earners. Interest
payments and other housing-related expenses often place economic and socialized
housing beyond their financial capacity.

       This bill seeks to institutionalize an Economic and Socialized Housing Interest
Subsidy Program to ease the burden of housing loan interest payments. It likewise
provides additional assistance for property-related insurance, estate management
costs, and temporary rental subsidies while qualified buyers await the turnover of their
housing units.

26           SEC. 4. Economic and Socialized Housing Interest Subsidy Program.
27   There is hereby established an Economic and Socialized Housing Interest Subsidy
28   Program, hereinafter referred to as the Program, to provide subsidies on the interest
29   payable under qualified housing loans obtained by qualified buyers for the acquisition
30   of economic and socialized housing. The availment of interest subsidy under the
31   Program shall be subject to the following conditions:
32           (a) The DHSUD shall administer the Program and, in coordination with the
33   Home Development Mutual Fund (HDMF) and participating financial institutions, shall
34   prescribe the standards and procedures for its implementation, including the
35   identification of eligible housing projects and program beneficiaries, accreditation of
36   participating financial institutions, processing and payment of interest subsidies, and
37   monitoring of qualified housing loans;
38           (b) The interest subsidy shall be applicable only to qualified housing loans
39   obtained from HDMF or other participating financial institutions and used solely for
40   the acquisition of eligible economic and socialized housing;
41           (c) The applicable interest subsidy rate shall be determined by the DHSUD,
42   taking into consideration the household income of the qualified buyer, the loan
                                                 2
 1   amount, the price of the housing unit subject of the qualified housing loan, the
 2   prevailing interest rates, and available appropriations for the Program: Provided, That
 3   the interest subsidy shall not exceed five percent (5%) per annum and shall not reduce
 4   the interest rate payable by the qualified borrower below one percent (1%) per
 5   annum;
 6           (d) The interest subsidy shall be applied on a monthly basis to the interest
 7   payable on the outstanding principal balance of the qualified housing loan: Provided,
 8   That interest subsidy shall not be applied to penalties, surcharges, fees, or other
 9   charges imposed by HDMF or participating financial institutions: Provided, further,
10   That the portion of the interest not covered by the interest subsidy shall be borne by
11   the qualified buyer concerned;
12           (e) The interest subsidy shall be paid directly to the HDMF or the participating
13   financial institution from which the qualified housing loan was obtained;
14           (f) The interest subsidy shall not form part of the principal amount of the
15   qualified housing loan and shall not be used as a basis for increasing the selling price
16   of the housing unit or for imposing any fees, charges or other amounts payable by
17   the qualified buyer; and
18           (g) The interest subsidy shall not be granted in addition to other govemment-
19   funded financial assistance for the same housing loan or housing unit, unless
20   otherwise authorized under applicable law.
21
22          SEC. 5. Eligibility. To qualify for interest subsidy under this Act, an individual
23   shall possess the following qualifications:
24          (a) Must be a Filipino citizen;
25          (b) Must not be a professional squatter or a member of a squatting syndicate
26   as defined under Section 3 of Republic Act No. 7279, as amended, otherwise known
27   as the "Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992;"
28          (c) Must be a first-time homebuyer or grantee and must not have been a
29   beneficiary or awardee of a residential housing unit offered or granted by any
30   government agency or instrumentality;
31          (d) Must not have any residential, commercial, agricultural, industrial,
32   recreational, or institutional real property registered under his or her name;
33          (e) Must not have any legal impediment to enter into contracts or to own or
34   exercise rights over any real property in the country; and
35          (f) Must comply with applicable standards or requirements imposed on
36   prospective borrowers by HDMF or the participating financial institution concerned.
37
38         SEC. 6. Amount and Duration of Subsidy. The interest subsidy shall be
39   granted for a period of fifteen (15) years from the date of the first monthly payment,
40   subject to review by the DHSUD every five (5) years.
