                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) are a significant component of the
Philippine economy, contributing substantially to employment generation, entrepreneurship,
innovation, and economic growth. According to the Department of Trade and Industry’s 2024
Philippine MSME Statistics, MSMEs accounted for 99.63%, or 1,236,908, of the 1,241,476
registered business establishments in the country.

       Republic Act No. 6977, as amended, or the “Magna Carta for Micro, Small and Medium
Enterprises (MSMEs),”provides the existing framework for the promotion and development of
MSMEs. However, changing economic conditions and emerging challenges call for stronger and
more responsive government support.

        This proposed measure seeks to further strengthen the Magna Carta by improving access to
financing, simplifying regulatory requirements, expanding market opportunities, supporting
digitalization and innovation, and strengthening MSME assistance at the local level.

        Among its key reforms are the proposed 20% allocation of government procurement
opportunities for eligible MSMEs, strengthened MSME financing, an Enterprise Rehabilitation
Fund of up to ₱500 million for calamity-stricken MSMEs, and an increase in the authorized capital
of the Small Business Corporation from ₱10 billion to ₱50 billion.

        The measure also promotes simplified loan requirements, cash-flow-based lending,
alternative credit data, digital financial literacy, entrepreneurship education, and the creation of
MSMED Councils in cities, municipalities, and barangays to ensure that MSME policies respond to
local needs.

        Through these reforms, the bill seeks to create a more enabling environment where Filipino
MSMEs can start, sustain, expand, and recover their businesses, contributing to inclusive and
resilient economic growth.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
