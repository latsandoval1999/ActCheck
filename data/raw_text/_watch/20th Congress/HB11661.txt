                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Santa Maria, Bulacan, has undergone rapid urbanization and
commercial expansion over recent years, driven by its strategic location and
growing population. Barangay Pulong Buhangin, one of the largest and most
populous barangays in the municipality, serves as a major hub for residential
communities, educational institutions, commercial establishments, and local
trade.

Connecting to primary transportation arteries, the Cityland – Perez Road in
Barangay Pulong Buhangin spans approximately 3.78 kilometers. It plays a
crucial role in facilitating the daily movement of commuters, agricultural goods,
and commercial transport within Santa Maria and neighboring municipalities in
Bulacan.

However, the continuous increase in traffic volume, heavy vehicle loads, and local
economic development have placed a heavy financial and operational strain on
the local government’s road maintenance capacity. Classifying this key
thoroughfare as a local road limits the resources available for necessary repairs,
drainage improvements, and safety upgrades.

Converting the Cityland – Perez Road into a National Road will transfer its
maintenance, repair, and improvement responsibilities to the Department of
Public Works and Highways (DPWH). This shift will ensure the upgrading of the
road to national engineering standards to handle high traffic density and heavy
freight safely.

In view of the foregoing benefits to the community and the region, the immediate
passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT CONVERTING THE CITYLAND – PEREZ ROAD IN BARANGAY
   PULONG BUHANGIN, MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF
     BULACAN INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
                           THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The Cityland – Perez Road located in Barangay Pulong Buhangin,
Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Bulacan, stretching a length of
approximately 3.78 kilometers, is hereby converted into a National Road.

SECTION 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department's program the improvement, repair, and maintenance of the said
road, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary
rules, regulations, and directives to implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved
