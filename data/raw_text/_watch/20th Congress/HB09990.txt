                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippine Identification System (PhilSys), enacted under Republic Act No.
11055, established a central identification platform for all citizens and resident
aliens. However, systemic gaps in government aid delivery demand that we
maximize the national ID’s utility by including a vital piece of information: an
individual's job, occupation, or profession.

This proposed measure mandates the inclusion of an individual's job,
occupation, or profession into both the PhilSys Registry and the physical PhilID
card to eliminate ghost beneficiaries and prevent the manipulation of state
assistance programs.Further, this amendment is highly relevant and urgent given
the recurring challenges in the distribution of government welfare assistance.
Past emergency relief operations have exposed weaknesses in the government's
ability to identify qualified beneficiaries. Efforts to provide targeted financial aid
(ayuda) to legitimate recipients were often compromised by ghost claimants,
forged credentials, and unlawful double-dipping, while unauthorized
individuals took advantage of the lack of a centralized and verified occupational
database.

Through this Act, the State strengthens identity verification by integrating a
citizen's occupation into the national ID system. Beyond securing ayuda, it cuts
financial red tape and provides real-time labor data for better economic
planning,while fully complying with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Given these distinct benefits to administrative efficiency, transparency and public
welfare, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT STRENGTHENING THE PHILIPPINE IDENTIFICATION SYSTEM
BY INCLUDING THE JOB, OCCUPATION, OR PROFESSION OF A CITIZEN
IN THE PHILIPPINE IDENTIFICATION CARD, AMENDING FOR THE
PURPOSE SECTIONS 8 AND 13 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11055,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "PHILIPPINE IDENTIFICATION SYSTEM
ACT"
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "PhilSys Job and
Occupation Inclusion Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to establish a
single, unified national identification system to promote seamless, efficient, and
transparent delivery of public and social services.

The State also recognizes the need to integrate occupational information into the
national ID network to streamline economic transactions and enhance labor
policies

SECTION 3. Amendment of Section 8 of Republic Act No. 11055. – Section 8 of
Republic Act No. 11055, otherwise known as the "Philippine Identification
System Act," is hereby amended to read as follows:

      "SEC. 8. PhilSys Registry Data. – Information to be collected and stored
      under the PhilSys shall be limited to the following:

      (a) Demographic Data

      (1) Full Name; (2) Sex; (3) Date of Birth; (4) Place of Birth; (5) Blood
      Type; (6) Address; [and] (7) Citizenship (Filipino or Resident Alien)[.];
      AND

      (8) JOB, OCCUPATION, OR PROFESSION; PROVIDED, THAT
      FOR INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE UNEMPLOYED, IN-BETWEEN
      JOBS, RETIRED, OR IN THE INFORMAL SECTOR, SUCH
      STATUS SHALL BE REFLECTED ACCORDINGLY, SUBJECT TO
      PERIODIC UPDATING.
                                   xxx xxx xxx"

SECTION 4. Amendment of Section 13 of Republic Act No. 11055. – Section 13 of
Republic Act No. 11055 is hereby amended to read as follows:

     "SEC. 13. The Philippine Identification (PhilID). – The PhilID shall be a
     non-transferable     card    that shall serve as the official
     government-issued identification platform of citizens and resident
     aliens in the Philippines...

     The physical card shall contain the following information:

     (a) PhilSys Number (PSN); (b) Full Name; (c) Date of Birth; (d) Place
     of Birth; (e) Blood Type; (f) Address; (g) Front-facing Photograph;
     [and] (h) Marital Status (optional)[.]; AND

     (I) JOB, OCCUPATION, OR PROFESSION.

                                   xxx xxx xxx"

SECTION 5. Verification and Updating of Data. – The Philippine Statistics
Authority (PSA), in coordination with the Department of Labor and Employment
(DOLE), the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), the Civil Service
Commission (CSC), and other relevant government entities, shall establish a
simplified, secure, and decentralized electronic mechanism allowing registered
individuals to update their occupational data on the PhilSys Registry.

The updating of occupational data shall be free of charge and may be integrated
into existing annual registration or renewal systems managed by the PRC, Civil
Service, or corporate employer networks to ensure real-time accuracy.

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), in
coordination with the Department of Information and Communications
Technology (DICT), the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), the
Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), and the National Privacy
Commission (NPC), shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act, incorporating data protection protocols in
compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the "Data Privacy
Act of 2012."

SECTION 7. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – Sections 8 and 13 of Republic Act No. 11055 are
hereby amended. All other laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and regulations
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.
SECTION 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
