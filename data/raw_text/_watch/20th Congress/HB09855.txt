                              EXPLANATORY NOTE


The state of oral health in the Philippines presents a silent but pervasive public
health crisis. Decades of data indicate that dental caries (tooth decay) and
periodontal diseases affect the vast majority of Filipinos. According to recent
epidemiological assessments, roughly 70% to 80% of Filipinos suffer from dental
cavities, while a significant portion of our school-aged children experience
chronic oral discomfort that directly impacts their school attendance, nutrition,
and overall development.

Republic Act No. 1082, enacted in 1954, was a landmark piece of legislation that
sought to strengthen dental health services in rural areas by creating rural
health units and deploying dental infrastructure. While RA 1082 laid the
groundwork for public health dentistry, its framework is now over seventy years
old. It predates modern advancements in preventive dentistry, teledentistry,
biometric health tracking, and minimally invasive oral care technologies.

Furthermore, our current public healthcare system remains heavily reactive—
focusing on extractions and emergency interventions rather than proactive
preservation. For marginalized communities, visual and physical access to a
dentist remains a luxury due to geographical isolation and economic barriers.

This Bill, aptly titled the "National Initiative for Innovative Oral Health Care Act,"
seeks to fundamentally overhaul and modernize our national oral health
strategy. It amends and strengthens the spirit of RA 1082 by integrating cutting-
edge innovation into the public dental sector.
By modernizing our infrastructure and shifting our philosophy from "extraction
to preservation," this measure will significantly reduce the economic and
physical burden of oral diseases on the Filipino workforce and youth.

In view of the foregoing urgent public health considerations, the immediate
passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR A NATIONAL GOVERNMENT INITIATIVE FOR
INNOVATIVE ORAL HEALTH CARE, THEREBY AMPLIFYING REPUBLIC ACT
NO. 1082, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “AN ACT STRENGTHENING HEALTH
AND DENTAL SERVICES IN THE RURAL AREAS, AND PROVIDING FUNDS
THEREFOR”
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


       SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "National
Initiative for Innovative Oral Health Care Act."
      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to protect and
advance the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among
them. Recognizing oral health is an inseparable component of overall physical
well-being, systemic health, and economic productivity, the State shall
implement a comprehensive, technology-driven, and preventive-oriented
national oral health care framework.
     The State shall actively upgrade existing public dental health structures,
expand access to underserved areas, and foster innovation in dental technologies
and delivery systems.
       SEC. 3. Amplifying and Reinforcing Republic Act No. 1082 – This Act hereby
reinforces, modernizes, and expands the mandate of Republic Act No. 1082 by
shifting the national public health dental paradigm from purely reactive and
extraction-based services to an innovation-driven, preventive, and highly
accessible care model integrated into the Universal Health Care framework.
       SEC. 4. The Government Initiative – The Department of Health (DOH), in
coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG)
and Local Government Units (LGU), shall formulate and execute the National
Initiative for an Innovative Oral Health Care Program, which shall include the
following components:

         •   Innovative Dental Mobile Fleet – the DOH shall procure and deploy
             specialized, solar-powered mobile dental vans to geographically
             isolated and disadvantaged areas (GIDAs). These units shall be
             equipped with modern diagnostic tools, digital intraoral cameras,
             and equipment capable of delivering minimally invasive preventive
             treatments.

         •   Teledentistry and Digital Triaging Network – The DOH shall
             establish a national public teledentistry platform within the existing
             eHealth framework. Rural Health Units (RHUs) and Barangay
             Health Stations (BHS) shall be equipped with digital imaging devices
             to allow community health workers to transmit oral health data to
             regional centers for expert diagnosis and triaging.

         •   National Preventive Supply Guarantee – Every LGU, supported by
             the DOH, shall ensure the regular, subsidized distribution of oral
             hygiene kits - consisting of high-quality fluoridated toothpaste, age-
             appropriate toothbrushes, and educational materials- to all public
             day-care centers, elementary schools, and pregnant women enrolled
             in public health programs.

         •   Advanced Dental Workforce Training – The DOH, in partnership with
             the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) and accredited
             dental associations, shall mandate and fund continuous education
             for public health dentists and public health workers, focusing on
             preventive dentistry, community oral health metrics, and modern
             clinical technologies.w
      SEC. 5. Oral Health Innovation and Research Fund. – There is hereby
established an Oral Health Innovation and Research Fund under the
Department of Science and Technology (DOST) – Philippine Council for Health
Research and Development (PCHRD). This fund shall be exclusively utilized to
give gants to local universities, research institutions, and Filipino inventors
developing affordable, localized dental materials, eco-friendly oral care
products,and data-driven epidemiological tracking software.
      SEC. 6. Institutional Arrangements. –

         •   The DOH shall serve as the lead implementing agency, establishing
             a specialized program management office under its Disease
             Prevention and Control Bureau.

         •   The Department of Education (DepED) shall integrate modern oral
             self-care routines into the daily curriculum of elementary school
             students and oversee the bi-annual topical fluoride application
             program in public schools.

         •   LGUs shall allocate local counterpart sources, including personnel
             and logistical support, to sustain the operations of the Innovative
             Mobile Dental fleet within their respective jurisdictions.
       SEC. 7. Appropriations – The amount necessary to cover the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s
appropriation of the Department of Health. Thereafter, such sums as may be
necessary for its continuous implementation shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act (GAA). A minimum of fifteen percent (15%) of the
total health enhancement allocations from sin taxes and relevant medical care
revenues shall be earmarked specifically for the procurement of dental
innovations and mobile fleets authorized under this Act.
       SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Health, in consultation with
the Secretary of the DOST, the Secretary of DepEd, the Philippine Dental
Association (PDA), and representatives from local government leagues, shall
promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation
of this Act.
       SEC. 9. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full
force and effect.
      SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and
regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
      SEC. 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
