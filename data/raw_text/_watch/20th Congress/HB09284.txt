                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

    The Verde Island Passage is one of the rich biodiversity areas in the country and
has been declared the center of center of the world’s marine shore fish diversity by
marine scientists. As such, the national government and local government units have
taken measures to protect, conserve and sustainably use biological diversity to ensure
and secure the well-being of the present and future generations of Filipinos.

    Then President Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo, for instance, issued Executive Order No.
578 which established a national policy on biological diversity and created an ad hoc
task force to ensure the protection, conservation and sustainable use of biological
diversity in the Verde Island Passage Marine Corridor, and formulate a Verde Island
Passage Management Plan in consultation with stakeholders, the private sector, civil
society and local communities.

     Local government units, in cooperation with national government agencies, law
enforcement, and civil society, signed a memorandum of agreement to create a network
for the protection and sustainable management of the Verde Island Passage.

     Despite these significant steps, the Verde Island Passage remains vulnerable.
Human activities such as fishing, shipping, tourism, and navigation still pose a serious
threat. Thus, this bill seeks to urgently declare the Verde Island Passage Marine
Corridor as a Marine Protected Area and an Ecotourism Zone. The bill also proposes
the creation of a management body that shall implement the existing management plan
and the policies enunciated in the proposed Act.

    In view of the foregoing, the early passage of this bill is earnestly requested.

  AN ACT DECLARING THE VERDE ISLAND PASSAGE MARINE
    CORRIDOR AS A MARITIME PROTECTED AREA AND AN
   ECOTOURISM ZONE, CREATING FOR THE PURPOSE THE
  VERDE ISLAND PASSAGE MANAGEMENT BOARD, DEFINING
      ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, PROVIDING FUNDS
           THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

   Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

   SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Verde Island Passage
Marine Corridor Protected Area Act”.

    SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – Considering the Verde Island Marine
Corridor is recognized as the world’s center of marine biodiversity, it is hereby declared
the policy of the State to conserve and protect its marine resources and promote the
welfare of the human communities therein, their culture and way of life insofar as they
are in harmony with nature. Towards this end, the State shall ensure the optimal
resource utilization, conservation, protection, and promotion of the sustainable coastal
and marine development of the areas covered by the Verde Island Passage Marine
Corridor.

    SECTION 3. Scope. – The Verde Island Passage Marine Corridor lies between the
southern coast of the province of Batangas and the northern coast of Mindoro Island,
more particularly described as follows:

               SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. – The terms used in this Act are as follows:

             Existing Management Plan shall refer to the Verde Island Passage Management
         Plan formulated and completed by the Ad Hoc Task Force on Verde Island Passage
         pursuant to Executive Order No. 578 issued on November 8, 2006.

              Non-Government Organization (NGO) – any civic, developmental, environmental,
         or philanthropic non-stock, non-profit organization, duly registered, having by-laws,
         democratically-elected representatives, with qualifications, expertise and objectivity in
         activities concerning community organization and development, or resource and
         environmental conservation, management relative to protected areas.

             Protected Area – a clearly defined geographical space, recognized, dedicated and
         managed, through legal or other effective means, to achieve the long term conservation
         of nature with associated ecosystem services and cultural values.

               VIPMB – Verde Island Passage Management Board.

               VIPMC – Verde Island Passage Marine Corridor.

             SECTION 5. Verde Island Passage Management Board. – A Verde Island Passage
         Management Board is hereby created under the Department of Environment and
         Natural Resources (DENR) to implement the existing management plan and policies
         enunciated under this Act. It shall be composed of:
    a. The Secretary of the DENR, as Chairperson;

    b. The Governors of the Province of Batangas, the Province of Marinduque, the
       Province of Mindoro Occidental, the Province of Mindoro Oriental, and the
       Province of Romblon, as co-chairpersons;

    c. The local chief executives of Batangas City, Muncipalities of Nasugbu, Lian,
       Calatagan, Balayan, Calaca, Lemery, Taal, San Luis, Mabini, Tingloy, Bauan,
       San Pascual, Lobo, San Juan in the Province of Batangas, Municipalities of
       Mogpog, Boac, Gasan and Buenavista in the Province of Marinduque,
       Municipalities of Abra de Ilog, Paluan, Lubang and Looc in the Province of
       Mindoro Occidental, Calapan City, Municipalities of Puerto Galera, San
       Teodoro, Naujan, Baco, Pola and Pinamalayan in the Province of Mindoro
       Oriental, Municipalities of Concepcion, Corcuera and Banton in the Province
       of Romblon as Vice Chairpersons;

    d. The Executive Director of the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (DA-
       BFAR) Regions IV-A and IV-B, as member;

    e. The Regional Director of the National Economic and Development Authority
       (NEDA) Regions IV-A and IV-B, as member;

    f. The Regional Directors (Region IV-A and IV-B) of the Departments of
       Tourism (DOT), Science and Technology (DOST), Health (DOH),
       Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), Interior and Local Government
       (DILG), Justice (DOJ), Energy (DOE), Public Works and Highways (DPWH),
       Health (DOH), Finance (DOF), Labor and Employment (DOLE), Social
       Welfare and Development (DSWD), Transportation (DOTr), Trade and
       Industry (DTI), National Defense (DND), Philippine National Police (PNP) for
       Regions IV-A and IV-B, as members;

    g. Two (2) representatives from the academe, to be appointed by the President
       upon recommendation of the DENR Secretary, as members; and

    h. Two (2) representatives from ecological or environmental NGOs, to be chosen
       from among themselves in an election duly called for the purpose, as members.

    SECTION 6. Term. – Except for government officials who shall serve ex-officio,
every VIPMB member shall serve for a term of three (3) years; Provided, That the said
member remains connected with the sector being represented. Whenever a vacancy
occurs during the term of a member who does not represent the government, a new
member shall be chosen in the same manner as the original process to serve the
remaining term of the predecessor.

     SECTION 7. Powers and Functions of the Board. – The VIPMB shall have the
following powers and functions:

    a. Formulate the Verde Island Passage Marine Protected and Ecotourism Master
       Plan that is consistent with the existing Management Plan.
    b. Ensure the proper implementation and enforcement of existing laws, rules and
       regulations, policies, and programs within the VIPMC;

    c. Decide matters relating to planning, resource use and protection, and general
       administration of the area in accordance with the existing Management Plan
       Framework;

    d. Coordinate with national and local agencies, local government units, local
       communities, the academe, non-government organizations, and such other
       institutions to ensure the conservation, management, and proper zoning of
       coastal waters to reduce possible conflict in the use of resources in VIPMC;

    e. Provide guidelines and best practices for coastal and foreshore development
       planning that take into account the likelihood for increase storm activity,
       saltwater intrusion, and other climate change impacts;

    f. Initiate partnerships with the private sector and the local government units
       concerned on establishing processes for dealing with pollution, including but
       not limited to oil spills, through the Environmental Impact Assessment System;

    g. Facilitate access to technical assistance and information among different
       agencies, sectors, and local government units;

    h. Regulate construction, operation, and maintenance of structures and utilities
       within the VIPMC;

    i. Generate funds and accept donations and grants and exercise accountability
       over all funds that may accrue to the VIPMC;

    j. Convene a conflict resolution mechanism;

    k. Provide adequate measures to ensure consultation and participation of
       stakeholders;

    SECTION 8. The Secretariat. – The Board, through a Resolution, shall establish
a Secretariat to provide administrative support to the VIPMC Management Board. The
Secretariat shall have the following functions:

    a. Coordinate day-to-day activities related to the implementation of the
       Management Plan;

    b. Arrange and service meetings of the Board; and

    c. Prepare the necessary reports and documents.

    SECTION 9. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial operating
expenses of the VIPMB shall be charge d to the appropriations authorized for the DENR.
Thereafter, the sum necessary for the continued operation and maintenance of the
VIPMB shall be charged from the user fees to be imposed by the respective local
government units concerned. This income shall be derived from the tourist fess,
contributions from industries and facilities directly benefiting from the use of the
portions of VIPMC.

     SECTION 10. Implementing Rules. – Within 90 days from its effectivity, the
VIPMB and other concerned agencies shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the proper implementation of this Act.

    SECTION 11. Separability Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees, rules and regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with any provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

    SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders,
memorandum circulars inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby amended,
repealed or modified accordingly.

    SECTION 13. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect within fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of
general circulation, whichever comes earlier.
