                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the San Isidro-Bahao Road in the Province of Camarines Sur, into a
national road.

Roads are considered as the backbone of one’s province’s infrastructure as it connects people
to goods and services, access to healthcare, social activities, and employment opportunities,
and efficient and faster travel to one’s workplace and school. In fact, roads is one of the major
dictations of a developing city or municipality or province as it promotes their overall
development due to their efficiencies on how they reduce transportation costs and travel time.

The San Isidro-Bahao Road is a linked road for eight (8) barangays within the Municipality of
Libmanan that stretches for a long twenty four kilometers and four hundred twenty four meters
(24.424km), which has made the lives of its constituents simple and efficient. It made a
pathway for them to deliver goods and services from one barangay to another, and transport
people to their destinations especially to their workplaces, schools, and other establishments
that help with the monetary flow of the province.

However, just like any other infrastructure, roads are prone to wear and tear that are in need
of either maintenance or immediate repair. As much as local government units acknowledge
their significance within their jurisdictions for efficient and effective flow of goods and services,
and resources, their budgets are limited and need the assistance of the national government
to both maintain and repair such roads.

Therefore, in order to ensure the San Isidro-Bahao Road’s orderly function in the province of
Camarines Sur, the conversion of the said road into a national road is essential.

  AN ACT CONVERTING THE SAN ISIDRO-BAHAO ROAD INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. – The San Isidro-Bahao Road connecting Barangays San Isidro and Bahao in
the Municipality of Libmanan in the Province of Camarines Sur, consisting of Twenty Four
Kilometers and Four Hundred Twenty Four Meters (24.424km), more or less, is hereby
converted into a national road.

SECTION 2. – The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue
the necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 3. – This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
