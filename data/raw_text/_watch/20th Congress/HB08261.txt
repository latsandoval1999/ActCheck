                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

      In recent weeks, escalating conflict in the Middle East — particularly military
strikes involving the United States, Israel, and Iran — has disrupted global energy
markets and driven a surge in crude oil prices. Attacks on infrastructure and retaliatory
actions have threatened shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, a narrow maritime
chokepoint through which roughly one-fifth of the world’s crude oil trade passes. These
developments have contributed to significant increases in international oil benchmarks,
with prices rising sharply on fears of prolonged supply disruption and logistical
bottlenecks. Analysts warn that, in extreme scenarios, Brent and other major crude
price benchmarks could spike substantially if regional tensions persist and shipping
routes remain constrained1

      The Philippines — like many Asian economies — is heavily dependent on
imported petroleum products for transportation, industry, and energy needs. Thus,
volatility in global crude prices quickly translates into higher domestic pump prices for
gasoline and diesel, which in turn contributes to inflationary pressures, increased
transport and logistics costs, and higher prices for basic goods and services. Local
authorities have already expressed concerns about weekly oil price hikes amid
lingering uncertainty in global markets2.

      The purpose of this bill is to provide a fiscal tool and mechanism that allows for
the temporary suspension or reduction of excise taxes on regular gasoline, unleaded
premium gasoline, and diesel when global crude price benchmarks reach levels that
pose significant inflationary risk to the Philippine economy. By amending Section 148
of the National Internal Revenue Code, the measure enables the President, upon
recommendation of the Secretary of Finance (in consultation with the Secretary of
Energy), to adjust excise rates in response to objective international price triggers —

       This approach aligns public fiscal policy with real-world market conditions,
protecting consumers and the broader economy from rapid fuel price inflation while
preserving the State’s ability to safeguard revenue and macroeconomic stability. The
bill also requires transparency by mandating that the Department of Finance submit a
report to Congress explaining the economic and fiscal basis for any recommended tax
action.

      By institutionalizing an evidence-based mechanism that activates during periods
of extraordinary global oil price strain — such as those triggered by geopolitical
conflicts — this measure provides a responsible, balanced way to mitigate external
cost shocks without undermining long-term revenue stability.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
