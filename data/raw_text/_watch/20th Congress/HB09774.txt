                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE



According to data from the Philippine Statistics Authority, employment among
persons with disabilities (PWDs) and senior citizens remains relatively low. In 2022,
only 353,000 of the estimated 1.9 million PWDs were employed1, while just 38.2
percent of the 2.538 million qualified senior citizens had gainful employment.2 This
situation reflects the need for greater access to employment opportunities for these
sectors.

The Constitution recognizes labor as a vital social and economic force and directs
the State to uphold the welfare of all workers. In line with this policy, there is a need
to expand employment opportunities for senior citizens and persons with
disabilities (PWDs), many of whom continue to face barriers to gainful employment
despite their skills and capabilities.

By providing greater access to employment and support services, the State can
promote economic independence, social inclusion, and equal opportunities for these
sectors while recognizing their valuable contributions to society and national
development.

This proposed measure seeks to strengthen existing Public Employment Service
Offices (PESOs) by enhancing their capacity to provide targeted employment
assistance, job matching, skills development, and other support services for senior
citizens and PWDs. By expanding access to employment opportunities, the measure

                                  AN ACT
          INSTITUTIONALIZING A NATIONAL EMPLOYMENT FACILITATION
  SERVICE NETWORK FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITY (PWDs) AND SENIOR
     CITIZENS THROUGH THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A LOCAL CENTER FOR
    INCLUSIVE EMPLOYMENT IN EVERY PROVINCE, KEY CITY AND OTHER
              STRATEGIC AREAS THROUGHOUT THE COUNTRY


      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Inclusive
Employment Act of 2026.”

        SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is a declared policy of the State to
promote employment opportunities for all and strengthen the existing employment
facilitation service machinery of the government for persons with disability and
senior citizens, particularly at the local levels.

      SECTION 3. Establishment of the Local Center for Inclusive Employment
(LCIE). - There is hereby established a Local Center for Inclusive Employment,
hereinafter referred to as “LCIE”, in all capital towns of provinces, key cities and
other strategic areas, which shall be a unit under the existing Public Employment
Service Office (PESO) in each local government unit.
The LCIE under each PESO shall be linked with the regional offices of the
Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) for coordination and technical
supervision, and to the DOLE Central Office, to constitute the national employment
service network.

       SECTION 4. Objectives of the LCIE. - The LCIE shall ensure prompt,
appropriate, and timely employment facilitation services for PWDs and senior
citizens and the provision of relevant information, education, and communication
(IEC) materials for the benefit of the PWDs and senior citizens seeking gainful
employment opportunities. Specifically, the LCIE shall:

      a. Provide a venue where PWDs and senior citizens could find and explore
      various employment options and seek training assistance for possible
      employment ventures;
      b. Serve as referral and information center for the various services and
      programs being provided by the DOLE and other government agencies with
      respect to the welfare and employment of PWDs and senior citizens;
      c. Provide PWDs and senior citizens with sufficient information on
      employment opportunities and labor market situation in the area; and
      d. Network with other LCIEs within the province and/or region on
      employment opportunities for PWDs and senior citizens.

       SECTION 5. Functions of the LCIE. - The LCIE shall have the following
functions:

      a. Establish partnerships with employers who shall regularly submit to the
      LCIE a list of job vacancies available for PWDs and senior citizens in their
      respective establishments;
      b. Facilitate the exchange of labor market information between the PWDs and
      senior citizens jobseekers and employers, by providing employment
      information services to the jobseekers and recruitment assistance to
      employers;
      c. Develop and administer testing and evaluation instruments for effective
      qualification, selection, training, and counseling;
      d. Develop training modules for PWDs and senior citizens in order to improve
      their skills and comply with the skills requirement of the employer;
      e. Undertake employability enhancement trainings/seminars for PWDs and
      senior citizens, as well as those PWDs and senior citizens who would like to
      enhance their employability;
      f. Provide occupational counseling, career guidance, motivation and values
      development activities to PWDs and senior citizen;
      g. Conduct pre-employment counseling and orientation to PWDs and senior
      citizens;
      h. Provide PWDs and senior citizens with access to the various livelihood and
      self employment programs offered by both government and non-government
      organizations (NGOs) at the provincial, city, municipal, and barangay levels by
      undertaking referrals for such programs;
      i. Conduct jobs fairs, Work Appreciation Programs (WAPs), and livelihood and
      self-employment bazaars, among other employment facilitation strategies;
      j. Provide reintegration assistance services to PWDs and senior citizens who
      are retrenched or terminated; and
      k. Perform such functions and willfully carry out the objectives of this Act.

       SECTION 6. Role of the DOLE and LGUs. – Upon the request of the local
government units through existing PESOs, the DOLE shall undertake the
establishment, operation, and maintenance of the LCIE.

It shall be the responsibility of the DOLE to:

      a. Establish and maintain a computerized PWD and senior citizens manpower
      registry of skills and employment and business opportunities to facilitate the
      provision and packaging of employment assistance to PWD and senior citizen
      clients and the setting-up of regional job clearance systems as part of the
      overall employment network;
      b. Designate, in consultation with the PESO or NGO concerned, a qualified
      project manager and/or key personnel who shall be responsible for the
      operation and management of the LCIE;
      c. Provide technical assistance and allied support services to the LCIE;
      d. Train LCIE personnel in the various aspects of employment facilitation
      functions;
      e. Extend/facilitate such other package of employment services through the
      provision of employment assistance to PWD and senior citizen clients of the
      LGU or NGO concerned, including the conduct of jobs fairs and other
      activities; and
      f. Undertake, monitor, assess and evaluate the LCIE program implementation.

       SECTION 7. Administration of the LCIE Program. - The DOLE shall be
primarily responsible for the administration of the LCIE Program through the
Bureau of Workers with Special Concerns, as well as its regional offices throughout
the country. The Secretary of Labor and Employment, in coordination with the
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), National Council on
Disability Affairs (NCDA), and National Commission Of Senior Citizens (NCSC), and
in consultation with the presidents of the various local government leagues, namely
the League of Provinces, the League of Cities, the League of Municipalities, and the
League of Barangays, shall accordingly promulgate, within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of the Program.

       SECTION 8. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the existing appropriations of
the DOLE and its other operating agencies. Thereafter, the amounts needed for the
full implementation of this Act shall be included in the budget of the DOLE in the
succeeding annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. - If any part or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof which are not
affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
