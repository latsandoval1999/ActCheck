                                AN ACT
CONSOLIDATING ALL PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT TOOLS AND REWARDS
SYSTEMS FOR ALL LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS BY INSTITUTIONALIZING
MERITOCRACY IN THE PHILIPPINES, AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS OF
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11292, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "THE SEAL OF GOOD LOCAL
GOVERNANCE ACT OF 2019"
                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The proposed measure seeks to institutionalize a modified version of the Human Development
Index (HDI) as a performance measurement tool for Philippine local government units (LGUs). It
proposes to amend the Seal of Good Local Governance (SGLG) Act of 2019 by adding a system that will
measure the progress of LGUs based on selected indicators of human development.

        Under the proposed system, LGUs will be assessed based on their progress and performance in
these indicators. The assessment will focus on whether LGUs are able to improve the outcomes within
their respective areas of responsibility. The indicators may also take into account the different
circumstances and capacities of LGUs to provide a fair and practical basis for evaluation.

        The measure also provides for the recognition of LGUs that consistently perform well under the
index. LGUs that meet the established standards may be given appropriate recognition and incentives.
On the other hand, LGUs that experience sustained decreases in their performance indicators may be
subject to appropriate interventions or disincentives, in accordance with existing laws, rules, and
regulations.

        The proposed index is intended to complement the existing SGLG framework by giving greater
consideration to actual development outcomes.

        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is respectfully sought.
