   AN ACT EXPANDING PHILHEALTH COVERAGE FOR AUTISM SPECTRUM
  DISORDER, STRENGTHENING BENEFIT PACKAGES FOR DEVELOPMENTAL
               DISABILITIES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a lifelong neurodevelopmental condition
requiring sustained, multidisciplinary, and often intensive interventions across the life
course. While the State has taken an important step through the establishment of the Z
Benefit Package for Children with Developmental Disabilities under PhilHealth Circular
No. 2017-0029, coverage remains limited in scope, duration, and financial adequacy.

       At present, PhilHealth benefits for autism are largely confined to early diagnosis
and a capped number of therapy sessions, leaving families to shoulder the long-term cost
of occupational therapy, speech therapy, behavioral interventions, assistive services, and
mental health support. Moreover, coverage is largely restricted to children, with minimal
support for adolescents and adults with autism who continue to require services for
independent living, employment readiness, and social integration.

       While the proposed “Autism Care Act of 2025” establishes a comprehensive
national program and mandates PhilHealth inclusion, there remains a critical need
to define, expand, and institutionalize specific benefit packages within
PhilHealth to ensure meaningful financial risk protection for persons with autism and their
families.
   This bill seeks to strengthen the National Health Insurance Program by:

   •   Expanding PhilHealth benefit packages for autism across the life span;
   •   Increasing coverage for diagnostic, therapeutic, behavioral, and assistive
       interventions;
   •   Institutionalizing outpatient, community-based, and long-term care coverage;
   •   Ensuring provider accreditation, quality standards, and nationwide accessibility;
       and
   •   Establishing sustainable financing mechanisms for autism-related services.

    This measure complements, operationalizes, and reinforces existing and proposed
policies without duplication, ensuring that the State’s commitment to universal health care
fully includes persons with autism.

   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “PhilHealth Autism Benefit
Expansion Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State to ensure that
all persons with autism have equitable access to affordable, adequate, and continuous
health care services under the National Health Insurance Program, consistent with the
principles of universal health care, inclusivity, and social justice.

SEC. 3. Coverage Expansion. PhilHealth shall expand existing benefit packages for
developmental disabilities to include comprehensive, life-course coverage for Autism
Spectrum Disorder (ASD), including but not limited to:

(a) Early screening and developmental surveillance;
(b) Comprehensive diagnostic services;
(c) Behavioral and developmental therapies, including Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA);
(d) Occupational, speech, and physical therapy without restrictive caps inconsistent with
clinical need;
(e) Mental health and psychosocial support services;
(f) Assistive technologies and communication devices;
(g) Caregiver training and family support interventions.

SEC. 4. Removal of Restrictive Benefit Caps. PhilHealth shall review and progressively
remove or increase existing limitations on the number of therapy sessions and benefit
ceilings, ensuring that coverage is needs-based, evidence-based, and developmentally
appropriate.

SEC. 5. Lifetime and Continuum of Care Coverage. Coverage shall not be limited to
children. PhilHealth shall ensure benefits for:

(a) Adolescents transitioning to adulthood;
(b) Adults with autism requiring ongoing therapy, mental health care, and functional
support;
(c) Geriatric individuals with autism, as applicable.

SEC.      6.    Outpatient    and    Community-Based         Care.    PhilHealth shall
institutionalize outpatient, home-based, and community-based service coverage,
recognizing that autism interventions are primarily non-hospital-based.

SEC. 7. Accreditation of Providers. PhilHealth shall expand accreditation to include:

   •   Developmental pediatricians
   •   Psychologists and psychiatrists
   •   Licensed therapists (OT, speech, PT)
   •   Behavioral therapists
   •   Autism centers and special education-linked therapy providers

Provided that quality and regulatory standards are met.

SEC. 8. Integration with Universal Health Care. Autism services shall be integrated
into primary care benefit packages under the Universal Health Care Act, including referral
systems and continuity of care.

SEC. 9. Financial Protection and No Balance Billing Policy. For indigent, low-income,
and PWD-registered individuals with autism, PhilHealth shall adopt zero or no balance
billing schemes for essential services.

SEC. 10. Data Collection and Monitoring. PhilHealth, in coordination with the
Department of Health and the National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA), shall
establish a dedicated autism utilization and outcomes database to guide policy, actuarial
adjustments, and program expansion.
SEC. 11. Periodic Review of Benefit Packages. PhilHealth shall conduct a biennial
review of autism benefit packages to ensure alignment with global clinical standards and
cost realities.

SEC. 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within six (6) months from effectivity,
PhilHealth, in coordination with the Department of Health, shall promulgate the necessary
rules and regulations.

SEC. 13. Appropriations. Funding necessary for implementation shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act and sourced from the National Health Insurance Fund.

SEC. 14. Separability Clause. If any provision or part hereof is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not otherwise affected shall
remain valid and subsisting.

SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive order,
letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation contrary to or inconsistent with
the provisions of this Act is hereby repealed, modified, or amended accordingly.

SEC. 16. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette and at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
