                            AN ACT
AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE REHABILITATION, SELF-DEVELOPMENT AND SELF-
RELIANCE OF DISABLED PERSONS AND THEIR INTEGRATION INTO THE
MAINSTREAM OF SOCIETY AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES,” TO MANDATE THE
RIGHT TO ACCESS TO INFORMATION AND THE RIGHT TO DIGITAL
ACCESSIBILITY

                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XIII, Section 1 of the 1987 Constitution provides that, “[t]he Congress
shall give highest priority to the enactment of measures that protect and enhance the
right of all the people to human dignity, reduce social, economic, and political
inequalities, and remove cultural inequities by equitably diffusing wealth and political
power for the common good.” Additionally, Section 13 of the same Article mandates
that the State establish a special agency for persons with disabilities to support their
rehabilitation, self-development, self-reliance, and integration into the mainstream of
society.

        Since its enactment in 1992, Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the
“Magna Carta for Disabled Persons,” has provided the primary framework for
advancing the rights and welfare of over 2.5 million Filipino persons with disabilities. 1
However, significant social and technological transformations over the past decades
have resulted in the emergence of new barriers for persons with disabilities. With
government services, banking, commerce, and everyday transactions shifting to the
digital space, the existing law must be amended not just to guarantee barrier-free
physical environments, but also accessibility and non-discrimination in the new digital
environment.

      The bill modernizes the RA 7277 by guaranteeing the right to access to
information and the right to digital accessibility. The bill further specifies mechanisms

environment for persons with disabilities that is free from barriers and free from

standards, the bill updates the statutory definition of persons with disabilities and
replaces the term “disabled persons” in Republic Act No. 7277 with the more
appropriate, person-first, widely acceptable, and empowering term “persons with
disabilities.

      This bill was crafted after consultations with multiple stakeholders in various
persons with disabilities groups, sectors, and advocacy networks.

      Early approval of this measure is earnestly sought.
