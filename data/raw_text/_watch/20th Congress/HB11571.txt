                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation (PDIC) was created under Republic Act
No. 3591 in 1963 to insure bank deposits and help maintain a sound and stable financial system.
Since then, its Charter has been amended only twice in over sixty years — through Republic Act
No. 9302 in 2004 and Republic Act No. 9576 in 2009 — even as the Philippine financial system
has grown substantially more complex, more digital, and more interconnected with non-bank
deposit-taking channels.

This bill seeks to enact the Responsive Deposit Insurance Act (ReDI) to modernize and strengthen
the country’s deposit insurance framework. It expands deposit insurance coverage beyond
traditional banks to include other deposit-taking institutions, subject to appropriate regulatory
safeguards, thereby extending protection to a broader segment of the depositing public.

The measure also empowers the PDIC to adopt a more responsive insurance system by allowing
periodic adjustments in deposit insurance coverage based on economic conditions, implementing
risk-based premium assessments, and enhancing its capacity to monitor and manage risks to the
Deposit Insurance Fund. It further institutionalizes mechanisms to address systemic risks and
ensure the sustainability of the deposit insurance system.

To improve operational efficiency and financial resilience, the bill strengthens the PDIC’s fiscal
and administrative autonomy, enhances its governance structure, expands its investment and
resolution authorities, and authorizes the creation of a subsidiary to manage and dispose of assets
acquired from closed banks. These reforms are intended to maximize asset recovery, reduce
resolution costs, and improve the Corporation’s overall effectiveness.

Ultimately, this measure aligns the Philippines’ deposit insurance regime with evolving financial
sector realities and international best practices. By enhancing depositor protection, strengthening
financial safety nets, and promoting confidence in the financial system, the proposed legislation
will contribute to a more stable, resilient, and inclusive financial sector.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought
