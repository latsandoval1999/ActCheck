                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

Agriculture remains the backbone of the Philippine economy, yet our farmers
and fisherfolk consistently face challenges related to economies of scale,
access to credit, and market linkages. While the cooperative model has proven
effective in empowering marginalized sectors by consolidating resources, the
specific needs of agricultural cooperatives require specialized, integrated
support that aligns directly with the nation's food security programs.

Currently, the regulation and development of cooperatives fall under the
general jurisdiction of the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA).
However, agricultural cooperatives face unique operational and sectoral
challenges—such as mechanization, crop insurance, supply chain logistics,
and agrarian reform integration—that require the direct technical expertise
and policy alignment of the Department of Agriculture (DA).

To optimize government structures and ensure a more focused, efficient
delivery of services, there is a distinct need to reorganize how the government
supports agricultural cooperatives. Consolidating these specific support
mechanisms under a dedicated bureau within the DA will eliminate
institutional fragmentation, streamline bureaucratic processes, and ensure
that agricultural cooperatives receive comprehensive support tailored to the
agricultural value chain.

This bill seeks to create the Bureau of Agriculture Cooperative (BAC) under
the Department of Agriculture. The BAC will be tasked with the promotion,
organization, capacity-building, and specialized support of agricultural
cooperatives, ensuring they are highly competitive, resilient, and central to
the modernization of Philippine agriculture.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate consideration of this measure is
earnestly sought.

  AN ACT CREATING THE BUREAU OF AGRICULTURE COOPERATIVE
UNDER THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, APPROPRIATING FUNDS
            THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:
      SECTION 1. Short Title. -     This Act shall be known as the "Bureau of
Agriculture Cooperative Act."

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the declared policy of the State to
foster the creation and growth of agricultural cooperatives as practical
vehicles for promoting self-reliance, enhancing agricultural productivity, and
increasing the income of farmers, fisherfolk, and agricultural workers.
Towards this end, the State shall establish a dedicated, streamlined
government structure to provide specialized technical, financial, and
institutional support to agricultural cooperatives.
      SEC. 3. Creation of the Bureau of Agriculture Cooperative. — The Bureau
of Agriculture Cooperative, hereinafter referred to as the Bureau, is hereby
created and attached to the Department of Agriculture (DA).
     SEC. 4. Mandate and Functions. — The Bureau shall be the primary
agency responsible for the formulation and implementation of policies, plans,
and programs for the development and empowerment of agricultural
cooperatives. It shall have the following functions:
   1. Formulation of Policies: Draft and recommend policies to the DA
      Secretary, integrating agricultural cooperatives into the national food
      security and agricultural modernization agenda.
   2. Organization and Promotion: Actively promote the organization of
      farmers, fisherfolk, and rural workers into viable agricultural
      cooperatives.
   3. Capacity Building: Provide specialized training on modern agricultural
      technologies, agribusiness management, value-chain optimization, and
      financial literacy.
   4. Financial and Technical Assistance: Facilitate access to credit, grants,
      farm machinery, and post-harvest facilities specifically earmarked for
      agricultural cooperatives.
   5. Market Linkage: Establish direct market linkages between agricultural
      cooperatives and institutional buyers, eliminating unnecessary
      middlemen.
   6. Coordination: Coordinate seamlessly with the Cooperative Development
      Authority (CDA) for the registration and regulatory compliance of
      agricultural cooperatives, ensuring that the Bureau focuses purely on
      developmental and technical agricultural support.
      SEC. 5. Organizational Structure. — The Bureau shall be headed by a
Director, who shall be assisted by an Assistant Director. Both shall be
appointed by the President of the Philippines upon the recommendation of the
Secretary of Agriculture.
       The DA Secretary shall determine the organizational structure and
staffing pattern of the Bureau, subject to the evaluation and approval of the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM) and in accordance with civil
service laws, rules, and regulations.
      SEC. 6. Transitory Provisions and Government Reorganization. — To
prevent duplication of functions and ensure the efficient reorganization of
government resources, all existing divisions, units, and programs within the
Department of Agriculture primarily dealing with the development of
agricultural cooperatives shall be collapsed and their functions, records,
equipment, and applicable unexpended appropriations transferred to the
Bureau.
      SEC. 7. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the Department of Agriculture. Thereafter, such sums as
may be necessary for the continued operation of the Bureau shall be included
in the annual General Appropriations Act.
      SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Agriculture, in
consultation with the CDA, DBM, and relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate
the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this
Act.
     SEC. 9. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.
      SEC. 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
