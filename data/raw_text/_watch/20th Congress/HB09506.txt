ESTABLISHING THE PHILIPPINE MSME FINANCING CORPORATION,
PROVIDING FOR A MODERN DEVELOPMENTAL FINANCING
FRAMEWORK FOR MICRO, SMALL, AND MEDIUM ENTERPRISES,
PROMOTING FINANCIAL INCLUSION, DIGITAL ENTERPRISE

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) are widely recognized as the
backbone of the Philippine economy, comprising the vast majority of business
establishments in the country and generating substantial employment across sectors
and regions. They play a critical role in fostering inclusive economic growth,
stimulating local production, supporting innovation, and advancing regional
development. Despite their importance, MSMEs continue to face persistent
structural challenges that constrain their growth and sustainability, particularly in
accessing adequate and affordable financing.

       Existing government financial institutions and programs have provided
various forms of support to MSMEs; however, these initiatives remain fragmented
across multiple agencies and often lack a unified developmental framework that
integrates financing with enterprise capability-building, digital transformation, and
                                                                                      1
 innovation-driven growth. There is therefore a need to establish a specialized
 institution that is explicitly mandated to address these gaps through a coherent,
 inclusive, and development-oriented approach.

        This proposed measure seeks to establish the Philippine MSME Financing
 Corporation (PMFC) as a dedicated government-owned and controlled corporation
 tasked with providing accessible, affordable, and sustainable financing programs for
 MSMEs. The Corporation shall serve as a specialized developmental financing
 institution designed not only to extend credit but also to promote enterprise
 modernization, strengthen financial capability, and support innovation,
 digitalization, and climate-resilient enterprise development.

       The bill institutionalizes a comprehensive framework that integrates financing
 with enterprise development services, financial literacy programs, and digital
 finance systems. It further introduces credit guarantee and risk-sharing mechanisms
 to expand access to credit while maintaining financial sustainability and responsible
 lending practices. The Corporation is likewise mandated to adopt risk-based and
 data-driven approaches, ensuring that lending decisions are guided by transparency,
 objectivity, and sound financial principles.

        To promote efficiency and accountability, the measure establishes a
 governance structure that includes key economic and financial agencies, private
 sector representatives, and MSME stakeholders, while instituting safeguards against
 political interference in lending decisions. It also mandates the implementation of
 digital finance systems, alternative credit scoring mechanisms, and interoperable
 platforms to modernize MSME financing and improve accessibility, especially for
 underserved sectors.

       By establishing a dedicated and modernized MSME financing institution, the
 State affirms its commitment to reducing inequality, expanding economic
 opportunities, and fostering sustainable and broad-based economic growth.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Philippine MSME
Financing Corporation Act.”


SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote inclusive economic growth, industrial development, entrepreneurship,


                                                                               1
innovation, and financial inclusion by ensuring that micro, small, and medium
enterprises (MSMEs) have access to affordable, accessible, sustainable, and
development-oriented financing.


The State recognizes that MSMEs constitute a vital pillar of the national economy,
generate employment opportunities, stimulate local production, and contribute
significantly to poverty reduction and regional economic development. The State
further recognizes that many MSMEs, particularly those in rural and underserved
communities, face structural barriers to financing due to insufficient collateral,
limited credit history, information asymmetry, and lack of access to formal financial
institutions.


Towards this end, the State shall establish a specialized developmental financing
institution that provides responsible and inclusive financing programs, promotes
enterprise modernization and digital transformation, strengthens entrepreneurial
capability, supports innovation and climate-resilient enterprises, and expands
economic opportunities for underserved sectors of society.


The State shall likewise promote transparency, financial sustainability, risk-based
governance, technological innovation, and responsible lending practices in order to
strengthen the long-term viability and developmental impact of MSME financing
programs.


SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:




                                                                                   2
(a) “MSMEs” shall refer to micro, small, and medium enterprises as defined under
Republic Act No. 9501 or the “Magna Carta for MSMEs”;


(b) “Developmental Financing” refers to financing programs intended to promote
inclusive economic development, enterprise growth, employment generation, and
financial inclusion through preferential or subsidized lending mechanisms;


(c) “Underserved Sectors” refer to individuals or enterprises with limited access to
formal financing, including rural enterprises, women entrepreneurs, persons with
disabilities, indigenous peoples, returning overseas Filipino workers, informal sector
workers, and startup enterprises;


(d) “Enterprise Development Services” refer to training, mentoring, technical
assistance, market linkage support, digital capability development, and related
services provided to MSMEs;


(e) “Credit Guarantee” refers to a risk-sharing mechanism whereby the Corporation
partially guarantees loans extended to qualified MSMEs;


(f) “Digital Finance Systems” refer to electronic, mobile-based, online, or
technology-enabled financial service platforms used for lending, payments, credit
evaluation, or enterprise support;


(g) “Green Enterprise” refers to an enterprise engaged in environmentally
sustainable, climate-resilient, energy-efficient, or low-carbon economic activities.




                                                                                       3
SEC. 4. Creation of the Philippine MSME Financing Corporation (PMFC). -
There is hereby created a government-owned and controlled corporation to be
known as the Philippine MSME Financing Corporation (PMFC), hereinafter
referred to as the “Corporation,” under the administrative supervision of the
Department of Trade and Industry (DTI).


The Corporation shall serve as a specialized developmental financing institution for
MSMEs and shall complement, and not duplicate, the mandates of government
financial institutions, including Land Bank of the Philippines, Development Bank of
the Philippines, Small Business Corporation, and other existing financing entities.


SEC. 5. Powers and Functions of the Corporation. - The Corporation shall have
the following powers and functions:


(a) Provide accessible, affordable, and development-oriented financing programs for
MSMEs;
(b) Extend startup financing, working capital loans, enterprise modernization
financing, disaster recovery loans, and expansion capital;
(c) Develop financing programs for underserved and priority sectors;
(d) Establish credit guarantee and risk-sharing programs for MSME borrowers;
(e) Develop digital finance systems and alternative credit assessment mechanisms;
(f) Partner with government financial institutions, cooperatives, private financial
institutions, fintech entities, and international development organizations;
(g) Provide enterprise development services and financial literacy programs;
(h) Promote innovation-driven, export-oriented, digital, and green enterprises;
(i) Develop performance-based and graduated lending programs; and


                                                                                      4
(j) Exercise such other powers necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.


SEC. 6. Developmental Financing Principles. - The Corporation shall adopt
developmental financing principles that prioritize:


(a) Financial inclusion and equitable access to credit;
(b) Responsible and sustainable lending practices;
(c) Risk-based and data-driven credit evaluation;
(d) Enterprise growth and long-term borrower sustainability;
(e) Regional economic development;
(f) Technology-enabled and innovation-driven enterprises;
(g) Climate resilience and sustainable enterprise development; and
(h) Economic empowerment of underserved sectors.


The Corporation shall ensure that financing programs remain financially sustainable
while maintaining developmental objectives.


SEC. 7. Loan Programs and Financing Features. - The Corporation shall establish
specialized financing programs for:


(a) Startup and early-stage enterprises;
(b) Rural and agricultural enterprises;
(c) Women-led enterprises;
(d) Digital and technology-enabled enterprises;
(e) Green and climate-resilient enterprises;
(f) Returning overseas Filipino workers;


                                                                                  5
(g) Informal sector workers seeking formalization; and
(h) MSMEs affected by calamities, economic shocks, or public emergencies.


Financing features may include:


(1) Preferential and developmental financing rates;
(2) Flexible repayment mechanisms;
(3) Grace periods for startup enterprises;
(4) Collateral-free or partially guaranteed loans for qualified borrowers;
(5) Digital loan application and processing systems; and
(6) Graduated financing pathways based on borrower performance and enterprise
growth.


SEC. 8. Credit Guarantee and Risk-Sharing Programs. - The Corporation may
establish credit guarantee facilities, co-guarantee arrangements, and risk-sharing
mechanisms with banks, cooperatives, rural financial institutions, and private
lenders to expand access to MSME financing.


The Corporation may provide partial guarantees for qualified MSME loans in
accordance with sound risk management principles.


SEC. 9. Digital Finance and Innovation Systems. - The Corporation shall establish
secure and interoperable digital finance systems that may include:


(a) Online and mobile-based loan applications;
(b) Alternative credit scoring systems;


                                                                                6
(c) Digital identity verification;
(d) Electronic payments and collections;
(e) AI-assisted credit evaluation systems;
(f) MSME data integration systems; and
(g) Cybersecurity and fraud prevention mechanisms.


All digital systems shall comply with applicable data privacy, cybersecurity, and
financial consumer protection laws.


SEC. 10. Financial Literacy and Enterprise Development. - All borrowers shall
undergo enterprise development and financial capability programs to be
implemented in coordination with:


(a) DTI Negosyo Centers;
(b) Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA);
(c) State Universities and Colleges (SUCs);
(d) Cooperatives and accredited civil society organizations;
(e) Private sector mentors and industry groups; and
(f) Online learning platforms operated or accredited by the Corporation.


Training programs shall include:
(1) Financial literacy;
(2) Credit and debt management;
(3) Digital entrepreneurship;
(4) Tax and regulatory compliance;
(5) Business planning;


                                                                               7
(6) E-commerce and digital trade; and
(7) Enterprise scaling and innovation.


SEC. 11. Organizational Structure and Governance. - The Corporation shall be
governed by a Board of Directors composed of:


(a) Secretary of Trade and Industry – Chairperson;
(b) Secretary of Finance – Vice Chairperson;
(c) Representative from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas;
(d) Representative from the National Economic and Development Authority;
(e) Representative from the Department of Science and Technology;
(f) One representative from the cooperative sector;
(g) One representative from the fintech or digital finance sector;
(h) Two representatives from the MSME sector, one of whom shall come from a
rural area;
(i) One independent director with expertise in banking, risk management, or
developmental finance; and
(j) President and Chief Executive Officer of the Corporation – ex officio member.


SEC. 12. Anti-Political Interference and Merit-Based Lending. - Loan approvals
and financing decisions shall be based on transparent, objective, and merit-based
criteria.


No elected official or public officer shall interfere in lending decisions, borrower
selection, or financing approvals.




                                                                                    8
The Corporation shall establish internal safeguards against political patronage,
conflicts of interest, and undue influence.


SEC. 13. Transparency, Accountability, and Auditing. - The Corporation shall
be subject to annual audit by the Commission on Audit (COA).


The Corporation shall submit:
(a) Quarterly reports to Congress;
(b) Annual performance evaluations to the DTI and DBM; and
(c) Public reports containing anonymized data on:
(1) borrower demographics;
(2) loan distribution;
(3) repayment and default rates;
(4) jobs generated;
(5) regional financing distribution; and
(6) enterprise survival and growth metrics.


SEC. 14. Coordination with National Agencies and Local Government Units. -
The Corporation shall coordinate with local government units, national government
agencies, cooperatives, financial institutions, and private sector stakeholders to:


(a) Identify eligible beneficiaries;
(b) Integrate financing support into Negosyo Centers and local business one-stop
shops;
(c) Promote place-based enterprise development programs;
(d) Expand digital and rural financing access; and


                                                                                      9
(e) Align MSME financing with national industrial, agricultural, export, and
innovation policies.


SEC. 15. Performance Evaluation and Congressional Review. - The Corporation
shall develop measurable performance indicators, including:


(a) Number of MSMEs financed;
(b) Rural and underserved sector penetration;
(c) Repayment and portfolio performance;
(d) Jobs generated or sustained;
(e) Enterprise growth and survival rates;
(f) Digital enterprise participation; and
(g) Economic impact indicators.


Congress shall conduct a comprehensive review of this Act every five (5) years.


SEC. 16. - Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act, including the organization, establishment, and operationalization of the
Philippine MSME Financing Corporation, shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the Department of Trade and Industry or such other available funds
as may be certified by the Department of Budget and Management.


Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued operation and
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.




                                                                                  10
The Corporation may also receive and utilize funds from official development
assistance, grants, donations, borrowings, loan repayments, service income, and
other lawful sources, subject to applicable budgeting, auditing, and accounting laws,
rules, and regulations.


SEC. 17. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within one hundred twenty
(120) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DTI, in coordination with the
Department of Finance, Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, Department of Budget and
Management, Commission on Audit, and relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the
implementing rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of
this Act.


SEC. 18. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.


SEC. 19. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


SEC. 20. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
