                           AN ACT
CONVERTING THE ALIAGA – QUEZON – GUIMBA ROAD STRETCHING FROM
BARANGAY BUCOT IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ALIAGA TO BARANGAY
SARANAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GUIMBA, ALL IN THE PROVINCE OF
NUEVA ECIJA INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The development of an integrated and efficient road network is vital to strengthening
rural economies and ensuring equitable access to opportunities. The Aliaga – Quezon – Guimba
Road, spanning approximately 20.55 kilometers, connects the municipalities of Aliaga,
Quezon, and Guimba in the Province of Nueva Ecija and serves as a major inter-municipal
route.

        The road links key local thoroughfares and provides access to major arteries in Central
Luzon. It facilitates the movement of agricultural products, supports local enterprises, and
enhances mobility for residents traveling for work, education, and essential services. With
increasing economic activity and traffic volume in the locality, the road has become a strategic
link that extends beyond purely local significance.

       Converting this road into a national road will ensure its systematic upgrading, regular
maintenance, and long-term inclusion in the national infrastructure program, thereby
promoting sustained economic growth in the First Legislative District of Nueva Ecija.
       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. The Aliaga – Quezon – Guimba Road stretching from Barangay Bucot in the
Municipality of Aliaga to Barangay Saranay in the Municipality of Guimba, all in the Province
of Nueva Ecija is hereby converted into a national road.

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the Department’s
program the improvement, repair and maintenance of the road, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
