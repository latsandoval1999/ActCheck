                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Municipality of Cuenca in the Province of Batangas is home to
several natural attractions that contribute to the province’s growing
eco-tourism industry. Among these is the Lumampao View Deck located in
Barangay Don Juan, a scenic destination known for its panoramic
landscapes in magnificent view of the Taal lake and Mt. Maculot, cool
mountain atmosphere, and peaceful environment that provide opportunities
for recreation, relaxation, and outdoor activities for both residents and
visitors.

      As tourism continues to play a vital role in local economic
development, there is a need to strengthen and institutionalize government
support for emerging eco-tourism destinations such as the Lumampao View
Deck. Declaring the area as an official tourism site will encourage
sustainable development, improve accessibility and infrastructure, generate
employment and livelihood opportunities for local communities, and
promote Cuenca as a family-friendly and environmentally responsible
destination in Batangas.
      This measure seeks to declare the Lumampao View Deck as a
tourism site. This bill ensures that tourism development in the area shall be
undertaken in a manner that preserves its natural beauty and ecological
integrity through coordinated planning among the Department of Tourism
(DOT), the Tourism Infrastructure and Enterprise Zone Authority (TIEZA),
and the local government unit of Cuenca, Batangas.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
earnestly sought.

                        AN ACT​
DECLARING THE LUMAMPAO VIEW DECK IN BARANGAY DON JUAN,
  MUNICIPALITY OF CUENCA, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS AS AN
                    ECOTOURISM SITE

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as “Lumampao
Eco-tourism Site Act.”

     SEC. 2. Tourism Site. - The Lumampao Viewdeck in Barangay Don
Juan, Municipality of Cuenca, Province of Batangas is hereby declared as
an eco-tourism site. As such, it shall be accorded priority development by
the Department of Tourism (DOT), subject to the rules and regulations
governing the development of tourism sites.

       SEC. 3. Development Plan. - The DOT, coordination with the
Tourism Infrastructure and Enterprise Zone Authority (TIEZA) and other
concerned agencies of government shall, within one (1) year from the
approval of this Act, prepare a development plan involving construction,
installation and maintenance of such appropriate facilities and infrastructure
as shall enhance tourism in said are, provided, that said development plan
shall ensure the preservation of the natural beauty and recreational
significance of the area.

      SEC. 4. Implementing Agency. - The DOT shall take immediate
steps to implement said development plan with the Municipal Government
of Cuenca and shall incorporate the same in the Department’s overall
tourism development program for the year following the approval of this
Act.

      SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOT, in consultation with relevant
local government units, shall issue the necessary rules and regulations to
implement this Act.

      SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, and
issuances, or portions thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act, are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 7. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
