                                           EXPLANATORY NOTE
       The 1987 Constitution mandates the State to protect and promote the right to
health of the people and to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health
development.1 As Artificial Intelligence (AI) Systems have become increasingly
accessible to the public and capable of use in various fields, including healthcare,
which is imbued with public interest, their ethical regulation has become a matter of
national interest. These constitutional mandates must therefore be applied to
emerging technologies that may affect patient care, public health, privacy, due
process, equal protection, and human dignity. The State therefore has a compelling
interest to ensure that the development, deployment, and use of AI Systems in

individual rights.
       AI Systems may assist in diagnosis, support clinical decisions, facilitate
access to health services, aid in administration, and strengthen public health
responses. However, without a clear legal framework, their use may also result in
unsafe recommendations, biased outcomes, privacy violations, lack of transparency,
and uncertainty as to accountability when harm occurs. Recognizing that Artificial
Intelligence (AI) Systems are increasingly accessible and capable of assisting the
healthcare field, despite the absence of a comprehensive national AI law, this
measure seeks to regulate its ethical development, deployment, and use consistent
with internationally accepted principles on human autonomy, transparency,
explainability, inclusiveness, accountability, safety, data privacy, and meaningful
human oversight, particularly the guidance of the World Health Organization.2

       This bill establishes a legal and ethical framework for the responsible use of
AI Systems in healthcare. It protects the rights of patients to human oversight,
information, privacy and data protection, safe and lawful AI-assisted healthcare,
transparency and explainability, equal and non-discriminatory treatment, and
redress. It likewise imposes duties upon Healthcare Providers and Healthcare
Institutions to disclose the use, purpose, and extent of AI Systems, answer
reasonable patient questions, ensure human oversight over significant clinical
decisions, protect patient data, prevent bias and discrimination, use AI Systems only
for their disclosed intended purpose and extent, maintain pre-deployment
documentation, monitor performance, and report and address material incidents,
malfunctions, or risks to patient safety.
       The goal of this measure is not to hinder innovation, but to ensure that
innovation remains ethical, accountable, and centered on the Filipino patient.
Artificial Intelligence Systems in healthcare are intended only to assist healthcare
delivery and shall not replace the professional judgment, accountability, and
responsibility of Healthcare Providers. While AI Systems may support diagnosis,
treatment, monitoring, administration, and other healthcare-related services, final
responsibility for patient care must remain with human professionals and healthcare
institutions. By establishing clear rights, duties, safeguards, and accountability
mechanisms, this bill seeks to build public trust and ensure that AI-assisted

                         AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A LEGAL AND ETHICAL FRAMEWORK FOR THE
DEVELOPMENT, DEPLOYMENT, GOVERNANCE, AND USE OF
ARTIFICIAL   INTELLIGENCE    SYSTEMS  IN   HEALTHCARE,
PROTECTING PATIENT RIGHTS, PROMOTING ACCOUNTABILITY,
TRANSPARENCY, SAFETY, AND DATA PRIVACY, PRESCRIBING THE

INSTITUTIONS, PROHIBITING CERTAIN ACTS, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Ethical Use of
Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare Act."

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - Pursuant to Article II, Section 15 of the
1987 Constitution, which provides that "The State shall protect and promote the right
to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them," and consistent
with the policy of Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise known as the Universal Health
Care Act, the State shall ensure that Artificial Intelligence (AI) Systems are
developed, deployed, and used in a manner that upholds patient safety, human
dignity, transparency, accountability, and the confidentiality of personal health
information. Artificial Intelligence Systems shall serve only as tools to assist
healthcare delivery and shall not replace the independent professional judgment,
responsibility, and accountability of duly licensed healthcare professionals.

      Section 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

      (a) Artificial Intelligence (AI) System. – Refers to a machine-based system
      that uses algorithms capable of learning or inferring from data to perform tasks
      or generate predictions, recommendations, decisions, or other outputs,
      including any similar or successor technologies.

      (b) Healthcare Provider. – Refers to any natural person who provides,
      performs, delivers, or assists in the provision of healthcare, medical, or allied
      health services, whether independently or under the supervision of another
      person, and whether licensed, registered, certified, accredited, or otherwise
      authorized under applicable law.

      (c) Healthcare Institution. – Refers to hospitals, clinics, infirmaries,
      laboratories, diagnostic centers, pharmacies, telemedicine providers,
      rehabilitation centers, healthcare research institutions, Health Maintenance
      Organizations (HMOs), and all other public or private entities engaged in
      healthcare delivery.
      (d) Human Oversight. - Refers to the supervision exercised by a duly
      licensed healthcare professional who retains the authority and responsibility
      to review, interpret, modify, reject, or override the outputs or
      recommendations of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) System.

      Section 4. Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all public and private
Healthcare Providers and Healthcare Institutions within the Philippines that develop,
procure, deploy, operate, maintain, distribute, or use Artificial Intelligence Systems
in the provision, administration, or support of healthcare services.

      Section 5. Objectives. - This Act seeks to:

      (a) Establish a comprehensive legal and ethical framework for the
      development, deployment, and governance of Artificial Intelligence Systems
      in healthcare;

      (b) Protect human dignity, patient autonomy, privacy, safety, and fundamental
      rights in the use of Artificial Intelligence Systems;

      (c) Promote the responsible, transparent, explainable, equitable, secure, and
      accountable use of Artificial Intelligence Systems in healthcare;

      (d) Ensure that Artificial Intelligence Systems support, and do not replace, the
      professional judgment and accountability of Healthcare Providers;

      (e) Foster public trust and confidence in Artificial Intelligence-assisted
      healthcare through effective oversight and governance; and

      (f) Encourage innovation that advances accessible, equitable, and high-quality
      healthcare while safeguarding the public interest.
      Section 6. Rights of Patients in AI-Assisted Healthcare. - Every patient
whose healthcare involves the use of an Artificial Intelligence System shall be
entitled to the following rights:

      (a) Right to Human Oversight. – To have significant clinical decisions
      involving an Artificial Intelligence System subject to the review, supervision,
      and approval of a licensed Healthcare Provider.

      (b) Right to Information. – To be immediately informed, in a clear and
      understandable manner, whenever an Artificial Intelligence System is used in
      the provision of healthcare or any healthcare-related service where such use
      may materially affect the patient's care, rights, or interests.

      (c) Right to Privacy and Data Protection. – To the confidentiality and
      security of their personal and health information, and to ensure that such
      information is collected, processed, stored, used, and disclosed only with the
      patient's consent or as otherwise authorized by applicable laws.

      (d) Right to Safe and Lawful AI-Assisted Healthcare. – To receive
      healthcare supported only by Artificial Intelligence Systems that are used for
      their disclosed intended purpose and extent, and that have complied with the
      requirements of this Act and its implementing rules and regulations.

      (e) Right to Transparency and Explainability. – To receive, upon request
      and to the extent reasonably practicable, an explanation of the role and
      material basis of an Artificial Intelligence System in decisions that
      significantly affect the patient's healthcare.
      (f) Right to Equal and Non-Discriminatory Treatment. – To be protected
      from unlawful discrimination arising from the development, deployment, or
      use of an Artificial Intelligence System, and to receive healthcare without bias
      based on race, ethnicity, sex, age, disability, socioeconomic status, religion,
      or any other protected characteristic.

      (g) Right to Accountability and Redress. – To seek appropriate remedies
      for harm arising from the use of an Artificial Intelligence System in
      healthcare, in accordance with this Act and its implementing rules and
      regulations, without prejudice to any rights or remedies available under
      existing laws.

      Section 7. Duties of Healthcare Providers and Healthcare Institutions. -
Healthcare Providers and Healthcare Institutions shall:

      (a) Inform patients, in a clear and understandable manner, whenever an
      Artificial Intelligence System is used in the provision of healthcare or any
      healthcare-related service where such use may materially affect the patient's
      care, rights, or interests.

      Such disclosure shall include, but shall not be limited to, the use, purpose, and
      extent of the Artificial Intelligence System in the patient's healthcare.
      Healthcare Providers shall, to the extent reasonably practicable, answer the
      patient's reasonable questions regarding its use in a clear and understandable
      manner.
      (b) Ensure that significant clinical decisions involving an Artificial
      Intelligence System remain subject to meaningful human oversight, review,
      supervision, and approval of a licensed Healthcare Provider.

      (c) Protect the confidentiality, privacy, and security of patients' personal and
      health information, and ensure its lawful collection, processing, storage, use,
      and disclosure in accordance with applicable laws.

      (d) Use Artificial Intelligence Systems only for their disclosed intended
      purpose and extent, and ensure that such systems are appropriately validated,
      monitored, and maintained to promote their safety, accuracy, reliability, and
      effectiveness, in accordance with this Act and its implementing rules and
      regulations.

      (e) Take reasonable measures to prevent bias and unlawful discrimination in
      the development, deployment, and use of Artificial Intelligence Systems, and
      ensure the equitable provision of healthcare services.

      (f) Comply with such other duties and standards as may be prescribed under
      this Act and its implementing rules and regulations.

      Section 8. Pre-Deployment Documentation and Performance Monitoring of
Artificial Intelligence Systems. - Prior to the deployment or use of an Artificial
Intelligence System, Healthcare Providers and Healthcare Institutions shall prepare,
maintain, and, when required under this Act or its implementing rules and
regulations, submit documentation demonstrating the Artificial Intelligence
System's intended purpose, validity, accuracy, reliability, and safeguards for its safe
and ethical use, in accordance with this Act and such standards as may be prescribed
in the implementing rules and regulations.

      Healthcare Providers and Healthcare Institutions shall likewise monitor the
performance of Artificial Intelligence Systems and promptly identify, document,
report, and address any material incident, malfunction, or risk to patient safety, in
accordance with this Act and such standards as may be prescribed in its
implementing rules and regulations.

      Section 9. Prohibited Acts. - The following acts are prohibited:

      (a) Deploying or using an Artificial Intelligence System without complying
      with the requirements of this Act and its implementing rules and regulations;

      (b) Using an Artificial Intelligence System for a purpose or to an extent other
      than its disclosed intended purpose and extent;

      (c) Failing to provide the required disclosure to patients regarding the use of
      an Artificial Intelligence System as prescribed under this Act;

      (d) Making or implementing significant clinical decisions solely on the basis
      of an Artificial Intelligence System without meaningful human oversight,
      where such oversight is required under this Act;

      (e) Collecting, processing, using, disclosing, or retaining personal or health
      information through an Artificial Intelligence System in violation of
      applicable laws or this Act;
      (f) Knowingly deploying, operating, or maintaining an Artificial Intelligence
      System that has not been appropriately validated or that poses an unreasonable
      risk to patient safety;

      (g) Knowingly concealing, falsifying, altering, or failing to document or
      report any material incident, malfunction, or risk to patient safety involving
      an Artificial Intelligence System, as required under this Act;

      (h) Using an Artificial Intelligence System in a manner that results in unlawful
      discrimination or violates the rights of patients under this Act; and

      (i) Obstructing or refusing any lawful inspection, investigation, or other
      regulatory action conducted pursuant to this Act.


      Section 10. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the implementation
of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.


      Section 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within one hundred
eighty (180) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health (DOH),
in coordination with the Department of Information and Communications
Technology (DICT), the National Privacy Commission, the Professional Regulation
Commission, and such other concerned government agencies and other relevant
stakeholders as may be necessary, shall promulgate the implementing rules and
regulations (IRR) for the effective implementation of this Act.

      Section 12. Construction and Interpretation. - Any doubt on the
interpretation of any provision in this Act shall be interpreted in favor of the patients.
      Section 13. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in full force and effect.


      Section 14. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, presidential proclamations, rules and regulations or parts thereof contrary to
or inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


      Section 15. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
