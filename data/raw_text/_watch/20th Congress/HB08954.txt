                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to grant lifetime validity to Philippine passports issued to
senior citizens, thereby easing the burden of repeated passport renewals and
recognizing the needs and contributions of the elderly sector.

       Under existing law, Philippine passports are issued with limited validity,
requiring periodic renewal regardless of age. While this system ensures updated
identification records, it also imposes logistical, financial, and physical burdens on
senior citizens, many of whom face mobility constraints, health challenges, and
limited access to passport processing facilities. The process of securing
appointments, traveling to consular offices, and complying with documentary
requirements can be particularly taxing for elderly individuals.

       This measure addresses these concerns by allowing passports issued or
renewed upon reaching sixty (60) years of age to have lifetime validity. At the same
time, the measure retains safeguards by allowing the government to limit passport
validity when necessary in the interest of national security, economic stability, or
other compelling considerations. It also provides for the issuance of new passports
when required, ensuring that the integrity of the passport system is maintained.

       By removing the need for repeated renewals, the bill promotes convenience,
reduces unnecessary expenses, and ensures that senior citizens can continue to
access identification and travel documents without undue hardship, while
simplifying access to essential government services and reducing bureaucratic
barriers that disproportionately affect them.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                             AN ACT
GRANTING LIFETIME VALIDITY OF PHILIPPINE PASSPORTS ISSUED TO
SENIOR CITIZENS, FURTHER AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 10
OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8239, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “PHILIPPINE
PASSPORT ACT OF 1996”, AS AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This act shall be known as the “Senior Citizens’ Lifetime
Passport Act”.

       SEC. 2. Validity. – Section 10 of Republic Act (RA) No. 8239, otherwise known
as the “Philippine Passport Act of 1996”, as amended by RA No. 10928, is hereby
further amended to read as follows:

              “SEC. 10. Validity. – Regular passports issued TO
       INDIVIDUALS FROM EIGHTEEN (18) YEARS OF AGE AND
       ABOVE under this Act shall be valid for a period of ten (10) years:
       Provided, however, That for individuals under eighteen (18) years of age,
       only a passport with five (5)- year validity shall be issued, AND FOR
       SENIOR CITIZENS ISSUED OR RENEWED AT THE AGE OF
       SIXTY (60) YEARS OLD SHALL HAVE LIFETIME VALIDITY:
       Provided, further, That the issuing authority may limit the period of
       validity to less than ten (10) years, whenever in the national economic
       interest or political stability of the country such restriction is necessary:
       Provided, finally, That a new passport may be issued to replace one
       which validity has expired, the old passport being returned to the
       holder after cancellation.”

       SEC. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – To carry out the provisions of
this Act, the Department of Foreign Affairs, in coordination with the National
Commission on Senior Citizens shall, within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of
this Act, issue the implementing rules and regulations for this Act.
