                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Persons with disabilities (PWDs) include those who have long-term
physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairments which in interaction with
various barriers may hinder their full and effective participation in society on an
equal basis with others, as recognized under the United Nations Convention on
the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.

       Along with the elderly, PWDs are vulnerable because of the multiple
barriers they face on a daily basis. Simply surviving is already a feat, thus, even
just the thought of being able to live lives they can find reason to value can feel
like a luxury. This learned helplessness further discourages members of these
two vulnerable groups from participating in public and civic affairs. We therefore
have an obligation to introduce legislation to assist the elderly and persons with
disabilities and to remove the barriers preventing them from effectively and
reasonably participating at all levels of social, political and economic decision-
making, consistent with Article XIII of the 1987 Constitution.

       The Congress was mandated to provide a system for securing the secrecy
and sanctity of the ballot. Thus, it would also be compulsory to design a
procedure for the disabled to vote without the assistance of other persons, as
Article V, Section 2 of the Constitution directs. This bill seeks to grant PWDs and
senior citizens the privilege to cast their votes at an earlier date at accessible
establishments designated by the Commission on Elections (COMELEC) to
ensure that they are able to exercise their constitutionally granted right to vote.

      There is no right that better embodies the concept of democracy and
people’s sovereignty than one’s right to suffrage. It is our duty to protect this
right and remove the barriers that prevent qualified citizens of the Philippines
from its effective exercise.
In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
   PROVIDING FOR EARLY VOTING BY QUALIFIED SENIOR CITIZENS,
 PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES, AND OTHER VULNERABLE SECTORS IN
                NATIONAL AND LOCAL ELECTIONS

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Early Voting for
Senior Citizens and Persons with Disabilities Act”.

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to ensure that the right of suffrage is exercised freely and without barriers,
and to make voting convenient for senior citizens and persons with disabilities
(PWDs) by giving them the option to vote earlier than the date set for the
elections.

       SECTION 3. Early Voting. – For national and local elections, there shall be
early voting where qualified senior citizens and PWDs may choose to cast their
ballot at accessible establishments designated by the Commission on Elections
(COMELEC), on such dates as the COMELEC may set within thirty (30) days
before the date set for the elections.

      The COMELEC may, after due consultation, extend the option of early
voting to pregnant women and to other vulnerable sectors or similarly situated
voters.

       SECTION 4. Registration. – There shall be a nationwide registration for
senior citizens and PWDs to qualify them to avail of this early voting privilege.
Those who are not registered under this Act shall vote on Election Day. The
registration shall be simple and free of charge, and shall build on the existing
voter records of the COMELEC.
     SECTION 5. Obligations of the COMELEC. – To ensure the effective
implementation of this Act, the COMELEC shall:

      (a)   Keep a record of senior citizens and PWDs who are registered voters;
      (b)   Design a registration system, in coordination with local government
            units, the National Council on Disability Affairs, the National
            Commission of Senior Citizens, the Commission on Human Rights,
            the Department of Health, the Department of the Interior and Local
            Government, and organizations of senior citizens and PWDs, that
            allows senior citizens and PWDs to register and opt for early voting;
      (c)   Ensure that polling places are safe and accessible to senior citizens
            and PWDs, and properly fitted with the necessary communication,
            visual and physical aids, as mandated under Republic Act No.
            10366 entitled, “An Act Authorizing the Commission on Elections to
            Establish Precincts Assigned to Accessible Polling Places Exclusively
            for Persons with Disabilities and Senior Citizens”;
      (d)   Adopt safeguards to secure the ballots cast during early voting,
            protect their secrecy, and ensure that these are counted only
            together with the ballots cast on Election Day; and
      (e)   Mount an intensive information campaign on early voting for
            qualified senior citizens and PWDs, using accessible formats and
            local languages.

       SECTION 6. Appropriations. – The initial amount for the implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of the
COMELEC. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued
implementation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 7. Applicability of Other Laws. – The provisions of Batas
Pambansa Blg. 881 or the Omnibus Election Code; Republic Act No. 7166,
entitled, “An Act Providing for Synchronized National and Local Elections and for
Electoral Reforms, Authorizing Appropriations Therefor, and for Other
Purposes”; Republic Act No. 8436, as amended by Republic Act No. 9369; and
other election laws not inconsistent herewith shall be applied to facilitate early
voting for qualified senior citizens and PWDs.

      SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from its effectivity, the COMELEC shall promulgate the rules and
regulations for the implementation of this Act, in consultation with the agencies
and organizations mentioned in Section 5 of this Act.

       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions or parts unaffected
shall remain in full force and effect.
      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules, and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 11. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
