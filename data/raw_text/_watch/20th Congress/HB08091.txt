                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to declare December 28 of every year as a special nonworking holiday
in the Municipality of Iguig, Province of Cagayan, in recognition of its founding
anniversary. The proposed measure aims to remind the residents of Iguig of their rich
heritage and historical origins, and to provide them with the opportunity to collectively
acknowledge and celebrate this significant milestone annually.

The Municipality of Iguig is a fourth-class municipality composed of twenty-three (23)
barangays. It is widely known as the “Holy Land of the North” and the “Pottery Center
of Cagayan,” reflecting both its deep religious roots and its longstanding pottery
industry. Among its most notable landmarks are the historic Iguig Parish Church, one
of the oldest churches in the Philippines, and the renowned Callao Caves, a significant
archaeological and natural site in the country.

The name “Iguig” is derived from the Ilocano word “Iggig,” meaning “a place where
they gather.” This etymology underscores the town’s historical role as a central hub
for surrounding communities and as a strategic meeting point for trade, owing largely
to its location along the Cagayan River.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought to honor the
enduring legacy of Iguig and to strengthen civic pride and cultural identity among its
people.

                           AN ACT
DECLARING DECEMBER 28 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF IGUIG, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, IN
COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. December 28 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking
holiday in the Municipality of Iguig, Province of Cagayan, in commemoration of its
founding anniversary

SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
