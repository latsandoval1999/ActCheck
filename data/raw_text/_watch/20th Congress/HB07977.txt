                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to exempt from all national and local taxes the incentives, rewards,
bonuses, donations, gifts, endowments, and other forms of emoluments received by
national athletes and their coaches in connection with international sports competitions.

       Filipino athletes who compete and win in international sports competitions bring
immense honor to the country. Their victories elevate the nation’s standing in the global
arena and inspire millions of Filipinos. However, despite the intent of existing law to
reward excellence in sports, financial incentives granted to athletes and coaches remain
subject to taxation, thereby diminishing the full measure of recognition and support that
the State seeks to provide.

        Republic Act No. 10699, otherwise known as the “National Athletes and Coaches
Benefits and Incentives Act”, grants cash incentives and other benefits to medalists in
prestigious competitions such as the Olympic Games, Paralympic Games, Asian Games,
and Southeast Asian Games. Yet, without explicit tax exemption, portions of these
incentives are reduced through income and other related taxes. This runs counter to the
spirit of the law, which aims to fully reward achievement and encourage continued
excellence in sports.

       This measure ensures that all incentives and related financial support received by
national athletes and coaches in connection with international sports competitions are
exempt from national and local taxes. It also provides tax deductibility for donations
and contributions directly used for training and competition-related expenses

       By removing the tax burden on these incentives, the State affirms its commitment
to genuinely support and honor its athletes and coaches who dedicate their lives to
representing the country.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
TO EXEMPT NATIONAL ATHLETES AND COACHES FROM TAXES
RECEIVED FROM INCENTIVES, REWARDS, BONUSES, AND OTHER
EMOLUMENTS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10699,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “NATIONAL ATHLETES AND COACHES
BENEFITS AND INCENTIVES ACT”, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Tax Exemption for
National Athletes and Coaches Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Principle. — The State shall give priority to sports to
foster patriotism and nationalism, accelerate social progress, and promote total
human liberation and development. Towards this end, the State shall look after the
welfare of national athletes and coaches by providing benefits and incentives for
qualified athletes and coaches who compete or win in international sports
competitions and bring honor and recognition to the country.

       SEC. 3. A new Section, designated as Section 4-A, is hereby inserted in
Republic Act No. 10699, otherwise known as the “National Athletes and Coaches
Benefits and Incentives Act”, to read as follows:

           “SEC. 4-A. EXEMPTION FROM TAXES OF INCENTIVES,
      REWARDS, BONUSES AND OTHER FORMS OF EMOLUMENTS
      RECEIVED BY COMPETING OR WINNING NATIONAL ATHLETES
      AND COACHES. — ALL INCENTIVES, REWARDS, BONUSES
      AND OTHER FORMS OF EMOLUMENTS, AS WELL AS
      DONATIONS, GIFTS, ENDOWMENTS AND CONTRIBUTIONS,
      WHETHER FROM PUBLIC OR PRIVATE PERSONS OR ENTITIES,
      RECEIVED BY NATIONAL ATHLETES WHO COMPETE OR WIN
      IN INTERNATIONAL SPORTS COMPETITIONS AS DEFINED IN
      THIS ACT, INCLUDING THOSE RECEIVED BY THEIR
      RESPECTIVE COACHES, SHALL BE EXEMPT FROM ANY AND

       SUBJECT TO RULES AND REGULATIONS TO BE ISSUED BY
       THE SECRETARY OF FINANCE, AS RECOMMENDED BY THE
       COMMISSIONER OF INTERNAL REVENUE: PROVIDED,

       INCOME FOR PURPOSES OF COMPUTING THE TAXABLE
       INCOME OF THE DONOR IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE
       PROVISIONS OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE
       OF 1997, AS AMENDED.

           THE   EXEMPTION     FROM   DONOR’S   TAX AND
       DEDUCTIBILITY FROM GROSS INCOME PROVIDED HEREIN
       MAY BE AVAILED OF WITHIN ONE (1) YEAR BEFORE THE
       COMMENCEMENT OF THE INTERNATIONAL SPORTS
       COMPETITION WHERE THE NATIONAL ATHLETE WILL
       COMPETE IN, AS CERTIFIED BY THE RESPECTIVE BODIES
       THAT ACCREDITED THEM AND THEIR COACHES, WHICH
       MAY BE THE PHILIPPINE SPORTS COMMISSION, PHILIPPINE
       OLYMPIC COMMITTEE, OR THE NATIONAL PARALYMPIC
       COMMITTEE OF THE PHILIPPINES, AND WITHIN THREE (3)
       MONTHS     AFTER    THE    INTERNATIONAL    SPORTS
       COMPETITION.   THE EXEMPTIONS AND       PRIVILEGES
       PROVIDED HEREIN SHALL TAKE EFFECT ON JANUARY 1, 2021
       AND SHALL NOT BE AVAILED IN LIEU OF ANY TAX
       EXEMPTION OR PRIVILEGE GRANTED UNDER EXISTING
       LAWS.”

        SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within thirty (30) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance shall, upon recommendation by the
Bureau of Internal Revenue, promulgate rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act: Provided, That failure to promulgate said rules and
regulations shall not prevent the implementation of the provisions of this Act.

        SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.
