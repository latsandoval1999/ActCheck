                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE


        Zamboanga City and the Zamboanga Peninsula occupy a strategic position at the confluence
of the Sulu and Celebes Seas, one of the world’s richest marine corridors. The region is recognized as
the Sardine Capital of the Philippines and serves as a major hub for tuna processing, seaweed
production, and shipbuilding. These industries sustain thousands of Filipino families and contribute
significantly to the nation’s blue economy. Yet, despite its abundant resources, the region remains
constrained by limited research capacity, inadequate technology transfer, and the absence of a
world-class academic and industrial ecosystem to fully harness its maritime potential.

       The Philippines faces a critical human capital challenge. According to the World Bank Human
Capital Index, Filipino children born today are projected to achieve only 52% of their potential
productivity as future workers due to gaps in health and education investments (HCI score: 0.516).
Similarly, the country’s Human Development Index (HDI) stands at 0.720, below the global average
of 0.744, while the Zamboanga Peninsula lags further behind with an HDI of 0.674, among the
lowest in Mindanao. These figures underscore systemic disparities in education and skills
development.

       The urgency is amplified by findings from the 2024 Functional Literacy, Education and Mass
Media Survey (FLEMMS): while 93.1% of Filipinos aged 10–64 are basically literate, only 70.8%
are functionally literate, meaning nearly 3 in 10 Filipinos struggle with comprehension—a gap most
severe in regions with high poverty, including Zamboanga Peninsula. This learning crisis limits
workforce readiness and perpetuates socio-economic vulnerability.

       Against this backdrop, the establishment of the Zamboanga City State University for Marine
Sciences and Technology (ZCSUMST) is not merely a regional initiative, it is a national imperative.
This specialized, autonomous, and research-intensive state university will serve as the premier hub
for fisheries science, aquaculture innovation, shipbuilding technology, and blue economy
development, integrating science, enterprise, and policy across the entire marine value chain.

        The University will host centers of excellence for sardine and tuna repopulation, marine
biosciences, seaweed bioproducts, shipbuilding and maritime engineering, and climate and water
systems. It will institutionalize a Blue Economy Innovation and Business Hub, a Smart Campus
Framework, and a Climate Resilience Observatory to align education and research with sustainable
coastal development.
        To attract investment and global partnerships, the measure provides fiscal incentives,
including a 15-year income tax holiday for BOI or PEZA-registered enterprises engaged in R&D,
hatchery, or shipbuilding operations within the University, followed by a preferential 5% tax rate,
consistent with the CREATE Act. It also introduces the Fisherfolk Equivalency Program, granting
academic credits for traditional maritime skills, thereby empowering fisherfolk and maritime workers
to access higher education.

         The proposed University aligns with the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023–2028, the
ASEAN Blue Economy Framework, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs),
particularly Goals 2 (Zero Hunger), 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure), 13 (Climate Action),
and 14 (Life Below Water). It is not a mere campus conversion but the creation of a world-class
institution that fuses science, industry, culture, and sustainability for the long-term prosperity of the
Zamboanga Peninsula and the nation.

        Given the region’s strategic maritime position, its economic potential, and the pressing need
to address human capital and literacy gaps, the immediate passage of this Act is earnestly sought to
transform Zamboanga into a global center for marine sciences and innovation.

      SEC. 1 Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga City State University for
Marine Sciences and Technology (ZCSUMST) Charter of 2025.”

        SEC. 2 Declaration of State Policy and Principle. It is the policy of the State to promote
innovation-driven and sustainable development of the marine, fisheries, and maritime sectors through
education, research, and technology transfer. Recognizing Zamboanga City as the Sardine Capital of
the Philippines and a major hub for tuna canning, shipbuilding, and seaweed industries, the State
shall establish a specialized university to advance science, innovation, blue economy growth, and
socio-cultural development across the Zamboanga Peninsula.

       SEC. 3 Definition of Terms.

For purposes of this Act, the following shall be defined as follows:

       1) Blue Economy.

       Refers to the sustainable use, management, and conservation of ocean, coastal, and inland
water resources to promote economic growth, improved livelihoods, social inclusion, and
environmental integrity. It encompasses all economic activities that derive their value from the seas
and aquatic ecosystems, including but not limited to fisheries, aquaculture, marine biotechnology,
maritime transport, coastal and marine tourism, shipbuilding, renewable energy, and ecosystem
services, pursued in a manner that ensures ecological balance, intergenerational equity, and resilience
to climate change.

        The Blue Economy framework integrates science, policy, and innovation to achieve inclusive
and sustainable development in line with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs),
particularly SDG 14 (“Life Below Water”).

       2) Fisherfolk Equivalency Program (FEP)
        Refers to an educational recognition, equivalency, and accreditation framework that validates
and formalizes the experiential learning, indigenous knowledge, and technical competencies of
fisherfolk, navigators, seafarers, shipbuilders, aquaculture workers, and other maritime practitioners.
        Under this program, individuals who have acquired significant expertise through professional
experience or traditional practice may be granted academic credits or degrees upon assessment by the
University, consistent with standards set by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the
Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA).

      The FEP promotes social equity, lifelong learning, and the integration of traditional
knowledge into formal higher education and research systems.

       3) Foreign Research and Innovation Partners

        Refers to duly recognized international universities, research institutions, non-government
organizations, or private enterprises registered under the Board of Investments (BOI) or the
Philippine Economic Zone Authority (PEZA), and accredited by the University to collaborate in
scientific research, technology transfer, and capacity development.

       These partners may engage in joint research projects, infrastructure investments, faculty and
student exchanges, and knowledge-sharing initiatives aimed at advancing innovation, sustainable
development, and global cooperation in marine sciences, fisheries, and maritime technology.

       4) Highly Technical and Highly Specialized Professors or Experts

        Refers to individuals possessing advanced academic credentials, professional licenses, or
internationally recognized expertise in highly specialized areas of study such as marine sciences,
oceanography, fisheries management, aquaculture systems, shipbuilding, marine and mechanical
engineering, maritime education, biosciences, and allied disciplines.

       They may be appointed by the University as permanent, visiting, or adjunct professors,
research fellows, or technical consultants. Their engagement shall support advanced instruction,
capacity building, research innovation, and technology transfer within the University’s programs and
research centers.

       5) Sardine and Tuna Repopulation Program

        Refers to a comprehensive, science-based initiative designed to restore, conserve, and sustain
sardine, tuna, and other pelagic fish stocks through hatchery development, broodstock management,
selective breeding, habitat rehabilitation, and the implementation of closed-season and
ecosystem-based management practices.

        The program shall integrate applied research, modern aquaculture technologies,
climate-resilient systems, and community-based resource management. It shall be implemented in
partnership with relevant government agencies, local government units, and industry stakeholders to
ensure long-term marine biodiversity conservation and food security.
        ​
        6) Zamboanga Peninsula Blue Economy Zone (ZPBEZ)

       Refers to the integrated academic, research, innovation, and industrial ecosystem established
under this Act, encompassing the University’s main and extension campuses, laboratories, hatcheries,
shipyards, aquaculture farms, and partner institutions throughout the Zamboanga Peninsula and
adjoining maritime areas. The Zone shall serve as a living laboratory and development platform for
the Blue Economy, promoting sustainable fisheries and aquaculture, marine biodiversity
conservation, shipbuilding and maritime technology, renewable energy, and coastal resource
management.
       It shall foster collaboration among government, academia, industry, and communities to
generate inclusive economic growth and enhance the Philippines’ leadership in marine sciences and
maritime innovation.
       ​




      SEC. 4. Establishment of the Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and
Technology.

       Pursuant to the State’s commitment to promote inclusive, innovation-driven, and sustainable
development in the marine, fisheries, and maritime sectors, this Act hereby establishes the
Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and Technology (ZCSUMST) as a specialized,
autonomous, and research-intensive institution of higher learning.

        The University shall be created through the conversion of the Zamboanga State College of
Marine Sciences and Technology (ZSCMST), which has long served as a center of excellence in
fisheries, marine sciences, and allied disciplines in Western Mindanao. The College’s legacy of
academic instruction, scientific research, and community extension has significantly contributed to
food security, environmental stewardship, and socio-economic advancement in the Zamboanga
Peninsula and its neighboring regions.

        As part of its institutional expansion and regional outreach, the College previously established
Site C in Barangay Victoria, Zamboanga City, as a dedicated extension hub for aquaculture education
and marine technology research. This facility was made possible through the generous donation of a
9.3874-hectare parcel of land by the late Ricardo A. Marmoleño, whose visionary support enabled the
development of a dynamic center for learning, innovation, and public service.

       In recognition of his enduring contribution to education and community development, Site C
shall henceforth be officially designated as the Marmoleño Memorial Campus, serving as a
permanent tribute to Ricardo A. Marmoleño’s legacy of civic generosity and commitment to fisheries
advancement.

        The newly established Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and Technology
(ZCSUMST) shall be located in the Second District of Zamboanga City, with the Marmoleño
Memorial Campus as one of its principal academic and research centers. The University shall be
mandated to deliver advanced instruction, conduct cutting-edge research, and promote technology
transfer and industry collaboration across the full spectrum of marine sciences, aquaculture,
shipbuilding, climate systems, and the blue economy.

        The University shall operate under the supervision of the Commission on Higher Education
(CHED) and shall possess full corporate powers in accordance with Republic Act No. 8292,
otherwise known as the “Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997,” and other applicable laws,
rules, and regulations.

       This Act shall serve as the legal foundation for the full institutional transformation of
ZSCMST into ZCSUMST, ensuring continuity of service, enhancement of academic and research
capabilities, and alignment with national and regional development priorities.




​      SEC. 5 Mandate. The Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and Technology
(ZCSUMST) shall serve as a comprehensive center of excellence in fisheries, marine sciences,
maritime technology, and blue economy development.
       It shall contribute to the national and regional goals of sustainable development, food security,
climate resilience, and inclusive growth through education, research, innovation, and community
engagement.

       In pursuit of its mission, the University shall have the following powers, functions, and
responsibilities:
       1.​ Provide advanced instruction, professional training, and continuing education in the fields
           of fisheries, aquaculture, marine sciences, oceanography, shipbuilding, marine and
           mechanical engineering, climate systems, and blue economy management.

       2.​ It shall develop curricula and academic programs responsive to industry needs, scientific
           advancements, and the socio-economic realities of coastal and maritime communities.

       3.​ Conduct applied and fundamental research that advances knowledge and innovation in
           marine biosciences, aquaculture systems, ocean engineering, fisheries management,
           seaweed biotechnology, and climate resilience.

       4.​ The University shall establish research and innovation hubs for sardine and tuna
           repopulation, hatchery development, and coastal ecosystem restoration, ensuring that
           science-based solutions directly support livelihood improvement and resource
           sustainability.

       5.​ Promote technology transfer, enterprise development, and commercialization of research
           outputs to benefit fisherfolk, cooperatives, and local industries.

       6.​ The University shall establish incubation centers and extension programs to develop
           marine products, packaging systems, processing technologies, and export innovations,
           thereby contributing to value addition, employment generation, and inclusive regional
           growth.

       7.​ Serve as a regional center for socio-economic and cultural transformation, fostering
           partnerships with local government units, coastal communities, and private enterprises to
           implement sustainable livelihood and resource management programs.

       8.​ The University shall institutionalize community-based extension services in fisheries,
           aquaculture, and marine conservation that integrate indigenous knowledge, environmental
           stewardship, and gender-sensitive approaches.

       9.​ Establish strategic partnerships and international collaborations with domestic and foreign
           universities, research institutions, and global organizations to strengthen human resource
           development, academic exchange, and technological cooperation.

       10.​It shall facilitate joint research programs, faculty and student exchanges, and global
           capacity-building initiatives to ensure international standards in instruction, research, and
           innovation.

       11.​Recognize and institutionalize the Fisherfolk Equivalency Program (FEP) as an avenue for
           the accreditation and certification of maritime experience, navigational expertise,
           shipbuilding skills, and traditional ecological knowledge.

       12.​Through the FEP, experienced fisherfolk and maritime workers may obtain formal
           academic recognition and access to higher learning opportunities, thus promoting social
           mobility and inclusive education.

       13.​Exercise academic freedom and institutional autonomy, in accordance with Republic Act
           No. 8292 and other pertinent laws, to determine its curricula, research priorities,
           admission and graduation standards, and internal organization.
       14.​It shall ensure that its programs adhere to the highest standards of academic integrity,
           excellence, and accountability.

       15.​Provide policy support and technical expertise to government agencies and industry
           stakeholders in the formulation of national and regional policies on fisheries, marine
           conservation, climate adaptation, and the blue economy.
       16.​The University shall serve as a knowledge resource institution for evidence-based
           governance and innovation-driven economic planning.

       17.​Uphold the principles of sustainability, innovation, inclusivity, and ethical stewardship,
           ensuring that education, research, and development initiatives contribute to the
           preservation of marine biodiversity, protection of the environment, and empowerment of
           the Filipino people.

       18.​Perform such other functions and exercise such powers as are necessary or incidental to
           carry out its mandate, consistent with the Constitution, existing laws, and the policies of
           the Commission on Higher Education (CHED).

​      SEC. 6. Fiscal and Non-Fiscal Incentives for Industry, Research, and Innovation Partners.

        To accelerate innovation, promote research-based enterprise development, and strengthen
collaboration between the public, academic, and private sectors, a system of fiscal and non-fiscal
incentives is hereby established for qualified enterprises, organizations, and research institutions
operating within the Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and Technology
(ZCSUMST) or its designated Zamboanga Peninsula Blue Economy Zone (ZPBEZ).

        These incentives shall be administered in coordination with the Department of Finance
(DOF), the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), the Board of Investments (BOI), the Philippine
Economic Zone Authority (PEZA), and the Department of Science and Technology (DOST),
consistent with the national investment promotion framework under Republic Act No. 11534
(CREATE Act) and other relevant laws.

         A. Fiscal Incentives. Income Tax Holiday (ITH) and Preferential Tax Rate.​
Foreign or local companies registered under BOI or PEZA that invest in research and development
(R&D), marine biotechnology, sustainable aquaculture and hatchery operations, shipbuilding and
maritime engineering, climate resilience systems, or infrastructure projects within the University or
its designated innovation hubs shall be granted an Income Tax Holiday for fifteen (15) years from the
start of operation.

         Thereafter, such enterprises shall be subject to a preferential five percent (5%) tax rate in lieu
of all national and local taxes for the lifetime of their registered operation, provided that such
activities remain consistent with the University’s academic and scientific objectives.

       1.​ Tax Credit for Research Infrastructure and Knowledge Partnerships.
              a.​ Donations, endowments, and contributions of real or personal property made to the
                  University for research, innovation, or educational purposes shall be fully
                  deductible from the donor’s gross income in accordance with the National Internal
                  Revenue Code.
              b.​ Corporate partners that establish research laboratories, pilot plants, innovation
                  centers, or digital infrastructure within the University may be granted a tax credit
                  equivalent to up to fifty percent (50%) of qualified investment costs, as certified
                  by the University and validated by the DOF and DTI.
              c.​ Collaborative R&D expenditures conducted jointly with the University shall
                  qualify for enhanced tax deductions under the CREATE Act.

       2.​ Duty-Free and VAT-Exempt Importation of Scientific Equipment.​
           All scientific instruments, research vessels, laboratory apparatus, educational materials,
           and technical equipment directly used for instruction, research, or innovation activities
           shall be exempt from customs duties, import taxes, and value-added tax (VAT), provided
           that such imports are certified by the University and used exclusively for academic or
           R&D purposes.

       3.​ Tax Deduction for Internship, Apprenticeship, and Skills Development Programs.​
           Partner companies providing internship, apprenticeship, or skills training to University
           students shall be entitled to an additional tax deduction of up to one hundred fifty percent
           (150%) of the actual costs incurred for stipends, training materials, safety equipment, and
           mentoring services.

      This incentive shall encourage deeper industry–academe collaboration and facilitate job-ready
competencies among graduates.


        B. Non-Fiscal Incentives. To complement fiscal measures, the following non-fiscal privileges
shall be granted to industry and research partners engaged with the University:

       1.​ Access to the University’s Research and Innovation Ecosystem.​
           Qualified partners shall enjoy priority access to the University’s laboratories, testing
           facilities, hatcheries, simulation centers, innovation hubs, and the Blue Economy Data and
           Knowledge Repository. Such access shall be governed by research collaboration
           agreements that ensure transparency, ethical use, and mutual benefit.
       2.​ Joint Research, Intellectual Property (IP), and Technology Commercialization.​
           Partners may co-own, license, or commercialize intellectual property generated through
           collaborative research with the University, in accordance with the Philippine Technology
           Transfer Act of 2009 (RA 10055) and University IP policies. Revenue-sharing
           arrangements shall prioritize fair compensation for inventors, research teams, and
           community beneficiaries.
       3.​ Priority in Project Accreditation and Regulatory Clearances. The University, through
           coordination with relevant agencies and local government units, shall facilitate
           streamlined and prioritized approval of permits, construction clearances, and research
           authorizations for accredited R&D and industry projects within the Blue Economy Zone.
       4.​ Recognition as Strategic Innovation and Development Partners. Qualified enterprises and
           institutions shall be formally recognized as University Innovation Partners, eligible for
           participation in co-branded research programs, regional development initiatives, and
           government-endorsed technology missions.
       5.​ Participation in Capacity-Building and Policy Advisory Programs. Industry and academic
           partners shall be invited to collaborate in curriculum design, professional certification,
           policy formulation, and technology foresight exercises, ensuring that academic outputs
           remain relevant to national and regional priorities.

    C. Guiding Principles for Incentive Implementation. Incentives granted under this section shall be
performance-based, transparent, and time-bound, ensuring measurable outcomes in research
productivity, technology adoption, employment generation, and environmental sustainability.
Preference shall be given to enterprises and projects that demonstrate:

       1.​ Innovation aligned with the University’s Blue Economy and sustainability agenda;
       2.​ High-impact employment or training opportunities for local communities; and
       3.​ Measurable contributions to marine resource rehabilitation, biodiversity conservation, or
           climate resilience.

    The University shall establish an Office for Industry Partnerships and Innovation (OIPI) to
oversee the coordination, monitoring, and reporting of all incentive programs, in partnership with
relevant national agencies.

   D. Periodic Review and Rationalization. The Commission on Higher Education (CHED), in
coordination with the Department of Finance (DOF), DTI, and DOST, shall conduct a periodic
review every five (5) years to assess the effectiveness, fiscal impact, and sustainability of these
incentives.

   Adjustments and rationalization measures shall be recommended to ensure continued alignment
with national development goals and fiscal policy reforms.


    E. Enhanced Research and Development (R&D) Deduction. To foster a dynamic innovation
ecosystem and accelerate the development of marine and maritime technologies, enterprises,
including start-ups, spin-offs, and innovation-driven ventures, that engage in qualified research and
development (R&D) activities in partnership with the Zamboanga City State University for Marine
Sciences and Technology (ZCSUMST) shall be entitled to enhanced deductions from taxable income,
consistent with the provisions of the National Internal Revenue Code, the Corporate Recovery and
Tax Incentives for Enterprises (CREATE) Act (RA 11534), the Innovative Startup Act (RA 11337),
and other relevant laws.

    These deductions shall apply to legitimate and verifiable expenditures directly incurred for
scientific research, technological innovation, pilot testing, product or process engineering,
environmental and oceanographic monitoring, aquaculture development, shipbuilding technology
improvement, feasibility and prototyping studies, or technology transfer programs, certified by the
University and endorsed by the Department of Science and Technology (DOST) in coordination with
the Department of Finance (DOF).

   Start-ups and industry partners may also claim enhanced deductions for qualified costs associated
with:

       1.​ Collaborative innovation projects conducted within the University or its designated Blue
           Economy Zone;
       2.​ Incubation or acceleration programs established or accredited by the University;
       3.​ Employment of technical experts, researchers, and faculty consultants engaged in the
           design, testing, or scaling of new technologies; and
       4.​ Development and commercialization of intellectual property (IP) resulting from joint
           research with the University.
       5.​ To ensure alignment with national development goals, only R&D expenditures that
           demonstrably contribute to:
       6.​ The advancement of marine and fisheries sciences;
       7.​ The development of sustainable aquaculture, shipbuilding, and maritime technology
           systems;
       8.​ The generation of employment and local entrepreneurship; and
       9.​ The promotion of environmental sustainability and climate resilience​
           shall qualify for the enhanced deduction.

    This incentive shall serve as a policy mechanism to attract private investment, nurture start-ups,
and accelerate locally anchored R&D and technology innovation, thereby reinforcing the Philippines’
leadership in blue economy research, marine sciences, and sustainable industrial development
through the University.

       SEC. 7 Incentives and Privileges for Highly Specialized Faculty and Researchers. In
recognition of the indispensable role of advanced knowledge, global expertise, and high-level
technical instruction in achieving the mandate of the Zamboanga City State University for Marine
Sciences and Technology (ZCSUMST), the following privileges and incentives shall be automatically
implemented for highly specialized faculty and researchers engaged by the University:

       (a) Highly technical and highly specialized professors, scientists, or industry experts —
whether Filipino citizens or foreign nationals — who are appointed or contracted by the University
for academic, research, or innovation functions shall be exempt from the payment of income tax on
all compensation, honoraria, or consultancy fees received for services rendered within the University.
This exemption shall take effect upon the commencement of employment and shall be automatically
applied by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) upon presentation of a certification issued by the
University and the Commission on Higher Education (CHED).

        (b) Foreign professors, researchers, scientists, and industry experts possessing highly
technical and specialized skills, whose engagement has been certified by the University as essential to
its instructional or research programs, shall be entitled to the immediate issuance of work and
residency visas, valid for the entire duration of their employment or research engagement with the
University.
Such visas shall be granted without the need for a separate endorsement from any other government
agency, except for security clearance when required by law.
        (c) The University shall ensure that all necessary working permits and clearances from the
Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA), and the
Bureau of Immigration (BI) are properly secured prior to assumption of duties by foreign nationals.
To expedite processing, these agencies are mandated to prioritize applications endorsed by the
University and to establish a unified one-stop clearance mechanism within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act.

        (d) The University shall be authorized to recruit, invite, and engage globally recognized
professors, scientists, and research fellows, whether on a full-time, visiting, or adjunct basis, from
within the Philippines or abroad, in order to ensure access to world-class instruction, technology
transfer, and research collaboration. It shall actively establish institutional partnerships with
multinational corporations, global universities, and international research centers to facilitate such
recruitment and knowledge exchange.

       (e) All government agencies, including the Department of Science and Technology (DOST),
CHED, Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA), Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), and
the Bureau of Immigration (BI), are hereby directed to adopt joint administrative guidelines to
immediately implement and operationalize this section. No subsequent issuance, regulation, or
approval shall be required for its execution.

       (f) For purposes of this Act, the term “Highly Technical and Highly Specialized Faculty or
Experts” shall include individuals with:
       1.​ Doctoral or post-doctoral qualifications in relevant scientific or engineering fields;
       2.​ Documented international recognition in their discipline or industry;
       3.​ Demonstrated technical mastery in specialized areas of marine sciences, shipbuilding,
           aquaculture, ocean engineering, biotechnology, or related domains; or
       4.​ Proven experience in innovation, technology transfer, or leadership in science-based
           enterprises.

      (g) The privileges and benefits provided under this section shall be deemed self-executing and
mandatory, and shall not be subject to suspension, delay, or discretionary interpretation.

      The University and relevant agencies are obliged to implement these provisions upon
enactment of this Act.


        SEC. 8 Governance. The Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and
Technology (ZCSUMST) shall be governed by a Board of Regents (BOR), which shall serve as the
highest policy-making body of the University. The Board shall exercise corporate powers and
authority in accordance with Republic Act No. 8292, otherwise known as the Higher Education
Modernization Act of 1997, and other pertinent laws.

       A. Composition of the Board of Regents. The Board of Regents shall be composed of the
following:

       Chairperson – The Chairperson of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED);
       Vice-Chairperson – The Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST);
       Members;
            a.​ The Secretary of the Department of Education (DepEd);
            b.​ The Secretary of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI);
            c.​ The Secretary of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR);
            d.​ The Secretary of the Department of Agriculture (DA);
            e.​ The Director General of the Technical Education and Skills Development
                Authority (TESDA);
            f.​ The Secretary of the Department of Economy, Planning and Development
                (DEPDev);
            g.​ The Mayor of Zamboanga City;
            h.​ A representative from the private industry sector involved in fisheries,
                shipbuilding, or maritime technology, to be appointed by the Board upon
                nomination by accredited business associations;
            i.​ A representative from the academic community, preferably a distinguished scholar
                or scientist in marine or maritime studies;
            j.​ The Faculty Regent, duly elected by the regular faculty members of the University;
                and
            k.​ The Student Regent, duly elected by the student body in accordance with
                University policies.

        B. Continuity of Leadership. To ensure institutional stability during the transition, the
incumbent officials of the Zamboanga State College of Marine Sciences and Technology (ZSCMST)
shall continue to perform their duties and functions in a holdover capacity until the expiration of their
respective terms or until their successors are duly appointed and qualified under this Act.

         All existing academic and administrative policies of the College shall remain in force and
effect, insofar as they are not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, until amended or repealed
by the Board of Regents.

       C. Appointment of the University President. Upon the effectivity of this Act and for all
succeeding terms thereafter, the President of the University shall be appointed by the President of the
Republic of the Philippines from a list of at least three (3) nominees submitted and jointly vetted by
the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), Technical Education and Skills Development
Authority (TESDA), Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Department of Education
(DepEd), Department of Agriculture (DA), Department of Science and Technology (DOST),
Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), and the Office of the President.

       The University President shall serve a fixed term of four (4) years, subject to reappointment
for another term upon the recommendation of the Board of Regents based on meritorious
performance and institutional accomplishments.

       D. Powers and Functions of the Board of Regents. The Board of Regents shall be the highest
governing and policy-making body of the Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and
Technology (ZCSUMST) and shall exercise the following powers and functions:

       1.​ Policy Formulation and Strategic Direction. To formulate, review, and approve
           institutional policies, strategic plans, and development programs consistent with the
           national vision for sustainable marine resource management, blue economy growth,
           innovation-led education, and inclusive regional development.
       2.​ Financial Management and Fees. To approve the University’s annual budget and
           authorize the generation and management of income, endowments, donations, and
           externally funded projects; and to fix and collect such reasonable fees and charges as may
           be necessary for laboratory use, student services, research activities, extension programs,
           or other operational purposes, consistent with the Universal Access to Quality Tertiary
           Education Act (RA 10931) and other pertinent laws ensuring free tuition in state
           universities and colleges.
       3.​ Academic and Research Authority. To confer degrees, diplomas, and certificates upon
           students who have satisfactorily completed all academic requirements; and to award
           honorary degrees in recognition of distinguished contributions to science, technology,
           innovation, education, fisheries development, maritime engineering, and public service,
           upon the recommendation of duly constituted academic bodies.
       4.​ Appointment of Key Officials. To recommend to the President of the Republic of the
           Philippines the appointment of the University President, from a shortlist of duly vetted
           nominees submitted in accordance with the provisions of this Act and the implementing
           guidelines jointly formulated by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED),

       E. Meetings and Quorum. The Board of Regents shall convene in regular session at least once
every quarter, and may hold special meetings as may be deemed necessary, upon the written call of
the Chairperson, the Vice-Chairperson, or a majority of the Board members.

        A majority of all members of the Board of Regents holding office at the time of the meeting
shall constitute a quorum for the valid transaction of business, provided that either the Chairperson or
the Vice-Chairperson is present and presiding. In the absence or incapacity of the CHED
Chairperson, the Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST), as
Vice-Chairperson, shall preside over the meeting.

        If both are unable to attend, the members present may elect an Acting Presiding Officer from
among themselves, provided that a quorum exists. Resolutions or decisions of the Board shall require
the affirmative vote of the majority of the members present and constituting a quorum, and shall be
binding and immediately executory, unless otherwise specified by the Board.
       Members of the Board may participate in meetings physically or through secure electronic
means, in accordance with existing government rules on digital governance and transparency,
provided that attendance and voting are properly recorded in the minutes of proceedings.

        F. Secretariat and Administrative Support. The University President shall serve as Secretary to
the Board of Regents and shall ensure the proper recording, dissemination, and execution of all
resolutions, policies, and actions approved by the Board. The University shall provide the necessary
administrative and technical support for the efficient operation of the Board.

         G. Implementing Guidelines. Within ninety (90) days from the approval of this Act, the Board

institutionalize the University’s internal policies, organizational structure, and governance standards.

       SEC. 9. Smart Campus and Climate Resilience Integration.. The Zamboanga City State
University for Marine Sciences and Technology (ZCSUMST) shall adopt a Smart Campus and
Climate Resilience Framework that integrates digital transformation, sustainable infrastructure, and
environmental stewardship across all its campuses, laboratories, and research facilities.

       (a) Smart Campus Framework. The University shall establish a Smart Campus Ecosystem
anchored on digital governance, data-driven decision-making, and technological innovation to
enhance the efficiency, accessibility, and sustainability of academic and administrative operations.

       The framework shall include:
          1.​ Digital Laboratories and AI-Based Learning Systems that utilize simulation
              technologies, big data analytics, and virtual reality for marine sciences, aquaculture
              engineering, shipbuilding, and climate modeling;
          2.​ Integrated Smart Classrooms and Research Facilities equipped with cloud-based
              systems, digital libraries, and advanced communication networks to support hybrid,
              open, and distance learning;
          3.​ Renewable Energy Infrastructure including solar, wind, and tidal energy systems to
              achieve energy efficiency, reduce carbon footprint, and promote environmental
              responsibility;
          4.​ Smart Water and Waste Management Systems that employ Internet of Things (IoT)
              technologies for efficient resource use and environmental monitoring; and
          5.​ Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Mechanisms to safeguard research information,
              institutional data, and online learning environments.

        The Smart Campus Framework shall serve as a living laboratory for innovation, linking
students, researchers, and industries in co-developing smart and sustainable technologies applicable
to coastal and island communities.

       (b) Center for Marine Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Adaptation. To enhance national
and regional resilience, the University shall establish a Center for Marine Disaster Risk Reduction
and Climate Adaptation (CMDRRCA) under its Institute of Water and Climate Systems.

       The Center shall:
       1.​ Conduct multidisciplinary research on climate change, ocean dynamics, marine hazards,
           and coastal resource management;
       2.​ Develop predictive models and early warning systems for typhoons, storm surges, coastal
           flooding, and other ocean-related disasters;
       3.​ Implement community-based climate adaptation and disaster risk reduction programs in
           collaboration with local government units, fisherfolk organizations, and civil society;
       4.​ Provide training, certification, and policy advisory services to enhance the capacity of
           regional institutions in climate governance, disaster management, and resilient coastal
           planning; and
       5.​ Serve as a national knowledge hub for integrating science, policy, and practice in the
           management of marine and coastal risks.
        (c) Green Infrastructure and Sustainability Standards. All new University buildings, research
centers, and facilities shall conform to green building and sustainable campus design standards,
incorporating low-impact construction materials, energy-efficient systems, rainwater harvesting, and
biodiversity-friendly landscaping.

      The University shall likewise pursue carbon neutrality and zero-waste campus initiatives by
implementing programs for waste segregation, recycling, and renewable energy adoption.

        (d) Partnerships and Global Collaboration. The University shall collaborate with international
climate research institutions, ASEAN centers of excellence, and United Nations agencies to
strengthen scientific exchange, access global data networks, and mainstream climate-smart
technologies in education, fisheries, and maritime operations.

        (e) Institutional Responsibility and Integration. The Office of the Vice President for Research,
Innovation, and Climate Systems, in coordination with the Institute of Water and Climate Systems,
shall oversee the implementation of this Framework and ensure that climate resilience, environmental
sustainability, and smart innovation are integrated into all aspects of University policy, research, and
community engagement.

SEC. 9 Funding and Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act
shall be charged against the current appropriations of the Zamboanga State College of Marine
Sciences and Technology (ZSCMST) or under the budget of the Commission on Higher Education
(CHED).

        Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued operation, development, and
expansion of the Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and Technology (ZCSUMST)
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

       The University is further authorized to retain and utilize all revenues generated from
authorized fees, research services, training programs, technology transfer, intellectual property
commercialization, consultancies, and other income-generating activities for institutional
development, capacity building, innovation, and infrastructure enhancement, subject to government
accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

       The University may also receive and administer grants, endowments, donations, and official
development assistance (ODA) from domestic and international sources in support of its academic,
research, and extension functions, consistent with national laws and CHED-DBM regulations
governing State Universities and Colleges.


       SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), in coordination with the Department of
Budget and Management (DBM), Department of Finance (DOF), Department of Trade and Industry
(DTI), Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA),
Department of Science and Technology (DOST), and the Bureau of Immigration (BI), shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

        Such rules shall include the guidelines for fiscal incentives, governance transition, faculty
integration, and the establishment of academic, research, and innovation programs, ensuring full
compliance with national higher education and fiscal policy standards.

      SEC. 11 Transitory and Governance Provisions. Upon the effectivity of this Act, the
Zamboanga State College of Marine Sciences and Technology (ZSCMST) shall be converted into and
subsumed under the Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and Technology
(ZCSUMST).
       All personnel, assets, records, properties, equipment, appropriations, and funds of the former
ZSCMST are hereby transferred and absorbed by the ZCSUMST, without prejudice to existing rights
and benefits of faculty and employees under existing civil service and labor laws.

        The incumbent officials of the ZSCMST shall continue to serve in a holdover capacity until
the expiration of their respective terms or until their successors have been duly appointed and
qualified in accordance with this Act.

        All academic, administrative, and financial operations of the ZSCMST shall continue
uninterrupted during the transition period, under the supervision of the Commission on Higher
Education (CHED) and the Board of Regents of the newly established ZCSUMST.

        SEC. 12 Separability Clause – If for any reason, any provision of this Act is declared to be
unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby
shall continue to be in full force and effect.

​       SEC. 13 Repealing Clause – Presidential Decree No. 2020, entitled “An Act Converting the
Zamboanga State School of Marine Sciences into the Zamboanga State College of Marine Sciences
and Technology, and for Other Purposes,” and all other laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby expressly repealed or modified accordingly.

         All powers, functions, assets, liabilities, records, personnel, appropriations, and properties of
the Zamboanga State College of Marine Sciences and Technology (ZSCMST) are hereby transferred
and vested in the Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences and Technology
(ZCSUMST), which shall assume its full legal personality as successor-in-interest of the former
institution.

       All rights and benefits of existing officials, faculty, and employees of the ZSCMST shall be
respected and preserved, and such personnel shall continue to serve under equivalent positions in the
new University, without diminution of rank, tenure, or privileges, in accordance with civil service
laws and CHED regulations.

       From the effectivity of this Act, the Zamboanga City State University for Marine Sciences
and Technology (ZCSUMST) shall be the sole legal successor to the ZSCMST, and shall hereafter
exercise all authority, functions, and responsibilities as provided in this Act and other applicable laws.

         SEC. 14 Effectivity Clause – This Act shall take effect upon completion of its publication in
at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
