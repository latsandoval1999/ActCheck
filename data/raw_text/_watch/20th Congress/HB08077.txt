                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Pawikan or sea turtles are endangered species that can be found in the
Philippines. There are five species of sea turtles that inhabit the local waters of the
Philippines – the Green, Hawksbill, Olive Ridley, Loggerhead, and Leatherback Turtles.

       The Municipality of Naic, Cavite is a coastal town which is frequented by Pawikans.
Every year during the second weekend in the month of February, the townsfolk of Naic
celebrate the Naic Pawikan Festival.

       The Naic Pawikan Festival has been celebrated every year for fifteen (15) years
now. During the festival, various activities that promote the preservation of sea turtles
are undertaken, including coastal clean-ups and fun-runs. The Naic Pawikan Festival is
highlighted by the release of Pawikan hatchlings.

       This representation believes that the Naic Pawikan Festival is a very significant
event that needs to be recognized by the entire nation. The said festival showcases the
determination of the Philippines in preserving the endangered sea turtles and that the
country is one with the world in the effort to protect mother nature. As such, it is just
proper to declare the Naic Pawikan Festival as an event of national significance and
importance. The passage of the bill herein will also be a reinforcement of the State policy
asserting that: The State shall protect and advance the right of the people to a balanced
ecology in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature (Section 16, Article II, 1987
Constitution).

      Approval of the bill is earnestly sought.

        “AN ACT DECLARING THE SECOND WEEKEND OF FEBRUARY OF
         EVERY YEAR AS SPECIAL HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF
         NAIC, CAVITE IN CELEBRATION OF THE PAWIKAN FESTIVAL”

                                             .
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in Congress assembled:

       Section 1. It is hereby declared that the Naic Pawikan Festival as an event of
national significance and importance, relating to the preservation of the sea turtles in the
Philippines.

       Section 2. The Naic Pawikan Festival shall be celebrated every year during the
Friday and Saturday of the second weekend of February. The Naic Pawikan Festival shall
be special working holidays in the Municipality of Naic.

       Section 3. This Act shall take effect upon its approval, and all acts, rules or
regulations, or laws inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed.
