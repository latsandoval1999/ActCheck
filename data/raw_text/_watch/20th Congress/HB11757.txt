                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Standardization is the process of establishing a framework to be observed
and adopted by involved parties to promote regularity, uniformity, and
compatibility. This bill seeks to standardize the written format of a date when
represented in numerical characters.

       Establishing a universal standard numerical date format will promote
efficiency and accuracy in day-to-day transactions. It will also prevent
misrepresentation, ambiguity, and confusion in written official and legal
documents.

      This bill proposes to adopt the International Organization for
Standardization (ISO) 8601 international standard on date representation, which
is a number sequence in decreasing units of time: year, month, and day (YYYY-
MM-DD). This format is now being observed by several countries in the world.

       This unambiguous calendar-and-clock standard is intentionally adapted
by the ISO and features the added benefit of being understood by machines
following the ISO Standard, further streamlining tech-based encoding and
transacting.

     It is for the foregoing premises that the approval of this bill is earnestly
sought.

  AN ACT PROVIDING FOR A STANDARD NUMERICAL DATE FORMAT IN
       ALL OFFICIAL AND LEGAL DOCUMENTS IN THE COUNTRY

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

    SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Standard
Numerical Date Format Act of 2026.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall promote efficiency and
accuracy in official and legal transactions by providing a standard numerical
date format to avoid ambiguity, confusion, and misrepresentation.

      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:

      (a) Official document refers to any form, certificate, permit, license,
record, communication, or other document issued by or filed with a government
agency or government-owned or -controlled corporation.

     (b) Legal document refers to any document recognized by law as creating,
modifying, transferring, proving, or extinguishing legal rights or obligations.

       SECTION 4. Standard Date Format. - Dates written in numerical format
shall follow the order of decreasing unit of time. The sequence shall consist of
the four-digit number representing the year (YYYY), followed by the two-digit
number representing the month (MM) and the two-digit number representing the
day (DD), each separated by a hyphen (-). The month and day shall always be
expressed using two digits, with leading zeros where necessary.

      The standard date format shall be written as YYYY-MM-DD. The basic
notation (YYYYMMDD) may be used where required for machine processing. The
date format with a slash (/) shall only be used when required by legacy systems
or international specifications.

      For example, June 12, 1898, in the standard date format, shall be
represented as 1898-06-12, and as 18980612 for machine processing.

       SECTION 5. Scope. - This Act shall apply to all official documents issued
by government agencies and government-owned or controlled corporations, and
to legal documents required or recognized under Philippine law whenever dates
are expressed in numerical notation, so long as they do not prevent the use of
another date format where required by an international agreement or by the
prescribed requirements of a foreign jurisdiction or international body.

       SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, DICT, in coordination with the National Archives
of the Philippines, the PSA, the DOJ, and other concerned government agencies,
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

      SECTION 7. Separability. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity and effectivity of the other
provisions hereof.

      SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, and issuances
or portions thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 9. Transition Period. - Existing forms and templates may
continue to be used until exhausted, replaced, or revised, but all newly issued
or revised forms after the effectivity of this Act shall conform to the prescribed
format.

      SECTION 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
