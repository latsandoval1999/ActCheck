 AN ACT MANDATING THE INCLUSION OF INDIGENOUS ROOT CROPS INTO
 THE NATIONAL FEEDING PROGRAM FOR UNDERNOURISHED CHILDREN IN
    PUBLIC DAY CARE, KINDERGARTEN AND ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS,
  AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11037, OTHERWISE
 KNOWN AS THE “MASUSTANSYANG PAGKAIN PARA SA BATANG PILIPINO
    ACT,” PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Malnutrition among Filipino children has long been one of the most persistent
challenges to the Philippine public health system. Through Republic Act No. 11037,
or the “Masustansyang Pagkain Para sa Batang Pilipino Act,” the government
established the National Feeding Program, which addresses this concern by providing
fortified meals to undernourished children in public day care centers and elementary
schools. However, one category of nutrient-dense food has remained conspicuously
absent from its implementing menus: indigenous root crops.

       The Philippines is home to a rich diversity of indigenous root crops such as
kamote, gabi, ube, and kamoteng kahoy, which have long served as traditional
sources of carbohydrates, dietary fiber, vitamins, and minerals. These crops are
climate-resilient, adaptable to a wide range of growing conditions, and cultivated by
thousands of smallholder farmers across the country. Despite their nutritional and
economic value, indigenous root crops remain underutilized in government feeding
programs, representing a missed opportunity to strengthen the nutritional content of
the program's menus while supporting local agriculture.

       This measure seeks to amend Republic Act No. 11037 by mandating the
inclusion of indigenous root crops and root crop-based products in the cycle menus of
the National Feeding Program, subject to regional availability and cultural
appropriateness. The bill further promotes the cultivation of indigenous root crops
through the Gulayan sa Paaralan Program and encourages the prioritization of
accredited root crop farmers’ cooperatives and enterprises in the procurement of
feeding program requirements.
       By linking child nutrition initiatives with local agricultural development, this
proposed measure advances the objectives of food security, sustainable agriculture,
rural economic development, and improved nutrition outcomes for Filipino children.
This bill introduces a specific, evidence-based, culturally grounded mandate that the
law’s current text does not contain—one that draws on a food source already familiar
to, and grown by, Filipino communities nationwide.

      The passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

     “Section 4. National Feeding Program. – The National Feeding

to address undernutrition among Filipino children.

     The Program shall have the following components and coverage:

           “(a) Supplemental Feeding Program for Day Care Children.
     – The DSWD, in coordination with the LGUs concerned, shall

     COORDINATION WITH THE DA AND THE FOOD AND
     NUTRITION RESEARCH INSTITUTE (FNRI);

           (b) School-Based Feeding Program. – The DepED shall
     implement a school-based feeding program for undernourished
     public school children from kindergarten to grade six

        SECTION 3. Section 12 of Republic Act No. 11037 is hereby amended to read
as follows:

                “Section 12. Procurement of Goods and Services. – The
          Department of Budget and Management, Government Procurement
          Policy Board, and the Commission on Audit, in consultation with the
          NGAs, are hereby mandated to specifically establish and promulgate
          a community-based mode of procurement, liquidation and audit that
          will ensure the efficient and effective implementation of the Program;
          PROVIDED, THAT IN PROCURING INDIGENOUS ROOT CROPS
          OR ROOT CROP-BASED PRODUCTS FOR THE NATIONAL
          FEEDING PROGRAM, THE DEPED AND THE DSWD SHALL GIVE
          PRIORITY TO ACCREDITED ROOT CROP FARMERS’
          COOPERATIVES AND ENTERPRISES; Provided, FURTHER, That
          this provision shall apply solely to the implementation of the Program
          by the NGAs and LGUs concerned.

       SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The NGAs in

        SECTION 5. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any section or provision

       SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
