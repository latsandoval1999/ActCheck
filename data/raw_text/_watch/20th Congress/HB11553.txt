                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Principle 3 of the 1991 United Nations Principles for Older Persons
recognizes the right of older persons to participate in determining when and
at what pace they withdraw from the labor force. Consistent with this
principle, this measure seeks to lower the optional retirement age of
government employees from sixty (60) years to fifty-six (56) years.
      Lowering the optional retirement age will allow government employees
to enjoy their retirement benefits after years of dedicated public service and
pursue other opportunities where their skills and experience may continue to
contribute to the economy. At the same time, vacancies created by retirement
will open opportunities for younger workers, bringing fresh skills and greater
familiarity with emerging technologies into government service.
      This measure seeks to promote a more dynamic and responsive public
service while expanding employment opportunities and supporting the
financial independence of retiring government employees.
      Hence, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                              AN ACT
       LOWERING THE OPTIONAL RETIREMENT AGE OF GOVERNMENT
        EMPLOYEES FROM SIXTY (60) YEARS TO FIFTY-SIX (56) YEARS,
      AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 13-A OF REPUBLIC ACT NO.
         8291, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE GOVERNMENT SERVICE
                    INSURANCE SYSTEM ACT OF 1997

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
           Congress assembled:


 1            SECTION 1. Section 13-A of Republic Act No. 8291, otherwise known as
 2      “The Government Service Insurance Act of 1997” is hereby amended to read as
 3      follows:
 4         "SECTION 13-A. Conditions for Entitlement. — A member who retires
 5         from the service shall be entitled to the retirement benefits in paragraph
 6         (a) of Section 13 hereof: Provided, That SUCH MEMBER:
 7                        1.     [he] has rendered at least fifteen (15) years of service;
 8                        2.     [he] is at least [sixty (60)] FIFTY-SIX (56) years of age at
 9                        the time of retirement; and
10                        3.     [he] is not receiving a monthly pension benefit from
11                        permanent total disability.”

12            SEC. 2. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules and
13      regulations, or other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
14      this Act are hereby amended, modified, or repealed accordingly.
