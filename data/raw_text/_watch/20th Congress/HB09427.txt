                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to mandate a comprehensive nationwide inspection of all
buildings and to strengthen Presidential Decree No. 1096, otherwise known as the
“National Building Code of the Philippines”, by imposing stricter penalties on officials
who violate the provisions highlighted on this measure.

        In light of the country’s heightened vulnerability to natural hazards such as
earthquakes, typhoons, and fire-related incidents, there is an urgent need to ensure
that all structures comply with established safety and engineering standards to
safeguard lives, property, and public welfare. This proposed measure introduces
stiffer penalties including imprisonment, perpetual disqualification from public
office, and revocation of professional licenses for officials found guilty of false or
fraudulent issuance of permits and certifications. It reinforces accountability, deter
negligence or misconduct, and ensures that regulatory authority is exercised with
integrity and that public safety is not compromised by corruption or abuse of
discretion.

        Overall, the measure advances the State’s duty to protect life and promote
resilient, safe, and disaster-prepared communities by ensuring strict compliance with
building safety standards and strengthening enforcement mechanisms under the
Code.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
STRENGTHENING PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 1096, THE NATIONAL
BUILDING CODE OF THE PHILIPPINES, AS AMENDED, BY MANDATING A
COMPREHENSIVE NATIONWIDE INSPECTION OF BUILDINGS AND
IMPOSING STIFFER PENALTIES AGAINST BUILDING OFFICIALS WHO
VIOLATE THE PERTINENT PROVISIONS OF THE SAID CODE

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Inspection and Clearance. – The Department of Public Works and
Highways (DPWH), in coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local
Government (DILG), the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council
(NDRRMC), and all leagues of Local Government Units shall, within three (3) years
from the effectivity of this Act, initiate and conduct a comprehensive inspection of all
buildings nationwide, and consequently cause the issuance by the Building Officials
concerned the corresponding Certificate of Inspection and Clearance stating, among
others, that the building so inspected conforms with the provisions of Presidential
Decree (PD) No. 1096, as amended.

       SEC. 2. Reportorial Requirement. – The result of the comprehensive inspection
under Section 1 hereof shall be contained in a report to be submitted to the Office of
the President and Congress with recommendations on how to improve and
strengthen further PD 1096, as amended.

        SEC. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DPWH, the NDRRMC and
the DILG shall issue the necessary implementing rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act.

        SEC. 4. Penalty. – Building Officials, as defined under PD 1096, as amended,
who shall fail to or falsely issue building permits, clearances, certificates or
certifications relative to their functions as such shall suffer the penalty of at least six
(6) years imprisonment, absolute perpetual disqualification to hold office and
permanent revocation of their professional license.

      For the purposes of this section, False Issuance refers to any form of
misrepresentation, deceit, machination or fraud in the inspection of buildings and
issuance of permits, clearances, certificates or certifications pursuant to PD 1096, as
amended, or this Act.

       SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
subsequently declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof which are
not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
proclamations and/or administrative regulations which are inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby amended, modified, superseded or repealed
accordingly: Provided, however, That nothing in this Act shall be construed as
repealing or amending the current standards set forth under PD No. 1096, as
amended.

       SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days from its
publication in the Official Gazette or at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation, whichever comes first.
