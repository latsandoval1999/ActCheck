                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish a Benguet Highland Farmers Assistance Program
to amend existing agricultural support frameworks under the Department of
Agriculture (DA). This creates a dedicated, institutionalized mechanism for direct
subsidies, climate resilience interventions, postharvest support, and market
development assistance for farmers in the Province of Benguet. The measure
recognizes the absence of an active funding mechanism under the Agricultural
Competitiveness Enhancement Fund (ACEF), which under Republic Act No. 10848
was set for termination upon the lapse of its collection period in 2022, thereby
necessitating a new statutory program anchored on annual national appropriations
to ensure continuity of agricultural support services for highland farmers.

        The Province of Benguet remains one of the country’s primary
vegetable-producing provinces, supplying a significant portion of highland
vegetables consumed in Metro Manila and other urban centers. However, despite its
critical role in national food security, Benguet farmers continue to experience severe
market volatility and income instability. In 2026, multiple reports documented that
cabbage farmgate prices in Benguet dropped to as low as Three pesos (P3.00) to Ten
pesos (P10.00) per kilogram, far below production costs. This forces farmers to either
sell at a loss or leave crops unharvested. The same reports show that production
costs reach approximately Eighteen pesos (P18.00) to Twenty pesos (P20.00) per
kilogram, creating persistent negative margins for farmers amid weak demand and
oversupply conditions. GMA News further reported that many farmers have been
forced to abandon harvesting altogether due to rising fuel and transport costs, which
make delivery to trading centers financially unsustainable1,2. Complementary
coverage also shows instances where vegetables are either sold at extremely low

       The proposed measure provides a comprehensive package of interventions,
including direct input subsidies, fuel assistance, climate-resilient farming
technologies, postharvest infrastructure, agricultural insurance support, and market
development initiatives. Unlike fragmented and project-based interventions, the bill
establishes a unified program under the DA to ensure coordinated planning,
implementation, and monitoring of agricultural assistance. It also strengthens
accountability mechanisms through annual reporting to Congress, guaranteeing that
government support translates into measurable improvements in farm productivity,
income stability, and sectoral resilience.

       This measure responds to a persistent and painful reality: the very farmers
who sustain the nation’s food supply remain among its most economically
vulnerable. Every season of price collapse, every field left unharvested, and every
farmer forced to sell below cost reflects a deeper structural failure that cannot be
ignored. Benguet farmers continue to feed millions, yet many struggle to feed their
own families with dignity. This bill seeks to break that cycle of vulnerability and
neglect. It carries the urgency of a sector that cannot wait for another harvest cycle of
loss, and it calls for immediate and sustained government action to ensure that those
who cultivate the nation’s food are finally given the support they have long
deserved.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING THE BENGUET HIGHLAND FARMERS ASSISTANCE
PROGRAM, PROVIDING DIRECT SUBSIDIES, CLIMATE RESILIENCE
SUPPORT, POSTHARVEST ASSISTANCE, AND MARKET DEVELOPMENT
INTERVENTIONS FOR FARMERS IN THE PROVINCE OF BENGUET,
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Benguet Highland
Farmers Assistance Act.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
ensure food security, strengthen agricultural competitiveness, improve farmer
incomes, and promote sustainable agricultural development. The State recognizes
the Province of Benguet as a major producer of highland vegetables and other
high-value crops and shall provide targeted interventions to address the unique
challenges confronting its agricultural sector.

       SEC. 3. Establishment of the Benguet Highland Farmers Assistance Program. –
There is hereby established a Benguet Highland Farmers Assistance Program under
the Department of Agriculture (DA) to provide direct assistance and support to
qualified farmers, farmers' associations, cooperatives, and agricultural producers in
the Province of Benguet.

       SEC. 4. Coverage. – The Program shall cover farmers engaged in the
production of vegetables, root crops, fruits, coffee, cut flowers, and other
agricultural commodities within the Province of Benguet.

       SEC. 5. Forms of Assistance. – Assistance under this Act may include:

          a) Direct farm input subsidies;

          b) Fuel assistance and transportation support;


                                            1
          c)​ Climate resilience and adaptation projects;

          d)​ Postharvest facilities and equipment assistance;

          e)​ Market development and price stabilization programs;

          f)​ Agricultural insurance premium assistance;

         g)​ Emergency recovery assistance following disasters and severe market
      disruptions;

         h)​ Agricultural extension, training, and technology transfer programs;
      and

         i)​ Other forms of assistance determined by the DA to improve farm
      productivity and farmer income.

       SEC. 5. Beneficiaries. – Beneficiaries shall be registered under the Registry
System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) and shall comply with the
qualifications prescribed by the DA.

       SEC. 6. Implementation. – The DA, through its Regional Field Office in the
Cordillera Administrative Region, and in coordination with the Provincial
Government of Benguet, local government units, farmers’ organizations, and other
concerned agencies, shall implement this Act.

      SEC. 7. Annual Report. – The DA shall submit an annual report to Congress on
the implementation of this Act, including fund utilization, beneficiaries served, and
measurable impacts on agricultural productivity and farmer incomes.

       SEC. 8. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act under the
budget of the DA. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be included in
the annual General Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DA, in coordination with relevant agencies, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the implementation of this Act.

      SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

      SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, rules and regulations,
and other issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.
