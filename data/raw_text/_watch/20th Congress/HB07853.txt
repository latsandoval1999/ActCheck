                              EXPLANATORY NOTE


      Under Article II, Section 18 of the 1987 Constitution, “[t]he State affirms
labor as a primary social economic force,” thus vowing to “protect the rights
of workers and promote their welfare.” In connection, under Article XIII,
Section 3(2), it is declared that workers “shall be entitled to… humane
conditions of work…and a living wage.”

       Given these provisions, the State has a two-fold duty to public-school
teachers: (1) subsidizing their expenses on teaching materials by providing
them with an adequate allowance for such purchases; (2) covering the costs
of their professional development needs.

     Teachers do have the so-called ‘chalk allowance.’ The Department of
Education’s (DepEd) Order No. 10, s. 2020 discusses this thusly:1

          augment the expenses incurred/spent by teachers in the
          actual performance of their teaching responsibilities.

       But there are two problems with this that make it insufficient as a form
of meaningful aid for our teachers: 1) given the new expenses that come with
our shift to blended learning, Php3,500.00 is hardly enough to cover our
teachers’ needs, especially if they are footing the monthly bill for their house’s
internet connection; and 2) this does not cover their expenses for their
professional growth, which they are constrained to pursue via seminars,
training programs, and the like.

      Neither of these expenses should be shouldered by our teachers. They
should not be pinned down in an impossible situation where they have no
choice but to reinvest their wages into the performance of their duties.

       This Bill presents a solution. It creates the Cost of Teaching and
Learning Continuity Allowance (COTALCA), amounting to P5,000.00 per
month for each school year, which shall benefit all public-school teachers.
COTALCA shall cover the personal expenses of teachers for their teaching
supplies and materials, preparation of lesson plans, and for conduct of
various modes of learning delivery, and/or to pursue continuous learning
opportunities for their professional development, such as attending
traditional education seminars, joining digital or online education training
programs, or other teaching development opportunities of their choice.

     It is for the foregoing premises that the approval of this Bill is earnestly
sought.

 AN ACT GRANTING COST OF TEACHING AND LEARNING CONTINUITY
  ALLOWANCE TO PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS AND APPROPRIATING
                    FUNDS, THEREFORE


       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Allowance ni
Titser Act.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy – Education is essential in national
and economic development. It is the policy of the State to promote and
improve the economic status and professional advancement of teachers so
that they may compare favorably with other walks of life. The State shall also
endeavor to attract and retain more individuals with suitable qualifications in
the teaching profession and provide them with the opportunity for continuous
learning.

      To this end, the Government shall grant public school teachers an
additional benefit to cushion the impact of the costs of teaching and learning
continuity.

      SECTION 3. Coverage. – This Act shall be limited to all permanent and
provisional teachers, including Alternative Learning System (ALS), Mobile
Teachers, and District ALS Coordinators (DALSCs), in all public elementary,
junior, and senior high schools and community learning centers, who are in
service in the Department of Education for the current school year, and
assigned with at least one teaching load.

      SECTION 4. Cost of Teaching and Learning Continuity Allowance
(COTALCA). – A Cost of Teaching and Learning Continuity Allowance
(COTALCA) amounting to Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) per month for
each school year is hereby granted to all public school teachers covered under
this Act.
      Cost of Teaching and Learning Continuity Allowance is the amount of
money a public school teacher receives in addition to their basic salary to
cover the personal expenses of teachers for their teaching supplies and
materials, preparation of lesson plans, and conduct of various modes of
learning delivery, and to pursue continuous learning opportunities for their
professional development, such as attending traditional education seminars,
joining digital or online education training programs or other teaching
development opportunities of their choice.

       SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within thirty (30)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Education (DepEd), in
coordination with the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the
Department of Finance (DoF), and other concerned government agencies,
shall formulate the necessary rules and regulations to carry out the provisions
of this Act.

       The implementing rules and regulations shall be published in the
Official Gazette or any newspaper of general circulation.

      SECTION 6. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to implement the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the Annual General Appropriations
Act.

       SECTION 7. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions or parts unaffected
shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules, and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 9. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
