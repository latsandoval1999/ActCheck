                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Persons with disability (PWDs) who dedicate their careers to government service
often face challenges in performing their work due to physical, mental, or sensory
limitations. While they continue to serve the public, their disability may reduce their
capacity to work effectively or safely, making it harder to sustain employment despite
years of dedicated service. Yet, current retirement options do not fully address their
unique needs for early and secure retirement.

This bill provides a clear and fair early optional retirement option for quali ied
PWD government employees, allowing those who meet age, service, and
certi ication requirements to retire and receive their GSIS bene its without waiting for
the normal retirement age. The measure ensures eligibility is carefully assessed,
bene its are properly computed, and safeguards are in place against misuse, while
aligning with existing GSIS rules and actuarial standards.

By offering PWDs a digni ied and secure pathway to retirement, this measure
promotes social justice, recognizes their contributions to public service, and af irms
the State’s commitment to the rights, welfare, and well-being of persons with
disability.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

                               AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR AN EARLY OPTIONAL RETIREMENT MODE FOR QUALIFIED
PERSONS WITH DISABILITY IN GOVERNMENT SERVICE, AMENDING FOR THE
PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8291, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Persons with Disability
Early Optional Retirement Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to recognize and
promote the rights of persons with disability (PWDs) as integral members of
Philippine society, and to ensure that they enjoy equal opportunity in public
employment and a meaningful, productive, and satisfying life during and after
government service.

In furtherance of social justice and protection of the right to security in old age and
disability, the State shall provide a clear, fair, and administrable early optional
retirement mode for quali ied PWD government employees, consistent with the
Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) framework.

SECTION 3. De inition of Terms. — As used in this Act:

   (a) Person with Disability (PWD) refers to an employee who has a long-term
       physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory impairment which, in interaction with

                                                                           2|Page
       various barriers, substantially limits one or more major life activities or
       functional capacities relevant to work, as certi ied in accordance with the
       standards and procedures under this Act and its implementing rules.

   (b) Disability for purposes of early optional retirement refers to a medically
       determinable impairment that results in a substantial limitation of
       functional capacity such that the employee experiences sustained dif iculty
       in performing the essential functions of the employee’s customary work, or
       work of similar nature for which the employee is trained, considering
       reasonable accommodation where applicable.

   (c) Quali ied service means government service creditable under the GSIS law
       and rules.

SECTION 4. Coverage. — This Act shall apply to all appointive of icials and employees
in the government service who are GSIS members, including those in the National
Government, local government units, and government-owned or controlled
corporations with original charters.

Uniformed personnel of the Armed Forces of the Philippines and the Philippine
National Police covered by separate retirement systems are excluded.

SECTION 5. Early Optional Retirement for Quali ied PWD Government
Employees. — A PWD government employee covered by this Act may opt to retire
and receive GSIS retirement bene its under R.A. No. 8291, under an early optional
retirement mode, if the employee meets all the following:

   (a) Has rendered at least ifteen (15) years of quali ied service;
   (b) Is at least ifty- ive (55) years of age at the time of retirement;
   (c) Is a certi ied PWD under Section 6 of this Act; and
   (d) Is not receiving a monthly pension bene it from permanent total disability
       under the GSIS law at the time of retirement, consistent with the statutory
       limitation under R.A. No. 8291.

SECTION 6. Certi ication and Evaluation; Safeguards. —

   (a) Certi ication. The GSIS shall determine eligibility under this Act based on
       medical evaluation and functional assessment, with due regard to actual work
       limitations and loss of earning capacity, consistent with jurisprudence that


                                                                         3|Page
       disability is determined by capacity to work and earn rather than diagnosis
       alone.

   (b) Anti-fraud safeguards. The IRR shall provide standards for documentary
       requirements, periodic validation where appropriate, and penalties for
       falsi ication or fraudulent claims.

   (c) Due process. Denial of eligibility shall be in writing stating reasons, and shall
       be appealable through existing GSIS processes and judicial remedies
       consistent with law.

SECTION 7. Bene it Computation; Funding; Non-impairment. —

   (a) Bene it level. A retiree under this Act shall receive retirement bene its
       computed under the applicable bene it formula of R.A. No. 8291 and GSIS rules
       for retirement, subject to the same actuarial and funding standards.

   (b) No double recovery. A retiree who is already receiving a monthly pension
       from permanent total disability shall not be eligible under this Act, consistent
       with R.A. No. 8291.

   (c) Liberal construction. This Act shall be liberally construed in favor of the
       retiree-bene iciary, consistent with the policy on retirement and social
       legislation, without expanding bene its beyond what the statute provides.

SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from
effectivity, the GSIS, in consultation with the Civil Service Commission, the National
Council on Disability Affairs, and stakeholders, shall promulgate the implementing
rules and regulations to carry out this Act.

SECTION 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder not affected thereby shall remain in full
force and effect.

SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, issuances or portions
thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modi ied accordingly.
