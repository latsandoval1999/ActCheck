                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to convert the Bayog External Study Unit of the Zamboanga
Peninsula Polytechnic State University (ZPPSU), located in Bayog, Zamboanga del
Sur, into a regular campus to be known as the ZPPSU–Bayog Campus. It also
provides for the necessary funding and plantilla positions for both teaching and non-
teaching personnel.
       Article XIV, Section 1 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution mandates the State
to protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education and to make such
education accessible at all levels.
       ZPPSU has long advanced technical, vocational, and higher education in the
Zamboanga Peninsula. Its Bayog External Study Unit has provided accessible
learning opportunities, but its current status limits its capacity to deliver programs
and services equal to those of a regular campus.
       Granting it full campus status will allow the expansion of academic offerings,
the strengthening of research and extension services, and compliance with
Commission on Higher Education standards. This will enhance program quality and
credibility while directly benefiting Bayog and the wider province by addressing the
growing demand for affordable higher education.
      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
     CONVERTING THE ZAMBOANGA PENINSULA POLYTECHNIC STATE
     UNIVERSITY-BAYOG EXTERNAL STUDY UNIT LOCATED IN THE
     MUNICIPALITY OF BAYOG, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL SUR, INTO
     A REGULAR CAMPUS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

            Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of the Representatives of the
     Philippines in Congress assembled:

 1        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga
 2   Peninsula Polytechnic State University Bayog Campus Act.”
 3
 4        Section 2. Conversion of ZPPSU-Bayog External Study Unit into a
 5   Regular Campus. – The Bayog External Study Unit of the Zamboanga
 6   Peninsula Polytechnic State University (ZPPSU) located in the Municipality of
 7   Bayog, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is hereby converted into a regular
 8   campus, to be known as the ZPPSU-Bayog Campus.
 9
10          Section 3. General Mandate. – The ZPPSU-Bayog Campus shall
11   primarily provide short-term, technical-vocational, undergraduate, and
12   graduate courses within its areas of competency and specialization, pursuant
13   to the mandate of the ZPPSU, and according to its capability, as the Board of
14   Regents, hereinafter referred to as the Board, may deem necessary to respond
15   to the human resource development needs of the Province of Zamboanga del
16   Sur and the Zamboanga Peninsula. It shall be mandated to undertake
17   research and extension services and to provide progressive leadership in these
18   areas.
19
20        Section 4. Supervision and Management of ZPPSU-Bayog Campus. –
21   The ZPPSU-Bayog Campus shall be headed by a Campus Administrator, to be
22   appointed by the Board subject to the guidelines, qualifications, and
23   standards set by the Board, and upon the recommendation of a search

 4         Section 5. Compliance with the Requirements of the Commission on
 5   Higher Education. – The ZPPSU shall ensure the compliance of the ZPPSU-
 6   Bayog Campus with the policies, standards, and guidelines of the
 7   Commission on Higher Education (CHED) for the operation of regular
 8   campuses, including those related to the offering of courses and the
 9   introduction of new or revised programs prior to its conversion as a regular
10   campus.
11
12        Section 6. Management of Personnel, Assets and Records of the
13   Campus. – All assets, both real and personal, personnel and records of the
14   ZPPSU-Bayog Campus, as well as its liabilities or obligations, shall be titled
15   under or named after the ZPPSU.
16
17          All parcels of land belonging to the government that shall be occupied
18   by the ZPPSU-Bayog Campus shall be the property of the ZPPSU, and shall
19   be tilted under its name: Provided, That should the campus cease to exist or
20   be abolished or should such aforementioned parcels of land be no longer
21   needed by the ZPPSU, the same shall be revert to the concerned local
22   government unit or to the Republic of the Philippines, as the case may be.
23
24          Section 7. Suppletory Application. – The provisions of Republic Act No.
25   8292, otherwise known as the “Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997”,
26   shall form part of this Act.
27
28         Section 8. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the
29   provisions of this Act, including those required to fund the plantilla positions
30   of teaching and non-teaching personnel of the ZPPSU-Bayog Campus, shall
31   be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).
32
33         The Municipality of Bayog shall continue to provide funding assistance
34   for the operations of the ZPPSU-Bayog Campus until such time that the
35   amount necessary for its continued operation and maintenance shall be
36   included in the annual GAA.
37
38          Section 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days
39   after the effectivity of this Act, the Board shall, in consultation with the CHED,
40   issued the rules and regulations to fully implement the provisions of this Act.
