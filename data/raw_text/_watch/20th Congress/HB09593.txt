                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Education remains one of the most powerful instruments for national
development, social mobility, technological advancement, and economic
transformation. As the country confronts the challenges of industrial modernization,
digital transformation, and global competitiveness, there is an urgent need to
strengthen institutions that specialize in technical, vocational, applied scientific, and
professional education. The Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP), as one of
the largest state universities in the country and a leading institution in providing
affordable and accessible higher education, is uniquely positioned to fulfill this national
role.

      Since its establishment, PUP has consistently produced graduates who
contribute significantly to the nation's workforce, public service, industry,
entrepreneurship, research, and innovation. However, the increasing complexity of
economic and technological demands requires a stronger institutional framework that
will enable the University to exercise greater academic leadership, expand its research
and extension functions, strengthen industry linkages, and serve as a model for
polytechnic education throughout the country.

      This measure seeks to formally strengthen the Polytechnic University of the
Philippines as the country's First National Polytechnic University. The bill affirms
PUP's leadership role in advancing polytechnic education and mandates it to provide
guidance and support to other polytechnic state universities and colleges in developing
professional, technical, and applied academic programs responsive to national
development priorities.

      The proposed legislation further enhances the University's institutional and fiscal
autonomy, strengthens its governance structure, expands its academic and
administrative capacities, protects and maximizes the productive use of its assets and
landholdings, and grants fiscal incentives necessary to support its educational mission.
It likewise provides safeguards to ensure transparency, accountability, and


                                            1
responsible stewardship of university resources while preserving the institution's
academic independence and public character.

     By elevating and strengthening PUP as the National Polytechnic University, the
State invests in a strategic institution capable of producing highly skilled professionals,
advancing applied research, promoting technological innovation, supporting industrial
development, and expanding educational opportunities for millions of Filipinos. This
measure is expected to contribute significantly to nation-building by cultivating a
globally competitive workforce equipped to meet the demands of the twenty-first
century economy.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
