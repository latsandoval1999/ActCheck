                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The State recognizes that infrastructure is a critical instrument for
regional integration and equitable access to opportunities. Strategic road
networks, particularly those that connect urban centers to hinterland
communities, play a vital role in unlocking economic potential and ensuring
that development reaches even the most geographically isolated and
disadvantaged areas.

       The Cagayan de Oro City–Iligan City Hinterland Road in Barangay
Rogongon is envisioned as a crucial corridor linking Iligan City to upland
barangays and connecting it further toward Cagayan de Oro City. A feasibility
study has already been conducted, affirming its viability and strategic
importance to regional connectivity and upland development. Traversing
agricultural production areas and remote communities, the road is intended
to facilitate the movement of goods, services, and people. Access across this
terrain has long been constrained by distance and inadequate infrastructure.

     While road opening and initial concreting works have commenced at
the regional level by the Department of Public Works and Highways
(DPWH), substantial portions of the alignment remain incomplete and
underdeveloped. This condition limits continuity, constrains mobility, and
prevents the road from fully serving as both an economic driver for upland
communities and a strategic inter-city linkage.

      For upland communities, improved road access directly translates to
better market integration and increased access to essential services such as
healthcare, education, and government programs. For the broader region,
the road strengthens connectivity between Iligan City and Cagayan de Oro
City, enhancing trade flows, supporting logistics, and contributing to a more
resilient and interconnected Northern Mindanao corridor.

      Declaring the Cagayan de Oro City–Iligan City Hinterland Road a
national road and ensuring its full construction and completion require
sustained national support. This will enable the DPWH to undertake
comprehensive infrastructure development, including the continued
opening, upgrading, and long-term maintenance, in accordance with
national standards.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
 DECLARING THE CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY–ILIGAN CITY HINTERLAND
  ROAD IN BARANGAY ROGONGON, ILIGAN CITY A NATIONAL ROAD,
PROVIDING FOR ITS CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT AND MAINTENANCE,
            AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Cagayan
de Oro City–Iligan City Hinterland National Road Development Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration. — The Cagayan de Oro City–Iligan City
Hinterland Road in Barangay Rogongon, Iligan City, traversing Purok Scaling
to the crossing near Balangkot Elementary School in Sitio Banyugan, shall be
declared a national road under the supervision and administration of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

      SECTION 3. Development and Maintenance. — The DPWH shall
undertake the construction, completion, improvement, and maintenance of
the road in accordance with national standards and specifications.

      SECTION 4. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the
continued construction, improvement, and maintenance of the road, as well
as other activities necessary for its development as a national road, shall be
included in the General Appropriations Act (GAA). Thereafter, the Secretary
of Public Works and Highways shall include the funding requirements
therefor in the Department’s program for inclusion in the annual GAA.
       SECTION 5. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
