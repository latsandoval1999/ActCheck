                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Constitution expressly mandates the State to make quality education
accessible to all at all levels1. Republic Act No. 11861, or the Expanded Solo
Parents’ Welfare Act, significantly strengthened social protection for solo parents
and their families, however, its provisions on educational benefits remain largely
priority-based and discretionary, providing a full scholarship to only one (1) child
and prioritization for other children under existing assistance programs.

      This measure addresses a continuing policy gap by institutionalizing
guaranteed educational support across the full education cycle for children of solo
parents, from basic education to tertiary education where financial vulnerability
is most acutely felt.

       This measure (a) anchors free tertiary education in public higher
educational institutions on the existing framework of Republic Act No. 10931 or
the Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act, thereby ensuring access
without creating a new funding mechanism or duplicating existing programs; (b)
provides reasonable and proportionate tuition discounts in private educational
institutions through discount grants in private elementary and secondary schools
together with enhanced discounts at the tertiary level, consistent with long-
standing social legislation that recognizes education as imbued with public
interest; and (c) transforms priority-based assistance into enforceable statutory
entitlements, ensuring that children of solo parents are not excluded from
education due to financial constraints at critical stages of learning.

maintaining fiscal prudence and respecting the regulatory framework governing
private educational institutions.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly
sought.

     AN ACT STRENGTHENING THE EDUCATIONAL BENEFITS FOR
     CHILDREN OF SOLO PARENTS BY PROVIDING FREE TERTIARY
     EDUCATION IN PUBLIC HIGHER EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS
     AND MANDATORY TUITION DISCOUNTS IN PRIVATE BASIC AND
     HIGHER EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS, AMENDING FOR THE
     PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8972, AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC
     ACT NO. 11861 OR THE EXPANDED SOLO PARENTS’ WELFARE ACT

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:


 1         SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Solo Parent
 2   Children’s Education Support Act”.
 3
 4         Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to:
 5         a. Ensure access to quality education at all levels as mandated by Article
 6            XIV of the 1987 Constitution;
 7         b. Provide enhanced and sustained social protection to solo parents and
 8            their dependent children as a recognized vulnerable sector under the
 9            law;
10         c. Promote intergenerational social mobility by reducing the financial
11            barriers to basic and tertiary education for children of solo parents; and
12         d. Harmonize educational assistance for solo parent families with existing
13            education and social welfare laws.
14
15          Sec. 3. Amendment to Republic Act No. 8972, as amended by Republic
16   Act No. 11861. Republic Act No. 8972, as amended by Republic Act No. 11861,
17   is hereby further amended by inserting a new section after Section 9, to read as
18   follows:
19
20         “SEC. 9-A. Educational Benefits for Dependent Children of Solo Parents.
21   –
22         (a) Tuition Discounts in Private Elementary and Secondary
23             Educational Institutions. Dependent children of duly registered solo
24             parents who are enrolled in private elementary and secondary
25             educational institutions, including junior high school and senior high
26             school, shall be entitled to a twenty percent (20%) discount on tuition
27             and other mandatory school fees.
28         (b) Tuition Discounts in Private Higher Educational Institutions.
29             Dependent children of solo parents enrolled in private higher
30             educational institutions shall be entitled to a fifty percent (50%)
31             discount on tuition and other mandatory school fees.
32         (c) Free Tertiary Education in Public Higher Educational Institutions.
33             All dependent children of solo parents shall be entitled to free tuition
34             and other mandatory school fees when enrolled in State Universities
35             and Colleges (SUCs), Local Universities and Colleges (LUCs), and
36             other public higher educational institutions, in accordance with
37             Republic Act No. 10931, otherwise known as the Universal Access to
38             Quality Tertiary Education Act.
39         (d) Application and Non-Exclusivity of Benefits. The discounts and
40             benefits provided under this section shall:
41             1. Apply only to tuition and other mandatory school fees;
42             2. Be applied after all applicable scholarships, grants, or subsidies; and
43             3. Be availed of without prejudice to other education assistance
44                programs under existing laws.
45         (e) Eligibility Requirements. To qualify for the benefits under this
46             section, the dependent child must:
47             1. Be a dependent child as defined under this Act:
48             2. Present a valid Solo Parent Identification Card; and
49             3. Comply with the admission, retention, and academic policies of the
50                educational institution concerned.
51         (f) Implementing Agencies. The Department of Education (DepEd), the
52             Commission on Higher Education (CHED), the Unified Student
53             Financial Assistance System for Tertiary Education (UniFAST), and
54             the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) shall
55             jointly promulgate the implementing rules and regulations within ninety
56             (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.”**
57
