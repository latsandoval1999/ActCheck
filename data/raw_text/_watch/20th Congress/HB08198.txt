                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Constitution guarantees every citizen the right to freedom of travel and to choose their
place of residence, placing upon the State the duty to uphold and protect this fundamental liberty.

         This measure seeks to make travel more accessible to Filipinos by abolishing the travel tax
currently being imposed on travelers. At present, international travelers departing from the Philippines
already shoulder airline fares, fuel surcharges, and various government-imposed fees. The travel tax,
imposed on top of these charges, further increases the total cost of outbound travel in a setting where
travel is already expensive.

        For many Filipinos, traveling abroad is not a luxury but a necessity for work, business,
education, medical treatment, or family obligations. Imposing an additional tax on international travel
places unnecessary financial strain on citizens and may undermine the country’s competitiveness in an
increasingly interconnected global economy. By reducing travel costs, this measure can encourage
greater mobility, stimulate tourism, and promote the freer flow of economic activity.

        Although the travel tax generates funds for certain tourism-related programs, its share in overall
national revenue is not substantial. Any revenue loss may be offset by increased economic activity
resulting from higher travel demand and a more dynamic tourism sector.

         Ultimately, this proposal removes an outdated levy that no longer aligns with present economic
realities. In doing so, it affirms the State’s commitment to protecting freedom of travel, supporting
Filipino mobility, strengthening tourism competitiveness, and fostering broader participation in the
global economy.

        In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                        AN ACT ABOLISHING THE TRAVEL TAX

  Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
  assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Travel Tax Abolition Act of 2026.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to promote tourism, encourage
mobility of Filipino citizens, reduce the financial burden on overseas travel, and enhance the
competitiveness of Philippine airports and carriers in the global aviation market. The State
recognizes that the imposition of a travel tax increases the cost of outbound travel and may
discourage international tourism exchanges, labor mobility, and business travel.

SECTION 3. Prohibition and Refund. – It shall be prohibited for any government agency and
private entity to impose and collect travel taxes upon the effectivity of this Act.

Travel Taxes collected by any authorized collecting agency for flights dated on or after the
effectivity of this Act shall be refunded by the same agency to the passengers.

SECTION 4. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to fund the programs affected by the
abolition of the travel tax shall be included in the annual budget of the concerned agency under
the General Appropriations Act (GAA).

SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the approval
of this Act, the Department of Budget and Management and the Department of Tourism, in
coordination with relevant agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for its
effective implementation.

SECTION 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.
SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, order, rules and regulations or parts thereof,
inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Act are repealed, amended or modified
accordingly.

SECTION 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
