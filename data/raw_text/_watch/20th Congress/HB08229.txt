                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to declare May 13 of every year as a special nonworking
holiday in the Province of Benguet in celebration of the Kankana-ey Festival. The
measure aims to honor the cultural heritage, history, and enduring contributions of
the Kankana-ey people, one of the major ethnolinguistic groups that have shaped the
cultural identity and socio-economic development of the province.

      The term “Kankana-ey,” also spelled Kankanaey, Kankanay, Kankanai, or
Kankana-i, refers to both the people and the culture predominantly found in Benguet
and Mountain Province within the Cordillera Administrative Region. In Benguet, the
Kankana-ey are largely concentrated in the municipalities of Mankayan, Bakun,
Kibungan, Buguias, the upper half of Kapangan, and parts of Atok. Kankana-ey
communities are also found in other municipalities of the province like La Trinidad,
Tublay, Bokod, Kabayan, Itogon, Sablan, Tuba. Collectively known as the Benguet
Kankana-ey, they are distinct from the Benguet Ibaloy, who mainly inhabit the
southern parts of the province, including the highly urban center of Baguio City.

       The Kankana-ey people, with their rich ethnolinguistic identity, deep-rooted
traditions, and long-standing presence in the highlands, remain one of the most
influential Indigenous Peoples in Benguet. Their customs, cultural practices, and
social systems continue to contribute significantly to the province’s collective
identity and its socio-economic landscape.

       To formally recognize and honor this legacy, the Kankana-ey-concentrated
municipalities of Kibungan, Kapangan, Bakun, Buguias, and Mankayan jointly
celebrated the first Kankana-ey Festival on May 13, 2023, an event now
institutionalized as an annual celebration and officially acknowledged by the
provincial government. It serves as the counterpart of the Ibaloy Festival, which
pays tribute to another historically significant ethnolinguistic group in the province.

       It is important to highlight that in the nineteenth (19th) Congress, similar bills
were filed separately for the municipalities of Bakun, Buguias, Kapangan, Kibungan,
and Mankayan. However, this consolidated measure is now being filed as one
unified bill to reflect the presence of Kankana-ey communities across the thirteen
(13) municipalities in Benguet and to avoid any perceived exclusion or unequal
recognition among them. Also, it ensures fairness, inclusivity, and consistency in
honoring the ethnolinguistic group as a whole.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is respectfully sought.

                           AN ACT
DECLARING MAY 13 OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE LONE DISTRICT OF BENGUET IN CELEBRATION OF THE
KANKANA-EY FESTIVAL

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. May 13 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking
holiday in the Province of Benguet, in celebration of the annual Kankana-ey Festival.

       SEC. 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
