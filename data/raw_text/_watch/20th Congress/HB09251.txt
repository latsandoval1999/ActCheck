                           AN ACT
  QUALIFYING ALL PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS TO RECEIVE THE
 TEACHING ALLOWANCE, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC
        ACT 11997 OR THE KABALIKAT SA PAGTUTURO ACT



                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act 11997 or An Act Institutionalizing the Grant of a Teaching
Allowance for Public School Teachers and Appropriating Funds Therefor was enacted on
March 13, 2024. Principally authored by ACT Teachers Party-List Rep. Antonio L. Tinio
and Rep. France L. Castro, this law was borne out of a longstanding campaign for
adequate support for the purchase of chalks, bond papers, cartolina, and countless other
materials that they use in the delivery of education. Otherwise known as the Kabalikat sa
Pagtuturo Act, the law not only institutionalized what was previously called the “chalk
allowance” but increased it to ₱10,000.

        But under current regulations implementing this law, only classroom teachers or
those with teaching loads are qualified to receive the allowance. Section 4 of the IRR
reiterates the coverage of the law—which has a broad language that should be interpreted
appropriately. However, per Section 8, only “qualified public school teachers assigned
with teaching load” are the ones entitled to the allowance. For instance, Principals, those
assigned as Assistant School Head Designates or Department Head Designates, and


                                                   1
others whose plantilla positions who do not as a general rule have teaching loads, are
required to present proof of actual teaching hours that they rendered, and they will be
given only pro-rated teaching allowance. If they had not been assigned teaching loads
within the school year, they do not qualify for it at all. There have been reports from the
field of teachers getting zero teaching allowance because they have been assigned only
non-teaching functions.

        DepEd’s interpretation closes its eyes to the intent of the law, that is, to provide
assistance to public school teachers by covering the expenses necessary for the delivery
of quality education, thus, “for the purchase of tangible or intangible teaching supplies
and materials, the payment of incidental expenses, and the implementation or conduct of
various learning delivery modalities.” (Sections 2 and 4) By doing so, the DepEd
excludes from the benefit many public school teachers who spend out of their own
pockets for supplies, materials, and other expenses incidental to the delivery of education
services.

        To cure this situation, this Bill seeks to amend Republic Act 11997 by clarifying
the legislative intent that all public school teachers are entitled to the grant of the teaching
allowance. Republic Act 4670 or the Magna Carta for Public School Teachers, defines
public school teachers as “all persons engaged in classroom teaching, in any level of
instruction, on full-time basis, including x x x all other persons performing supervisory
and/or administrative functions in all schools x x x operated by the Government or its
political subdivisions; but shall not include school nurses, school physicians, school
dentists, and other school employees.” This means that even Principals or School Heads
and Head Teachers, as well as those holding minimal classroom teaching loads such as
Master Teachers, are public school teachers. In the course of their duties as such, they
buy tangible and intangible teaching supplies, pay incidental expenses, and in general,
implement the various learning delivery modalities—all of which must be paid for by the
government through the teaching allowance per Sections 2 and 4 of Republic Act 11997.

       For these reasons, immediate approval of this Bill is sought.

                           AN ACT
  QUALIFYING ALL PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS TO RECEIVE THE
 TEACHING ALLOWANCE, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC
        ACT 11997 OR THE KABALIKAT SA PAGTUTURO ACT


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Section 3 of Republic Act 11997 is hereby amended to read as follows:

        “Section 3. Coverage. - This grant of the cash allowance authorized in this Act
        shall cover all public school teachers AS DEFINED IN REPUBLIC ACT
        4670, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE MAGNA CARTA FOR PUBLIC
        SCHOOL TEACHERS[, who are engaged in basic education curriculum,
        consistent with the policy guidelines and learning delivery modalities
        recognized and implemented by the Department of Education (DepEd)];
        PROVIDED, THAT THE TEACHING ALLOWANCE SHALL BE
        GIVEN TO PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS REGARDLESS OF THE
        NUMBER OF TEACHING LOADS TO WHICH THEY HAVE BEEN
        ASSIGNED, AND DESPITE THE ASSIGNMENT TO THEM OF NON-
        TEACHING FUNCTIONS.”

SEC. 2. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, resolutions, orders or ordinances, rules
and regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act, are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 3. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
