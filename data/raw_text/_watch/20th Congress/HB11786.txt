                             AN ACT
    INSTITUTING A COMPREHENSIVE NATIONAL CARE TO ENCOURAGE
  COOPERATION, ACCOUNTABILITY AND RESEARCH FOR THE EDUCATION
   AND SUPPORT TO PERSONS WITH AUTISM AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Autism spectrum disorder is a developmental condition that may affect
communication, social interaction, behavior, and sensory processing in different
ways. Persons with autism have diverse abilities and support requirements, and their
needs may change as they progress from early childhood to school age, adolescence,
and adulthood. Access to timely assessment, appropriate educational support,
healthcare, and family guidance can help improve participation and quality of life.

       A comprehensive national framework can help address fragmentation among
education, health, and social welfare services. It can encourage cooperation among
agencies, strengthen professional training, improve access to evidence-based
support, and support research that informs national policy. The legislation should
also ensure that programs respect the rights and preferences of persons with autism
and do not treat a diagnosis as a basis for discrimination or exclusion.

       The World Health Organization (WHO) estimates that approximately one in
every 100 individuals is on the autism spectrum. Applied to the Philippine
population, this translates to an estimated 1.13 million Filipinos with Autism
Spectrum Disorder (ASD). However, government records show that only 98,245
individuals with ASD were registered in the country's identification card registry from
2021 to 2025. This substantial disparity suggests that many persons with autism
remain unidentified or undiagnosed and are consequently not reflected in official
records. These figures underscore the need to strengthen early identification,
diagnostic services, intervention programs, and sustained support for persons with
autism and their families through a comprehensive and coordinated national
framework.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
  INSTITUTING A COMPREHENSIVE NATIONAL CARE TO ENCOURAGE
     COOPERATION, ACCOUNTABILITY AND RESEARCH FOR THE
   EDUCATION AND SUPPORT TO PERSONS WITH AUTISM AND FOR
                      OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Comprehensive
National Care for Persons with Autism.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to recognize the dignity, rights, abilities, and diverse needs of persons with
autism and to promote their full and meaningful participation in society. The
State shall uphold their rights to inclusive education, appropriate healthcare,
habilitation and rehabilitation services, social protection, accessible public
facilities, and equal opportunities for personal development and community
participation.

The State shall promote a coordinated national framework that strengthens
cooperation among government agencies, local government units, educational
institutions, healthcare providers, families, and organizations representing
persons with autism. It shall support evidence-based research, early
identification, appropriate interventions, inclusive learning environments, family
assistance, and the development of services throughout the life course, with due
regard for individual autonomy and the diversity of support needs.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

         a) Autism or Autism Spectrum Disorder refers to a neurological
            condition characterized by challenges with social skills, repetitive
            behaviors, and communication, as well as by unique strengths, as
             defined in the latest version of the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual
             of Mental Disorders.

          b) Diagnosis refers to necessary assessments, including but not limited
             to neuropsychological evaluations, genetic testing, MRIs, hearing
             tests, and psychometric tests to diagnose or rule out autism or
             developmental disabilities in individuals.

          c) Intervention refers to interceding action undertaken to help improve
             the autism prognosis. Intervention in autism may take the form of
             medical, pharmacologic, therapeutic, educational, and non-
             traditional habilitative care.

          d) Epidemiology refers to the study and analysis of the distribution and
             determinants of health conditions, like autism, in defined
             populations.

SECTION 4. Treatment as Persons with Disability. - All PWAs shall be treated
as persons with disabilities, as defined under Republic Act No. 7277, as
amended. They shall be entitled to all the rights, privileges, and benefits provided
for persons with disabilities under existing laws.

SECTION 5. Continuing Education and Training on Autism for Public
Service Providers. - All officers and staff in charge of providing services to the
public, including but not limited to the Department of Health (DOH), Department
of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of Education (DepEd),
Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Department of Justice (DOJ),
Department of Transportation (DOTr), Department of Interior and Local
Government (DILG), Department of National Defense (DND), Department of
Science and Technology (DOST), Commission on Higher Education (CHED),
Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDSA), National
Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA), Persons with Disabilities Affairs Office
(PDAO) at the Local Government Units (LGUs), Public Safety Units (military,
police, firemen and other first responders), shall undergo mandatory continuing
education to sensitize and empower service providers to become effective
frontliners in the delivery of services to PWAs and their families.

SECTION 6. Epidemiological Surveillance Programs. - The DOH, in
coordination with different academic institutions and non-governmental
organizations, is hereby mandated to conduct an epidemiological survey to
determine the prevalence of autism in the country annually to provide data to
government agencies and the private sector, to make necessary services available
to PWAs and their families.

SECTION 7. Evidence-based Research. - The DOH, the Philippine Council for
Health Research and Development (PCHRD), and the National Institute of Health
(NIH) are mandated, in coordination with local and international educational
institutions, to strengthen their research focus on autism; to invest in clinical
and molecular studies and grants to support the development of best practices
in autism intervention in the Philippines.

SECTION 8. Early Detection and Intervention of Autism. - The DOH, in
coordination with the DepEd, is hereby mandated to establish a program for early
screening and detection of autism, to provide the appropriate services to children
and toddlers with developmental delays associated with autism, and to ensure
the central PWD registry is updated accordingly.

SECTION 9. Mandatory PhilHealth Coverage. - All persons with autism shall
be covered by the National Health Insurance Program of the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PHIC). Funds necessary to ensure the enrolment of
PWAs, who are currently covered under any existing category, shall be sourced
from the National Health Insurance Fund of PHIC, earmarked from the proceeds
of sin tax collections, as provided for in Republic Act No. 10351. It shall likewise
include the cost of early screening and detection programs, as one of the available
benefits of its members.

SECTION 10. Protection Against Discrimination in Schools, Colleges,
Universities and Training Institutions. - The DepEd, CHED and TESDA shall
formulate or update policies and protocols to ensure that all educational
institutions shall be inclusive to learners with autism, in matters including, but
not limited to, acceptance to academic or training programs, instructional
supports and behavior management.

SECTION 11. Provision for Equal Opportunities for Employment. - The DOLE
and the Civil Service Commission (CSC) shall formulate policies and programs to
ensure that adult PWAs are provided equal opportunities for gainful employment
in the private and public sector.

SECTION 12. Protection against Discrimination in Insurance Coverage. -
The Department of Finance (DOF) shall formulate or update policies and
protocols to ensure that all insurance providers shall provide PWAs access to life
insurance instruments and shall include allied medical procedures (like
occupational and speech therapies) in health insurance benefits of policyholders.

SECTION 13. Protection in cases of Disasters and Emergencies. – The DILG
and the DND shall formulate the necessary policies and protocols to ensure the
military, public safety professionals, LGUs, and first responders shall provide the
necessary life-saving support to PWAs with behavioral and communication
challenges.

SECTION 14. Access to Transportation. – The DOTr shall formulate the
necessary policies and protocols to ensure PWAs with behavioral and
communication challenges and their families are given reasonable
accommodation in accessing all forms of transportation service providers over
land, sea and air.

SECTION 15. Access to Transportation. – The DOTr shall formulate the
necessary policies and protocols to ensure PWAs with behavioral and
communication challenges and their families are given reasonable
accommodation in accessing all forms of transportation service providers over
land, sea and air.

SECTION 16. The Philippine Council for Mental Health. – The Philippine
Council for Mental Health (PCMH), a multi-sector body established by the Mental
Health Act to provide for a coherent, rational and unified response to mental
health in the country, shall spearhead and coordinate the government actions to
address the needs of Filipinos with autism and their families.

SECTION 17. The National Autism Care Plan. – The PCMH, in coordination
with other government agencies and stakeholders, shall develop, regularly
update, and monitor the implementation of the National Autism Care Plan
(NACP), a holistic medium-term plan to respond to the needs of PWAs and their
families, such as but not limited to financial assistance, therapy, medications
and equipment, for the guidance of the stakeholders in the public and private
sectors. The PCMH shall likewise coordinate the development and
implementation of necessary policies and programs to operationalize the NACP
and to ensure the needs of PWAs are served.

SECTION 18. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the
provisions of this Act shall be included and incorporated in the annual general
appropriations of the DOH.

SECTION 12. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force
and effect.

SECTION 13. Repealing Clause. - All laws, rules, and regulations inconsistent
with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 14. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
