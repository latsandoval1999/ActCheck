                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Philippines is increasingly confronted with the twin challenges of
groundwater depletion and groundwater contamination brought about by rapid
urbanization, population growth, unsustainable extraction practices, and the
worsening impacts of climate change. Groundwater remains a critical source of
potable water, agricultural irrigation, industrial use, and ecological stability,
particularly in areas vulnerable to drought, saltwater intrusion, and seasonal
water shortages.

      Consistent with the State policy to protect and advance the right of the
people to a balanced and healthful ecology, there is a need to institutionalize a
comprehensive and science-based approach to groundwater preservation and
management.

       Despite its importance, the country has yet to adopt a comprehensive
national framework specifically focused on the preservation, sustainable
management, and restoration of groundwater resources. Excessive groundwater
withdrawal has already resulted in declining water tables, land subsidence,
saltwater intrusion in coastal communities, and the degradation of aquifers in
several parts of the country. These threats endanger water security, food
production, public health, environmental sustainability, and long-term economic
resilience.

      This proposed measure seeks to establish a National Groundwater
Preservation and Management Program that institutionalizes groundwater
monitoring, recharge enhancement, aquifer protection, sustainable extraction
practices, and inter-agency coordination among national government agencies
and local government units. It further integrates groundwater preservation into
climate change adaptation and disaster risk reduction strategies while promoting
water-efficient technologies and responsible resource management.

      The bill likewise strengthens local participation by encouraging the
integration of groundwater preservation measures into local land use and
climate action planning, while providing incentives for industries, farmers,
cooperatives, and communities adopting sustainable water conservation and
recharge practices.
       By harmonizing national and local efforts toward the protection of the
country’s groundwater resources, this measure aims to secure a sustainable and
resilient water future for present and succeeding generations.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
      ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL GROUNDWATER PRESERVATION
      AND   MANAGEMENT   PROGRAM,   PROVIDING  FOR  THE
      SUSTAINABLE USE, PROTECTION, AND RESTORATION OF
      GROUNDWATER RESOURCES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the "National
Groundwater Preservation and Management Act."

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to protect
and conserve groundwater as a vital source of potable water, agricultural
irrigation, industrial supply, and ecological balance. The State shall regulate its
extraction, ensure sustainable recharge, prevent contamination, and integrate
groundwater management into climate change adaptation and disaster risk
reduction consistent with the Philippine Water Code, the Local Government
Code, the Climate Change Act, and the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Act.

      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. For purposes of this Act:

      (a) Groundwater refers to water found beneath the surface of the ground,
          occupying the pores and crevices of soil, sand, and rock formations.

      (b) Aquifer refers to a geological formation that can store, transmit, and
          yield significant quantities of groundwater.

      (c) Groundwater recharge refers to the process of water infiltrating from
          the surface to replenish aquifers.
      (d) Over-extraction refers to the withdrawal of groundwater beyond the safe
          yield or recharge capacity of the aquifer, leading to depletion or
          degradation.

      (e) Saltwater intrusion refers to the movement of saline water into
          freshwater aquifers due to excessive pumping or reduced recharge.
      SECTION 4. National Groundwater Preservation and Management
Program. There is hereby established a National Groundwater Preservation and
Management Program (NGPMP) to be implemented by the Department of
Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) in coordination with the National
Water Resources Board (NWRB) as the primary regulatory agency for water
resources, the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), the
Department of Agriculture (DA), the Department of Health (DOH), and local
government units (LGUs).

     The Program shall:

     (a) Establish a nationwide groundwater monitoring network for water
         levels, quality and recharge rates;

     (b) Designate critical groundwater areas subject            to   regulation,
         rehabilitation, or moratorium on new wells;

     (c) Promote artificial recharge methods such as rainwater harvesting,
         infiltration ponds, and percolation tanks;

     (d) Protect recharge zones from        land   conversion,   pollution,   and
         unsustainable development;

     (e) Support and strengthen the regulation by the NWRB of the drilling of
         new wells through groundwater assessments, monitoring systems, and
         technical recommendations on spacing requirements and allowable
         abstraction limits;

     (f) Provide technical and financial assistance to LUs and water districts to
         implement sustainable extraction and recharge projects; and

     (g) Promote the reuse of treated wastewater and water-saving technologies
         to reduce pressure on aquifers.




     SECTION 5. Role of Local Government Units (LGUs). LGUs shall:

     (a)   Incorporate groundwater preservation strategies in their
           Comprehensive Land Use Plans (CLUPs) and Local Climate Change
           Action Plans (LCCAPs);

     (b)   Designate or utilize existing local environment, planning, or disaster
           risk reduction offices or councils to coordinate groundwater
           preservation initiatives;
(c)   Subject to the availability of local funds and applicable budgeting
      laws, allocate appropriate funds for groundwater recharge and
      monitoring projects; and

(d)   Maintain local databases of registered wells, water quality, and
      abstraction volumes, linked to the national database.

SECTION 6. Incentives.

(a) Industries, cooperatives, and communities that adopt water recycling,
    aquifer recharge, or efficient irrigation technologies shall be eligible for
    tax incentives under the Green Jobs Act (RA 10771).

(b) Farmers practicing groundwater-saving irrigation methods (e.g.,
    alternate wetting and drying for rice) shall be eligible for crop insurance
    premium discounts from the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation
    (PCIC).

(c) LGUs with exemplary groundwater preservation programs shall be
    recognized under the Seal of Good Local Governance.

SECTION 7. Funding. The initial amount necessary to carry out this Act
shall be charged against current appropriations of the DENR, DPWH, and
NWRB. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be included in
the annual General Appropriations Act. LGUs may also access the People's
Survival Fund (RA 10174) and their Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Fund for eligible climate adaptation, mitigation, and disaster
resilience measures related to groundwater protection, consistent with
existing laws and auditing rules.

SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90)
days from effectivity of this Act, the DENR, with the NWRB, in coordination
with DPWH, DA, DOH, and LGUs, shall promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary for its implementation.

SECTION 9. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive issuances,
and regulations inconsistent with this Act are repealed or modified
accordingly.

Nothing in this Act shall diminish or modify the powers and functions of
the National Water Resources Board under Presidential Decree No. 1067
or other existing laws.”

SECTION 11. Non-Impairment Clause. — Nothing in this Act shall
diminish or modify the powers and functions of the National Water
Resources Board under Presidential Decree No. 1067 or other existing
laws.

SECTION 12. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.

      Approved.
