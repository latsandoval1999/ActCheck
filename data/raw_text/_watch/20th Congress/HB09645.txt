                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines remains highly vulnerable to typhoons, earthquakes, floods, volcanic
eruptions, and other natural and human-induced disasters that place communities at
risk and disrupt the delivery of essential government services. In times of calamity, the
swift declaration of a state of calamity is crucial in order to immediately mobilize
government resources, facilitate rescue and relief operations, and ensure the timely
delivery of assistance to affected communities.

Under existing law, the declaration of a state of calamity in local government units
requires action by the local Sanggunian upon recommendation of the Local Disaster
Risk Reduction and Management Council (LDRRMC). However, in actual emergency
situations, convening the Sanggunian and securing the necessary quorum and
approval may cause delays at a time when immediate government response is
urgently needed.

Therefore, this bill seeks to amend Republic Act No. 10121 by authorizing the local
chief executive to declare a state of calamity within the local government unit upon
recommendation of the LDRRMC. By streamlining the declaration process,
government agencies and local officials will be able to respond more rapidly and
efficiently during emergencies and disaster situations.

In effect, this measure should strengthen disaster response mechanisms, improve the
delivery of emergency assistance, and protect the welfare and safety of communities
affected by calamities and disasters.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10121, AND TRANSFERRING
     THE POWER TO DECLARE A STATE OF CALAMITY IN A LOCAL
         GOVERNMENT UNIT TO THE LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:


SEC. 1. Section 16 of Republic Act Numbered 10121 is hereby amended to read as
follows:
       “Section 16. Declaration of State of Calamity. - The National Council
       shall recommend to the President of the Philippines the declaration of a
       cluster of barangays, municipalities, cities, provinces, and regions under
       a state of calamity, and the lifting thereof, based on the criteria set by
       the National Council. The President's declaration may warrant
       international humanitarian assistance as deemed necessary.
       “The declaration and lifting of the state of calamity may also be issued
       by the local CHIEF EXECUTIVE [sanggunian], or any official acting in
       such capacity, upon the recommendation of the LDRRMC, based on the
       results of the damage assessment and needs analysis. THE
       DECLARATION MAY BE LIFTED AT ANY TIME BY THE LOCAL
       SANGGUNIAN.”
SEC. 2. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and regulations which are
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation, whichever comes
earlier.
