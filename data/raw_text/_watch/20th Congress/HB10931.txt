                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Saint Bernard–Hinunangan Diversion Road is a vital lifeline for the Paci ic Towns
of Southern Leyte. By providing a shorter and more direct route between the
municipalities of Saint Bernard and Hinunangan, it reduces travel time, lowers
transportation costs, and makes it easier for people to reach schools, hospitals,
markets, government of ices, and places of work. It also allows farmers, isherfolk, and
local entrepreneurs to transport their products more quickly and ef iciently, creating
greater opportunities for economic growth and improving the quality of life of
communities along its route.

Beyond its economic value, the diversion road plays a critical role during emergencies
and natural disasters. Whenever the Agas-Agas route becomes impassable due to
landslides or other hazards, this road serves as an important alternative route for
residents, emergency responders, relief operations, and the delivery of essential
goods and services. Its importance was clearly demonstrated during Typhoon Odette,
when access to several areas became dif icult. This bill seeks to convert the Saint
Bernard–Hinunangan Diversion Road into a national road, placing it under the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) for its continued improvement,
maintenance, and development. The measure also ensures that the project will be
implemented in accordance with engineering, environmental, disaster-resilience, and
right-of-way requirements to guarantee a safe, durable, and reliable road network.

throughout the province. By providing the national government's sustained support

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Saint Bernard–
Hinunangan Diversion Road Nationalization Act.”

SECTION 2. Conversion into a National Road. — The Saint Bernard–Hinunangan
Diversion Road, beginning at Barangay Mahayahay in the Municipality of Saint
Bernard and ending at Barangay Badiangon in the Municipality of Hinunangan,
Province of Southern Leyte, including its existing and succeeding road sections,
bridges, drainage structures, slope-protection works, and other appurtenant
facilities, is hereby converted into a national road.

The inal technical description, length, alignment, road classi ication, and boundaries
of the converted road shall be determined and certi ied by the Department of Public
Works and Highways (DPWH), based on an approved inventory, survey, and detailed
engineering design.

SECTION 3. Administration and Supervision. — The DPWH shall have
administrative jurisdiction over, and shall be responsible for the planning,


                                                                           3|Page
programming, design, construction, improvement, rehabilitation, repair, and
maintenance of the Saint Bernard–Hinunangan Diversion Road.

The DPWH shall coordinate with the Provincial Government of Southern Leyte, the
Municipal Governments of Saint Bernard and Hinunangan, and other concerned
government agencies in implementing this Act.

SECTION 4. Project Development and Implementation. — The DPWH shall
undertake the necessary feasibility study, engineering surveys, geotechnical and
geohazard assessments, environmental studies, right-of-way investigation, and
detailed engineering design for the road.

The project shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws, rules,
regulations, DPWH standards, environmental requirements, public-procurement
laws, and disaster-risk-reduction and climate-resilience standards.

The DPWH may implement the project by phases, taking into account the road’s
connectivity bene its, structural and safety requirements, right-of-way availability,
technical readiness, and the availability of appropriations.

SECTION 5. Right-of-Way and Other Site Requirements. — The acquisition,
donation, negotiation, expropriation, valuation, payment, and other arrangements
concerning the right-of-way and project site shall be undertaken in accordance with
the Constitution and applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

No private property shall be taken for the project without due process and payment
of just compensation as required by law. The DPWH shall prioritize the prompt
resolution of right-of-way issues to avoid unnecessary delay in project
implementation.

SECTION 6. Funding. — The amount necessary for the feasibility study, engineering
design, surveys, environmental and geohazard assessments, right-of-way acquisition,
construction, improvement, rehabilitation, repair, and maintenance of the road shall
be included in the General Appropriations Act.

The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include the project in the
Department’s annual and multi-year infrastructure programs, subject to the
availability of appropriations and compliance with applicable budgetary,
procurement, accounting, auditing, and other laws, rules, and regulations.


                                                                         4|Page
Nothing in this Act shall be construed as an immediate appropriation of a speci ic
amount not provided in a duly enacted appropriations law.

SECTION 7. Maintenance. — Upon the completion or acceptance of any section of
the road, the DPWH shall include that section and its appurtenant structures in its
regular national-road maintenance, repair, rehabilitation, and improvement
programs.

The DPWH shall ensure that the road remains safe, passable, and serviceable
throughout the year, consistent with applicable national-road standards and available
appropriations.

SECTION 8. Disaster Resilience and Road Safety. — In improving and maintaining
the road, the DPWH shall prioritize measures addressing landslides, looding,
earthquakes, coastal hazards, road subsidence, and other foreseeable risks.

The DPWH shall also provide, where necessary, appropriate road signs, pavement
markings, guardrails, lighting, drainage systems, retaining structures, slope
protection, and other road-safety facilities.

SECTION 9. Reporting and Accountability. — The DPWH shall submit an annual
report to the Senate and the House of Representatives, through their respective
committees on public works, on the implementation of this Act.

The report shall include the status of the road’s conversion, project plans,
appropriations, obligations and expenditures, right-of-way acquisition, physical and
 inancial accomplishments, implementation issues, and target completion dates.

SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DPWH, in coordination with the concerned local
government units and other relevant government agencies, shall promulgate the rules
and regulations necessary for its effective implementation.

SECTION 11. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected by such declaration shall
remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.
