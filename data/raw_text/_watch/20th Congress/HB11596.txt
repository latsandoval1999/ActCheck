               “REALTY INSTALLMENT BUYER ACT”

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act (R.A) No. 6552, otherwise known as the “Realty Installment Buyer
Act”, was enacted to protect buyers of real property on installment from onerous and
oppressive conditions. Through the years, as real estate transactions and modes of
payment have evolved, there is a need to further strengthen these safeguards and
ensure that buyers are adequately protected from inequitable contractual practices.

       This bill seeks to strengthen the rights of buyers availing installment schemes
by providing clearer rules on grace periods and cancellation of contracts, allowing
restructuring or renegotiation in cases of financial difficulty, regulating penalties and
additional interest on delayed payments, and ensuring the timely transfer and
registration of ownership upon full payment. It likewise recognizes modern means of
communication to ensure that buyers are properly informed before cancellation may
take effect. These amendments seek to provide greater fairness, transparency, and
certainty in real estate installment transactions, while maintaining reasonable
protections for both buyers and sellers.

      In view of the foregoing, which is a counterpart measure of Senate Bill 2504, is
earnestly sought.
