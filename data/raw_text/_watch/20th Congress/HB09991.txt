                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

When Republic Act No. 9003, also known as the Ecological Solid Waste Management
Act of 2000, was passed over twenty years ago, it set the standard for proper waste
management to keep our communities clean. However, its original penalties (a fine of
₱300 to ₱1,000 or 1 to 15 days of community service) are outdated and no longer stop
people from littering.

Today, minor nominal fines are easily dismissed as a negligible cost of non-compliance,
while short community service windows fail to inspire genuine behavioral change.
Consequently, this persistent littering cumulatively obstructs our drainage systems and
waterways, directly causing severe street flooding and leaving local governments to
exhaust valuable resources on emergency cleanups.

This measure scales the penalties to a range of ₱1,000 to ₱10,000 and extends the
alternative community service to 15 to 30 days. This ensures a meaningful reformative
impact on offenders while simultaneously rendering meaningful civic service to support
the Local Government Units (LGUs) in community cleanup efforts and environmental
restoration.

In view of this urgency, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT AMENDING SECTION 49 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9003,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "ECOLOGICAL SOLID WASTE
MANAGEMENT ACT OF 2000," BY INCREASING THE PENALTIES FOR
LITTERING, DUMPING, AND IMPROPER DISPOSAL OF WASTE
MATTER, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Stricter Penalties for Public
Littering Act."

SECTION 2. Amendment of Section 49. – Section 49, paragraph (a) of Republic Act No.
9003 is hereby amended to read as follows:

      "SEC. 49. Fines and Penalties. – (a) Any person who violates Sec. 48,
      paragraph (1) shall, upon conviction, be punished with a fine of not less than
      ONE THOUSAND PESOS (PHP 1,000.00) but not more than TEN
      THOUSAND PESOS (PHP 10,000.00) or render community service for not
      less than FIFTEEN (15) DAYS nor more than THIRTY (30) DAYS to the
      LGU where such violation was committed, or both;

                                          xxx”

SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Environment and Natural Resources
(DENR), in coordination with the National Solid Waste Management Commission
(NSWMC) and the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to ensure the implementation of the
adjusted penalties across all local government units.

SECTION 4. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules,
and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
SECTION 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
