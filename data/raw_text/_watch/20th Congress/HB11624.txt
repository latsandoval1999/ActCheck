20                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   The Philippines has a substantial and growing demand for motor vehicles, but this
23   demand does not automatically translate into a strong domestic manufacturing base.
24   The country needs to further develop its capacity to manufacture vehicles and
25   automotive components, create skilled jobs, attract investments, and participate more
26   meaningfully in global automotive value chains.
27
28   The proposed Philippine Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Industry Act establishes a
29   national framework to strengthen this industry. It creates a six-year National Motor
30   Vehicle Manufacturing Development Plan with specific manufacturing and export
31   targets, identifies priority products for development, and provides for incentives for
32   vehicle and component manufacturers.
33
34   It also requires the national government, GOCCs, and LGUs to prioritize domestically
35   manufactured vehicles for at least 50% of their existing fleets, when locally produced
36   vehicles meet the required specifications and quality.
37
38   Ultimately, the measure seeks to make the country’s growing vehicle demand a driver
39   of Philippine manufacturing, jobs, and industrial growth.
40
41   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

21                               AN ACT
22       STRENGTHENING THE COMPETITIVENESS OF THE PHILIPPINE MOTOR
23                   VEHICLE MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY
24
25
26   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in

28
29                                                  ARTICLE I
30                                             GENERAL PROVISIONS
31
32         SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as “The Philippine Motor
33   Vehicle Manufacturing Industry Act”.
34
35         SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall develop the national
36   economy by promoting industrialization through competitive industries and by
37   encouraging the participation of private enterprise.
38
39          Recognizing that the Philippine Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Industry forms the
40   core of the country’s industrial strength and plays a vital role in economic development
41   and nation-building, the following are the declared policies of the State:
42
43           (a) The State shall develop a comprehensive policy that shall accelerate the
44               sound development of the Philippine Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Industry
45               thereby contributing to industrial capital formation, technology transfer,
46               technical skills development, and employment generation;
47           (b) The State shall ensure a balanced transition to open trade by fully developing
48               the competitive capacities of the Philippine Motor Vehicle Manufacturing
49               Industry and promoting greater participation in global value chains;
50           (c) The State shall promote the maximum scale integration of vehicle
51               manufacturing and the manufacture of its parts and components; and
52           (d) The State shall integrate motor vehicle manufacturing into the overall
 1             transport sector policy to encourage objective market conditions for locally-
 2             produced vehicles in the expansion and upgrading of the country’s vehicle
 3             fleet.
 4
 5         SECTION 3. Scope. – This Act shall apply to the range of activities in the Motor
 6   Vehicle Manufacturing Industry as defined under Section 4(e).
 7
 8         SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act, the following terms are
 9   defined as follows:
10
11         (a) Board of Investments (BOI) refers to an attached agency of the Department
12             of Trade and Industry created under Republic Act No. 5186, otherwise known
13             as the “Investment Incentives Act”;
14         (b) Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) refers to the government agency
15             created pursuant to Executive Order No. 133, series of 1987, “Reorganizing
16             the Department of Trade and Industry, its Attached Agencies, and for Other
17             Purposes”;
18         (c) Fiscal Incentives Review Board (FIRB) refers to the Government Agency
19             reconstituted under Republic Act No. 11534, otherwise known as the
20             Corporate Recovery and Tax Incentives for Enterprises Act (CREATE), which
21             administers the grant of tax incentives to registered business enterprises;
22         (d) Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Industry refers to enterprises that are engaged
23             in the manufacture of brand new motor vehicles, bodies (coachwork) for
24             motor vehicles, and parts, components, accessories and systems of motor
25             vehicles;
26         (e) National Transport Policy refers to the long-term development framework for
27             the transportation sector formulated by the National Economic and
28             Development Authority (NEDA), which shall be used by stakeholders as
29             guidance in transport development, management, operations and use;
30         (f) Philippine Development Plan (PDP) refers to the master plan for economic
31             development implemented by the NEDA, which identifies priority sectors with
32             the greatest potentials to contribute to realizing the PDP goals for the next six
33             (6) years and defines the programs, policies, strategies and action plans to
34             attain economic development goals;
35         (g) Philippine Export Development Plan (PEDP) refers to the export development
36             plan prepared by the DTI, which defines the country’s annual and medium-
37             term export thrusts, strategies, programs and projects jointly implemented by
38             the government export and concerned sectors; and
39         (h) Strategic Investments Priorities Plan (SIPP) refers to the overall plan
40             formulated by the BOI, in coordination with the FIRB, Investment Promotion
41             Agencies, and other government agencies pursuant to the guidelines set forth
42             in the CREATE Law.

44                                 ARTICLE II
45           ORGANIZATION, INSTITUTIONAL SUPPORT, AND STRATEGIES
 1        SECTION 5. Lead Agency. - The DTI through the BOI shall be the lead agency
 2   mandated to implement the provisions and attain the objectives of this Act.
 3
 4           In order to strengthen its capacity to implement this Act, the DTI is hereby
 5   authorized to create the Motor Vehicle Industry Development Office (MVIDO) in the BOI,
 6   which shall be headed by a Director. The Secretary of the DTI shall determine the
 7   organizational structure and staffing pattern of the motor vehicle industry development
 8   office, in accordance with the revised compensation and position classification system
 9   subject to the evaluation and approval of the Department of Budget and Management
10   and in compliance with the civil service laws, rules and regulations, and other pertinent
11   laws.
12
13         SECTION 6. National Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Development Plan. - The
14   President of the Republic of the Philippines shall approve a six year National Motor
15   Vehicle Manufacturing Development Plan, hereinafter referred to as the “Plan”,
16   prepared by the BOI in consultation with concerned stakeholders and endorsed by the
17   Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Development Council.
18
19          The Plan, which shall be integrated into the PEDP and the PDP, shall define the
20   Industry’s annual and medium-term manufacturing and export targets, key industry
21   issues, and programs and strategies to support manufacturing and export thrusts. The
22   Plan shall also identify priority development products and/or niche models, which shall
23   be characterized by high manufacturing value-added and with high potentials for
24   economic efficiency, as the primary focus of incentives. Annual updates shall be done
25   to monitor the results and targets identified in the Plan.
26
27          SECTION 7. Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Development Council. - For the
28   purpose of coordinating the formulation and implementation of policy reforms to support
29   the Plan, the Motor Vehicle Manufacturing Development Council, hereinafter referred to
30   as the “Council”, shall be created to act as the central planning, coordinating and
31   monitoring body and national advisory committee for the comprehensive and integrated
32   policy on motor vehicle manufacturing industry development.
33
34           SECTION 8. Composition of the Council. - The Council shall be composed of
35   the following:
36         (a) Secretary of the DTI as Chairperson;
37         (b) Vice Chairperson and Managing Head of the BOI and Undersecretary for
38             Industry Development and Trade Policy of the DTI as Co-Vice Chairperson;
39         (c) Director General of the National Economic Development Authority;
40         (d) Secretary of the Department of Finance;
41         (e) Secretary of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources;
42         (f) Secretary of the Department of Energy;
43         (g) Secretary of the Department of Transportation;
44         (h) Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology;
45         (i) Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways;
46         (j) Commissioner of the Commission on Higher Education;
 1          (k) Commissioner of the Philippine Competition Commission;
 2          (l) Director General of the Technical Education and Skills Development
 3              Authority;
 4          (m)Four (4) representatives from the private sector, one of whom shall be
 5             appointed as Co-Vice Chairperson: Provided, That the representatives shall
 6             have a proven track record of direct involvement in manufacturing and the
 7             development of the motor vehicle industry; and shall be nominated in a
 8             process determined by key industry players and/or organizations
 9             representing the motor vehicle manufacturing industry; and
10          (n) Two (2) representatives from the academe/research community: Provided,
11              That the representatives shall each be of recognized competence in fields of:
12              (1) automotive industrial, economic and trade policies; and (2) automotive
13              engineering and technology.
14
15         Except for the representatives from the private sector and academe/research
16   community, any member of the Council may designate an alternate with a rank not lower
17   than Undersecretary or its equivalent to represent their respective offices in the Council:
18   Provided, That the designated alternate must be fully authorized to decide on behalf of
19   the members.
20
21          Upon the effectivity of this Act, the President of the Republic of the Philippines
22   shall appoint the four (4) representatives from the private sector and the two (2)
23   representatives from the academe/research who shall serve for a term of two (2) years.
24   Only citizens of the Philippines shall be members of the Council. In case of vacancy,
25   the appointed successors shall serve only the unexpired portion of the term of his
26   predecessor.
27
28          Other heads of executive agencies, private organizations, or individuals may be
29   called upon by the Council to attend any Council meeting and to assist the Council to
30   resolve issues and problems that concern their respective offices.
31
32          Likewise, heads of executive agencies, private organizations or individuals shall
33   respond to the queries of the Council within two (2) weeks from the time such queries
34   are received.
35
36          SECTION 9. Duties of the Council. - The Council shall function solely as a
37   central planning, coordinating and monitoring body and national advisory committee to
38   the DTI as the lead implementing agency of this Act.
39
40          The Council shall have the following duties:
41
42          (a) Advise the DTI Secretary on the implementation of the Plan;
43          (b) Periodically review and assess the performance of the Plan and prospects of
44              the industry;
45          (c) Provide a forum for discussing and proposing solutions to motor vehicle
46              manufacturing industry-related problems, and emerging industry issues;
47          (d) Recommend measures to support the positioning of the Philippines as an
48              alternative production and export hub in the ASEAN region;
 1          (e) Recommend measures that would hasten the development of innovative
 2              technologies, niche products and high-value added manufacturing activities;
 3          (f) Recommend measures to increase the domestic market performance of the
 4              motor vehicle industry;
 5          (g) Recommend measures to address constraints in the development of the
 6              motor vehicle manufacturing industry; and
 7          (h) Perform such other acts as may be necessary or incidental to the exercise of
 8              its functions and discharge of its duties.
 9
10         SECTION 10. Meetings of the Council. - The Council shall meet at least once
11   every quarter: Provided, That the Chairperson may convene the Council anytime
12   whenever it is deemed necessary.
13
14          The members of the Council shall not receive any compensation: Provided, That
15   appointive members shall be entitled to per diem for each meeting actually attended at
16   the rates to be formulated by the Council.
17
18         SECTION 11. Operation of the Council. - The Council shall immediately
19   convene and function one (1) month after the effectivity of this Act.
20
21        The MVIDO created under Section 5 hereof shall act as the Secretariat for the
22   Council.

24                                             ARTICLE III
25                                            INCENTIVES
26
27          SECTION 12. Fiscal Incentives. - The Philippine motor vehicle and motor
28   vehicle component manufacturing industries are hereby declared as priority investment
29   sectors that shall form part of the country’s four (4) successive SIPPs registered
30   business enterprises of the Philippines and motor vehicle component industries shall
31   be entitled to pertinent fiscal incentives granted under Title XIII (tax incentives) of the
32   national internal revenue code of 1997, as amended.
33
34          SECTION 13. Industry Development Programs. - The DTI, through the BOI, in
35   coordination with the FIRB and other pertinent government agencies, shall recommend
36   an incentive strategy to the FIRB for approval, similar to Executive Order No. 182, series
37   of 2015, otherwise known as the “Comprehensive Automotive Resurgence Strategy
38   Program.”
39
40          The incentive strategy shall provide time-bound, targeted, performance-based
41   and transparent fiscal support to integrated vehicle manufacturing and automotive
42   supporting industries. Such programs shall be endorsed by the Council and approved
43   by the President: Provided, That fiscal support granted under this Section shall be non-
44   taxable, non-negotiable and applicable up to its full amount: Provided, further, That the
45   budget for fiscal support shall be indicated in the annual National Expenditure Program
46   and deemed automatically appropriated.
47
48         In addition, the DTI through the BOI and in coordination with the aforementioned
49   government agencies, shall formulate and implement programs to support the
50   development of motor vehicle manufacturing supporting industries.
 1                                      ARTICLE IV
 2                 PRIORITIZATION OF DOMESTICALLY MANUFACTURED
 3                        VEHICLES IN PUBLIC PROCUREMENT
 4
 5          SECTION 14. Government Procurement of Motor Vehicles. - Subject to
 6   pertinent laws, rules and guidelines on the procurement of motor vehicles, the national
 7   government, including government-owned or controlled corporations (GOCCs), and
 8   local government units (LGUs) shall prioritize the procurement of domestically
 9   manufactured motor vehicles which shall be at least fifty percent (50%) of their existing
10   motor vehicle fleet, unless when no such motor vehicles meeting the required
11   specifications and possessing the capabilities and desired quality of the procuring entity
12   are manufactured or produced in the Philippines.
13
14           Likewise, subject to existing laws, rules and guidelines on the lease or rental of
15   vehicles, the national government, including GOCCs and LGUs shall give preference to
16   the lease or rental of motor vehicles from service providers that can provide a motor
17   vehicle fleet of at least fifty percent (50%) domestically manufactured vehicles acquired
18   in the five (5) years preceding the date of procurement, unless no such vehicles meeting
19   the required specifications and possessing the capabilities and desired quality of the
20   procuring entity are manufactured or produced in the Philippines.

22                                              ARTICLE V
23                                          FINAL PROVISIONS
24
25           SECTION 15. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within three (3) months
26   after the effectivity of this Act, the DTI through the BOI shall, in consultation with all the
27   stakeholders concerned, promulgate the Implementing Rules and Regulations of this
28   Act.
29
30           SECTION 16. Oversight Committee. – There is hereby created a
31   Congressional Oversight Committee, hereinafter referred to as the Philippine Motor
32   Vehicle Manufacturing Industry Congressional Oversight Committee (PMVMI-COC),
33   with representations from the Minority, to be composed of five (5) members from the
34   Senate, which shall include the Chairpersons of the Senate Committees on Trade,
35   Commerce and Entrepreneurship; Public Services; and Appropriations, and five (5)
36   members from the House of Representatives, which shall include the Chairpersons of
37   the House of Representatives Committees on Trade and Industry; Transportation; and
38   Appropriations. The PMVMI-COC shall be jointly chaired by the Chairpersons of the
39   Senate Committee on Trade, Commerce and Entrepreneurship and the House of
40   Representatives Committee on Trade and Industry. It shall meet at least every quarter
41   of the first two (2) years and every semester of the third year after the approval of this
42   Act to review the implementation thereof, and recommend the necessary remedial
43   legislation or executive measures: Provided, That the PMVMI-COC shall cease to exist
44   after five (5) years upon the effectivity of this Act.
45
46           The Council shall submit the Plan and a report on the implementation of this
47   Act to the PMVI-COC not later than the first day of December every year.
48
49          The Secretariat of the PMVMI-COC shall be drawn from the existing personnel
50   of the Senate and House of Representatives Committees comprising the PMVMI-
51   COC.
 1         SECTION 17. Appropriations. - The amount necessary to carry out the initial
 2   implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of
 3   the DTI. Thereafter, such amount as may be necessary for the continued
 4   implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
 5
 6          SECTION 18. Repealing Clause. - Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
 7   executive order, administrative rule or regulation contrary to or inconsistent with the
 8   provisions of this Act is hereby repealed, modified or amended accordingly.
 9
10          SECTION 19. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is held
11   unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder of the Act or the provisions not otherwise
12   affected shall remain valid and subsisting.
13
14          SECTION 20. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
15   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
