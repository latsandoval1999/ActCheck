                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

     This bill seeks to enact the Magna Carta for Taxpayers, a policy
measure that seeks to institutionalize a comprehensive and enforceable
framework of rights and obligations in all dealings between taxpayers and
revenue authorities.

      While existing tax laws provide remedies and procedures, these
protections are dispersed and often difficult for ordinary citizens and small
businesses to navigate. This measure consolidates and strengthens
taxpayer protections to ensure that taxes are assessed and collected fairly,
transparently, and responsibly thereby reinforcing public confidence in the
tax system.

      The proposed law enhances taxpayer protection by expressly
guaranteeing the right to clear and simplified information, fair and uniform
application of tax laws, due process in audits and assessments, protection
against baseless or arbitrary assessments, timely resolution of cases, relief
from penalties caused by government delay, and respect for privacy and
confidentiality. If a small business receives a deficiency tax assessment
without a clear explanation or supporting computation, the Magna Carta
ensures that the taxpayer can demand a detailed legal and factual basis,
present evidence to contest errors, and seek timely resolution. These
safeguards promote transparency and accountability in tax administration.

       Taxes are the lifeblood of the State, indispensable to the government’s
ability to function, deliver public services, and promote national development.
With this impetus, the power to tax must be exercised with fairness,
transparency, and accountability to preserve public trust. By institutionalizing
clear and enforceable taxpayer rights alongside defined obligations, the
Magna Carta for Taxpayers fosters a culture of mutual responsibility between
the State and its citizens. When taxpayers are assured that assessments are
grounded in law, procedures are consistently observed, and remedies are
accessible, voluntary compliance is strengthened and disputes are
minimized thereby securing stable revenues essential to effective
governance.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

17             PROVIDING THE MAGNA CARTA FOR TAXPAYERS

18         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the

21         SECTION 1. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy
22   of the State to provide increased protection of taxpayers' rights in complying
23   with their obligation to pay national and local taxes to the government, to the
24   end that abuses committed against taxpayers 4 are prevented, if not
25   completely eradicated, and taxes are collected responsibly. In turn,taxpayers
26   are expected to faithfully comply with their tax obligations to the government.

27         SEC. 2. Definition of Terms. - As used in this Act:

28            (a) Assessment refers to the process of determining the amount of
29                taxes, duties, and other charges to be paid by the taxpayer;
30            (b) Revenue Authority refers, singly or collectively, to the Bureau of
31                Internal Revenue, Bureau of Customs, Local Government Units,
32                and other tax collecting agencies;
33            (c) Revenue Officers refer to the officers, employees, and agents of
34                the Revenue Authorities;
35            (d) Summary Collection refers to the civil remedy of the government
36                for the collection of accounts receivable or delinquent accounts
37                by garnishment or distraint of personal property, or levy of real
38                property;
39            (e) Taxes refer to all exactions for revenue-raising purposes
40                imposed under the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as
41                amended, the Customs Modernization and Tariff Act, the Local
42                Government Code of 1991, as amended, and all other tax laws;
43            (f) Taxpayer refers to any person, whether natural or juridical,
44                subject to the tax imposed by the national and local
45                governments; and
46            (g) Tax Audit refers to the examination into a taxpayer's obligation,
47                and compliance with tax laws, rules, and regulations by Revenue
48                Authorities.

50         SEC. 3. Taxpayer's Bill of Rights and Obligations. - Without
51   prejudice to the other rights and remedies of the taxpayer provided for in the
52   National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended; Customs
53   Modernization and Tariff Act; Republic Act No. 1125, creating the Court of
54   Tax Appeals, as amended; Book II of the Local Government Code of 1991,
55   as amended; and all other laws, rules and regulations, issuances, and
56   circulars in support thereof, the taxpayer is hereto granted basic taxpayer
57   rights, whether at the administrative level or at the judicial level: Provided,
58   That nothing in this Act shall amend, modify, or repeal the provisions of the
59   abovementioned laws: Provided, further, That the taxpayer is expected to
60   observe and comply with tax obligations enumerated in this Act and in other
61   relevant laws, rules, and regulations implemented by Revenue Authorities.
62
63         SEC. 4. Taxpayer's Basic Rights. - The following are the taxpayer's
64   basic rights:
65   (a) Right to be Informed. - The taxpayer shall have the right to know
66       in a simplified manner how one can comply with laws, rules, and
67       regulations implemented by the Revenue Authorities. The
68       taxpayer is entitled to clear, complete, accurate, and simplified
69       explanation of tax laws, rules, regulations, procedures, and other
70       issuances, as well as be apprised of taxpayer's rights and
71       obligations in all tax forms, publications, notices,
72       correspondences, assessments, rulings, and decisions.
73              In case of any deficiency tax finding, alleged violation, or
74       decision or ruling rendered by Revenue Authorities, the taxpayer
75       shall be informed in the written decision or ruling, clearly and
76       sufficiently of the facts and laws thereon, including any
77       supporting computation and documentation upon which these
78       are based.
79              The taxpayer shall have the right to be informed of a
80       summary collection action by the Revenue Authorities, which
81       requires sale or seizure of property or the freezing of assets.
82              The service of the notices and decisions to the taxpayers
83       shall be made in accordance with laws, rules, and regulations;
84   (b) Right to Fair, Uniform, and Faithful Application of Laws. - The
85       taxpayer shall have the right to a fair, uniform, and faithful
86       application of laws, rules, regulations, and other issuances of
87       Revenue Authorities in all proceedings involving the taxpayer;
88   (c) Right to Challenge the Position of and be Heard by Revenue
89       Authorities. - The taxpayer shall have the right to challenge and
90       raise objections against the findings, assessments, rulings, or
91       decisions of the Revenue Authorities.The taxpayer shall have the
92       right to be heard, and the evidence presented by the taxpayer
93       shall be duly considered by the Revenue Authorities in deciding
94       any matter involving the taxpayer;
95   (d) Right to Fair and Impartial Proceedings Before the Revenue
96       Authorities. - The taxpayer shall at all times be entitled to fair and
97       impartial disposition of assessment cases, deficiency findings,
98       requests for rulings, and decisions made by the Revenue
99       Authorities;
100   (e) Right to Present Evidence and Witnesses. - The taxpayer shall
101       have the right to be given ample and adequate opportunity to
102       present evidence, including those in the possession of any third
103       party, and call on witnesses to testify, as the case may be, that
104       would assist the taxpayer in connection with any assessment or
105       matter involving it;
106   (f) Right to be Assisted or Represented by a Qualified Professional
107       of their Choice. - The taxpayer shall have the right to be assisted
108       or represented by a competent and independent qualified
109       professional, such as a legal counsel, accountant, or customs
110       broker, of the taxpayer's own choice, in all administrative, civil,
111       or criminal tax proceedings: Provided, That any communication,
112       correspondence, or documents submitted or exchanged
113       between the taxpayer and the qualified professional that they
114       engage shall be considered strictly confidential, except as
115       otherwise provided under existing laws, rules, and regulations;
116   (g) Right to Timely and Expeditious Completion and Finality of Tax
117       Audits. - The taxpayer shall have the right to have the Revenue
118       Authorities begin and complete tax audits and address pending
119       requests and other matters in a timely and expeditious manner.
120       The taxpayer shall have the right to know when the Revenue
121       Authorities have completed an audit or examination of the
122       taxpayer;
123   (h) Right Against Baseless Assessment. - Tax authorities are
124       prohibited from issuing assessments against taxpayers without
125       the benefit of a proper audit conducted by authorized Revenue
126       Authorities against taxpayers, otherwise, such assessments
127       shall be void;
128   (i) Right to be Given an Opportunity for Amicable Settlement. -
129       Unless contrary to law, the taxpayer shall have the opportunity to
130       amicably settle deficiencies and delinquencies, at any stage of
131       the proceedings: Provided, That said settlement must be in
132       writing and duly signed by the parties or their agent;
133   (j) Right to Avail of Installment Payment. - In so far as may be
134       permitted by law, the taxpayer shall have the right to settle tax
135       liabilities through installment payments;
136   (k) Right to Pay the Correct Amount of Tax. - The taxpayer shall
137       have the right to pay only the amount of tax legally due, including
138       applicable interests, surcharges, fines, and penalties;
139   (l) Right to Relief from Paying Interests, Surcharges, and Penalties.
140       - The taxpayer shall be entitled to a reduction of interests,
141       surcharges, and penalties levied on tax liabilities in case of any
142       excessive assessment or undue delay caused by Revenue
143       Authorities, including instances of loss of records, personnel
144       transfers, extended illness or leaves of absence, and personnel
145       training, resignation, retirement, or termination from service of
146       the concerned personnel;
147   (m)         Right to Recover Actual Costs and Actual Damages Arising
148       from Judicial Actions. - The taxpayer shall have the right to
149       recover actual costs and actual damages, including attorney's
150       fees and expenses of litigation, arising from judicial actions, as
151       may be determined by a court of competent jurisdiction;
152   (n) Right to Quality Service and Assistance. - The taxpayer shall
153       have the right to receive prompt, courteous, and professional
154       assistance during dealings with the Revenue Authorities, to be
155       spoken to in a way the taxpayer can easily understand, to receive
156       clear and easily understandable communications from Revenue
157       Authorities, and to be provided with a mechanism to address
158       inadequate service;
159   (o) Right to Privacy. - The taxpayer shall have the right to expect that
160       any inquiry, examination, or enforcement action by government
161       authorities shall comply with the law and be no more intrusive
162       than necessary, and shall respect all due process rights; and
163   (p) Right to Confidentiality. - The taxpayer shall have the right to
164       privacy of communication and correspondence, as guaranteed
165       by the Constitution and by Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise
166       known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012. The taxpayer shall have
167       the right to require that any information provided to Revenue
168              Authorities shall be kept confidential unless disclosure is
169              expressly authorized by law or by the taxpayer.

171        SEC. 5. Taxpayer's Basic Obligations. - Complementary to the
172   above taxpayer's rights are the following duties and responsibilities of the
173   taxpayer:
174           (a) Obligation to Comply with Tax Laws. - The taxpayer shall
175               observe and comply with tax laws, rules, and regulations with
176               reasonable care and diligence;
177           (b) Obligation to Truthfully and Timely Disclose Information. - The
178               taxpayer shall truthfully and timely disclose complete and
179               accurate information as required by tax laws, rules, and
180               regulations, including registered address, official email address,
181               registered activities, tax type details, and accounting period;
182           (c) Obligation to Lodge and Submit Required Documents
183               Completely and on Time. - The taxpayer shall lodge and submit,
184               either personally or through electronic means, in a timely manner
185               all relevant information, books of accounts, reports, records,
186               returns, and other documents to Revenue Authorities on or
187               before the dates set by tax laws, rules, and regulations;
188           (d) Obligation to Keep and Make Tax Records Available for
189               Examination. - The taxpayer shall keep accurate records of the
190               required books of accounts and other tax information for the
191               duration required by law, rules, and regulations, and make such
192               available for examination by revenue officers following proper
193               procedures;
194           (e) Obligation to Cooperate with Revenue Authorities in Tax Audit. -
195               The taxpayer shall cooperate with Revenue Authorities in the
196               conduct of tax audits in accordance with laws, rules, and
197               regulations; and
198           (f) Obligation to Timely Pay Taxes. - The taxpayer shall timely pay
199               the taxes due to the government through any available payment
200               channel.

202         SEC. 6. Creation of the Office of the National Taxpayer Advocate.
203   - To protect the rights of taxpayers, both substantive and procedural, there
204   is hereby created an Office of the National Taxpayer Advocate (ONTA)
205   attached to the Department of Finance (DOF for purposes of administrative
206   supervision.
207         The ONTA shall determine the organizational structure and staffing
208   pattern of the office in accordance with the revised compensation and
209   position classification system subject to the evaluation and approval of the
210   Department of Budget and Management and in compliance with the civil
211   service laws, rules and regulations and other relevant laws.
212
213         SEC. 7. Powers, Functions, and Responsibilities of the Office of
214   the National Taxpayer Advocate. - The ONTA shall have the following
215   functions, powers, and responsibilities:
216
217           (a) Collaborate with qualified professionals to assist in the
218               representation of low income taxpayers who have a dispute with
219               Revenue Authorities and provide education and outreach
220               program to low income taxpayers;
221           (b) Engage in regular information, education, and communication
222               programs for taxpayers on tax laws, rules, regulations, circulars,
223               including those that implement Supreme Court decisions, and
224               other issuances that affect them, including their rights and
225               remedies under the law, to ensure that the public is properly
226               apprised thereof;
227           (c) Recommend measures to implement the policy declared under
228               Section 1 of this Act, by way of continuing policy review;
229           (d) Monitor tax issuances of Revenue Authorities, and propose
230               amendments, when necessary, at the administrative level, to
231               ensure that taxpayers' rights as provided in this Act are upheld;
232               and
233           (e) Perform such other powers and functions as may be necessary
234               to enhance the ONTA's performance of its powers, functions,
235               and responsibilities, as enumerated in this Act.
236
237          SEC. 8. Qualifications, Appointment, Term, and Rank of the
238   National Taxpayer Advocate. - The ONTA shall be headed by the National
239   Taxpayer Advocate, who shall have the authority and responsibility for the
240   effective exercise of the mandate of the ONTA and the discharge of its
241   powers and functions.
242          The National Taxpayer Advocate shall be a Filipino citizen, a member
243   of the Philippine Bar in good standing or a Certified Public Accountant, with
244   at least ten (10) years of practice in the field of taxation, and with proven
245   integrity and competence.
246          The National Taxpayer Advocate shall be appointed by the President
247   and shall serve for a term of three (3) years with one (1) re-appointment. In
248   case of vacancy arising from retirement, resignation, removal, permanent
249   incapacity, or death of the National Taxpayer Advocate, the President shall
250   appoint a successor to serve a fresh term of three (3) years with one (1) re-
251   appointment.
252          The appointment and re-appointment of the National Taxpayer
253   Advocate shall be subject to the mandatory retirement age provided under
254   the Civil Service Commission rules.
255          The National Taxpayer Advocate shall have the equivalent rank,
256   salaries, allowances, and retirement benefits of an Assistant Secretary.
257
258          SEC. 9. Disqualification. - An individual cannot be appointed as the
259   National Taxpayer Advocate if such individual has been an employee or
260   officer of, or has rendered service as consultant or in any other capacity to
261   any of the Revenue Authorities for two (2) years prior to such appointment,
262   or has been convicted of any crime involving moral turpitude or a violation of
263   any tax law.

273          SEC. 11. Violation of the Bill of Rights of Taxpayers. - Any person
274   or revenue officer who is found to have violated Section 4 of this Act on
275   Taxpayer's Basic Rights shall be subject to the administrative penalties
276   under Republic Act No. 9485, otherwise known as the "Anti-Red Tape Act of
277   2007", as amended, and other administrative and civil service laws and rules.
278          The finding of administrative liability for a violation under this Act shall
279   not be a bar to the filing of an applicable criminal or civil case under existing
280   laws for the same violation. The violation of Section 4(n) of this Act on the
281   Right to Quality Service and Assistance shall be penalized under the
282   provisions of Republic Act No. 6713, otherwise known as the "Code of
283   Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees."
284          Violations penalized under this Act shall prescribe after twelve (12)
285   years. Prescription shall begin to run from the day of the commission of the
286   violation of the law, and if the same is not known at the time, from the
287   discovery thereof and the institution of judicial proceedings for its
288   investigation and punishment: Provided, That the prescription shall be
289   interrupted when proceedings are instituted against the guilty person, and
290   shall begin to run again if the proceedings are dismissed for reasons not
291   constituting double jeopardy.
292
293         SEC. 12. Accessibility of the Taxpayer's Bill of Rights and
294   Obligations. - The Taxpayer's Bill of Rights and Obligations shall be made
295   available in all offices of Revenue Authorities, free of charge, whether
296   printed, digital, or otherwise, and shall be posted in their respective websites,
297   in English and Filipino languages.
298
299          SEC. 13. Integration of Taxpayer's Bill of Rights and Obligations
300   in Higher Education Curriculum. - All public and private higher education

302   part of the curriculum. The Commission on Higher Education shall, pursuant
303   to its mandate under Republic Act No. 7722, otherwise known as the "Higher
304   Education Act of 1994", develop the program to execute and implement this
