                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Within the last few years, the demand for tertiary education in the
country has steadily increased. From 26.63% in 2018, it has increased to 49.9%
in 2024, reflecting a 23% percent increase in just six years. To respond to this
growing demand, more local universities and colleges (LUCs) have been
opened, increasing from 95 in 2011 to 174 in 2024.

       The expansion of LUCs has enabled thousands of Filipino students to
access higher education and complete their studies, particularly those from
underserved communities. However, despite their critical role, LUCs remain
structurally vulnerable due to their dependence on local government units.
Their operations, funding, and policy direction are largely influenced by the
priorities of local chief executives and the Sanggunian, making them highly
susceptible to political transitions.

       This dynamic undermines institutional stability. Funding allocations
and scholarship programs are often subject to shifting political interests,
resulting in inconsistent support for students and academic programs. The
absence of long-term planning and institutional safeguards has contributed to
persistent challenges, including limited research capacity, inadequate
infrastructure, gaps in faculty development, and weak quality assurance and
evaluation systems.

      Local universities and colleges serve as vital instruments for
democratizing access to higher education. They provide affordable and
community-based learning opportunities, helping bridge educational
disparities across regions. LUCs also contribute to local development by
producing a skilled workforce attuned to the specific needs of their
communities.

       This measure seeks to strengthen the institutional capacity, governance,
and sustainability of LUCs by establishing clear standards, ensuring stable
and adequate funding mechanisms, and promoting merit-based and
transparent systems for scholarships and administration. It aims to insulate
LUCs from undue political interference, enhance quality assurance
mechanisms, and support long-term development through faculty training,
infrastructure investment, and research support.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
     PRESCRIBING MANDATORY GUIDELINES ON THE ESTABLISHMENT
     AND OPERATION OF LOCAL UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES


     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
           Congress assembled:


 1             SECTION 1.       Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Local
 2      Universities and Colleges Governance Act.”

 3             SEC. 2. – It is the policy of the State to protect and promote the right of
 4      all citizens to quality education and to take appropriate steps to make education
 5      accessible to all. It shall establish, maintain, and support a complete, adequate, and
 6      integrated system of education relevant to the needs of the people and society.
 7              Towards this end, the mandatory guidelines on the establishment and
 8      operation of Local Universities and Colleges (LUCs) are prescribed to achieve a
 9      standardized and integrated system of higher education, and provide a relevant
10      direction for their governance to ensure continuous enhancement of educational
11      standards in LUCs that are on par with both local and international practices.
12             SEC. 3. – As used in this Act:

 1                a. Institutional Recognition (IR) refers to a process by which the
 2                    Commission on Higher Education (CHED) grants recognition to
 3                    an educational institution as a higher education institution (HEI)
 4                    that meets the minimum standards for quality education.
 5                b. Local Government Units (LGUs) refer to the municipalities,
 6                    cities, or provinces that established the LUC.

 8                    known as the “Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education

15                e. Sanggunian refers to the local legislative body which includes
16                    Sangguniang Bayan for municipalities, Sangguniang Panlungsod
17                    for cities, and Sangguniang Panlalawigan for provinces.
18         SEC. 4. Coverage. – The provisions of this Act shall apply to the
19   following:
20                a. HEIs to be established and operated by LGUs for the purpose of
21                   offering higher academic programs;
22                b. Educational institutions established and operated by LGUs
23                   offering non-degree programs, but would later offer higher
24                   academic programs; and
25               c. Existing LUCs.

17                        operation of the LUC.

19                    concerned, as provided under the pertinent provisions of R.A. No.
20                    7160, otherwise known as the “Local Government Code of
21                    1991”.

23                    school site with appropriate size and location pursuant to the

 8          SEC. 7. Prior Approval of the Commission on Higher Education for the
 9   Offering of Academic Programs. – A duly established HEI and other educational
10   institutions covered under Section 4 (b) of this Act shall secure approval from the
11   CHED prior to the offering of higher academic programs. The Chairperson of the
12   Board, or its President when so authorized by the Board, shall file with the CHED
13   an application to offer a higher academic program. The application shall be
14   supported with documents indicating compliance with the policies, standards, and
15   guidelines relative to such academic program.
16          SEC. 8. Benefits and Incentives. – All LUCs established in accordance
17   with the provisions of Sections 5 and 6 of this Act shall be eligible to avail of the
18   benefits provided under R.A. No. 10931.
19          They shall likewise be eligible to apply for any grant or subsidy provided
20   by the CHED, subject to its existing requirements and guidelines. They shall also
21   be eligible to avail of support provided by the CHED for the development of their
22   human resources through appropriate scholarships for post-graduate studies,
23   continuing professional development programs, and other capacity-building
24   activities that ensure the delivery of quality education.
25          LUCs shall be listed in the registry of Philippine HEIs, which will assist and
26   facilitate the mobility of students and faculty to another country for purposes of
27   study, research, teaching, or work.

 5          SEC. 11. Meetings; Quorum; Compensation. – The Board shall regularly
 6   convene at least once a month. The Chairperson of the Board may call for a special
 7   meeting whenever necessary: Provided, That members are notified in writing at
 8   least three (3) days prior to the said special meeting.
 9          A majority of all the members of the Board holding office at the time of its
10   regular or special meeting shall constitute a quorum: Provided, That the Local

13          The members shall serve without compensation, but they shall be
14   reimbursed for necessary expenses incurred in their attendance to meetings of the
15   Board or in connection with their official business, as may be authorized by
16   resolutions of the Board.

27             functions of the LUC;

25         SEC. 13. The President as Administrator of the Local University or
26   College; Other Officials and Employees. – The administration of the LUC shall be
27   vested in the President who shall render full-time service to implement the policies
28   promulgated by the Board.




                                                                                       8
 1          The President shall hold a doctoral degree in education, administration, or
 2   other related fields, with at least three (3) years of relevant administrative
 3   experience, without prejudice to the provision of additional qualifications to be set
 4   by the Board.
 5          The President shall serve a term of three (3) years, subject to reappointment
 6   for not more than two (2) terms. In case of vacancy by reason of death,
 7   compulsory retirement, resignation, removal for cause, or permanent incapacity of
 8   the President to perform the functions of his or her office, the Board shall have the
 9   authority to designate an Officer-in-Charge who shall serve no longer than the
10   unexpired term.
11          The powers and duties of the President, in addition to those specifically
12   provided in this Act, shall be those usually pertaining to the Office of the President
13   of other colleges and universities, and those delegated by the Board.
14          The salary of the President shall be set by the Board, taking into
15   consideration the applicable rules and regulations of the CSC and the Department
16   of Budget and Management and ensuring that the same shall be comparable to that
17   being received by the presidents of similar educational institutions.
18          The vice-presidents, deans, directors, heads of departments, faculty, non-
19   teaching personnel, and such other officials and employees of the LUC shall be
20   appointed by the President, subject to the confirmation of the Board. The
21   appointment, as well as removal for cause, of these LUC officials and employees
22   shall conform to existing civil service laws, rules, and regulations.
23          SEC. 14. Search Committee. – In case of vacancy in the Office of the
24   President brought about by any of the causes mentioned in the preceding section,
25   the Board shall organize and convene a Search Committee for the selection of a
26   new President.
27          The Search Committee shall have three (3) members to be appointed by the
28   Board and shall not come from the Board. It shall elect a Chairperson and ensure

 1   that the new President is selected within three (3) months from the time the
 2   position is vacated, or before the end of the unexpired term, whichever is earlier.
 3           For a newly established LUC, the Local Chief Executive shall organize a
 4   Search Committee, composed of three (3) members, upon whose recommendation

 6          SEC. 15. The Secretary of the Local University or College. – The Board
 7   shall appoint a Secretary who shall serve as such for both the Board and the LUC
 8   upon the recommendation of the President. The Secretary shall be the custodian of
 9   all official records of the LUC and shall communicate the notices of meetings to
10   each member of the Board.
11          SEC. 16. Transitory Provision. – In the interest of efficient control and
12   regulation by the CHED, all LUCs which are already existing at the time of the
13   effectivity of this Act shall be allowed to continue their operations for a non-
14   extendible period of three (3) years to comply with the requirements set forth in
15   this Act. Non-compliance shall result in the phase-out of the programs being
16   offered by the concerned LUCs.
17          SEC. 17. Penalties for Non-Compliance. – A person who violates any
18   provision of this Act or the rules and regulations issued pursuant thereto shall be
19   subject to an administrative fine of up to One million pesos (Php 1,000,000),
20   depending on the severity of the violation, as may be determined in the
21   Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of this Act.
22          SEC. 18. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days
23   from the effectivity of this Act, the CHED, in coordination with the Department of
24   the Interior and Local Government, and other relevant agencies and sectors, shall
25   issue the rules and regulations implementing its provisions. The IRR issued
26   pursuant to this section shall take effect thirty (30) days after its publication in a
27   newspaper of general circulation.
