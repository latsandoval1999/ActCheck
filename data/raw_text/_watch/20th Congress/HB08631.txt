                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to create Barangay Balili North in the Municipality of
Mankayan, Province of Benguet, through the separation of a portion of the existing
Barangay Balili. The measure aims to strengthen local governance, improve the
delivery of basic public services, and promote the socio-economic development and
general welfare of the residents within the affected communities.

        Barangay Balili is one of the largest and most populated barangays in the
Municipality of Mankayan. Over time, the increase in population and the wide
territorial coverage of the barangay have created administrative and logistical
challenges in delivering essential services efficiently to all residents. The creation of a
new barangay will enable the local government to provide more responsive
governance, facilitate better coordination in implementing development programs,
and ensure that basic services reach communities in a timely and effective manner.

       The proposed Barangay Balili North shall be composed of the sitios of
Am-am, Paykek, Pangkikan, Nalicob, Agaki, Sayapot, and Tanobong, which
presently form part of Barangay Balili. The remaining territory of the mother
barangay shall constitute Barangay Balili South. The territorial boundaries of
Barangay Balili North are clearly defined through technical descriptions based on
surveys conducted by the appropriate government authorities to ensure proper
delineation of jurisdiction.

       The proposal to subdivide Barangay Balili into two (2) separate barangays
originated from the Barangay Local Government Unit through Barangay Resolution
No. 054, Series of 2019, dated August 23, 2019, which requested the creation of
Barangay Balili North and Barangay Balili South. The proposal was subsequently
endorsed by the Sangguniang Bayan of Mankayan through Resolution No. 2019-37,
with the support of the Municipal Mayor as evidenced by the 1st Indorsement dated
October 14, 2019. Thereafter, the Sangguniang Panlalawigan of Benguet enacted
Provincial Ordinance No. 2021-274, dated June 30, 2021, which provided for the
creation of Barangay Balili North in the Municipality of Mankayan, Province of
Benguet.
      Pursuant to Section 10 of Republic Act No. 7160, the creation of a local
government unit must be ratified in a plebiscite to be conducted by the Commission
on Elections (COMELEC). However, despite the enactment of the provincial
ordinance and the expressed willingness of the Provincial Government of Benguet to
shoulder the full cost of conducting the plebiscite, the said plebiscite was not
conducted. As a result, the creation of Barangay Balili North has yet to be ratified
and implemented.

      Given the expressed support of the local government units concerned and the
demonstrated need to enhance administrative efficiency and public service delivery
in the area, it is necessary for Congress to enact this measure to formally create
Barangay Balili North and provide for the conduct of the required plebiscite.

        The establishment of Barangay Balili North will strengthen grassroots
governance, promote greater community participation, and accelerate development
initiatives in the Municipality of Mankayan.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is respectfully sought.

                          AN ACT
CREATING BARANGAY BALILI NORTH IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF
MANKAYAN, PROVINCE OF BENGUET

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Creation of Barangay Balili North. – There is hereby created a new
barangay in the Municipality of Mankayan, Province of Benguet, to be known as
Barangay Balili North, which shall be separated from the existing Barangay Balili.

The creation of Barangay Balili North is intended to promote responsive governance
and improve the delivery of basic services to the residents of the area, as well as to
enhance socio-economic development, cultural advancement, and the general
welfare of the people.

       SEC. 2. Composition. - Barangay Balili North shall be composed of the
following seven (7) sitios presently forming part of Barangay Balili in the
Municipality of Mankayan, Province of Benguet:

   1.   Am-am
   2.   Paykek
   3.   Pangkikan
   4.   Nalicob
   5.   Agaki
   6.   Sayapot
   7.   Tanobong

      The remaining territory of the mother barangay shall continue to constitute
Barangay Balili South.

       SEC. 3. Territorial and Political Jurisdiction. - The territorial and political
jurisdiction of Barangay Balili North shall be defined by the following technical
description and boundaries as surveyed and identified by the appropriate
government authorities:

​      SEC. 4. Compliance with Statutory Requirements. - The creation of Barangay
Balili North complies with the requirements set forth under Sections 385 and 386 of
Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991,
including the population and territorial requirements for the creation of a barangay.

       SEC. 5. Plebiscite. - The Commission on Elections (COMELEC) shall conduct
and supervise a plebiscite within one hundred twenty (120) days from the effectivity
of this Act for the purpose of ratifying the creation of Barangay Balili North. The
plebiscite shall be conducted in the affected areas in accordance with Section 10 of
Republic Act No. 7160.

       SEC. 6. Appointment of Barangay Officials. - Upon ratification of this Act in a
plebiscite, the first set of barangay officials of Barangay Balili North shall be
appointed by the Municipal Mayor of the Municipality of Mankayan and shall serve
until their successors shall have been duly elected and qualified in the next regular
barangay elections.
​
​      SEC. 7. Funding. - The amount necessary for the initial organization and
operation of Barangay Balili North shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the Municipality of Mankayan and/or the Province of Benguet.

       The Province of Benguet shall provide an initial subsidy or assistance of not
less than Two Million Pesos (₱2,000,000.00) for 5 years to cover the annual operating
expenditures and capital outlays of the newly created barangay.

     Thereafter, Barangay Balili North shall be entitled to its appropriate share in
the National Tax Allotment (NTA) pursuant to the provisions of the Local
Government Code of 1991.

       SEC. 8. Powers and Functions. - Barangay Balili North and Barangay Balili
South shall exercise the powers and perform the functions of a barangay as provided
under Republic Act No. 7160, as well as other applicable laws, decrees, and
regulations.

      SEC. 9. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, rules and regulations,
or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.
