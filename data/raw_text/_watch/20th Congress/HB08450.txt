                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE


December 23 marks the significant date when Mayantoc was founded as a municipality. This day
holds historical importance for the residents, symbolizing community growth, unity, and the
collective efforts of its citizens who contributed to the development of their hometown.


By declaring December 23 as a special non-working holiday, the municipality intends to:
   1. Celebrate Local Heritage: This holiday offers an opportunity for residents to reflect on their
       local history, appreciate their cultural identity, and celebrate the milestones achieved since
       the municipality's founding.
   2. Enhance Community Engagement: A designated holiday will encourage greater
       participation in Foundation Day activities, fostering stronger community ties through
       various events such as parades, cultural performances, and community gatherings.
   3. Support Economic Growth: By attracting visitors for the celebrations, local businesses and
       vendors can benefit from increased foot traffic and sales, contributing to the overall
       economic vitality of Mayantoc.
   4. Promote Civic Pride: Recognizing the Foundation Day of Mayantoc instills a sense of pride
       among residents, encouraging collective responsibility towards the municipality’s growth
       and development.


The declaration of December 23 as a special non-working holiday will serve to honor the founding
of the Municipality of Mayantoc, celebrating its rich heritage and vibrant community. The bill
seeks support to acknowledge this important day, promote community involvement, stimulate
local economic activity, and foster civic pride among its citizens. Together, let us commemorate
the foundation of Mayantoc and look forward to a prosperous future for our municipality.


In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

         DECLARING DECEMBER 23 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-

         PROVINCE OF TARLAC, TO COMMEMORATE THE FOUNDATION
         ANNIVERSARY OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF MAYANTOC


    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
    assembled:
1              Section 1. Declaration of December 23 as a Special Non-working Holiday in the
2   Municipality of Mayantoc, Province of Tarlac. - December 23 of every year is hereby declared
3   a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Mayantoc, Province of Tarlac, in celebration
4   of its Foundation day.
5
6              Section. 2. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
