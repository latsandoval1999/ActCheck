                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the existing Buguey Extension Campus of the Cagayan State
University (CSU) in the Municipality of Buguey, Province of Cagayan, into a regular
campus of the University, to be known as the Cagayan State University – Buguey
Campus.

The 1987 Constitution recognizes the vital role of education in national development
and mandates the State to establish, maintain, and support a complete, adequate,
and integrated system of education responsive to the needs of the people and society.
Consistent with this constitutional mandate, the government must continue to expand
access to quality higher education, particularly in underserved and developing
communities.

Buguey is a coastal municipality that serves as a strategic center for agriculture,
fisheries, trade, and other economic activities in the northeastern part of the Province
of Cagayan. As the municipality and its neighboring localities continue to grow, so
does the demand for accessible, affordable, and quality tertiary education. However,
many students and aspiring professionals continue to face financial and logistical
challenges in pursuing higher education, particularly when they are compelled to enroll
in campuses located far from their communities.

The CSU - Buguey Extension Campus has played a significant role in broadening
access to higher education in the municipality and nearby areas. It has provided
educational opportunities to countless students and produced graduates who
contribute meaningfully to local and national development. Nonetheless, its status as
an extension campus limits its capacity to expand academic programs, strengthen
institutional operations, and access the resources necessary to effectively address the
growing educational needs of the region.

The conversion of the Buguey Extension Campus into a regular campus will enhance
the University's capacity to deliver quality instruction, research, and extension
services. It will enable the campus to accommodate more students, expand its
academic offerings, improve infrastructure and learning facilities, strengthen its faculty
and personnel complement, and provide adequate student support services. These
developments are essential to sustaining academic excellence and fostering
institutional growth.

Moreover, the establishment of the CSU – Buguey Campus as a regular campus will
promote greater access to higher education for students from Buguey and neighboring
municipalities. It will help reduce educational disparities, support local human resource
development, and contribute to inclusive socio-economic growth in the Province of
Cagayan.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
CONVERTING THE BUGUEY EXTENSION CAMPUS OF THE CAGAYAN STATE
UNIVERSITY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BUGUEY, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN,
INTO A REGULAR CAMPUS OF THE CAGAYAN STATE UNIVERSITY, TO BE
KNOWN AS THE “CAGAYAN STATE UNIVERSITY – BUGUEY CAMPUS”, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Cagayan State University (CSU) – Buguey
Campus Act."

SECTION 2. The Buguey Extension Campus of the CSU located in the Municipality of
Buguey, Province of Cagayan is hereby converted into a regular campus to be known
as the CSU – Buguey Campus.

SECTION 3. The CSU – Buguey Campus shall primarily provide short-term technical-
vocational, undergraduate, and graduate courses within its areas of competency and
specialization, pursuant to the mandate of the CSU, and according to its capability, as
the Board of Regents, hereinafter referred to as the Board, may deem necessary to
respond to the human resource development needs of the Province of Cagayan and
the entire Region II. It is also mandated to undertake research and extension services
and to provide progressive leadership in these areas.

SECTION 4. The CSU – Buguey Campus shall be headed by a Campus Administrator
who shall be appointed by the Board, subject to its guidelines on qualifications and
standards, and based on the recommendation of a search committee duly constituted
for the purpose of selecting the Campus Administrator.

SECTION 5. The CSU shall ensure that the minimum requirements of the Commission
on Higher Education (CHED) for the operation of the CSU – Buguey Campus, including
those related to its course offerings, are fully complied with.
SECTION 6. All assets, both real and personal, personnel, and records of the Buguey
Extension Campus of the CSU, as well as liabilities or obligations, shall be transferred
to the CSU. The positions, rights, and security of tenure of faculty members and
personnel therein employed under existing laws prior to its conversion into a regular
campus shall be respected.

All parcels of land belonging to the government that shall be occupied by the Buguey
Extension Campus of the CSU shall be transferred to the CSU: Provided, That should
the CSU – Buguey Campus or the CSU cease to exist or be abolished, or should such
aforementioned parcels of land be no longer needed by the CSU, the same shall revert
to the local government unit concerned or to the Republic of the Philippines, as the
case may be.

SECTION 7. The provisions of Republic Act No. 8292, otherwise known as the “Higher
Education Modernization Act of 1997” shall form part of this Act.

SECTION 8. The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

The Municipality of Buguey shall continue to fund the operation and maintenance of
the CSU – Buguey Campus until such time that the amount needed to carry out the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual GAA.

SECTION 9. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Board shall, in
consultation with the CHED, issue the rules and regulations to fully implement the
provisions of this Act.

SECTION 10. If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall remain in full
force and effect.

SECTION 11. All other laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, and rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are repealed or
modified accordingly.

SECTION 12. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
