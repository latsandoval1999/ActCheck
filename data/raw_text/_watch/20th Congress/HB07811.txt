                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Republic Act No. 11292, or the Seal of Good Local Governance
(SGLG) Act of 2019, institutionalized a system for recognizing well-
performing local government units (LGUs). While effective in promoting
compliance and good governance processes, the current framework
remains largely input and process-oriented and does not fully capture
actual development outcomes experienced by citizens.

       This bill seeks to strengthen the SGLG by consolidating existing
performance measurement tools and institutionalizing a unified, data-
driven meritocracy system for LGUs. It introduces a Human Development
Meritocracy Criteria that complements existing governance standards by
emphasizing measurable outcomes in health, education, standard of
living, peace and order, and inflation, while ensuring that LGUs are
assessed based on their own historical performance rather than against
one another.

      The measure further enhances inter-agency coordination, assigns
a central role to the Philippine Statistics Authority in data governance,
improves transparency through standardized and accessible data
systems, and rationalizes the incentive structure to reward sustained
improvement while respecting local autonomy and the principle of
subsidiarity.

       By aligning incentives with actual development outcomes, this bill
aims to promote more responsive, evidence-based, and results-oriented
local governance for the benefit of the Filipino people.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
earnestly prayed for.
