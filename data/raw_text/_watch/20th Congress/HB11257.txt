                       AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE
         OVERSEAS FILIPINO WORKERS PENSION FUND TO PROVIDE
             PENSION TO OVERSEAS FILIPINO WORKERS AND

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The constitutional foundation is clear: the State affirms labor as a primary social
and economic force and shall protect workers and promote their welfare. A permanent
OFW pension responds to a measurable need in a sector that sustains national
income and household consumption. Official statistics show the scale and persistence
of overseas employment: the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) estimates 2.16
million OFWs worked abroad from April to September 2023, up 9.8 percent from 2022.
This is not an abstract constituency but a large, continuing labor cohort whose
earnings drive remittance flows and anchor macroeconomic stability.

        Remittances remain a pillar of external accounts. The Bangko Sentral ng
Pilipinas reports bank-coursed cash remittances reached US$34.49 billion in 2024,
three percent higher than 2023, with monthly inflows approaching US$3.0 billion by
August 2025. The reliability of these inflows is precisely why social protection for the
workers who generate them merits statutory support.

       Institutional readiness also exists. The Department of Migrant Workers (DMW),
created under Republic Act No. 11641, is mandated to protect OFWs throughout the
migration cycle, including reintegration. Assigning the DMW to design, administer, and
regulate the OFW pension mechanism aligns with its legal mandate and consolidates
accountability in a single implementing agency with sector expertise, existing field
networks, and regulatory reach.
         The policy substance of this measure is straightforward and implementable.
First, it establishes an OFW Pension Fund and a DMW-administered pension plan that
guarantees not less than ₱5,000 per month to eligible retirees, separate from SSS or
GSIS benefits, thereby avoiding overlap and preserving the integrity of existing social
insurance systems. Second, it defines clear eligibility grounded in verifiable
employment history and registration records, minimizing administrative discretion and
enabling credible targeting. Third, it mandates inter-agency coordination among DMW,
DOF, OWWA, POEA’s successor offices, and DFA to ensure sound fund governance,
accurate data matching, and international servicing through posts and welfare offices.
These features mirror institutional arrangements already used for OFW services and
can be implemented with existing registries and remittance data, reducing transaction
costs and leakage risk.

       The economic and social rationale is compelling:

       One, the OFW population skews toward occupations with physical strain and
irregular income continuity; a predictable pension offsets late-life income volatility and
reduces old-age poverty risk in households historically sustained by remittances.

       Two, remittance-dependent localities benefit when returning workers have
stable purchasing power, supporting local enterprise and mitigating negative demand
shocks.

      Three, the program codifies the State’s recognition of a group whose foreign
earnings cushion the current account and stabilize consumption during domestic
downturns, strengthening social cohesion and trust in public institutions.

      Each of these impacts is anchored in the documented size of the OFW
workforce and the volume and stability of remittance inflows cited above.

       This bill is a counterpart measure to Senate Bill No. 252, “OFW Pension Act of
2025,” filed by Sen. Erwin Tulfo, which seeks to establish a dedicated pension fund
for Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) to provide a minimum monthly pension upon
retirement.

     In view of the magnitude of the sector, the proven role of remittances in the
economy, and the DMW’s statutory mandate to protect and reintegrate OFWs, the
immediate passage of this counterpart measure is respectfully sought.

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Overseas Filipino Workers
Pension Act of 2026.”

       Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the welfare of overseas Filipino workers. Towards this end, the
State shall accord due recognition to overseas Filipino workers’ contribution to the
country’s development by providing them with pension benefits to assist them with
their basic necessities when they reach the age of retirement.

       Sec. 3. Definition of Terms. - As used in this Act, Overseas Filipino Worker
(OFW) shall refer to a person who is to be engaged, is engaged or has been engaged
in a remunerated activity in a state of which he or she is not a citizen or on board a
vessel navigating the foreign seas other than a government ship used for military or
non commercial purposes or on an installation located offshore or on the high seas,
including but not limited to those who are engaged in manual, skilled, or technical
occupations requiring physical labor, specialized training, or vocational skills.

      Sec. 4. Establishment of the Overseas Filipino Workers (OFW) Pension Fund.
- There is hereby established an OFW Pension Fund to provide pension benefits to
overseas Filipino workers, especially those engaged in manual, skilled, or technical
occupations that require physical labor, specialized training, or vocational skills.
     Sec. 5. Eligibility. - The benefits under this Act shall apply to an OFW who:
     a) Is at least sixty (60) years of age upon retirement with an accumulated fifteen
(15)         years            of          overseas          employment;             and
     b) Is registered with the Philippine Overseas Employment Administration
(POEA).

      Provided, That the pension under this Act shall be separate and distinct from
any benefits or pensions received by OFWs under the Social Security System (SSS)
or Government Service Insurance System (GSIS).

       Sec. 6. Overseas Filipino Workers Pension (OFW Pension). - All eligible
Overseas Filipino Workers shall be entitled to a monthly pension of not less than Five
Thousand Pesos (₱5,000.00) upon retirement, subject to the pension plan to be
established by the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW).

        Sec. 7. Role of the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW). - The DMW shall
be the lead implementing agency and administrator of this Act. In addition to its existing
functions,                                    it                                    shall:
        a)       Establish        a        pension          plan        for        OFWs;
        b) Manage the OFW Pension Fund to ensure sustainability;
        c) Determine eligibility criteria in coordination with relevant government
agencies;                                                                             and
        d) Perform other duties necessary for the efficient and effective implementation
of this Act.

       Sec. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). - Within ninety (90) days
from the approval of this Act, the DMW, in consultation with the Department of Finance
(DOF), the POEA, the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA), the
Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA), and non-government organizations advocating
for OFW rights, shall formulate the IRR necessary for its proper implementation.

      Sec. 9. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual appropriations of the Department of Migrant
Workers.

      Sec. 10. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

       Sec. 11. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, issuances, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       Sec. 12. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
Approved.
