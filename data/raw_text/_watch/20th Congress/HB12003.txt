                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The 1987 Constitution mandates the State to protect and promote the right of
every Filipino to quality and accessible education at all levels. Fulfilling this mandate
requires an integrated and coordinated approach among government agencies
responsible for education and workforce development.

       While the Department of Education (DepEd), the Commission on Higher
Education (CHED), and the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority
(TESDA) have distinct mandates, the challenges facing the education sector
necessitate stronger coordination in addressing learning gaps, improving educational
quality, and aligning academic and technical training with labor market demands.

      The Second Congressional Commission on Education (EDCOM II) has
emphasized the need to address institutional fragmentation, policy inconsistencies,
and gaps in accountability that hinder the effective implementation of education
reforms.

       This measure therefore seeks to establish the National Coordinating Council for
Education (NCCE) as a permanent inter-agency body under the Office of the President
to harmonize education policies, programs, and budgets; coordinate priority reforms;
strengthen workforce and learner data systems; and ensure accountability among
concerned agencies.

       Through this measure, the State seeks to promote a more coherent,
responsive, and efficient education system and ensure the sustained implementation
of national education and workforce development priorities.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
     CREATING THE NATIONAL COORDINATING COUNCIL FOR EDUCATION,
     DEFINING   ITS  MANDATE,   POWERS  AND   FUNCTIONS,  AND
     APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “National
 2   Coordinating Council for Education (NCCE) Act.”
 3
 4          SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
 5   State to protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, accessible, and
 6   relevant education at all levels. Towards this end, the State shall establish an
 7   integrated and responsive education system that promotes lifelong learning, skills
 8   development, and the development of human resources responsive to the country's
 9   economic and social development goals.
10
11          The State recognizes the need to strengthen coordination among the
12   Department of Education (DepEd), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), the
13   Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), and other concerned
14   government agencies to ensure continuity of educational policies, harmonization of
15   programs, efficient utilization of resources, and alignment of educational outcomes
16   with labor market requirements.
17
18          Accordingly, the State shall institutionalize a permanent inter-agency
19   coordinating mechanism to ensure the effective implementation of national education
20   and workforce development policies and programs.
21
22        SECTION 3. Creation of the National Coordinating Council for
23   Education. – There is hereby created under the Office of the President a high-level
 1   inter-agency body to be known as the National Coordinating Council for Education
 2   (NCCE), hereinafter referred to as the Council.
 3
 4         The Council shall serve as the principal policy coordination, integration, and
 5   monitoring body for education and workforce development reforms across the
 6   Executive Branch.
 7
 8         It shall ensure the alignment of policies, plans, programs, projects, and
 9   budgetary priorities of concerned government agencies with the National Education
10   and Workforce Development Plan and other relevant national development plans.
11
12          SECTION 4. Composition of the Council. – The Council shall be composed
13   of the following:
14          (a) President of the Philippines, as Chairperson;
15          (b) A Cabinet Secretary designated by the President, as Vice Chairperson;
16          (c) Secretary of the Department of Education (DepEd);
17          (d) Chairperson of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED);
18          (e) Director General of the Technical Education and Skills Development
19          Authority (TESDA);
20          (f) Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST);
21          (g) Secretary of the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE);
22          (h) Secretary of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM);
23          (i) Secretary of the Department of Economy, Planning, and Development
24          (DEPDev);
25          (j) Chairperson of the National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA);
26          and
27          (k) Chairperson of the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC).
28
29           The Chairperson may invite representatives of other government agencies,
30   local government units, educational institutions, industry associations, and relevant
31   civil society organizations to participate in Council meetings whenever their expertise
32   and assistance are necessary.
33
34           The members shall designate permanent alternate representatives with a rank
35   not lower than Assistant Secretary or its equivalent, who shall attend meetings and
36   participate in the deliberations in their absence.
37
38          SECTION 5. Powers and Functions. – The Council shall exercise the
39   following powers and functions:
40
41         (a) Formulate, coordinate, and harmonize national policies, plans, programs,
42         and projects concerning formal, non-formal, technical-vocational, and higher
43         education;
44
45         (b) Oversee the formulation, implementation, and periodic updating of the
46         National Education and Workforce Development Plan to ensure consistency in
47         national targets, priorities, and resource allocation;
48

 4
 5   (d) Establish mechanisms to ensure the smooth transition of learners from early
 6   childhood education to basic education, higher education, technical-vocational
 7   education and training, and employment;
 8
 9   (e) Develop a comprehensive human resource development plan for the
10   education sector, including the supply, demand, recruitment, training, and
11   professional development of teaching and non-teaching personnel;
12
13   (f) Review and coordinate major budgetary requirements and proposals of
14   member agencies to ensure that priority education programs and projects are
15   adequately funded;
16
17   (g) Promote collaboration among government agencies in the implementation

19   other capacity-building activities;
20

24
25   (i) Establish and maintain an integrated and interoperable education data

 8         SECTION 6. Convergence and Accountability. – All member agencies shall
 9   extend full cooperation and assistance to the Council and shall provide relevant
10   information, data, and reports necessary for the performance of its functions.
11
12         Each member agency shall be responsible for implementing programs, projects,
13   and activities within its respective mandate that are aligned with the National
14   Education and Workforce Development Plan.
15
16          The Council shall establish appropriate monitoring and evaluation mechanisms,
17   including measurable performance indicators and reporting requirements, to ensure
18   accountability and the timely implementation of agreed policies and programs.
19
20         SECTION 7. Technical Working Groups. – The Council may create
21   Technical Working Groups (TWGs) to assist in the development, coordination, and
22   implementation of specific policies and programs.
23
24   The TWGs shall be composed of representatives from concerned member agencies,
25   with a rank not lower than Assistant Secretary or its equivalent.
26
27   The Council may likewise invite representatives from educational institutions, the
28   private sector, professional organizations, and other relevant stakeholders to provide
29   technical assistance and recommendations.
30
31         SECTION 8. Meetings of the Council. – The Council shall meet at least once
32   every quarter. Special meetings may be convened by the Chairperson or upon the
33   request of a majority of its members.
34
35          A quorum shall consist of a majority of the members currently holding office,
36   including the Chairperson or the duly designated Vice Chairperson.
37
38           The members shall serve without additional compensation, except for
39   reasonable travel expenses and other necessary expenses incurred in the performance
40   of their official functions, subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws,
41   rules, and regulations.
42
43         SECTION 9. The Council Secretariat. – The Council shall have a permanent
44   Secretariat which shall serve as its principal technical, administrative, research, and
45   program management arm.
46
47          The Secretariat shall be responsible for policy research, program coordination,
48   data integration, monitoring and evaluation, preparation of reports, and the provision
 1   of administrative and technical support necessary for the effective discharge of the
 2   Council's functions.
 3
 4         It shall be headed by an Executive Director with the rank of Undersecretary,
 5   who shall be appointed by the President.
 6
 7          The Secretariat may be staffed by permanent personnel and augmented by
 8   technical experts and personnel from concerned government agencies, subject to
 9   existing civil service, budgeting, procurement, and auditing laws, rules, and
10   regulations.
11
12         SECTION 10. Rationalization and Coordination of Existing Inter-
13   Agency Bodies. – To promote administrative efficiency and policy coherence, the
14   Council shall serve as the primary coordinating platform for matters relating to
15   education and workforce development.
16
17          Existing inter-agency councils, committees, and similar bodies with
18   substantially overlapping functions may, to the extent practicable and consistent with
19   their respective enabling laws and mandates, coordinate their activities through the
20   Council or its Technical Working Groups.
21
22         Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish or transfer the statutory
23   powers and functions of the DepEd, CHED, TESDA, or other concerned government
24   agencies.
25
26          SECTION 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60)
27   days from the effectivity of this Act, the Council shall promulgate the rules and
28   regulations necessary for its effective implementation.
29
30          SECTION 12. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
31   implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the
32   Office of the President.
33
34          Thereafter, such amounts as may be necessary for the continued
35   implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act,
36   subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.
37

39   unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain
40   in full force and effect.
