                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Municipality of Samal, the second oldest town in the Province of Bataan,
was founded by Dominican friars on 20 April 1641, and placed under the spiritual
patronage of St. Catherine of Sienna. Rich in history and culture, Samal is recognized
as the pioneer of the capiz shell industry along the surrounding coastlines, the
birthplace of the province's wooden shoe ("bakya") making industry, and the site of
the country's first pulp mills.

       Over the centuries, Samal has gracefully evolved from a quaint coastal
settlement into a progressive municipality. Driven by the remarkable resilience and
industriousness of its people, the town has sustained its thriving agricultural and
fishing sectors, ensuring food security and economic vitality. Commemorating its
foundation day serves not only as a tribute to this colorful past but also as a
celebration of the community's continuous growth and achievements.

       Every year, the people of Samal proudly commemorate their foundation day.
Recognizing the profound historical significance of this date, the Sangguniang Bayan
of Samal passed Resolution No. 26-103, advocating for the legislative enactment of
April 20th of every year as a special non-working holiday in the municipality.

        Declaring April 20 as a special non-working holiday will allow the people of
Samal to fully participate in meaningful civic, cultural, and commemorative
festivities. It provides the working population and students the opportunity to honor
their ancestors and strengthen their sense of community without the constraints of
work or school. Furthermore, institutionalizing this celebration will promote local
tourism, attract visitors, and contribute to the economic growth and cultural
development of the municipality.
      In view of the foregoing, and to give due recognition to the socio-historical
importance of Samal's founding, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
DECLARING APRIL 20 OF EVERY YEAR AS "ARAW NG SAMAL,” A SPECIAL
NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAMAL, PROVINCE
 OF BATAAN, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. – April 20 of every year is hereby declared a special non-working
holiday in the Municipality of Samal, Province of Bataan, in commemoration of its
founding anniversary, to be known as "Araw ng Samal.”

SEC. 2. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation in the Philippines.
