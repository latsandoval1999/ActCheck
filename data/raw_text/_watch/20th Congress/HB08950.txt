                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to provide comprehensive post-harvest facilities for palay
and rice farmers by mandating the construction of warehouses and rice mills,
and the provision of essential equipment and machinery. This reduces
post-harvest losses, improves product quality, and increases farmers’ incomes.
It also aims to strengthen the agricultural value chain by ensuring that
farmers have access to adequate infrastructure necessary to process, store, and
market their produce efficiently.

       Despite the vital role of rice farmers in ensuring national food security,
many continue to suffer from inadequate post-harvest support. The lack of
accessible and modern facilities such as warehouses and rice mills results in
significant losses due to spoilage, inefficient processing, and poor storage
conditions. These challenges force farmers to sell their produce at lower prices
immediately after harvest, often to middlemen, thereby reducing their
potential earnings and limiting their participation in value-added activities.

      This proposed measure directly addresses these concerns by directing
the Department of Agriculture (DA) and the Department of Agrarian Reform
(DAR), in coordination with the Department of Public Works and Highways
(DPWH), to construct post-harvest facilities in rice-producing areas across the
country. It ensures that every legislative district with rice-producing
municipalities will have access to at least one warehouse and one rice mill,
particularly during the initial phase of implementation.

      Furthermore, the bill mandates the Department of Trade and Industry
(DTI), in coordination with relevant agencies and farmer cooperatives, to
provide the necessary equipment, machinery, and transport facilities to
support the efficient operation of these infrastructures. It also empowers
farmers’ cooperatives by allowing them to acquire these facilities through an
affordable, long-term, and interest-free amortization scheme, thereby
promoting ownership, sustainability, and inclusive growth within the
agricultural sector.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                      AN ACT
PROVIDING POST-HARVEST FACILITIES FOR PALAY AND RICE
FARMERS

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Post-Harvest
Facilities Support Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to ensure national food security through the promotion of agricultural
and rural development. Towards this end, the State shall endeavor to provide
for agricultural infrastructure and facilities necessary to increase the farmers’
incomes and reduce the cost of agricultural produce, particularly palay.
Furthermore, the State shall strengthen farmers’ organizations and
cooperatives as essential partners in achieving its economic and agricultural
goals.

       SEC. 3. Construction of Post-Harvest Facilities. – The Department of
Agriculture (DA) and the Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR), in
coordination with the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH),
and in consultation with concerned farmer cooperatives, shall construct
warehouses and rice mills in every rice-producing municipality and city in
the Philippines: Provided, That in the initial phase of the program, every
legislative district with at least one-rice producing municipality shall be the
beneficiary of at least one (1) warehouse and one (1) rice mill.

      SEC. 4. Provision of Equipment and Machineries. – The Department of
Trade and Industry (DTI), in consultation with the DA, the DAR and farmers’
cooperatives, shall provide the equipment and machineries, including


                                         1
transport facilities, necessary to complement and operate the warehouses and
rice mills: Provided, That such warehouses and rice milIs, including the
equipment, machineries and transport facilities required to operate them,
shall be sold to the concerned accredited farmers’ cooperatives which shall be
given a period of twenty-five (25) years within which to amortize the cost of
the same without interest.

       SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DA, the DAR, the DPWH and the DTI shall
jointly formulate and promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the
effective implementation of this Act.

      SEC. 6. Appropriations. – The amount of One Billion Pesos
(P1,000,000,000.00) is hereby initially appropriated for the program upon
approval of this Act. Thereafter, the amount necessary to pursue the program
shall be incorporated in the General Appropriations Act, funding of which
shall partly be sourced from the savings realized from the phasing out of
palay and rice subsidies, and from the amortizations accumulated thereof.

       SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act, or any parts
thereof, is declared unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity and
effectivity of the other portions.

      SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees, proclamations, rules, regulations, issuances and enactments of parts
thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

       SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days from its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation in the Philippines, whichever comes earlier.
