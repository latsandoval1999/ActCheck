                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE


       This bill proposes the creation of an additional Regional Trial Court (RTC) branch
to be stationed in the City of Carmona, with jurisdiction over the City of Carmona and
the Municipality of General Mariano Alvarez (GMA).

       The historic progress of Carmona, following its cityhood, together with the
continued development of GMA, has led to an anticipated rise in civil, criminal, and
commercial litigation. To meet these growing demands and to ensure that justice
remains accessible and efficient for the people of these rapidly advancing communities,
the establishment of a new Regional Trial Court in Carmona is both timely and relevant.

       The creation of a new RTC branch is a necessary measure to strengthen the
administration of justice in the area. By establishing a court dedicated to these
communities, this measure will ease the load of existing dockets and help maintain the
prompt disposition of cases. This is not merely an administrative adjustment but a
substantive fulfillment of the constitutional mandate for the speedy disposition of cases
under Article III, Section 16 of the Constitution. It gives life to the judiciary's core duty
to resolve legal controversies promptly and ensures that the right to speedy justice
becomes an accessible reality for every Carmonense and resident of GMA.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                             AN ACT

     CREATING ONE (1) ADDITIONAL BRANCH OF THE REGIONAL TRIAL COURT
     IN THE FOURTH JUDICIAL REGION TO BE STATIONED IN THE CITY OF
     CARMONA, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, WITH JURISDICTION OVER THE CITY OF
     CARMONA AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF GENERAL MARIANO ALVAREZ,
     AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 14, PARAGRAPH (E) OF BATAS
     PAMBANSA BLG. 129, AS AMENDED, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “THE
     JUDICIARY REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1980,” AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
     THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

1           SECTION 1. One (1) additional branch of the Regional Trial Court (RTC) in the
2    Fourth Judicial Region is hereby created and stationed in the City of Carmona, Province
3    of Cavite.

4           This newly created branch shall have territorial jurisdiction over the City of
5    Carmona and the Municipality of General Mariano Alvarez.

6           The Supreme Court shall assign the corresponding branch number for the newly
7    created branch.

8           SEC. 2. The Supreme Court, in coordination with the Secretary of the Department
9    of Justice, shall immediately include in the Judiciary's program the operationalization of
10   the additional RTC branch in the City of Carmona. The Supreme Court shall issue the
11   necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act, which may
12   include, if warranted, the realignment of jurisdiction over cases from other branches
13   within the Fourth Judicial Region to the branch created herein.
