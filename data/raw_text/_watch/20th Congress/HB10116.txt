                            AN ACT
 INSTITUTIONALIZING DETAILED UNIT PRICE ANALYSIS SYSTEM IN THE
       DETERMINATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR ANY AND ALL
INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS IN THE NATIONAL EXPENDITURE PROGRAM
             AND THE GENERAL APPROPRIATIONS ACT

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Public infrastructure accounts for nearly one-fifth of total government
expenditure. Under the 2026 General Appropriations Act, ₱1.289 trillion out of the
₱6.793 trillion national budget is allocated for infrastructure. Despite its substantial
share, infrastructure spending has long been vulnerable to overpricing, fund leakages,
and other procurement irregularities.

       To strengthen transparency and fiscal accountability, this measure mandates
the adoption of a standardized Detailed Unit Price Analysis (DUPA) as the basis for
determining appropriations and project costs for all public works. Prepared during the
pre-construction and procurement phases, the DUPA establishes the fair and
reasonable cost for every work item prior to project implementation and provides line-
item specifications for all identifiable and verifiable components including labor, raw
materials, heavy equipment operational costs, and other expenses. The DUPA also
creates a precise financial baseline. This objective cost structure prevents overpricing,
curbs budget inflation, and ensures that unit costs remain strictly within legal and
technical standards.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

 1          SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the "Detailed Unit Price
 2   Analysis System for Infrastructure Projects Act".
 3
 4           SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State
 5   to take positive and effective measures against graft and corruption. Towards this
 6   end, the State shall institutionalize standards and procedures for estimating costs of
 7   any and all infrastructure projects of the government and adopt measures to promote
 8   fiscal discipline, enhance efficiency in the allocation of government resources, and
 9   deter unscrupulous budget practices.
10
11         SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. As used in this Act:
12
13                (a) Implementing Agency shall refer to any department, bureau, office,
14         commission, authority or agency of the national government, including any
15         government-owned or -controlled corporation or state college or university,
16         duly authorized by law or its charter to undertake national government
17         projects;

18                (b) Infrastructure Project shall refer to the construction, improvement,
19         rehabilitation, demolition, repair, restoration, or maintenance of roads and
20         bridges, railways, airports, seaports, communication facilities, civil works
21         components of information technology projects, irrigation, flood control and
22         drainage, water supply, sanitation, sewerage and solid waste management
23         systems, shore protection, energy/power and electrification facilities, national
24         buildings, school buildings, hospital buildings, and other related construction
25         projects of the government; and

                                               1
 1                 (c) Multi-Year Project shall refer to a government project that would
 2          require more than one (1) year to complete and would consequently need
 3          appropriations across multiple years as evidenced by a duly approved multi-
 4          year obligational authority.

 5          SEC. 4. Coverage. This Act shall apply to all infrastructure projects, including
 6   multi-year projects, of national government agencies and instrumentalities, including
 7   constitutional offices, state universities and colleges, and government-owned or
 8   controlled corporations with or without original charter.
 9
10          SEC. 5. Technical and Financial Basis for Appropriations. Implementing
11   agencies shall ensure that appropriations for infrastructure projects are supported by
12   documentation that establishes the technical and financial basis for their
13   determination. Such documentation shall be contained in a Detailed Unit Price Analysis
14   (DUPA) that provides detailed itemization of the cost and quantity of labor, equipment,
15   and materials required in a given project, as well as other ancillary expenditures
16   necessary for its implementation and completion. The DUPA shall be deemed as a
17   requisite or a condition sine qua non for the inclusion of any infrastructure project in
18   the National Expenditure Program and the General Appropriations Act, and for the
19   conduct of any procurement activity and the subsequent release of any allotment for
20   such project.
21
22          SEC. 6. Components of Detailed Unit Price Analysis. The DUPA shall
23   include the following information:
24
25                 (a) Name, location, and brief description of the infrastructure project;

26                 (b) Projected duration of project implementation;

27                (c) Labor, including the type and number of workers required, their
28          respective wage rates, and the number of days such workers will be contracted;

29                 (d) Equipment and machinery, including the type and number of
30          equipment or machinery required, their respective acquisition cost or rental
31          rates, and the number of days such equipment or machinery will be rented, if
32          applicable;

33                 (e) Detailed list and quantity of materials required and their respective
34          unit costs;

35                (f) Projected overhead, contingency, and miscellaneous expenses;
36          contractor's profit; tax expenditure; and other relevant expenses;

37                 (g) Such other information as may be prescribed under the national
38          reference manual as provided under Section 7 of this Act, or as may be required
39          by the implementing agencies concerned.

40          SEC. 7. National Reference Manual. The DUPA for infrastructure project
41   shall be prepared in accordance with a national reference manual to be prepared by

                                                2
 1   the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) in collaboration with the
 2   Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Department of Economy, Planning,
 3   and Development (DEPDev), and Commission on Audit (COA). The manual shall
 4   contain standard criteria and methodologies for preparing and updating DUPAs, cost
 5   references and templates, and other information necessary for ensuring accuracy,
 6   uniformity, and transparency in cost estimation of all implementing agencies. The
 7   manual shall be updated at least once every three (3) years to incorporate regional
 8   price variances, price inflation, technology improvements, and other factors affecting
 9   the cost of infrastructure projects.

10          SEC. 8. Capacity Building and Technical Support. The DPWH, in
11   coordination with DBM, DEPDev, Civil Service Commission, and COA, shall design and
12   implement a capacity building program for government engineers, budget officers,
13   auditors, and other personnel involved in preparing and evaluating budget and project
14   proposals, which shall be included in the list of regular training program of government
15   agencies concerned.

16          SEC. 9. Penalties. Any public official or employee who knowingly falsifies or
17   omits, or causes the falsification or omission of any information contained in the DUPA
18   shall suffer the penalty of perpetual absolute disqualification to hold office without
19   prejudice to any other liabilities, criminal, civil, administrative or otherwise, as may be
20   provided by law and regulations.

21          SEC. 10. Transitory Provision. For Fiscal Year 2026, compliance with the
22   requirements under Section 5 of this Act shall apply to infrastructure projects
23   implemented by national executive agencies, including, but not limited to, projects
24   implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Department of Education, Department
25   of Health, Department of Information and Communication Technology, Department of
26   the Interior and Local Government (DILG), Department of National Defense, DPWH,
27   and Department of Transportation. Consequently, no funds shall be released, nor any
28   procurement activity shall be performed for such infrastructure projects

29          SEC. 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days
30   from the effectivity of this Act, the DPWH shall promulgate the rules and regulations
31   to effectively implement the provisions of this Act.

32         SEC. 12. Separability Clause. If any provision or part hereof is declared
33   unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not affected thereby shall
34   remain in full force and effect.

35          SEC. 13. Repealing Clause. All laws, acts, decrees, executive orders, issuances,
36   and rules and regulations or parts thereof which are contrary to and inconsistent with
37   this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

38          SEC. 14. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
39   publication in the Official Gazette or at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
