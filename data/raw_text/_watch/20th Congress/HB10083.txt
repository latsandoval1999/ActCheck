                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to increase the duration of paternity leaves from seven
(7) to thirty (30) working days. It expands Republic Act (RA) No. 8187,
otherwise known as the “Paternity Leave Act of 1996” to extend its coverage to
all male employees regardless of civil or employment status, and recognizes
partners in exclusive cohabiting relationships who have no legal impediment
to marry.

       Paternal involvement during the postnatal period improves family
well-being, strengthens child development, supports maternal health,
advances gender equality in the home and the workplace, and promotes
shared parental responsibility. Despite the enactment of RA No. 8187, the
existing seven (7-)-day paternity leave is such an insufficient time for fathers
to provide the support that their partners’ needs.

      This proposed measure also recognizes that many Filipino families are
formed outside marriage but deserve equal support during childbirth. Thus,
it extends the paternity leave benefits to soon-to-be fathers in exclusive
cohabiting relationships. It also includes miscarries and emergency pregnancy
termination among the circumstances covered by the paternity leave.

      Expanding paternity leave affirms the State’s commitment to protect
the basic unit of society, promote the welfare of parents and their children,
and encourage shared parental responsibility. It ensures that the law keeps
pace with evolving family structures while providing fathers with meaningful
opportunities to participate in the care and upbringing of their children.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is
earnestly sought.

                                     AN ACT
EXPANDING THE SCOPE OF AND PROVIDING FOR EXTENDED
PATERNITY LEAVE, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT
NO. 8187, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “PATERNITY LEAVE ACT OF
1996”, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Section 2 of Republic Act No. 8187, otherwise known as the
“Paternity Leave Act of 1996”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

             “Section 2. PATERNITY LEAVE. – Notwithstanding any
      law, rules, and regulations to the contrary, every [married] male
      employee in the private and public sectors, REGARDLESS OF
      EMPLOYMENT OR CIVIL STATUS, shall be entitled to a
      paternity leave of [seven (7) days] THIRTY (30) WORKING
      DAYS with full pay for the first four (4) deliveries of [the
      legitimate spouse] HIS PARTNER with whom he is
      EXCLUSIVELY cohabiting WITHOUT THE BENEFIT OF
      MARRIAGE; PROVIDED, THAT THE MALE EMPLOYEE
      AND HIS PARTNER HAVE NO LEGAL IMPEDIMENT TO
      MARRY EACH OTHER. The male employee applying for
      paternity leave shall notify his employer of the pregnancy of his
      [legitimate spouse] PARTNER and the expected date of such
      delivery. For purposes of this Act, delivery shall include
      childbirth,   [or   any]     miscarriage,   OR     EMERGENCY
      TERMINATION OF PREGNANCY.”

      SEC. 2. Section 3 of the same Act is hereby removed.
