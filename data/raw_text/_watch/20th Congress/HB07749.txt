                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Republic Act No. 8291, or the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) Act of 1997,
requires at least fifteen (15) years of credited service for entitlement to retirement
benefits. Under the existing framework, members in the non-career service who often
serve on fixed-term, co-terminus, or contractual appointments are frequently unable to
meet this requirement, despite years of government service, and are thus excluded from
GSIS retirement benefits.

This bill seeks to address this gap by expressly extending GSIS retirement coverage to
qualified members in the non-career service. It amends Section 13(b) of RA 8291 to
recognize non-career personnel as eligible to avail of retirement and other applicable
benefits, subject to the conditions provided under the Act. Amendments to Section 13-
A are likewise introduced to clarify and standardize the conditions for entitlement to
retirement benefits.

Most importantly, the bill inserts a new Section 13-B, which allows members in the
non-career service who have not completed the required fifteen (15) years of
contributions to voluntarily continue paying the full GSIS premiums, including both the
personal and agency share, until they qualify for retirement benefits. To ensure
affordability, the measure further authorizes the GSIS to allow payment based on a
lower salary grade, under reasonable terms and conditions.

This proposed measure is a refiled bill approved on Third Reading during the 19th
Congress, reflecting prior legislative approval and sustained policy support. Its
enactment advances equity, social protection, and inclusiveness in the government
retirement system, ensuring that non-career government workers are not left without
retirement security after years of public service.

                             AN ACT
PROVIDING AN OPTION FOR QUALIFIED MEMBERS IN THE NON-CAREER
SERVICE TO PAY VOLUNTARY CONTRIBUTION TO THE GOVERNMENT SERVICE
INSURANCE SYSTEM, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8291,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “THE GOVERNMENT SERVICE INSURANCE SYSTEM ACT
OF 1997”

Be it enacted in the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Republic of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Section 13 (b) of Republic Act (R.A.) No. 8291, otherwise known as
“The Government Service Insurance System Act of 1997” is hereby amended to read as
follows:

             "SEC. 13. Retirement Benefits. -

             x x x…

            (b) Unless the service is extended by appropriate authorities,
      retirement shall be compulsory for an employee at sixty-five (65) years of
      age, with at least fifteen (15) years of service: Provided, That [if he] A
      MEMBER WHO has less than fifteen (15) years of service [, he] may be
      allowed to continue in the service in accordance with existing civil service
      rules and regulations[.]: PROVIDED FURTHER, THAT MEMBERS IN THE
      NON-CAREER SERVICE, AS DEFINED IN SECTION 9, CHAPTER 2,
      SUBTITLE A, TITLE I, BOOK V, OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 292,
      OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “ADMINISTRATIVE CODE OF 1987,”
      MAY AVAIL OF THE RETIREMENT AND OTHER APPLICABLE
      BENEFITS UNDER THIS ACT, AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 13-B
      HEREIN.”

      SEC. 2. Section 13-A of RA 8291 is hereby amended to read as follows:

            "SEC. 13-A. Conditions for Entitlement. - A member who retires
      from the service shall be entitled to the retirement benefits in paragraph (a)
      of Section 13 hereof: Provided, That THE MEMBER:

             (1) [he] has rendered at least fifteen (15) years of service;
            (2) [he] is at least sixty (60) years of age at the time of
                 retirement; and

            (3) [he] is not receiving a monthly pension benefit from
                 permanent total disability”

      SEC. 3. A new Section 13-B is hereby inserted to RA 8291, to read as follows:

            "SEC. 13-B. OPTION FOR MEMBERS IN THE NON-CAREER
      SERVICE. - A MEMBER IN THE NON-CAREER SERVICE WHO HAS NOT
      CONTRIBUTED AT LEAST FIFTEEN (15) YEARS OF PREMIUM SHALL
      BE ALLOWED TO CONTINUE TO PAY THE FULL PREMIUMS,
      REPRESENTING THE PERSONAL AND AGENCY SHARE, UNTIL SUCH
      MEMBER SHALL HAVE COMPLETED THE MINIMUM NUMBER OF
      YEARS TO QUALIFY FOR RETIREMENT BENEFIT: PROVIDED,
      HOWEVER, THAT THE GSIS SHALL ALLOW A MEMBER TO PAY THE
      PREMIUM FOR A LOWER SALARY GRADE, UNDER REASONABLE
      TERMS AND CONDITIONS, TO PROVIDE FLEXIBILITY AND
      AFFORDABILITY ON THE PART OF THE MEMBER.”
      SEC. 4. Nothing herein shall diminish the retirement and separation benefits
already enjoyed under the existing laws at the time of retirement.

     SEC. 5. Within sixty (60) days after the effectivity of this Act, the GSIS shall
promulgate the rules and regulations to implement this Act.

       SEC. 6. If any provision of this Act or part hereof is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such declaration shall remain in
full force and effect.

      SEC. 7. All laws, executive and administrative orders, rules and regulations
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 8. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
