14                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE
15
16   The 1987 Philippine Constitution explicitly mandates the State to prioritize the needs of the
17   underprivileged, sick, elderly, disabled, women, and children. Specifically, Section 13 of
18   Article XIII provides that the State shall establish a special agency for disabled persons for
19   their rehabilitation, self-development, and self-reliance, and their integration into the
20   mainstream of society.
21
22   While the passage of Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the "Magna Carta for
23   Persons with Disabilities," and its subsequent amendments have significantly advanced the
24   rights of Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) through tax exemptions, mandatory discounts,
25   and health coverage, substantial economic gaps remain. PWDs in the Philippines
26   continually face a compounding cycle of poverty and vulnerability.
27
28   Living with a disability entails significant, unavoidable extra costs. Families and individuals
29   must routinely absorb expenses for specialized medical care, rehabilitation, assistive
30   devices, therapy, accessible transportation, and personal assistance. These "disability costs"
31   run parallel to an severely restricted capacity to generate income, as PWDs encounter
32   disproportionate barriers to formal employment and livelihood opportunities.
33
34   This bill seeks to address these systemic inequities by establishing a guaranteed, non-
35   conditional Monthly Disability Support Allowance in the initial amount of Two
36   Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) for all registered PWDs.
37
38   Crucially, to prevent this financial lifeline from being eroded by macroeconomic pressures,
39   this measure introduces a mandatory Inflation Indexation mechanism. By legally tethering
40   the monthly allowance to the annual Consumer Price Index (CPI) through the Philippine
41   Statistics Authority (PSA), the State ensures that the real purchasing power of the social
42   safety net is shielded against inflation and rising utility and commodity costs.
43
44   To ensure long-term fiscal sustainability and accountability, the bill mandates that funding
45   be directly and explicitly integrated into the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).
46   This allowance acts not as a handout, but as a crucial economic equalizer aimed at lowering
47   poverty poverty rates, mitigating the financial shocks of living with a disability, and
48   fostering authentic social inclusion.
49
50   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

70     AN ACT ESTABLISHING A MONTHLY DISABILITY SUPPORT ALLOWANCE
71    FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES (PWDs), PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR,
72                       AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

73          Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
74    Congress assembled:

 75   SECTION 1. Short Title.
 76
 77          This Act shall be known as the "Monthly Disability Support Allowance Act."
 78
 79   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.
 80
 81          It is the policy of the State to promote social justice, protect the rights of Persons
 82   with Disabilities (PWDs), and ensure their integration into mainstream society. Recognizing
 83   that PWDs face disproportionate economic barriers, extra living costs, and limited
 84   livelihood opportunities, the State shall provide regular, predictable financial assistance to
 85   safeguard their standard of living and foster dignity.
 86
 87   SECTION 3. Monthly Disability Support Allowance.
 88
 89          All registered and qualified Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) holding a valid PWD
 90   ID issued by the National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA) or through their respective
 91   Local Government Units (LGUs) shall be entitled to a Monthly Disability Support
 92   Allowance in the initial amount of Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00).
 93
 94   SECTION 4. Inflation Indexation and Periodic Adjustment.
 95
 96           To preserve the purchasing power and impact of the allowance against changes in
 97   the cost of living, the baseline amount of ₱2,000.00 shall be adjusted annually based on
 98   inflation.
 99
100       a. The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), in coordination with
101          the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), shall review and index the monthly
102          financial assistance to the annual Consumer Price Index (CPI) for the relevant
103          calendar year.
104
105       b. The adjusted amount shall never fall below the baseline amount established under
106          Section 3 of this Act.
107
108   SECTION 5. Non-diminution of Benefits.
109        The allowance provided under this Act shall be paid in addition to any existing tax
110   exemptions, discounts, or subsidies granted under Republic Act No. 7277 (Magna Carta for
111   PWDs), Republic Act No. 10754, and other existing social safety protection laws.
112
113   SECTION 6. Appropriations.
114
115          The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be integrated
116   into the budget of the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) under the
117   General Appropriations Act (GAA) of the fiscal year following its enactment. Thereafter,
118   the funding required for the continuous implementation of this program, including the
119   adjusted increases resulting from annual inflation indexation, shall be explicitly and
120   automatically included in the annual GAA.
121
122   SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).
123
124          Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DSWD, in consultation
125   with the NCDA, Department of Health (DOH), Department of Budget and Management
126   (DBM), and relevant non-government organizations (NGOs) representing PWDs, shall
127   promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for its effective implementation.
128
129   SECTION 8. Separability Clause.
130
131          If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining
132   parts or provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.
133
134   SECTION 9. Repealing Clause.
135
136          All laws, executive orders, rules, and regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with
137   the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
138
139   SECTION 10. Effectivity.
140
141           This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
142   or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
