                               EXPLANATORY NOTE
        Republic Act No. 11032, or the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient
Government Service Delivery Act of 2018, was enacted to streamline government
procedures, reduce red tape, and improve the delivery of public services. Despite
this, challenges persist at the local level, particularly in the processing of business
permits and licenses, where fragmented functions and overlapping responsibilities
continue to delay transactions and discourage business formation.

        This bill seeks to institutionalize a Business Permit and Licensing Office
(BPLO) in every city and municipality, consolidating the processing of permits,
enforcement of tax ordinances, and regulation of business activities under a single
office. It also transfers specific revenue-related and enforcement functions from the
local treasurer to the Business Permit and Licensing Officer to ensure a more
efficient, accountable, and business-friendly system.

        By strengthening local business permitting systems and clarifying
institutional responsibilities, this measure aims to improve ease of doing business at
the local level, enhance revenue generation, and promote a more conducive
environment for investment and economic growth.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought

 1         SEC. 3. Establishment of a Business Permit and Licensing Office. - A
 2   Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO) shall be established in every city or
 3   municipality in the country.
 4         SEC. 4. Powers, Duties and Functions. - The Business Permits and
 5   Licensing Office shall be responsible for the following:
 6         (a) Process and act on all applications for business and mayor’s permits
 7               required under applicable law, ordinance, rules and regulations, and
 8               licenses, as well as occupational permits, including the assessment of
 9               business taxes, fees, and charges of the various businesses in the city
10               or municipality;
11         (b) Assess and determine the local business tax liabilities of a business
12               operating within the territorial jurisdiction of the city or municipality.
13               For this purpose, the BPLO may examine the books of accounts of the
14               said business;
15         (c) Monitor and enforce the applicable tax ordinances (except those
16               pertaining to real property taxes) and other pertinent laws, ordinances,
17               rules and regulations in the operations of businesses and occupations
18               within the jurisdiction of the city or municipality;
19         (d) Formulate policies to draw local and foreign investments especially in
20               priority areas and industries as a tool to define the development thrust
21               of the city or municipality;
22         (e) Initiate, review, and recommend to the mayor revenue-generating
23               measures to increase local revenues;
24         (f)   Conduct tax mapping and regular inspection of all business
25               establishments existing and operating within the territorial jurisdiction
26               of the city or municipality;
27         (g) Issue and implement necessary notices and orders to concerned
28               establishments in relation to its roles under this Act;




                                                                                         2
 1          (h) Perform such other duties and functions, and exercise such other
 2               powers as may be prescribed by law or ordinance.”
 3          SEC. 5. Composition and Office Structure. - The Business Permits and
 4   Licensing Office shall be composed of divisions, but not limited to the following
 5   four (4) principal divisions, namely:
 6          (a) Administration Division - This division is tasked to handle
 7               administrative functions of the Investments and Business Permits and
 8               Licensing Office;
 9          (b) Business Permits and Licensing or Occupational Permits Division -
10               This division shall act on all applications for business and mayor's
11               permits and occupational permits, as required under applicable laws,
12               ordinances, rules and regulations;
13          (c) Investments Division - This division is tasked to craft policies that
14               shall increase local investments in the local government unit in
15               coordination with concerned offices for submission to the local
16               sanggunian for ratification. It shall also supervise compliance of
17               business establishments that have partnered with the local government
18               on investment opportunities.
19          (d) Inspection Division - This division shall handle the technical function
20               of the Business Permits and Licensing Office through:
21                  1. Tax mapping and regular inspections of all business
22                      establishments existing and/or operation within the territorial
23                      jurisdiction of the LGU;
24                  2. Issue and/or implement notices and/or orders to the concerned
25                      establishments; and
26                  3. Perform such other matters and functions that may be assigned
27                      by the Business Permits and Licensing Officer.
