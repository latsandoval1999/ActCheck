                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to mandate the appointment of a city or municipal agriculturist
in local government units where agriculture is a significant industry or the primary
source of livelihood. It amends Section 482(a) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise
known as the “Local Government Code of 1991”. It aims to strengthen local agricultural
governance by ensuring that qualified professionals lead the planning,
implementation, and monitoring of agricultural programs at the local level.

       Agriculture remains a cornerstone of the Philippine economy, particularly in
rural areas where it serves as the main source of income and employment. However,
many cities and municipalities with strong agricultural sectors do not have
designated agriculturists to provide technical guidance, coordinate programs, and
support farmers. This gap weakens the delivery of essential services such as
extension support, technology transfer, crop management assistance, and disaster
response, ultimately affecting productivity and farmers’ incomes.

        This measure addresses this concern by requiring local government units
with significant agricultural activity to appoint a qualified agriculturist.
Institutionalizing this position will ensure that local policies and programs are
science-based, responsive to farmers’ needs, and aligned with national agricultural
priorities. It also enhances coordination between local governments and national
agencies such as the Department of Agriculture DA, thereby improving the
efficiency and impact of agricultural interventions.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
MAKING THE APPOINTMENT OF AN AGRICULTURIST MANDATORY IN
MUNICIPALITIES WHERE AGRICULTURE IS A SIGNIFICANT INDUSTRY,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE AND 482 (A) OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991, AS
AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Section 482 (a) of Republic Act (RA) No. 7160, otherwise known as
the “Local Government Code of 1991”, as amended, is hereby further amended to read
as follows:

              “Sec 482. Qualifications, Powers and Duties. - (a) x x x

             “The position of the agriculturist shall be mandatory for the
       provincial government and optional for the city and municipal
       governments: PROVIDED, THAT INSOFAR AS CITIES AND
       MUNICIPALITIES WHERE AGRICULTURE IS A SIGNIFICANT
       INDUSTRY OR WHERE THE MAIN LIVELIHOOD IS
       AGRICULTURAL IN NATURE, THE APPOINTMENT OF A CITY
       OR MUNICIPAL AGRICULTURIST SHALL BE MANDATORY.”

        SEC. 2. Implementing Agency. – The Department of Interior and Local
Government (DILG) and the Department of Agriculture (DA), in coordination with
local government units, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the
effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 3. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act shall be included in the general appropriations Act of the
year following its enactment.

     SEC. 4. Repealing Clause. – Other relevant provisions of RA No. 7160, as
amended, insofar as the appointment of agriculturist is concerned, and any law,

                                           1
decree, executive order, and rules and regulations, or portions thereof not consistent

       SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – Should any part or provisions of this Act be

thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

      SEC. 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following

general circulation in the Philippines.
