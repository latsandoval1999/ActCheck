                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

 1           The Seventh District of Cebu is rich in natural resources, coastal areas, forests,
 2   rivers, and tourism sites that provide livelihood and economic opportunities to its
 3   people. However, the district continues to face growing environmental problems such
 4   as improper waste disposal, pollution, coastal and watershed degradation, climate
 5   change, and environmental hazards.

 6           At present, local government units in the district have limited manpower,
 7   technical support, and resources to effectively address these environmental concerns.
 8   Existing environmental offices and agencies also cover large areas, making it difficult
 9   to provide immediate and focused attention to the specific needs of the Seventh
10   District.

11         The creation of a Community Environment and Natural Resources Office
12   (CENRO) in the Municipality of Badian seeks to strengthen environmental protection
13   and management in the district. The proposed office will help improve the enforcement
14   of environmental laws, coordination among local government units and national
15   agencies, protection of natural resources, solid waste management, climate change
16   adaptation, and disaster risk reduction efforts.

17          This measure will also support sustainable tourism, protect the livelihood of
18   local communities, preserve natural resources for future generations, and promote a
19   cleaner, safer, and healthier environment for the people of the Seventh District of
20   Cebu.

21          In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 3       AN ACT CREATING A COMMUNITY ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL
 4   RESOURCES OFFICE (CENRO) IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BADIAN, PROVINCE
 5     OF CEBU, TO CATER TO ENVIRONMENTAL AND NATURAL RESOURCES
 6    MATTERS IN THE 7TH DISTRICT OF CEBU, PROVIDING FOR ITS MANDATE,
 7   POWERS, FUNCTIONS, ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE, APPROPRIATIONS,
 8                       AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

 9   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
10   Congress assembled:

11   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Community Environment
12   and Natural Resources Office for the Seventh District of Cebu Act.”
13
14   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to:

15      1. Protect and advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology
16         in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature, pursuant to Section 16, Article
17         II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution;
18      2. Promote sustainable development, environmental integrity, and the rational and
19         equitable utilization of natural resources;
20      3. Strengthen local government units (LGUs), particularly municipalities, as
21         frontliners in environmental protection and natural resources management;
22      4. Support and enhance the capacity of the Municipality of Badian and the
23         municipalities within the 7th District of Cebu to efficiently and effectively address
24         environmental concerns within their jurisdiction; and
25      5. Encourage coordination among LGUs and national government agencies in
26         addressing environmental issues that transcend local boundaries, particularly
27         those affecting the 7th District of Cebu.

28   SEC. 3. Creation of the Community Environment and Natural Resources Office
29   (CENRO) of the Municipality of Badian. - There is hereby created a Community

 1   Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) in the Municipality of Badian,
 2   Province of Cebu, hereinafter referred to as the Office.

 3   The Office shall be a regular, permanent office stationed in the Municipality of Badian
 4   and shall serve as the primary office responsible for the formulation, implementation,
 5   coordination, and monitoring of environmental and natural resources management
 6   programs, projects, and activities within the 7th District of Cebu, and for such other
 7   functions as may be provided in this Act.

 8   SEC. 4. Mandate and General Functions. - The CENRO of the 7th District of Cebu
 9   shall have the following general mandate and functions:

10      1. Formulate and recommend to the LGU concerned the policies, plans,
11         programs, and strategies for environmental protection, preservation,
12         rehabilitation, and sustainable natural resources management within the 7th
13         District of Cebu;
14      2. Implement and assist in the enforcement of environmental laws, rules, and
15         regulations, and local ordinances within the territorial jurisdiction of Cebu’s 7th
16         District, in coordination with the Department of Environment and Natural
17         Resources (DENR) and other competent agencies;
18      3. Coordinate and collaborate, as may be allowed by law, with national
19         government agencies, the Provincial Government of Cebu, and neighboring
20         LGUs within the 7th District of Cebu on environmental programs and projects
21         of mutual concern; and
22      4. Undertake such other related functions as may be necessary to achieve the
23         objectives of this Act.

24   SEC. 5. Specific Powers and Functions. - The Office shall have the following
25   specific powers and functions, without prejudice to other functions that may be
26   assigned by law or ordinance:

27   (a) Planning and Policy Development

28      1. Prepare, update, and implement an Environmental Management Plan covering,
29         among others, solid waste management, air and water quality, land use and
30         green spaces, climate change adaptation and mitigation, disaster risk reduction
31         as it relates to the environment, and coastal and marine resources
32         management;
33      2. Recommend to the Sangguniang Bayan concerned the enactment of
34         ordinances and resolutions on environmental protection, conservation,
35         rehabilitation, and sustainable use of natural resources; and
36      3. Ensure that environmental considerations are integrated into the Local
37         Government Unit (LGU) development plans, land use plans, and investment
38         programs.

39   (b) Implementation, Regulation, and Enforcement

 1      1. Assist in the enforcement of, and promote compliance with, national
 2         environmental laws including, but not limited to, Republic Act No. 9003
 3         (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act), Republic Act No. 8749 (Philippine
 4         Clean Air Act), Republic Act No. 9275 (Philippine Clean Water Act), Republic
 5         Act No. 9729 (Climate Change Act), and related laws and regulations;
 6      2. Conduct regular monitoring and inspections of activities, projects, and
 7         establishments within the Municipality or City that may affect environmental
 8         quality and natural resources, in coordination with appropriate national and
 9         local authorities; and
10      3. Recommend appropriate administrative or legal actions against violators of
11         environmental laws and local ordinances, subject to applicable procedures and
12         due process.

13   (c) Solid Waste Management

14      1. Lead the formulation, updating, and implementation of the Municipality’s Solid
15         Waste Management Plan in accordance with Republic Act No. 9003;
16      2. Oversee systems of waste segregation, collection, recycling, composting, and
17         proper disposal, and the establishment and operation of materials recovery
18         facilities (MRFs), sanitary landfills, or other appropriate facilities; and
19      3. Promote community-based and barangay-level solid waste management
20         initiatives.

21   (d) Natural Resources and Ecosystems Management

22      1. Plan and implement programs for the protection, rehabilitation, and sustainable
23         management of forests, watersheds, rivers, wetlands, coastal and marine
24         ecosystems, and other critical habitats within the 7th District of Cebu;
25      2. Coordinate tree-growing, reforestation, urban greening, and mangrove
26         rehabilitation initiatives; and
27      3. Support biodiversity conservation activities in coordination with relevant
28         agencies, LGUs, academic institutions, and civil society organizations.

29   (e) Environmental Education, Advocacy, and Community Participation

30      1. Conduct and support environmental awareness campaigns, trainings, and
31         capacity-building for schools, barangays, civil society, and the private sector;
32      2. Develop and disseminate Information, Education, and Communication (IEC)
33         materials on environmental laws, policies, and best practices; and
34      3. Promote multi-sectoral partnerships and citizen participation in environmental
35         programs.

36   (f) Climate Change and Disaster Risk Reduction (Environment-Related)

37      1. Integrate climate change adaptation and mitigation considerations into local
38         environmental programs and policies;
39      2. Coordinate with the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office
40         (CDRRMO) and Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office

 1         (MDRRMO) on environmental aspects of disaster risk reduction and climate
 2         resilience; and
 3      3. Develop and implement, in coordination with concerned offices, measures to
 4         reduce environmental and climate-related risks.

 5   (g) Coordination with Neighboring LGUs in the 7th District of Cebu

 6      1. Coordinate, as may be allowed by law, with neighboring LGUs within the 7th
 7         District of Cebu on inter-LGU environmental concerns such as shared
 8         watersheds, rivers, air sheds, and coastal and marine areas;
 9      2. Participate in district- or province-wide environmental councils, committees, or
10         mechanisms where the LGUs within the 7th District of Cebu are represented;
11         and
12      3. Support initiatives of the Office of the Representative of the 7th District of Cebu
13         and the Provincial Government of Cebu that promote environmental protection
14         and sustainable natural resources management.

15   (h) Research, Monitoring, and Data Management

16      1. Maintain a database of environmental indicators, projects, programs, and
17         activities within the 7th District of Cebu;
18      2. Conduct or support research and baseline assessments on environmental
19         quality and natural resources; and
20      3. Prepare periodic environmental status reports for submission to the LGUs
21         concerned and other concerned bodies.

22   SEC. 6. Organizational Structure and Personnel.

23   (a) City Environment and Natural Resources Officer.

24   There is hereby created the position of City Environment and Natural Resources
25   Officer (City ENRO) who shall be the head of the Office.

26      1. The City ENRO shall be appointed in accordance with civil service laws, rules,
27         and regulations;
28      2. The City ENRO shall possess education, experience, and training in the fields
29         of environment, natural resources, forestry, ecology, environmental planning,
30         engineering, or related disciplines, as may be provided in the implementing
31         rules and regulations (IRR) of this Act and by the Civil Service Commission
32         (CSC).

33   (b) Staff and Divisions. - The Office shall be composed of such divisions, units, and
34   staff as may be necessary for the effective and efficient performance of its functions,
35   including but not limited to:

36      1. Planning, Research and Policy Division;
37      2. Solid Waste Management Division;
38      3. Environmental Protection and Enforcement Division;

 3   The plantilla of positions, including their qualifications, salary grades, and job
 4   descriptions, shall be determined by the Secretary of DENR, subject to existing laws,

 7   SEC. 7. Appropriations. - The Secretary of the Department of Environment and
 8   Natural Resources shall include in the Department’s program the operationalization of
 9   the CENRO office for the 7th District of Cebu to be stationed at the Municipality of
10   Badian, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
11   Act.
12
13   SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
14   effectivity of this Act, the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR),
15   in coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), the
16   DBM, and the CSC, shall promulgate the necessary Implementing Rules and
17   Regulations (IRR) to effectively carry out the provisions of this Act.
18

20   unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
21   continue to be in full force and effect.
22

23   SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
24   regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof that are inconsistent with the
