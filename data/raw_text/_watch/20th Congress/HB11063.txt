                                          AN ACT
                        UPGRADING THE SALARY GRADE OF BOOKKEEPERS
                              IN SCHOOL IMPLEMENTING UNITS

             Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 1          SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “DepEd Bookkeepers Salary
 2   Modification Act.”

 3            SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is a policy of the State to promote full employment, a
 4   rising standard of living, and an improved quality of life for all. Furthermore, it is also the policy of State
 5   to ensure the right of all citizens to quality education, which is achieved through classroom teaching
 6   anchored on the harmonious administration of the school, and its proper financial management.

 7           To this end, the State shall implement just and equitable measures to ensure that the
 8   remuneration of bookkeepers employed in the Department of Education (DepEd) are commensurate
 9   with their workload.

10           SEC. 3. Creation of New Plantilla Positions. – For purposes of this Act, the Department
11   of Budget and Management (DBM) shall, in consultation with the DepEd, the Civil Service Commission
12   (CSC), and relevant professional organizations, create the following new Plantilla positions in the
13   DepEd and include the same in the revised compensation and position classification system, and index
14   of occupational services, occupational groups, classes, and salary grades:

                       Plantilla Position Title                        Salary Grade
                        School Finance Assistant                               9
                       School Finance Specialist I                            11
                       School Finance Specialist II                           13
                      School Finance Specialist III                           16
15   The positions Administrative Assistant III (Senior Bookkeeper) and Accountant I shall be converted or
16   reclassified, or both, to the pertinent newly created positions. The DepEd, DBM, and CSC shall
17   determine the eligibility and qualifications of the newly created positions in accordance with this Act.

18            SEC. 4. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act
19   shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of DepEd. Thereafter, such sums as may be
 1   necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General
 2   Appropriations Act (GAA).

 3           SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, resolutions,
 4   rules and regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
 5   repealed or amended accordingly.

 6           SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – Should any provision of this Act or any part hereof be
 7   declared unconstitutional, the other provisions or parts not affected thereby shall remain in force and
 8   effect.

 9           SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its publication
10   in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

11           Approved,
