20                                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   Chronic kidney disease (CKD) remains a significant public health concern in the
23   Philippines, with thousands of patients requiring regular dialysis to survive. However,
24   access to dialysis services remains limited, particularly in public hospitals outside major
25   urban centers, and the high cost of treatment continues to impose a heavy financial
26   burden on patients and their families¹.
27
28   This bill seeks to require all national, regional, and provincial government hospitals to
29   establish and maintain dialysis wards or units, ensuring that life-saving treatment
30   becomes more accessible across the country. It further mandates the provision of free
31   dialysis treatment to indigent patients, recognizing the State’s duty to make healthcare
32   services available, accessible, and affordable to all.
33
34   By strengthening the capacity of government hospitals and expanding access to
35   essential health services, this measure supports the implementation of the Universal
36   Health Care Act (Republic Act No. 11223) and promotes equitable health outcomes,
37   particularly for vulnerable sectors.
38
39   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

22                                AN ACТ
23   REQUIRING ALL NATIONAL, REGIONAL, AND PROVINCIAL GOVERNMENT
24   HOSPITALS TO ESTABLISH, OPERATE, AND MAINTAIN A DIALYSIS WARD OR
25   UNIT IN THEIR RESPECTIVE HOSPITAL AND PROVIDING FREE DIALYSIS
26   TREATMENT TO INDIGENT PATIENTS
27
28   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
29   Congress assembled:
30
31
32   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Dialysis Center Act”.
33
34   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the declared policy of the State to improve the
35   delivery of health care services to the people and to ensure hospital facilities are
36   available, affordable and accessible to the people.
37
38   SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act, the following terms shall mean:
39
40            (a) "National Government Hospital" shall refer to a hospital operated and
41                maintained either partially or wholly by the national government or by any
42                department, division, board or other agency thereof;
43            (b) "Regional Government Hospital" shall refer to a hospital operated and
44                maintained either partially or wholly by the national government or by any
45                department, division, board or other agency thereof;
46            (c) "Provincial Government Hospital" shall refer to a hospital operated and
47                maintained either partially or wholly by the provincial government or other
48                political subdivision, or by any department, division, board or agency thereof;
49                and
50            (d) "Indigent Patient" shall refer to a patient who has no visible means of income,
51                or whose income is insufficient for the subsistence of his/her family, as
 1               assessed by the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD),
 2               local government social worker, Municipał Social Worker of the health
 3               facility, or a social worker of any government entity providing medical and
 4               financial assistance.

 5   SEC. 4. Establishment, Operation and Maintenance of a Dialysis Ward or Unit. -
 6   Within two (2) years from the effectivity of this Act, all national, regional, and provincial
 7   government hospitals are hereby required to establish, operate and maintain a dialysis
 8   ward or unit in their hospital. The dialysis ward or unit shall be equipped with complete
 9   dialysis machine, equipment, and supplies.
10
11   SEC. 5. Free Dialysis Treatment to Indigent Patients. - Dialysis treatment in all
12   national, regional, and provincial government hospitals shall be provided free of charge
13   to indigent patients as defined in Section 3(d) hereof.
14
15   SEC. 6. Penalty. - Any hospital chief, administrator or officer-in-charge who fails to
16   comply with this Act shall be punished with a fine of Fifty Thousand Pesos (PHP
17   50,000.00), but not more than One Hundred Thousand Pesos (PHP 100,000.00).
18
19   SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60) days upon the
20   effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health (DOH) shall promulgate the necessary
21   rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this Act.
22
23   SEC. 8. Appropriations. - The sum necessary for the effective implementation of this
24   Act shall be taken from the current applicable appropriations of the Department of
25   Health (DOH) and overall savings of the National Government. Thereafter, funds for its
26   continued implementation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
27
28   SEC. 9. Separability Clause. - If any provision or part hereof is held invalid or
29   unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not otherwise affected shall
30   remain valid and subsisting.
31
32   SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. - Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive
33   order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation contrary to or
34   inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, modified or amended
35   accordingly.
36
37   SEC. 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
38   at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
