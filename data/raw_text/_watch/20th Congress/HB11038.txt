                             AN ACT
CONVERTING THE MARITIME INDUSTRY AUTHORITY SATELLITE OFFICE IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF SOGOD, PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE, INTO AN
EXTENSION OFFICE AND TRANSFERRING ITS LOCATION TO THE MUNICIPALITY
OF LILOAN, PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Second Legislative District of Southern Leyte consists of coastal municipalities
that have a long history of ishing, maritime trade, and participation in the country’s
Overseas Employment Program. It is known as the “Gateway to Mindanao,” since both
Liloan Ferry Terminal and San Ricardo Port provide passenger and cargo transport to
Lipata, Surigao City.

The easing of domestic and international travel restrictions has increased inter-island
transport and maritime activity. This development has underscored the need to bring
government maritime services closer to the public, vessel owners and operators,
maritime education and training institutions, and Filipino seafarers.

The Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA) is the government agency responsible for
the regulation, promotion, and development of the Philippine maritime industry. Its
functions include the registration and licensing of vessels and the implementation of
applicable laws, rules, and standards relating to maritime safety, maritime education
and training, and the certi ication of seafarers. MARINA’s regulatory powers and
functions have been strengthened by existing law, including the transfer to MARINA
of powers relating to the issuance, validation, veri ication, correction, revocation, or

                                                                            1|Page
cancellation of certi icates and other documentary evidence required of seafarers
under the applicable provisions of Republic Act No. 10635.

At present, MARINA maintains an extension of ice in Maasin City and a satellite of ice
in the Municipality of Sogod, Province of Southern Leyte. This bill seeks to convert the
Sogod satellite of ice into an extension of ice and transfer its location to the
Municipality of Liloan.

Liloan is strategically located within the Second Legislative District of Southern Leyte.
It is home to the Liloan Ferry Terminal, which connects the Luzon-Visayas segment of
the Pan-Philippine Highway with Mindanao. It is also in proximity to the Port of San
Ricardo and the Port of Sogod and is accessible to the municipalities of Silago,
Hinunangan, Hinundayan, Anahawan, San Juan, and Saint Bernard.

The transfer and conversion of the of ice will improve access to vessel registration,
licensing, inspection, maritime safety, seafarer certi ication, and related MARINA
services. It will also enable the Authority to more effectively monitor maritime
transport operations and maritime education and training activities within its area of
responsibility.

The measure is consistent with the policy of strengthening MARINA’s regional and
local presence. Similar legislation has converted MARINA satellite of ices into
extension of ices in Maasin City, Southern Leyte, and Calbayog City, Samar.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

                             AN ACT
CONVERTING THE MARITIME INDUSTRY AUTHORITY SATELLITE OFFICE IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF SOGOD, PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE, INTO AN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Conversion and Transfer. — The existing Maritime Industry Authority
(MARINA) Satellite Of ice located in the Municipality of Sogod, Province of Southern
Leyte, is hereby converted into a regular MARINA Extension Of ice and its location is
hereby transferred to the Municipality of Liloan, Province of Southern Leyte.

The MARINA Extension Of ice in Liloan shall provide services to the residents, vessel
owners and operators, maritime education and training institutions, and seafarers of
the Province of Southern Leyte and, subject to the determination of MARINA, other
nearby provinces and municipalities.

SECTION 2. Powers and Functions. — The MARINA Extension Of ice in Liloan shall
perform such powers, functions, and duties as may be delegated by the MARINA
Administrator pursuant to existing laws, rules, and regulations.




                                                                           3|Page
It shall assist in the delivery of MARINA services, including vessel registration and
licensing, maritime safety regulation, inspection, seafarer certi ication, and other
related services within its area of responsibility.

SECTION 3. Operationalization. — The MARINA Administrator shall immediately
include in the programs of the Authority the operationalization of the MARINA
Extension Of ice in Liloan, Province of Southern Leyte.

For this purpose, MARINA shall determine the appropriate organizational structure,
staf ing pattern, personnel complement, equipment, facilities, and other
requirements of the Extension Of ice, subject to existing laws, rules, and regulations
and to the approval of the appropriate government agencies.

SECTION 4. Transfer of Personnel, Properties, and Records. — Upon the
operationalization of the MARINA Extension Of ice in Liloan, the personnel, records,
equipment, supplies, appropriations, funds, and other properties of the MARINA
Satellite Of ice in Sogod may be transferred, reallocated, or otherwise appropriately
utilized for the Extension Of ice, subject to existing civil service, budgeting,
accounting, auditing, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

No personnel shall be transferred or separated except in accordance with existing
laws and applicable civil service rules and regulations.

SEC. 5. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be charged against the current appropriations of MARINA. Thereafter, the
funding necessary for the continued operation and maintenance of the MARINA
Extension Of ice in Liloan shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the MARINA Administrator shall promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary for its effective implementation.

SEC. 7. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force
and effect.

SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.
