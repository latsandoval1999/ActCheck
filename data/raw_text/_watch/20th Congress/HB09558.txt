                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The youth are among the nation's most valuable assets and they play a
vital role in shaping the country's future. Developing competent, ethical, and
service-oriented young leaders is essential to strengthening democratic
institutions, promoting civic engagement, and fostering responsible citizenship.

      Despite the presence of student governments and youth organizations
across the country, opportunities for structured leadership development remain
uneven and often inaccessible, particularly in underserved regions. There is a
need to institutionalize a comprehensive leadership and nation-building program
that equips young Filipinos with the knowledge, skills, values, and commitment
necessary for public service and community development.

      This bill seeks to establish Regional Youth Leadership and Nation-Building
Training Centers in all regions of the country to provide accessible and sustained
leadership education, civic training, governance awareness, and community
engagement opportunities for student leaders and young Filipinos. Through
partnerships among government agencies, educational institutions, civil society
organizations, and the private sector, the program aims to cultivate a new
generation of responsible leaders dedicated to nation-building and inclusive
development.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                             “AN ACT
      ESTABLISHING REGIONAL YOUTH LEADERSHIP AND NATION-
      BUILDING TRAINING CENTERS FOR STUDENT LEADERS IN ALL
      REGIONS OF THE COUNTRY, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

        Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
 Philippines in Congress assembled:

                                CHAPTER1
                            GENERAL PROVISIONS

     SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Youth
Leadership and Nation-Building Training Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to foster
the development of responsible, ethical, and service-oriented youth leaders.
Recognizing the role of young people in shaping the nation’s future, the State
shall provide structured and accessible leadership training focused on democratic
values, civic responsibility, nation-building, and good governance. The State
shall promote youth participation in nation-building, democratic governance,
volunteerism, social responsibility, and community development, and shall
ensure equal access to leadership development opportunities for all sectors of
the youth.

   SECTION 3. Objectives. – This Act aims to:

   a) Establish youth leadership and nation-building training centers in all
      regions of the Philippines.

   b) Develop a generation of student leaders who embody ethical leadership,
      critical thinking, and a commitment to public service.

   c) Promote civic education, democratic participation, and youth involvement
      in governance.
   d) Institutionalize regular leadership training programs in partnership with
      schools, government, and civil society organizations.

   e) Promote volunteerism and active citizenship among Filipino youth.

   f) Strengthen youth      participation   in public    affairs   and   community
      development.

   g) Develop future leaders committed to integrity, accountability, and national
      development.

      SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

   (a) Youth refers to persons aged fifteen (15) to thirty (30) years, consistent
       with the provisions of Republic Act No. 8044, as amended.

   (b) Student Leader refers to any elected or appointed officer of a recognized
       student government, student council, campus publication, student
       organization, or youth organization in an educational institution.

   (c) Youth Leader refers to a young person actively engaged in community,
       civic, volunteer, cultural, environmental, entrepreneurial, or public service
       initiatives and duly endorsed by a local government unit, educational
       institution, or recognized youth organization.

   (d) Center refers to a Regional Youth Leadership and Nation-Building
       Training Center established pursuant to this Act.

   (e) Nation-Building Training Program refers to the leadership
       development, civic education, governance, volunteerism, and community
       engagement activities conducted under this Act.

      SECTION 5. Establishment, Functions, and Program Components of
the Regional Youth Leadership and Nation-Building Training Centers. -
There shall be established Regional Youth Leadership and Nation-Building
Training Centers in all regions of the country under the overall policy
coordination of the National Youth Commission (NYC), in partnership with the
Department of Education (DepEd), the Commission on Higher Education
(CHED), the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), and other
concerned agencies.

      Existing government training facilities, state universities and colleges,
public educational institutions, or other suitable government properties may be
designated as Regional Youth Leadership and Nation-Building Training Centers
whenever practicable.
     The Centers shall serve as centers for leadership development, civic
education, and nation-building initiatives and shall:

  (a) Conduct capacity-building programs for elementary, secondary, tertiary,
      and out-of-school youth participants through age-appropriate and
      developmentally responsive learning modules.

  (b) Organize seminars, workshops, conferences, and training activities on
      leadership, citizenship, governance, public service, volunteerism, and
      community development.

  (c) Facilitate inter-school, inter-regional, and multi-sectoral collaboration
      among youth leaders and organizations.

  (d) Provide mentoring, leadership immersion, simulation exercises, and
      experiential learning opportunities.

  (e) Support youth-led community projects, innovation initiatives, and social
      development programs.

  The training curriculum shall include, but not be limited to:

    1.  Responsible and Ethical Leadership;
    2.  Civic Responsibility and Democratic Participation;
    3.  Philippine History, Constitution, Human Rights, and Rule of Law;
    4.  Nation-Building, Peacebuilding, and Social Cohesion;
    5.  Public Speaking, Communication, and Advocacy;
    6.  Project Development, Innovation, and Social Entrepreneurship;
    7.  Anti-Corruption and Good Governance;
    8.  Community Organizing and Volunteerism;
    9.  Environmental Stewardship, Climate Action, and Sustainable
        Development; and
    10. Digital Citizenship, Media Literacy, and Responsible Use of Technology.

    SECTION 6. Eligibility and Participation. - The program shall be open to:

    (a) Student government leaders from public and private
        schools and universities.

    (b) Officers and members of duly recognized youth organizations.

    (c) Young leaders nominated by their local government units
        (LGUs), NGOs, or community organizations.

    (d) Out-of-school youth, indigenous youth, youth with disabilities,
        Muslim youth, and youth from geographically isolated and
         disadvantaged areas (GIDAs), subject to guidelines issued by the NYC.

    SECTION 7. Role of the National Youth Commission. - The NYC shall:

    (a) Serve as the lead coordinating and policy-making agency for the
        implementation of this Act.

    (b) Work with DepEd, CHED, and the Department of the Interior and
        Local Government (DILG) to implement regional programs.

    (c) Provide training modules, facilitator guidelines, and certification;

    (d) Conduct national leadership         summits     and    recognition     for
        outstanding youth leaders.

    (e) Establish standards, accreditation systems, and              evaluation
        mechanisms for the Centers and training programs.

    (f) Monitor and assess program implementation and submit annual
        reports to Congress.

        SECTION 8. Partnerships and Support, - The centers shall collaborate
with:

    (a) Local government units (LGUs);
    (b) Civil society organizations and youth NGOs;
    (c) Leadership institutes and training providers;
    (d) The private sector, especially those with youth empowerment programs.
    (e) State universities and colleges (SUCs);
    (f) Professional organizations and academic institutions;
    (g) International development organizations and institutions, subject to
         existing laws and regulations.

     Such partnerships may include sponsorships, mentorship, logistical
 support, and access to facilities.

      The Centers may enter into memoranda of agreement with partner
 institutions for the provision of training facilities, resource persons, technical
 assistance, scholarships, internships, and other forms of support.

      SECTION 9. Integration with National Youth Commission Programs.
 – The Centers shall coordinate with the National Youth Commission to ensure
 that leadership development activities, policy consultations, and youth
 representation initiatives are aligned with existing programs, including the
 National Youth Parliament and other youth participation mechanisms
established under law.

     SECTION 10. Monitoring and Evaluation. - The NYC shall establish a
monitoring and evaluation system to assess the effectiveness, participation
rates, outcomes, and impact of the programs established under this Act and
shall submit an annual report to Congress.

     SECTION 11. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to implement the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act, under the budgets of DepEd, CHED, and the NYC. Additional funding may
be sourced from donations, partnerships, and development grants.

     SECTION 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety
(90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DepEd, CHED, and NYC, in
consultation with the DILG and other stakeholders, shall issue the
Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).

     SECTION 13. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act
is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected
thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

     SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders or issuances
or any part thereof, which are inconsistent herewith, are hereby repealed or
amended accordingly.

     SECTION 15. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.

    Approved.
