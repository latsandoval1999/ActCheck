                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

              Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) affects many Filipino individuals
      and families who continue to face significant challenges in accessing
      timely developmental screening, diagnosis, intervention, therapy,
      healthcare, education, and community support services. These
      challenges are compounded by the limited availability of specialized
      facilities and trained professionals, financial barriers, and unequal
      access to developmental services, particularly in underserved and
      geographically disadvantaged areas.

            This proposed measure seeks to establish a nationwide network
      of accessible and affordable Autism Centers that will serve as regional
      hubs for developmental screening, diagnostic assessment, intervention,
      rehabilitation, family support, educational coordination, and referral
      services. It likewise seeks to strengthen early detection and intervention
      by integrating developmental screening into existing health and
      community-based programs and by training frontline healthcare,
      education, and community personnel to recognize developmental
      concerns and facilitate timely referrals.

            The measure further promotes inclusive education and
      community support, strengthens the capacity of healthcare
      professionals, therapists, educators, and other service providers, and
      encourages research and data-driven policymaking to improve the
      availability, quality, accessibility, and affordability of autism-related
      services.

            Recognizing that timely and continuous support is essential to
      improving outcomes, the bill provides for coordinated referral
      mechanisms among Autism Centers and relevant healthcare, education,
      social welfare, skills training, and employment institutions. It also
      encourages the expansion of appropriate health coverage and financial
      assistance for indigent and financially vulnerable families.
      Consistent with Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the
“Magna Carta for Persons with Disability,” as amended, Republic Act
No. 11036, otherwise known as the “Mental Health Act,” Republic Act
No. 11223, otherwise known as the “Universal Health Care Act,”
Republic Act No. 11650, otherwise known as the “Instituting a Policy of
Inclusion and Services for Learners with Disabilities in Support of
Inclusive Education Act,” and other relevant laws, this measure adopts
a rights-based, inclusive, family-centered, community-based, and
evidence-informed approach to autism care and support.

      By establishing accessible Autism Centers and strengthening
early detection, referral, intervention, and support systems nationwide,
this measure seeks to reduce disparities in access to services and enable
persons with ASD and their families to receive timely, appropriate, and
continuing support regardless of their place of residence or economic
circumstances.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
earnestly sought.

                               “AN ACT
            ESTABLISHING  AUTISM    CENTERS  AND    EARLY
            DETECTION PROGRAMS NATIONWIDE, PROVIDING
            FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

             Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
      Philippines in Congress assembled:

            SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the
      “National Autism Care and Support Act.”

             SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the
      policy of the State to protect and promote the rights, dignity, inclusion,
      and well-being of persons with disabilities, including persons with
      Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), consistent with the Constitution,
      Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the “Magna Carta for
      Persons with Disability,” as amended, Republic Act No. 11036, otherwise
      known as the “Mental Health Act,” Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise
      known as the “Universal Health Care Act,” Republic Act No. 11650,
      otherwise known as the “Instituting a Policy of Inclusion and Services
      for Learners with Disabilities in Support of Inclusive Education Act,” and
      other relevant laws.

             The State shall adopt a comprehensive, inclusive, family-centered,
      community-based, and evidence-informed approach to autism care and
      support through accessible screening, early detection, intervention,
      healthcare, education, rehabilitation, skills development, employment
      support, and social inclusion programs for persons with ASD and their
      families.

             SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act, the
      following terms are hereby defined:
     a) “Autism     Spectrum     Disorder      (ASD)”     refers   to    a
        neurodevelopmental condition characterized by persistent
        differences in social communication and social interaction,
        patterns of behavior, interests, or activities, sensory processing
        differences, and varying levels of support needs;

     b) “Autism Centers” refer to specialized facilities or designated
        units within healthcare institutions that provide developmental
        screening,    diagnostic    assessment,     referral,   therapy,
        rehabilitation, family support, educational coordination, and
        community-based services for persons with ASD;

     c) “Early Detection and Intervention Program” refers to a
        coordinated system of developmental screening, assessment,
        referral, and evidence-informed intervention and support
        services designed to identify developmental concerns and
        facilitate timely access to appropriate services;

     d) “Persons with Autism Spectrum Disorder” refer to individuals
        diagnosed with ASD who may require varying levels of support
        and services; and

     e) “Individualized Education Plan (IEP)” refers to a learner-
        centered educational support plan designed to address the
        specific developmental, behavioral, communication, and
        learning needs of a learner with ASD, consistent with
        applicable laws, policies, and regulations on inclusive
        education.

      SECTION 4. Establishment of Regional Autism Centers. - The
Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with local government
units (LGUs), government hospitals, state universities and colleges
(SUCs), and qualified and accredited non-government organizations
(NGOs), shall establish or designate at least one (1) Autism Center in
every region.

      Priority shall be given to underserved and geographically
disadvantaged    areas,  including geographically isolated and
disadvantaged areas (GIDAs).

      The Autism Centers shall provide accessible and affordable
services, including:

     a) Developmental screening, diagnostic assessment, and referral
        services;
      b) Occupational therapy, speech and language therapy,
         behavioral and developmental interventions, physical therapy,
         psychosocial support, and other appropriate interventions;

      c) Parent, caregiver, and family counseling, education, and
         training;

      d) Educational assessment and IEP support in coordination with
         schools and the Department of Education (DepEd);

      e) Community-based rehabilitation and support services; and

      f)   Referral and coordination with healthcare providers, social
           welfare agencies, educational institutions, skills training
           institutions, and employment support programs.

      The DOH may accredit existing qualified public or private facilities
as Autism Centers, subject to standards, requirements, and
accreditation guidelines established pursuant to this Act.

     The DOH shall establish a referral and coordination mechanism
among Autism Centers to facilitate continuity of care and access to
appropriate services.

      SECTION 5. National Early Detection and Intervention
Program. - The DOH, in coordination with the DepEd, Department of
Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), LGUs, and other relevant
agencies, shall establish a nationwide Early Detection and Intervention
Program for ASD.

      The Program shall:

      a) Integrate developmental screening and, when appropriate,
         autism-related screening or risk assessment into existing
         maternal, child health, and other appropriate public health
         programs;

      b) Train    barangay    health   workers,     nurses, midwives,
         pediatricians, teachers, daycare workers, and other
         community-based healthcare and education personnel to
         recognize developmental delays and indicators associated with
         ASD and to facilitate appropriate referrals;
      c) Establish clear and accessible referral pathways for children
         requiring further developmental assessment, diagnosis,
         intervention, or support;
      d) Facilitate timely access to evidence-informed interventions and
         support services, particularly for children in underserved and
         geographically disadvantaged areas; and

      e) Conduct nationwide information, education, communication,
         and anti-stigma campaigns promoting autism awareness,
         inclusion, early detection, and access to appropriate services.

       Developmental screening under this Act shall respect parental
participation, informed consent, child rights, and applicable privacy and
confidentiality laws.

       SECTION 6. Inclusive Education and Community Support. -
Consistent with Republic Act No. 11650 and other applicable laws, rules,
and regulations, the DepEd, in coordination with the DOH, Commission
on Higher Education (CHED), Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (TESDA), LGUs, and other relevant agencies,
shall strengthen inclusive education and community support systems
for learners and individuals with ASD by:

      a) Expanding access to Special Education (SPED) and inclusive
         education programs;

      b) Providing training for teachers, guidance counselors, and
         school   personnel    on   autism    inclusion, classroom
         accommodations, and learner support strategies;

      c) Developing inclusive learning      materials   and   reasonable
         accommodation guidelines;

      d) Supporting transition programs for adolescents and young
         adults with ASD toward higher education, skills training,
         employment, and independent living; and

      e) Promoting autism-friendly schools, workplaces, and public
         spaces.

      LGUs and communities are encouraged to establish support
networks and inclusive community programs for persons with ASD and
their families and to coordinate with Autism Centers in providing
appropriate referral and support services.

      SECTION 7. Capacity Building and Workforce Development. -
The DOH, Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), CHED, TESDA,
and relevant professional organizations shall:
      a) Develop and strengthen training, certification, fellowship, and
         continuing professional education programs related to autism
         care, developmental services, and            evidence-informed
         interventions;

      b) Encourage the expansion of developmental and behavioral
         healthcare specialization and training programs;

      c) Provide or facilitate scholarships, grants, incentives, or return-
         service programs for healthcare workers, therapists,
         educators, and other qualified specialists who serve or commit
         to serving underserved and geographically disadvantaged
         areas; and

      d) Promote interdisciplinary and community-based approaches
         to autism care and support.

      SECTION 8. Research, Data Management, and Policy
Development. - The DOH, in coordination with the Philippine Council
for Health Research and Development (PCHRD), academic institutions,
and relevant government agencies, shall:

      a) Establish and maintain a national autism database and
         information system for program planning, service delivery,
         monitoring, and research, consistent with Republic Act No.
         10173, otherwise known as the ‘Data Privacy Act of 2012,’ and
         other applicable laws;

      b) Support research on autism prevalence, early detection,
         intervention outcomes, inclusive education, mental health,
         employment, and family and community support systems;

      c) Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness, accessibility,
         affordability, and regional coverage of autism-related services
         and programs; and

      d) Develop evidence-informed policies and recommendations to
         improve autism care, inclusion, and support.

      Personal information collected and processed pursuant to this Act
shall be protected in accordance with applicable data privacy,
confidentiality, and child protection laws.
      SECTION 9. Financial Accessibility and Health Coverage. - The
DOH and the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) shall
endeavor to improve access to affordable developmental, diagnostic,
therapeutic, rehabilitative, and appropriate mental health services for
persons with ASD, consistent with existing laws and available resources.

       The government shall encourage the inclusion and expansion of
appropriate ASD-related benefit packages under PhilHealth and other
public healthcare financing mechanisms, subject to applicable laws,
rules, regulations, and actuarial requirements.

      Indigent and financially vulnerable families shall be prioritized for
subsidized services and other appropriate forms of government
assistance.

     SECTION 10. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual
appropriations of the DOH and participating agencies under the General
Appropriations Act.

     LGUs may allocate counterpart funding, facilities, personnel,
equipment, and logistical support for the implementation of this Act.

       The Program may also receive grants, donations, and development
assistance from lawful domestic or international sources, subject to
existing laws, rules, and regulations.

     SECTION 11. Monitoring, Evaluation, and Accountability. -
The DOH shall establish a monitoring and evaluation framework for the
implementation of this Act and shall submit an annual report to
Congress and the Office of the President containing:

      a) The number and geographic distribution of Autism Centers
         established or accredited;

      b) The coverage of developmental screening, diagnostic referral,
         intervention, and support services;

      c) The availability and geographic distribution of trained
         professionals and support personnel;

      d) Budget utilization and program outcomes;

      e) The accessibility and affordability of services; and

      f)   Challenges encountered, recommendations, and proposed
           policy improvements.
      Periodic independent program reviews may likewise be conducted
to assess the effectiveness and impact of the programs established under
this Act.

      SECTION 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within
ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOH, in coordination
with the DepEd, DSWD, CHED, TESDA, PRC, PhilHealth, LGUs, autism
advocacy organizations, healthcare professionals, educators, and other
relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary implementing
rules and regulations to carry out the provisions of this Act.

       SECTION 13. Separability Clause. - If any provision or part of
this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions
not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive
orders, rules, and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 15. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of
general circulation.

      Approved.
