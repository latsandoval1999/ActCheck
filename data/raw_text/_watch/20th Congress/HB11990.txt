                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

     “Let the elders who rule well be counted worthy of double honor, especially
     those who labor in the word and doctrine.”
                                                          — 1 Timothy 5:17

     “And we urge you, brethren, to recognize those who labor among you, and
     are over you in the Lord and admonish you, and to esteem them very highly
     in love for their work's sake. Be at peace among yourselves.”
                                                 — 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13

         The knowledge of God and the awareness concerning spiritual life is very
central to the Filipinos. No less than the Preamble of the 1987 Constitution affirms our
belief in the all-powerful God when it acknowledges that the sovereign Filipino people
implores “the aid of Almighty God.” Article III, Section 5 also guarantees the religious
freedom of the people when it states that “No law shall be made respecting an
establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof. The free exercise
and enjoyment of religious profession and worship, without discrimination or
preference, shall forever be allowed. No religious test shall be required for the exercise
of civil or political rights.”

       Consequently, the State puts premium on the moral and spiritual development of
the Filipino people. Article II, Section 13 of the Constitution states that “the State
recognizes the vital role of the youth in nation-building and shall promote and protect
their physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual, and social well-being.” Further, it mandates
all educational institutions to strengthen the ethical and spiritual values, and develop
the moral character and personal discipline of the Filipino youth (Article XIV, Section
3). These Constitutional provisions reflect the enduring place of faith in the life of the
Filipino people.

     Surely, the State and the society as a whole directly benefit when the people
possess strong spiritual and moral values. With a spiritually nourished people, public
order and the rule of law are greatly improved because crimes and other socially
destructive behaviors are minimized. In addition, a strong spiritual well-being of a

person correlates with positive personal traits such empathy, gratitude, willingness to
help others, honesty, sense of responsibility, respect for others, and resilience in time
of life distress. These values translate into a healthy social capital. This is where the
role of our spiritual ministers or those recognized leaders who provide spiritual
guidance, conduct religious services, and care for the emotional and moral needs of
individuals or congregations – such as pastors, priests, chaplains, imams,
missionaries, and other religious ministers – proves to be essential in nation building.

      Spiritual ministers play a significant role in shaping the spiritual, moral, and
ethical fabric of the nation. They are regarded as agents of change – inspiring people
towards positive personal and societal transformation. They do not only serve as
spiritual guides but also as counselors, educators, peacemakers, and, sometimes, first
responders in times of crisis. They console the grieving, care for the poor and the sick,
mediate disputes, and nurture the moral and ethical values that sustain families and
communities. Their labor is often rendered with little material reward and largely
without public acknowledgment.

      The concept of an appreciation day for our spiritual ministers has been practiced
in various countries around the world. These celebrations are generally held during
the month of October. Congregations and religious communities set aside occasions
to honor the dedication and hard work of their spiritual ministers. These practices and
traditions, however, remain informal.

      This bill seeks to establish and institutionalize a simple day of appreciation of
spiritual ministers for their profound contribution to nation building. It seeks to declare
the first Sunday of the month of October of every year as Spiritual Ministers
Appreciation Day. In doing so, the measure affirms the State’s recognition of spiritual
ministers as partners in nation-building and hopes to provide a boost of inspiration to
them in further rearing the spiritual, moral and ethical well-being of our people.

      The measure does not favor any particular faith, church or denomination, but
extends recognition to ministers of all religions alike, consistent with the constitutional
guarantee of religious freedom without discrimination or preference. A special day of
appreciation is envisioned to encourage Filipinos to acknowledge the sacrifices of
those who have abandoned their earthly dreams and ambitions and devoted their lives
to help nourish and revive the spiritual lives of others. It aims to strengthen the bonds
between religious ministers and the broader communities they serve, thus reaffirming
the value our nation places on faith, service, and moral leadership as foundations of a
just and humane society.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
DECLARING EVERY FIRST SUNDAY OF OCTOBER OF EACH YEAR AS THE
SPIRITUAL MINISTERS APPRECIATION DAY

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

    SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Spiritual Ministers
Appreciation Day.”

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is a declared policy of the State that the
government shall aid and encourage the development of the moral and spiritual
foundation of the Filipino people. In this regard, it acknowledges the valuable role and
contribution of spiritual ministers in nourishing and shaping the spiritual, moral, and
ethical well-being of the people.

     SEC. 3. Spiritual Ministers Appreciation Day. – Every first Sunday of October
of each year is hereby declared as the Spiritual Ministers Appreciation Day.

      SEC. 4. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

     Approved,
