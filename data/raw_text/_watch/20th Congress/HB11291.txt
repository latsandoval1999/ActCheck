                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to restore the National Food Authority’s (NFA) authority to
perform market regulation and price stabilization functions. It amends Republic Act
(RA) No. 8178, otherwise known as the “Agricultural Tariffication Act”, and
authorizes the NFA to procure palay directly from local farmers at competitive
support prices and regulate rice warehouses and grain business operators to deter
hoarding, price manipulation, and the circulation of smuggled rice.

       Agriculture remains a vital pillar of the Philippine economy, yet Filipino rice
farmers continue to face depressed farmgate prices, rising production costs, and
increasing competition from imported rice. While RA No. 11203, otherwise known
as the “Rice Tariffication Law”, liberalized rice importation to stabilize consumer
prices, its implementation has also diminished the regulatory functions of the NFA.
Consequently, many small-scale farmers remain vulnerable to market volatility,
while consumers continue to experience fluctuations in rice prices.

        The bill expands direct input subsidies under the Rice Competitiveness
Enhancement Fund, supports crop insurance and community-based post-harvest
facilities, and ensures the meaningful participation of farmers’ organizations and
civil society organizations in the formulation of rice industry policies. It further
authorizes the Department of Agriculture to suspend the issuance of rice import
clearances during peak harvest periods, following mandatory consultation with
farmer representatives, to prevent the artificial depression of farmgate prices.

      Restoring the NFA’s regulatory mandate and expanding direct support to
farmers shall contribute to a more resilient, competitive, and sustainable rice
industry.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.
