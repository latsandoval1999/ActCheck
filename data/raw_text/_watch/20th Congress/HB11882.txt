EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to designate the Mountain Province State University (MPSU) as
the Highland Coffee University of the Philippines. It further seeks to strengthen
the university by expanding its current curricular offerings and the composition
of the Governing Board.

The highlands of Northern Luzon possess environmental conditions, indigenous
knowledge systems, agricultural traditions, and community practices that
provide a strong foundation for the development of a globally competitive
highland coffee industry. Coffee, particularly Arabica, has become an important
commodity in the Cordillera, providing livelihood opportunities while creating
potential for value addition, tourism, entrepreneurship, and rural economic
development.

Mountain Province State University (MPSU), as the premier public higher
education institution in Mountain Province, has a unique mandate and
opportunity to serve as the leading institution in highland coffee education,
research and development.

On the other hand, it is imperative to update the university’s curricular offerings
to answer to the pressing present needs of the community and to better address
the needs of the coffee industry and the agricultural sector.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

MAXIMO JR. Y. DAL

AN ACT STRENGTHENING THE MOUNTAIN PROVINCE STATE
UNIVERSITY, DESIGNATING IT AS THE HIGHLAND COFFEE
UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES, EXPANDING ITS CURRICULAR
OFFERINGS AND THE COMPOSITION OF THE GOVERNING BOARD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Designation. Mountain Province State University, hereinafter
referred to as the “University”, is hereby designated as the Highland Coffee
University, tasked with advancing education, research, and innovation in the
field of highland coffee education, research and development and related fields.

SECTION 2. General Mandate. The University shall primarily provide
advanced instruction, higher technological, professional and technical training,
in the fields of coffee science and quality, agriculture particularly coffee
production and post-harvest management, forestry, arts, humanities, science
and mathematics, business and management particularly enterprise and
entrepreneurship relating to agribusiness, cooperative management, product
branding, digital marketing, financial management and market development,
criminal justice, engineering and architecture, health, digital and information
technology, law, social sciences, teacher education and other disciplines as
provided by law. It shall also promote and undertake research, extension
services and production activities in support of the socioeconomic development
of Mountain Province and provide and nurture holistic, developmental, visionary
and transformational leadership in its areas of specialization.

SECTION 3. Purpose of the University as a Highland Coffee University. As
a Highland Coffee University, the primary purpose of MPSU, shall:

===== [page 3] =====
1. Develop and offer new degree, diploma, certificate, and competency-
based programs in areas such as coffee science and technology,
agribusiness, agricultural entrepreneurship, food technology, hospitality
and culinary arts, tourism, agricultural engineering, environmental
management, digital agriculture, business development, coffee quality
management, and other emerging disciplines relevant to the highland
economy;

2. Promote and conduct research and development (R&D) activities related
to the highland coffee industry;

3. Provide technical support to the Department of Agriculture, Department
of Science and Technology, Department of Trade and Industry and other
agencies related to the entire coffee value chain;

4. Provide support for research and development, capacity building,
planning, and policy formulation related to the coffee industry;

5. Serve as a center of excellence in coffee research and development;

6. Produce highly skilled professional experts in the field of coffee
production, post-harvest management, coffee science and quality,
technology and engineering, coffee entrepreneurship, hospitality and
tourism, culture and heritage and other related fields;

7. Establish specialized centers, laboratories, training _ facilities,
demonstration farms, nurseries, coffee processing and roasting facilities,
sensory laboratories, innovation hubs and enterprise incubation spaces;

8. Provide for the integration of Technical and Vocational Education and
Training (TVET) into the University’s educational system;

9. Establish partnerships with international institutions and organizations to
facilitate knowledge exchange and cooperation in coffee research and
development;

10.Offer capacity-building programs and training for professionals, skilled
workers, policymakers, and communities involved in the coffee value
chain;

11.Serve as a repository of knowledge and data related to the coffee industry
and entire coffee value chain, making research findings and information
accessible to the public, researchers and policymakers.

SECTION 4. Curricular Offerings. As a comprehensive university system,
MPSU shall offer postgraduate, graduate, undergraduate and short-term
technical and vocational courses within its areas of specialization and according
to its capabilities as the Board of Regents may deem necessary, to carry out its
mission, particularly in order to meet the needs of the country and the global
community.

SECTION 5. Academic Freedom and Institutional Autonomy. The University
shall enjoy academic freedom as provided for in Republic Act No. 8292, and
paragraph 2, Section 5, Article XIV of the 1987 Constitution.

SECTION 6. Academic Excellence. The University has the responsibility to
maintain and enhance its high academic standards in the performance of its
functions of instruction, research and extension, and public service.

a corporation set forth in Batas Pambansa Big. 68, as amended, otherwise
known as “The Corporation Code of the Philippines.”. The administration of the
University and the exercise of its corporate powers shall be vested exclusively
in the Board of Regents and the President of the University.

SECTION 10. Meetings of the Board. The Board shall regularly convene once
every three (3) months. Whenever necessary, the Chairperson of the Board
may call, upon three (3) days’ prior notice, a maximum of two (2) special
meetings within the same period.

A quorum of the Board shall consist of majority of all its members holding office
at the time of the meeting: Provided, however, That the Chairperson of the
Board or the President of the University is among those present in the meeting.

in one’s absence, the Chairperson of the CHED shail designate a
Commissioner of the CHED as representative in the meeting with all the rights
and responsibilities of a regular member: Provided, however, That during this
meeting, the President of the University as Vice Chairperson shall be the
Presiding Officer: Provided, further, That this proviso notwithstanding, the
Chairperson of the CHED is hereby authorized to designate a CHED
Commissioner as the regular Chair of the Board, in which case said CHED
Commissioner shall act as the Presiding Officer.

===== [page 9] =====
In case the Chairpersons of the Congressional Committees on Education shall
not be able to attend the Board meeting, they may duly designate their
respective representatives to attend the said meeting, who shall have the same
rights and responsibilities as regular member of the Board.

The members of the Board shall not receive any salary but shall be entitled
reimbursements for actual and necessary expenses incurred, either in their
attendance to meetings of the Board or in connection with other official business
authorized by resolution of the Board, subject to existing laws and regulations.

SECTION 11. The University President. The University shall be headed by a
President who shall be elected by the Board subject to its guidelines on
qualifications and standards, based on the recommendation of a search
committee duly constituted for the purpose of selecting the President. The
University President shall render full-time service, serve a term of four (4) years,
and may be reappointed for another term subject to the policies and guidelines
implemented by the government.

Within six (6) months before the expiration of the term of office of the incumbent
President, the Board shall constitute the Search Committee for Presidency.

The powers and duties of the University President, in addition to those
specifically provided in this Act, shall be those usually pertaining to the Office
of the President of universities and those delegated by the Board.

The salary of the University President shall be in accordance with law.

In case of a vacancy in the Office of the President by reason of death,
compulsory retirement, resignation, removal for cause, or incapacity of the
President to perform the functions of the office, the Board shall designate an
Officer-in-Charge pending the appointment of a new President: Provided, That
the Officer-in-Charge shall serve only during the unexpired portion of the term.

The University President shall be assisted by vice presidents who shall be
appointed by the Board upon the University President's recommendation.

in case of vacancy by reason of death, resignation, removal for cause or
incapacity of the President to perform the functions of his office, the Board shall
have the authority to designate an officer-in-charge pending the appointment of
a new president.

In case of vacancy in the Office of the President as mentioned in the
immediately preceding paragraph, his successor shall hold office for the
unexpired term.

SECTION 12. Administrative Council. There shall be an administrative
council consisting of the President of the University as chairperson, the vice
president(s), deans, directors and other officials of equal rank as members, and
whose duty is to review and recommend to the Board of Regents policies
governing the administration, management and development planning of the
University for appropriate action.

As a comprehensive university system, a Campus Administrative Council for its
constituent campuses shall be established. Provided, that these constituent
campuses shall have equal representation in the University Administrative
Council.

SECTION 13. Academic Council. There shall be an academic council with the
President of the University as chairperson and all members of the instructional
staff with the rank of not lower than assistant professor as members.

The academic council shall have the power to determine, review and
recommend the curricular offerings and rules on student discipline of the
University subject to appropriate action of the Board of Regents. It shall fix the
requirements for admission of students as well as for graduation and the
conferment of degrees subject to review and/or approval by the Board of
Regents through the President of the University.

SECTION 14. The Secretary of the University. The Board shall appoint a
secretary who shail serve as such for both the Board and the University and
shall keep all records and proceedings of the Board. The secretary shall
communicate notice of all regular meetings, special sessions, and other
undertakings of the Governing Board to each member of the Board. The
President must ensure, through the Board Secretary, the preparation of agenda
folders for Board Meetings to be provided to the members of the Board at least
five (5) days prior to any regular meeting and one (1) day prior to a special
session.

SECTION 15. The Treasurer of the Philippines. The Treasurer of the
Philippines shall be the ex officio treasurer of the University.

SECTION 16. Faculty. No religious opinion or affiliation shall be the matter of
inquiry in the appointment of faculty members of the State University. Provided,
however, that no member of the faculty shall teach for or against any particular
church or religious sect.

SECTION 17. Scholarships and Admission. The University shall provide a
scholarship 18 program and other affirmative action programs to assist poor but
deserving students 19 to qualify for admission to the University.

===== [page 11] =====
No student shall be denied admission to the University by reason of sex,
religion, cultural or community affiliation or ethnic origin.

SECTION 18. Authority to Loan or Transfer Apparatus, Equipment,
Supplies and Detail of Personnel. The heads of bureaus and offices of the
national government are hereby authorized to loan or transfer, upon request of
the President of the University, such apparatus, equipment or supplies as may
be needed by the University, and to detail employees for duty therein when, in
the judgment of the bureau or office, such apparatus, equipment, supplies or
services of such employees can be spared without serious detriment to the
public service. Employees so detailed shall perform such duties as required of
them by the President of the University, and the time so employed shall be
counted as part of their regular service.

SECTION 19. Affiliation. The University may accept the affiliation and/or
integration within its operation of any existing national school within the province
whose programs can contribute largely and effectively to the attainment of its
objectives, in coordination with the CHED and in consultation with the DBM:
Provided, That funds, properties and other assets, as well as obligations of the
integrated school, shall be transferred to the University. Provided further that,
the school to be integrated into the System complies with the policy requisites
of the University which has the right to allow a particular curricular program that
can only be offered by said school.

SECTION 20. Parity Clause. Ail other powers, functions and privileges,
responsibilities and limitations to state universities and/or its officials under
relevant and existing laws shall be deemed granted to or imposed upon the
University and/or its officials whenever appropriate.

SECTION 21. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Board, in consultation with the CHED, shall
formulate the rules and regulations to fully implement the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 22. Transitory Provision. To provide strategic direction and
leadership as the Mountain Province State University transitions to its pioneer
status as the Highland Coffee University, and to ensure the attainment of its
distinctive mandate, as declared and provided for in this Act, the incumbent
President of the Mountain Province State University shall serve a new term as
the first President of the Highland Coffee University.

SECTION 23. Separability Clause. lf, for any reason, any part or provision of
this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions
not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 24. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 25. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
