                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Section 3, Article XIII, of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that "The
State shall afford full protection to labor, local and overseas, organized and
unorganized, and promote full employment and equality of employment opportunities
for all." This provision establishes the State's obligation to ensure the welfare and
security of its workforce, particularly those engaged in hazardous occupations.

       As of December 2025, approximately 480 million workers are employed in the
construction industry according to the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)1. Due to
the precarious nature of their work such as working at heights, operating heavy
machinery, digging deep foundations, and manually handling heavy materials such as
steel bars and concrete pipes, construction workers face significantly risks of
workplace injuries and fatalities. Despite existing occupational health and safety
standards, workplace incidents remain alarmingly2 prevalent3.

        This measure seeks to establish mandatory accident and life insurance
coverage for all construction workers across the Philippines. The bill ensures that in
the event of work-related injury, disability, or death, affected workers and their
beneficiaries receive timely financial assistance to support them during this difficult
period.

        We recognize the fundamental importance of ensuring the safety of those who
build our homes and construct our vital infrastructure. It is both a moral obligation
and a matter of national responsibility to honor their contributions by recognizing
their efforts and guaranteeing their safety on the job.

                                         AN ACT
        MANDATING AN ACCIDENT AND LIFE INSURANCE COVERAGE
                FOR CONSTRUCTION WORKERS
       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:
Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “ Construction Workers
Insurance Act of 2026”

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
ensure the safety and promote the welfare of construction workers because of their
hazardous and dangerous work environment. To this end, the State shall ensure that
workers safety and welfare are protected by requiring contractors of construction
workers to provide for higher mandatory group personal accident insurance and hold
them liable for workplace accidents to guarantee the quick and efficient delivery of
indemnity to injured workers at a reasonable cost to the employers.

Section 3. Definition of Terms. - The following terms, for the purposes of this Act,
shall be defined as follows:
     a) The terms Construction shall cover:

        1) Building activities, including excavation; construction; structural
           alteration; renovation; repair; maintenance (including cleaning and
           painting); and demolition of all types of buildings and structures,
           including pipelines, tunnels, and other infrastructure utilized for
           transportation, water supply, energy distribution, and communications
           systems; and

        2) The erection and dismantling of prefabricated buildings and structures,
           as well as manufacturing of prefabricated elements on the construction
           site.

    b) Construction worker refers to a person engaged in construction that is
       exposed to a considerable risk, danger, or hazard while in the line of work
       regardless of employment status, including but not limited to regular and
       contractual employees, or daily-wage workers ;



                                         3
    c) Employer refers to a contractor or a subcontractor that employs at least ten
       (10) construction workers to perform a service for hire and includes a
       corporation, partnership, limited liability company, association, group of
       persons, state, city, municipality, school district, or any government
       subdivision;

    d) Construction site refers to any place or location where building or structural
       construction activities are being undertaken;

    e) Insurance refers to a contractual agreement whereby one undertakes to
       indemnify another against loss or injury relating to a particular subject as a
       result of the occurrence of designated hazards;

    f)   Group Personal Accident Insurance refers to a type of insurance coverage
         offered to a group of people, which provides benefit to the individual or
         beneficiaries if the covered individual becomes permanently disabled or dies
         during the defined insurance covered period resulting from accidental, death,
         disability, and injuries;

    g) Insurance company is a corporation, duly accredited by the Insurance
       Commission that provides for the insurance coverage of the construction
       worker upon payment by the employer of the required insurance premium.

Section 4. Mandatory Insurance Coverage.- All construction workers employed in
a construction project or site shall be provided a group personal accident insurance
coverage by the employer. The duration of the insurance shall start from the
commencement of the service of the construction worker until the completion of the
construction project or upon the termination of the employment contract.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish or reduce any benefit and other
privileges which the worker may be entitled under existing laws, decrees, executive
order, company policy or practice or any agreement or contract between the employer
and employees.

Section 5. Cost of Insurance.- The premiums to be paid to the insurance company
shall be completely paid for by the employer and shall not be deducted from the
wages of the construction workers.

Section 6. Minimum Insurance Coverage.- The group personal accident insurance
shall provide for financial assistance including but not limited to the following:
     a) For natural death, the amount of One hundred fifty thousand pesos
         (P150,000.00);

    b) For accidental death, the amount of Two hundred fifty thousand pesos
       (P250,000.00);

    c) For death in the line of work, the amount of Three hundred fifty thousand
       pesos (P350,000.00);




                                          4
    d) For loss of both hands, the amount of One hundred twenty five thousand
       pesos (P125,000.00);

    e) For loss of both feet, the amount of One hundred twenty-five thousand pesos
       (P125,000.00);

    f)   For loss of sight for both eyes, the amount of One hundred twenty-five
         thousand pesos (P125,000.00);

    g) For the loss of one hand and one foot, the amount of On hundred twenty-five
       thousand pesos (P125,000.00);

    h) For the loss of one hand and the sight of one eye, the amount of One hundred
       twenty five thousand pesos (P125,000.00);

    i)   For the loss of one hand or one foot, the amount of seventy five thousand
         pesos (P75,000.00); and

    j)   For the loss of one eye, the amount is seventy- five thousand pesos
         (P75,000.00)

Provided, that the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), in consultation
with relevant stakeholders, shall adjust to inflation such amounts every five years,
based on data reported by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).

Section 7. Health Insurance- In addition to the compensation that shall accrue to the
worker in the preceding section, every construction worker shall likewise be entitled
to receive fair and prompt medical benefits in cases where the illness is directly
attributable to or was developed in the course of employment. This is meant to cover
loss of wages due to workplace exposure.

Section 8. Contractor Liability for Workplace Accidents.- Contractors shall be held
liable for workplace accidents involving their employed construction workers. In the
event of an accident resulting in injury or death, the contractor shall be responsible for
providing immediate assistance, including medical attention, and financial
compensation to the affected worker or the worker’s family.

Section 9. Limitations.- No compensation shall be given to the construction worker
or his or her dependents when the injury, illness, disability, or death was due to any of
the following:
     a) Intoxication or under the influence of dangerous drugs;
     b) Willful intention to injure or kill himself or another; or
     c) Gross negligence.

Section 10. Penalties.- Any person, partnership, or corporation who violates any
provision of this Act shall, upon conviction, suffer the following penalties:

    a) For the first violation, a fine of not less that Three hundred thousand pesos
       (P300,000.00) but not more than Five hundred thousand pesos (P500,00.00);
       and


                                            5
    b) For any subsequent violation, a fine of not less than Five hundred thousand
       pesos (P500,000.00) but not more than One million pesos (P1,000,000.00).

If the offender is a corporation, organization or any similar entity, the officials thereof
directly involved in the violation shall be liable.

If the offender is an alien or foreigner, he shall be deported immediately upon service
of sentence without further proceedings.

Upon the filing of the appropriate complaint or information, the Secretary of Labor
and Employment shall request the appropriate local government unit to cancel or
revoke the business permit, permit to operate and other similar privileges granted to
any business entity or person that fails to abide by or violates the provisions of this
Act.

Section 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations.- Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Labor and Employment in coordination
with the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), Department of Interior
and Local Government (DILG), the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI),
Department of Health (DOH), Insurance Commission (IC), representatives of
construction groups and associations, workers groups, and other relevant stakeholders
shall promulgate the implementing rules and regulations as may be necessary to
ensure the efficient and effective implementation of this Act.

Section 12. Separability Clause.- If any part or provision of this Act is held invalid
or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall remain in full
force and effect.

Section 13. Repealing Clause.- All laws, executive orders, presidential decrees or
issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules and regulations contrary
to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.

Section 14. Effectivity.- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
