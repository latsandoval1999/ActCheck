                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to grant Benjamin (“Ben”) W. Mead Philippine citizenship with
all the rights, privileges, and prerogatives, as well as the duties and obligations
appurtenant thereto, under the Constitution and the laws of the Republic of the
Philippines.

       Benjamin W. Mead is a 45-year old Australian, has been a permanent resident
of the Philippines since 2015, and married to a Filipina.

      Since residing in the Philippines, Ben has been dedicating his time and efforts
towards a range of impactful initiatives through his Agriculture-focused enterprises,
such as Iba Botanicals Inc, United Agarwood Planters Inc, BioPrime Agri Industries
Inc. He also contributes a substantial effort in advancing the charitable initiatives of
the Mead Foundation Inc. (“TMFI”), a SEC-registered, non-profit organization of which
he serves as founder and Chairman. TMFI advances causes aligned with the Filipino
community (health, education, community infrastructure) and the country’s
environment.

       Ben is the founder and CEO of Iba Botanicals, Inc. (“IBI”), a BOI-accredited
export enterprise and a pioneer in the production of high-volume and high-value
botanical extracts, such as pure essential oils, that exports to large multinational flavor
and fragrance companies in Europe and USA. Due to these efforts, the Philippines
has become recognized as a reputable global player in the production of these high-
value extracts. IBI’s extracts have been supplied to fragrance brands such as Chanel,
Dior, and Victoria’s Secret, consumer goods brands such as Bath and Body Works,
and wellness companies such as Young Living and doTERRA. IBI’s business
showcases both premium Filipino botanical extracts as well as community impact and
sustainable practices to global consumers, and exerts great pride in positioning as a
dedicated Filipino producer into premium and luxury global markets.

       Iba Botanicals has invested more than Php 150 million that provided fulltime
employment to more than 140 Filipinos, as well as more than 700 farm workers – a
vast majority of which are wither indigenous (Aeta and Lumad) community or returned
rebels. Presently, IBI has 3 plantations for its production –280 hectares in Camarines
Sur, 230 hectares in Bukidnon and 180 hectares in Zambales, together with
associated distillation facilities and laboratories.
       Iba Botanicals, through its wholly-owned subsidiary United Agarwood Planters
Inc (“UAPI”), is also the leader of the emerging Philippines agarwood industry.
Agarwood is a critically endangered tree species that is considered the world’s highest
value forest product, is one of the tree species that Ben aims to protect by propagating
Agarwood plantations in full compliance with local and international rules and
regulations. UAPI has been undertaking the establishment of agarwood plantations
at scale, as well as support third-party planters who wish to participate in this new and
highly regulated high-value agro-forestry industry. Through these efforts, legal and
sustainable agarwood should help to reduce or eliminate the poaching of wild
agarwood trees in the Philippines.

        Under Ben’s Chairmanship, BioPrime Agri Industries has become the leader in
the Philippines BioFertilizer industry; technology that shows great promise to revitalize
the soil and accelerate plant growth rates, demonstrating immense impact for Filipino
farmers. Since 2018, through a disciplined engagement with the Department of
Agriculture (DA) and PhilRice, the product proved to permit Filipino rice and corn
farmers to substantially reduce chemical fertilizer whilst increasing yields and earnings
significantly. This paved the way for DA to confidently adopt a “balanced fertilization”
as the main strategy to address the country’s reliance on imported chemical fertilizer,
which benefited thousands of Filipino farmers. The BioPrime Agri Industries, Inc. has
likewise employed more than 150 Filipinos.

         In fact, during the global pandemic, Ben contributed Php 6 billion of nano bio-
fertilizer inventory in the Philippines to ensure his continuous support to the needs of
the Filipino farmers with almost 1 million hectares of farmland, providing a valuable
tool to counter the emerging fertilizer crisis.

       On top of these outstanding performances in the agricultural sector, Ben is
likewise prioritizing philanthropic activities as the founder and CEO of ‘The Mead
Foundation (“TMF”), a non-profit enterprise with three (3) active divisions: medical,
community development, and environmental conservation:

       •   TMF’s medical division offers free cleft lip and palate surgeries for
           underprivileged Filipinos. As of today, more than 3,500 free surgeries have
           been performed by both international and local surgeons through its annual
           medical mission, as well as sponsoring medical cases in government
           hospitals located within Metro Manila.

       •   TMF’s community development program has a range of initiatives that
           supporting communities’ general development, especially for Indigenous
           Peoples communities, through school infrastructures, clean water systems,
           disaster relief operations, feeding programs, materials sponsorship, and
           other related activities.

       •   TMF’s marine conservation division aims at taking an integrated approach
           to Marine Protected Area administration, coral reef restoration, livelihood
           creation and other aligned site-specific efforts such as sea turtle
           conservation and protection. TMF has undertaken projects in six provinces
           of the Philippines, with the support of local and international partners such
          as the University of the Philippines (UP) and Southern Cross University in
          Australia.

       The Mead Foundation in its 2025 Year-End Impact Review highlighted its
accomplishments such as its ‘Remote Community Medical Mission’ in Divilacan and
Maconacon in Isabela with more than 5,900 services performed, ‘Earthquake Relief
Operations’ in Medellin, Cebu, ‘AgriKatutubo Scholarship & Career Pathway’
scholarship program for agri students, ‘Burn Care Donation for Community-Based
Medical Response’ in Zambales, donated learning equipment and Starlink internet
access to Pamplona Integrated School in Del Gallego, Camarines Sur and Poonbato
Integrated School in Botolan, Zambales, and its Feeding Programs in Saint Brother
Miguel Academy in Antipolo, Rizal and Olongapo City. TMF’s ongoing 2026 thrust is
to advance the ‘Itayo ang Pag-asa Classrooms’ program; building climate-resilient and
sustainable engineered bamboo school buildings for Indigenous Peoples and
underserved communities, with school buildings under development in Camarines Sur
and Bukidnon for completion by the end of 2026.

   Ben’s allegiance to the Philippines is indubitable. His values, goals and wide
knowledge, and competency in the field of agriculture shall truly advance the entire
nation and its economic development. Thus, providing Ben the rights and privileges of
a Filipino citizen is deserving of him.

      Thus, with the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is most humbly and
earnestly sought.

                                AN ACT
         GRANTING PHILIPPINE CITIZENSHIP TO BENJAMIN W. MEAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Benjamin W. Mead is hereby granted Philippine citizenship with all the
rights, privileges, and prérogatives, as well as the duties and obligations appurtenant
thereto, under the Constitution and the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

SEC. 2. Benjamin W. Mead shall take the Oath of Allegiance to the Republic of the
Philippines before an officer duly authorized to administer the same. The Oath of
Allegiance shall be registered with the Bureau of Immigration.

SEC. 3. Upon registration of the Oath of Allegiance, the Bureau of Immigration shall
issue a Certificate of Naturalization to Benjamin W. Mead who shall thereupon enter
into the full employment of Philippine citizenship.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect immediately upon its publication in the Official
Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.
