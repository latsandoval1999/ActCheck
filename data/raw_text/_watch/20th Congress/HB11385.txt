21                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
22
23   Every payday, Ana sets aside ₱500 for her paluwagan. She does this faithfully because
24   she hopes that when her turn comes, the money will be enough to pay her child’s school
25   expenses. She trusts the person keeping the contributions because the group is made
26   up of people from her own community.
27
28   But when the time comes to collect her share, she learns that the money is no longer
29   there. The person who managed the contributions has disappeared, leaving Ana and
30   the other members with little protection and no clear way to recover their savings.
31
32   This is the risk faced by many Filipinos who rely on informal paluwagan arrangements.
33   While paluwagan can help people save and access money for their needs, the absence
34   of a clear legal framework and government supervision as well as intervention, when
35   appropriate, can leave members vulnerable to fraud, misuse of funds, and poor financial
36   management.
37
38   Hence, this proposed measure seeks to establish rules for the organization and
39   operation of community paluwagan, provide supervision and safeguards for members'
40   contributions, and give registered groups access to affordable credit, insurance,
41   financial education, and entrepreneurship programs to make better use of the
42   contributions without affecting liquidity.
43
44   For families who save what little they can, every contribution matters. Their trust and
45   hard-earned money deserve protection.
46
47   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

21                               AN ACT
22    REGULATING THE ORGANIZATION AND OPERATION OF COMMUNITY FUND
23      POOLING OR PALUWAGAN GROUPS AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR
24                         VIOLATION THEREOF

27   Congress assembled:

29                                                  CHAPTER I
30                                            TITLE AND DEFINITIONS
31
32         SECTION 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bagong Paluwagan ng
33   Bayan Act”.
34
35          SEC. 2. Declaration of Policies. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
36   State to pursue a program that emboldens the people to achieve financial autonomy
37   through capacity building and self- financing activities. The Filipino people shall be
38   encouraged to come together and undertake self-financing activities to inspire them
39   to cultivate a culture of entrepreneurship, meet their basic needs, improve their
40   financial resources, and promote income security.
41
42           In this regard, the State shall place great importance on facilitating easy and
43   efficient access to necessary funds and credits, which are crucial elements in
44   realizing the people's aspirations towards financial stability and self-reliance.
45
46          To achieve this end, the paluwagan, a traditional community fund pooling
47   system for savings and investments, shall be formalized and strengthened for the
48   protection and benefit of the people. Through this system, the people shall have
49   access to affordable credit and financial assistance, and shall be empowered to
50   engage in entrepreneurial activities and other forms of self-sustenance.
51
52           SEC. 3. Definitions. – As used in this Act:
53                  (a) Community Paluwagan refers to a small group of persons with residence
54                  or employment in a defined community, created in accordance with the
 1               provisions laid out in this Act and supervised by the Community Paluwagan
 2               Administration created in Section 4 of this Act, for the noble purpose of
 3               instilling a culture of entrepreneurship, while simultaneously promoting
 4               their financial welfare by providing them with a ready source of credit for
 5               provident and productive objectives; and
 6               (b) Member refers to a person who holds an account in a duly registered
 7               paluwagan, which is evidenced by a member’s contribution (hulog)
 8               received or held in the usual course of business and for which it has given
 9               or is obligated to give credit to the account of the member.

35          SEC. 10. Creation of a Paluwagan. - Any nine (9) or more persons with
36   residence, occupation, employment, or activities in a defined community, who desire
37   to form a paluwagan shall apply for registration with the Administration. The application
38   shall specifically state the following:
39               (a) Name of the paluwagan;
40               (b) Location of the proposed paluwagan;
41               (c) Names and addresses of the members;
42               (d) Proposed field of membership;
43               (e) Term of existence, not exceeding fifty (50) years, renewable for
44               another fifty (50) years upon approval of the Administration;
45               (f) Articles of the paluwagan; and
 1               (g) By-Laws.
 2          SEC. 11. Application for Registration. - In order to simplify the organization of
 3   the paluwagan, the Board shall from time-to-time cause to be prepared a form of
 4   application and a form of bylaws, consistent with this Act, which shall be used by
 5   applicants, and shall be supplied to them on request. Before any application for
 6   registration is approved, an appropriate investigation shall be made for the purpose of
 7   determining: a) whether the paluwagan conforms to the provisions of this Act; b) the
 8   general character and fitness of members; and c) economic viability of establishing
 9   the proposed paluwagan.
10          An application for registration shall be processed within fifteen (15) calendar
11   days upon submission of complete requirements, otherwise, the said application shall
12   be deemed approved. The Board may prescribe the payment of a reasonable amount of
13   fees for the processing of the application for registration under this section.

28               its members, in accordance with the following:
29                   1) Loans to members shall be made in conformity with the criteria
30                   established in the By-laws of the paluwagan, subject to the following:
31                       (i) A residential real estate loan on a family dwelling, that is or will
32                       be used as the principal residence of a paluwagan member, and

35                       be set by the Board, subject to the rules and regulations of the

39                       supervisory or credit committee of the paluwagan making the loan

42                       That the said trustee or member has no participation in the approval
43                       of said loan;
44                       (iii) Loans to other members for which trustees or members of
45                       the supervisory or credit committee act as guarantor or endorser

13          SEC. 13. Duties of the Paluwagan. – In accordance with the rules prescribed
14   by the Board, each paluwagan shall pay to the Administration an annual operating fee
15   which may be composed of one or more charges identified as to the function or functions
16   for which it is assessed. The fee assessed under this section shall be determined
17   according to a schedule, or schedules, or other method determined by the Board to
18   be appropriate, which gives due consideration to the expenses of the Administration

21          The Board shall, among other things, determine the periods for which the fee shall
22   be assessed and the date or dates for the payment of the fee or increments thereof. All
23   operating fees shall be for the account of the Administration and may be expended by
24   the Board to defray the expenses incurred in carrying out the provisions of this
25   Act including the examination and supervision of the paluwagan.

27                                         CHAPTER IV
28                                         Membership
29          SEC. 14. Membership. – Each member of a paluwagan shall invest an amount
30   which represents capital contribution, and pay the installments thereon, as may be
31   prescribed by the Bylaws.
32
33          SEC. 15. Membership Field. - The membership of any paluwagan shall be
34   limited to a defined community, which shall be set out in the by-laws.
35
36         SEC. 16. Additional Membership Eligibility. - No individual shall be eligible for
37   membership in a paluwagan on the basis of the relationship of the individual to another
38   person who is eligible for membership in the paluwagan, unless the individual is within
39   the second degree of consanguinity of the member.
40         SEC. 17. Retention of Membership. - Except as provided in this Act, once a
41   person becomes a member of a paluwagan in accordance with this title, that person or
42   organization may remain a member of that paluwagan until the person or organization
43   chooses to withdraw from the membership.
44         SEC. 18. General Membership Meeting. - The annual meeting of each
45   paluwagan shall be held at such place as its bylaws shall prescribe. Special meetings
46   may be held in the manner indicated in the bylaws. Unless the Articles or bylaws of the
47   paluwagan provide for a greater number, a quorum shall consist of the majority of the
 1   members of the paluwagan. The members shall also be entitled to vote by proxy, when
 2   so authorized by the bylaws.
 3
 4           SEC. 19. Expulsion of Members. - A member may be expelled for cause by
 5   a vote of the majority of all of the members of the board of trustees, ratified by the vote
 6   of the majority of all of the members of a paluwagan present at a special meeting called
 7   for the purpose, but only after opportunity has been given to the member to be heard.
 8   The board of trustees of a paluwagan may, by a majority vote, there being a quorum,
 9   adopt and enforce a policy with respect to expulsion from membership based on non-
10   participation by a member in the affairs of the paluwagan. In establishing its policy, the
11   board of trustees shall consider a member’s failure to vote in the paluwagan elections,
12   failure to obtain or pay one’s loan from the paluwagan, among others. If such a policy
13   is adopted, a written or digital notice of the policy and the effective date of such policy
14   shall be mailed to each member of the paluwagan at the member’s current address
15   appearing on the records of the paluwagan within ten (10) days from the adoption of
16   such policy. In addition, each new member shall be provided a written notice of any
17   such policy prior to or upon applying for membership.
18           The term “cause” under this section shall mean a substantial or repeated
19   violation of the membership agreement of the paluwagan; a substantial or repeated
20   disruption, including dangerous or abusive behavior to the operations of a paluwagan;
21   or fraud, attempted fraud, or other illegal conduct that a member has been convicted of
22   in relation to the paluwagan, including the employees conducting business on behalf of
23   the paluwagan. Expulsion of a member pursuant to this section shall not operate to
24   relieve the member from any civil or criminal liability to the paluwagan.
25
26          SEC. 20. Due Process. - Subject to the policy adopted herein, if a member shall
27   be subject to expulsion, such member shall be notified in advance of the proceedings
28   for expulsion, along with the reason for such expulsion. Such notice shall be provided
29   in person, by mail to the member’s address, or, if the member has elected to receive
30   electronic communications from the paluwagan, may be provided electronically. A
31   member shall have thirty (30) days from the date of receipt of such notification to request
32   for a hearing from the board of trustees of the paluwagan. If a member does not request
33   for a hearing during the 30-day period as described, the member shall be expelled after
34   the expiration of the 30-day period. If a member requests for a hearing during the 30-
35   day period as described, the board of trustees of the paluwagan shall provide the
36   member with a hearing within thirty (30) days from receipt of the request. After such
37   hearing, the board of trustees of the paluwagan shall hold a vote within fifteen (15) days
38   whether or not to expel the member. If a member is expelled pursuant to the decision of
39   the Board, the notice of the expulsion of the member shall be provided to the member
40   in person or by mail to the member’s address, in written form or, if the member has
41   elected to receive electronic communications from the paluwagan, may be provided
42   electronically. The expulsion of a member pursuant to this Act shall be done individually,
43   on a case-by-case basis, and neither the Administration nor any paluwagan may expel
44   a class of members.
45
46          SEC. 21. Reinstatement. - A member expelled under Section 19 of this Act
47   shall be given an opportunity to request for reinstatement of membership; and may be
48   reinstated by either a majority vote of a quorum of the trustees of the paluwagan; or a
 1   majority vote of the members of the paluwagan present at a meeting called for such
 2   purpose.

 4                                       CHAPTER V
 5                                      MANAGEMENT
 6
 7           SEC. 22. Management of the Paluwagan. - The management of a paluwagan
 8   shall be by a board of trustees, a supervisory committee, and a credit committee. The
 9   board of trustees shall consist of an odd number of trustees, at least five (5) in number,
10   to be elected annually by and from the members as the bylaws provide. Any vacancy
11   occurring in the board of trustees shall be filled through appointment by the remaining
12   members of the board of trustees: Provided, That the appointee shall only serve the
13   unexpired term of the member being substituted. At their first board meeting, the board
14   of trustees shall appoint corporate officers specified in the bylaws, which shall include
15   a President, Secretary, and Treasurer. Corporate officers may be compensated in
16   accordance with the industry standard. The bylaws shall specify the specific duties of
17   each corporate officer.
18
19           SEC. 23. Composition of the Supervisory and Credit Committees. - The
20   Committee members shall be appointed by the board of trustees and shall consist of not
21   less than three (3) members nor more than five (5) members. A record of the names
22   and addresses of the executive officers, members of the supervisory committee, credit
23   committee, and loan officers, shall be filed with the Administration within ten (10) days
24   after their election or appointment
25
26         SEC. 24. Compensation. - No member of the board of trustees or of any other
27   committee shall, as such, be compensated. Per diems, health, accident, similar
28   insurance protection, and the reimbursement of reasonable expenses incurred in the
29   execution of the duties of the position shall not be considered compensation.
30
31          SEC. 25. Meetings of the Board of Trustees. - The board of trustees shall meet
32   at least once a month and shall have the general direction and control of the affairs of
33   the paluwagan. Minutes of all meetings shall be kept by the Secretary. The board of
34   trustees shall have the following powers and functions:

28           SEC. 29. Regulation of the Paluwagan. -The Board may prescribe rules and
29   regulations for the administration of this Act. Any duly registered paluwagan shall be
30   subject to such rules, regulations, and orders as the Board deems appropriate and,
31   except as otherwise specifically provided in such rules, regulations, or orders, shall be
32   vested with or subject to the same rights, privileges, duties, restrictions, penalties,

34
35         SEC. 30. Power to Revoke and Suspend. - The Board may suspend or revoke
36   the registration of any paluwagan, or place the same in involuntary liquidation and
37   appoint an authorized liquidating agent therefor, upon its finding that the organization is
38   bankrupt or insolvent, or has violated any of the provisions of its charter, its bylaws, this
39   Act, or any regulations issued thereunder. The Board, through such persons as it shall
40   designate, may examine any paluwagan in voluntary liquidation and, upon its finding
41   that such voluntary liquidation is not being conducted in an orderly or efficient manner
42   or in the best interests of its members, may terminate such voluntary liquidation and
43   place such organization in involuntary liquidation and appoint a liquidating agent
44   therefor.
45         Such liquidating agent shall, subject to the control and supervision of the Board
46   and under such rules and regulations as the Board may prescribe, have the following
47   powers and authorities:
 1               (a) Receive and take possession of the books, records, assets, and
 2               property of every description of the paluwagan in liquidation;
 3               (b) Sell, enforce collection of, and liquidate all such assets and property, to
 4               compound all bad or doubtful debts, and to sue in one’s name or in the name
 5               of the paluwagan in liquidation, and defend such actions as may be
 6               brought against such liquidating agent or against the paluwagan;
 7               (c) Receive, examine, and pass upon all claims against the paluwagan
 8               in liquidation, including claims of members on member accounts;
 9               (d) Make distribution and payment to creditors and members as their
10               interests may appear; and
11               (e) Execute such documents and papers and to do such other acts and
12               things deemed necessary or desirable to discharge the duties hereunder.
13               Subject to the control and supervision of the Board, and under such rules
14               and regulations as the Board may prescribe, the liquidating agent of a
15               paluwagan in involuntary liquidation shall cause notice to be given to
16               creditors and members to present their claims and make legal proof
17               thereof. Such notice shall be published once a week for three (3)
18               successive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation in the place in
19               which the paluwagan in liquidation maintained an office or branch for the
20               transaction of business on the date it ceased unrestricted operations. Upon
21               certification by the liquidating agent in the case of an involuntary
22               liquidation, and upon such proof as shall be satisfactory to the Board in
23               the case of a voluntary liquidation, that distribution has been made and
24               that liquidation has been completed, as provided herein, the Board shall
25               cancel the charter of such paluwagan. The corporate existence of the
26               paluwagan shall continue for a period of three (3) years from the date of
27               such cancellation of its charter. During this period, the liquidating agent, or
28               the duly appointed successor, or such persons as the Board shall
29               designate, may act on behalf of the paluwagan for the purpose of paying,
30               satisfying, and discharging any existing liabilities or obligations, collecting
31               and distributing its assets, and doing all other acts required to adjust and
32               wind up its business and affairs, and may sue and be sued in its corporate
33               name. After the expiration of five (5) years from the date of cancellation
34               of the charter of a paluwagan, the Board may, in its discretion, destroy
35               any or all books and records of such paluwagan in its possession or under
36               its control.

41          SEC. 31. Paluwagan Insurance. – The Board, as hereinafter provided, shall
42   insure the member accounts of all paluwagan to the extent of amount as they may
43   determine. Applications for such insurance shall be in such form as the Board shall
44   provide and shall contain the following terms to be agreed upon by the applicant:
45               (a) To pay the reasonable cost of such examinations as the Board may
46               deem necessary in connection with determining the eligibility of the
47               applicant for insurance: Provided, That examinations required under this
48               Act shall be so conducted that the information derived therefrom may be
 1                 utilized for insurance purposes;
 2                 (b) To permit and pay the reasonable cost of such examinations as, in
 3                 the judgment of the Board, may from time to time be necessary for the
 4                 protection of the fund and of other insured paluwagan;
 5                 (c) To provide protection and indemnity against burglary, defalcation,
 6                 and other similar insurable losses, of the type, in the form, and in an
 7                 amount at least equal to that required by the laws under which the
 8                 paluwagan is organized and operates;
 9                 (d) To maintain such special reserves as the Board, by regulation or in
10                 special cases, may require for protecting the interest of members or to
11                 assure that all insured paluwagan maintain regular reserves as may be
12                 required by the Board;
13                 (e) To pay the premium charges for insurance imposed by this Chapter;
14                 and
15                 (f) To comply with the requirements of this chapter and of regulations
16                 prescribed by the Board pursuant thereto.
17         Before approving the application of any paluwagan for insurance of its member
18   accounts, the Board shall consider the following:
19                 (a) The history, financial condition, and management policies of the
20                 applicant;
21                 (b) The economic advisability of insuring the applicant without undue risk
22                 of the fund;
23                 (c) The general character and fitness of the applicant’s management;
24                 (d) The convenience and needs of the members to be served by the
25                 applicant; and
26                 (e) Whether the applicant is a paluwagan organized for the purpose of
27                 creating a source of credit for entrepreneurial or productive purposes.
28          The Board shall disapprove the application of any paluwagan for insurance of
29   its member accounts if it finds that its reserves are inadequate, its financial condition
30   and policies are unsafe or unsound, its management is unfit, insurance of its member
31   accounts would otherwise involve undue risk to the fund, or its powers and purposes
32   are inconsistent with the promotion of a culture of entrepreneurship, and the creation
33   of a source of credit for provident or productive purposes.
34          Upon the approval of any application for insurance, the Board shall notify the
35   applicant and shall issue to it a certificate evidencing the fact that it is, as of the date of
36   issuance of the certificate, an insured paluwagan under the provisions of this title.
37
38          SEC. 32. Insurance Premium Charges. – All paluwagan shall, at such times
39   as the Board prescribes, pay to the fund a premium charge of one-fifth of one per
40   centum (1/5 of 1%) of Total Guaranteed Contribution of the paluwagan. The term
41   "Guaranteed Contributions" shall pertain to funds enjoying insurance coverage as
42   prescribed by the provisions delineated in this section. These contributions shall
43   yield guaranteed income and offer inherent withdrawal flexibility for members.
 1          Minimum Fixed Contribution shall be maintained by all paluwagan and shall refer
 2   to funds not covered by the insurance coverage. It shall not be withdrawable until the
 3   membership to the paluwagan is terminated.
 4            SEC. 33. Payment of Insurance. - Payment of an insured account to any
 5   person by the Administration shall discharge the Administration of the liability of
 6   insurance. The Administration may withhold payment of such portion of the insured
 7   amount of any member in a closed paluwagan as may be required to provide for the
 8   payment of any liability to the closed paluwagan or its receiver, which is not offset against
 9   the claim due from such paluwagan, pending the determination and payment of such
10   liability by such member or any other person liable therefor/

12         SEC. 34. Prohibitions on Insured Paluwagan. - No insured paluwagan may be
13   sponsored by or accept financial support, directly or indirectly, from any government-
14   sponsored enterprise. This provision shall not be construed as prohibiting any
15   employee of a government-sponsored enterprise from becoming a member of a
16   paluwagan.

18          SEC. 35. Reports of Condition. - Each insured paluwagan shall make reports
19   of condition to the Board upon dates which shall be selected by them. Such reports of
20   condition shall be in such form and shall contain such information as the Board may
21   require. The reporting dates selected for reports of condition shall be the same for
22   all insured paluwagan except that when any of said reporting dates is a nonbusiness
23   day for any paluwagan, the preceding business day shall be its reporting date. The total
24   amount of the member accounts of each insured paluwagan as of each reporting date
25   shall be reported in such reports of condition in accordance with regulations prescribed
26   by the Board. Each report of condition shall contain a declaration by the president, vice
27   president, treasurer, or any other officer designated by the board of trustees of the
28   reporting paluwagan to make such declaration, that the report is true and correct to the
29   best of such officer’s knowledge and belief. Unless such requirement is waived by the
30   Board, the correctness of each report of condition shall be attested by the signatures of
31   three of the officers of the reporting paluwagan with the declaration that the report has
32   been examined by them and to the best of their knowledge and belief is true and
33   correct.
34          The Board may call for such other reports as it may from time to time require. The
35   Board may require reports of condition to be published in such manner, not inconsistent
36   with any applicable law, as it may direct. Reports required under this section shall be
37   so prepared that they can be used for insurance purposes.

19          SEC. 46. Grant of Tax Exemption. – Notwithstanding the provisions of any law
20   or regulation to the contrary, a paluwagan shall enjoy the following tax exemptions:
21                (a) Any paluwagan shall be exempt from payment of tax in respect to all
22                income it receives, including interest on its deposits with any financial
23                institution and loan transactions with members: Provided, however, That
24                income derived from any of its properties, real or personal, or any activity
25                conducted for profit, regardless of the disposition thereof, is subject to
26                the corresponding internal revenue taxes imposed under the National
27                Internal Revenue Code (NIRC) of 1997, as amended: Provided, further,
28                That the exemption from income tax shall not include those taxes wherein
29                the paluwagan is only constituted as the withholding or remitting agent;
30                (b) Interest on member’s contribution with any paluwagan as well as the
31                shares of its members from the net income of the paluwagan shall be
32                exempt from income tax;
33                (c) Any paluwagan duly registered with the Administration shall be exempt
34                from the payment of value-added tax and documentary stamp tax, in
35                relation with its operations and transactions with members;
36                (d) Any paluwagan, regardless of the amount of accumulated reserves
37                and undivided net savings shall be exempt from payment of local taxes.
38                Fees and charges assessed by the cities or municipalities on the
39                application of mayor’s or local business permits, and other licenses and
40                approvals shall in no case exceed Three thousand pesos (P3,000.00).

12                                 OFFENSES AND PENALTIES
13          SEC. 48. Penal Clause. – Penalties shall be imposed for violation of the following
14   prohibited acts:
15                 (a) Refusal to Make Reports or Permit Examination. – Any trustee, officer,
16                 or employee of any paluwagan within the purview of this Act who, being
17                 required in writing by the Administration, willfully refuses to file the
18                 required report or permit any lawful examination into the affairs of such
19                 paluwagan shall be punished by a fine of not less than Fifty thousand
20                 pesos (P50,000.00) nor more than One hundred thousand pesos
21                 (P100,000.00) or by imprisonment of not less than one (1) year nor more
22                 than five (5) years, or both, in the discretion of the court.
23                 (b) False Statement. - The willful making of a false or misleading
24                 statement on a material fact before the Administration or any of its
25                 authorized representatives shall be punished by a fine of not less than
26                 One hundred thousand pesos (P100,000.00) nor more than Two hundred
27                 thousand pesos (P200,000.00), or by imprisonment of not more than (5)
28                 years, or both, at the discretion of the court.
29                 (c) Proceedings upon Violation of this Act and Other Laws, Rules,
30                 Regulations, Orders or Instructions. - Whenever a paluwagan willfully
31                 violates any of the provisions of this Act or any other order, instruction, rule
32                 or regulation issued by the Administration, the person or persons
33                 responsible for such violation shall, unless otherwise provided in this Act,
34                 be punished by a fine of not less than Fifty thousand pesos (P50,000.00)
35                 nor more than Five hundred thousand pesos (P500,000.00) or by
36                 imprisonment of not less than two (2) years nor more than ten (10) years,
37                 or both, at the discretion of the court.
