                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Women play an indispensable role in modern law enforcement. Their
presence in the police service strengthens the capacity of law enforcement
institutions to respond effectively and sensitively to cases involving women and
children, sexual and gender-based violence, domestic abuse, and other crimes
and situations requiring gender-responsive policing.

       Recognizing this need, Republic Act No. 8551, otherwise known as the
“Philippine National Police Reform and Reorganization Act of 1998,” established
women's desks in police stations and provided for the prioritization of women for
recruitment and training. Section 58 of the law currently requires the Philippine
National Police (PNP) to reserve ten percent (10%) of its annual recruitment,
training, and education quota for women.

       The statutory quota was established more than two decades ago. The
evolving demands of policing and the continuing need for a more representative
and gender-responsive police organization warrant a progressive increase in the
participation of women in the PNP.

       This measure therefore seeks to increase the existing quota from ten
percent (10%) to fifteen percent (15%) during the first five (5) years from the
effectivity of the proposed law, and to twenty percent (20%) beginning on the
sixth (6th) year and every year thereafter.

       The measure does not dispense with the qualifications, merit, fitness,
eligibility, and other requirements prescribed by law for appointment to the PNP.
Rather, it seeks to ensure that qualified women are given meaningful and
increased opportunities to enter the police service within the reserved quota.

       The measure likewise requires the PNP to incorporate the women's quota
in its recruitment and manpower planning and to undertake reasonable and
sustained efforts to attract, recruit, and qualify women applicants. This will help
ensure that the statutory quota is treated as an integral component of PNP
recruitment policy and not merely as an after-the-fact numerical target.

       To promote accountability, the measure establishes a monitoring and
reporting mechanism requiring the PNP to submit an annual report to the
NAPOLCOM and Congress on its compliance with the women's quota, including
the number of women who applied, qualified, were appointed, and underwent
training, as well as any unfilled portion of the reserved quota and the reasons
therefor.

      The measure further recognizes that increasing the number of women in
the PNP should not confine them to women's desks. Women recruited and
trained under the program should be afforded equal opportunities for
assignment, promotion, professional development, and career advancement
throughout the police organization. This is consistent with the existing statutory
policy that the women's-desk provisions should not restrict the assignment of
policewomen to other positions or be used to prevent their promotion.

      By progressively increasing the representation of women in the PNP, this
measure seeks to build a more representative, professional, responsive, gender-
sensitive, and effective police force capable of providing accessible and
appropriate police services to all sectors of Philippine society.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                             “AN ACT
      INCREASING THE RECRUITMENT, TRAINING, AND EDUCATION
      QUOTA FOR WOMEN IN THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE,
      AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 58 OF REPUBLIC ACT
      NO. 8551, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “PHILIPPINE NATIONAL
      POLICE REFORM AND REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1998,” AND
      APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR”

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Amendment of Section 58 of Republic Act No. 8551. -
Section 58 of Republic Act No. 8551, otherwise known as the “Philippine
National Police Reform and Reorganization Act of 1998,” is hereby amended to
read as follows:

            “SEC. 58. Prioritization of Women for Recruitment. -
            The Philippine National Police (PNP) shall prioritize the
            recruitment and training of qualified women and shall
            ensure increased participation and representation of
            women in the police service.

            For the first five (5) years from the effectivity of this
            Act, the PNP shall reserve not less than FIFTEEN
            PERCENT (15%) of its annual recruitment, training,
            and education quota for women.

            Beginning on the sixth (6th) year from the effectivity of
            this Act and every year thereafter, the PNP shall
            reserve not less than TWENTY PERCENT (20%) of its
            annual recruitment, training, and education quota for
            women.
            The PNP shall undertake reasonable and sustained
            measures to attract, recruit, and qualify women
            applicants to fill the quota reserved under this
            Section. Qualified women applicants shall be
            considered within the reserved quota subject to the
            qualifications, merit, fitness, and other requirements
            prescribed by law and applicable rules and
            regulations.

            Women recruited and trained pursuant to this Section
            shall be given appropriate opportunities for
            assignment, including assignment to women's desks,
            without prejudice to their subsequent assignment,
            promotion, professional development, or career
            advancement in other positions and units of the PNP.”

       SECTION 2. Compliance with the Women's Quota. - The PNP shall
take all necessary and reasonable measures to ensure the effective utilization
of the quota reserved for women under Section 58 of Republic Act No. 8551,
as amended by this Act.

      The quota reserved for women shall be determined based on the total
annual recruitment, training, and education quota authorized for the PNP for
the applicable recruitment cycle or calendar year, as may be prescribed by the
NAPOLCOM.

      The PNP shall incorporate the women's quota in its recruitment and
manpower planning and shall undertake reasonable and sustained efforts to
attract, recruit, and qualify sufficient women applicants to fill the reserved
quota.

      Nothing in this Act shall be construed as dispensing with, modifying, or
lowering the qualifications, merit, fitness, eligibility, or other requirements
prescribed by law for appointment and training in the PNP.

      SECTION 3. Monitoring and Reporting. – The NAPOLCOM, in
coordination with the PNP, shall establish a system for monitoring compliance
with the women's quota provided under this Act.

     The PNP shall submit an annual report to the NAPOLCOM and to
Congress, through the appropriate committees, containing the following:

      (a) the total annual recruitment, training, and education quota of the
          PNP;
       (b) the number and percentage of women who applied, qualified, were
           appointed, and underwent training;

       (c) the number and percentage of women appointed and trained
           pursuant to the reserved quota;

       (d) any unfilled portion of the reserved quota and the reasons therefor;

       (e) the measures undertaken to attract, recruit, and qualify women
           applicants; and

       (f) such other information as may be necessary to assess compliance
           with and the effective implementation of this Act.

      SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety
(90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the NAPOLCOM, in coordination with
the PNP, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.

       The implementing rules and regulations shall, among others, provide
for:
       (a) the determination and computation of the annual quota reserved for
           women;

       (b) the incorporation of the women's quota in the PNP's recruitment and
           manpower planning;

       (c) the recruitment, selection, appointment, and training procedures
           applicable to the reserved quota;

       (d) measures to ensure the effective utilization of the reserved quota;

       (e) the monitoring and reporting of compliance;

       (f) measures and procedures for addressing any unfilled portion of the
           reserved quota, including the documentation and reporting of the
           reasons therefor; and

       (g) such other measures as may be necessary to carry out the purposes
           of this Act.

      The implementing rules and regulations shall be consistent with the
Constitution, Republic Act No. 8551, as amended, and other applicable laws,
rules, and regulations governing recruitment, appointment, training, and
career development in the PNP.
      SECTION 5. Gender-Responsive Recruitment and Career
Development. - The PNP and the NAPOLCOM shall, within their respective
mandates, strengthen programs for the recruitment, orientation, training,
professional development, and career advancement of women in the police
service.

      Such programs shall promote equal opportunity, gender sensitivity,
professional development, and a safe and non-discriminatory working
environment for women in the PNP consistent with Republic Act No. 8551, as
amended, and other applicable laws.

      The implementation of this Section shall be without prejudice to the
gender sensitivity program and other protections provided under Republic Act
No. 8551, as amended.

      SECTION 6. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the
implementation of this Act shall be included in the appropriations of the PNP
and the NAPOLCOM in the annual General Appropriations Act.

      Thereafter, the funds necessary for the continued implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 7. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with
the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified
accordingly.

       SECTION 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of
general circulation.

      Approved.
