                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

Motor vehicle drivers and riders are essential to Philippine society. They
transport people, goods, and services, helping support the country’s economy
and everyday life. This includes jeepney and bus drivers, delivery riders, and
private vehicle owners.

However, they face many challenges, such as rising fuel prices, expensive
vehicle maintenance, and the costs and inconvenience of complying with
government requirements. One major burden is the yearly renewal of motor
vehicle registration. For those who depend on their vehicles for their livelihood,
this process takes time, money, and effort.

This bill proposes extending the validity of motor vehicle registrations. The
measure would provide financial and practical relief to millions of Filipino
drivers and riders by reducing the need for annual renewal. It recognizes their
important contribution and seeks to lessen the difficulties they face.

Furthermore, recognizing that technological advances have made newer
vehicles more reliable, consequently, extended registration periods may be
warranted beyond the three-year exemption.

The extension would also benefit the government. It would reduce the workload
of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) and allow its personnel to focus on
other important services and improvements. It could also help reduce long
lines and overcrowding in LTO offices and make the registration process more
efficient.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
      EXTENDING THE PERIOD OF VALIDITY OF REGISTRATION
 CERTIFICATES OF MOTOR VEHICLES, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
 SECTION 14 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 4136, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
           “LAND TRANSPORTATION AND TRAFFIC CODE”


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the EXTENDED
MOTOR VEHICLE REGISTRATION VALIDITY ACT.


SECTION 2. Section 14 of Republic Act No. 4136 is hereby amended to read as
follows:

      “SECTION 14. CERTIFICATES OF REGISTRATION

            A. ISSUANCE OF CERTIFICATES OF REGISTRATION. – A
            properly numbered Certificate of Registration shall be issued for
            each separate motor vehicle after due inspection and payment of
            corresponding fees.

            B. VALIDITY OF CERTIFICATES OF REGISTRATION. – The
            validity of Certificates of Registration shall be five (5) years for
            Brand new motor vehicles and Four (4) years for Brand new
            motorcycles and should be renewed afterward unless sooner
            revoked or suspended.


            C. RENEWAL OF CERTIFICATES OF REGISTRATION. – The
            renewed Certificates of Registration shall have the following validity
            periods.

                  MOTOR VEHICLE’S AGE                   RENEWAL VALIDITY

                  1. 5-7 years                          3 years

                  2. 8-9 years                          2 years

                  3. 10 years and older                 1 year
                  MOTORCYCLE’S AGE                      RENEWAL VALIDITY

                  1. 4-7 years                          3 years

                  2. 8 years and older                  1 year


            Provided, That the extended registration validity period shall be
            granted only to motor vehicles that have passed the inspection and
            safety requirements prescribed by the Land Transportation Office
            and have no recorded history of major mechanical, structural, or
            safety-related failure

            Every Certificate of Registration shall be valid unless sooner revoked
            or suspended.”

SECTION 3. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not
affected thereby shall remain in force and effect.

SECTION 4. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, rules and regulations and other issuances, or any part thereof,
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, modified, or amended
accordingly.

SECTION 5. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of
general circulation.
