                                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to strengthen the field offices of the Commission on Elections
(COMELEC) by upgrading and reclassifying existing plantilla positions, creating
additional personnel posts, and rationalizing staffing structures in accordance with
the growing demands of election administration. It amends Batas Pambansa Blg.
881, otherwise known as the “Omnibus Election Code of the Philippines”, to modernize
the structure and compensation of COMELEC field offices and ensure electoral
operations keep pace with needs of voter management and democratic governance.

       COMELEC field offices face increasing operational pressure amid a rapidly
expanding electorate and evolving election demands. COMELEC reported that
registered voters for the 2022 national and local elections exceeded 65.7 million
nationwide, reflecting a significant increase from previous election cycles1. This
translates into heavier workloads for election personnel tasked with voter
registration, precinct management, logistics, and enforcement of election laws, often
with limited manpower and outdated staffing structures. The continuing rise in
voter registration2, including the addition of millions of new registrants even during
short registration periods, has further strained field operations. Despite these
growing responsibilities, many field offices remain understaffed and inadequately
equipped to meet present operational requirements.

        The absence of adequate technical, administrative, and support personnel has
contributed to operational inefficiencies and delays in frontline services during
critical electoral processes. These impair the ability of field offices to effectively
discharge their mandates and maintain efficient election administration.

       To address these, the proposed measure establishes a more responsive and
professional organizational structure within COMELEC field offices through the

        Strengthening the capacity of COMELEC field offices is essential in the

                            AN ACT
STRENGTHENING THE FIELD OFFICES OF THE COMMISSION ON
ELECTIONS BY UPGRADING AND CREATING CERTAIN POSITIONS,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 53 OF BATAS PAMBANSA
BILANG 881, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “OMNIBUS ELECTION CODE OF
THE PHILIPPINES”, AS AMENDED, AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

    Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Commission on Elections
(COMELEC) Field Offices Strengthening Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the state to
ensure free, orderly, honest, peaceful, and credible elections by strengthening the
institutional capacity of the COMELEC. Towards this end, the State shall promote a
professional and competent electoral workforce through the rationalization,
reclassification, and upgrading of plantilla positions, consistent with the demands of
election administration and the need to attract and retain qualified personnel. It
shall likewise ensure adequate and just compensation, in accordance with the
principles of merit, fitness, and equal pay for equal work, without diminution of
salaries and benefits.

       SEC. 3. Reclassification and Upgrading of Plantilla Positions in Commission on
Election Field Offices. – All existing plantilla positions in Commission on Election
(COMELEC) field offices shall be reclassified, with their salaries upgraded
accordingly. For purposes of this Act, amendments to Section 53 of Batas Pambansa
Bilang 881 provides for the reclassified positions and salary grades. Reclassified
positions that will not be filled by existing employees shall be subject to hiring.

       SEC. 4. Field Offices. – Section 53 of Batas Pambansa Bilang 881, otherwise
known as the “Omnibus Election Code of the Philippines”, as amended, is hereby
further amended to read as follows:

       SEC. 5. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the available appropriations of the COMELEC
under the current General Appropriations Act. Thereafter, such sums as may be



                                         4
necessary for its continued implementation shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
approval of this Act, the COMELEC, the Civil Service Commission, in coordination
with the Department of Budget and Management, shall issue the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof not affected thereby
shall continue to be in full force and effect.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential decrees,
presidential proclamations, letters of instruction, rules and regulations or part
thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

      SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette, or in a newspaper of general circulation.
