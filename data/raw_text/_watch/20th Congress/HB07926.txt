                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Members of Congress are trusted with public money and public responsibility.
While the Constitution gives Congress control over its own rules and discipline,
the public rightly expects that allowances paid from taxpayers’ funds are used
properly and fairly. Concerns arise when per diems and allowances appear to be
given without a clear connection to actual work or attendance, which can weaken
trust in public institutions.

This bill addresses that concern by linking certain allowances and per diems, not
basic salaries, to verified attendance or official legislative work. It recognizes that
lawmakers do important work not only during plenary sessions, but also in
committee hearings, bicameral meetings, official missions, and district duties
connected to lawmaking and oversight. These legitimate activities are counted as
“deemed attendance,” so Members are not unfairly penalized for working outside
the session hall.

By requiring clear records, proper documentation, and public summaries of
attendance-based payments, the measure promotes transparency and
accountability while respecting Congress’s independence. Simply put, the bill
ensures that attendance-based allowances are released only when work is actually
done and can be verified. This strengthens public confidence, protects public
funds, and reinforces the principle that government service must always be
accountable to the people.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the
undersigned respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR ATTENDANCE-BASED WITHHOLDING OF CERTAIN
ALLOWANCES AND PER DIEMS OF MEMBERS OF CONGRESS, DEFINING
DEEMED ATTENDANCE INCLUDING OFFICIAL DISTRICT DUTIES, AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Attendance-Based
Allowances for Members of Congress Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote
accountability, responsibility, and integrity in public service. Members of
Congress, as elected representatives of the people, shall be transparent in the
discharge of legislative and official duties. Allowances and per diems that are
intended to support official functions shall be released only upon verified official
attendance or deemed attendance.

SECTION 3. Coverage. — This Act shall apply to all Members of the Senate and
the House of Representatives.

SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:

   (a) Member of Congress – refers to any duly elected Senator or Member of the
       House of Representatives.




                                                                          2 | Page
  (b) Plenary Session – refers to a regular or special session of Congress where
      legislative business is conducted with the presence of a quorum, as
      officially recorded in the Journal of the Senate or the House of
      Representatives.


  (c) Committee Meeting – refers to any duly constituted meeting of a standing,
      special, or ad hoc committee, including hearings and investigations in aid
      of legislation, as scheduled and recorded by the Secretariat.

  (d) Official Attendance – refers to physical presence or such mode of
      attendance as may be officially authorized by the chamber concerned, in a
      plenary session or committee meeting, and duly recorded by the
      Secretariat.


  (e) Deemed Attendance – refers to attendance credited to a Member who is
      not physically present in a plenary session because the Member is
      performing other official duties recognized under this Act and duly
      documented.

  (f) Attendance-Based Compensation – refers only to daily per diems, session
      or committee attendance allowances, and such other allowances or
      emoluments as are expressly designated by the Senate President or the
      Speaker, as applicable, as attendance-based and not forming part of the
      basic salary fixed by law.

SECTION 5. Attendance-Based Compensation Scheme. —

  (a) A Member of Congress shall be entitled to Attendance-Based
      Compensation only for days when the Member has Official Attendance of
      Deemed Attendance.

  (b) A Member who incurs an absence that is neither Official Attendance nor
      Deemed Attendance shall not be entitled to Attendance-Based
      Compensation for the day or days of such absence.


  (c) Nothing in this Act shall be construed to:
       1. Reduce, increase, or otherwise alter the basic salary of Members of
           Congress fixed by law; or
       2. Impair the constitutional powers of each chamber to discipline its
           Members

SECTION 6. Deemed Attendance; Authorized Absences. — While Congress is
in session, a Member shall be deemed present in a plenary session on any day that
the Member’s absence is due to any of the following official duties, as duly certified
under Section 7:

   (a) Attendance in committee meetings as authorized by the Committee on
       Rules, upon written notification to the Secretary General by the concerned
       committee secretary;

   (b) Attendance in meetings of:

       (1) The Commission on Appointments;
       (2) The Electoral Tribunal of the chamber concerned; and
       (3) Bicameral Conference Committees;

   (c) Official mission or official travel as approved by the Senate President or the
       Speaker, as applicable; and

   (d) Official duties in the district (for Members of the House of Representatives)
       or in the region/province/city/municipality concerned (for Senators, as
       may be applicable), including but not limited to:

       (1) constituent consultations, public hearings, or stakeholder conferences
           officially convened or participated in by the Member in relation to
           pending or proposed legislation or oversight;
       (2) field inspections, site visits, or project monitoring undertaken in
           relation to legislative oversight functions, including verification of
           implementation issues relevant to legislation;
       (3) attendance in official government meetings or inter-agency
           coordination sessions upon written invitation by a national
           government agency or local government unit, where the Member
           participates in an official capacity connected to legislative functions;
           and
       (4) emergency response coordination in declared states of calamity or
           public emergencies, when the Member is requested in writing by
           competent authorities to participate in official coordination, provided
           that the participation is documented and reported to the chamber
           concerned.

SECTION 7. Documentation, Certification, and Recording. —

   (a) Each chamber shall maintain daily records of plenary and committee
       attendance.

                                                                            4 | Page
   (b) For Deemed Attendance under Section 6(c) and (d), the Member shall
       submit, within a reasonable period prescribed by the chamber’s rules,
       supporting documents such as travel authority, mission order, invitation,
       program, minutes, attendance sheets, or certification by the relevant
       government office.


   (c) The Senate President or the Speaker, as applicable, shall designate the
       office responsible for verifying documents and issuing certifications for
       Deemed Attendance.

SECTION 8. Transparency. — Each chamber shall publish, in a manner consistent
with data privacy and security requirements, periodic summaries of Members’
attendance and deemed attendance, and corresponding Attendance-Based
Compensation released.


SECTION 9. Fraudulent Claims and Administrative Consequences. —

   (a) Any Member or officer who knowingly causes the recording of false
       attendance or submits falsified documents to obtain Attendance-Based
       Compensation shall be subject to appropriate proceedings under the rules
       of the chamber concerned, without prejudice to liability under other
       applicable laws.

   (b) Officers and employees of either chamber who participate in falsification
       of attendance records shall likewise be subject to administrative discipline.



SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Senate and the House of Representatives
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to implement the provisions
of this Act within their respective chambers, including:

   (a) The list of attendance-based allowances/per diems covered;
   (b) Documentary requirements;
   (c) Verification procedures; and
   (d) Publication formats.

SECTION 11. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain
in full force and effect.
