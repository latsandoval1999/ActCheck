                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Department of Education (DepEd) affirms that education must
cultivate not only intellectual competence but also moral character and civic
responsibility, as embodied in its core values of Makadiyos, Makatao,
Makakalikasan, at Makabansa. While values formation is presently delivered
through Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (EsP) and Good Manners and Right
Conduct (GMRC), the integration of moral education across other learning areas
remains inconsistent and largely dependent on individual teaching approaches.

      Existing policies recognize the centrality of values formation in basic
education. DepEd Order No. 6, s. 1988 institutionalized a national Values
Education Program to promote moral development in schools, while DepEd
Order No. 30, s. 1990 reinforced civic-oriented values and national
consciousness. Further, Executive Order No. 570 mandated the integration of
peace education across basic education. Notwithstanding these issuances, the
implementation of values education remains largely compartmentalized rather
than systemically embedded across the curriculum and school environment.

      Accordingly, the proposed “Pamilyang Marangal Program for Education
Act” seeks to institutionalize an integrated and comprehensive framework for

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Pamilyang Marangal
Program for Education Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to promote holistic and learner-centered education that integrates the
development of values, character, and competencies necessary for nation-
building. Towards this end, the State shall ensure that moral and values
formation is systematically embedded in the K to 12 Basic Education Program,
aligned with national identity and the core values espoused by the Department
of Education, and reinforced through both curricular and co-curricular
mechanisms.

SECTION 3. Scope and Coverage. This Act shall apply to all public and private
basic education institutions under the supervision and regulation of the
Department of Education, including those implementing the K to 12 Basic
Education Program and the Alternative Learning System (ALS). All learners,
teachers, school administrators, and education stakeholders shall be covered by
and shall comply with the provisions of this Act.
