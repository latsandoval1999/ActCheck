                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to rename the Sta. Rosa Community Hospital located in Barangay
Talima, Olango Island, Lapu-Lapu City, to Lapu-Lapu City Hospital-Olango (LLCH-O).

        Established more than 44 years ago by virtue of Batas Pambansa Blg. 134 enacted on
12 September 1981, the Sta. Rosa Community Hospital serves as the City-owned health facility
that caters to the residents of Olango and the City of Lapu-Lapu. Renaming the hospital to
LLCH-O will reflect this present role, provide a unified and updated hospital identity, and
ultimately contribute to the fulfilment of the City’s expanded health services delivery.
Moreover, this move will reduce the risk of misrouting in emergency referrals and inter-facility
transfers, ensure consistency in Department of Health licensing, PhilHealth accreditation and
claims, procurement, and official records. Renaming it will likewise eliminate confusion with
similarly named hospitals in the country, including those in the province of Laguna.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
RENAMING THE STA. ROSA COMMUNITY HOSPITAL LOCATED IN OLANGO
ISLAND, LAPU-LAPU CITY TO LAPU-LAPU CITY HOSPITAL-OLANGO (LLCH-
O)

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

        SECTION 1. The Sta. Rosa Community Hospital located at Barangay Talima, Olango
Island, Lapu-Lapu City, is hereby renamed to Lapu-Lapu City Hospital-Olango.

       SEC. 2. Section 1 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 134, entitled “An Act Establishing a
Community Hospital with a Ten-Bed Capacity and Secondary Level Capability, in the Island
of Olango, City of Lapu-Lapu, to be Known as Sta. Rosa Community Hospital, and
Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor” is hereby amended accordingly.

       SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
