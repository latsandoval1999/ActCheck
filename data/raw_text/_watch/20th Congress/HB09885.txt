                           EXPLANATORY NOTE
      Our Constitution directs the State to give priority to the needs of the
underprivileged, the sick, the elderly, the disabled, women, and children
within an integrated approach to health and social development.1 The
Magna Carta for Persons with Disabilities, as amended, affirms the same
commitment.2 Yet while the State already extends a monthly social pension
to indigent senior citizens under Republic Act No. 11916, no equivalent,
predictable, and universal cash support exists for persons with disability.
      Disability imposes recurring costs that ordinary income does not
capture, among them maintenance medicines, assistive devices,
rehabilitation, caregiver support, and specialized transportation. These costs
are borne by every person with a disability, regardless of economic status.
For those without income, the twenty percent discount under existing law
offers little relief because a discount helps only those who already have
money to spend. For those with limited income, the additional burden of
disability-related expenses erodes what little they earn. The result is a
structural gap in the social protection system that falls across the full
spectrum of persons with disability.
      By converting scattered and discretionary assistance into an
enforceable and universal statutory entitlement, this measure affirms the
dignity, inclusion, and full participation of all persons with disability in
national life.
     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR A MONTHLY SOCIAL PENSION FOR PERSONS
       WITH DISABILITY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:​
​
​    SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the Social
Pension for Persons with Disability Act.​
​
​      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
promote a just and dynamic social order that ensures the well-being and full
participation of all sectors of society, including persons with disability. The
State recognizes that disability imposes recurring and substantial costs of
living that are additive to the ordinary expenses of every household.​
​
​       SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the
following terms are defined as follows:
    a.​ Person with Disability or PWD refers to an individual with a
        long-term physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory impairment
        which, in interaction with various barriers, may hinder the full and
        effective participation of that individual in society on an equal basis
        with others, as contemplated under Republic Act No. 7277, as
        amended.​
    b.​ Social Pension refers to the monthly cash grant provided by the
        government under this Act to augment the daily subsistence and
        disability-related needs of a PWD.​
    c.​ Social pension of the same nature refers to a regular and recurring
        non-contributory cash pension granted by the national government
        on the basis of vulnerability status, such as the social pension for
        indigent senior citizens, and does not include conditional cash
        transfers or other distinct forms of assistance.​
    d.​ PRPWD refers to the Philippine Registry for Persons with Disabilities
        maintained for the registration and recording of PWDs.
​
​      SECTION 4. Grant of Social Pension. – Every PWD who is duly
registered in the PRPWD and holds a valid PWD Identification Card issued
pursuant to Republic Act No. 7277, as amended, shall be entitled to a
monthly social pension of not less than One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) to
augment daily subsistence and to help meet disability-related needs. The
grant shall be subject to existing government auditing rules and regulations.
​
​      SECTION 5. Eligibility and Verification. – To qualify, a person must
be a PWD who is duly registered in the PRPWD and holds a valid PWD
Identification Card issued pursuant to Republic Act No. 7277, as amended.
Eligibility shall be determined and validated by the Department of Social
Welfare and Development and shall be cross-validated against the Philippine
Identification System established under Republic Act No. 11055 and the
PRPWD to prevent duplication and fraud. The list of beneficiaries shall be
validated and updated at least annually.​
​
​     SECTION 6. Coordination and Non-Duplication of Benefits. – A
PWD who is a beneficiary under this Act shall not, at the same time, receive
another social pension of the same nature, including the social pension for
indigent senior citizens. A PWD who qualifies for more than one social
pension of the same nature shall elect the more beneficial one. This Act
shall not, however, disqualify a beneficiary from receiving the
Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program or any other distinct form of
government assistance whose purpose differs from this social pension.
The social pension under this Act shall not reduce, offset, or diminish any
benefit already extended to PWDs by local government units.​
​
​      SECTION 7. Lead Agency. – The Department of Social Welfare and
Development (DSWD) shall lead the implementation of this Act, coordinating
with the National Council on Disability Affairs, the Department of Health for
certification standards, the Department of the Interior and Local
Government and local government units for registration, and the
Department of Budget and Management for funding. In the Bangsamoro
Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao, the Ministry of Social Services
and Development shall implement the program following national guidelines.​
​
​      SECTION 8. Periodic Review and Indexation. – Every two years, the
DSWD, with the NCDA and DBM, shall review and, if necessary, increase
the social pension based on PSA economic indicators and the consumer
price index. The adjusted amount must never fall below the current rate.​
       SECTION 9. Database and Data Privacy. – The National Council on
Disability Affairs, Department of Social Welfare and Development, and local
governments shall maintain a secure PWD beneficiary database for
verification and planning. Data handling must comply with the Data Privacy
Act of 2012, utilizing access controls and audit logs strictly for program
administration and policy development.​
​
​     SECTION 10. Grievance and Appeals Mechanism. – Implementing
rules shall provide an accessible grievance and appeals system for
contesting pension denials, suspensions, or terminations. Decisions must
be rendered promptly and provided to the applicant or beneficiary in an
accessible format.​
​
​       SECTION 11. Penal Provisions. – Any person who deliberately
commits a fraudulent act to enroll a non-qualified person, or who knowingly
facilitates the issuance of a fraudulent PWD Identification Card to obtain the
social pension, shall, for the first violation, be fined not less than
Twenty-Five Thousand Pesos (P25,000.00) but not more than Fifty
Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00), and for any subsequent violation, not less
than Fifty Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00) but not more than One Hundred
Thousand Pesos (P100,000.00). If the offender is a public officer,
administrative liability shall attach in addition to the foregoing. The
penalties under this Act are without prejudice to liability under other laws.​
​
​    SECTION 12. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the Department of Social Welfare and Development.
Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued
implementation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.​
​
​     SECTION 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The
Secretary of Social Welfare and Development shall, within sixty (60) days of
this Act's effectivity, promulgate its implementing rules and regulations in
coordination with the National Council on Disability Affairs, the Department
of Budget and Management, and disabled persons organizations.​
​
​      SECTION 14. Suppletory Clause. – The provisions of Republic Act
No. 7277, the Magna Carta for Persons with Disabilities, as amended by
Republic Act No. 9442 and Republic Act No. 10754, shall have suppletory
effect to this Act.​
​
​     SECTION 15. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this
Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected shall
remain in full force and effect.​
​
​     SECTION 16. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders,
proclamations, rules and regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof
that are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.​
​
​      SECTION 17. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
