                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Philippine International Trading Corporation (PITC) was created
under Presidential Decree No. 252, with its charter subsequently revised by
Presidential Decree No. 1071, otherwise known as the “Revised Charter of
the Philippine International Trading Corporation.” Under Section 2 of P.D.
No. 1071, PITC is authorized to exist for fifty (50) years, with its present
corporate term set to expire on 25 January 2027.

      For decades, PITC has served as a government corporate vehicle in
advancing the State’s objectives in international trade, including facilitating
trade, promoting Philippine products and enterprises, and supporting
government procurement and marketing activities within the scope of its
charter.

      With the impending expiration of its corporate term, there is a need to
ensure the continuity of PITC’s juridical personality, operations, contracts,
obligations, assets, liabilities, and other lawful commitments. The proposed
measure therefore seeks to provide PITC with a perpetual corporate term,
unless otherwise provided by law.
      Perpetual existence, however, shall not exempt PITC from
government oversight and accountability. The Corporation shall remain
subject to the evaluation, monitoring, and other appropriate actions of the
Governance Commission for Government-Owned or -Controlled
Corporations (GCG) pursuant to Republic Act No. 10149, or the “GOCC
Governance Act of 2011,” and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.
Such evaluation may consider PITC’s relevance, financial viability,
operational efficiency, governance, performance, and contribution to
Philippine international trade.

      The proposed measure does not expand PITC’s substantive powers
and functions under P.D. No. 1071. Rather, it seeks to provide institutional
continuity while preserving the authority of Congress and the appropriate
government agencies to evaluate, amend, or otherwise take lawful action
concerning the Corporation when warranted.

      The immediate enactment of this measure will prevent disruption
arising from the expiration of PITC’s corporate term and enable the
Corporation to continue performing its existing mandate in support of
Philippine international trade, subject to continuing government oversight
and accountability.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate enactment of this measure is
earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE PERPETUAL EXISTENCE OF THE PHILIPPINE
INTERNATIONAL TRADING CORPORATION (PITC), AMENDING FOR
THE PURPOSE SECTION 2 OF PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 1071,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “THE REVISED CHARTER OF THE
PHILIPPINE INTERNATIONAL TRADING CORPORATION”



      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Amendment of Section 2 of Presidential Decree No.
1071. Section 2 of Presidential Decree 1071, otherwise known as the
“Revised Charter of the Philippine International Trading Corporation”, is
hereby amended to read as follows:

      "SECTION 2. Name, Domicile and Place of Business. — There
      is hereby created a body corporate to be known as the
      Philippine International Trading Corporation, hereinafter referred
       to as the Corporation, which shall exist for fifty years HAVE
       PERPETUAL EXISTENCE, UNLESS OTHERWISE PROVIDED
       BY LAW, and shall have its principal office in Metro Manila.

       THE CORPORATION SHALL REMAIN SUBJECT TO THE
       PERFORMANCE    EVALUATION,     MONITORING,  AND
       OVERSIGHT OF THE GOVERNANCE COMMISSION FOR
       GOVERNMENT-OWNED           OR       -CONTROLLED
       CORPORATIONS    (GCG),   IN   ACCORDANCE   WITH
       REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10149, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
       ‘GOCC GOVERNANCE ACT OF 2011,’ AND OTHER
       APPLICABLE LAWS, RULES, AND REGULATIONS.

       NOTHING IN THIS ACT SHALL BE CONSTRUED AS
       LIMITING OR DIMINISHING THE POWER OF CONGRESS TO
       AMEND, REPEAL, OR OTHERWISE MODIFY THE CHARTER
       OR CORPORATE EXISTENCE OF THE CORPORATION BY
       SUBSEQUENT LEGISLATION.”

       Sec. 2. The Philippine International Trading Corporation shall
continue to exist as a body corporate without interruption upon the
expiration of its existing corporate term, with all the rights, powers,
privileges, assets, liabilities, contracts, obligations, and responsibilities
lawfully vested in or assumed by it prior to such expiration.

       All pending actions, proceedings, contracts, transactions, and
commitments of the Corporation shall continue and shall not be
affected by the expiration of its existing corporate term or the provision
for its perpetual existence under this Act.

     Sec. 3. If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

      Sec. 4. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations,
or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
      Sec. 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
