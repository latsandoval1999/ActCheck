                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to further strengthen the implementation of the Anti-Hospital
Deposit Law by increasing the penalties imposed on hospitals, medical clinics, and
their responsible officials who refuse or fail to provide appropriate initial medical
treatment and support in emergency or serious cases due to the non-payment of
deposits or advance payments.

       Batas Pambansa Bilang 702, as amended, was enacted to ensure that no
person in need of urgent medical attention is denied life-saving treatment because of
financial incapacity. Despite the existence of this law, reports persist of hospitals
requiring deposits or refusing admission or treatment to patients in emergency
situations.

        This measure strengthens accountability by increasing the penalties imposed
on medical practitioners, hospital personnel, and responsible officers who violate the
law. In cases where the refusal to administer emergency treatment is carried out
pursuant to an established hospital policy or upon the instruction of management,
the bill imposes higher penalties on the hospital directors or officers responsible for
formulating and implementing such policies. Moreover, repeated violations may
lead to the revocation of the health facility’s license to operate, without prejudice to
other administrative and professional sanctions.

       By strengthening penalties, improving accountability, and creating an
accessible reporting mechanism, this measure aims to ensure that hospitals and
medical facilities comply with their legal and ethical obligation to provide
emergency medical care without requiring deposits.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
INCREASING THE PENALTIES FOR THE REFUSAL OF HOSPITALS AND
MEDICAL CLINICS TO ADMINISTER APPROPRIATE INITIAL MEDICAL
TREATMENT AND SUPPORT IN EMERGENCY OR SERIOUS CASES,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE BATAS PAMBANSA BILANG 702,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “ANTI-HOSPITAL DEPOSIT LAW”, AS
AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Section 4 of Batas Pambansa Bilang 702, as amended, is hereby
further amended to read as follows:

             “Sec. 4. Any official, medical practitioner or employee of the
      hospital or medical clinic who violates the provisions of this Act shall,
      upon conviction by final judgment, be punished by imprisonment of
      FOUR (4) TO [not less than] six (6) [months and one (1) day but not
      more than two (2) years and four (4) months] YEARS, or a fine of not
      less than [One] FIVE hundred thousand pesos (₱[1]500,000.00), but not
      more than [Three hundred thousand] ONE MILLION pesos
      (₱[3]1,000,000.00) or both, at the discretion of the court:

             “Provided, however, That if such violation was committed
      pursuant to an established policy of the hospital or clinic or upon
      instruction of its management, the director or officer of such hospital
      or clinic responsible for the formulation and implementation of such
      policy shall, upon conviction by final judgment, suffer imprisonment
      of [four (4) to] six (6) years AND ONE (1) DAY TO TWELVE (12)
      YEARS, or a fine of not less than [Five hundred thousand] TWO
      MILLION pesos (₱[5]2,000,000.00), but not more than [One] FIVE
      million pesos (₱[1]5,000,000.00) or both, at the discretion of the court,
      without prejudice to damages that may be awarded to the
      patient-complainant:

       SEC. 2. A new Section 9 shall be inserted after Section 8 of Batas Pambansa
Bilang 702, as amended, to read as follows:

            “SEC. 9. ANTI-HOSPITAL DEPOSIT LAW HOTLINE. - THE
       DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH, WITH THE ASSISTANCE OF THE
       NATIONAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION (NTC)
       AND      DEPARTMENT        OF   INFORMATION       AND
       COMMUNICATIONS TECHNOLOGY (DICT) SHALL ESTABLISH
       A NATIONAL HOTLINE AIMED AT PROVIDING TELEPHONE
       AND CELLPHONE NUMBERS WHICH THE PUBLIC MAY CALL
       FOR   IMMEDIATE      ASSISTANCE   AND INTERVENTION
       REGARDING      THEIR CONCERNS ON ANTI-HOSPITAL
       DEPOSIT. THE HOTLINE SHALL RUN TWENTY-FOUR (24)
       HOURS A DAY, SEVEN DAYS A WEEK, AND SHALL BE
       AVAILABLE TO ANSWER CALLS AND PROVIDE ASSISTANCE
       IN THE FASTEST POSSIBLE TIME.”

       SEC. 3. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 4. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 5. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
