MANAGEMENT BY HARMONIZING AND UPGRADING THE QUALIFICATION

POSITIONS IN THE BUREAU OF CORRECTIONS AND THE BUREAU OF JAIL
MANAGEMENT AND PENOLOGY, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Safe, humane, and well-run prisons and jails depend on the quality of their leaders.
Wardens, superintendents, and other key of icials make daily decisions that affect
security, discipline, rehabilitation, and respect for human rights. Our laws already
recognize this by requiring education, rank, and training for top of icials in the Bureau
of Corrections (BuCor) and the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP).
However, these standards are not always applied consistently, updated regularly, or
supported by enough training opportunities.

This bill strengthens and harmonizes existing laws by making sure that all key prison
and jail leadership positions meet clear and uniform minimum quali ications. It sets
a irm baseline so standards can never be lowered, requires continuing education and
periodic re-certi ication, and ensures that leaders stay competent, it, and ethical
throughout their service. The measure also gives current of icials reasonable time to
meet new requirements and directs government agencies to make training accessible,
so compliance is fair and realistic.

By professionalizing prison and jail management, this measure helps prevent abuse,
improves safety inside facilities, and supports rehabilitation rather than mere

those entrusted with managing persons deprived of liberty are properly trained and
accountable. For these reasons, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Professionalization of
Prison and Jail Management Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to ensure a
professional, competent, and accountable corrections system. Key corrections
positions shall be occupied only by quali ied personnel with appropriate education,
training, and integrity, consistent with existing laws professionalizing the Bureau of
Corrections (BuCor) and the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP).

SECTION 3. Coverage. — This Act shall apply to key prison management positions in
the BuCor and key jail management positions in the BJMP, without prejudice to the
minimum appointment quali ications for uniformed personnel under existing laws.

SECTION 4. De inition of Key Positions. — For purposes of this Act, “key positions”
shall include, at a minimum:


                                                                           3|Page
   (a) for BuCor: Sub-Colony Supervisor, Colony Assistant Superintendent, Colony
       Superintendent, and Regional Superintendent; and

   (b) for BJMP: Municipal Jail Warden, City Jail Warden, District Jail Warden,
       Provincial Jail Administrator, Regional Director, and other positions as may be
       provided by law.

SECTION 5. Minimum Quali ication Standards (Floor). — The minimum
quali ication standards for designation/assignment to key positions in the BuCor and
BJMP shall, at all times, be not lower than those provided in existing laws, including
Republic Act No. 10575 and Republic Act No. 9263.

SECTION 6. Updated Education and Training Requirements. —

   (a) BuCor Key Positions. — No person shall be designated to a key position in the
       BuCor unless the person meets the quali ication standards for the speci ic
       position, including educational requirements and completion of necessary
       training or career courses, consistent with Republic Act No. 10575.

   (b) BJMP Key Positions. — No person shall be designated to a key position in the
       BJMP unless the person meets the quali ication standards for the speci ic
       position, including educational requirements and completion of necessary
       training or career courses, consistent with Republic Act No. 9263.

   (c) Continuing Professional Education. — The Department of Justice (DOJ),
       through the BuCor, and the Department of the Interior and Local Government
       (DILG), through the BJMP, shall establish continuing professional education
       programs on corrections administration, custodial management, human rights
       standards, crisis management, and rehabilitation and reintegration programs,
       including periodic re-certi ication as may be provided in the implementing
       rules and regulations.

SECTION 7. Integrity, Fitness, and Continuing Quali ications. — In addition to
education and training, no person shall be designated or continue to serve in a key
position if the person has been convicted by inal judgment of an offense involving
moral turpitude, or has been dismissed from the service for cause, consistent with
existing professionalization requirements for corrections personnel.

SECTION 8. Transitional Compliance Period. — Any incumbent who is currently
occupying a key position but lacks any of the statutory quali ications shall be given

                                                                          4|Page
the compliance period provided under existing law for the relevant bureau;
otherwise, the incumbent shall be relieved from the position.

SECTION 9. Funding and Implementation. — The amounts necessary to carry out
this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The DOJ and DILG
shall ensure that bureau training institutions and career courses necessary for
compliance are accessible to affected personnel.

SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days
from effectivity of this Act, the DOJ and the DILG, in coordination with the Civil Service
Commission and the concerned bureaus, shall promulgate the implementing rules
and regulations (IRR) to carry out the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 11. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected shall remain in full force
and effect.

SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, issuances or portions
thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 13. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
