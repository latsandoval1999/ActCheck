                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        On July 12, 2016, the Republic of the Philippines secured a monumental legal victory
when the Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA) in The Hague ruled unequivocally in our favor,
invalidating China's expansive and baseless "nine-dash line" claim over the South China Sea.
The historic arbitral award firmly established the Philippines' sovereign rights over its Exclusive
Economic Zone (EEZ) in the West Philippine Sea under the United Nations Convention on the
Law of the Sea (UNCLOS). A decade later, this landmark ruling remains the unassailable
bedrock of our maritime rights and territorial integrity.

       However, despite the final and binding nature of this international legal decision, the
Philippines continues to face relentless and escalating acts of aggression within our own waters.
Over the past few years, state-sanctioned incursions, violent blockades, and coordinated
intimidation tactics against our maritime personnel and fisherfolk have intensified,
demonstrating a blatant disregard for international law.

         The necessity of this legislation is underscored by a deeply troubling pattern of recent
hostilities. In June 2024, a routine rotation and resupply mission to the grounded BRP Sierra
Madre at Ayungin Shoal escalated violently when China Coast Guard personnel, armed with
axes and bladed weapons, attacked and boarded Philippine Navy vessels. This brazen assault
severely damaged our communications equipment and tragically resulted in a Filipino Navy
seaman losing his thumb. By late 2025, aggression expanded geographically and tactically, with
Chinese vessels utilizing water cannons against civilian Filipino fishing boats near Sabina Shoal;
an alarming escalation targeting the livelihoods of our citizens.

        Most recently, on July 20, 2026, just days after the tenth anniversary of our arbitral
victory, Chinese forces once again subjected our personnel at Ayungin Shoal to unprovoked
violence. During an unlawful intrusion by the China Coast Guard, a Chinese officer struck a
Philippine Navy sailor on the head with a wooden baton. This physical violence was
accompanied by a sophisticated psychological warfare campaign, including the circulation of an
AI-generated, racist video by Chinese state media intended to distort facts and humiliate the
Filipino people. These continuous illegal, coercive, aggressive, and deceptive (ICAD) activities
highlight the urgent need for a unified national response.
         This proposed measure seeks to declare July 12 of every year as the National West
Philippine Sea Victory Day, to be observed as a special working holiday. Establishing this
national observance serves a multi-fold purpose: it preserves the legacy of our diplomatic and
legal triumph, fortifies the patriotic resolve of the Filipino people, and institutionalizes a national
consciousness that our sovereign rights cannot be diluted, coerced, or negotiated away. While
several local government units across the country have already passed ordinances
commemorating this victory, this bill elevates those grassroots initiatives to a national mandate,
cementing the 2016 arbitral award into our permanent historical and cultural record.

        Celebrating this victory is not an act of hostility, but a steadfast affirmation of
international law. It’s a constant reminder to our government and our citizens that right makes
might, and that the West Philippine Sea undeniably belongs to the Filipino people.

        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:
 1   SECTION. 1. National West Philippine Sea Victory Day. – In order to commemorate the
 2   Philippines' triumph on the West Philippine Sea dispute before the Permanent Court of
 3   Arbitration (PCA) in The Hague, Netherlands, July twelve of every year is hereby declared the
 4   National West Philippine Sea Victory Day which will be observed annually as a special working
 5   holiday.
 6   SEC. 2. Lead Agency. – The Department of Foreign Affairs, in coordination with relevant
 7   government agencies, is designated as the lead agency in organizing and coordinating activities
 8   for the National West Philippine Sea Victory Day. The planned activities must highlight the
 9   importance of the historic PCA decision upholding the Philippines' sovereign rights in the West
10   Philippine Sea, inspire and instill a sense of patriotism and pride in every Filipino, constantly
11   remind the government to continually uphold our sovereign rights and territorial integrity, and
12   emphasize that the policies the government must pursue are consistent with Philippine
13   Constitution, national interest, and recognized principles of international law, particularly the
14   United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS).
15   SEC. 3. Information Dissemination and Public Observance. – Copies of this Act shall be
16   distributed to all colleges and universities, local government units (LGUs), and all government
17   agencies. Furthermore, these institutions and agencies are hereby enjoined to hold
18   comprehensive information activities to rally support and instill in the consciousness of the
19   Filipino people the significance of the 12 July 2016 Arbitral Award on the South China Sea.
20   These activities shall emphasize the Philippines' sovereign rights within its exclusive economic
21   zone, particularly with respect to the exploration and utilization of marine resources.
22   SEC. 4. Appropriations. – This Act will be implemented initially within the framework of the
23   budget of the DFA for the year it is enacted into law. As far as practicable, the funds required
24   will be drawn from its line budget appropriations for policy formulation, development, conduct
25   of personnel development, technical research, publication and dissemination of studies in
26   Philippine foreign policy. Thereafter, the amounts as may be necessary for the implementation
27   of this Act will be included in the budget of the DFA in the annual General Appropriations Act.
28   SEC. 5. Separability Clause. - Should any provision or part of this Act be declared
29   unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions and parts hereof, insofar as they are separable
30   from the invalid ones, shall remain in full force and effect.
31   SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders or parts thereof inconsistent with this
32   Act are hereby repealed, modified, or amended accordingly.
33   SEC. 7. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
34   Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
35   Approved,
