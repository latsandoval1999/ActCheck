                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Dangling and poorly maintained utility wires have become a common
sight in many cities and municipalities across the Philippines. These
unbundled, sagging, and abandoned cables not only contribute to visual clutter
but also pose serious risks to public safety. During typhoons, heavy rains, or
strong winds, loose wires can easily fall and endanger pedestrians, motorists,
and nearby residents. In some cases, these wires obstruct roads, interfere with
emergency operations, and increase the risk of electrical hazards.


       With the rapid expansion of telecommunications, cable, and internet
services, overhead wiring systems have multiplied in urban communities.
However, many non-operational or unused cables are left attached to utility
poles, resulting in excessive and unmanaged wiring infrastructure.


       To address this growing concern, this measure mandates all power,
telecommunications, cable, and internet providers to properly bundle sagging
wires, remove unused or non-operational cables, and ensure that aerial utility
installations meet the required safety clearances. It also empowers local
government units to enforce compliance through the regulation of permits.

                               AN ACT
     MANDATING THE PROPER MANAGEMENT, BUNDLING, AND REMOVAL
     OF DANGLING AND NON-OPERATIONAL OVERHEAD UTILITY WIRES
     AND CABLES, AND PRESCRIBING PENALTIES THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
           Congress assembled:


 1             SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Anti-Spaghetti
 2      Wires Act.”

 3             SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to ensure public
 4      safety and promote orderly infrastructure development in communities. The State
 5      recognizes that unmaintained, sagging, and abandoned overhead wires pose hazards
 6      to pedestrians and motorists, increase the risk of fire incidents, and may cause danger
 7      during typhoons and other severe weather disturbances. Toward this end, the State
 8      shall require the proper maintenance, bundling, and removal of non-operational
 9      aerial utility wires to ensure safer, more organized, and efficient utility services.




10             SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:




                                                                                                1
 1          a. Dangling Wires refer to sagging, unsecured, or improperly installed
 2             overhead utility cables that pose a risk to public safety.
 3          b. Non-Operational Wires or Cables refer to overhead wires or cables that
 4             are no longer used for the transmission or delivery of electricity,
 5             telecommunications, cable television, or internet services.
 6          c. Public Utility Providers refer to electric power distribution utilities,
 7             telecommunications companies, cable television operators, internet
 8             service providers, and other entities authorized to install or maintain aerial
 9             utility cables.
10          d. Aerial Wires or Cables refer to utility lines installed above ground using
11             poles, posts, or other overhead infrastructure.
12          SEC. 4. Responsibilities of Public Utility Providers. – All public utility
13   providers covered by this Act shall:
14          a. Properly bundle, secure, and maintain sagging or dangling aerial utility
15             wires and cables;
16          b. Remove all non-operational or abandoned wires and cables attached to
17             poles, posts, or other overhead infrastructure;
18          c. Ensure that aerial wires and cables comply with the minimum safety
19             clearance standards prescribed by relevant government agencies;
20          d. Relocate, repair, or replace damaged poles or posts that contribute to
21             unsafe or improperly positioned overhead wires; and
22          e. Conduct periodic inspections and maintenance of aerial utility
23             infrastructure within their service areas.
24          SEC. 5. Compliance Period. - All public utility providers shall comply
25   with the requirements of this Act within one (1) year from its effectivity. Thereafter,
26   continuous maintenance and removal of non-operational wires shall be undertaken
27   as part of regular infrastructure management.

 1          SEC. 6. Enforcement and Monitoring. - Local government units (LGUs)
 2   shall monitor and ensure compliance with the provisions of this Act within their
 3   respective jurisdictions. LGUs may conduct inspections and coordinate with
 4   national regulatory agencies in identifying violations and enforcing corrective
 5   actions.
 6          SEC. 7. Penalties.
 7          a. Any public utility company and similar entities covered by this Act that
 8              fail to comply with the requirements of Section 3 of this Act shall pay a
 9              fine of not less than One Hundred Thousand Pesos (P100,000.00) for each
10              day of non-compliance, computed from the time of filing of the complaint
11              to the proper authority.
12          b. Any public utility company and similar entities covered by this Act that
13              fail to comply with the requirements of Section 4 and 5 of this Act shall
14              pay a fine of not less than Three Hundred Thousand Pesos (P300,000.00)
15              The Head of Agency and other concerned officials of the LGU who fail
16              to comply with the requirements of Section 5 of this Act shall be liable
17              for administrative sanctions.
18          c. Any public utility company and similar entities covered by this Act whose
19              installation or relocation of its wires or cables caused any damage to
20              property or injury to persons shall shoulder the full costs of the damage
21              and/or medical expenses incurred as a result thereof, and pay a fine of not
22              less than double the amount of such costs.
23          d. The LGU concerned may suspend or revoke the permits of any public
24              utility company and similar entities covered by this Act for any violations
25              of this Act.
