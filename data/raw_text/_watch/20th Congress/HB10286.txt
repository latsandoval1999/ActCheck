 AN ACT PROVIDING INCENTIVES AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR
THE MODERNIZATION OF PUBLIC UTILITY JEEPNEYS AND TRICYCLES,
 ENSURING THE PROTECTION OF DRIVERS AND SMALL OPERATORS
                  DURING THE TRANSITION


                           EXPLANATORY NOTE


The transition to modernized public utility vehicles has left many small,
individual jeepney and tricycle operators and their drivers behind, unable to
shoulder the cost of new units and fearful of losing their livelihood to
consolidation requirements favoring large corporate operators.

This bill ensures that the modernization program includes accessible financing,
extended and graduated compliance timelines for small operators, and guaranteed
priority hiring of displaced drivers by modernized fleets, so that modernization
improves rather than eliminates the livelihood of ordinary drivers.

Respectfully submitted,



REP. ALEXANDER T. PIMENTEL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

    SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Inclusive Public Transport
Modernization Act."
      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State shall pursue the
modernization of public transportation in a manner that protects the livelihood of
small operators and drivers, and does not result in their displacement without
adequate support.
      SECTION 3. Low-Interest Financing. — The Land Bank of the Philippines
and the Development Bank of the Philippines shall offer a special financing
window for the acquisition of modernized public utility vehicles by individual and
small cooperative operators, at an interest rate not exceeding four percent (4%) per
annum, payable over a period of not less than seven (7) years.
       SECTION 4. Graduated Compliance Timeline. — Small operators owning
three (3) units or fewer shall be given an extended compliance period of not less
than five (5) years from the effectivity of this Act, and shall not be compelled to
consolidate with corporate operators against their will.
       SECTION 5. Driver Protection. — Displaced drivers of phased-out units
shall be given priority in hiring by modernized fleet operators servicing the same
route, and shall be entitled to free retraining under the Technical Education and
Skills Development Authority (TESDA).

       SECTION 6. Transitional Livelihood Assistance. — Drivers whose units are
undergoing modernization-related downtime shall receive a transitional
livelihood allowance for a period not exceeding three (3) months.
      SECTION 7. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid, the other provisions not affected shall remain in force.
      SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, and
issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
      SECTION 9. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
