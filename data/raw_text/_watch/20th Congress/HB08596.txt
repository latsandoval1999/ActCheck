                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

The number of senior citizens in the Philippines continues to grow, and many elderly
Filipinos now require long-term care, medical attention, and assistance with daily
activities. While families traditionally provide this care, many households face
 inancial dif iculties, work obligations, migration, or distance that make full-time
caregiving dif icult. As a result, frail, sickly, disabled, abandoned, or indigent senior
citizens are often left without the consistent support they need, especially in
provinces where institutional care facilities are limited.

The Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010 (Republic Act No. 9994) recognizes that
caring for the elderly is a shared responsibility of families, communities, and the
government. The law calls for integrated health and social services for senior citizens,
including specialized geriatric care and community support programs. Meanwhile,
the Senior Citizens Center Act of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 7876) requires
every city and municipality to establish senior citizen centers. However, these
community centers are not always designed to provide residential care, assisted
living, or continuous supervision for seniors who require intensive support.

This bill therefore proposes the establishment of a Senior Citizen Caregiving Facility
in the Second District of the Province of Southern Leyte. The facility will provide
residential and day-care services for quali ied elderly individuals, particularly

indigent and high-dependency seniors who need continuous care, rehabilitation,
nutrition support, and psychosocial services. By offering a safe and professionally
managed environment, the facility will complement existing community programs,
help ease the burden on families and hospitals, and ensure that vulnerable seniors
receive proper care and dignity in their later years.

The measure also aligns with regional development priorities that recognize the
growing need for structured elderly care services in Eastern Visayas. Through
coordination with the Department of Social Welfare and Development, the
Department of Health, and local government units, the proposed facility will operate
within the government’s existing social protection framework while remaining
subject to regular appropriations and oversight.

respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Second District of Southern
Leyte Senior Citizen Caregiving Facility Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the welfare of senior citizens by ensuring access to appropriate long-term
care, assisted living, health support, and social services, particularly for indigent,
abandoned, neglected, unattached, homeless, frail, sickly, bedridden, or high-
dependency senior citizens who can no longer be adequately supported at home or in
the community. This policy shall be implemented in a manner that complements
existing senior citizens programs, including LGU-based senior citizens centers and
integrated health services for senior citizens.

SECTION 3. De inition of Terms. — As used in this Act:

   (a) Facility refers to the Senior Citizen Caregiving Facility established under this
       Act.

   (b) Senior Citizen has the meaning provided under existing senior citizens laws.

                                                                           3|Page
   (c) Indigent Senior Citizen refers to a senior citizen who is frail, sickly, or with
       disability, and without any pension or permanent source of income,
       compensation, or financial assistance from relatives to support basic needs, as
       determined in accordance with applicable guidelines.

   (d) High-Dependency Senior Citizen refers to a senior citizen requiring
       assistance with activities of daily living (ADLs) such as feeding, bathing,
       toileting, mobility, medication administration, or continuous supervision due
       to physical or cognitive impairment, as may be further defined in the
       implementing rules and regulations.

SECTION 4. Establishment of the Facility. — There is hereby established a
government-run Senior Citizen Caregiving Facility in the Second District of the
Province of Southern Leyte, which shall serve as a residential and day-care institution
providing assisted living, continuous care, and other services for quali ied senior
citizens, subject to admission and discharge policies under this Act and its
implementing rules and regulations.

SECTION 5. Implementing and Supervising Agencies. —

   (a) Lead Agency. — The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)
       shall be the lead implementing and supervising agency for the establishment,
       operation, and management of the Facility.

   (b) Technical Support and Coordination. — The DSWD shall coordinate with
       the Department of Health (DOH) for medical, nursing, geriatric, preventive
       health, and referral protocols, consistent with the national health program and
       integrated health services for senior citizens.

   (c) Coordination with NCSC and LGUs. — The DSWD shall coordinate with the
       National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC), the Provincial Government of
       Southern Leyte, and the concerned LGUs for bene iciary identi ication,
       community linkages, after-care, and reintegration services, and to complement
       the programs and services of senior citizens centers established under law.

   (d) Facility Management. — The DSWD shall designate a Facility Administrator
       and create an internal management structure to ensure ef icient and
       accountable delivery of services.


                                                                           4|Page
SECTION 6. Intended Bene iciaries; Admission Priority. — The Facility shall
prioritize admission of:

   (a) Indigent senior citizens;
   (b) Abandoned, neglected, unattached, homeless, frail, sickly, or bedridden senior
       citizens;
   (c) Senior citizens with disability or high-dependency care needs; and
   (d) Other senior citizens who may be temporarily admitted for respite care or
       short-term custodial care, subject to capacity, standards, and payment
       arrangements, if any, as may be authorized in the implementing rules and
       regulations.

SECTION 7. Scope of Services. — The Facility shall provide, at a minimum, the
following:

   (a) Residential care and temporary custodial care, including respite care;
   (b) Basic medical and nursing services and medication management, consistent
       with DOH standards;
   (c) Rehabilitation and physical therapy services, or referral arrangements for the
       same;
   (d) Nutrition, wellness, and preventive health programs, including health
       education;
   (e) Mental health, psychosocial, and recreational and spiritual support activities;
   (f) Case management, counseling, and family/community reintegration services;
       and
   (g) Referral and transport coordination to hospitals and specialized institutions
       when necessary.

SECTION 8. Site and LGU Participation. — The Provincial Government of Southern
Leyte and the LGUs within the Second District shall assist in identifying or providing
a suitable site in the Municipality of Sogod, Southern Leyte, securing permits, and
integrating local health and social welfare support services to reduce national
government expenditures.

SECTION 9. Staf ing Pattern and Capability Building. — The DSWD, in
coordination with the DOH and the Civil Service Commission (CSC), shall determine a
cost-ef icient staf ing pattern consisting of quali ied caregivers, nurses, social
workers, nutritionists, rehabilitation personnel, and administrative and support staff,

                                                                           5|Page
subject to existing civil service, budgeting, and compensation laws. The DOH shall
support capability building and training of staff in geriatric care, consistent with the
State’s policy of training personnel to specialize in geriatric health concerns.

SECTION 10. Standards, Licensing, and Safeguards. —

   (a) Standards of Care. — The DOH, in coordination with the DSWD, shall issue
       minimum standards for basic medical and nursing services, infection
       prevention and control, medication storage and administration, emergency
       response, and referral protocols.

   (b) Operations Manual. — The DSWD shall adopt an operations manual covering
       admission/discharge criteria, consent and decision-making support,
       residents’ rights and grievance procedures, visiting policies, record-keeping,
       and coordination with senior citizens centers and community-based services.

   (c) Protection of Residents. — The Facility shall adopt measures to prevent
       abuse, neglect, exploitation, and discrimination of residents, and shall
       establish internal reporting and response mechanisms consistent with
       existing laws and regulations.

SECTION 11. Appropriations. —

   (a) Initial Capital Outlay. — The amount necessary for the construction,
       renovation, or establishment of the Facility shall be charged against the capital
       outlay appropriations of the DSWD under the current or subsequent General
       Appropriations Act (GAA), and thereafter included in the annual GAA.

   (b) Annual Operating Budget. — The amount necessary for personnel services,
       maintenance and other operating expenses, and program implementation
       shall be included in the annual budget of the DSWD.

   (c) Counterpart Support. — The Provincial Government of Southern Leyte and
       concerned LGUs may provide counterpart funding, utilities, land, personnel
       augmentation, or in-kind contributions, consistent with local development
       plans and applicable laws.

SECTION 12. Acceptance of Grants and Donations. — The DSWD may accept
grants, donations, and endowments from public or private sources for the


                                                                            6|Page
improvement and operation of the Facility, subject to existing accounting and auditing
laws and regulations.

SECTION 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DSWD, in coordination with the DOH, the NCSC, the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM), and the concerned LGUs, shall
promulgate the implementing rules and regulations (IRR) necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act. The IRR shall be consistent with this Act and shall not
diminish or unduly restrict the bene iciaries and services herein established, but may
prescribe procedures, standards, and documentation requirements necessary to
ensure proper implementation and accountability.

SECTION 14. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force
and effect.

SECTION 15. Repealing Clause. — All laws, rules, and regulations inconsistent with
this Act are hereby repealed or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 16. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
