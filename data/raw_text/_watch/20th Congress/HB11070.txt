                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE


     The Philippines continues to face the challenges of high electricity costs, grid
congestion, energy security, and the need to accelerate the adoption of renewable energy
and energy storage technologies.

      Behind-the-meter electricity generation and energy storage systems, including
rooftop solar photovoltaic systems and battery energy storage systems, enable households,
businesses, and other electricity end-users to generate and store electricity primarily for
their own consumption. These systems can reduce dependence on electricity supplied
through the distribution system, improve energy resilience, and contribute to the
reduction of system losses and peak demand.

       Existing regulatory and administrative requirements, however, may unnecessarily
increase the cost and delay the installation of such systems, particularly where the system
is designed and equipped to prevent the export of electricity to the distribution system.

      This Act seeks to remove unnecessary regulatory and administrative barriers while
preserving legitimate electrical, structural, fire, environmental, and public safety
requirements. It distinguishes between behind-the-meter systems that are incapable of
exporting electricity and systems capable of exporting electricity to the distribution system,
subjecting the latter to appropriate interconnection requirements.

      Accordingly, this measure promotes consumer choice, encourages private
investment in distributed energy resources, strengthens energy security, and facilitates the
wider adoption of renewable energy and energy storage technologies.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                              “AN ACT
     PROMOTING THE INSTALLATION OF BEHIND-THE-METER ELECTRICITY
     GENERATION AND ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS, AMENDING FOR THE
     PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9136, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
     "ELECTRIC  POWER    INDUSTRY    REFORM    ACT   OF   2001”

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Sariling Kuryente Act."

    SECTION 2. Generation Sector. - Section 6 of Republic Act No. 9136 is hereby
amended to read as follows:

                "SEC. 6. Generation Sector. - Generation of electric power, a
           business affected with public interest, shall be competitive and open.

                 Upon the effectivity of this Act, any new generation company shall,
           before it operates, secure from the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC)
           a certificate of compliance pursuant to the standards set forth in this
           Act, as well as health, safety and environmental clearances from the
           appropriate government agencies under existing laws.

                AN END-USER THAT INSTALLS AND OPERATES A BEHIND-THE-
           METER ELECTRICITY GENERATION OR ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM
           FOR ITS OWN USE SHALL NOT, BY REASON THEREOF, BE
           CONSIDERED A GENERATION COMPANY. A "BEHIND-THE-METER
           ELECTRICITY GENERATION OR ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM"
           REFERS TO A SYSTEM INSTALLED ON THE END-USER'S SIDE OF
           THE POINT OF CONNECTION WITH THE DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM
           AND INTENDED PRIMARILY TO GENERATE OR STORE ELECTRICITY
           FOR THE END-USER'S OWN USE.

               A SYSTEM USING EQUIPMENT CERTIFIED UNDER STANDARDS
           RECOGNIZED BY THE ERC TO PREVENT THE EXPORT OF
           ELECTRICITY TO THE DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM SHALL NOT REQUIRE
           A  CERTIFICATE   OF   COMPLIANCE                  OR     OTHER       PRIOR
           AUTHORIZATION FROM THE ERC.

                NOTHING IN THIS SECTION SHALL BE CONSTRUED AS
           EXEMPTING AN END-USER FROM APPLICABLE ELECTRICAL,
           BUILDING, FIRE, STRUCTURAL, ENVIRONMENTAL, OR OTHER
           PUBLIC SAFETY STANDARDS AND REQUIREMENTS, EXCEPT AS
           EXPRESSLY PROVIDED UNDER THIS ACT OR ITS IMPLEMENTING
           RULES AND REGULATIONS.

                 x x x.”

      SECTION 3. Functions of Distribution Utilities. - Section 23 of Republic Act No.
9136 is hereby amended to read as follows:

                  "SEC 23. Functions of Distribution Utilities. - A distribution utility
           shall have the obligation to provide distribution services and connections
           to its system for any end-user within its franchise area consistent with
           the Distribution Code.

                  Any entity engaged therein shall provide open and non-
           discriminatory access to its distribution system to all users. Any
           distribution utility shall be entitled to impose and collect distribution
           wheeling charges and connection fees from such end-users as approved
           by the ERC. A distribution utility shall have the obligation to supply
           electricity in the least-cost manner to its captive market, subject to the
           collection of retail rate duly approved by the ERC.

                A BEHIND-THE-METER ELECTRICITY GENERATION OR
           ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM USING EQUIPMENT CERTIFIED UNDER
           STANDARDS RECOGNIZED BY THE ERC TO PREVENT THE EXPORT
           OF ELECTRICITY TO THE DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM SHALL NOT
           REQUIRE THE PRIOR APPROVAL OF, OR ANY AGREEMENT, STUDY,
           METER   REPLACEMENT,     OR   CHARGE   IMPOSED  BY  THE
           DISTRIBUTION UTILITY. THE END-USER MAY BE REQUIRED ONLY
           TO GIVE NOTICE OF THE INSTALLATION IN THE FORM AND
           MANNER PRESCRIBED BY THE ERC, WHICH NOTICE SHALL NOT
           CONSTITUTE    AN     APPLICATION   FOR    APPROVAL   OR
           INTERCONNECTION AND SHALL NOT BE USED BY THE
           DISTRIBUTION UTILITY AS A BASIS TO DELAY, DENY, OR
           CONDITION THE INSTALLATION.

                THE DISTRIBUTION UTILITY SHALL NOT DELAY, CONDITION,
           OR OTHERWISE RESTRICT THE INSTALLATION OF A SYSTEM
           COVERED BY THIS PARAGRAPH THROUGH ANY REQUIREMENT,
           PROCEDURE, INSPECTION, STUDY, AGREEMENT, EQUIPMENT
           SPECIFICATION, OR CHARGE NOT EXPRESSLY AUTHORIZED BY
           THE ERC.

               AN APPLICATION FOR THE INTERCONNECTION OF A SYSTEM
           CAPABLE OF EXPORTING ELECTRICITY TO THE DISTRIBUTION
            SYSTEM SHALL BE ACTED UPON WITHIN TEN (10) WORKING DAYS
            FROM RECEIPT OF THE COMPLETE REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED
            BY THE ERC. UPON THE LAPSE OF SUCH PERIOD WITHOUT ACTION,
            THE APPLICATION SHALL BE DEEMED APPROVED: PROVIDED,
            THAT THE DISTRIBUTION UTILITY SHALL NOT DECLARE AN
            APPLICATION INCOMPLETE ON THE BASIS OF ANY REQUIREMENT
            NOT EXPRESSLY PRESCRIBED BY THE ERC.

                NO DISTRIBUTION UTILITY MAY IMPOSE ANY REQUIREMENT,
            PROCEDURE, STUDY, INSPECTION, EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATION, OR
            CHARGE    NOT   AUTHORIZED     UNDER    UNIFORM    RULES
            PROMULGATED BY THE ERC.

                 ANY DISPUTE ARISING FROM THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THIS
            SECTION SHALL BE RESOLVED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE RULES
            AND PROCEDURES PRESCRIBED BY THE ERC.

                  x x x."

      DEVELOPMENT."

       SECTION 5. Protection Against Unreasonable Restrictions. - No homeowners'
association, subdivision developer, property manager, or similar entity shall prohibit the
installation of a behind-the-meter electricity generation or energy storage system on
property owned or exclusively controlled by an end-user.

      No prior approval, consent, or authorization from such entity shall be required as a
condition for the installation of a system covered by this Act, except for reasonable
requirements that are necessary to address documented structural safety, public safety, or
the protection of common property. Such requirements shall not be used to prohibit or
unreasonably delay the installation of the system. For this purpose, the Department of
Energy shall develop and publish model rules that such entities may adopt.

      SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Energy, in consultation with the Energy
Regulatory Commission and other relevant government agencies and stakeholders, shall
promulgate the implementing rules and regulations of this Act.

      The ERC shall establish uniform, simplified, and technology-neutral rules for the
notice, certification, and interconnection of behind-the-meter electricity generation and
energy storage systems, including clear standards for equipment capable of preventing the
export of electricity to the distribution system.


      The implementing rules and regulations shall not impose requirements, procedures,
or charges inconsistent with the provisions of this Act.

       SECTION 7. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full
force and effect.

      SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
