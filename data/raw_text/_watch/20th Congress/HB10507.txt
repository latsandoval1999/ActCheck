                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Republic Act No. 11213, otherwise known as the “Tax Amnesty Act,” established an
Estate Tax Amnesty to enable quali ied estates to settle estate tax obligations under
simpli ied procedures and subject to the immunities and privileges provided by law.

The period for availing of the Estate Tax Amnesty was subsequently extended by
Republic Act No. 11569 and Republic Act No. 11956. The most recent statutory
extension allowed availment until June 14, 2025, covering the estates of decedents
who died on or before May 31, 2022.

The continued existence of unsettled estates demonstrates the need for a further
extension. Heirs, executors, administrators, transferees, and bene iciaries continue to
encounter dif iculties in obtaining civil registry records, titles, tax declarations,
certi ications, and other documents required for estate settlement and transfer.

A further extension will encourage voluntary compliance, facilitate the transfer and
registration of inherited properties, reduce administrative backlogs, and generate
government revenue from estates that may otherwise remain unsettled. It will
likewise provide taxpayers additional time to comply with the documentary and
procedural requirements of the Estate Tax Amnesty.

until June 30, 2028, while retaining the existing statutory coverage, quali ications,

Act No. 11213, as amended.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Further Extension of the
Estate Tax Amnesty Act of 2026.”

SECTION 2. Amendment of Section 4 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended. —

Section 4 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended, is hereby further amended to read
as follows:

     “SEC. 4. Estate Tax Amnesty. — Any person, whether natural or juridical, who is
     liable for the payment of estate tax may avail of the Estate Tax Amnesty under this
     Act with respect to the estate of a decedent who died on or before May 31, 2024,
     subject to the conditions and requirements provided in this Act and its
     implementing rules and regulations.

     “The Estate Tax Amnesty shall cover the estate of the decedent, whether the estate
     tax liability is delinquent, unpaid, or has accrued penalties, surcharges, and
     interest, subject to the exclusions and limitations provided under this Act.

     of the requirements for the iling of the Estate Tax Amnesty Return, payment of the
     estate amnesty tax, and submission of the prescribed documentary
     requirements.””

SECTION 3. Amendment of Section 6 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended. —

Section 6 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended, is hereby further amended to read
as follows:

     “SEC. 6. Availment of the Estate Tax Amnesty; When and Where to File and
     Pay. — The executor or administrator of the estate, or, if there is no executor or
     administrator appointed, the legal heirs, transferees, or bene iciaries, who wish to
     avail of the Estate Tax Amnesty shall ile the Estate Tax Amnesty Return and pay
     the corresponding estate amnesty tax within June 15, 2025 until June 30, 2028,
     in accordance with the forms, procedures, and documentary requirements
     prescribed under this Act and its implementing rules and regulations.

     “The Estate Tax Amnesty Return shall be iled with, and the estate amnesty tax
     shall be paid through, the Revenue District Of ice having jurisdiction over the
     estate, any authorized agent bank, revenue collection agent, or other authorized
     payment facility, as may be prescribed by the Bureau of Internal Revenue.

     “The iling of the Estate Tax Amnesty Return, payment of the estate amnesty tax,
     and submission of the complete documentary requirements within the period
     prescribed in this Section shall be required for valid availment of the Estate Tax
     Amnesty.

     “For purposes of this Act, the Estate Tax Amnesty shall be available only with
     respect to the estate of a decedent who died on or before May 31, 2024, subject
     to the other quali ications, exclusions, conditions, and requirements provided in
     this Act and its implementing rules and regulations.””

SECTION 4. Amendment of Section 8 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended. —

Section 8 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended, is hereby further amended to read
as follows:

     “SEC. 8. Immunities and Privileges. — Estates covered by the Estate Tax
     Amnesty, which have fully complied with all the conditions set forth in this Act,

SECTION 5. Preservation of Other Requirements and Conditions. — Except as
expressly amended by this Act, all quali ications, exclusions, conditions, tax rates,
documentary requirements, procedures, and other provisions of Republic Act No.
11213, as amended, shall remain in full force and effect.

All implementing rules and regulations issued under Republic Act No. 11213, as
amended, shall continue to apply insofar as they are not inconsistent with this Act.

SECTION 6. Treatment of Payments and Documentary Requirements. —

Payments made in connection with the Estate Tax Amnesty on or before June 30, 2028
shall be recognized as estate amnesty tax payments only upon the iling of the Estate
Tax Amnesty Return and submission of the complete documentary requirements
within the period and in the manner prescribed by this Act and its implementing rules
and regulations.

If the prescribed documentary requirements are not submitted within the applicable
period, the payment shall be treated in accordance with applicable tax laws and
regulations, without prejudice to its application against the regular estate tax liability,
including applicable penalties, interests, and additions to the tax.

SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within thirty (30) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Finance, upon the recommendation of
