                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to mandate the National Food Authority (NFA) to establish,
operate, and maintain rice drying facilities nationwide to support Filipino rice
farmers, reduce post-harvest losses, and strengthen the country’s rice supply chain.
Ensuring accessible and adequate drying infrastructure will enable the farmers to
preserve the quality of their harvest and directly sell their produce to the
government at fair and competitive prices.

       Rice farmers across the Philippines continue to face significant post-harvest
challenges, particularly in drying palay during peak harvest seasons and adverse
weather conditions. Without access to reliable drying facilities, farmers often incur
losses due to spoilage, reduced grain quality, and lower market value. These
constraints force many farmers to sell their produce immediately at lower prices to
private traders who possess drying capabilities, thereby limiting farmers’ income
and bargaining power.

       This proposed measure addresses these gaps by directing the NFA to procure,
construct, install, and manage sufficient rice drying facilities that are strategically
located and easily accessible to farming communities. It also allows farmers to use
these facilities at no cost, provided that the dried palay is intended for direct sale to
the NFA. This system strengthens the NFA’s capacity to procure high-quality rice
while empowering farmers to secure better returns for their harvest.

      Furthermore, this measure promotes inclusive agricultural development,
enhances food security, and supports the livelihood of Filipino rice farmers by
improving post-harvest systems and reinforcing government support mechanisms.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
MANDATING THE NATIONAL FOOD AUTHORITY TO ESTABLISH RICE
DRYING FACILITIES, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “NFA Rice Drying
Facilities Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby the declared policy of the State to
recognize the indispensable role of the agricultural sector in national development
and to ensure its sustainability and competitiveness. Towards this end, the State
shall promote an enabling environment for rice producers by providing access to
appropriate equipment, post-harvest facilities, and necessary infrastructure to
enhance their capacity to efficiently sell and market their produce, thereby
improving their productivity and income.

       SEC. 3. Drying Facilities. – The National Food Authority (NFA) shall procure,
build, install, operate, manage, and/or maintain rice drying facilities sufficient in
quantity and geographically accessible to local farmers to enable it to buy rice
directly from local farmers. These drying facilities shall be used by the NFA solely to
dry rice to be bought directly from local farmers.

        The NFA shall allow local farmers to use these drying facilities at no cost for
rice to be directly sold to the NFA.

       SEC. 4. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the immediate
implementation of this Act shall be primarily charged against the current year’s
available appropriations of the DA. The State may also utilize additional funding
sourced from the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR), the
Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), and the Malampaya Fund: Provided,
That such allocations are made in accordance with existing laws and special
budgetary rules governing said funds.

      Thereafter, funding for the continued implementation of this Act shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act, following the submission of a

        SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DA shall, through the NFA, issue the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act, including the procedures for
use of drying facilities by local farmers.

       SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – Should any part of this Act be declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions hereof that are not affected thereby
shall continue to be in full force and effect.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, proclamations,
rules and regulations, and issuances, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper or general circulation.
