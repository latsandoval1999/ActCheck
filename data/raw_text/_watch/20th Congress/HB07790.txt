                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Pasacao-Balatan Coastal Road in the Province of Camarines
Sur, into a national road.

Roads are considered as the backbone of one’s province’s infrastructure as it connects people
to goods and services, access to healthcare, social activities, and employment opportunities,
and efficient and faster travel to one’s workplace and school. In fact, roads is one of the major
dictations of a developing city or municipality or province as it promotes their overall
development due to their efficiencies on how they reduce transportation costs and travel time.

The Pasacao-Balatan Coastal Road is a road that connects two (2) barangays in the
Municipality of Pasacao, four (4) barangays in the Municipality of San Fernando, four (4)
barangays in the Municipality of Minalabac, two (2) barangays in the Municipality of Bula and
three (3) barangays in the Municipality of Balatan that stretches from the second congressional
district to the fifth congressional district for a long forty kilometers and sixty-nine meters
(40.69km). This is one of the longest roads in the province that has truly made an impact to
the entire province for the delivery of goods and services within the province that contributed
to its economic development.

However, just like any other infrastructure, roads are prone to wear and tear that are in need
of either maintenance or immediate repair. As much as local government units acknowledge
their significance within their jurisdictions for efficient and effective flow of goods and services,
and resources, their budgets are limited and need the assistance of the national government
to both maintain and repair such roads.

Therefore, in order to ensure the Pasacao-Balatan Coastal Road’s orderly function in the
province of Camarines Sur, the conversion of the said road into a national road is essential.

 AN ACT CONVERTING THE PASACAO-BALATAN COASTAL BYPASS ROAD INTO A
                           NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. – The Pasacao-Balatan Coastal Road connecting Barangays Odicon and
Balagan in the Municipality of Pasacao, Barangays Bical, Gñaran, Pinamasagan and Cotmo
in the Municipality of San Fernando, Barangays Bagolatao, San Antonio, Salingogon, and
Hamoraon in the Municipality of Minalabac, Barangays Itanganon and Caorasan in the
Municipality of Buila, and Barangays Camangahan, Pararahan, and Luluasan in the
Municipality of Balatan in the Province of Camarines Sur, consisting of Forty Kilometers and
Sixty-Nine Meters (40.69km), more or less, is hereby converted into a national road.

SECTION 2. – The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue
the necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 3. – This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
