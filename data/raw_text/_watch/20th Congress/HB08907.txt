                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) have long been regarded as modern-
day heroes for their significant contributions to the Philippine economy and to
the welfare of their families. After spending much of their productive years
working abroad, many OFWs return home upon reaching senior age, often facing
limited employment prospects and economic uncertainty.

       As a declared policy, the State recognizes and upholds the fundamental
rights of senior citizens, ensuring their equal access to government services,
programs, discounts, and privileges in acknowledgment of their invaluable
contributions to society. Returning senior OFWs, who have likewise contributed
immensely to national development, merit targeted support to facilitate their
reintegration.

      This proposed measure seeks to establish employment facilitation and
entrepreneurship programs specifically designed for returning senior OFWs. By
promoting job placement assistance, skills enhancement, and enterprise
development opportunities, the bill aims to support their continued productivity,
financial security, and dignified participation in society.
         Through this legislation, the State affirms its commitment to honoring the
  sacrifices of senior OFWs and empowering them to remain active contributors to
  the nation’s socio-economic development.

        In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

                               CHAPTER 1
                           GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Returning Senior
OFW Act.”


SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is declared the policy of the State to
acknowledge the fundamental rights of senior citizens, ensuring their equal
access to government services, discounts, and privileges, in recognition of their
invaluable contribution to society. It is likewise the policy of the State to afford
full protection to labor, local and overseas, organized and unorganized, and
promote full employment opportunities for all. Towards this end, it shall be the
State’s responsibility to protect the Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs).


       The State shall promote inclusivity, equity, and accessibility to goods and
services so that senior OFWs, through standardized benefits and incentives, are
afforded adequate social services, decent standard of living, and improved quality
of life.


SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act, the following terms and
phrases are defined as follows:


   (a) Overseas Filipino Worker (OFW) refers to a Filipino who is to be engaged,
       is engaged, or has been engaged in renumerated activity in a country of
       which he or she is not an immigrant, citizen, or permanent resident or is
       not awaiting naturalization, recognition, or admission, whether land-based
       or sea-based regardless of status; excluding a Filipino engaged under a
       government-recognized exchange visitor program for cultural and
       educational purposes. For purposes of this guidelines, a person engaged in
       renumerated activity covers a person who has been contracted for overseas
       employment but has yet to leave the Philippines, regardless of status, and
       includes “Overseas Contract Workers”. The term “OFW” is synonymous
       to “Migrant Worker”.
   (b) Senior OFW refers to an OFW who is a senior citizen or at least sixty (60)
       years old.
   (c) Health Care Voucher refers to assistance given to a qualified senior OFW
       beneficiary in the form of a voucher that has monetary value, to be utilized
       for the healthcare needs of the senior OFW beneficiary.
   (d) Health care provider refers to any of the following:

SEC. 4. Coverage. – OFWs, as defined in Republic Act No. 11641 or the
“Department of Migrant Workers Act”, who are resident senior citizens aged 60
years old and above, returned to the Philippines irrespective of date, and have
served and completed at least one employment or service contract overseas.


SEC. 5. Health Care Voucher Program for Senior OFWs. – Senior OFWs who
are 71 years old and above shall be entitled a one-time voucher with monetary
value of Fifty Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00). This voucher shall be used
exclusively for the healthcare needs of the beneficiary and may be applied solely
toward essential healthcare services, including but not limited to primary care,
diagnostic, laboratory, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative services, as well as
essential healthcare products, including prescription medicines and other
approved medical supplies, provided or dispensed by duly accredited healthcare
providers. Further, the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) shall formulate

SEC. 6. Employment Facilitation Program for Senior OFWs. – The
government shall encourage and provide incentives to local employers that
employ senior OFWs, regardless of employment status, to enable them to
continue being economically productive and to share their knowledge and
expertise to the local economy. Private entities that shall employ senior citizens
as employees, upon the effectivity of this Act, shall be entitled to an additional
deduction from their gross income equivalent to thirty five (35%) of the total
amount paid as salaries, wages, benefits, and trainings provided to senior OFWs,
subject to the provision of Section 34 of the National Internal Revenue Code
(NIRC), as amended: Provided, however, That such employment shall continue
for a period of at least six (6) months: Provided, further, That the annual income
of the senior OFW does not exceed the latest poverty threshold as determined by
the National Statistical Coordination Board (NSCB) of the Department of
Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) for that year.


SEC. 7. Entrepreneurship Program for Senior OFWs. – The Department of
Migrant Workers and the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA)
shall establish and sustain a dedicated entrepreneurship and training program for
senior OFWs, whether individual or group, who have developed an
entrepreneurial mindset to enable them to continue being economically
productive upon their return to the Philippines, in collaboration with national
government agencies, local government units, Department of Trade and Industry
(DTI), Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA),
Department of Tourism (DOT), Department of Finance (DOF), Department of

SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DMW shall, in coordination with the OWWA,
DOLE, Department of Budget and Management (DBM), DTI, DEPDev, NCSC,
TESDA, DOT, DOT, DepEd, CHED, and other concerned government agencies,
as well as civil society organizations, non-government organizations, and
people’s organizations, promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act.


      The intended guidelines must include the establishment of the
corresponding Reintegration Application System for the proper administration,
operation, and maintenance thereof. It shall likewise set the mechanism in the
identification of beneficiaries of the programs and benefits under this Act.


SEC. 9. Implementing Guidelines for Programs. – The DMW shall promulgate
the necessary implementing guidelines of the programs and services covered
under this Act.


SEC. 10. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the implementation of the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act
following its enactment into law.


SEC. 11. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act shall be held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not otherwise affected shall
remain in full force and effect.
