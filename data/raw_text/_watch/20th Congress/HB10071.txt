                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

     The barangay, as one’s immediate community, plays a significant role
in one’s personal growth and development as one creates ties to peers, the
local sports team, and their neighborhood and its residents. As such, it is
important that the barangay is a safe setting where correct values are
appreciated and positive influences are reinforced.

     In this era of digital technology, however, people are more susceptible to
various forms of influence through multimedia sources, making them more
exposed to external influences. The rise of microcelebrities in the form of
social media influencers has been widespread, causing millions of followers
on the digital platform.

     Although many influencers have had positive effects on followers,
especially in pushing for various social advocacies, studies have shown that
exposure and consumption of social media are highly linked with tendencies
of depression, anxiety, and antisocial personality disorder, to name a few,
which can lead to impairments in vital areas of life.

     This initiative seeks to promote the beneficial effects of influencers at
the barangay level, where people can relate to, engage with, and interact with
one another in person rather than online. These community influencers
could be leaders and champions in public service, volunteer or civic
organizations, or they could be strong advocates for education, climate
change impact, reproductive health, and care for the elderly, among others.

     The purpose of this measure is to honor and celebrate excellent
residents of the Barangay naming them “Pambihirang Mamayan ng North
Fairview”, who can influence others within the barangay and inspire more
leaders and champions in the community who may serve as role models,
particularly for the younger generations.

    In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is urgently sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Pambihirang
Mamamayan sa Barangay North Fairview Act”

SECTION 2. Recognizing the Pambihirang Mamamayan in the
Barangay. – The barangay shall award seven (7) exemplary citizens, of any
age, from the barangay who embody leadership skills, integrity, perseverance,
and service for others. The awardee shall come from the nominations of
citizens who have proven records and evidence that they have either served
or are strong advocates of education, health, environment, public safety,
entertainment, nongovernment or civic organizations, or the government
among others.

SECTION 3. Selection Board.- The barangay shall create a selection board
that will set the criteria for the nominees and the awardees. The Board shall
consist of barangay officials who shall create the following:
   1. The Selection Committee, which shall comprise five (5) Barangay
      officials and a jury who may or may not be residents of the barangay.
   2. Criteria for Qualified Nominees,
   3. Schedule the call for nominations
   4. Awarding, which shall be done before the end of every January
SECTION 4. Honorarium and Benefits. – All qualified awardees, shall be
honored with:
  1. A letter of felicitation from the Barangay Captain and the Quezon City
     District V Representative;
  2. A monetary gift of not more than Php 5,000.

SECTION 5. Funding. – The requisite fund shall be sourced from the
available funds of the Barangay.

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within thirty (30) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Barangay shall promulgate necessary
guidelines for and shall take the lead in the effective implementation of this
Act.

SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. –All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations, which are inconsistent with this Act, are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

SECTION 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general
circulation.
