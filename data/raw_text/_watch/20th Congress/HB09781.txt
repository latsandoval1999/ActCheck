                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Over the years, Parañaque City has experienced sustained population growth
driven by residential expansion and urban development. This increase has inevitably
translated into greater demand for public education, particularly at the secondary level.
As enrollment continues to grow, ensuring that students receive quality instruction and
adequate support services requires a more responsive institutional arrangement.

       This measure seeks to separate the Senior High School Department of Masville
National High School in Barangay BF Homes, Parañaque City and convert it into an
independent institution to be known as the Masville Senior High School. Barangay BF
Homes remains one of the city's largest residential communities, serving thousands of
families whose children rely on public schools for their education. As of School Year
2025-2026, Masville National High School serves approximately 500 senior high school
students sharing only ten (10) classrooms. The sheer size of the student population
presents operational and administrative challenges that can affect the quality of
instruction, student support services, and school management.
       Separating the Masville National High School Senior High School department will
help decongest existing facilities, improve the allocation of educational resources, and
create a learning environment better suited to preparing students for higher education,
employment, and entrepreneurship. It will likewise allow the school to focus exclusively
on the unique academic, technical-vocational, sports, and arts education needs of
senior high school learners.

         The establishment of the Masville Senior High School is a necessary investment
in the future of Parañaque's youth. As enrollment continues to grow, our educational
institutions must grow with it. Every learner deserves a school system that is equipped
to provide the enabling conditions where dreams of young people may be nurtured.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Establishment of an Independent Senior High School. — The Senior
High School of Masville National High School in Barangay BF Homes, City of
Paranaque, Metro Manila is hereby separated from the Masville National High School
and converted into an independent senior high school to be known as the Masville
Senior High School.

The Masville Senior High School shall be under the supervision of the Department of
Education (DepEd) and shall offer academic, technical-vocational- livelihood (TVL),
sports, and arts and design tracks as may be determined appropriate by the DepEd, in
accordance with existing laws, rules, and regulations.

SEC. 2. General Supervision. — The Masville Senior High School shall operate under
the general supervision of the DepEd, through the Schools Division Office of Paranaque
City, and in coordination with the Regional Office of the DepEd.
The Secretary of Education shall issue the necessary guidelines for the effective
implementation of this Act, including the organization, staffing, and operational
standards of the school.

SEC. 3. Appropriations. — The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of the Masville Senior High School.

The funding requirement for its initial implementation shall be charged against the
current appropriations of the DepEd, and thereafter, such sums as may be necessary
for its continued operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act (GAA).

SEC. 4. Implementation. — The DepEd shall take the necessary steps to ensure that
the required infrastructure, teaching and non-teaching personnel, equipment, and
instructional materials are provided. The Department may enter into partnerships with
the local government unit (LGU) and other stakeholders to facilitate implementation.

The Secretary of Education shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary
to carry out the purpose of this Act.

SEC. 5. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force
and effect.

SEC. 6. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
