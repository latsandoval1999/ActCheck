                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Since the creation of the Regional Trial Court (RTC) Branch 121 stationed in the City
of Meycauayan, in the Province of Bulacan, pursuant to Republic Act No. 10702, the court has
been an important facet for the dispensation of justice in our judicial system in the City of
Meycauayan and nearby municipalities. However, the current statistics of the pending cases of
lone RTC in the City of Meycauyan are barely manageable at this point. As per records of the
court, an average of 115 Criminal cases and 60 Civil cases are raffled to the court every month.
While the Court aims to dispose as many cases as it could in a month to offset the influx of
cases within the same period, it has become nearly impossible to manage both the old cases
(which includes re-raffled or transferred) and the new ones. If this will not be addressed through
the creation of additional branches of RTC, the right of every Filipino to a speedy
administration of justice will be adversely curtailed.

       It may be noted that the Supreme court has already reduced the number of
municipalities under the jurisdiction of the court. From the original one (1) city and six (6)
municipalities, it has been reduced to just one (1) city and three (3) municipalities to address
the concerns on overloaded dockets. The court has also scheduled additional hearing dates and
assigned additional prosecutors to augment the court calendar. But despite the efforts made to
address the issue of overloaded cases, the problem persists especially with the recent
designation of the court as a Family Court and Expropriation Court.
       It is also highly inconceivable that the City of Meycauayan has only ONE (1) Regional
Trial Court, this is despite its economic dynamism and consistent high performance in the

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:



       Section 1. There shall be created four (4) additional branches of the Regional Trial Court in,
Province of Bulacan, to be stationed in the City of Meycauayan City.

        The Supreme Court shall assign the respective branch number for the newly-created branches of
the Regional Trial Court.

         Section 2. The Chief Justice of the Supreme Court, in coordination with the Secretary of the
Justice, shall take the necessary steps to immediately operationalize the newly-created RTC branches,
including the appointment of the judges, clerks of court, and other personnel to be assigned therein.

        Section 3. The funds necessary for the implementation of this Act, including salaries and court
operations under maintenance and other operating expense (MOOE), and provisions for Halls of Justice
or courtrooms to accommodate four (4) judges and respective court staff, shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act (GAA) and released upon the organization of such courts and appointment
of personnel.

        Section 4. Sections 14 (c) of Batas Pambansa Blg. 129, otherwise known as “The Judiciary
Reorganization Act of 1980”, as amended, and all other laws, rules and regulations which are
inconsistent with this Act are hereby amended, repealed or modified accordingly.



         Section 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
