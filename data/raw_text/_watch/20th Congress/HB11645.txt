                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Our Constitution requires that teaching "attract and retain its rightful
share of the best available talents through adequate remuneration."1 For
faculty in State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), higher pay comes mainly
through reclassification, from Instructor I (SG 12, PhP 33,947 monthly) to
College/University Professor (SG 30, PhP 210,718).2 Since 1998 this system
has rested only on executive circulars, now DBM-CHED Joint Circular No. 3,
s. 2022, as enhanced by Joint Circular No. 01, s. 2026.3 No law requires
cycles to be held, limits processing time, or funds to adjust benefit amounts
to account for normative cost of teaching, inflation, changing educational
cost, regional variations and other considerations., which each SUC must
now certify from its own resources.4 Work done from August 2023 reaches
the DBM only by June 2027.5
      No institution can demand academic excellence while offering
systemic precarity. In placing this mandate squarely on national funding,
the State affirms that investing in legal educators is indispensable to the
administration of justice.
     In view of the foregoing, the swift approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

AN ACT UPGRADING THE SALARY GRADES OF FACULTY POSITIONS IN
    STATE UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES AND INSTITUTIONALIZING
THEIR RECLASSIFICATION AND SALARY PROGRESSION SYSTEM, AND
                   APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:
       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Public
Higher Education Faculty Salary Upgrading and Progression Act."
      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
attract and retain competent faculty in public higher education through
competitive pay and a fair, merit-based, regular, and adequately funded
system of advancement in rank, while respecting institutional academic
freedom.
      SECTION 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all faculty members
holding permanent plantilla positions in State Universities and Colleges
(SUCs): Provided, That the University of the Philippines and Mindanao State
University Systems shall retain their own promotion systems: Provided,
further, That a local university or college may adopt this system by
ordinance, subject to Section 325 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise
known as the "Local Government Code of 1991."
      SECTION 4. Upgrading of Faculty Salary Grades. – The salary
grades of faculty positions in SUCs are hereby upgraded, as follows:

​     The upgraded salary grades shall take effect on the first day of
January of the year following the effectivity of this Act. Incumbents shall be
placed in the upgraded salary grade at the step they occupy on that date.
These salary grades are minimum levels, and salaries shall follow the
prevailing government salary schedule. The number of Professor positions in
an SUC shall not be less than twenty percent (20%) of its total authorized
faculty positions, and at least one (1) College/University Professor position
per SUC shall be authorized for every evaluation cycle, not exceeding five
percent (5%) of its total Professor positions.​
​
​     SECTION 5. Periodic Reclassification. – Reclassification, or the
advancement of a faculty member to a higher rank or sub-rank based on an
evaluation of performance and accomplishments, shall be conducted for
every three-year evaluation cycle. Each cycle shall begin on the first day of
August immediately following the close of the preceding cycle. No cycle shall
be deferred for failure to issue new guidelines; the preceding guidelines shall
then apply.​
​
​     SECTION 6. Evaluation Criteria. – The Commission on Higher
Education (CHED), with DBM concurrence and after consulting SUCs and
faculty organizations, shall prescribe reclassification criteria, which shall:
          (a) be based on the following key result areas: instruction;
              research, invention, and creative work; extension services; and
              professional development;
          (b) grant an automatic one (1) sub-rank reclassification to a faculty
              member holding the rank of Instructor I to Associate Professor
              IV upon completion of a first doctoral degree that meets
              standards set by CHED;
          (c) be published not later than twelve (12) months before the start
              of the evaluation cycle to which they apply; and
          (d) not be amended during an evaluation cycle in a manner that
              imposes stricter requirements on accomplishments made within
              that cycle.
​
SECTION 7. Evaluation and Approval. – Applications shall be evaluated
by the SUC's institutional evaluation committee and validated by external
committees constituted by CHED. The Governing Board, under Republic Act

No. 8292, otherwise known as the "Higher Education Modernization Act of
1997," shall have the final authority to decide on the reclassification of
faculty members, including its extent, on the basis of the validated results
and within the limits of this Act.​
       SECTION 8. Processing and Effectivity of Salary Adjustments. –
The SUC shall submit the complete documents to the DBM Regional Office
within thirty (30) days from the Governing Board resolution, and the DBM
shall act thereon and, where proper, issue the Notice of Organization,
Staffing and Compensation Action (NOSCA) within sixty (60) days from
receipt. Any official who fails without justifiable cause to act within these
periods shall be administratively liable under civil service laws. The salary
adjustment shall take effect on the first day of the month following the date
of the Governing Board resolution, and shall be paid upon issuance of the
NOSCA, retroactive to such date: Provided, That for faculty members on
full-time study leave, the adjustment shall take effect upon actual
resumption of duty in accordance with civil service rules.​
​
​     SECTION 9. Funding of Reclassification. – The DBM shall include
in the National Expenditure Program of each SUC the personnel services
requirements arising from reclassification under this Act.​
​
​      SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within
ninety (90) days from effectivity, CHED and the DBM, after consulting the
Civil Service Commission, SUCs, and faculty organizations, shall issue the
implementing rules.​
​
​     SECTION 11. Transitory Provision and Non-Diminution. – The
evaluation cycle from 1 August 2023 to 31 July 2026 shall be completed
under DBM-CHED Joint Circular No. 01, s. 2026: Provided, That Sections 8
and 9 of this Act shall apply to all reclassifications in such cycle not yet
covered by a NOSCA: Provided, further, That no existing rank, salary, or
benefit shall be diminished by this Act.​
​
​     SECTION 12. Appropriations. – The cost of the upgrade under
Section 4 shall be included in the General Appropriations Act for the year it
takes effect. ​
​
​     SECTION 13. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.​
​
​     SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. – Issuances inconsistent herewith
and its implementing guidelines, are repealed or modified accordingly.​
​     ​
​      SECTION 15. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.​
​
​     Approved.
