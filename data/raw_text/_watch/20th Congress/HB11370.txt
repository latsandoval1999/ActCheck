                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article II, Section 24 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution recognizes the vital
role of communication and information in nation-building. Communication services,
such as the internet, serve as major tools in driving economic and social development
by connecting people and industry linkages which contribute toward a cohesive and
progressive nation.


       However, the increasing reliance on digital systems and internet-based
technologies has made cybersecurity a critical concern for national security, economic
stability, and public safety. Government services, financial institutions, critical
infrastructure, and private individuals are now more exposed to cyber threats such as
hacking, data breaches, cyber fraud, and attacks on essential systems. As of 2025, data
from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas showed that identity theft, account takeover, and
social engineering made up 76 % of reported cyber fraud losses, followed by hacking
at 13 % , and card not present fraud at 8 % . The fi gures reflect the growing shift toward
scam-driven, human-centered attacks as digital financial services expand and
