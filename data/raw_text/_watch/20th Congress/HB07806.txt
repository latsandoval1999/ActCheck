                                     AN ACT
       STRENGTHENING THE BARANGAY HEALTH CARE SYSTEM BY MANDATING THE
     APPOINTMENT OF BARANGAY HEALTH WORKERS AS REGULAR MUNICIPAL OR CITY
         EMPLOYEES, ELEVATING THEIR STATUS FROM VOLUNTEERS TO REGULAR
     EMPLOYEES, PROVIDING FOR THEIR COMPENSATION, BENEFITS, AND SECURITY OF
                  TENURE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "BHW Employment and
 2   Professionalization Act."


 3   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to protect and promote the
4    right to health of the people (Art. II, Sec. 15, 1987 Constitution). Recognizing Barangay
 s   Health Workers (BHWs) as the backbone of the healthcare system, the State shall:

 6          (a) Guarantee Labor Rights and Tenure: Pursuant to the Constitutional mandate
 7          to protect the rights of all workers to security of tenure and a living wage (Art.
8           XIII, Sec. 3), the State shall abolish the "volunteer" status of BHWs and grant
9           them the status of regular public servants, ensuring protection against arbitrary
10          removal and political displacement.
