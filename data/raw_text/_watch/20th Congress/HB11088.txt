                        AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR FREE PUBLIC ACCESS TO INTERNATIONAL AND
MAJOR LOCAL SPORTING EVENTS INVOLVING FILIPINO
ATHLETES AND PARA-ATHLETES THROUGH STATE-RUN
BROADCAST AND DIGITAL PLATFORMS, PROMOTING MEDIA
PARTNERSHIPS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The State recognizes the vital role of sports in fostering national unity,
pride, and identity. Filipino athletes and para-athletes who compete in
international and local sporting events serve as symbols of discipline,
perseverance, and excellence, inspiring millions of Filipinos across the country.

       Pursuant to Section 19, Article XIV of the 1987 Philippine Constitution,
the State shall promote physical education and encourage sports programs, league
competitions, and amateur sports, including training for international
competitions, to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for developing
a healthy and alert citizenry. Complementarily, Section 24, Article II mandates
the State to recognize the vital role of communication and information in nation-
building. Ensuring accessible platforms for the dissemination of sports content
aligns with this constitutional directive by enabling broader public engagement
in national events.
       Despite widespread public interest, access to coverage of international
competitions such as the Olympic Games, Asian Games, Southeast Asian Games,
and Asian Beach Games remains limited, as broadcast rights are often held by
private entities and made available primarily through subscription-based
platforms. This restricts the ability of a significant portion of the Filipino public
to readily watch and support national athletes and para-athletes in real time,
resulting in limited lawful and accessible viewing opportunities and reducing
broader public engagement in national sporting events.

       More critically, coverage of major local sporting events remains even more
limited and, in many instances, inaccessible. Competitions that serve as training
grounds, qualifiers, and talent-identification platforms for future national
representatives are rarely televised or livestreamed, resulting in limited public
awareness and engagement. This lack of visibility weakens the pipeline of
support for emerging athletes and undermines efforts to build a sustained and
inclusive sports culture.

        At the same time, media consumption patterns in the Philippines have
increasingly shifted toward digital and mobile-based platforms, with a substantial
number of Filipinos accessing audiovisual content through freely accessible
online applications and social media platforms. Leveraging these technologies
presents a strategic opportunity for the State to democratize access to sports
content and ensure that Filipino representation in sporting events, whether at the
elite, grassroots, or developmental level, becomes more visible and accessible to
the public.

       In anticipation of the Philippines’ participation in the upcoming Southeast
Asian Games, there is a timely need to ensure that Filipinos are able to follow and
support their athletes and para-athletes through accessible and inclusive
platforms.

       This proposed measure seeks to address these gaps by providing for free
public access to sporting events involving Filipino athletes and para-athletes in
both international and major local competitions through state-run television and
official digital platforms, while respecting existing broadcast rights. It further
promotes partnerships with media entities to expand coverage, improve reach,
and ensure sustainability.

                        AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR FREE PUBLIC ACCESS TO INTERNATIONAL AND
MAJOR LOCAL SPORTING EVENTS INVOLVING FILIPINO
ATHLETES AND PARA-ATHLETES THROUGH STATE-RUN
BROADCAST AND DIGITAL PLATFORMS, PROMOTING MEDIA

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Free Public
Access to Philippine Sports Representation Act” or the “Libreng Panonood ng
Laban ng Pilipino Act.”

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to:
       a. Promote national pride, unity, and identity through support for Filipino
          athletes and para-athletes competing in international and major local
          sporting events;
       b. Ensure equitable and accessible public access to coverage of such
          events;


                                                                                4
      c. Recognize and highlight the participation of all Filipino representatives,
         regardless of outcome, as a source of national inspiration;
      d. Utilize digital platforms and public broadcasting systems to democratize
         access to sports content; and,
      e. Support the visibility and development of both athletes and para-athletes
         as integral contributors to national development.

      Section 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following
terms are hereby defined:
      a. International Sporting Events refer to competitions participated in by
          Filipino athletes, para-athletes, or teams, including but not limited to the
          Olympic Games, Asian Games, Southeast Asian Games, Asian Beach
          Games, and other recognized international competitions;
      b. Major Local Sporting Events refer to national or regional competitions
          recognized by the Philippine Sports Commission or relevant National
          Sports Associations, including those serving as qualifiers, training
          grounds, or talent identification platforms for international
          competitions;
      c. Filipino Athletes refer to individuals competing for and representing the
          Philippines in international sporting events, including, but not limited
          to, those endorsed, accredited, or recognized by the Philippine Olympic
          Committee, Philippine Paralympic Committee, Philippine Sports
          Commission, National Sports Associations, or other duly recognized
          governing sports bodies, as well as those competing in local sporting
          events: Provided, That for purposes of this Act, the term shall likewise
          include professional athletes, independent competitors, or any Filipino
          citizen competing in globally or regionally recognized top-tier sports
          competitions, regardless of formal local institutional accreditation;
      d. Para-athletes refer to persons with disabilities, as defined under
          Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the "Magna Carta for
          Disabled Persons," as amended, who are Filipino citizens, competing
          for and representing the Philippines in international sporting events,
          including, but not limited to, those endorsed, accredited, or recognized
          by the Philippine Olympic Committee, Philippine Paralympic
          Committee, Philippine Sports Commission, National Sports
          Associations, or other duly recognized governing sports bodies, as well
          as those competing in local sporting events: Provided, That this
                                                                                    5
         definition shall encompass both institutionally accredited para-athletes
         and independent self-represented para-competitors performing in local,
         regional, or international parasports events;
      e. Free Public Access refers to the availability of viewing without
         subscription fees or paywalls, either through free-to-air television or
         open-access digital platforms;
      f. Official Digital Platforms refer to publicly accessible digital and social
         media platforms maintained or utilized by relevant government agencies
         and sports governing bodies for livestreaming, broadcasting, and
         dissemination of sports-related content;
      g. Media Entities refer to broadcast networks, television stations, radio
         stations, digital streaming platforms, online media providers, production
         companies, content distributors, and other entities engaged in the
         production, transmission, broadcast, livestreaming, or dissemination of
         sports-related audiovisual content through traditional or digital media
         platforms;
      h. Broadcast Rights Holders refer to persons, organizations, international
         or local sports governing bodies, event organizers, media companies, or
         other entities holding, owning, licensing, sublicensing, controlling, or
         distributing lawful broadcast, transmission, livestreaming, media, or
         audiovisual rights over international or major local sporting events
         through traditional or digital media platforms.

       Section 4. Public Access Coverage. – The State shall promote and facilitate
free public access to sporting events involving Filipino athletes and para-athletes
in international sporting events, as well as major local sporting events, through:
          a. State-run television networks, including but not limited to People's
              Television Network and Intercontinental Broadcasting Corporation;
              and/or,
          a. Official digital livestreaming through the official digital platforms of
              the Philippine Sports Commission, Philippine Olympic Committee,
              Philippine Paralympic Committee, and other publicly accessible
              digital and social media platforms maintained by relevant
              government agencies.
       Consistent with existing broadcast rights agreements, intellectual property
laws, and applicable rules and regulations, such access shall, at a minimum,

                                                                                   6
endeavor to include events or matches where Filipino athletes or para-athletes are
participating.

      Section 5. Coverage Safeguards. –
      a. Coverage under this Act shall be subject to existing broadcast rights
         agreements and applicable intellectual property laws;
      b. Where full live coverage is not feasible due to international contractual
         limitations or signal blackouts beyond the control of local networks,
         concerned agencies and network partners shall endeavor to make
         delayed broadcasts, highlights, or partial coverage available through
         free public platforms; and,
      c. Efforts shall be made to provide inclusive and proportional coverage of
         para-athletes and parasports competitions, ensuring their visibility
         alongside other national representatives.

      Section 6. Role of Government Agencies. – The Philippine Sports
Commission (PSC) shall serve as the lead implementing agency, in coordination
with the Philippine Olympic Committee, the Philippine Paralympic Committee,
and other relevant stakeholders, including media entities and broadcast rights
holders.
      The implementing agencies shall:
      a. Facilitate access to coverage of international and major local sporting
         events involving Filipino athletes and para-athletes;
      b. Maintain and manage official digital platforms for livestreaming and
         content dissemination;
      c. Coordinate with broadcast rights holders and media entities to maximize
         free public access; and,
      d. Ensure accessibility of content to the general public, including those in
         geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas.

      Section 7. Public-Private Partnerships for Sports Coverage. – The State,
through the Philippine Sports Commission, shall actively pursue partnerships with
media entities and broadcast rights holders to:
      a. Expand free public access to sporting events involving Filipino athletes
          and para-athletes;
      b. Facilitate cost-sharing arrangements for production and dissemination
          of content;
                                                                                7
      c. Promote wider reach and engagement across diverse audiences; and,
      d. Support the sustainable and continuous coverage of both international
         and major local sporting events.

      Section 8. Incentives for Voluntary Compliance. – Media entities and
broadcast rights holders that provide free public access or share coverage for
events involving Filipino athletes and para-athletes may be granted the following
incentives and privileges:
      a. Official Institutional Recognition. – Official recognition as National
          Sports Development Partners;
      b. Priority Initiative Consideration. – Priority consideration in future
          government-supported sports coverage initiatives;
      c. Historical Content and Archival Access. – Access to government-owned
          historical archives, pre-produced athlete feature content, documentaries,
          and official highlight reels generated post-event for public information
          and national sports development purposes, subject to existing laws,
          rules, regulations, broadcast rights agreements, and intellectual property
          rights, including Republic Act No. 8293, otherwise known as the
          “Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines,” as amended, Republic
          Act No. 3846, otherwise known as the “Radio Control Law,” as
          amended, Republic Act No. 7306, otherwise known as the “People’s
          Television Network, Inc. Charter,” as amended, and other applicable
          rules and regulations issued by the National Telecommunications
          Commission and other concerned agencies;
      d. Live Media Pool and Press Access. – Subject to availability,
          accreditation protocols, and applicable broadcast rights restrictions,
          priority physical and technical access to state-managed media pools, raw
          signal feeds, and official post-match athlete press conferences:
          Provided, That such access does not violate or conflict with the
          exclusive broadcast rights protocols established by international sports
          governing bodies or primary global rights holders; and,
      e. Prominent On-Air Attribution. – Prominent, continuous on-air
          acknowledgment, including lower-third graphic attributions, digital
          watermarks, and verbal credits on state-run broadcasting networks
          recognizing the compliant private network as an "Official National
          Sports Development Partner" during all shared sports broadcasts.

                                                                                  8
       Section 9. Protection of Broadcast and Press Freedom. – Nothing in this
Act shall be construed to authorize the State to compel any Media Entity or
Broadcast Rights Holder to surrender, transfer, waive, or forfeit its lawful
broadcast rights, franchises, contractual rights, editorial discretion, or intellectual
property rights, nor to impair the freedom of speech or of the press guaranteed
under the Constitution.
       The implementation of this Act shall be consistent with existing laws,
jurisprudence, and regulations governing broadcast media, telecommunications,
intellectual property, and contractual obligations, and shall primarily encourage
voluntary cooperation, partnerships, and public-interest arrangements to expand
free public access to sporting events involving Filipino athletes and para-athletes.

      Section 10. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of
the implementing agencies. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

      Section 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Philippine Sports Commission, in
coordination with relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations.

      Section 12. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

      Section 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, issuances,
decrees, rules, and regulations inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of
this Act are deemed amended, modified, or repealed accordingly.

       Section 14. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved,
