                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Constitution recognizes the vital role of public servants and mandates
the State to promote their welfare and ensure humane working conditions. Local
government employees who perform duties involving exposure to physical,
biological, chemical, environmental, and safety hazards play a crucial role in the
delivery of essential public services.

       During the COVID-19 pandemic, many local government personnel—
including health workers, traffic enforcers, street sweepers, cemetery workers,
social welfare personnel, and other frontline employees—continued to perform
their duties despite serious risks to their health and safety. However, many of
these workers did not receive adequate hazard compensation despite the
dangerous nature of their assignments.

      While existing laws provide hazard pay for specific sectors such as public
health workers, there remains no comprehensive policy mandating all Local
Government Units (LGUs) to grant hazard pay to local government personnel
whose work inherently exposes them to danger, regardless of employment status.

      This bill seeks to institutionalize the grant of hazard pay to qualified local
government employees, including those under temporary, casual, contractual,
job order, and contract of service arrangements, in recognition of the risks
attendant to their work and their indispensable contribution to public service.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             AN ACT
      MANDATING LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS TO GRANT HAZARD
      PAY TO ALL LOCAL GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES WHOSE WORK
      ENTAILS INHERENT RISK OR DANGER TO THEIR HEALTH AND
      SAFETY REGARDLESS OF EMPLOYMENT STATUS, AND
      PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the “Local
Government Employees Hazard Pay Act of 2026.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to protect and promote the welfare of all public servants, especially
those exposed to occupational risks and hazards in the performance of their
duties. The State shall ensure the grant of just and equitable additional
compensation to local government employees whose work entails inherent risk
or danger to their health and safety, regardless of employment status.

       SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. - For the purposes of this Act, the
following terms shall mean:

         a) Hazard Pay- refers to the additional compensation granted to
            qualified local government employees for the performance of duties
            that expose them to imminent danger, difficulty and health risks,
            which are over and above their usual risks in public service.

         b) Work entailing Risk or Danger - refers to duties that regularly
            expose an employee to:

             (1)   Physical hazards such as dangerous machinery, vehicular
                   traffic, unstable structures, or working at heights;
              (2)   Chemical hazards such as toxic, corrosive, or flammable
                    substances;

              (3)   Biological hazards such as infectious agents, human or
                    animal waste, or contaminated environments;

              (4)   Environmental hazards, such as extreme weather conditions,
                    pollution, or prolonged exposure to unsanitary outdoor
                    environments; or

              (5)   Situations involving direct threat to personal safety or life due
                    to the nature of the work environment or interaction with the
                    public in high-risk scenarios.

         c) Employment Status - refers to any form of engagement with the LGU,
            including permanent, temporary, casual, contractual, contract of
            service, or job order positions.

      SECTION 4. Grant of Hazard Pay. - All local government employees
whose work assignments involve inherent risk or danger to their health and
safety, as determined in accordance with the guidelines issued pursuant to this
Act, shall be entitled to Hazard Pay regardless of their Employment Status.

      SECTION 5. Amount of the Hazard pay. - the amount of Hazard Pay
shall be equivalent to not less than twenty-five percent (25%) of the employee's
basic monthly salary and shall be paid monthly together with the employee's
regular compensation.

      SECTION 6. Conditions for Entitlement. - The grant of Hazard Pay shall
be based on the actual services rendered by the qualified employees under
hazardous conditions. It shall not be considered as part of the employees’ basic
salary for purposes of computing other benefits, except for contributions to GSIS
and Pag-ibig Fund.

     SECTION 7. Appropriations. - The funding for the continuous grant of
Hazard Pay under this Act shall be sourced from the annual General Fund of the
LGUs and shall be allocated in their respective annual budgets.

       The National Government through the Department of Budget and
Management (DBM) shall establish a means to provide supplementary financial
assistance for LGUs with insufficient fiscal capacity to fully implement such
grant.

      SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The Department
of the Interior and Local Government, in coordination with the DBM, the Civil
Service Commission (CSC) and the Department of Labor and Employment
(DOLE), shall jointly promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
implementation of this Act within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. - If any part or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other parts hereof not affected thereby
shall remain valid.

      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, acts, presidential decrees,
executive orders, administrative orders, rules and regulations inconsistent with
or contrary to the provisions of this Act are deemed amended, modified or
repealed accordingly.

      SECTION 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.

      Approved.
