                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

The coconut industry remains an important pillar of Philippine agriculture, providing
livelihoods to millions of Filipino farmers and contributing significantly to the country’s
agricultural economy. Despite its importance, coconut farmers continue to face
challenges such as low productivity, aging coconut trees, limited access to technology,
pests and diseases, and increasingly severe weather disturbances.

Republic Act No. 11524, or the “Coconut Farmers and Industry Trust Fund Act,” provides
for the utilization of the Coconut Farmers and Industry Trust Fund for the benefit of
coconut farmers and the development of the industry. However, meaningful participation
of farmers in decisions involving the trust fund remains essential to ensure that its
programs and resources respond to their actual needs.

Therefore, this bill seeks to establish the Coconut Farmers Advisory Board (COCOFAB),
composed of representatives from registered coconut farmers’ federations, cooperatives,
and/or people’s organizations. The COCOFAB shall participate in reviewing development
priorities and proposed budget allocations, monitoring trust fund programs, and providing
policy recommendations to the Philippine Coconut Authority and relevant agencies.

By giving coconut farmers a meaningful voice in the management of resources intended
for their benefit, this measure seeks to promote a more transparent, accountable,
responsive, and sustainable coconut industry.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

SEC. 2. There is hereby inserted a new Section 5-A in the same Act to read as follows:

      “SECTION 5-A. COCONUT FARMERS ADVISORY BOARD. – THERE IS
      HEREBY CREATED A COCONUT FARMERS ADVISORY BOARD
      (COCOFAB) COMPOSED OF FIVE (5) MEMBERS REPRESENTING
      REGISTERED       COCONUT     FARMERS’     FEDERATIONS,
      COOPERATIVES, AND/OR PEOPLE’S ORGANIZATIONS. THE
      MEMBERS OF THE COCOFAB SHALL BE APPOINTED BY THE PCA
      BOARD FROM AMONG NOMINEES SUBMITTED BY RECOGNIZED
      FARMERS’ ORGANIZATIONS. THEY SHALL ELECT FROM AMONG
      THEMSELVES THE CHAIRMAN.

      “THE COCOFAB SHALL HAVE THE FOLLOWING FUNCTIONS:

      “(A) REVIEW AND RECOMMEND TO THE PCA BOARD THE ANNUAL
      PRIORITIES AND PROPOSED BUDGET ALLOCATIONS UNDER THE
      COCONUT FARMERS AND INDUSTRY DEVELOPMENT PLAN (CFIDP);
      “(B) MONITOR AND EVALUATE THE IMPLEMENTATION OF TRUST
      FUND-FINANCED PROGRAMS; AND

      “(C) SUBMIT POLICY AND PROGRAM RECOMMENDATIONS TO THE
      PCA BOARD AND RELEVANT AGENCIES TO ENSURE THAT TRUST
      FUND PROGRAMS ARE ALIGNED WITH THE NEEDS AND PRIORITIES
      OF COCONUT FARMERS.

      “THE COCOFAB SHALL BE CONSULTED PRIOR TO THE APPROVAL
      OF ANY INVESTMENT, DISBURSEMENT, OR UTILIZATION OF THE
      TRUST FUND. FOR THIS PURPOSE, THE TRUST FUND MANAGEMENT
      COMMITTEE SHALL FURNISH THE COCOFAB WITH ALL RELEVANT
      DOCUMENTS AND PROPOSALS AT LEAST FIFTEEN (15) DAYS
      PRIOR TO APPROVAL, AND SHALL DULY CONSIDER ITS COMMENTS
      AND RECOMMENDATIONS IN THE FINAL DECISION.”
