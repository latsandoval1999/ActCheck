                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Bill seeks to segregate Lot 3 and Lot 4 located at Fort Gregorio Del Pilar,
Kias, Baguio City, from the Military Reservation under Proclamation 2405 and
to convert them into alienable and disposable public land and award it directly to
actual occupants and/or qualified applicants to promote social justice and support
the government's housing and urban development goals.

It was in 1985 when President Ferdinand E. Marcos passed Proclamation 2405
wherein Lots 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 were set aside for military purposes subject to private
rights.

The land consisting of an area of Sixty-Five Thousand Eight Hundred Eleven
(65,811 sq.m.) square meters, more or less, has never been utilized and used for
its purpose and have long been occupied by more than 221 families, who are
mostly members of 345 Kias Neighborhhod, Inc.

Upon the actual investigation of the Land Management Division, Technical
Services, DENR-CAR, Lot Nos. 3 & 4 are fully built-up areas being occupied by
families who have established the area as permanent residence and have
constructed permanent structures.

This measure recognizes the legitimacy of long-term settlers who have built
permanent homes and communities in the said area, people who have lived on
these lands not as intruders but as residents whose presence predates modern
administrative arrangements.

Specifically, this Bill aims to: (1) provide security to qualified actual occupants;
(2) Promote social justice and equitable land distribution; and (3) Support the
creation of stable, well-planned human settlements that respect the rights of long-
standing settlers.

In view thereof, the immediate passage of this Bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT SEGREGATING LOT 3 AND LOT 4, OF BARANGAY KIAS,
BAGUIO CITY FROM MILITARY RESERVATION UNDER
PROCLAMATION NO. 2405, CONVERTING THEM INTO ALIENABLE
AND DISPOSABLE PUBLIC LAND AND DECLARING THE SAME
OPEN FOR THE DISPOSITION TO BONA FIDE QUALIFIED
OCCUPANTS AND/OR APPLICANTS UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 730
OR REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10023

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress Assembled:

      SECTION 1. Declaration of Policy – It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to pursue a continuing program of urban land reform and housing
which will make available at affordable cost decent housing and basic
services to underprivileged citizens in urban center

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Exclusion. — Lot 3 and Lot 4, located at
Fort Del Pilar, Barangay Kias, Baguio City, more particularly described as
follows:

       SECTION 2. Validation of the Survey. — The Department of
Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) shall conduct a validation survey
of the area to be excluded from the Military Reservation.

      SECTION 3. Disposition. — The DENR shall dispose of and award the
segregated land to bona fide occupants and/or qualified applicants pursuant to
Republic Act No. 730 and Republic Act No. 10023.

       SECTION 4. Coordination with Local Government. — The DENR
shall coordinate with the City Government of Baguio for the integration of the
segregated area into the city’s land use and housing plans.

      SECTION 5. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

       SECTION 6. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
