20                                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   Access to timely and effective emergency medical services is essential in saving lives
23   during accidents, disasters, and other medical emergencies. However, many
24   communities in the Philippines continue to face challenges such as delayed emergency
25   response, lack of trained personnel, inadequate ambulance services, and the absence
26   of a unified and standardized emergency medical system. These gaps place countless
27   Filipinos, particularly those in underserved and geographically isolated areas, at greater
28   risk during emergencies.
29
30   According to the World Health Organization (WHO), organized emergency care
31   systems significantly reduce preventable deaths and disabilities caused by trauma,
32   cardiac arrest, stroke, and other life-threatening conditions. Meanwhile, the Department
33   of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) has recognized the importance of
34   strengthening emergency response systems, including the nationwide implementation
35   of the 911 emergency hotline.
36
37   This measure seeks to institutionalize a comprehensive and integrated Emergency
38   Medical Services System (EMSS) in the country through the creation of the Emergency
39   Medical Services Systems Council (EMSSC), establishment of national standards for
40   emergency medical services, strengthening of emergency response and ambulance
41   systems, and professionalization of EMS personnel. It also strengthens the unified and
42   free nationwide 911 emergency hotline to improve coordination and accessibility of
43   emergency services.
44
45   By institutionalizing a standardized and responsive emergency medical services
46   system, this proposed measure aims to enhance public safety, improve access to life-
47   saving emergency care, and strengthen the country’s overall disaster and health
48   emergency preparedness.

21                                AN ACT
22    INSTITUTIONALIZING AN EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES SYSTEM (EMSS),
23          CREATING FOR THE PURPOSE THE NATIONAL EMSS COUNCIL,
24                   AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
25
26   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
27   Congress assembled:
28
29   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Emergency Medical Services
30   System (EMSS) Act”.
31
32   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect
33   and promote the right to health of the people. Pursuant to this policy, the government
34   shall institutionalize a comprehensive, accessible, integrated and standardized system
35   of emergency medical services and provide an environment that will maximize the
36   capability and potential of emergency medical services personnel.
37
38   SEC. 3. Objectives. - This Act has the following objectives:
39         a. To create a national Emergency Medical Services System Council;
40         b. To develop and institutionalize an emergency medical services system at the
41            national, regional and local levels of government;
42         c. To encourage and promote the active participation of the private sector in the
43            provision of emergency medical services;
44         d. To establish a national standard for an emergency medical services
45            including, but not limited to, accreditation of EMS institutions and/or training
46            of EMS personnel, establishing standards for design, accreditation and
47            regulation of emergency medical vehicles;
 1         e. To institutionalize the use of 911 as the Nationwide Emergency Hotline
 2            Number;
 3         f. To adopt an emergency medical service protocol in times of natural and man-
 4            made disasters;
 5         g. To promote public safety and accessible emergency medical services to the
 6            people especially those in the under-served and unserved communities; and
 7         h. To establish and provide support services, both internal and external, to
 8            emergency medical services.

 9
10   SEC. 4. Definition of Terms. - As used in this Act:
11         a. Accredited training and assessment institutions - organizations, in good
12            standing with the Emergency Medical Service System Council (EMSSC),
13            accredited to offer training programs, courses, continuing education and
14            competency-based assessment in emergency medical services for EMS
15            personnel that meet the standards established by the EMSSC, hereinafter
16            referred to as the Council created under this Act and, in case of uniformed
17            personnel, in coordination with the Philippine Public Safety
18         b. Ambulance/Emergency medical vehicle - a vehicle designed and equipped
19            for transporting sick or injured patients to, from, and between places of
20            treatment by land, water or air, affording safety and comfort to the patients
21            and avoiding the aggravation of illness or injury.
22         c. Basic Life Support (BLS) - a group of actions and interventions used to
23            resuscitate and stabilize victims of cardiac and respiratory arrest. These BLS
24            actions and interventions includes the recognition of a cardiac or a respiratory
25            emergency or stroke, activation of the emergency response system, CPR and
26            relief of foreign-body airway obstruction.
27         d. Competency-based assessment - confirmation of an EMS personnel's
28            capability and capacity to perform one's duties and responsibilities, through
29            the process of collective evidence and judgment, in accordance with the
30            standards and guidelines established by the Council.
31         e. Emergency Medical Dispatch - immediate identification and prioritization of
32            emergency situations, the timely dispatch of appropriate resources, providing
33            essential pre-arrival medical instructions and full endorsement to the
34            receiving hospital. Dispatch encompasses all aspects of communication
35            including request processing, coordination and support, documentation and
36            monitoring.
37         f. Emergency medical services (EMS) refer to:
38               i. Emergency care - the independent delivery of pre-hospital emergency
39                    medical services by appropriately trained and certified EMS
40                    personnel, usually in a mobile or community setting, in full accordance
41                    with the Emergency Medical Services Treatment Protocols
42                    established by the Emergency Medical Services System Council
43                    (EMSSC), hereinafter referred to as the Council, created under this
44                    Act.
 1        ii.  Advance life support - a set of life saving protocols and skills that
 2             extend beyond BLS to further support the circulation and provide an
 3             advanced airway and adequate ventilation.
 4                    a. Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) - a group of
 5                       interventions used to treat and stabilize adult victims of life-
 6                       threatening cardio respiratory emergencies and to
 7                       resuscitate victims of cardiac arrest.
 8                    b. Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS) - a group protocol for
 9                       managing trauma victims and designed to maximize
10                       management in the first hour after trauma and ensure an
11                       optimal long-term outcome.
12                    c. Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) - a system of critical
13                       care procedures and facilities, such as intensive care nursery,
14                       for the basic and advanced treatment of seriously ill or injured
15                       infants and children.
16                    d. Neonatal Advanced Life Support (NALS) - a group of
17                       interventions for the basic and advanced treatment of injured
18                       and critically ill new born infants.
19   g. Emergency Medical Services Personnel - a trained and certified personnel
20      engaged in the provision of emergency medical services during emergencies.
21   h. Emergency Medical Services (EMS) System - the arrangement and
22      coordination of personnel, facilities, and equipment for the effective delivery
23      of EMS required in the management of medical emergencies; for the
24      management and prevention of further incidents or accidents and the broad
25      range of emergency care from emergency care to transport in an intensive
26      care setting.
27   i. Emergency Medical Technician - the EMS personnel who provide basic
28      emergency medical care and transportation for critical and emergent patients
29      who access emergency medical system.
30   j. Emergency Response and Care - the arrival of resources at the scene and
31      the timely initiation and provision of appropriate medical interventions.
32   k. Emergency Transport - the transporting of a patient to the most appropriate
33      and definitive health facility with continued provision of care and appropriate
34      interventions en route to the appropriate health facility.
35   l. Inter-Facility Referral and Transport - the transport of patient with medical
36      escort, from one referring facility or agency to another receiving facility or
37      agency for definitive care, as the patient requires, in an event that further
38      appropriate services are not available in the referring facility.
39   m. Medical Direction - the communication between an EMS personnel and a
40      physician from the field via radio or other means to obtain instruction on
41      further care of a patient.
42   n. Medical Emergency - any acute or life-threatening condition that requires
43      immediate intervention by competent medical personnel.
 1          o. National Emergency Medical Services Treatment Protocols - emergency
 2             medical procedures outlining approved clinical practices and therapies to be
 3             observed by EMS personnel, as established by the Council created under
 4             Section 5 hereof.

 6                                CHAPTER II
 7                EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES SYSTEMS COUNCIL
 8
 9   SEC. 5. Creation of the Emergency Medical Services Systems Council. - A body to
10   be known as the "Emergency Medical Services Systems Council (EMSSC)" is hereby
11   created under the Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG).
12
13   SEC. 6. Powers and Functions of the EMSSC. - The Council shall perform the
14   following functions:
15          a. To formulate policies on EMS systems;
16          b. To develop national standards of emergency medical services to include,
17              among others, the skills and competencies required for EMS personnel and
18              the development of mandatory national emergency medical services
19              treatment protocols to be observed by EMS personnel and such other entities
20              as it may consider appropriate;
21          c. To promulgate a Code of Ethics for EMS personnel;
22          d. To develop appropriate standards of operation for EMS personnel;
23          e. To develop standards and protocols for the design, accreditation,
24              construction, outfitting and operations of emergency medical vehicles;
25          f. To ensure the establishment of a system of networking and coordination
26              among all existing government health agencies, LGUs, and private and non-
27              government medical institutions for the effective implementation of this Act;
28          g. To establish a Secretariat, pursuant to Section 9 of this Act, under an
29              Executive Director for the administrative and day-to-day operations of the
30              Council;
31          h. To create committees and other mechanisms to help expedite the
32              implementation of plans and strategies;
33          i. To generate resources from local, national and international
34              organizations/agencies, whether government or private sector, for its
35              operation; and receive and accept donations and other conveyances
36              including funds, materials and services by gratuitous title: Provided, That not
37              more than thirty percent (30%) of said funds shall be used for administrative
38              expenses;
39          j. To prepare an annual budget of the Council, through the Department of
40              Interior and Local Government (DILG), and submit the same to the President
41              for inclusion in the annual General Appropriations Act;
42          k. To advise the President on matters pertaining to EMS;
 1         l. To investigate complaints of motu propio and impose penalties for the
 2            violation against any of the provisions of this Act;
 3         m. To request any department, instrumentality, office, bureau or agency of the
 4            government, including LGUs, to render such assistance as it may require in
 5            order to carry out, enforce or implement the provisions of this Act;
 6         n. To regulate activities inimical to the delivery of emergency medical services;
 7            and
 8         o. To promulgate rules and regulations and policies and enforce the provisions
 9            of this Act.

10
11   SEC. 7. Membership of the Council. - The members of the Council shall be composed
12   of the following:
13           a. The Secretary of the DILG or duly authorized representative as Chairperson
14               of the Council;
15           b. The Secretary of the Department of Health (DOH) or duly authorized
16               representative as Co-Chairperson;
17           c. The Secretary of the Department of Information and Communications
18               Technology (DICT) or duly authorized representative;
19           d. The Chairman of the Philippine National Red Cross (PNRC) or duly
20               authorized representative;
21           e. The President of the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) or
22               duly authorized representative; and
23           f. The President of the Philippine College of Emergency Medicine (PCEM) or
24               duly authorized
25           g. representative;
26           h. One (1) representative of a national organization or society of emergency
27               medical technicians (EMT’s) who will undergo a nomination and selection
28               process by the Council;
29
30   The Executive Director of the Emergency 911 Office, as created by Executive Order
31   No. 56-2018, and the Director General of TESDA, shall be required to participate in all
32   meetings of the Council. Relevant stakeholders from the different government offices,
33   private entities and civil society organizations (CSOs) who are involved in the practice
34   and implementation of emergency medical services can be invited as resource persons
35   or representatives in the Council’s meetings.
36
37   SEC. 8. Compensation and Remuneration. - The Secretaries of the DILG, DOH and
38   DICT shall perform their duties as such without compensation or remuneration, subject
39   to reasonable per diem allowances as approved by the Council and subject to existing
40   rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM). Members
41   thereof who are not government officials or employees shall be entitled to necessary
42   travelling expenses, per diem and representation allowances chargeable against funds
43   of the DILG, as approved by the Council, subject to existing rules and regulations of the
44   DBM.
 1   SEC. 9. The Secretariat. - The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), an attached agency of
 2   the DILG, shall act as the Secretariat of the Council. The BFP shall be strengthened by
 3   augmenting its additional manpower and budget for its operations.
 4
 5   Sec. 10. Meetings. - The Council shall meet at least once every quarter.
 6
 7   Sec. 11. Program Plans. - The Council shall within six (6) months after having been
 8   officially constituted and finally staffed, adopt and immediately cause to be
 9   implemented, in coordination with medical and related agencies, a short-range program
10   in support of relevant existing projects and activities and, within one (1) year, a long-
11   range five (5)-year development program. This development program shall be
12   developed and subjected to annual review and revision by the Council, in coordination
13   with relevant public and private medical agencies and organizations.
14
15   SEC. 12. Accreditation. - The Council shall issue authorization for the following:
16         a. Accreditation of training institutions for EMS personnel; and
17         b. Accreditation of emergency medical vehicle providers.

19                                 CHAPTER III
20                    EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES PERSONNEL
21
22   SEC. 13. Creation of Plantilla Positions for Emergency Medical Services
23   Personnel. - There shall be created a minimum number of plantilla positions for EMS
24   personnel in the following government hospitals/health facilities within the next five (5)
25   years upon approval of this Act:
26         a. Level 3 Hospitals -Five (5) EMS personnel and at least one (1) Ambulance
27            Assistant;
28         b. Level 1 and 2 Hospitals - Three (3) EMS personnel and at least one (1)
29            AmbulanceAssistant;
30         c. Local Government Units: -
31                i. Highly-urbanized city (HUC) - Ten (10) EMS personnel and at least
32                    one (1)Ambulance Assistant;
33               ii. Component city - Eight (8) EMS personnel and at least one (1)
34                    AmbulanceAssistant;
35              iii. First to third class municipality - Four (4) EMS personnel and at least
36                    one (1)Ambulance Assistant;
37              iv. Fourth to sixth class municipality - Two (2) EMS personnel and at least
38                    one (1) Ambulance Assistant; and
39         d. Other Health Facilities - as may be deemed necessary by the Council.

40
41   Upon the approval of the necessary plantilla positions for EMS personnel, the Council
42   shall submit a proposed qualification standard for positions to the Civil Service
43   Commission (CSC) for its approval, pursuant to E.O. No. 292, entitled "The Revised
 1   Administrative Code of 1987 on the Civil Service Commission". The annual financial
 2   requirements needed to pay for the salaries of EMS personnel shall be included in the
 3   annual general appropriations of the respective hospitals, health facilities and LGUs.
 4
 5   SEC. 14. Authorized Training Institution. - Training programs, courses and
 6   continuing education for an EMS personnel shall be conducted by an institution that has
 7   been granted a Certificate of Program Registration (COPR) by the TESDA, in case of
 8   technical non-degree courses falling under the TESDA jurisdiction, and the Philippine
 9   Public Safety College (PPSC) for uniformed personnel. The requirements prescribed
10   by the Council shall serve as the minimum requirement for program registration. The
11   DOH can provide training programs for EMS personnel: Provided, That these shall be
12   in accordance with the standards set by the Council.
13
14                                 CHAPTER IV
15                      EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES SYSTEM
16
17   SEC. 15. Emergency Medical Vehicles. - The Council shall develop minimum
18   requirements for the design, construction, performance, equipment, testing and
19   appearance of emergency medical vehicles. As such, only emergency medical vehicles
20   shall be allowed to display the word "Ambulance" and the universally-accepted "Star of
21   Life" symbol. It shall also provide for the operation protocols of said vehicles. The
22   Council shall design a specification and an accreditation systems and procedures,
23   subject to the existing regulations of the DOH on licensure of medical vehicles.
24
25   SEC. 16. Emergency dispatch. - All local government units are mandated to establish
26   their dispatch centers where constituents can call for all cases of emergencies, with
27   adequate and qualified personnel. The dispatch centers shall follow the prescribed
28   guidelines on dispatch protocol as determined by the Council.
29
30   SEC. 17. Emergency Response, Care, and Transport. - All LGUs shall ensure the
31   availability of adequate emergency transport vehicles or ambulances with qualified
32   EMS personnel. All emergency transport vehicles and the procedures to be undertaken
33   in responding and caring for patients shall follow the prescribed guidelines of the
34   Council on ambulance services and on emergency response and care.
35
36   SEC. 18. Inter-facility Referral and Transport. - The Council shall establish the
37   prescribed protocols/guidelines on inter-facility referral and transport, in accordance
38   with appropriate medical direction.
39
40                                   CHAPTER V
41                            EMERGENCY COMMUNICATIONS
42
43   SEC. 19. Adoption of a Free National Emergency 911 Hotline Number. - There shall
44   only be one (1) free national emergency 911 hotline number to enable the public to
 1   access emergency medical services. The operations of the national emergency 911
 2   hotline number shall be under the DILG, in coordination with the Council.
 3
 4   SEC. 20. Compliance. - It shall be the duty of every telecommunications to provide its
 5   subscribers with access to the national emergency 911 hotline number in accordance
 6   with the implementing rules and regulations to be adopted pursuant to this Act.
 7
 8   SEC. 21. Prohibited Acts and Penalties on Emergency Communications. –
 9         a. Any person making a telephone call to an emergency hotline number who
10            annoys, abuses, threatens or harasses any person who is answering the
11            telephone call shall be guilty with the first offense and, subject to subsection
12            (c) of this section, shall be compelled to attend a seminar on the proper use
13            of the nationwide emergency hotline number. Upon commission of the
14            offense for the second time, the offender shall, upon conviction, be imposed
15            with a fine of not less than Five hundred pesos (P500.00) but not more than
16            One thousand pesos (P1,000.00). Upon commission of the offense for the
17            third and succeeding times, the offender shall, upon conviction, be imposed
18            with a penalty of imprisonment of not less than one (1) month but not more
19            than six (6) months or a fine of not less than Two thousand pesos (P2,000.00)
20            but not more than Five thousand pesos (P5,000.00), or both, at the discretion
21            of the court.
22         b. Any person making a telephone call to an emergency hotline number and,
23            upon the call being answered, making or soliciting any comment, request,
24            suggestion, proposal or sound which is obscene, lewd, lascivious, filthy or
25            indecent, shall be mandated to attend a seminar on the proper use of the
26            nationwide emergency hotline number for the first offense. Upon commission
27            of the offense for the second time, the offender shall, upon conviction, be
28            imposed with a fine of not less than Five hundred pesos (P500.00) but not
29            more than One thousand pesos (P1,000.00). Upon commission of the
30            offense for the third and succeeding times, the offender shall, upon
31            conviction, be imposed with a penalty of imprisonment of not less than one
32            (1) month but not more than six (6) months or a fine of not less than Two
33            thousand pesos (P2,000.00) but not more than Five thousand pesos
34            (P5,000.00), or both, at the discretion of the court.
35         c. A person giving false report of a medical emergency or giving false
36            information in connection with a medical emergency, or making false alarm
37            of a medical emergency, knowing the report or information or alarm to be
38            false; or making a false request for ambulance service to an ambulance
39            service provider, knowing the request to be false, shall be mandated to attend
40            a seminar on the proper use of the nationwide emergency hotline number for
41            the first offense. Upon commission of the offense for the second time, the
42            offender shall, upon conviction, be imposed with a fine of not less than Two
43            thousand pesos (P2,000.00) but not more than Five thousand pesos
44            (P5,000.00) and payment of damages. Upon commission of the offense for
 1             the third and succeeding times, the offender shall, upon conviction, be
 2             imposed with a penalty of imprisonment of not less than one (1) year but not
 3             more than three (3) years or a fine of not less than Five thousand pesos
 4             (P5,000.00) but not more than Ten thousand pesos (P10,000.00) and
 5             payment of damages, or both, at the discretion of the court.

 7                                      CHAPTER VI
 8                                   OTHER PROVISIONS
 9
10   SEC. 22. Role of the LGUs. - The LGUs are hereby mandated to develop and
11   institutionalize an emergency medical service system within their area of jurisdiction.
12   The Council shall include in its programs, activities that will support and enable the
13   LGUs to accomplish such task. Non-compliance by public officials of the provisions of
14   this Act shall be subject to administrative sanctions under civil service regulations.
15
16   SEC. 23. Establishment of the EMSS Fund. - There is hereby established an EMSS
17   Fund which may be sourced from fees, donations and grants, to be administered by the
18   Council, and subject to COA regulations. The amount collected shall be used to
19   augment the budget for the EMSS operations of LGUs, particularly for geographically
20   isolated and disadvantaged areas (GIDAs), and the Council's administrative functions,
21   provided, that not more than thirty percent (30%) of said funds shall be used for
22   administrative expenses.
23
24   SEC. 24. Appropriations. - The amount needed for the implementation of this Act shall
25   be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
26
27   SEC. 25. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DILG, in consultation with the
28   DOH, DICT, DOJ, and Philippine Red Cross, shall issue and promulgate the rules and
29   regulations to implement the provisions of this Act within one hundred twenty (120) days
30   upon constitution of the Council.
31
32   SEC. 26. Separability Clause. - If any clause, sentence, paragraph or part of this Act
33   shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, such judgment shall not affect, invalidate
34   or impact any other part of this Act.
35
36   SEC. 27. Transitory Clause. - Pursuant to Sections 4 (a) and 14 of this Act, all existing
37   rules of TESDA pertaining to the accreditation of EMS personnel shall remain effective
38   until new sets of the rules are established.
39
40   SEC. 28. Repealing Clause. - Any provision of laws, orders, agreements, rules or
41   regulations contrary to and inconsistent with this Act is hereby repealed, amended or
42   modified accordingly.
