                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

        In October 2021, Republic Act No. 11594 was enacted increasing the
penalties for perjury from arresto mayor in its maximum period to prison
correccional in its minimum period, to prison mayor in its minimum period.
However, experience has shown that the existing penalty framework remains
insufficient to deter the malicious filing of fabricated criminal complaint.
        False criminal accusations, especially those involving serious and non-
bailable offenses can result in the wrongful arrest and detention of innocent
persons. The deprivation of liberty caused by fabricated sworn statements inflicts
not only reputational damage but also severe psychological, economic, and social
harm. When a perjured statement becomes the basis for detention, the injustice is
magnified and undermines the public trust in the justice system.
      This proposed measure recognizes the grave consequences of fabricated
criminal complaints by imposing a penalty commensurate to the offense falsely
imputed. By aligning the penalty with the severity of the fabricated charge, the
law ensures proportional accountability and reinforces the constitutional
guarantee of due process.
        The penalty further ensures that the penalty shall not be lower than that
prescribed under Republic Act No. 11594, thereby maintaining the strengthened
framework already established.
          In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

 AN ACT AMENDING ARTICLE 183 OF ACT NO. 3815, OTHERWISE
  KNOWN AS “THE REVISED PENAL CODE”, AS AMENDED BY
   REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11594, PROVIDING FOR AN INCREASED
 PENALTY FOR PERJURY COMMITTED THROUGH FABRICATED
                 CRIMINAL COMPLAINT
      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:
SECTION 1. Article 183 of Republic Act No. 3815, otherwise known as
“The Revised Penal Code”, as amended by Republic Act No. 11594, is hereby
further amended to read as follows:
     “Article 183. False testimony in other cases and perjury in solemn
affirmation - The penalty of prison mayor in its minimum period shall be imposed
upon any person who, knowingly making untruthful statements and not being
included in the provisions of the next preceding articles, shall testify under oath,
or make an affidavit, upon any material matter before a competent person
authorized to administer an oath in cases in which the law so requires.
     Any person who, in case of a solemn affirmation made in lieu of an oath,
shall commit any of the falsehoods mentioned in this and the preceding articles
of this section, shall suffer the respective penalties provided therein: Provided,
That is IF the person responsible for the commission of this felony is a public
officer or employee, the penalty shall be imposed in its maximum period:
Provided, finally, That the offender shall also suffer a fine not to exceed One
Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00) and perpetual disqualification from holding any
appointive or elective position in the government or in any agency, entity, or
instrumentality thereof.”
    WHEN THE PERJURY IS COMMITTED THROUGH THE
EXECUTION OF A FABRICATED CRIMINAL COMPLAINT,
AFFIDAVIT, OR SWORN STATEMENT FALSELY IMPUTING THE
COMMISSION OF A CRIME UPON ANOTHER PERSON, AND THE
PENALTY PRESCRIBED BY LAW FOR THE OFFENSE FALSELY
IMPUTED IS HIGHER THAN PRISION MAYOR IN ITS MINIMUM
PERIOD, THE PENALTY TO BE IMPOSED SHALL BE EQUIVALENT
TO THAT PRESCRIBED BY LAW FOR THE OFFENSE FALSELY
IMPUTED.
SEC. 2. If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional, the same shall
not affect the validity and effectivity of the other provisions thereof.
SEC. 3. All laws, rules and regulations that may be inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.
SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete publication
in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation in the
Philippines.
