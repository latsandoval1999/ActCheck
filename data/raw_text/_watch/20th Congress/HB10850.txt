VITAL STATISTICS SYSTEM, PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to establish a comprehensive, responsive, and modern Civil Registration and Vital Statistics
(CRVS) system that will ensure the universal, timely, and secure registration of vital events involving
Filipinos, whether in the country or abroad. It aims to address fragmented civil registration processes by
creating an integrated framework that strengthens legal identity, improves public service delivery, and
ensures the accuracy and reliability of civil registry records.

The measure recognizes civil registration as an important component of protecting individual rights and
facilitating access to essential government services. It seeks to ensure that the registration of births, deaths,
marriages, and other civil status events remains free, accessible, and responsive, particularly for marginalized
and vulnerable groups such as indigenous peoples, foundlings, children in need of special protection, persons
with disabilities, and overseas Filipinos.

The bill also supports the modernization and digitization of civil registration through the interconnection of
existing systems and the development of a national database. It promotes closer coordination among the
Philippine Statistics Authority, local government units, and other concerned government agencies to
streamline processes, reduce delays, strengthen data management, and improve the integrity of civil registry
information.

Ultimately, the measure seeks to strengthen the country's civil registration system while upholding the
principles of legal identity, data privacy, accessibility, and inclusive governance. By ensuring that every
Filipino is properly registered and recognized before the law, the State can help provide individuals with
more reliable access to public services, legal protections, and other opportunities that depend on official civil
registry records.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

 1   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Civil Registration and Vital Statistics
 2   (CRVS) Act."
 3   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to establish and
 4   implement a comprehensive, responsive, and fraud-resistant Civil Registration and Vital Statistics
 5   (CRVS) system in accordance with Republic Act (RA) No. 10173, otherwise known as the “Data
 6   Privacy Act of 2012,” and RA No. 11055, or the “Philippine Identification System Act.” The State
 7   shall ensure that the rights of every person to privacy, confidentiality, legal identity, and other
 8   fundamental rights are respected, protected, and upheld at all times.

 9   The State likewise reaffirms its commitment to international human rights standards by upholding
10   the principles set forth in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR), particularly the right
11   of every individual to be recognized as a person before the law under Article VI. Consistent with
12   the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child (UN CRC), the State shall recognize and
13   protect every child’s right to nationality, immediate registration after birth, and preservation of
14   identity, as provided under Articles VII and VIII thereof.

15   The State shall further uphold the right of every person to protection against discrimination on the
16   grounds of race, sex, color, language, religion, or national origin, consistent with Article XXIV of
17   the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR). It shall also recognize the right to
18   nationality of persons with disabilities in accordance with Article XVIII of the Convention on the
19   Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD), as well as the right of adolescents to birth registration
20   as recognized under Article VIII of the UN CRC General Comment (GC) No. 20.

21   Furthermore, the State shall ensure that children in street situations are provided proactive assistance
22   in securing legal identity documents through free, accessible, and timely birth registration, consistent
23   with Article XV of the UN CRC GC No. 21. The State shall likewise ensure that all Filipino migrant
24   workers are recognized as persons before the law, in accordance with Article XXIV of the
25   International Convention on the Protection of the Rights of All Migrant Workers and Members of
26   their Families (ICMW), within the framework of the CRVS system.

380      9. Place of death;
381      10. Full names of the deceased's parents; Cause of death; and
382      11. Such other relevant data that may be required.
383   The surname to be entered in the registry of death and the Death Certificate shall be the surname
384   used prior to their death. In case the deceased is a legally married woman, informants shall register
385   the death using the surname she chose as a legally married person.
386   The registration of death shall be made within thirty (30) calendar days from the time and date of
387   death before the Civil Registrar's Office of the city or municipality where the death occurred, or the
388   Philippine Consulate Office in the foreign country where the death happened.
389   In the absence of the city or municipal health officer, or their authorized representative in the place
390   of registration, or when it is a non-working day and the health officer or their authorized
391   representative is not expected to be in the office, the death shall be reported by the nearest kin of the
392   deceased or by any person having knowledge of the death within forty-eight (48) hours after its
393   occurrence, to the mayor or to the vice mayor or any member of the Sangguniang Bayan, or to the
394   municipal secretary, who shall issue and sign the medical certification of the Certificate of Death
395   for burial, cremation, and registration purposes.
396   When the death is subjected to a medico-legal examination, or the cause of death is under
397   investigation by the National Bureau of Investigation (NBI), or other concerned government office
398   and the body of the deceased is subjected to autopsy or examination of the medico-legal officers,
399   and no record of death has been registered, the head of the NBI or other investigative agency or the
400   duly authorized representative shall cause the registration of such death through the City or
401   Municipal Health Officer of the city or municipality where the death occurred. The medico-legal
402   officer shall accomplish and sign the medical certification portion of the certificate of death, subject
403   to the review of the City or Municipal Health Officer.
404   SEC. 18. Delayed Registration of Death. - A deceased person's facts of death that have not been
405   within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of death shall be allowed delayed registration before
406   the Civil Registrar's Office at the place of death. In any case, out-of-town registration of death may
407   be allowed.
408   SEC. 19. Registration Of the Application for Marriage License. - An application for a marriage
409   license shall be filed by the contracting parties before the Civil Registrar's Office of the city or
410   municipality where one of the parties resides.
411   SEC. 20. Registration of Marriage. - All marriages shall be registered by the solemnizing officer
412   before the Civil Registrar's Office of the place where they were solemnized.
413      a.   In case of marriages solemnized while in transit:
414             i.   En route to, or within the Philippines, the marriage shall be registered at the place of
415                  residence of either party; or
416            ii.   Leaving the Philippines, the marriage shall be registered at the consulate of the
417                  Philippines in the place of destination of the parties, or in the absence thereof, to the
418                  nearest Philippine consulate from the place of destination.
419      b. In cases of registration of traditional marriages and dissolution of marriages among and
420         between IPs.
421   The report of marriage among and between IPs solemnized in accordance with customary laws, rites,
422   traditions, and by the person duly authorized to perform the ritual or ceremony and by the parties to
423   the marriage shall be conclusive upon the LCR or PSA without need for further proof. For this
424   purpose, the NCIP shall submit to the PSA a list of IPs by each indigenous community to solemnize
425   marriage according to its customary laws or practices. The PSA shall issue a Certificate of
426   Registration of Authority to Solemnize Marriage (CRASM) to such IPs for free.
427   In the case of indigenous communities whose traditional marriage ceremony does not require the
428   presence of a solemnizing officer, as determined by the NCIP, the report by the contracting parties
429   shall be conclusive upon the LCR/PSA without the need for further proof except personal
430   confirmation, orally or in writing, by any of the parents or by a tribal leader or elder of the indigenous
431   community to which any of the spouses belong. For this purpose, the NCIP shall furnish the PSA
432   and the LCR with a list of indigenous communities falling under this paragraph; Provided, That
433   pending the submission of such list. The oral or written confirmation of the tribal leader or elder
434   shall be conclusive upon the LCR/PSA.
435   The dissolution of marriage Of IPs following customary indigenous laws and practices shall be
436   directly recorded with the LCR/PSA without need of court intervention; Provided, That such facts
437   and information shall be confirmed orally or in writing by a tribal leader or elder of the indigenous
438   community to which any of the spouses belongs.
439   All marriages shall be registered within thirty (30) days from the date of the marriage. Marriages
440   registered after the stipulated period shall be considered late registration and an administrative fee
441   shall be imposed on the solemnizing officer, in no shall double or multiple registration of marriages
442   be except for cases provided by law.
443   SEC. 21. Multiple Registration. - In cases of multiple registrations of birth, marriage or death, the
444   first duly registered document shall prevail, unless the Court orders otherwise. For multiple
445   marriages by and between the same parties, the first marriage shall prevail, and not the first
446   registered marriage of such parties
447   SEC. 22. Registration of Solemnizing Officers. - All solemnizing officers shall be registered with
448   the Office of the CRG, except those who are not to be so registered as provided for by law or existing
449   rules. The Mayor, with the assistance of the Civil Registrar's Office, shall determine documents
450   pertaining to the existence of religious sects.
451   SEC. 23. Registration of Court Orders. - In case of court issuance or order concerning the status of
452   a person, the Clerk of Court shall advise the successful petitioner to register such document before
453   the Civil Registrar's Office which has jurisdiction on the issuing court.
454   In cases of legal separation, absolute nullity of void marriages and annulment of voidable marriages,
455   the prevailing party shall cause the registration of the decree in the Civil Registrar's Office where
456   the marriage was registered, the Civil Registrar's Office where the family court is situated, and in
457   the PSA. Such party shall report to the Court compliance with this requirement within thirty (30)
458   days from receipt of the copy of the decree.
459   In case a person other than the petitioner shall apply to register a court order before the Civil
460   Registrar's Office, the LCR has the responsibility to verify the veracity of the court issuance or order,
461   otherwise, registration thereof shall be refused.
462   The Civil Register Office where the event was registered shall forward a true copy of the order to
463   the Civil Registrar's Office where the vital event affected was originally registered. The Civil
464   Registrar's Office where the Vital event was originally registered shall make proper annotations in
465   the document and in the applicable registry book. The Civil Registrar's Office which has jurisdiction
466   over the original registry shall send a certified true copy of the annotated document and the registered
467   court order to the CRG.
468   Each corrected document shall be reported to the Office of the CRG during the usual reporting
469   month. All court orders shall be registered within thirty (30) days after its finality, otherwise it shall
470   be considered as delayed registration. All court orders shall be affected through annotations in the
471   civil registry document except in an adoption case when the child shall be entitled to the issuance of
472   an amended COLB.
473   SEC. 24. Registration of a Child born Abroad to at least one Filipino. - The parents shall cause
474   the registration of the birth of their child to the Philippine Consulate which in tum shall issue a
475   Report of Birth within thirty (30) calendar days from date of birth of a Filipino child declaring the
476   following when available:
477       1. Full name of the child;
478       2. Sex;
479      3. Date of birth;
480      4. Place of birth or where found;
481      5. Name of mother;
482      6. Citizenship of the mother;
483      7. Name of father;
484      8. Citizenship of the father;
485      9. Date and place of marriage of parents; and
486      10. Such other data as may be required in the implementing rules an regulations of this Act.
487   SEC. 25. Registration of Persons without Known Parents. - All without known parents, guardians
488   or relatives, or a person committed in an orphanage or charitable institution with unknown facts of
489   birth and parentage, shall be reported by the finder to the Barangay Chairperson or any member of
490   the Barangay Council for the Protection of Children (BCPC) of the place where the person was
491   found, or to the Philippine National Police Women and Children's Protection Desk (PNP-WCPD),
492   whichever is nearer or convenient to the finder, stating the facts and circumstances surrounding the
493   finding of when the report is duly noted.
494   The finder shall bring the child to the nearest DSWD office in the region, for proper screening,
495   assessment. and evaluation by an authorized social worker within fifteen (15) calendar days from
496   the date of finding or commitment of the child, the finder shall execute a sworn statement before the
497   Barangay Captain stating the facts and circumstances surrounding the finding of the person.
498   In case of foundlings found in Philippine Consulates, embassies and territories, the relevant
499   Philippine Consulate shall be governed by RA No. 11767, otherwise known as the "Foundling
500   Recognition and Protection Act", the finder or charitable institution concerned shall cause the
501   registration before the Civil Registrar's Office where such person was found within thirty (30)
502   calendar days from the date of entrustment to the institution.
503   Any report made after the thirty (30) day shall be considered late registration, and the concerned
504   party shall be required to state in a sworn statement the circumstances that caused the late reporting
505   to the civil registrar.
506   Should the foundling become adopted, the Local Civil Registrar shall stamp the simulated birth
507   record "cancelled" with an annotation of the issuance of a new rectified certificate of birth in its
508   place. The rectified birth certificate bearing the names of the biological parents of the child or the
509   foundling certificate shall then likewise be stamped "cancelled" with the annotation of the issuance
510   of a new birth record in its place, and shall be sealed in the civil registry records. Thereafter, the
511   Local Civil shall record, register, and issue a new certificate of birth which shall not bear any
512   notation that it is a new or amended issue, as stated in Sec. 12 of RA No. 11222, otherwise known
513   as the "Simulated Birth Rectification Act".
514   SEC. 26. Registration of Adoption. - The decree of adoption shall be in the civil register within thirty
515   (30) days after the date of issue of the final judgment of the court. After registration of the decree of
516   adoption, the Civil Registrar's Office where the birth of the adopted was registered shall immediately
517   issue an amended COLB.
518   Such COLB of an adopted child shall not have any mark or annotation or any indication of the facts
519   of adoption. It shall have the same entries as those appearing in the original records of birth, except
520   for the following items of information:
521      a. Name of the adopted. - The first name of the adopted child shall be the same as in the original
522         COLB unless the order of adoption carried an order changing it to another name. The middle
523         name of the adopted child shall be the same as the middle name of the adopter, except when
524         the husband and the wife jointly adopted the child, in which case, the middle name of the
525         adopted shall be the surname of the adopting mother.
526   The surname of the adopted child shall be that of the adopter, If the husband and wife jointly adopt
527   the child, the adopted child shall use the surname of the adopting father.
528      a. Names of parents. - The names of the natural parents of the adopted child shall be substituted
529         by the names of the adopting parents, to be indicated in the appropriate spaces in the COLB
530         as the or mother, as the case maybe; and
531      b. Other information about the adopting parents such as citizenship and religion shall be
532         indicated in the appropriate spaces of the amended certificate of live birth.
533   The birth order of the adopted and other information about the adopted shall follow the information
534   given by the adopting parents, unless the court order states otherwise. In cases where legally married
535   husband and wife jointly adopt the child, the date and place of marriage of the adopting parents shall
536   be entered in the appropriate spaces of the amended certificate of live birth of the adopted child.
537   In all cases of adoption, no amendment or changes shall be affected in the civil registry books where
538   the original birth records were duly registered, except for the marginal annotation by virtue of the
539   adoption decree.
540   In case the adopted child has no known parents, the basis for the issuance of the amended COLB
541   shall be the court order.
542   SEC. 27. Registration Of Presumptive Death. - A judicial order or decree declaring a person
543   presumably dead for purposes of remarriage shall be registered in the office of the local civil registrar
544   of the place where the marriage was solemnized. It shall be annotated and recorded. The registration
545   shall be annotated in the marriage register as well as in the marriage certificate.
546   SEC. 28. Registration of Foreign Court Orders Decided Abroad. - All foreign court orders
547   involving status of persons residing in the Philippines shall be registered before the Civil Registrar's
548   Office where the affected document is registered, These foreign court orders, to be legal and binding
549   in Philippine jurisdiction, must first be recognized by the appropriate embassy of such country here
550   in the Philippines or Regional Trial Court which has the lawful jurisdiction over such case.
551   SEC. 29. Registration Of Legal Instruments. - As a general rule, all legal instruments Shall be
552   registered in the Civil registry of the place where they were executed except for the following:
553      a. Affidavit of Reappearance. - where the parties to the subsequent marriage are residing;
554      b. Marriage Settlement. - where the marriage is recorded; or
555      c. Admission of Paternity, Acknowledgment, Legitimation, Voluntary Emancipation of Minor,
556         Artificial Insemination. - where the birth of a child was recorded.
557   SEC. 30. Registration of Affidavit of Reappearance. - A sworn statement of the facts and
558   circumstances of reappearance of the absentee spouse shall be in the Civil Registry Office where
559   marriage of the present spouse was recorded, at the instance of any interested person, with due notice
560   to the spouses of the subsequent marriage and without prejudice to the fact of reappearance being
561   judicially determined in cases where such a fact is disputed.
562   SEC. 31. Registration of Affidavit of Acknowledgment or Affidavit of Admission of Paternity. - It
563   shall be the duty of parents or parents who executed the affidavit of acknowledgment to send the
564   original copy to the Civil Registrar's Office where the entries on the birth of the child were recorded
565   for registration in the Register of Legal Instruments and for proper annotation in the Register of
566   Births.
567   SEC. 32. Registration Of Repatriation. - The instrument of repatriation and the oath of allegiance
568   to the Constitution and the Government of the Philippines. Shall be filed in the Civil Registrar's
569   Office of the place where the instrument was executed; Provided, That if the Philippine citizenship
570   is reacquired by naturalization, the order of the court or any mandated (omnibus) authority granting
571   citizenship shall be recorded in the Register of Court Order.
572   SEC. 33. Registration of Muslim Filipinos, Indigenous Cultural Communities (ICCS) or

574   or IPS, and CNSP, by reason of their religious, and indigenous peculiarities, and vulnerabilities,

582      c. Children in Need of Special Protection. - Civil registration of CNSP shall be by the

584         with the Department of Welfare and Development, and other concerned agencies.
585   SEC. 34. Civil Registry Documents as Public Documents. - The registry books making up the civil
586   register, the office files in hard copies and its corresponding digital files of the Civil Registrar's
587   Offices, certification or certified copies from these files and all other documents relating thereto
588   shall considered public and shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of the facts therein contained.
589   These public documents, except those documents which are to be kept strictly confidential as
590   provided for under this Act, and other applicable laws shall be to the public during office hours and
591   shall be kept in a secured place which shall be furnished to the civil registrar at the expenses of the
592   funds of the Civil Registrar's Office, Philippine Consulate Office, or the Sharia court concerned.
593   The civil registrar shall not, under any circumstances, permit any document entrusted to his/her care
594   to be removed from his/her office, except upon lawful order of the court, in which case the proper
595   receipt shall be secured.
596   SEC. 35. Digital or Electronic Reporting of Births, Deaths, and Marriages. - There shall be a
597   compulsory reporting of all occurrences of births, deaths, and marriages from all Barangays in the
598   Philippines. As Barangay Registration Agents, all Barangay Secretaries, or their representatives, are
599   hereby mandated to report these events using appropriate and secure electronic devices and systems
600   to the LCR/PSA and other government agencies mandated by law to monitor the status of
601   registration of vital civil registry events and shall assist the subsequent registration thereof.
602   Continuing skills training relevant to digital or electronic reporting of births, deaths, and marriages
603   of Barangay Registration Agents shall be provided by the PSA, I-GU, PACR, and other accredited
604   government and private institutions and organizations. This training shall equip Barangay
605   Registration Agents with the knowledge and skills to properly implement digital/electronic civil
606   registration.
607   SEC. 36. Development of National Civil Registry Database Software. - The National Government

609   shall develop official software responsive to the National Civil Registry Database (NCRD)
610   implementation. Participation and assistance of international and local private organizations having
611   parallel objectives on the full development of digital civil registration may be sought in the

619   unless it was done for the best interest of the child.

621   capable LCR/PSA shall be interconnected to facilitate retrieval, online certifications, and for local

625   SEC. 40. Penal Provisions. - Persons in authority who knowingly fail to perform their duties in
626   relation to this Act shall be held liable in accordance to law. Any solemnizing officer without being
627   authorized by the Civil Registrar or who, upon solemnizing marriage, refuses to exhibit his authority
628   to solemnize marriage when called upon to do so by the parties or parents, grandparents, guardians,
629   or persons having charge of the parties; or any bishop or officer, priest, minister, or imam of any
630   church, religion or sect, the regulations and practices whereof require bans or publication previous
631   to the solemnization of marriage that is subsequently declared illegal. or any solemnizing officer in
632   violation of this Act, shall punished with imprisonment of not than six (6) months but not to three
633   (3) years, a fine of not less than Fifty Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00) but not more than One Hundred
634   Thousand Pesos (P100,000.00), or both, and the revocation of the authority to solemnize upon the
635   discretion of the Court.
636   The attending physician, midwife, nurse, birth attendant, hospital administrator in-charge to cause
