13                                EXPLANATORY NOTE
14
15   Electricity is no longer a luxury. It is a basic necessity that powers every Filipino
16   home, every classroom, every hospital, every small business, every farm, and every
17   workplace. Yet every month, Filipino consumers continue to pay a twelve percent
18   (12%) Value-Added Tax (VAT) on their electricity consumption—a tax imposed on an
19   essential service that no household or enterprise can reasonably do without.
20
21   At a time when families are struggling with rising prices of food, transportation,
22   housing, education, and healthcare, the answer should not always be another subsidy
23   funded by taxpayers. The more sustainable and equitable solution is to allow Filipinos
24   to keep more of what they rightfully earn. Every peso that government chooses not to
25   collect from essential consumption is a peso that remains in the pockets of
26   hardworking families to spend on their children's education, better nutrition,
27   healthcare, savings, or investment. Rather than taxing citizens on nearly every aspect
28   of daily life and later redistributing assistance through various government programs,
29   public policy should first recognize that the fruits of one's labor belong primarily to
30   the worker and his or her family.
31
32   The removal of VAT on electricity is one of the most direct and meaningful forms of
33   tax relief that Congress can provide. Unlike subsidies that benefit only selected
34   sectors, a VAT exemption on electricity benefits every Filipino consumer. It
35   immediately reduces monthly electric bills, lowers operating costs for businesses,
36   eases the burden on farmers and manufacturers, and enhances the competitiveness
37   of Philippine industries. Lower electricity costs likewise help moderate inflation by
38   reducing production and distribution expenses across the economy.
39
40   This measure likewise promotes transparency and efficiency in governance. A twelve
41   percent (12%) reduction in the VAT component of electricity bills is immediate,
42   automatic, and requires no application, qualification, or government intervention.
43   Consumers receive the benefit directly through lower monthly bills without
44   additional bureaucracy or administrative expense.
45

 2   the extent necessary to provide essential public services and should avoid imposing
 3   unnecessary burdens on basic necessities. Allowing Filipinos to retain more of the

 5   encourages investment, and ultimately contributes to sustainable national growth.
 6
 7   For these reasons, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

14                              AN ACT
15   EXEMPTING THE SALE OF ELECTRICITY FOR RESIDENTIAL,
16   AGRICULTURAL, COMMERCIAL, INDUSTRIAL, AND OTHER END-
17   USER CONSUMPTION FROM VALUE-ADDED TAX, AMENDING FOR
18   THE PURPOSE SECTIONS 108 AND 109 OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL
19   REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
20
21   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
22   Congress assembled:
23
24   SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the "Electricity VAT Relief
25   Act."
26
27   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
28   protect consumers from rising electricity costs, promote affordable and reliable
29   access to electric power, support economic productivity, and enhance the
30   competitiveness of Philippine industries.
31
32   Recognizing electricity as an essential public necessity indispensable to households,
33   education, healthcare, agriculture, commerce, and national development, the State
34   shall remove the burden of value-added tax on electricity consumption in order to
35   reduce the cost of living and improve the purchasing power of Filipino consumers.
36
37   SECTION 3. Section 108(A) of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as
38   amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:
39
40         “SEC. 108. Value-Added Tax on the Sale of Services, Including Digital Services,
41         and the Use or Lease of Properties. —
42
43         “(A) Rate and Base of Tax. — There shall be levied, assessed, and collected a
44         value-added tax equivalent to twelve percent (12%) of the gross sales derived
45         from the sale or exchange of services, including digital services, and the use or
46         lease of properties.

 7   SECTION 5. VAT Treatment of Purchases. VAT paid on purchases directly
 8   attributable to VAT-exempt sales of electricity under this Act shall be treated in
 9   accordance with the provisions of the National Internal Revenue Code governing
10   VAT-exempt transactions.
11
12   Nothing herein shall be construed to exempt power generation companies,
13   transmission providers, distribution utilities, electric cooperatives, retail electricity
14   suppliers, or other entities from taxes other than the value-added tax on the sale of
15   electricity.
16
17   SECTION 6. Pass-Through Requirement. All generation companies, the
18   National Grid Corporation of the Philippines, distribution utilities, electric
19   cooperatives, retail electricity suppliers, and other electricity service providers shall
20   fully pass on the benefit of the VAT exemption granted under this Act to end-users.
21
22   The Energy Regulatory Commission shall ensure full compliance with this
23   requirement and shall issue the necessary regulations to ensure that the exemption is
24   fully reflected in electricity bills.
25
26   SECTION 7. Transparency in Billing. Electricity bills issued after the effectivity
27   of this Act shall separately indicate:
28   (a) the amount of VAT savings granted under this Act;
29   (b) the total reduction in the customer's electricity bill resulting from the exemption;
30   and
31   (c) such other information as may be prescribed by the Energy Regulatory
32   Commission.
33
34   SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days
35   from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance, upon the recommendation
36   of the Bureau of Internal Revenue and in coordination with the Department of
37   Energy, the Energy Regulatory Commission, and the National Electrification
38   Administration, shall promulgate the necessary implementing rules and regulations.
39
40   SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
41   regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended,
42   or modified accordingly.
43
44   SECTION 10. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared
45   unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
46   continue to be in full force and effect.
