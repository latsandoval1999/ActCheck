                        AN ACT
MAINSTREAMING THE USE OF BAMBOO AS A SUSTAINABLE
MATERIAL FOR THE BUILT ENVIRONMENT


                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The increasing demand for sustainable, climate-resilient, and resource-
efficient construction materials calls for innovative approaches in the country’s
built environment. As the Philippines continues to confront the impacts of climate
change, rapid urbanization, and infrastructure expansion, there is an urgent need
to promote building materials that are environmentally sustainable, locally
available, and structurally reliable.

      Bamboo presents a viable and strategic solution. As a rapidly renewable
material with high strength-to-weight ratio and significant carbon sequestration
capacity, bamboo has long been utilized in traditional construction and has gained
global recognition as a sustainable alternative to conventional building materials.
However, despite its potential, the absence of comprehensive and unified
technical standards has limited its broader and systematic integration into
mainstream architectural and structural practice.

      This proposed measure seeks to institutionalize bamboo as a recognized
and regulated construction material within the Philippine built environment. The
bill mandates the development of a Bamboo Architectural Code (BAC) and a
Bamboo Structural Code (BSC), to be adopted into the Architectural Code of the
  Philippines and the Structural Code of the Philippines, respectively. It further
  requires that all construction projects utilizing bamboo as a primary load-bearing
  material comply with the provisions of both Codes to ensure safety, structural
  integrity, and quality standards.

         The measure likewise promotes bamboo as a sustainable building material
  and integrates the practical education of bamboo utilization into the curricula of
  architecture, engineering, and related disciplines, thereby equipping future
  professionals with the technical competence to apply bamboo responsibly and
  innovatively. Additionally, it provides that bamboo products, by-products, and
  derivatives intended for construction must comply with Philippine National
  Standards, the National Building Code of the Philippines, and other applicable
  referral codes.

        By establishing clear standards, strengthening professional education, and
  promoting sustainable practices, this legislation advances environmental
  stewardship, supports local industries, enhances disaster-resilient construction,
  and contributes to a more sustainable and climate-responsive built environment.

        In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

                              CHAPTER 1
                          GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bamboo for a
Sustainable Built Environment Act”.


SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the declared policy of the State to protect
and advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord
with the rhythm and harmony of nature.


It is likewise the policy of the State to promote inclusive and sustainable
economic growth and productive employment; make human settlements safe,
resilient and sustainable; protect, restore and promote sustainable use of
terrestrial ecosystems, manage forests sustainably, combat desertification, and
halt and reverse land degradation and biodiversity loss; accelerate climate action
and strengthen disaster resilience.


In pursuance of this policy, the State shall encourage the propagation and use of
bamboo for a sustainable built environment.


SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act,
   (a) Bamboo refers to any species within the subfamily Bambusoideae of the
      family Poaceae, defined as a tropical, subtropical, or temperate fast-
      growing grass with lignocellulosic matrix, usually having hollow culms,
      recognized by the relevant government agencies as a suitable material for
      a bamboo structure or the built environment;
   (b) Bamboo Architectural Code (BAC) refers to the set of regulations and
      standards governing the design, construction, inspection and maintenance
      of bamboo used as architectural building materials. In accordance with
      Section 4 of this Act, the BAC shall be incorporated in the Architectural
      Code of the Philippines;
   (c) Bamboo Structural Code (BSC) refers to the set of regulations and
      standards governing the design, construction, inspection, and maintenance
      of a bamboo structure. In accordance with Section 4 of this Act, the BSC
      shall be incorporated in the Structural Code of the Philippines;
   (d) Bamboo structure refers to any building or structure in which bamboo is
      used as primary load-bearing material;
   (e) Built environment refers to the external and internal environments and any
      element, component, or fitting that is commissioned, designed, constructed
      and managed for use by people;



                                                                                 2
   (f) Engineered bamboo refers to composite products manufactured by
      binding together either veneers, strands, particles, fibers, strips, or slats of
      bamboo with a suitable adhesive designed to meet specific uses and
      compliant in accordance with Sec. 5 (b) and Sec. 10 hereof;
   (g) Structural bamboo refers to bamboo in a type or form utilized for
      structure, construction, and load-bearing purposes; and
   (h) Structural engineered bamboo refers to engineered bamboo utilized as a
      load-bearing material.


SEC. 4. Development of the Bamboo Referral Codes for Incorporation in the
Structural Code of the Philippines and Architectural Code of the Philippines.
– To mainstream the use of bamboo in the built environment for reduced
environmental impact throughout its lifecycle, integrate and implement green
practices, promote the development of a green local market for bamboo, and
ensure adherence to the framework of minimum standards and requirements
governing buildings and structures, the following are hereby mandated:


   (a) Development of the BAC for Adoption in the Architectural Code of the
      Philippines. – The Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture, in
      consultation with the Board of Environmental Planning, the Board of
      Interior Design, the Board of Landscape Architecture and relevant
      stakeholders and experts in the use of bamboo for the built environment,
      shall develop the BAC. The BAC shall include guidelines and standards
      taking into account best practices for the sustainable use of bamboo,
      aesthetics, safety, environmental impact, and cultural considerations. The
      Board of Architects shall incorporate the BAC in the current and
      subsequent amendments to the Architectural Code of the Philippines.



                                                                                     3
(b) Development of the BSC for Adoption in the Structural Code of the
  Philippines. – The Professional Regulatory Board of Civil Engineers, in
  consultation with the Board of Chemistry, the Board of Structural
  Engineers, the Institute of Civil Engineers, the Department of Human
  Settlements and Urban Development, and other relevant stakeholders in
  the use of bamboo for the built environment, shall develop the BSC.


  The BSC shall include guidelines and standards taking into account
  structural integrity and safety. The Board of Civil Engineers shall
  incorporate the BSC in the current and subsequent amendments to the
  Structural Code of the Philippines.


(c) Research and Development for Bamboo in the Built Environment, and
  Establishment and Management of Comprehensive Database for Product
  Development. – The Department of Science and Technology – Forest

     i.   Develop research and development programs on bamboo and its
          uses in the built environment;
    ii.   Innovate and create new products and uses of bamboo in the built
          environment;
   iii.   Maintain   a comprehensive database on bamboo research,
          engineered bamboo, and structural bamboo products and services,
          for the use of relevant government agencies, the private sector and
          the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) in the development of
          a green local market and to facilitate         sustainable public
          procurement; and
   iv.    Coordinate closely with the BAC and BSC stakeholders, relevant
          government agencies and the private sector to incorporate

              engineering and construction pursuant to Section 12 of this Act.


SEC. 5. Implementation of the Bamboo Referral Codes. –
   (a) All construction projects that incorporate bamboo as a primary load-
         bearing material shall comply with the provisions of both the BSC and
         BAC.


   (b) The Philippine Bamboo Industry Development Council (PBIDC) shall
         enforce compliance with the BSC and BAC during construction
         inspections, work with the DTI for the cost-efficiency validation and
         certification of green products, and issue certificates of compliance in
         accordance with applicable certification rules for green products under
         the governing regulatory framework. The Board of Architecture and
         Board of Engineering, in coordination with the Department of Public
         Works and Highways (DPWH), shall incorporate the BSC into the
         existing building code, referral codes and permit application procedures.


SEC. 6. Promoting Bamboo as a Sustainable Building Material. – The PBIDC
shall:
   (a) In coordination with the DOST and other relevant agencies, conduct and
         encourage the conduct of research and development in bamboo
         engineering, construction techniques, and the utilization of bamboo
         composites to ensure sustainability for construction and form resilient
         supply chains;




                                                                                 5
   (b) Promote awareness of the benefits of bamboo as a sustainable building
       material through public campaigns, education programs, and incentives
       for bamboo cultivation; and


   (c) Develop programs, projects and initiatives that will promote the
       cultivation of bamboo nationwide, ensure its commercialization and
       widespread use in the built environment.


SEC.7. Baseline Utilization of Bamboo for Building Construction. – Within
three (3) years from the enactment of this Act, government agencies shall adopt
measures for the construction of government infrastructure utilizing bamboo for
the built environment pursuant to applicable laws, rules and regulations on
sustainable, green, and inclusive public procurement.
   (a) For all new building construction, at least fifty percent (50%) of the total
       wood-based structural requirements shall use bamboo, subject to the
       availability of supply in the green local market and the procuring entity’s
       inclusive procurement program.


   (b) For identified structures, the total wood-based volume requirement shall
       utilize structural bamboo.


   Within five (5) years from the enactment of this Act, the private sector shall
be encouraged to adopt similar measures utilizing bamboo for the built
environment for new construction.


SEC. 8. Introduction of the Practical Education of Bamboo Utilization in the
Built Environment Architecture, Engineering, and Related Services Curricula.
– To ensure widespread adoption of the use of bamboo, its products, byproducts,

                                                                                  6
and derivatives in building construction, furniture production, and design
utilizing bamboo in the built environment, and to ensure the creation of
sustainable and resilient supply chains for products and services in relation to the
use of bamboo in the built environment, the Commission on Higher Education
(CHED) shall, after consultation with the Board of Architecture and the Board of
Engineering, immediately include bamboo utilization in the existing curricula in
architecture, engineering, and related services in the next policies, standards, and
guidelines curriculum update of respective programs, or create a specialization
course that integrates bamboo utilization for the built environment in the existing
curricula. A minor subject of at least two (2) units shall be offered to architecture
and engineering undergraduate students regarding “Introduction to Structural
Bamboo,” including model-making classes.


SEC. 9. Introduction of the Practical Education of Bamboo Utilization in the
Continuing Professional Development (CPD) and Professional Licensure
Examinations. – Within five (5) years from the enactment of this Act, the
Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) shall require the Professional
Regulatory Boards for Architecture, Interior Design, and Engineering disciplines
to include practical education on bamboo utilization in the built environment as
part of the expected learning competency outcomes for licensure examination
takers. Moreover, the PRC shall encourage and prioritize continuing professional
education providers who can develop and offer practical education on bamboo
utilization in the built environment in the form of hands-on workshops for
architects, interior designers, and engineers.


      The CPD of concerned professionals shall have a seminar of at least five
(5) CPD units on “Introduction to Structural Engineered Bamboo,” which shall
include model-making.

SEC. 10. Certification of Bamboo for Construction. – The bamboo products,
by-products, and their derivatives that will be used for building construction shall
be compliant with the Philippine National Standards, the Philippine Building
Code, and other applicable referral codes. The concerned government agencies,
including the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority, shall offer
bamboo training programs to farmer cooperatives and micro-business enterprises
(BMBEs) to encourage bamboo farming and production and enhance knowledge
in proper planting and propagation of bamboo, including the bamboo species used
as structural elements in construction. The Department of Environment and
Natural Resources (DENR) and the Department of Agriculture (DA) shall
provide bamboo saplings for free to farmer cooperatives and BMBEs.


SEC. 11. Incentives. – For the purpose of encouraging registered farmer
cooperatives and BMBEs in the bamboo value chain, they shall enjoy the
applicable fiscal and non-fiscal incentives provided under existing laws, orders,
issuances, and regulations, and Title XII of the National Internal Revenue Code
of 1997, as amended by Republic Act No. 12066, otherwise known as the
Corporate Recovery and Tax Incentives for Enterprises to Maximize
Opportunities for Reinvigorating the Economy (CREATE MORE) Act, Republic
Act No. 11321 or the “Sagip Saka Act”, and Republic Act No. 7900, otherwise
known as High Value Crops Development Act of 1995.


Moreover, the farmer cooperatives and business enterprises shall enjoy tax and
duty-free importation of capital equipment that are actually, directly, and
exclusively used in the planting, propagation, production, and manufacture of
bamboo, engineered bamboo, structural bamboo, structural engineered bamboo
as well as the construction of bamboo structure.

SEC. 12. Reporting and Review. – The PBIDC, DTI and DPWH shall conduct
periodic reviews and updates of the BSC and BAC to incorporate technological
advancements and best practices in bamboo engineering and construction.



                                                                               10
SEC.13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the PBIDC shall, in coordination with the DPWH, DTI,
DENR, DA, DOST, Department of the Interior and Local Government, NEDA,
(CHED), Department of Education, Philippine Information Agency, and other
relevant stakeholders, formulate and promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.


SEC.14. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.


SEC.15. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, or regulations inconsistent with
the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


SEC. 16. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
