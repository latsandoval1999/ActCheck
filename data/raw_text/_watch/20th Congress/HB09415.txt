      This proposed measure seeks to institutionalize a rights-based, family-
                                                                              parents and
centered, and disability-inclusive framework of protection and support for
primary caregivers of children with disabilities. The bill provides labor protections and
flexible work arrangements for caregiver-employees; grants caregiver leave benefits;
establishes means-tested monthly cash subsidies for low-income caregiving
households; expands access to PhilHealth coverage and tax relief measures for
disability-related expenses; and institutionalizes psychosocial support and respite care
services to safeguard the mental and emotionalwell-being of caregivers.

       The measure likewise ensures that households with children with disabilities
are accorded priority in social protection, emergency assistance, and social
amelioration programs, recognizing the heightened vulnerability of caregiving families
during times of crisis and economic hardship.

       Ultimately, this bill proceeds from the recognition that empowering parents and
primary caregivers is indispensable to protecting the dignity, welfare, and future of
children with disabilities themselves. A truly inclusive society cannot be achieved if
caregiving families are left unsupported, overburdened, and invisible within our social
protection system.

       ln view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is           sought.

                               GENERAL PROVISIONS

      SEGTION 1, ShortTitte.- This Act shall be known as the "Parents and Primary
Caregivers of Children with Disabilities Acf."

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Poticy. - The State reaffirms its unwavering
commitment to uphold the dignity, rights, and welfare of persons with disabilities
(pWDs), including children with disabilities (CWDs), consistent with Article ll, Section
11 of the Philippine Constitution, as well as the Philippines' binding covenants under
the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. The State
further reaffirms its policy of promoting the welfare and defending the rights of children,
especially CWDs, to assistance, including proper care and nutrition, and special
protection. ln line with this, and recognizing that the realization of these rights
necessarily depends on the support, stability, and well-being of those primarily
responsible for their care and development, the State therefore recognizes that the
caregiving families of CWDs are entitled to comprehensive protection, equitable
access to social services, and meaningful participation in society.
       Towards this end, the State declares that parents and primary caregivers of
CWDs occupy an indispensable role in the habilitation, rehabilitation, and social
inclusion of their children and wards. The State recognizes the disproportionate
financial, emotional, and opportunity burdens borne by such caregivers and hereby
                                                                     policy framework
commits to a rights-based, family-centered, and disability-inclusive
to address these burdens.

         SECTION   l. Obiectiyes. - This Act shall pursue the following objectives:
   (a) To grant labor rights and workplace accommodations to parents and primary
         caregivers of CWDs in order to maintain employment while fulfilling their
         caregiving resPonsibilities;


   (b)    To provide direct financial assistance to parents and primary caregivers of
          CWDs to alleviate poverty and reduce disability-related costs;


   (c) To ensure access to social protection benefits, psychosocial support, and
          respite care services;


   (d) To provide tax relief measures for disability-related expenses incurred by the
          parents and primary caregivers of CWDs;


   (e) To ensure the psychological and mental well-being of parents and primary
          caregivers of cwDs by providing caregiver support programs; and


   (0 To promote disability-inclusive emergency preparedness and social
          amelioration frameworks.
