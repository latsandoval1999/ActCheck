EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Land Transportation Office (LTO), under the Department of
Transportation, provides essential motor vehicle registration, driver licensing,
permits, and related regulatory services. The accessibility and adequacy of its
frontline offices are important for efficient public service and for reducing
unnecessary travel and waiting time for the public.

The LTO Talisay City Mobile Unit was established in 1995 and was converted
into an Extension Office in 2000. Located in Barangay Lawaan Ill, Talisay City,
Cebu, the office has served Talisay City and clients from nearby communities,
particularly Minglanilla, for more than two decades.

The service area has a substantial population base. The 2024 Census of
Population reported 263,832 residents in Talisay City and 155,934 in Minglanilla,
or a combined 419,766 people. The position paper submitted by LTO Region VII
also reports combined registration and licensing revenue averaging
P119,957,110.83 annually for 2023-2025 and 439,249 registration and licensing
transactions over the same period.

Despite its sustained operations and workload, the Talisay office lacks a
regular District Office structure, dedicated Plantilla positions, and corresponding
appropriations. Its operations rely partly on personnel from existing offices and Job
Order personnel. Its conversion into a regular District Office would provide an
appropriate organizational structure, staffing complement, facilities, and
operational funding.

The proposed conversion will strengthen the accessibility, continuity, and
accountability of LTO services in Talisay City and nearby communities. The LTO
and other appropriate agencies should determine the final classification, staffing
pattern, and resource requirements under applicable policies and subject to official
validation and approval.

in view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL No. 11719

Introduced by HON. RHEA MAE A. GULLAS

AN ACT
CONVERTING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) TALISAY CITY
EXTENSION OFFICE LOCATED IN TALISAY CITY, PROVINCE OF CEBU, INTO
A REGULAR DISTRICT OFFICE — CLASS A, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The Land Transportation Office (LTO) Talisay City Extension
Office located in Talisay City, Province of Cebu, is hereby converted into a regular
LTO District Office — Class A.

SEC. 2. The Secretary of the Department of Transportation (DOTr) shall

immediately include in the department's program the operationalization of the LTO
Extension in Talisay City, the funding of which shall be included in the annual

General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 3. The Secretary of the (DOTr) shall issue the necessary rules and
regulations within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
