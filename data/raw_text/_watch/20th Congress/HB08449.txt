 PORTAL TO ENHANCE TRANSPARENCY, ACCOUNTABILITY, AND PUBLIC
        PARTICIPATION IN THE PHILIPPINE BUDGET PROCESS


                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Transparency, accountability, and good governance are the most important
pillars of any government, which enable it to fairly, judiciously, and efficiently fulfill its
responsibility to deliver public services and goods. Embedding these qualities in the
very institutions that govern the Filipino people fosters democracy and improves the
quality of life for all Filipinos.

       As one of the most significant and crucial pieces of legislation that the Congress
of the Philippines passes, the annual National Budget is among the most essential
instruments of governance. Despite this, its deliberation and appropriation continue
to happen behind closed doors, with its presentation to the public often peppered with
complex, highly technical language that hinders the ordinary Filipino from scrutinizing
it.

       This measure institutionalizes the use of blockchain technology across our
financial, budget, and procurement frameworks, modernizing and enhancing the
State’s financial transparency and the accountability of public officials to the people
they serve. The goal of this bill is not only to ensure that the National Budget is publicly
available, but also to improve citizens’ participation and engagement, and national
awareness of the Government’s fiscal policies and budget utilization.

       Blockchain technology is a decentralized digital ledger system that records
transactions across a network of computers in a secure, transparent, and tamper-
resistant manner. It is a powerful tool that has the potential to eradicate corrupt
practices within our fiscal systems.

       The Blockchain System promises a more democratic, inclusive, and accountable
system of governance by granting the Filipino people with unprecedented access to
the National Budget. By institutionalizing transparency through blockchain technology,
the State affirms that public funds are a public trust—open to scrutiny, safeguarded
against misuse, and managed with integrity. In doing so, the system strengthens
democratic accountability and restores citizens’ confidence in the stewardship of the
national budget.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 3   SEC. 2. Declaration of P olicy. – It is the policy of the State to uphold transparency,
 4   accountability, and good governance in the management and disbursement of public
 5   funds. The State shall maintain honesty and integrity in public service, adopt effective
 6   measures against graft and corruption, and institutionalize public disclosure through
 7   open, verifiable, and citizen-accessible digital systems. To this end, the government
 8   shall establish a digital platform that provides timely, accurate, tamper-resistance,
 9   interoperable, and traceable publication of government budget transactions. The
10   system shall guarantee that all government appropriations, disbursements,
11   procurements, and expenditures are disclosed and made publicly accessible to citizens
12   and oversight institutions.
13
14   In pursuit of a forward-looking, resilient, and future-ready governance framework, the
15   State shall apply futures thinking and anticipatory governance in the design,
16   implementation, and continuous enhancement of digital budget disclosure systems.
17   This approach ensures adaptability to emerging technologies, long-term risk, evolving
18   public needs, and potential societal impacts.

 5   SEC. 7. Establishm ent of the CADENA. – The CADENA shall serve as the official
 6   and publicly accessible portal for all public budget data required under this Act.
 7
 8   The CADENA shall be the primary access point through which the public may freely
 9   access, search, and download public budget data in open-source, interoperable,
10   tamper-resistant, and structured digital format. It shall provide features that promote
11   transparency, accountability, and citizen participation, subject to tiered-access
12   controls to protect personal data and national security information.
13
14   The CADENA shall be developed using ledger technology including, but not limited to,
15   blockchain, secure data integrity technology, or other technology that conforms with
16   the standards and features prescribed under Section 8 of this Act. The NBTAC shall,
17   at intervals of note more than three (3) years, review and recommend the adoption
18   of alternative or emerging technologies.
19
20   The CADENA shall interface with existing government systems including, but not
21   limited to, the Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS), Budget
22   and Treasury Management System (BTMS), Philippine Government Electronic
23   Procurement System (PhilGEPS), and other platforms through standardized
24   Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) or newer technologies. These systems shall
25   maintain parallel operation during transition periods as specified by the NBTAC.

27   SEC. 8. Core Features of the CADENA. – The CADENA shall be designated to
28   promote transparency and accountability in all phases of the national budget cycle. It
29   shall, at a minimum, have the following features:
30
31   (a) Data Integrity and Authenticity – All published records shall bear digital and non-
32       repudiable signatures, timestamps, and metadata ensuring verifiable origin and
33       traceability;
34   (b) Transparency and Accessibility – All budget records and reports shall be made
35       available through public dashboards and open APIs in structured digital formats
36       for real-time access, visualization, and reuse, subject to tiered access controls to
37       protect personal data and privileged information;
38   (c) Redundancy and Oversight – Independent mirror sites, hosted by qualified and
39       accredited institutions, including government agencies, the private sector, civil
40       society, and the academe, and other third-party audit mechanisms shall be
41       established to safeguard against data loss, tampering, and concentration of control
42       and strengthen mutual oversight and institutional accountability;
43   (d) Traceability Across the Budget Cycle – The system shall maintain a structured audit
44       trail linking each stage of the budget process from call and approval to release,
45       procurement, and liquidation, identifying the responsible committee or agency at
 1       each decision point, while observing data minimization and purpose limitation
 2       under applicable privacy laws. The CADENA shall display all budget records without
 3       alteration and shall ensure that all documents, dashboards, public data sets, and
 4       APIs reflect the complete national budget cycle;
 5   (e) Programmability and Process Validation – The CADENA may employ rule-based or
 6       automated validation tools to ensure compliance with budgetary laws, detect
 7       irregularities, and flag inconsistencies for oversight review, without substituting or
 8       interfering with lawful approval procedures;
 9   (f) Security and Resilience – The system shall employ encryption, redundancy, access
10       controls, and disaster recovery protocols that comply with international
11       cybersecurity standards;
12   (g) Interoperability and Open Standards – The CADENA shall adopt open-source,
13       technology-neutral frameworks and ensure compatibility with existing and future
14       government information systems. It shall be designed for integration with other
15       fully secured national digital platforms to allow seamless data exchange,
16       interoperability, and unified public access. The CADENA shall likewise be accessible
17       to the general public through its own portal and/or other government portals to
18       promote ease of use, transparency, and citizen engagement;
19   (h) Auditability – The system shall maintain verifiable activity logs accessible to the
20       COA and other public auditors and civil society organizations duly authorized by
21       the NBTAC for post-factum review, without exposing personal or security-sensitive
22       information on public endpoints;
23   (i) Citizen Feedback and Participation – The CADENA shall include real-time channels
24       for citizens and civil society organizations to report, track, and receive
25       acknowledgment of verified discrepancies or irregularities in published data. Each
26       report shall be logged, traceable, and subject to timely resolution by the concerned
27       agency;
28   (j) Scalability and Performance – The system shall be designed to handle high
29       transaction volumes, ensure continuity of operations during peak usage, and
30       include forward-looking capacity planning sufficient to accommodate at least ten
31       (10) years of projected data growth and system demand; and
32   (k) Outcome-based Metrics – The system shall include performance dashboards linking
33       budget disbursements to outputs and outcomes, allowing public tracking of agency
34       performance and corrective actions.

30   SEC. 15. Evidentiary Value of CADENA R ecords. – All records, data, and
31   transactions stored in CADENA shall be considered prima facie evidence of the facts
32   stated therein and shall be admissible in all judicial, quasi-judicial, and administrative
33   proceedings, Provided, That the record:
34      (a) Bears a digital timestamp;
35      (b) Is cryptographically signed or verified through the digital portal; and
36      (c) Has not been altered without authorization, as verified by CADENA’s integrity
37          controls.

39   SEC. 16. Adm inistrative P enalties. – Any public official or employee who performs
40   or causes the performance of the following acts shall be liable:
 1      (a) Failure to disclose the required public budget data within seven (7) working
 2          days from the date of approval, official issuance, receipt by the concerned
 3          agency, or completion or generation of the document or work, whichever is
 4          applicable; and
 5      (b) Issuance, approval, or implementation of policies, rules and regulations that
 6          are manifestly contrary or intended to circumvent the provisions of this Act, or
 7          obstructs or interferes with the publication, audit, or verification of information
 8          under CADENA.
 9   Any violation of the preceding actions shall warrant the following penalties and
10   liabilities:
11      (a) First Offense. Administrative penalty of reprimand or up to six (6) months
12          suspension from the service; and
13      (b) Second Offense. Administrative penalty of dismissal from the service and
14          perpetual disqualification from holding office.
15   In cases where no specific officer, employee, or unit is designated as responsible for
16   the submission, publication, or safekeeping of documents required under this Act, the
17   HoPE shall be presumed responsible and held administratively liable under applicable
18   laws.
19
20   Non-compliance detected through CADENA shall trigger automatic referral to the CSC
21   and the Ombudsman. Quarterly compliance dashboards shall be publicly displayed.
22
23   SEC. 17. P enal P rovisions. – (a) Any public official, employee, private individual or
24   entity who commits any of the following shall be liable:
25      (1) Fails to upload, disclose, or publish the required public budget data within thirty
26          (30) working days, without justifiable cause, from the date of approval, official
27          issuance, receipt by the concerned agency, or completion or generation of the
28          document or work, whichever is applicable;
29      (2) Knowingly publishes false, or misleading information to stimulate compliance
30          with this Act; or
31      (3) Willfully denies, conceals the existence of, or destroys or causes to be
32          destroyed, information and/or any document mandated for disclosure under
33          this Act.
34   Any violation of the preceding actions shall be meted out with the penalty of
35   imprisonment ranging from six (6) years and one (1) day to not more than fifteen (15)
36   years and a fine of up to Three Million Pesos (PHP 3,000,000.00).
37
38   (b) If such act was committed to conceal or facilitate fraud, graft, or corruption
39   involving public funds or property, the public officer or employee shall be liable as a
40   principal or co-conspirator to crimes punishable under Republic Act No. 3019 or the
 1   “Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act” and Republic Act No. 7080 or the “Anti-Plunder
 2   Act”; and
 3
 4   (c) Any private individual or entity who knowingly induces or causes the commission
 5   of the foregoing acts shall be liable as principal by inducement in the prosecution of
 6   public officials or employees under this section.
 7
 8   Criminal liability shall also be incurred through bribery, extortion, or malicious and
 9   deliberate solicitation of favors in cash or in kind. In such cases, the pertinent
10   provisions of the Revised Penal Code and other special laws shall apply.
