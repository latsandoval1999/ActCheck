                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to reclassify specific portions of the Busol Watershed located in
Barangays Ambiong, Lubas, and Beckel in the Municipality of La Trinidad, Province
of Benguet. This shall reflect actual land use and shall recognize the ancestral land
rights of Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs) who
have long occupied and cultivated these areas. The measure aims to correct a
long-standing inconsistency between the present legal classification of the land and
the historical and continuing use of the area by ICCs/IPs, while still upholding
essential environmental safeguards.

       The Busol Watershed, covering 336.58 hectares1, was designated as a forest
and watershed reservation under Proclamation No. 15 issued on April 27, 1922.
While its ecological importance remains clear, particularly its role in water and
timber resource conservation, the proclamation did not account for communities that
had already settled, farmed, and managed portions of the area since time
immemorial. These lands have long served as ancestral domains and traditional
livelihood sites for ICCs/IPs residing in Barangays Ambiong, Lubas, and Beckel.

        Today, because the land remains classified as a watershed reservation,
ICCs/IPs face legal and administrative barriers in securing ownership documents
and obtaining formal recognition of their ancestral land rights. Despite their
deep-rooted presence and stewardship of the area, they cannot secure Certificates of
Ancestral Land Titles (CALTs) under Republic Act No. 8371, otherwise known as
“The Indigenous Peoples Rights Act of 1997” (IPRA) due to the current land
classification.

       This bill reclassifies specific, validated portions of the Busol Watershed into
Alienable and Disposable (A&D) lands exclusively for the purpose of recognizing
qualified ancestral land claims. The proposed measure ensures that only areas
historically occupied and used by ICCs/IPs will be reclassified, and excludes
ecologically sensitive portions of the watershed such as protected forests, riparian

                          AN ACT
RECLASSIFYING SPECIFIC PORTIONS OF THE BUSOL WATERSHED
LOCATED IN BARANGAYS AMBIONG, LUBAS, AND BECKEL IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF LA TRINIDAD, PROVINCE OF BENGUET, TO REFLECT
ACTUAL LAND USE AND RECOGNIZING THE RIGHTS OF INDIGENOUS
CULTURAL COMMUNITIES/INDIGENOUS PEOPLES (ICCs/IPs) THEREIN,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Busol Watershed
Reclassification and ICC/IP Rights Recognition Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and conserve forest and watershed reservations while ensuring the
recognition, promotion, and protection of the rights of Indigenous Cultural
Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs) to their ancestral lands as mandated
under Republic Act (RA) No. 8371, otherwise known as “The Indigenous Peoples
Rights Act of 1997”.

       Towards this end, the State recognizes that protecting the environment and
upholding social justice must go hand in hand. It affirms its commitment to ensure
that the lands long lived in, cared for, and cultivated by ICCs/IPs are properly
recognized and classified according to how these communities genuinely use them,
while still upholding environmental safeguards and complying with existing laws.
This honors both the people who have nurtured these lands for generations and the
need to preserve them for the future.

      SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to specific portions of the Busol
Watershed located within Barangays Ambiong, Lubas, and Beckel in the
Municipality of La Trinidad, Province of Benguet, which:




                                           1
          a.​ have been occupied, developed, or utilized since time immemorial by
       ICCs/IPs for residential, agricultural, and traditional livelihood purposes;
       and

          b.​ are no longer consistent with the current classification as watershed
       and forest reservation under Proclamation No. 15, series of 1922.

      The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), in
coordination with the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP), and the
Municipality of La Trinidad, shall delineate the specific areas to be reclassified based
on cadastral surveys, actual land use, and validated historical occupation by
ICCs/IPs.

       SEC. 4. Reclassification. – The identified portions of the Busol Watershed shall
be reclassified from Watershed and Forest Reservation to Alienable and Disposable
(A&D) lands, strictly limited to the validated ancestral lands of ICCs/IPs.

      Such reclassification shall not include protected areas, riparian zones,
recharge areas, or areas crucial to the ecological function of the watershed.

       SEC. 5. Recognition of ICC/IP Ancestral Lands. – Upon reclassification and
delineation, the NCIP shall initiate and fast-track the processing of Certificates of
Ancestral Land Titles (CALTs) for qualified ICC/IP claimants consistent with RA No.
8371, and its implementing rules and regulations.

      The CALTs issued shall serve as evidence of ownership, subject to the
customary laws, traditions, and collective rights of the ICCs/IPs.

       SEC. 6. Environmental Safeguards. – Nothing in this Act shall diminish the
ecological significance of the Busol Watershed. All reclassified areas shall remain
subject to:

          a.​ sustainable land use practices;

          b.​ environmental compliance requirements;

          c.​ development restrictions necessary to protect the watershed; and

          d.​ the implementation      of   DENR,     NCIP,   and    local government
       environmental policies.

       SEC. 7. Inter-Agency Committee. – An Inter-Agency Committee, herein referred
to as the Committee, is hereby created to implement this Act. It shall be composed of
members from the Municipality of La Trinidad, the Provincial Government of
Benguet, and representatives of ICCs/IPs of Barangays Ambiong, Lubas, and Beckel.
The Committee shall be jointly chaired by the Secretary of the Natural Resources and
the Chairperson of the NCIP.

      SEC. 8. Funding. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be charged to the appropriations of the DENR and NCIP. Thereafter,
funding shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

        SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DENR and NCIP, in coordination with the Municipality of
La Trinidad, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to effectively
implement the provisions of this Act.

      SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

      SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, proclamations, orders, and
issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby amended, modified, or repealed
accordingly.

       SEC. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
