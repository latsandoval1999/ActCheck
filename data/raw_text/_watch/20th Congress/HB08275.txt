                            EXPLANATORY NOTE


       The growing tension in the Middle East and the conflicts between states
compel us to adjust or take immediate precautionary measures to mitigate their
effects on our economy and ensure it remains buoyant and stable. Oil and its by-
products are the most affected commodities in these situations. Suspensions and
restrictions on their production, manufacturing, and shipment will automatically
create high volatility in fuel prices in the Philippines. The increase in fuel prices
creates a domino effect on the prices of commodities, affecting most
significantly vulnerable sectors.

       This measure aims to empower the President to intervene and protect the
Filipino people. It authorizes the President to either suspend the imposition of
excise tax on petroleum products or reduce the tax rates imposed upon them.
This authorization is specifically granted during global or national economic
emergencies or when public interest is at stake. A favorable economic condition
warrants that such an emergency measure shall be lifted immediately.

      This bill also seeks to amend Section 148 of the National Internal
Revenue Code of 1997to guarantee any sitting President the power of excise tax
suspension or rate reduction when warranted by public interest and unfavorable
economic situations.

      The Department of Finance and the Bureau of Internal Revenue are
required to provide mechanisms for compliance and monitoring to ensure that
any suspension or reduction shall benefit the consumers.

                               AN ACT
       AUTHORIZING THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF THE
      PHILIPPINES TO SUSPEND OR REDUCE EXCISE TAX RATES ON
        PETROLEUM PRODUCTS UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10963,
            OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE TAX REFORM FOR
        ACCELERATION AND INCLUSION (TRAIN) LAW, DURING A
       GLOBAL OR NATIONAL ECONOMIC EMERGENCY OR WHEN
     PUBLIC INTEREST REQUIRES, AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE,
     SECTION 148 OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF
                         1997, AS AMENDED

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1         SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Excise Tax
 2   Flexibility Act of 2026”

 3          SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
 4   ensure economic stability in times of national or economic emergency, or when
 5   public interest requires, to protect its citizens from the high volatility of
 6   petroleum product prices and to mitigate inflationary pressures on the economy.

 7          SECTION 3. Authority to Suspend or Reduce Excise Tax on Petroleum
 8   Products – The President of the Republic of the Philippines is hereby
 9   authorized to suspend or reduce the excise tax rates imposed on petroleum
10   products during global or national economic emergencies or when public
11   interest requires, upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Finance.

22         The President of the Republic of the Philippines may suspend the
23   imposition of or reduce the excise tax on petroleum products during global
24   or national economic emergencies, or when public interest so requires,
25   upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Finance, with due
26   consideration to national fiscal stability and its inflationary effects on the
27   economy. Provided, that such suspension or reduction may be lifted when
28   conditions that warrant such suspension or reduction no longer exist.

29          SECTION 5. Reportorial Requirement. – The President shall submit to
30   Congress within thirty (30) days after the effectivity of this Act, a detailed
31   report of the effect of the suspension or reduction, as the case may be, on
32   inflation, fiscal stability, and compliance.

33          SECTION 6. Safeguards. – The Department of Finance and the Bureau
34   of Internal Revenue shall ensure that the suspension or rate reduction shall not
35   be used to the disadvantage of the consumers. They shall impose and monitor
36   the compliance of all companies and businesses engaged in manufacturing,
37   producing, importing, trading, or selling petroleum products.
