                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

     Accurate and internationally traceable measurements form the foundation of
scientific research, industrial productivity, consumer protection, public health,
environmental monitoring, trade facilitation, and technological innovation. Virtually
every sector of the economy depends upon reliable measurement systems to ensure
product quality, regulatory compliance, manufacturing precision, and international
market acceptance. As global trade and advanced technologies become increasingly
dependent on measurement accuracy, a strong and internationally recognized
national metrology institution has become indispensable for national development.

      While the Philippines has made significant progress in developing its national
measurement capabilities through the National Metrology Division of the Department
of Science and Technology–Industrial Technology Development Institute (DOST-ITDI),
the country's expanding industrial, scientific, and technological requirements demand
a more robust, permanent, and internationally credible institutional framework.
Strengthening the National Measurement System will improve the country's
competitiveness, facilitate exports, support innovation, enhance consumer confidence,
and ensure compliance with international standards and mutual recognition
arrangements.

      This measure seeks to establish the Philippine National Metrology Institute
(PNMI) by transforming the existing National Metrology Division into a dedicated
national institution responsible for maintaining national measurement standards,
providing calibration and technical services, conducting metrology research,
developing human capital, and representing the Philippines in regional and
international metrology organizations. The bill likewise strengthens governance
through the creation of a Governing Board, institutionalizes regional metrology
laboratories, establishes a National Metrology Training Center, and provides a long-
term Capacity Enhancement Plan to ensure sustained development of the country's
measurement infrastructure.

    The proposed legislation likewise promotes stronger coordination among
government agencies, industry, academe, and international partners, while supporting

research, innovation, education, and workforce development in measurement science.
By reinforcing the National Quality Infrastructure, the measure enhances the country's
ability to compete in global markets, improve manufacturing capability, strengthen
regulatory systems, and safeguard public welfare through accurate and reliable
measurements.

      Establishing the Philippine National Metrology Institute is a strategic investment
in the country's scientific and technological future. It lays the foundation for a modern,
innovation-driven economy supported by internationally recognized measurement
standards that advance industrial development, public confidence, and sustainable
national growth.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 5         SEC. 3. Creation of the Philippine National Metrology Institute. - The
 6   National Metrology Division of the Department of Science and Technology-Industrial
 7   Technology Development Institute (DOST-ITDI) is hereby transformed into the
 8   Philippine National Metrology Institute (PNMI).
 9
10       The PNMI shall be attached to the DOST for policy coordination and
11   administrative supervision.
12
13        All assets, facilities, equipment, records, and resources presently assigned to the
14   National Metrology Division shall be transferred to the PNMI.
15
16        SEC. 4. Objectives. - The PNMI shall pursue the following objectives:
17        (a) strengthen the National Measurement System to ensure accurate, reliable,
18            and internationally traceable measurements in the Philippines;
19        (b) support the development of a robust National Quality Infrastructure to
20            promote scientific advancement, industrial competitiveness, and innovation;
21        (c) enhance the country's capability to support trade facilitation, consumer
22            protection, public health and safety, and other national development priorities
23            through reliable measurements;
24        (d) promote harmonization of national metrological standards with international
25            standards and strengthen the Philippines' participation in regional and global
26            metrology systems;
27        (e) develop and sustain national technical capability, institutional capacity, and
28            human resources in metrology and measurement science; and
29        (f) promote a culture of measurement excellence in support of national
30            development and global competitiveness.
31
32        SEC. 5. Powers and Functions. - The PNMI shall:
33
34        (a) establish, maintain, improve, and disseminate national measurement
35            standards and ensure their traceability to international references;
36        (b) provide calibration, measurement, reference, and other technical services
37            necessary to support the National Measurement System;
38        (c) conduct and promote research and development in metrology,
39            measurement science, and related scientific and technical fields;
40        (d) support designated institutions and laboratories in specialized measurement
41            services and technical capability development;
42        (e) participate in international comparisons, mutual recognition arrangements,
43            and regional and international metrology cooperation mechanisms;
44        (f) provide technical support to government agencies, industry, academe, and
45            other stakeholders on matters relating to metrology and measurement
46            systems;
47        (g) undertake education, training, and capacity-building programs, including
48            support for regional metrology development;
49        (h) collect reasonable fees and charges for services rendered, subject to
50            existing laws;
