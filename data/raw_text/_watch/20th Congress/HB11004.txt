                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to grant retirement, health care, and death benefits to
professional Filipino athletes who win championship titles in international
professional sports competitions or other equally prestigious international sporting
events.

       Professional Filipino athletes bring honor and international recognition to the
Philippines through exceptional athletic achievement. However, many world
champions face financial insecurity, limited access to health care, and inadequate
social protection after retirement due to the absence of a dedicated welfare system.
While Republic Act No. 10699, otherwise known as the “National Athletes and Coaches
Benefits and Incentives Act,” provides incentives for amateur athletes, there is no
comparable legal framework recognizing the long-term welfare needs of
professional athletes who likewise achieve distinction for the country.

      This measure provides lifetime retirement pensions, PhilHealth coverage,
hospitalization assistance, death benefits, and a Professional Filipino Athletes
Welfare Development Trust Fund. It likewise authorizes the Games and Amusement
Board to administer the program and ensure its long-term sustainability.

       The proposed measure recognizes the invaluable contributions of
professional Filipino athletes and affirms the State’s commitment to safeguarding
their welfare beyond their competitive careers.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
GRANTING RETIREMENT, HEALTH CARE AND MORTUARY BENEFITS TO
PROFESSIONAL FILIPINO ATHLETES WHO WIN CHAMPIONSHIP TITLES
IN INTERNATIONAL PROFESSIONAL SPORTS COMPETITIONS OR OTHER
EQUALLY PRESTIGIOUS INTERNATIONAL GAMES, AND PROVIDING
FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Professional Filipino
Athletes Retirement, Health Care, and Mortuary Benefits Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
recognize the invaluable contribution of professional Filipino athletes who bring
honor and distinction to the nation through triumph in international sports
competitions. Towards this end, the State shall extend retirement, health care, and
mortuary benefits to safeguard their long-term welfare beyond their competitive
years.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

          a. Board refers to the Games and Amusement Board (GAB);

          b. Filipino Athlete refers to professional athletes who are citizens of the
       Philippines, except those enjoying dual citizenship by virtue of Republic Act
       No. 9225 otherwise known as the “Citizenship Retention and Reacquisition Act of
       2003”;

           c. International Professional Sports Competitions refers to international
       bodies that are reorganized, accredited, and have a mutual working
       relationship, agreement, and/or memorandum of understanding with the
       Board;

         d. International Sporting Organizations refers to international bodies that
      are recognized, accredited, and have a mutual working relationship,
      agreement, and/or memorandum of understanding with the Board;

          e. Other Equally Prestigious World Championship Games refers to other
      international professional sports competitions that confer the same rank or
      stature as world championship titles and are recognized as such by the Board;

          f. Primary Beneficiaries refer to the legitimate spouse, and legitimate or
      illegitimate children of the Filipino athlete;

          g. Professional Athlete refers to any natural person who is paid a sum of
      money or other equivalent compensation as a salary or prize money for
      participating in a game, bout, tournament or contest of professional sport,
      either as an individual contestant or a member of a team, under a contract
      entered into with a professional sports promoter/ operator or team owner
      and licensed by the Board;

         h. Professional Sports refer to individual or team sports, games, contest,
      bouts, tournaments or competitions whereby the participating athlete is paid
      sums of money or other forms of compensation as regular salary or prize for
      participation, which are accredited and/or recognized by the Board; and

         i. Secondary beneficiaries refer to the parents, and in their absence, the
      brother/s or sister/s of professional athletes.

        SEC. 4. Retirement Benefits. – Professional Filipino Athletes, as defined under
this Act, who win in international professional sports or other equally prestigious
competitions shall, upon reaching fifty (50) years old, receive the following
retirement benefits:

          a. Lifetime monthly pension of at least Fifteen thousand pesos (Php
      15,000.00) for the individual winner for individual events; or

          b. Lifetime monthly pension of at least Ten thousand pesos (Php 10,000.00)
      for each team member for team events: Provided, That for athletes who win in
      individual and team events, the retirement benefits for individual events shall
      apply.

       Athletes who are actively employed at the time they reach fifty (50) years old
and have availed of the retirement benefits under this Act may continue with such
employment: Provided, That the benefits under this Act shall be separate from any
retirement benefits provided under existing laws or company policies.

       SEC. 5. Health Care Benefits. – Immediately upon winning in any international
professional sports competitions, Professional Filipino Athletes shall be covered by
the National Health Insurance Program of the Philippine Health Insurance
Corporation (PhilHealth), through a special program sponsored by the Board, which

will provide the required local government premium counterpart to be directly
remitted to the PhilHealth. Upon reaching the age of fifty (50), the athletes shall
continue to be a member of the program, subject to existing rules and regulations of
the PhilHealth.

        In case of hospitalization of the Professional Filipino athlete in any hospital or
clinic, any amount in excess of the PhilHealth benefit shall be shouldered by the
Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) under its Individual Medical
Assistance Program (IMAP): Provided, That the requirement for qualification and
grant of the health care assistance to be extended by the PCSO shall be made
consistent with and subject to compliance with PCSO’s existing rules and guidelines:
Provided, further, That any qualified beneficiary shall have the option to waive the
benefits mentioned under this Section.

       SEC. 6. Mortuary Benefits. – Upon the death of a professional Filipino Athlete,
the primary beneficiaries shall be entitled to a lump sum mortuary benefit of Fifty
thousand pesos (Php 50,000.00) to cover funeral services and expenses: Provided,
That if there are no primary beneficiaries, the secondary beneficiaries shall be
entitled to said benefits.

        SEC. 7. Adjustment of Retirement Benefits. – The Board is hereby authorized to
adjust the retirement benefits provided in Section 4 hereof, based on studies,
consultations, and public hearings with relevant agencies and entities. However, no
memorandum, order, or issuance shall diminish the prescribed retirement benefits
provided herein.

       SEC. 8. Appropriations. – The Chairman of the Board shall immediately
include in the Board’s program the implementation of the benefits under this Act,
the funding of which shall be included in the Annual General Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 9. Professional Filipino Athletes Welfare Development Trust Fund. – There is
hereby established an independent and separate trust fund established under this
Act to be administered by the Board. An amount not less than eighty percent (80%)
for the first five (5) years, accrued from the licensing, registration and accreditation
fees, fines and charges, shall be used for the purpose of the professional Filipino
athletes’ welfare development trust fund. The fund shall be used for the continued
upgrading of training facilities to conform with international standards,
accreditation of, and other forms of assistance and support to the professional
athlete’s sector. The trust fund may also accept grants and donations from national
and foreign entities and individuals interested in the professional athletes’
development.

       SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, through a technical working committee, and in
consultation with the sectors concerned, shall promulgate and issue the rules and
regulations necessary for the full implementation of this Act: Provided, however, That
copies of the formulated rules and regulations shall be submitted to the Committee

      SEC. 11. Reportorial Requirements. – The Board shall submit an annual report
of the implementation of this Act to the relevant committees of both Houses of
Congress.

       SEC. 12. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act shall be held
unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder of the law or other provisions, which are
not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

       SEC. 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 14. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
