                              AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE EFFECTIVE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE OPTIONAL
TEACHING OF RELIGION IN PUBLIC ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY SCHOOLS,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Constitution protects religious freedom and requires a clear separation between
Church and State. At the same time, it expressly allows optional religious instruction
in public schools, if it is voluntary, requested by parents, and does not cost the
government anything. The Supreme Court has explained that this is part of the
Philippine approach of respectful accommodation: the State stays neutral among
religions but allows space for people to practice their faith without unnecessary
barriers.

In practice, however, optional religious instruction is often unclear or unevenly
applied in public schools. Some parents and schools are unsure of the rules, schedules
differ widely, and there are concerns that students may feel pressured to join or
excluded if they do not. On the other hand, some requests for religious instruction are
denied simply because there are no clear procedures. These gaps create confusion
and raise fears that the policy could either violate the separation of Church and State
or unfairly limit religious freedom.

This bill provides clear and simple rules to make optional religious instruction work
as the Constitution intends. It con irms that participation is voluntary and requires
written consent from parents or guardians, ensures that teaching is done by
authorized religious representatives, protects students who choose not to participate,

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Optional Religious
Instruction in Public Schools Act of 2026.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State, consistent with the
Constitution and the principle of separation of Church and State, to ensure the
effective, orderly, and non-discriminatory implementation of optional religious
instruction in public elementary and secondary schools, within regular class hours,
upon the written request of parents or guardians, and without additional cost to the
Government.

SECTION 3. Coverage. — This Act shall apply to all public elementary and secondary
schools under the Department of Education (DepEd), including public schools in all
regions, without prejudice to education-related policies applicable to autonomous
regions consistent with law.

SECTION 4. De inition of Terms. — As used in this Act, the following terms shall
mean:


                                                                           3|Page
   (a) “Optional Religious Instruction” refers to religious instruction conducted
       within the regular class hours in public elementary and secondary schools for
       learners whose parents or legal guardians request such instruction in writing,
       and which is taught by duly authorized representatives of the religion to which
       the learner belongs, without additional cost to the Government.

   (b) “Written Request” refers to a signed request by the parent or legal guardian,
       on a DepEd-prescribed form, indicating (i) the learner’s religion/faith
       community and (ii) consent to the learner’s attendance in optional religious
       instruction.

   (c) “Regular Class Hours” refers to the of icial instructional time scheduled by
       the school for the grade level concerned.

   (d) “Religious Authority” refers to the duly recognized governing body, church,
       mosque, congregation, denomination, or faith community competent to
       designate and authorize its religious instructors for purposes of this Act.

   (e) “Religious Instructor” refers to a person designated by a Religious Authority
       to teach optional religious instruction under this Act, who submits proof of
       authority and complies with DepEd’s reasonable time, place, security, and
       child-protection requirements.

SECTION 5. Voluntariness; Non-Discrimination; No Coercion. —

   (a) Optional religious instruction shall be strictly voluntary on the part of learners
       and their parents or guardians.

   (b) No learner shall be required to attend optional religious instruction.

   (c) Learners who do not attend shall not be discriminated against, penalized, or
       disadvantaged in grades, awards, conduct ratings, privileges, or school
       services.

   (d) No public school personnel shall coerce, induce, or pressure any learner to
       attend or not attend.

SECTION 6. Provision of Time and Space; No Additional Cost to Government. —



                                                                             4|Page
   (a) Schools shall, as practicable, make available a reasonable time slot within
       regular class hours and a suitable space within school premises for optional
       religious instruction, subject to safety, security, and school operations.

   (b) The conduct of optional religious instruction shall be without additional cost
       to the Government. No honoraria, salary, or allowance shall be paid out of
       public funds to religious instructors by reason of such instruction.

SECTION 7. Designation and Quali ications of Religious Instructors. —

   (a) Religious instructors shall be designated by their respective Religious
       Authorities.

   (b) Religious instructors shall submit to the school head:
       (i)    proof of designation/authority from the Religious Authority, and
       (ii)   such information as DepEd may require for child protection and
              campus security consistent with law.

   (c) Public school teachers may serve as religious instructors only when
       (i)    duly designated by their Religious Authority,
       (ii)   it is outside their of icial teaching load, and
       (iii)   it entails no additional cost to the Government and no impairment of
              their public duties.

SECTION 8. Enrollment in Optional Religious Instruction. —

   (a) A learner may attend optional religious instruction only upon submission of
       the Written Request.

   (b) DepEd shall provide a standard form and a simple process to facilitate
       submission, updating, and withdrawal of consent.

   (c) Parents or guardians may withdraw consent at any time in writing, effective
       immediately or on a reasonable date set by DepEd to avoid disruption.

SECTION 9. Scheduling; Class Size; Administrative Coordination. —

   (a) The school head shall ix schedules in a fair and orderly manner, taking into
       account the availability of religious instructors and the needs of the school.


                                                                         5|Page
   (b) DepEd may prescribe reasonable class size guidelines and scheduling
       protocols to ensure child safety, classroom order, and continuity of instruction.

   (c) Scheduling shall be arranged so that learners who do not attend are provided
       appropriate supervised learning activities during the period.

SECTION 10. Relationship with Values Education and Similar Subjects. —

   (a) Values education, education in good manners and right conduct, character
       education, and similar DepEd subjects are secular components of the basic
       education curriculum and shall not be treated as substitutes for optional
       religious instruction.

   (b) Optional religious instruction shall not displace required secular subjects.
       DepEd shall issue guidelines to harmonize scheduling consistent with “within
       regular class hours” and “without additional cost to the Government.”

SECTION 11. Prohibited Acts. — The following acts are prohibited:

   (a) Using optional religious instruction to discriminate against learners of another
       religion or against non-participants;

   (b) Using school authority or resources to promote or favor one religion over
       another, or to penalize learners based on religious belief;

   (c) Requiring any learner to disclose religious af iliation except as necessary to
       implement a Written Request;

   (d) Conducting optional religious instruction in a manner that materially disrupts
       classes, compromises child safety, or violates DepEd child protection policies.

SECTION 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90)
days from effectivity, DepEd shall promulgate the IRR of this Act, after consultation
with stakeholders, including representatives of major faith communities, and
consistent with the constitutional principle of separation of Church and State.

SECTION 13. Administrative Sanctions. — Any public school personnel who
violates this Act or its IRR shall be subject to appropriate administrative proceedings
and penalties under existing civil service, education, and related laws, without
prejudice to criminal or other liabilities when warranted.
