                                AN ACT
           PROVIDING A FUEL SUBSIDY FOR MUNICIPAL FISHERIES,
         APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XIII, Section 7 of the 1987 Constitution explicitly provides that the State
shall protect the rights of subsistence fisherfolk, especially of local communities, to
the preferential use of the communal marine and fishing resources, both inland and
offshore.

        The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) reported that total fisheries output
from July to September 2025 reached only 894,320 metric tons, marking a significant
7.5 percent decline from the 966,730 MT recorded a year earlier. During this period,
production setbacks affected nearly every subsector, including aquaculture, marine
municipal fisheries, and inland municipal fisheries. In stark contrast, only commercial
fisheries posted growth, with an output rise of 4.7 percent to 224,560 MT, accounting
for 25.1 percent of the total production. This disparity highlighted the vulnerability of
small-scale operations compared to commercial fishing. Most notably, marine
municipal fisheries, the very lifeblood of our coastal communities, suffered a sharp 9.7
percent drop, falling to 168,920 MT from the 187,130 MT produced in the same
quarter of 2024. These figures illustrated a deteriorating environment for municipal
fisherfolk, whose diminishing catch was increasingly insufficient to cover the rising
costs of fuel and basic survival. 1

       Local studies in Sorsogon, particularly in municipalities like Magallanes and
Bulan, highlight that municipal fisherfolk are disproportionately affected by high input
costs. In Magallanes, research indicates that the majority of fishing households live
on the edge of food insecurity, largely due to the high cost of fuel and supplies.2 In
Bulan, while sardine production remains high, small-scale fishers have seen their

       Thus, the measure proposes to guarantee a fuel subsidy for municipal fisherfolk
who utilize motorized boats. It proposes to provide a fuel voucher worth at least One
Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PHP 1,500.00) per month for each eligible beneficiary.
In addition, beneficiaries of this program shall also be automatically enrolled and
covered under the National Health Insurance program of the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth). The Social Security System (SSS) shall also
provide microinsurance mechanisms to beneficiaries of the program.

            In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 1          Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Pantawid Pambangka
 2   Act
 3           Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State recognizes the crucial role of
 4   municipal fisherfolk in ensuring food security in the country. It is hereby declared a
 5   policy of the State to provide support to municipal fisherfolk, in recognition of their
 6   sacrifices and contributions to the nation, and in pursuit of the Constitutional mandate
 7   to promote social justice in all phases of development.
 8           Sec. 3. The Pantawid Pambangka Program. - Pursuant to the declared
 9   policy of this Act, the Pantawid Pambangka Program is hereby established, herein
10   referred to as the Program which shall provide a fuel voucher worth of at least One
11   Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PHP 1,500.00) per month for each eligible beneficiary
12   under this Act: Provided, that the amount shall be revised every year to account for
13   inflation. The Program shall be administered by the Department of Agriculture (DA).
14
15          Sec. 4. Program Beneficiaries. - This beneficiaries of this Act shall be
16   municipal fisherfolk who fish within municipal waters, whether coastal or inland, and
17   who use motorized fishing vessels of three (3) gross tons or less: Provided, that
18   registration under the National Program for Municipal Fisherfolk Registration of the
19   Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources shall be a precondition for the receipt of
20   benefits under this Act.
21         Sec. 5. Convergence of Social Services. - Beneficiaries of this program shall
