                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       When an individual dies and leaves behind assets or properties, there arises an
obligation to se4le the corresponding estate tax, among other tax implications. This
process undoubtedly burdens the grieving families both emotionally and ﬁnancially.
Without the se4lement of the estate and the payment of the estate tax, the property of
the deceased become dormant, encumbered by legal impediment that hinder the heirs
from developing and/or transferring them.


       The Estate Tax Amnesty by the government provides an opportunity for the
family members or heirs to se4le the obligations a4ached to the decedent assets. This
grants them the liberty to ﬁnally decide how to utilize the properties left behind by
their loved ones, free from the paralyzing burden of compounded penalties and
surcharges.


       This bill seeks to extend the Estate Tax Amnesty until June 30, 2028. It also
proposes to expand the coverage of the amnesty to include the estates of those
decedents who died on or before May 31, 2024. Through this, we are aﬀording this
privilege to more Filipinos.


       Further, this bill will serve as a strategic economic measure. It will signiﬁcantly
improve overall tax collection despite the waiver of penalties that would have been
otherwise imposed on the estates. Families who were previously deterred from
se4ling their obligations out of fear of exorbitant ﬁnes and penalties will now be
encouraged to ﬁnally se4le the estates of their decedents and pay the estate tax.
       Through the removal of legal impediments a4ached to unse4led estates,
families are empowered to legally act on the assets they stand to inherit, whether to
transfer, sell, or develop their inheritance. This, in turn, will maximize the use of idle
assets and boost productivity as dormant "dead capital" is brought back into the active
market, broadly stimulating the Philippine economy.


       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

            AN ACT FURTHER EXTENDING THE ESTATE TAX AMNESTY
         UNTIL JUNE 30, 2028, THEREBY AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11213,
                                AS AMENDED

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

        Section 1. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to promote a fair,
efficient, and equitable system of taxation by encouraging voluntary compliance with tax laws
and strengthening tax administration through measures that improve taxpayer participation and
compliance.

       Accordingly, the State shall continue to provide qualified heirs and beneficiaries a
reasonable opportunity to settle estate tax liabilities of their decedents through the estate tax
amnesty.

      Section 2. Section 24 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended, is hereby further
amended to read as follows:

        “SEC. 4. Coverage. – There is hereby authorized and granted a tax amnesty,
        hereinafter called Estate Tax Amnesty, which shall cover the estate of
        decedents who died on or before [May 31, 2022] MAY 31, 2024, with or
        without assessments duly issued therefor, whose estate taxes have remained
        unpaid or have accrued as of [May 31, 2022] MAY 31, 2024: Provided,
        however, That the Estate Tax Amnesty hereby authorized and granted shall not
        cover instances enumerated under Section 9 hereof.”

      Section 3. Section 6 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended, is hereby further
amended to read as follows:

         “SEC. 6. Availment of the Estate Tax Amnesty; When and Where to File and
         Pay. - The executor or administrator of the estate, or if there is no executor or
         administrator appointed, the legal heirs, transferees or beneficiaries, who wish
         to avail of the Estate Tax Amnesty shall, within [June 15, 2023] JUNE 15,
         2025 until [June 14, 2025] JUNE 30, 2028, file, either electronically or
         manually, with any authorized agent bank, Revenue District Office through
         Revenue Collection Officer, or authorized tax software provider, a sworn
         Estate Tax Amnesty Return, in such forms as may be prescribed in the
         Implementing Rules and Regulations. The payments of the amnesty tax shall
         be made, either electronically or manually, at the time the Return is filed with
         any authorized agent bank, Revenue District Office through Revenue
         Collection Officer, or authorized tax software provider: Provided, That the
         appropriate Revenue District Officer shall issue and endorse an acceptance
         payment form, in such form as may be prescribed in the Implementing Rules
         and Regulations of this Act for the authorized agent bank, or in the absence
         thereof, the revenue collection agent or authorized tax software provider
         concerned, to accept the tax amnesty payment: Provided, further, That for the
         availment of the estate tax amnesty, the requirements to be submitted to the
         Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) by the filers of the estate tax amnesty shall
         be limited to the following:

         “xxx"

        Section 4. Section 8 of Republic Act No. 11213, as amended, is hereby further amended
to read as follows:

          "SEC. 8. Immunities and Privileges. - Estates covered by the Estate Tax
          Amnesty, which have fully complied with all the conditions set forth in this
          Act, including the payment of the estate amnesty tax shall be immune from
          the payment of all estate taxes, as well as any increments and additions
          thereto, arising from the failure to pay any and all estate taxes for the period
          ending [May 31, 2022] MAY 31, 2024 and prior years, and from all
          appurtenant civil, criminal, and administrative cases and penalties under the
          National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended.”

       Section 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations – The Secretary of Finance shall, in
coordination with the Commissioner of Internal Revenue, promulgate and publish the
necessary rules and regulations within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.

        Section 6. Separability Clause. – If any section or provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions not affected thereby shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

        Section 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended,
or modified accordingly.

       Section 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
