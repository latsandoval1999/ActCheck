EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Caramoran By-pass Road, in the Municipality of
Caramoran, Province of Catanduanes, into a national road, placing its supervision,
improvement, repair, and maintenance under the Department of Public Works and Highways
(DP WH).

The Municipality of Caramoran is an important agricultural and coastal community,
with abaca farming, fishing, and other agricultural activities serving as major sources of
livelihood. The Caramoran By-pass Road is intended to provide an alternative route that will
ease traffic congestion in the town center, improve access to surrounding communities, and
facilitate the movement of residents, agricultural products, goods, and essential services.

Given its importance, the conversion of the road into a national road will facilitate the
allocation of appropriate national funding for its improvement, rehabilitation, and long-term
maintenance, while easing the financial burden on the provincial government. This will help
ensure safer, more accessible, and disaster-resilient road infrastructure, strengthen
connectivity, and further promote agricultural productivity, trade, and socioeconomic
development in Caramoran and the Province of Catanduanes.

This proposal is anchored on DPWH Department Order No. 112, s. 2024, which sets
the standards for road conversion. The road proposed is eligible for conversion as it meets the
standard as of connecting to bayans and businesses and providing an alternative route to divert
traffic from the municipal center, improve access to surrounding communities, and facilitate
the efficient movement of agricultural and fishery products, goods, and essential services in
the province of Caramoran.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this proposed measure is earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL NO.

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

AN ACT CONVERTING THE CARAMORAN BY-PASS ROAD, IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF BATO, CATANDUANES, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. It is hereby declared that the Caramoran By-pass Road, in the
Municipality of Caramoran, Catanduanes, is converted into a national road under the
jurisdiction and supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DP WH).

SECTION 2. The Secretary of the DPWH shall include in its program the expansion,
improvement, rehabilitation, maintenance, and repair of the said road, the funding of which
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
