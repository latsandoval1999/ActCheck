                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to upgrade the salary grades of dentists employed in the public
sector. It ensures that their compensation remains commensurate with their
qualifications, scope of practice, and specialized competencies.

       Oral health is an integral component of a person’s overall health. Dentists
provide preventive, diagnostic, curative, and rehabilitative dental services that
reduce the burden of oral diseases and improve the overall quality of life of
individuals and communities. Public dentists likewise serve as frontline providers of
oral healthcare in public hospitals, government health facilities, and underserved
communities where access to dental services remains limited.

      Despite their responsibilities, the existing salary structure for public dentists
has become less competitive relative to the demands of the profession and
opportunities available in the private sector and overseas. This weakens the
government’s ability to attract and retain qualified dentists and contributes to
workforce shortages in public health facilities.

       This measure establishes a more equitable salary structure for public dentists
across all levels of government service and recognizes their indispensable value in
the health sector.

        Investing in public dentists strengthens the government’s capacity to deliver
equitable health services, expands access to quality dental care, and affirms the
State’s commitment to promoting the health and well-being of every Filipino.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                         AN ACT
UPGRADING THE SALARY GRADE OF DENTISTS EMPLOYED IN THE
PUBLIC SECTOR, PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Public Dentist Salary
Modification Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness
among them. In line with this policy, and in recognition of the critical role of dentists
in promoting oral health among Filipinos, and the need to encourage qualified
health care professionals to join and remain in government service, the State shall
ensure that the compensation received by public dentists remain commensurate to
the demands of the service, and that their social and economic well-being, living and
working conditions, and terms of employment are continuously promoted and
improved.

       SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall cover all dentists employed in all agencies
and Instrumentalities of government, including national government agencies,
government-owned and -controlled corporations (GOCCS), state universities and
colleges, and local government units (LGUs), regardless of their status of
employment.

      SEC. 4. Adjustment of Salary Grade of Public Dentists. – Upon the effectivity of
this Act, the salary grade levels for dentists employed in all government agencies
and instrumentalities shall be modified as follows:

       Position Title             Old Salary Grade              New Salary Grade
         Dentist I                       13                            17
         Dentist II                      16                            20

         Dentist IV                        22                            25

         Dentist VII                       28                            30

       Provided, That public dentists employed on part-time or casual basis under the
Human Resources for Health and other similar programs and projects of the
government shall receive remuneration in accordance with the modified salary
grade schedule and shall be paid proportionate to the actual service they have
rendered: Provided, further, That government agencies and instrumentalities that are
not covered under Republic Act (RA) No. 11466, otherwise known as the “Salary
Standardization Law of 2019”, shall continue to receive their current remuneration so
long as the salaries they receive correspond to or are higher than the salary schedule
provided under this Act: Provided, finally, That the modified salaries of public
dentists employed by LGUs shall correspond to the income classification of the LGU
concerned and shall not exceed the percentage of the applicable salary schedule as
provided in Section 10(a) of RA No. 11466.

       SEC. 5. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to effectively implement the
provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act, in
the respective annual appropriations ordinances of the LGUs, and in the respective
annual corporate operating budgets of the GOCCS.

        SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within thirty (30) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Budget and Management shall, in
coordination with other government agencies concerned, promulgate the rules and
regulations to effectively implement the provisions of this Act.

        SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
