                             AN ACT
     ESTABLISHING A MINDANAO STATE UNIVERSITY CAMPUS IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF MUNAI, PROVINCE OF LANAO DEL NORTE TO BE KNOWN
AS MINDANAO STATE UNIVERSITY - MUNAI CAMPUS, APPROPRIATING FUNDS
                THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be ii enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       SECTION 1. There is hereby established in Munai, Lanao de! Norte, a campus to be
known as the Mindanao State University - Munai Campus.


       SECTION 2. Establishment - The Board of Regents of the Mindanao State University is
hereby authorized to conduct the establishment of a constituent campus of Mindanao State
University Main to be known as the Mindanao State University - Munai Campus, hereinafter
referred to as MSU Munai.


       The Board of Regents of the Mindanao state University shall have full authority to
determine the instruction, research, extension programs, schedule of preparatory and initial
operations, organizational structure, qualifications and number of academic and other personnel,
enrollment and other matters related to the administration and operation of MSU Munai:
Provided, That the academic program shall put priority on Business Administration, Public
Policy and Administration, Sciences (such as Geology, Meteorology and Materials Science), and
Entrepreneurship.


       SECTION 3 . Location. - The campus of MSU Munai shall be located in the
