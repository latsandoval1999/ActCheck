                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The need for sustained pharmaceutical support has become even more pressing as the
country's elderly population continues to expand. According to the Philippine Statistics Authority
(PSA), there were approximately 11.42 million Filipinos aged 60 years and above in 2024,
accounting for 10.2% of the country's household population, up from 9.22 million or 8.5% in 2020.
This growing segment of the population underscores the urgent need for stronger and more
accessible health interventions specifically designed to address the evolving needs of Filipino senior
citizens.

         Chronic non-communicable diseases remain the leading causes of illness and death among
older Filipinos. According to the Department of Health, cardiovascular diseases, diabetes mellitus,
chronic respiratory diseases, and hypertension account for a substantial share of morbidity and
mortality in the country, with older persons disproportionately affected. These conditions require
uninterrupted maintenance medications to prevent complications such as stroke, heart attack, kidney
failure, blindness, and disability. However, many senior citizens, especially those from low-income
households, are unable to consistently purchase their prescribed medicines because of financial
constraints. According to the 2023 National Health Accounts, household out-of-pocket spending
continues to account for 44.3% of the country's current health expenditures, indicating that many
Filipino families still bear the primary burden of healthcare costs.

        This bill seeks to institutionalize a comprehensive government program that provides free
maintenance medications, vitamins, and supplements to all qualified senior citizens through
barangay health centers, Botika ng Bayan, accredited pharmacies, and direct delivery by barangay
health workers for frail, oldest-old, and bedridden beneficiaries. It likewise strengthens existing
health financing mechanisms by expanding the coverage of PhilHealth's Guaranteed and Accessible
Medications for Outpatient Treatment (GAMOT) package to include medicines that may not be
readily available through regular government channels. By integrating medicine provision with
primary healthcare services and local government implementation, the measure promotes better
treatment adherence, disease prevention, and healthier aging among Filipino senior citizens.

       Ensuring that senior citizens receive their prescribed maintenance is a long-term investment
in public health, social protection, and human dignity. By reducing preventable complications,
avoidable hospital admissions, and catastrophic healthcare expenses among elderly Filipinos, the
proposed measure advances the State's constitutional duty to protect the health of its people and
uphold the welfare of one of the nation's most vulnerable sectors.

8    Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Lingap Gamot at Resistensya Pack Para
9    sa Nakatatandang Pinoy Act."
10   Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to
11   health development, which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health, and other social services
12   available to all the people at affordable cost, giving priority to the needs of the underprivileged, sick,
13   elderly, persons with disabilities, women, and children.
14   It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote and protect the health and well-being of
15   senior citizens by providing free medical care and support to ensure their continued and meaningful
16   participation and contribution in society.
17   Sec. 3. Free Maintenance Medications, Vitamins, and Supplements. – The government shall
18   provide free maintenance medications, vitamins, and supplements to all senior citizens, as defined
19   under Republic Act No. 9994, otherwise known as the Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010.
20   Sec. 4. Eligibility and Registration. – To qualify for the free monthly maintenance medications,
21   vitamins, and supplements, a senior citizen must:
22           a) be a registered senior citizen with the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA);
23           b) be enlisted in the target client list of the barangay health center where they reside;
24           c) be assigned to a primary healthcare provider; and
25           d) have a valid prescription issued within the last three (3) months.
26   Sec. 5. Access to Maintenance Medications, Vitamins, and Supplements. – The free maintenance
27   medications, vitamins, and supplements shall be accessed through barangay health centers, Botika
28   ng Bayan, and/or deputized and accredited pharmacies: Provided, That access to and delivery of
29   these items for the oldest-old, frail, and bedridden senior citizens shall be done directly by designated
30   barangay health workers.
31   Sec. 6. PhilHealth GAMOT for Senior Citizens. – Maintenance medications, vitamins, and
32   supplements needed by senior citizens but not readily available shall be covered under, and in
33   addition to, PhilHealth's current Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment
34   (GAMOT) package.

14   (90) days from its effectivity.
15   Sec. 10. Separability Clause. – If any part, section, or provision of this Act is held invalid or

17   Sec. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations, or parts thereof,
18   inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

19   Sec. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
20   Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
