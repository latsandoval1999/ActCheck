      ATHLETES AND COACHES BENEFITS AND INCENTIVES ACT"

                                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Sports has always been more than a game. For many young Filipinos, especially
those in the provinces, sports is discipline, education, confidence, community, and
hope. As an advocate of both education and sports, this representation believes that
every young person who is given a court, a coach, a uniform, and a chance to compete
is also given a stronger chance to dream beyond poverty, distance, and circumstance.

        This assertion is supported by both international and local policy. The World
Health Organization recognizes that regular physical activity provides significant
physical and mental health benefits, while UNESCO emphasizes that physical
education and sport are key to youth development. UNICEF likewise recognizes sport
and play as instruments for education, protection, social inclusion, and empowerment,
especially for vulnerable children.1 In the Philippines, the Department of Education
has institutionalized School Sports Clubs in public schools to promote learners’ holistic
development, physical fitness, well-being, and more accessible sports participation.
The Philippine Sports Commission also recognizes grassroots and “Sports for All”
development as a national mandate for inclusive sports growth nationwide.2

       Filipino athletes and coaches have brought pride and honor to the country. The
Philippines emerged as overall champion of the 2019 Southeast Asian Games. Hidilyn
Diaz won the country’s first Olympic gold medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games,
and Carlos Yulo won two Olympic gold medals at the Paris 2024 Olympics. These

       While Republic Act No. 10699 already grants benefits and incentives to national
athletes and coaches, and while the Bureau of Internal Revenue has recognized the
income tax exemption of prizes, awards, rewards, gifts, and donations received by
athletes such as Carlos Yulo, gaps remain in the tax treatment of private support. In
particular, private gifts and rewards may still create donor-side tax and deductibility
concerns, which can discourage individuals and corporations from giving more
generously and consistently to athletes and coaches.

       This measure therefore seeks to exempt from applicable taxes and fees the
winnings, prizes, awards, bonuses, donations, gifts, endowments, and other forms of
support received by national athletes and coaches in connection with international
sports competitions.

        It also seeks to encourage greater private sector participation by making such
support more meaningful, direct, and unburdened by unnecessary tax friction. Various
state policies strongly encourage sports programs, league competitions, and training
for international competitions to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for a
healthy and alert citizenry. This bill gives life to that mandate. It honors not only the
athlete on the podium, but also the coach, the family, the school, the community, and
the local sports programs that helped make that victory possible.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.
