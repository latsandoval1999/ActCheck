                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

While Republic Act No. 8485 penalizes animal cruelty, the law lacks a localized,
community-level enforcement mechanism to handle everyday violations.
Consequently, animal welfare concerns, neighborhood pet disputes, and
localized abuse cases are left to barangay officials who operate without authority,
technical training, or administrative guidelines. This lack of local enforcement
leads to neighborhood fights and illegal animal poisonings, while stalling city
efforts to control rabies and manage stray populations.

This Bill seeks to address this gap by mandating the establishment of a Barangay
Animal Welfare Desk (BAWD) in every barangay nationwide under the direct
supervision of the Barangay Chairperson. The BAWD will serve as a frontline
venue to mediate local pet-ownership disputes, maintain a community-level
registry to track and report severe neglect or abuse, and act as a public health
coordination hub for targeted rabies vaccinations and humane population
control drives in partnership with municipal veterinarians. This measure
mandates DILG training for appointed officers to ensure standardized
implementation, thereby establishing clear local accountability, protecting public
health, and strengthening neighborhood peace and order.

Hence, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT MANDATING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A DEDICATED
BARANGAY ANIMAL WELFARE DESK (BAWD) IN ALL BARANGAYS
NATIONWIDE TO ENHANCE COMMUNITY-LEVEL MEDIATION,
ENFORCEMENT, AND PUBLIC HEALTH COORDINATION, AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Barangay Animal
Welfare Desk (BAWD) Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the welfare of all animals by creating localized mechanisms for
enforcement, conflict resolution, and public health coordination.

The State recognizes that maintaining peace, order, and public health at the
community level requires proactive, standardized management of animal-related
concerns and the prevention of neighborhood conflicts arising therefrom.

SECTION 3. Mandated Establishment of the Barangay Animal Welfare Desk
(BAWD). — Every barangay nationwide shall establish a dedicated Barangay
Animal Welfare Desk (BAWD), which shall be under the direct supervision and
control of the Barangay Chairperson.

SECTION 4. Functions of the BAWD. — The BAWD shall serve as the frontline
community mechanism for animal welfare and shall perform the following
operational functions:

(a) Receive, log, and investigate community-level complaints regarding animal
cruelty, maltreatment, and severe neglect, and escalate verified violations to
appropriate law enforcement authorities;

(b) Coordinate proactively with the city or municipal veterinarian for the
targeted deployment of localized humane population controls and rabies
vaccination drives;
(c) Act as the local administrative repository and registry for community
caregivers and non-governmental organizations managing community-based
animal welfare initiatives; and

(d) Mediate localized conflicts arising from pet ownership disputes and
neighborhood stray animal disturbances to prevent civil friction, public
disorders, and retaliatory violence or poisoning against animals.

SECTION 5. Mandatory Training and Capacity Building. — The Department of
the Interior and Local Government (DILG), in coordination with the Bureau of
Animal Industry (BAI) and accredited animal welfare non-governmental
organizations (NGOs), shall design and implement a mandatory training
curriculum for appointed Barangay Animal Welfare Officers to ensure
standardized, professional, and humane enforcement nationwide.

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within twelve (12)
months following the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the Department of
the Interior and Local Government (DILG), in close coordination with the
Secretary of the Department of Agriculture and the League of Barangays (Liga ng
mga Barangay), shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for its
effective implementation.

SECTION 7. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining
provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. — Republic Act No. 8485, as amended, is hereby
modified accordingly. All other laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.

Approved.
