                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Section 1, Article XIV of the 1987 Constitution mandates the State to
protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education and to take
appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all. Central to "quality
education" is the holistic development of the learner - a process that is currently
under threat due to a systemic crisis in our mental health support infrastructure.


      The Department of Education (DepEd) acknowledges that guidance and
counseling are indispensable in fostering student resilience and self-discipline.
However, the country is currently grappling with a staggering counselor-to-
student ratio of 1:14,000, based on 2022 figures. This is a far cry from the
internationally recognized standard of 1:250, and even falls short of DepEd’s
modest target of 1:500.
      While the recently enacted Republic Act No. 12080, otherwise known as
the Basic Education Mental Health and Well-Being Promotion Act, is a laudable
step in institutionalizing mental health services, it fails to address the root cause
of the professional exodus: the gap between the qualification and the
compensation.


      Under Republic Act No. 9258, otherwise known as the Guidance and
Counseling Act of 2004, a licensed Guidance Counselor must possess a master’s
degree. Despite this rigorous requirement, which involves years of post-graduate
study and a professional board exam, the entry-level position (Guidance
Counselor I) remains pegged at Salary Grade 11.


      This discrepancy creates a "brain drain" where licensed professionals either
opt for private practice, high-paying BPO wellness roles, or migration. Data from
the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) consistently show high
vacancy rates for these positions, not because of a lack of graduates, but because
the public sector's offer is economically unviable.


      This bill seeks to bridge this gap by: 1) mandating plantilla positions to
ensure every elementary school has a dedicated counselor, moving away from the
"designated teacher" stop-gap measure; and, 2) upgrading the salary grades of
guidance counselors and increasing the minimum entry level to Salary Grade 16
to acknowledge the Master’s Degree requirement and aligns the position with
other specialized technical roles in the government.


      By offering competitive compensation, the State incentivizes Psychology
and Guidance and Counseling graduates to remain in the country and serve in the
public school system. Addressing the "mental health pandemic" among the youth,
manifested in rising rates of bullying and self-harm as reported by the Philippine
Statistics Authority (PSA), requires more than just policy; it requires people. We
cannot protect our students with empty offices.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Creation of Plantilla Positions. – The Department of Education
(DepEd), in consultation with the Department of Budget and Management, shall
ensure that there is a sufficient number of plantilla positions for guidance
counselors in every public elementary and high school.


      For purposes of this Act, the DepEd shall endeavor to meet the
recommended ratios of one guidance counselor for every 200 students in the
public elementary and high schools.
      Section 2. Increase in Salary Grades of Guidance Coordinators,
Guidance Counselors, Guidance Services Associates, and Guidance Services
Specialists. – The present minimum salary grade levels of guidance coordinators,
guidance counselors, guidance services associates, and guidance services
specialists shall be increased in the following manner:

    Current Position Classification

          Guidance Counselor I               11             16
         Guidance Counselor II                12                 17
         Guidance Counselor III               13                 18
         Guidance Coordinator I               14                 19
        Guidance Coordinator II               15                 20
        Guidance Coordinator III              16                 21
     Guidance Services Associate I            12                 17
     Guidance Services Associate II           14                 19
     Guidance Services Specialist I           16                 20
     Guidance Services Specialist II          18                 21
    Guidance Services Specialist III          20                 22
    Guidance Services Specialist IV           22                 23


      Section 3. Appropriations. – The amounts necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the savings of the Department
of Education. Thereafter, the funds shall be provided in the annual General
Appropriations Act.
