ELECTRIC POWER CONSUMPTION”

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Batas Pambansa Bilang 36, entitled “An Act imposing an Energy Tax on Electric Power
Consumption”, was enacted in 1979, at a time when the country was confronted with
economic difficulties, justifying the imposition of an energy tax.

At present, the conditions that BP 36 tried to address no longer hold, given that electricity is
now a necessity and a crucial component of people’s daily lives, which renders such additional
imposition outdated and an unnecessary burden to every Filipino household.

The continued collection of an additional energy tax increases the financial burden on families
already struggling with rising prices and cost of living expenses, particularly on electricity,
which includes generation, transmission, distribution, system losses, universal charges, and
other government-imposed taxes and fees.

This bill seeks to repeal Batas Pambansa Blg. 36 to ensure that taxes remain responsive to the
reality and do not unnecessarily burden the public. Repealing this outdated tax shall help
reduce electricity costs, provide meaningful relief to Filipino families, and allow households
greater flexibility in managing their income for food, education, healthcare, and other

necessities.

Republic of the Philippines
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
Quezon City, Metro Manila

20 CONGRESS

HOUSE BILL NO. 1] 824

Introduced by Representative SITTIE SHAHARA “BAI DIMPLE” I. MASTURA

AN ACT
REPEALING BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 36, ENTITLED “AN ACT IMPOSING AN ENERGY TAX ON
ELECTRIC POWER CONSUMPTION”

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

Section 1. Batas Pambansa (BP) Blg. 36, entitled “An Act Imposing an Energy Tax on Electric
Power Consumption”, is hereby repealed.

SEC. 2. Upon the effectivity of this Act:

(a) The collection of energy tax imposed under BP Blg. 36 shail immediately cease;

(b) Electric distribution utilities, electric cooperatives, generation companies, retail
electricity suppliers, and all other entities responsible for billing electricity consumers
shall discontinue the imposition, collection, and remittance of the energy tax; and

(c) Any implementing rules, regulations, circulars, or issuances issued pursuant to BP Blg.
36 shall likewise cease to have force and effect, insofar as they are inconsistent with
this Act.

SEC. 3. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance, in
coordination with the Bureau of Internal Revenue, the Department of Energy, and the Energy
Regulatory Commission, shall promulgate the rules and regulations and other issuances as
may be necessary to ensure the effective implementation of this Act.

SEC. 4. if any portion or provision in this Act be declared unconstitutional, the remainder of
this Act or any provision not affected thereby shall remain in force and effect.

SEC. 5. All laws, decrees, rules and regulations, and executive orders contrary to or
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 6. This Act shall take effect upon its approval and completion of its publication in at least
two (2) national newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,
