                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The protection of life and property from the dangers of fire is a
fundamental responsibility of the State. Republic Act No. 9514, known
as the "Revised Fire Code of the Philippines of 2008," was enacted to
establish a comprehensive framework for fire prevention, safety, and the
professionalization of the fire service. The law reflects the policy of the
State to ensure public safety and promote economic development by
preventing and suppressing destructive fires, as well as by promoting
accountability in fire protection and prevention services (Section 2, R.A.
No. 9514).
      In recent years, the country has witnessed significant growth in
the number of economic zones, information technology parks, tourism
hubs, and other specialized developments. These areas have become
vital engines of economic activity and employment. However, the
current text of the Fire Code does not expressly mention these zones,
which may lead to uncertainty regarding the full applicability of fire
safety regulations within their boundaries.
      This bill seeks to amend Section 4 of R.A. No. 9514 to make it clear
that the Fire Code applies to all persons and all private and public
buildings, facilities, or structures, regardless of whether they are
located within economic zones, agro-industrial areas, free ports,
information technology parks, tourism economic zones, or similar
entities. By doing so, the bill aims to remove any ambiguity and ensure
that fire safety standards are uniformly enforced throughout the
country, including in areas that are critical to national development.
        Meanwhile, the proposed amendment to Section 12 of R.A. No.
9514 addresses the need for a sustainable and transparent funding
mechanism for the Bureau of Fire Protection. By removing the cap on
the fee based on the estimated value of buildings or structures to be
erected, the bill allows the BFP to collect fees that are more
proportionate to the scale and value of modern developments. This
change is intended to provide the BFP with the resources necessary to
fulfill its expanding mandate, as envisioned by both the Fire Code (R.A.
No. 9514) and the BFP Modernization Act (R.A. No. 11589). The
imposition of specific fees and levies on insurance premiums, sales of
firefighting equipment, and related services is consistent with the
principle that those who benefit from fire protection and risk mitigation
should contribute to the system’s sustainability.
      In summary, this measure responds to the evolving needs of
Philippine society by ensuring that all communities and enterprises are
covered by fire safety laws, and by strengthening the capacity of the
Bureau of Fire Protection to deliver its essential services. The approval
of this bill is respectfully sought.

                             AN ACT
AMENDING SECTIONS 4 AND 12 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9514, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS THE “REVISED FIRE CODE OF THE PHILIPPINES OF 2008”, TO
EXPAND ITS APPLICABILITY AND UPDATE THE SOURCES OF INCOME FOR
THE BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION


SECTION 1. Section 4 of Republic Act No. 9514 is hereby amended to read as
follows:

    “SECTION 4. Applicability of the Code. The provisions of the Fire Code
    shall apply to all persons and all private and public buildings, facilities
    or structures erected or constructed before and after its effectivity
    including those that are classified as economic zone, agro -industrial
    economic, free ports and special economic zone, Information
    Technology (IT) parks and centers, tourism economic zones, and other
    similar entities.


SECTION 2. Section 12 of Republic Act No. 9514 is hereby amended to read as
follows

“SEC. 12. Appropriation and Sources of Income. —

      a. To support the manpower, infrastructure and equipment needs of the fire
service of the BFP, such amount as may be necessary to attain the objectives of the
Fire Code shall be appropriated and included in the annual appropriation of the BFP.

      b. To partially provide for the funding of the fire service, the following taxes
and fees which shall accrue to the general fund of the National Government, are
hereby imposed:

      (1) Fees to be charged. for the issuance of certificates, permits and licenses
as provided for in Section 7(a) hereof;
       (2) One-tenth of one per centum (0.1%) of the verified estimated value of
buildings or structures to be erected, from the owner thereof, one half to be paid
prior to the issuance of the building permit, and the balance, after final inspection
and prior to the issuance of the occupancy permit;

       (3) One-hundredth of one per centum. (0.10%) of the assessed -value of
buildings or structures annually payable upon payment of the real estate tax, except
on structures used as single family dwellings;

      (4) Two per centum (2%) of all premiums, excluding reinsurance premiums for
the sale of fire, earthquake and explosion hazard insurance collected by companies,
persons or agents licensed to sell such insurances in the Philippines;

        (5) Two per centum (2%) of gross sales of companies, persons or agents selling
firefighting equipment, appliances or devices, including hazard detection and warning
systems; and

      (6) Two per centum (2%) of the service fees received from fire, earthquake, and
explosion hazard reinsurance surveys and post loss service of insurance adjustment
companies doing business in the Philippines directly through agents.

SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations.
Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior and
Local Government shall issue the rules and regulations for its effective
implementation.

SECTION 4. Separability Clause.
If any provision of this Act is declared constitutional or invalid, the other provision not
affected thereby shall remain in full fore and effect.

SECTION 5. Repealing Clause.
All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulation, or parts therefor, which are
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SECTION 6. Effectivity Clause.
This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
