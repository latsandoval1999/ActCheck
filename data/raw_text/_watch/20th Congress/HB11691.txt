EXPLANATORY NOTE

As of today, four (4) former officials of the Department of Public Works and Highways
("DPWH") previously publicly identified as involved in the “Flood Control” Scam
identified by the President in his State of the Nation Address on July 28, 2025, have been
granted State Witness and immunity status from suit through discharge as accused
pursuant to Memorandum of Agreement with the National Prosecution Service. The
Ombudsman and the Department of Justice have repeatedly and publicly announced
that the former DPWH officials may only be accepted as State Witness with their
commitment “to return” the ill-gotten wealth from the Flood Control Scam;

As of April 24, 2026, former DPWH Regional Director Gerard Opulencia, has been
reported as having returned Php 130 Million, while pledging to return a total of Php 150
Million. Former DPWH Undersecretary Roberto Bernardo has also been reported to have
vowed to return Php 1 Billion as part of his restitution to be admitted to the Witness
Protection Program. Additionally, Former DPWH District Engineer Henry Alcantara has
been reported as having vowed to return Php 300 Million.

In the consolidated cases of G.R. No. 274778, Aquilino Pimentel III et al. v. House of
Representatives et al. ; G.R. No. 275405, Bayan Muna Chairman Neri Colmenares et al.
v. Executive Secretary Lucas P. Bersamin et al; and G.R. No. 276233, 7Sambayan Coalition
et al. v. House of Representatives et al, the Supreme Court invalidated the said transfer
and ordered the return of the said funds to Philhealth..

As the returned funds from the Flood Control Scam are public funds that should have
been utilized to implement programs, projects and activities that would benefit the
Filipino people and considering the mandate of the Government under the 1987
Constitution, to recognize every Filipino’s right to health, adopt an integrated and
comprehensive approach to health development which shall endeavor to make essential
goods, health and other social services available to all people at affordable costs, and
establish and maintain an effective food and drug regulatory system and undertake

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SEQUESTERED, SURRENDERED OR RETURNED ILL-
GOTTEN WEALTH FOR HEALTH INFRASTRUCTURE

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Declaration of Policy. — Article Il, Section 15 of the 1987 Constitution
recognizes every Filipino’s right to health. Article XIII, Sections 11 and 12 mandates the
Government to perform the following:

a. Adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development
which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health and other social
services available to all people at affordable costs; and

b. Establish and maintain an effective food and drug regulatory system and
undertake appropriate health manpower development and_ research,
responsive to the country's health needs and problems.

SECTION 2. Appropriation of Ill-Gotten Wealth to Improve Healthcare Infrastructure. —
All sequestered, surrendered and/or returned ill-gotten wealth in relation to the flood
control scandal shall be appropriated to programs, projects and activities related to the
improvement of healthcare infrastructure.

SECTION 3. Creation of Health Infrastructure Development Plan. - Within one hundred
eighty (180) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Executive Branch, through the
Department of Health ("DOH") and Department of Budget and Management ("DBM"),
shall submit to Congress a Health Infrastructure Development Plan (“Plan”), which shall
enumerate health programs, projects and activities that would require additional
funding aside from those granted in the General Appropriations Act (“GAA”) and other
special appropriations laws. The Plan shall be evaluated by Congress and shall also be
considered in the formulation of subsequent GAAs.

In the creation of the Plan, the DOH and DBM are required to consult major
stakeholders, including local government units, non-government organizations,
industry specialists, among others.

SECTION 4. Congressional Oversight. - The House Committee on Health and Senate
Committee on Health and Demography shall exercise oversight in the implementation
of this Act.

SECTION 5. Separability Clause. — \f any portion or provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other portions or provisions hereof, which are not
affected thereby, shall continue in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. - All other laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations or

parts thereof inconsistent with the Act or rules and regulations promulgated pursuant
thereto are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.
