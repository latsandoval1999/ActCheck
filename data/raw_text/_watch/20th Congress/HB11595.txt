                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to upgrade the Batangas Provincial Medical Center into a Tertiary
Level III General Hospital under the control, supervision, and management of the
Department of Health. The proposed upgrade will enable the hospital to better
respond to the medical needs of Batangueños and lessen the need for patients to seek
higher-level care in hospitals located farther from their communities.

         A strong public healthcare system depends on hospitals that are capable of
meeting the actual needs of the communities they serve. As demand for medical
services increases, local health institutions must also be strengthened so that patients
can receive appropriate and timely care without having to travel far from their homes.
Improving hospital capacity is therefore an important step toward making quality
healthcare more accessible, especially for families who rely heavily on public medical
facilities.

       For years the Batangas Provincial Medical Center in the Municipality of Tuy has
served as an important healthcare facility for residents of the municipality and nearby
communities in Batangas. The continuing growth in demand for medical care further
emphasizes the need for the institution to expand its comprehensive and specialized
services. Strengthening the functions of this hospital will contribute to a more
accessible and responsive healthcare system in the Province of Batangas and help
ensure that patients receive appropriate care when they need it most.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
