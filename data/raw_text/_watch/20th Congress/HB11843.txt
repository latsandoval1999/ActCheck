EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to amalgamate the Rombion Nationai Institute of Technology (RNIT) in the
Municipality of Alcantara and the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority
(TESDA) Provincial Training Center (PTC) in the Municipality of Odiongan, both in the
province of Romblon, and to convert the amalgamated training school and cengter into
a single polytechnic institution to be known as the Romblon State Polytechnic Institute
(RSPI), under the supervision and administration of the TESDA.

Once converted into the RSPI, the institute will have two campuses — one in the
Municipality of Alcantara and the second one in the Municipality of Odiongon. The main
campus and the seat of administration shall be in the RSPI-Alcantara Campus.

Access to quality technical-vocational education and skilis training is essential to
expanding employment opportunities and improving the lives of Romblomanons.
However, many young people, out-of-school youth, workers, and aspiring entrepreneurs
stili need greater access to practical, industry-relevant training that will prepare them for
employment, entrepreneurship, and the changing demands of the labor market.
Strengthening the province’s technical-vocational education system is therefore an
important step toward developing a skilled workforce and creating more opportunities for
local communities.

The establishment of the RSPI will bring together existing training resources and broaden
the range of programs available to its students and trainees. The Institute shall offer
competency-based technical-vocational programs, certificate and diploma courses, and
specialized training in fields such as industrial technology, agriculture and aquaculture,
tourism and hospitality, information technology, entrepreneurship, construction, and other
emerging industries. Through industry partnerships, skills assessment and certification,
and community-based training, it can help ensure that the competencies acquired by its
graduates remain relevant to actual employment opportunities and local development
needs.

===== [page 2] =====
Equally important, the Institute shall promote inclusive access to skills development,
particularly for economically disadvantaged students, persons with disabilities, and other
underserved sectors. By providing more accessible pathways to training, certification,
employment, and entrepreneurship, the RSPI can help more Romblomanons build
sustainable livelihoods and participate meaningfully in the province’s economic progress.

Integrating the RNIT and the PTC into one institution will also enable the more efficient
use of existing facilities, equipment, personnel, and other government resources. With
coordinated administration and a stronger institutional framework, the RSPI can expand
training opportunities within Romblon and serve as a center for skills development that
may also benefit neighboring communities.

Investing in technical-vocational education is an investment in the people of Romblion. By
equipping them with practical skills, recognized qualifications, and greater access to
economic opportunities, this measure seeks to build a more capable workforce and lay a
stronger foundation for inclusive and sustainable provincial development.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT

AMALGAMATING THE ROMBLON NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ALCANTARA AND THE TECHNICAL EDUCATION
AND SKILLS DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY PROVINCIAL TRAINING CENTER
IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ODIONGAN, BOTH LOCATED IN THE PROVINCE
OF ROMBLON, AND CONVERTING THEM INTO A_ POLYTECHNIC
INSTITUTION TO BE KNOWN AS THE “ROMBLON STATE POLYTECHNIC
INSTITUTE”, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION. 1. This Act shali be known as the “Rombion State
Polytechnic Institute (RSPI) Act".

SEC. 2. Conversion. - The Romblion National Institute of Technology
(RNIT) located in the Municipality of Alcantara, Province of Romblon, and the
Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) Provincial
Training Center (PTC) located in the Municipality of Odiongan, also in Province of
Rombion, are hereby amalgamated and converted into a polytechnic institution
under the supervision and administration of the TESDA. It shall be known as the
“Romblon State Polytechnic Institute (RSPI),” hereinafter referred to as the
Institute.

The main campus and seat of administration of the institute shall be located
in the Municipality of Alcantara, Province of Romblon and shall be known as the
RSPI-Main Campus. The RSPI Campus in the Municipality of Odiongan, Province
of Rombion, shall be known as the RSPI-Odiongan Campus.

SEC. 3. General Mandate. - The Institute shall restructure its Technical-
Vocational Education and Training (TVET) programs to become the provider of
leading-edge technical-vocational skills for the focal and international labor
market, and ensure alignment with the standards of the industry.

To this end, the Institute shall upgrade its curricular standards and provide

higher level competency qualifications for instruction in TESDA-registered TVET
programs and certificate courses. It shail provide a wide array of relevant skills

training and trade specialization courses to facilitate the acquisition by the citizenry
of the necessary technical-vocational skills that shall enhance their access to gainful
employment, practical livelihood, and entrepreneurial opportunities, and thus
hasten the development of the host areas and the adjacent localities in the
Province of Romblon into human resource and productivity hubs.

Priority admission to the Institute, including avaiiment of scholarships,
grants-in-aid and other forms of student financial assistance, shall be given to
the most economically disadvantaged students, out-of-school youths, persons
with disabilities, and indigenous peoples of the Municipalities of Alcantara and
Odiongan, as well as the adjacent localities, to ensure their development into
productive, self-reliant and globally competitive skilled labor assets.

The Institute shall serve as a TESDA-accredited assessment center,
subject to the accreditation requirements under the Philippine TVET
Competency Assessment and Certification System for acquired competencies
in TVET programs in the Province of Rombion.

SEC. 4. Curricular Offerings and Training Programs. - The RSPI shall
offer TESDA-registered TVET programs, postsecondary short-term and
undergraduate certificate/diploma courses, and modular trainings in preferred
technical-vocational skills specialization.

To this end, the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Department
of Trade and Industry (DTI), Department of Science and Technology (DOST),
Department of Agriculture (DA), Department of the Interior and Local Government
(DILG), Department of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev),
business-industry partners, and other relevant agencies and instrumentalities,
both local and international, shall provide the necessary assistance so the
Institute can produce skilled manpower to supply the needs of a robust and
inclusive economy.

The Institute may establish research and technology hubs, technology
development farms, satellite or extension campuses, promote mobile training
programs, and strengthen linkages with industry partners and the academe.

It shail also adopt the use of innovative training modes such as
information technology-assisted instruction, dual training system, open distance
learning pursuant to Republic Act No. 10650 or the "Open Distance Learning
Act", community laboratory, virtual and blended learning modes, and such other
instructional programs that shall allow local residents greater access to the TVET
program offerings of the Institute.

Course completion at the Institute shall be credited through skills
competency assessment under National Certification Levels Ii! to V of the
Philippine Qualifications Framework (PQF) pursuant to Republic Act No. 10968
or the "PQF Act’. The TVET competency of the student may also qualify for
academic degree recognition pursuant to Republic Act No. 10647 or the
"Ladderized Education Act of 2014": Provided, That the requirements of the
Commission on Higher Education for credit transfer are complied with.

The institute shall offer skills development for higher competency
qualifications in areas relating to technical industrial education, trade
technology, tourism, agricultural technology, business literacy, and innovation in
TESDA-registered skills, as follows:

(a) Skills training in industrial technology and hard trades such as metal
and steelwork, machine fabrication and operation, heavy equipment
operation, automotive mechanical assembly, refrigeration and air
conditioning, electrical, photovoltaic systems installation
and services, electronics, and operation of power tools and equipment
for both medium skills grade and industrial purposes;

(b) Tourism and hospitality-related courses relating to hotel, restaurant,
and tourism development management;

(c) Agriculture and aquaculture-related trainings and skills development in
such areas as farming technology, fishing operations technology, backyard
farming and home-based aquaculture and propagation innovations,
mechanized farming, fishing tools and equipment operation, agribusiness,
agri-aqua trade technology and innovations, and relevant training seminars
for farmers and fisherfolk to encourage entrepreneurship and thus increase
their productivity;

(d) Livelihood skills development courses for preferred skills employment and
small-scale entrepreneurship, including practical skills education in arts and
crafts, workmanship and design, high-speed sewing, dressmaking and
tailoring, horticulture, livestock raising and breeding, food processing
technology, home technology, culinary arts and commercial cooking,
cosmetology, and health and wellness training;

(e) Basic business literacy training in financial management, marketing,
accountancy, bookkeeping, office procedures, business processes and
application procedures including registration, licensing, documentation,
business patent and intellectual property regulation, business financing
and investment opportunities sourcing, import-export accreditation, online
home-based business operation, and social media business marketing;

(f) Technical-vocational occupation and trades skills such as carpentry,
masonry, plumbing, welding, practical electricity and installation, automotive
servicing, electronics servicing, personal computer servicing, and such
other relevant practical skills courses;

(g) Computer literacy and information technology-related skills, web
development, animation, game development, and computer-aided design
application;

(h) Social communication skills and language proficiency courses in

English, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, and other languages for
business process outsourcing employment and overseas job placement;

(j) Other preferred skills and trades training that may be needed by the

SEC. 5. Compliance with TESDA Requirements. - The provisions of
this Act notwithstanding, the conversion of the RNIT into the Institute shall become
operational only upon the issuance by the TESDA of a formal recommendation and
certificate of compliance attesting that the Institute has satisfactorily complied with
the minimum standards of quality it has prescribed governing the following: a)
standard procedures and guidelines for the conversion and operation of a
TESDA polytechnic institute; b) operational sustainability of the Institute in terms
of licensed faculty-trainers and personnel, equipment, training and laboratory
facilities, instructional materiais, and other standard requirements of a TESDA-
accredited and supervised school; c) sustainable funding source and regular
allocation of budgetary requirement for TESDA-operated schools; d) full
alignment of the training programs offered with the minimum standards of
competency-based quality technical-vocational technology skills and the needs
of the host area and adjacent localities served within the context of the ASEAN
integration and the PQF for TVET; and e) compliance with such other TESDA
conditionalities and standards, as may be necessary and applicable, on the
conversion of the RNIT into the Institute.

SEC. 6. Administration. - The Institute shall be headed by a Vocational
School Superintendent (VSS) under the supervision of the TESDA, who shall
render full-time service and be responsible for its administration and operation.
The VSS shall be appointed by the TESDA Director General in accordance
with the rules and regulations of the Civil Service Commission (CSC) and the
qualification requirements for such office.

The VSS shall enter into agreement with focally-based private and public
counterpart agencies or instrumentalities and persons, and seek such assistance
as may be necessary to effectively implement this Act, subject to the approval of
the TESDA Director General.

The academic and administrative staff of the Institute shall be organized
following the staffing pattern of the TESDA for such type of schools, pursuant to
existing laws, rules and regulations of the CSC.

The main campus of the Institute shall be under the direct administration and
supervision of the Vocational School Superintendent (VSS), who shall be stationed
therein and shall exercise overall supervision, management, and contro! over the
operations of the Institute and all its campuses.

Each annex campus of the Institute, including those that may be established in
the future, shall be headed by a Vocational School Administrator (VSA). The VSA shall
be appointed by the TESDA Director General pursuant to existing jaws, rules, and
regulations of the Civil Service Commission (CSC).

SEC. 7. Assets, Liabilities and Personnel. — All assets, fixed and
movable, personnel, records and documents, as well as the liabilities or obligations
of the RNIT and the TESDA PTC shall be transferred to the Institute: Provided,
That the positions, rights and security of tenure of faculty and personnel therein
employed prior to its conversion into the Institute, shall not be impaired.

All parcels of land belonging to the government occupied by the RNIT and
the TESDA PTC are henceforth declared the property of the Institute and shall be
titled under that name: Provided, That should the RSP! cease to exist or such
aforementioned parcels of land be no longer needed by the Institute, the same
shall revert to the concerned Local Government Unit (LGU), or to the Republic of
the Philippines, as the case may be.

SEC. 8. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial operation
of the Institute shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of the
RNIT. Thereafter, the funding for the continued operation of the Institute shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 9. implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days
after the approval of this Act, the TESDA shall, in coordination with the DOLE,
DTI, DOST, DA, DBM, DILG, DEPDev, the concerned LGUs, and such other
relevant agencies and industry-business partners of the host locality, prepare and
issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this
Act.

SEC.10. Separability Clause. - If, for any reason, any part or provision of
this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions
not affected are in full force and effect.

SEC.11. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, and
rules and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 12. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
