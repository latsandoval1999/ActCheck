                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to expand the health benefits and wellness programs available
to senior citizens by further amending Republic Act No. 9994, otherwise known as
the “Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010”. It also seeks to promote digital literacy
and inclusion among senior citizens, and identify sustainable funding mechanisms
to support these enhanced services.

       The Philippines is experiencing a steady increase in its elderly population.
The Philippine Statistics Authority reported that the country’s population reached
112.7 million in 2024, while demographic data continue to reflect a growing number
and proportion of senior citizens nationwide.¹ As life expectancy increases, so does
the demand for accessible healthcare, preventive medical services, mental health
support, and programs that promote active and healthy aging.

       Many senior citizens continue to face barriers in accessing healthcare and
social services due to limited mobility, inadequate health resources, and financial
constraints. At the same time, the rapid digitalization of government services and
everyday transactions has also created challenges for many older Filipinos who lack
the necessary skills and confidence to navigate online systems, making them more
vulnerable to exclusion, misinformation, and cyber-related risks.²

        This measure mandates a more comprehensive and integrated health
program for senior citizens and establishes nationwide digital literacy and inclusion
initiatives. It seeks to ensure that older Filipinos remain healthy, informed,
connected, and empowered members of society.

      Those who have spent their lives contributing to their families, communities,
and the country’s development deserve not only protection but also meaningful
opportunities to enjoy healthy, productive, and fulfilling lives in their later years.
Strengthening health and wellness programs for senior citizens is both a moral

       SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DOH, DSWD, DICT, and NCSC,
in consultation with LGUs, relevant nongovernmental organizations, and other key
stakeholders, shall jointly promulgate the necessary implementing rules and
regulations within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.

       SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
