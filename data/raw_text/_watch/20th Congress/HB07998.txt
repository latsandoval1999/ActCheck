                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to convert the A. Velez St. – J.R. Borja – Bukidnon Cagayan de Oro
Iligan Road (BCIR) in Cagayan de Oro City from a city road to a national road.

      The road is presently in dire need of expansion, repair and rehabilitation to
accommodate the ever increasing volume of vehicles using said road. With its improvement
and expansion, easier access to both points would be ensured and the potentials for
commercial and industrial development would be realized.

       With its conversion into a national road, it would receive the much needed funding and
assistance it deserves.

       Passage of this measure is earnestly requested.

                                 AN ACT
CONVERTING THE A. VELEZ ST. – J.R. BORJA – BCIR ROAD IN CAGAYAN DE ORO
CITY INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress be
assembled:

       Section 1. The A. Velez St. – J.R. Borja – BCIR Road is hereby converted into a
National Road.

      Sec 2. The Secretary of Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the
necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

       Sec. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper
of general circulation.
