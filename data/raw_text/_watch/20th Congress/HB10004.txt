                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that the family has the duty to care for its elderly
members but the State may also do so through just programs of social security. At present,
Republic Act No. 7432, as amended, particularly by R.A No. 9994 otherwise known as the
Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010, grants senior citizens discounts and privileges on select
good and services, but does not expressly include legal assistance.

       Senior citizens, while no longer at the prime of their lives, continue to face legal issues
involving their persons, properties, and rights. They may be parties to civil, criminal,
administrative, and quasi-judicial proceedings, and may require counsel to prevent or address
harassment suits, property disputes, and abuses.

        This proposed measure seeks to institutionalize free legal assistance for all senior citizens,
delivered through government legal assistance offices and coordinated legal aid
mechanisms, consistent with the Supreme Court’s Rules on Unified Legal Aid Service (ULAS)
and the Supreme Court’s exclusive rule-making power over pleading, practice and procedure, and
legal assistance. It likewise recognizes existing incentives for pro bono legal aid, including MCLE
crediting and other incentives under ULAS, and tax incentives under Republic Act No.
9999, where applicable and subject to existing laws and Supreme Court rules.

       In view of the foregoing , the passage of this proposed bill is earnestly sought.
