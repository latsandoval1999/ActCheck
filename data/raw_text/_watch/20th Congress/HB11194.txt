                                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The role of labor as a primary social force is affirmed by the State in no
less than the 1987 Constitution. In the same article, the State commits itself
to the protection of workers and protection of their welfare.1 Article XIII,
Section 3 also imposes on the State the duty to afford full protection to labor,
local and overseas, organized and unorganized, and promote full employment
and equality of employment opportunities for all.

      This bill seeks to link skilled workers with employers by establishing an
online national registry of skilled workers. The online registry shall be made
available to prospective employers and shall contain all relevant details to
allow the employer to contact the skilled workers but shall be subjected to
safeguard measures to protect the privacy of the skilled workers as well.

       Under Article XIII, Section 2 of the 1987 Constitution, the State’s policy
in the promotion of social justice shall include the commitment to create
economic opportunities based on freedom of initiative and self-reliance. This
measure is envisioned to promote the employment of skilled workers in
furtherance of the State’s efforts to reduce property and improve the lives of
Filipinos.

      It is for the foregoing premises that the approval of this Bill is
earnestly sought.

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National
Skilled Workers Registry Act.”

      SECTION 2. Definition of Terms. – The following terms shall be used in
this Act:
         a) “Skilled workers” – refer to persons who regularly perform jobs
            that cater to household needs which usually involve the use of
            physical strength or skills such as plumbers, carpenters, and
            electricians. The skilled workers referred hereto shall in no case
            be limited to holders of national certificates issued by the
            Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA)
         b) “Skilled Workers Registry” – refers to the database of all skilled
            workers who have registered information on their skills and
            qualifications to make their services readily available.

      SECTION 3. Establishment of the National Skilled Workers Registry. – A
National Registry of Skilled Workers shall be created, which shall serve as a
database for all skilled workers who voluntarily register and are willing to
make their services available.

       SECTION 4. Contents of the Registry. – All skilled workers who want
their names included in the Skilled Workers Registry shall provide the
following details or information, to be disclosed and made readily available to
the public:

         a) Name of the skilled worker;
         b) Address;
         c) Contact information;
         d) Skills and nature of jobs or services offered;
         e) TESDA National Certificate number and expiration date, if any,
            or any other seminar or training attended relevant to the services
            offered; and
         f) Photocopies of at least two (2) government-issued identity
            documents (IDs).

      SECTION 5. Online and In-Person Application. – Inclusion in the online
Registry shall be on a voluntary basis. Skilled workers who want their names
to appear on the online Registry may apply online or in-person at application
desks to be designated by the TESDA.

      SECTION 6. Access to the Registry. – The Registry shall be made
available to the public through a website, which shall include all the relevant
information of the skilled worker such as photo, name, contact details, skills
and nature of jobs or services offered, and the TESDA National Certificate
number and expiration date, if any. The database shall be made accessible to
interested persons upon entering of the following information on the website:

         a) Name;
         b) Address;
         c) Contact information;
         d) Nature of services sought;
         e) Project location;

       SECTION 7. Prohibition on Collection of Registration Fees. – The
inclusion of a skilled worker’s name and corresponding details in the Registry
shall be free from any registration fees or any similar fees.

      SECTION 8. Public Information and Education. – There shall be
information and education campaigns to raise public awareness on the
existence of the Registry and shall provide instructions on how to apply for
inclusion and to access said Registry.

      SECTION 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

       SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders,
presidential decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders,
rules, and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 11. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
