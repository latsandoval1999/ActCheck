20                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22
23   The birth of a child is recorded in a certificate, but the beginning of that child's life need
24   not be recorded only on paper. It can also take root in the soil. The proposed Family
25   Tree Planting Act seeks to make every birth an opportunity for parents to plant two
26   trees, creating a living legacy that grows alongside the child and contributes to the
27   environment that he or she will one day inherit.
28
29   A birth certificate records a child's arrival into the world. This measure seeks to make
30   that arrival meaningful in another way by allowing every Filipino child to begin life with
31   two trees growing in his or her name. As the child grows, those trees can grow with
32   them, providing shade, supporting biodiversity, protecting the soil, and contributing to a
33   healthier environment for generations to come.
34
35   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

20                               AN ACT
21   REQUIRING PARENTS TO PLANT TWO (2) TREES FOR EVERY CHILD BORN TO
22               THEM AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
23
24   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
25   Congress assembled:
26
27           SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Family Tree Planting
28   Act.”
29
30          SECTION. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the declared policy of the State to
31   protect and advance the right of the Filipino people to a balanced and healthful ecology
32   in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature. Towards this end, the State shall
33   promote and pursue reforestation programs and encourage tree planting as part of the
34   family’s responsibility and legacy to protect the environment and conserve our natural
35   resources for the future generation.
36
37           SECTION 3. Mandatory Tree Planting. – All parents residing in the Philippines
38   whether legally married or not, shall be required to plant two (2) trees for every child to
39   them. These trees shall be planted within the premises of their own home. If not
40   possible, trees shall be planted in an area to be designated by the Barangay Council
41   where they reside, with the approval of the Department of Environment and Natural
42   Resources (DENR). The planted species should be appropriate to the location, climate
43   and topography of the area, with preference for the planting of indigenous species and
44   fruit-bearing trees.
45
46          SECTION 4. Provision of Seedlings. – The Community Environment and
47   Natural Resources Office (CENRO) of the DENR and the Department of Agriculture
48   (DA), in partnership with local government units (LGUs) throughout the Philippines, shall
49   maintain a seedling bank that shall provide seedlings to parents planting the trees in
50   accordance with this Act.
51          SECTION 5. Tree Planting as a Requirement for Birth Certificate Release. –
52   To ensure compliance with this Act, the Local Civil Registry where the birth of the child
53   shall be registered, shall release the Certificate of Live Birth of the child only upon the
 1   presentation of the parents’ sworn statement of compliance. : Provided, however, That
 2   a certificate of compliance from the DENR or the LGU concerned shall suffice the
 3   requirement for the release of the Certificate of Live Birth of the child. Planting of the
 4   trees must be done within thirty (30) days after the child’s birth. Longer compliance
 5   period maybe provided for parents under extraordinary circumstances such as solo
 6   parent or physically incapacitated to perform such activity.
 7
 8          SECTION 6. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
 9   implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of
10   the departments and agencies concerned. Thereafter, the funding of which shall be
11   included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The LGU concerned may set aside
12   funds from any local revenue an amount deemed appropriate for the implementation of
13   this Act.
14
15          SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days
16   from the effectivity of this Act, the DENR shall, in consultation with the Department of
17   the Interior and Local Government, DA, the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) and the
18   Department of Social Welfare and Development, promulgate the implementing rules
19   and regulations to implement this Act.
20
21         SECTION 8. Separability Clause. – Should any portion or provision of this Act
22   be declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions hereof shall continue to be
23   enforced.
24
25          SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, letters of
26   implementation, rules and regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with any provision of
27   this Act are hereby repealed, modified, superseded or amended accordingly.
28
29          SECTION 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
30   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
