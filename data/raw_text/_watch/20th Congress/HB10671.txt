                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Based on the Civil Service Commission’s (CSC) Inventory of Government Human
Resources (IGHR), non-regular personnel constitute a significant portion of the
Philippine public sector workforce. As of the 2025 data, there are approximately
919,868 Job Order (JO) and Contract of Service (COS) workers nationwide,
representing about 30.5% of the total government workforce. Among these, JO
workers account for 629,862 personnel, while COS workers comprise 290,006
personnel. Likewise, Casual employees, who hold temporary civil service appointments
under the Non-Career Service category, comprise another segment of non-regular
government workers. They number 119,476, representing approximately 53.0% of
the Non-Career Service workforce. Combined, JO, COS, and Casual personnel account
for approximately 35% of the government workforce, highlighting their significant
contribution to the delivery of public services. However, despite these, workers
continue to face persistent challenges arising from their employment classification and
limited access to benefits.1

        Under existing Civil Service Commission (CSC) policies, JO and COS workers
lack security of tenure due to the absence of an employer-employee relationship under
civil service laws. Their continued employment depends on contract renewal, project
requirements, available funding, and changes in administration, while casual
employees remain vulnerable due to temporary appointments and budget limitations.

       These workers also experience significant disparities in compensation and
benefits compared to regular government employees, as JO, Casual, and COS

       Considering these circumstances, this bill seeks to provide financial security,
health protection, and employment benefits to qualified JO, Casual, and COS
employees in the government who have rendered continuous and essential services.
It aims to address the lack of benefits experienced by many non-regular government
workers who perform critical functions but remain excluded from privileges such as
health insurance, government contributions, bonuses, and leave entitlements.
Furthermore, this bill provides qualified workers with protection against unlawful
termination intended to evade the payment of these benefits and requires contractors
and service providers to comply with the benefits mandated under this Act.

       By granting these benefits and providing protection against unjust termination,
this bill upholds the welfare, dignity, and rights of government workers while
recognizing their vital contributions to public service delivery. It also prevents the
improper use of contractual arrangements by ensuring that government agencies and
service contractors provide appropriate benefits to qualified workers.

      In light of the foregoing, support for this bill is earnestly sought.

 8           SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. For the purposes of this Act, the following
 9   definitions shall apply:
10
11         a. Casual Appointment - shall refer to an appointment issued to a person to
12         perform essential and necessary services when there are not enough regular
13         staff to meet the demands of the services and for emergency cases and
14         intermittent period not exceeding one (1) year;
15
16         b. Contractual Appointment - shall refer to an appointment issued to a person
17         whose employment in the government is governed by a special contract to
18         undertake a specific work or job requiring special or technical skills not available
19         in the employing agency, to be accomplished within a specified period. Each
20         contractual appointment shall not exceed one (1) year, but may be renewed
21         annually, subject to performance, availability of funds, and completion of the
22         specific work or job.
23
24         c. Contract of Service (COS) - shall refer to the engagement of the services of
25         an individual, private firm, other government agency, non-government
26         organization, or international organization as a consultant, learning service
