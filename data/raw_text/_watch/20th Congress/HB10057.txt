                        AN ACT
CREATING AN ADVANCED STUDIES DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM
FOR EXCEPTIONAL EMPLOYEES FROM THE GOVERNMENT AND
THE PRIVATE SECTOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Human capital is the foundation of national progress. In an increasingly
knowledge-driven and innovation-centered global economy, the Philippines must
invest deliberately and strategically in developing a critical mass of highly skilled
professionals who can lead in science, technology, research, and other disciplines
essential to economic transformation. While the country produces talented
graduates and professionals, there remains a pressing need to systematically
nurture exceptional individuals who can acquire advanced expertise from leading
international institutions and translate such knowledge into tangible national
gains. The proposed Pensionado Act institutionalizes a forward-looking and
merit-based fellowship program designed to cultivate first-rate specialists who
will serve as catalysts of innovation, productivity, and policy excellence.

       This measure establishes the Pensionado Program under the Commission
on Higher Education (CHED), in coordination with key development agencies,
to provide fellowships for exceptionally capable and highly motivated Filipinos
from government, the private sector, and the self-employed sector. The Program
shall support advanced studies and research in priority disciplines identified as
vital to national development. It creates a National Secretariat to oversee
administration, coordination of foreign and local grants, and reintegration of
scholars, as well as a Selection Panel composed of distinguished academic leaders
to ensure integrity and merit in the selection process. The fellowship shall provide
comprehensive support, including paid study leave, travel, stipends, research
allowances, and insurance, through a cost-sharing mechanism between the
National Government and the scholar’s employer, with opportunities for
international cost-sharing arrangements.

       Crucially, the bill ensures that public investment yields measurable
national returns. Pensionados shall be required to render return service equivalent
to twice the period of their fellowship and to share their expertise through
seminars, training programs, and research engagement. A National Network of
Specialized Institutes for Advanced Studies shall be developed to provide
returning scholars with platforms for sustained collaboration, innovation, and
policy contribution. By consolidating and strategically managing scholarship
grants, the State positions itself to harness global knowledge for domestic
advancement. Ultimately, the Pensionado Act strengthens the country’s long-
term competitiveness by building a community of elite Filipino professionals
committed to national service and transformative leadership.

      In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:
 SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Pensionado Act”


 SEC. 2. Declaration of Policies. — Human resources developments are
 essential for economic development and progress. In the pursuit of economic
 development and progress, the State shall give priority to the creation of a
 critical mass of elite professionals who shall take the lead in harnessing science,
 technology, and related disciplines towards accelerating the productivity of the
 country's human resources. Consonant with this thrust, it is hereby declared a
 policy of the State to devise a program for exceptionally able and highly
motivated employees, preferably aged thirty (30) to forty (40) years, from the
government, the private sector and/or the self-employed to undertake advanced
studies in a branch of science, technology, or related fields of learning on which
depends the development and progress of the country. As such, it shall provide
for fellowships to enable them to pursue advanced studies or research in their
field of expertise. The State shall develop a critical mass of first-rate specialists
in various disciplines and areas of specializations vital to national development
to form a collective national "think tank" who shall place continuously their
knowledge and skills at the service of the nation, be it in industry, the academe,
or the highest policy-making levels of government, with the government taking
the lead in harnessing their knowledge and skills for the greatest good of the
country.


SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act, the following terms
are hereunder defined as follows:
     (a) Post-graduate refers to a formal degree course program of training
           research requiring at least a Master's Degree for entry; a student
           pursuing such program;
     (b) Advanced studies refer to a post-graduate problem-oriented program of
     studies and/or research, not necessarily leading to a degree, undertaken for
     further specialization or advanced knowledge in a specific field of study,
     or to acquire specialized knowledge and/or skills in a particular area of
     science or technology; and
     (c) Pensionado refers to a Filipino citizen selected and supported by the
     Philippine government under this program to undertake advanced studies
     in selected institutions of higher learning or recognized research centers,
     18 in fields relevant to the officially-declared priority areas for
     development.

SEC. 4. Establishment of Pensionado Program. — There shall be established
under the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), in coordination the
Department of Science and Technology (DOST), the Department of Trade and
Industry (DTI), the National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA), the
private sector and non-government organizations (NGOs) a fellowship program
to be called as the Pensionado Program for Filipinos employed in the government
and the private sector, and those who are self-employed.


The Program shall pool together all fellowships, scholarships, training grants
sponsored or extended by foreign government, agencies and other bodies, as well
as by local agencies and organizations, whether public or private, intended for
advanced studies in international centers of advanced learning, scholarship and
research.


SEC. 5. Establishment of a National Secretariat; Functions and Composition.
— There shall be established a National Secretariat with the following functions:
(a) Identify and assist in negotiations with sponsors of fellowships, scholarships
and grants;
(b) Provide assistance to the selection panel in accomplishing its work;
(c) Provide the Pensionados continuing assistance from the time of selection thru
the time of their return to the country, including assistance for the productive
utilization of their newly-gained expertise;
(d) Serve as the administrative arm of the government for the program; and
(e) In general, promulgate and implement the policies and guidelines governing
the Program.
The National Secretariat shall be headed by an Executive Officer with the rank
of Assistant Secretary, to be appointed by the President of the Republic of the
Philippines, upon the recommendation of CHED. The members of the Secretariat
                                                                                 3
shall consist of representatives from CHED, NEDA, DTI, and DOST. These
representatives shall be seconded to the Secretariat for a period of one (1) year,
subject to renewal every year. Service rendered thereat shall be considered as part
of their performance for promotions purposes.


SEC. 6. Number of Pensionados and Length of Grant. — There shall be
selected twenty-four (24) Fellows annually — three (3) Fellows for each priority
discipline / industry field of the eight (8) vital disciplines or areas of
specializations identified by the CHED in consultation with the NEDA as
frontiers or new areas essential for pushing the economic and social development
thrusts of the country. The scholarship shall be for a period not exceeding three
(3) years.


SEC. 7. Selection Process. — There shall be a Selection Panel under the
Secretariat consisting of three (3) university presidents and two (2) eminent
members from the academe to be appointed by the CHED. They shall serve on a
staggered basis for the purposes of continuity and consistency, each for a term of
three (3) years. The Selection Panel shall make the final selection of the
Pensionados from among the nominees from the academe, industry and research
centers. The integrity and objectives of the program are paramount considerations
for the panel in making the selection.


SEC. 8. Qualifications. — In order to qualify for the Fellowship Program, the
applicant must be:
      (a) a Filipino citizen;
      (b) gainfully employed;
      (c) a college degree graduate, with honors;


                                                                                  4
      (d) with demonstrated leadership and proven exceptional performance in
      his/her present work;
      (e) enthusiastic in undergoing further training; and
      (f) willing to sign a contract requiring him/her to return to his/her job in 8
      another capacity after training.


SEC. 9. Place of Study. — The Pensionados shall be sent to a foreign country
well-known for its advanced stage in particular discipline of area of
specialization. Training and education may be an academic institution, research
and development (R&D) center or a major industrial establishment with R&D
program. It may or may not lead to an advanced academic degree.


SEC. 10. Assistance to Grantees. — The Pensionado is entitled to the following
benefits:
(a) Up to three (3) years leave with pay, including allowances and other benefits;
(b) One economy round-trip air fare to and from the host country plus other travel
expenses required for foreign travel;
(c) Clothing allowance to be determined by the Secretariat;
(d) Monthly stipend while on study abroad, of such amount to be determined by
the Secretariat;
(e) Books / instructional materials / research allowance of such amount to be
determined by the Secretariat; and
(f) Health, travel and insurance fees of such amount to be determined by the
Secretariat.


SEC. 11. Program Funding. — The cost of each fellowship grant shall be shared
by the Pensionado's employer and the National Government. The employer pays

the salary, allowances and other benefits due the Fellow as employed for the
whole period of the advanced studies overseas while the National Government,
thru the Program, shall be responsible for items b, c, d, e, and f as enumerated in
Section 10 of this Act. With the transfer of all pertinent foreign and local grants
for coordination under the Program, the government through the Program, may
negotiate bilateral arrangements with host governments, host universities, host
research institutions and host corporations to share at least fifty percent (50%) of
the total cost of items b, c, d, e, and f under Section 10 hereof. The National
Government may also negotiate bilateral agreements with host governments, host
universities, research institutions and corporations to enable our scholars to stay
abroad and continue their studies or training at a minimal cost to the National
Government. A sharing program can be established where the host government,
host-universities, research institutions and corporations can shoulder 10 the
expenses of the scholars while abroad.



SEC. 12. Service Obligation. — Immediately upon completion of the studies or
training, a Fellow shall be required to return to their job or in another capacity
approved by the Secretariat and serve on a full-time basis for a minimum period
equivalent to twice the period covered by the fellowship grant. In addition, the
Fellow shall be required to hold seminars or training sessions for employees from
the government and the private sectors on topics related to the course or training
that he/she has completed. Any recipient of the program who has not completed
his course shall reimburse the fellowship grant in cash or through equivalent
service. Any Pensionado who violates the service obligation imposed under this
Section shall reimburse the sponsoring agency or company and the National
Government of the fund assistance received under this Act in full or in part as the
case may be.


                                                                                   6
SEC. 13. Mechanism for Utilization of Returned Fellows. — There shall be
established and developed a National Network of Specialized Institutes for
Advanced Studies in universities selected by CHED in consultation with the
DOST and other appropriate agencies to be determined by the Secretariat to
provide returning scholars an office, laboratory, library/ and conference facilities
for their continuing activeties. The Institute shall also serve as a place for an
Exchange Program and Collaborative Research among local and international
scientists and scholars in priority areas of research and development.



SEC. 14. Appropriations. — The amount needed to initially implement the
provisions of this Act shall be charged against the appropriations of the current
year of the concerned departments and agencies. Thereafter, such sums as may
be necessary for its continued implementation shall be included in the
appropriation of the concerned departments and agencies in the annual General
Appropriations Act.



SEC. 15. Transfer of Other Fellowship, Scholarship, and Training Grants —
All foreign and locally sponsored fellowship, scholarship and training grants will
be transferred to the program for coordination. With respect to government
employees who may be affected by the transfer, the pertinent rules and
regulations of the Civil Service Commission (CSC) shall apply.



SEC. 16. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from
the approval of this Act, CHED, in coordination with NEDA, DTI, DOST, and
CSC, and upon consultation with the private sector stakeholders, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation
of the provisions hereof.
                                                                                   7
SEC. 17. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part hereof is held invalid
or unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision or part not otherwise
affected shall remain valid and subsisting.



SEC. 18. Repealing Clause. — Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule, or regulation
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
modified, or amended accordingly.



SEC. 19. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
