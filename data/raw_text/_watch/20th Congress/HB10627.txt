                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Municipality of Pinamungajan, one of the oldest municipalities in the
Province of Cebu, possesses a rich historical, cultural, and socio-economic heritage that
has significantly contributed to the development of the province and the region. For
generations, the people of Pinamungajan have celebrated their founding anniversary on
the 4th of September, tracing their roots to the town's separation from its matrix, the
Municipality of Barili.

         To institutionalize this deeply held tradition, the local government sought the
expertise of the National Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP) to verify its
origins. In a letter dated March 31, 2026, NHCP Chairman Regalado Trota Jose, Jr.
officially confirmed that Pinamungajan was established on September 4, 1850, under
the patronage of Santa Mónica. This affirmation is heavily corroborated by archival
sources, specifically Felipe Redondo y Sendino's Breve Reseña de lo que fue y de lo
que es la Diócesis de Cebú en las Islas Filipinas.

        Anchored on this official historical confirmation, the Sangguniang Bayan of
Pinamungajan enacted Ordinance No. 041-2026, recognizing 4 September 1850 as the
municipality's official founding day and declaring September 4 of every year as the
official Founding Anniversary of the Municipality of Pinamungajan. This legislative
enactment reflects the collective recognition of the local community of its historical
identity and its commitment to preserving and promoting its cultural heritage for future
generations.

      Complementing this ordinance, the Sangguniang Bayan likewise adopted
Resolution No. 091, Series of 2026, respectfully requesting the filing of a bill in
Congress declaring September 4 as Pinamungajan Charter Day and a special
non-working holiday within the municipality. The initiative was further endorsed by the
Sangguniang Panlalawigan of Cebu through Resolution No. 1597-2026, recognizing the
historical, cultural, and educational significance of the proposed measure and
expressing the Province's full support for its enactment into law.

       The formal declaration of September 4 as "Pinamungajan Charter Day" will serve
as a fitting tribute to the municipality's enduring legacy. It provides a dedicated
opportunity for residents, educational institutions, and government entities to reflect on
their shared history, honor the achievements of their forebears, and strengthen
community solidarity for future generations.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
  DECLARING SEPTEMBER 4 OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
 HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PINAMUNGAJAN, PROVINCE OF CEBU, TO
     BE KNOWN AS "MUNICIPALITY OF PINAMUNGAJAN CHARTER DAY”


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


       Section 1. Declaration of Policy and Historical Basis. – The Sangguniang
Bayan of Pinamungajan, Province of Cebu, enacted Ordinance No. 041-2026,
recognizing September 4, 1850 as the official founding day of the Municipality of
Pinamungajan and declaring September 4 of every year as its official Founding
Anniversary. The ordinance affirms the historical significance of the municipality's
establishment and recognizes the importance of commemorating its rich cultural
heritage, local history, and contributions to the development of the Province of Cebu. It
is therefore fitting that such occasion be accorded national legislative recognition
through the declaration of a special non-working holiday within the Municipality of
Pinamungajan.

      Section 2. Pinamungajan Charter Day. – September 4 of every year is hereby
declared a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Pinamungajan, Province of
Cebu, to be known as "Pinamungajan Charter Day", in commemoration of its founding
anniversary.

       Section 3. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
