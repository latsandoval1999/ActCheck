                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

The State recognizes the vital role of technical education and skills development in fostering
inclusive economic growth, reducing unemployment, and enhancing the global
competitiveness of Filipino workers. The Technical Education and Skills Development
Authority (TESDA) has consistently provided accessible technical-vocational education and
training (TVET) programs that respond to the evolving demands of industry.

This bill seeks the establishment of a district office of the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (TESDA) in the City of Cagayan de Oro

Cagayan de Oro City serves as the regional growth center of Northern Mindanao, with thriving
sectors in logistics, trade, agro-industry, and services. Its strategic location, supported by key
infrastructure such as the Port of Cagayan de Oro, makes it a hub for economic activity in the
region.

The population of Cagayan de Oro City has been steadily growing over the years, thanks to
its booming economy and strategic location. As the city continues to develop, it is highly
possible that the city will remain to attract more migrants and tourists, further contributing to
its population growth.

Given the city's population dynamics and more investments coming in, it is likely that
requirement for the skilled workers will be needed for it to be able to respond to infrastructure
development, job creation, and social services provision.

The growth in population and the labor force requirements in Cagayan de Oro and the Misamis
Oriental province continued to increase owing to the growing investments in the City and the
entire province.

Despite this progress, gaps remain in access to technical education and certification services,
particularly among the urban poor, out-of-school youth, and displaced workers. Existing
TESDA facilities in the province of Misamis Oriental are often overstretched, limiting their
capacity to fully address the increasing demand for skills training and assessment.

The TESDA Provincial Offices in the region was created by virtue of RA 7796 or TESDA Act
in 1994 which provided the channel for access to TESDA programs and services. At present,
TESDA Misamis Oriental Provincial Office caters to 23 municipalities and 2 component cities
and 1 chartered city (CdO) for Technical vocational provision in various training delivery
modes. There are 108 TVET providers in the province of which 49% are in Cagayan de Oro
City.
The establishment of a TESDA District Office in Cagayan de Oro City will decentralize service
delivery, improve coordination with local government units, industry stakeholders, and
educational institutions, and ensure more efficient implementation of skills development
programs. It will also strengthen workforce readiness and contribute to sustained regional
development.

Therefore it is incumbent to propose for the separation of the TESDA District Office from the
existing TESDA Provincial Office to ascertain requirements in the city for TVET is catered
where its manpower can concentrate in its coordination, supervision and monitoring of the
TVET development in the city.

In view of the foregoing, immediate approval of this measure is earnestly requested.

                                AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A DISTRICT OFFICE OF THE TECHNICAL EDUCATION AND SKILLS
DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (TESDA) IN CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “TESDA Cagayan de Oro District
Office Act”.

SEC 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote and
strengthen technical education and skills development to ensure a productive, competitive,
and globally responsive workforce. Toward this end, the State shall expand access to
technical-vocational education and training through the establishment of TESDA offices in
strategic areas.

SEC 3. Establishment of TESDA District Office. – There is hereby established a District Office
of the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority in Cagayan de Oro City,
hereinafter referred to as the “TESDA-CDO District Office,” which shall serve as the primary
center for the implementation, coordination, and monitoring of TESDA programs within its
jurisdiction.

SEC 4. Functions and Responsibilities. – The TESDA-CDO District Office shall have the
following functions:
     a. Implement TESDA programs, projects, and initiatives at the district level;
     b. Provide technical-vocational education and training (TVET), skills assessment,
        certification, and accreditation services;
     c. Coordinate with local government units (LGUs), industry partners, and educational and
        training institutions;
     d. Promote and expand access to TVET programs, especially for marginalized sectors;
     e. Conduct labor market intelligence and identify priority skills requirements; and
     f. Perform such other functions as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this
        Act.

SEC 6. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall
be charged against the current appropriations of TESDA. Thereafter, such sums as may be
necessary for its continued operation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

SEC 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of
this Act, TESDA shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

SEC 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid,
the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SEC 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
