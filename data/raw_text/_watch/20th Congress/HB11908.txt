                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Zamboanga Del Norte Electric Cooperative, Inc. (ZANECO) as organized
and registered pursuant to the provisions of Presidential Decree No. 269, as
amended, and under the supervisory authority of the National Electrification
Administration (NEA) with a mandate to perform electrification in the cities of
Dipolog and Dapitan and the twenty-one (21) municipalities of the Province of
Zamboanga Del Norte on an area coverage basis.

Pursuant to Section 27 of Republic Act No. 9136, otherwise known as the
Electric Power Industry Reform Act (EPIRA), the power to grant franchises
shall be vested exclusively in the Congress of the Philippines. Thus, this bill
seeks to grant a congressional franchise to ZANECO to provide an electric
power distribution system to the people of the Cities of Dipolog and Dapitan
and the 21 municipalities of the Province of Zamboanga Del Norte.
Furthermore, it seeks to require standards that the grantee must attain in
order to secure an effective partnership between the State and the grantee.

ZANECO has energized 99.66% of the barangays within its franchise area.
Notably, ZANECO has been consistently categorized for several years as an
“AAA” cooperative, the highest categorization that can be given by the
supervising government agency, the NEA.

The enactment of this bill will ensure the continuous delivery of electric
service from ZANECO to the mentioned cities and municipalities and will
create a path towards progress in Zamboanga del Norte. Likewise, it will be in
line with the policy of the State as provided for in EPIRA, to ensure and
accelerate the total electrification of the country; and to ensure the quality,
reliability, security, and affordability of the supply of electric power.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                              AN ACT
    GRANTING THE ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE ELECTRIC COOPERATIVE
    (ZANECO) A FRANCHISE TO CONSTRUCT, INSTALL, ESTABLISH,
    OPERATE, OWN, MANAGE AND MAINTAIN DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS FOR
    THE CONVEYANCE OF ELECTRIC POWER TO THE END-USERS IN THE
    CITIES OF DIPOLOG AND DAPITAN, AND THE MUNICIPALITIES OF
    SIBUTAD, RIZAL, LA LIBERTAD, MUTIA, PIÑAN, POLANCO, SERGIO
    OSMEÑA, KATIPUNAN, ROXAS, MANUKAN, JOSE DALMAN, SINDANGAN,
    SIAYAN, LEON B. POSTIGO, SALUG, GODOD, LILOY, TAMPILISAN,
    KALAWIT, LABASON, AND GUTALAC, ALL IN THE PROVINCE OF
    ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE

    Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Republic
    of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

3           As used in this Act, “distribution system” refers to the system of wires
4    and associated facilities including subtransmission lines belonging to or used
5    by a franchised distribution utility extending between the delivery point on
6    the national transmission system or generating facility and the metering point
7    or facility of the end-user.

 8           SEC. 2. Manner of Operation of Facilities. – All electric distribution
 9   facilities, lines and systems for electric services installed, owned, maintained,
10   operated, managed by the Grantee, its successors or assigns shall be operated
11   or maintained at all times in a superior manner, and it shall be the duty of
12   the Grantee, its successors or assigns, whenever required to do so by the
13   Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), or its legal successor, or the
14   Department of Energy (DOE), or its legal successor, or any other government
15   agency concerned to modify, improve and change such facilities or systems in
16   such manner and to such extent as the progress in science or technology and
17   improvements or innovations in the electric power services may render
18   reasonable and proper.

19         Whenever practicable, and for purposes of maintaining order, safety
20   and aesthetics along the highways, roads, streets, alleys or right-of-way, the
21   Grantee may allow the use of free spaces in its poles, facilities, or right-of-way
22   by interested parties upon reasonable compensation to the Grantee
23   considering the costs incurred to accommodate and administer the use of the
24   Grantee’s facilities by such parties. The ERC shall decide in case of dispute
25   or disagreement between the parties.

26          SEC. 3. Authority of the ERC and NEA. – The Grantee shall secure
27   from the ERC or NEA any government agency having jurisdiction over their
28   operations, the Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity and any other
29   license, permit or authority indispensable for the construction and operation
30   of the electric power distribution system.

31           SEC. 4. Excavation and Restoration Works. – For the purpose of
32   erecting and maintaining the poles or other supports for said facilities, wires
33   or other conductors or for the purpose of laying and maintaining said
34   facilities, wires, cables or other conductors, it shall be lawful for the Grantee,
35   its successors, or assignees, in coordination with the Department of Public
36   Works and Highways (DPWH) or the local government unit (LGU) concerned,
37   as may be appropriate, to make excavations or lay conduits in any of the
38   public places, highways, road, streets, lanes, alleys, avenues, sidewalks, or
39   bridges of the province, cities or municipalities: Provided, however, That a
 1   public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley, avenue, sidewalk, or bridge
 2   disturbed, altered, or changed by reason of erection of poles or other supports
 3   or the underground laying of wires, other conductors or conduits, shall be
 4   repaired and replaced in an efficient manner by the Grantee, its successors
 5   or assignees, in accordance with the standards set by the DPWH or the LGU
 6   concerned. Should the Grantee, its successors or assignees, after the ten
 7   (10)-day notice from the grant of authority, fail, refuse or neglect to repair or
 8   replace any part of public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley, avenue,
 9   sidewalk, or bridge altered, changed or disturbed by the said Grantee, its
10   successors or assignees, then the DPWH or the LGU concerned shall have the
11   right to have the same repaired and replaced in good order and condition and
12   charge the Grantee, its successors or assignees, double the amount of the
13   cost and expenses for such repair or replacement.

14          SEC. 5. Responsibility to the Public. – The Grantee shall supply
15   electricity to its captive market in the least costly manner. In the interest of
16   the public good and as far as feasible and whenever required by the ERC, the
17   Grantee shall modify, improve, or change its facilities, poles, lines, systems
18   and equipment for the purpose of providing efficient and reliable service and
19   reduced electricity costs. The Grantee shall charge reasonable and just power
20   rates for its services to all types of consumers within its franchised area in
21   order that business and industries shall be able to compete.

22         The Grantee shall have the obligation to provide open and
23   nondiscriminatory access to its distribution system and services for any end-
24   user within its franchise area consistent with Republic Act (RA) No. 9136,
25   otherwise known as the “Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001”. The
26   Grantee shall not engage in any activity that will constitute an abuse of
27   market power such as unfair trade practices, monopolistic schemes, and
28   other activities that will hinder competitiveness of business and industries.

29          SEC. 6. Rates for Services. – The retail rates and charges for the
30   distribution of electric power by the Grantee to its end-users shall be
31   regulated by and subject to the approval of the ERC or its legal successor.

32         The Grantee shall identify and segregate in its electricity bill to the end-
33   users the components of the retail rate pursuant to RA 9136, unless otherwise
34   amended. Such rates charged by the Grantee to the end-users shall be made
35   public and transparent. The Grantee shall implement lifeline rate to
36   marginalized end-users as mandated under RA 9136.

37         SEC. 7. Promotion of Consumer Interests. – The Grantee herein shall
38   establish a consumer desk that will handle consumer complaints and ensure
39   adequate promotion of consumer interests. The Grantee shall act with
1    dispatch on all complaints brought before it.

2           SEC. 8. Commitment to Provide and Promote the Creation of
3    Employment Opportunities. – The Grantee shall create employment
4    opportunities and shall allow on-the-job training in its franchise operation:
5    Provided, That priority shall be accorded to the residents where their principal
6    office is located: Provided, further, That the Grantee shall comply with the
7    applicable labor standards and allowance entitlement under existing labor
8    laws, rules and regulations, and similar issuances.

 9         SEC. 9. Right of the Government. – A special right is hereby reserved
10   to the President of the Philippines, in times of war, rebellion, public peril,
11   calamity, emergency, disaster or disturbance of peace and order: to
12   temporarily take over and operate the stations or facilities of the Grantee; to
13   temporarily suspend the operation of any station or facility in the interest of
14   public safety, security, and public welfare; or to authorize the temporary use
15   and operation thereof by any agency of the government, upon due
16   compensation to the Grantee, for the use of the stations or facilities during
17   the period when these shall be so operated.

18          SEC. 10. Right of Eminent Domain. – Subject to the limitations and
19   procedures prescribed by law, the Grantee is authorized to exercise the right
20   of eminent domain insofar as it may be reasonably necessary for the efficient
21   maintenance and operation of services. The Grantee is authorized to install
22   and maintain its poles, wires, and other facilities over and across public
23   property, including streets, highways, forest reserves, and other similar
24   property of the Government of the Philippines, its branches, or any of its
25   instrumentalities. The Grantee may acquire such private property as is
26   actually necessary for the realization of the purposes for which this franchise
27   is granted: Provided, That proper expropriation proceedings shall have been
28   instituted and just compensation paid.

29          SEC. 11. Term of Franchise. – Unless sooner cancelled, the franchise
30   shall be in effect for a period of twenty-five (25) years from the date of
31   effectivity of this Act. This franchise shall be deemed ipso facto revoked in the
32   event the Grantee fails to operate continuously for two (2) years.

33        SEC. 12. Warranty in Favor of the National and Local
34   Governments. – The Grantee shall hold the national, provincial, city, and
35   municipal governments of the Philippines free from all claims, accounts,
36   demands, or actions arising from accidents causing injury to persons or
37   damage to properties, during the construction, installation, operation, and
38   maintenance of the distribution system of the Grantee.

 6   Franchise. – The Grantee shall not sell, lease, transfer, grant the usufruct,
 7   or assign this franchise or the rights and privileges acquired thereunder to

23          SEC. 15. Reportorial Requirement. – The Grantee shall submit an
24   annual report to Congress, through the Committee on Legislative Franchises
25   of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Public Services of the
26   Senate, on its compliance with the terms and conditions of the franchise and
27   on its operations on or before April 30 of every year during the term of the
28   franchise. The reportorial compliance certificate issued by Congress shall be
29   required before any application for permit or certificate is accepted by the
30   ERC.

31          SEC. 16. Fine. – The failure of the Grantee to submit the requisite
32   annual report to Congress shall be penalized by a fine in the amount of Five
33   hundred pesos (P500.00) per working day of noncompliance which shall be
34   collected by the ERC. The fine shall be collected separately from the
35   reportorial penalties imposed by the ERC and shall be remitted to the Bureau
36   of Treasury.

37         SEC. 17. Equality Clause. – Any advantage, favor, privilege,
38   exemption, or immunity granted under existing franchises, or which may
39   hereafter be granted, upon prior review and approval of Congress, shall
40   become part of this franchise and shall be accorded immediately and
1    unconditionally to the herein Grantee: Provided, however, That the foregoing
2    shall neither apply to nor affect provisions concerning territory covered by the
3    franchise, the life span of the franchise or the type of service authorized by
4    the franchise: Provided, further, That the foregoing shall not apply to the sale,

 7           SEC. 18. Applicability of Existing Laws. – The Grantee shall comply
 8   with and be subject to the provisions of Commonwealth Act No. 146 or the
 9   “Public Service Act”, as amended, RA 9136, and RA 10531 or the “National
10   Electrification Administration Reform Act of 2013.”

11         SEC. 19. Existing Powers. – Existing mandates, powers, functions,
12   and privileges granted to electric cooperatives under existing laws, including
13   Section 10 of RA 10531 shall remain valid and effective, unless expressly
14   repealed by succeeding laws.

15          SEC. 20. Repealability and Non-Exclusivity Clause. – This franchise
16   shall be subject to amendment, alteration, or repeal by Congress when the
17   public interest so requires and shall not be interpreted as an exclusive grant
18   of the privileges herein provided for.

19          SEC. 21. Separability Clause. – If any of the sections or provisions of
20   this Act is held invalid, all other provisions not affected thereby shall remain
21   valid.

22         SEC. 22. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive
23   orders, letters of instruction, administrative rules and regulations or parts
24   thereof which is are contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act
25   are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

26         SEC. 23. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
27   days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
28   circulation.
