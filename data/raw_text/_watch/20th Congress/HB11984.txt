                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Barangay officials and personnel play a vital role in delivering basic
services, maintaining peace and order, and addressing the needs of their
communities. Despite their significant responsibilities, compensation and
benefits may vary depending on the financial capacity of their respective
barangays.

       This measure seeks to standardize the compensation of barangay officials
and authorized personnel by prescribing Salary Grade-equivalent honoraria and
compensation benchmarks. It also provides for appropriate funding assistance
to ensure equitable implementation nationwide while preserving the existing
legal status of barangay officials and personnel.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                             AN ACT
     STANDARDIZING THE COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS OF
     BARANGAY OFFICIALS AND PERSONNEL, AMENDING FOR THE
     PURPOSE SECTION 393 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160,
     OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF
     1991,” AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Standardized Honorarium of the Punong Barangay. - The
Punong Barangay of every barangay shall receive a monthly honorarium
equivalent to the first step of Salary Grade 14 (SG-14) under the applicable
government salary schedule.

      SECTION 2. Standardized Honoraria of Members of the Sangguniang
Barangay, Barangay Secretary, and Barangay Treasurer. - Each member of
the Sangguniang Barangay, the Barangay Secretary, and the Barangay
Treasurer shall receive a monthly honorarium equivalent to the first step of
Salary Grade 10 (SG-10) under the applicable government salary schedule.

      The standardized honoraria prescribed under this section shall apply
uniformly to all covered barangays, subject to the funding arrangements and
implementation provisions of this Act.

      SECTION 3. Compensation of Other Authorized Barangay Personnel.
- The compensation of other authorized barangay personnel shall be determined
in accordance with the following indicative positions and corresponding salary
grades:

      (a) Utility Worker I - Salary Grade 1 (SG-1);
      (b) Clerk I - Salary Grade 3 (SG-3);
      (c) Accounting Clerk I - Salary Grade 4 (SG-4);
      (d) Barangay Health Aide - Salary Grade 4 (SG-4);
      (e) Revenue Collection Clerk I - Salary Grade 5 (SG-5);
      (f) Day Care Worker I - Salary Grade 6 (SG-6); and
      (g) Administrative Assistant - Salary Grade 8 (SG-8).

       Duly appointed barangay tanods and members of the Lupong
Tagapamayapa shall be entitled to standardized monthly honoraria equivalent
to the minimum salary rate of Salary Grade 1 (SG-1), subject to applicable laws
and the funding provisions of this Act.

     Such honoraria shall not, by themselves, confer regular government
employee status. Nothing in this section shall automatically convert volunteer
workers into regular government employees.

      The foregoing salary grades shall serve as the prescribed compensation
benchmarks for positions duly created and authorized in accordance with law,
subject to applicable qualification standards, position classification rules, and
the funding provisions of this Act.

      SECTION 4. Amendment of Section 393 of Republic Act No. 7160. —
Section 393(a) of Republic Act No. 7160 is hereby amended to read as follows:

            “(a) Barangay officials, including barangay tanods and
         members of the Lupong Tagapamayapa, shall receive
         honoraria, allowances, and such other emoluments as may
         be authorized by law or ordinance in accordance with the
         provisions of this Code: Provided, That the monthly
         honorarium of the Punong Barangay shall be equivalent to
         the first step of Salary Grade 14 (SG-14), and that of each
         member of the Sangguniang Barangay, the Barangay
         Secretary, and the Barangay Treasurer shall be equivalent
         to the first step of Salary Grade 10 (SG-10), under the
         applicable government salary schedule: Provided, further,
         That the standardized honoraria prescribed herein shall
         be implemented in accordance with the funding
         arrangements and implementation period established
         under this Act: Provided, finally, That the annual
         appropriations for personal services shall remain subject
         to applicable laws, except as expressly modified by
         subsequent legislation.”

      SECTION 5. Preservation of Legal Status and Existing Benefits. —
Nothing in this Act shall be construed to convert elected barangay officials into
regular government employees solely by reason of the standardized honoraria
prescribed herein.
       The existing legal status, functions, qualifications, and tenure of barangay
officials shall remain unchanged. Benefits expressly granted under existing laws
shall continue to be governed by the applicable statutory provisions.

      SECTION 6. Funding and Implementation. — The Department of Budget
and Management, in coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local
Government and the Department of Finance, shall formulate the implementing
rules and regulations necessary to carry out this Act.

      The implementing rules shall establish the implementation schedule, the
applicable salary schedule, the funding responsibilities of the national and local
governments, and the measures necessary to ensure that the standardized
compensation prescribed herein can be implemented nationwide.

     The national government shall identify and provide the funding assistance
necessary for the implementation of this Act, subject to appropriations
authorized by law. The implementing rules shall establish a mechanism to assist
barangays whose available resources are insufficient to meet the standardized
compensation requirements.

      SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

     SECTION 8. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 9. Effectivity Clause. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of
general circulation, in accordance with applicable law.

      Approved.
