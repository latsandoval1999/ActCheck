                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to require the installation of Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV)
systems in government service counters and public areas. It strengthens Republic
Act No. 6713, otherwise known as the “Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for
Public Officials and Employees,”, and also establishes accessible public service hotlines
for reporting misconduct, unethical behavior, and violations committed by public
officials and employees.

        Public office is a public trust. Government agencies are expected to deliver
services with integrity, accountability, transparency, and professionalism. However,
complaints involving discourteous conduct, inefficiency, abuse of authority,
solicitation of favors, and other forms of misconduct continue to erode public
confidence in government institutions. In many instances, the absence of effective
monitoring mechanisms and accessible reporting channels discourages citizens from
reporting wrongdoing and seeking redress.

       This bill institutionalizes the use of CCTV monitoring systems in government
offices and establishes confidential reporting hotlines to promote greater
accountability, improve service delivery, and strengthen public trust in government.
It likewise ensures that all recorded footage and reports are handled in accordance
with applicable privacy and data protection laws while supporting the effective
enforcement of ethical standards in the public sector.

       Integrity, transparency, and accountability are fundamental pillars of good
governance. The adoption of modern mechanisms to enhance oversight and
encourage public participation in reporting misconduct will help significantly in
upholding the highest standards of ethical conduct in public service and in ensuring
that government remains responsive to the people it serves.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
MANDATING THE INSTALLATION OF CLOSED-CIRCUIT TELEVISION
SYSTEMS AT GOVERNMENT TRANSACTION COUNTERS AND THE
ESTABLISHMENT OF PUBLIC SERVICE HOTLINES, STRENGTHENING
TRANSPARENCY,  ACCOUNTABILITY,   AND RESPONSIVENESS   IN
GOVERNMENT SERVICE DELIVERY, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6713, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “CODE OF
CONDUCT AND ETHICAL STANDARDS FOR PUBLIC OFFICIALS AND
EMPLOYEES”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. A new Section 5-A is hereby inserted after Section 5 of Republic Act
No. 6713, otherwise known as the “Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public
Officials and Employees”, to read as follows:

          “SEC. 5-A. CCTV INSTALLATION AT TRANSACTION
      COUNTERS. – ALL GOVERNMENT OFFICES AND AGENCIES
      MUST INSTALL CLOSED-CIRCUIT TELEVISION (CCTV)
      CAMERAS AT THEIR SERVICE COUNTERS AND PUBLIC
      AREAS. THIS IS TO:

               “(A) IMPROVE TRANSPARENCY IN GOVERNMENT
             TRANSACTIONS;

               “(B) PREVENT AND RECORD                       CORRUPT        OR
             UNETHICAL BEHAVIOR; AND

                “(C) ENSURE THE SAFETY OF BOTH OFFICIALS AND
             CITIZENS.




                                           1
           “ONLY AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL CAN ACCESS
      RECORDED FOOTAGE, WHICH CAN ONLY BE USED FOR
      OFFICIAL INVESTIGATIONS INTO MISCONDUCT. THIS
      FOOTAGE MUST BE HANDLED ACCORDING TO DATA
      PRIVACY LAWS TO PROTECT INDIVIDUALS’ RIGHTS.

           “EACH OFFICE OR AGENCY MUST SUBMIT AN ANNUAL
      REPORT TO THE CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION DETAILING
      THE STATUS OF THEIR CCTV SYSTEMS, MAINTENANCE, AND
      ANY MISCONDUCT ISSUES REPORTED.”

      SEC. 2. A new Section 5-B shall be inserted after the new Section 5-A of the
same Act to read as follows:

           “SEC. 5-B. PUBLIC SERVICE HOTLINE FOR REPORTING
      MISCONDUCT. – ALL GOVERNMENT OFFICES AND AGENCIES
      MUST HAVE CLEAR AND EASILY ACCESSIBLE HOTLINES (BY
      PHONE, SMS, OR ONLINE) FOR THE PUBLIC AND EMPLOYEES
      TO REPORT UNETHICAL BEHAVIOR, MISCONDUCT, OR
      VIOLATIONS CONFIDENTIALLY.

           “THESE HOTLINES WILL KEEP REPORTS CONFIDENTIAL
      AND PROTECT THE IDENTITIES OF THOSE REPORTING.
      REPORTS WILL BE SENT TO THE APPROPRIATE AUTHORITIES
      FOR INVESTIGATION.”

      SEC. 3. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, issuances, rules
and regulations which may be inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are
hereby deemed repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 4. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, such holding shall not affect other provisions not
affected thereby.

      SEC. 5. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
