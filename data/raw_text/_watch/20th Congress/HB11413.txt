                                EXPLANATORY NOTE



        Section 12, Article XI of the 1987 Constitution declares the Ombudsman and
his Deputies as protectors of the people and directs them to act promptly on
complaints against public officials and employees of the government. This
constitutional mandate is further operationalized under Republic Act No. 6770,
otherwise known as the “Ombudsman Act of 1989,” which vests the Office of the
Ombudsman with broad powers to investigate and act upon acts or omissions of public
officers and employees.

       The effective discharge of this mandate requires not only investigative and
prosecutorial authority, but also an institutional capability to secure evidence, protect
witnesses, enforce lawful orders, and undertake necessary law enforcement
operations. Strengthening these capabilities will enable the Office of the Ombudsman
to respond more effectively to acts of corruption, abuse, inefficiency, and other forms
of misconduct in government.

        For this purpose, this measure seeks to establish an Anti-Corruption Law
Enforcement Service within the Office of the Ombudsman, to be known as the
Ombudsman Marshal Service. The proposed Service is intended to provide the Office
with a dedicated law enforcement arm capable of conducting proactive inspections,
inquiries, investigations, arrests, detention, searches, seizures, and other lawful
measures necessary for the effective implementation of the Ombudsman’s mandate.
       The bill likewise provides for the appropriate organization of the Office of the
Ombudsman, including the Office of the Special Prosecutor and the Ombudsman
Marshal Service, and defines their respective roles and authority under the supervision
and control of the Ombudsman. It further provides for the necessary rank, salary,
benefits, retirement privileges, staffing, and operational support for personnel
performing these functions.

       Recognizing the importance of protecting witnesses, whistleblowers, detainees
under investigation, and other persons placed in the custody of the proposed Service,
the measure likewise provides for adequate facilities and appropriate safeguards. It
also ensures that the implementation of the Service is supported by regular
appropriations of the Office of the Ombudsman, with such augmentation as may be
necessary for the sustained performance of its mandate.

        By strengthening the enforcement and institutional mechanisms available to
the Office of the Ombudsman, this measure seeks to ensure that investigations are
conducted effectively, lawful orders are carried out, evidence is secured, and cases
involving corruption and other irregularities in government are pursued with greater
efficiency and consistency.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

     AMENDING SECTION 15 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6770, OTHERWISE KNOWN
     AS THE "OMBUDSMAN ACT OF 1989", GRANTING ANTI-CORRUPTION LAW
     ENFORCEMENT POWER TO THE OFFICE OF THE OMBUDSMAN

 6
 7          “SECTION 15. Powers, Functions and Duties. — The Office of the Ombudsman
 8   shall have the following powers, functions and duties:
 9
10           (1) Investigate and prosecute on its own or on complaint by any person, any
11   act or omission of any public officer or employee, office or agency, when such act or
12   omission appears to be illegal, unjust, improper or inefficient. It has primary
13   jurisdiction over cases cognizable by the Sandiganbayan and, in the exercise of this
14   primary jurisdiction, it may take over, at any stage, from any investigatory agency of
15   Government, the investigation of such cases;
16
17         (2) Direct, upon complaint or at its own instance, any officer or employee of
18   the Government, or of any subdivision, agency or instrumentality thereof, as well as
19   any government-owned or controlled corporations with original charter, to perform
20   and expedite any act or duty required by law, or to stop, prevent, and correct any
21   abuse or impropriety in the performance of duties;
22
23           (3) Direct the officer concerned to take appropriate action against a public
24   officer or employee at fault or who neglect to perform an act or discharge a duty
 1   required by law, and recommend his removal, suspension, demotion, fine, censure, or
 2   prosecution, and ensure compliance therewith; or enforce its disciplinary authority as
 3   provided in Section 21 of this Act: Provided, That the refusal by any officer without
 4   just cause to comply with an order of the Ombudsman to remove, suspend, demote,
 5   fine, censure, or prosecute an officer or employee who is at fault or who neglects to
 6   perform an act or discharge a duty required by law shall be a ground for disciplinary
 7   action against said officer;
 8
 9           (4) Direct the officer concerned, in any appropriate case, and subject to such
10   limitations as it may provide in its rules of procedure, to furnish it with copies of
11   documents relating to contracts or transactions entered into by his office involving the

13   Commission on Audit for appropriate action;
14
15          (5) Request any government agency for assistance and information necessary
16   in the discharge of its responsibilities, and to examine, if necessary, pertinent records
17   and documents;
18
19          (6) Publicize matters covered by its investigation mentioned in paragraphs (1),
20   (2), (3) and (4) hereof, when circumstances so warrant and with due prudence:

22   cases may not be made public: Provided, further, that any publicity issued by the
23   Ombudsman shall be balanced, fair and true;
24
25          (7) Determine the causes of inefficiency, red tape, mismanagement, fraud, and
26   corruption in the Government, and make recommendations for their elimination and
27   the observance of high standards of ethics and efficiency;
28
29          (8) Administer oaths, issue subpoena and subpoena duces tecum, and take
30   testimony in any investigation or inquiry, including the power to examine and have
31   access to bank accounts and records;
32
33         (9) Punish for contempt in accordance with the Rules of Court and under the
34   same procedure and with the same penalties provided therein;
35
36          (10) Delegate to the Deputies, or its investigators or representatives such
37   authority or duty as shall ensure the effective exercise or performance of the powers,
38   functions, and duties herein or hereinafter provided;
39
40          (11) Investigate and initiate the proper action for the recovery of ill-gotten
41   and/or unexplained wealth amassed after February 25, 1986 and the prosecution of
42   parties involved therein;
43
44         (12) ESTABLISH AN ANTI-CORRUPTION LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE, THAT
45   WILL BE KNOWN AS THE OMBUDSMAN MARSHAL SERVICE, TO BE HEADED BY A
46   SPECIAL INVESTIGATOR WITH THE RANK OF A DEPUTY OMBUDSMAN, WITH THE
47   MEMBERS THEREOF BEING PEACE OFFICERS TO BE KNOWN AS OMBUDSMAN
48   MARSHALS WITH THE POWERS TO EFFECT ARRESTS, DETENTION, SEARCHES AND
 1   SEIZURES AND VARIOUS FORMS OF INVESTIGATIONS IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE
 2   LAW AND TO POSSESS AND CARRY SUITABLE AND ADEQUATE FIREARMS FOR THEIR
 3   PERSONAL PROTECTION IN CONNECTION WITH THEIR DUTIES AND FOR THE
 4   PROPER PROTECTION OF WITNESSES AND PERSONS IN CUSTODY WITHOUT NEED
 5   OF ANY SPECIAL PERMIT FROM ANY OTHER GOVERNMENT AGENCY: PROVIDED,
 6   THAT THE SAID SERVICE MAY BE CALLED UPON TO ENFORCE ANY ORDER, WRIT
 7   OR PROCESS OF THE OFFICE OF THE OMBUDSMAN, SANDIGANBAYAN OR ANY ANTI-
 8   CORRUPTION COURT, AGENCY OR UNIT: AND, PROVIDED, FINALLY, THAT
 9   ADEQUATE FACILITIES SHALL BE MAINTAINED FOR PERSONS IN CUSTODY,
10   INCLUDING DETAINEES UNDER INVESTIGATION, WHISTLEBLOWERS AND
11   WITNESSES. THE FUNDING FOR THE SERVICE SHALL BE SOURCED FROM THE
12   REGULAR APPROPRIATIONS OF THE OFFICE OF THE OMBUDSMAN AND SHALL BE
13   AUGMENTED ON A YEAR-TO-YEAR BASIS AS MAY BE APPROPRIATE TO ENSURE
14   PERFORMANCE OF ITS MANDATE.”
