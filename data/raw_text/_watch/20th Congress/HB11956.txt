EXPLANATORY NOTE

Strategically located in the heart of the 5° Congressional District of Isabela, the
LTO District Office plays a vital role in delivering core LTO services, including motor
vehicle registration, issuance of driver’s licenses and permits, and enforcement of land
transportation laws, rules, and regulations, to the municipalities of Roxas, Mallig,
Quezon, San Manuel, Aurora, Quirino, and Burgos. The growing demand for these
services within its jurisdiction has correspondingly increased the office’s
responsibilities and workload.

As a result, the current class “C” classification no longer reflects the office's
expanding functions and service demands. Its existing organizational structure and
resource allocation limit its capacity to efficiently serve the increasing number of clients
while maintaining quality public service.

Reciassifying the Roxas District Office to Class “B” will provide the appropriate
organizational structure, additional personnel, and operational resources needed to
effectively carry out its mandated functions. It will also enhance service efficiency,
strengthen enforcement activities, and improve the delivery of LTO services to the
communities within its jurisdiction.

In view of the strategic role of the Roxas District Office in serving the 5%
Congressional District of Isabela, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT
UPGRADING THE CLASSIFICATION OF ROXAS DISTRICT OFFICE OF THE
LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE IN ROXAS, ISABELA FROM CLASS “C” TO
CLASS “B” DISTRICT OFFICE AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Creation. - The district office of the Land Transportation Office
(LTO) in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Isabela is hereby upgraded from a
Class "C" to a Class "B" district office.

SECTION 2. Appropriations. - The Secretary of the Department of
Transportation shail immediately include in the Department's program the
operationalization of a Class “B” LTO district office in the Municipality of Roxas,
Province of Isabela, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current
year’s appropriation of the Regional LTO office. Thereafter, such sum as may be
necessary for the continued operation of the Class “B” LTO district office shali be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 3. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
