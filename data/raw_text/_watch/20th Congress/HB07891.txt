                          AN ACT
PROVIDING PHILHEALTH COVERAGE TO BARANGAY FRONTLINE
HEALTH AND SOCIAL SERVICE WORKERS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Barangay Service Point Officers (BSPOs), Barangay Health Workers (BHWs), and
Barangay Nutrition Scholars (BNS) are the frontline of the government’s grassroots health,
nutrition, and population programs. They often serve as the first — and sometimes only —
point of contact for families requiring medical attention, maternal and childcare, nutrition
counseling, and health referrals in barangays.

        Recent studies and surveys indicate that while these workers play an indispensable role
in public health, they often operate under precarious conditions. Many remain uninsured,
receive delayed or insufficient honoraria, and face occupational hazards, including exposure to
illnesses and overwork. In some cases, they even shoulder costs out of their own pockets to
ensure continuity of care. Nationwide, there are over 250,000 BHWs alone, mostly women,
whose dedication sustains critical health services in remote and underserved communities.

       Providing PhilHealth coverage to BSPOs, BHWs, and BNSs under this proposed
measure ensures that these essential workers are protected from financial risk and have access
to health services for themselves, strengthening the delivery of primary care. Insurance
coverage acknowledges their contribution, incentivizes sustained engagement, and supports the
broader goals of the Universal Health Care Act (RA 11223), which emphasizes the vital role of
community-based health providers in achieving equitable and effective healthcare delivery.

       By institutionalizing PhilHealth coverage, this bill safeguards the welfare of frontline
barangay workers while enabling them to continue serving their communities effectively. It is
a necessary step to protect those who protect the health and well-being of the Filipino people.

       The immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Barangay Frontline Workers
PhilHealth Coverage Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to recognize the essential role of
BSPOs, BHWs, and BNSs in delivering vital health, nutrition, and population services to
communities and to ensure they have adequate health insurance coverage to protect their well-
being while serving the public.

SEC. 3. PhilHealth Coverage. – All duly-accredited BSPOs, BHWs, and BNSs in every
barangay of the country shall be covered under the National Health Insurance Program (NHIP)
administered by the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth).

SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the implementing agency, in coordination with relevant government
offices and stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act. The IRR shall provide guidance on the procedures for enrollment,
coverage, or compliance under this Act; establish guidelines for benefits, claims processing, or
utilization as applicable; and set up monitoring and reporting mechanisms to ensure proper
implementation, transparency, and accountability.

SEC. 5. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the initial implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. Thereafter, such sums as may
be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall likewise be included in the
annual budget. Additional funding may be sourced from any lawful revenues as determined by
the implementing agency.

SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.
SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and regulations or parts
thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
