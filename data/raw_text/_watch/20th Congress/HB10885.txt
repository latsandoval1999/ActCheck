                         AN ACT
MANDATING A SMOKE-FREE AND VAPE-FREE ENVIRONMENT IN
ALL PUBLIC AND WORKPLACES, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9211 AND REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11900, AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES

                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


       The State has a constitutional duty to protect and promote the people’s right
to health and to instill health consciousness among them. Consistent with this
mandate, as well as with the country’s obligations under the World Health
Organization Framework Convention on Tobacco Control, there is a need to
strengthen the legal framework for creating genuinely smoke-free and vape-free
public spaces and workplaces.

        The proposed “Smoke-Free and Vape-Free Environment Act” seeks to
establish a clear and comprehensive national policy prohibiting smoking and vaping
in public places, public conveyances, and workplaces, while strengthening the
protection afforded to children, workers, commuters, students, patients, and the
general public from exposure to tobacco smoke and vape emissions. It expands and
clarifies the definitions of public places, public conveyances, smoking, vaping,
workplaces, and other relevant terms to reflect the realities of modern transportation,

                                                                                      1
 employment, commerce, education, and public life. The measure likewise
 recognizes that protection from exposure must extend beyond enclosed
 establishments to appropriate outdoor areas where people commonly gather,
 particularly schools, playgrounds, health facilities, transportation terminals,
 markets, parks, walkways, and other places frequented by the public.

        At the same time, the measure provides a carefully regulated framework for
 designated smoking and vaping areas in places where such areas may legally be
 established. Protection of public health remains the governing principle while
 providing clear and enforceable standards for establishments that choose to maintain
 designated areas. Further, this bill requires provinces, cities, and municipalities to
 establish Smoke-Free and Vape-Free Task Forces that will coordinate enforcement,
 monitoring, education, and community-level implementation.

       Ultimately, this bill seeks to move beyond a limited regulation of smoking
 and vaping toward the establishment of a comprehensive smoke-free and vape-free
 environment in which the right to breathe clean and healthy air is protected in places
 where people live, work, study, travel, and gather.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
 sought.

      Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and

      Sec. 6. Prohibition on Smoking and Vaping. — Smoking and vaping shall
be prohibited in all public places, public conveyances, and workplaces. This
prohibition extends to all indoor areas, including but not limited to those enumerated
in the amended definitions under Section 4 of this Act.


      Sec. 7. Designated Smoking and Vaping Areas (DSVA). — In all enclosed
places that are open to the general public, workplaces, and other places not covered
under the preceding section, where smoking/vaping may expose a person other than
the smoker to tobacco smoke and vape emissions, the owner, proprietor, operator,


                                                                                     5
possessor, manager, or administrator of such places may establish DSVA. Such area
must be in an outdoor space with the following requirements:


   a. The DSVA shall be located in an open outdoor space with no roof or enclosing
      walls;
   b. It shall not be situated within ten (10) meters from any entrance, exit, window,
      or area where people commonly pass or congregate;
   c. It shall not be located in public conveyances nor within one hundred (100)
      meters of schools, playgrounds, and other places or facilities frequented by
      minors.
   d. It shall not exceed ten (10) square meters in area;
   e. Only one DSVA shall be allowed per building or establishment;
   f. No food or beverage shall be served or consumed inside the DSVA; and
   g. It shall comply with such other standards as may be prescribed by the
      Department of Health or the local Smoke-Free and Vape-Free Task Force.


Nothing in this Act shall be construed to compel persons-in-charge to establish
designated smoking or vaping areas, nor to preclude them from adopting and
implementing more stringent measures within their buildings and establishments, in
accordance with existing laws and regulations, and consistent with a human rights-
based approach and the State’s obligation to protect and promote the right to health
and to a safe, healthy, and smoke-free environment.


These non-smoking and non-vaping signage shall be made part of the requirements
for the issuance or renewal of a business permit to operate an establishment, health
facility, or school. Failure to comply therewith is a ground for the denial or


                                                                                     6
revocation of such permit; provided, that for schools, these signages should be
prominently displayed whenever youth activities are being conducted, declaring that
the area is a smoke-free/vape-free zone.”


      Sec. 8. Obligations of Persons-in-Charge. — Persons-in-charge of public
places and workplaces shall have the following obligations:


   a. Prominently display “No Smoking, No Vaping” signs in conspicuous
      locations. Such signs shall measure at least 8 x 11 inches and shall include
      versions in English and the local dialect;
   b. Remove all ashtrays and other receptacles used for smoking or vaping in
      prohibited areas;
   c. Establish internal policies and procedures for the effective implementation
      and enforcement of this Act;
   d. Ensure that all employees are properly oriented on the provisions of this Act;
      and
   e. Comply with such other requirements as may be imposed by the Local
      Government Units (LGUs) or concerned agencies.


      Sec. 9. Creation of Smoke-Free and Vape-Free Task Forces. — All
provinces, cities, and municipalities shall establish a Smoke-Free and Vape-Free
(SF/VF) Task Force. The Task Force shall be chaired by the Local Chief Executive
and co-chaired by the City or Municipal Health Officer.


The Task Force shall have the following members:




                                                                                   7
   a. Business Processing and Licensing Office
   b. Engineering Office
   c. Offices involved in Peace and Order
   d. Association of Barangay Captains Chair
   e. SK Federation President
   f. School District Representative
   g. Tertiary Institution Representatives
   h. Environmental Protection Officer
   i. A representative from the private sector
   j. Other office/s as may be deemed necessary by the Local Chief Executive


It shall be responsible for the implementation, monitoring, and enforcement of this
Act in coordination with the Philippine National Police, the Metropolitan Manila
Development Authority (MMDA) in the National Capital Region, and other
concerned agencies. The Task Force is likewise empowered to deputize enforcers,
including but not limited to barangay tanods and other authorized personnel, to
ensure the effective enforcement of this Act at the community level.


LGUs shall allocate a minimum of three percent (3%) of their Local Health Budget
as a dedicated fund for the operation of the Smoke-Free and Vape-Free Task Force
and for the development and implementation of supportive programs and activities
that promote a smoke-free and vape-free environment. Nothing in this Act shall
prevent or limit LGUs from allocating an amount greater than the prescribed
minimum dedicated fund for tobacco control and the promotion of a smoke-free and
vape-free environment.

should not be connected, directly or indirectly, or represent the interests of the

All representatives of government agencies and organizations, including civil
society, non-government, grassroots, international organizations and private sector,
that take any part, directly or indirectly, in the setting and implementing of tobacco

copy of such declarations shall be made available to the public primarily via the
LGUs' website and other means to readily access such information.


In the event of any change in circumstances that may affect the truthfulness,
validity, or completeness of the initial declaration of non-affiliation with the tobacco
and vape industry, all representatives of government agencies and organizations,
including    civil   society,   non-government,      grassroots,   and    international
organizations, shall be required to file an updated declaration under oath
immediately. Such updated declaration shall disclose any new, emerging, or
potential conflict of interest, including but not limited to affiliations, financial
interests, partnerships, or any other form of involvement that may directly or
indirectly link the individual or their organization to the tobacco industry or its
representatives.


      Sec. 10. Tobacco and Vape Sales License. — No person or establishment
shall sell, distribute, or offer for sale tobacco products, heated tobacco products, or




                                                                                      10
electronic nicotine/non-nicotine delivery systems without a valid Tobacco and Vape
Sales License issued by the LGUs.


The application for such license shall include the following minimum requirements:
   a. Proof of business registration;
   b. Certification that the establishment is at least one hundred (100) meters away
      from any school, playground, or other places frequented by minors;
   c. Submission of a vicinity map;
   d. Certification from the barangay confirming public notice of the application;
      and
   e. Such other requirements as may be prescribed by the LGUs.


The license shall be displayed conspicuously at the place of business and shall be
valid only for the specific location indicated therein.


      Sec. 11. Livelihood Assistance Program. — Persons whose livelihood may
be adversely affected by the restrictions under this Act, such as ambulant vendors of
tobacco and vape products, may be referred to appropriate livelihood assistance
programs of the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) or the local Social
Welfare and Development Office. LGUs are encouraged to provide incentives, such
as recognition awards or business permit fee waivers, to establishments that
voluntarily adopt stricter smoke-free and vape-free measures.


      Sec. 12. Awards and Recognition. — The Department of Health and the
Department of the Interior and Local Government shall include the effective
implementation of this Act and the performance of the Smoke-Free and Vape-Free

Task Forces among the criteria for awards and recognition programs, including but

      Sec. 13. Penalties. — The following penalties shall be imposed for violations
of this Act:


   a. Smoking or Vaping in Prohibited Places. - Any person who smokes or vapes
      in prohibited areas shall be fined as follows:


               1. First offense — Fine of Five Thousand Pesos (₱5,000.00);
               2. Second offense — Fine of Ten Thousand Pesos (₱10,000.00); and
               3. Third and subsequent offenses — Fine of Twenty Thousand Pesos
               (₱20,000.00).


   b. Violation by Persons-in-Charge or Establishments. - Any person-in-charge
      who allows, abets, or tolerates smoking or vaping in prohibited places, or who
      fails to perform the obligations under this Act, shall be subject to the same
      penalties provided above. For juridical entities, the business permit or license
      may be suspended or revoked, in addition to fines.
   c. Illegal or Non-Compliant Designated Smoking and Vaping Area (DSVA). -
      Any person or establishment that establishes or maintains a DSVA in violation
      of any of the requirements of this Act shall be fined not less than Ten Thousand
      Pesos (₱10,000.00), but not more than Twenty Thousand Pesos (₱20,000.00),
      and the non-compliant structure shall be removed or demolished, subject to




                                                                                   12
      existing laws and regulations. This is without prejudice to the suspension,
      cancellation, or revocation of business permits.
   d. Selling tobacco and vape products without the required license — LGUs are
      hereby mandated to order the closure of, or impose appropriate fines and
      penalties on, non-compliant point-of-sale establishments pursuant to existing
      revenue and other applicable regulatory ordinances.


      Sec. 14. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.


      Sec. 15. Repealing Clause. — Section 4 and Section 6 of Republic Act No.
9211, and Section 15 and other relevant provisions of Republic Act No. 11900, are
hereby amended or modified accordingly. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.


      Sec. 16. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect one (1) year after its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
