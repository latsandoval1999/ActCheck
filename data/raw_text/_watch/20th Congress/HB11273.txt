                                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to provide accessible and comprehensive eye care services
for senior citizens with functional visual impairments. It establishes a National
Eye Health Program for Senior Citizens which aims to improve early detection,
treatment, rehabilitation, and vision support services to help older Filipinos
maintain their independence and quality of life.

       Visual impairment is one of the leading causes of disability among older
persons and is often caused by preventable or treatable conditions such as
cataracts, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and age-related macular degeneration.
According to the World Health Organization, at least 2.2 billion people
worldwide have a vision impairment, and over one (1) billion cases could have
been prevented or have yet to be addressed.1 In the Philippines, the growing
senior citizen population underscores the need to strengthen accessible eye care
services to prevent avoidable blindness and vision loss.2

       Despite existing public health programs, many elderly Filipinos continue
to experience barriers to eye care due to financial constraints, limited access to
specialists, and inadequate community-based vision services. This measure
addresses these gaps by institutionalizing a dedicated national program under
the Department of Health to deliver essential eye services and improve the
functional capacity and overall well-being of older persons.

       Protecting the eyesight of senior citizens enables them to remain active,
productive, and independent members of society while reducing preventable
disability and improving their quality of life. Establishing a comprehensive

                         AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR A NATIONAL EYE HEALTH PROGRAM FOR SENIOR
CITIZENS WITH FUNCTIONAL VISUAL IMPAIRMENTS, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Eye Health Program
for Senior Citizens Act”.

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right of every senior citizen to accessible, affordable,
equitable, and quality eye health services. The State shall establish a
comprehensive eye health program that emphasizes prevention, early detection,
timely treatment, rehabilitation, and continuing vision care to reduce avoidable
blindness and visual impairment among older persons.

      SEC. 2. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

         a. Eye health for senior citizens refers to the provision of medical and
      non-medical but essential services to senior citizens who have a functional
      visual impairment;​

         b. Functional visual impairment refers to an impairment of sight that
      substantially interferes with a senior citizen’s ability to perform specific
      daily living skills and tasks. Persons who are not regarded as legally blind,
      pursuant to this paragraph, but who experience such an impairment of
      sight, shall be deemed eligible for the program pursuant to this Act; and​

          c. Senior Citizens refer to individuals over sixty (60) years of age.

       SEC. 3. National Eye Health Program for Senior Citizens. – There is hereby
established, under the Department of Health (DOH), a National Eye Health
Program for Senior Citizens with functional visual impairments.

      SEC. 4. Implementing Agency. – The DOH shall implement the program
enunciated in the preceding section of this Act in coordination with the
Department of the Interior and Local Government as part of its services intended
to benefit the elderly.

      SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the DOH shall promulgate the
implementing rules and regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this
Act.

       SEC. 6. Appropriations. – The sum of Fifty million pesos (Php 50,000,000.00)
is hereby authorized to be appropriated from the National Treasury for the first
three (3) years of implementation of this Act immediately following its
enactment. Thereafter, the sums necessary for the continuous implementation of
this Act shall be included in the annual appropriations of the DOH as a separate
and distinct item.

       SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
force and effect.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general
circulation.

      Approved,
