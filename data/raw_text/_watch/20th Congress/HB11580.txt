                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Barangay oﬃcials and members serve as the primary frontliners of our
government. They are the ﬁrst responders in times of crisis, the primary implementers
of national and local policies, and the closest and immediate representatives of the
government at the grassroots level.


       Despite their round-the-clock duties and heavy responsibilities, they do not
receive a regular salary per se, but are instead compensated merely through honoraria
and allowances.


       Currently, the Local Government Code of 1991 mandates a minimum
honorarium of only One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) per month for the Punong
Barangay, and Six Hundred Pesos (P600.00) for the Sangguniang Barangay members,
Barangay Treasurer, and Barangay Secretary. While local ordinances may increase
these ﬁgures, many lower-income barangays are unable to provide living wages to
their oﬃcials.


       In view of the continuous rising cost of living and the severe economic
challenges that we all face today, these statutory minimums set over three decades
ago are no longer just or realistic.


       This bill seeks to amend Section 393 of Republic Act No. 7160, or the Local
Government Code of 1991, to provide the long-overdue ﬁnancial upgrade for our
barangay oﬃcials. This measure proposes to increase the minimum honoraria to Ten
Thousand Pesos (P10,000.00) for the Punong Barangay, Eight Thousand Pesos
(P8,000.00) for the Sangguniang Barangay members, Treasurer, and Secretary, and
Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) for Barangay Tanods and members of the Lupon ng
Tagapamayapa. Further, it increases their Christmas bonus to Five Thousand Pesos
(P5,000.00) and grants a lump sum retirement gratuity pay for their dedicated years
of service.


       Through this, we empower our barangay oﬃcials to serve their constituents
with greater eﬃciency, dignity, and dedication.


       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT INCREASING THE ALLOWANCES OF BARANGAY OFFICIALS,
  GRANTING HONORARIA AND OTHER BENEFITS TO THE BARANGAY
 WORKERS, GRANTING A LUMP SUMP RETIREMENT PAY, AND PROVIDING
    FUNDS THEREFOR, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE THE LOCAL
                  GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Section 393 of the Republic Act No. 7160 is hereby amended to read as
follows:

       “SEC. 393. Benefits of Barangay Officials – (a) Barangay officials, including
       barangay tanods and members of Lupong Tagapamayapa shall receive honoraria,
       allowances and such other emoluments as may be authorized by law or barangay,
       municipal, or city ordinance with the provisions of this Code, but in no case shall it be
       less than One thousand pesos (P1,000) TEN THOUSAND PESOS (P10,000.00) per
       month for the punong barangay and Six hundred pesos (P600.00) EIGHT
       THOUSAND PESOS per month for the sangguniang barangay members, barangay
       treasurer, and barangay secretary AND FIVE THOUSAND PESOS (P5,000.00)
       FOR BARANGAY TANODS AND MEMBERS OF THE LUPON NG
       TAGAPAMAYA: Provided, however, That the annual appropriations for personal
       services shall be subject to the budgetary limitations prescribed under Title Five, Book
       II of this Code.

        (b) The punong barangay, the sangguniang barangay members, the barangay treasurer,
        [and] the barangay secretary AND BARANGAY TANODS shall also:

        (1) Be entitled to Christmas bonus of at least One thousand pesos (P1,000.00) FIVE
        THOUSAND PESOS (P5,000.00) each, the funds for which shall be taken from the
        general fund of the barangay or from such other funds appropriated by the national
        government for the purpose;

        (5) Be entitled to appropriate civil service eligibility on the basis of the number of
        years of service to the barangay, pursuant to the rules and regulations issued by the

        (6) BE ENTITLED TO A LUMP SUM RETIREMENT GRATUITY PAY
        EQUIVALENT TO ONE (1) YEAR HONORARIUM, BUT IN NO CASE SHALL
        EXCEED ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P100,000.00) TO BE TAKEN
        FROM THE BARANGAY RETIREMENT GRATUITY FUND, PROVIDED,
        HOWEVER, THAT A RETIREE IS AT LEAST SIXTY (60) YEARS OF AGE
        WITH AT LEAST NINE (9) YEARS SERVICE AT THE TIME OF THE
        RETIREMENT.

        Section 2. Appropriations – The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act shall be charged to the appropriations of the Department of Interior and Local
Government (DILG). Thereafter, such amount as necessary for the continuous implementation
of this Act shall be included in the annual budget of the city or municipality concerned.

        Section 3. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended,
or modified accordingly.

       Section 4. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in newspaper of general circulation.
