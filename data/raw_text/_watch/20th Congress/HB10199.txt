                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Philippines continues to face persistent challenges in health workforce
distribution, with shortages in rural and geographically isolated areas,
maldistribution across regions, and limited real-time data for workforce planning.
These gaps hinder the effective implementation of Universal Health Care and
weaken the country’s capacity to respond to public health emergencies.

       This bill seeks to establish a National Health Workforce Registry as a
centralized, real-time, and interoperable database of all health workers in the
country. It aims to strengthen health system governance by providing accurate
and updated information on the supply, distribution, and specialization of health
professionals and community health workers.

      The registry will support evidence-based planning, equitable deployment
of health personnel, emergency surge mobilization, and health workforce
development. It will also be fully integrated with existing government systems,
including the Professional Regulation Commission, PhilHealth, and local
government health information systems, while ensuring strict compliance with
data privacy and cybersecurity standards.
        By institutionalizing a unified health workforce information system, this
  measure strengthens health system resilience, improves service delivery in
  underserved areas, and supports long-term human resource planning for the
  health sector.

        In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National Health
Workforce Registry Act.”


SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
strengthen the health system by ensuring the efficient planning, management,
equitable deployment, and continuous development of the health workforce.


The State recognizes that timely, accurate, and interoperable data on health
human resources is essential for achieving Universal Health Care, improving
service delivery in underserved areas, and ensuring rapid mobilization during
public health emergencies and disasters.


Accordingly, the State shall establish a secure, real-time, centralized, and
interoperable National Health Workforce Registry that supports evidence-based
planning, regulation, and workforce development while ensuring data privacy
and ethical use of information in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data
Privacy Act of 2012) and related laws.


SEC. 3. Creation of National Health Workforce Registry (NHWR) – There is
hereby created a National Health Workforce Registry (NHWR), to be established
and maintained by the Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with the
Department of Information and Communications               Technology (DICT),
Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), Civil Service Commission (CSC),
and local government units (LGUs).


The NHWR shall serve as the official national reference system for all health
workforce data in the Philippines.


SEC. 4. Coverage. – The NHWR shall include all health workers in the
Philippines, whether in public or private practice, including:
   a) Licensed health professionals such as physicians, nurses, midwives,
      dentists, pharmacists, medical technologists, radiologic technologists, and
      other PRC-regulated health professions;
   b) Allied health professionals and hospital-based health personnel;
   c) Community-based health workers, including Barangay Health Workers
      (BHWs), Barangay Nutrition Scholars, and other accredited community
      health volunteers;

                                                                                2
      d) Emergency response and public health personnel involved in disaster
         response and health emergencies;
      e) Retired, inactive, or overseas-based Filipino health professionals who
         voluntarily register for potential deployment or policy inclusion.


SEC. 5. Objectives and Functions of the NHWR. – The NHWR shall be used
to:
      a) Workforce Mapping and Planning – Maintain an updated national database
         of health worker numbers, distribution, specialization, employment status,
         and sectoral allocation.
      b) Equity and Deployment Support – Identify workforce gaps and support
         equitable deployment of health workers to Geographically Isolated and
         Disadvantaged Areas (GIDAs).
      c) Emergency Preparedness and Surge Capacity – Enable rapid mobilization
         of health workers during disasters, epidemics, and public health
         emergencies.
      d) Policy and Budget Planning – Support DOH, DBM, LGUs, and PhilHealth
         in workforce planning, budgeting, and resource allocation.
      e) Training and Development – Inform continuing professional development,
         specialization needs, and capacity-building programs.
      f) Health System Integration – Serve as a foundational dataset for health
         human resource governance under the Universal Health Care system.


SEC. 6. Data Governance, Privacy, and Security. – All data collected under this
Act shall be governed by the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and related issuances of
the National Privacy Commission.


The DOH shall ensure:

                                                                                  3
   a) Secure, encrypted, and access-controlled data storage systems;
   b) Role-based access for authorized agencies only;
   c) Clear data-sharing agreements among DOH, DICT, PRC, CSC,
      PhilHealth, and LGUs;
   d) Strict prohibition on unauthorized access, commercial use, or political
      exploitation of registry data;
   e) Regular cybersecurity audits and system integrity checks;
   f) Anonymized data use for research and policy analysis where appropriate.


SEC. 7. Interoperability and System Integration. – The NHWR shall be fully
interoperable with:
   a) PRC licensure and renewal databases;
   b) PhilHealth provider accreditation and claims systems;
   c) DOH health facility and service databases;
   d) LGU health information systems;
   e) Other relevant government human resource and civil service databases.


The DICT shall provide technical standards for interoperability and digital
architecture.


SEC. 8. Registration, Updating, and Validation. –
   a) Mandatory Registration – All licensed health professionals shall be
      automatically enrolled in the NHWR through the PRC, subject to
      validation and consent mechanisms defined in the IRR.
   b) LGU and Employer Reporting – Public and private health institutions shall
      regularly update employment status, deployment location, and role
      assignments.



                                                                              4
   c) Periodic Updating – Health workers shall update their records at least once
      every three (3) years or upon licensure renewal, employment change, or
      deployment.
   d) Data Correction Mechanism – The DOH shall establish a clear process for
      correction, verification, and dispute resolution of registry information.


SEC. 9. Workforce Analytics and Reporting. – The DOH, in coordination with
DICT and CSC, shall:
   a) Generate annual national and regional health workforce reports;
   b) Publish workforce distribution gap analyses;
   c) Provide evidence-based recommendations for workforce expansion and
      deployment;
   d) Support legislative and executive planning for health human resource
      investments.


SEC. 10. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the establishment,
operation, and maintenance of the NHWR shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act under the budgets of the DOH and DICT.


SEC. 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DOH, DICT, PRC, CSC, and other concerned
agencies shall jointly promulgate the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).


SEC. 12. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.
