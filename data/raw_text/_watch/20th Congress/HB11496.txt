                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Agriculture used to be one of the Philippines’ strongest sectors in the
economy. We prided ourselves with the ability of our farmers to compete in the
global market. However, present times show a completely different picture. What
used to be a robust agricultural sector now has given way to other sectors such
as the services and manufacturing sector. From exporters of products such as
rice, we now import rice to be able to feed our growing population.

       But the agricultural sector should not be left to decline, in fact it may
present the solution to many of our economic woes. A boosted agricultural sector
would help us with food security issues. If agriculture becomes economically
viable, rural economies would undergo a drastic transformation. Farmers would
no longer have to venture into cities to find work, thereby reducing rural-urban
migration. This in turn would lead to decongested cities and reduce the pressure
to create more and more jobs in urban environments.

     This is why we need to inculcate advancements made in agricultural
technology with our farmers. While there exist programs that already seek to
impart such knowledge directly to our farmers, its effects may be attained faster
and in greater magnitude if there is an assigned an expert in agricultural
technology that routinely aids and assesses each farmer’s yield and capabilities.

     It is for these foregoing premises that approval of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
 MANDATING THE ASSIGNMENT OF AN AGRICULTURAL TECHNICIAN IN
 EACH LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT AND ENHANCING THE OPERATIONAL
          CAPABILITIES OF THE TECHNICIANS THEREOF

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Agricultural
Technician Act”.

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to uplift the
socio-economic conditions of farmers and farming communities nationwide in
order to promote their welfare and empower their sector in the overall
development and progress of the country. Pursuant to this, an empowered
agricultural sector must be predicated on enhancing the operational and
technical capabilities of its workers with an end of view of capacitating its entire
agricultural sector, uplifting the people within it, increasing food security and
production, and maximizing the sustainable use of its agricultural resources.

      SECTION 3. Definitions of Terms. – As used in this Act, Agricultural
Technician or Agri-Technician – refers to individuals who are trained in the hard
sciences or engineering, using such knowledge and skills in agricultural
applications such as production, processing, and research.

      SECTION 4. Assignment of Agri-Technicians. – The Department of
Agriculture, through their regional and local offices, is hereby directed to provide
at least one (1) agri-technician for each level of local government. Agri-
technicians are mandated to conduct research, maintain the various equipment
and tools used by farmers, and to craft recommendations to their local office to
provide better conditions for agriculture in their assigned area. Such office of
agri-technician cannot be concurrently held in conjunction with another public
office.

      SECTION 5. Supervision and Control. – The Department of Agriculture,
through their regional and local offices, shall exercise control and supervision
over the activities and outputs of the assigned agri-technicians per locale.

      SECTION 6. Technical Support. – Each Agri-technician shall be furnished
with an appropriate vehicle such as but not limited to: motorcycles, tricycles, or
jeeps, to allow them to better carry out their functions. The issuance of such
would be subject to normal auditing rules.

      SECTION 7. Appropriations. – The funds necessary for the initial and
continuous implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions or parts unaffected
shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules, and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 10. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
