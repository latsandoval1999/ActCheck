EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article XII, Section 11 of the 1987 Constitution provides that the State shall adopt an
integrated and comprehensive approach to health development which shall endeavor to make
essential goods, health and other social services available to all the people at affordable cost.
The highly urbanized City of Las Pifias has seen continuous population growth and urban
density, which brings a corresponding surge in demand for public health and social protection
services, particularly in substance abuse treatment, mental health support, and community
rehabilitation.

While local health initiatives and outpatient drug treatment facilities exist in the city, the
current physical capacity, medical infrastructure, and specialized personnel remain
insufficient to meet the volume of individuals seeking residential rehabilitation and long-term
recovery support. This measure seeks to expand and modernize the existing drug treatment
and rehabilitation infrastructure in the City of Las Pifias by converting the existing locai
facility into a high-capacity center named the Las Pifias City Drug Treatment and
Rehabilitation Center. The bill explicitly mandates an increase in bed capacity to at least one
thousand (1,000) beds, upgrades existing clinical, psychiatric, and vocational facilities to
guarantee that the government provides adequate support to the city’s health sector.

By enhancing the facility's bed capacity and integrating comprehensive medical,
psychological, and vocational services, this Act ensures that residents of Las Pifias and
neighboring localities in southern Metro Manila receive accessible, humane, and
evidence-based rehabilitation without being displaced from their support systems.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

House Bill No._ 11558

Introduced by: HON. MARK ANTHONY G. SANTOS

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE DRUG ABUSE
TREATMENT AND REHABILITATION CENTER IN THE CITY OF LAS PINAS
FROM ITS CURRENT CAPACITY TO ONE THOUSAND (1000) BEDS,
UPGRADING ITS FACILITIES AND PROFESSIONAL HEALTHCARE SERVICES,
AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. The authorized bed capacity of the Department of Health - Drug Abuse
Treatment and Rehabilitation Center (DOH-DATRC) in the City of Las Pifias is hereby
increased to one thousand (1,000) beds.

Any future increase in bed capacity shall be consistent with its facility development
plan, which shall be prepared by the DATRC as approved by the Department of Health.

SECTION 2. The DATRC's operating fund and existing service facilities,
infrastructure, and professional healthcare services are also hereby increased and upgraded to
conform with and be commensurate to the increase in bed capacity as provided for in Section
| hereof.

SECTION 3. Appropriations Clause. — The Secretary of Health shal! immediately
include in the Department's program the implementation of this Act, the funding of which
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 4. Separability Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended. or
modified accordingly.

SECTION 5. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation
