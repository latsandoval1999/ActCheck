                             AN ACT
DECLARING NOVEMBER 06 OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NON-

SIQUIJOR, IN CELEBRATION OF ITS CHARTER DAY ANNIVERSARY


                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of San Juan, Province of Siquijor, celebrates its Charter Day every 6th of
November, commemorating its foundation and recognizing the rich history, culture,
heritage, and progress of the municipality.

This annual celebration provides an opportunity for the people of the Municipality of San
Juan, Province of Siquijor to come together, honor their shared history, recognize the
achievements of the local government and its constituents, and reaffirm their commitment
to the continued development of their community.

In this regard, the Sangguniang Bayan Resolution No. 2026-87, unanimously approved on
May 13, 2026, the Sangguniang Bayan of the Municipality of San Juan, Province of Siquijor
requested the declaration of November 06, 2026, as a Non-Working Holiday in celebration
of its Charter Day.

To make this celebration a yearly occasion, this proposed measure seeks to declare
November 6 of every year as a Special Non-Working Holiday in the Municipality of San
Juan, Province of Siquijor.

This will allow residents, government employees, students, and other members of the
community to participate in the annual celebration and activities. It will also help promote
the municipality’s culture, tourism, and local identity.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in the congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title – This Act shall be known as “Municipality of San Juan, Province
of Siquijor Charter Day Act”.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Holiday – November 6 of every year is hereby declared a
Special Non-Working Holiday in the Municipality of San Juan, Province of Siquijor, in
celebration of its Charter Day.

SECTION 3. Purpose – The annual celebration of Charter Day is an opportunity for the
people of the Municipality of San Juan, Province of Siquijor to remember their
municipality’s history, celebrate its achievements, and recognize its growth and
development.

It also allows residents, government employees, students, and other members of the
community to take part in the activities and celebrations prepared for this special occasion.

SECTION 4. Implementation – The Municipal Government of the Municipality of San
Juan, Province of Siquijor shall lead the annual celebration and coordinate with concerned
government agencies and other organizations to ensure the successful observance of the
Charter Day.

SECTION 5. Separability Clause. – If any provision, or part hereof is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not otherwise affected shall
remain valid and subsisting.
SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive order,
letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation contrary to or consistent with,
the provisions of this Act is hereby repealed, modified, or amended accordingly.

SECTION 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
complete publication in the Official Gazette.




Approved.
