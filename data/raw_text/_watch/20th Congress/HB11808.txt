EXPLANATORY NOTE

The agricultural sector, food processing, manufacturing, research, entreprencurship,
employment generation, and international trade in the Zamboanga Peninsula offer
significant opportunities for the development of the halal industry. To realize these
opportunities, coordinated technical assistance must be provided, access to shared service
facilities ensured, business incubation and product innovation supported, compliance with
reliable standards facilitated, and sustained assistance extended to enterprises secking to
participate in domestic and international markets.

Republic Act No. 10817, otherwise known as the “Philippine Halal Export
Development and Promotion Act of 2016,” establishes the national policy and institutional
framework for the development and promotion of the Philippine halal industry. It seeks to
promote halal industries, improve export competitiveness, protect consumers, provide
common service facilities, strengthen institutional capacity, develop human resources, and
advance research, development, and extension services.

Despite the existence of this national framework, enterprises in the Zamboanga
Peninsula may continue to face challenges in accessing coordinated technical services,
laboratory facilities, product development expertise, business incubation, standards-
compliance assistance, and export-readiness support. These challenges underscore the need
for a dedicated regional institution that can consolidate and facilitate access to the resources
and services necessary for the growth of the halal industry.

For this purpose, the proposed establishment of the Zamboanga Peninsula Halal
Industry Innovation Center, hereinafter referred to as the “Center,” is envisioned as a
regional institution dedicated to halal industry development, enterprise innovation,
research coordination, technical assistance, business incubation, and market access
facilitation. Its headquarters and principal office shall be established in Zamboanga City,
which shall serve as its principal administrative, operational, and industry-development
hub.

The designation of Zamboanga City as the permanent headquarters will provide a
stable institutional base for the Center to implement its programs, coordinate interagency
initiatives, develop research partnerships, facilitate access to technical and laboratory

===== [page 2] =====
services, support business incubation, and promote innovation and commercialization.
Through appropriate partnerships, satellite offices, shared service facilities, mobile
technical assistance units, and other outreach mechanisms, the Center may extend its
services to stakeholders throughout the Zamboanga Peninsula and other eligible
communities in Western Mindanao without altering the location of its designated
headquarters.

The Center shall be attached to the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) for
policy coordination, administrative supervision, and program implementation. It shall work
in cooperation with the Department of Agriculture, the Food and Drug Administration, the
Department of Science and Technology, the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos,
the Department of Health, the Philippine Accreditation Bureau, the Bureau of Philippine
Standards, the Bureau of Agriculture and Fisheries Standards, the Technical Education and
Skills Development Authority, the Commission on Higher Education, the Mindanao
Development Authority, local government units, academic and research institutions,
private-sector organizations, and other appropriate agencies. It shall coordinate with the
Philippine Halal Export Development and Promotion Board and respect the statutory
mandates and regulatory authority of all competent government agencies.

The Center shall prioritize enterprise development. product innovation, halal
certification assistance, regulatory-compliance preparation, research and laboratory access,
human-resource development, technology transfer, and domestic and international market
access. It shall provide targeted support to Muslim Filipino entrepreneurs, women-led
enterprises, young entrepreneurs, cooperatives, farmers, fisherfolk, manufacturers,
processors, and micro, small, and medium enterprises, with particular attention to
underserved communities.

The establishment of the Zamboanga Peninsula Halal Industry Innovation Center is
intended to strengthen the competitiveness, inclusiveness, and sustainability of the halal
industry; create employment and livelihood opportunities; encourage investment; advance
research and innovation; improve access to appropriate facilities and technical services;
and increase the participation of enterprises based in the Zamboanga Peninsula in the
national and global halal economy.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

TRINA REIKO CHUA-TAI

AN ACT

AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga Peninsula Halal

Industry Innovation Center Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote
the development, competitiveness, integrity, innovation, inclusiveness, and sustainability of the
Philippine halal industry; expand employment and livelihood opportunities; strengthen micro,
small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs); support Muslim Filipino entrepreneurs, farmers,
fisherfolk, manufacturers, processors, and service providers; and enhance the capacity of

Philippine halal enterprises to participate in domestic, regional, and international markets

Consistent with Republic Act No. 10817, otherwise known as the “Philippine Halal Export
Development and Promotion Act of 2016,” the State shall encourage research and development,
technology transfer, human-resource development, quality assurance, appropriate infrastructure,

halal standards compliance, product innovation, commercialization, and export promotion.

Toward these ends, the State shall establish the Zamboanga Peninsula Halal Industry
Innovation Center, with its headquarters and principal office in Zamboanga City, to provide
integrated industry-development services, business incubation, technical assistance, research
coordination, access to laboratory and shared service facilities, product innovation support, and

SEC. 3. Objectives. — The Zamboanga Peninsula Halal Industry Innovation Center,

hereinafter referred to as the “Center,” shall pursue the following objectives:

(a) Develop a competitive, inclusive, innovative, and sustainable halal industry in the
Zamboanga Peninsula and support eligible stakeholders in other areas of Western

Mindanao;

(b) Assist halal MSMEs, cooperatives, farmers, fisherfolk, manufacturers, processors,
traders, and service providers in developing their enterprises, improving productivity,

and increasing value addition;

(c) Facilitate compliance with applicable Philippine National Standards for Halal, food
safety requirements, sanitary regulations, product registration requirements, and other

applicable domestic and international standards;

(d) Provide or facilitate access to halal-related research. laboratory testing, technical

services, product formulation, packaging, and innovation facilities;

(e) Promote halal product development, value addition, commercialization, technology

transfer, and the adoption of appropriate technologies;

(f) Strengthen the capacity of halal enterprises to participate in domestic trade,

government-supported trade promotion activities, and international export markets;

(g) Develop the human resources, technical competencies, research capabilities, and

entrepreneurial skills necessary for halal industry growth;

SEC. 4. Creation of the Zamboanga Peninsula Halal Industry Innovation Center. —
There is hereby created a Zamboanga Peninsula Halal Industry Innovation Center, which shall be
attached to the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) for policy coordination, administrative
supervision, and program implementation, subject to applicable laws, civil service rules, budgeting

requirements, and government accounting and auditing regulations.

The Center shall serve as a regional industry-development, innovation, business
incubation, technical assistance, research coordination, and export facilitation institution in
support of the Philippine Halal Export Development and Promotion Program established under
Republic Act No. 10817. Its headquarters and principal office shall be located in Zamboanga City,
which shall serve as the Center’s principal administrative, operational, research coordination, and

industry-development hub.

The Center shall coordinate with, and complement the functions of, the Philippine Halal
Export Development and Promotion Board, hereinafter referred to as the “Halal Board,” without
duplicating or usurping the statutory powers and functions of the Board or other government

agencies.

SEC. 5. Headquarters and Territorial Coverage. — The headquarters and principal office
of the Center shall be established and stationed in Zamboanga City, which shall serve as its official
seat and principal administrative, operational, research coordination, innovation, and industry-

development hub.

The headquarters shall house the principal administrative offices of the Center and
coordinate the implementation of its programs, projects, and services throughout its territorial

coverage. The designation of Zamboanga City as the headquarters and principal office shall be

SEC. 10. Halal Certification, Accreditation, and Regulatory Authority. — The Center
shall provide assistance, information, training, compliance preparation, technical referrals, and

other support to enterprises seeking halal certification.

The Center shall not, by virtue of this Act alone, be authorized to accredit or recognize
halal certification bodies, issue halal certifications in its own name, issue or independently
authorize the use of the Philippine Halal Logo, or exercise regulatory powers assigned by law to

another government agency.

Halal certification, accreditation, product registration, regulatory inspection, and
authorization to use applicable official logos shall remain with the competent bodies and agencies

under Republic Act No. 10817 and other applicable laws and regulations.

The Center may establish or facilitate laboratory facilities only in accordance with
applicable licensing, accreditation, testing, calibration, quality assurance, and biosafety

requirements, as relevant to the services offered.

SEC. 11. Halal Research, Laboratory, and Product Innovation Facilities. — Subject to
feasibility studies, technical requirements, and the availability of funds, the Center shall develop,
establish, or facilitate access to shared research, laboratory, product development, and innovation

facilities.
These facilities may provide or coordinate services involving:
(a) Testing and analysis of raw materials, ingredients, and finished products;
(b) Food safety, hygiene, sanitation, and quality assurance:
(c) Product formulation, process validation, packaging, and shelf-life assessment:
(d) Appropriate analytical methods and technical support relevant to halal integrity;

(e) Research on agricultural inputs, food processing, logistics, storage, and supply-chain

management;

enterprises, cooperatives, start-ups, and entrepreneurs.

(b) Product development, packaging, branding, innovation, and commercialization

assistance;

(c) Support for business registration, permits, product registration, and other compliance

processes,

(f) Access to shared service facilities and appropriate technical resources;
(g) Market research, business matching, and participation in trade promotion activities; and

(h) Special outreach and capability-building initiatives for qualified Muslim Filipino
entrepreneurs, women-led enterprises, youth entrepreneurs, cooperatives, and

Participation in the Program shall be governed by transparent eligibility criteria and shall

not guarantee certification, financing, procurement awards, or export approval.

SEC. 13. Halal Export Development and International Market Access. — The Center
shall assist qualified enterprises in preparing for domestic and international markets through
coordinated programs with DTI, DA, DOH, the Department of Foreign Affairs, and other

competent agencies.

The Center may facilitate export documentation assistance, market intelligence, buyer
identification, trade missions, business matching, participation in trade fairs, product adaptation,
packaging improvement, technology commercialization, and information on importing-country

requirements.

Enterprises seeking to export halal products, processes, or services shall comply with the
applicable certification, Philippine National Standards for Halal, registration, and other

requirements prescribed by Republic Act No. 10817 and relevant laws and regulations.

SEC. 14. Industry Database, Monitoring, and Evaluation. — The Center shall develop a
regional halal industry information system to support program planning, research coordination,

innovation initiatives, monitoring, and evaluation.

The system may include information on enterprises assisted, products developed,
innovations commercialized, training conducted, certification assistance provided, laboratory

services facilitated, investments supported, jobs generated, and export transactions facilitated.

The Center shall establish measurable performance indicators and submit an annual
accomplishment report to the DTI and the Governing Council. Relevant findings may be provided
to the Halal Board and other competent agencies to support national policy development and

evidence-based industry planning.

All data collection, storage, processing, and disclosure shall comply with the Data Privacy

Act of 2012 and other applicable confidentiality and information security requirements.

SEC. 15. Appropriations and Funding. — The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act following its

enactment.
