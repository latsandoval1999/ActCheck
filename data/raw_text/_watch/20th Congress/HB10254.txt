                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Filipino professional workforce plays a critical role in nation-building,
economic growth, public service delivery, and global competitiveness. As industries
become increasingly specialized and technology-driven, professionals are expected
not only to maintain competence in their respective fields but also to continuously
develop advanced skills, specialized expertise, and interdisciplinary competencies.
However, while the country's regulatory framework provides mechanisms for licensure
and continuing professional development, there remains no comprehensive and
institutionalized system that recognizes career progression, specialization pathways,
and transferable professional qualifications across regulated professions.

     The increasing complexity of professional practice demands a more responsive
framework that recognizes diverse career trajectories and promotes lifelong learning.
Many professions now require structured post-licensure training, specialization
programs, and competency-based qualifications that are not adequately captured
under existing professional regulatory mechanisms. Without a unified system for
recognizing and transferring professional competencies and qualifications,
opportunities for career advancement, professional mobility, and international
recognition remain limited.

      This measure seeks to institutionalize the Career Progression and Specialization
Program and Credit Accumulation and Transfer System (CPSP-CATS) for all
regulated professions under the Professional Regulation Commission. The proposed
legislation establishes an integrated framework for career progression, specialization,
competency assessment, and credit recognition that aligns professional qualifications
with the Philippine Qualifications Framework and internationally accepted standards
of professional practice.

     The bill empowers the Professional Regulation Commission and the Professional
Regulatory Boards to formulate profession-specific career progression pathways,
accredit specialty societies and training institutions, establish professional practice
standards, and implement mechanisms for recognizing accumulated professional
learning and competencies. It likewise creates the necessary institutional

                                          1
mechanisms—including the CPSP-CATS Program Management Committee,
profession-specific CPSP-CATS Committees, Technical Working Groups, and
dedicated secretariat support—to ensure effective and transparent implementation of
the program.

      Furthermore, the proposed measure strengthens collaboration among
government agencies, professional organizations, academic institutions, and industry
stakeholders to ensure that professional qualifications remain responsive to evolving
labor market demands and technological developments. By facilitating the transfer and
recognition of professional competencies and qualifications, the bill promotes lifelong
learning, enhances professional mobility, and supports both domestic and
international competitiveness of Filipino professionals.

     Institutionalizing career progression and specialization pathways will not only
benefit individual professionals but will also contribute to higher standards of
professional practice, improved public service delivery, and greater confidence in the
competence and integrity of Philippine professionals across all sectors.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 3         SEC. 4. Career Progression and Specialization Program. – The Professional
 4   Regulation Commission, hereafter referred to as the Commission, and the PRBs, in
 5   consultation with the professional organizations, the Civil Service Commission (CSC),
 6   if applicable, other concerned government agencies and industry stakeholders, shall
 7   formulate and implement a career progression and specialization program for every
 8   profession.
 9
10         The Commission shall prescribe a set of criteria in identifying a career
11   progression and specialization program, which shall include but not be limited to the
12   following:
13   (a) It should be a well-defined and distinct field of expertise that will meet the needs
14         of the Filipino people and contribute to improving the quality and safety of the
15         community;
16   (b) It has an extensive impact on the industry, and it is beneficial to the profession
17         and professional; and
18   (c) It represents a widely accepted and recognized area of expertise supported by
19         evidence-based practice.
