                            EXPLANATORY NOTE
       This bill seeks to provide full educational scholarship to the children
and dependents of farmers and fisherfolks, ensuring their access to quality
education from basic education up to the completion of tertiary level, while
establishing a dedicated fund to support this program and promote inclusive
and equitable development in the agricultural sector.

       Farmers and fisherfolks remain among the most economically
vulnerable sectors in the country, despite their indispensable role in ensuring
food security and sustaining the national economy. Limited access to
education continues to hinder opportunities for their families, perpetuating
cycles of poverty and limiting social mobility.

       This measure grants comprehensive scholarship benefits covering
tuition and other school fees, as well as essential support such as board and
lodging, transportation, allowances for books, clothing, and food. It ensures
that financial constraints will not prevent qualified beneficiaries from
pursuing and completing their education, whether in public or private
institutions.

       To guarantee the sustainability of the program, the bill establishes the
“Educational Benefits for Farmers and Fisherfolks Fund,” with an initial
allocation of Fifty Million Pesos (₱50,000,000.00), to be used exclusively for the
implementation of the scholarship program.

      The measure designates the UniFAST Governing Board, in coordination
with the Department of Agriculture and representatives from the sector, to
administer and manage the fund, as well as to formulate the necessary
guidelines to ensure efficient and transparent implementation.
      This proposed legislation strengthens the State’s policy of promoting
social justice, improving access to education, and uplifting the living
conditions of farmers and fisherfolks. It recognizes that education is a
powerful tool in breaking the cycle of poverty and in building a more resilient
and productive agricultural sector.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                         AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE SCHOLARSHIP OF CHILDREN AND DEPENDENTS
OF FARMERS AND FISHERFOLKS, PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND
FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Children and
Dependents of Farmers and Fisherfolks Scholarship Act”.

       SEC. 2. Full scholarship for Children and Dependents of Farmers. – Children and
dependents of farmers listed in the Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture
(RSBSA) shall be entitled to full scholarship from basic education until their
completion of tertiary education, subject to rules and regulations as mandated by
this Act. The benefits shall include all expenses for education, whether in public or
private school, such as but not limited to tuition fees, miscellaneous and other fees,
board and lodging, transportation expenses, allowances for books, clothing, food
and other similar expenses.

       SEC. 3. Special Fund for Educational Benefits. – There is hereby established a
Special Fund, to be known as the “Educational Benefits for Farmers and Fisherfolks
Fund”, hereinafter referred to as the “Fund”, in the amount of Fifty million pesos
(P50,000,000.00). Such fund shall be perpetually maintained and shall be used
exclusively to provide educational benefits to the children and dependents of
farmers and fisherfolk; Provided, That not more than five percent (5%) of the Fund
shall be allocated for administrative expenses related to the implementation of this
Act.

       SEC. 4. Administration of the Fund. – The Unified Student Financial Assistance
for Tertiary Education (UniFAST) Governing Board, in consultation with the
Department of Agriculture (DA), and three (3) representatives from the farmers and
fisherfolks selected from a verified procedure of selection as agreed upon by the
farmers and fisherfolks, shall have the primary responsibility of managing and
supervising the Fund. It shall also be responsible in formulating the mechanics,

scope and limitations, and requirements for the scholarship program. The UniFAST
Secretariat shall serve as the Secretariat providing administrative support to the
Board and shall perform functions as authorized by the UniFAST Board.

        SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the UniFAST Board, in consultation with the DA, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of
this Act.

        SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part hereof, is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not otherwise affected
shall remain valid and subsisting.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive
order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation contrary to, or
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is hereby repealed, modified or amended
accordingly.

      SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
