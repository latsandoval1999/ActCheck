                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE


       Purple yam (Discorea alata), locally known as ube, is taking the world by storm.
This root crop is now very popular in the entire world, used in cakes, pastries, drinks and
other concoctions. But long before the world knew about ube, all Filipino households are
familiar to it. From ube halaya to ginataang bilo-bilo with ube, from ube ice cream to ube
jam, Filipinos have long made ube as an important part of their lives.

         News reports show that forecasts for the growth of the worldwide ube industry
will hit almost One Billion US dollars by the year 2035. Big countries like the Unites States,
Canada, Australia, United Kingdom, and Netherlands are the top exporting countries of
ube.

       Before other countries also make ube part of each household worldwide, it is
imperative that the Philippines lay its claim to this beloved agricultural product by
declaring it as the National Root Crop of the country. The declaration of ube as the
national root crop of the Philippines will also instill nationalism and pride to Filipinos
worldwide. This representation also sees the proposed legislation as necessary to make
the country competitive in the growing purple yam industry.

       For the Filipino people, approval of the foregoing bill is earnestly being sought.

              AN ACT DECLARING UBE (DIOSCOREA ALATA)
    OR PURPLE YAM AS THE NATIONAL ROOT CROP OF THE PHILIPPINES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress
Assembled:

Section 1. Ube as National Root Crop of the Philippines. Ube has long been part of
different food products in the Philippines; all Filipinos are aware and have tasted it. For
its importance in the lives of Filipinos, Ube known scientifically as Dioscorea alata, or known
worldwide as purple yam, is hereby declared as the National Root Crop of the Philippines.

Section 2. Responsibility of Agencies. The Department of Agriculture and Department of
Trade and Industry shall hereby lead in the promotion of the farming and trading of ube.
The DA and DTI shall promulgate rules and regulations for this purpose within one
hundred twenty (120) days from the effectivity of this Act. The Department of Education
(DepEd) and the National Museum shall work together in the informing the public about
the significance of ube to the natural and cultural heritage to the Filipino people.

Section 3. Separability Clause. - Should any provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity of the other provisions
of this Act.

Section 4. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations or
other issuances or parts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed
or modified accordingly.

Section 5. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication in
the Official Gazette and in two (2) newspapers of general circulation in the Philippines.
