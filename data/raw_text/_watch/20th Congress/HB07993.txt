                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

     This bill seeks to convert the Jct. Bukidnon Cagayan de Oro Iligan Road (BCIR) –
Cugman – F.S. Catanico Road in Cagayan de Oro City from a city road to a national road.

        Portions of the road which lead to a tourist site, the FS Catanico Falls, are presently in
poor condition and in dire need of repair and rehabilitation. City funds are not available for its
maintenance and/or upgrading. Hence, the need to tap other sources. With its improvement,
easier access to both points would be ensured and the potentials for tourism, commercial and
industrial development would be realized.

       With its conversion into a national road, it would receive the much needed funding and
assistance it deserves.

       Passage of this measure is earnestly requested.

                              AN ACT
CONVERTING THE JCT. BUKIDNON CAGAYAN DE ORO ILIGAN ROAD (BCIR) –
CUGMAN – F.S. CATANICO ROAD IN CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY INTO A NATIONAL
ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress be
assembled:

       Section 1. The Jct. Bukidnon Cagayan de Oro Iligan Road (BCIR) – Cugman – F.S.
Catanico Road is hereby converted into a national road.

      Sec 2. The Secretary of Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the
necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

       Sec. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper
of general circulation.
