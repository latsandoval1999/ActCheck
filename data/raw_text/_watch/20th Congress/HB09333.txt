29   AN ACT ESTABLISHING AN EXTENSION CAMPUS OF THE UNIVERSITY OF
30   SOUTHERN MINDANAO (USM) IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LIBUNGAN, PROVINCE
31   OF COTABATO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHERN MINDANAO–
32   LIBUNGAN CAMPUS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

33   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
34   assembled:

35         Section 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “University of Southern Mindanao–
36   Libungan Campus Act.”
37          Section 2. Establishment of Libungan Extension Campus. It is hereby established an
38   extension campus of the University of Southern Mindanao (USM) in the Municipality of
39   Libungan, Province of Cotabato, to be known as the University of Southern Mindanao–Libungan
40   Campus (USM–Libungan Campus).
41           Section 3. Mandate. The USM–Libungan Campus shall offer graduate and undergraduate
42   programs within fields determined by the Board of Regents (BOR) of USM, and other priority
43   areas aligned with local and regional development needs, particularly of Cotabato Province. The
44   campus shall also undertake research and extension services and provide progressive leadership in
45   its areas of specialization.
46           Section 4. Campus Administration. The USM–Libungan Campus shall be headed by a
47   Campus Administrator who shall render full-time service and shall be appointed or designated by
48   the BOR of USM upon the recommendation of a search committee and the University President,
49   subject to the guidelines, qualifications, and standards set by the BOR.
50           Section 5. Compliance with CHED Requirements. The establishment of the USM–
51   Libungan Campus shall ensure that the minimum requirements for its operation and program
52   offerings, including faculty qualifications, facilities, and academic standards, are complied with as
53   prescribed by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED).
54         Section 6. Post-Enactment Institutional Requirements. In coordination with the main
55   campus of USM, and within a period of one year after the approval of this Act, the USM–Libungan
56   Campus shall:
