                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The 1987 Constitution mandates the State to protect and promote the right to health
of the people (Article II, Section 15) and to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach
to health development that makes essential health services available to all at affordable cost
(Article XIII, Section 11). Republic Act No. 11223, or the Universal Health Care Act, builds
on this mandate by requiring a responsive network of public health facilities guided by a
national facility development plan.

       Hospitals under the Department of Health (DOH) form the backbone of this public
network, particularly for patients who cannot afford private care. Yet the authorized bed
capacity and service capability of most DOH hospitals are fixed by statute, so any increase
or change, however urgent, must pass through the full legislative process. As a result,
Congress each term considers numerous local bills seeking to raise the bed capacity of
individual hospitals. The process takes time, and in the interim hospitals operate beyond
their authorized capacity, patients are placed in hallways and shared beds, and the plantilla
positions, budget, and equipment that should accompany expansion lag behind actual
demand.

       This bill proposes a more responsive arrangement. It authorizes the DOH, through
an administrative issuance, to adjust and approve the bed capacities and corresponding
service capabilities of hospitals under its supervision and control. Such decisions must be
grounded on objective considerations, namely each hospital’s development plan, catchment
population, burden of disease, available human resources, and health care provider
networks, together with other standards the DOH may set. The DOH is directed to continue
updating and implementing the Philippine Health Facility Development Plan (PHFDP) to

guide the modernization of government health facilities, and to include in its programs the
funding requirements for each approved adjustment.

       Importantly, the measure preserves the constitutional power of the purse. Funding
for bed capacity adjustments remains subject to the regular budget process and must be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The DOH is likewise required to submit
an annual report to the Committee on Health of the House of Representatives and the
Committee on Health and Demography of the Senate on hospitals with approved bed
capacities and the corresponding funding requirements, ensuring that Congress retains
effective oversight.

       This measure adopts as its reference Senate Bill No. 1508, entitled “An Act
Authorizing the Department of Health (DOH) to Set and Approve the Bed Capacity and
Service Capability of All DOH Hospitals,” the substitute bill recommended for approval of
the Senate of the Twentieth Congress. Filing a counterpart measure in the House of
Representatives is intended to facilitate the timely enactment of this legislation and its
harmonization with the Senate version.

       For communities such as Marikina and its neighboring localities, which rely on DOH
hospitals like the Amang Rodriguez Memorial Medical Center, the ability of the health
system to expand capacity promptly where the need is greatest is not an administrative detail
but a matter of timely and dignified care. Rationalizing how DOH hospital capacity is set
will allow resources to follow patients, rather than wait for a separate law for every hospital.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                              HOSPITALS

1            Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
2    Congress assembled:

3            SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “DOH Hospital Bed
4    Capacity and Service Capability Rationalization Act.”

5            SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State shall protect and promote the right to
6    health of the people and pursue universal health care by strengthening the public health care
7    delivery system and by ensuring timely and equitable access to quality and essential health
8    and social services for all.

9            To this end, the State shall empower the Department of Health (DOH) to manage
10   the bed capacity and service capability of its hospitals efficiently and responsively, so that
11   vital health facilities can adapt effectively to changing public health needs and meet the
12   demands of the population nationwide.

13           SEC. 3. Purpose, Scope, and Coverage. – This Act shall streamline the process of
14   approving changes in the authorized bed capacities and corresponding service capabilities
15   of all DOH hospitals.

16           SEC. 4. Authority to Approve. – The DOH is hereby authorized to adjust and
17   approve the authorized bed capacities and corresponding service capabilities of hospitals
18   under its supervision and control. In doing so, the DOH shall take into account the
19   development plan of each hospital, its catchment population, the burden of disease, the

 1   availability of human resources, the health care provider networks, and such other standards
 2   as the DOH may determine.

 3          The bed capacities and service capabilities of DOH hospitals shall be determined by
 4   the DOH through an administrative issuance, subject to specific guidelines which shall be
 5   issued and promulgated within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.

 6          SEC. 5. Philippine Health Facility Development Plan (PHFDP). – The DOH shall
 7   continue to update, develop, and implement the Philippine Health Facility Development
 8   Plan (PHFDP), which shall guide the modernization and development of all government
 9   health facilities and facilitate their access to capital outlay investments through the DOH
10   Health Facilities Enhancement Program and other national government capital outlay
11   investment mechanisms.

12          The Secretary of Health shall include in the programs of the DOH the funding
13   requirements for the adjustment of the bed capacity and service capability of each DOH
14   hospital, in accordance with the PHFDP and the modernization and development plan of the
15   hospital concerned. Such funding shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
16   Act, subject to the regular budget process and authorization by Congress.

17          SEC. 6. Conversion, Upgrading, and Modernization of DOH Hospitals. – The
18   DOH shall upgrade and modernize the existing equipment and facilities of DOH hospitals,
19   consistent with their authorized bed capacities and service capabilities, to ensure that they
20   remain responsive to the health needs of the population.

21          SEC. 7. Annual Report to Congress. – The DOH shall submit to Congress, through
22   the Committee on Health of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Health and
23   Demography of the Senate, an annual report identifying the hospitals with approved bed
24   capacities and the corresponding funding requirements.

25          SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
26   effectivity of this Act, the DOH shall formulate the implementing rules and regulations of
27   this Act.

28          SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is declared
29   unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full
30   force and effect.
