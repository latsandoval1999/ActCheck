                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Constitution declares that the State shall protect and promote the right to health of
the people and adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development.
Consistent with this policy, the enactment of the Universal Health Care Act and the continued
expansion of PhilHealth benefits have significantly improved access to healthcare services
for millions of Filipinos.

        Despite these reforms, many Filipino workers and their families continue to incur
substantial out-of-pocket hospital expenses. Even after PhilHealth benefits are applied,
patients are often billed additional amounts for medicines, diagnostic procedures, medical
supplies, and other hospital charges. As a result, many households are forced to deplete their
savings, incur debt, or postpone necessary treatment despite years of mandatory PhilHealth
contributions.
        The challenge today is no longer merely expanding membership but ensuring that
existing benefits are fully utilized and effectively translated into meaningful financial
protection. Workers who faithfully contribute to PhilHealth should receive the maximum
benefits available under the law, while hospitals should adopt transparent and
patient-centered billing practices that minimize unnecessary financial burdens.
       This measure seeks to strengthen the existing healthcare framework by requiring the
automatic maximization of PhilHealth benefits, improving transparency in hospital billing,
and establishing mechanisms to reduce out-of-pocket expenses for qualified PhilHealth
members and their dependents in accordance with existing laws and available government
financing.
        In doing so, the bill reinforces the objective of universal health care that no Filipino
should suffer financial hardship because of illness and that every contribution to the national
health insurance system should provide genuine and meaningful protection.
       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT STRENGTHENING THE FINANCIAL PROTECTION OF PHILHEALTH
  MEMBERS BY MAXIMIZING HOSPITAL BENEFITS, ENHANCING BILLING
     TRANSPARENCY, AND REDUCING OUT-OF-POCKET HEALTHCARE
               EXPENSES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

     Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as “PhilHealth Benefit
Maximization Act.”

        Section 2. Declaration of Policy.- It is the policy of the State to protect and promote
the right to health and to ensure that every Filipino has equitable access to affordable and
quality healthcare services. The State likewise recognizes that PhilHealth members faithfully
contribute to the National Health Insurance Program and should therefore receive the fullest
benefits available under existing laws.

        To reduce unnecessary out-of-pocket healthcare expenditures, it is hereby declared the
policy of the State to promote the automatic utilization and maximization of PhilHealth
benefits and other applicable government healthcare assistance through a transparent,
patient-centered, and accessible hospital billing system.

        Section 3. PhilHealth Benefit Maximization Program.- There is hereby established a
PhilHealth Benefit Maximization Program, which shall ensure that all qualified PhilHealth
members and their eligible dependents are informed of, assessed for, and assisted in availing
of all PhilHealth benefits and other applicable government healthcare assistance before the
issuance of their final hospital bill.

       Section 4. Automatic Assessment and Application of Benefits.- All
PhilHealth-accredited hospitals shall, prior to the issuance of a final hospital bill:
        (a) Verify the patient's PhilHealth membership and eligibility;
        (b) Assess all PhilHealth benefits and other applicable government healthcare
assistance for which the patient may qualify;
        (c) Automatically apply such benefits and assistance to the patient's hospital charges
to the fullest extent permitted by law; and
        (d) Inform the patient or authorized representative of the benefits applied and the
corresponding deductions from the total bill.

        Section 5. Duty to maximize benefits.– All PhilHealth-accredited hospitals shall
exercise due diligence to ensure that every qualified patient receives the maximum PhilHealth
benefits and other government healthcare assistance available under existing laws and
programs before the issuance of the final hospital bill.

       The hospital shall not require a patient to separately apply for benefits or assistance
that may reasonably be verified or processed by the hospital through available government
systems or coordination mechanisms, except where the personal participation of the patient is
required by law.

       PhilHealth and the Department of Health shall establish a standardized benefit
assessment and certification process to ensure that all available benefits have been considered
and applied prior to billing.

       Section 6. Benefit Maximization Statement – Prior to the issuance of the final
hospital bill, every PhilHealth-accredited hospital shall provide the patient or his or her
authorized representative with a Benefit Maximization Statement, which shall contain:

       (a) The total amount of hospital charges;

       (b) The PhilHealth benefits applied;

       (c) Other government healthcare assistance or subsidy applied, if any;

       (d) The remaining balance payable by the patient, if any; and

        (e) A certification that the hospital has assessed and applied, to the fullest extent
practicable and in accordance with existing laws and regulations, all available PhilHealth
benefits and other government healthcare assistance for which the patient may qualify.

      Section 7. Right to Information. – Every PhilHealth member and qualified
dependent shall have the right to be informed of the healthcare benefits and
government assistance programs for which they may be eligible.
PhilHealth-accredited hospitals shall, prior to discharge, explain the benefits applied
and provide a copy of the Benefit Maximization Statement to the patient or
authorized representative.

       Section 8. Information and Public Awareness Program.-                 PhilHealth, in
coordination with the Department of Health and other concerned agencies, shall conduct
continuing nationwide information and education campaigns to increase public awareness of
PhilHealth benefits and existing government healthcare assistance programs. Such campaigns
shall utilize digital platforms, healthcare institutions, local government units, and other
appropriate means to ensure that members are informed of their rights and entitlements.

        Section 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the the Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with PhilHealth,
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations. Compliance with the provisions of this
Act shall be made a mandatory requirement for the renewal of PhilHealth institutional
accreditation and DOH hospital licensure.

        Section 10. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

        Section 11. Separability Clause. – If any portion of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other portions which are not affected thereby shall continue in
full force and effect.

       Section 12. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
