                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      A progressive system of taxation requires that the burden of taxation be
distributed in a manner that is fair, equitable, and responsive to the economic realities
faced by the general public. The Value-Added Tax (VAT), while an efficient and
reliable source of government revenue, is inherently regressive in nature, as it applies
uniformly to consumption regardless of income level. As a result, lower-income
households bear a disproportionately higher burden relative to their capacity to pay.

       In the context of rising prices of basic goods and services, as well as ongoing
economic pressures affecting Filipino families, there is a compelling need to
recalibrate consumption-based taxation in order to provide immediate and tangible
relief to consumers. A reduction in the VAT rate serves as a direct mechanism to ease
the cost of living, stimulate household consumption, and support economic activity.

      This proposed measure seeks to reduce the VAT rate from twelve percent (12%)
to ten percent (10%) across the sale of goods, services, and importation of goods. At
the same time, the bill incorporates a built-in fiscal safeguard by authorizing the
President, upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Finance and based on
objective fiscal indicators determined by the Development Budget Coordination
Committee (DBCC), to temporarily revert the VAT rate to twelve percent (12%) when
necessary to maintain fiscal stability.

     By introducing a flexible and data-driven mechanism, the measure strikes a
balance between providing immediate economic relief and preserving the
government’s capacity to respond to fiscal pressures. It ensures that tax policy
remains adaptive to changing economic conditions while maintaining discipline in
public finance management.

     Ultimately, this bill advances the principle that taxation must not only be efficient
but also equitable and responsive to the needs of the people. By reducing the VAT
rate while safeguarding fiscal stability, the measure promotes inclusive economic
growth and supports the welfare of Filipino consumers.

                                AN ACT
     REDUCING THE VALUE-ADDED TAX (VAT) FROM 12% TO 10% AMENDING FOR
     THE PURPOSE THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS
     AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1        SECTION. 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "VAT Reduction Act."
