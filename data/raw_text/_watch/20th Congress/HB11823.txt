EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to provide comprehensive eye care services for senior citizens by establishing a National
Eye Health Program for Senior Citizens and improving early detection, treatment, rehabilitation, and
vision support services to enable the elderly to maintain their independence.

As one of the leading causes of disability among older persons, visual impairment is often caused by
preventable or treatable conditions such as cataracts, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and age-related
macular degeneration.

Worldwide, at least 2.2 billion people suffer from vision impairment, and of these, over one (1) billion
cases could have been prevented or addressed early on.

In the Philippines, the growing senior citizen population underscores the need to strengthen accessible
eye care services to prevent avoidable blindness and vision loss due to financial constraints, limited
access to specialists, and inadequate community-based vision services.

This measure is designed to address these gaps by institutionalizing a dedicated national program
under the Department of Health to address these critical concerns, provide essential eye services, and
improve the functional capacity and overall well-being of older persons.

By this measure, we hope to protect the eyesight of our senior citizens so they may remain productive
and independent members of society while reducing preventable disability and improving their quality
of life.

A national eye health program is a worthwhile investment in geriatric medicine and dignified

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Title. — This Act shall be known as the “National Eye Health Program for Senior
Citizens Act of 2026”.

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right of every senior citizen to accessible, affordable, equitable, and quality eye
health services. The State shall establish a comprehensive eye health program that
emphasizes prevention, early detection, timely treatment, rehabilitation, and continuing
vision care to reduce avoidable blindness and visual impairment among older persons.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — As used in this Act:

(a) Eye health for senior citizens refers to the provision of medical and non-medical but
essential services to senior citizens who have a functional visual impairment;

(b) Functional visual impairment refers to an impairment of sight that substantially
interferes with a senior citizen’s ability to perform specific daily living skills and tasks.
Persons who are not regarded as legally blind, pursuant to this paragraph, but who
experience such an impairment of sight, shall be deemed eligible for the program
pursuant to this Act; and

(c) Senior Citizens refer to individuals over sixty (60) years of age.

SEC. 4. National Eye Health Program for Senior Citizens. — There is hereby established, under
the Department of Health (DOH), a National Eye Health Program for Senior Citizens with
functional visual impairments.

SEC. 5. Implementing Agency. — The DOH shall implement the program enunciated in the
preceding section of this Act in coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local
Government as part of its services intended to benefit the elderly.

SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the Secretary of the DOH shall promulgate the implementing rules and regulations
necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 7. Appropriations. — The sum of Fifty million pesos (Php 50,000,000.00) is hereby
authorized to be appropriated from the National Treasury for the first three (3) years of
implementation of this Act immediately following its enactment. Thereafter, the sums
necessary for the continuous implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual
appropriations of the DOH as a separate and distinct item.

SEC. 8. Separability Clause. — If any portion or provision in this Act be declared
unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not affected thereby shall remain

in force and effect.

SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, rules and regulations, and executive orders
contrary to or inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 10. Effectivity Clause. — This Act shall take effect upon its approval and completion of its
publication in at least two (2) national newspapers of general circulation.
