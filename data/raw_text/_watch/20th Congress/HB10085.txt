                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to strengthen Republic Act (RA) No. 10586, otherwise known
as the “Anti-Drunk and Drugged Driving Act of 2013”. It upgrades penalties for
impaired driving, expands enforcement authority, and institutionalizes proactive
measures to further prevent alcohol and drug-impaired driving.

       Driving under the influence of alcohol or dangerous drugs remains a serious
threat to public safety. Despite the enactment of RA No. 10586, impaired driving
continues to endanger the traveling public and signals a need for stronger
deterrence and more effective enforcement. Further, existing penalties no longer
adequately reflect the gravity of the offense or its consequences.

        This measure imposes higher penalties for violations resulting in serious
physical injury or homicide, strengthens administrative sanctions on driver’s
license, and provides for the periodic review of penalties to ensure that they remain
effective and responsive. It likewise requires the continuous acquisition and
maintenance of roadside testing equipment and expands its testing in high risk
areas. It institutionalizes public awareness campaigns and provides preventive
measures that discourage impaired driving before motorists take the wheel.

        Effective road safety laws require both meaningful penalties and reliable
enforcement. This vital piece of legislation shall not only improve compliance with
the law, but also deter impaired driving and better protect the lives and welfare of
the Filipino people.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.
