EXPLANATORY NOTE

Rice is the staple food of the Filipino people and lies at the very heart of our culture and
daily life. More than a basic commodity, it is present in nearly every Filipino meal, celebration,
and tradition. Rice farming also remains the primary source of livelihood for millions of Filipino
farmers and their families, making a stable and sufficient rice supply central to the country’s food
security and rural development.

Palay production in the Philippines reached 19.68 million metric tons in 2025.! Leading
this effort is the Province of Nueva Ecija, which has consistently been the country’s top palay-
producing province. In the second quarter of 2024 alone, Nueva Ecija recorded the highest palay
production in the country at 757.59 thousand metric tons, accounting for 19.7 percent of total
national production for the quarter.? For 2023, the province produced some 1.84 million metric
tons of palay from nearly 300,000 hectares.3 It is for this reason that Nueva Ecija has long been
nationally known as the “Rice Granary of the Philippines.”4

This bill seeks to formally declare the Province of Nueva Ecija as the “Rice Granary of the
Philippines” in recognition of its unparalleled contribution to the country’s rice production and
food security, and the hard work and dedication of its rice farmers. Being home to the Philippine
Rice Research Institute in the Science City of Mufioz, the province likewise stands at the forefront
of rice research and innovation in the country. The bill further mandates the Department of
Tourism to feature and promote the province as such in its regional and national promotion
programs, which will help boost local tourism, attract investments, and further uplift its farming
communities.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate adoption of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING THE PROVINCE OF NUEVA ECIJA AS THE “RICE
GRANARY OF THE PHILIPPINES”

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. The Province of Nueva Ecija is hereby declared the “Rice Granary
of the Philippines” in recognition of its contribution as the leading rice-producing
province in the country and to the food security of the nation.

SECTION 2. The Department of Tourism shall feature and promote the
Province of Nueva Ecija as the “Rice Granary of the Philippines” in all its regional and
national promotion programs.

SECTION 3. All laws, rules, regulations, presidential decrees, and other
issuances inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed
repealed or amended accordingly.

SECTION 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
