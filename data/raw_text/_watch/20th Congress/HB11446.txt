                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Our farmers and ﬁsherfolk remain to be among those with the highest poverty
incidence in the country. It is unfortunate that despite their sacriﬁce and hard work,
they struggle to meet the basic needs of their families. According to the Philippine
Statistics Authority (PSA), the ﬁsherfolk sector recorded a poverty incidence of 27.4%,
while farmers recorded a poverty incidence at 27%.

      Their livelihoods are highly susceptible to the adverse eﬀects of climate change,
natural disasters, and ﬂuctuating market prices. In the face of these hardships,
education serves as the great equalizer and the most sustainable pathway out of
poverty.

       Under Article XIV of the 1987 Constitution, the State is mandated to protect
and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels, ensuring it is
accessible to all. It is our moral and social obligation to make true of this commitment
to our agricultural workers. Through extending targeted educational assistance to
their children and dependents, we ensure that the youth are not deprived of a brighter
future simply due to ﬁnancial constraints.

       To this end, this bill seeks to provide a comprehensive educational assistance,
speciﬁcally for the children and dependents of farmers and ﬁsherfolk. The proposed
measure will cover all all the necessary expenses for primary, secondary and tertiary
education, which includes tuition fees, miscellaneous fees, learning materials, board
and lodging, transportation expenses, clothing allowance, and other similar expenses.
By covering these miscellaneous expenses, our farmers and ﬁsherfolk will not be
forced to make a diﬃcult choice between ﬁnancing the next harvest and sending their
children to school.
      In addition, the bill proposes to establish a special fund to sustain this
government assistance. Through this dedicated fund, we can guarantee the program’s
continuity and wider reach across the country. This, in turn, will empower the next
generation of our agricultural sector to break free from the vicious cycle of poverty.

       Let us honor the blood, sweat, and tears of our agricultural workers by giving
their sons and daughters the ﬁghting chance they deserve. Through this measure, we
are saying investing in a brighter and more digniﬁed future for the families who feed
our entire nation.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      AN ACT GRANTING EDUCATIONAL ASSISTANCE TO THE CHILDREN
  AND DEPENDENTS OF FARMERS AND FISHERFOLKS, PROVIDING FUNDS
               THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Educational Assistance for
Children and Dependents of Farmers and Fisherfolk Act.”

        Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to protect and promote
the right of all citizens to quality and accessible education at all levels and to establish and
maintain a financial assistance system that shall be available to deserving students, especially
the underprivileged.

        Section 3. Educational Assistance for Children and Dependent of Farmers and
Fisherfolk – The children and dependents of farmers and fisherfolk, as listed in the Registered
System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) or as certified by Department of Agriculture,
shall be entitled educational assistance from basic education until their competition of tertiary
education in public school.

       The educational assistance shall include all expenses for primary, secondary and tertiary
education, such as but not limited to tuition fees, miscellaneous fees, learning materials, board
and lodging, transportation expenses, clothing allowance, and other similar expenses.

        Section 4. Special Funds for Educational Benefits – To carry out the provisions of
this Act, there is hereby established a Special Fund, to be known as the “Education Benefits
for Farmers and Fisherfolk Fund,” in the amount of Fifty Million Pesos (P50,000,000.00). Such
Fund shall be perpetually maintained and used exclusively for the benefit of the children and
dependents of farmers and fisherfolks; Provided, That not more than 5% of this Fund shall be
used for the administration of the Special Fund.

        Section 5. Administration of the Fund – The Unified Student Financial Assistance
System for Tertiary Education Governing (UniFAST) Board, in consultation with the
Department of Agriculture (DA), the Department of Education (DepEd), and the Commission
on Higher Education (CHED), and three (3) representatives from the farmers and fisherfolk
selected from a verified procedure of selection as agreed upon by the farmers and fisherfolk,
shall have the primary responsibility of managing and supervising the Fund.

       Section 6. Non-Duplication of Benefits – Nothing in this Act shall be construed to
duplicate the benefits provided under Republic Act No. 10931 or the Universal Access to
Quality Tertiary Education Act, Republic Act No. 11524 or the Coconut Farmers and Industry
Trust Fund Act, and other government programs such as Agricultural Competitiveness
Enhancement Fund-Grants-in-Aid for Higher Education Program (ACEF-GIAHEP) and
Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources – Fisheries Scholarship Program (BFAR-FSP).

        Section 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DA, in coordination with DepEd and CHED, shall promulgate and
publish the necessary rules and regulations for the effectivity of this Act.

        Section 8. Separability Clause. – If any section or provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions not affected thereby shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

        Section 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended,
or modified accordingly.

       Section 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in newspaper of general circulation.
