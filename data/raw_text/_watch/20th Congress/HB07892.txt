                        AN ACT
ENHANCING TRANSPARENCY IN GOVERNMENT HIRING THROUGH
THE MANDATORY ONLINE POSTING OF VACANT POSITIONS IN ALL
GOVERNMENT AGENCIES AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7041 OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS THE “PUBLICATION OF EXISTING VACANT POSITIONS IN
GOVERNMENT OFFICES ACT”

                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

         The Constitution provides, under Article II Section 5, the duty of the State to protect
life, liberty and property, and under Article XVI Section 9, the obligation to safeguard
consumers from deceptive and unfair practices. These principles extend to public sector hiring
where transparency and accessibility must guide all recruitment processes to ensure equal
opportunity in the civil service.

        Republic Act No. 7041, enacted in 1991, requires the regular publication of vacant
government positions to promote fairness and efficiency. Its safeguards remain vital, yet its
mechanisms were designed for a pre-digital era. Today, the government workforce operates in
a radically different information environment. According to the 2024 National ICT Household
Survey of the PSA and DICT, 48.8 percent of Philippine households now have internet access
compared to only 17.7 percent in 2019. In the National Capital Region, more than 90 percent
of individuals aged ten and above own a smartphone, and among the working-age population,
online platforms have become the primary source of job information.

       Despite this shift, the posting of government vacancies remains uneven across agencies
and LGUs. Many jobseekers rely on unofficial social media pages, fragmented announcements
or informal networks, which limit access for qualified individuals who lack personal
connections to personnel offices. Civil Service Commission monitoring also shows
inconsistent compliance with existing publication rules, which undermines equal opportunity
and merit-based recruitment.

       CSC Memorandum Circular No. 14 s. 2018 already integrates online posting through
the CSC Bulletin of Vacant Positions Online, yet the lack of a clear statutory mandate prevents
uniform adoption of digital transparency practices. This bill addresses that gap by requiring all
government agencies and LGUs to post their vacancies in their official new media platforms
such as agency websites, officially recognized social media accounts and the CSC’s digital
recruitment portals. These digital postings supplement, not replace, the existing requirements
of RA 7041 including posting in three conspicuous places and the monthly reporting of
vacancies to the CSC.

        The bill retains the core duties of Personnel and Administrative Officers, preserves the
authority of the Chairman and members of the Civil Service Commission in publishing the
nationwide list of vacancies and upholds the rule that no appointment may be processed unless
the vacancy has been duly reported and published. The modernization simply strengthens these
safeguards by ensuring complete online disclosure of qualification standards, salary grades,
application procedures and deadlines. It also improves auditability through required digital
archiving.

        By enhancing transparency through the mandatory use of official new media platforms,
the bill brings public recruitment practices in line with current technological realities. It
expands access to employment information for the youth, professionals in remote areas and all
Filipinos who depend on digital mediums for job opportunities. Strengthening the visibility of
government vacancies supports a more competitive and merit-based civil service and promotes
public trust in recruitment processes.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “E-Transparency in Government
Hiring Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to uphold equal opportunity, promote
transparency in public service recruitment and ensure public access to employment information
through efficient and modern channels of communication. The State shall strengthen the merit
and fitness system by requiring the timely, complete and accessible posting of all government
job vacancies in accordance with Civil Service Commission standards.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. For the purposes of this Act:

      a) Official New Media Platforms refer to digital communication channels officially
         managed or authorized by a government agency or local government unit including
         but not limited to official websites, officially recognized social media accounts such
         as Facebook, X, Instagram or LinkedIn, and digital portals operated or recognized by
         the Civil Service Commission for the posting of job vacancies.

      b) Digital Bulletin of Vacant Positions refers to the consolidated online list of
        government job vacancies maintained and updated by the Civil Service Commission.

      c) Government Agency refers to any national government department, bureau, office or
         instrumentality including government-owned or controlled corporations with original
         charters.
SEC. 4. Section 2 of Republic Act No. 7041 is hereby amended to read as follows:

        “SECTION 2. Duty of Personnel Officers and Mandatory Posting of Vacancies.

        It shall be the duty of all Chief Personnel or Administrative Officers of all branches,
        subdivisions, instrumentalities and agencies of the Government, including
        government-owned or controlled corporations with original charters and local
        government units, to post in three (3) conspicuous places in their offices for a period
        of ten (10) days a complete list of all existing vacant positions in their respective
        offices which are authorized to be filled, and to transmit a copy of such list and the
        corresponding qualification standards to the Civil Service Commission not later than
        the tenth day of every month.

        Vacant positions shall not be filled until after publication: Provided, however, That
        vacant unfilled positions that are:

           a.   primarily confidential
           b.   policy-determining
           c.   highly technical
           d.   coterminous with that of the appointing authority
           e.   limited to the duration of a particular project shall be excluded from the list
                required under this Law.

        IN ADDITION TO THESE PUBLICATION REQUIREMENTS, ALL
        AGENCIES AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS SHALL MANDATORILY
        POST ALL VACANT POSITIONS, INCLUDING PERMANENT,
        TEMPORARY, CONTRACTUAL AND CASUAL POSITIONS, IN THEIR
        OFFICIAL NEW MEDIA PLATFORMS AS DEFINED IN THIS ACT. SUCH
        DIGITAL POSTING SHALL BE MADE NOT LATER THAN TEN (10) DAYS
        AFTER THE VACANCY OCCURS AND SHALL REMAIN POSTED FOR A
        MINIMUM OF TEN (10) CALENDAR DAYS.

        DIGITAL POSTING SHALL INCLUDE PUBLICATION THROUGH THE
        AGENCY’S OFFICIAL WEBSITE, ITS OFFICIALLY RECOGNIZED
        SOCIAL MEDIA ACCOUNTS AND THE CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION’S
        BULLETIN OF VACANT POSITIONS ONLINE OR ANY OTHER OFFICIAL
        DIGITAL RECRUITMENT PLATFORM RECOGNIZED BY THE CIVIL
        SERVICE COMMISSION.

        ALL DIGITAL POSTINGS SHALL CONTAIN COMPLETE INFORMATION
        ON THE POSITION TITLE, SALARY GRADE, QUALIFICATION
        STANDARDS INCLUDING REQUIRED EDUCATION, TRAINING,
        EXPERIENCE, ELIGIBILITY AND COMPETENCIES, PLACE OF
        ASSIGNMENT,    APPLICATION   PROCEDURES,   DOCUMENTARY
        REQUIREMENTS, DEADLINE OF SUBMISSION AND THE CONTACT
        INFORMATION OF THE HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT OFFICE
        OR ITS EQUIVALENT UNIT.
        AGENCIES AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS SHALL ENSURE THAT
        ALL ONLINE POSTINGS ARE REGULARLY UPDATED AND THAT
        EXPIRED POSTINGS ARE REMOVED AND ARCHIVED FOR A MINIMUM
        PERIOD OF ONE (1) YEAR FOR PURPOSES OF AUDIT, VERIFICATION
        AND COMPLIANCE WITH CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION AND
        COMMISSION ON AUDIT REQUIREMENTS.”

SEC. 5. Section 3 of Republic Act No. 7041 is hereby amended to read as follows:

       “SECTION 3. Publication of Vacancies.

       The Chairman and members of the Civil Service Commission shall publish once every
       quarter a complete list of all the existing vacant positions in the Government throughout
       the country, including the qualification standards required for each position and,
       thereafter, certify under oath to the completion of publication. Copies of such
       publication shall be sold at cost to the public and distributed free of charge to the various
       personnel offices of the government where they shall be available for inspection by the
       public: Provided, That said publication shall be posted by the Chief Personnel or
       Administrative Officer of all local government units in at least three (3) public and
       conspicuous places in their respective municipalities and provinces: Provided, further,
       That any vacant position published therein shall be open to any qualified person who
       does not necessarily belong to the same office with the vacancy or who occupies a
       position next in rank to the vacancy: Provided, finally, That the Civil Service
       Commission shall not act on any appointment to fill up a vacant position unless the
       same has been reported to and published by the Commission.

       IN ADDITION TO THE FOREGOING, THE CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION
       SHALL MAINTAIN AND REGULARLY UPDATE A CONSOLIDATED
       NATIONWIDE DIGITAL BULLETIN OF VACANT GOVERNMENT
       POSITIONS WHICH SHALL INCLUDE THE QUALIFICATION STANDARDS
       REQUIRED FOR EACH POSITION. THE DIGITAL BULLETIN SHALL BE
       ACCESSIBLE TO THE PUBLIC AT ALL TIMES THROUGH THE OFFICIAL
       CSC WEBSITE AND OTHER OFFICIAL DIGITAL PLATFORMS
       DESIGNATED BY THE COMMISSION. THE COMMISSION SHALL ENSURE
       THAT ALL VACANCIES SUBMITTED BY AGENCIES AND LOCAL
       GOVERNMENT UNITS ARE PROMPTLY INCLUDED IN THE DIGITAL
       BULLETIN AND SHALL CERTIFY THE COMPLETION AND TIMELINESS
       OF SUCH DIGITAL PUBLICATION FOR PURPOSES OF AUDIT,
       COMPLIANCE AND VERIFICATION.”

SEC. 6. Role of the Department of Information and Communications Technology. The
Department of Information and Communications Technology shall provide technical assistance
to government agencies and local government units in the establishment, maintenance and
security of their official new media platforms. The DICT shall develop minimum technical
standards to ensure the accessibility, authenticity, interoperability and cybersecurity of the
digital postings required under this Act. The DICT shall coordinate with the Civil Service
Commission to ensure that digital systems used for publication comply with recruitment and
transparency standards prescribed by the Commission.
SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the Civil Service Commission in consultation with the Department of Information and
Communications Technology shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for its
implementation.

SEC. 8. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid,
the remaining provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, rules, regulations or parts thereof inconsistent
with this Act are repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 10. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
