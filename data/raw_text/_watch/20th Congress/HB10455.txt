                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to grant comprehensive duty and tax exemptions on revenues
and assets that are actually, directly, and exclusively used in furtherance of the
Benguet State University’s (BSU) educational mandate. It strengthens Presidential
Decree No. 2010, otherwise known as the “Charter of Benguet State University”, and
establishes the legal basis for the retroactive application of such exemptions.

        As a state university, BSU is mandated to provide quality higher education,
advance research, and promote extension services and agribusiness. However,
revenues generated from the utilization of its assets and income-generating
activities, including the rental income, have been subjected to tax assessments due to
the absence of an explicit and comprehensive tax exemption under its Charter. This
limits resources intended for the University’s academic and public service functions.

      This measure clearly defines the scope of BSU’s duty and tax exemptions. It
recognizes that the University’s revenues and assets are not maintained for private
gain but are continuously reinvested in its operations and services. It shall also
reduce recurring disputes over tax liabilities and allow BSU to devote more of its
resources to the students and communities that it serves.

       This vital piece of legislation reaffirms the State’s commitment to accessible
and quality public higher education, and recognizes the vital role of state
universities in regional and national development. It likewise places BSU on a more
equitable footing with other state universities whose charters expressly provide
comparable tax privileges. Greater fiscal flexibility shall strengthen BSU’s capacity to
serve and contribute to development of the province of Benguet and the Cordillera
region.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is respectfully
sought.

                            AN ACT
GRANTING COMPREHENSIVE DUTY AND TAX EXEMPTIONS TO BENGUET
STATE UNIVERSITY, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE PRESIDENTIAL
DECREE NO. 2010, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE CHARTER OF BENGUET
STATE UNIVERSITY, AND FOR THE RETROACTIVE APPLICATION
THEREOF

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as “Benguet State University
(BSU) Tax Exemption”.

      SEC. 2. A new Section 21-A is hereby inserted after Section 21 of Presidential
Decree No. 2010, entitled “Converting the Mountain State Agricultural College in the
Municipality of La Trinidad, Province of Benguet, into a University, to be known as the
Benguet State University, and Appropriating Funds Therefor”, to read as follows:

          “SEC. 21-A. DUTY AND TAX EXEMPTION ON REVENUES
      AND ASSETS USED FOR EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES. – THE
      PROVISIONS OF ANY GENERAL OR SPECIAL LAW TO THE
      CONTRARY NOTWITHSTANDING:

               “(A) ALL REVENUES AND ASSETS OF THE
             UNIVERSITY, FROM WHATEVER SOURCE DERIVED,
             WHICH ARE ACTUALLY, DIRECTLY, AND EXCLUSIVELY
             USED FOR INSTRUCTION, RESEARCH, EXTENSION,
             PRODUCTION, OR IN FURTHERANCE OF ITS MANDATE,
             SHALL BE EXEMPT FROM ALL KINDS OF TAXES AND
             DUTIES, WHETHER IMPOSED BY THE NATIONAL
             GOVERNMENT OR ANY OF ITS AGENCIES AND
             INSTRUMENTALITIES,                 INCLUDING
             GOVERNMENT-OWNED         OR      -CONTROLLED
             CORPORATIONS, AND BY LOCAL GOVERNMENT
             UNITS;

​       SEC. 3. Retroactivity. – The duty and tax exemptions granted under this Act
shall be applied retroactively to all revenues received by the University prior to the
effectivity of this Act, to the extent that such revenues are proven to have been
actually, directly, and exclusively used for instruction, research, extension,
production, or other purposes in furtherance of its mandate: Provided, That any claim
for refund, tax credit, or recovery of taxes and duties paid shall be subject to the
applicable substantiation requirements and prescriptive periods under existing laws,
rules, and jurisprudence: Provided, further, That nothing herein shall be construed to
disturb final and executory judicial decisions.

        SEC 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Board, in consultation with the Commission on Higher
Education, shall formulate the guidelines to fully implement its provisions.

      SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, letters of instructions,
rules and regulations and other issuances, or provision thereof, which are
inconsistent with the of this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified
accordingly.

       SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision or portion of this Act are
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected, shall remain
valid and subsisting.

      SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
