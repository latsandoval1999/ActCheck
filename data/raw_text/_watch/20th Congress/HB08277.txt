                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to recognize the importance of the Municipality’s founding anniversary by
declaring January 25 of every year a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of
Carigara, Province of Leyte.

Carigara, Leyte is among the oldest towns in the province, distinguished by its pivotal historical
role in the cultural and economic development of the region. Through the years, the
municipality has continued to progress while preserving its deep-rooted traditions and identity.

For several years, the founding anniversary of Carigara has been recognized and celebrated by
its people. In previous occasions, the Office of the President has issued proclamations declaring
the anniversary a special holiday for specific years, underscoring the significance of the event
to the local community.

Hence, this bill seeks to institutionalize the observance of Carigara’s founding anniversary as
a permanent special non-working holiday, thereby granting the municipality the opportunity to
commemorate its rich history, strengthen the community’s camaraderie, and promote tourism
and local economic activity.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING JANUARY 25 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-
WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARIGARA, PROVINCE OF
LEYTE, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. January 25 of every year is hereby declared as a special non-working
holiday in the Municipality of Carigara, Province of Leyte.

SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
