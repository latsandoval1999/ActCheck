                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Presidential Decree (“PD”) No. 1570 (1987), or the Philippine Aeronautical
Engineering Decree of 1978, governs the licensure and regulation of aeronautical
engineers in the country. However, several of its provisions, particularly those relating
to licensure examination frequency, subject structure, and passing standard, have
remained unchanged for decades despite significant developments in the aviation
industry and the evolving regulatory practices of the Professional Regulation
Commission (PRC). As a result, the current framework has created inconsistencies with
other engineering professions and may constrain the timely entry and preparation of
qualified aeronautical engineers.1

       Recent research examining the implementation of PD No. 1570 highlights these
structural gaps, noting that the once-a-year examination schedule, relatively rigid subject
coverage, and higher passing threshold compared to other engineering disciplines pose
challenges for examinees and may no longer be fully responsive to present industry
conditions.2 These concerns are particularly relevant as the Philippines continues to
position itself as a competitive player in the global aviation industry, including through
developments such as the Clark Aviation Hub and the expansion of Maintenance, Repair,
and Overhaul (MRO) operations.

       This bill seeks to address these concerns by introducing targeted amendments to
Sections 15, 16, and 17 of PD No. 1570. It provides for at least twice-yearly licensure
examinations and the adoption of modern examination modalities, and establishes a
competency-based and clustered examination framework while adjusting the passing
standard to a 70% general weighted average. These reforms are designed to improve
accessibility, align licensure standards with current industry practices, and promote a
more responsive and equitable system for aspiring aeronautical engineers.

AN ACT AMENDING SECTIONS 15, 16, AND 17 OF PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO.
     1570, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE PHILIPPINE AERONAUTICAL
     ENGINEERING DECREE OF 1978, TO MODERNIZE LICENSURE
     EXAMINATION STANDARDS, INCREASE EXAMINATION FREQUENCY,
     PROMOTE COMPETENCY-BASED AND CLUSTERED TESTING, AND
     ENCOURAGE CURRICULUM MODERNIZATION IN RESPONSE TO
     INDUSTRY NEEDS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Aeronautical Engineering
Licensure Modernization Act.”

      SEC. 2. Section 15 of Presidential Decree No. 1570 is hereby amended to read as
follows:

       “SECTION 15. Schedule of Examinations. – Written examinations of
       candidates desiring to practice aeronautical engineering in the Philippines
       shall be given by the Board [once a year] AT LEAST TWICE A YEAR at [a]
       dateS and placeS to be fixed by the Commission.

       THE COMMISSION MAY ADOPT COMPUTER-BASED LICENSURE
       EXAMINATIONS AND OTHER APPROPRIATE MODALITIES TO
       IMPROVE THE EFFICIENCY, ACCESSIBILITY, AND INTEGRITY OF
       THE EXAMINATION PROCESS.”

      SEC. 3. Section 16 of Presidential Decree No. 1570 is hereby amended to read as
follows:

       “SECTION 16. Scope of Examinations. – The examinations provided in this
       Decree shall consist of written OR COMPUTER-BASED testS which shall
       cover the following subjects: Mathematics, Aerodynamics; Aircraft
       Structures and Design; Powerplant Theory and Practice; and Air Laws and
       Regulations, and such other related subjects as the Board may deem
       necessary;
       THE BOARD, SUBJECT TO THE APPROVAL OF THE COMMISSION,
       SHALL ADOPT A CLUSTERED AND COMPETENCY-BASED
       APPROACH IN DETERMINING THE SCOPE AND STRUCTURE OF
       THE EXAMINATIONS, AND MAY REVISE, MERGE, OR REORGANIZE
       SUBJECTS AND THEIR CORRESPONDING RELATIVE WEIGHTS TO
       ENSURE ALIGNMENT WITH INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS,
       TECHNOLOGICAL      ADVANCEMENTS,     AND      INDUSTRY
       REQUIREMENTS, INCLUDING THOSE ARISING FROM AVIATION
       HUBS AND MAINTENANCE, REPAIR, AND OVERHAUL (MRO)
       OPERATIONS.

       [Provided, That in case of increase in the number of subjects, the
       corresponding relative weight credited for every subject shall be reduced
       accordingly and the relative weights that may be deducted therefrom shall
       be credited to the additional subject or subjects; Provided, further, That the
       relative weights that will be deducted shall always be divisible exactly by
       five.]

       THE BOARD SHALL PRESCRIBE THE RELATIVE WEIGHTS OF
       SUBJECTS OR COMPETENCY AREAS IN A MANNER THAT ENSURES
       FAIRNESS, RELEVANCE, AND PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCE.”

      SEC. 4. Section 17 of Presidential Decree No. 1570 is hereby amended to read as
follows:

       “SECTION 17. Passing Average. – In order that a candidate may be deemed
       to have passed the examinations successfully, he OR SHE must have
       obtained a general weighted average of seventy[-five] (70%) percent in all
       subjects, with no rating below fifty percent in any subject.

       SUBJECT TO APPROVAL OF THE COMMISSION, THE BOARD SHALL
       HAVE THE POWER TO PRESCRIBE, AMEND, OR REVISE THE
       SUBJECTS, COMPETENCY AREAS, AND THEIR CORRESPONDING
       RELATIVE WEIGHTS, PROVIDED THAT SUCH STANDARDS SHALL
       ENSURE THE COMPETENCE OF PASSERS AND THE PROTECTION
       OF PUBLIC SAFETY.”

       SEC. 5. Transitory Provision. – Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this
Act, the Professional Regulation Commission and the Board of Aeronautical Engineering
shall revise and align existing examination policies, schedules, and structures in
accordance with this Act.

       SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Professional Regulation
Commission, in coordination with the Commission on Higher Education and relevant
industry stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations within
ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.
       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All other laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.

       SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
