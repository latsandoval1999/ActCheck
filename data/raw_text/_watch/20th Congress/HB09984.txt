                               EXPLANATORY NOTE
Article II, Section 11 of the Constitution declares that the State values the dignity of
every human person and guarantees full respect for human rights. This includes the right
of workers to social security and a dignified retirement.

Under Republic Act No. 8291, otherwise known as the “Government Service Insurance
System Act of 1997,” a government employee must complete at least fifteen (15) years of
creditable government service to qualify for retirement pension benefits. Consequently,
many public servants who have devoted years of their lives to government service but are
separated from employment before reaching the minimum service requirement are
permanently disqualified from receiving a monthly pension.

This situation often affects employees who leave government service due to
circumstances beyond their control, such as agency reorganization, abolition of positions,
medical incapacity, disability, family emergencies, or other compelling circumstances.
While these individuals may have rendered substantial service to the government, the law
currently provides no mechanism by which they may complete the remaining service
requirement necessary to qualify for retirement pension benefits.

The absence of such a mechanism may result in severe financial hardship, particularly
among former government employees who spent a significant portion of their working
lives in public service and reasonably expected to qualify for retirement protection.

This measure seeks to address this gap by establishing a GSIS Service Credit Completion
Program. The Program allows qualified separated members to obtain the remaining
service credits necessary to satisfy the minimum retirement eligibility requirement
through the payment of the full actuarial cost as determined by the Government Service
Insurance System. The Program is designed to be fiscally responsible and self-sustaining,
ensuring that no additional burden is imposed upon the GSIS Social Insurance Fund or
the National Government.

The bill likewise recognizes that members who have completed at least fourteen (14)
years of creditable service have substantially fulfilled the statutory requirement and
should be afforded a reasonable opportunity to complete the remaining service credits. At
the same time, the measure extends relief to members with at least ten (10) years of
service whose separation resulted from circumstances beyond their control, subject to
appropriate safeguards.
This measure promotes fairness, strengthens retirement security, and honors the
contributions of government employees while preserving the actuarial integrity of the
GSIS.

In light of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A GSIS SERVICE CREDIT COMPLETION
PROGRAM FOR QUALIFIED SEPARATED MEMBERS OF THE
GOVERNMENT SERVICE INSURANCE SYSTEM, AMENDING FOR THE
PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8291, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
“GOVERNMENT SERVICE INSURANCE SYSTEM ACT OF 1997”
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "GSIS Pension Equity Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote social
justice and retirement security for government employees and to provide equitable
opportunities for qualified separated members of the Government Service Insurance
System to complete the minimum service requirement for retirement pension eligibility
through a fiscally responsible and actuarially sound mechanism.

SECTION 3. GSIS Service Credit Completion Program. – A new subsection is hereby
inserted under Section 13-A of Republic Act No. 8291, to read as follows:

       “(c) GSIS Service Credit Completion Program. – Qualified separated members
       may obtain the service credits necessary to satisfy the minimum service
       requirement for retirement pension eligibility, subject to the conditions provided
       under this subsection.

              (1) Automatic Eligibility. – A member who has completed at least fourteen
              (14) years but less than fifteen (15) years of creditable government service
              at the time of separation shall be eligible to participate in the Program.

              (2) Conditional Eligibility. – A member who has completed at least ten
              (10) years but less than fourteen (14) years of creditable government
              service may likewise participate in the Program, provided that the
              separation from service resulted from circumstances beyond the member’s
              control, including but not limited to:

                      (a) abolition of office or position;

                      (b) reorganization of the agency;

                      (c) disability or medical incapacity;
                      (d) involuntary separation authorized by law; or

                      (e) other analogous circumstances as may be determined by the
                      GSIS.

              (3) Exclusions. – The Program shall not apply to members separated from
              service by reason of dismissal for cause, serious misconduct, gross neglect
              of duty, dishonesty, conduct prejudicial to the best interest of the service,
              conviction of a crime involving moral turpitude, or any other ground
              resulting in forfeiture of retirement benefits under existing laws.

              (4) Actuarial Cost. – The applicant shall pay the full actuarial cost of the
              corresponding service credits as determined by the GSIS.

              (5) Method of Payment. – Payment may be made in lump sum or through
              such installment arrangements as may be authorized by the GSIS.

              (6) Effect of Full Payment. – Upon full payment of the required actuarial
              cost, the member shall be deemed to have completed the minimum service
              requirement solely for purposes of determining eligibility for retirement
              pension benefits under this Act.

              (7) Coverage. – The Program shall be available to qualified members who
              were separated from service before or after the effectivity of this Act.

              (8) Additional Safeguards. – The GSIS may prescribe reasonable
              qualifications, documentary requirements, limitations, and safeguards
              necessary to implement this subsection and prevent abuse thereof.”

SECTION 4. Actuarial Safeguards. – The GSIS shall ensure that all amounts collected
under the Service Credit Completion Program are actuarially sufficient to cover the
corresponding retirement obligations arising from the grant of service credits under this
Act. Nothing in this Act shall be construed as requiring the GSIS to approve any
computation or application that would impair the actuarial soundness or solvency of the
GSIS Social Insurance Fund.

SECTION 5. Non-Diminution of Existing Benefits. – Nothing in this Act shall be
construed to reduce, impair, or diminish any retirement, separation, or other benefits
otherwise available under existing laws.




SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the GSIS shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to
implement the provisions of this Act, including the actuarial adjustments necessary to
maintain the solvency of the GSIS Social Insurance Fund.

SECTION 7. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, or administrative circulars
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved.
