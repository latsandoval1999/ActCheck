                             AN ACT
   GRANTING A MONTHLY SOCIAL PENSION TO INDIGENT PERSONS WITH
           DISABILITIES AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

Persons with disabilities (PWDs) remain among the most vulnerable sectors in Philippine
society. Despite existing laws that promote their rights and welfare, many PWDs.
particularly those who are indigent, continue to face significant economic and social
challenges. Limited employment opportunities, mobility constraints, health-related
expenses, and barriers to accessing public services often prevent them from attaining
financial independence and adequate living conditions.

While the government currently provides a social pension for indigent senior citizens,
there remains no similar nationwide support mechanism specifically designed for
indigent persons with disabilities who are unable to sustain themselves due to physical,
sensory, intellectual, or psychosocial limitations. Many of these individuals rely heavily
on family members or community support, which is often insufficient to meet their daily
subsistence and medical needs.

This bill seeks to establish a monthly social pension for indigent persons with disabilities
to help alleviate the financial burden associated with their condition and to provide a
measure of economic security. The proposed assistance aims to support their basic needs,
promote dignity and social inclusion, and strengthen the country’s social protection
system for vulnerable populations.

By extending financial support to indigent PWDs, the State affirms its commitment to
uphold the principles embodied in Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the Magna
Carta for Persons with Disabilities, and to ensure that no Filipino is left behind in the
pursuit of inclusive development and social justice.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
                                       assembled:

Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Indigent Persons with Disabilities
Social Pension Act."

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote
the rights, welfare, and social protection of persons with disabilities (PWDs). The State
shall ensure that indigent persons with disabilities are provided with adequate assistance
and social support to help them meet their basic needs and live with dignity.

Section 3. Definition of Terms. – This Act aims to:

   (a) Person with Disability (PWD) refers to an individual suffering from restriction
       or different abilities as a result of mental, physical, or sensory impairment,
       consistent with the definition provided under Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise
       known as the Magna Carta for Persons with Disabilities, as amended.
   (b) Indigent Person with Disability refers to a PWD who has no regular source of
       income or whose income is insufficient for subsistence, as determined through the
       assessment guidelines of the Department of Social Welfare and Development
       (DSWD).

Section 4. Grant of Monthly Social Pension – Indigent persons with disabilities shall
receive a monthly social pension of not less than One Thousand Pesos (₱1,000.00) to help
defray daily living and medical expenses.
The amount may be periodically reviewed and adjusted by the Department of Social
Welfare and Development, subject to the approval of Congress and the availability of
funds.

Section 5. Coverage and Eligibility. – To qualify for the pension under this Act, a
beneficiary must:
   (a) Be a Filipino citizen;
   (b) Be a registered person with disability under existing laws and regulations;
   (c) Be classified as indigent by the DSWD or the appropriate local social welfare office;
       and
   (d) Not be receiving any other government pension, except disability-related benefits from
       social insurance programs.

Priority shall be given to PWDs who are unemployed, living alone, or without family support.

Section 6. Implementing Agency. – The Department of Social Welfare and Development
(DSWD) shall be the lead implementing agency of this Act and shall:
   (a) Identify and validate eligible beneficiaries;
   (b) Establish a national registry of indigent PWD pension beneficiaries;
   (c) Coordinate with local government units and the National Council on Disability Affairs
       (NCDA); and
   (d) Ensure efficient and transparent distribution of the pension.

Section 7. Role of Local Government Units. –Local government units shall assist in the
identification, registration, and monitoring of qualified beneficiaries and may provide
additional support services consistent with their local social protection programs.

Section 8. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to implement this Act shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act under the budget of the Department
of Social Welfare and Development.

Section 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DSWD, in coordination with the National Council on Disability
Affairs and other relevant agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations
for its implementation.

Section 10. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act
shall be declared as unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not
affected shall remain in full force and effect.

Section 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders and rules
and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

Section 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
