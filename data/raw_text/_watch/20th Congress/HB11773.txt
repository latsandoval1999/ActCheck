                          AN ACT
    PROVIDING FREE LEGAL ASSISTANCE TO ANY OFFICERS OR
ENLISTED PERSONNEL OF THE ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES
 AND THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE ON ANY CHARGE BEFORE
  THE PROSECUTOR’S OFFICE, COURT OR ANY COMPETENT BODY
   ARISING FROM AN INCIDENT OR INCIDENTS RELATED TO THE
               PERFORMANCE OF OFFICIAL DUTY

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The members of our Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) and
Philippine National Police (PNP), have the highest duty of safeguarding the
people’s freedom, preserving lives and properties, and protecting the
constitutional rights of every citizen.

      In the recent years, many uniformed officers have been involved in
various administrative and criminal cases in their line of duty. In 2019, the
PNP reported a total of more than six thousand cases against uniformed
personnel.

      As defenders of people’s freedom, it is important that, while performing
their duties, our military and police are also protected in their own rights.
When and if faced with cases that require legal representation, they should
also be allowed access to legal assistance from the government.

        By ensuring that our uniformed personnel can afford the cost of
litigation, the State also protects the very sector that protects the citizens and
is adequately safeguarded.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Free Legal
Assistance to AFP and PNP Act.”

SECTION 2. Free Legal Assistance to AFP and PNP. – Any officer or enlisted
personnel of the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) and the Philippine
National Police (PNP) who is facing, before the prosecutor’s office, court,
administrative body, or any competent body, any charge arising from an
incident or incidents related to the performance of official duty shall be
entitled to free legal assistance.

SECTION 3. Authorization. – Within twenty-four (24) hours from the official
receipt of the information that the officer or enlisted personnel is charged, the
Chief of Staff of the AFP, the Chairperson of the National Police Commission
(NAPOLCOM) or the Chief of the PNP shall authorize the legal office of their
respective agencies, with the assistance of the Public Attorneys Office (PAO),
to provide free legal assistance to any officer or enlisted personnel of the AFP
and the PNP for the purpose of the immediately preceding section.

SECTION 4. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act shall be sourced from the Legal Defense Fund
funded through the General Appropriations Act.
SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Public Attorney’s Office, in coordination
with AFP, NAPOLCOM and the PNP, shall issue the necessary rules and
regulations to implement this Act.

SECTION 6. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is held invalid
or unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity and effectivity of the
other provisions hereof.

SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, and issuances, or
portions thereof, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 8. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
