                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

The family is recognized under the Constitution as the foundation of the nation. As
such, the State has the duty to protect and strengthen the family as a basic social
institution, especially in matters involving children, domestic relations, adoption,
violence against women and children, custody, support, and other cases affecting the
welfare of Filipino families.

In the Province of Sorsogon, residents from the Municipality of Gubat and neighboring
towns are constrained to travel considerable distances in order to access Family
Courts located in other areas of the judicial region. This situation poses financial and
logistical burdens, particularly on women, children, and senior citizens – litigants who
are the usual parties in family-related cases.

The current number of Family Courts serving the area remains insufficient to
adequately address the growing volume of family-related cases in the Province of
Sorsogon. This situation contributes to court congestion, delays in the disposition of
cases, and difficulties for litigants seeking timely judicial relief. Establishing a Family
Court in the municipality will help decongest existing court dockets, improve access to
justice, and ensure the timely resolution of sensitive family-related matters.

Therefore, this measure seeks to create a new branch of the Family Court to be
stationed in the Municipality of Gubat, Province of Sorsogon, pursuant to Republic Act
No. 8369, otherwise known as the “Family Courts Act of 1997.” Through this measure,
the State reaffirms its commitment to protecting the welfare of Filipino families and
ensuring that justice remains accessible, responsive, and compassionate to the needs
of the people.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT CREATING ONE (1) ADDITIONAL BRANCH OF FAMILY COURT IN THE
FIFTH JUDICIAL REGION TO BE STATIONED IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GUBAT,
   PROVINCE OF SORSOGON, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:


SEC. 1. One (1) additional branch of Family Court in the Fifth Judicial Region is hereby
created and stationed in the Municipality of Gubat, Province of Sorsogon, in
accordance with the provisions of Republic Act No. 8369, otherwise known as the
“Family Courts Act of 1997”.
The Supreme Court shall assign a branch number to the newly created branch of
Family Court.
SEC. 2. The Chief Justice of the Supreme Court, in coordination with the Secretary of
the Department of Justice, shall immediately include in the Court’s program the
operationalization of the additional branch of Family Court in the Fifth Judicial Region
stationed in the Municipality of Gubat, Province of Sorsogon.
SEC. 3. The funds necessary for the implementation of this Act, including
appropriations for personnel services such as salaries, benefits, and emoluments for
judges and court personnel, court operations under maintenance and other operating
expenses (MOOE), and provisions for Halls of Justice or courtrooms to accommodate
judges and court staff, shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
SEC. 4. Sections 14(k) of Batas Pambansa Blg. 129, otherwise known as “The
Judiciary Reorganization Act of 1980”, as amended, and all other laws, rules, and
regulations which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby amended, repealed, or
modified accordingly.
SEC. 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
