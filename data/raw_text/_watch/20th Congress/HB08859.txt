                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE


Article 14, Section 19, Paragraph (1) of the 1987 Constitution provides:

       “SECTION 19. (1) The State shall promote physical education and
       encourage sports programs, league competitions, and amateur sports,
       including training for international competitions, to foster self-discipline,
       teamwork, and excellence for the development of a healthy and alert
       citizenry.”

Filipino athletes have consistently demonstrated remarkable talent and resilience on the global
stage, earning distinction in prestigious competitions such as the Asian Games, Paralympic
Games, World Championships, and the Olympics. Their victories and achievements serve as
a source of national pride and unity, inspiring future generations to pursue sports and uphold
the values of determination, perseverance, and dedication.

Republic Act No. 10699 was passed to broaden the system of incentives provided to national
athletes and their coaches in recognition of their invaluable role in promoting Philippine sports.
However, gaps remain in the current framework, particularly in the unequal treatment of para-
athletes, whose incentives are not commensurate with those received by their counterparts in
other international competitions. This disparity undermines the principle of fairness and fails
to fully acknowledge the accomplishments of athletes with disabilities.

In view of this, the proposed measure aims to rectify these inequities by enhancing and
standardizing the incentives granted to para-athletes, ensuring that they are on equal footing
with other athletes competing at the same level. It also seeks to advance inclusivity and equal
recognition across all sporting disciplines. Furthermore, the measure proposes to exempt from
taxation all forms of incentives, rewards, bonuses, and similar benefits received by athletes,
thereby ensuring that they fully enjoy the fruits of their hard-earned success.

In view of the foregoing, immediate approval of this measure is earnestly requested.

SEC 3. A new Section 10-A is hereby inserted after Section 10 of Republic Act No. 10699 to
read as follows:

        “SEC. 10-A. TAX EXEMPTION AND DEDUCTION. – INCENTIVES,
        BONUSES, REWARDS, AND OTHER        FORMS OF EMOLUMENTS,
              GIFTS, ENDOWMENTS, AND CONTRIBUTIONS GRANTED,
        WHETHER BY PUBLIC OR PRIVATE ENTITIES OR INDIVIDUALS, TO
        NATIONAL ATHLETES WHO WIN IN INTERNATIONAL SPORTS
        COMPETITIONS AND THEIR COACHES, SHALL BE EXEMPT FROM
        ALL NATIONAL AND LOCAL TAXES, FEES, AND CHARGES.

        DONATIONS, GIFTS, ENDOWMENTS, AND CONTRIBUTIONS FROM
        PRIVATE ENTITIES AND INDIVIDUALS MADE IN FAVOR OF SUCH
        ATHLETES AND COACHES SHALL BE FULLY DEDUCTIBLE FROM THE
        DONOR’S GROSS INCOME AND EXEMPT FROM THE PAYMENT OF
        DONOR’S TAX.

        THE TAX EXEMPTION OR DEDUCTION GRANTED UNDER THIS ACT
        SHALL BE SUBJECT TO THE RULES AND REGULATIONS ISSUED BY
        THE SECRETARY OF FINANCE, UPON THE RECOMMENDATION OF
        THE COMMISSIONER OF INTERNAL REVENUE.

        THE AVAILMENT OF SUCH EXEMPTION OR REDUCTION SHALL BE A
        BAR TO ANY CLAIM OF SIMILAR TAX RELIEFS UNDER OTHER
        GENERAL OR SPECIAL LAWS.

SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within thirty (30) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC), in consultation with the Philippine Olympic
Committee (POC), the National Paralympic Committee of the Philippines (NPC PHIL), and
other concerned agencies, shall promulgate the rules and regulations to effectively implement
the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional, the
remainder hereof not otherwise affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. - All laws, presidential decrees,executive orders, letters of
instruction, proclamations, administrative orders, rules, or regulations that are inconsistent
with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
