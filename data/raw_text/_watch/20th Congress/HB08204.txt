                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to institutionalize the provision of a one-time maternity kit to
pregnant Filipino women belonging to low-income and near-poor households in
order to improve maternal and newborn health outcomes and ease the financial
burden associated with childbirth.

       Despite existing maternal and child health programs, many indigent
expectant mothers continue to face barriers in accessing adequate prenatal, delivery,
and postnatal services. Out-of-pocket expenses for essential newborn items, such as
blankets, diapers, or other infant care products, add to the economic strain of already
vulnerable families. For households living below or near the poverty threshold, these
costs compete with daily subsistence needs.

       The proposed measure establishes the Maternity Kit for Pregnant Filipino
Women Program, to be implemented by the Department of Social Welfare and
Development (DSWD). The program will provide a maternity kit, free of charge and
distributed once, to qualified beneficiaries identified as poor or near-poor under the
Standardized Targeting System pursuant to Republic Act No. 11310, or the “Pantawid
Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) Act”.

      This vital piece of legislation also encourages facility-based deliveries,
promotes safer pregnancies, and supports early newborn care. It aims to improve
prenatal care utilization, reduce child mortality among low-income families, and
empower mothers to perform their duties more meaningfully.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
MANDATING THE PROVISION OF MATERNITY PACKAGES TO PREGNANT
FILIPINO WOMEN BELONGING TO THE LOW-INCOME GROUP,
PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Maternity Kit for
Pregnant Filipino Women Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State recognizes the role of women in
nation-building. Further, the State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive
approach to health development which shall endeavor to make essential goods,
health and other social services available to all the people at affordable cost. There
shall be priority for the needs of the underprivileged sick, elderly, disabled, women,
and children. Finally, the State shall endeavor to provide medical care to paupers.

       SEC. 3. Maternity Kit for Pregnant Filipino Women Program. — This Act hereby
establishes the Maternity Kit for Pregnant Filipino Women Program which may be
availed of by expectant mothers who have already been identified as poor or
near-poor based on the Standardized Targeting System under Republic Act No.
11310, otherwise known as the “Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) Act”. In
order to qualify, pregnant women must avail of pre-natal services, give birth in a
health facility attended by a skilled health professional, and receive post-partum care
and post-natal care for her newborn.

       The maternity package or kit, to be distributed once free of charge to qualified
beneficiaries, shall contain necessary items such as a blanket, diapers, gauze, towels,
nappies, bedding, cloth or fabric which could be used to make clothing for the baby,
and child care products. The contents of the package shall be updated by the
implementing agency every two (2) years.

       SEC. 4. Objectives of the Program. — The Program shall have the following
objectives:

                                          1
          a.​ Improve prenatal care for poor or near-poor pregnant Filipino women:

          b.​ Reduce child mortality, especially for those belonging to families in the
       low-income group; and

          c.​ Empower women to perform their role, more meaningfully, in
       nation-building.

      SEC. 5. Implementing Agency. — The Department of Social Welfare and
Development (DSWD) shall serve as the central planning, implementing and
monitoring body of the Program.

      SEC. 6. Appropriations. — The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of
this Act shall be charged against those authorized in the current and subsequent
General Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days after the
approval of this Act, the DSWD shall issue the necessary rules and regulations for
the effective implementation hereof.

       SEC. 8. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
