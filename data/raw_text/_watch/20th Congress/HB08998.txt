                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to strengthen the Anti-Money Laundering Council (AMLC) by
amending Republic Act No. 9160 to protect and preserve the integrity of the financial
system and ensure that the Philippines is not used as a site for laundering the
proceeds from unlawful activities. It provides AMLC with operational independence to
function as a Financial Intelligence Unit, specialized in anti-money laundering and
counter-terrorism financing investigation, and acts as the anti-money laundering and
counter-terrorism regulator and supervisor of the Philippines.

       The bill addresses evolving crimes, which the current legal framework has not
fully covered, such as cybercrimes, agricultural economic sabotage, and violations of
the anti-POGO Act of 2025, among others. The bill likewise addresses the challenges
of overseeing digital finance by bringing Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) and
virtual assets, such as cryptocurrencies used for payment or investment, under the
AMLC to prevent their misuse in financial crimes. It enhances deterrence by increasing
fines for law violations and includes a mechanism to raise them by 10% every five
years to account for inflation. Additionally, the bill introduces stricter customer due
diligence requirements to ensure a more resilient financial system. It grants AMLC the
ability to issue administrative freeze orders, allowing forfeiture of assets of “equivalent
value” when the original unlawfully acquired property has been concealed, destroyed,
or moved outside the country, and ensuring that offenders do not benefit from their
crimes by simply hiding the proceeds. Finally, the proposed bill ensures that the
Philippines can continue to extend cooperation in transnational investigations and
implement financial sanctions related to financing terrorism and proliferation of
weapons of mass destruction, consistent with the United Nations Security Council
resolutions.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
