                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Nurses play a vital role in the delivery of healthcare services and are
indispensable to the promotion, protection, and restoration of the health of the
Filipino people. As frontline healthcare professionals, they perform critical
functions in hospitals, health centers, schools, communities, and other
healthcare settings, often under demanding and high-risk conditions.

       Despite their invaluable contribution to the healthcare system, many
Filipino nurses continue to face inadequate compensation, prompting many to
seek employment opportunities abroad or leave the profession altogether. This
continuing exodus has contributed to shortages in the nursing workforce,
placing additional strain on the country's healthcare system and affecting the
quality and accessibility of healthcare services.

      While Republic Act No. 9173, otherwise known as the "Philippine Nursing
Act of 2002," and subsequent issuances improved the compensation of
government nurses, there remains a need to further enhance their salaries in
recognition of their professional qualifications, responsibilities, and
indispensable role in safeguarding public health.

       This bill seeks to upgrade the minimum salary grade level of nurses in
government service from Salary Grade 15 to Salary Grade 19. By providing more
competitive compensation, the measure aims to strengthen the recruitment and
retention of nurses in the public sector, improve morale and productivity, and
ensure a more stable and responsive healthcare workforce for the benefit of all
Filipinos.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                             “AN ACT
     UPGRADING THE MINIMUM SALARY GRADE LEVEL OF NURSES
     FROM SALARY GRADE 15 TO 19, AMENDING SEC. 32 OF
     REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9173, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "THE
     PHILIPPINE NURSING ACT OF 2002"

        Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
 Philippines in Congress assembled:

    SECTION 1. Section 32 of Republic Act No. 9173 is hereby
 amended to read as follows:

     "SEC. 32. Salary. – In order to enhance the general welfare,
     commitment to service and professionalism of nurses, the
     minimum base pay of nurses working in the public health
     institutions shall not be lower than SALARY GRADE
     NINETEEN (19) prescribed under Republic Act No. 6758,
     otherwise known as the "Compensation and Classification Act
     of 1989": Provided, That for nurses working in local
     government units, adjustments to their salaries shall be in
     accordance with Section 10 of the said law."

      SECTION 2. Appropriations. – The amounts necessary for the
 implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year
 available appropriations of the Department of Health (DOH) and other
 concerned agencies. Thereafter, the amount necessary for its
 continuous implementation shall be included in the annual general
 appropriations.

     SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within
 ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of
 Health (DOH), in coordination with the Department of Budget and
 Management (DBM), the Civil Service Commission (CSC), and other
 concerned agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

    SECTION 4. Repealing Clause. – Al provisions of laws, orders,
decrees, including rules and regulations inconsistent herewith are
hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

     SECTION 5. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this
Act shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, other provisions hereof
which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

    SECTION 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of
general circulation.

    Approved
