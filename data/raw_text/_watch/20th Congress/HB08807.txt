                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to strengthen and institutionalize the implementation of
Republic Act (RA) No. 11337, otherwise known as the “Innovative Startup Act,” by
refining key definitions, streamlining processes, enhancing incentives, and
establishing a centralized mechanism to accelerate the growth of the Philippine
startup ecosystem.

       While the country has seen steady growth in startup activity over the past
decade, its global competitiveness remains constrained by legal ambiguities,
fragmented implementation, and regulatory bottlenecks. The current definition of
“startup” is overly broad and does not sufficiently distinguish innovation-driven,
high-growth enterprises from traditional micro, small, and medium enterprises. As a
result, government resources intended for high-impact ventures are diluted, and
policy focus becomes diffused.

       The bill refines the definition of a startup to focus on entities that develop
innovative solutions addressing systemic or complex societal, technological, or
market problems, with clear potential for scalability. It also expands recognition to
include informal groups and pre-registration teams, acknowledging the realities of
early-stage innovation and allowing more founders to access government support.

       The measure establishes a centralized Secretariat under the Department of
Trade and Industry to harmonize inter-agency coordination, standardize procedures,
and serve as a single entry point for startup-related applications. The bill further
mandates simplified documentary requirements for the Startup Grant Fund and
aligns procedures with RA No. 11032, otherwise known as the “Ease of Doing
Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act”. It introduces enhanced tax
incentives, including additional deductions and tax exemptions for grants and
prizes, to mobilize greater private sector participation and unlock much-needed
domestic capital.

      By clarifying policy direction, reducing red tape, institutionalizing
coordination, and expanding incentives, this bill aims to create a more focused, agile,
and globally competitive startup ecosystem that can drive innovation, generate
quality jobs, and contribute meaningfully to national development.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.
