20                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   Voter registration remains largely a manual and paper-based process, despite the
23   availability of technology that could make registration more accessible, efficient, and
24   convenient for the public.
25
26   During the voter registration period that ran from October 20, 2025 to May 18, 2026,
27   the Commission on Elections (COMELEC) processed 5,470,889 applications
28   nationwide, including applications for new registration, transfer of registration,
29   reactivation, and correction of entries.
30
31   This figure alone represents an overwhelming burden not only on our hardworking
32   COMELEC personnel, but also raises a more important concern. Behind these millions
33   of applications may be qualified Filipinos who remain unregistered because the existing
34   manual process is difficult, time-consuming, or otherwise inaccessible to them.
35
36   The existing framework under Republic Act No. 8189, otherwise known as the “Voter’s
37   Registration Act of 1996,” was established at a time when electronic registration, digital
38   records, and secure online transactions were not yet part of ordinary government
39   services. The law therefore needs to be updated to respond to the realities of modern
40   voter registration.
41
42   Hence, this proposed measure seeks to modernize the voter registration system without
43   compromising the integrity and accuracy of the voters’ list.
44
45   First, the bill allows online voter registration through the official website of the
46   COMELEC as another option based the manual one. Second, the bill addresses a
47   practical difficulty faced by voters who may be qualified to register but lack conventional
48   documents proving their residence. It now allows alternative forms of proof of residence,
 1   including utility bills, certifications from landlords or lessors, and affidavits of qualified
 2   disinterested witnesses. These are more practical, yet the bill also proposes safeguards
 3   against fraudulent registration.
 4
 5   Further, the bill strengthens coordination between the COMELEC and the Philippine
 6   Statistics Authority (PSA) for the identification and cancellation of the registration
 7   records of deceased voters. This is intended to help prevent outdated records from
 8   remaining in the voters’ list.
 9
10   This proposed bill recognizes that voter registration should not be made unnecessarily
11   difficult by outdated procedures, nor should modernization come at the expense of
12   electoral integrity. The objective is to modernize the voter registration system and make
13   the exercise of the right to vote a little less burdensome to qualified Filipinos, so that
14   more citizens are encouraged and able to register and participate in elections.
15
16   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
