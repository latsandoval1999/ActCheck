                                  Explanatory Note

The Land Transportation Office (LTO), under the Department of Transportation, plays
a vital role in promoting road safety and providing essential driver’s licensing services
to the public.

Caloocan City, one of the major urban centers in Metro Manila, has a substantial and
growing population, resulting in an increasing demand for driver’s licensing services.
Records of the LTO Kalookan Licensing Extension Office show that from January to
October 2026, a total of forty-seven thousand eight hundred forty-four (47,844)
transactions were processed, with October recording the highest monthly volume of
six thousand three hundred eighty-nine (6,389) transactions.

As the number of motorists and vehicle owners continues to increase, the need for
more accessible and efficient government licensing facilities becomes increasingly
evident. While the existing LTO Licensing Extension Office in Caloocan City serves an
important purpose, its conversion into a regular LTO Licensing Center would
strengthen its operational capacity, expand the availability of authorized licensing
services, and enable it to better respond to the growing demand for driver’s licensing
and related transactions within its service area. Such conversion would also facilitate
the establishment of a more comprehensive operational structure, supported by
adequate personnel, appropriate facilities, modern equipment, information technology
infrastructure, and logistical resources.

Converting the existing Licensing Extension Office into a regular LTO Licensing Center
is a practical and responsive measure to improve the accessibility, efficiency, and
reliability of government services in Caloocan City and neighboring cities and
municipalities. It would help accommodate the growing volume of licensing
transactions, reduce the need for residents to travel to other LTO offices for services
unavailable at the extension office, and ease the burden of travel time and
transportation costs on motorists and applicants. It would likewise promote more
convenient transactions, better service delivery, and greater public satisfaction.

Ultimately, the proposed conversion seeks to bring government services closer to the
people, strengthen the delivery of driver’s licensing services, and promote greater
efficiency in public administration. It reflects the State’s continuing commitment to
responsive governance and the provision of accessible, timely, and quality public
services.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
CONVERTING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) KALOOKAN
LICENSING EXTENSION OFFICE (KLEO) INTO A REGULAR LTO LICENSING
CENTER AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. The          Licensing    Extension   Office    of    the   Land
Transportation Office (LTO) located in Caloocan City is hereby converted into a
regular l icensing center to be known as the LTO C a l o o c a n C i t y Licensing
Center.

        SEC. 2. The Secretary of Transportation shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of LTO C a l o o c a n C i t y Licensing
Center, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current year’s
appropriation of the LTO Kalookan Licensing Extension Office. Thereafter, such
sum as may be necessary for the continued operation of the LTO C a l o o c a n
C i t y Licensing Center shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
