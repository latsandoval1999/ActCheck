      Section 15 of Article II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution declares that
the State shall protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill
health consciousness among them. In fulfillment of this mandate, Republic Act

       Philippine studies emphasize that effective health research systems require
not only research production but also institutional capacity for policy
engagement, communication, networking, and knowledge translation. Research
on organizational capacities in the Philippine health policy and systems research
ecosystem highlights the importance of stronger institutional leadership, financial
sustainability, policy orientation, and collaboration, while studies on the research
workforce point to the importance of mentoring and professional development
for sustaining research capacity.2 These findings are consistent with international
research showing that evidence is less likely to influence health policy when
structured mechanisms for researcher policymaker engagement, knowledge
translation, and institutional support are weak.3

       Recognizing these gaps, this proposed legislation seeks to strengthen and
expand the existing PNHRS through a Health Evidence Assessment and
Research-to-Policy Reporting Framework that will identify high-impact health
research and require concerned government agencies to document how relevant
findings were considered, adopted, further assessed, or not utilized. The measure
also strengthens regional and local research capacity through institutional
development, early-career researcher mentoring, competitive research grants, and
collaborative networks; promotes responsible interoperability and sharing of
health research data consistent with privacy, ethical, and surveillance laws; and
establishes a Health Research Translation and Capacity-Building Fund to support
research utilization, evidence assessment, knowledge translation, dissemination,
and capacity-building.

      The measure seeks to build upon the foundation established by Republic
Act No. 10532 by strengthening the implementation of the Philippine National
Health Research System (PNHRS) through more effective mechanisms for
research-to-policy translation, institutional and regional research capacity-
building, responsible health research data interoperability, and sustainable
support for research utilization. Rather than creating new national institutions or
duplicating existing programs, the measure seeks to address identified policy and
implementation gaps within the existing PNHRS framework and strengthen its

      SEC. 12. Separability Clause.      If any provision of this Act is declared

in full force and effect.

      SEC. 13. Repealing Clause.           All laws, decrees, executive orders,
issuances, rules, regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
