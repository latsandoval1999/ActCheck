                           EXPLANATORY NOTE


       The Philippines is consistently ranked among the most disaster-prone
countries in the world, facing recurring typhoons, floods, earthquakes, volcanic
eruptions, and public health emergencies. These hazards pose serious threats to
the safety of learners, teachers, and education personnel, disrupt the delivery of
education, and result in significant damage to school infrastructure. While
administrative policies on school-based disaster preparedness are currently in
place, there remains no single comprehensive law that institutionalizes and
sustains these measures across all educational institutions.

       This bill seeks to establish a Comprehensive School Disaster Preparedness
and Resilience Framework that consolidates and strengthens existing initiatives
on disaster risk reduction and management in schools. It mandates the creation of
School Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committees, the designation
of trained coordinators, and the regular conduct of disaster preparedness and
emergency drills. It further requires the integration of disaster awareness, climate
change adaptation, and hazard mitigation into the school curriculum, thereby
fostering a culture of preparedness and resilience among learners.

       The proposed measure also emphasizes area-specific planning, safe and
resilient school infrastructure, early warning systems, continuity of education
during emergencies, psychosocial support, and community participation.
Through strengthened coordination among schools, local government units, and
national agencies, this Act ensures alignment with the national disaster risk
reduction and management framework under Republic Act No. 10121.

       By institutionalizing comprehensive school disaster preparedness, this bill
affirms the State’s commitment to safeguarding lives, protecting public
investment in education, and ensuring that no learner is deprived of the right to
quality education due to disasters. The immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.


      In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “University of the
Philippines National Climate Resilience Institute Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It shall be the policy of the State to:

   a. Uphold the right of all persons to life, safety, and human dignity by
      ensuring the protection of lives, livelihoods, infrastructure, cultural
      heritage, and ecosystems before, during, and after disasters;
   b. Recognize climate resilience as an integral component of national
      development, environmental sustainability, and human security, and
      mainstream it into all levels of governance and sectors of society;
   c. Promote a proactive, anticipatory, and risk-informed approach to disaster
      risk reduction and management (DRRM), grounded in scientific evidence,
      multi-hazard assessments, and inclusive stakeholder engagement;
   d. Ensure the establishment of a strong institutional foundation through the
      creation of a permanent national institute with the requisite authority,
      capacity, and mandate to oversee, and coordinate resilience programs and
      policies;
   e. Institutionalize integrated national and local systems for early warning,
      emergency operations, recovery planning, data management, and public
      communication, including the use of digital governance, predictive
      analytics, and innovative technologies;
   f. Facilitate sustained investment in resilience-building measures, including
      but not limited to public budgeting, private sector participation, risk
      financing instruments, and climate-related investment mechanisms;
   g. Encourage innovation, localization, and community empowerment
      through research and development, resilience education, participatory
      governance, and indigenous and local knowledge systems; and
   h. Monitor and evaluate disaster risk governance outcomes through evidence-
      based performance metrics, transparency mechanisms, and a robust system
      of policy feedback, public accountability, and continuous improvement.

SEC. 3. Establishment of the University of the Philippines National Climate
Resilience Institute. – The University of the Philippines Research Institute (“UP
RI”) is hereby converted into a national institute to be known as the UP National
Climate Resilience Institute (“UP NCRI”).

SEC. 4. Mandate of the UP NCRI. – The UP NCRI shall continue the mandate
of the UP RI as a proactive hub of benchmark innovative information vital to the



                                                                                2
nation’s efforts in climate change mitigation and adaptation. It shall empower
local communities through multidisciplinary actions toward resilience.

Among others, it shall:

   i.      Undertake policy research, action research, and interdisciplinary or
           transdisciplinary research or creative work;
   ii.     Disseminate research findings, creative works, and innovations;
   iii.    Establish non-degree educational programs and support degree
           programs;
   iv.     Establish and institutionalize a national system for resilience-oriented
           research, foresight, and knowledge integration to advance science-
           based, anticipatory, and adaptive resilience governance;
   v.      Facilitate collaborative projects with universities, local government
           units (LGU), scientific institutions, civil society organizations, and
           private sector partners, and shall support the translation of research
           into actionable policies and local solutions;
   vi.     Develop and maintain a centralized and interoperable Resilience
           Knowledge Platform to serve as a public repository of resilience data,
           research findings, learning modules, analytics tools, and best
           practices;
   vii.    Ensure that resilience-related research outputs, innovations, and
           knowledge products are converted into practical guidance materials,
           toolkits, planning aids, and operational templates for use by LGUs,
           local communities, and institutions; and
   viii.   Improve the capability of relevant national agencies and offices as an
           agent of change for climate resilience in the Philippines and the
           Pacific Rim region.



                                                                                      3
SEC. 5. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s available appropriations of
UP RI whose functions are herein absorbed, transferred, or realigned to the UP
NCRI.

Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation of
this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the University of the Philippines, in coordination with
the Department of Budget and Management, and other relevant agencies, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation
of this Act.

The Implementing Rules and Regulations shall be developed through a
consultative   process    involving   local   government units,      civil   society
organizations, the private sector, academic institutions, and other stakeholders,
as may be applicable.

SEC. 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.

Approved.
