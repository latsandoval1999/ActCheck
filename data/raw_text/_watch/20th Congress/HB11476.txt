                                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines is a country rich in marine resources. With 1,065 seaweed species
found in our waters, so far, only four are farmed. These are Eucheuma, Kappaphycus,
Gracilaria, and Caulerpa1.

The seaweed industry is one of the major contributors of our economy. In 20242,
seaweed aquaculture accounted for an impressive 66% of overall fishery production
and remains as one of the agriculture sector’s top export commodities. It sustains
thousands of families in coastal communities and contributes significantly to national
employment and aquaculture output.

Beyond food, seaweed has diverse applications such as animal feed, fertilizer,
cosmetic ingredient, and most recently, as raw material for bio-plastics 3 among others.

However, the industry continues to face mounting challenges such as global warming,
degradation, disease outbreaks like “ice-ice”, volatile market prices, and insufficient
post-harvest infrastructure that significantly affect this industry. These challenges are
no longer theoretical. Recently, the Philippine Statistics Authority logged a 3.3%
production decline in seaweed for the second quarter of 2026.4

This bill seeks to strengthen and future-proof our seaweed industry by providing
support in promoting sustainable farming practices to protect marine ecosystems and
ensure long-term productivity, advancing research and technology to improve yield,
disease resistance, and product development, expanding support for seaweed farmers,
and strengthening market competitiveness to secure the sector's position in both
domestic and international trade.

           AN ACT TO PROMOTE THE DEVELOPMENT AND
SUSTAINABILITY OF THE SEAWEED INDUSTRY IN THE PHILIPPINES,
ENHANCING RESEARCH, SUPPORT, AND GLOBAL COMPETITIVENESS
           AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Seaweed Industry
Development and Sustainability Act of 2026”

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
revitalize and promote the sustainable development of the seaweed industry,
recognizing its importance to the national economy and the livelihood of coastal
communities, and to reassert the Philippines’ status as a global leader in seaweed
production and export.

       SEC. 3. Objectives. – The Objectives of this Act are to:

         a) Promote sustainable, environmentally friendly, and socially responsible
            seaweed farming practices;

         b) Support research and development initiatives for the advancement of the
            seaweed industry;

         c) Build a resilient seaweed sector by providing comprehensive support and
            assistance to seaweed farmers and producers;

         d) Enhance the competitiveness of the Philippine seaweed industry in the
            global market.

        SEC. 4. Sustainable Seaweed Farming Practices. – The Department of
Agriculture (DA), in coordination with the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources
(BFAR), shall develop and promote sustainable seaweed farming practices, ensuring
the protection or marine ecosystems and biodiversity.



                                           3
       SEC. 5. Research and Development. – The DA, through the BFAR and in
collaboration with Department of Science and Technology (DOST), shall initiate and
support research and development projects aimed at improving seaweed production
technologies, disease management, and product innovation.

        SEC. 6. Support and Assistance for Seaweed Farmers. – The government
shall provide comprehensive support to seaweed farmers, including access to quality
seedlings, farm implements, and post-harvesting equipment, technical training, and
financial assistance. Special emphasis shall be placed on small-scale farmers and
community-based initiatives.

        SEC. 7. Market Competitiveness and Export Promotion. – The Department of
Trade and Industry (DTI), in collaboration with the DA and BFAR, shall implement
strategies to enhance the market competitiveness of Philippine seaweed, including
branding, marketing, and establishing trade partnerships to expand export
opportunities.

       SEC. 8. Climate Change Adaption and Resilience. – Recognizing the
vulnerability of seaweed farming to climate change, the government shall develop and
implement strategies to increase the resilience of the seaweed industry to
environmental challenges, including rising sea levels and increasing ocean
temperatures.

         SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DA, in consultation with
BFAR, DTI, Department of Interior and Local Government, academe, civil society
organizations, non-government organizations, and other relevant stakeholders, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of
this act within ninety (90) days after its enactment.

         SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall remain
in full force and effect.

       SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential decrees
or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules and regulations
contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

        SEC. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
