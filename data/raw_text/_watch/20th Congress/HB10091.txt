                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish equal criminal liability and penalties for marital
infidelity. It amends Articles 333, 334, and 344 of Act No. 3815, otherwise known as
“The Revised Penal Code”, to remove gender-based distinctions in the treatment of
spouses.

       Marriage is recognized by the Constitution as an inviolable social institution
and the foundation of the family. The legal obligation of fidelity is reciprocal and
must be enforced without regard to the gender of the offending spouse. The existing
provisions on adultery and concubinage impose unequal standards of criminal
responsibility. A wife may be held criminally liable for a single act of sexual relations
outside the marriage, while a husband incurs liability only under specific
circumstances. Criminal laws shall therefore be updated to impose the same
standard of conduct and corresponding penalties upon husbands and wives alike,
thereby upholding the sanctity of marriage.

        The proposed measure reinforces the principles of equality before the law,
fairness in the administration of justice, and mutual accountability within marriage.
It ensures that the State’s protection of the marital institution is carried out through a
legal framework that is impartial and consistent to contemporary standards of
justice.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
ESTABLISHING EQUAL CRIMINAL LIABILITY AND PENALTIES FOR
MARITAL INFIDELITY AND MAINTAINING A PARAMOUR, PROMOTING
GENDER EQUALITY AND UPHOLDING THE SANCTITY OF MARRIAGE,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE ACT NO. 3815, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS
“THE REVISED PENAL CODE”, AS AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Article 333 of Act No. 3815, otherwise known as “The Revised Penal
Code”, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

             “Article 333. [Who are guilty of adultery] MARITAL INFIDELITY.
          – [Adultery] MARITAL INFIDELITY is committed by any married
          [woman] PERSON who shall have sexual [intercourse]
          RELATIONS with a [man] PERSON not HIS/her [husband]
          SPOUSE and by the [man] PERSON who has carnal knowledge of
          HIM/her knowing HIM/her to be married, even if the marriage be
          subsequently declared void.

              “[Adultery] MARITAL INFIDELITY shall be punished by
          prison correccional in its MINIMUM AND medium [and maximum]
          periods.

             “[If the] ANY person [guilty] ACCUSED of [adultery]
          MARITAL INFIDELITY SHALL BE EXEMPT FROM CRIMINAL
          LIABILITY IF THE [committed this] offense WAS COMMITTED
          AFTER [while being abandoned without justification by] the
          ISSUANCE OF A FINAL DECREE OF LEGAL SEPARATION BY
          A COMPETENT COURT. HOWEVER, IF [offended spouse,] the
          OFFENSE WAS COMMITTED AFTER THE ACCUSED SPOUSE
          HAD BEEN JUSTIFIABLY ABANDONED OR SUBJECTED TO
          PHYSICAL VIOLENCE OF ABUSIVE CONDUCT BY [penalty
          next lower in degree than that provided in] the OFFENDED

SEC. 2. Article 334 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

       “Article 334. [Concubinage] MAINTAINING A PARAMOUR. -
   Any [husband] MARRIED PERSON who shall keep a [mistress]
   PARAMOUR in the conjugal dwelling, or shall have sexual
   [intercourse] RELATIONS, under scandalous circumstances, with
   aNOTHER [woman] PERSON who is not [his wife] THE SPOUSE,
   or shall cohabit with [her] A PARAMOUR in any other place, shall
   be punished by prison correccional in its minimum and medium
   periods.

       “The [concubine] PARAMOUR shall suffer the penalty of
   destierro.

     “AS USED IN THIS ARTICLE, ‘PARAMOUR’ SHALL
   INCLUDE ANY PERSON WHO COHABITS WITH A MARRIED
   PERSON UNDER CIRCUMSTANCES WHERE THEY ASSUME
   AND EXERCISE TOWARDS EACH OTHER THE RIGHTS AND
   PRIVILEGES WHICH PROPERLY BELONG TO PERSONS
   MARRIED TO EACH OTHER.”

SEC. 3. Article 344 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

       “Article 344. Prosecution of the crimes of [adultery] MARITAL
   INFIDELITY, [concubinage] MAINTAINING A PARAMOUR,
   secution, abduction, rape and acts of lasciviousness. — The crimes of
   [adultery]    MARITAL         INFIDELITY         and    [concubinage]
   MAINTAINING A PARAMOUR shall not be prosecuted except
   upon a complaint filed by the offended spouse.

       “The offended party cannot institute criminal prosecution
   without including both the guilty parties, if they are both alive, nor,
   in any case, if he OR SHE shall have consented or pardoned the
   offenders[.], NOR, IN ANY CASE, IF HE OR SHE IS LIKEWISE
   GUILTY OF MARITAL INFIDELITY OR MAINTAINING A
   PARAMOUR, NOR, IN ANY CASE, IF HE OR SHE SHALL
   HAVE ABANDONED THE GUILTY SPOUSE WITHOUT JUST
   CAUSE FOR MORE THAN ONE (1) YEAR.
