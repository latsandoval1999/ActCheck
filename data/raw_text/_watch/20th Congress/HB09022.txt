                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Public Transport Modernization Program {PTMP), formerly known as the
Public Utility Vehicle Modernization Program (PUVMP), is a key reform aimed at
improving the safety, efficiency, and sustainability of the country's public transport
system. However, the success of this program is closely tied to the financial capacity
of its beneficiaries, such as the public utility drivers, operators, and transport
cooperatives, many of whom rely on loans to acquire modernized units.

        In recent years, the transport sector has been severely affected by persistent
and unpredictable increases in oil prices, mostly due to global market volatility and
geopolitical tensions. Empirical studies show that fuel price increases significantly
elevate operational costs in the transport sector, directly affecting income and
livelihood stability. A 2024 study found that rising fuel prices "significantly affect
livelihoods and lifestyles" of transport workers, underscoring the vulnerability of the
sector to oil price shocks. 1

have broader economic consequences, as increases in fuel costs are transmitted

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “PTMP Loan Grace
Period Act.”

         SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to protect public utility drivers and operators from the adverse economic effects of
extraordinary increases in oil prices, to ensure the continued viability and sustainability
of the Public Transport Modernization Program (PTMP), formerly known as the Public
Utility Vehicle Modernization Program (PUVMP), or by whatever name it may hereafter
be called, and to provide timely and appropriate temporary financial relief mechanisms
to mitigate the impact of such economic disruptions.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following terms
shall be defined as follows:

   a. PTMP Loans – loans extended to public utility vehicle operators, cooperatives,
      or corporations under the government’s Public Transport Program (PTMP),
      formerly known as Public Utility Vehicle Modernization Program (PUVMP)
      Utility Vehicle Modernization Program.

   b. Oil Crisis – a period of extraordinary increase in global or domestic oil prices,
      as determined by the Department of Energy (DOE), based on criteria to be
      defined in the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).
   c. Lending Institutions – government financial institutions (GFIs), private banks,
      and other entities extending loans under the PTMP.

       SEC. 4. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all PTMP beneficiaries, including
Individual operators, transport cooperatives; and corporations participating in the
modernization program.

        SEC. 5. Mandatory Grace Period. – During the occurrence of an Oil Crisis, all
banks, quasi-banks, financing companies, lending companies, and other financial
institutions, public and private, are directed to implement a minimum of a thirty (30)-
day grace period for the payment of motor vehicle loans acquired through the PTMP
falling due within the period of oil crisis without incurring interests, penalties, fees, or
other charges, persons with multiple loans shall likewise be given the minimum thirty
(30)-day grace period for every loan. The grace period shall apply to both principal
and interest payments.

        In the event that the Oil Crisis persists beyond the initial thirty (30)-day period,
the grace period shall be automatically extended for successive thirty (30)-day periods
until such time that the DOE officially declares the cessation of the Oil Crisis.

       SEC. 6. Deferment of Payments. – Loan payments due during the grace
period shall be deferred and shall be payable after the end of the grace period.
Provided, That, such deferment shall not result in the compounding of interest or
additional financial burden on the borrower.

        SEC. 7. Role of Government Agencies. – The DOE shall determine and
declare the existence of an Oil Crisis. The Department of Transportation (DOTr), in
coordination with the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board
(LTFRB), shall ensure proper dissemination and implementation of this Act. The
Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) shall issue the necessary guidelines to all lending
institutions for compliance.

       SEC. 8. Penalties. – Refusal by any lending institution to provide the thirty (30)-
day grace period as required under Section 4 of this Act shall constitute a violation of
this Act.

      If the offender is a natural person, such person shall, upon conviction, be
punished with imprisonment of not less than two (2) months or a fine of not less than
Ten Thousand Pesos (₱10,000.00) but not more than One Million Pesos
(₱1,000,000.00), or both, at the discretion of the court.

      If the offender is a juridical person, the penalty shall be imposed upon the
responsible officers, including but not limited to its president, directors, managers, or
other officers who knowingly participated in or allowed the commission of the violation.
The juridical entity shall likewise be subject to a fine of not less than Ten Thousand
Pesos (₱10,000.00) but not more than One Million Pesos (₱1,000,000.00), without
prejudice to the imposition of administrative sanctions by the appropriate regulatory
authority.

       SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DOE, DOTr, BSP, and other relevant agencies shall
promulgate the necessary IRR.

      SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, rules, and regulations inconsistent with
this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
