                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to recognize the importance of the Municipality of Tunga’s founding anniversary
by declaring November 15 of every year a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of
Tunga, Province of Leyte.

Prior to its municipal status, Tunga was a humble barrio dating back to at least 1860, populated by
settlers from Barugo, Carigara, Jaro, and Tacloban, and named “Tunga;” a Waray word meaning
“half” because of its location halfway between key towns or because a river bisected its territory.
The Municipality of Tunga was formally established on November 15, 1949, when then President
Elpidio Quirino issued Executive Order No. 266, separating it from Barugo and was officially
proclaimed by provincial authorities.

While it is the smallest municipality in the 2nd District of Leyte in both population and area, this
municipality’s journey from a small barrio to an independent town is commendable and because
of that, declaring the municipality’s founding anniversary, November 15 of every year, deserves
to be celebrated as a special non-working holiday.

In lieu of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING NOVEMBER 15 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TUNGA, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, IN
COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. November 15 of every year is hereby declared as a special non-working holiday
in the Municipality of Tunga, Province of Leyte.

SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
