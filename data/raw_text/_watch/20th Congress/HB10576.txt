     AN ACT CONVERTING THE OSPITAL NG PARAÑAQUE - DISTRICT TWO IN THE

                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE


        Health is a fundamental right of every Filipino guaranteed by the 1987 Constitution.
Article II, Section 15 provides: “The State shall protect and promote the right to health of
the people and instill consciousness among them. Further, Article XIII, Section 11 further
mandates the State to “adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health
development which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health, and other services
available to all the people at affordable cost. There shall be priority for the needs of the
underprivileged, sick, elderly, disabled, women, and children.”

       Despite this clear constitutional mandate, access to affordable, quality health and
medical services remains a challenge for many Filipinos. In the Second District of
Parañaque alone, the District Hospital, which serves more than 420,000 residents,1 lacks
specialized services, such as Pediatrics, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, and Surgery, as well
as an Intensive Care Unit and enhanced diagnostics facilities. As a result, patients requiring
complex or critical care are often referred to hospitals in neighboring cities.

       To address this need, this bill seeks to upgrade the Ospital ng Parañaque District 2
in the City of Parañaque from a Level I to a Level II health facility and increase its bed
capacity to one (100). The proposed upgrade will expand access to specialized medical
services, reduce the need for patients to seek treatment outside the city, and strengthen
the district’s local health system. It will also foster the development of a more capable
healthcare workforce by creating opportunities for specialized training and improving the
quality of patient care.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Title — This Act shall be known as the “Ospital ng Parañaque District 2
General Hospital Act.”

SEC. 2. Conversion into a Level II Hospital —The Ospital ng Parañaque District 2 in
the City of Parañaque is hereby upgraded into a Level II General Hospital.

SEC. 3. Authorized Bed Capacity — The Ospital ng Parañaque District 2 shall have
the capabilities of a Level II Hospital, increasing its authorized bed capacity from 70 to
100 beds.

SEC. 4. Upgrade of Service and Facilities — The existing professional healthcare
services, equipment, and facilities of the Ospital ng Parañaque District 2 shall also be
upgraded to conform with and be commensurate to the increase in bed capacity as
provided for in Section 3 hereof.

SEC. 5. Increase in Personnel — The existing workforce of the Ospital ng Parañaque
District 2, including medical, nursing, and auxiliary personnel, shall be increased
correspondingly to meet the demands of a Level II hospital.

SEC. 6. Appropriations — The City Local Government Unit shall provide the necessary
funds for the maintenance of Ospital ng Parañaque District 2. The City Local
Government Unit shall appropriate funds for human resource complement to support
the increase in bed capacity and the corresponding upgrading of its service facilities
and professional health care services.

SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations — Within sixty (60) days from the
approval of this Act, the Secretary of Health, in consultation with the Local Government
Unit of Parañaque, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act.
