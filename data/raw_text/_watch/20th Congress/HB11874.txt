                                         AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A MULTI-PURPOSE FISHERY BREEDING FARM AND HATCHERY FOR THE PRODUCTION AND
MULTIPLICATION OF CRABS, SHRIMPS AND MILKFISH IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SULTAN MASTURA,
PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO DEL NORTE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill proposes the establishment of a multi-purpose fishery breeding farm and hatchery in the
Municipality of Sultan Mastura, Province of Maguindanao del Norte, to address the pressing
nutritional and economic needs of the residents in Sultan Mastura's coastal barangays, whose
lives are intrinsically linked to fishing for both sustenance and livelihood.

The establishment of a modern breeding farm and hatchery will significantly improve the
economic lives of our people, not only within the Municipality of Sultan Mastura but also across
the entire Province of Maguindanao del Norte, with the availability of a stable and increased
supply of high-value marine products.

This facility will also provide employment opportunities for local fishermen and coastal
communities, specifically for women, by creating sustainable livelihoods and enhancing
household incomes.

As a vital hub for the supply of crabs, shrimps, and milkfish through controlled breeding and
hatching, the breeding farm and hatchery will contribute to the restoration of local fish stocks,
ensuring the long-term sustainability of the marine ecosystem and bolstering food security for
the local population.

In view of these, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Establishment of a Multi-Purpose Fishery Breeding Farm and Hatchery. There shall be
established, under the direct supervision of the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR)
of the Department of Agriculture (DA), a multi-purpose fishery breeding farm and hatchery for
the production and multiplication of crabs, shrimps, and milkfish in the Municipality of Sultan
Mastura, Province of Maguindanao del Norte.

This establishment aims to directly meet the nutritional and economic needs of the residents,
particularly those in the coastal barangays.

SEC 2. Research and Experimentation. – The BFAR shall be mandated to conduct continuing
research and experimentation on crab, shrimp, and milkfish culture, specifically as they apply to
local conditions. The objective of such research and experimentation shall be to discover new
methods and technologies that will rejuvenate and enhance the fishing industry in the area and
beyond.

SEC 3. Appropriations. – The Secretary of the Department of Agriculture shall include in the
Department's program the initial establishment and subsequent operation of the multi-purpose
fishery breeding farm and hatchery for the production and multiplication of crabs, shrimps, and
milkfish in the Municipality of Sultan Mastura, Province of Maguindanao del Norte. The funding
for this purpose shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).
SEC 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Agriculture (DA) and its related
agencies, including BFAR, shall jointly promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act within sixty (60) days from its effectivity.

SEC 5. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the
other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential decrees, rules or regulations,
and other issuances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
