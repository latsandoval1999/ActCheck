                              EXPLANATORY NOTE



Under Republic Act No. 8291, otherwise known as the "Government Service
Insurance System Act of 1997", public servants who dedicated their lifetime to
government service are entitled to old-age monthly pensions upon reaching
retirement. However, decades after the enactment of RA 8291, the purchasing
power of these hard-earned pensions has drastically eroded due to sustained
headline inflation, escalating cost-of-living adjustments (COLA), rising utility
rates, and the soaring prices of essential maintenance medicines and healthcare
services.

While periodic adjustments in monthly pensions are occasionally granted subject
to the approval of the GSIS Board of Trustees, there is currently no legal mandate
requiring a systematic, automatic annual adjustment linked to real inflation and
macroeconomic indicators. Consequently, thousands of state pensioners face
financial hardship as their fixed monthly stipends fail to meet current economic
reality.

Article II, Section 10 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution mandates that the State
shall promote social justice in all phases of national development. Furthermore,
Article XIII, Section 11 directs the State to prioritize the needs of the elderly and
underprivileged.

This bill addresses this legal gap by amending Republic Act No. 8291 to institute
an Automatic Annual Pension Adjustment Mechanism. Under this proposed
measure, the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) shall automatically
adjust all basic monthly pensions annually on January 1, taking into account the
annual headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation rate reported by the
Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).
By indexing GSIS pensions to national economic realities, this legislation
guarantees our retired public servants a dignified, secure, and inflation-protected
post-retirement life.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

  AN ACT MANDATING THE AUTOMATIC ANNUAL ADJUSTMENT OF ALL
GOVERNMENT SERVICE INSURANCE SYSTEM (GSIS) MONTHLY PENSIONS
      TO MATCH THE NATIONAL INFLATION RATE AND ECONOMIC
 INDICATORS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8291,
   OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "THE GOVERNMENT SERVICE INSURANCE
         SYSTEM ACT OF 1997," AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
                              Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the "GSIS Pension Inflation
Protection and Economic Indexation Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to provide adequate
social security protection to former government employees and ensure that their
retirement benefits retain purchasing power, dignity, and real value against
inflationary pressures and changing economic conditions.

SEC. 3. Indexation and Automatic Annual Adjustment of Monthly Pensions. — All
Basic Monthly Pension (BMP) received by old-age and disability pensioners under
this Act shall be automatically adjusted with the rate equivalent to the average
inflation rate of the immediate preceding year as officially published by the
Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).

In the event of deflation or a negative inflation rate in any given calendar year, no
downward adjustment shall be applied to the monthly pension received by the
pensioners.
SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), in
coordination with the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), the Department of
Finance (DOF), and the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), shall
promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation
of this Act.

SEC. 5. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, administrative orders,
rules, and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
