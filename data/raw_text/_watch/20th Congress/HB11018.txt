                              EXPLANATORY NOTE


       The Bicol River Basin is one of the most significant river systems in the
Philippines, serving as a vital source of water for agriculture, domestic use,
fisheries, and ecological sustainability in the Bicol Region. It plays a critical role
in supporting food security, livelihoods, and regional economic development.
However, the basin continues to face persistent and complex challenges,
including recurrent flooding, watershed degradation, sedimentation, climate
change impacts, and fragmented governance mechanisms.

       Despite the existence of various national and local initiatives, the
management of the Bicol River Basin remains institutionally fragmented, with
multiple agencies exercising overlapping mandates over water resources,
irrigation, flood control, environmental protection, and land use. This has
resulted in inefficiencies, lack of coordination, and suboptimal outcomes in
addressing both environmental and socio-economic concerns.

       The Constitution mandates the State to protect and advance the right of
the people to a balanced and healthful ecology (Article II, Section 16), and to
ensure the rational utilization and conservation of natural resources. In line with
this, there is a compelling need to adopt an integrated river basin management
(IRBM) approach, which recognizes the interdependence of land, water,
ecosystems, and human communities within a river basin.

      While prior executive issuances, such as Presidential Decree No. 926 and
Executive Order No. 359, have established project-level and programmatic
mechanisms for the Bicol River Basin, these measures remain limited in scope,
continuity, and institutional authority. Moreover, existing structures under the
Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), including the River
Basin Control Office, primarily provide policy direction at the national level and
are not designed to function as dedicated, basin-specific coordinating bodies
with sustained operational capacity.

       This proposed measure seeks to address these gaps through the creation
of the Bicol River Basin Management Office (BRBMO), which shall serve as a
central coordinating and integrative body for all programs, projects, and policies
affecting the Bicol River Basin. The BRBMO is designed not to duplicate or
supplant existing agencies, but to harmonize their efforts, facilitate inter-agency
collaboration, and ensure a coherent and science-based approach to river basin
management.

      Consistent with the principle of local autonomy under the Local
Government Code of 1991, the proposed Office shall work in close coordination
with local government units, ensuring that local development plans are aligned
with basin-wide strategies while respecting the powers and functions of LGUs.

       Furthermore,     the    measure     institutionalizes  multi-stakeholder
participation, recognizing that sustainable river basin management requires the
active involvement of communities, farmers, fisherfolk, indigenous peoples, civil
society, and the private sector.

       By establishing a dedicated institutional mechanism anchored on
integrated, participatory, and science-driven management, this bill aims to
enhance the resilience, sustainability, and productivity of the Bicol River Basin,
thereby contributing to national priorities on environmental protection, disaster
risk reduction, food security, and inclusive development.

     It is for the foregoing premises that the approval of this Bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
        CREATING THE BICOL RIVER BASIN MANAGEMENT OFFICE
(BRBMO), DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, AND APPROPRIATING
                        FUNDS THEREFOR

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:


    SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Bicol River Basin
Management Office Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to ensure the sustainable development, management, and protection of
river basins consistent with ecological balance and climate resilience and
promote integrated river basin management (IRBM) through inter-agency
coordination and stakeholder participation. Toward this end, the State shall
strengthen institutional mechanisms for the Bicol River Basin, recognizing its
critical role in flood control, agriculture, water security, and regional
development.

      SECTION 3. Creation of the Bicol River Basin Management Office (BRBMO).
The created Bicol River Basin Management Office (BRBMO) shall serve as the
central coordinating and implementing body for all river basin-related programs,
projects, and policies within the Bicol River Basin.

     The BRBMO shall be attached to the Department of Environment and
Natural Resources (DENR) for policy and program coordination.

      SECTION 4. Coverage. - The jurisdiction of the BRBMO shall cover the
entire Bicol River Basin, including but not limited to areas in the provinces of
Camarines Sur, Albay, and other adjoining areas as may be determined by the
DENR.

     SECTION 5. Powers and Functions. - The BRBMO shall have the following
powers and functions:

      a) Formulate and implement an Integrated River Basin Management Plan
         for the Bicol River Basin;
      b) Serve as the primary coordinating mechanism among national
         agencies, LGUs, and stakeholders;
      c) Monitor and evaluate river basin projects and ensure alignment with
         national policies;
      d) Facilitate flood control, watershed protection, irrigation, and water
         resource management initiatives;
      e) Promote climate resilience, disaster risk reduction, and sustainable
         land and water use; and
      f) Exercise such other powers necessary to achieve the objectives of this
         Act.

     SECTION 6. Organizational Structure. - The BRBMO shall be headed by
an Executive Director, who shall be appointed by the President of the Philippines.

     The Office shall include technical, administrative, and support staff as
may be necessary.

      SECTION 7. Coordination with Existing Agencies (Non-Impairment Clause).
– The BRBMO shall not diminish or impair the mandates of existing agencies,
including but not limited to:
   (a) Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR)
   (b) River Basin Control Office (RBCO)
   (c) National Irrigation Administration (NIA)
   (d) Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH)
   (e) Local Government Units (LGUs)

      It shall operate primarily as a coordinative, integrative, and policy-aligning
body, consistent with existing laws.
      SECTION 8. Consultation with Communities, Local People’s Organizations,
and Civil Society Organizations. – The BRBMO shall ensure the meaningful and
continuing participation of affected communities, local people’s organizations
(POs), and civil society organizations (CSOs) in the planning, implementation,
monitoring, and evaluation of all programs, projects, and policies under this Act.
Toward this end, the BRBMO shall:
   (a) Conduct regular public consultations, dialogues, and information
       campaigns with affected communities, POs, and CSOs prior to the
       adoption or implementation of river basin plans, programs, and projects;
   (b) Ensure representation of community leaders, POs, and CSOs in relevant
       planning bodies, technical working groups, or advisory councils created
       pursuant to this Act;
   (c) Establish accessible mechanisms for the submission of feedback,
       grievances, and recommendations by communities, POs, and CSOs;
   (d) Give due consideration to the welfare, livelihood, and traditional
       knowledge of affected communities, including indigenous peoples,
       farmers, and fisherfolk, in all decision-making processes; and
   (e) Coordinate with LGUs to ensure that community consultations are
       conducted in a manner that is accessible, culturally appropriate, and
       inclusive of vulnerable and marginalized groups.
     Consultations conducted pursuant to this Section shall be documented
and shall form part of the official records of the BRBMO.
      SECTION 9. Roles of Local Government Units. – LGUs within the Bicol River
Basin shall:

      a) Participate in planning and implementation;
      b) Align local development plans with the basin plan;
      c) Retain autonomy pursuant to the Local Government Code of 1991.

     SECTION 10. Appropriation. - The amount necessary for the
implementation of the provisions of this Act shall be incorporated in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

     SECTION 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from effectivity, DENR, in coordination with relevant agencies, shall
promulgate the Implementing Rules and Regulations.

      SECTION 12. Separability Clause. – If for any reason, any part or provision
of this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or
provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules, and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
      SECTION 14. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
