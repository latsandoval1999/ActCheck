                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to grant a one(1)-time internship allowance of Ten thousand
pesos (Php 10,000.00) to qualified students in public and private higher education
institutions and technical-vocational institutions who successfully complete their
required internship, practicum, or on-the-job training (OJT). It provides financial
assistance to help students fulfill this requirement and recognizes that experiential
learning is an essential component of education.

       Internship and practicum programs equip students with practical skills,
professional discipline, and workplace experience that improve their readiness for
employment. However, the costs associated with transportation, meals, uniforms,
and other documentary requirements needed for internships place an undue
financial burden on students from low-income households. These costs may hinder
a student’s ability to complete mandatory training despite meeting academic
requirements.

       The provision of an internship allowance to students who have successfully
completed their internships and OJTs helps defray expenses incurred during the
training period. It also incentivizes the discipline and hard work these students
invest both in themselves and the future of the country, while enabling them to
complete their studies without unnecessary financial hardship.

       The support we extend to our students as they transition from academic
instruction to workplace training strengthens the country’s human capital and
reaffirms the State’s commitment to accessible and quality education and its
investment in a more skilled, productive, and globally competitive workforce.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE GRANT OF A ONE-TIME INTERNSHIP
ALLOWANCE FOR STUDENTS IN PUBLIC OR PRIVATE HIGHER
EDUCATION     INSTITUTIONS    AND    TECHNICAL-VOCATIONAL
INSTITUTIONS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Student Internship
Allowance Act.”

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby the declared policy of the State to
uphold the constitutional mandate to promote accessible and quality education for
all, and to provide adequate support to students in the fulfillment of their academic
and professional requirements. Towards this end, the State recognizes that
internship and practicum programs are integral to the development of a competent
and job-ready workforce, and that the financial burden associated with these
programs may hinder students from lower-income backgrounds from completing
their education. In this regard, the State shall institutionalize financial support for
students who complete their required internship or practicum, as a measure to
foster inclusive growth, uphold equity in education, and encourage successful
transition from school to work.

       SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all Filipino students enrolled in any
public or private Higher Education Institution (HEl) duly recognized by the
Commission on Higher Education (CHED), or in any Technical-Vocational
Institution (TVI) accredited by the Technical Education and Skills Development
Authority (TESDA), who are required to complete an internship, practicum, or
on-the-job training (OJT) as part of their academic or certification requirements.

        The grant of allowance shall be extended to students in undergraduate,
diploma, certificate, and technical-vocational programs, whether enrolled on a
full-time or part-time basis, provided they have satisfactorily completed the
required number of internship, practicum, or OJT hours. Eligibility shall be certified

                                            1
by their respective HEl or TVI, based on their compliance with the approved
curriculum or training program guidelines.

       SEC. 4. Student Internship Allowance. – A one-time Internship Allowance in the
amount of Ten thousand pesos (Php 10,000.00) shall be granted to each qualified
student who successfully completed the internship, practicum, or on-the-job
training (OJT) required by their academic or certification program. The allowance
shall serve as financial assistance to support the student's subsistence,
transportation, documentation, and incidental costs incurred during the internship
period.

       For the purposes of this Act, successful completion shall mean the student
intern has:​

           a.​ fulfilled the required number of internship or practicum hours as
       certified by the host training establishment and the HEl or TVI;​

          b.​ submitted all post-internship reports, evaluations, and related
       documentation as required by their institution; and​

           c.​ received a passing or satisfactory final assessment from both the
       institution and the host entity, if applicable.

       The allowance shall be non-taxable and non-transferable, and shall not be
subject to any deductions by the host establishment or the educational institution.
Provided, This Act shall not diminish other rights or benefits due to student interns
under separate applicable laws or regulations.

      SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
approval of this Act, the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the
Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), in coordination
with the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), shall jointly issue an
implementing rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

        SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
