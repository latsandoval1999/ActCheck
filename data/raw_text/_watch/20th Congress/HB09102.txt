                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to establish a government-supported burial assistance program
to help indigent and extremely poor families cope with the high cost of funeral and
burial services. It aims to ensure that every Filipino, regardless of financial capacity,
is afforded a dignified and respectful farewell for their departed loved ones.

      In the Philippines, death is marked by deeply rooted cultural practices that
honor the deceased and bring communities together in mourning. Funeral rites
serve not only as expressions of grief but also as affirmations of familial and social
bonds. However, for many low-income households, these traditions are often
overshadowed by the prohibitive costs associated with funeral arrangements and
interment.

       Basic funeral services can cost at least P10,000.00, an amount that is beyond
the reach of many indigent families. As a result, some are compelled to seek
financial assistance through informal lenders who may charge excessive interest,
further worsening their economic situation. In extreme cases, families are unable to
secure even the most modest funeral services, depriving their loved ones of a proper
and dignified burial.

        This proposed measure provides a fifty percent (50%) discount on funeral
services for indigent families, and free funeral services for those classified as
extremely poor. Availment of these benefits shall be subject to standard
documentation requirements and a formal funeral agreement involving the
bereaved family, accredited service providers, and authorized personnel from the
Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD). By alleviating the financial
strain associated with funeral expenses, this bill promotes compassion, dignity, and
social equity during times of loss.

       In view of the foregoing, immediate approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
GRANTING A FIFTY PERCENT (50%) DISCOUNT FROM FUNERAL SERVICES
TO INDIGENT FAMILIES AND FREE FUNERAL SERVICES TO EXTREMELY
POOR FAMILIES NATIONWIDE, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR AND
FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Free and Discounted
Funeral Services Act.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to promote a just and
dynamic social order that will ensure the prosperity and independence of the nation
and free the people from poverty through policies that provide adequate social
services, promote full employment, a rising standard of living, and an improved
quality of life for all. As part of fulfilling this, the State shall give discounts from
funeral services to indigent families and free funeral services to extremely poor
families.

       SEC 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

           a. Poor refers to families whose income falls below the poverty threshold
       as defined by the Department of Economy, Planning, and Development
       (DEPDev) and/or who cannot afford in a sustained manner to provide their
       minimum basic needs of food, health, education, housing or other essential
       amenities of life, as defined under Republic Act (RA) No. 8425, otherwise
       known as the “Social Reform and Poverty Alleviation Act”. The
       Community-Based Monitoring System (CBMS) established under RA No.
       11315 of the local government unit, with the supervision of the Philippine
       Statistics Authority (PSA), shall be used to determine the degree of indigency
       of the family, whether indigent or extremely poor;

         b. Funeral services refer to preparation of funeral documents,
       embalming, viewing, interment or burial services, cremation and inurnment
       services. including the provision of a casket or urn, as the case may be,
       offered by mortuaries. The standard package of funeral services will cover:

                  1.​ Transfer of deceased from place of death to the mortuary for
              embalming and applying of cosmetics within a twenty-five
              (25)-kilometer radius of mortuary;

                  2.​ An Ordinary Plain Casket and casket stand for viewing of body
              or urn in case of cremation;

                 3.​ Two (2) to three (3) days viewing outside the funeral chapels
              such as the Barangay Hall or residence of the family;

                 4.​ Use of funeral viewing equipment including lighting and
              arania;

                  5.​ Use of funeral vehicle for interment within a twenty-five
              (25)-kilometer radius from the place of viewing to the place of burial;
              and

                 6.​ Unless for compelling reason, interment shall only be on
              weekdays and on official time, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. until 5:00
              p.m., except on regular holidays.

       SEC 4. Free Funeral Services for Extremely Poor Families. – Free funeral services
shall be provided for extremely poor families by accredited mortuaries anywhere in
the country. The said free services shall be given to only one (1) family member per
month per mortuary branch: Provided, That said funeral service does not exceed the
cost of the mortuary’s minimum funeral service package: Provided further, That the
grant of free funeral services under this Act is without prejudice to the right of the
beneficiary to avail of upgraded funeral services given by the mortuary. The
difference in cost shall be borne by the family of the deceased.

       SEC 5. Discounted Funeral Services for Indigent Families. – A fifty percent (50%)
discount on funeral services shall be granted to indigent families by mortuaries
anywhere in the country but would only be applicable to At-Need funeral services
only: Provided, That said funeral service does not exceed the cost of the mortuary’s
minimum funeral service package. The grant of a discount under this Act is without
prejudice to the right of the beneficiary to avail of other financial assistance given by
the government.

       SEC 6. Requirements for Availing Free or Discounted Funeral Services. – Before an
indigent or extremely poor family can avail of the free or discounted funeral
services, the following documents shall be submitted: (a) certificate of indigency
issued by the barangay or the local social welfare office of the local government unit
(LGU), stating whether the family is either indigent or extremely poor as defined
under Section 3; Provided, however, That the certification shall not be a requirement
before any member of the family of the deceased could avail the services of the


​
mortuary; (b) death certificate; and (c) funeral contract between the
beneficiary-family, the accredited mortuary and the authorized DSWD personnel.

       SEC 7. Reimbursement. – Mortuaries granting discounted or free services to
indigent or extremely poor beneficiaries may seek reimbursement for the cost of the
services from any regional office of the DSWD upon the approval of the Regional
Director, the reimbursement will be based on the stipulations of the signed contract
between the poor family, the mortuary, and the authorized DSWD personnel.

      SEC. 8. Lead Agency. – The DSWD shall be the lead agency in the
implementation of the provisions of this Act and shall engage the funeral services of
mortuaries in the country.

       SEC 9. Role of Local Government Units (LGUs). — Notwithstanding the
preceding section, the LGUs are enjoined to fulfill their devolved function as
mandated by RA No. 7160, otherwise known as the “Local Government Code”, and
Presidential Decree No. 856, otherwise known as the “Sanitation Code”, in providing
assistance to indigent and extremely poor families in their respective areas at their
own expense. Further, the LGUs will waive fees and charges which include burial or
interment permit, cremation permit, transfer permit, disinterment permit, and cost
of Death Certificate.

        SEC 10. Appropriations. — The amount necessary to implement the provisions
of this Act shall be charged against the allocation in the General Appropriations Act
(GAA) of the DSWD.

​       SEC 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. —Within six (6) months from the
effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the DSWD shall, in coordination with the
Department of the Interior and Local Government and the Philippine Mortuary
Association, with the participation of LGUs, promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations to carry out the provisions of this Act.

​      SEC 12. Separability Clause. — If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared void or unconstitutional, the remaining portions or provisions hereof shall
not be affected by such declaration.

​     SEC 13. Repealing Clause. — Any provision of law or regulations inconsistent
herewith is hereby repealed, modified or amended accordingly.

      SEC 14. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.

     Approved,
