                              AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A SEEDLING BANK OF THE BUREAU OF PLANT INDUSTRY IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF HINUNANGAN, PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Agriculture remains the backbone of many communities in the Philippines, providing
food on every Filipino table and livelihood to millions of families. Yet despite the hard
work and dedication of our farmers, many continue to struggle with low productivity,
limited access to quality seeds and planting materials, and the increasing challenges
brought about by pests, diseases, and climate change. Without the proper support,
farmers find it difficult to increase their harvests, improve their incomes, and meet
the growing demand for food.

One of the biggest challenges faced by farmers, especially in rural areas, is the lack of
affordable access to certified seeds, improved planting materials, and modern
farming technologies. Many small farmers cannot afford expensive farm inputs or
must travel long distances just to obtain quality seedlings and technical assistance. As
a result, they often rely on low-quality planting materials that produce poor yields,
limiting both their productivity and their opportunities to earn a better living.

This bill seeks to establish a Seedling Bank of the Bureau of Plant Industry (BPI) in
the Municipality of Hinunangan, Province of Southern Leyte. Strategically located to
serve the municipalities of the province, particularly those along the Pacific area, the
proposed facility will provide quality seeds and planting materials, technical
assistance, farmer training, and modern crop production technologies. It will also

serve as a center for research, demonstration, and the promotion of climate-resilient
and high-value crops, enabling farmers to produce more, earn more, and become
more resilient to the effects of climate change.

The establishment of a BPI Seedling Bank in Southern Leyte is more than an
investment in agriculture, it is an investment in food security, rural development, and
the future of Filipino farming families. By bringing government services closer to
farmers, this measure will reduce production costs, improve crop quality, increase
farm productivity, and create more livelihood opportunities in the countryside. It will
also strengthen the province's capacity to supply quality planting materials that will
benefit not only Southern Leyte but neighboring areas as well.

Through this measure, the State reaffirms its commitment to empowering Filipino
farmers with the tools, knowledge, and support they need to succeed. A stronger
agriculture sector means stronger rural communities, greater food security, and a
more resilient economy. By helping our farmers grow more today, we secure a better
future for generations to come.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

                              AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A SEEDLING BANK OF THE BUREAU OF PLANT INDUSTRY IN THE

APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Establishment. — There shall be established a seedling bank and field
office of the Bureau of Plant Industry (BPI) of the Department of Agriculture (DA) in
the Municipality of Hinundayan, Province of Southern Leyte, to be known as the BPI-
DA Southern Leyte Seedling Bank, which shall serve as a center for training and
provision of technical advice and relevant services on crop culture and management
and ensure the continuous supply, production, and distribution of quality seeds and
planting materials of staple food crops and other high-value crops for food security
and livelihood generation of the farmers in Southern Leyte.

SECTION 2. Coordination and Partnerships. — The BPI-DA may enter into a
memorandum of agreement with the Southern Leyte State University (SLSU) and
other institutions for the purpose of conducting research and extension programs
geared towards the development of high-yielding crop varieties suitable for the area,
food security, providing support to small farmers, and the promotion of the local plant
industry.




                                                                              3 | Page
SECTION 3. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the establishment and
operations of the BPI-DA Southern Leyte Seedling Bank shall initially come from the
savings of the DA. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for the efficient
operation and maintenance of such office shall be included in the allocation of the DA
in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within six (6) months
following the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Agriculture, upon the
recommendation of the Director of the Bureau of Plant Industry and in consultation
with the Southern Leyte State University, the Province of Southern Leyte, the
Municipality of Hinunangan, concerned local government units, farmers’
organizations, and other relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 5. Separability Clause. — If any part, section or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such
declaration shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
