                           AN ACT
    MANDATING THE PROVISION OF CHILD-FRIENDLY FACILITIES IN
          EVERY DINE-IN RESTAURANT IN THE COUNTRY

                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The State provides that it shall defend the right of children to
assistance, including proper care and nutrition, and special protection from
all forms of neglect, abuse, cruelty, exploitation, and other conditions
prejudicial to their development.

       The country additionally is a signatory to the United Nations
Convention on the Rights of the Child, signifying its commitment to upholding
the rights and welfare of children, including their right to be nurtured in an
environment that fosters understanding, tolerance, and peace and enables
growth and development.

       In recognition of the importance of ensuring that children dining out
with their families have access to child-friendly spaces that cater to their
needs as well as promote a positive and enjoyable atmosphere for all patrons,
this bill proposes that food establishments such as restaurants, cafes, diners,
and similar establishments must provide child-friendly facilities as a part of
their establishments.

        Specifically, the proposed “Child-friendly Restaurants Act” mandates
proportional child-friendly seating in food establishments and baby-changing
facilities in restrooms.

      By mandating the provision of child-friendly facilities in food
establishments, this legislation encourages businesses to prioritize their
youngest patrons and support the well-being of dining parents and families
with young children, thereby creating inclusive and welcoming environments
while helping the food establishments boost their economy.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
    MANDATING THE PROVISION OF CHILD-FRIENDLY FACILITIES IN
          EVERY DINE-IN RESTAURANT IN THE COUNTRY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Child-Friendly
Restaurants Act”.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes that all
Filipinos are entitled to access to convenient facilities that will enable
them to properly undertake their childcare responsibilities. Towards
this end, this State shall ensure that these facilities are sanitary, safe ,
readily available and accessible for all persons, and shall specialize
seating for infants and children.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the National Council on Disability Affairs shall, in
coordination with the Department of Interior and Local Government,
Department of Health, Department of Social Welfare and Development,
and other relevant government agencies and stakeholders, issue the
necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this
Act.

SECTION 4. Provision of Child-friendly Seating. – Food service
establishments that allow dine-in are mandated, at all times, to have child-
friendly seating equipment for patrons who have at least one (1) early-stage
child present with them at the time that they procure the food or the services
of the establishment.
SECTION 5. Proportion of Child-friendly seating to Common Seating-
Food service establishments shall have at least ten percent child-friendly
seats out of the total common seats available.

SECTION 6. Access to Diaper-Changing Stations in the Restrooms- All
major food establishments shall have at least one (1) safe, sanitary, and
convenient baby diaper-changing station that is accessible to restrooms either
exclusively used by men or women or a baby-diaper changing station that is
accessible to both men and women; Provided, that a diaper-changing station
shall be available on each floor occupied by the food establishment.

SECTION 7. Exceptions. Section 6 of this Act shall not apply to a restaurant
with a seating capacity of at least 50 people if there is a centrally located
facility with a baby-diaper changing station located within 300 feet of the
entrance to the restaurant.

SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health (DOH) and other
relevant stakeholders shall promulgate the implementing rules and
regulations for the proper implementation of this Act.

SECTION 9. Transition Period. All covered food service establishments
existing at the time of the approval of this Act shall have a period of one (1)
year from the effectivity of this Act to comply with the provisions hereof.

SECTION 10. Penalties. A major food service establishment that fails to
comply with any provisions of this Act shall be liable for a fine of one hundred
pesos (Php 100) for every day of violation.

SECTION 11. Separability Clause.- If any provision or part hereof is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not
otherwise affected

SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees, Implementing Rules and Regulations inconsistent with this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
