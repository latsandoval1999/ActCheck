                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The State recognizes that an efficient and integrated road network is
essential to urban mobility, economic activity, and the orderly expansion of
growing cities. Circumferential roads play a critical role in distributing
traffic, reducing congestion along primary corridors, and improving
connectivity across urban and peri-urban areas.

      The Iligan City Circumferential Road (C3) Extension, traversing
Barangays Pugaan, Mandulog, Lanipao, and Digkilaan, serves as a vital
continuation of the City’s circumferential network. Extending from the
Green Gold Gas Crossing in Barangay Pugaan to Runay Bridge in Barangay
Digkilaan, the road strengthens linkages across multiple growth areas and
supports access to both residential and production zones.

       A feasibility study has already been conducted, affirming its viability
and strategic importance. The road has likewise been validated for
conversion into a national road, recognizing its significance in completing
the circumferential road system of Iligan City. However, it remains under
local jurisdiction, which constrains sustained development, maintenance,
and integration into the broader national road network.

       Converting the Iligan City Circumferential Road (C3) Extension into a
national road will enable the Department of Public Works and Highways to
undertake comprehensive improvement and long-term maintenance. This
will ensure that the road meets national standards and fully functions as a
critical component of the city’s transport network.
     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly

                           AN ACT
     CONVERTING THE ILIGAN CITY CIRCUMFERENTIAL ROAD
      (C3) EXTENSION, FROM GREEN GOLD GAS CROSSING IN
  BARANGAY PUGAAN TO RUNAY BRIDGE IN BARANGAY DIGKILAAN,
   TRAVERSING BARANGAYS PUGAAN, MANDULOG, LANIPAO, AND
        DIGKILAAN, ILIGAN CITY, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD
             AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Iligan City
Circumferential Road (C3) Extension National Road Act.”

     SECTION 2. Conversion. — The Iligan City Circumferential Road (C3)
Extension, from Green Gold Gas Crossing in Barangay Pugaan to Runay
Bridge in Barangay Digkilaan, traversing Barangays Pugaan, Mandulog,
Lanipao, and Digkilaan, Iligan City, is hereby converted into a national road
under the supervision and administration of the Department of Public
Works and Highways (DPWH).

      SECTION 3. Appropriations. — The Secretary of Public Works and
Highways shall include in the Department’s program the improvement,
repair, and maintenance of the said road, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 4. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
