                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The City of Lipa, Batangas continues to experience steady population and
economic growth. With the rise in population, the demand for services from the Land
Transportation Office (LTO) has likewise significantly increased. According to the
Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), the city is projected to have a population of
approximately 448,000 by 2026.

      At present, the LTO Lipa District Office is classified as a Class “C” district office,
which typically operates with limited personnel, facilities and service capacity.
Upgrading it to a Class “A” district office will allow for a larger personnel complement,
expanded operational resources and improved facilities to accommodate a higher
volume of transactions.

        This upgrade will enable the office to process motor vehicle registration, driver
licensing and other LTO services more efficiently, thereby improving service delivery
to the growing number of motorists and residents in Lipa and nearby areas.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
