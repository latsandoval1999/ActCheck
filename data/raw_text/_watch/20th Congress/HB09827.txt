                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to increase the tax-exempt threshold for personal income
tax from Two hundred fifty thousand pesos (P250,000.00) to Five hundred
thousand pesos (P500,000.00). It also seeks to raise the tax-exempt ceiling for
the thirteenth (13th) month pay and other benefits from Ninety thousand
pesos (P90,000.00) to Two hundred thousand pesos (P200,000.00).

       The 1987 Constitution recognizes labor as a primary social and
economic force and mandates the protection of workers’ rights, and the
promotion of their welfare. Republic Act No. 10963, otherwise known as the
“Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion (TRAIN) Law”, restructured
individual income taxes to provide relief to taxpayers. However, persistent
inflation and the continued increase in the prices of basic goods and services
have substantially diminished the purchasing power of Filipino households
since its implementation.

      As a result, the current tax-exempt thresholds no longer provide the
same level of relief originally intended under the law. The existing tax-exempt
income threshold and tax-exempt ceiling for employee benefits have become
inadequate because of increasing cost of living, thus reducing the real value of
workers’ earnings and take home pay.

       The proposed measure allows workers to retain a greater portion of
their earnings, provides additional financial relief to low- and middle- income
families, and supports domestic consumption and economic activity.

      This bill advances social equity and the welfare of every Filipino
worker in the country who earnestly works hard to provide for the daily
subsistence of their families. Ensuring that the tax system remains fair,
responsive, and attuned to the realities faced by the labor sector reaffirms the
State’s commitment to protecting the welfare of labor amid evolving
economic conditions.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.
