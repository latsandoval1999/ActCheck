AN ACT

THE ENTIRE COUNTRY

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article XIV, Section 19 of the 1987 Constitution mandates that the State shall promote physical
education and encourage sports programs, competitions, and related activities to foster self-
discipline, teamwork, and excellence for the development of a healthy and alert citizenry. This
constitutional policy recognizes sports not only as a driver of personal health and discipline,
but also as a powerful vehicle for broader social and economic development.

Consistent with this mandate, Republic Act No. 9593, or the Tourism Act of 2009, declares
tourism an industry of national interest and importance, recognizing its critical role in
generating investment, employment, foreign exchange, and inclusive national growth. It
explicitly directs the Government to promote sustainable tourism, encourage private-sector
participation, strengthen local tourism development, and position the Philippines as a premier,
competitive global destination.

This bill seeks to declare the year 2027 as the “National Golf Tourism Year” in the Philippines
to promote golf as a key component of the country’s tourism strategy and to highlight the
Philippines as a premier destination for sports, leisure, and high-value travel.

This measure is both strategic and timely. While the Philippine tourism sector continues its
steady recovery, international visitor arrivals and overall visitor spending require further
diversification to reach and surpass pre-pandemic levels. Golf tourism presents a unique
economic opportunity: golf travelers spend significantly more than average tourists, stay for
longer durations, and create extended demand across accommodation, food and dining,
transportation, retail, and local recreation services.

The Philippines already possesses a rich, substantial foundation for golf tourism, boasting
world-class courses across Luzon, the Visayas, and Mindanao. Importantly, the year 2027
carries profound historical significance for Philippine sports and heritage. The Iloilo Golf and
Country Club in Santa Barbara, Iloilo—established in 1907 by British engineers associated
with the Panay Railways—will celebrate its 120th anniversary in 2027. As the oldest golf
course in the Philippines, its milestone provides a fitting, historic centerpiece to showcase the

This national observance also builds upon demonstrated industry capabilities and prior
congressional momentum. The Philippines successfully hosted the Asian Golf Tourism
Convention in 2018, and the Department of Tourism launched the Philippine Golf Experience
in 2025. Furthermore, this initiative aligns with earlier legislative actions, notably House Bill
No. 7905 (substituting HB 5770) filed by former 2" District of lloilo Representative Michael
B. Gorriceta in the 18 Congress, which was favorably recommended by the Committee on
Tourism under Committee Report No. 567.

The proposed 2027 observance consolidates these historical, administrative, and private-sector
efforts under a single national platform. Rather than creating a new bureaucracy, the measure
authorizes the Department of Tourism to lead the observance in voluntary collaboration with
local government units, private golf clubs, and tourism stakeholders.

By unifying the country’s golf courses, historical landmarks, local governments, and sports
organizations under a shared national umbrella, the 2027 National Golf Tourism Year is
expected to elevate the Philippines’ standing in global sports tourism and generate vibrant
economic opportunities for host communities across the archipelago.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT
DECLARING THE YEAR 2027 AS “NATIONAL GOLF TOURISM YEAR” IN
THE ENTIRE COUNTRY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. The year 2027 is declared as “National Golf Tourism Year” in the Philippines,
in recognition of the growing interest in the sport, the world-class and unique golf courses
located all over the country, and the potential of golf as a high-value niche of sports tourism.

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Tourism shall, in coordination with concerned agencies of the
government, local government units, and recognized golf clubs in the Philippines, spearhead
the celebration of the “National Golf Tourism Year” throughout the country, to encourage the
development of golf tourism and enhance its popularity among sports enthusiasts. Participation
of private golf clubs shall be voluntary. The funds necessary for this purpose shall be charged
against the existing appropriations of the Department of Tourism, subject to budgeting,
accounting, and auditing rules.

SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
