                           AN ACT
CONVERTING THE GUIMBA – PURA PROVINCIAL ROAD STRETCHING FROM
BARANGAY CASONGSONG TO BARANGAY SAN MIGUEL, MUNICIPALITY OF
GUIMBA INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Strategic roads that connect local communities to establish national routes are essential
to promoting balanced countryside development. The Guimba – Pura Road, approximately
8.145 kilometers in length, links several barangays in the Municipality of Guimba and connects
directly to the Nueva Ecija – Pangasinan Road, a national secondary road.
         Despite its current local classification, the road functions as an essential access route to
the national highway network. It supports agricultural transport, eases vehicular congestion
along alternative routes, and enhances accessibility for residents and businesses. Its role in
facilitating intra- and inter-provincial mobility highlights its broader economic and logistical
value.
        The proposed conversion of this road into a national road will guarantee consistent
infrastructure support, promote safer and more efficient travel, and contribute to sustained
economic activity within the locality.
        In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. The Guimba – Pura Provincial Road, stretching from Barangay Casongsong to
Barangay San Miguel, Municipality of Guimba is hereby converted into a national road.

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the Department’s
program the improvement, repair and maintenance of the road, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
