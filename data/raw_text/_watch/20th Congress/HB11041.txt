                              AN ACT
INSTITUTIONALIZING AND EXPANDING THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR AND
EMPLOYMENT GOVERNMENT INTERNSHIP PROGRAM, PROVIDING INTERNSHIP
OPPORTUNITIES IN NATIONAL GOVERNMENT AGENCIES, LOCAL GOVERNMENT
UNITS, AND OTHER GOVERNMENT INSTRUMENTALITIES, GRANTING A STIPEND
TIED TO THE PREVAILING REGIONAL MINIMUM WAGE, AND PROVIDING FUNDS
THEREFOR

                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

The lack of work experience remains one of the biggest barriers faced by Filipinos
seeking employment. While the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE)
Government Internship Program (GIP) gives people a chance to gain skills and
practical experience, its regular age requirement of 18 to 30 years old leaves many
capable Filipinos behind. Not everyone gets their irst opportunity while they are
young. For many, the need for a irst job comes later in life.

This measure seeks to expand the GIP to Filipinos aged 18 years old and above who
have no substantial prior work experience. This recognizes that irst-time jobseekers
are not limited to young people. Adults who have never had stable employment,
displaced workers, persons returning to the workforce, and older Filipinos who still
want to work deserve a fair chance to learn, gain con idence, and become employable.

The bill keeps the internship temporary and focused on training and skills
development. It does not create a permanent position in government, guarantee
employment, or allow interns to replace regular government employees. Instead, it
gives participants meaningful exposure to actual workplaces in national government

agencies, local government units, and other government instrumentalities, while
helping them develop skills that can open doors to future employment.

To make the opportunity meaningful and accessible, interns shall receive a daily
stipend equivalent to the highest prevailing regional minimum wage in their region of
assignment. The measure also provides for government-funded accident insurance,
proper supervision and training, fair and transparent selection, protection from
discrimination and abuse, and employment facilitation after the internship. These
safeguards ensure that interns are not merely given work but are given a genuine
opportunity to grow.

Every Filipino who wants to work deserves a chance to prove what they can do,
regardless of when that opportunity comes in their life. Age should not be a barrier
for a quali ied person who is willing to learn, work, and build a better future. By
expanding the GIP, the government can give more Filipinos the experience, skills, and
con idence they need to enter the workforce.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Expanded DOLE
Government Internship Program Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote the right
of every Filipino to decent, productive, and meaningful work; facilitate access to
employment; strengthen employability; and provide practical work experience to
persons who have not previously entered the labor market.

The State recognizes that lack of work experience is a signi icant barrier to
employment. Accordingly, the Government shall establish and maintain an inclusive
internship program that provides quali ied Filipinos with supervised work
experience, workplace orientation, skills development, and employment facilitation
in national and local government agencies and other quali ied government entities.



                                                                           3|Page
The Program shall be implemented without discrimination on the basis of age, sex,
gender, civil status, disability, ethnicity, religion, political belief, or socioeconomic
status, subject to the quali ications and limitations provided in this Act.

SECTION 3. De inition of Terms. —

For purposes of this Act:

   (a) Bene iciary or Intern refers to a quali ied Filipino accepted into the
       Government Internship Program established under this Act;

   (b) DOLE refers to the Department of Labor and Employment;

   (c) Government Internship Program or GIP refers to the supervised,
       temporary, and non-career internship program administered by the DOLE
       under this Act;

   (d) Host Agency refers to a national government agency, local government unit,
       government-owned or controlled corporation, government inancial
       institution, public school, public hospital, public employment service of ice, or
       other government instrumentality authorized to participate in the Program;

   (e) Internship Agreement refers to the written agreement among the DOLE, the
       Host Agency, and the Intern specifying the period of engagement, duties,
       stipend, supervision, insurance coverage, and other program conditions;

   (f) Prevailing Regional Minimum Wage refers to the highest applicable
       minimum wage rate prevailing in the region where the Intern is assigned, as
       determined under applicable wage orders and labor regulations; and

   (g) No Prior Work Experience refers to the absence of substantial previous
       employment or work experience, as may be de ined and veri ied under the
       implementing rules and regulations of this Act. Volunteer work, school-based
       activities, apprenticeships, training, and internships shall not by themselves
       disqualify an applicant.

SECTION 4. Establishment of the Expanded Government Internship Program. —
There is hereby established an Expanded DOLE Government Internship Program,
which shall provide quali ied Filipinos with opportunities to acquire practical work


                                                                             4|Page
experience and develop employment-related competencies through supervised
service in Host Agencies.

The Program shall build upon and strengthen the existing DOLE-GIP. Under the
existing guidelines, internship engagements generally run for a minimum of three (3)
months and a maximum of six (6) months.

The Program shall include the following components:
   (a) Pre-internship orientation and basic workplace preparation;
   (b) Supervised assignment to a Host Agency;
   (c) Practical training in administrative, technical, public service, digital, and other
       non-highly technical functions;
   (d) Life skills, workplace ethics, labor rights, occupational safety and health, and
       career development training;
   (e) Employment counseling, job referral, and registration with appropriate public
       employment services; and
   (f) Evaluation and certi ication of competencies acquired during the internship.

SECTION 5. Coverage and Quali ications. — A person may qualify as a Bene iciary
if he or she:

   (a) Is a Filipino citizen;
   (b) Is at least eighteen (18) years old at the time of application;
   (c) Is unemployed or not engaged in regular employment at the time of
       application;
   (d) Has no prior substantial work experience, as de ined in the implementing
       rules and regulations;
   (e) Is at least a graduate of senior high school, high school under the former
       curriculum, the Alternative Learning System, or a technical-vocational
       program, or possesses an equivalent quali ication appropriate to the
       internship assignment; and
   (f) Meets the documentary, medical, and other reasonable requirements
       prescribed by the DOLE.

The DOLE may give priority to applicants belonging to disadvantaged or vulnerable
groups, including low-income individuals, displaced workers, persons with
disabilities, Indigenous Peoples, victims of armed con lict, rebel returnees, persons
affected by disasters, and residents of geographically isolated and disadvantaged
areas.


                                                                              5|Page
No applicant shall be disquali ied solely because he or she is a senior citizen, provided
that the applicant is physically and mentally capable of performing the duties stated
in the Internship Agreement and satis ies the other requirements of this Act.

SECTION 6. Duration of Internship. — The Internship Agreement shall be for a
minimum period of three (3) months and a maximum period of six (6) months.

An Intern may be admitted only once under the Program, unless the implementing
rules allow a subsequent engagement for a different assignment designed to provide
additional competencies and the following conditions are met:
    (a) The Intern received a satisfactory performance evaluation;
    (b) The subsequent assignment will provide substantially different or additional
        competencies; and
    (c) The subsequent engagement will not be used to evade the regular hiring rules
        of the Host Agency.

The total period of engagement under the Program shall not exceed twelve (12)
months, unless otherwise authorized by the DOLE for exceptional circumstances
stated in the implementing rules and regulations.

SECTION 7. Stipend. — An Intern shall receive a daily stipend equivalent to the
Prevailing Regional Minimum Wage in the region where the Intern is assigned.

The stipend shall be paid for each authorized day of attendance and actual service,
subject to the rules on attendance, lawful absences, holidays, and other circumstances
provided in the implementing rules and regulations.

The stipend shall not be lower than the applicable statutory minimum wage rate for
the region. The DOLE shall ensure that the amount of the stipend is periodically
adjusted whenever the applicable regional minimum wage is increased.

The stipend shall be treated as program assistance and shall not, by itself, be
considered a salary, wage, or compensation arising from a regular employer-
employee relationship with the DOLE or the Host Agency. This provision shall not
excuse the Government or the Host Agency from complying with labor standards,
occupational safety and health requirements, and other applicable laws.

SECTION 8. Insurance and Other Program Support. — Every Intern shall be
provided government-funded group personal accident insurance or an equivalent
insurance coverage for the duration of the Internship Agreement.

Subject to the availability of appropriations, the DOLE may provide or facilitate:

   (a) Transportation or communication assistance for quali ied indigent
       Bene iciaries;
   (b) Reasonable accommodation and support services for persons with
       disabilities;
   (c) Personal protective equipment and other equipment necessary for the
       assignment;
   (d) Training materials and certi ication expenses; and
   (e) Other forms of assistance necessary to ensure meaningful and safe
       participation in the Program.

SECTION 9. Host Agencies. —

The following may participate as Host Agencies:

   (a) National government agencies and their attached agencies;
   (b) Provinces, cities, municipalities, and barangays;
   (c) Government-owned or controlled corporations;
   (d) Government inancial institutions;
   (e) Public schools, state universities and colleges, public hospitals, and other
       public educational or health institutions;
   (f) Public Employment Service Of ices; and
   (g) Other government instrumentalities authorized by the DOLE.

A Host Agency shall:

   (a) Submit a work program specifying the number of Interns requested, the
       assignments, required quali ications, work schedule, training objectives, and
       designated supervisors;
   (b) Execute an Internship Agreement or Memorandum of Agreement with the
       DOLE;
   (c) Provide a safe, accessible, and non-discriminatory work environment;
   (d) Assign a quali ied supervisor to orient, guide, and evaluate each Intern;
   (e) Assign only duties that are appropriate to the Intern’s quali ications and
       training objectives;
   (f) Prohibit the assignment of Interns to hazardous, unlawful, or highly technical
       work without the necessary quali ications, training, and safeguards;
   (g) Maintain accurate attendance and performance records;

                                                                            7|Page
   (h) Ensure that Interns are not used to displace regular employees or ill
       permanent plantilla positions; and
   (i) Submit periodic reports to the DOLE.

SECTION 10. Nature of Internship. — Participation in the Program shall not create
a permanent, temporary, casual, contractual, or career civil service appointment.

An Intern shall not acquire security of tenure, eligibility for automatic appointment,
or a vested right to employment in the DOLE or the Host Agency by reason of
participation in the Program.

Nothing in this Act shall prevent a Host Agency from hiring a former Intern in
accordance with the Constitution, civil service laws, existing quali ication standards,
appropriations laws, and applicable rules on government appointment.

An Intern who is hired by the same Host Agency after completion of the Program shall
undergo the applicable appointment and hiring process. Participation in the Program
shall be considered relevant experience where allowed by applicable quali ication
standards.

SECTION 11. Prohibited Acts. — The following acts are prohibited:

   (a) Charging an applicant any fee as a condition for application, selection,
       admission, or continued participation;
   (b) Requiring an Intern to perform personal errands or duties unrelated to the
       approved work program;
   (c) Using Interns to replace regular employees, evade the creation of authorized
       positions, or perform continuing functions intended for regular personnel;
   (d) Discriminating against an applicant or Intern on any prohibited ground;
   (e) Falsifying attendance, payroll, performance, or bene iciary records;
   (f) Withholding, deducting, or diverting any portion of the stipend without lawful
       basis; and
   (g) Subjecting an Intern to harassment, abuse, exploitation, retaliation, or unsafe
       working conditions.

Any public of icer or employee who violates this section shall be subject to
appropriate administrative, civil, and criminal liability under existing laws, without
prejudice to the termination of the Host Agency’s participation in the Program.



                                                                           8|Page
SECTION 12. Administration of the Program. — The DOLE shall be the lead agency
responsible for the administration, coordination, monitoring, and evaluation of the
Program.

For this purpose, the DOLE shall:

   (a) Establish uniform national guidelines and standards;
   (b) Accredit and monitor Host Agencies;
   (c) Develop a transparent and accessible application and selection system;
   (d) Maintain a national registry and database of Bene iciaries, subject to the Data
       Privacy Act and other applicable laws;
   (e) Facilitate the payment of stipends;
   (f) Provide or coordinate orientation, training, and employment facilitation
       services;
   (g) Establish a grievance and complaints mechanism;
   (h) Monitor compliance with this Act and its implementing rules;
   (i) Publish an annual report on the Program; and
   (j) Coordinate with the Civil Service Commission, Department of Budget and
       Management, Department of the Interior and Local Government, Technical
       Education and Skills Development Authority, Public Employment Service
       Of ices, and other relevant agencies.

The DOLE shall ensure that the selection and deployment of Interns are based on
objective, transparent, and publicly available criteria.

SECTION 13. Local Government Participation. — Local government units may
participate as Host Agencies or implementing partners, subject to the standards and
monitoring requirements of the DOLE.

The DOLE may enter into agreements with local government units for the
identi ication of applicants, conduct of orientations, supervision of assignments,
payment of stipends, and submission of reports.

Participation by a local government unit shall not diminish the authority of the DOLE
to investigate complaints or suspend the participation of a non-compliant Host
Agency.

SECTION 14. Monitoring and Evaluation. — The DOLE shall establish a monitoring
and evaluation system that shall measure, among others:


                                                                          9|Page
   (a) The number and pro ile of applicants and Bene iciaries;
   (b) Completion rates;
   (c) Skills and competencies acquired;
   (d) Timeliness and accuracy of stipend payments;
   (e) Compliance of Host Agencies;
   (f) Employment outcomes after completion of the internship; and
   (g) The accessibility of the Program to disadvantaged groups and persons aged
       above thirty (30) years.

The DOLE shall submit an annual report to Congress containing the results of the
evaluation and recommendations for improving the Program.

SECTION 15. Funding. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the DOLE for the
Government Internship Program and other applicable programs.

Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall
be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

The DOLE may receive grants, donations, and assistance from government agencies,
local government units, and lawfully authorized domestic or international sources,
subject to existing budgeting, accounting, auditing, procurement, and public inancial
management laws.

No external assistance shall impair the Government’s control over the Program or
compromise the rights and welfare of Bene iciaries.

SECTION 16. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Labor and Employment, in
consultation with the Civil Service Commission, Department of Budget and
Management, Department of the Interior and Local Government, Technical Education
and Skills Development Authority, representatives of local government units, public
employment service of ices, employers, workers, senior citizens, persons with
disabilities, and other relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the implementing rules
and regulations of this Act.

The implementing rules shall include:

   (a) The de inition and veri ication of no prior substantial work experience;
   (b) The application, selection, and placement process;

   (c) Documentary and eligibility requirements;
   (d) Stipend computation and payment procedures;
   (e) Attendance, leave, and holiday rules;
   (f) Standards for supervision and performance evaluation;
   (g) Insurance coverage and claims procedures;
   (h) Accessibility and reasonable accommodation measures;
   (i) Grievance and complaint procedures;
   (j) Data protection and recordkeeping requirements; and
   (k) Penalties and administrative sanctions for violations by Host Agencies.

SECTION 17. Transitory Provision. — All existing DOLE-GIP bene iciaries and
Internship Agreements at the time of the effectivity of this Act shall continue to be
governed by the terms of their existing agreements, unless they elect to be covered by
this Act and such coverage is legally and administratively practicable.

The DOLE shall review and revise existing guidelines, agreements, forms, and
procedures to conform to this Act.

SECTION 18. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full
force and effect.

SECTION 19. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 20. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
