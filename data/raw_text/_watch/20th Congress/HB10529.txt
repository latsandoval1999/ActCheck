                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE
       This bill seeks to increase the privilege discount granted to senior
citizens from twenty percent (20%) to twenty five percent (25%).
      On April 23, 1992, Republic Act (RA) No. 7432 was enacted granting
benefits and special privileges to senior citizens in recognition of their
contributions to nation building. RA 7432 was later amended by RA 9257 and
RA 9994, expanding the benefits and privileges to senior citizens. Although the
benefits under the latter law have been extended to other services and
establishments, the twenty percent (20%) discount remains the same.
       Consistent with worldwide trends on population demographics, the
Philippines is also expected to face an ageing population in the coming
decades.1 This will have far-reaching impacts on policy, infrastructure, and
distribution of social benefits. Thus, as early as now, it is crucial for the nation
to prepare our national infrastructure for the upcoming demographic shift.
While a growing older segment of the population presents its own unique
challenges, adaptation of a “silver economy” hinges on a senior population that
are fit, healthy, and strong2.
       On the long run, tackling this demographic shift will depend on how
equipped the country’s senior population will be. Thus, there is a need to
update this benefit to keep on pace with the continuing rise in the cost of
living, current inflation rates, and the positive increase in lifespan of Filipinos,

               AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9444

Congress assembled:

Senior Citizens Benefits Act."

             "(a) the grant of twenty five percent (25%) discount and
