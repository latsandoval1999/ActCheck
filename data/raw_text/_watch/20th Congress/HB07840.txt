                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill aims to reclassify and convert parcels of timerblands in the province of Camarines
Sur into alienable and disposable lands to maximize the potential of the land for whatever legal
and reasonable means to contribute to the economic development of the entire province.
Furthermore, its reclassification and conversion will likewise support social justice, strengthen
land tenure security, and prevent illegal encroachment on the land, as well as increase
employment opportunities within the community, and improve and protect the land.

This kind of reclassification and conversion is embedded within the 1987 Constitution,
specifically under Article XII, Section 2 which states that agricultural, forest or timber, and
mineral lands may be reclassified into alienable and disposable lands. Under the Public Land
Act, the President of the Philippines, through Department of Environmental and Natural
Resources (DENR) is given the mandate to reclassify these lands.

Therefore, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT RECLASSIFYING AND CONVERTING PARCELS OF TIMBERLANDS IN THE
ENTIRE PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR INTO ALIENABLE AND DISPOSABLE PUBLIC
LANDS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to reclassify
and convert certain parcels of agricultural lands into alienable and disposable publics lands
for the benefit of the communities within the province, consistent with the principles of
sustainable development, environmental protection, support social jusice and economic
development, strengthen land tenure security, and prevention of illegal encroachment on the
land.

SECTION 2. Reclassification of Certain Parcels of Timberlands. – Subject to the
evaluation of the Department and Natural Resources (DENR), parcels of timberland in the
entire Province of Camarines Sur are hereby reclassified and declared as alienable and
disposable public lands.

SECTION 3. Technical Description of the Timberlands. – The specific metes and bounds
of the parcels of timberlands in the entire province of Camarines Sur shall be determined,
upon the evaluation and approval of the DENR. The final land technical description shall form
part of this Act upon approval.

SECTION 4. Disposition of Lands. – The reclassified and converted parcels of timberland
shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of the Public Land Act, as amended,
and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations. Qualified bona fide residents and occupants
who have been cultivating or residing in the area for the last ten (10) years prior to the
effectivity of this Act shall be prioritized.

SECTION 5. Environmental Safeguards. – Areas who are classified as forest reserves or
protected areas under Republic Act No. 11038 otherwise known as the Expanded National
Integrated Protected Areas System Act of 2018, watersheds, riverbanks, and other
environmentally critical areas shall not be covered by this Act to ensure that the reclassification
shall not adversely affect ecological balance of the land and its environmental sustainability.

SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – DENR, in coordination with the local
government of Camarines Sur, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations within
ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.
