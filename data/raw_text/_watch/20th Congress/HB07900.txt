                                EXPLANATORY NOTE


        Tourism is an indispensable element of our national economy. It has many
components that create employment, increase investment and foreign exchange earnings,
improve infrastructure, and contribute significantly to the country’s GDP. The 2024 data
from the Philippine Statistics Authority showed that tourism contributed Php2.35 trillion
to our economy, equivalent to 8.9% of our GDP. In terms of employment, 13.8% of our total
employment is in the tourism industry, translated to 6.75 million tourism jobs. Inbound
tourism expenditure was posted at Php 699.98 billion in 2024, an increase of 0.4%. Notably,
there is a 35% increase in outbound tourism expenditure, equivalent to Php 345.68 billion.
This shows that more Filipinos are travelling to international destinations.

         The abolition of the travel tax will be a crucial step in boosting the tourism sector.
It will eliminate the additional travel cost, which impedes the mobility of people. This is the
time when travelling has become affordable for ordinary Filipinos who desire to travel for
leisure, business, or any other purpose. It is projected that the benefits from outbound and
inbound tourism expenditures will surpass the revenues collected from travel tax.

        The approval of this bill and its enactment into law will attune our tourism policy
with our ASEAN agreements that facilitate the seamless movement of people within ASEAN
states and reduce barriers to connectivity, mobility, and the free flow of services.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                     AN ACT
     ABOLISHING THE TRAVEL TAX, REPEALING FOR THE PURPOSE THE PRESIDENTIAL
              DECREE NO. 1183, AS AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1          SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Travel Tax Abolition Act of
 2   2026 .”

 3          SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – The state recognizes the indispensable role of
 4   tourism as a driver of economic development. Towards this end, it encourages and
 5   promotes the unimpeded movement of people for business, leisure, cultural, medical, and
 6   other purposes. It also fosters the growth and development of private enterprises related
 7   to tourism promotion and activities.

 8           In pursuit of this policy, it shall abolish the travel tax levied on all passengers
 9   travelling to international destinations.

10          SECTION 3. Abolition of Travel Tax. – The travel tax levied by the state is hereby
11   abolished, and Presidential Decree No. 1183, as amended, is hereby repealed.

12                 a.) Section 73 of Republic Act 9593, otherwise known as The Tourism Act of
13                     2009, is hereby repealed.
14                 b.) All other references related to the travel tax in any law, decree, regulation
15                     or administrative issuance are hereby repealed.

16          SECTION 4. Refund – Travel taxes collected for flights on or after the effectivity of
17   this Act shall be refunded.

18          SECTION 5. Appropriations – The amount necessary to fund the programs affected
19   by the abolition of travel tax shall be included in the annual budget of the concerned agency
20   under the General Appropriations Act (GAA).

21          SECTION 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations – The Department of Budget and
22   Management and the Department of Tourism, in consultation with relevant government
23   agencies, shall formulate, within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the
24   necessary rules and regulations for its effective implementation.
1         SECTION 6. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
2   unconstitutional, the provisions not hereby affected shall remain in full force and effect.
3
4          SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations or
5   parts thereof, inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Act are repealed,
6   amended or modified accordingly.
7
8          SECTION 8. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days from the date of
9   its publication in the Official Gazette, or a website provided for by law.
