                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Retirement should provide security and dignity to those who have spent
their productive years contributing to the Social Security System (SSS). Yet, the
purchasing power of pensions can gradually decline as the cost of food,
healthcare, utilities, and other basic necessities increases. Without a
predictable mechanism for periodic adjustment, pensioners may find it
increasingly difficult to meet their daily needs.

      The SSS has recognized the need to improve pension adequacy through
its pension reform initiatives, including increases for retirement, disability,
death, and survivor pensioners. These measures demonstrate the importance
of providing pensioners with meaningful and predictable adjustments while
taking into account the long-term sustainability of the Social Security Fund.

     This measure seeks to institutionalize an automatic pension escalation
mechanism so that pension increases will no longer depend solely on periodic
administrative action or discretionary adjustments.

       Under the proposed measure, retirement and disability pensioners shall
receive an annual increase of ten percent (10%) or the official annual average
inflation rate, whichever is higher, while death and survivor pensioners shall
receive an annual increase of five percent (5%) or the official annual average
inflation rate, whichever is higher. The adjustment shall be applied to the
pension in effect immediately prior to the adjustment and shall be compounded
annually.

      The ten percent (10%) and five percent (5%) rates are intended not
merely to offset inflation but also to improve pension adequacy and provide a
predictable minimum annual enhancement to pension income

     The proposed mechanism provides pensioners with a predictable
minimum increase while protecting the purchasing power of their pensions
against higher inflation. At the same time, the measure does not diminish any
existing pension benefit or increase granted under existing law.

      Recognizing the importance of maintaining the long-term viability of the
SSS, the measure requires an annual actuarial valuation and review of the
Social Security Fund. The review will assess the financial impact of the pension
escalation and consider contribution collections, investment income,
membership growth, demographic trends, reserves, and other factors affecting
the Fund. It will also provide Congress and the Department of Finance with
timely information necessary to ensure the continued sustainability of the
pension system.

       The measure further provides for a periodic Congressional review every
five (5) years, allowing Congress to assess the adequacy of pension benefits in
light of prevailing economic and demographic conditions while ensuring the
long-term sustainability of the Social Security Fund.

      Through this measure, pensioners are provided with a fair, predictable,
and inflation-responsive mechanism for maintaining the value of their
pensions, while preserving the financial integrity of the SSS.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

                           “AN ACT
       INSTITUTIONALIZING  THE     AUTOMATIC   PENSION
      ESCALATION MECHANISM FOR RETIREMENT, DISABILITY,
      DEATH, AND SURVIVOR PENSIONERS OF THE SOCIAL
      SECURITY SYSTEM (SSS), PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR,
      AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “SSS
Automatic Pension Escalation Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to
safeguard the welfare of private sector retirees, persons with disabilities, and
qualified beneficiaries of deceased SSS members by ensuring that their
pensions are regularly adjusted to preserve their purchasing power, protect
them against inflation, and provide adequate and predictable income.

      SECTION 3. Institutionalization of Automatic Pension Escalation. -
The automatic pension escalation mechanism of the Social Security System
(SSS) is hereby institutionalized.

      All SSS retirement and disability pensioners shall receive an annual
increase in their monthly pension equivalent to ten percent (10%) or the official
annual average inflation rate, as measured by the Consumer Price Index (CPI)
and published by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), for the immediately
preceding calendar year, whichever is higher.

      Those receiving death and survivor pensions shall receive an annual
increase in their monthly pension equivalent to five percent (5%) or the official
annual average inflation rate, as measured by the CPI and published by the
PSA, for the immediately preceding calendar year, whichever is higher.
    The annual increase shall be applied to the monthly pension in effect
immediately prior to the adjustment and shall be compounded annually.

     The adjustment shall take effect every January 1, beginning on the first
January 1 following the effectivity of this Act.

       SECTION 4. Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all SSS retirement,
disability, death, and survivor pensioners, including existing pensioners as of
the effectivity of this Act.

      It shall not apply to persons whose SSS benefit is payable solely as a
lump-sum benefit in lieu of a monthly pension, in accordance with applicable
laws, rules, and regulations.

      SECTION 5. Funding Source. - The pension escalation provided under
this Act shall be funded from SSS funds and investment income lawfully
available for the payment of benefits, consistent with Republic Act No. 11199,
as amended, and applicable actuarial standards.

      The SSS Board of Directors shall ensure that adequate funds are
allocated for the implementation of the pension escalation mechanism while
maintaining the long-term actuarial viability and financial sustainability of the
Social Security Fund.

      The implementation of this Act shall not authorize the diversion or use of
funds legally earmarked for other statutory obligations of the SSS.

      SECTION 6. Annual Actuarial Review and Report. - The SSS shall
conduct an annual actuarial valuation and review of the Social Security Fund
to assess the financial impact and long-term sustainability of the automatic
pension escalation mechanism established under this Act.

      The actuarial review shall consider, among others:

      (a) The projected financial impact of the pension escalation for the
          succeeding ten (10) years;

      (b) The effect of the pension escalation on the actuarial life, reserves,
          and funding position of the Social Security Fund;

      (c) Contribution collections, investment income, membership growth,
          demographic trends, and other factors affecting the financial
          condition of the Fund; and

      (d) Measures necessary to preserve the long-term actuarial viability and
          sustainability of the Fund.
      The SSS shall submit the annual actuarial report to the Speaker of the
House of Representatives, the Chairpersons of the House Committees on
Appropriations and on Government Enterprises and Privatization, the Senate
President, the Chairpersons of the Senate Committees on Finance and on
Government Corporations and Public Enterprises, and the Secretary of the
Department of Finance, within thirty (30) days from the completion of the
actuarial valuation.

     The actuarial review shall not, by itself, constitute authority to suspend,
reduce, or withhold the pension escalation mandated under this Act.

      SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the SSS, in coordination with the
Governance Commission for GOCCs (GCG), the Department of Finance (DOF),
the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), and other appropriate government
agencies and stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SECTION 8. Periodic Congressional Review. - Every five (5) years from
the effectivity of this Act, Congress shall undertake a review of the automatic
pension escalation mechanism established herein.

      The review shall consider the prevailing inflation rate, pension adequacy,
contribution rates, investment performance, demographic trends, fund
reserves, actuarial life of the Social Security Fund, and other relevant economic
and financial conditions.

      Based on the review, Congress may enact such legislative measures as
may be necessary to ensure the continued adequacy of pension benefits and
the long-term sustainability of the Social Security Fund.

      SECTION 9. Non-Diminution of Benefits. - Nothing in this Act shall be
construed to diminish, reduce, or impair any existing pension benefit, increase,
adjustment, or other benefit granted under Republic Act No. 11199, as
amended, or other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

       SECTION 10. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 11. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
       SECTION 12. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of
general circulation.

      Approved.
