                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to grant citizenship to Sr. Maria Teresa “Vera” Casali
Ravasio in deference to her humanitarian & charitable good works, conducted
over 37 years in the Philippines, devoted to the poor and marginalized in the
spheres of poverty amelioration, education, healthcare and social services.

      An Italian citizen by birth, Sr. Vera, as fondly called by many, has been a
bonafide residence of San Pedro, Laguna, since her arrival to the Philippines in
1985.

       Upon graduation from high school in 1969, she entered convent as a
novitiate, earning a Diploma in Elementary Education 7 yrs later. She taught
elementary school in Cittadini, Bergamo, Italy, for five years. Her missionary
works commenced in India, lasting two years prior to her arrival in the
Philippines, numbered among a group of 7 Indian Ursuline sister nuns.

       The Ursuline Sisters of St. Jerome in Somasca, is a religious congregation
of women founded in Somasca, Italy, in 1826 by Caterina Cittadini and her
sister Giuditta. The congregation was inspired by and informed by the life and
work of St. Jerome Emiliani, a 15th-century priest who founded the Somascan
Fathers.

      The order was invited to the Philippines in 1929 by the Bishop of San
Pablo, Msgr. Teofilo Camomot. He was a friend of the congregation's founder,
Mother Caterina Cittadini, impressed by their work in Italy and believed the
order could make a significant contribution to the Church and the Filipino
people.

      The first group of Ursuline Sisters, pioneered by Sr. Vera, arrived in the
Philippines in 1929 and opened their first convent in the town of Sta. Cruz,
commencing work in education, healthcare, and social services.

       The sisters have made a significant contribution to the Philippines over
the past 90 years. They have educated generations of young people, cared for
the sick and elderly, and promoted social justice. They are considered a valuable
part of the Catholic Church and the Philippine community.

      Today, the Ursuline Sisters of St. Jerome in Somasca have a presence in
11 dioceses in the Philippines.

      As Local Superior, Sr. Vera today oversees 16 schools, 3 hospitals, 2
orphanages, and a number of other social service programs. The congregation
has about 200 members, most of whom are Filipino. The order services
approximately 2,600 families growing year by year.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.
