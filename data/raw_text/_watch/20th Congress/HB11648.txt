                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The NCR Polytechnic Institute Act establishes a modern, unified
polytechnic system in the National Capital Region to strengthen Technical
Vocational Education and Training (TVET) as a strategic driver of national
competitiveness, industrial transformation, innovation, and inclusive growth.
Operating under the supervision of TESDA NCR, the measure integrates and
transforms the TESDA NCR Regional Training Center (NCR RTC) and six (6)
District Training Centers into a single institutional framework. This consolidation
rationalizes existing TESDA technology institutions into an integrated
polytechnic system to achieve stronger governance, operational efficiency,
enhanced institutional performance, and academic excellence across the region.

       As the lead regional institution for higher-level and technology-driven
TVET, the Institute is mandated to deliver Technical Diploma programs and
specialized workforce courses designed in accordance with the Philippine
Qualifications Framework (PQF). The academic framework promotes lifelong
learning through stackable microcredentials, flexible learning pathways, and
articulation agreements with higher education institutions to enable credit
recognition and ladderization toward related bachelor's degree programs.
Furthermore, the legislation establishes Regional TVET Innovation Centers to
serve as regional hubs for applied research, technology demonstration,
prototyping, entrepreneurship, and emerging technology training.

       Governance is structured around a centralized "One System" framework
featuring One Governing Board, One Executive Management System, One
Support Services System, and One Academic Delivery System. The multi-
sectoral Governing Board includes representatives from TESDA, DOST, DOLE,
DTI, LGUs, industry sectors, employers, workers, educational institutions, civil
society, and students. Crucially, the legislation safeguards existing workforce
rights by transferring all assets, personnel, and records without impairing the
positions, rights, or security of tenure of faculty and staff. Initial implementation
will be funded through current TESDA appropriations, with subsequent
operations supported by the annual General Appropriations Act and local
government contributions.


      In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION. 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “NCR Polytechnic
Institute Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
strengthen Technical Vocational Education and Training (TVET) as a strategic
driver of national competitiveness, industrial transformation, innovation, and
inclusive growth.

The State shall establish a modern polytechnic system capable of producing
highly adaptable, technologically proficient, and globally competitive Filipino
workers and technicians through integrated, industry-responsive, and future-
oriented technical education and training.

SEC. 3. Statement of Goals and Objectives. - This Act aims to:

   a. Rationalize and institutionalize TTIs into an integrated polytechnic system
      to achieve stronger governance, operational efficiency, increased
      institutional performance, and academic excellence;
   b. Support workforce development in priority and emerging sectors identified
      under the national and regional development plans;
   c. Deliver/offer higher-level, technology-motivated, and demand-driven
      Technical-Vocational Education and Training (TVET) programs,
      including Technical Diploma and specialized workforce development
      programs;
   d. Promote higher-level TVET by developing curricula designed to support
      ladderization, credit transfer, and progression toward related degree
      programs consistent with the Philippine Qualifications Framework (PQF);
   e. Promote     lifelong    learning   through   microcredentials,   stackable
      qualifications, and flexible learning pathways;
   f. Serve as regional center of excellence and innovation in TVET;
   g. Establish and expand high-leverage partnerships with local and
      international enterprises, government agencies, industry associations, and
      development partners;
   h. Strengthen applied research, technology incubation and innovation,
      ecosystems within the TVET sector; and knowledge generation within the
      TVET ecosystem; and
   i. Operate Regional TVET Innovation Centers that shall serve as hubs for
      applied research, technology demonstration, prototyping, innovation,
      entrepreneurship, and emerging technology training.

SEC. 4. Establishment. – The NCR Polytechnic Institute (the Institute) is hereby
established in the NCR under the supervision of TESDA National Capital Region,
integrating and transforming the TESDA NCR Regional Training Center (NCR
RTC), and six (6) District Training Centers into a unified polytechnic system,
strengthening higher-level technical education and training.

SEC 5. Powers and Functions of the Polytechnic Institute. – The Polytechnic
Institutes shall:

 a. Develop and deliver higher-level TVET and Technical Diploma Programs;
 b. Conduct training for priority, emerging and future industries;
 c. Undertake applied research and innovation activities;
 d. Develop learning resources and instructional technologies;
 e. Establish industry partnerships and enterprise-based education and training
      programs;
 f. Provide trainer development and certification programs;
 g. Operate Regional TVET Innovation Centers;
 h. Enter into local and international partnerships;
 i. Generate and retain income pursuant to existing laws; and
 j.   Establish scholarship and student assistance programs.\

TESDA shall develop an effective, efficient, and organized structure taking into
account program offerings, institutional capacity, geographic considerations,
stakeholder consultations, political realities, and the presence of Regional TVET
Innovation Centers.

SEC. 6. Academic Framework and Curricular Offering. – The NCR
Polytechnic Institute shall serve as the lead institution for higher-level,
technology-driven, and industry-responsive TVET within its area of jurisdiction.
Programs offered by the Polytechnic Institute shall be designed according to the

                                                                               3
Philippine Qualifications Framework (PQF) and shall support ladderized,
stackable, and degree-articulated pathways.

      The Institute may enter into articulation agreements with higher education
institutions to facilitate credit transfer and progression toward bachelor's degrees
and higher qualifications.

Program structures shall incorporate appropriate general education, technical, and
industry-based learning components to facilitate credit recognition, ladderization,
and progression toward related bachelor's degree programs.

      Subject to applicable laws, policies, and articulation agreements with
higher education institutions, competencies and academic credits earned through
Polytechnic Institute programs may be recognized and credited toward related
degree programs.

All programs shall be subject to TESDA quality assurance mechanisms, PQF
standards, national competency standards, international benchmarking, and other
accreditation systems as may be applicable.

SEC. 7. Governance and Administration. – The Institute shall operate under a
unified “One System” framework consisting of One Governing Board, One
Executive Management System; One Support Services System, and One
Academic Delivery System.

      The Governing Board shall be composed of representatives from TESDA,
Department of Science and Technology (DOST), Department of Labor (DOLE),
Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), Local Government Units (LGU),
industry sector representatives, employers, workers, educational institutions, civil
society organizations, and students.

The Governing Board shall have the authority to:
                                                                                  4
   a. Review and Approve institutional development plans;
   b. Review and Approve annual budgets;
   c. Create and review academic policies;
   d. Review and Approve new programs and campuses;
   e. Enter into partnerships; and
   f. Approve organizational structures.

The Institute shall be headed by a President or an Executive Director, who is also
a member of the Governing Board. Under the One Executive Management
System, the President or Executive Director shall be supported by the Executive
Office, Academic Affairs Office, Research and Development Office, Finance and
Administrative Services Office, and other institutional support units.

The One Support Services System shall centralize planning, finance,
procurement, human resources, quality management, legal services, information
systems, and partnership development across all campuses.

The One Academic Delivery System shall oversee curriculum development,
learning materials development, research and innovation, academic policies,
admission and registration systems, scholarship programs, and training delivery
standards.

An Academic Council shall be created and composed of:

   a. President/Executive Director;
   b. Campus Directors;
   c. Academic Heads; and
   d. Industry Representatives.

The administrative staff of the Institute shall be organized by TESDA pursuant
to existing laws, rules, and regulations.

                                                                                5
SEC. 8. Campus Organizational and Staffing Framework. – Each campus shall
be headed by a Campus Director or Chief TESD Specialist and shall be supported
by dedicated personnel responsible for academic delivery, student services,
registration, guidance and counseling, library services, financial services, health
services, and supply and property management.

TESDA shall propose a staffing structure subject to the review and approval of
the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Civil Service Commission
(CSC), and other relevant authorities.

SEC 9. Assets, Liabilities and Personnel. – All fixed and movable assets,
personnel, records and documents of the integrated RTC, D/PTCs, as well as their
liabilities or obligations shall be transferred to the Institute: Provided, that the
positions, rights, and security of tenure of faculty, trainers, and personnel therein
employed prior to their merger into the Institute shall not be impaired in the new
organizational structure and staffing pattern.

All parcels of land belonging to the said RTCs, PTCs, are hereby declared
property of the Institute and shall be titled accordingly: Provided that, should the
Institute cease to exist or such aforementioned parcels of land no longer be needed
by the Institute, the same shall revert to the individual RTCs, PTCs, and TAS
prior to their merger under this Act.

SEC. 10. Appropriations. - The amount needed to initially implement the
provisions of this Act shall be charged against TESDA’s appropriations for the
current year. Thereafter, such amount as may be necessary for the continued
operation of the Institute shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

The respective LGUs concerned, in consultation with TESDA, shall likewise set
aside from any available local revenue an amount deemed appropriate as fund
support to the operation of the Institute established herein.

SEC. 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from
the approval of this Act, the TESDA in coordination with DOST, DOLE, DTI,
DBM, DILG, the concerned LGUs, and such other relevant agencies and
stakeholders, shall prepare and issue the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this ACT.

Sec. 12. Institutional Transition - Within one hundred eighty (180) days from
effectivity, TESDA NCR shall formulate a Transition Plan covering:

   a. Transfer of Assets;
   b. Personnel Integration;
   c. Organizational restructuring;
   d. Academic harmonization;
   e. Budget integration; and
   f. Campus specialization.

SEC. 13. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder thereof not otherwise affected shall
remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 14. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
