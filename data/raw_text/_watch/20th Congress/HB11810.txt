EXPLANATORY NOTE

Since the Philippines is an island country with abundant marine ecosystems and
large coastal waters, it has come to realize how important maritime technology. ocean
research, and coastal monitoring are for promoting national development. The seas and
oceans of the country are a vital source of food, provide people with a means of livelihood,
are used for transportation, contribute to scientific knowledge and offer economic
opportunities. At the same time, the growing problems of marine pollution, coastal crosion,
climate change, the decline in marine resources, and the demand for technological progress
show how essential it is to improve the nation's scientific and institutional abilities in the
fields of maritime research and innovation.

Even though the maritime sector plays a significant part in the country's
development, there is still a requirement to further strengthen a specific institutional
framework which will promote and coordinate marine technology development,
oceanographic research, coastal monitoring, and maritime innovation. Creating a
specialized government agency would offer a more targeted and coordinated method of
developing scientific solutions, progressing marine engineering, supporting research
projects, and encouraging the sustainable use of the country's marine and coastal resources.

We are therefore proposing the creation of the Zamboanga City Maritime
Technology and Ocean Research Agency (ZCMTORA). The Agency will function as a
national government body whose role it will be to promote and coordinate marine scientific
research, to develop maritime technologies, to set up coastal monitoring systems, to support
technology transfer, and to promote innovation in the maritime field. By means of research
collaboration, scientific capacity-building, and the development of suitable marine
research infrastructure, the Agency will aid in enhancing the country's capacity to meet
emerging maritime challenges and to obtain the maximum benefits from its marine
resources.

Zamboanga City is also suggested as the main headquarters of the Agency in view
of its strategic position in south-western Mindanao and its capacity to function as a centre
for maritime research, marine technology development, and scientific cooperation. It is
expected that setting up the Agency in the city will provide opportunities for research
partnerships, technical education, local employment, and the development of maritime-

all over the Philippines.

The Agency should also work together with the appropriate national government
agencies, state universities and colleges, research institutions, local government bodies,
and private-sector organizations in order to facilitate the exchange of scientific know ledge,
prevent unnecessary duplication of current programs, and make sure that its maritime
research activities align with the mandates of existing institutions. Its programs must
support coastal environmental protection, disaster risk reduction, sustainable fisheries, the
conservation of marine biodiversity, and the responsible development of the blue economy.

The measure also includes provision for the Agency's organizational structure,
powers and functions, interagency coordination, the rules and regulations necessary for its
implementation, and the initial appropriation required for its establishment and operation.
It is suggested that Five Hundred Million Pesos (P500,000,000.00) be made available to
assist with the Agency's establishment, research facilities, equipment, personnel, and
priority programs, on condition that it receives congressional authorization and complies
with the government's relevant budgetary and auditing requirements.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT
ESTABLISHING THE ZAMBOANGA CITY MARITIME TECHNOLOGY AND OCEAN
RESEARCH AGENCY, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, PROVIDING FOR
ITS ORGANIZATION AND OPERATIONS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga City Maritime

Technology and Ocean Research Act.”

SEC. 2. Creation of the Agency. — There is hereby established a national government
agency to be known as the Zamboanga City Maritime Technology and Ocean Research Agency

(ZCMTORA), hereinafter referred to as the Agency.

The Agency shall serve as the principal government institution responsible for promoting
and coordinating marine technology development, ocean research, coastal monitoring, and

maritime innovation, in accordance with this Act and other applicable laws.

SEC. 3. Principal Headquarters. — The principal headquarters of the Agency shall be
established in Zamboanga City, Philippines, which shall serve as its principal center for maritime
technology development, oceanographic research, marine environmental monitoring, and

scientific innovation.

The national government shall support the establishment of the Agency's principal research
facilities in Zamboanga City, subject to feasibility studies, environmental requirements, applicable

laws, and the availability of appropriations.

SEC. 4. Mandate, Powers, and Functions. — The Agency shall exercise the mandate,

powers, and functions provided under this Act, including:
(a) Conducting and supporting oceanographic and marine scientific research;

(b) Developing marine technologies, underwater research instruments, ocean observation

systems, and related engineering applications;

(c) Establishing coastal monitoring systems and supporting marine environmental

assessment;

(d) Promoting maritime innovation, technology transfer, research commercialization, and

the sustainable development of the blue economy;

(e) Establishing and maintaining marine research laboratories, coastal monitoring

facilities, and other authorized research infrastructure in Zamboanga City;

(f) Providing scientific information and technical assistance to national government
agencies, local government units, academic institutions, and other authorized

stakeholders;

(g) Supporting the education and training of Filipino marine scientists, oceanographers,

engineers, and maritime technology professionals; and
(h) Performing other lawful functions necessary to accomplish its mandate.

SEC. 5. Organizational Structure. — The Agency shall be headed by a Director-(seneral
appointed by the President of the Philippines and shall have such offices, divisions, personnel, and

facilities as may be authorized under this Act and applicable laws.

SEC. 6. Interagency Coordination. — The Agency shall coordinate with relevant national

government agencies, state universities and colleges, research institutions, local government units,

SEC. 7. Appropriations. — The amount of FIVE HUNDRED MILLION PESOS
(P500,000,000.00) is hereby proposed as the initial establishment and operating fund of the
Agency, subject to congressional authorization and inclusion in the General Appropriations Act or

other lawful funding sources.

Subsequent appropriations necessary for the continued operation and development of the

Agency shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Agency, in coordination with the appropriate government agencies, shall

promulgate the necessary implementing rules and regulations.

SEC. 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or

invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and regulations

or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in

the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation in the Philippines.
