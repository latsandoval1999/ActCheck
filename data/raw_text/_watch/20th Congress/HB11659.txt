                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


This bill seeks to convert the Angat - San Rafael Road, located in the Municipality
of Angat, Province of Bulacan, into a national road.

The Angat - San Rafael Road spans approximately 11.85 kilometers. It serves as
a crucial arterial route connecting the municipalities of Angat and San Rafael,
facilitating the movement of people, agricultural produce, commercial goods, and
public services across eastern Bulacan.

Due to increasing traffic volume and the growing economic activities in the region,
the local government unit faces significant financial constraints in maintaining,
expanding, and upgrading this road to meet safety and structural standards.

Converting this local road into a national road will transfer its repair,
maintenance, and future improvement under the jurisdiction and funding of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH). This development will
enhance regional connectivity, lower transportation costs, ensure public safety,
and bolster economic growth not only for the Municipality of Angat but for the
entire Province of Bulacan.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT CONVERTING THE ANGAT - SAN RAFAEL ROAD IN THE
  MUNICIPALITY OF ANGAT, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A NATIONAL
           ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The Angat - San Rafael Road, stretching approximately eleven point
eighty-five (11.85) kilometers in the Municipality of Angat, Province of Bulacan,
is hereby converted into a national road.

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department’s program the improvement, repair, and maintenance of the Angat -
San Rafael Road, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

SEC. 3. The DPWH shall issue the necessary rules, regulations, and directives to
implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
