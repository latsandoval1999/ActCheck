                             AN ACT
   ESTABLISHING THE PHILIPPINE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ETHICS
 ADVISORY BOARD UNDER THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT, DEFINING ITS
  POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, PROVIDING FOR A NATIONAL FRAMEWORK
  FOR THE ETHICAL GOVERNANCE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, AND
                      FOR OTHER PURPOSES


                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Philippines is among the world's fastest-growing digital economies and stands to
benefit significantly from AI adoption across government, business, agriculture,
manufacturing, education, healthcare, and the business process outsourcing sector. However,
the country currently lacks a centralized institutional mechanism dedicated to examining the
ethical implications of Artificial Intelligence and providing coherent policy guidance across
sectors.

        As AI technologies become more integrated into Philippine society, the absence of a
national ethical governance framework risks creating fragmented policies, regulatory
uncertainty, and insufficient safeguards for Filipino citizens. Questions surrounding
algorithmic fairness, data privacy, synthetic media, automated decision-making, labor
transitions, and the use of AI in public services require sustained multidisciplinary expertise
that extends beyond the mandates of existing agencies.

       This measure seeks to establish the Philippine Artificial Intelligence Ethics Advisory
Board (PAIEAB) under the Office of the President as the country's principal advisory and
coordinating body on AI ethics and governance. By bringing together government agencies,
industry leaders, scientists, academics, legal experts, civil society organizations, labor
representatives, and the youth sector, the Board shall provide a whole-of-nation approach to
addressing the opportunities and risks presented by AI technologies.
       The proposed Board shall formulate a National AI Ethics Framework, conduct
stakeholder consultations, monitor emerging technological developments, recommend policy
safeguards, and advise government institutions on issues involving transparency,
accountability, fairness, human dignity, and public welfare in the deployment of AI systems.

        The establishment of the Philippine Artificial Intelligence Ethics Board represents an
investment not only in technological governance but also in public trust. Responsible
innovation requires institutions capable of ensuring that technological progress remains
aligned with democratic values, human rights, and the long-term interests of the Filipino
people.

         In consideration of the foregoing, the prompt approval of this bill is respectfully
urged.

                             AN ACT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Artificial
Intelligence Ethics Advisory Board Act.”

        SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State recognizes that
Artificial Intelligence (AI) presents unprecedented opportunities to improve public services,
strengthen economic competitiveness, advance scientific research, promote innovation, and
improve the quality of life of Filipinos.

       The State likewise recognizes that AI raises significant ethical considerations
involving human dignity, privacy, fairness, transparency, accountability, democratic
governance, labor, education, public safety, national security, and fundamental rights.

        It is therefore the policy of the State to promote the ethical, responsible, transparent,
inclusive, and human-centered development, deployment, and use of Artificial Intelligence
and to ensure that such technologies remain aligned with the values, interests, and welfare
of the Filipino people.
        SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:
          (a)​ Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to machine-based systems that can infer
               from inputs how to generate outputs such as predictions, content,
               recommendations, or decisions that influence real or virtual environments;

          (b)​Ethical Governance refers to the application of principles, standards, and
              oversight mechanisms that ensure AI systems are developed and used in a
              manner consistent with human rights, public welfare, accountability, fairness,
              transparency, and the rule of law;

          (c)​ High-Risk AI System refers to any AI application that may significantly affect
               the rights, safety, opportunities, or welfare of individuals or communities.

       SECTION 4. Creation of the Philippine Artificial Intelligence Ethics Board. –
There is hereby created the Philippine Artificial Intelligence Ethics Advisory Board
(PAIEAB), hereinafter referred to as the Board, which shall serve as the principal advisory
body of the President on ethical issues relating to Artificial Intelligence.

       The Board shall be attached to the Office of the President for administrative
supervision and policy coordination.

      SECTION 5. Composition. – The Board shall consist of twenty-one (21) members,
composed as follows:

       A. Government Representatives

          1.​ Executive Secretary or authorized representative, as Chairperson;
          2.​ Secretary of the Department of Information and Communications Technology;
          3.​ Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology;
          4.​ Secretary of the Department of Economy, Planning and Development;
          5.​ Secretary of the Department of Justice;
          6.​ Secretary of the Department of National Defense and
          7.​ Secretary of the Department of Trade and Industry.

       B. Private Sector Expert Representatives

       To be appointed by the President from nominees submitted by recognized
       organizations representing:

          1.​ Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning;
          2.​ Information Technology and Software Development;
          3.​ Telecommunications and Digital Infrastructure;
          4.​ Financial Technology;
          5.​ Healthcare and Biotechnology;
          6.​ Manufacturing and Industry; and
          7.​ Startup and Innovation Ecosystems.

       C. Civil Society, Academic, and Professional Expert Representatives

          1.​ A representative from a digital rights or technology governance organization;
           2.​ A representative from a consumer protection organization;
           3.​ A representative from a labor organization;
           4.​ A representative from academia with expertise in AI;
           5.​ A representative from the legal profession with expertise in technology law;
           6.​ A representative from the scientific and research community; and
           7.​ A representative from the youth sector.

        SECTION 6. Terms of Office. – Non-government members shall serve for a term of
three (3) years and may be reappointed for one (1) additional term.

       SECTION 7. Powers and Functions. – The Board shall:

           (a)​ Formulate and periodically update a Philippine AI Ethics Framework;

           (b)​Advise the President, Congress, national government agencies, local
               government units, state universities and colleges, and government-owned and
               controlled corporations on ethical issues relating to AI;

           (c)​ Study and evaluate the ethical implications of emerging AI technologies;

           (d)​Recommend principles, guidelines, and best practices for the ethical
               development and use of AI;

           (e)​ Conduct consultations with stakeholders from government, industry,
                academia, civil society, and affected sectors;

           (f)​ Promote transparency, fairness, accountability, inclusiveness, and respect for
                human dignity in AI systems;

           (g)​Examine ethical issues relating to privacy, algorithmic bias, misinformation,
               synthetic media, labor displacement, education, healthcare, public safety, and
               national security;

           (h)​Encourage public awareness and understanding of AI ethics;

           (i)​ Provide expert guidance to all government entities in crafting internal ethical
                AI-usage policies based on the Philippine AI Ethics Framework

           (j)​ Regularly review government entities’ internal ethical AI-usage policies

           (k)​Establish technical working groups and expert panels as may be necessary;
               and

           (l)​ Perform such other functions as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of
                this Act.

        SECTION 8. National AI Ethics Framework. – Within one (1) year from the
effectivity of this Act, the Board shall formulate a National AI Ethics Framework to serve as
a guide for government agencies, educational institutions, private entities, and other
stakeholders involved in the development or use of AI technologies.
      SECTION 9. Technical Working Groups. – The Board may establish Technical
Working Groups on specialized topics, including:

           (a) AI and Human Rights;
           (b) AI and Democratic Governance;
           (c) AI and Education;
           (d) AI and Labor;
           (e) AI and Healthcare;
           (f) AI and National Security;
           (g) AI and Emerging Technologies; and
           (h) Such other areas as the Board may deem necessary.

       SECTION 10. Annual Report. – The Board shall submit an Annual Report to the
President and Congress detailing:

           (a) Emerging ethical issues relating to AI;

           (b) Developments in AI governance and international best practices;

           (c) Recommendations for legislation and public policy; and

           (d) The activities and accomplishments of the Board.

        SECTION 11. Secretariat. – The Office of the President shall establish a Secretariat
to provide technical, legal, administrative, and research support to the Board.

       SECTION 12. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the
concerned agencies under the General Appropriations Act (GAA).

        SECTION 13. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full
force and effect.

        SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.




      SECTION 15. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
