                                AN ACT
          DECLARING THE WALING-WALING (VANDA SANDERIANA) AS THE
                   NATIONAL ORCIDD OF THE PIDLIPPINES

                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE


           The Philippines is home to one of the most diverse collections of flora in the world,
with the Orchidaceae family being one ofthe largest monocotyledonous families in the country.
There are approximately 1,200 orchid species recorded in the Philippines, of which around
85% are endemic. Among these botanical treasures is Vanda sanderiana, commonly known as
the Waling-waling. The genus Vanda consists of 17 species in the country, 10 of which are
endemic to the Philippine archipelago 1 .
            Vanda sanderiana is a strap-leaved orchid species that is deeply intertwined with our
natural heritage. It is celebrated globally for its stunning blooms and is highly valued in the
international orchid cut flower trade, where it serves as an important parent species in
numerous hybridization programs.
           Despite its global fame and ecological significance-as orchids often serve as vital
bioindicators of environmental health-the Waling-waling faces an existential threat in its
natural habitat. Unregulated commercial collection and habitat destruction have severely
decimated its wild populations. Under the Department of Environment and Natural Resources
(DENR) Administrative Order No. 2017-11, Vanda sanderiana is officially classified as
critically endangered2.
