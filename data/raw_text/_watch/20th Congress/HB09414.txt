                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The 1987 Philippine Constitution mandates the State to protect and
promote the right of all citizens to quality education and to advance the right of
the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with the rhythm and
harmony of nature.

In furtherance of this constitutional policy, Republic Act No. 9512, otherwise
known as the National Environmental Awareness and Education Act of 2008,
recognizes the vital role of educational institutions in promoting environmental
awareness, sustainability education, ecological protection, and responsible
stewardship of natural resources. The law likewise encourages the integration of
environmental education in academic instruction and institutional programs.
The University of Eastern Philippines, being the first state university in the
Visayas region, has consistently demonstrated its commitment to accessible and
quality tertiary education while addressing the socio-economic and
environmental needs of Eastern Visayas and the nation. Guided by its vision of
becoming “a globally competitive green entrepreneurship university,” UEP has
institutionalized sustainability-oriented instruction, research, extension,
governance, and community engagement programs.
The university has also established several sustainability and innovation-centered
research facilities and programs, including the Center for Environmental Studies
and Advocacy (CESA), the Research Center for Climate Change and Resiliency,
the Affiliated Renewable Energy Center, the University Technology Innovation
Center, and other research and extension initiatives dedicated to environmental
protection, sustainable development, and green innovation.

        This proposed measure seeks to formally declare the University of Eastern
Philippines as the National University for Sustainability Studies in recognition of
its institutional leadership in sustainability education, environmental stewardship,
green entrepreneurship, climate resiliency, and community empowerment.

        Moreover, this bill aims to strengthen the university’s capacity to advance
sustainability-centered higher education, expand research and innovation
initiatives, promote environmentally responsible development practices, foster
global academic linkages, and produce graduates equipped to address
contemporary environmental and developmental challenges.

      The enactment of this measure shall further reinforce the State’s
commitment to sustainable development, ecological protection, and inclusive
national progress through transformative and future-oriented higher education.

      In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

in Congress assembled:
SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Revised University
of Eastern Philippines Charter”.

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State is mandated to protect and promote
the right to quality education for all citizens, ensuring it is accessible and relevant
to the national needs. On the other hand, the State is also mandated to protect and
advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with
the rhythm and harmony of nature.

Pursuant to Republic Act No. 9512, otherwise known as the “National
Environmental Awareness and Education Act of 2008”, universities have a
significant and vital role in promoting environmental education, including
programs and projects that seek the promotion of environmental awareness
through environmental education which shall encompass environmental concepts
and principles, environmental laws, the state of international and local
environment, local environmental best practices, the threats of environmental
degradation and its impact on human well-being, the responsibility of the
citizenry to the environment and the value of conservation, protection and
rehabilitation of natural resources and the environment, thus integrating the
principles of sustainability, innovation, entrepreneurship, and environmental
stewardship in the higher educational landscape.

Further, as the first state university in the Visayas region, UEP is tasked with
providing accessible, quality education while addressing the socio-economic
needs of the region.

Guided by national priorities and global educational standards, UEP strives to
empower learners, advance research, and engage communities in sustainable
development initiatives. This commitment is captured in its Mission Statement:

      “Empower students and the community with the integration of green
      entrepreneurship in instruction, research, extension and production for
      sustainable development, and global impact.”

The Vision Statement provides UEP with a long term perspective, aligning the
choices of its leaders and stakeholders with the university’s ultimate aspirations.
A well crafted vision enables UEP to rise above immediate challenges and focus
on achieving its highest goals.

This aspiration is embodied in its Vision Statement: “A globally competitive
green entrepreneurship university.”

To this end, the University of Eastern Philippines (UEP) is hereby declared as the
National University for Sustainability Studies.

SEC. 3. Purposes of the University of Eastern Philippines. – As the National
University for Sustainability Studies, UEP is committed to ensuring relevant and
quality tertiary education with the integration of sustainability principles and
environmental stewardship through green innovations covering instruction,
research and extension, external affairs and internal governance, the UEP shall:

   1. Provide technical and professional training, advanced instruction in
      literature, philosophy, science, and arts, promote scientific and
      technological research, and ensure democratic access to education for
      deserving students both in the Province of Northern Samar and nationwide;

   2. Ensure relevant and quality tertiary education to achieve inclusive growth
      and increase access to quality tertiary education for poor but deserving
      students;

   3. Improve higher education research to promote productivity and
      innovations;

   4. Increase community engagement;

   5. Establish a robust alumni network and strengthen linkages and
      partnerships with a global presence; and

   6. Achieve participatory, transparent, and accountable governance.

These outcomes encompass several key components, including academic
excellence, research innovation, community empowerment, sustainability, and
advocacy for equitable access to education.

SEC. 4. The University. – The University of Eastern Philippines shall be
composed of both the main and external campuses established by its governing
board upon the recommendation of its University President.

The Main Campus is located in the Municipality of Catarman, Province of
Northern Samar and the external campuses shall include the following: UEP
                                                                                   3
Laoang Campus – Laoang, Northern Samar, the UEP Pedro Rebadulla Memorial
Campus – Catubig, Northern Samar and UEP Lavezares Campus – Lavezares,
Northern Samar and those that may be created in the future.

SEC. 5. Academic Freedom. – The University of Eastern Philippines shall enjoy
academic freedom as provided for in Republic Act No. 8292, otherwise known
as the "Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997" and paragraph 2, Section
5 of Article XIV of the 1987 Philippine Constitution.

SEC.    6.   Commitment       to   become     a   globally    competitive    Green
Entrepreneurship academic tertiary education as a significant contribution to
National Development. – The University of Eastern Philippines is committed to
provide accessible high quality tertiary education while addressing the socio-
economic needs of the region. As such, the university through its long-term
perspective pursues the integration of the principles of sustainability, innovation,
entrepreneurship, and environmental stewardship into its core functions in
instruction, research, extension and production for national development.

As a globally competitive green entrepreneurship university, the university
fosters a culture of entrepreneurship focused on green practices and eco-friendly
solutions, equipping the students and stakeholders with the skills and knowledge,
and values necessary to address environmental challenges and drive sustainable
development. It has established research centers for green sustainability duly
approved by the UEP Board of Regents, to wit: Affiliated Renewable Energy
Center, Center for Environmental Studies and Advocacy (CESA), Integrated
Research Laboratory, Diagnostic Laboratory, PhilRice Research Center, Local
Governance Training and Research Institute, Research Center for Climate
Change and Resiliency, UEP Bio-composting: A sustainable Biodegradable
Waste Project, University Biocontrol Technology Learning Facility, University
Technology Innovation Center, Center for Natural Products Research, DTI-

and the UEP Materials Recovery Facility.

SEC. 7. Commitment to the University's Core Values. – The University is
grounded in a set of core values that underpin its operations and culture, namely:
INCLUSIVITY,           RESILIENCE,            INTEGRITY,           INNOVATION,
STEWARDSHIP, COLLABORATION, & EXCELLENCE.

These fundamental principles drive the university's pursuit to excellence ensuring
a culture of continuous improvement strengthening the University of Eastern
Philippines resilience and adaptability in the face of evolving challenges.

SEC. 8. Commitment to Inclusivity and Social Responsibility. – The University
of Eastern Philippines shall serve the Filipino Nation and the humanity. The UEP
commits to foster inclusivity and equal access to tertiary quality education to
uplift economic welfare. In the continuous pursuit of attaining its mission and
vision, the university is committed to fulfill its social responsibility for the
general welfare of the people as magnified by its tagline, "UEP Cares".

SEC. 9. Support to Sports, Culture, and the Arts. – The University of Eastern
Philippines shall undertake and support comprehensive sports programs that
promote physical education, uphold excellence, and encourage competitive
participation in sports activities, instill the university's identity and solidarity,
cultivate discipline and responsibility. It shall continue to support culture and the
arts to promote the importance of our Filipino identity, social well being, and
preserve culture and traditions of the county.

SEC. 10. Administrative Autonomy. – As the National University for
Sustainability Studies, the UEP shall be treated with greater flexibility. It shall
determine its teaching, research and extension programs and policies, thrust,
plans, and standards consistent with its purpose, and according to the national

                                                                                   5
priorities and global educational standards aligned with sustainability and
innovation.

SEC. 11. Administration. – The University of Eastern Philippines shall have
general powers of a corporation as set forth in the Revised Corporation Code of
the Philippines. The administration of the UEP and the exercise of its corporate
powers shall be vested exclusively in the Board of Regents and the President of
the University of Eastern Philippines. There shall be a central administration
headed by the UEP President who shall provide leadership and overall strategic
direction in the four pillars of the university, to wit: instruction, research and
extension, external affairs, and internal governance, including infrastructure
development, linkages and partnerships and resource management to achieve its
vision and mission. The administration of the external campuses shall be vested
in the CAMPUS DIRECTORS, who shall be designated by the Board of Regents,
upon the recommendation of the UEP President, subject to the limitations and
insofar as delegated by the UEP President and authorized by the Governing
Board.

Eastern Philippines shall be the UEP Board of Regents, referred to herein as the
Board, which shall be chaired by the Commissioner, Commission on Higher
Education (CHED) and the UEP President as Vice- Chairperson, respectively,
with the following members:

   a. Chairperson, Committee on Higher Technical Education of the Senate of
      the Philippines;
   b. Chairperson of the Higher and Technical Education of the House of
      Representatives;
   c. Regional Director, Department of Economy, Planning and Development

SEC. 13. Powers of the Board of Regents. – The Board of Regents shall have
the following specific powers and duties:

   a. To receive and appropriate to the ends specified by law such sum as may
      be provided by law for the support of the University;
   b. To enter into Memorandum of Agreement with other agencies
      (government or private) in the fulfillment of the core functions of the
      university in instruction, research and extension, external affairs, and
      administration;
   c. To confer the usual honorary degrees upon persons in recognition of
      learning, statesmanship, or eminence in literature, science or art: Provided,
      that such degrees shall not be conferred in consideration of the payment of
      money or other valuable consideration:
   d. To establish chairs in the colleges and schools mentioned in paragraph (b)
      of this section and to provide for the maintenance or endowment of such
      chairs;
   e. To approve the courses of study and rules of discipline drawn up by the
      University Council as hereinafter provided; to fix the tuition fees required
      of students, as well as matriculation fees, graduation fees and fees for
      laboratory courses; and all special fees;
   f. To provide fellowships for faculty members and scholarships to students
      showing evidence of special merit;
   g. To contract for technical services, under certain conditions;
   h. To prescribe rules for its own government;
   i. To receive in trust legacies, gifts, and donations of real and personal
      property of all kinds and to administer the same for the benefit of the
      University, or of a department thereof, or for aid to any student or students,
      in accordance with the direction or instructions of the donor, and, in default

                                                                                  8
      thereof, in such manner as the Board of Regents may, in its discretion,
      determine;
   j. To appoint upon recommendation of the President of the University,
      professors, associate professors, assistant professors, other members of the
      teaching staff, and all other employees; to fix their compensation, hours of
      service, and such other duties and conditions as he may deem proper; to
      grant to them in his discretion leave of absence under such regulations as
      he may promulgate, and to remove them for cause after an investigation
      and hearing shall have been had; and
   k. To delegate its powers and duties to the University President as it may
      deem appropriate as to expedite the administration of the affairs of the
      University of Eastern Philippines.
   l. To enact for the Government of the University such general ordinances and
      regulations, not contrary to law, as are consistent with the purposes of the
      University.
   m. To promulgate rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the
      purposes and functions of the University of Eastern Philippines.

SEC. 14. Quorum; service of process. – A quorum of the Board of Regents shall
consist of a majority of all the members holding office at the time the meeting of
the Board is called. All processes of the Board of Regents shall be served by the
President of the University or the Board Secretary thereof.

SEC. 15. The University President of the University of Eastern Philippines. –
The President of UEP shall be its chief academic officer, head of the university,
faculty, and the chief executive officer. As such, the President shall exercise the
powers specifically provided for in this Act, those determined by the UEP Board
of Regents, those which pertain to the Office of the University President, and
those which are related or necessary of its functions.
                                                                                 9
The UEP President who shall be elected by the UEP Board of Regents upon the
recommendation of a duly constituted search committee, who shall render full-
time service.

The UEP President shall have a term of four (4) years and may be reappointed to
another term, upon the recommendation of a duly constituted search committee.

In case of vacancy in the Office of the University President by reason of death,
resignation, removal for cause or incapacity of the President to perform the
functions of the office, the Board shall designate an Officer-in-Charge, pending
the appointment of a new President. In no case shall the search and appointment
of the next President be longer than ninety (90) calendar days from the date when
the vacancy occurs. The newly appointed President shall serve a full term,
provided that the designated officer-in-charge shall not be allowed to join as
candidate in the search process and shall be ineligible to vote.

The powers and duties of the President of the University of Eastern Philippines
in addition to those specifically provided in this Act, shall be usually pertaining
to the office of the president similar educational institution, and those delegated
by the Board.

The President of the University shall be assisted by the Vice President for
Administration & Finance, Vice President for Academic Affairs, Vice President
for Student Prosperity and Community Engagement and the Vice President for
Research, Development and Extension.

SEC. 16. The Secretary of the University. – There shall be a Secretary of the
University of Eastern Philippines designated by the UEP Board of Regents, who
shall also be the Secretary of the Board, and who shall keep such records of the
University of Eastern Philippines.
                                                                                10
SEC. 17. The Academic Council. – There shall be an academic council
consisting of the President of the UEP as Chairperson, the Vice President for
Academic Affairs as Vice Chairperson, and all faculty members of the UEP
holding the rank of assistant professors and higher as members.

SEC. 18. The Administrative Council. – There shall be an Administrative
Council consisting of the President of the UEP as Chairperson and the Vice
Presidents, Campus Directors, Deans and Directors and other officials of equal
rank as members.

SEC. 19. The Research Council. – There shall be created a UEP Research
Council, which shall be composed of the University President as the Chairperson,
the Vice President for Research, Development, and Extension as Vice
Chairperson; and with the Vice President for Student Prosperity and Community
Engagement, Vice President for Administration and Finance, Campus Directors,
Director for Research and Extension Services, and Heads of Established Research
Centers, as members.

SEC. 20. Rules of Construction. – No Statutory or other issuances shall diminish
the powers, rights, privileges and benefits accorded to the University of Eastern
Philippines under this Act, or those enjoyed at present by virtue of other issuances
not otherwise modified or repealed under this Act unless subsequent legislation
expressly provides for their repeal, amendment or modifications. Any case of
doubt in the interpretation of the provisions of this Charter shall be resolved in
favor of the academic freedom and fiscal autonomy of the UEP.

SEC. 21. Appropriation. – The amount needed to carry out the implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the
University of Eastern Philippines and its other internal funds. Thereafter, the

                                                                                 11
amount necessary to strengthen its capacity as the National University for
Sustainability Studies shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 22. Transitory provision. – to provide strategic direction and leadership as
the university of eastern Philippines transitions to its original status into a national
university for sustainability studies, and to ensure the effective attainment of its
distinctive and purposeful mandate as such, as declared for in this act, the
incumbent president of the university of eastern Philippines shall serve a new
term as its first president as the national university for sustainability studies
subject to one (1) reappointment; provided, that such reappointment is in pursuant
to the guidelines as stated in section 11 of CHED memorandum no. 16, series of
2009.

SEC. 23. Separability Clause. – Should any provision herein be declared
unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity of the other provisions of
this Act.

SEC. 24. Repealing Clause. – Republic Act No. 4126, otherwise known as the
"An Act Converting the Samar Institute of Technology in the Municipality of
Catarman, Province of Samar, into a university to be Known as the University of
Eastern Philippines, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor" and
all laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations, including other issuances or parts
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are amended, repealed, or modified
accordingly.

SEC. 25. The Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – The Implementing
Rules and Regulations (IRR) of this Act providing for a detailed rules and policies
shall be made within 30 days from its effectivity.
