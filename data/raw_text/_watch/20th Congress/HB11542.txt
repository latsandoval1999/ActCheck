                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE


       Article XIV, Section 1, of the 1987 Constitution declares that the State shall protect and
promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels and shall take appropriate
steps to make such education accessible to all. Further, Section 10 states that science and
technology are essential for national development and progress.

       Pursuant to this constitutional mandate, the Iriga City Science High School was
granted authority by the Department of Education to offer Junior High School specializing in
Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) beginning School Year 2024–
2025. Since then, the school has demonstrated academic excellence and has earned
recognition through its outstanding performances in national and international academic
competitions.

         This bill seeks to provide the school with a statutory basis for its continued operation
and development. It likewise seeks to strengthen the school's institutional mandate and
facilitate its access to appropriate national government support, thereby ensuring the
continuity and sustainability of the quality science-oriented education it provides. Various
stakeholders, including the Local School Board of the City of Iriga, concerned local
government officials, and the DepEd Region V, have expressed their support for this measure.

       The establishment of the Iriga City National Science High School will expand access
to specialized science and STEM education for academically capable learners in the district
and neighboring municipalities, particularly those who may not have the financial means to
pursue similar programs outside the Rinconada area. In the long run, it will contribute to the
development of a pool of future scientists, engineers, and other professionals in Rinconada
by providing learners with a specialized educational environment that nurtures their aptitude
in STEM.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY
  SALVACION, CITY OF IRIGA, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR, TO BE KNOWN AS
 THE IRIGA CITY NATIONAL SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
                                THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Establishment of a National Science High School. – There is hereby
established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, a national science high
school in Barangay Salvacion, City of Iriga, Province of Camarines Sur to be known as Iriga
City National Science High School.

SECTION 2. Appropriations. – The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of the Iriga City National Science High School,
the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
approval of this Act, the Secretary of Education shall formulate the rules and regulations
implementing the provisions of this Act. Such implementing rules and regulations shall provide
for the school's organization, administration, operations, and management, in accordance with
Republic Act No. 9155 or the "Governance of Basic Education Act of 2001", Republic Act No.
10533 or the "Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013", and other basic education-related laws.

SECTION 4. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
