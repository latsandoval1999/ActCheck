                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Santa Maria in the Province of Bulacan is a rapidly expanding
suburban and commercial hub in Central Luzon. Serving as a crucial gateway
between Metro Manila and inner Bulacan municipalities, its road networks
experience an increasingly heavy volume of daily vehicular traffic, ranging from
private vehicles to commercial trucks carrying agricultural goods.

The Santa Maria River Side Road, which spans a length of 2.18 kilometers within
the municipality, serves as a critical bypass and feeder road that helps decongest
primary arterial roads, facilitates faster local transportation, and connects
surrounding barangays to vital economic centers, public markets, and schools.

However, due to its current status as a municipal road, maintenance and repair
are funded solely through the limited local government budget. Given the high
traffic density and environmental exposure along the river basin, the road
requires continuous structural upgrades, concrete re-paving, adequate drainage
infrastructure, and regular safety enhancements that exceed the financial
capacity of the local government unit.

Converting the Santa Maria River Side Road into a National Road will transfer its
maintenance, repair, and future improvement responsibilities to the Department
of Public Works and Highways (DPWH). This transition will ensure a higher
standard of road quality, enhanced safety features, and sustained national
infrastructure funding

In view of the foregoing urgent considerations, the immediate passage of this bill
is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT CONVERTING THE SANTA MARIA RIVER SIDE ROAD IN THE
   MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A
       NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The Santa Maria River Side Road, with a length of 2.18 kilometers,
situated in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Bulacan, is hereby
converted into a National Road under the jurisdiction and supervision of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).
SECTION 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department's program the improvement, repair, and maintenance of the said
road, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary
rules, regulations, and directives to implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved
