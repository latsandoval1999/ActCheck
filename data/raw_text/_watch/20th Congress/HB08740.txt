                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

            Mindanao plays a critical role in ensuring the Philippines’ food security and
    agricultural productivity. The island possesses vast agricultural lands, favorable climatic
    conditions, and abundant fisheries resources that allow it to contribute significantly to the
    country’s food supply.

        Studies show that Mindanao produces around 40% of the country’s food requirements and
contributes over 30% of the Philippines’ total food trade, making it a key pillar of the national food
system. In addition, Mindanao accounts for about one-third of the country’s total agricultural
production, with the region dominating the production of several key commodities such as bananas,
pineapples, coconuts, corn, and cacao. The island produces more than 80% of the Philippines’
bananas, one of the country’s major agricultural exports.

        Despite this immense potential, Mindanao’s agricultural sector continues to face several
structural challenges, including inadequate infrastructure, limited access to credit and modern
technology, insufficient research and development support, and logistical constraints that affect the
efficient movement of agricultural goods.

         This bill seeks to formally declare Mindanao as the “Food Basket of the Philippines” and
to establish a comprehensive framework to strengthen agricultural development in the region. The
measure promotes investments in agricultural infrastructure, research and innovation, climate-
resilient farming practices, access to financing, and stronger market linkages for farmers and
fisherfolk.

        By strengthening agricultural productivity and support systems in Mindanao, the State aims
to enhance national food security, stimulate rural economic development, and improve the
livelihoods of farmers and fisherfolk across the region.

     AN ACT DECLARING MINDANAO AS THE FOOD BASKET OF THE PHILIPPINES,
     PROVIDING FOR ITS SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURAL DEVELOPMENT, AND FOR
                              OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

1            SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Mindanao Food Basket Act”.

2             SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to ensure
3    national food security, alleviate poverty, and promote inclusive economic growth through the
4    sustainable development of the agricultural and fisheries sectors. Towards this end, the State
5    recognizes Mindanao's crucial role as the primary source of the nation's food supply and shall
6    prioritize its development as the “Food Basket of the Philippines.”

 7           SEC. 3. Declaration of Mindanao as the Food Basket of the Philippines. – Mindanao,
 8   comprising its various regions and provinces, is hereby officially declared the “Food Basket of the
 9   Philippines” in recognition of its significant contribution to the country's agricultural and fisheries
10   production and its immense potential for further growth.

11           SEC. 4. Sustainable Agricultural Development Mandate. – To realize Mindanao's full
12   potential as the Food Basket of the Philippines, the following shall be prioritized and implemented
13   by relevant government agencies, including but not limited to the Department of Agriculture (DA),
14   Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR), Department of Environment and Natural               Resources
15   (DENR), Department of Science and Technology (DOST), Department of Trade and Industry
16   (DTI), Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), and local government units (LGUs):

17               a) Infrastructure Development: Accelerate the construction and rehabilitation of vital
18                  agricultural infrastructure, including irrigation systems, farm-to-markets roads,
19                  post-harvest facilities, cold storage, and modern port facilities;

20               b) Research and Development (R&D): Intensify R&D initiatives for climate-resilient
21                  crops, improved livestock and aquaculture practices, pest and disease management,
22                  and value-adding technologies;




                                                                                                          1
1               c) Access to Credit and Markets: Facilitate farmers and fisherfolks access to
2                  affordable credit, crop insurance, and direct market linkages, reducing reliance on
3                  intermediaries.

4               d) Capacity Building and Extension Services: Provide comprehensive training
5                  programs, technical assistance, and extension services on modern farming
6                  techniques, sustainable agriculture, and entrepreneurship.

7               e)    Sustainable Practices: Promote and incentivize environmentally sound and
8                    sustainable agricultural practices, including organic farming, agroforestry, and
9                    responsible resource management.

10              f) Investment Promotion: Attract and facilitate public and private investments in
11                 Mindanao's agricultural and agribusiness sectors, including processing and
12                 manufacturing.

13              g) Peace and Order: Sustain efforts to ensure peace and stability in Mindanao, creating
14                 a secure environment conducive to agricultural productivity and investment.

15           SEC. 5. Mindanao Food Basket Development Council. – A Mindanao Food Basket
16   Development Council (MFBDC) shall be created to be chaired by the Secretary of the Department
17   of Agriculture, with representatives from relevant government agencies, local government units,
18   and agricultural stakeholders in Mindanao. The MFBDC shall be responsible for formulating,
19   coordinating, and monitoring the implementation of strategic plans and programs to achieve the
20   objectives of this Act.

21           SEC. 6. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act
22   shall be included in the General Appropriations Act.

23           SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
24   effectivity of this Act, the Department of Agriculture, in coordination with other relevant
25   government agencies and stakeholders, shall formulate the necessary rules and regulations for its
26   effective implementation.

27          SEC. 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional,
28   such declaration shall not affect the validity and effectivity of the other provisions hereof.

29           SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations, or, parts
30   thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

31           SEC. 10. Effectivity – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days from its publication in
32   the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

33          Approved,
