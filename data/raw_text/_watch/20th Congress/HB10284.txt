                            EXPLANATORY NOTE


Filipino farmers frequently sell their harvest at prices below the actual cost of
production, squeezed between high input costs and the market power of traders
and middlemen. Meanwhile, government support often arrives as in-kind
assistance that fails to address farmers' most urgent need: cash to cover the gap
between production cost and market price.

This bill guarantees a minimum farmgate price for staple crops and provides a
direct, unconditional cash subsidy to registered farmers whenever prevailing
market prices fall below the guaranteed floor, ensuring farming remains a viable
livelihood.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Farmers' Fair Price and Direct
Subsidy Act."
      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State shall ensure that farmers
receive fair and just compensation for their produce and shall protect them from
the volatility of market prices.
       SECTION 3. Guaranteed Floor Price. — The Department of Agriculture
shall annually compute and publish a guaranteed floor price for palay, corn, and
priority vegetable crops, based on the actual cost of production plus a reasonable
margin, to be reviewed every cropping season.
       SECTION 4. Direct Cash Subsidy. — Whenever the prevailing market price
of a covered crop falls below the guaranteed floor price, registered farmers under
the Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) shall automatically
receive a direct cash subsidy equivalent to the difference, up to a maximum area
of three (3) hectares per farmer.
      SECTION 5. Farmers' Price Stabilization Fund. — There is hereby created a
Farmers' Price Stabilization Fund to finance the subsidy under Section 4, to be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act and augmented by any
unutilized balance of existing agricultural support funds.

      SECTION 6. Direct Disbursement. — Subsidies shall be disbursed directly
to farmers through government or accredited rural bank accounts or electronic
wallets, without passing through intermediaries, cooperatives officers, or traders.
     SECTION 7. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year's
appropriations of the Department of Agriculture, and thereafter included in the
annual General Appropriations Act.
      SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The Department of
Agriculture shall issue implementing rules within sixty (60) days from effectivity.
      SECTION 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid, the other provisions not affected shall remain in force.
      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, and
issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
      SECTION 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
