                        AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL DIGITAL SPORTS ARCHIVE FOR THE
SYSTEMATIC COLLECTION, PRESERVATION, AND PUBLIC
DISSEMINATION OF SPORTS RECORDS AND DATA INVOLVING
FILIPINO ATHLETES AND PARA-ATHLETES, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The State recognizes the importance of preserving and promoting the
achievements of Filipino athletes and para-athletes as part of the nation’s cultural,
historical, and sporting heritage. Sporting events, whether at the international or
local level, document the journey, performance, and progress of athletes and
serve as valuable resources for public engagement, education, and future
development.

      Pursuant to Section 15, Article XIV of the 1987 Philippine Constitution,
the State shall conserve, promote, and popularize the nation’s historical and
cultural heritage and resources. Complementarily, Section 24, Article II
recognizes the vital role of communication and information in nation-building,
underscoring the need for accessible platforms for the dissemination and
preservation of information.

      Despite the increasing participation of Filipino athletes and para-athletes
in both international and local competitions, there is currently no centralized and
accessible repository of sports records, game footage, athlete profiles, and related
  data. Existing materials are often fragmented across various platforms,
  inconsistently documented, or entirely unavailable to the public. This results in
  the loss of valuable historical records and limits opportunities for analysis,
  recognition, and sustained engagement.

        Moreover, the absence of a unified digital archive, supported by
  standardized records management and archival practices, hinders the ability of
  coaches, researchers, policymakers, and aspiring athletes to access reliable data
  for performance analysis, training development, and evidence-based decision-
  making.

         This proposed measure seeks to establish a National Digital Sports Archive
  that will serve as a centralized, publicly accessible platform for the systematic
  collection, preservation, and dissemination of Philippine sports data and
  audiovisual materials, in coordination with relevant agencies, including the
  National Archives of the Philippines, to ensure proper records management and
  long-term preservation.

        By institutionalizing a digital archive, the State affirms its commitment to
  preserving the nation’s sporting heritage, promoting transparency and
  accessibility, and strengthening the overall sports ecosystem.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
  sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National Digital
Sports Archive Act.”

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to:
       a. Preserve and promote the achievements and contributions of Filipino
          athletes and para-athletes as part of the nation’s cultural and sporting
          heritage;
       b. Ensure the systematic collection, documentation, and preservation of
          sports records, data, and audiovisual materials;
       c. Provide accessible and reliable information to support athlete
          development, research, policymaking, and public engagement;


                                                                                3
      d. Utilize digital technologies to enhance transparency, accessibility, and
         efficiency in sports information management; and,
      e. Strengthen the overall sports ecosystem through data-driven
         development and historical preservation.

      Section 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following
terms are hereby defined:
      a. Digital Sports Archive refers to a centralized and publicly accessible
          digital repository of sports-related records, data, and audiovisual
          materials;
      b. Sports Records refer to official results, statistics, rankings, and
          performance data of athletes and teams;
      c. Audiovisual Materials refer to recordings of sporting events, including
          video footage, photographs, and related media;
      d. Athletes and Para-Athletes refer to individuals engaged in organized
          sports at all levels, including those representing the Philippines in
          international competitions; and
      e. Sports Organizations refer to public or private entities involved in the
          administration, training, development, and promotion of athletes, para-
          athletes, and sporting events, including, but not limited to, the Philippine
          Sports Commission (PSC), the Philippine Olympic Committee (POC),
          the Philippine Paralympic Committee (PPC), recognized National
          Sports Associations (NSAs), local government units (LGUs),
          educational institutions, collegiate athletic associations, and
          professional sports leagues.

       Section 4. Establishment of the National Digital Sports Archive. – A
National Digital Sports Archive, hereinafter referred to as Archive, is hereby
established as a centralized platform for the collection, preservation, and
dissemination of sports records, athlete information, and audiovisual materials
involving Filipino athletes and para-athletes.

      Section 5. Coverage of the Archive. – The Archive shall include, but not
be limited to:
      a. Records and data from international and major local sporting events;
      b. Athlete and para-athlete profiles, including participation history and
          achievements;
                                                                                    4
      c. Statistical data, rankings, and performance metrics;
      d. Audiovisual materials documenting competitions and authorized
         training activities; and,
      e. Historical records of Philippine sports.

       Section 6. Data Collection and Mandatory Submission. – All government
agencies, government-owned or -controlled corporations, LGUs, and private
sports organizations covered under Section 3(e) of this Act shall regularly submit,
upload, and update official sports records, historical data, and public-domain
audiovisual materials to the Archive. The PSC, in coordination with the
Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), shall
establish secure digital submission protocols and data templates to ensure the
standardization and interoperability of all submitted files, subject to the provisions
of Sections 11 and 12 of this Act.

      Section 7. Archival Standards and Records Management. – The National
Archives of the Philippines (NAP) shall provide technical guidance on records
management, archival standards, metadata systems, and long-term preservation of
materials under the National Digital Sports Archive, in accordance with Republic
Act No. 9470, otherwise known as the “National Archives of the Philippines Act
of 2007,” and other applicable laws and regulations.

       Section 8. Digital Preservation and System Security. – The PSC, in
coordination with the DICT and the NAP, shall establish and maintain appropriate
digital infrastructure, cybersecurity protocols, backup storage systems,
interoperability standards, and long-term preservation strategies for the
protection, maintenance, and continued accessibility of records and audiovisual
materials contained in the Archive.
       The implementing agencies shall likewise undertake the digitization and
preservation of historical and legacy sports records, subject to the availability of
funds and applicable intellectual property laws.

      Section 9. Public Access. – The Archive shall be made accessible to the
public through a digital platform. Access to materials contained therein may be
subject to tiered or restricted viewing classifications, consistent with applicable
laws on data privacy, intellectual property, licensing agreements, commercial
broadcasting rights, and national security concerns.
                                                                                    5
      Publicly available materials may include official statistics, historical
records, athlete profiles, and other non-restricted content, while copyrighted or
commercially protected audiovisual materials may be subject to controlled,
limited, delayed, or research-only access, in accordance with this Act and other
applicable laws.

      Section 10. Inter-Agency Coordination. – The Philippine Sports
Commission (PSC) shall serve as the lead implementing agency of this Act. In the
management, operationalization, and technical development of the Archive, the
PSC shall act in close coordination with concerned public and private entities,
including, but not limited to, the DICT, the NAP, the Philippine Olympic
Committee (POC), the Philippine Paralympic Committee (PPC), recognized
NSAs, and concerned LGUs.

       Section 11. Data Privacy and Intellectual Property Protection. – The
collection, processing, storage, and dissemination of data under this Act shall
comply with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the “Data Privacy Act
of 2012,” and applicable intellectual property laws.
       No protected materials shall be reproduced or distributed without proper
authorization from rights holders, except as may be allowed under law.

       Section 12. Protection of Commercial Licensing, Broadcasting, and
Intellectual Property Rights of Private Leagues and Media Networks. – The
collection and preservation of sports records, statistics, and audiovisual materials
by the Archive shall not diminish, alter, transfer, or infringe upon the exclusive
intellectual property rights, copyrights, and related rights of professional sports
leagues, collegiate athletic associations, tournament organizers, and their
authorized media broadcast partners, as protected under Republic Act No. 8293,
otherwise known as the “Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines.”
       To safeguard these commercial and proprietary interests, the
implementation of this Act shall be governed by the following parameters:
       a. Retention of Ownership and Exclusive Exploitation – The submission
          of broadcast footage, photographs, or related media to the Archive shall
          not constitute a transfer of copyright or a waiver of the right holder's
          exclusive authority to commercially exploit, license, sell, or syndicate
          such content. Professional leagues and media networks shall retain

                                                                                  6
         absolute ownership over their respective tape vaults and archival
         properties;
      b. Digital Rights Management (DRM) and Access Restrictions –
         Audiovisual materials submitted to the Archive that are subject to active
         commercial licenses or proprietary copyrights shall be protected by
         appropriate digital protection measures. Public access to copyrighted or
         broadcast-protected materials within the digital platform shall be strictly
         limited to non-commercial, academic, historical, and research purposes,
         in accordance with the principles of Fair Use under Section 185 of
         Republic Act No. 8293. The Archive shall strictly prohibit the
         unauthorized reproduction, downloading, distribution, or rebroadcasting
         of such secured media files;
      c. Moratorium on Public Availability – Commercial broadcast networks
         and private leagues may negotiate a reasonable moratorium period with
         the PSC, during which time newly submitted high-value commercial
         broadcasts or footage shall remain sealed from public viewing to protect
         primary broadcast windows and commercial syndication cycles; and
      d. Exclusion of Proprietary Commercial Data – The mandatory data
         submission requirements under Section 6 of this Act shall apply strictly
         to public performance statistics, tournament results, and historical
         achievements, and shall not compel private leagues or sports
         organizations to disclose proprietary sports analytics software,
         confidential corporate data, trade secrets, or financial records.

      Section 13. Monitoring, Evaluation, and Reporting. – The Philippine
Sports Commission shall establish a monitoring and evaluation system to ensure
the continuous updating, maintenance, and effectiveness of the Archive.
      An annual report shall be submitted to Congress, through the House
Committee on Youth and Sports Development and the Senate Committee on
Sports, detailing implementation status, utilization of the Archive, challenges
encountered, and recommendations for improvement.

     Section 14. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

       Section 15. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Philippine Sports
Commission, in coordination with relevant agencies, shall promulgate the

      Section 16. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

      Section 17. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, issuances,
decrees, rules, and regulations inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of
this Act are deemed amended, modified, or repealed accordingly.

       Section 18. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
