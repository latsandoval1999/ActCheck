                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to provide hospital benefits and other healthcare privileges to
public school teachers and their qualified dependents. It grants discounts on
consultations and other hospital services, and expands their Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) coverage for medical expenses.

        Public school teachers shoulder responsibilities that extend beyond classroom
instruction. Amid the demands of the profession and rising healthcare costs, public
school teachers and their families face financial and health-related risks that may affect
their capacity to perform their duties and remain in public service.

        Republic Act (RA) No. 4670, otherwise known as the “Magna Carta for Public
School Teachers”, provides annual medical examinations and protects teachers against
work related illnesses and injuries. However, it does not provide hospital discounts,
priority access to hospital services, expanded health insurance coverage, or healthcare
benefits for qualified dependents.

       This measure augments the benefits provided under RA No. 4670 by providing
discounts on consultations and other hospital services, fast lane facilities in government
hospitals, and an additional ten percent (10%) PhilHealth coverage for public school
teachers and their qualified dependents.

       The nation’s education system depends on the well-being of those entrusted to
teach future generations. Expanding healthcare support for public school teachers
affirms the State’s commitment to protecting the welfare of our public school teachers in
recognition of their indispensable role in national development.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR HOSPITAL BENEFITS AND OTHER PRIVILEGES FOR PUBLIC
SCHOOL TEACHERS AND THEIR DEPENDENTS, AND APPROPRIATING
FUNDS THEREFOR

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Public School Teachers’
Hospital Benefits Act”.

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby the declared policy of the State to adopt
an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development which shall endeavor
to make essential health and other social services available to all people at affordable
costs. Towards this end, the State shall protect the interests and promote the well-being
of Filipino public school teachers and their dependents through the provision of medical
services.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

           a. Public School Teacher refers to a person who is registered at the
       Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) and is engaged in classroom teaching
       in all schools operated by the government, with the exception of professorial staff
       of state colleges and universities;​

           b. Dependent refers to:

                 i.   Legitimate spouse;

                 ii.  Legitimate, illegitimate or legally adopted children, chiefly
               dependent upon and living with the teacher if such child is not more than
               eighteen (18) years of age, unmarried and not gainfully employed or is
               over the age of majority but incapacitated and incapable of self-support
               due to a mental or physical defect; and​

                iii. The teachers’ parents who are dependent upon the teacher for
               support.

      SEC. 4. Mandate. – To ensure the availability, accessibility and affordability of
medical benefits, all government hospitals are hereby mandated to:

          a.​ Provide for health care services to all teachers and their legal dependents
       which shall include preventive, promotive, diagnostic, curative and rehabilitative
       programs;​

          b.​ Conduct medical and other relevant diagnostic examination to ensure the
       physical and mental capability of all teacher applicants; and​

           c.​ Set-up a system that will effectively monitor the health condition of
       teachers and their dependents.

      SEC. 5. Benefits and Privileges. – To carry out the purposes of this Act, all
government hospitals shall:​

          a.​ Grant at least ten percent (10%) discount on consultations and other
       hospital needs for teachers and their dependents; and​

          b.​ Provide fast-lane facilities to serve teachers and their dependents.

       SEC. 6. Requirements for Availing Hospital Benefits. – Public School Teachers must
bring their Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) Identification Card and valid
proof of employment in a public school (like ID). Furthermore, if their dependents wish
to avail of this benefit, they must also be accompanied by the teacher and must bring
one (1) government ID or Birth Certificate.

      SEC. 7. Philippine Health Insurance Corporation. – The Philippine Health Insurance
Corporation is hereby mandated to increase by ten percent (10%) the health care
coverage of teachers and their dependents for medical expenses.

        SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Health shall formulate the rules and regulations
necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.

       Approved,
