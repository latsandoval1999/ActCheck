                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The continuing modernization of public transportation is necessary to provide the
riding public with a safer, more reliable, accessible, and efficient transport system. The
transition, however, also presents significant financial and livelihood challenges to small
public utility jeepney and tricycle operators and drivers who may not have the resources
to immediately comply with modernization requirements.

      This measure seeks to ensure that modernization is inclusive and does not
unnecessarily displace small transport operators and drivers. It provides a special low-
interest financing window, with an interest rate not exceeding four percent (4%) per
annum and a repayment period of at least seven (7) years, together with reasonable
transition assistance for qualified small transport operators.

      It likewise provides a minimum five-year transition period for small transport
operators owning three (3) units or fewer and protects them from being compelled to
consolidate with corporate operators solely to participate in the modernization program
or access assistance under the Act.

       To protect affected drivers, the measure provides for priority consideration for
employment in modernized fleets, free retraining through TESDA, and transitional
livelihood assistance for up to three (3) months, subject to eligibility requirements and
availability of appropriations.

       Through these measures, the bill seeks to make public transport modernization a
just, gradual, and inclusive transition—modernizing the system while protecting the
livelihood of those who have long provided this essential public service.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                               “AN ACT
      PROVIDING INCENTIVES AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR THE
      MODERNIZATION OF PUBLIC UTILITY JEEPNEYS AND TRICYCLES,
      ENSURING THE PROTECTION OF DRIVERS AND SMALL TRANSPORT
      OPERATORS DURING THE TRANSITION, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Inclusive Public Transport
Modernization Act.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to continue the
modernization of public transportation in a manner that promotes safe, reliable, accessible,
affordable, and efficient public transport while protecting the livelihood of small transport
operators and drivers during the transition.

      SECTION 3. Low-Interest Financing. - The Land Bank of the Philippines and the
Development Bank of the Philippines shall establish a special financing window for the
acquisition of modernized public utility vehicles by qualified individual transport operators
and small cooperative operators, with an interest rate not exceeding four percent (4%) per
annum and a repayment period of not less than seven (7) years, subject to applicable
banking, credit, prudential, and government financial regulations.

      The concerned government financial institutions shall adopt simplified and
reasonable documentary and credit requirements appropriate to qualified small transport
operators, consistent with prudent lending standards.

      The National Government shall, subject to the availability of appropriations, provide
such interest-rate support, credit guarantees, or other appropriate financial mechanisms as
may be necessary to facilitate the implementation of this Section.

      SECTION 4. Graduated Compliance and Voluntary Consolidation. - Small
transport operators owning three (3) units or fewer shall be given an extended compliance
period of not less than five (5) years from the effectivity of this Act to comply with applicable
modernization requirements.

      No qualified small transport operator shall be compelled to consolidate with a
corporate operator solely as a condition for continued participation in the modernization
program or access to assistance under this Act.

      Nothing herein shall impair the lawful authority of the Land Transportation
Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) or other competent government agencies to
enforce safety, environmental, route, and public-service requirements.

      SECTION 5. Driver Protection. — Displaced drivers of phased-out or replaced
covered vehicles shall be given priority consideration for employment by modernized fleet
operators serving the same route and shall be entitled to free retraining and skills
development through the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA),
subject to applicable licensing, safety, and competency requirements.

       SECTION 6. Transitional Livelihood Assistance. - Drivers whose livelihood is
temporarily interrupted by modernization-related vehicle replacement or transition shall be
entitled, subject to eligibility requirements and availability of appropriations, to a
transitional livelihood allowance for a period not exceeding three (3) months.

       SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Transportation (DOTr), in coordination with the
LTFRB, TESDA, Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), Land Bank of the
Philippines, Development Bank of the Philippines, and other concerned agencies, shall
promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the implementation of this Act.

      SECTION 8. Appropriations.- The amount necessary for the implementation of this
Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the concerned agencies and
thereafter included in the annual General Appropriations Act, subject to existing budgeting,
accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

      SECTION 9. Harmonization, Separability, Repealing and Effectivity Clauses. -
This Act shall be implemented in harmony with existing laws and government programs on
public transport modernization.

      If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining
provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

      All laws, rules, regulations, and issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with this Act
are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

       This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
