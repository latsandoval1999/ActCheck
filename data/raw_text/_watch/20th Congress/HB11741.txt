16                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

17          The Technological University of the Philippines (TUP) is a premier state
18   university specializing in engineering and technology education. Its extension
19   campus in the Municipality of Cuenca, Province of Batangas has served as a vital
20   institution for students in the provinces of Calamba, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and
21   Quezon (CALABARZON Region), providing access to quality education in
22   industrial teaching education.

23         Converting the Cuenca Extension Campus into a regular campus will
24   strengthen its capacity to deliver higher education, expand academic programs,
25   improve faculty and library resources, and ensure compliance with the Commission
26   on Higher Education (CHED) standards. This measure will promote regional
27   development, enhance human resource competitiveness, and contribute to national
28   progress.

29         In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
30   sought.

16                             AN ACT
17      CONVERTING THE TECHNOLOGICAL UNIVERSITY OF THE
18    PHILIPPINES (TUP) EXTENSION CAMPUS IN THE MUNICIPALITY
19   OF CUENCA, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS, INTO A REGULAR CAMPUS
20                 AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
21
22        Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
23   in Congress assembled:

24        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Technological
25   University of the Philippines (TUP)-Cuenca Campus Act."

26        SEC. 2. Conversion. - The Technological University of the Philippines
27   (TUP) Extension Campus located in the Municipality of Cuenca, Province of
28   Batangas, is hereby converted into a regular campus of the Technological
29   University of the Philippines, to be known as the “TUP-Cuenca Campus”.

30         SEC. 3. Mandate. - The TUP Cuenca Campus shall provide advanced
31   technological, professional, and vocational instruction and training in industrial
32   education, engineering, technology, and other related fields. It shall also undertake
33   research, extension, and production services, consistent with the mandate of the
34   Technological University of the Philippines.

35         SEC. 4. Administration. - The TUP Cuenca Campus shall be headed by a
36   Campus Director, who shall be appointed by the University President, subject to the
37   approval of the Board of Regents. The Director shall exercise administrative and
38   supervisory authority over the campus, in accordance with existing laws and
39   university policies.
40         SEC. 5. Academic Programs. - The TUP Cuenca Campus shall offer
41   undergraduate and graduate programs as approved by the Board of Regents, in
42   coordination with the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), ensuring
43   compliance with national standards for higher education.

44         SEC. 6. Appropriations. -The amount necessary to carry out the provisions
45   of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The
46   Department of Budget and Management shall ensure the equitable allocation of
47   funds for the development, operation, and maintenance of the TUP Cuenca Campus.

48         SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days
49   from the effectivity of this Act, the Board of Regents of the Technological
50   University of the Philippines, in consultation with CHED and other concerned
51   agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to implement the
52   provisions of this Act.

53        SEC. 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
54   unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall not be affected thereby.

55         SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
56   regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
57   modified accordingly.

58         SEC. 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
59   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
