                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish a special benefit package that shall provide senior
citizens and indigents free dental, vision, and hearing assistive devices.

       Untreated vision impairment, hearing loss, and oral health conditions are
common medical issues that are most oftentimes overlooked. These conditions
drastically reduce productivity and compromise the quality of life of our elderly and
economically disadvantaged citizens. Essential medical devices remain painfully out
of reach due to prohibitive cots: dentures can cost up to Forty thousand pesos (Php
40,000.00) per arch, prescription eyeglasses typically ranges from One thousand five
hundred pesos to more than Five thousand pesos (Php 5,000.00) per pair, and
hearing aids may cost up to over Two hundred fifty thousand pesos (Php 250,000.00)
per ear.

       Senior citizens commonly experience age-related deterioration in eyesight,
hearing, and oral health, while indigent individuals frequently forego treatment
because of financial constraints. The absence of appropriate assistive devices
contribute to preventable disability and a lower quality of life.

      The proposed measure implements a special benefit package that will fully
cover the cost of assistive devices such as dentures, prescription eyeglasses, and
hearing aids for qualified beneficiaries. It likewise ensures that recipients undergo
proper medical, dental, or ophthalmologic evaluation to guarantee appropriate
diagnosis and prescription.

       This vital piece of legislation strengthens the State’s commitment to protect
the welfare, dignity, and well-being of senior citizens and indigent Filipinos. The
provision of these necessary healthcare devices restores their autonomy and
empowers them to participate actively in community life.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR FREE DENTAL, VISION, AND HEARING ASSISTIVE
DEVICES, INCLUDING DENTURES, PRESCRIPTION GLASSES, AND
HEARING AIDS TO SENIOR CITIZENS AND INDIGENTS, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Free Health Aids for
Seniors and Indigents Act”.

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby the declared policy of the State to
adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development that
ensures the availability of essential goods, health services, and other social services
to all people. In line with its commitment to accessible and inclusive healthcare, the
State shall provide assistive devices such as dentures, hearing aids, and prescription
glasses. Towards this end, the State shall give full support to the improvement of the
total well-being of senior citizens and indigents.

       SEC. 3. Free Dental, Vision, and Hearing Assistive Devices. — The Philippine
Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), in coordination with the Department of
Health (DOH), shall develop and implement a special benefit package that shall
fully cover the cost of assistive devices, including but not limited to complete or
partial dentures, prescription eyeglasses, and hearing aids, for qualified senior
citizens and indigents.

      Medical and dental consultations, including the prescription of the assistive
devices covered under this Act, shall be conducted in the Ear, Nose, and Throat
(ENT), dental, or ophthalmology departments, or, in the case of senior citizens,
through the geriatric center of national or local government hospitals.

       SEC. 4. Persons Covered. — This Act shall cover: (a) senior citizens, defined as
Filipino citizens who are at least sixty (60) years of age; and (b) indigent persons,
defined as individuals with no visible means of income or whose income is deemed

insufficient for family subsistence, as determined by the Department of Social
Welfare and Development (DSWD).

        SEC. 5. Appropriations. — The appropriations necessary to implement the
provisions of this Act and its implementing rules shall be included in the respective
budgets of the DOH, the PhilHealth, and the DSWD under the General
Appropriations Act (GAA).
​
        SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within thirty (30) days from the
approval of this Act, the DOH and the PhilHealth, in coordination with the National
Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC), the DSWD, and other relevant agencies,
shall jointly formulate rules and guidelines necessary for the proper implementation
and enforcement of this Act.

        SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
