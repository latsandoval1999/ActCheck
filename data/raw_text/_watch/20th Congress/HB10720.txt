                               EXPLANATORY NOTE


      The prevailing economic conditions that our country faces have compelled us
to reconsider restructuring the current tax schedule, as it exacerbates the financial
burdens carried by Filipinos. It is imperative to enact measures to provide
immediate economic relief to low- to middle-income earners, as well as self-
employed professionals.

      This proposed bill seeks to amend the individual tax schedule by increasing
the non-taxable annual income threshold from P250,000 to P350,000. While this
adjustment may result in a short-term reduction in direct income tax collections,
expanding the take-home pay of our low- to middle income earners directly
strengthens their purchasing power. Beyond cushioning households against the
rising costs of basic goods and services, this expanded exemption will give vital
liquidity into local commerce and stimulate broader macroeconomic growth.

      Further, the possible fiscal impact is balanced by the fact that the increase of
the disposable income of the low- and middle-income families leads to a
proportionate increase in spending on basic commodities. This, in effect, directly
increase market activity.
      The restructuring of the individual tax rates schedule aligns with the
constitutional mandate to establish a progressive system of taxation, while
effectively responding to the economic and societal realities.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
