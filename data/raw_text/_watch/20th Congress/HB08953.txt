                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to ensure the affordable and accessible distribution of imported
rice to Filipino consumers by mandating its sale through Kadiwa stores, centers, and
outlets at actual importation cost, while amending Republic Act (RA) No. 8178,
otherwise known as the “Agricultural Tariffication Act”, as amended by RA No. 11203.
It aims to stabilize rice prices, protect vulnerable sectors, and strengthen government
intervention in the rice market through transparent and targeted distribution
mechanisms.

        Despite the liberalization of rice importation under existing laws, many
Filipino households, particularly those belonging to low-income and vulnerable
sectors, continue to experience high and unstable rice prices. Market-driven
importation, while increasing supply, does not always translate to affordable retail
prices due to layers of distribution costs, mark-ups, and market inefficiencies. As a
result, access to affordable rice remains a pressing concern, especially during periods
of inflation and supply disruptions.

      This measure addresses these challenges by authorizing the Department of
Agriculture (DA), through the National Food Authority (NFA) and other
appropriate agencies, to undertake government-to-government rice importation and
ensure its direct distribution through licensed Kadiwa outlets. Selling rice at actual
import cost will eliminate excessive intermediaries and profit margins, thereby
making rice more affordable and accessible to the public.

       The bill further directs that priority be given to low-income households and
vulnerable sectors in the distribution of imported rice. It also mandates the use of
available government resources to support procurement and distribution, while
ensuring transparency, accountability, and efficiency in program implementation. At
the same time, it provides safeguards to protect local farmers by ensuring that
domestic production remains supported and prioritized in national and local
markets.
        This proposed measure balances the need for immediate consumer relief with
the long-term goal of sustaining local agricultural development. It reinforces the
State’s commitment to food security, price stability, and inclusive economic growth.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE DISTRIBUTION OF IMPORTED RICE AT KADIWA
STORES, CENTERS, AND OUTLETS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8178, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “AGRICULTURAL
TARIFFICATION ACT”, AS AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Section 2 of Republic Act (RA) No. 8178, otherwise known as the
“Agricultural Tariffication Act”, as amended by RA No. 11203, otherwise known as the
“Rice Tariffication Law”, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

              “SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
      ensure food security and to make the country’s agricultural sector
      viable, efficient, and globally competitive. The State adopts the use of
      tariffs in lieu of non-tariff import restrictions to protect local producers
      of agricultural products.

             “x x x

           “IT IS LIKEWISE THE POLICY OF THE STATE TO ENSURE
      FOOD SECURITY, ESPECIALLY FOR LOW-INCOME AND
      VULNERABLE SECTORS, BY FACILITATING THE AFFORDABLE
      DISTRIBUTION OF RICE. TOWARDS THIS END, THE STATE
      SHALL UTILIZE GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT RICE
      IMPORTATION TO MAINTAIN A STABLE AND SUFFICIENT
      SUPPLY OF AFFORDABLE RICE. RICE PROCURED THROUGH
      SUCH SHALL BE DISTRIBUTED THROUGH LICENSED KADIWA
      STORES, CENTERS, AND OUTLETS AT THE ACTUAL
      IMPORTATION PRICE, THEREBY ENSURING EQUITABLE
      ACCESS TO RICE FOR ALL FILIPINOS.”

           “To FURTHER ENHANCE [help] the COMPETITIVENESS OF
      THE agricultural sector [compete globally], the State shall seek to raise

      farm productivity levels by providing the necessary support services
      such as, but not limited to, irrigation, farm-to-market roads,
      post-harvest equipment and facilities, credit, research and
      development, extension services, other market infrastructure, and
      market information.”

      SEC. 2. A new Section 17 is hereby inserted after the new Section 16 of the
same Act, to read as follows:

           “SECTION 17. GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT RICE
      IMPORTATION AND KADIWA DISTRIBUTION. – THE
      DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE (DA), THROUGH THE
      NATIONAL    FOOD   AUTHORITY   (NFA)  OR   OTHER
      APPROPRIATE AGENCIES, IS HEREBY MANDATED TO
      FACILITATE    GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT        RICE
      IMPORTATION TO ENSURE FOOD SECURITY AND RICE PRICE
      STABILITY.

           “RICE            IMPORTED           THROUGH
      GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT AGREEMENTS SHALL BE
      DISTRIBUTED DIRECTLY TO AUTHORIZED AND LICENSED
      KADIWA STORES, CENTERS, AND OUTLETS AT THE ACTUAL
      PRICE OF THE RICE UNDER SAID AGREEMENTS.

           “THE DA SHALL UTILIZE THE FUNDS OF THE
      DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT
      (DSWD), THE NFA, AND OTHER AGENCIES MANDATED BY
      LAW TO PROCURE RICE FROM FOREIGN GOVERNMENTS.
      THE FUNDS SHALL BE ALLOCATED FOR THE DIRECT
      PURCHASE OF IMPORTED RICE AND ITS DISTRIBUTION
      THROUGH KADIWA OUTLETS, WHICH SHALL PRIORITIZE
      LOW-INCOME HOUSEHOLDS AND OTHER VULNERABLE
      SECTORS.

           “THE DISTRIBUTION OF RICE AT KADIWA OUTLETS
      SHALL BE SUBJECT TO GUIDELINES SET BY THE DA,
      ENSURING    TRANSPARENCY,    ACCOUNTABILITY,   AND
      ACCESSIBILITY, AND SHALL AIM TO MAKE RICE AVAILABLE
      AT PRICES REFLECTIVE OF THE ACTUAL IMPORT COST,
      EXCLUDING ANY ADDITIONAL MARK-UP OR PROFIT
      MARGINS: PROVIDED, THAT THE DISTRIBUTION AND SALE
      OF IMPORTED RICE IN KADIWA OUTLETS SHALL NOT
      UNDERMINE, REDUCE, OR NEGLECT THE SUPPORT FOR
      LOCAL FARMERS AND DOMESTIC RICE PRODUCTION;
      PROVIDED, FURTHER, THAT THE GOVERNMENT SHALL TAKE
      ALL NECESSARY MEASURES TO ENSURE THAT LOCAL
      FARMERS ARE NOT ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY THE
      AVAILABILITY OF IMPORTED RICE IN KADIWA STORES;

       KADIWA STORES AND THEIR PRODUCE SHALL BE

       SEC. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Agriculture
(DA), in consultation with the Department of Social Welfare and Development
(DSWD), the National Food Authority (NFA), and other relevant government
agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act for its effective implementation.

       SEC. 4. Appropriations. – The amounts necessary to carry out the provisions of
this Act shall be sourced from the current appropriations of the DA, DSWD,
National Food Authority, and other relevant government agencies, or from such
other sources as may be identified by the Department of Budget and Management
(DBM).

       SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions thereof which are not affected
shall continue to remain in full force and effect.

     SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following
completion of its publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
