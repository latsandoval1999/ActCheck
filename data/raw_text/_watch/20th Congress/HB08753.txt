                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

             The Philippines faces evolving national security threats
      arising from espionage, cyber-espionage, and unauthorized foreign
      intelligence activities targeting government institutions, defense
      systems, critical infrastructure, and strategic industries. Advances
      in digital technology and global connectivity have expanded the
      methods by which hostile actors may infiltrate computer networks,
      compromise communications systems, and obtain sensitive national
      defense information.

             While existing laws such as Commonwealth Act No. 616
      (Espionage Act) and Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime Prevention
      Act of 2012) address certain aspects of espionage and cybercrime,
      these statutes were enacted in vastly different technological contexts
      and do not fully address the complex and coordinated intelligence
      operations that may now occur through cyberspace, research
      institutions, and critical digital infrastructure.

            This measure seeks to strengthen the State’s capacity to
      prevent, detect, investigate, and prosecute espionage and cyber-
      espionage by establishing a comprehensive legal framework that
      protects classified national defense information, secures critical
      information infrastructure, and deters foreign intelligence
      operations directed against the Republic of the Philippines, while
      ensuring that enforcement remains consistent with the Constitution
      and respectful of civil liberties.

            In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is
      earnestly sought.

                         “AN ACT
   STRENGTHENING      NATIONAL     DEFENSE     AGAINST
   ESPIONAGE, CYBER-ESPIONAGE, AND UNAUTHORIZED
   FOREIGN    INTELLIGENCE    ACTIVITIES;  PROTECTING
   CLASSIFIED GOVERNMENT INFORMATION AND CRITICAL
   INFORMATION    INFRASTRUCTURE;     ESTABLISHING   A
   NATIONAL FRAMEWORK FOR COUNTER-ESPIONAGE AND
   CYBER-INTELLIGENCE DEFENSE; PRESCRIBING PENALTIES
   FOR VIOLATIONS; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “National
Espionage and Cyber-Intelligence Defense Act of 2026.”

                          ARTICLE I.
                    DECLARATION OF POLICY

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to:

   a. Protect the sovereignty, territorial integrity, and national
      security of the Republic of the Philippines;
   b. Safeguard critical communication infrastructure, digital
      networks, military systems, and strategic industries from
      espionage, cyber intrusion, sabotage, and foreign intelligence
      operations;
   c. Strengthen the ability of the State to detect, prevent,
      investigate, and prosecute acts of espionage whether
      conducted through physical infiltration, cyber intrusion, or
      technological means;
   d. Protect sensitive national defense information, critical
      infrastructure data, and government communications from
      unauthorized acquisition, hacking, or disclosure;
   e. Ensure that measures undertaken pursuant to this Act are
      implemented consistent with the Constitution and with due
      respect for civil liberties, academic freedom, and freedom of
      expression.
                           ARTICLE II
                      DEFINITION OF TERMS

Section 3. Definitions. — For purposes of this Act:

   a. Espionage — Refers to the act of knowingly obtaining,
      collecting, transmitting, delivering, or communicating
      information relating to national defense, critical infrastructure,
      intelligence operations, or government security to a foreign
      state, foreign intelligence entity, or foreign agent with intent or
      reason to believe that such information will be used to injure
      the national security of the Republic of the Philippines or to the
      advantage of a foreign state.

   b. Cyber Espionage — Refers to intentional and unauthorized
      access to computer systems, communications networks,
      satellites, or digital infrastructure for the purpose of obtaining
      classified national defense information or strategic data for the
      benefit of a foreign state, foreign intelligence entity, or foreign
      agent.

   c. Cyber Sabotage — Refers to the intentional disruption,
      degradation, manipulation, or destruction of computer
      systems, communications networks, satellites, or critical
      information infrastructure for the purpose of impairing
      national defense, national security, or essential government
      operations.

   d. Critical Information Infrastructure — Refers to systems,
      assets, or networks whose incapacity or destruction would
      have a debilitating impact on national security, economic
      security, public safety, or public health, including but not
      limited to:
             i.    telecommunications networks;
             ii.   satellite systems;
             iii.  military communications systems;
             iv.   government information systems;
             v.    financial and banking systems;
             vi.   energy and power grids;
             vii.  transportation networks;
             viii. submarine communication cables.

   e. National Defense Information — Refers to classified or
      restricted information relating to military operations,
      intelligence activities, defense technology, national security
      strategies, or strategic infrastructure of the Republic of the
      Philippines.
   f. Foreign Intelligence Entity — Refers to any foreign government,
      military organization, intelligence service, state-owned
      enterprise, or organization acting on behalf of or under the
      direction of a foreign state.

   g. Foreign Agent — Refers to any individual who knowingly acts
      on behalf of a foreign intelligence entity for the purpose of
      conducting espionage or intelligence activities against the
      Republic of the Philippines.


                             ARTICLE III
                          PROHIBITED ACTS

Section 4. Espionage. — Any person who knowingly obtains, copies,
records, transmits, or communicates national defense information to
a foreign state, foreign intelligence entity, or foreign agent, with intent
or reason to believe that such information will be used to the
detriment of the national security of the Republic of the Philippines,
shall be guilty of espionage punishable under Section 14 of this Act.

Section 5. Cyber Espionage. — Any person who intentionally and
without lawful authority commits any of the following acts for the
benefit of a foreign state, foreign intelligence entity, or foreign agent
shall be guilty of cyber espionage punishable under Section 15 of this
Act:

   a. hacks or infiltrates government computer systems;
   b. accesses classified or sensitive national security databases;
   c. penetrates military or intelligence communications networks;
      or
   d. obtains strategic data from critical information infrastructure
      systems.

Acts punishable under this section shall be without prejudice to
liability under Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime Prevention Act of
2012).

Section 6. Hacking of Communication Infrastructure. — Any person
who intentionally and without lawful authority commits any of the
following acts for the benefit of a foreign state, foreign intelligence
entity, or foreign agent:

   a. interferes with or infiltrates telecommunications networks;
   b. accesses, disrupts, or sabotages satellite communication
      systems;
   c. tampers with submarine communication cables or internet
      backbone infrastructure; or
   d. disrupts military command-and-control communications
      systems, shall be guilty of cyber espionage or cyber sabotage,
      punishable under Section 15 of this Act.

Section 7. Possession of Espionage Devices. — Any person who
possesses, manufactures, develops, or deploys equipment or software
designed or primarily intended for espionage activities, including but
not limited to:

   a. surveillance malware;
   b. signal interception devices;
   c. covert communication transmitters; or
   d. satellite interception equipment,

with intent to use such equipment or software to commit espionage
shall be guilty of espionage punishable under Section 14 of this Act.

Section 8. Economic Espionage. — Any person who knowingly
steals, copies, transmits, or delivers sensitive industrial,
technological, or proprietary information relating to:

   a. defense industries;
   b. telecommunications technology;
   c. semiconductor technology;
   d. military equipment; or
   e. cybersecurity systems,

for the benefit of a foreign state, foreign intelligence entity, or foreign
agent shall be guilty of espionage punishable under Section 14 of this
Act.

Section 9. Espionage through Research Institutions. — Any person
who knowingly and intentionally uses universities, research
institutions, laboratories, or joint technology programs to obtain
classified national defense information or strategic technology for the
benefit of a foreign intelligence entity shall be guilty of espionage
punishable under Section 14 of this Act.

Nothing in this section shall be interpreted to restrict legitimate
academic collaboration or scientific research not involving classified
or restricted national defense information.

Section 10. Espionage through Digital Platforms. — Any person who
uses online platforms, encrypted communications systems, or social
media networks to knowingly recruit agents, transmit classified
national defense information, or coordinate espionage activities for a
foreign intelligence entity shall be guilty of espionage punishable
under Section 14 of this Act.
Nothing in this section shall be interpreted to restrict legitimate
journalism, academic discourse, or lawful public expression that
does not involve the unauthorized disclosure of classified national
defense information.

Section 11. Foreign Intelligence Recruitment. — Any person who,
acting on behalf of a foreign state, foreign intelligence entity, or
foreign agent, knowingly recruits, solicits, induces, coerces, or
attempts to recruit any Filipino citizen, government employee,
member of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, researcher,
contractor, or employee of critical infrastructure to obtain, disclose,
transmit, or provide national defense information, strategic
technology, or sensitive government data shall be guilty of espionage
punishable under Section 14 of this Act.

The offense shall be deemed committed whether or not the
recruitment effort succeeds or the information is actually obtained.

Section 12. Conspiracy to Commit Espionage. — When two (2) or
more persons conspire to commit any offense punishable under this
Act and one or more of such persons performs any overt act to effect
the object of the conspiracy, each conspirator shall be liable for the
offense and shall be punished in the same manner as the principal
offender.

Section 13. Attempted Espionage. — Any person who attempts to
commit espionage or cyber espionage under this Act by performing
overt acts toward the commission of the offense, but who does not
complete the act by reason of causes independent of his or her will
shall suffer the penalty one degree lower than that prescribed for the
consummated offense.

                              ARTICLE IV
                              PENALTIES

Section 14. Espionage. — Any person who commits espionage as
defined under Article III of this Act shall, upon conviction, suffer the
penalty of reclusion perpetua and a fine of not less than Ten Million
Pesos (₱10,000,000.00) but not more than Fifty Million Pesos
(₱50,000,000.00).

Where two (2) or more acts of espionage are committed in violation of
Article III of this Act, each act shall constitute a separate and distinct
offense and shall be punished independently under this Act.

Section 15. Cyber Espionage. — Any person who commits cyber
espionage as defined under Article III of this Act shall, upon
conviction, suffer the penalty of imprisonment of twenty (20) years to
reclusion perpetua and a fine of not less than Five Million Pesos
(₱5,000,000.00) but       not   more    than    Thirty   Million   Pesos
(₱30,000,000.00).

Where two (2) or more acts of cyber espionage are committed in
violation of Article III of this Act, each act shall constitute a separate
and distinct offense and shall be punished independently under this
Act.

Section 16. Espionage Involving Critical Infrastructure. — When the
offense of espionage or cyber espionage under Article III of this Act
involves telecommunications networks, military communications
systems, satellite systems, submarine communication cables, or
other facilities officially designated as critical infrastructure by the
National Security Council or by law, the penalty of reclusion perpetua
shall be imposed.

Persons convicted under this section shall not be eligible for parole
under existing laws.

Section 17. Liability of Foreign Nationals. — Any foreign national
who commits espionage or cyber espionage under Article III of this
Act shall:

   a. upon completion of service of sentence, be deported in
      accordance with existing immigration laws and regulations;
      and
   b. be permanently barred from re—entering the Republic of the
      Philippines.


                            ARTICLE V
                      INVESTIGATIVE POWERS

Section 18. Lead Agencies — The following agencies shall exercise
investigative and enforcement authority under this Act:

   a. National Intelligence Coordinating Agency (NICA);
   b. National Bureau of Investigation (NBI);
   c. Philippine National Police (PNP);
   d. Department of Information and Communications Technology
      (DICT);
   e. Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP).

These agencies shall coordinate in accordance with existing national
security and intelligence frameworks subject to their respective
mandates under existing laws.
Section 19. Cyber Counter-Espionage Center — The National
Intelligence Coordinating Agency, in coordination with the
Department of Information and Communications Technology and the
Armed Forces of the Philippines, shall establish a Cyber Counter-
Espionage Center to:

   a. detect cyber espionage attacks;
   b. monitor foreign cyber intelligence activities targeting Philippine
      systems; and
   c. protect national digital and communications infrastructure.

                     ARTICLE VI
  PROTECTION OF NATIONAL COMMUNICATION NETWORKS

Section 20. Mandatory Security Standards — Telecommunications
companies and operators of critical information infrastructure
operating in the Philippines shall comply with national cybersecurity
and national security standards issued by the Department of
Information and Communications Technology, in coordination with
the National Telecommunications Commission and national security
agencies.

Failure to implement adequate cybersecurity protections may result
in administrative fines; suspension or revocation of operating
licenses; or criminal liability when negligence results in espionage
activities affecting national security.

                        ARTICLE VII
               EXTRATERRITORIAL JURISDICTION

Section 21. This Act shall apply to the following whether committed
within or outside the territory of the Philippines::

   a. Filipino citizens committing acts of espionage outside
      Philippine territory;
   b. foreign nationals conducting espionage targeting Philippine
      national defense information or critical infrastructure; and
   c. cyber espionage attacks originating outside Philippine territory
      but directed at Philippine government systems or national
      security assets.

                        ARTICLE VIII
                 WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION

Section 22. Whistleblower Protection. — Individuals who in good
faith report espionage activities to competent authorities shall be
protected from retaliation and may be eligible for protection under
existing witness protection or government security programs.
                         ARTICLE IX
                  RELATION TO EXISTING LAWS


Section 23. Relation to Existing Laws. — This Act shall supplement
and, where applicable, amend the provisions of Commonwealth Act
No. 616 (Espionage Act), Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime
Prevention Act of 2012), Republic Act No. 10844 (Department of
Information and Communications Technology Act), and other
relevant national security laws.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to limit or diminish liability
under other applicable laws where the acts committed constitute
offenses punishable under such laws.

All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and regulations
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.


                         ARTICLE X
                SEPARABILITY AND EFFECTIVITY

Section 24. Separability Clause — If any provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remainder of the Act shall remain valid
and enforceable.

Section 25. Effectivity — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
