                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

Traffic management and enforcement in the Philippines are currently
characterized by a fragmented system where different Local Government Units
(LGUs) and national agencies—such as the Metropolitan Manila Development
Authority (MMDA), the Land Transportation Office (LTO), and the Philippine
National Police-Highway Patrol Group (PNP-HPG)—implement varying
procedures, fines, and ticketing systems. This lack of uniformity causes immense
confusion among motorists, creates opportunities for extortion or "kotong," and
undermines the rule of law on our roads.

Furthermore, motorists who are wrongfully apprehended face tedious,
unstandardized, and heavily bureaucratic processes to contest their violations.
The absence of a unified and accessible adjudicatory body often forces citizens to
simply pay unjust fines rather than exercise their right to due process.

This bill seeks to address these systemic issues by establishing a nationwide,
standardized procedure for traffic apprehensions. It introduces the Uniform
Traffic Citation Ticket (UTCT) to replace the current system. This measure
institutionalizes the Traffic Adjudication Unit (TAU) in all regions, providing a
clear and standardized grievance mechanism for motorists to contest traffic
citations.

By streamlining traffic apprehension procedures and guaranteeing due process
through the TAU, this proposed measure will protect the rights of motorists,
professionalize traffic enforcement, and foster a culture of transparency and
discipline on our roads.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

     AN ACT STANDARDIZING TRAFFIC APPREHENSION PROCEDURES,
 INSTITUTIONALIZING THE TRAFFIC ADJUDICATION UNIT, PRESCRIBING
       RULES FOR CITATION PURPOSES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Standardized Traffic
Apprehension and Adjudication Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
ensure safe, efficient, and orderly traffic flow on all public roads. Towards this
end, the State shall establish uniform, transparent, and fair procedures in the
apprehension of traffic violators, standardize citation protocols, and guarantee
due process through the institutionalization of dedicated traffic adjudication
units.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act, the following terms shall mean:

   1. Apprehension – the act of stopping a motorist for a perceived violation of
      traffic laws, rules, and regulations, and the subsequent issuance of a
      citation ticket.

   2. Traffic Enforcer – any deputized or authorized personnel from the Land
      Transportation Office (LTO), Metropolitan Manila Development Authority
      (MMDA), Philippine National Police (PNP), or Local Government Units
      (LGUs) mandated to enforce traffic laws.

   3. Uniform Traffic Citation Ticket (UTCT) – the single, standardized physical
      or electronic document issued to motorists for traffic violations
      nationwide.
SEC. 4. Standardized Traffic Apprehension Procedures. – All traffic enforcement
agencies, including LGUs, shall strictly observe the following procedures during
apprehension:

   1. Clear Identification: The traffic enforcer must wear a highly visible uniform
      displaying their nameplate and institutional affiliation.
   2. No-Contact Apprehension Priority: Where available, No-Contact
      Apprehension Policies (NCAP) using closed-circuit television (CCTV) and
      digital cameras shall be harmonized with this Act.
   3. Physical Apprehension:
         o The enforcer shall flag down the vehicle safely and courteously inform
             the driver of the specific violation.
         o The enforcer shall request the driver's license and vehicle
             registration. The driver shall not be required to alight from the vehicle
             unless necessary for safety or in cases of suspected driving under the
             influence.
         o Confiscation of the driver's license is strictly prohibited except in
             instances where the driver is involved in a traffic crash resulting in
             injury or death, has accumulated the maximum allowable demerit
             points, or is driving an unregistered vehicle.
   4. Time Limit: The entire apprehension process must be completed within a
      reasonable time, not exceeding ten (10) minutes, barring exceptional
      circumstances.

SEC. 5. Institutionalization of the Traffic Adjudication Unit (TAU). – There is hereby
created a Traffic Adjudication Unit (TAU) under the jurisdiction of the LTO, which
shall have regional and provincial branches nationwide.

   1. Mandate: The TAU shall have exclusive original jurisdiction to hear and
      resolve protests, contests, and complaints filed by motorists regarding
      traffic apprehensions and citations.
   2. Composition: The TAU shall be composed of trained hearing officers with a
      background in law or public administration, independent from the
      apprehension units of the LTO, MMDA, and LGUs.
   3. Procedure: Protests must be filed physically or electronically within seven
      (7) working days from the issuance of the UTCT. The TAU must resolve the
      contest within fifteen (15) working days from the filing of the complaint.

SEC. 6. Uniform Traffic Citation Ticket (UTCT). – The LTO, in coordination with the
Department of Transportation (DOTr) and the Department of Interior and Local
Government (DILG), shall design and implement a Uniform Traffic Citation Ticket
(UTCT) which shall be recognized nationwide. All LGUs and national agencies
shall exclusively use the UTCT for all traffic violations. The UTCT shall clearly
indicate:

   1. The specific traffic violation and its corresponding legal basis;
   2. The exact penalty and fine amount;
   3. The name and signature of the apprehending officer; and
   4. Clear instructions on how to pay the fine or contest the apprehension
      before the TAU.

SEC. 7. Prohibited Acts and Penalties. – Any traffic enforcer or official who violates
the provisions of Section 4 or refuses to recognize the UTCT or the authority of
the TAU shall be subject to administrative sanctions ranging from a minimum
suspension of thirty (30) days to dismissal from service, without prejudice to the
filing of appropriate criminal charges under existing laws.

SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DOTr, LTO, MMDA, DILG, and representatives from
transport and motorist advocacy groups shall jointly formulate the necessary
rules and regulations to effectively implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other parts or provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, local ordinances,
rules, and regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
