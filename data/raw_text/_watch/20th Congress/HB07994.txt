                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to convert the Jct. Bukidnon Cagayan de Oro Iligan Road (BCIR) –
Corrales Street – North Divisoria Road in Cagayan de Oro City from a city road to a national
road.

        The road is presently in poor condition and in dire need of expansion, repair and
rehabilitation to accommodate the ever increasing volume of vehicles using said road. With its
improvement, easier access to both points would be ensured and the potentials for commercial
and industrial development would be realized.

       With its conversion into a national road, it would receive the much needed funding and
assistance it deserves.

       Passage of this measure is earnestly requested.

                              AN ACT
CONVERTING THE JCT. BUKIDNON CAGAYAN DE ORO ILIGAN ROAD (BCIR) –
CORRALES STREET – NORTH DIVISORIA ROAD IN CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY INTO A
NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress be
assembled:

       Section 1. The Jct. Bukidnon Cagayan de Oro Iligan Road (BCIR) – Corrales Street –
North Divisoria Road is hereby converted into a national road.

      Sec 2. The Secretary of Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the
necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

       Sec. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper
of general circulation.
