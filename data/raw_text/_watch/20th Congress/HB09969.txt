                            AN ACT
UPGRADING THE ENTRY-LEVEL SALARY OF GOVERNMENT MIDWIVES FROM
                  SALARY GRADE 9 TO GRADE 11

                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The role of midwives is very significant as they provide frontline healthcare services,
ensuring the delivery of maternal and neonatal care to mothers and children in the community.

       During the pandemic, they are among the manpower mobilized to provide medical care
and were assigned to administer vaccines. Sadly, despite their contributions, government midwives
are among the lowest-compensated health personnel in the public sector.

        At present, entry-level salary for midwives is at salary grade 9 which does not justify the
responsibilities they carry out in reducing maternal and infant mortality. This proposed measure
seeks to gain approval for the salary adjustment for entry-level midwives from the current Salary
Grad 9 to Salary Grad 11, in recognition of the valuable service they selflessly give.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.
