                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to mandate a truly smoke-free and vape-free environment in
all public places and workplaces. It amends Republic Act (RA) No. 9211, otherwise
known as the “Tobacco Regulation Act of 2003” and RA No. 11900, otherwise known
as the “Vaporized Nicotine and Non-Nicotine Products Regulation Act” to expand and
modernize key definitions to ensure comprehensive coverage regardless of
ownership or physical enclosure. It strengthens enforcement by requiring strict
standards for designated smoking and vaping areas, creating local Smoke-Free and
Vape-Free Task Forces with dedicated budget allocations, and instituting a Tobacco
and Vape Sales License system, all while imposing graduated penalties for
violations.

       The abovementioned laws were enacted to regulate the sale, use, and
promotion of tobacco and vape products. However, gaps in the current definitions of
public places, public conveyances, and smoking and vaping activities have allowed
inconsistent enforcement of smoke-free policies, particularly with the rise of vaping
and heated tobacco products, which were not adequately contemplated when RA
No. 9211 was first passed.

      This bill closes definitional loopholes and strengthens local enforcement
mechanisms. It upholds the State’s policy to protect public health consistent with the
World Health Organization Framework Convention on Tobacco Control,
safeguarding non-smokers, workers, and minors from the harmful effects of tobacco
smoke and vape emissions.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

PUBLIC PLACES AND WORKPLACES, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9211, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “TOBACCO
REGULATION ACT OF 2003” AND REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11900, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS THE “VAPORIZED NICOTINE AND NON-NICOTINE

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Smoke–Free and
Vape–Free Environment Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness
among them. The State recognizes that effective protection from the hazards of
tobacco smoke and vape emissions, consistent with Article 8 of the World Health
Organization Framework Convention on Tobacco Control (WHO FCTC), requires
the total elimination of such emissions in indoor public places and workplaces in
order to establish a one hundred percent (100%) smoke–free and vape–free
environment.

       SEC. 3. Construction. – In case of doubt, the provisions of this Act and its
implementing rules and regulations shall be construed liberally in favor of the
protection and promotion of public health.

      SEC. 6. Prohibition on Smoking and Vaping. – Smoking and vaping shall be
prohibited in all public places, public conveyances, and workplaces. This prohibition
extends to all indoor areas, including but not limited to those enumerated in the
amended definitions under Section 4 of this Act.

       SEC. 7. Designated Smoking and Vaping Areas (DSVA). – In all enclosed places
that are open to the general public, workplaces, and other places not covered under
the preceding section, where smoking or vaping may expose a person other than the
smoker to tobacco smoke and vape emissions, the owner, proprietor, operator,
possessor, manager, or administrator of such places may establish DSVA. The DSVA
must be in an outdoor space with no roof or enclosed walls, with the following
requirements:

         a.​ It shall not be situated within ten (10) meters from any entrance, exit,
      window, or area where people commonly pass or congregate;​

         b.​ It shall not be located in public conveyances nor within one hundred
      (100) meters of schools, playgrounds, and other places or facilities frequented
      by minors;​

          c.​ It shall not exceed ten (10) square meters in area;​

          d.​ Only one (1) DSVA shall be allowed per building or establishment;​

         e.​ No food or beverage shall be served or consumed inside the DSVA;
      and​




                                           5
          f.​ It shall comply with such other standards as may be prescribed by the
       Department of Health (DOH), or the local Smoke–Free and Vape–Free Task
       Force.

       Nothing in this Act shall be construed to compel persons–in–charge to
establish designated smoking or vaping areas, nor to preclude them from adopting
and implementing more stringent measures within their buildings and
establishments, in accordance with existing laws and regulations, and consistent
with a human rights–based approach and the State’s obligation to protect and
promote the right to health and to a safe, healthy, and smoke–free environment.

       The non–smoking and non–vaping signage shall be made part of the
requirements for the issuance or renewal of a business permit to operate an
establishment, health facility, or school. Failure to comply therewith is a ground for
the denial or revocation of such permit; Provided, That for schools, these signages
should be prominently displayed whenever youth activities are being conducted,
declaring that the area is a smoke–free/vape–free zone.

     SEC. 8. Obligations of Persons-in-Charge. – Persons-in-charge of public places
and workplaces shall have the following obligations:

          a.​ Prominently display “No Smoking, No Vaping” signs in conspicuous
       locations. Such signs shall measure at least eight by eleven (8 x 11) inches and
       shall include versions in English and the local language;​

           b.​ Remove all ashtrays and other receptacles used for smoking or vaping
       in prohibited areas;​

          c.​ Establish internal policies and procedures            for   the   effective
       implementation and enforcement of this Act;​

           d.​ Ensure that all employees are properly oriented on the provisions of
       this Act; and​

         e.​ Comply with such other requirements as may be imposed by the Local
       Government Units (LGUs) or concerned agencies.

       SEC. 9. Creation of Smoke-Free and Vape-Free Task Forces. – All provinces, cities,
and municipalities shall establish a Smoke–Free and Vape–Free (SF/VF) Task Force,
herein referred to as the Task Force. The Task Force shall be chaired by the Local
Chief Executive and co–chaired by the City or Municipal Health Officer. It shall be
responsible for the implementation, monitoring, and enforcement of this Act in
coordination with the Philippine National Police, the Metropolitan Manila
Development Authority (MMDA), and other concerned agencies. The Task Force is
likewise mandated to deputize enforcers, including but not limited to barangay
tanods and other authorized personnel, to ensure the effective enforcement of this
Act at the community level.

       The Task Force shall also include at least one (1) representative from civil
society organizations, community or people’s organizations promoting public
health, which should not be connected, directly or indirectly, or represent the
interests of the tobacco industry.

       All representatives of government agencies and organizations, including civil
society, non–government, grassroots, international organizations and private sector,
that take any part, directly or indirectly, in the setting and implementing of tobacco
control policies shall be required to fully accomplish a signed declaration under oath
that states, among others, that they are not affiliated with the tobacco industry, and
that they do not act on such industry’s behalfs or interests. The signed and notarized
copy of such declarations shall be made available to the public primarily via the
LGU’s website and other means to readily access such information.

       In the event of any change in circumstances that may affect the truthfulness,
validity, or completeness of the initial declaration of non–affiliation with the tobacco
and vape industry, all representatives of government agencies and organizations,
including civil society, non–government, grassroots, and international organizations,
shall be required to file an updated declaration under oath immediately. Such
updated declaration shall disclose any new, emerging, or potential conflict of
interest, including but not limited to affiliations, financial interests, partnerships, or
any other form of involvement that may directly or indirectly link the individual or
their organization to the tobacco industry or its representatives.

      The lack of a duly constituted Task Force shall not be a bar to the

       SEC. 10. Tobacco and Vape Sales License. – No person or establishment shall sell,
distribute, or offer for sale tobacco products, heated tobacco products, or electronic
nicotine/non–nicotine delivery systems without a valid Tobacco and Vape Sales
License issued by the LGU.

       The application for such license shall include the following minimum
requirements:

          a.​ Proof of business registration;​

         b.​ Certification that the establishment is at least one hundred (100) meters
       away from any school, playground, or other places frequented by minors;​

          c.​ Submission of a vicinity map;​

          d.​ Certification from the barangay confirming public notice of the
       application; and​

          e.​ Such other requirements as may be prescribed by the LGU.

       The license shall be displayed conspicuously at the place of business and shall
be valid only for the specific location indicated therein.

      SEC. 11. Livelihood Assistance Program. – Persons whose livelihood may be
adversely affected by the restrictions under this Act, such as ambulant vendors of
tobacco and vape products, may be referred to appropriate livelihood assistance
programs of the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) or the local Social Welfare
and Development Office. LGUs are encouraged to provide incentives, such as
recognition awards or business permit fee waivers, to establishments that
voluntarily adopt stricter smoke–free and vape–free measures.


                                           8
        SEC. 12. Awards and Recognition. – The DOH and the Department of the
Interior and Local Government shall include the effective implementation of this Act
and the performance of the Smoke–Free and Vape–Free Task Forces among the
criteria for awards and recognition programs, including but not limited to the Red
Orchid Awards, the Seal of Good Local Governance, and other similar incentive or
recognition mechanisms.

       SEC. 13. Penalties. – The following penalties shall be imposed for violations of
this Act:

           a.​ Any person who smokes or vapes in prohibited areas shall be fined as
       follows:

                 i.​   First offense: Fine of Five thousand pesos (Php 5,000.00);

                ii.​   Second offense: Fine of Ten thousand pesos (Php 10,000.00); and

               iii.​ Third and subsequent offenses: Fine of Twenty thousand pesos
              (Php 20,000.00).

          b.​ Any person–in–charge who allows, abets, or tolerates smoking or
       vaping in prohibited places, or who fails to perform the obligations under this
       Act, shall be subject to the same penalties provided above. For juridical
       entities, the business permit or license may be suspended or revoked, in
       addition to fines.​

          c.​ Any person or establishment that establishes or maintains a DSVA in
       violation of any of the requirements of this Act shall be fined not less than Ten
       thousand pesos (Php 10,000.00), but not more than Twenty thousand pesos
       (Php 20,000.00), and the non–compliant structure shall be removed or
       demolished, subject to existing laws and regulations. This is without
       prejudice to the suspension, cancellation, or revocation of business permits.​

       LGUs are hereby mandated to order the closure of, or impose appropriate
fines and penalties on, non–compliant point–of–sale establishments pursuant to
existing revenue and other applicable regulatory ordinances.

       SEC. 14. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force
and effect.

       SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. – Section 4 of RA No. 9211, and Section 3 of RA No.
11900, are hereby amended or modified accordingly. All laws, decrees, executive
orders, rules, regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
