                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

Under existing Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) regulations and
Department of National Defense (DND) directives, the Libingan ng mga Bayani
(LNMB) serves as the primary resting place reserved for individuals who
rendered exemplary service to the nation. This includes former Presidents,
military personnel, Medal for Valor awardees, statesmen, and National Artists
or National Scientists.

However, current regulations do not explicitly extend eligibility for interment
at the Libingan ng mga Bayani to Filipino sports heroes—athletes and sports
icons whose extraordinary accomplishments on the global stage have brought
immense honor, international prestige, and national pride to the Republic of
the Philippines. Sports heroes represent the finest traits of the Filipino spirit:
perseverance, discipline, patriotism, and unity. From pioneer champions to
modern Olympic gold medalists, World Champions, and international Hall of
Famers, these individuals have elevated the nation's standing globally, united
millions of Filipinos across generations, and inspired youth toward civic
excellence and healthy living.

This bill seeks to formally accord Filipino sports heroes the honor of burial at
the Libingan ng mga Bayani in recognition of their legendary contributions to
Philippine society and international sports history. By establishing clear
eligibility criteria—such as winning Olympic medals, securing official World
Championship titles, or receiving prestigious national orders for sports
achievement—this measure ensures that our national sports icons are
permanently commemorated alongside the nation's honored statesmen and
patriots.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE INTERNMENT OF QUALIFIED FILIPINO
SPORTS HEROES AT THE LIBINGAN NG MGA BAYANI AND FOR OTHER
                         PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the "Filipino Sports Heroes
Memorial Act."
SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote physical
education and sports, encourage athletic excellence, and recognize individuals
who have rendered exceptional service to the nation through sports. The State
acknowledges that extraordinary achievements in international athletic
competitions elevate national dignity, foster national unity, and inspire present
and future generations of Filipinos.
Accordingly, the nation shall accord the highest honor and respect to qualified
sports heroes upon their passing by granting them eligibility for interment at
the Libingan ng mga Bayani.
SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:
      Filipino Sports Hero - refers to any Filipino citizen who has achieved
      extraordinary distinction in international athletic competition,
      demonstrated sustained commitment to national prestige, and met the
      qualification criteria under Section 4 of this Act.
      Libingan ng mga Bayani (LNMB) - refers to the national shrine located
      in Fort Bonifacio, Taguig City, Metro Manila, designated as the military
      cemetery and memorial site for Filipino patriots, soldiers, and recognized
      national leaders.
      Primary Governing Bodies - refer to the Philippine Sports Commission
      (PSC), the Philippine Olympic Committee (POC), and the Department of
      National Defense (DND).
SEC. 4. Eligibility Criteria. — The remains of deceased Filipino sports heroes
shall be eligible for interment at the Libingan ng mga Bayani, subject to the
following qualifications:
      Olympic Medalists: Gold, Silver, or Bronze medalists in the Summer or
      Winter Olympic Games;
      Paralympic Medalists: Gold, Silver, or Bronze medalists in the Summer
      or Winter Paralympic Games;
      World Champions: Athletes who have won an official World
      Championship in a recognized individual or team sport governed by an
      International Federation recognized by the International Olympic
      Committee (IOC) or the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC);
      Hall of Fame Inductees: Athletes inducted into globally recognized
      international sports Halls of Fame; or
      National Honor Recipients: Sports icons awarded the Order of
      Lakandula, the Presidential Medal of Merit for Sports, or equivalent
      highest national civil honors for athletic service.
SEC. 5. Disqualifications. — No person otherwise qualified under Section 4 of
this Act shall be interred at the Libingan ng mga Bayani if they were convicted
by final judgment of any crime involving moral turpitude, treason, espionage,
or offenses against national security.
SEC. 6. Right of Next of Kin. — The interment of a qualified sports hero at the
Libingan ng mga Bayani shall be optional and dependent upon the express
consent and written request of the surviving legal spouse, or in their absence,
the nearest next of kin as defined by existing laws on succession and burial
rights.
SEC. 7. Allocation of Burial Plots and Funeral Rites. — The Department of
National Defense (DND) and the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) shall
allocate a dedicated section or plots within the Libingan ng mga Bayani for
qualified Filipino sports heroes. The AFP, in coordination with the PSC and
POC, shall prescribe appropriate military funeral honors, escorts, and protocol
commensurate with national state honors accorded to national figures interred
at the shrine.
SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of National Defense, in
consultation with the Chairman of the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC),
the President of the Philippine Olympic Committee (POC), and the National
Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP), shall formulate and issue
the rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this
Act.
SEC. 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.
SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, presidential decrees,
military regulations, administrative rules, and issuances inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
SEC. 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
Approved
