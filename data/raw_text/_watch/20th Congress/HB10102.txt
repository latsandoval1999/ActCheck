                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to strengthen rural communities through a Rural Development
National Framework. This entails fortifying livelihood opportunities, improving access
to financial services, and enhancing rural infrastructure. The proposed measure aims to
reduce rural poverty, improve economic productivity, and ensure that the benefits of
national growth reach communities that have long remained underserved.

       Despite the country's sustained economic growth, many rural communities
continue to experience high poverty incidence, limited employment opportunities,
inadequate infrastructure, and restricted access to credit, technology, and markets.
Farmers, fisherfolk, rural workers, and micro-entrepreneurs often struggle with low
productivity, poor farm-to-market connectivity, limited value-adding opportunities, and
increasing vulnerability to climate change and natural disasters. These longstanding
challenges continue to widen the development gap between urban and rural areas and
constrain inclusive economic growth.

       This measure adopts a comprehensive approach to rural development by
strengthening livelihood generation, expanding access to affordable financial services,
and improving rural infrastructure. It also promotes entrepreneurship, value chain
development, and the enhancement of technical and digital skills to improve rural
productivity and competitiveness. Moreover, it empowers local government units,
cooperatives, and community organizations to actively participate in the planning,
implementation, and monitoring of development programs.

        Rural development is fundamental to achieving food security, reducing
inequality, generating employment, and promoting balanced national development.
Investing in our rural communities goes far beyond an investment in agriculture. This
move means our nation’s long-term economic resilience and inclusive growth are being
prioritized.


      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
PROMOTING INCLUSIVE AND SUSTAINABLE RURAL DEVELOPMENT AND
LIVELIHOOD ENHANCEMENT IN THE PHILIPPINES, PROVIDING FUNDS
THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Rural Development and
Livelihood Enhancement Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to foster
inclusive and sustainable economic growth in rural areas of the Philippines by creating
and strengthening opportunities for livelihood, improving access to essential services,
and empowering rural communities. The State recognizes the vital role of rural areas in
national development and commits to uplifting the socio-economic conditions of rural
populations, reducing poverty incidence, and bridging the development gap between
urban and rural areas, ensuring that the benefits of national growth are equitably
distributed.

       SEC. 3. Objectives. – This Act shall specifically seek to:

          a. Establish and expand programs that provide diverse and sustainable
       income-generating activities for rural residents, particularly those in
       poverty-stricken areas, including support for agriculture, fisheries, agri-tourism,
       micro-enterprises, and small and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs);​

           b. Enhance the availability of affordable credit, microfinance, and other
       financial products tailored to the needs of rural communities, farmers, fisherfolk,
       and small entrepreneurs, thereby stimulating economic activity;​

           c. Provide relevant training, technical assistance, and capacity-building
       programs that equip rural residents with the necessary skills for
       entrepreneurship, modern agricultural practices, new technologies, and access to
       better employment opportunities;​

      rural areas, such as farm-to-market roads (FMR), irrigation systems, post-harvest

          e.​ Encourage the development of robust value chains in agriculture and

          g.​ Uplift the socio-economic conditions of rural populations, reduce poverty

      ensure that the benefits of national growth are equitably distributed.

​     SEC. 4. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

          a.​ Community empowerment refers to the process by which communities
      gain increased control over their lives and environment, and actively participate
      in decision-making processes affecting their development;

         b.​ ﻿Micro-enterprises refers to business activities with assets of not more than
      Three million pesos (Php 3,000,000.00).

         c.​ Rural Areas refer to geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas, and
      other communities outside of highly urbanized cities, primarily dependent on
      agriculture, fisheries, or other natural resource-based industries;​

         d.​ ﻿Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (MSMEs) refer to enterprises with
      assets of more than Three million pesos (Php 3,000,000.00) up to One Hundred
      million pesos (Php 100,000,000.00);​

         e.​ ﻿Sustainable Livelihood refers to income-generating activities that are
      environmentally sound, socially equitable, and economically viable in the long
      term; and​

         f.​ ﻿Value Chain refers to the full range of activities required to bring a
      product or service from conception to delivery to final consumers, including
      production, processing, marketing, and distribution.​

​      SEC. 5. Creation of the Rural Development and Livelihood Enhancement Council. –
There is hereby created a Rural Development and Livelihood Enhancement Council,
hereinafter referred to as the “Council”, which shall be the primary coordinating and
policy-making body for the implementation of this Act. The Council shall be composed
of:​

          a.​ The Secretary of the Department of Agriculture (DA) as Chairperson;​

       The Council shall formulate a comprehensive National Rural Development and
Livelihood Enhancement Plan, monitor its implementation, and recommend policies
and programs to achieve the objectives of this Act.

       SEC. 6. Sustainable Livelihood Opportunities Program. – The DA, in coordination
with the DTI, DSWD, and other relevant agencies, shall establish and expand programs
for sustainable livelihood generation in rural areas. These programs shall include, but
not be limited to:

        a. Provision of start-up capital and equipment for micro-enterprises and
      MSMEs;​

          b. Support for diversified farming systems, organic agriculture, and
      climate-resilient agricultural practices;​

          c. Development of community-based agri-tourism initiatives;

          d. Promotion of sustainable aquaculture and fisheries; and

         e. Facilitation of access to raw materials, technology, and market linkages for
      rural products.

       SEC. 7. Improved Access to Financial Services. – The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas
(BSP), in coordination with the Department of Finance (DOF), shall develop and
implement policies to enhance the availability of affordable and accessible financial



                                          3
services in rural areas. These shall include, but not be limited to:​

          a. Encouraging the establishment of rural banks, cooperative banks, and
       microfinance institutions;​

          b. Promoting innovative financial products such as agricultural insurance,
       crop loans, and flexible repayment schemes;​

          c. Facilitating the digitalization of financial services in rural communities;
       and​

           d. Providing financial literacy programs for rural residents.

       SEC. 8. Capacity Building and Skills Development. – The Technical Education and
Skills Development Authority (TESDA), in coordination with the DA, DTI, and other
relevant agencies, shall design and implement skills development and capacity-building
programs tailored to the needs of rural communities. These programs shall focus on:​

           a. Modern agricultural and fishing techniques;

           b. Entrepreneurship and business management;

           c. Digital literacy and e-commerce;

           d. Vocational and technical skills relevant to emerging rural industries; and

           e. Value-adding and processing of agricultural and fishery products.

       SEC. 9. Rural Infrastructure Development. – The DPWH, in coordination with the
DA, Department of Transportation (DOTr), and other relevant agencies, shall prioritize
the development and improvement of essential rural infrastructure. These shall include,
but not be limited to:​

           a. Construction and rehabilitation of FMRs;

           b. Development and maintenance of irrigation systems;

          c. Establishment of post-harvest facilities, cold storage, and processing
       centers; and​

          d. Expansion of communication networks, including internet connectivity, in
       remote areas.

       SEC. 10. Value Chain Development. – The DTI, in coordination with the DA and
other relevant agencies, shall lead efforts in developing robust value chains for key
agricultural and rural products. These shall involve:​

           a. Facilitating market access for rural producers through trade fairs, online
       platforms, and direct linkages with buyers;​



                                              4
          b. Promoting product standardization, quality control, and branding;

           c. Encouraging investments in value-added processing and manufacturing
       in rural areas; and​

          d. Strengthening partnerships between producers, processors, distributors,
       and consumers.

       SEC. 11. Community Empowerment and Participation. – The Department of the
Interior and Local Government (DILG), in coordination with the CDA and DSWD, shall
strengthen the capacity of local government units (LGUs) and rural communities to
actively participate in rural development initiatives. It shall include:​

           a. Providing technical assistance to LGUs in developing and implementing
       their local rural development plans;​

          b. Promoting the formation and strengthening of cooperatives, associations,
       and other community-based organizations; and​

           c. Ensuring meaningful consultation and participation of rural communities
       in the planning, implementation, and monitoring of projects under this Act.

       SEC. 12. Funding. – The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The Council may also seek
and receive grants, donations, and other forms of assistance from local and international
sources, subject to existing laws and regulations.

        SEC. 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Rural Development and Livelihood Enhancement Council, in
consultation with relevant government agencies, LGUs, and stakeholders, shall
formulate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this
Act.

       SEC. 14. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 15. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 16. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
