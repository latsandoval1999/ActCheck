     AN ACT GRANTING A TWENTY PERCENT (20%) DISCOUNT AND
 EXEMPTION FROM VALUE-ADDED TAX TO ACTIVE AND RETIRED
MILITARY PERSONNEL OF THE ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES
            AND THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE


   The men and women of the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) and the
   Philippine National Police (PNP) are among the most dedicated servants of the
   Filipino people. Day in and day out, they put their lives on the line to defend our
   nation's sovereignty, protect our citizens from crime and lawlessness, and ensure
   the peace and order necessary for our society to function and thrive.


   Despite their unwavering commitment and immense sacrifices, our military and
   police personnel remain among the most financially challenged members of
   Philippine society. Many of them earn modest salaries that are often insufficient to
   meet their family's basic needs. This is especially true for retired personnel, who,
   despite having devoted a significant portion of their lives to national service, often
   struggle financially in their twilight years with limited pension incomes and rising
   costs of living.


   The Philippines has a long-standing precedent of providing special benefits and
   privileges to specific sectors of society in recognition of their contributions or
   vulnerabilities. Republic Act No. 9994, or the Expanded Senior Citizens Act of
   2010, grants senior citizens a twenty percent (20%) discount and VAT exemption
   on a wide range of goods and services. Similarly, Republic Act No. 7277, as
   amended by Republic Act Nos. 9442 and 10754, provides persons with disabilities
   with the same rate of discount and VAT exemption. These laws reflect the State's
   recognition of the social contract it has with these sectors.
 It is only fitting and just that our military and police personnel be accorded the
 same treatment. They have sacrificed their youth, health, time with their families,
 and in many cases, their very lives, in the service of the Filipino people. A twenty
 percent (20%) discount, modeled after the senior citizens' discount, would go a
 long way in improving the quality of life of these national heroes and their families.


This bill proposes to provide all active duty and retired personnel of the AFP and
PNP with a twenty percent (20%) discount and exemption from value-added tax on
a wide range of essential goods and services, including food, medicine, medical
services, transportation, and recreation. The discount shall be non-transferable and
personal to the qualified military or police personnel, to prevent abuse.


For establishments, the discount shall be treated as a tax-deductible expense,
consistent with the treatment under the Senior Citizens Act, so as not to unduly
burden the business sector. This approach has been proven workable and effective
under existing law.


The bill also provides for strict penalties against establishments that refuse to grant
the discount, as well as against individuals who abuse or misuse the privilege. This
is necessary to ensure that the intent of the law is faithfully carried out and that the
beneficiaries actually enjoy the privileges to which they are entitled.


It is further worth noting that extending this benefit to our military and police
personnel carries significant non-monetary value. It sends a clear and unequivocal
message that the Filipino nation recognizes and honors the sacrifices of its
defenders. It is a concrete expression of national gratitude.


In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      AN ACT GRANTING A TWENTY PERCENT (20%) DISCOUNT AND
         EXEMPTION FROM VALUE-ADDED TAX TO ACTIVE AND
       RETIRED MILITARY PERSONNEL OF THE ARMED FORCES OF
        THE PHILIPPINES AND THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:


1    CHAPTER I – GENERAL PROVISIONS
2
3       Section 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Military Personnel Discount
4          Act."
5
 6      Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
 7         to honor and uphold the dignity and welfare of military and police
 8         personnel who risk their lives in the service of the nation. Consistent with
 9         the principle of social justice and the constitutional mandate to promote the
10         well-being of all Filipinos, the State shall provide benefits and privileges
11         to active and retired personnel of the Armed Forces of the Philippines
12         (AFP) and the Philippine National Police (PNP) as recognition of their
13         invaluable service and sacrifices to the country.
14
15      Section 3. Objectives. – This Act aims to: (a) provide financial relief to active
16         and retired military and police personnel through a discount on goods and
17         services; (b) recognize the sacrifices and contributions of military and
18         police personnel to the nation; (c) promote the welfare and well-being of
19         active and retired AFP and PNP personnel and their dependents; and (d)
20         establish a clear and comprehensive legal framework for the
21        implementation of military and police discounts.
22
23     Section 4. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:
24           (a) "Military Personnel" refers to all active duty officers and enlisted
25           personnel of the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP), including the
26           Philippine Army, Philippine Navy, Philippine Air Force, and all
27           branches, units, and attached agencies thereof;
28           (b) "Police Personnel" refers to all active duty officers and enlisted
29           personnel of the Philippine National Police (PNP) and its attached
30           agencies;
31           (c) "Retired Military/Police Personnel" refers to AFP or PNP personnel
32           who have been honorably discharged or retired from service after
33           rendering at least twenty (20) years of service, or those retired on
34           account of disability incurred in the line of duty regardless of length of
35           service, and who are in receipt of a retirement pension from the
36           government;
37           (d) "Discount" refers to the reduction from the regular price by twenty
38           percent (20%) of the purchase price of goods and services granted under
39           this Act;
40           (e) "Establishment" refers to any business entity, whether publicly or
41           privately owned, that provides goods or services covered by this Act;
42           (f) "Identification Card" refers to the valid military or police
43           identification card issued by the AFP or PNP, as the case may be,
44           certifying the holder's status as active or retired military/police
45           personnel;
46           (g) "Value-Added Tax (VAT)" refers to the tax levied on goods and
47           services as provided under the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC)
48           of 1997, as amended.
49

50   CHAPTER II – PRIVILEGES AND BENEFITS
51
52     Section 5. Privileges Granted to Military and Police Personnel. – All active
53        and retired military and police personnel shall be entitled to the following
54        privileges:
55           (a) A discount of twenty percent (20%) from all establishments relative
56           to the sale of all goods and services for their exclusive use and
57           enjoyment;
58           (b) Exemption from the Value-Added Tax (VAT) on transactions
59           covered by this Act; and
60         (c) Priority lanes in all establishments and government offices.
61
62   Section 6. Establishments Required to Grant Discount. – The discount and
63      VAT exemption provided under this Act shall apply to the following:
64         (a) Hotels, restaurants, recreation centers, and similar lodging
65         establishments;
66         (b) Drugstores, pharmacies, and similar establishments for the purchase
67         of medicines, vitamins, and health supplements;
68         (c) Medical and dental clinics, diagnostics laboratories, hospitals, and
69         similar establishments for professional fees, medical services, and
70         laboratory procedures;
71         (d) Movie houses, theaters, concert halls, circuses, leisure, and
72         amusement establishments;
73         (e) Public land and water transportation utilities, including air transport,
74         for the purchase of transportation tickets;
75         (f) Funeral parlors and similar establishments for the purchase of
76         funeral and burial services for the military or police personnel;
77         (g) Supermarkets, grocery stores, and similar retail establishments for
78         the purchase of food, grocery items, and basic necessities;
79         (h) All government and private educational institutions for the purchase
80         of school supplies, materials, and textbooks;
81         (i) Telecommunications companies for subscription and usage of basic
82         communication services; and
83         (j) Such other establishments as may be determined by the Department
84         of National Defense (DND), the Department of the Interior and Local
85         Government (DILG), and the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI).
86
87   Section 7. Non-transferability of Privilege. – The privileges granted under this
88      Act are non-transferable and shall be used exclusively by the qualified
89      military or police personnel. Abuse of this privilege, including lending or
90      allowing others to use one's identification card for the purpose of availing
91      of the discount, shall be punishable under this Act.
92
93   Section 8. Identification and Proof of Eligibility. – To avail of the discount
94      and other benefits under this Act, the military or police personnel
95      concerned shall present a valid and current AFP or PNP identification card
96      issued by the respective branch or office. For retired personnel, a valid
97      retirement identification card issued by the AFP Retirement and Separation
98      Benefits System (RSBS) or the PNP Retirement Benefits Administration
99         Service (RBAS) shall be required.
100

101   CHAPTER III – TAX TREATMENT AND INCENTIVES
102
103     Section 9. Tax Deduction for Establishments. – The amount of discount
104        granted under this Act shall be treated as a deductible expense for income
105        tax purposes and shall not be passed on to other consumers. All businesses
106        granting the prescribed discount shall be entitled to deduct the said
107        discount from their gross income: Provided, That only the twenty percent
108        (20%) discount actually granted shall be deductible; and Provided, Further,
109        That the total deductible amount shall not exceed the cost of the goods sold
110        or services rendered to the military or police personnel.
111
112     Section 10. VAT Exemption. – Transactions covered by this Act shall be
113        exempt from the value-added tax (VAT) as defined under Section 4.109-1
114        of Revenue Regulations No. 16-2005, or as may be amended by the Bureau
115        of Internal Revenue (BIR). The BIR shall issue the necessary revenue
116        regulations to implement the VAT exemption.
117

118   CHAPTER IV – IMPLEMENTATION AND MONITORING
119
120     Section 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within sixty (60)
121        days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of National Defense
122        (DND), the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), the
123        Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), the Department of Finance
124        (DOF), and the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), in coordination with the
125        Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) and the Philippine National Police
126        (PNP), shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the
127        effective implementation of this Act.
128
129     Section 12. Monitoring and Compliance. – The DND and DILG, in
130        coordination with the DTI and the BIR, shall be responsible for monitoring
131        and ensuring compliance with this Act. They shall establish an efficient
132        mechanism to: (a) receive and act on complaints from military and police
133        personnel against establishments that refuse to grant the discount; (b)
134        conduct regular inspections of establishments covered by this Act; and (c)
135        impose penalties on establishments found to be in violation.
136
137     Section 13. Grievance Mechanism. – A military and police personnel who has
138        been denied the discount or has been aggrieved by any act in violation of
139        this Act may file a complaint with the DND, DILG, or DTI, or the
140        appropriate regional office, which shall act on such complaint within thirty
141        (30) days from receipt thereof.
142
143     Section 14. Annual Report to Congress. – The DND and DILG shall submit
144        an annual report to both chambers of Congress on the status of
145        implementation of this Act, including the number of beneficiaries,
146        complaints received, and actions taken thereon.
147

148   CHAPTER V – PROHIBITED ACTS AND PENALTIES
149
150     Section 15. Prohibited Acts. – The following shall be prohibited:
151           (a) Refusal by establishments to grant the twenty percent (20%)
152           discount and VAT exemption as prescribed herein;
153           (b) Imposition of additional charges or surcharges by establishments to
154           compensate for the discount granted under this Act;
155           (c) Misuse or fraudulent use of the military or police identification card
156           by persons not entitled thereto;
157           (d) Lending or allowing another person to use one's identification card
158           for the purpose of availing of the discount;
159           (e) Falsification, tampering, or counterfeiting of identification cards for
160           the purpose of fraudulently availing of the discount; and
161           (f) Any act that discriminates against, humiliates, or abuses military or
162           police personnel in the exercise of their rights under this Act.
163
164     Section 16. Penalties. – Any person found guilty of violating any provision of
165        this Act shall be punished as follows:
166            (a) For establishments: A fine of not less than Fifty Thousand Pesos
167            (Php 50,000.00) but not more than Two Hundred Thousand Pesos (Php
168            200,000.00), and revocation of its business permit upon the third
169            offense;
170            (b) For individuals who misuse or fraudulently use the identification
171            card: imprisonment of not less than six (6) months but not more than
172            two (2) years, or a fine of not less than Twenty Thousand Pesos (Php
173            20,000.00) but not more than One Hundred Thousand Pesos (Php
174            100,000.00), or both, at the discretion of the court;
175           (c) For military or police personnel who lend their identification cards:
176           administrative sanctions in accordance with existing military and police
177           rules and regulations, without prejudice to criminal liability under
178           applicable laws.
179

180   CHAPTER VI – FINAL PROVISIONS
181
182     Section 17. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
183        implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current
184        appropriations of the DND and the DILG. Thereafter, the necessary
185        appropriations shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
186
187     Section 18. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
188        unconstitutional or invalid by a competent court, the remaining provisions
189        not affected thereby shall continue to remain in full force and effect.
190
191     Section 19. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules and
192        regulations, or other issuances inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed
193        or modified accordingly.
194
195     Section 20. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following
196        its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of
197        general circulation.
