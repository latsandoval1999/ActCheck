                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Agriculture in the Philippines remains highly vulnerable to climate shocks, natural
disasters, pests, diseases, and other risks that may result in significant losses for farmers
and fisherfolk. Crop insurance serves as an important mechanism for protecting
agricultural producers against such losses and promoting the stability of the agricultural
sector.

Presidential Decree No. 1467 created the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC)
to provide insurance protection to agricultural producers. The government likewise
extends agricultural credit through various subsidized lending programs administered by
government agencies and government financial institutions.

While agricultural credit and crop insurance programs presently exist, there is no
statutory mechanism that automatically links government-supported agricultural credit
with crop insurance coverage. As a result, insurance enrollment remains dependent on
separate administrative processes and the timely exchange of information among lending
institutions and implementing agencies. Existing laws likewise do not clearly provide that
a qualified agricultural borrower shall remain entitled to insurance protection
notwithstanding delays, omissions, or failures in enrollment, data transmission, or other
administrative requirements beyond the borrower's control.

This bill seeks to address this gap by providing automatic agricultural insurance coverage
for qualified recipients of government-supported agricultural credit. Under this measure,
insurance coverage shall attach by operation of law upon approval of the loan, credit
accommodation, or financial assistance. The bill further ensures that a qualified borrower
shall not be deprived of such coverage by reason of administrative, technical, or
procedural deficiencies attributable to participating institutions.

To facilitate implementation, the bill establishes a One-Stop Automated Integration
System among participating institutions to streamline enrollment and insurance
administration. The bill likewise provides funding mechanisms necessary to sustain the
insurance coverage mandated under this Act.

By strengthening the linkage between agricultural credit and agricultural insurance, this
measure seeks to provide greater protection to farmers and fisherfolk, encourage
participation in government agricultural programs, and promote agricultural productivity,
food security, and rural development.
In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT PROVIDING AUTOMATIC AGRICULTURAL INSURANCE
COVERAGE FOR QUALIFIED RECIPIENTS OF GOVERNMENT-SUPPORTED
  AGRICULTURAL CREDIT, ESTABLISHING A ONE-STOP INTEGRATED
   ENROLLMENT SYSTEM, AND PROVIDING FUNDING MECHANISMS
                         THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Automatic Agricultural
Insurance Coverage Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. —It is the policy of the State to protect small
farmers and fisherfolk from financial losses arising from natural disasters, pests, diseases,
and other agricultural risks. The State recognizes that agricultural credit and agricultural
insurance are complementary mechanisms for promoting agricultural productivity, food
security, and rural development. Towards this end, the State shall ensure that qualified
recipients of government-supported agricultural credit are automatically afforded
corresponding insurance coverage and that such coverage shall not be impaired, delayed,
or denied by reason of administrative, technical, or procedural deficiencies beyond the
control of the borrower.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. — As used in this Act:

       (a) Subsidized Credit Program refers to any agricultural loan, credit
       accommodation, credit line, or financial assistance program funded, managed,
       guaranteed, administered, or subsidized by the National Government,
       Government Financial Institutions (GFIs), or the Agricultural Credit Policy
       Council (ACPC).

       (b) Small Farmer and Fisherfolk refers to individuals engaged in agriculture or
       fishing as defined by applicable laws, rules, and regulations of the Department of
       Agriculture (DA) and registered under the Registry System for Basic Sectors in
       Agriculture (RSBSA).

       (c) Automatic Insurance Coverage refers to insurance protection provided under
       the appropriate insurance program of the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation
       (PCIC) which attaches by operation of law upon approval of a loan, credit
       accommodation, or financial assistance under a Subsidized Credit Program.
       (d) Interoperability refers to the ability of information technology systems and
       software applications to communicate, exchange data accurately, and utilize the
       information exchanged for purposes of implementing this Act.

SECTION 4. Automatic Insurance Coverage for Qualified Borrowers. — Every Small
Farmer and Fisherfolk who is granted a loan, credit accommodation, or financial
assistance under a Subsidized Credit Program shall automatically be covered by the
appropriate insurance program of the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC).
Coverage shall attach by operation of law upon approval of the loan, credit
accommodation, or financial assistance. Provided that no qualified borrower shall be
denied, impaired, or deprived of insurance coverage solely by reason of the failure, delay,
omission, or technical inability of any participating institution to complete enrollment
procedures, transmit information, process applications, or comply with administrative
requirements. Provided further that the approval of a qualified loan, credit
accommodation, or financial assistance shall constitute sufficient basis for coverage
under the applicable PCIC insurance program, subject to the terms and conditions
prescribed by law and the implementing rules and regulations.

SECTION 5. "One-Stop" Automated Integration System. — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC), in
coordination with the Land Bank of the Philippines (LBP), the Development Bank of the
Philippines (DBP), the Agricultural Credit Policy Council (ACPC), and the Bangko
Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), shall design, develop, and deploy a unified and interoperable
digital platform to facilitate the administration of insurance coverage under this Act.

The system shall adhere to the following operational parameters:

       (a) Upon approval of a loan, credit accommodation, or financial assistance under
       a Subsidized Credit Program, participating institutions shall electronically
       transmit to the PCIC all information reasonably necessary for the administration
       of insurance coverage, including borrower demographics, crop type, farm
       location, and farm size;

       (b) Such transmission shall satisfy the enrollment and reporting requirements of
       the applicable PCIC insurance program. No separate insurance application or
       submission of substantially similar information shall be required from the
       borrower;

       (c) Participating institutions shall incorporate into their loan application forms the
       necessary consent provisions for data sharing and insurance administration in
       accordance with applicable laws; and

       (d) The PCIC and participating institutions shall maintain interoperable systems
       capable of secure and timely transmission of information necessary to implement
       this Act.

SECTION 6. Funding for Insurance Coverage Under this Act. — The amounts necessary
to support the insurance coverage provided under Section 4 of this Act shall form part of
the program costs of Subsidized Credit Programs covered by this Act. Government
agencies, government financial institutions, and other entities administering Subsidized
Credit Programs shall include the funding requirements necessary to support such
insurance coverage in their respective annual budget proposals and program allocations,
subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations. The
Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC), in coordination with the Department of
Agriculture (DA), the Department of Finance (DOF), the Department of Budget and
Management (DBM), and participating institutions, shall annually determine the funding
requirements necessary to sustain the insurance coverage provided under this Act. The
amounts necessary for the implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 7. Inter-Agency Compliance and Oversight. — The Department of
Information and Communications Technology (DICT) shall provide technical oversight
over the One-Stop Automated Integration System established under this Act and shall
ensure compliance with applicable cybersecurity, data privacy, and government
digitalization standards. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) shall monitor compliance
by participating financial institutions with the requirements of this Act, including the
timely transmission of information necessary for the administration of insurance
coverage. The Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC), in coordination with
participating institutions, shall oversee the implementation of the automatic insurance
coverage provided under this Act and ensure that qualified borrowers are not deprived of
coverage by reason of administrative, technical, or procedural deficiencies beyond their
control.

SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC), in
coordination with the Department of Agriculture (DA), the Department of Finance
(DOF), the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Bangko Sentral ng
Pilipinas (BSP), the Department of Information and Communications Technology
(DICT), and other concerned agencies, shall promulgate the rules and regulations
necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, administrative orders,
rules, and regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act,
including relevant provisions of Presidential Decree No. 1467 and Republic Act No.
8175, are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved.
