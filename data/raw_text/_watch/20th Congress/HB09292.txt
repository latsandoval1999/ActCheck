                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Diabetes and other endocrine disorders continue to affect millions of Filipinos and
place a heavy burden on families and the healthcare system. These illnesses often lead
to serious complications such as heart disease, stroke, kidney failure, blindness, and
amputations, many of which can be prevented through early diagnosis, proper
treatment, and specialized care. While the government has already taken important
steps through the National Diabetes Act of 1996, the growing number of cases shows
that stronger and more permanent solutions are needed.

This bill seeks to establish the Philippine Center for Diabetes and Endocrinology
(PCDE), a dedicated national specialty center that will lead the country’s efforts in the
prevention, treatment, research, and management of diabetes and endocrine
disorders. The PCDE will provide high-quality medical care for patients, especially
those with complex and life-threatening conditions, while also serving as a center for
advanced training of healthcare workers and research on better treatments and
prevention strategies.

The proposed measure will also help improve access to specialized healthcare
services beyond Metro Manila by supporting regional networks and partnerships
with local hospitals and health systems. In line with the goals of Universal Health Care,
the PCDE will strengthen coordination among government agencies, healthcare

affordable, and quality endocrine care wherever they may be.

The establishment of a dedicated specialty institution for diabetes and endocrine
disorders follows the successful model of other government specialty centers that
have greatly improved healthcare services in the country. By creating the PCDE, the
State affirms its commitment to protecting public health, reducing preventable deaths
and complications, and improving the quality of life of millions of Filipinos.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Center for
Diabetes and Endocrinology Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among
them, and to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health
development, with priority for the needs of the underprivileged sick, elderly, persons
with disabilities, women, and children. Consistent with this policy, the State shall
strengthen specialized care, prevention, research, education, and training responsive
to the country’s burden of diabetes and endocrine disorders.

SECTION 3. Establishment of the Philippine Center for Diabetes and
Endocrinology. —

   (a) Establishment. There is hereby established a specialty hospital to be known as
       the Philippine Center for Diabetes and Endocrinology (PCDE), which shall
       be attached to the Department of Health (DOH) for policy coordination and
       shall serve as a national specialty center for diabetes and endocrine disorders.

                                                                              3 | Page
  (b) Principal Office; Facilities. The PCDE shall have its principal office in Metro
      Manila and may establish satellite clinics, specialty centers, or regional
      service units in the Visayas, Mindanao, and other strategic areas, subject to
      DOH planning standards and availability of funds.

  (c) Nature. The PCDE shall have corporate powers necessary to carry out its
      functions, subject to applicable government auditing, procurement, budgeting,
      and civil service laws.

SECTION 4. Mandate and Objectives. — The PCDE shall the lead government
specialty institution for diabetes and endocrinology, and shall:

  (a) Establish, operate, and maintain clinical facilities that deliver integrated,
      evidence-based specialty services for diabetes and endocrine disorders;

  (b) Develop, implement, and support programs for screening, early detection,
      diagnosis, and management of diabetes, obesity, and metabolic syndrome, in
      coordination with the DOH and local government units;

  (c) Promote and undertake clinical, public health, and translational research, and
      publish and disseminate research outputs for public benefit;

  (d) Provide education, residency/fellowship training, continuing professional
      development, and capability-building programs for health workers involved
      in diabetes and endocrine care;

  (e) Provide technical assistance to universities, hospitals, and research
      institutions, and administer scholarships and grants for advanced training,
      subject to applicable government rules;

  (f) Coordinate with relevant national and local stakeholders to harmonize
      programs on diabetes and endocrine disorders, consistent with national policy
      under the National Diabetes Act of 1996; and

  (g) Establish telemedicine-enabled referral and co-management networks,
      particularly for geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas, subject to
      the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable DOH standards.

SECTION 5. Corporate Powers. —

To carry out its mandate, the PCDE shall have the power to:

   (a) Acquire, hold, lease, encumber, and dispose of real and personal property,
       subject to applicable laws and regulations;

   (b) Receive grants, donations, endowments, and contributions, subject to
       applicable laws, accounting, and auditing rules;

   (c) Enter into contracts and agreements necessary for its operations, subject to
       applicable procurement and government contracting rules;

   (d) Open and maintain bank accounts and manage its funds in accordance with
       applicable government accounting and auditing rules;

   (e) Impose and collect reasonable fees and charges for services, subject to DOH
       policies and existing laws on government fees and hospital billing, with due
       regard to indigent and financially vulnerable patients; and

   (f) Perform such other acts as may be necessary and incidental to the attainment
       of its purposes.

SECTION 6. Governing Board; Composition; Term. —

   (a) Governing Board. — The PCDE shall be governed by a Board of
       Trustees composed of:

       1. The Secretary of Health, as Chairperson;
       2. The Director-General of the National Economic and Development
          Authority (NEDA), or duly authorized representative;
       3. The Secretary of Science and Technology (DOST), or duly authorized
          representative;
       4. The President of the University of the Philippines (UP), or duly
          authorized representative;
       5. One (1) representative from the Philippine Society of Endocrinology,
          Diabetes and Metabolism (PSEDM); and



                                                                          5 | Page
       6. Two (2) members from the private sector or academe with recognized
          competence in endocrinology/diabetology, hospital administration,
          public health, health financing, or biomedical research.



   (b) Appointment and Term. — The ex officio members shall serve by virtue of
       office. The appointive members shall be appointed by the President of the
       Philippines for a term of three (3) years, and may be reappointed once.

   (c) Compensation. — Board compensation and per diems, if any, shall be in
       accordance with existing laws and rules.

SECTION 7. Powers and Functions of the Board. — The Board shall have the
following powers and functions:

   (a) Set strategic directions, approve institutional policies, and ensure alignment
       with DOH plans and national health priorities;

   (b) Approve the PCDE’s organizational structure, staffing pattern, and major
       programs, subject to applicable laws and budgeting rules;

   (c) Approve the annual budget proposal and authorize expenditures consistent
       with law;

   (d) Approve the schedule of reasonable fees and charges and policies on
       charity/discount mechanisms for indigent and vulnerable patients;

   (e) Adopt internal rules for governance and operations consistent with law; and

   (f) Exercise such other powers necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.

SECTION 8. Chief Executive Officer; Key Officers. —

   (a) Executive Director. — The PCDE shall be headed by an Executive
       Director who shall serve as Chief Executive Officer and shall be appointed by
       the President of the Philippines upon recommendation of the Board.

   (b) Term. — The Executive Director shall serve for a term of three (3) years,
       renewable once, unless sooner removed for cause.


                                                                            6 | Page
   (c) Other Officers. — The Board may create and fill such offices as may be
       necessary for efficient operations, subject to applicable civil service and
       compensation laws.

SECTION 9. Powers and Duties of the Executive Director. —

The Executive Director shall:
   (a) Implement Board policies and manage day-to-day operations;
   (b) Direct and supervise all personnel and units of the PCDE in accordance with
       law and approved staffing patterns;
   (c) Represent the PCDE in official dealings and execute contracts and instruments
       consistent with Board authority and applicable laws;
   (d) Prepare and submit to the Board the PCDE’s plans, budget proposals, and
       periodic performance reports; and
   (e) Perform such other functions as may be assigned by the Board.

SECTION 10. Inter-Agency and Local Government Assistance. — The PCDE may
call upon any department, bureau, office, agency, government-owned or -controlled
corporation, local government unit, and state university or college for technical,
training, research, or operational assistance necessary to carry out this Act.

SECTION 11. Emergency Authority. — In cases of declared public health
emergencies or urgent circumstances requiring immediate action where a Board
meeting cannot be timely convened, the Executive Director may act within delegated
limits as may be provided by the Board, subject to ratification at the soonest possible
time.

SECTION 12. Donations, Grants, and Partnerships. — The PCDE may solicit and
receive donations, grants, and assistance from domestic or foreign sources, public or
private, subject to existing laws on foreign assistance, reporting, auditing, and
anti-corruption safeguards.

SECTION 13. Tax Treatment. — Any tax exemptions and deductibility of donations
in favor of the PCDE shall be subject to the National Internal Revenue Code and
applicable rules of the Bureau of Internal Revenue, and shall not be construed as
an automatic blanket exemption unless expressly authorized under existing tax laws.

SECTION 14. Appropriations. —



                                                                              7 | Page
   (a) Initial Funding. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
       Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the DOH and such
       other available sources as may be identified under existing laws.

   (b) Thereafter. — The amounts necessary for the continued operation and
       maintenance of the PCDE shall be included in the annual General
       Appropriations Act.

SECTION 15. Annual Report. — The PCDE shall submit to the President of the
Philippines and to Congress an annual report on its accomplishments, utilization,
outcomes, audited financial statements, and such key performance indicators as may
be prescribed, on or before 30 April of each year.

SECTION 16. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOH, in consultation with relevant agencies
and stakeholders, including DOST, NEDA, UP, and the Philippine Society of
Endocrinology, Diabetes and Metabolism (PSEDM), shall promulgate the
implementing rules and regulations necessary for its effective implementation.

SECTION 17. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.

SECTION 18. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations,
and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

SECTION 19. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
