                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE


        Annual health examinations are important for the early detection and
management of silent medical conditions. However, data shows 40% of Filipinos
undergo annual medical checkups.1 Among the primary reasons behind this low rate
are the high cost attributed to consultation and diagnostic tests, and prioritization of
basic needs over healthcare concerns.

        We must shift our perspective from a reactive mindset, that is “If it is not
broken, don’t fix it”, to a preventive and proactive health care. By prioritizing early
intervention, we optimize the window of opportunity to treat and manage diseases
before symptoms manifest, which will result to saving more lives at a much lower
healthcare costs.

        Under Article XIII of the 1987 Philippine Constitution, the State is mandated
to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development. While
we took a big step towards this mandate with the enactment of Republic Act No.
11223 or the Universal Health Care Act in 2018, there should be a continuous efforts
to expand and enhance the range of healthcare services being offered to the Filipino
people.

      The saying “health is wealth” applies not only to individual but to the nation
as a whole. Maintaining a healthy population is a good foundation of a robust
economy, efficient and effective workforce, and high national productivity. The
expansion and strengthening of our healthcare services is an essential investment in
our human capital.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT PROVIDING FOR FREE ANNUAL MEDICAL CHECK-UP FOR ALL
           FILIPINOS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

      Section 1. Shor t Title. – This Act shall be known as “Fr ee Annual Medical Check-
up Act”

        Section 2. Declar ation of Pr inciple. – It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right to health of all Filipinos and instill health consciousness among them. To
this end, the State shall provide programs that will ensure that all Filipinos have access to
quality and cost-effective preventive and curative health services.

       Section 3. Cover age. – All Filipinos Citizens, who are direct or indirect contributors
members of the National Health Insurance Program (NHIC) under Republic Act no. 11223 or
the “Universal Health Care Law”, shall be entitled to the existing and applicfable benefits
under the Philippine Health Insurance Program. All members shall be entitled to a free
Annual Medical Check-up.

        For the purpose of this Act, “Annual Medical Check-up” is a yearly preventive routine
health evaluation, which includes consultation, physical examination, routine laboratory or
diagnostic examinations, complete blood count (CBC), lipid profile, fasting blood sugar,
chest x-ray, urinalysis, stool analysis, complete physical examinations, among others.

         Section 4. Schedule of Fr ee Medical Check-up. – To promote health, identify risks,
ensure effective early diagnosis of diseases, PhilHealth shall establish a scheduling system
that allows timely and equitable access to health care services.

       Section 5. Appr opr iations. – The amount necessary to implement this Act shall be
charged against the PhilHealth funds.
       Section. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) calendar
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health, in coordination with
PhilHealth, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for its implementation.

        Section 7. Separ ability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such declaration shall remain in full
force and effect.

        Section 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, decrees, rules and
regulations, or any part thereof inconsistent with the provision of this Act are deemed
repealed or modified accordingly.

        Section 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
