AN ACT DECLARING DECEMBER 10 OF EVERY YEAR AS A LOCAL HOLIDAY IN
THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARRASCAL, SURIGAO DEL SUR, IN CELEBRATION OF
THE “ADLAW NAN CARRASCAL”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Carrascal in the Province of Surigao del Sur commemorates its
Charter Day every 10th of December, known as “Adlaw nan Carrascal.” This annual
celebration marks a significant milestone in the municipality's history, recognizing its
establishment as a distinct local government unit and honoring the generations of people
of Carrascal whose dedication, contributions, and collective efforts have shaped the
community into what it is today.

The observance of Adlaw nan Carrascal provides an opportunity for the people of
Carrascal to reflect on their municipality's historical roots, celebrate its accomplishments,
and recognize the progress it has achieved through the years. More than a
commemoration of its founding, the occasion strengthens the people’s sense of identity,
civic pride, and solidarity, while reaffirming their shared commitment to the continued
growth and development of their municipality.

Declaring December 10 of every year a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of
Carrascal will allow its residents, local government officials and employees, and other
stakeholders to fully participate in the activities commemorating this important occasion.

It will provide the community with an opportunity to honor its heritage, promote local
traditions, and celebrate the achievements and aspirations of the municipality.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly soyght.

Republic of the Philippines
House of Representatives
Queson City, Philippines

TWENTIETH CONGRESS
Second Regular Session

House BILLNO. 11814

Introduced by REPRESENTATIVE ROMEO S. MOMO, SR.

AN ACT DECLARING DECEMBER 10 OF EVERY YEAR AS A LOCAL HOLIDAY IN
THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARRASCAL, SURIGAO DEL SUR, IN CELEBRATION OF
THE “ADLAW NAN CARRASCAL”

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. December 10 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking
holiday in the Municipality of Carrascal, Surigao dei Sur, to be known as “Adlaw nan
Carrascal,” in commemoration of its founding anniversary.

Sec. 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
