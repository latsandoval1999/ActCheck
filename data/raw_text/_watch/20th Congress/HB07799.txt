THE NATIONAL ATHLETES AND COACHES BENEFITS AND INCENTIVES ACT

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 17, Article II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution mandates that the State shall
give priority to education, science and technology, arts, culture, and sports to foster
patriotism and nationalism, accelerate social progress, and promote total human
liberation and development.

Meanwhile, Section 19, Article XIV of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that
(1) The State shall promote physical education and encourage sports programs,
league competitions, and amateur sports, including training for international
competitions, to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for the development
of a healthy and alert citizenry, and (2) All educational institutions shall undertake
regular sports activities throughout the country in cooperation with athletic clubs and
other sectors.

In line with this, Republic Act No. 10699 or the “National Athletes and Coaches Benefits
and Incentives Act” mandates the State to promote excellence in sports by looking
after the welfare of national athletes and coaches competing for the country and by
providing benefits and incentives for national athletes and other athletes who win in
international sports competitions and bring honor and recognition to the country.




                                           1
In the recent 2025 ASEAN Para Games held in Nakhon Ratchasima, Thailand from
January 20 to 26, 2026, the Philippine delegation, representing the nation’s talent in
various sports, achieved an outstanding performance for a total of 134 medals,
consisting of 45 gold, 37 silver, and 52 bronze medals. The performance of the
Philippine delegation in the said competition is the best medal haul since the country’s
participation in 2001, finishing 4th place overall in the medal tally.

Although our para athlete medalists are entitled to cash incentives according to
Republic Act No. 10699, they will only receive half the amount of what other athletes
would receive at the same level of competition. This is a sad reality that our para
athletes experience, even if they pour the same amount or even more than double
the effort of training to deliver an outstanding performance at the international stage.

Thus, this bill seeks to amend Section 8 of Republic Act No. 10699 in order to increase
the cash incentives that will be received by para athletes who win gold, silver, and
bronze medals in international sports competitions which is equal to the amounts
received by other athletes who participate at the same level of competition. In
addition, this bill also seeks to amend Section 7 of the said law to increase the amount
of death benefit to be received upon the death of any national athlete and coach.

In line with this, the approval of the bill is earnestly being sought.
