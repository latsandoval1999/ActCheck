AN ACT RENAMING THE NAGA CITY NATIONAL ROAD, FROM THE
BOUNDARY OF BARANGAY PEÑAFRANCIA TRAVERSING BARANGAYS
SAN FELIPE, PACOL, CAROLINA AND PANTCUASON IN THE CITY OF NAGA,
PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR TO SENATOR TECLA R. SAN ANDRES
ZIGA AVENUE

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Title and Renaming. The Naga City National Road, from the
Boundary of Barangay Pefiafrancia traversing Barangays San Felipe, Pacol,
Carolina and Panicuason in the City of Naga, Province of Camarines Sur,
comprising a total distance of 16.4 kilometers, is hereby renamed to Senator Tecla
R. San Andres Ziga Avenue.

     SECTION 2. Implementation and Signage. The Department of Public Works
and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement
the provisions of this Act within sixty (60) days from its effectivity.

     SECTION 3. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

     Approved,
