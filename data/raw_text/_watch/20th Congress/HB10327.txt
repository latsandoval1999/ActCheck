                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to repeal Batas Pambansa Blg. 36, entitled “An Act Imposing an
Energy Tax on Electric Power Consumption, and For Other Purposes”. It recognizes that
electricity should no longer be burdened by an outdated tax that adds to the cost of
every Filipino household’s monthly electric bill.

        When BP Blg. 36 was enacted in 1979, the country faced economic and energy
challenges that justified the imposition of an energy tax. More than four (4) decades
later, the circumstances that gave rise to BP Blg. 36 have changed. Electricity has
become an indispensable necessity that powers homes, supports education, enables
livelihoods, and allows families to access essential services.

        Currently, Filipino consumers already shoulder numerous charges in their
monthly electricity bills, which include generation, transmission, distribution,
system loss, universal charges, and other government-imposed taxes and fees. The
continued collection of an additional energy tax only increases the financial burden
on households that are already struggling with rising prices and the increasing cost
of living.

        The government must ensure that taxes remain responsive to present-day
realities and do not unnecessarily burden the public. Repealing this outdated tax
shall help reduce electricity costs, provide meaningful relief to Filipino families, and
allow households to devote more of their income to food, education, healthcare, and
other basic necessities.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
REPEALING BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 36, ENTITLED “AN ACT IMPOSING
AN ENERGY TAX ON ELECTRIC POWER CONSUMPTION”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Energy Tax Repeal Act”.

       SEC. 2. Energy Tax Repeal. – Batas Pambansa (BP) Blg. 36, entitled “An Act
Imposing an Energy Tax on Electric Power Consumption”, is hereby repealed.

       SEC. 3. Effect of Repeal. – Upon the effectivity of this Act:

         a. The collection of energy tax imposed under BP Blg. 36 shall
       immediately cease;

           b. Electric distribution utilities, electric cooperatives, generation
       companies, retail electricity suppliers, and all other entities responsible for
       billing electricity consumers shall discontinue the imposition, collection, and
       remittance of the energy tax; and

          c. Any implementing rules, regulations, circulars, or issuances issued
       pursuant to BP Blg. 36 shall likewise cease to have force and effect, insofar as
       they are inconsistent with this Act.

        SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance, in coordination with the Bureau of
Internal Revenue, the Department of Energy, and Energy Regulatory Commission,
shall promulgate the rules and regulations and other issuances as may be necessary
to ensure the effective implementation of this Act.

        SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
