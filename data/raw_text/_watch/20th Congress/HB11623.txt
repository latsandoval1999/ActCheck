20                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   At 7:30 in the morning, Juan books a motorcycle ride through an app to get to work.
23   The trip is quick and convenient…until the motorcycle is involved in a road crash. Juan
24   is injured and rushed to the hospital, but questions now arise: Who will shoulder his
25   medical expenses? Who is responsible for the accident? And who will provide
26   immediate assistance?
27
28   The proposed Motorcycles-for-Hire Act seeks to address these gaps by establishing
29   clear mechanisms and guidelines for the entire operation of motorcycles-for-hire, from
30   registration, licensing, and safety standards to franchising, fares, platform accreditation,
31   insurance, emergency response, enforcement, and penalties. It seeks to ensure that as
32   motorcycle-based transport continues to grow, convenience is matched by safety,
33   accountability, and proper government regulation.
34
35   Because behind every motorcycle-for-hire is not just a rider or a passenger, but a
36   person trying to get to work, get home, or make a living. The measure seeks to ensure
37   that the convenience of this service never comes at the expense of the safety of the
38   people who depend on it.
39
40   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

20                               AN ACT
21        REGULATING THE OPERATION OF MOTORCYCLES-FOR-HIRE AND
22              PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF
23
24   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
25   Congress assembled:
26
27          SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Motorcycles-
28   for-Hire Act”.
29
30          SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the
31   importance of transportation in economic development. The recent innovations
32   in technology have opened an opportunity for other modes of public conveyance,
33   such as motorcycles-for-hire, to thrive as a reliable and accessible mode of
34   transportation. However, despite its promise of convenience and fast service,
35   it must be regulated to ensure that the interest of the general public is protected.
36   Thus, the State shall allow the regulated operation of motorcycles-for-hire
37   to provide the public with an additional mode of public transportation, which is
38   sufficient, safe, secure and economical. To this end, the State shall establish
39   mechanisms and guidelines in regulating all the operational aspects of
40   motorcycles-for-hire.
41
42         SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:
43
44         a) Digital   platform   refers to    information and communications
45         technology-enabled mechanism that connects and integrate producers and
46         users in online environments where goods and services are requested,
47         developed, and sold, and data is generated and exchanged;
48
49         b) Motorcycle refers to any two (2)-wheeled motor vehicle without
50         appendages;
51
52         c) Motorcycle-for-hire refers to a motorcycle, duly registered as such under
53         this Act, and used to carry passengers or goods on a for hire basis, offering
 1         its services to the public. A motorcycle-for-hire shall be considered a public
 2         utility vehicle and its operation a public utility;
 3
 4         d) Motorcycle taxi platform provider (MTPP) refers to an entity which
 5         provides pre-arranged transportation services for compensation using a
 6         digital platform to connect clients with riders to facilitate a contract of
 7         carriage of persons using a motorcycle-for-hire;
 8
 9         e) Multi-homing refers to an approach where an operator may accredit a
10         particular motorcycle-for-hire to more than one digital platform providers in
11         delivering transport services;
12
13         f) Online E-commerce platform provider (OEPP) refers to an entity
14         which provides pre-arranged transportation services for compensation
15         using a digital platform to connect clients with transport services to
16         facilitate a contract of carriage of goods, including carriage of goods over
17         goods sold on said digital platform;
18
19         g) Operator refers to the person in whose name the motorcycle-for-hire is
20         registered;
21
22         h) Parcel means a package or rectangular box, the dimension and
23         weight of which is as specified in the implementing rules and regulations of
24         this Act, containing goods or some form of transportable property intended
25         for delivery to an addressee prominently displayed on at least one (1) of its
26         sides; and
27
28         i) Rider refers to the driver of the motorcycle-for-hire.
29
30          SECTION 4. Scope and Application. – This Act shall apply to all
31   aspects of the      operation    of    motorcycles used as        a      common
32   carrier for  the transportation of passengers or goods. For this purpose, goods
33   shall include parcels and mail. This Act shall not apply to motorcycles directly
34   owned by entities and used exclusively in the line of business for delivery of goods
35   and other services.
36
37          SECTION 5. Motorcycles-For-Hire as Common Carriers. – The
38   operation of motorcycles-for-hire is imbued with public interest. As such, the same
39   shall be governed by the applicable provisions of the Civil Code of the Philippines
40   and other pertinent laws, rules and regulations on common carriers,
41   particularly on the required degree of diligence to be observed in the course of
42   transporting passengers or goods, and on the presumption of culpability in case
43   of breach of a contract of carriage. MTPPs and OEPPs should exercise
44   extraordinary diligence in the accreditation and supervision of motorcycle-for-
45   hire operators and riders operating under their platforms.
46
47         SECTION 6. Registration of Motorcycles-For-Hire. – A motorcycle
48   intended to be used as a motorcycle-for-hire must be duly registered with the
49   Land Transportation Office (LTO) in accordance with the requirements set forth
50   in Section 7 of this Act. The LTO shall ensure the roadworthiness of all
51   motorcycles-for-hire before their registration, or any renewal thereof.
 1         SECTION 7. Standards and Specifications of Motorcycles-For-Hire.
 2   – The Department of Transportation (DOTr), upon the recommendation of the
 3   Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), Department of Environment and
 4   Natural Resources, LTO and Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory
 5   Board (LTFRB), shall prescribe in the implementing rules and regulations the
 6   standards and specifications of motorcycles that may be allowed to operate as
 7   motorcycles-for-hire, taking into consideration the safety of the passengers and
 8   riders and the protection of goods.
 9
10          No modifications shall be made on any motorcycles-for-hire, except for the
11   installation of the appropriate accessories, such as motorcycle bracket, top box,
12   luggage carrier, saddlebag, step board or foot rest, crash guards, speed limiter
13   or monitoring devices, in accordance with the standards approved by the DTI,
14   if applicable, and shall be used as reference by the LTO for registration
15   purposes. In case the DTI has no approved standards, the LTO, upon
16   consultation and agreement with all stakeholders, shall prescribe the same,
17   taking into consideration road worthiness and road safety, until such time the
18   DTI has issued appropriate standards.
19
20          Within five (5) years upon the enactment of this Act, the DTI shall ensure
21   the availability of standards of all products and parts covered by this Act and its
22   implementing rules and regulations, and its future amendments or revisions.
23          SECTION 8. Authority to Grant Franchise to Operate Motorcycles-
24   For-Hire. – The franchising or grant of authority to operate motorcycles-for-hire
25   shall be as follows:
26
27          a) Motorcycles-for-Hire Under MTPPs. – In areas with an existing and
28   operating MTPPs, the authority and regulation of the operation of motorcycles-
29   for-hire is vested in the LTFRB through a franchise or certificate of public
30   convenience (CPC). The LTFRB shall set forth in the franchise the terms and
31   conditions to be observed in the operation of motorcycles-for-hire. In
32   determining the number of franchises to be issued, the LTFRB shall take into
33   consideration the Local Public Transport Route Plan (LPTRP) or studies
34   approved by the DOTr and their impact on the other modes of public
35   transportation. The franchise fee shall be determined by the LTFRB after public
36   consultation and shall only be implemented upon the approval of the DOTr.
37
38         b) Motorcycles-for-Hire in Areas Without an Operating MTPP or OEPP.
39   – The authority and regulation of operation of motorcycles-for-hire in areas
40   without an operating MTPP or OEPP is vested in the LTFRB. The number of
41   routes or motorcycles-for-hire that may be allowed to operate shall be determined
42   in accordance with the LPTRP as recommended by the local government unit
43   (LGU) concerned. The LTFRB shall provide a uniform guidelines as to the
44   standards of operation of motorcycles-for-hire in consultation with the LGUs
45   and stakeholders.
46
47         c) Motorcycles-for-Hire Operating Under an OEPP. – Motorcycles-for-
48   Hire operating under an OEPP shall be regulated by the LTFRB in
49   accordance with its mandate to issue CPC, subject to compliance with the
50   requirements provided under the applicable provisions of this Act.
51
 1         SECTION 9. Requirements for the Grant of a Franchise or
 2   Certificate of Public Convenience. – A CPC shall only be issued to an
 3   operator upon submission or determination of the presence of the following
 4   requirements:
 5              a) Proof of Filipino citizenship;
 6
 7              b) Financial capacity the standard of which to be determined by the
 8              LTFRB;
 9
10              c) Certificate of Registration duly issued by the LTO;
11
12              d) Insurance coverage for the death of or injuries suffered by the
13              rider, passenger or any third party or damage to property by reason
14              or on occasion of its operation; and
15
16              e) Tax Identification Number or Certificate of Registration as
17              Common Carrier issued by the Bureau of Internal Revenue
18              (BIR), if applicable. No operator shall operate more than one (1)
19              franchise nor operate more than one (1) motorcycle-for-hire in a
20              franchise.
21
22          SECTION 10. Qualifications of Motorcycles-For-Hire Riders. – The
23   LTO shall promulgate the necessary guidelines setting forth the requirements
24   for the issuance of a professional driver’s license, including the content of or the
25   conduct of theoretical and practical examinations, appropriate for the operation
26   of a motorcycle-for-hire. Only riders possessing professional driver’s license
27   shall be allowed to operate motorcycles-for-hire. The MTPPs and OEPPs shall
28   provide and conduct a continuing safety training seminar for riders operating
29   under their respective platforms: Provided, That for motorcycles-for-hire riders
30   operating outside the digital platform providers, the conduct of continuing safety
31   training seminar shall be conducted by the LTO.
32
33         SECTION 11. Accreditation Requirements of MTPPs. – A MTPP or
34   OEPP must be duly registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission
35   (SEC). A MTPP or OEPP, as far as the operation of motorcycles-for-hire is
36   concerned, before being allowed to operate as such, must comply with the
37   accreditation requirement of the LTFRB. The DTI, LTFRB and Department of
38   Information and Communications Technology (DICT) shall jointly issue the
39   guidelines that shall govern the operations of MTPPs and OEPPs. MTPPs and
40   OEPPs must possess and maintain the following minimum requirements for its
41   accreditation:
42
43         a) Financial capacity the standard of which to be determined in the joint
44         guidelines;
45
46         b) SEC registration;
47
48         c) BIR Tax Identification Number;
49
50         d) An established Road Crash Quick Response System;
51
 1        e) Capability to evaluate and determine the competence of riders through
 2        an established training center, duly certified by the LTO and Technical
 3        Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA). The LTFRB
 4        may, as a requirement for renewal of accreditation, consider the previous
 5        safety record of the MTPP as certified by the LTO during the effectivity
 6        of its accreditation being applied for renewal.
 7
 8         SECTION 12. Operation of Motorcycles-For-Hire. – Motorcycles-for-
 9   hire may be allowed to operate using the digital platforms, or on designated
10   areas of operation. To maximize their operation, motorcycles-for-hire shall not be
11   bound by a single MTPP or OEPP but shall be allowed to adopt a multi-homing
12   approach within a particular area of operation under the given franchise,
13   license or accreditation: Provided, That multi-homing operation shall be limited
14   to two (2) digital platforms but in no case shall a motorcycle-for-hire be allowed
15   multi-homing operation alternately between two (2) MTPPs or two (2) OEPPs:
16   Provided, further, That a motorcycle-for-hire accredited under any MTPP or
17   OEPP platform may not be allowed to operate as motorcycle-for-hire without
18   using such platform.
19
20         Except for private use, any operation of motorcycle-for-hire in areas with
21   operating MTPP or OEPP outside the digital platform shall be considered
22   colorum and, therefore, illegal.
23
24          For purposes of multi-homing operation of motorcycles-for-hire, the
25   platform providers may not refuse an application for accreditation solely on the
26   ground that an operator or rider is accredited to another platform provider.
27
28         A motorcycle-for-hire may be allowed to carry, alternately, both passengers
29   or goods.
30
31         The maximum number of hours that a rider may operate a motorcycle-for-
32   hire shall be specified in the implementing rules and regulations of this Act.
33
34         Motorcycles-for-hire shall operate at speeds allowable under Republic Act
35   (RA) No. 4136, otherwise known as the “Land Transportation and Traffic
36   Code,” as amended, local ordinances and other applicable laws: Provided,
37   That in no case shall a motorcycle-for-hire operate beyond sixty kilometers per
38   hour (60kph).
39
40         SECTION 13.           Fare Setting.   –      The fares, surcharges and
41   other transportation fees that may be charged by operators and MTPPs under
42   which they operate for motorcycles-for-hire services shall be in accordance with
43   the fare structure and within the parameters determined, prescribed and issued
44   by the LTFRB, in consultation with the motorcycle-for-hire industry
45   stakeholders. The fare structure shall take into consideration supply and
46   demand components and shall allow dynamic pricing on account of market
47   variations based on location and time. The fare structure shall also include the
48   maximum percentage that can be charged by the MTPPs upon the operators or
49   riders for a particular transaction. The LTFRB shall periodically review the fare
50   structure and parameters of determining the applicable fares. The        booking
51   system of MTPPs shall feature a mechanism that enables clients to compare the
 1   transportation cost charged by each of the available digital platforms.
 2
 3         In the case of motorcycles-for-hire operating outside the digital platforms,
 4   the fare shall be determined by the LTFRB, after a public consultation with
 5   LGUs, the operators and other stakeholders.
 6
 7          SECTION 14. Processing of Personal Data. – Any processing,
 8   collection, use and/or storage of personal information and sensitive personal
 9   information involved in the registration/accreditation of motorcycles-for-hire,
10   digital bookings, and other requirements under this Act shall be made in
11   accordance with the provisions of RA 10173, otherwise known as the “Data Privacy
12   Act of 2012.”
13
14          SECTION 15. Liabilities for Death, Injuries or Damage to Property.
15   – The operator and/or the MTPP or OEPP providing the digital platform at the
16   time when the death, injury or damage to property occurred in the course of
17   operating a motorcycle-for-hire shall be jointly and solidarily liable, with rights
18   of subrogation against any party at fault: Provided, That the liability of the MTPP
19   or OEPP shall not exceed the amount of the insurance coverage of the operator.
20   MTPPs and OEPPs shall be responsible for the insurance coverage of all its
21   accredited riders. The MTPP or OEPP shall likewise be responsible for the
22   procurement of Personal Passenger Accident Insurance for the protection of
23   the passengers and/or goods, and, Third Party Liability insurance to cover
24   for damages caused to any third party in the course of operation of motorcycles-
25   for-hire.
26
27         SECTION 16. Road Crash Quick Response System. – Each MTPP or
28   OEPP shall establish a quick response team to provide immediate medical care
29   or bring any person injured by the operation of the motorcycle-for-hire booked
30   through its digital platform to the nearest hospital or medical facility. A function
31   that will allow the rider or passenger to alert the quick response team during
32   emergency or road crash shall be part of the booking app options of the digital
33   platforms. When necessary, the MTPP or OEPP shall advance any amount
34   necessary for the admission of such injured person or persons to the hospital or
35   medical facility up to the insured amount.
36
37          SECTION 17. Enforcement. – To ensure the safe and secure operation
38   of motorcycle-for-hire, the LTO shall deputize LGUs and other relevant
39   government agencies to assist in the enforcement of traffic rules and
40   regulations, and to monitor strict compliance with the provisions of this Act,
41   particularly, on the observance of carrying capacity and speed limits. The LTO
42   shall establish a centralized database to ensure the accurate recording of
43   violations and road crashes involving motorcycles-for-hire for the purpose of
44   applying the penalties as well as other sanctions under this Act. It shall publish
45   quarterly and annual reports of such data on its website which shall be accessible
46   to the public.
47
48         Consistent with its mandate under RA 7924, entitled “An Act Creating the
49   Metropolitan Manila Development Authority, Defining Its Powers And Function,
50   Providing Funds Therefor And Other Purposes,” the Metropolitan Manila
51   Development Authority (MMDA) shall continue to exercise its traffic
 1   enforcement functions within Metro Manila in furtherance of this Act.
 2
 3         All violations related to the terms and conditions of the franchise shall be
 4   endorsed by the LTO enforcement officers, deputized LGU enforcers and MMDA
 5   personnel to the LTFRB for appropriate action. The foregoing shall not preclude
 6   the MTPPs or OEPPs from exercising the power to impose sanctions such as
 7   suspension or blacklisting of their riders or operators on account of violation of
 8   internal rules made known to the latter, acts detrimental to safety and security
 9   and other reasonable or valid grounds.
10
11        SECTION 18. Penalties. –
12
13         a) Any rider who operates a motorcycle-for-hire in violation of traffic rules
14   and regulations, carrying capacity, and licensing requirement shall suffer the
15   maximum penalty imposed under the applicable law, ordinance, rule or
16   regulation for such violation. In addition, the LTO shall impose a penalty of
17   suspension or revocation of the driver’s license of an erring rider commensurate
18   to the gravity of the offense and frequency of commission. The table of penalties
19   shall be included in the implementing rules and regulations of this Act.
20
21         b) An operator who allows a rider who does not possess a valid
22   Professional Driver’s License to operate a motorcycle-for-hire shall, in addition
23   to penalties under existing laws and regulations, suffer a penalty of suspension
24   or revocation of franchise, after due notice and hearing.
25
26          c) The penal provisions of RA 7394, otherwise known as the “Consumer
27   Act of the Philippines,” RA 11967 or the “Internet Transactions Act of 2023,”
28   and RA 10173, when applicable, shall apply for acts or omissions committed
29   in the course of motorcycle-for-hire operation.
30
31          SECTION 19. Travel Demand and Operational Data. – To assist the
32   DOTr in transportation planning and management, the MTPPs and OEPPs
33   shall, not later than thirty (30) calendar days after the end of every year, submit
34   to the DOTr their travel demand and operational data.
35
36         SECTION 20. Transitory Provision. – In order to provide a
37   smooth transition in the full implementation of this Act, the authority and
38   accreditation granted to existing MTPPs, OEPPs and operators shall
39   continue to be effective until their expiration or six (6) months from the
40   promulgation of the implementing rules and regulations of this Act,
41   whichever comes later.
42
43          SECTION 21. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
44   days from the effectivity of this Act, the DOTr shall, in coordination with the
45   LTFRB, LTO, DTI, DICT, BIR, TESDA, Department of Health, Department
46   of the Interior and Local Government, MMDA, and Philippine National Police-
47   Highway      Patrol Group,      and in      consultation with     stakeholders,
48   promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to carry out the implementation
49   of this Act.
50
