                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to declare August 30 of every year as a special non-working
holiday in the City of Mandaue in the Province of Cebu in commemoration of its
Charter Anniversary.

      On June 21, 1969, Republic Act No. 5519 otherwise known as the “Charter
of the City of Mandaue”, was signed into law by President Ferdinand Emmanuel
Edralin Marcos, Sr. Presidential Proclamation No. 586 series of 1969, signed on
August 6, 1969, designated August 30, 1969 as the date of formal organization of
the government of the City of Mandaue. Since then, every year, the Mandauehanons
have celebrated the Mandaue City Charter Day on August 30.

       Mandaue City has a total land area of 34.87 square kilometers (13.46 sq mi)
and is composed of 27 barangays. According to the 2024 census, the city is home to
364, 482 Mandauehanons. As of 1991, Mandaue City was recognized as a Highly
Urbanized City (HUC) and is a thriving first income class city known as the
industrial heartland of Cebu. In 2019, Mandaue City separated from the sixth
legislative district of Cebu and since 2022, it is represented under its Lone District.

       Every year, upon the request of the City Government of Mandaue, the Office
of the President declares August 30 a special non-working day in the city to allow
its people to celebrate and participate in its annual celebrations. Fifty-Seven (57)
years after the enactment of Republic Act No. 5519, it is high time that Mandaue
City’s Charter Anniversary be declared a special non-working holiday through
legislation.

      In doing so, the people of Mandaue City will automatically be given an
opportunity to recognize the men and women who has contributed to the city’s
progress and development as well as encourage the full participation of
Mandauehanons in the annual cultural celebration while ruminating on its historic
founding in 1969.

      For the foregoing reasons, the passage of this proposed legislative measure is
earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. August 30 of every year is hereby declared a special non-
working holiday in the City of Mandaue, Province of Cebu, in commemoration of
its Charter Anniversary.

       SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
