                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Everyone will get old; it is inevitable. When we reach that age, we are hopeful
that we can spend it surrounded by our family. However, the sad reality is that this is
not an absolute fact that applies to every Filipino. There are heartbreaking stories of
elderly Filipinos abandoned by their children, left homeless on the streets, or
struggling to get by in charitable institutions due to the inability of their families to
provide the supportive care that they need.

      While our culture reveres the elderly, the State must step in when traditional
family support structures fail, break down, or simply lack the financial capacity to
provide adequate medical and daily care.

       This bill seeks to establish and maintain a Senior Citizens Nursing Home in
every city and municipality. This is crucial for elderly individuals who are abandoned,
neglected, homeless, or whose families cannot meet or provide for their special needs.
A key feature of these Senior Citizens Nursing Homes is the provision of continuous,
specialized care 24 hours a day, seven days a week. These facilities will include
Residential Care Services (RCS) that offer long-term or temporary multi-disciplinary
care. This ensures that Senior Citizens, those who require support with daily activities
due to specific health, medical, or cognitive needs, receive the specialized attention
they require when they have no surviving significant others to care for them.

       Our lolas and lolos have dedicated their youth, strength, and love to raising our
families and building our communities. It is now our collective duty as a nation to
give back to them. By providing a safe haven, proper medical attention, and a roof
over their heads, we can ensure that they receive the compassion, dignity, and care
that they truly deserve as they safely and comfortably enjoy the sunset of their lives.
In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF SENIOR CITIZENS
  NURSING HOMES IN ALL CITIES AND MUNICIPALITIES, AND PROVIDING
                        FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Senior Citizens Nursing
Homes Act.”

        Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It shall be declared policy of the State to protect
and promote the health and well-being of Filipino senior citizens, and ensure a decent and
dignified standard of living and quality of life by providing a comprehensive and holistic
healthcare and welfare system, with accessible programs and affordable services.

        Section 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all Filipino senior citizens who are in
need of and wish to avail of the programs and services of nursing homes, particularly residential
and housing, specialized medical interventions and support care, either because they have been
abandoned, neglected, homeless, or due to inability of their families to meet or provide for their
special needs.

       Priority shall be given to homeless senior citizens with no living relatives.

       Section 4. Definition of Terms. – The following terms used in this Act are defined as
follows:

       a) Senior Citizen refers to any resident citizen of the Philippines at least sixty (60)
          years old, as defined in Republic Act No. 9994;
       b) Dependent Senior Citizen refers to senior citizen who requires support and
          assistance with activities of daily living due to specific health, medical, cognitive
          or mobility needs;

       c) Senior Citizens Nursing Homes refer to group homes or facilities that provide
          skilled and specialized care for senior citizens, 24 hours a day, seven (7) days a
          week. This includes Residential Care Services (RCS) that provides long term or
          temporary multi-disciplinary care to senior citizens who are abandoned by their
          families or without surviving or living significant others or partners to provide the
          necessary supervision and/or supportive care.

        Section 5. Establishment of Senior Citizens Nursing Homes. – Each City and
Municipality is hereby mandated to establish and maintain a Senior Citizens Nursing Home
dedicated for their respective constituency. These nursing homes shall provide elderly care and
shall be jointly managed by local Social Welfare office and the local Office for Senior Citizen
Affairs.

       The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) and the National
Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC) shall provide technical assistance and supervision in
the implementation of this Act.

       Each nursing home shall provide the following:
       a. Facilities, rooms and spaces suitable for the elderly;
       b. Basic utilities, such as electricity and clean water;
       c. Sufficient qualified staff to render appropriate services; and
       d. Basic health and recreational facilities.

       Section 6. Tax Exemption – All Senior Citizens Nursing Homes established hereunder
are hereby declared exempt from all income and all other internal revenue taxes, tariff and
customs duties and all other kinds of taxes, fees, charges and assessments levied by the
Government and its political subdivisions, agencies and instrumentalities.

       Section 7. Utility Discount. – All Senior Citizens Nursing home shall be entitled to
discounts in electricity, water and telecommunications pursuant to Republic Act No. 9994.

        Section 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DSWD and the NCSC, in coordination with other concerned
agencies, shall have joint responsibility in the formulation and issuance of the rules and
regulation for the implementation of the provisions of this Act.

        Section 10. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this
Act is hereby authorized to be appropriated from the General Appropriations Act of the year
following its enactment, and every year thereafter.
       Section 11. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act be held invalid
or unconstitutional, the other parts or provisions that are not affected shall remain in full force
and effect.

       Section 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, or rules
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       Section 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in newspaper of general circulation.
