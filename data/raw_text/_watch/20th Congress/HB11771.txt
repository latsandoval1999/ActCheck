                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Libingan ng mga Bayani (LNMB) in Fort Bonifacio, Taguig City, serves as
the final resting place for the nation’s military personnel, veterans, national
heroes, and martyrs. It is a hallowed ground that symbolizes the Republic’s
eternal gratitude for the sacrifices made by these extraordinary men and
women in defense of freedom and democracy.

However, the geographical location of the LNMB in Metro Manila poses a
significant logistical and financial burden for the families of heroes and
veterans residing in the northern provinces of the country. Many descendants
and surviving spouses from Regions I, II, and the Cordillera Administrative
Region (CAR) are unable to pay their respects or visit their departed loved ones
due to the prohibitive cost and time required to travel to the capital.

To truly honor their memory, the State must ensure that the families of our
fallen heroes have accessible means to visit and commemorate them. This bill
proposes the establishment of the Libingan ng mga Bayani - Northern Luzon
Extension to be situated within a suitable military reservation in the northern
part of the country. This extension will serve as a regional pantheon for
deceased veterans, military personnel, and recognized heroes from Northern
Luzon, granting them the dignified burial they deserve closer to their ancestral
homes.

By bringing this sacred institution closer to the provinces, we not only alleviate
the burden on grieving families but also create a regional monument that will
inspire local communities and future generations with the values of patriotism
and selfless service.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

  AN ACT ESTABLISHING A LIBINGAN NG MGA BAYANI EXTENSION IN
     NORTHERN LUZON AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the "Libingan ng mga
Bayani - Northern Luzon Extension Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the declared policy of the State to honor
the memory of its national heroes, military personnel, and veterans who have
fought for the country's freedom, independence, and peace. Towards this end,
the State shall ensure that their final resting places are preserved, dignified,
and reasonably accessible to their surviving families and the public to foster
patriotism and national pride.

SEC. 3. Establishment of the Libingan ng mga Bayani - Northern Luzon
Extension. — There is hereby established a Libingan ng mga Bayani (LNMB)
Extension in Northern Luzon. The cemetery shall serve as the final resting
place for deceased military personnel, veterans, national artists, scientists,
and other recognized heroes who hail from or wish to be interred in Northern
Luzon.

SEC. 4. Site Selection and Location. — The Department of National Defense
(DND), in coordination with the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP), shall
determine a suitable and accessible site for the LNMB - Northern Luzon
Extension within an existing military reservation in Region I, Region II, or the
Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR). The chosen site must be expansive
enough to accommodate future interments, memorial markers, and
appropriate facilities for ceremonial and public activities.

SEC. 5. Administration and Maintenance. — The administration, maintenance,
and development of the LNMB - Northern Luzon Extension shall be jointly
managed by the Philippine Veterans Affairs Office (PVAO) and the Armed
Forces of the Philippines (AFP). They shall ensure that the grounds are
maintained with the same level of dignity, security, and honor as the primary
Libingan ng mga Bayani in Metro Manila.

SEC. 6. Qualifications for Interment. — The qualifications for interment in the
LNMB - Northern Luzon Extension shall be strictly identical to the existing
rules and regulations governing burials at the Libingan ng mga Bayani in Fort
Bonifacio, Metro Manila, as prescribed by the Armed Forces of the Philippines.

SEC. 7. Appropriations. — The Secretary of National Defense shall immediately
include in the Department’s programs the operationalization and maintenance
of the Libingan ng mga Bayani - Northern Luzon Extension, the funding of
which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DND, AFP, and PVAO shall promulgate the
necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

SEC. 9. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in full force and effect.

SEC.10. Repealing Clause. — All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
memoranda, rules, and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC.11. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved
