EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to declare Mt. Minandar, located in Barangay Kusiong in the Municipality of Datu Odin
Sinsuat, as an eco-tourism site.

Rising about 450 meters above sea level, the summit overlooks the shores of illana Bay, offers
breathtaking views of Cotabato City from afar, and overlooks nearby Timako Hill, attracting hikers for
its beautiful coastal panoramas.

A short trek from the nearby village as a starting point, the sunrise and sunset views at Mt. Minandar
make it all worth the day trip for local hikers and nature lovers.

Because of its potential to attract hiking enthusiasts and beginner mountaineers, Mt. Minandar can be
a better alternative for landscape and nature photographers looking for new and exciting areas to
discover.

Declaring Mt. Minandar as an ecotourism site will result in its development and improvement as a
tourist destination, and more importantly, contribute to the overall economic progress of the local

community and Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

AN ACT

SINSUAT, PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO DEL NORTE, AN ECOTOURISM SITE

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
Assembled:

SECTION 1. Mt. Minandar located in Barangay Kusiong, Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat, Province of
Maguindanao del Norte, is hereby declared an ecotourism site. Pursuant thereto, its development shall
be prioritized and supported by the Department of Tourism (DOT), subject to the rules and regulations
governing the development of ecotourism sites.

SEC. 2. Within one (1) year after the approval of this Act, the local government of the Municipality of
Datu Odin Sinsuat shail, in coordination with the DOT, Department of Environment and Natural
Resources, and concerned agencies of the government, prepare a tourism development plan that shall
include the construction, installation, and maintenance of such appropriate infrastructure and facilities
to enhance tourism in the area: Provided, That said development plan shall ensure the preservation
and conservation of the natural beauty and resources of the area: Provided further, That the
implementation of the tourism development plan shall be included in the budget of the local
government of the Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat.

SEC. 3. The DOT shall assist the local government of the Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat in the
implementation of the tourism development plan by providing technical assistance in tourism capacity
building. it shall also include Mt. Minandar in its national, and regional promotion programs.

SEC. 4. The funding for the implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual budget of the
local government of the Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat. The DOT may augment such allocation with
internally-generated funds.

SEC. 5. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the local government of the Municipality
of Datu Odin Sinsuat shall, in consultation with the DOT and concerned agencies of the government,
promulgate rules and regulations to implement this Act.

SEC. 6. All laws, rules, regulations, executive orders, proclamations, presidential decrees and other
issuances inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed repealed or amended
accordingly.

SEC. 7. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or ina
newspaper of general circulation.
