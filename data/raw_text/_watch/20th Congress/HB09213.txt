                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        According to estimates of the World Health Organization (WHO), at least 2.2
billion people worldwide experience near or distance vision impairment. Despite
significant advances in digital technology, a substantial portion of web-based and
audiovisual content remains inaccessible to individuals who are blind, have low vision,
color blindness, or other visual impairments.

       In view of this, there is a compelling need to adopt measures that ensure digital
and audiovisual content is accessible to persons with visual disabilities. This objective
is consistent with the principles embodied in the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
and the international standards established under the Web Content Accessibility
Guidelines (WCAG), which promote equal access to information and communication
technologies.

        Audio description is a recognized accessibility feature designed to narrate key
visual elements of media content. It enables persons who are blind or visually impaired
to fully understand and enjoy audiovisual materials on an equal footing with sighted
audiences.

       Beyond its benefits for individuals with visual impairments, audio description
also serves as an effective support for persons with cognitive disabilities who may
experience difficulty interpreting visual cues or who prefer auditory learning
modalities. It further provides practical benefits to users who engage with content in
eyes-free environments, as well as to individuals learning a foreign language, for
whom audio description can enhance comprehension and contextual understanding.

       Moreover, audio description contributes positively to language development,
particularly among children and individuals with autism spectrum disorder. By explicitly
describing actions, emotions, and facial expressions, audio description aids in the
development of vocabulary, language skills, and the comprehension of social and
emotional cues that may otherwise be challenging to interpret.

        Finally, audio description offers flexibility in content consumption by allowing
users to engage with audiovisual materials without continuous visual attention. This
has led to emerging usage patterns wherein audio-described media is consumed
similarly to audiobooks, enabling individuals to listen while performing other activities
such as driving, cooking, or household tasks. As listening plays a critical role in
language acquisition and cognitive development, audio description may also serve as
a valuable pedagogical tool that reinforces learning, introduces new vocabulary, and
promotes active engagement among learners.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
     MANDATING THE PROVISION OF AUDIO DESCRIPTION IN AUDIOVISUAL
                     MEDIA AND PUBLIC CONTENTS

           Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
     in Congress assembled:

 1           SECTION 1. Short Title: - This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Audio
 2   Description Act.”
 3
 4          Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 5   promote and protect the rights of persons with disabilities and other sectors with
 6   sensory, cognitive, or communication barriers by ensuring their full, effective, and
 7   equal access to information, education, culture, and entertainment.
 8
 9           In line with the Constitution, the United Nations Convention on the Rights of
10   Persons with Disabilities (UNCRPD), and existing accessibility laws, the State shall
11   institutionalize audio description as a reasonable accommodation and accessibility
12   feature in audiovisual and public content to enable meaningful participation, inclusion,
13   and enjoyment by all members of society.
14
15           Sec 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following terms shall
16   mean:
17
18           a)     Audio Description (AD) refers to an accessibility service that provides
19           narrated descriptions of key visual elements in media and live settings,
20           including actions, facial expressions, settings, graphics, and on-screen text,
21           inserted during natural pauses in dialogue or delivered through assistive
22           devices.
23
24           b)    Franchise Holder or Operator refers to any individual, corporation,
25           partnership, or entity granted authority to produce, distribute, broadcast,
26           stream, or publicly exhibit audiovisual content.

                                                                                            1
 1         c)     Audiovisual media refers to any content combining sound and visual
 2         elements, including but not limited to television programs, films, online videos,
 3         learning materials, and advertising.
 4
 5         d)    Live Events refers to performances or activities presented in real time to
 6         an audience, including theatrical productions, sports events, cultural
 7         performances, and State-related events.
 8
 9          Sec. 4. Mandatory Provision of Audio Description. – All establishments and
10   platforms are required to provide audio description services in every cinema during
11   the Metro Manila Film Festival (MMFF) in selected malls in every region.
12
13          Participating cinemas shall ensure that at least one (1) scheduled screening
14   time slot for every MMFF entry is made available with audio description features
15   accessible to viewers with visual impairments and other beneficiaries under this Act.
16
17          In addition thereto, cinemas offering special promotional screening days for
18   senior citizens, including Mondays or similar designated schedules, shall ensure that
19   at least one (1) audio-described screening is made available during such periods,
20   subject to technical feasibility and scheduling considerations.
21
22          Such screenings shall be offered in selected cinemas and time slots, subject to
23   guidelines and minimum accessibility standards to be determined under the
24   Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).
25
26          The extent and phasing of compliance shall be determined in the IRR, taking
27   into account technical feasibility, program type, and public interest.
28
29          Sec. 5. Purpose of Audio Description. – Audio description is intended to provide
30   persons who are blind, have low vision, are deafblind, or have other sensory or
31   cognitive impairments with access to essential visual information necessary to
32   understand, appreciate, and fully enjoy audiovisual content.
33
34          Sec. 6. Beneficiaries. – The following individuals shall be considered
35   beneficiaries of this Act:

41         c)     Senior Citizens
42
43         d)     Persons with Autism Spectrum Disorder
44
45         e)     Persons with Learning Difficulties or cognitive impairments
46
