 11210, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Republic Act No. 11210 or the 105-day Expanded Maternity Leave Law is a vital
piece of legislation that ensures that women are provided with a mechanism allowing
them ample time to recover their health and overall well-being as well as to assume
maternal roles before resuming paid work. The effects of this law clearly strengthened
the rights of women to health and decent work. However, women workers in informal
economy, who are non-members of the Social Security System (SSS), are unable to
fully enjoy the benefits of the Expanded Maternity Leave Law.

       A study conducted by the Philippine Institute for Development Studies shows
that women contribute 76% of unpaid work compared to just 24% by men. The study
further explains that if this unpaid labor were compensated, it would amount to trillions
of pesos—equivalent to 20% of the country’s GDP. 1 Despite these substantial
contributions, women continue to face significant challenges in accessing social
protection and overcoming economic constraints.

       In this regard, such gaps in social protection such as the benefits received by
women workers in the informal economy who are non-members of the SSS should be
addressed. The proposed measure seeks to fill this gap by mandating the Department
of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) to administer a one-time direct maternity
cash benefit for each delivery. The benefit shall be equivalent to the prevailing daily

14        Section 11. MATERNITY BENEFITS FOR FEMALE WORKERS WHO ARE

16   VOLUNTARY NOR REGULAR MEMBERS OF THE SSS SHALL RECEIVE,

19   EQUIVALENT TO THE PREVAILING DAILY MINIMUM WAGE RATE IN HER
20   REGION OF RESIDENCE MULTIPLIED BY FORTY-FOUR (44) DAYS.
