                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to exempt persons with disabilities (PWDs) and solo parents from
government fees and charges for the issuance of basic documentary requirements for
employment. It aims to eliminate financial barriers that may prevent qualified jobseekers
from securing gainful work.

         The Philippine Statistics Authority’s 2025 Labor Force Survey1 recorded more than
fifty-two (52) million individuals in the labor force, with more than two (2) million
unemployed. When applying for jobs, applicants are often required to secure clearances and
certificates, like barangay or police clearances, medical certificates, and academic and other
government-issued records. Although individually modest, the hefty accumulative cost of
these pre-employment requirements can burden PWDs and solo parents who may find
themselves having to prioritize expenses related to the everyday sustenance of their
families.

       The bill therefore waives government fees and charges for essential pre-employment
documents issued by national government agencies and local government units (LGU).
Qualified beneficiaries may avail themselves of the exemption upon presentation of the
appropriate identification issued or recognized by the concerned LGU.

       Employment should be determined through a person’s competence, determination,
and potential, not merely by their ability to pay for documentary requirements before
getting their first paycheck. This measure promotes equal opportunities and strengthens the
economic participation of PWDs and solo parents without lowering employment standards.

        In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                         AN ACT
EXEMPTING PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES AND SOLO PARENTS FROM
GOVERNMENT FEES AND CHARGES IN THE ISSUANCE OF BASIC
DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS FOR EMPLOYMENT

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section. 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Employment
Opportunity Support Act for Persons with Disabilities and Solo Parents”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
uphold the rights and welfare of persons with disabilities and solo parents, ensure
their full integration into society, and promote equal opportunities for participation.
It also recognizes the financial constraints faced by the marginalized sectors in
obtaining necessary documents for employment, it is imperative to waive
government fees to facilitate their access to essential services and privileges.

       SEC. 3. Definitions. – For the purposes of this Act:

           a. Basic Employment Documents refer to documents usually requested
       by private or public employers as a pre-requisite for employment which
       include but not limited to, police clearance, clearance from the National
       Bureau of Investigation (NBI), and barangay clearance;

           b. Government Fees and Charges include any fees, charges, or expenses
       imposed by government agencies in the issuance of basic documents required
       for the employment of persons with disabilities and solo parent.

           c. Person with Disability (PWD) refers to any individual who has long
       term physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory impairments which in
       interaction with various barriers may hinder their full and effective
       participation in society on equal basis with others.
           d. Solo Parent pertains to any individual who falls in categories stated in
       Sec. 4 of Republic Act 8972 as amended.


                                           1
       SEC. 4. Waiver of Fees and Charges. – All government fees and charges related
to the issuance of basic documents required for the employment of PWDs and solo
parents shall be waived. These documents include, but are not limited to:

          a.​ Police clearance certificate;

          b.​ National Bureau of Investigation clearance;

          c.​ Barangay clearance;

         d.​ Medical certificate from a public hospital, including laboratory tests
      and other medical procedures;

          e.​ Birth Certificate;

          f.​ Marriage Certificate;

          g.​ Transcript of academic records issued by state college and universities;

          h.​ Tax Identification Number (TIN);

          i.​ Unified Multi-Purpose ID (UMID) card;

          j.​ Philippine Identification card; and

          k.​ Other documentary requirements issued by the government that may
      be required by employers from job applicants.

       SEC. 5. Qualifications. – PWDs and solo parents shall present their respective
identification cards obtained from the Local Government Unit (LGU) to avail of the
exemption provided herein. These identification cards serve as crucial
documentation to verify eligibility for the program, ensuring that benefits are
accessed by qualified individuals recognized at the local level.

        SEC. 6. Implementation. – The Department of Social Welfare and Development
(DSWD), Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), and other
concerned agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

       SEC. 7. Public Awareness Campaign. – The DSWD, in partnership with relevant
stakeholders, shall conduct a nationwide information and education campaign to
raise awareness about the provisions of this Act and the procedures for availing of
the waiver of fees and charges.

        SEC. 8. Monitoring and Evaluation. – The DSWD shall establish a monitoring
and evaluation system to ensure the proper implementation of this Act and the
efficient delivery of services to PWDs and solo parents.



                                              2
       SEC. 9. Funding. – The necessary funds for the implementation of this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. Government agencies
tasked with the issuance of the aforementioned documents shall include budgetary
provisions for the waived fees and charges in their respective budgets.

       SEC. 10. Penal Provisions. – Any government official or personnel found
guilty of imposing fees and charges contrary to the provisions of this Act shall be
subjected to administrative sanctions and shall be held liable under existing laws.

       SEC. 11. Periodic Review. - The DSWD, in consultation with relevant
stakeholders, shall conduct a periodic review of this Act and may recommend
revisions or amendments as necessary to enhance its effectiveness in achieving its
objectives.

       SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations or
parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

       Approved.
