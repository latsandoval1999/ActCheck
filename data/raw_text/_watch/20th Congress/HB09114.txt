                       AN ACT
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE CREATION OF ECONOMIC AND

UNITS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF
1991

                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Local government units (LGUs) play a critical role in promoting economic
development, attracting investments, and creating employment opportunities
within their respective jurisdictions. With increasing competition among regions
and localities for both domestic and foreign investments, local governments must
develop the institutional capacity to promote their economic potential effectively
and facilitate business development.

       Under Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government
Code of 1991, as amended, however, the position of an Economic and Investment
Promotion Officer is not formally institutionalized within the structure of local
government units. In practice, the functions related to economic and investment
promotion are often assigned on a temporary or designated basis to existing
personnel who have other assignments, resulting in inconsistent implementation
of local investment promotion programs across LGUs. Currently, only a limited
number of local government units, including Quezon City, Puerto Princesa,
Legazpi City, Albay, Negros Occidental, and San Fernando, Bukidnon, have
  established dedicated economic and investment promotion offices to actively
  attract investments and stimulate local economic activity.

         Institutionalizing the position of an Economic and Investment Promotion
  Officer will strengthen the capacity of LGUs to formulate and implement local
  investment promotion strategies, develop competitive investment environments,
  and coordinate effectively with national government agencies and the private
  sector.

         This measure therefore seeks to amend the Local Government Code of
  1991 to formally establish the position of Economic and Investment Promotion
  Officer in provincial and city governments and in first- and second-class
  municipalities, while allowing third to sixth class municipalities the option to
  create the position depending on their resources and development priorities.

         By strengthening local economic governance, this bill aims to promote
  inclusive economic growth, enhance local competitiveness, and encourage
  sustainable investments in communities across the country.

        This measure was initially filed during the 18th Congress

        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Section 4. A new section to be denominated as Section 490-A under
the new Article Twenty-One of Book Ill, Title Five, of the same Act is
hereby inserted to read as follows:


      "ARTICLE TWENTY-ONE.               - THE ECONOMIC AND
INVESTMENT PROMOTION OFFICER


      SEC. 490-A. QUALIFICATIONS, POWERS, AND DUTIES. -
(A) NO PERSON SHALL BE APPOINTED ECONOMIC AND
INVESTMENT PROMOTION OFFICER UNLESS THE PERSON
IS A CITIZEN OF THE PHILIPPINES, A RESIDENT OF THE
PROVINCE, CITY, OR MUNICIPALITY WHERE SUCH PERSON
WILL BE APPOINTED, OF GOOD MORAL CHARACTER, A
HOLDER       OF    A    COLLEGE        DEGREE       PREFERABLY          IN

                                                                          3
ECONOMICS, BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION, OR BUSINESS
MANAGEMENT, OR ANY RELATED                COURSE FROM A
RECOGNIZED COLLEGE OR UNIVERSITY, AND A FIRST
GRADE CIVIL SERVICE ELIGIBLE OR ITS EQUIVALENT. THE
ECONOMIC AND INVESTMENT PROMOTION OFFICER MUST
HAVE     ACQUIRED    EXPERIENCE      IN    ECONOMIC   OR
INVESTMENT POLICY MAKING OR MANAGEMENT OR
RELATED FIELD FOR AT LEAST FIVE (5) YEARS IN THE CASE
OF    THE   PROVINCIAL     AND   CITY     ECONOMIC    AND
INVESTMENT OFFICER, AND THREE (3) YEARS IN THE CASE
OF    THE   MUNICIPAL     ECONOMIC      AND    INVESTMENT
OFFICER.
      FOR THE PROVINCIAL, CITY, AND FIRST AND SECOND
CLASS MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENTS, THE APPOINTMENT OF
AN ECONOMIC AND INVESTMENT PROMOTION OFFICER IS
MANDATORY, WHILE OPTIONAL FOR THIRD TO SIXTH
CLASS MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENTS.


      (B) THE ECONOMIC AND INVESTMENT PROMOTION
OFFICER SHALL TAKE CHARGE OF THE ECONOMIC AND
INVESTMENT PROMOTION OFFICE AND SHALL:
1. FORMULATE        AND    IMPLEMENT          THE   LOCAL
     INVESTMENT AND INCENTIVES CODE;
2. FACILITATE THE PREPARATION, COORDINATION, AND
     EXECUTION OF LOCAL ECONOMIC AND INVESTMENT
     PROMOTION POLICIES, PROJECTS, AND ACTIVITIES OF
     THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT;
3. FACILITATE THE ESTABLISHMENT OF SYSTEMATIC,

         MARKETING OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT AS AN

         POTENTIALS OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT TO
         PROSPECTIVE         INVESTORS         TO     ATTRACT      MORE

     7. COORDINATE         WITH     THE     LOCAL      AND    NATIONAL
