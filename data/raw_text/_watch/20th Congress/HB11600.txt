                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Human Settlements Adjudication Commission (HSAC) plays a
vital role in protecting the rights and interests of homebuyers, homeowners,
beneficiaries, and other stakeholders in the housing and real estate sector. As
the principal adjudicatory body for disputes involving subdivision and
condominium developments, homeowners associations, and related housing
matters, the effective and timely exercise of its jurisdiction is essential to
ensuring accessible and meaningful remedies for the public.

      Republic Act No. 11201, otherwise known as the "Department of
Human Settlements and Urban Development Act," established the HSAC and
defined its powers, jurisdiction, and organizational structure. However, the
increasing complexity and volume of housing and real estate disputes
necessitate further strengthening of the Commission's institutional and
adjudicatory framework.

       This measure seeks to enhance the HSAC's organizational structure,
personnel complement, jurisdiction, adjudicatory mechanisms, and
enforcement powers to enable it to discharge its mandate more efficiently and
effectively. It likewise strengthens the Commission's legal, administrative,
technical, and operational support through additional personnel and
appropriate organizational units.

and Regional Adjudicators by requiring relevant experience in areas within
