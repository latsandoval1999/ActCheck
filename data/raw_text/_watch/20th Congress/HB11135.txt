                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The City of Iriga traces its roots to the Spanish colonial period, when it was
established as a pueblo and later developed into a municipality that became a center
of commerce, culture, education, and governance in the Rinconada District of
Camarines Sur.

       With the enactment of Republic Act No. 5261 on June 15, 1968, the Municipality
of Iriga was converted into a component city, granting it greater autonomy and
strengthening its capacity for local governance and development. On September 3,
1968, the formal inauguration of the City of Iriga was held, marking a defining
milestone in the history and identity of the Irigueño people.

       Since then, September 3 has been observed as an important occasion to
commemorate Iriga's cityhood, celebrate its heritage, and recognize its continuing
progress. The annual Charter Day provides an opportunity to honor the contributions
of past and present generations while fostering civic pride, community unity, cultural
appreciation, tourism, and economic development.

      Several cities across the country have similarly been granted special non-
working holidays through national legislation to commemorate their charter
anniversaries and cityhood. Consistent with this practice, declaring September 3 as a
Special Non-Working Holiday in the City of Iriga would allow its residents to
meaningfully participate in civic, historical, cultural, educational, religious, and
community activities celebrating its cityhood and development.

      Recognizing Iriga's cityhood likewise affirms its distinct identity and
acknowledges the significant contributions of the Irigueño people to the social, cultural,
and economic life of Camarines Sur and the Bicol Region.

       In view of the foregoing, it is fitting and proper to declare September 3 of every
year as a Special Non-Working Holiday in the City of Iriga. The measure will provide
the people of Iriga with an opportunity to celebrate their shared history and heritage,
strengthen community solidarity, and honor the city's continuing growth and
development.

       The immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
