21                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
22
23   A bamboo pole sold cheaply at the farm gate can become furniture, a construction
24   material, a household product, or an export commodity.
25
26   The Department of Science and Technology estimates that the Philippines has
27   approximately 39,000 to 53,000 hectares of bamboo stands. Yet these resources
28   remain largely scattered across backyards, forests, and even private properties, with
29   relatively few established as commercial plantations. At the same time, DOST-
30   PCAARRD has recognized that existing bamboo resources have struggled to meet the
31   industry's demand for quality and quantity of raw materials.
32
33   This is a missed economic opportunity.
34
35   In 2023, Philippine bamboo production was reported at approximately 1.8 million poles,
36   while recorded exports stood at around 2,000 kilograms. These figures might be
37   commendable but somehow, it reveals a striking gap between the country's natural
38   resource base and its ability to convert bamboo into a globally competitive industry.
39
40   The problem, therefore, is not the absence of bamboo’s economic potential. Despite
41   the country's substantial bamboo resources, farmers and entrepreneurs continue to
42   face barriers in plantation development, access to technology and financing,
43   processing, and market access.
44
45   Without addressing these gaps, much of the economic value of bamboo will remain
46   unrealized.
 1   Hence, this proposed measure seeks to transform bamboo from an underutilized
 2   natural resource into a sustainable engine of rural livelihood, domestic manufacturing,
 3   employment, and economic growth.
 4
 5   The proposed measure seeks to establish a national framework for the development of
 6   the Philippine bamboo industry. It strengthens the Philippine Bamboo Industry
 7   Development Council and creates a Bamboo Industry Research and Development
 8   Center to coordinate programs on plantation development, research, technology,
 9   training, processing, and market access. It also provides incentives to encourage
10   bamboo plantations and bamboo based enterprises.
11
12   By developing the bamboo value chain from production to processing and
13   commercialization, the measure aims to create rural jobs and livelihood, support Filipino
14   businesses, strengthen local manufacturing, and expand Philippine bamboo products
15   in domestic and international markets. It seeks to turn bamboo into a sustainable
16   source of income and economic growth for Filipinos.
17
18   The Philippines already has the bamboo. What we need now is the system to make it
19   work for the Filipino people.
20
21   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

21                              AN ACT
22        PROMOTING THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE BAMBOO     INDUSTRY BY
23       STRENGTHENING THE PHILIPPINE BAMBOO INDUSTRY DEVELOPMENT
24          COUNCIL, CREATING THE BAMBOO INDUSTRY RESEARCH AND
25          DEVELOPMENT CENTER, PROVIDING INCENTIVES FOR BAMBOO
26       PLANTATION DEVELOPMENT, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
27
28   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
29   Congress assembled:
30
31          SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Bamboo
32   Industry Development Act.”
33
34         SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to promote
35    and support industries that generate employment especially in the rural areas,
36    reduce poverty, foster a sustainable environment, and mitigate the impacts of
37    climate change.
38
39           It shall also be the policy of the State to foster a self-reliant and independent
40   economy that supports indigenous, scientific and technological capabilities, and skills.
41   To this end, the State shall recognize the strategic importance of the bamboo as a
42   sturdy, versatile, and replenishable material that can be a sustainable source of
43   livelihood and catalyst for agricultural productivity.
44
45          The State shall likewise ensure the integrated and continuous development
46   of the Philippine bamboo industry through policies and programs that encourage the
47   planting of bamboo, accelerate the development of bamboo-based products and
48   designs, and promote Philippine bamboo products in international markets. It shall
49   establish a program for a sustainable utilization, propagation and promotion of bamboo
50   as furniture, food, construction and design material, and other uses.
51
52          SEC. 3. The Philippine Bamboo Industry Development Program. The
53   Philippine Bamboo Industry Development Council, herein after referred to as the
     1   Council, shall formulate the Philippine Bamboo Industry Development Program, herein
     2   after referred to as the Program which shall include plans of actions and projects for
     3   the scientific propagation, development and management, processing, utilization,
     4   business development, and commercialization of Philippine bamboo and bamboo
     5   products, through the cooperation of all stakeholders of the industry that include
     6   producers of raw materials, processors, marketing and promotion service providers,
     7   and concerned national agencies and local government units (LGUs).

4    8           SEC. 4. Objectives of the Philippine Bamboo Industry Development
     9   Program. The Program shall aim to make the Philippine bamboo industry competitive
    10   in the local and global markets and provide opportunities for local employment and the
    11   establishment of bamboo-based community enterprises. The Program shall:
    12
    13         (a) Ensure that the bamboo industry has sufficient quality raw materials
    14         through the establishment and management of bamboo nurseries and
    15         plantations;
    16         (b) Ensure that the bamboo industry continuously receives from the
    17         research and development sector information about relevant technologies and
    18         new products;
    19         (c) Ensure that the industry receives pertinent and accurate data from the
    20         PBIDC upon which the industry can make business decisions;
    21         (d) Promote investment in the bamboo industry by providing substantial
    22         incentives to investors and encouraging the establishment of bamboo
    23         plantations and bamboo processing operations;
    24         (e) Provide skilled labor and manpower through continuous relevant capacity
    25         building;
    26         (f) Provide continuous training and capacity building in bamboo plantation
    27         operations, bamboo processing, and product development and design;
    28         (g) Ensure provision of locally manufactured machines of improved capacity and
    29         quality at competitive costs;
    30         (h) Ensure markets of bamboo products locally and globally; and
    31         (i) Ensure sustainable resources for implementing the bamboo industry
    32         development program.
    33
    34          SEC. 5. Role of the Philippine Bamboo Industry Development Council. The
    35   Philippine Bamboo Industry Development Council, created under Executive Order No.
    36   879, series of 2010, hereinafter referred to as the Council, shall provide the overall
    37   policy and program directions of various agencies and instrumentalities to ensure the
    38   implementation of the Program.
    39          The Council shall be administratively attached to the Department of Trade and
    40   Industry (DTI) and shall be composed of the following:
    41
    42      a) Secretary of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) as Chairperson;
    43      b) Secretary of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR);
 1      c) Secretary of the Department of Agriculture (DA);
 2      d) Secretary of the Department of Education (DepEd)
 3      e) Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST);
 4      f) Secretary of the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE);
 5      g) Executive Director of the Bamboo Industry Research and Development Center;
 6      h) Chairperson of the National Commission of Indigenous Peoples;
 7      i) National President of the League of Municipalities of the Philippines;
 8      j) Executive Director of the Union of Local Authorities of the Philippines;
 9      k) Two (2) Representatives from Non-Government Organizations;
10      l) One Representative from a reputable private industry association; and
11      m) One Representative each from the Bamboo Farmers,                    the    Bamboo
12         Manufacturers, and the Bamboo Processors’ Associations.
13
14         The Council shall promulgate such rules and regulations as may be
15   necessary for the attainment of the objectives of this Act.
16
17          SEC. 6. Bamboo Industry Research and Development Center. The
18   Bamboo Industry Research and Development Center, hereinafter referred to as the
19   Center, is hereby created to serve as the secretariat of the Council. It shall be headed
20   by an Executive Director and shall consist of divisions as may be necessary to ensure
21   the effective implementation of the goals and objectives of the Center, including the
22   following functions: research and development; trade promotion and education;
23   networking and linkages; internal administration and finance; and such other divisions
24   as may be necessary.
25          The organizational structure, staffing pattern and compensation schedule of
26   the Center shall be prepared in accordance with existing laws, rules and regulations.
27   Other member agencies of the Council shall provide additional administrative and
28   technical staff support to the Center upon the determination by the Council of the
29   necessity for such augmentation.
30         The Center shall perform the following powers and functions:
31         a) Promote and encourage the establishment and management of bamboo
32            nurseries, plantations and processing facilities that would accelerate the
33            production and commercialization of bamboo and various bamboo products;
34         b) Promote the commercialization and market access of appropriate,
35            innovative and viable bamboo industry development technologies and
36            products;
37         c) Monitor and evaluate the performance of bamboo industry development
38            programs and projects;
39         d) Coordinate and partner with other government agencies in the
40            implementation of bamboo industry development programs and projects;
41         e) Coordinate and partner with the private sector, people's organizations
42            and non-governmental organizations, and the academe in providing
43            assistance on matters pertaining to bamboo industry development;
44         f) Accept grants, donations and contributions from local and international
 1           donors and such similar sources of funds for the implementation of the
 2           provisions of this Act, subject to the usual budget, accounting and auditing
 3           rules and regulations;
 4        g) Conduct capability-building initiatives for farmers, processors, designers, and
 5           other stakeholders in the bamboo industry;
 6        h) Conduct research and development in partnership with appropriate
 7           government agencies to advance sustainable bamboo industry development
 8           practices;
 9        i) Supervise the implementation of the plans and programs of the Council;
10        j) Manage and administer the projects identified by the Council;
11        k) Conduct, in cooperation or partnership with appropriate government
12           agencies, a periodic review of the Philippine Bamboo Industry Development
13           Roadmap; and
14        l)     Submit periodic reports to the Council              on     the   progress   and
15               accomplishment of programs and projects.
16
17         SEC. 7. The Executive Director of the Center. The Executive Director shall be
18   appointed by the Chairperson of the Center with the approval of the Council. The
19   Executive Director shall be a citizen and resident of the Philippines and must
20   possess executive and management experience of at least three (3) years, and
21   considerable knowledge about the bamboo industry.
22
23         In addition, the Executive Director shall have the following powers and
24         functions:
25             a) Oversee the day-to-day operations of the Center;
26             b) Provide technical and administrative support to the Council;
27             c) Assist the Council in the performance of its tasks; and
28             d) Perform such other functions, duties and responsibilities as may be
29                necessary to implement this Act.
30
31         SEC. 8. Support to the Program by Local Government Units. Local
32   government units (LGUs) shall participate in the implementation of the Program.
33   All provinces, cities, and municipalities shall establish a Bamboo Industry
34   Development Council and shall endeavor to capacitate agricultural workers and
35   encourage entrepreneurs in harvesting and post-harvesting operations.
36
37          Provincial governments shall formulate and implement their respective bamboo
38   industry development programs within two (2) years from the enactment of this law.
39
40        SEC. 9. Incentives for bamboo in plantation development. Investors in
41   bamboo plantation development shall enjoy the following incentives:
42             a) Exemption from the payment of rent for the use of government lands for
43                commercial bamboo plantation for the first five (5) years of operation
44                reckoned from the date of the first harvest of the plantation;
 1   b) In the case of private plantations, exemption from the requirement to secure
 2      a cutting permit for the harvesting of bamboo and a transport permit for as
 3      long as the plantation is registered with the Community Environment and
 4      Natural Resources Office (CENRO) of the DENR: Provided, that the
 5      plantation owner shall inform the CENRO about the date of harvest, and the
 6      estimated volume of the harvest. Inventory of the plantation prior to harvest
 7      shall not be required. All CENROs shall approve and enter into a registry, the
 8      names of private bamboo plantation investors;
 9   c) Exemption from the payment of forest charges and other fees for taxes
10      that local government units may impose;
11   d) Access to development loans from public financial institutions for
12      plantation developers;
13   e) Insurance coverage of plantations and plantation development equipment
14      by the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation;
15   f) Tax free importation of plantation development equipment, including tree
16      nursery facilities, pursuant to Republic Act No. 10863, otherwise known
17      as the Customs Modernization and Tariff Act (CMTA);
18   g) To claim expenses incurred for plantation operations as deductible
19      expenses for income tax purposes, subject to the provisions of the National
20      Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended: Provided, that the deduction
21      shall only apply to the taxable period when the expenses were incurred;
22   h) To claim as ordinary and necessary expenses or as capital expenditure
23      expenses incurred in the development and operation of a bamboo plantation
24      prior to the initial commercial harvest;
25   i) Prioritized access for plantation developers to credit assistance from
26      government- owned, controlled or supported financial institutions;
27   j) Acceptance of plantation as collateral for loans in government-owned-or-
28      controlled banks;
29   k) In the case of a tenured developer, entitlement to fair compensation upon the
30      premature termination of the agreement at no fault of the holder: Provided,
31      That all depreciable permanent and semi-permanent improvements such as
32      roads, buildings, and nurseries including the planted and standing trees and
33      other forest crops introduced and retained in the area, shall be properly
34      evaluated: Provided further, That the amount of compensation shall be
35      mutually agreed upon by both the Department and the tenure-holder:
36      Provided finally, That in case of disagreement between them, the matter
37      shall be submitted to arbitration by a mutually acceptable and
38      impartial third party adjudicator;
39   l) No volume restriction in the exportation of bamboo culms from plantation;
40   m) Tax free importation of processing equipment by processors engaged
41      in bamboo industry, pursuant to the Customs Modernization and Tariff Act
42      (CMTA); and
43   n) Inclusion in the annual investment program of the National Economic
44      Development Authority of industrial bamboo plantations in forest lands and
45      priority access to funds from external and internal sources.
 1          SEC. 10. Appropriations. The funds necessary to carry out the initial
 2   implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s budget of the
 3   DTI. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation of
 4   this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
 5
 6          SEC. 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days
 7   after the effectivity of this Act, the DTI and the Council shall, in coordination with the
 8   DENR, and DOST promulgate the rules and regulations to implement this Act.
 9
10           SEC. 12. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act or part hereof is
11   declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such declaration
12   shall remain in full force and effect.
13
14          SEC. 13. Repealing Clause. All laws, executive and administrative orders,
15   rules and regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or amended
16   accordingly.
17
18          SEC. 14. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
