                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act No. 9136, or the Electric Power Industry Reform Act of
2001 (EPIRA), restructured the power sector into a competitive but regulated
industry and established the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) to ensure
consumer protection and fair market operations. While the ERC performs a
critical balancing role in rate-setting and regulation, there remains a clear gap
in the representation of end-users in its proceedings.

       This bill seeks to address this gap by creating an Energy Advocate
Office (EAO), led by an Energy Advocate, to serve as the independent
representative of consumers in all rate-setting, rule-making, and other energy-
related proceedings before government, judicial, and quasi-judicial bodies.

      By institutionalizing consumer representation, the measure aims to
strengthen regulatory oversight, promote fairness in decision-making, and
help ensure more reasonable electricity rates.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.

 3            SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
 4      State to recognize the right of energy end-users to reasonable rates, adequate and

 6      Towards this end, there is a need for institutionalized and independent end-user
 7      representation in rate-setting, rule-making, and other energy related cases and
 8      proceedings before government agencies including judicial and quasi-judicial

 3         SEC. 4. Powers and Functions. - The EAO shall be the representative of
 4   all end-users in energy sector matters affecting public interest before any
 5   department, commission, agency, or court. It shall independently discharge its

13                 Electrification Administration (NEA), Philippine Competition
14                 Commission (PCC), and other judicial and quasi-judicial bodies; and
15              b. Actions before any trial or appellate court, including the Supreme
16                 Court, which involve the validity of any decision, memoranda, rule,
17                 regulation, circular, or order of the DOE, ERC, NEA, PCC, and
18                 other departments, commissions, and agencies insofar as these
19                 decisions and policy issuances affect energy rates and services;
20      (2) Monitor all issuances of the DOE, ERC, NEA, PCC, and other departments,
21         commissions, and agencies insofar as these affect energy rates and services;

23         all end-users on laws, rules, regulations, decisions, circulars, and orders that
