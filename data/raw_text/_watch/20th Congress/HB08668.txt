                               EXPLANATORY NOTE


       For far too long, the people of the Zamboanga Peninsula have endured a journey
that is not merely long, but burdensome, unpredictable, and at times unsafe. Travel
between Dipolog City and Zamboanga City currently spans approximately 333
kilometers and takes up to seven (7) hours or more, due to narrow, winding, and
hazard-prone roads. This daily burden costs families time, raises transport expenses,
delays the movement of goods, and constrains access to essential services. In a region
rich in potential yet challenged by connectivity, mobility remains a barrier to the
opportunity to rise above poverty and eventual progress.

      This measure seeks to change that reality through the establishment of the
Zamboanga Peninsula Expressway with a Parallel Railway System, an integrated
transport corridor that will shorten the route to approximately 242 kilometers and
reduce travel time to as fast as 2 hours and 41 minutes. By combining a high-speed
expressway with a parallel railway within a shared right-of-way, the project introduces
a modern, efficient, and resilient multi-modal system—improving mobility for
passengers while enabling cost-effective transport of goods across the region.

       More than infrastructure, this is an investment in people and progress. The
project will strengthen trade, unlock tourism, support agriculture, and generate jobs,
while positioning the Zamboanga Peninsula as a vital gateway in Mindanao. Above all,
it will bring communities closer—making opportunities more accessible and
development more inclusive.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
   MANDATING THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE ZAMBOANGA PENINSULA
       EXPRESSWAY WITH A PARALLEL RAILWAY SYSTEM AND
               APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga Peninsula
Expressway and Railway Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote
balanced regional development, improve interconnectivity, and ensure efficient, safe,
and sustainable transport systems. Toward this end, the State shall develop integrated,
multi-modal infrastructure that enhances mobility, strengthens economic linkages, and
improves the quality of life of communities.

SEC. 3. Establishment of the Zamboanga Peninsula Expressway with Parallel
Railway System. — There is hereby established a Zamboanga Peninsula Expressway
with a Parallel Railway System, an integrated transport corridor that shall connect
Dipolog City and Zamboanga City, traversing key municipalities across the Zamboanga
Peninsula.

The project shall consist of:

   a) A limited-access, four-lane expressway designed for high-speed vehicular travel;
      and
   b) A parallel railway system constructed within or adjacent to the same right-of-
      way, intended for passenger and freight transport.

This integrated corridor shall serve as the primary north–south backbone of the
Zamboanga Peninsula transport network, significantly reducing travel time and
improving logistics efficiency.

SEC. 4. Route and Alignment. — The expressway and railway system shall generally
follow a direct inland alignment between Dipolog City and Zamboanga City, covering
approximately 242 kilometers, as identified in the concept plan, with strategic
interchanges in major growth centers including but not limited to:
   (a) Dapitan City;
   (b) Dipolog City;
   (c) Municipality of Manukan;
   (d) Municipality of Sindangan;
   (e) Municipality of Salug;
   (f) Municipality of Liloy;
   (g) Baliguian–Siocon–Sirawai–Sibuco (3SB) corridor; and
   (h) Zamboanga City.

The final alignment shall be determined based on detailed engineering, feasibility,
environmental, and hazard assessments.

SEC. 5. Implementing Agencies. — The Department of Public Works and Highways
(DPWH) shall serve as the lead agency for the planning, construction, and maintenance
of the expressway component. Meanwhile, the Department of Transportation (DOTr), in
coordination with the Philippine National Railways (PNR) or its successor agency, shall
be responsible for the development, operation, and regulation of the railway system.

The DPWH and DOTr shall ensure integrated planning and development of the corridor
to maximize land use efficiency and reduce costs.

SEC. 6. Project Development and Financing. — The project may be implemented
through: a) Public-Private Partnership (PPP) schemes under Republic Act (R.A.) No.
6957, as amended by R.A. No. 7718; b) national government appropriations; and c)
financing from multilateral and bilateral development partners. The integration of road
and rail within a single corridor shall be prioritized to optimize right-of-way acquisition
and long-term operational efficiency.

SEC. 7. Feasibility Study and Detailed Engineering Design. — Within one hundred
eighty (180) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DPWH, in coordination with DOTr
and other relevant agencies, shall conduct:
   (a) Comprehensive feasibility studies;
   (b) Detailed engineering design;
   (c) Environmental Impact Assessment; and
   (d) Disaster risk and climate resilience studies. The design shall incorporate
       measures addressing landslides, flooding, seismic risks, and coastal hazards
       identified in the concept study.

SEC. 8. Right-of-Way Acquisition. — The government shall ensure timely acquisition
of the necessary right-of-way in accordance with existing laws, prioritizing fair
compensation, stakeholder consultation, and minimal displacement. The co-location of
the railway within the expressway corridor shall be adopted to reduce land acquisition
requirements.

SEC. 9. Integration with Existing Transport Systems. — The expressway and
railway system shall be integrated with national and local road networks, seaports and
airports, and logistics hubs and economic zones to enhance intermodal connectivity
and regional economic development.

SEC. 10. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act, including feasibility studies and preparatory activities, shall be included in the
General Appropriations Act (GAA). Thereafter, funding requirements shall be
programmed accordingly.
SEC. 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DPWH and DOTr, in consultation with relevant stakeholders,
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations


SEC. 12. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.

SEC. 13. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 14. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
