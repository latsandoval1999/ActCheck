                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act No. 11697, otherwise known as the Electric Vehicle Industry
Development Act, was enacted to promote the development and adoption of electric
vehicles (EVs) in the Philippines. Since its passage, the global shift towards electric
mobility has accelerated as countries worldwide strengthened policies and incentives
to reduce carbon emissions, improve air quality, and lessen dependence on fossil fuels.

       The Philippines continues to face rising fuel costs, heavy dependence on
imported oil, and increasing transportation-related pollution. These challenges
highlight the importance of expanding and extending the incentives and support
mechanisms for electric vehicles and charging infrastructure in the country.

       This bill seeks to amend Republic Act No. 11697 by increasing the required EV
share in government and private fleets, expanding EV parking and charging
infrastructure, and extending fiscal and non-fiscal incentives for EVs and charging
stations until 2035. The measure also enhances discounts on vehicle registration and
user charges for battery electric and hybrid electric vehicles to encourage wider
adoption.

       Strengthening the country’s EV policy will help reduce fuel dependency, lower
greenhouse gas emissions, improve public health through cleaner air, attract
investments in green industries, and create new economic opportunities and jobs for
Filipinos. Moreover, it will support the government’s commitment to sustainable
transportation and long-term energy security.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is respectfully sought.

                          AN ACT
 AMENDING R.A.11697, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ACT PROVIDING
  FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE ELECTRIC VEHICLE INDUSTRY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
Congress Assembled:

Section 1. Section 16 of Republic Act No. 11697 or the Electric Vehicle
Industry Development Act shall be amended to read as follows:

Section 16. Mandatory EV Share in Corporate and Government Entities. -
The following entities shall ensure that at least five percent (5%) TEN PERCENT
(10%) of their fleet, whether owned or leased, shall be EVs within the time frame
indicated in the CREVI:


     (a) Industrial and commercial companies such as cargo logistics companies,
         food delivery companies, tour agencies, hotels, power utilities, and water
         utilities;
     (b) Public transport operators, including minibuses, buses, jeepneys, vans,
         tricycles, taxis, and transport network vehicle services; and
     (c) LGUs, NGAs, and GOCCs; Provided, That the electrification of government
         fleets shall be considered as a government energy efficiency project under
         Republic Act No. 11285, otherwise known as the "Energy Efficiency and
         Conservation Act".

Section 2. Section 17 of Republic Act No. 11697 or the Electric Vehicle
Industry Development Act shall be amended to read as follows:

Section 17. Dedicated Parking Slots for Electric Vehicles in Private and Public
Buildings and Establishments. - Private and public buildings and establishments
constructed after the effectivity of this Act and pursuant to the National Building Code
of the Philippines shall designate dedicated parking slots for the exclusive use of EVs
to include light electric vehicles; Provided, That the number of dedicated parking slots
shall be proportional to the total number of parking slots within the building or
establishment as provided in the CREVI; Provided, further, That if there are twenty
(20) TEN (10) or more parking slots, there should be at least five percent (5%) TEN
(10%) dedicated parking slots for EVs of the total number of parking slots within the
building or establishment FIT WITH DEDICATED CHARGER; Provided, finally,
That existing private and public buildings and establishments shall comply with the
foregoing requirements within the time frame indicated in the CREVI.

No permit shall be issued by the LGU for the construction or renovation of a building
or establishment unless this section has been complied with.

Section 3. Section 19 of Republic Act No. 11697 or the Electric Vehicle
Industry Development Act shall be amended to read as follows:

Section 19. Construction or Installation of Charging Stations in Gasoline Stations. -
Select Gasoline stations identified in the CREVI shall designate dedicated spaces for
the installation of commercial use charging stations for the general public. The CREVI
shall determine when AND HOW MANY dedicated spaces in gasoline stations shall
be installed with a commercial use charging station either by the owner of the gasoline
station or a charging station service provider; Provided, That the owner of the gasoline
station shall have the first priority to install, operate, or maintain a commercial use
charging station in its premises. A gasoline station, if required under the CREVI to
have a designated space for c-charging station, shall not be issued a construction,
operation, or compliance permit by the DOE unless the owner shows that there is
ample space for the construction or installation of charging stations within its
premises.

Section 4. Section 24 of Republic Act No. 11697 or the Electric Vehicle
Industry Development Act shall be amended to read as follows:

Section 24. Fiscal Incentives. -

       (b) Importation

       The importation of completely built units of charging stations shall be exempt
       from the payment of duties for eight (8) years from the effectivity of this Act
       UNTIL 2035.

       (c) Utilization

       A thirty percent (30%) FORTY PERCENT (40%) discount for BEVs and
       fifteen percent (15%) TWENTY-FIVE PERCENT (25%) discount for HEVs
       from the payment of the motor vehicle user's charge imposed by the LTO under
       Republic Act No. 8794, otherwise known as "Motor Vehicle User's Charge Act",
       as well as vehicle registration and inspection fees shall be available for eight (8)
       years from the effectivity of this Act UNTIL 2035.
