       AN ACT REMOVING THE SYSTEMS LOSS CHARGE AND ITS
CORRESPONDING VALUE-ADDED TAX FROM THE ELECTRICITY BILLS OF
CONSUMERS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTIONS 40, 43(f), AND 60 OF
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9136, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "ELECTRIC POWER
INDUSTRY REFORM ACT OF 2001", SECTION 10 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7832,
  AS AMENDED, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "ANTI-ELECTRICITY AND
 ELECTRIC TRANSMISSION LINES/MATERIALS PILFERAGE ACT OF 1994",
 AND SECTION 108 OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997,
              AS AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Systems Loss and VAT
Relief Act of 2026."

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect electricity
consumers from unreasonable and inequitable charges, and to promote transparency,
accountability, and efficiency in the electric power industry. Towards this end, the State shall
ensure that the cost of system losses arising from technical deficiencies, pilferage, and other
non-technical causes is borne by the distribution utility or electric cooperative primarily
responsible therefor, and is not passed on to consumers as a separate, itemized, and separately-
taxed charge, without unduly impairing the financial viability of distribution utilities and
electric cooperatives or diminishing legitimate government revenue collection.
