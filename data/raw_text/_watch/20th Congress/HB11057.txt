                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE
       Access to quality medical education is vital to strengthening the country's healthcare
system and improving the delivery of health services, particularly in underserved and
geographically isolated communities. However, aspiring medical students from the Province
of Davao Oriental are compelled to pursue medical education outside the province due to the
absence of a medical school, resulting in additional financial and logistical burdens that limit
educational opportunities for many deserving students.

       The establishment of a College of Medicine in the Davao Oriental State University
(DOrSU) will address this gap by providing accessible and affordable medical education while
strengthening the University's role in instruction, research, extension, and community
engagement. As the first public medical school in the province, it will also help expand higher
education opportunities in the field of medicine within Davao Oriental.

       The proposed Davao Oriental State University - College of Medicine is envisioned to
become a premier institution for medical education, fostering academic excellence, research,
innovation, and community engagement while contributing to the continued advancement of
healthcare and national development.

        In view of the foregoing, immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
