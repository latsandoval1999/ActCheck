20                                                EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   After graduating from senior high school under the Technical, Vocational and Livelihood
23   (TVL) track, a young Filipino applies for a job as an automotive technician. He has
24   learned the fundamentals in school and has a senior high school diploma in hand.
25   During the application, however, he is asked for a National Certificate and specific
26   competencies required by the employer. He realizes that although he completed the
27   TVL track, he does not have all the certifications and skills that the industry expects.
28
29   This situation reflects a gap that the TVL curriculum must address. For many students,
30   choosing the technical and vocational education track is a deliberate choice to gain
31   practical skills that can help them find work to support themselves and their families
32   after graduation. They place their hopes in the skills they learn in school, trusting that
33   these skills will open the door to a decent livelihood. Their education should therefore
34   be closely connected to the realities of the workplace, giving them recognized
35   certifications that employers value and significantly consider in employing them.
36
37   This proposed measure seeks to make sure that TVL graduates leave school with skills
38   and National Certificates that can help them find work. They should not have to discover
39   after graduation that the skills they learned are not enough for the job they want.
40
41   By bringing schools and industries closer together, and by specifically addressing what
42   employers want, the measure seeks to give TVL graduates a fair chance to be hired,
43   earn decent wages, and build a better life for themselves and their families.
44
45   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

21                                 AN ACT
22          STRENGTHENING THE TECHNICAL, VOCATIONAL AND LIVELIHOOD
23         CURRICULUM FOR THE SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING
24                             FUNDS THEREFOR
25
26   Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
27   Congress assembled:
28
29            Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “TVL Curriculum Act.”
30
31           SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State shall protect and promote the right of
32   all citizens to quality education at all levels and take appropriate steps to make such
33   education accessible to all. The right to education shall be promoted with the end goal
34   of producing graduates who are prepared to become civically active and economically
35   productive members of society.

36         The State shall also ensure that education aligns with the goals of national
37   development and industrialization. To this end, the State shall ensure access to
38   affordable and competitive training of skilled graduates who are able to meet the
39   requirements of various industries, and find decent employment without need of a
40   college degree.

41          SEC. 3. Objective. — Graduates of the Technical, Vocational and Livelihood
42   (TVL) Curriculum shall be given senior high school diplomas and pertinent national
43   certificates for acquiring the competencies and skills necessary for employment, and
44   the various industries shall have confidence in securing their employment and providing
45   equitable compensation.
 1        SEC. 4. TVL Council. - The TVL Council, herein referred to as the “Council”, is
 2   hereby created which shall be attached to the Department of Education (DepEd), and
 3   composed of seven (7) members, namely: (1) Secretary of the DepEd, as Chairperson;
 4   (2) Director-General of the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority
 5   (TESDA), as Vice-Chairperson; (3) Secretary of the Department of Labor and
 6   Employment (DOLE); (4) Secretary of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI); (5)
 7   Secretary of the Department of Information and Communications Technology; (6)
 8   President of the Philippine Chamber of Commerce and Industry (PCCI); and (7)
 9   President of the Employers Confederation of the Philippines (ECOP).

10        Members of the Council shall serve without compensation but shall be entitled to
11   reimbursement for expenses related to their participation in the official activities of the
12   Council, in accordance with pertinent laws, rules and regulations.

13        Members may designate a representative who shall be of rank no lower than that
14   of an Undersecretary.

15       The Council shall regularly meet at least once every quarter of the academic year.
16   The Chairperson or a majority of the Council's members may call a special meeting
17   whenever necessary.

18        A meeting may be conducted virtually only in circumstances when meeting in
19   person is impossible or impractical and when all the Council members are able to
20   communicate adequately during the meeting.

21         SEC. 5. Duties and Functions of the Council. — The Council shall have the
22   following duties and functions:

23         (a) Periodically consults with various industries to ensure that the programs,
24   curriculum, courses, and other activities of the TVL Curriculum respond to their skills
25   and human resource requirements to strengthen the eligibility and employability of the
26   graduates;

27          (b) Ensures that the examinations or assessments for National Certification or
28   Certificate of Competency for TESDA shall be included in the curriculum;

29      (c) Guarantees that the TVL Curriculum shall be in accordance with the qualification
30   levels in terms of the three (3) Philippine Qualifications Framework domains:
31   knowledge, skills, and values; application; and degree of independence;

32        (d) Encourages various industries to employ TVL graduates by raising awareness
33   of their employment eligibility through information campaign and different programs
34   such as job fairs;

35      (e) Calls upon the local government units (LGUs), their respective local school
36   boards and other agencies, especially where the schools with TVL curriculum are
37   located, for assistance, coordination and collaboration in the alignment, synchronicity,
 1   and complementarity of plans, resources and efforts of the schools and the pertinent
 2   LGUs; and

 3      (f) Undertakes other duties and functions that may be necessary or incidental to the
 4   effective implementation of this Act.

 5       SEC. 6. Roles of Government Agencies. — To achieve the objectives of this Act, the
 6   following government agencies shall carry out their respective roles:

 7       (a) DepEd — The DepEd shall formulate the academic curriculum to ensure that
 8   learners under the TVL Curriculum possess the knowledge and competencies required
 9   to function effectively in the workplace and to be eligible for higher education in the
10   event that graduates choose the pathway. The DepEd shall appoint and designate the
11   public school teachers who shall implement the academic curriculum and the qualified
12   instructors who shall train the learners in accordance with the curriculum set by TESDA.

13      (b) TESDA — The TESDA shall formulate and implement the TVL curriculum for
14   equipping the learners with the necessary skills and abilities to obtain the National
15   Certifications or Certificates of Competency. It shall provide the qualifications of
16   instructors for the effective implementation of this Act.

17      (c) DOLE — The DOLE shall, in consultation with the DepEd and TESDA, formulate
18   guidelines for Work Immersion of the learners to protect their rights during the
19   immersion and as workers upon their employment after graduation. The DOLE shall
20   apprise the Council of the skills needed by various industries to prevent mismatch of
21   jobs and skills.

22       (d) DTl —The DTI shall advocate for the employment of TVL graduates to different
23   industries by linking them to schools with TVL curriculum for work immersion and
24   eventual job posts.

25      (e) Other government agencies are hereby authorized to loan or transfer, upon the
26   request of the DepEd or TESDA, such apparatus, supplies, and other properties as may
27   be needed by the schools with TVL curriculum, and to detail employees for duty therein,
28   when in the judgment of the head of the bureau or office, they can be spared without
29   serious detriment to the public service. Employees so detailed shall perform such duties
30   as required under such detail, and the time employed shall be counted as part of their
31   regular official hours, all laws, rules and regulations limiting the detail of government
32   employees notwithstanding.

33      SEC. 7. Partnerships with the Private Sector. — The PCCIl and ECOP shall notify
34   the Council of the skills and expertise needed by their members. They shall further
35   encourage their respective members to participate in the Work Immersion as Partner
36   Institutions who are able and willing to lend expertise and resources to the learners.

37     SEC. 8. Tax Exemption on Donations. — All grants, bequest, endowments,
38   donations and contributions made to the Council and used actually, directly and
 1   exclusively for the effective implementation of this Act, shall be exempt from donor’s tax
 2   and the same shall be considered as allowable deductions from gross income in the
 3   computation of the income tax of the donor in accordance with the provisions of the
 4   National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended.

 5      The payment of national taxes, including customs duties, of the Council shall be
 6   charged against the Tax Expenditure Fund provided for in the annual General
 7   Appropriations Act.

 8      SEC. 9. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act
 9   shall be included in the DepEd budget in the annual General Appropriations Act.

10      SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
11   approval of this Act, the Secretary of the DepEd shall, in consultation with the Director
12   General of the TESDA, members of the Council, and other stakeholders, formulate the
13   rules and regulations implementing the provisions of this Act.

14      SEC. 11. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is held invalid or
15   unconstitutional, the other provisions not so declared shall remain in force and effect.

16      SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders and rules and regulations
17   contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
18   amended accordingly.

19      SEC. 13. Effectivity. — Notwithstanding the non-issuance of the IRR, this Act shall
20   take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper
21   of general circulation.
