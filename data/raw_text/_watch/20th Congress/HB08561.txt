                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The State is mandated to protect and advance the right of the people to a balanced and
healthful ecology in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature. Currently, this mandate is
under severe pressure from the country's mounting plastic crisis. The Philippines generates an
estimated 2.7 million tons of plastic waste annually. But a staggering 91% or 2.46 million remains
uncollected or improperly managed. In urban centers like Metro Manila, the consumption of
single-use plastics has reached critical levels with over 164 million plastic bags discarded daily.
This high rate of disposal has led the Philippines to rank as the third largest contributor to global
ocean plastic pollution, with plastic materials now comprising 36% of all marine debris. Beyond
the environmental degradation, the socio-economic cost is profound - poor waste handling drains
approximately P400 billion from the national economy every year.

        To address these systemic challenges, this Act introduces a strategic excise tax designed to
discourage the use of harmful primary and secondary packaging, such as "ice," "labo," or "sando"
bags. International precedents support this fiscal approach; for instance, Ireland achieved a 90%
reduction in plastic bag usage through similar taxation, proving that market-based interventions
can effectively shift consumer behavior toward sustainable alternatives. By amending the National
Internal Revenue Code of 1997 (NIRC) to include Section 150-C, this measure imposes an excise
tax of Seventy Pesos (P70.00) per kilogram on single-use plastic bags removed from production
or customs custody after effectivity hereof. To ensure the tax remains a potent deterrent against
inflation and changing market conditions, the rate shall automatically increase by 4% annually
beginning January 1, 2027.

        The implementation of this tax serves a dual purpose: environmental protection and
resource mobilization. Under the amended Section 288 of the NIRC, all incremental revenues
generated from this tax are strictly earmarked for the Department of Environment and Natural
Resources (DENR). These funds are specifically allocated to support the implementation of
Republic Act No. 9003, as amended by RA 11898 (Extended Producer Responsibility Act), at the
municipal level. By channeling these funds into waste management infrastructure, recycling
programs, and EPR enforcement, this Act empowers local governments, promotes a circular
economy, ensuring that the cost of environmental restoration is shared by those who introduce
plastic waste into the ecosystem while securing a cleaner future for all Filipinos.

  AN ACT IMPOSING EXCISE TAX ON PLASTIC BAGS, AMENDING FOR THE
    PURPOSE SECTION 288 AND ADDING A NEW SECTION 150-C IN THE
      NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Single Use Plastic (SUP)
Taxation Act.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared as the policy of the State to protect
and advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with the rhythm
and harmony of nature through the proper exercise of its power of taxation.

       SEC. 3. A new section designated as Section 150-C of the National Internal Revenue
Code of 1997, as amended, is hereby added to read as follows:

       “Sec. 150. Non-Essential Goods. - xxx

       SEC. 150-C. SINGLE USE PLASTIC BAGS. –

     (A) RATE AND BASE OF TAX – THERE SHALL BE LEVIED, ASSESSED, AND
COLLECTED AN EXCISE TAX IN THE AMOUNT OF SEVENTY PESOS (PHP 70.00)
FOR EVERY KILOGRAM OF SINGLE-USE PLASTIC BAGS REMOVED FROM THE
PLACE OF PRODUCTION OR RELEASED FROM THE CUSTODY OF THE BUREAU
OF CUSTOMS (BOC).

      THE RATE OF TAX IMPOSED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION SHALL BE
INCREASED BY FOUR PERCENT (4%) EVERY YEAR, EFFECTIVE ON JANUARY 1,
2027, THROUGH REVENUE REGULATIONS ISSUED BY THE SECRETARY OF
FINANCE.

     “SINGLE-USE PLASTIC BAGS” SHALL REFER TO SECONDARY LEVEL
PLASTICS MADE OF SYNTHETIC OR SEMISYNTHETIC ORGANIC POLYMER

SUCH AS “ICE”, “LABO”, OR “SANDO” BAGS, WITH OR WITHOUT HANDLE,
USED AS PACKAGING FOR GOODS OR PRODUCTS.

     (B) FILING OF RETURN AND PAYMENT OF EXCISE TAX ON PLASTIC
BAGS. – THE PROVISIONS OF SECTIONS 130 AND 131 OF THE NATIONAL
INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED, ON THE FILING OF RETURN
AND PAYMENT OF EXCISE TAX SHALL APPLY.

     (C) NON-PAYMENT OF EXCISE TAX – NON-PAYMENT OF THE EXCISE TAX
IMPOSED UNDER THIS ACT SHALL BE SUBJECT TO THE PERTINENT
PROVISIONS UNDER TITLES VIII AND X OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL
REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED, REGARDING COLLECTION OF UNPAID
TAXES AND IMPOSITION OF CORRESPONDING FINES, SURCHARGES,
PENALTIES, AND IMPRISONMENT OR CLOSURE OF BUSINESS.”

        SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Finance shall, upon the recommendation of the
Commissioners of the Bureau of Internal Revenue and the Bureau of Customs and, in
consultation with the Department of Environment and Natural Resources, issue and promulgate
rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.
