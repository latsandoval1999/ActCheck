                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The State recognizes that efficient urban road networks are essential
to sustaining economic activity, facilitating mobility, and supporting critical
infrastructure within city centers. Roads that connect commercial districts
to port facilities are particularly vital, as they enable the seamless movement
of goods and services that drive local and regional economies.

       The Tibanga Road segment, connecting Pala-o Junction to South Wharf
Junction and extending to the North Wharf in Iligan City, serves as a key
access route linking the city’s urban core to its port area. Traversing a dense
commercial zone and terminating at major wharf facilities, the road supports
daily traffic flow, logistics operations, and port-related activities.

      A feasibility study has already been conducted, affirming its viability
and strategic importance within the city’s transport and logistics network.
The road has likewise been validated for conversion into a national road.
However, it remains under local jurisdiction, limiting the capacity for
sustained maintenance and integration into the national road system.

      Converting this road into a national road will enable the Department
of Public Works and Highways to assume responsibility for its improvement
and upkeep. This will ensure that the road meets national standards and
continues to support the efficient movement of goods, services, and people,
particularly in relation to port operations.
     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                              AN ACT
      CONVERTING TIBANGA ROAD, FROM PALA-O JUNCTION TO
    SOUTH WHARF JUNCTION AND EXTENDING TO NORTH WHARF
           IN ILIGAN CITY, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND
                APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Tibanga
National Road Conversion Act.”

      SECTION 2. Conversion. — Tibanga Road, from Pala-o Junction to
South Wharf Junction and extending to North Wharf in Iligan City, is hereby
converted into a national road under the supervision and administration of
the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

      SECTION 3. Appropriations. — The Secretary of Public Works and
Highways shall include in the Department’s program the improvement,
repair, and maintenance of the said road, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 4. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
