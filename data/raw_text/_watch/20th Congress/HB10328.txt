                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to exempt the System Loss Charge billed by distribution
utilities and electric cooperatives to residential end-user consumers from the
coverage of the Value-Added Tax (VAT). It aims to reduce electricity costs and
provide financial relief to Filipino households.

       Electricity is an essential service that powers homes, supports livelihoods, and
drives economic activity. For many families, every peso spent on electricity is a peso
taken away from other basic necessities like food, education, and healthcare.
However, Filipino consumers continue to pay among the highest electricity rates in
Southeast Asia1. Apart from the cost of electricity consumed, consumers also
shoulder the System Loss Charge which represents electricity losses incurred during
transmission and distribution. This charge is also subjected to the twelve percent
(12%) VAT which further increases the financial burden on consumers.

       Removing the VAT on the Systems Loss Charge provides meaningful relief to
residential consumers and helps make electricity more affordable. It likewife affirms
the State’s commitment to protect consumer welfare and ensure access to reliable
and reasonably priced electricity.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
EXEMPTING THE SYSTEM LOSS CHARGE BILLED TO RESIDENTIAL
END-USER CONSUMERS BY DISTRIBUTION UTILITIES OR ELECTRIC
COOPERATIVES FROM THE COVERAGE OF THE VALUE-ADDED TAX,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 109 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8424,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF
1997”, AS AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Section 109 of Republic Act No. 8424, otherwise known as the
National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended, is hereby further amended to
read as follows:

            “SEC. 109. Exempt Transactions. – The following shall be exempt
      from the value-added tax:

                “(a) x x x;

                “x x x;

               “(EE)  SYSTEM   LOSS    CHARGE   BILLED   BY
             DISTRIBUTION     UTILITIES    OR      ELECTRIC
             COOPERATIVES    TO     RESIDENTIAL   END-USER
             CONSUMERS.

               “FOR PURPOSES OF THIS PARAGRAPH, “SYSTEM
             LOSS CHARGE” REFERS TO THE COMPONENT OF THE
             ELECTRIC BILL, AS APPROVED BY THE ENERGY
             REGULATORY COMMISSION (ERC), THAT COVERS THE
             COST   OF   ELECTRICITY  LOST   DURING   THE
             TRANSMISSION AND DISTRIBUTION OF POWER,
             WHICH DISTRIBUTION UTILITIES OR ELECTRIC
             COOPERATIVES MAY RECOVER FROM CONSUMERS

                                         1
              UNDER RAPUBLIC ACT NO. 9136, OTHERWISE KNOWN
              AS THE “ELECTRIC POWER INDUSTRY REFORM ACT OF
              2001”, AS AMENDED.”

​      SEC. 2. ERC Oversight. – The Energy Regulatory Commission shall ensure that
the VAT exemption provided under this Act is properly reflected in the monthly
billing statements of covered consumers and shall monitor the compliance of
distribution utilities and electric cooperatives therewith.

​       SEC. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance, in consultation with the Bureau of
Internal Revenue, the Department of Energy, and the Energy Regulatory
Commission, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for its effective
implementation.

        SEC. 4. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
