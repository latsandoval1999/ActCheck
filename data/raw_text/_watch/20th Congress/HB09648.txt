                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Filipino professionals play a vital role in nation-building by providing essential services
that contribute to the growth, safety, welfare, and development of communities. The
Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), through its mandate to regulate and
supervise the practice of professions in the country, ensures that Filipino professionals
remain competent, qualified, and globally competitive.

However, many professionals and aspiring examinees in provincial areas continue to
face difficulties in accessing PRC services due to the limited number of service centers
and the considerable distance they must travel to process applications, secure
certifications, renew licenses, and avail themselves of other regulatory services. These
challenges result in additional transportation costs, lost work opportunities, and delays
in transactions.

The growing number of graduates and licensed professionals further underscores the
need to expand the accessibility of PRC services across the country. Establishing an
additional PRC Service Center will help decongest existing offices, improve efficiency
in the processing of transactions, and bring government services closer to the people,
particularly those residing in underserved and geographically distant areas.

Therefore, this bill seeks to establish a Professional Regulation Commission (PRC)
Service Center in the Municipality of Irosin, Province of Sorsogon, in order to provide
more accessible, efficient, and responsive services to professionals, board
examinees, and the general public within the province and nearby localities.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A PROFESSIONAL REGULATION COMMISSION (PRC)
    SERVICE CENTER IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF IROSIN, PROVINCE OF
         SORSOGON, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:


SEC. 1. There shall be established a Professional Regulation Commission (PRC)
Service Center in the Municipality of Irosin, in the Province of Sorsogon, which shall
be under the supervision of the PRC.
SEC. 2. The PRC Service Center shall provide services such as the processing of
applications for licensure examinations, initial registration, renewal, and duplication of
Professional Identification Cards, and issuance of certifications of board rating and
passing.
SEC. 3. The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act shall be included in
the annual General Appropriations Act.
SEC. 4. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Chairperson of the
PRC shall issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation
of this Act.
SEC. 5. All laws, executive orders, decrees, instructions, rules, and regulations
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or amended
accordingly.
SEC. 6. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
