                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to grant benefits and privileges to indigent Overseas Filipino
Workers (OFWs) who have returned to the Philippines after years of employment
abroad and who remain economically vulnerable despite their significant
contributions to the country’s national development.

       The migration of Filipino labor serves as a primary pillar of national
macroeconomic stability. The remittances of our OFWs sustain millions of Filipino
families and provide a stable source of foreign exchange that supports economic
growth. Their sacrifices have earned them recognition as modern-day heroes, often
requiring years of separation from their families in pursuit of better opportunities
for their loved ones and the nation.

       Despite these contributions, many returning OFWs face significant
reintegration challenges. Limited employment opportunities, inadequate savings,
rising cost of living, and restricted access to affordable healthcare often leave them in
financially precarious situations. These were particularly evident during the
COVID-19 pandemic, when thousands of repatriated OFWs struggled to secure
sustainable sources of income upon their return.

       This proposed measure grants qualified indigent OFWs discount and
privileges on essential goods and services. This includes medicines, medical and
dental care, transportation, utilities, lodging, burial services, and government
programs. It likewise provides access to livelihood, housing, training, and other
forms of assistance intended to promote their welfare and improve their quality of
life.

        The State has a responsibility not only to protect Filipino workers while they
are employed overseas but also to support them when they return home.
Reintegration assistance should extend beyond employment facilitation and
livelihood programs to include measures that help address the immediate social and
economic needs of vulnerable returning workers. Those who have devoted years of
their lives to supporting their families and contributing to the country’s prosperity
deserve the opportunity to return to a homeland that values their sacrifice and
enables them to rebuild their lives with dignity, hope, and security.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                       AN ACT
GRANTING BENEFITS AND PRIVILEGES TO INDIGENT OVERSEAS
FILIPINO WORKERS

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Indigent Overseas
Filipino Workers (OFWs) Discounts Act."

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The Constitution affirms that labor is the
primary social economic force and mandates the State to protect the rights of the
workers and promote their welfare.

       Recognizing the significant contributions of our Overseas Filipino Workers
(OFWs) to our country's national economy, the State shall adopt measures to protect
and grant benefits to our modern-day heroes who return to the country after their
overseas employment and assist them as they continue to contribute to
nation-building.

      SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

         a. Overseas Filipino Worker refers to any resident citizen of the
      Philippines previously employed overseas and has been certified by the
      Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) for previous overseas employment
      purposes who has rendered employment services overseas for at least one (1)
      year including but not limited to seafarers, entertainers, domestic helpers,
      and professionals, regardless of their current employment status;

         b. Lodging establishment refers to a building, edifice, structure, apartment
      or house including tourist inn, apartelle, motorist hotel, and pension house
      engaged in catering, leasing or providing facilities to transients, tourists or
      travelers;

          c. Medical services refer to hospital services, professional services of
      physicians and other health care professionals and diagnostics and laboratory
      tests that the necessary for the diagnosis or treatment of an illness or injury;

          d. Dental services refer to oral examination, cleaning, permanent and
      temporary filling, extractions and gum treatments, restoration, replacement
      or repositioning of teeth, or alteration of the alveolar or periodontium process
      of the maxilla and the mandible that are necessary for the diagnosis or
      treatment of an illness or injury;

         e. Indigent OFW refers to any OFW who is without pension or permanent
      source of income, compensation or financial assistance from his/her relatives
      to support his/her basic needs, as determined by the Department of Social
      Welfare and Development (DSWD), in consultation with the National
      Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC).

        SEC. 4. Privileges and Discounts granted to OFWs and Former OFWs. – Any
qualified indigent OFW as determined by the DMW and DSWD shall be entitled to
the following:

         a.​ The grant of twenty percent (20%) discount and exemption from the
      value-added tax (VAT), if applicable, on the sale of the following goods and
      services from all establishments, for the exclusive use and enjoyment or
      availment of the OFW:

                1.​ On the purchase of medicines, and such other essential medical
            supplies, accessories and equipment to be determined by the
            Department of Health (DOH); Provided, That the DOH shall administer
            free vaccination against the influenza virus and pneumococcal disease
            for indigent OFW patients.

                    The DOH shall establish guidelines and mechanism of
            compulsory rebates in the sharing of burden of discounts among
            retailers, manufacturers and distributors, taking into consideration
            their respective margins;

               2.​ On the professional fees of attending physician/s in all private
            hospitals, medical facilities, outpatient clinics and home health care
            services;

                3.​ On the professional fees of licensed professional health
            providing home health care services as endorsed by private hospitals
            or employed through home health care employment agencies;

                4.​ On medical and dental services, diagnostic and laboratory fees
            in all private hospitals, medical facilities, outpatient clinics, and home
            health care services, in accordance with the rules and regulations to be

       issued by the DOH, in coordination with the Philippine Health
       Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth);

          5.​ In actual fare for land transportation travel in public utility
       buses (PUBs), public utility jeepneys (PUJs), taxis, asian utility vehicles
       (AUVs), shuttle services and public railways, including Light Rail
       Transit (LRT), Mass Rail Transit (MRT), and Philippine National
       Railways (PNR);

          6.​ In actual transportation fare for domestic air transport services
       and sea shipping vessels and the like, based on the actual fare and
       advanced booking;

          7.​ On the utilization of all establishments relative to hotels and
       similar lodging establishments, restaurants and recreation centers
       nationwide;

          8.​ On admission fees charged by theaters, cinema houses and
       concert halls, circuses, leisure and amusement; and

         9.​ On funeral and burial services for the death of the indigent
       OFW, regardless of the cause of death.

    b.​ The grant of a minimum of five percent (5%) discount relative to the
monthly utilization of water and electricity supplied by the public utilities:
Provided, That the individual meters for the foregoing utilities are registered
in the name of the OFW residing therein: Provided, further, That the monthly
consumption does not exceed one hundred kilowatt hours (100 kWh) of
electricity and thirty cubic meters (30 m3) of water: Provided, furthermore, that
the privilege is granted per household regardless of the number of OFWs
residing therein;

   c.​ Exemption from training fees for socioeconomic programs;

    d.​ Free medical and dental services, diagnostic and laboratory fees such
as, but not limited to, x-rays, computerized tomography scans and blood
tests, in all government facilities, subject to the guidelines to be issued by the
DOH in coordination with the PhilHealth;

   e.​ To the extent possible, the government may grant special discounts in
special programs for OFWs on purchase of basic commodities, subject to the
guidelines to be issued for the purpose by the Department of Trade and
Industry (DTI) and the Department of Agriculture (DA);

   f.​ Priority in existing livelihood programs being undertaken by various
government agencies subject to the guidelines and qualifications by the
implementing body;

      other housing opportunities subject to the guidelines and qualifications set by
      the National Housing Authority or the Home Development Mutual Fund

       SEC. 5. Monitoring and Data Gathering on all OFWs. – The DMW, in
coordination with DSWD and the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration
(OWWA), shall maintain and regularly update at least on a quarterly basis the list of
all OFWs, including indigent OFWs, and shall issue a nationally uniform
identification card which shall be valid anywhere nationwide.

       SEC. 6. Penalties. – Notwithstanding any administrative or civil liability from
existing laws, any person or company who violates the provision of this Act by
denying or hindering an indigent OFW from availing of any of the benefits under
this Act shall be penalized by a fine of:

          a. Not less than Fifty thousand pesos (P50,000.00) for the first violation;

         b. One hundred thousand pesos (P100,000.00) for the second violation;
      and

          c. Two hundred thousand pesos (P200,000.00) for the third violation.

       SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Migrant
Workers, Department of Social Welfare and Development, Department of Health,
Department of Tourism, and the Department of Finance shall issue the necessary
rules and regulations to carry out the objectives of this Act.

        SEC. 8. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriation of the concerned
departments and agencies. Thereafter, such amount shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

      SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, issuances, rules
and regulations which may be inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are
hereby deemed repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, such holding shall not affect other provisions not
affected thereby.

      SEC. 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
