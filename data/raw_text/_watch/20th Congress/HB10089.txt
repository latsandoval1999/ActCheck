                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to further strengthen Republic Act (RA) No. 9994, otherwise known as
the “Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010”. It expands health and wellness programs
provided to senior citizens, and provides for the inclusion of digital inclusion programs to
promote their digital literacy.

         The number of senior citizens in the country continue to grow as life expectancy
improves and demographic trends point toward an aging population. The Philippine
Statistics Authority reported that Filipinos aged sixty (60) and above account for more than
nine percent (9.6%) of the country’s one hundred and thirteen million (113.7) million
population. While this remains below the ten percent (10%) threshold used by World Bank
to define an aging society, the country’s senior citizen population is projected to double by
2035-20401. This highlights the need to strengthen programs that promote healthy, active,
and dignified aging.

       This measure expands the government assistance provided to our senior citizens by
requiring annual preventive health check-ups, community-based wellness and rehabilitation
services, home and mobile healthcare for vulnerable seniors, and expanded access to
telemedicine. It also institutionalizes nationwide digital literacy and inclusion programs to
equip our elderly with the knowledge and skills necessary to safely access digital
technologies and government services to close the digital divide once and for all.

        More than strengthening health, wellness, and digital inclusion programs extended
to our senior citizens for them to live healthier, safer, and more productive lives in a digital
society, this vital piece of legislation reinforces the State’s commitment to safeguard the
welfare, dignity, and well-being of our senior citizens.

        In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is immediately sought.
