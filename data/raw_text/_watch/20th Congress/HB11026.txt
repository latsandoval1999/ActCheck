                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XIV, Section 1 of the 1987 Constitution provides that “[t]he State shall
protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels, and shall
take appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all.”

        Almost three (3) decades ago, Republic Act No. 8525, otherwise known as the
“Adopt-a-School Act of 1998,” was enacted as a step towards achieving accessible
quality education by facilitating the participation of the private sector in the country’s
education sector. Under the law, tax incentives are given to private entities supporting
public schools. However, recent data reflect the need for the law to be amended to
better respond to current challenges, such as bureaucratic red tape and administrative
complexity that prevent private entities from receiving their mandated tax incentives.1

        Amidst the education crisis, exacerbated by inadequate learning facilities and
classroom shortages, limited digital connectivity, and insufficient teaching resources,
Filipino children and students increasingly struggle with access to quality education.2
These gaps accumulate and become barriers hindering the Filipino youth from
enjoying their freedoms and rights to education, to self-development and self-
actualization, and to social mobility.

      This bill amends Republic Act No. 8525 to allow private sector donors to support
a wider range of educational programs, including early childhood care, technical-

       This bill recognizes that addressing the education crisis requires not only
sustained government investment and action, but also participation from the private
sector, communities, and civil society. This bill affirms the principle that the equal
opportunity for a better life through quality education cannot and should not be
achieved by the government alone, but also through institutions that empower citizens,
communities, and the private sector to participate in the endeavor of nation-building.

       In the 20th Congress, similar bills were filed by Representatives Jaime Fresnedi,
Crispin Diego Remulla, Ryan Recto, Kenneth Gatchalian, Walfredo Dimaguila, Roman
Romulo, Agatha Paula Cruz, Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo, David Suarez, Eric Go Yap,
and Antonio Legarda in the House of Representatives, and Senators Win Gatchalian,
Bam Aquino, Pia Cayetano, Loren Legarda, and Joel Villanueva. This bill adopts
Senator Bam Aquino’s Senate Bill No. 129.

       Early approval of this measure is earnestly sought.
