                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish at least one (1) e-learning center in every city and
municipality, expand access to digital learning resources, and strengthen
community-based education infrastructure nationwide. It institutionalizes an
integrated system of learning spaces and digital technologies to ensure that
Filipinos, especially those in underserved and geographically isolated areas, can
access quality education, skills training, and lifelong learning opportunities.

        Despite the increasing use of digital technology in education, many Filipinos
still lack adequate access to information and communication technology (ICT). The
Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) showed that in 2024, only forty-eight percent
(48.8%) of households had internet access at home1. Rural and low-income
communities continue to face limited digital infrastructure, inadequate devices, and
insufficient learning resources, which restrict access to online learning and skills
development. PSA further emphasized that despite improvements, millions of
households still rely on shared or public connections, which are often unstable and
inadequate for sustained educational use2. The Philippine Institute for Development
Studies likewise confirms that infrastructure gaps continue to constrain digital
learning opportunities across several regions3. These conditions limit access to
learning materials and weaken the preparedness of the Filipino youth for an
increasingly digital economy.

                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF AN E-LEARNING CENTER IN
EVERY CITY AND MUNICIPALITY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “E-Learning Center Act.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby the declared policy of the State to
provide institutional mechanisms for education to be made available and accessible
for the Filipino people, without discrimination on the basis of age, sex, ethnicity,
culture, religion, or ability.

       SEC. 3. Establishment of E-Learning Centers. – There shall be established at least
one (1) e-learning center in every city and municipality in the Philippines; Provided,
That additional e-learning centers shall be established such that there is one (1)
e-learning center per fifty thousand (50,000) persons in every city and every
municipality with a population of more than one hundred thousand (100,000)
persons; Provided, further, That where e-learning centers already exist, its upgrading
shall be undertaken, while existing public libraries and reading centers shall be
upgraded into an e-learning center.

       SEC. 4. Functions of E-Learning Centers. – It shall make available:

          a. Printed materials, such as books, journals, newspapers, magazines,
       posters, maps, and charts;

          b. Audio-visual aids or media, such as film, television, and radio;

          c. Digital devices, such as desktop or portable computers and electronic
       blackboards, equipped with appropriate software and stable Internet
       connection to access online courses, e-books, and other online resources, as
       well as enable web conferencing;

          d. a space provided with stable and secure wi-fi connection; and

           c. a venue for alternative learning classes, training, presentations
       exhibitions, lectures, seminars, workshops, debates, and other learning
       activities;

       SEC. 5. Implementing Agencies. – The Department of Education (DepEd), in
coordination with the Department of Public Works and Highways, shall undertake
the construction of e-learning centers or the repair and rehabilitation of existing
e-learning centers according to mutually agreed specifications, criteria and details;
Provided, That the design of the e-learning center shall ensure a conducive learning
environment, as well as provide sufficient space for educational materials,
equipment, and administrative services.

        The National Library of the Philippines (NLP) shall provide the standard set
of relevant printed and audio-visual materials and shall digitize its catalog and
collections to provide free and full offsite access.

       The Department of Information and Communication Technology (DICT) shall
provide the necessary infrastructure to ensure reliable Internet connection and
maximize digital technology in e-learning centers. DepEd and DICT shall jointly
develop digital educational materials for use in e-learning centers, as well as mobile
applications and similar software that will enable remote access to community
learning center (CLC) materials, and other sources of information.

      SEC. 6. Role of Local Government Units. – Cities and municipalities shall
provide the site for e-learning centers and shall undertake its operation and
maintenance. Local government units (LGUs) may allocate such funds necessary for
the operation and maintenance of e-learning centers from the Special Education
Fund, upon approval and authorization of the Local School Board.

      SEC. 7. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of
this Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the
implementing agencies and shall be thereafter included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
approval of this Act, the DepEd, in coordination with the NLP, DICT, Department of
Interior and Local Government, and other concerned government agencies and civil
society, organizations, shall formulate and issue the necessary guidelines to
effectively implement the provisions of this Act. The implementing rules and
regulations shall take effect thirty (30) days after its publication in a national
newspaper of general circulation.

       SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part thereof is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the same shall not affect the validity and effectivity of other
provisions hereof.
