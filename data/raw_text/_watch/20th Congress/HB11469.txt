                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Corpulence or obesity has become significant public health concern in the
Philippines. The 2023 National Nutrition Survey of the Department of Science and
Technology–Food and Nutrition Research Institute (DOST-FNRI) reported that
39.8% of Filipino adults aged 20 to 59 were classified as Obese I or II under the
Asia-Pacific BMI classification. The survey also provides national data on
anthropometry, blood pressure, blood glucose, lipid profiles, dietary intake, physical
activity, and other factors relevant to nutrition and health.

      Despite the availability of national nutrition and health data, there remains a
need for sustained and focused research on the prevention and management of
overweight and obesity. Evidence on risk factors, effective interventions, treatment
outcomes, costs, and the health and socioeconomic consequences associated with
obesity can contribute to the development of effective and evidence-based public
health policies and programs.

      This measure seeks to mandate the Department of Health to establish an
Obesity Prevention Research Center that will conduct, support, coordinate, and
promote research on overweight and obesity. The Center shall study their prevalence
and determinants, evaluate preventive treatment interventions, assess their health
and economic consequences, and generate evidence to guide the formulation and
improvement of government policies and programs.

      The proposed Center shall also coordinate with existing government agencies,
research institutions, academic institutions, local government units, and other
stakeholders to maximize existing data and research resources and avoid
unnecessary duplication of efforts. The collection and use of health information shall
be undertaken in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable
ethical standards for health research.

      Through a dedicated research mechanism, this measure seeks to strengthen
the country's capacity to generate reliable evidence, evaluate interventions, and
support informed strategies for the prevention and control of overweight and obesity.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                       “AN ACT
      MANDATING THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH TO
      ESTABLISH A RESEARCH CENTER ON OBESITY
      PREVENTION”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Obesity
Prevention Act.”

      SECTION 2. Overweight and Obesity Defined. - As used in this Act,
unless the context otherwise requires, the terms “overweight” and “obesity” refer
to abnormal or excessive accumulation of body fat that may impair health. A
commonly used measure of overweight and obesity is the body mass index (BMI),
which is a person’s weight in kilograms divided by the square of the individual’s
height in meters. For children and adolescents, BMI-for-age and other age-
appropriate standards shall be used in accordance with standards adopted by
the Department of Health (DOH).

      Overweight and obesity may be associated with various health conditions,
including type 2 diabetes, impaired glucose tolerance, hyperinsulinemia,
dyslipidemia, hypertension, cardiovascular disease, cerebrovascular disease,
osteoarthritis of the hips or knees, sleep apnea, gastroesophageal reflux disease,
and gallbladder disease.

      SECTION 3. Establishment of Research Center by the Department of
Health. – The DOH shall establish, within its existing organizational structure,
an “Obesity Prevention Research Center,” which shall serve as the Department's
dedicated research and evidence-generation center for the prevention and
control of overweight and obesity. The Center shall conduct, support, coordinate,
and promote research on the epidemiology, prevention, management, and
control of overweight and obesity.

       The Center may collect and analyze relevant individual-level or aggregate
health and treatment data, only to the extent necessary for specified and
legitimate research purposes, subject to Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise
known as the “Data Privacy Act of 2012,” applicable ethical standards for health
research, and other relevant laws, rules, and regulations. Whenever practicable,
                                       2
research data shall be de-identified or anonymized. The Center shall implement
appropriate organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect personal
and health information processed pursuant to this Act.

      The research activities of the Center shall include the following:

      a. The epidemiology, prevalence, risk factors, screening and diagnosis,
         complications, prevention, management, and control of overweight and
         obesity in Filipino adults, adolescents, and children;

      b. The effectiveness of existing methods for treating or preventing
         overweight and obesity;

      c. The effectiveness, safety, accessibility, affordability, and cost-
         effectiveness of other methods and interventions for treating or
         preventing overweight and obesity;

      d. The fiscal, social, and economic impact of treating or preventing
         overweight and obesity;

      e. The adherence to, acceptability of, and factors affecting participation in
         various methods of treating or preventing overweight and obesity; and

      f. The health outcomes and complications associated with the treatment
         or prevention of overweight and obesity, including diabetes and other
         non-communicable diseases.

       The Center shall coordinate with relevant government agencies, academic
and research institutions, local government units, health facilities, professional
organizations, and other stakeholders in the conduct, dissemination, and
utilization of research under this Act, and shall endeavor to maximize existing
data and research resources and avoid unnecessary duplication of research
activities.

      SECTION 4. Funding. – The amount necessary for the implementation of
this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The DOH
may also receive gifts, grants, donations, endowments, and other contributions
from individuals, private organizations, foundations, or any governmental unit,
subject to existing laws, accounting and auditing rules, and applicable
safeguards on conflict of interest and research independence; except that no gift,
grant, donation, or contribution may be accepted by the DOH if it is subject to
conditions that are inconsistent with this Act or any other law. The DOH shall
ensure that such gifts, grants, donations, endowments, and other contributions
are used exclusively for the purposes of this Act.

       The DOH shall submit to Congress, not later than June 1 of every year, a
report on the activities, research findings, policy recommendations, and the
utilization of funds for the Research Center during the preceding calendar year.

       SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DOH shall be
primarily responsible for implementing the provisions of this Act. The DOH, in
consultation with relevant government agencies, academic and research
institutions, health professionals, and other stakeholders, shall promulgate the
necessary implementing rules and regulations within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act.

       SECTION 6. Separability Clause. - If any provision or part hereof is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not
otherwise affected shall remain valid and subsisting.

      SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. - Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation, or
part thereof, contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is hereby
repealed, modified, or amended accordingly.

       SECTION 8. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
following its publication in the Official Gazette and/or in a newspaper of general
circulation in the Philippines.
