                            EXPLANATORY NOTE


Since its enactment in 2021, Republic Act No. 11524 or the Coconut Farmers
and Industry Trust Fund Act, has been regarded as the much-awaited hope
for the coconut industry. With over 3.5 million coconut farmers in the
Philippines and 14.5 million metric tons of coconuts produced in 2025, we
are recognized as the second-largest coconut producer in the world. As such,
it is imperative upon the government that we continue to improve and develop
this industry.

The Coconut Farmers and Industry Trust Fund (CFITF) established pursuant
to RA 11524, aims to boost the coconut industry through programs geared
towards development and sustainability. However, coconut farmers have yet
to experience the benefits of the programs under this law as much of the fund
remains unused. President Marcos himself sought for the amendment of the
law in order to facilitate faster utilization and disbursement of the funds and
ensure effective and responsive programs for the industry.

This bill is thus filed to address these issues and strengthen its
implementation. The bill provides for the creation of the Coconut Farmers and
Industry Development Plan and identified the programs where the CFITF will
be utilized. This includes the massive coconut planting, fertilization, and
irrigation which ensures continuous planting of coconut trees to sustain
production. This bill also includes the Phlippine Coconut Authority as part of
the Trust Fund Management Committee to ensure that funds are properly
managed to fund relevant programs for coconut farmers.

Through this bill, it is hoped that the CFITF will be efficiently and effectively
utilized to support the industry and adequately address their needs. The
passage of this bill is thus earnestly sought.

     SECTION 1. Section 3 (a) of Republic Act 11524, also known as “The

new sub-item (e) is added, to read as follows:

            “Section 3. Definition of Terms. - As used in this Act:

         (a) Coconut Farmer shall refer to:

            (1) An owner of a coconut farm who:
            (i) Tills the land (owner-cultivation); or
            (ii) Does not till the land, but has control and supervision
      over the cultivation of the coconut farm;

             (2) A leaseholder or tenant who tills or supervises the
      cultivation of the coconut farm; or

            xxx

            Provided That, for purposes of programs and benefits
      under this Act, the maximum benefit that may be derived by a
      coconut farmer shall be equivalent to the services for up to five
      (5) hectares of landholding, regardless of the actual size of the
      farm, with priority given to smallholders.

      SEC. 2. Section 4 of Republic Act 11524, is hereby amended to read as
follows:

             “Section 4. Preparation of the Coconut Farmers and
     Industry Development Plan. – The Coconut Farmers and
     Industry Development Plan (Plan) shall be formulated, to be
     facilitated by Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA), in
     coordination with relevant government agencies and industry
     stakeholders, and shall be submitted to the President of the
     Philippines for approval. The utilization of the Trust Fund
     created under this Act, or any portion thereof, shall be in
     accordance with the Plan, subject to such adjustments as the
     PCA may deem necessary to address agency absorptive
     capacities, needs of coconut farmers and the industry.

            In the performance of this mandate, the PCA shall ensure
     participatory consultation with coconut farmers and their
     organizations, industry associations, civil society organizations,
     the academe, relevant government agencies, and other
     stakeholders in the coconut industry, to guarantee that the Plan
     reflects the needs of its intended beneficiaries.

            The Plan shall establish the long-term vision, goals,
     policies, and strategies for the development, rehabilitation, and
     modernization of the coconut industry over a period of fifty (50)
     years and shall include mechanisms for periodic review and
     updating to respond to emerging challenges and opportunities.

           xxx

           The Plan shall include a national program for:

        (a) Increased coconut productivity and sustainability thru

            coconut farm irrigation;

            processing of products and downstream products,
            intended to increase incomes of coconut farmers;
        (c) Social protection that directly benefits coconut farmers,
            farm workers, and their families, taking into consideration
            existing social protection programs of the government; and
        (d) Innovative research projects and their practical
            application on coconut processing, production and

       The Plan shall utilize the annual allocation from the Trust

    (a) Massive coconut planting, coconut fertilization, and

    (b) Development of hybrid coconut seed farms, nursery for

            Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Arrangements for
      CFIDP     Implementation.       To   enhance    the    efficiency,
      sustainability, and impact of the CFIDP, the PCA, in coordination
      with the TFMC may enter into Public-Private Partnership (PPP)
      arrangements with qualified private sector entities for the
      financing, design, construction, operation, maintenance, or
      management of programs, projects, and activities under the
      CFIDP.

            Such PPP arrangements shall be governed by the
      provisions of Republic Act No. 11966, otherwise known as the
      "Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Code of the Philippines," and
      its Implementing Rules and Regulations, as may be applicable,
      and shall be consistent with the objectives and priorities of the
      CFIDP.

            All PPP contracts entered into pursuant to this section
