                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       State universities and colleges (SUCs) serve as vital institutions in advancing
equitable access to quality higher education and in developing the country's human
capital. They are instrumental in reducing educational disparities and promoting
inclusive growth through the delivery of instruction, research and development,
extension services, and technology-driven programs responsive to the needs of
society.1
       Human capital development remains a fundamental pillar of sustainable
economic growth and national competitiveness. As the Philippines strives toward
greater economic progress and adaptation to an increasingly knowledge-driven and
technology-oriented global landscape, the need for a highly skilled, innovative, and
productive workforce becomes even more imperative. Knowledge and technological
advancement are key drivers of economic transformation, making investments in
higher education essential for long-term national development.2
      The Technological University of the Philippines–Cavite Campus has played an
important role in providing quality technological education and skills development

        However, with the increasing demand for accessible higher education and the
growing need for professionals in engineering, industrial technology, applied sciences,
and related fields, there is a compelling necessity to strengthen the institutional
capacity and autonomy of the campus. Converting the Technological University of the
Philippines–Cavite Campus into a regular campus of the Technological University of
the Philippines, to be known as the Technological University of the Philippines–Cavite
(TUP-Cavite), will enhance its capability to broaden academic offerings, improve
institutional governance, expand research initiatives, and upgrade facilities and
support services for students and faculty.
      This measure will likewise contribute significantly to the socio-economic
development of Cavite and surrounding areas by improving access to quality
technological education, promoting innovation and research, and producing a
workforce capable of supporting industrial growth and national progress.


      Early approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

 1         SECTION. 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Technological
 2   University of the Philippines-Cavite (TUP-Cavite) Act of 2026.”
 3
 4          SEC. 2. Creation of TUP-Cavite. - The existing TUP Cavite Campus of the
 5   Technological University of the Philippines (TUP), located in the City of Dasmariñas,
 6   Province of Cavite, is hereby converted into a regular campus of the TUP, to be known
 7   as the TUP-Cavite.
 8
 9           SEC. 3. General Mandate. - The TUP-Cavite shall provide higher and advanced
10   vocational, technical, industrial, technological, and professional education and training
11   in the industries and technology, and practical arts leading to certificates, diplomas,
12   and degrees. It shall provide progressive leadership in applied research and
13   developmental studies in technical, industrial, and technological fields, as well in the
14   production of goods and services that make use of indigenous materials. It shall
15   likewise provide a key role in effecting technology transfer in the countryside, and in
16   the development of small and medium-scale industries in identified growth centers.
17
18          SEC. 4. Campus Administration at the TUP-Cavite. - The University President
19   of the Technological University of the Philippines shall hold overall supervision and

                                                1
20   management of TUP-Cavite. The campus shall be led by a Campus Director, who
21   shall be appointed or designated by the TUP Board of Regents, hereinafter referred
22   to as the “Board,” upon recommendation of the University President, subject to the
23   qualifications, standards, and guidelines established by the Board.
24
25          The TUP-Cavite Campus Director shall have such functions, powers, and
26   duties as may be assigned or delegated by the Board, including but not limited to the
27   exercise of general administrative supervision and control over all academic and non-
28   academic operations of the Campus, such as instruction, research, extension,
29   personnel administration, resource generation, and financial operations.
30
31           SEC. 5. Personnel and Staffing Pattern. – The Department of Budget and
32   Management (DBM) and the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), in
33   consultation with the Technological University of the Philippines, shall, subject to
34   existing laws, rules, and regulations, determine and approve the appropriate staffing
35   pattern, organizational structure, and personnel complement necessary for the
36   effective operation of the TUP - Cavite.
37
38         SEC. 6. Compliance with CHED and other Governing Agencies. – The
39   conversion of TUP-Cavite into a regular campus shall ensure compliance with the
40   minimum prerequisites for its academic programs and overall operations, in
41   accordance with the regulations and standards set forth by the Commission on Higher
42   Education and other regulatory agencies.
43
44             SEC. 7. Transfer of Assets, Personnel, and Records. – All personnel, assets,
45   whether real or personal, and records of the TUP-Cavite Campus, as well as its
46   liabilities and obligations, shall be transferred to, and absorbed by the newly converted
47   TUP-Cavite. The plantilla items, rights, and security of tenure of existing faculty
48   members and administrative personnel of the TUP-Cavite Campus shall be respected.
49
50         All parcels of land and structures thereon that are owned by the government
51   and occupied by the TUP-Cavite Campus shall be transferred to TUP-Cavite
52   accordingly. Provided, that in the event the TUP-Cavite Campus or the TUP itself
53   ceases to exist, is abolished, or if such parcels of land are no longer required by the
54   TUP, they shall revert to the Republic of the Philippines.
55
56         SEC. 8. Land and Campus Facilities. – All parcels of land, buildings, and
57   campus facilities presently owned, occupied, and/or in the possession of the TUP-
58   Cavite Campus shall continue to be used exclusively for the operation and
59   development of the TUP-Cavite Campus.
60
61            The TUP-Cavite Campus may acquire additional parcels of land, buildings, and
62   facilities through donation, purchase, expropriation, or other lawful means, subject to
63   existing laws.
