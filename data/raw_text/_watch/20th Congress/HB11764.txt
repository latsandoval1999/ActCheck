                            AN ACT
    AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10361, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
        “DOMESTIC WORKERS ACT” OR “BATAS KASAMBAHAY”

       Republic Act No. 10361, otherwise known as the “Domestic Workers Act” or
“Batas Kasambahay,” was enacted in 2013 to institutionalize policies for the
protection and welfare of domestic workers and to establish minimum standards
governing their employment. The law was a significant step in recognizing domestic
work as legitimate work entitled to labor rights, social protection, and humane
conditions of employment.

       More than a decade since its enactment, however, the nature and conditions
of domestic work have evolved considerably. New forms of employment, including
live-out, part-time, hourly, multi-employer, and platform-mediated domestic work,
have emerged and require clearer legal recognition and protection. The experience
in implementing the law has likewise highlighted the need for stronger mechanisms
for registration, monitoring, occupational safety and health, social security coverage,
dispute resolution, and enforcement.

        Moreover, the broader Philippine labor and social protection framework has
developed substantially since the passage of Republic Act No. 10361. Subsequent
legislation, including Republic Act No. 11058, or the Occupational Safety and Health
Standards Law, has strengthened the State’s policy on safe and healthful working
conditions. These developments underscore the need to ensure that the protections
accorded to domestic workers remain consistent with current labor standards and
related laws.
       The proposed amendments therefore seek to modernize and strengthen the
Batas Kasambahay by expanding its coverage, establishing clearer standards on
working hours, rest periods, wages, occupational safety and health, and social
protection, and improving access to government services through a digital registry
and unified registration system. The measure likewise introduces mechanisms
addressing domestic work facilitated through online platforms, strengthens
information dissemination and worker organization, and provides more effective
systems for complaints, monitoring, inspection, and enforcement.

       Ultimately, this measure seeks to ensure that the protection of domestic
workers keeps pace with changing employment arrangements, emerging workplace
issues, technological developments, and the evolving body of labor and social
protection laws. It seeks to preserve the fundamental protections of Republic Act No.
10361 while providing a more responsive, comprehensive, and enforceable
framework for the rights and welfare of every kasambahay.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
