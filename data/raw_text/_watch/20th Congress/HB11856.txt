EXPLANATORY NOTE

Onions are among the most important cash crops in the Philippines. Valued as an
indispensable ingredient that makes our meals distinctly Filipino, onions also serve as a viable
source of sustainable livelihood for many farming families and contribute significantly to the
growth of our agricultural sector.

The local onion industry has continued to grow in recent years. Based on data from
the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), the national onion harvest reached 264,323.89 metric
tons in 2024, a 4.48 percent increase from 2023.! For 2025, the PSA reported before the Senate
that the country produced 308,660 metric tons of onions.? At the heart of this industry is the
Municipality of Bongabon in the Province of Nueva Ecija, the country’s leading onion
producer, which alone accounted for roughly 15 percent of the country’s total onion output
in 2024 and was chosen by the Department of Agriculture as the site of the country’s first
onion research and extension center.3 Indeed, Bongabon has long been hailed as the “Onion
Basket of the Philippines.”4

This bill seeks to formally declare the Municipality of Bongabon as the “Onion Capital
of the Philippines” in recognition of its significant contribution to the country’s agricultural
sector and the hard work of its onion farmers. It also mandates the Department of Tourism to
feature and promote the municipality as such in its regional and national promotion
programs, which will help boost local tourism, attract investments, and further uplift its onion
farming communities.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate adoption of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING THE MUNICIPALITY OF BONGABON IN THE
PROVINCE OF NUEVA ECIJA AS THE “ONION CAPITAL OF THE
PHILIPPINES”

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. The Municipality of Bongabon in the Province of Nueva Ecija is
hereby declared the “Onion Capital of the Philippines” in recognition of its
contribution as the leading producer of onions in the country and to the livelihood of
its onion farming communities.

SECTION 2. The Department of Tourism shall feature and promote the
Municipality of Bongabon as the “Onion Capital of the Philippines” in all its regional
and national promotion programs.

SECTION 3. All laws, rules, regulations, presidential decrees, and other
issuances inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed
repealed or amended accordingly.

SECTION 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
