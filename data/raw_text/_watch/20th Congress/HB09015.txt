                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        A spate of recent incidents involving drivers under the influence of alcohol or
dangerous drugs have occurred in the Province of Cebu. This has raised serious
concerns on road safety and have sparked calls to revisit Republic Act No. 10586 or the
“Anti-Drunk and Drugged Driving Act of 2013.” Despite the penalties currently being
imposed, there seems to be a blatant disregard for the law. This goes to show that there
needs to be stricter enforcement and more severe consequences to deter dangerous
behavior. The proposed measure reinforces accountability by increasing the penalties
for violations of the law.

        In 2022 alone, the Land Transportation Office (LTO) has recorded around 402
road crash incidents from January to August, some of which have resulted in fatalities,
with 353 cases involving intoxicated drivers. Despite repeated warnings to motorists,
similar incidents persist.

        Strengthening the penalties for drunk and drugged driving, including imposing
higher fines, longer periods of license suspension, or possibly license revocation, aims
to ensure that drivers will feel personally responsible for their behavior on public roads
as they operate their vehicles.

        Driving is a privilege that carries with it the responsibility to protect the lives of
others. A motor vehicle which is operated irresponsibly is nothing more than an
instrument of harm. For this reason, it shall never be an acceptable excuse for a driver
not to be in a proper condition to operate a vehicle. The grant of a driver’s license comes
with the commitment to obey traffic rules and regulations and uphold responsible road
use.

        For the foregoing reasons, approval of this bill is earnestly sought.
