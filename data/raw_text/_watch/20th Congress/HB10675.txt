                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991,"
decentralized key public services to local government units (LGUs) to foster local
autonomy and self-reliance. However, under Section 443(a) and Section 489(a) of
the Code, the appointment of a Veterinarian Officer is mandated only for
provincial and city governments, leaving the position merely optional for
municipal governments.
This statutory limitation creates a significant coverage gap in rural and
agricultural communities. Most backyard livestock raisers, small-scale poultry
farmers, and pet owners reside in municipalities where access to licensed
veterinary professionals is severely lacking. As a result, animal diseases are
frequently untreated, unmonitored, or managed using traditional methods that
fail to prevent contagion.
The absence of dedicated municipal veterinarians exposes local livestock
industries to devastating outbreaks of transmissible and zoonotic diseases—such
as African Swine Fever (ASF), Avian Influenza (Bird Flu), Rabies, and Foot-and-
Mouth Disease (FMD). These diseases cause immense economic loss to farming
families, threaten national food security, and present serious public health risks
to human populations.
This bill seeks to amend Sections 443 and 489 of Republic Act No. 7160 to make
the appointment of a Municipal Veterinarian Officer mandatory across all
municipalities in the Philippines.
In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
  PROVIDING FOR THE MANDATORY APPOINTMENT OF A MUNICIPAL
   VETERINARIAN OFFICER, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE CERTAIN
 PROVISIONS OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
              “LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991”


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


     SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Mandatory
Municipal Veterinarian Act.”

      SEC. 2. Amendment of Section 443. — Section 443, Paragraph (a) of
Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991,"
is hereby amended to read as follows:

      "SECTION 443. Officials of the Municipal Government. — (a) There shall be
      in each municipality a municipal mayor, a municipal vice-mayor,
      sangguniang bayan members, a secretary to the sangguniang bayan, a
      municipal treasurer, a municipal assessor, a municipal accountant, a
      municipal budget officer, a municipal planning and development
      coordinator, a municipal engineer/building official, a municipal health
      officer, A MUNICIPAL VETERINARIAN, and a municipal civil registrar."
      (b) ...

     SEC. 3. Amendment of Section 489. — Section 489, Paragraph (a) of
Republic Act No. 7160 is hereby amended to read as follows:

      "SECTION 489. Qualifications, Powers, and Duties.

      (a) No person shall be appointed veterinarian unless he is a citizen of the
      Philippines, a resident of the local government unit concerned, of good
      moral character, and a licensed doctor of veterinary medicine. He must
      have practiced his profession for at least three (3) years in the case of
      the provincial or city veterinarian, and at least one (1) year in the case
      of the municipal veterinarian.

      The appointment of a veterinarian officer is mandatory for provincial, city,
      and MUNICIPAL governments.

       SEC. 4. Funding and Implementation. — Local Government Units (LGUs)
shall allocate the necessary funds for the creation of the position and office of the
Municipal Veterinarian in their annual budgets, subject to the personal services
limitations provided under Section 325 of Republic Act No. 7160. National
agencies such as the Department of Agriculture (DA) and the Department of the
Interior and Local Government (DILG) may provide technical and operational
support during the transition phase.

       SEC. 5. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in full force and effect.

      SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
