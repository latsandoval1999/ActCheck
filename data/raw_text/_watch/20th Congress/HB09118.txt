                       AN ACT

REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6975, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE

OF 1990, AS AMENDED

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Under Republic Act No. 6975, otherwise known as the Department of
Interior and Local Government Act of 1990, the administration of local detention
facilities is divided between two authorities: city, municipal, and district jails are
managed by the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP), while
provincial and sub-provincial jails are administered by their respective provincial
governments. This dual system of jail administration has resulted in disparities in
jail management, detention conditions, operational policies, and personnel
standards across the country. The absence of a unified authority overseeing all
local jails has made it difficult to implement consistent custodial standards,
rehabilitation programs, and detention management systems nationwide.

       Given the BJMP’s mandate and professional expertise in jail
administration, integrating provincial and sub-provincial jails into its jurisdiction
would enable the establishment of uniform operational standards, improved
detention management practices, and enhanced institutional accountability.
         Thus, this bill seeks to amend Republic Act No. 6975 to integrate all
  provincial and sub-provincial jails under the Bureau of Jail Management and
  Penology. Through this reform, the BJMP will be able to implement standardized
  policies for jail operations, personnel training, inmate welfare, and rehabilitation
  programs across all local detention facilities. The bill also provides for a transition
  period to ensure an orderly transfer of functions, personnel, facilities, and
  resources from provincial governments to the BJMP.

          By consolidating jail administration under a single professionalized
  agency, this measure aims to improve detention facility management, strengthen
  institutional oversight, and promote the welfare and rights of detainees and
  inmates.

        This measure has been repeatedly filed since the 14th Congress but has
  remained pending at the committee level.

        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. – Section 61 of Republic Act No. 6975, otherwise known as the
Department of the Interior and Local Government Act of 1990, is hereby
amended to read as follows:


            “Sec. 61. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS. - The Jail Bureau shall
      exercise    supervision      and    control       over   all   PROVINCIAL,
      SUB-PROVINCIAL, city and municipal jails.”


      Section 2. – Section 62 of the same Act is hereby amended to read as
follows:
             “Sec. 62. ORGANIZATION. -
                                         XXX
             The Jail Bureau shall be composed of PROVINCIAL, SUB-
      PROVINCIAL, city or municipal jails, each headed by a PROVINCIAL,
      SUB-PROVINCIAL, city or municipal jail warden: Provided, That, in the
      case of large cities and municipalities, a district jail warden may be
      established as necessary.”


      Section 3. – Section 63 of the same act is hereby amended to read as
follows:
             “Sec.     63.     ESTABLISHMENT             OF      PROVINCIAL,
      SUB-PROVINCIAL, DISTRICT, CITY OR MUNICIPAL JAIL. - There
      shall be established and maintained in every PROVINCE, district, city and
      municipality a secured, clean adequately equipped and sanitary jail for the
      custody and safekeeping of PROVINCIAL, city and municipal prisoners,
      any fugitive from justice, or person detained awaiting investigation or trial
      and/or transfer to the national penitentiary, and/or violent mentally-ill
      person who endangers himself or the safety of others, duly certified as such
      by the proper medical or health officer, pending the transfer to a medical
      Institution.”


      Section 4. Role of Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP). –
The management, supervision and control or provincial and sub-provincial jails,
including all its property, equipment, and finances, are hereby transferred to the
Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP).


      Provided, however, that the three (3) year transition period is hereby

                                                                                  2
established, to commence from the date of effectivity of this Act. During the
transition period, the Provincial Government shall subsidize the subsistence
allowance of inmates and the maintenance of the jail facility, including, but not
limited     to   electric   and   water   expenses   and    necessary    repairs.


      The transfer, and/or absorption of the functions, appropriations, funds,
records, equipment, facilities, chooses in action, rights, and other assets of
Provincial and Sub-Provincial Jails to/by the BJMP shall be without prejudice to
the provisions of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local
Government Code of 1991, as amended, that are not otherwise inconsistent with
this Act.


      All personnel of provincial and sub-provincial jails, unless removed for
cause, may be absorbed by the BJMP: Provided, That such guidelines must be
consistent with the provisions of Republic Act No. 9263, otherwise known as the
Bureau of Fire Protection and Bureau of Jail Management and Penology
Professionalization Act of 2004, as amended, and its Implementing Rules and
Regulations.


      The Chief of the BJMP shall, within six (6) months from the effectivity of
this Act, recommend the organizational structure and staffing pattern of the
assumed provincial and sub-provincial jails.


      Section 5. Rehabilitation of Inmates and Maintenance of Provincial
Jails. – The local government of the province shall be a partner of the BJMP in
the rehabilitation of the inmates in the provincial jails. For this purpose, the
Sangguniang Panlalawigan of each province shall provide for the maintenance
and improvement of jails and detention centers and shall appropriate funds to

                                                                                3
supplement the subsistence of detainees and convicted prisoners in the province.


         Section 6. Provincial and Sub-Provincial Jail Warden. – The Provincial
and Sub-Provincial Jail Warden shall have the rank of Superintendent: Provided,
That if the jail population is one thousand (1,000) or more inmates, the Warden
shall be a graduate of Bachelor of Laws or a holder of a master's degree in
management, public administration, public safety, criminology, penology,
sociology, national security administration, defense studies or other related
discipline from a recognized institution of learning, and must satisfactorily pass
the necessary training and career courses for such position as may be established
by law.


         Section 7. Special Oversight Committee. – A Special Oversight
Committee, composed of the Secretary of the Interior and Local Government as
Chairman, the Secretary of the Department of Budget and Management as Co-
Chairman, and the Chairman of the Civil Service Commission, President of the
League of Provinces, and the Chief of the Bureau of Jail Management and
Penology, as members, is hereby created to oversee the expeditious transfer and
absorption into the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology of all personnel,
property, appropriations and installations of the provincial and sub- provincial
jails.


         Section 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Oversight Committee shall promulgate
the rules and regulations necessary to ensure the effective implementation of this
Act.


         Section 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision, or part hereof, is held

      Section 10. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance,

modified, or amended accordingly.


      Section 11. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15)
