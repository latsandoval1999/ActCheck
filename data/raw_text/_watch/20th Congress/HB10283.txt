                              EXPLANATORY NOTE


      Sari-sari stores and public market vendors form the backbone of
neighborhood commerce, yet they are routinely excluded from formal credit
because they lack collateral, credit history, or business registration. Many are
forced to rely on informal lenders charging predatory interest rates just to restock
inventory. This bill establishes a low-interest, collateral-free microcredit and relief
grant program specifically for sari-sari store owners and market vendors,
administered through existing government financial institutions and cooperatives,
to help them recover from economic shocks and grow their small enterprises.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Sari-Sari Store and Market
Vendor Relief Act."
       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State recognizes micro-
enterprises such as sari-sari stores and market vending as vital sources of
livelihood and shall extend affordable credit and relief support to them.
      SECTION 3. Creation of the Program. — There is hereby established the
Sari-Sari Store and Market Vendor Relief Program, to be administered by the Small
Business Corporation (SB Corp) in partnership with local government units and
accredited cooperatives, providing collateral-free loans of up to Fifty Thousand
Pesos (Php50,000.00) at an interest rate not exceeding two percent (2%) per annum.
      SECTION 4. Simplified Application. — Applicants shall only be required to
present a barangay business certificate or market stall permit, a valid government-
issued identification, and a simple sworn statement of income; no collateral, credit
history, or audited financial statement shall be required.
        SECTION 5. Emergency Relief Grants. — In the event of calamities, market
fires, or demolitions, qualified beneficiaries may receive a one-time, non-repayable
relief grant of not more than Ten Thousand Pesos (Php10,000.00) to help restart
their business.
      SECTION 6. Financial Literacy Support. — The Department of Trade and
Industry shall provide free basic bookkeeping and financial literacy training to
program beneficiaries.
