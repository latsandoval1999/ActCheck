EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines thrives with a vibrant tapestry of Indigenous Cultural Communities and
Peoples, whose unique traditions, languages, wisdom, arts, histories, and institutions form the very
heart of the nation's cultural heritage.

The Constitution recognizes the rights of Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous
Peoples within the framework of national unity and development. Republic Act No. 8371,
otherwise known as the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997, further recognizes and protects
the rights of Indigenous Peoples to preserve and develop their cultures, traditions, and institutions
and created the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples as the primary government agency
responsible for protecting and promoting their rights and well-being. EeLibrary+1

Zamboanga City stands out as a beacon of cultural significance, home to a rich mgsaic of

Indigenous Peoples and communities whose heritage shapes the vibrant identity of both the city
and Western Mindanao.

Recognizing this need, the City Government of Zamboanga acted in 2020 by emacting
Ordinance No. 547, which established the Zamboanga Cultural Complex for Indigenous Peoples
and other major cultures, with dedicated funding. This commitment was further strengthened with
the passage of Ordinance No. 661 in 2024.

This proposed measure aims to secure national government support and establish a lasting
institutional foundation for a dedicated cultural center in Zamboanga City. The proposed
Zamboanga Cultural Center for Indigenous Peoples shall serve as a venue for cultural education,
exhibitions, research, documentation, preservation, performances, traditional arts, conimunity
activities, and intergenerational transmission of indigenous knowledge systems and practi¢es.

The Center shall not replace existing cultural institutions. Instead, it shall complement the
programs of the National Commission for Culture and the Arts, the National Commission on
Indigenous Peoples, the National Museum of the Philippines, the National Library of the
Philippines, the National Archives of the Philippines, and the City Government of Zamboanga.

More importantly, the proposed Center shall ensure that Indigenous Peoples themselves
have a meaningful role in determining how their cultures are documented, presented, taught,
preserved, and transmitted. This is consistent with the principles of cultural integrity, self-
determination, and participation embodied in Republic Act No. 8371.

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga Cultural Center

for Indigenous Peoples Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. —\t is hereby declared the policy of the State to recagnize,
respect, protect, preserve, and promote the cultural identity, traditions, languages, indigenous
knowledge systems and practices, arts, heritage, and institutions of Indigenous Cultural

Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs).

Pursuant to the Constitution and Republic Act No. 8371, otherwise known as the
“Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997,” the State shall support programs and institutions that
enable Indigenous Peoples to preserve, develop, and transmit their cultural heritage to present and

future generations.

The State further recognizes the City of Zamboanga as a culturally diverse urban center

and a significant meeting place of Indigenous Peoples and other cultural communities in Western

SEC. 3. Establishment of the Zamboanga Cultural Center for Indigenous Peoples. —
There is hereby established in the City of Zamboanga a public cultural institution to be knéwn as

the Zamboanga Cultural Center for Indigenous Peoples, hereinafter referred to as the Center.

The Center shall be a government cultural facility primarily dedicated to the preservation,
promotion, transmission, and development of the cultures, traditions, arts, histories, indigenous
knowledge systems and practices, and contemporary expressions of Indigenous Peoples in

Zamboanga City and the surrounding areas of Western Mindanao.

The Center shall complement, and shall not diminish or supersede, existing cultural
institutions, programs, and facilities of the National Government and the City Governmient of

Zamboanga.

SEC. 4. Location. — The Center shall be established in the City of Zamboanga on a suitable
government-owned property or on such other property as may be lawfully acquired, donated,

transferred, or otherwise made available for the purpose.

The site shall be accessible to Indigenous Peoples, residents, students, researchers, artists,

cultural workers, tourists, and the general public.

The selection, development, and use of the site shall comply with applicable laws
concerning ancestral domains, ancestral lands, environmental protection, cultural heritage, land

use, zoning, public procurement, and government property.

SEC. 7. Indigenous Peoples' Participation and Cultural Governance. — The planning,
development, programming, and operation of the Center shall ensure the meaningful parti¢ipation

of Indigenous Peoples.

Indigenous elders, traditional leaders, cultural bearers, artists, scholars, women, youth, and
representatives of recognized Indigenous Peoples' organizations shall be provided appropriate

mechanisms to participate in the development of policies and programs of the Center.

Nothing in this Act shall authorize the appropriation, reproduction, commercialization,
publication, or public display of sacred, confidential, restricted, or culturally sensitive knowledge
or materials without the appropriate consent and safeguards required under existing laws,

customary laws, and applicable policies.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed as abolishing, transferring, or diminishing the

functions of any existing government agency or cultural institution.

SEC. 13. Relationship with Existing Zamboanga City Cultural Programs. — The Center
shall coordinate with existing cultural programs and facilities established by the City Government
of Zamboanga, including the cultural complex established under City Ordinance No. 547, as

amended.

The National Government and the City Government of Zamboanga are encouraged to
harmonize their respective cultural programs and facilities to avoid unnecessary duplication and

maximize public resources.

SEC. 14. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the appropriate agencies, subject

to existing budgeting, accounting, auditing, and procurement laws, rules, and regulations.

Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation and operation

of the Center shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

The City Government of Zamboanga may provide counterpart funding, property, facilities,
personnel, services, or other forms of assistance, subject to applicable laws, ordinances, and

budgetary rules.

SEC. 15. Donations, Grants, and Endowment. — The Center may receive donations,
grants, endowments, bequests, technical assistance, and other contributions from domestic or

foreign sources, subject to existing laws, rules, regulations, and applicable safeguards.
All donations shall be used exclusively for purposes consistent with this Act.

SEC. 16. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days ffom the
effectivity of this Act, the NCCA and NCIP, in coordination with the City Government of
Zamboanga and representatives of Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples, shall

promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

SEC. 17. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or

invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 18. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, regulations, and
other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,

SEC. 19. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
