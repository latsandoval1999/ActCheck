15                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

16   The Personnel Economic Relief Allowance (PERA) is a cost-of-living allowance granted
17   to government employees to help cushion the effects of inflation and rising prices of
18   basic goods and services.

19   PERA was first granted in 1991 through DBM Budget Circular No. 4 dated June 28,
20   1991, which authorized a monthly allowance of Five Hundred Pesos (₱500.00) for
21   qualified government employees. The allowance was intended to augment government
22   compensation and mitigate the adverse effects of inflation and increases in fuel and
23   commodity prices on public sector workers.

24   Recognizing the continuing erosion of purchasing power caused by rising prices, the
25   government subsequently expanded and enhanced the benefit. In 2009, through Senate
26   and House of Representatives Joint Resolution No. 4, s. 2009, the Personnel Economic
27   Relief Allowance was consolidated with the Additional Compensation (ADCOM) and
28   increased to the current amount of Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) per month, in
29   recognition of the rising cost of living and the need to provide additional economic relief
30   to government personnel.

31   However, the PERA has remained unchanged since 2009. During the intervening years,
32   inflation has steadily increased the cost of food, transportation, housing, utilities,
33   healthcare, education, and other basic necessities. As a result, the real value and
34   purchasing power of the allowance have significantly diminished, undermining its
35   original purpose as a meaningful source of economic relief for government employees.

36   Had the allowance been adjusted to keep pace with inflation, its present value would be
37   substantially higher than the current monthly rate. The prolonged absence of any
38   adjustment has effectively reduced the level of assistance provided by the government to
39   its employees despite the continued rise in the cost of living.

40   This measure seeks to restore the value of the Personnel Economic Relief Allowance by
41   increasing the monthly PERA from Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) to Four
42   Thousand Pesos (₱4,000.00) and establishing an automatic annual adjustment
43   mechanism based on the official inflation rate published by the Philippine Statistics
44   Authority (PSA). By linking future adjustments to inflation, the measure ensures that the
45   allowance remains responsive to economic conditions and continues to provide
46   meaningful relief to government personnel.
47   The proposed increase and indexation mechanism embody the State's commitment to
48   promoting the welfare of government employees and preserving their purchasing power
49   in the face of rising prices and changing economic circumstances.

50   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

113   AN ACT INCREASING THE PERSONNEL ECONOMIC RELIEF ALLOWANCE
114    (PERA) GRANTED TO GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES, PROVIDING FOR AN
115    AUTOMATIC INFLATION ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM, APPROPRIATING
116              FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

117   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
118   Congress assembled:

119   This Act shall be known as the "PERA Inflation Adjustment Act of 2026."

120   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.

121   It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote the economic welfare of
122   government employees by ensuring that economic relief allowances maintain their real
123   value and remain responsive to increases in the cost of living.

124   SECTION 3. Increase in Personnel Economic Relief Allowance.

125   The Personnel Economic Relief Allowance (PERA) granted to civilian government
126   personnel, military personnel, and uniformed personnel covered by existing laws and
127   regulations is hereby increased from Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) to Four
128   Thousand Pesos (₱4,000.00) per month.

129   The increase shall apply to all personnel presently entitled to receive PERA under
130   existing laws, rules, and regulations.

131   SECTION 4. Automatic Inflation Adjustment.

132   Beginning January 1 three (3) years immediately following the effectivity of this Act, the
133   monthly PERA shall be adjusted annually based on the official annual average inflation
134   rate for the preceding calendar year as determined and published by the Philippine
135   Statistics Authority (PSA).

136   SECTION 5. Coverage.

137   The benefits under this Act shall cover:

138      1. Civilian government personnel occupying regular, contractual, casual, appointive,
139         or elective positions covered by the compensation and position classification
140         system;
141      2. Military personnel of the Armed Forces of the Philippines;
142      3. Uniformed personnel of the Philippine National Police, Bureau of Fire Protection,
143         Bureau of Jail Management and Penology, Philippine Coast Guard, and other
144         uniformed services authorized by law.

145   SECTION 6. Tax Exemption.

146   The increased PERA and all future inflation adjustments under this Act shall continue to
147   be exempt from income tax and other deductions to the extent allowed under existing
148   laws.

149   SECTION 7. Funding.

150   The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against
151   available appropriations under the current General Appropriations Act.

152   Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued implementation shall be
153   included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

154   SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations.

155   Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DBM, in coordination with
156   the PSA, the Department of Finance, and the Civil Service Commission, shall promulgate
157   the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

158   SECTION 9. Separability Clause.

159   If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining
160   provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

161   SECTION 10. Repealing Clause.

162   All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent
163   with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

164   SECTION 11. Effectivity.

165       This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
166   Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
