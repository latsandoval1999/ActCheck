                          AN ACT
   INSTITUTIONALIZING SCHOOL-BASED SPORTS DEVELOPMENT
 PROGRAMS AND VARSITY TEAMS IN BASIC EDUCATION, PROVIDING
  SUPPORT FOR STUDENT-ATHLETES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The 1987 Constitution directs the State to promote physical education,
sports programs, league competitions, and amateur sports, and requires
educational institutions to undertake regular sports activities. Republic Act
No. 5708, or the Schools Physical Education and Sports Development Act of
1969, likewise provides for an integrated physical education and sports
development program in schools. Consistent with these mandates, the
Department of Education has adopted policies supporting the organization of
junior and senior varsity teams and the gradual expansion of school sports
programs.

        Despite these existing frameworks, implementation remains uneven.
Many schools, particularly those that are small, geographically isolated, or
economically disadvantaged, continue to face limitations in qualified coaches,
facilities, equipment, training opportunities, competition, and medical and
safety support. Some schools also lack the resources to maintain independent
varsity teams or participate regularly in organized competitions.

      This measure seeks to strengthen and institutionalize school-based
sports development by establishing a clear and sustainable framework for
varsity teams in elementary and secondary schools. Its implementation shall
be phased and responsive to the resources, facilities, student population, and
sports interests of each school or school cluster.

      The proposed Act shall provide standards for the identification and
development of student-athletes, training and recognition of coaches,
provision of equipment, organization of training and competitions,
maintenance of athlete records, and coordination among schools, local
government units, the Philippine Sports Commission, national sports
associations, and other stakeholders. It shall also promote equitable access
to sports, including traditional and indigenous games, while protecting the
health, safety, academic welfare, and rights of student-athletes.

       School sports contribute not only to athlete development but also to the
physical, social, and personal growth of learners by fostering fitness,
discipline, teamwork, leadership, resilience, and mental well-being. A properly
supported varsity system can provide a structured pathway for grassroots
talent development while ensuring that sports opportunities are accessible,
safe, and inclusive.

       The measure therefore seeks to complement and strengthen existing
constitutional, legislative, and DepEd policies by providing statutory support
for the sustainable and equitable implementation of school sports programs
throughout the country.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT

 PROGRAMS AND VARSITY TEAMS IN BASIC EDUCATION, PROVIDING
  SUPPORT FOR STUDENT-ATHLETES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Sports Varsity
Teams in Basic Education Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to promote physical education and encourage sports programs, league
competitions, and amateur sports, including training for international
competitions, to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for the
development of a healthy and alert citizenry.

To this end, the State shall provide opportunities for optimal and long-term
development of Filipino athletes by expanding the base of student athletes,
increasing the time and frequency for their early exposure, training, and
competition through the institutionalization of sports varsity teams in the
basic education cycle.

SECTION 3. Institutionalization of Sports Varsity Teams. – All public and
private elementary and secondary schools shall establish or participate in a
school-based sports development program, including varsity teams or inter-
school sports clusters, subject to minimum standards, available resources,
student interest, and the phased implementation plan prescribed under this
Act.

The sports covered by the program shall include athletics, traditional and
indigenous games, and other sports identified by DepEd in consultation with
the PSC, relevant national sports associations, and other stakeholders.
SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. As used in this Act:

   a. Sports Varsity Team refers to a school-recognized group of eligible
      learners selected and trained to represent a public or private elementary

   b. Student-athlete refers to any learner officially enrolled in an elementary
      or secondary education program, including the Alternative Learning
      System, who is selected, authorized, or recognized by the school or an
      authorized school cluster to participate in organized sports training,
      competition, or related athletic development activities while continuing
      to fulfill the learner’s academic responsibilities

SECTION 4. Organization of Sports Varsity Team. – The organization of
sports varsity teams shall ensure the following:

      a. Presence of competent and committed teacher-coaches;
      b. Availability of facilities and equipment;
      c. Administrative support; and
      d. Support of the community.

SECTION 5. Identification of Athletes. – The Department of Education
(DepEd) shall provide a sports varsity selection and admission system.

All K-12 learners who are officially enrolled in Elementary, Junior High School,
Senior High School, Open High School, and Alternative Learning Systems,
especially those who participate in the after-school sports program (ASSP) and
who can meet the following requirements, can join their school’s sports varsity
teams:

      a. Must have passed the physical fitness tests and skill tests;
      b. With parental consent; and
      c. In good academic standing.

SECTION 6. Selection and Deployment of Competent Coaches. – It shall
be the responsibility of the school administration to secure the services of
competent coaches. Teachers, including the sports coordinator, trained in
sports coaching are the priority in the selection of coaches.

Former outstanding athletes from among the parents or community residents
may be tapped to serve as volunteer coaches in the sports where they excel
and gained national or international recognition. Likewise, students and
graduates of the Sports Track in the Senior High School (SHS) shall be tapped
to provide assistance in the training of Sports Varsity Teams.

The Sports Varsity Teams may also serve as a venue for the practicum and/or
immersion of students in the Sports Track.

SECTION 7. Recognition and Training of Coaches. – DepEd shall create a
mechanism to provide incentives and recognition to volunteer coaches.
DepEd, in consultation with the Philippine Sports Commission, National
Sports Associations, Philippine Olympic Committee, and the private sector,
shall develop programs to train varsity coaches to become certified trainers
based on international standards.

SECTION 8. Time and Frequency of Training. – The implementing school
has the discretion to determine the time and frequency of training of the
varsity teams, provided that the trainings are conducted after the regular class
hours and shall not interfere with instructionally relevant activities pursuant
to engaging students in learning the lessons of the curriculum.

SECTION 9. More Competition. – Aside from the calendar of regular sports
competitions conducted by the DepEd, private and public schools shall
organize varsity matches with neighboring schools. A league may be composed
of public and private schools.

Furthermore, elementary and secondary schools may partner with existing
stadiums and sports centers in the community to host varsity matches.

SECTION 10. Equipment for Sports Varsity. – It shall be the responsibility
of the school administration to secure the equipment necessary to sustain the
training of sports varsity teams. Such equipment may be sourced from regular
budget and extra-budgetary means such as donations from the community.

The DepEd, in consultation with the Philippine Sports Commission, shall be
responsible for maintaining a databank of standard facilities and equipment
their technical specifications suppliers and indicative prices for every sport
covered in international competitions.

SECTION 11. Performance Profiling of Student Athletes. – There shall be
a school-based profiling of student athletes. In case of a student athlete’s
transfer to another school, it is the responsibility of the former coaches to
transmit the student’s athletic profile to the new school to ensure proper
recognition and continuous training and development. The student athlete’s
profile shall show results of previous competitions and improvement in
clocking in the case of measurable sports.

All elementary and secondary schools shall register and update their student
athletes’ profile from time to time.

SECTION 12. Registry of Student Athletes. – DepEd shall maintain a
registry of student athletes and shall make this accessible to colleges and
universities, Philippine Sports Commission, National Sports Associations,
Philippine Olympic Committee, and other private sport groups and
organizations and shall be uses solely for identification of potential inclusion
to varsity teams in college and universities or in the case of the Philippine
Sports Commission, selection of national athletes who can represent the
Philippines in international sporting events.
SECTION 13. Incentives. – Recognitions and incentives shall be awarded to
school coaches and school heads for their support to the organization and
registration of their school varsity teams.

Teacher-coaches shall be given service credits and/or additional
compensation or overtime pay for work performed in excess of eight (8) hours
in accordance with the law.

SECTION 14. Appropriations. – The funds necessary for the
institutionalization of sports varsity teams, especially the provision of
equipment, in elementary and secondary schools, shall come from the regular
appropriation of DepEd.

SECTION 15. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days after the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Education and the
Chairperson of the Philippine Sports Commission, upon consultation with
relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the rules and regulations needed for
the implementation of this Act.

SECTION 16. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this bill is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof shall remain in force.

SECTION 17. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 18. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days after
publication in a newspaper of general circulation.
