                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The 1987 Constitution provides under Article II, Section 17 that the State
shall give priority to education, science and technology, arts, culture, and sports
to foster patriotism and nationalism, accelerate social progress, and promote
total human liberation and development. Furthermore, the Constitution provides
under Article XIV, Section 1 that the State shall protect and promote the right of
all citizens to quality education at all levels and shall take appropriate steps to
make such education accessible to all.

       During the pandemic, the country has witnessed how the education
system was forced to transform and adopt digital means of doing things. With
this transformation comes the need to likewise transform academic materials
into digitized formats.

      The need has outlived the pandemic. Every year, typhoons, floods, and
extreme heat suspend in-person classes and damage printed textbooks, and
many public schools, especially in the provinces, still wait for complete sets of
books. A learner in a far-flung barangay should be able to open the same
textbook as a learner in the city.

       There are several benefits to the digitization of academic materials.
Digitized formats of academic materials allow several learners to access the same
material at the same time. Digitization also promotes space efficiency and
savings on handling costs since repositories no longer have to maintain and
transport physical copies of books. Lastly, digitization safeguards the contents
of academic materials from loss and destruction since the academic resource
materials are stored in online cloud storage.

       This bill seeks to establish the Philippine Online Library, where the
digitized format of academic resource materials necessary for the public
education of Philippine elementary and secondary schools shall be compiled. The
Philippine Online Library shall be made accessible to learners free of charge
through the internet and also by providing computers, laptops, or appropriate
equipment to all public elementary and secondary schools nationwide, with
offline access for areas where the internet connection is weak or absent.

      Based on the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
   DIGITIZING ALL BOOKS NECESSARY FOR PUBLIC EDUCATION AND
  ESTABLISHING THE PHILIPPINE ONLINE LIBRARY, PROVIDING FUNDS
               THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Online
Library Act”.

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all
levels and to take appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all.
Thus, the State shall endeavor to introduce measures that will allow its citizens
to catch up with the increasingly digitized approach to education and remove
barriers to access to necessary online educational materials, with particular
attention to learners in rural, remote, and underserved communities.

      SECTION 3. Philippine Online Library. – The Department of Education
(DepEd) shall create digitized copies of all textbooks and reference books as it
deems necessary for the public education of Philippine elementary and
secondary school students, including learners in the Alternative Learning
System. These digitized copies shall be compiled in the Philippine Online Library
which shall be jointly managed by the DepEd, the Department of Information
and Communications Technology (DICT), and the Intellectual Property Office of
the Philippines (IPOPHL).

      Access to the Philippine Online Library shall be free of charge for learners,
teachers, and parents. As far as practicable, its materials shall be available in
formats accessible to learners with disabilities and usable offline.
      SECTION 4. Provision for Access to the Digitized Copies of Textbooks. – The
DepEd shall ensure access to the digitized copies of textbooks by providing
computers, laptops, or appropriate devices and internet connectivity to all public
elementary and secondary schools nationwide, giving priority to last-mile schools
and those in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas. For this purpose,
the DepEd may coordinate with the DICT, local government units, and the
private sector.

      SECTION 5. Additional Online Educational Materials. – The DepEd shall
coordinate with the National Library of the Philippines to supplement the
Philippine Online Library’s digital repository of textbooks with additional
materials that are representative of Filipino culture and literature, including
materials in regional and indigenous languages. DepEd shall also be allowed to
add other online materials that it deems necessary for expanding the learning
opportunities of students.

       SECTION 6. Custody Over Digitized Copies of Textbooks. – The DepEd and
the National Library of the Philippines shall have joint custody over digitized
copies of the textbooks. The DICT in consultation with the IPOPHL shall likewise
ensure the technological management of the digital repository to ensure the
integrity and security of the system, the protection of the personal data of its
users, and its compliance with intellectual property laws. The DepEd may enter
into licensing or similar arrangements with authors and publishers for materials
not owned by the government.

     SECTION 7. Appropriations. – Such amount as may be necessary for the
immediate implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the DepEd and the DICT. Thereafter, such amount as may be
necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DepEd, the DICT,
and the National Library of the Philippines shall have joint responsibility for the
formulation and promulgation of the Implementing Rules and Regulations
necessary for the effective execution and enforcement of this Act. They shall do
so in close coordination and consultation with the Department of Science and
Technology, IPOPHL, the National Book Development Board, organizations of
teachers, parents, and publishers, and other stakeholders. The Implementing
Rules and Regulations shall be released within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act.

       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions or parts unaffected
shall remain in full force and effect.
      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules, and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 11. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
