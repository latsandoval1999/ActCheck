EXPLANATORY NOTE

Education is one of the most powerful instruments for achieving inclusive and
sustainable development. Access to quality higher education and technical-
vocational training should not be limited by geographic location or economic
circumstance. Students in geographically distant and underserved communities
should likewise be given meaningful opportunities to pursue higher education
and acquire the knowledge, skills, and competencies necessary to improve their
lives and contribute to the development of their communities.

Sultan Kudarat is a geographically expansive province with communities located
at considerable distances from existing institutions of higher learning. For many
students, particularly those coming from low-income families, the cost of
transportation, board and lodging, and other expenses associated with studying
outside their municipalities constitutes a significant barrier to obtaining a college
education.

Establishing campuses of the Sultan Kudarat State University (SKSU) in
municipalities will substantially reduce these barriers and bring quality higher
education closer to the people.

This proposed measure seeks to establish campuses of the Sultan Kudarat State
University (SKSU) in the Municipalities of Bagumbayan, Columbio,
Esperanza, Lambayong, Lebak, President Quirino, Senator Ninoy Aquino,
and SKSU-ACCESS in Tacurong City to expand access to affordable and quality
higher education, technical-vocational training, research, extension services, and
community development in these unserved municipalities.

===== [page 2] =====
The proposed campuses shall primarily offer short-term, technical-vocational,
undergraduate, and graduate programs consistent with their respective
competencies, specializations, capabilities, and the standards prescribed by the
Commission on Higher Education (CHED). Their programs shall be responsive
to the human-resource requirements not only of the municipalities concerned but
also of the Province of Sultan Kudarat and Region XII.

The measure also further provides for the appointment or designation of Campus
Director who shall be responsible for the effective administration and operation
of the respective campuses, subject to the qualifications, standards, and
guidelines prescribed by the University Board of Regents. It also requires
compliance with the minimum requirements prescribed by CHED before the
operation and program offerings of the campuses.

The establishment of these provincial campuses is consistent with the
constitutional policy of making quality education accessible to all and with the
mandate of state universities and colleges to provide higher education, promote
research and innovation, and extend their services to the communities they serve.

More importantly, this measure will help ensure that the youth of Sultan Kudarat
are given opportunities to obtain quality education without having to leave their
respective municipalities merely to pursue their studies. It will also help develop
a pool of competent professionals, skilled workers, researchers, entrepreneurs,
teachers, and public servants who can contribute directly to the socioeconomic
advancement of the Province of Sultan Kudarat and the SOCCSKSARGEN
Region.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT

ESTABLISHING CAMPUSES OF SULTAN KUDARAT STATE
UNIVERSITY IN THE MUNICIPALITIES OF THE PROVINCE OF
SULTAN KUDARAT, TO BE KNOWN RESPECTIVELY AS SKSU-
BAGUMBAYAN, SKSU-COLUMBIO, SKSU-ESPERANZA, SKSU-
LAMBAYONG, SKSU-LEBAK, SKSU-PRESIDENT QUIRINO, SKSU-
SENATOR NINOY AQUINO AND SKSU-ACCESS IN TACURONG
CITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

Section 1. This Act shall be known as the "Sultan Kudarat State University-
Municipal Campuses Act.

Section 2. There are hereby established campuses of the Sultan Kudarat State
University (SKSU), hereinafter referred to as the University, in the
Municipalities of Bagumbayan, Columbio, Esperanza, Lambayong, Lebak,
President Quirino, Senator Ninoy Aquino, and Tacurong City, Province of
Sultan Kudarat, to be known respectively as the following:

a. SKSU-Bagumbayan Campus, Municipality of Bagumbayan
SKSU-Columbio Campus, Municipality of Columbio
SKSU-Esperanza Campus, Municipality of Esperanza
SKSU- Lambayong Campus, Municipality of Lambayong
SKSU-Lebak Campus, Municipality of Lebak
SKSU-President Quirino Campus, Municipality of President
Quirino
g. SKSU-Senator Ninoy Aquino Campus, Municipality of Senator

Ninoy Aquino
h. SKSU-ACCESS in Tacurong City

Section 3. The SKSU-Bagumbayan Campus, SKSU-Columbio Campus, SKSU
Esperanza Campus, SKSU-Lambayong Campus, SKSU-Lebak Campus,
SKSU-President Quirino Campus, SKSU-Senator Ninoy Aquino Campus, and
SKSU-ACCESS in Tacurong City shall primarily provide short-term, technical-
vocational, undergraduate, and graduate courses within their areas of
competency and specialization, pursuant to their mandate, and according to their
capabilities as the Board of Regents, hereinafter referred to as the Board, may
deem necessary for the accomplishment of their objectives, and those which
respond to the human resources development needs of the Province of Sultan
Kudarat and Region XII.

The aforesaid Campuses are also mandated to undertake research and extension
services, and to provide progressive leadership in these areas.

Section 4. SKSU-Bagumbayan Campus, SKSU-Columbio Campus, SKSU
Esperanza Campus, SKSU-Lambayong Campus, SKSU-Lebak Campus,
SKSU-President Quirino Campus, SKSU-Senator Ninoy Aquino Campus, and
SKSU-ACCESS in Tacurong City, shall be headed by Campus Directors who
shall render full-time services and be appointed or designated by the Board upon
the recommendation of the search committee and the President of the
University. Such appointment or designation shall be subject to the guidelines,
qualifications, and standards set by the Board.

Section 5. The establishment of the SKSU-Bagumbayan Campus, SKSU-
Columbio Campus, SKSU Esperanza Campus, SKSU-Lambayong Campus,
SKSU-Lebak Campus, SKSU-President Quirino Campus, SKSU-Senator
Ninoy Aquino Campus, and SKSU-ACCESS in Tacurong City shall ensure that
the minimum requirements for their operation and program offerings have been
complied with, as duly prescribed by the Commission on Higher Education.

Section 6. All assets, real and personal, personnel, and records of the SKSU-
Bagumbayan Campus, SKSU-Columbio Campus, SKSU-Esperanza Campus,
SKSU-Lambayong Campus, SKSU-Lebak Campus, SKSU-President Quirino
Campus, SKSU-Senator Ninoy Aquino Campus, and SKSU-ACCESS in
Tacurong City as well as liabilities or obligations, are hereby transferred to the
University. The positions, rights, and security of tenure of faculty members and
personnel employed at the SKSU-Bagumbayan Campus, SKSU-Columbio
Campus, SKSU-Esperanza Campus, SKSU-Lambayong Campus, SKSU-Lebak
Campus, SKSU-President Quirino Campus, SKSU-Senator Ninoy Aquino
Campus, and SKSU-ACCESS in Tacurong City under existing laws prior to the
integration to the University shall be respected.

All parcels of land belonging to the government occupied by the SKSU-
Bagumbayan Campus, SKSU-Columbio Campus, SKSU Esperanza Campus,

SKSU- Lambayong Campus, SKSU-Lebak Campus, SKSU-President Quirino
Campus, SKSU-Senator Ninoy Aquino Campus, and SKSU-ACCESS in
Tacurong City are hereby declared to be the property of the University and shall

be abolished or should such aforementioned parcels of land to be no longer
needed by the University, the same shall revert to the concerned local
government unit or the Republic of the Philippines, as the case may be.

Section 7. The provisions of Republic Act No. 8292, otherwise known as the
"Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997", shall form part of this Act, and
together, shall serve as the governing charter of the University.

Section 8. All other powers, functions, privileges, responsibilities, and
limitations to state universities and their officials under existing laws shall be
deemed granted to or imposed upon the University and its officials, whenever
appropriate.

Section 9. The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

Section 10. If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall
remain in full force and effect.

Section 11. All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
repealed or modified accordingly.

Section 12. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in newspaper of general circulation.
