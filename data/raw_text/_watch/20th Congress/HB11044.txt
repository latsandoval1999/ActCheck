DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT ACT OF 2010

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to harness their power and ensure the participation and entry of the
youth sector, especially those who are no longer members of the Sangguniang
Kabataan, in the country’s disaster risk reduction and management system.

Considering the country’s vulnerability to natural disasters, especially with the
impacts of climate change, it is important that all sectors, especially the majority of
the population who are at the prime of their lives and technologically aware, are
reminded and legally mandated to participate in and play an important role in
disaster risk reduction.

Speci ically, the bill mandates the inclusion of the Chairperson of the National Youth
Commission as member of the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management
Council (NDRRMC). Individuals and Youth organizations are likewise mandated to be
included in all organizational levels of the disaster risk reduction and management
system, in order to afford them a venue to participate and engage with government
of icials and community leaders in formulating, planning and programming activities.

SECTION 12. Non-impairment Clause. — Nothing in this Act shall be construed as
to diminish, impair, or repeal rights recognized, granted, or made available to
disadvantaged, marginalized, or speci ic sectors under existing laws.

SECTION 13. Separability Clause. — If any part, section or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such
declaration shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 15. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
