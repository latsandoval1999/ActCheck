                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The necessity for reform in the system of benefits for the military and
uniformed personnel has never been more urgent in light of the unexpected
spending brought about by the ongoing war in Gulf area.

      The reality of the budgetary burden should of course not turn a blind eye on
our Constitutional duty to ensure professionalism in the service and adequate
remuneration and benefits of its members. Reformation in the system of benefits is
an appropriate response in recognition of the invaluable role of the military and
uniformed personnel in protecting the nation and its citizens from various threats.

       This bill intends to find a balance between the existing ballooning financial
burden of meeting the benefits to uniformed personnel and ensuring provision on
adequate remuneration and benefits to them. It is after all the State’s constitutional
duty to, from time to time, review the pensions and other benefits due to retirees of
both the government and the private sectors.

      Accordingly, this bill primarily intends to establish a Uniformed Personnel Trust
Fund Committee in order to establish a system of contributions that would ensure the
continuity of the benefits.

      The immediate passage of this bill is most earnestly sought.

19          SEC. 44. Penal Clause. – Any person who participates directly or
20   indirectly in the commission of fraud, falsification, misrepresentation of facts,
21   collusion or any similar anomaly in the issuance of any certificate or document for
22   any purpose connected with this Act shall, upon conviction, be punished by a fine
23   of not less than the amount defrauded but not more than threefold of such amount
24   or imprisonment of not less than six (6) months and one (1) day to six (6) years
25   and perpetual disqualification from holding public office and from practicing any
26   profession licensed by the government: Provided, That a conviction or acquittal
27   obtained under this section shall not be a bar to the filing of an administrative,
28   civil, or criminal case under existing laws.
