                                         EXPLANATORY NOTE


       This bill seeks to amend Republic Act (RA) No. 11350, otherwise known as the “National
Commission of Senior Citizens Act”, providing for its efficient transition from the Department of Social
Welfare and Development (DSWD) through a more coherent organizational structure, establishing clear
mechanisms for its effective operationalization and clarifying institutional arrangements to ensure
maximum functionality.
         RA 11350 or the National Commission of Senior Citizens Act was passed as a response to the
decades-long clamor from the elderly sector that there finally be a primary government agency focused on
their rights, interests and welfare. While RA 7432 providing for the benefits and privileges of senior citizens
was twice amended, first by RA 9257 and then by the prevailing RA 9994, there was a need for a law
establishing organizational structures and institutional arrangements that will support mechanisms
operationalizing elderly programs and services.
        Unfortunately, since the RA 11350’s passage in 2019, the efficient transition from the DSWD to
the NCSC has not happened. Moreover, even with the appointment of Commissioners starting in 2020, the
NCSC continues to face challenges in assuming the full responsibilities and functions of the NCSC
pertaining to the senior citizens’ sector. It is for this reason that the law needs to be amended to provide
more guidance and clearer directions on the law’s original intention, thereby ensuring that the elderly sector
does not suffer the consequences of an inefficient and non-functional body.
        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly recommended.

    AN ACT AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11350, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
    “NATIONAL COMMISSION OF SENIOR CITIZENS ACT”, PROVIDING FOR ITS
         ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE, EFFICIENT TRANSITION AND
 OPERATIONALIZATION, AND MAXIMIZING ITS FUNCTIONALITY FOR THE BENEFIT
                          OF SENIOR CITIZENS


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress Assembled


SECTION 1. Section (3) of RA 11350 on the creation of the National Commission of Senior Citizens
(NCSC) is hereby amended to read as follows:

        “SEC. 3. Creation of the National Commission of Senior Citizens.- There is hereby created the
        National Commission of Senior Citizens, hereinafter referred to as the Commission, under the
        Office of the President, with the Department of Social Welfare and Development having oversight
        function. The Commission shall establish its principal office in the National Capital Region, and
        shall set up regional and provincial field offices.


SECTION 2. Section (4) on the Composition of the NCSC is hereby amended to read as follows:

        “SEC. 4. Composition.- The Commission shall be composed of a Chairperson and six (6)
        Commissioners and shall act as a collegial body and shall decide by consensus all substantive
        matters pertaining to NCSC functions, including hiring and appointment of staff. The Chairperson
        shall be the Chief Executive Officer of the Commission. The six (6) Commissioners shall represent
        different geographical regions of the Philippines, equally covering the major Luzon, Visayas, and
        Mindanao clusters, and shall meet at least once every MONTH, or as need arises.”


SECTION 3. Section (5) on the Term of Office of the Chairperson and Commissioners is hereby amended
to read as follows:

       “SEC. 5. Term of Office – The Chairperson and Commissioners shall have a term of office of six
(6) years, strictly WITHOUT REAPPOINTMENT but without prejudice to a maximum extension of one
(1) year by the President if exigency so requires, and only to facilitate efficient transition within the
Commission in a clear HOLD-OVER CAPACITY.


SECTION 4. Section (6) on the Qualifications of the Commissioners which shall be the basis for
appointment is hereby amended to read as follows:

        “SEC. 6. Qualifications.— The members of the Commission must be citizens and residents of the
        Philippines; at least sixty (60) years old at the time of their appointment; of good moral character;
        bona fide members of legitimate senior citizens associations (duly-registered and accredited with
        the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), or Cooperative Development Authority (CDA),
        or DSWD, or LGU-registered or NCSC-RECOGNIZED ) with a good track record (specifically at
        least three (3) consecutive years of active, consistent, and documented operation of effective
        program delivery that promote and protect the health, well-being, and social integration of seniors
        and representing their interest in local or national communities, and a positive standing in their
        community) ; have relevant experience with matters affecting the affairs of senior citizens; of good
       health and sound mind; have not been convicted by a final judgment of a crime involving moral
       turpitude; There shall be at least three (3) Commissioners women.


       The above-mentioned Qualifications shall be the basis for appointment and hence, “Relevant
       experience, extensive knowledge and expertise on senior citizens affairs” as important qualification
       requirements refers to familiarity with laws, policies and programs affecting senior citizens,
       longtime active membership in a legitimate senior citizen organization, previous leadership
       position or official representation in a board or committee organized by the DSWD, NAPC, or local
       government unit, and prior participation in advocacies for elderly rights and welfare.”


SECTION 5. Section (7) on the Functions of the NCSC is hereby amended to read as follows:
     SEC. 7. Functions. -The Commission shall have the authority :

   (a) Ensure the full implementation and uniform enforcement of laws, policies, and programs of the
       government pertaining to senior citizens; Review and conduct studies on the same; and formulate,
       develop, and advocate for new policies and responsive legislation for the promotion and protection
       of the rights and well-being of senior citizens;
   (b) Perform such relevant quasi-judicial functions in resolving certain issues and concerns encountered
       in the course of its policy-making and program implementation, and acting as an arbiter amongst
       duty-registered and recognized stakeholders;
   (c) Develop and establish an effective Monitoring and Reporting System, including a Complaints
       Handling Mechanism, to ensure the efficient, consistent and uniform implementation of all relevant
       laws, programs and services, especially those under Republic Act No. 9994;
   (d) Coordinate and evaluate the programs, projects and activities of government agencies especially
       those with responsibilities under RA No. 9994 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations; Call
       upon and utilize any department, bureau, office, agency or instrumentality, public, private or
       voluntary, for such assistance to ensure proper implementation of all other senior citizens laws;
   (e) Develop an integrated and comprehensive long-term National Plan for the social protection of
       senior citizens at all levels, including establishing and maintaining a National Database on the
       Profiling and Referral system of senior citizens;
   (f) Raise a level of public awareness on the rights and welfare of senior citizens through the conduct
       of nationwide, strategic information, education, and communication (IEC) programs or campaigns;
   (g) Initiate and conduct regular orientations, trainings, and other capability-building programs and
       activities on RA 9994 implementation and other senior citizens laws and programs like the
       Centenarian Act and the Senior Citizens Center Act;
   (h) Coordinate the conduct and evaluation of the Philippine Plan of Action for Senior Citizens
       (PPASC), related researches and documentation of Good Practices, gaps and disparities for policy
       and program development; Develop and institute effective and innovative approaches and methods
       with which to address emerging concerns of the senior citizens;
   (i) Establish and maintain cooperation and consultations with local government units and national
       government agencies on all matters pertaining to the general welfare of senior citizens;
   (j) Establish, maintain and expand partnerships, linkages and other networks with national, regional
       and international organizations working with senior citizens to optimize resource generation,
       technical cooperation and exchange; Initiate consultative dialogues, special meetings, relevant fora
       and symposia to support elderly advocacies and programs;
   (k) Represent the Philippines in international functions, fora, or conferences on senior citizens and
       keep abreast with international standards of human rights, latest developments in social protection,
       and other emerging trends and issues affecting senior citizens;
   (l) Prepare yearly accomplishment report for the Office of the President and Congress, and any other
       concerned national government and local government units;
   (m) Constitute and convene when necessary, a special advisory body to be composed of such
       representatives from the government, nongovernment organizations, civil society, and the private
       sector, as the Commission may deem appropriate, to assist in the performance of its functions; Act
       as a consultative forum and coordinating body to rationalize the functions and activities of
       government agencies involved in the social protection of senior citizens;
   (n) Develop appropriate mechanisms and procedures for the selection and nomination of the members
       of the Commission for appointment by the President; and
   (o) Perform such other actions and functions for the promotion of the welfare of senior citizens, as
       may be directed by the President.


SECTION 6. Section (8) on the Organizational Structure of the NCSC is hereby amended to read as
follows:

       SEC. 8. Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern. - The Commission shall have a more
       streamlined organizational and operational structure with staffing patterns, offices, divisions, or
units based on the responsibilities and functions it shall also absorb and take over from the DSWD
and such other elderly-related concerns and emerging trends.


(a) The Commission as a Collegial Body. The National Commission of Senior Citizens,
hereinafter referred to as the Commission, shall exercise its substantive policymaking, decision-
making, and implementing powers as a collegial body. It shall be composed of a Chairperson,
who shall serve as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), and six (6) Commissioners representing
distinct geographical regional clusters across the Philippines.

(b) The Secretariat. There is hereby established a Secretariat to serve as the executive and
administrative arm of the Commission, to be headed by an Executive Director. The Executive
Director shall be responsible for the implementation of policies, rules, regulations, and directives
promulgated by the Commission.

(c) Technical and Operational Bureaus. The Secretariat shall operate through specialized
technical divisions, which shall initially include:

    1. Program Operations and Planning Bureau: Tasked with sector-specific project
    conceptualization, policy translation, monitoring, and database management; and

    2. Wellness and Health, Emergency Response, and Benefits Division (WHEREBED):
    Tasked with designing health frameworks, emergency relief operations, and targeted senior
    citizen social safety nets.

(d) Field Operations. To ensure the localized delivery of programs, the Commission shall establish
and maintain:

     1. Regional Offices (ROs): Operating in strategic geographical clusters, each headed by a
     Regional Director responsible for regional execution and program monitoring;

     2. Provincial Offices (POs): Operating in each province, headed by a Provincial Director
     responsible for provincial execution and program monitoring;

     2. Coordination with Local Government Units: The Commission, through its Regional
     Offices and Provincial Offices, shall maintain direct operational links with the Office of the
     Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) in every city and municipality to manage grass- roots
     database registration and community welfare drives.

     Plantilla Positions for OSCA Head shall be created and funded under the NCSC while the
     Local Chief Executives (LCEs) maintain the authority to select and appoint the OSCA Head.

     In addition, the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) created under Republic Act No.
     7432, as amended by RA 9257 and RA 9994, and lodged with cities and municipalities, shall
     be staffed with senior citizen personnel under Job Orders or Memorandum of Agreement
     (MOA) contracts compliant with Civil Service Commission (CSC) and Department of Budget
     and Management (DBM) rules.

(e) National Advisory Body. The Commission shall constitute a regular National Advisory Body
composed of ex-officio representatives from PhilHealth, the Social Security System (SSS), the
Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), and accredited senior citizens’ non-government
organizations (NGOs) to act as a consultative and rationalizing panel.

(f) Approval of Staffing Details. Subject to the evaluation and approval of the Department of
Budget and Management, the Commission shall determine the detailed staffing pattern, position
classifications, and salary grades of its personnel in accordance with existing civil service laws.


The NCSC shall have an organizational structure that will absorb all related functions from DSWD
mentioned in Section 10 (Abolition of the NCMB) and Section 11 (Transfer of Functions from
DSWD). As such, there shall be a counterpart office/division/unit with appropriate staffing
requirements for the following purposes:

To ensure the re-activation of all functions and responsibilities being handled by the former
National Coordinating and Monitoring Board (NCMB) and its Technical Secretariat, especially
those pertaining to RA 9994 implementation, monitoring and reporting;
       To ensure continued operations of various sectoral programs and services especially the Social
       Pension Program, the Centenarian award/benefit, and financial assistance/AICS under the DSWD
       Programs Management Bureau (PMB);

       For Elderly Group Homes, Residential Care Facilities and other centers for senior citizens operated
       and monitored by the DSWD Programs Management Bureau, and those privately-operated;

       For the regulation, licensing and accreditation requirements being enforced by the DSWD
       Standards Bureau for Non-Government Organizations (NGOs) and local government units (LGUs)
       recognizing senior citizens groups and associations, as well as People’s Organizations (POs) or
       private organizations catering to older persons, or operating and maintaining elderly centers and
       facilities, including the mandated Senior Citizens Center under RA 7876;

       For the development of social welfare relevant interventions that will address elderly concerns,
       including the piloting and marketing of new technology programs.

       For administrative and finance matters, as well human resources/personnel and procurement
       concerns, the necessary administrative support office shall be established; while a division for
       information technology and communications systems shall be included in this office for technical,
       data, and information system support.


SECTION 6. Section (9) on the appointment of the Executive Director is hereby amended to read as
follows:

       SEC. 9. Executive Director. -The Commission shall recommend appropriate candidates to the
       Office of the President (OP) for appointment of an Executive Director in accordance with civil
       service laws, rules and regulations, and shall be responsible for implementing the policies, rules,
       regulations and directives of the Commission; the management of the Commission’s daily
       operations; and for coordinating and supervising the activities of the different offices, divisions,
       and units of the Commission.


       The Executive Director shall be a Career Executive Service Officer, and shall have an educational
       background in any of the following fields: law; economics; commerce; management; social
       services; or finance. The Executive Director should shall have at least five (5) years of relevant
       experience, knowledge and expertise in senior citizens’ policies, programs and services.

       The Executive Director shall have a term of office of six (6) years co-terminus with the
       Chairperson.


SECTION 7. Section (10) on the abolition of the National Coordinating and Monitoring Board
(NCMB) is hereby amended to read as follows:

       SEC. 10-A. Abolition of the National Coordinating and Monitoring Board. -The National
       Coordinating and Monitoring Board created by virtue of Republic Act No. 9994, otherwise known
       as the "Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010", is hereby abolished. Provided, that all the previous
       functions, duties and responsibilities of the NCMB are fully absorbed and taken over by the NCSC
       to prevent a gap or vacuum in the implementation and monitoring of Republic Act No. 9994, the
       primary senior citizens law granting benefits and privileges. These shall include the following:

       a)    Regularly review the status of implementation of RA 9994 benefits and privileges of senior
            citizens under the different categories of businesses or industries, taking note of any emerging
            problems or confusing gray areas that need clarification;
       b)   Coordinate with other relevant government agencies to resolve specific policy questions and
            for the issuance and updating of necessary supplemental guidelines, rules or regulations to help
            implement RA 9994 provisions;
       c)     Discuss and cooperate with private sector representatives, including different businesses and
            industry associations, to clarify implementation issues and ensure uniform application of RA
            9994 privileges;
       d)    Provide regular capability-building and training for Officer of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)
            Heads who are tasked to receive complaints for violations of RA 9994 especially non-availment
            of discount privileges, as well as officers of the Federation of Senior Citizens Associations of
            the Philippines (FSCAP) and other senior citizens organizations who are entitled to be updated
            on developments;
       e)    Maintain and update the NCMB webpage and online resources page, as well as the official
            NCMB email and query hotline;
        f)   Compile and consolidate relevant materials and documentations on RA 9994, the Social
           Pension Program, the Centenarians Act, and other elderly laws and programs
        g) Discuss and resolve issues and concerns pertaining to RA 9994 benefits availment, complaints
           and violations, and issue clarificatory NCSC inter-agency Resolutions for guidance;

There shall be established NCSC Regional Offices and Provincial Offices where the respective
Commissioners shall hold office. To help operationalize these offices, the Institutional Arrangements at the
Regional Level shall be maintained as follows under Section 10-B to read as follows:

        SEC. 10-B - The former Regional Inter-Agency Committee for Senior Citizens (RIASC) and
        Regional Coordinating and Monitoring Body (RCMBs) organized by the DSWD Field Offices shall
        be reactivated and maintained as implementing and monitoring arms at the regional level for the
        NCSC, and shall regularly coordinate with the designated Commissioner with oversight over said
        area. The previous Terms of Reference (TOR) governing the RIASCs and RCMBs may be amended
        and updated to address any changes as may be called for.


        SEC. 10-C. - An Inter-Agency Advisory Committee composed of relevant national line agencies
        formerly tasked with implementing and monitoring RA 9994 shall be created to assist the NCSC
        in its functions. The National Commission for Senior Citizens (NCSC), shall coordinate with other
        relevant government agencies like the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD),
        the Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), the Department of Health (DOH), the
        Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI),
        the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), the Department of Labor
        and Employment (DOLE), the Department of Agriculture (DA), the Department of Transportation
        (DOTr), the Department of Justice (DOJ), the Commission on Human Rights (CHRP), the National
        Economic Development Authority (NEDA), the Department of Education (DepEd), the
        Commission on Higher Education (CHED), the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA) or other
        government agencies and local government units, which shall be tasked to facilitate related
        programs and services to the senior citizens shall assist in the effective implementation of this Act
        and provide the necessary support services.”


SECTION 8. Section (11) on the Transfer of Functions from the DSWD is hereby amended to read as
follows:

        SEC. 11. Transfer of Functions from the Department of Social Welfare and Development
        (DSWD) to the National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC). -All functions, programs,
        projects and activities which are being undertaken by the Department of Social Welfare and
        Development (DSWD) for the formulation, implementation, and coordination of social welfare and
        development (SWD) policies and programs for the poor, vulnerable and disadvantaged senior
        citizens shall be fully transitioned and transferred to the National Commission of Senior Citizens
        (NCSC).


SECTION 9. Section (12) On Appropriations of RA 11350 shall be amended to read as follows:

        “SEC. 12-A. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to fund the implementation of this Act shall
        be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 12-B Donations. – The Commission shall be authorized to receive grants, donations,
        contributions or gifts which will be administered or disbursed for the purpose of which they were
        given, such as for DSWD-run senior citizens centers and residential group homes, or in the absence
        of such, as the Commission may determine. Such donation shall be exempt from donor’s tax in
        accordance with the provisions of the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC) of 1997. In all cases,
        government rules and regulations shall apply.



SECTION 10. Oversight – The NCSC, while retaining its operational independence, shall be under the
Oversight of the Office of the President. and shall regularly report to the Office of the President (OP) and
the respective Senate and House Committees tackling senior citizens’ concerns.


SECTION 11. Section (13) on the Implementing Rules and Regulations of this Act is hereby amended
to read as follows:
        “Sec. 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within one hundred eighty (180) days from
        the approval of this Act, the National Commission for Senior Citizens (NCSC) in coordination with
        the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), and proper consultation with the
        Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Civil Service Commission (CSC), the
        Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), the League of City Mayors of the
        Philippines (LCP), League of Municipal Mayors of the Philippines (LMP) and Union of Local
        Authorities of the Philippines (ULAP), shall issue the rules and regulations to effectively
        implement the provisions of this Act. Any violation of this section shall render the concerned
        official(s) liable under Republic Act No. 6713, otherwise known as the "Code of Conduct and
        Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees" and other existing administrative and/or
        criminal laws.”
SECTION 12. Separability Clause – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, other provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 13. Repealing Clause – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules or regulations contrary to or
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
SECTION 14. Effectivity – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official
Gazette or in newspaper of general circulation.


Approved
