                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Many public schools in the Philippines operate on land that has never been formally
titled in the name of the government, despite being used as school sites for decades.
This lack of clear ownership creates legal uncertainty, delays infrastructure projects,
and exposes schools to the risk of land disputes, hampering the delivery of safe and
adequate learning environments for students.

This bill provides a simple and summary administrative process to transfer and
register titles of lands that have been continuously used as public school sites for at
least ive years in the name of the Department of Education. By avoiding lengthy court
proceedings, the measure ensures faster, more ef icient titling while safeguarding the
State’s interest in protecting public school properties.

By securing legal ownership of school sites, this measure strengthens the foundation
of public education. It enables timely school improvements, protects public assets,
and ensures that school lands are preserved solely for educational purposes,
ultimately bene iting learners, teachers, and communities nationwide.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

                               AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE ADMINISTRATIVE AND SUMMARY TITLING OF REAL
PROPERTIES USED AS SITES FOR PUBLIC SCHOOLS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Purpose of this act. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
provide all lands utilized as public school sites with titles under the name of the
Department of Education to enable it to have legal ownership over these school sites.

SECTION 2. Mandatory Titling of School Sites. — Titles to real properties, public or
private, including those owned or possessed by the national, provincial, city or
municipal governments which are being utilized as actual sites for public schools for
a period of at least ive (5) years shall be transferred to the Department of Education
by simple motion iled by the Of ice of the Solicitor General or by the City or Provincial
Prosecutor.

The proceedings shall be administrative and summary in nature and the decision shall
be inal and executory and not subject to appeal except in a petition for certiorari to
the Supreme Court.

SECTION 3. Registration of Land Titles. — The Registry of Deeds concerned shall
forthwith register the properties as provided herein and issue the corresponding
certi icate of title to the Department of Education.

SECTION 4. Rules and Regulations. — The Department of Justice, the Department
of Education, the Commission on Higher Education and the Technical Education and
Skills Development Authority shall jointly promulgate the implementing rules and
regulations within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act.

SECTION 5. Appropriations. — Funds necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be charged to the appropriations for the Department of Education under the
General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 6. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared

force and effect.

SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, issuances or portions

SECTION 8. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
