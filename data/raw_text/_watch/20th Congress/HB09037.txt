                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Plastic waste remains one of the most pressing environmental
challenges facing communities across the country. Despite existing laws
regulating solid waste management, the widespread use of single-use plastic
bags known as “labo” and “sando” bags continue to strain waste collection
systems, clog waterways and pollute our communities.

      The degradation of our environment due to single-use plastics is
clearly seen in our local communities. Local government units (LGUs) bear
the primary responsibility for solid waste management and many face fiscal
and logistical constraints in fully complying with these national environmental
standards.

      This bill proposes the imposition of an excise tax of Twenty Pesos
(₱20.00) per kilogram on plastic bags removed from the place of production
or released from the custody of the Bureau of Customs. By imposing an
excise tax at the point of production or importation, this bill encourages
manufacturers, retailers, and consumers to shift toward more sustainable
and reusable alternatives, thereby reducing plastic waste at its source.
       The 1987 Constitution mandates the State to protect and advance the
right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accordance with
the rhythm and harmony of nature. In discharging this mandate, this policy
measure seeks to curb the proliferation of single-use plastic bags, which
significantly contribute to pollution, flooding, and the degradation of terrestrial
and marine ecosystems.

       Moreover, the revenues collected from the excise tax for single-use
plastic bags shall be allocated exclusively to local government units (LGUs)
for the implementation of their solid waste management plans. These funds
may be used for the acquisition of land, construction and operation of
sanitary landfill, and other waste management facilities. By providing a
sustainable and earmarked funding mechanism, this measure reinforces the
partnership between the national government and LGUs, enhances local
capacity to protect public health and the environment, and empowers
communities to build cleaner, more resilient, and environmentally
responsible localities.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

16                              AN ACT
17   IMPOSING EXCISE TAX ON PLASTIC BAGS, THEREBY ADDING NEW
18   SECTIONS 150-C AND 288(H) IN THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE
19                    CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED

20         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
21   Philippines in Congress assembled:

22

23        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Plastic Bags
24   Tax Act."

25         SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the
26   State to protect and advance the right of the people to a balanced and
27   healthful ecology in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature through
28   the proper exercise of its power of taxation.

29         SEC. 3. A new section designated as Section 150-C of the National
30   Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended, is hereby added to read as
31   follows:
32             "SEC. 150-C. PLASTIC BAGS. -

33            (A) RATE AND BASE OF TAX. - THERE SHALL BE LEVIED,
34        ASSESSED, AND COLLECTED AN EXCISE TAX IN THE AMOUNT
35        OF TWENTY PESOS (PHP20.00) FOR EVERY KILOGRAM OF
36        PLASTIC BAG REMOVED FROM THE PLACE OF PRODUCTION OR
37        RELEASED FROM THE CUSTODY OF THE BUREAU OF
38        CUSTOMS.

39            PLASTIC BAGS SHALL REFER TO SECONDARY LEVEL
40        PLASTICS MADE OF SYNTHETIC OR SEMISYNTHETIC ORGANIC
41        POLYMER, COMMONLY KNOWN AS "LABO" OR "SANDO" BAGS,
42        WITH OR WITHOUT HANDLE, USED AS PACKAGING FOR GOODS
43        OR PRODUCTS.

44            (B) FILING OF RETURN AND PAYMENT OF EXCISE TAX ON
45        PLASTIC BAGS. - THE PROVISIONS OF SECTIONS 130 AND 131
46        OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS
47        AMENDED, ON THE FILING OF RETURN AND PAYMENT OF
48        EXCISE TAX SHALL APPLY.

49            (C) NON-PAYMENT OF EXCISE TAX - NON-PAYMENT OF
50        THE EXCISE TAX IMPOSED UNDER THIS ACT SHALL BE
51        SUBJECT TO THE PERTINENT PROVISIONS UNDER TITLES VIII
52        AND X OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997,
53        AS AMENDED, REGARDING COLLECTION OF UNPAID TAXES
54        AND IMPOSITION OF CORRESPONDING FINES, SURCHARGES,
55        PENALTIES, AND IMPRISONMENT OR CLOSURE OF BUSINESS."

56        SEC. 4. Section 288 of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997,
57   as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

58             "SEC. 288. Disposition of Incremental AND OTHER Revenues.
59        -

60             (A)   XXX

67               (H) REVENUES FROM THE EXCISE TAX ON PLASTIC BAG. -

68               ONE HUNDRED PERCENT (100%) OF THE REVENUES
69               COLLECTED FROM THE EXCISE TAX ON PLASTIC BAGS

77               MANAGEMENT ACT OF 2000’.”

78         SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60)
79   days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Finance shall, upon the
80   recommendation of the Commissioners of the Bureau of Internal Revenue
81   and the Bureau of Customs and, in consultation with the Department of
82   Environment and Natural Resources, issue and promulgate rules and
83   regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

84        SEC. 6. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act shall be held
85   unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not otherwise affected shall
86   remain in full force and effect.

87         SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders,
88   rules, and regulations or parts hereof which are contrary to or inconsistent
89   with any provision of this Act are hereby repealed, modified, or amended
90   accordingly.

91         SEC. 8. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
92   its publication either in the Official Gazette or in at least one (1) newspaper
93   of general circulation.
