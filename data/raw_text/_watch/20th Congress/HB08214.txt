                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

A modern, resilient, and well-integrated road network is indispensable to inclusive growth,
especially in geographically dispersed and resource-rich provinces. The State, therefore, prioritizes
the upgrading of critical local roads that function as economic lifelines but remain constrained by
limited local resources.

The Claveria–San Isidro–Peñafrancia Road, located in the Province of Masbate, is presently under
the administrative jurisdiction of the concerned Local Government Units. Despite its local
classification, the road performs a function that is undeniably national in character. It interconnects
multiple barangays and municipalities, serving as a principal corridor for the transport of
agricultural produce, fishery resources, and other goods that sustain both local markets and inter -
island trade. This road likewise plays a strategic role in ensuring access to essential public services.
It connects communities to schools, rural health units, government centers, and commercial hubs,
thereby directly influencing the quality of life of residents.

Moreover, it serves as a vital access route during emergencies, including disaster response and
evacuation operations, particularly in a province frequently exposed to typhoons and other natural
hazards. Notwithstanding its importance, the Claveria–San Isidro–Peñafrancia Road remains
inadequately developed. Several segments are narrow, poorly paved, or susceptible to damage
during adverse weather conditions. The financial limitations of the Local Government Units
concerned have hindered the implementation of comprehensive and sustained infrastructure
improvements, resulting in inefficiencies in transport, increased travel time, and higher costs for
goods and services.

The conversion of this road into a national road will vest its administration, planning, and
maintenance in the National Government through the Department of Public Works and Highways
(DPWH). Such reclassification will facilitate the infusion of national funding, the application of
higher engineering standards, and the execution of long-term development plans, including
widening, paving, and climate-resilient upgrades.
More importantly, the proposed measure is expected to unlock the economic potential of the
affected areas. By improving road conditions and connectivity, it will enhance market access for
farmers and fisherfolk, encourage investments, promote tourism, and generate employment
opportunities. It will also strengthen regional integration within Masbate and improve linkage to
nearby provinces. In essence, the conversion of the Claveria–San Isidro–Peñafrancia Road into a
national road is not merely a matter of administrative reclassification, but a strategic intervention
aimed at fostering equitable development, improving public welfare, and building resilient
communities.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT CONVERTING THE CLAVERIA–SAN ISIDRO–PEÑAFRANCIA ROAD,
PROVINCE OF MASBATE, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING
FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. The Claveria–San Isidro–Peñafrancia Road in the Municipality of Claveria, Province
of Masbate is hereby converted into a National Road under the jurisdiction and supervision of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

SECTION 2. The technical description of the road shall be determined by the Engineering District
Office and the Secretary of Public Works and Highways.

SECTION 3. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the Department’s
program the improvement, repair and maintenance of the said road, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 4. All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations contrary to or
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the official gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
