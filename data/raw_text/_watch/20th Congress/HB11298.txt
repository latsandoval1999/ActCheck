      AN ACT REGULATING ADVERTISEMENTS ON GAMBLING-RELATED
          ACTIVITIES, PROVIDING LIMITATIONS AND SAFEGUARDS,
                       AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Gambling has become increasingly accessible and visible to the public due to
the rapid expansion of digital platforms, online gaming services, social media, and
other forms of electronic communication. While gambling activities may be subject to
existing laws and regulations, the manner and extent to which gambling-related
activities are advertised and promoted present emerging concerns that warrant
appropriate legislative action.

       In the Philippines, gambling has long been part of the country's entertainment
landscape. However, the rapid growth of online gambling, coupled with the widespread
use of smartphones, social media, streaming services, and other digital platforms, has
transformed the way gambling is promoted and accessed. Gambling-related
advertisements can now reach Filipinos directly and repeatedly through the digital
content they consume, including content viewed by minors.

       The increasing prevalence of gambling advertisements may contribute to the
normalization of gambling as an ordinary form of entertainment or, more concerningly,
as a means of earning money or addressing financial difficulties. This is particularly
troubling for Filipino families and individuals who may be financially vulnerable and
more susceptible to advertisements portraying gambling as a quick or easy path to
financial gain.

       The use of celebrities, athletes, influencers, content creators, and other public
personalities in gambling promotions may further enhance the appeal and perceived
legitimacy of gambling, particularly among younger audiences. Similarly, gambling
promotions integrated into sporting events, entertainment programs, social media
content, and other popular forms of media can substantially increase public exposure
to gambling-related messages.
       Of particular concern are advertisements that downplay the risks of gambling
or create misleading impressions regarding the likelihood of winning. Representations
that gambling can provide a reliable source of income, achieve financial security, or
solve financial problems may encourage irresponsible gambling. Promotional offers,
bonuses, and rewards may likewise be misleading when material terms and risks are
not clearly disclosed.

        The State therefore has a legitimate interest in ensuring that gambling-related
advertisements are truthful, transparent, and responsibly presented, while protecting
minors and vulnerable persons from exploitative or deceptive marketing practices.
Regulation of gambling advertising does not seek to prohibit lawful gambling activities,
but to ensure that their promotion is consistent with consumer protection and the public
welfare.

        This measure seeks to establish clear and comprehensive standards for
gambling-related advertising and promotion in both traditional and digital media. It
covers advertisements, sponsorships, endorsements, influencer and affiliate
marketing, branded content, and other analogous commercial communications. It
likewise provides for appropriate age-assurance measures, responsible gambling
messages, restrictions on advertising directed toward minors, and safeguards against
misleading claims concerning winnings, financial returns, bonuses, and promotional
offers.

       The bill further promotes a whole-of-society approach to responsible gambling
by providing for continuing public awareness and education on the risks of excessive,
compulsive, and irresponsible gambling and on available counseling, treatment,
rehabilitation, and support services.

       Ultimately, this measure seeks to foster a safer and more responsible
advertising environment in the Philippines, balancing legitimate commercial interests
with the State's responsibility to protect Filipino consumers, families, minors, and other
vulnerable persons from harmful, deceptive, and irresponsible gambling promotion.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

      AN ACT REGULATING ADVERTISEMENTS ON GAMBLING-RELATED
          ACTIVITIES, PROVIDING LIMITATIONS AND SAFEGUARDS,
                       AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled.

       Section 1. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to protect the
public, particularly minors and vulnerable persons, from deceptive, misleading,
excessive, and irresponsible advertising and promotion of gambling-related activities.

      The State recognizes that while gambling activities may be lawfully conducted
pursuant to existing laws, licenses, franchises, or regulatory authority, their advertising
and promotion must likewise be subject to reasonable standards designed to protect
consumers and promote responsible gambling.

       Accordingly, the State shall establish clear and uniform standards governing
gambling-related advertisements and promotional activities across traditional, digital,
online, and emerging forms of media.

        Section 2. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:

        (a) Advertisement refers to any communication, material, representation,
        announcement, or message disseminated through television, radio, print,
        outdoor media, websites, mobile applications, social media, streaming
        platforms, electronic communications, or any other medium for the
        purpose of promoting, directly or indirectly, a gambling-related activity,
        operator, product, service, or facility;
(b) Consumer Communication shall refer to any commercial
communication, whether direct or indirect, disseminated through mass
media, print and outdoor advertising, mobile applications, social media
platforms, e-commerce platforms, and other such digital platforms or
analogous means of communication accessible to the general public,
which publicizes, promotes, markets, or otherwise influences or
encourages consumer awareness, patronage, participation in, or use of a
product, service, activity, operator, establishment, or platform. For the
purpose of this Act, consumer communications shall include
advertisements, promotions, sponsorships, marketing, and other
analogous commercial communications.

(c) Gambling-related activity refers to any game, scheme, activity, or
undertaking involving the staking or risking of money or anything of value
upon the outcome of a game, contest, event, or other uncertain
occurrence, where the participant stands to receive money or anything of
value, as may be authorized or regulated under applicable law;

(d) Gambling operator refers to any person, corporation, partnership,
association, entity, or other organization duly authorized by law or by the
appropriate government authority to conduct or offer gambling-related
activities;

(e) Gambling advertisement refers to any advertisement that directly or
indirectly promotes or encourages participation in a gambling-related
activity;

(f) Promotional activity refers to any campaign, endorsement, sponsorship,
bonus, incentive, contest, event, influencer campaign, affiliate marketing
arrangement, or other activity intended to encourage or facilitate
participation in a gambling-related activity;

(g) Minor refers to a person below eighteen (18) years of age;

(h) Influencer refers to any individual who uses a social media account,
website, streaming platform, or other digital platform to disseminate
content to an audience and who receives compensation, consideration,
commission, benefit, or other valuable consideration for promoting or
endorsing a gambling-related activity;

(i) Vulnerable person refers to a person who, by reason of age, financial
circumstances, or other circumstances recognized by applicable law or
regulation, may be particularly susceptible to the harmful effects of
gambling; and
       (j) Responsible gambling message refers to a clear and conspicuous
       notice informing the public that gambling involves risk and that gambling
       should be undertaken only by persons legally permitted to do so and within
       their means.

       Section 3. Scope of Application. – This Act shall apply to all gambling-related
advertisements and promotional activities disseminated or made accessible within the
Philippines, regardless of whether such advertisements originate from within or
outside the country.

      This Act shall apply to gambling operators, advertisers, advertising agencies,
broadcasters, publishers, digital platforms, influencers, affiliates, sponsors, and other
persons who create, commission, disseminate, publish, or materially facilitate
gambling-related advertisements, to the extent provided herein.

       Section 4. Responsible Gambling Advisory. – PAGCOR shall develop,
prescribe, and periodically update standardized responsible gambling advisories and
warning messages for use in consumer communications authorized under this Act.
Such advisories and warning messages shall be prominently displayed or
communicated by all gambling operators, gambling establishments, and gambling
platforms in the manner and form prescribed by PAGCOR.

      Section 5. General Standards for Gambling Advertisements. – All
gambling-related advertisements shall:

       (a) Be truthful, accurate, and capable of substantiation;

       (b) Clearly identify the gambling operator, brand, or entity responsible for the
       advertisement;

       (c) Display or communicate the applicable responsible gambling message in a
       clear and conspicuous manner;

       (d) Clearly indicate applicable age restrictions;

       (e) Not contain false, deceptive, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims;

       (f) Not represent gambling as a means of securing financial stability,
       employment, investment, or guaranteed income;

       (g) Not suggest that gambling is a risk-free or guaranteed method of obtaining
       money or other valuable consideration;
      (h) Not misrepresent the probability of winning or the nature of any prize, bonus,
      promotion, or incentive;

      (i) Not encourage excessive, compulsive, or irresponsible gambling; and

      (j) Comply with such additional advertising standards as may be prescribed by
      the appropriate regulatory authority.

      No person shall disseminate, publish, broadcast, display, or otherwise make
      available any advertisements relating to any gambling activity, gambling
      operator, gambling establishment, or gambling platform; Provided, that such
      restriction shall not apply to the following:

        i.    Advertisements displayed within the premises of a gambling
              establishment; or
        ii.   Advertisements appearing on the official websites or official mobile
              application of the gambling operators, gambling establishments, or
              gambling mobile application of the gambling operators, gambling
              establishments, or gambling platforms.

      Section 6. Prohibition on Advertising Practices. – It shall be unlawful to
disseminate or cause the dissemination of any gambling-related advertisement that:

      (a) Encourages a person to borrow money, incur debt, sell property, or
      otherwise obtain funds for the purpose of gambling;

      (b) Represents or implies that gambling can solve financial problems or provide
      a dependable source of income;

      (c) Uses false claims concerning winning probabilities, jackpots, bonuses,
      rewards, or promotional offers;

      (d) Omits material terms and conditions in a manner that is likely to mislead
      consumers;

      (e) Portrays gambling as a test of skill, intelligence, courage, or social status
      where such representation is misleading or deceptive;

      (f) Suggests that gambling is necessary for social acceptance, popularity,
      success, or personal fulfillment;

      (g) Exploits persons experiencing financial hardship or other circumstances that
      make them particularly susceptible to gambling-related harm;
       (h) Uses content that is primarily directed toward minors or is reasonably likely
       to appeal particularly to minors;

       (i) Uses characters, personalities, imagery, language, themes, or activities
       principally associated with children;

       (j) Is placed in or immediately adjacent to content specifically intended for
       children; or

       (k) Otherwise violates the standards established under this Act or its
       implementing rules and regulations.

       Section 7. Protection of Minors and Vulnerable Persons. – No gambling-
related advertisement or promotional activity shall be knowingly directed at minors or
vulnerable persons.

       Gambling operators, advertisers, and digital platforms shall implement
reasonable age-targeting and audience-filtering mechanisms for online
advertisements.

       No person shall use a minor or vulnerable in any gambling-related
advertisement, endorsement, promotional campaign, or other marketing material.

      No gambling-related advertisement shall be disseminated in schools,
playgrounds, child-care facilities, or other locations primarily intended for minors,
except where specifically authorized by law for purposes of responsible gambling
education.

       Section 8. Regulation on Influencers, Endorsements, and Affiliates. – No
influencer, celebrity, public personality, athlete, content creator, affiliate, or other
individual shall promote or endorse a gambling-related activity in violation of this Act.

       Any paid or compensated endorsement shall clearly disclose the commercial
nature of the communication.

       No person covered by this Section shall make statements that falsely represent
gambling as a reliable means of obtaining income, achieving financial success, or
eliminating financial difficulties.

       The appropriate regulatory authority may prescribe additional rules governing
gambling-related endorsements, including restrictions on endorsements that are
primarily directed toward or likely to influence minors.
        Section 9. Sponsorships and Promotional Events. – Sponsorship of
sporting events, entertainment programs, concerts, digital content, or other public
activities by gambling operators shall comply with this Act and its implementing rules
and regulations.

       No sponsorship shall be structured or presented in a manner that primarily
targets minors or encourages irresponsible gambling.

       The appropriate regulatory authority may establish additional restrictions on the
display of gambling brands, logos, promotional materials, and other gambling-related
content in events with substantial participation or viewership by minors.

       Section 10. Promotional Offers, Bonuses, and Incentives. – No gambling
operator shall advertise or offer any bonus, rebate, reward, free play, or similar
incentive in a manner that is false, deceptive, or misleading.

       All material terms and conditions applicable to a promotional offer shall be
clearly disclosed and reasonably accessible to consumers before they participate.

      Promotional offers shall not be designed or marketed in a manner that
encourages excessive or repeated gambling.

        Section 11. Advertising Clearance and Regulatory Authority. – The
appropriate government regulatory authority with jurisdiction over the gambling activity
shall issue and enforce standards governing gambling-related advertisements.

       Such authority may:

       (a) Require prior review or clearance of specified categories of gambling
       advertisements;

       (b) Issue guidelines concerning placement, frequency, content, timing,
       audience targeting, and format;

       (c) Order the removal, suspension, or correction of advertisements that violate
       this Act;

       (d) Require gambling operators and advertisers to maintain records of
       advertisements and promotional campaigns;

       (e) Coordinate with other government agencies and digital platforms for
       enforcement; and
       (f) Promulgate such rules and regulations as may be necessary to implement
       this Act.

      Nothing in this Act shall diminish the regulatory authority of any government
agency over gambling activities within its lawful jurisdiction.

       Section 12. Responsible Gambling Awareness and Education Campaign.
– Subject to the provisions of this Act and other applicable laws, the Philippine
Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR), in coordination with relevant
government agencies and other stakeholders, shall develop, implement, and sustain
a continuing nationwide responsible gambling awareness and education campaign to
inform the public of the risks and potential harmful effects associated with excessive,
compulsive, or irresponsible gambling.

       The campaign shall, among others, provide accessible and evidence-based
information on responsible gambling practices, the signs and risks of problematic or
harmful gambling, measures for preventing and reducing gambling-related harm, and
available interventions, counseling, treatment, rehabilitation, and support services for
individuals and families affected by gambling-related concerns.

      PAGCOR shall promote the integration of responsible gambling education into
appropriate public information and community-based initiatives and shall ensure that
campaign materials are accessible, age-appropriate, and disseminated through
appropriate traditional and digital media channels.

       Consistent with a whole-of-society approach to the prevention and reduction of
gambling-related harm, private-sector entities, including gambling operators, media
organizations, advertising agencies, digital platforms, and other relevant stakeholders,
shall be encouraged to develop, undertake, fund, or otherwise support responsible
gambling awareness and education initiatives, subject to standards and guidelines
that may be prescribed by PAGCOR.

       Nothing in this Section shall relieve gambling operators or other entities covered
by this Act of any mandatory responsible gambling, consumer protection, or
advertising obligations imposed by law or regulation.

      Section 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the PAGCOR and other relevant agencies, in
consultation with other relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules
and regulations for its implementation.

        Section 14. Transitory Provision. – Gambling operators, advertisers, media
entities, digital platforms, and other persons covered by this Act shall be given a period
of ninety (90) days from the effectivity of the implementing rules and regulations to
comply with the requirements of this Act.

      Existing advertisements that do not comply with this Act shall be modified,
suspended, or withdrawn within the prescribed period.

      Section 15. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provisions not affected
thereby shall remain in force and effect.

        Section 16. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, rule or regulation inconsistent with the provisions
of this Act is hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       Section 17. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following
its complete publication in a newspaper of general circulation.
