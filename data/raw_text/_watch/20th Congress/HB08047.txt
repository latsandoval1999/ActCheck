                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to mandate the National Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Council (NDRRMC), in coordination with the Department of
Agriculture and the Committee on Animal Welfare, to promulgate clear and
enforceable guidelines for the rescue, evacuation, and temporary shelter of livestock
and working animals during calamities.

       The Philippines remains highly vulnerable to typhoons, floods, earthquakes,
volcanic eruptions, and other natural hazards. In times of disaster, response efforts
understandably prioritize human life and safety. However, livestock and working
animals, on which many Filipino families depend for food security and livelihood,
are often left without structured rescue mechanisms. Despite the protections under
Republic Act No. 8485, otherwise known as the “Animal Welfare Act of 1998”, as
amended by RA No. 10631, there is currently no comprehensive framework that
operationalizes animal welfare standards specifically in disaster situations.

       This measure institutionalizes disaster-responsive protocols for livestock and
working animals. It directs the Department of Agriculture, in consultation with the
NDRRMC and the Bureau of Animal Industry, to issue implementing rules that
harmonize disaster response systems with animal welfare protections against
neglect, and abandonment.

       Moreover, the bill also encourages partnerships with the private sector,
non-governmental organizations, civic groups, and the academe in implementing
rescue and shelter programs. While human safety remains paramount, this measure
ensures that animal protection forms part of disaster preparedness and response
planning.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                       AN ACT
MANDATING THE NATIONAL DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND
MANAGEMENT COUNCIL TO PROMULGATE GUIDELINES FOR THE
RESCUE OF LIVESTOCK AND WORKING ANIMALS IN CASES OF
CALAMITY

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Animal Rescue in
Calamities Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in
accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature. To this end, the State shall also
recognize the ownership of livestock and working animals as indispensable to the
business of several Filipinos.

       Consistent with Republic Act (RA) No. 8485, otherwise known as “The Animal
Welfare act of 1998”, as amended by RA No. 10631, which prohibits cruelty, neglect,
and abandonment of animals, the State shall ensure that these protections are
operationalized during calamities. Accordingly, the government shall establish clear
guidelines for the rescue, evacuation, and temporary shelter of livestock and
working animals in disaster situations. By safeguarding these animals, the
government affirms its duty to protect both human welfare and the economic
survival of communities that depend on them.

      Through this policy, the State affirms its duty to safeguard both human
welfare and the economic survival of communities, while exercising humane
treatment of animals in accordance with the Animal Welfare Act.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — As used in this Act:




                                           1
          a.​ Livestock refers to domesticated animals raised in an agricultural
      setting to produce labor and commodities such as, but not limited to, meat,
      eggs, fur, or leather;

         b.​ Working animal refers to an animal, usually domesticated, that is kept
      by humans and trained to perform tasks; and

          c.​ Calamity refers to a disastrous event marked by great loss and lasting
      distress and/or suffering.
​
        SEC. 4. Scope. — This Act shall apply to all livestock and working animals,
whether publicly or privately owned.
​
        SEC. 5. Roles and Responsibilities. — The Committee on Animal Welfare
attached to the Department of Agriculture, as mandated by RA No. 8485, otherwise
known as the Animal Welfare Act, as amended by RA No. 10631, in coordination
with the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC),
shall identify, prepare, and provide policy guidance and technical assistance for the
rescue, evacuation, and temporary shelter of livestock and working animals in cases
of calamity.

      In line with Section 6 and Section 7 of RA No. 10631, which prohibit neglect
and abandonment of animals, this Act operationalizes such protections by requiring
owners of livestock and working animals to register them with their respective
barangays. Barangays shall maintain local records, in coordination with the Bureau
of Animal Industry and municipal agricultural offices, to facilitate rescue and
evacuation planning.

       In the event of a calamity, barangays and animal owners shall take all
reasonable measures to secure the safety of animals within a reasonable period
following the evacuation of residents, subject to the paramount consideration of
human safety. Where such measures are not possible due to risks to human life, the
matter shall be referred to the NDRRMC for appropriate action, ensuring that
reasonable steps are taken to minimize economic loss to animal owners.
​
       SEC. 6. Socio-civic organizations and the Private Sector. — This Act shall also
empower the private sector in engaging and partnering with the government in
promulgating policy measures with regard to the rescue of livestock and working
animals in cases of calamity. Non-governmental organizations, civic organizations,
the academe, people’s organizations, and the like shall be tapped by the government
in providing assistance in the promulgation and enforcement of such guidelines.

        Such participation shall be consistent with the humane treatment standards
and anti-cruelty provisions under RA No. 10631, thereby ensuring that rescue
operations uphold animal welfare principles while complementing disaster response
efforts.
​



                                          2
      SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The Department of Agriculture,
in consultation with the NDRRMC, the Bureau of Animal Industry, and relevant
stakeholders, shall promulgate the implementing rules and regulations of this Act
within ninety (90) days from its effectivity. These rules shall harmonize disaster
rescue protocols with the protections against cruelty, neglect, and abandonment
provided under RA No. 10631.

        SEC. 8. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
