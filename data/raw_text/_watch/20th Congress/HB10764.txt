                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Some traditions survive because they are written in books. Others survive because
people continue to live them.

       For generations, the people of Bocaue have gathered along the river for the Pagoda
Festival, or “Pagoda sa Wawa”, a celebration inseparable from the town’s history, identity,
and devotion to the Holy Cross of Wawa. The pagoda, the Festival’s most enduring image,
not only carries participants, but also memories, from one generation of Bocaueños to the
next.

        A tradition of such consequence deserves more than passing recognition. It
deserves preservation that is thoughtful, promotion that is responsible, and celebration
that is safe.

       This bill seeks to declared the Bocaue Pagoda Festival as the Official Cultural
Festival of the Municipality of Bocaue and a Major Tourist Attraction. It aims to recognize
the Festival for its legitimate secular significance as cultural heritage, as living local
history, as a source of tourism and livelihood, and as an important public gathering
requiring proper planning and protection.

       Accordingly, this measure provides for the sustainable development and
promotion of the Festival, the documentation and safeguarding of its traditions, and the
preservation of its historical narrative. It brings together the appropriate expertise of the
Department of Tourism and the Tourism Promotions Board in tourism development; the
National Commission for Culture and the Arts in cultural heritage; and the National
Historical Commission of the Philippines in historical research, documentation, and
interpretation.

      Considering that the Pagoda Festival’s defining feature is that its procession takes
place on water, this bill requires an annual Festival Safety and Fluvial Procession
Management Plan, including vessel inspection and applicable clearances, enforceable
passenger and load limits, adequate life-saving equipment, controlled boarding, rescue
and medical capability, unified incident command, and a documented go/no-go safety
determination before the procession begins.

       The Bocaue River has long carried one of Bulacan’s most distinctive traditions. As
the pagoda continues to carry the memory and devotion of generations, this measure
ensures that the tradition be preserved with fidelity, promoted with dignity, and
celebrated with every reasonable safeguard for the lives entrusted to it.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate enactment of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                   AN ACT
      DECLARING THE BOCAUE PAGODA FESTIVAL, ALSO KNOWN AS “PAGODA
      SA WAWA,” AS THE OFFICIAL CULTURAL FESTIVAL OF THE MUNICIPALITY
           OF BOCAUE, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, AND A MAJOR TOURIST
          ATTRACTION, PROVIDING FOR ITS DEVELOPMENT, PROMOTION,
         PRESERVATION, AND SAFE CONDUCT, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

            Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1           Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bocaue Pagoda Festival
 2   Act.”
 3
 4          Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 5   preserve and promote local cultural heritage and traditions; support sustainable and
 6   community-based tourism; strengthen local livelihoods arising from cultural activities;
 7   and ensure the safe, orderly, and environmentally responsible conduct of public
 8   celebrations.
 9
10          Towards this end, the State shall support the preservation, development, and
11   promotion of the Bocaue Pagoda Festival as an enduring cultural tradition of the people
12   of Bocaue and as a significant tourism attraction of the Province of Bulacan.
13
14          Section 3. Official Cultural Festival and Major Tourist Attraction. – The Bocaue
15   Pagoda Festival, also known as “Pagoda sa Wawa,” traditionally celebrated in the
16   Municipality of Bocaue, Province of Bulacan, in connection with the local devotion to the
17   Holy Cross of Wawa, is hereby declared the Official Cultural Festival of the Municipality
18   of Bocaue and a Major Tourist Attraction.
19
20          The recognition granted under this Act shall be for the secular purposes of cultural
21   preservation, tourism development, local economic development, public safety, and
22   environmental protection. Nothing in this Act shall be construed to authorize any

 2   doctrines, or ecclesiastical matters associated with the celebration.

 4           Section 4. Sustainable Tourism Development and Promotion. – The Municipality
 5   of Bocaue, in coordination with the Department of Tourism (DOT), the Tourism
 6   Promotions Board (TPB), and other concerned government agencies and stakeholders,
 7   shall ensure the inclusion of the Bocaue Pagoda Festival in the local tourism development
 8   plan and undertake measures for its sustainable development and promotion.
 9
10          Such measures shall, as appropriate, include:
11          (a) Domestic and international promotion of the Festival as a cultural and tourism
12              attraction;
13          (b) Visitor, traffic, crowd, and accessibility management;
14          (c) Improvement and maintenance of appropriate tourism facilities, access,
15              wayfinding, sanitation, and other support services;
16          (d) Programs supporting local enterprises, artisans, cultural workers, food
17              producers, tourism establishments, and other community-based economic
18              activities associated with the Festival;
19          (e) Heritage interpretation, information, and educational materials concerning the
20              history and cultural significance of the Festival; and
21          (f) Collection of appropriate tourism and socioeconomic data to assess the
22              Festival’s contribution to local tourism and development.
23
24         The DOT and its attached agencies may provide technical, promotional, and
25   development assistance for these purposes in accordance with Republic Act No. 9593,
26   otherwise known as the “Tourism Act of 2009,” and other applicable laws.
27
28           Section 5. Cultural Preservation, Historical Research, and Documentation. – The
29   Municipality of Bocaue, with the technical assistance of the National Commission for
30   Culture and the Arts (NCCA) and, on matters pertaining to local history, the National
31   Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP), and other appropriate cultural
32   agencies, shall promote the documentation, preservation, and transmission of the history,
33   traditions, practices, oral histories, craftsmanship, music, rituals of cultural significance,
34   and other cultural expressions associated with the Bocaue Pagoda Festival.
35
36          The NHCP may provide technical assistance in the historical research,
37   documentation, verification, interpretation, and dissemination of information concerning
38   the origins, development, and historical significance of the Festival, consistent with
39   Republic Act No. 10086, otherwise known as the “Strengthening Peoples’ Nationalism
40   Through Philippine History Act.”
41
42          For this purpose, the Municipality shall maintain, update, and, where appropriate,
43   expand the documentation of the Festival and its associated cultural traditions through
44   the local cultural mapping and inventory processes, consistent with Republic Act No.

 4           The participation of concerned communities, cultural organizations, religious

 8           Section 6. Fluvial Procession Safety and Emergency Preparedness. – In view of the
 9   fluvial character of the Festival, the Municipality of Bocaue, in coordination with the
10   Philippine Coast Guard (PCG), the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management
11   Office, local law enforcement, health and emergency-response authorities, and other
12   concerned agencies, shall prepare and implement an annual Festival Safety and Fluvial
13   Procession Management Plan.
14
15          The Plan shall, at a minimum, provide for the following:
16          (a) Inspection and safety clearance, as may be required under applicable laws and
17              regulations, of barges, floating platforms, boats, and other watercraft
18              participating in the fluvial procession;
19          (b) Determination, certification, posting, and strict enforcement of the maximum
20              safe passenger and load capacity of each participating barge, floating platform,
21              boat, or other watercraft, taking into account its design, stability, condition,
22              intended use, and prevailing river and weather conditions;
23          (c) Mandatory availability and proper use of an adequate number of appropriate
24              and serviceable life jackets or personal flotation devices for all persons aboard
25              participating watercraft, together with such other life-saving, fire-safety,
26              communication, and emergency equipment as may be required by competent
27              authorities;
28          (d) Designated and controlled embarkation and disembarkation areas, passenger-
29              accounting procedures, and appropriate barriers and crowd-control measures
30              to prevent unauthorized boarding and overloading;
31          (e) River traffic, navigation, vessel-spacing, speed, route, and movement controls
32              during the fluvial procession;
33          (f) Continuous monitoring of weather, river, visibility, current, water-level, and
34              other conditions that may materially affect navigation or public safety;
35          (g) Adequate communication, medical, rescue, evacuation, and emergency-
36              response arrangements, including the strategic deployment of rescue vessels,
37              trained personnel, medical teams, and emergency equipment;
38          (h) Establishment of a unified incident command and coordination mechanism for
39              the fluvial procession, utilizing existing local disaster risk reduction and
40              emergency-management structures and clearly identifying the responsibilities
41              and operational authority of participating government agencies;
42          (i) Designation of an authorized safety officer or coordinating official responsible
43              for consolidating safety assessments and ensuring compliance with the
44              requirements of the Plan; and

20   development of the Festival shall be consistent with applicable environmental laws and
21   local environmental programs.
22

29          Programs and infrastructure developed pursuant to this Act shall, insofar as
