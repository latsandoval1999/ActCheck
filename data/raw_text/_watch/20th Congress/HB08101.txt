                                    AN ACT
       TO STRENGTHEN THE MECHANISM FOR THE IMMEDIATE SETTLEMENT OF
       MONETARY CLAIMS OF OVERSEAS FILIPINO WORKERS, MANDATING THE
      PRIMARY LIABILITY OF LOCAL RECRUITMENT AGENCIES AND ESTABLISHING
      AN ELECTRONIC FILING PORTAL, AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE REPUBLIC
             ACT NO. 8042, AS AMENDED, AND OTHER PERTINENT LAWS

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1   SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “OFW Claims Assurance Act of
 2   2026.”

 3   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the declared policy of the State to afford full
 4   protection to labor, local and overseas, organized and unorganized, and to promote their
 5   well-being. The State shall guarantee the rights of all migrant workers to the immediate
 6   and effective settlement of claims arising from the violation of their employment contracts.
 7   Recognizing the joint and solidary liability of local recruitment agencies and their foreign
 8   principals, the State shall establish mechanisms to ensure that the more accessible local
 9   entity provides immediate financial relief to the aggrieved worker. The State shall further
10   harness information and communications technology to provide a transparent, efficient,
11   and accessible system for filing and monitoring claims, thereby upholding the dignity of
12   every Overseas Filipino Worker as decreed by the Constitution and existing laws.

13   SECTION 3. Amendment to Section 10 of Republic Act No. 8042, as amended. –
14   Section 10 of Republic Act No. 8042, as amended by Republic Act No. 10022, is hereby
15   further amended to read as follows:

16          “SEC. 10. Money Claims, Immediate Liability of the Local Recruitment
17          Agency, and Electronic Filing. – Notwithstanding any provision of law to the
18          contrary, the liability of the local recruitment agency and the foreign employer for all
19          claims arising out of an employment contract or by virtue of any law or regulation
20          shall be joint and several. This provision shall be incorporated in the contract for
21          overseas employment and shall be a condition precedent for its approval.

22          “(a) Primary and Immediate Liability of the Local Recruitment Agency. – In the
23          enforcement of such liability, the local recruitment agency shall be primarily and
 1   immediately liable for the payment of any and all monetary claims filed by the
 2   Overseas Filipino Worker, including but not limited to unpaid salaries, overtime pay,
 3   holiday pay, separation pay, and other benefits stipulated in the contract or
 4   mandated by law.

 5   “(b) Summary Adjudication and Mandatory Payment. – To ensure the swift
 6   resolution of claims, the DMW shall observe an expedited summary procedure
 7   through the Electronic Portal:

 8      1. Summary Procedure: Upon the filing of a claim, the DMW shall, within
 9         twenty-four (24) hours, issue an electronic summons to the local recruitment
10         agency, which shall then have a non-extendible period of five (5) calendar
11         days to file its response. Within five (5) calendar days from receipt of the
12         response or the expiration of the period to file one, the DMW shall issue a
13         Summary Order of Payment based on a preponderance of evidence.
14      2. Period to Pay: The local recruitment agency shall have a period of fifteen
15         (15) calendar days from receipt of the Summary Order of Payment to pay
16         the validated amount to the claimant-OFW.
17      3. Non-Stay of Execution: The filing of an appeal or any other petition shall
18         not stay the execution of this payment. In the event of an appeal, the amount
19         shall be paid by the agency and held in trust or escrow by the DMW. These
20         funds shall be released to the worker immediately upon affirmation of the
21         Order, or returned to the agency should the Order be reversed with finality.
22         Any willful failure to comply with the 15-day payment window shall be
23         deemed a waiver of the agency’s right to appeal and shall trigger the
24         immediate imposition of administrative sanctions provided under this Act.

25   “(c) Subrogation and Indemnity. – Payment by the local recruitment agency
26   under this section shall entitle it to a right of subrogation and indemnity against its
27   foreign principal for the full amount paid to the worker, including applicable interests
28   and costs. Any willful failure or refusal of the foreign principal to reimburse the local
29   recruitment agency shall be a ground for the suspension or cancellation of the
30   principal’s accreditation or license with the DMW.

31   “(d) Electronic Overseas Filipino Worker Claims Portal. – To ensure a swift,
32   transparent, and accessible claims process, the Department of Migrant Workers
33   (DMW) is hereby mandated to establish, operate, and maintain a
34   centralized Electronic Overseas Filipino Worker Claims Portal within one hundred
35   eighty (180) days from the effectivity of this Act.

36      •   (1) The Portal shall serve as the primary and mandatory electronic
37          platform for the filing, processing, and monitoring of all monetary and
38          benefits claims by OFWs against their local recruitment agencies and/or
39          foreign employers.
40      •   (2) It shall allow OFWs or their duly authorized representatives to securely
41          file claims, upload supporting documents, receive official notifications, and
42          track the real-time status of their claims online.
43      •   (3) The Portal shall be designed to be accessible, user-friendly, and
44          available in major Philippine languages. The DMW shall ensure adequate
45          public information campaigns and technical support for its use.
46      •   (4) All local recruitment agencies shall be required to register with the Portal
47          and to use it for all communications related to claims filed against them. The
48          DMW shall issue implementing rules and regulations governing the technical
49          specifications, data privacy, and security protocols of the Portal.”
 1   SECTION 4. Evidentiary Standards and Summary Nature of Proceedings. – For
 2   purposes of Section 10(b) of R.A. 8042, as amended, the proceedings for the recovery of
 3   monetary claims through the Electronic OFW Claims Portal shall be summary in nature.
 4   “Sufficient evidence” shall be determined based on the preponderance of evidence and
 5   shall include, but not be limited to:

 6      a) A duly notarized affidavit of the claimant;
 7      b) Digital or physical copies of the employment contract duly authenticated by the
 8         DMW or the Migrant Workers Office (MWO);
 9      c) Electronic Evidence: Screenshots of mobile messages (e.g., WhatsApp,
10         Messenger, Viber), emails, or other digital communications between the worker and
11         the agency or foreign principal, pursuant to the Rules on Electronic Evidence (A.M.
12         No. 01-7-01-SC);
13      d) Proof of underpayment or non-payment, such as bank statements, remittance slips,
14         or payslips;
15      e) Reports, incident logs, or certifications issued by the MWO or the relevant Philippine
16         Embassy/Consulate.

17   The DMW shall ensure that the Portal allows for the seamless uploading of these formats.
18   The technical rules of evidence used in courts of law shall not be strictly applied, provided
19   that the essential requirements of due process are met through a summary opportunity for
20   the local recruitment agency to submit a counter-affidavit within five (5) calendar days from
21   electronic notification.

22   SECTION 5. Administrative Sanctions and Protection Against Fraud. – Any local
23   recruitment agency which fails or refuses to comply with a Final Order of Payment under
24   Section 10(b) of R.A. 8042, as amended, without a valid Temporary Restraining Order
25   (TRO) from a court of competent jurisdiction, shall be subject to the following administrative
26   penalties:

27      •   First Offense: A fine of not less than One Hundred Thousand Pesos
28          (PhP100,000.00) and the immediate suspension of its license to recruit and deploy
29          workers for a period of six (6) months.
30      •   Second Offense: Permanent cancellation of its license to operate and perpetual
31          disqualification of its officers and directors from engaging in the business of
32          recruitment.

33   SECTION 5-A. Sanctions for Fraudulent Claims. – Any person who, with intent to
34   defraud, submits false documents or makes untruthful statements in the Electronic OFW
35   Claims Portal shall be liable for Perjury under the Revised Penal Code and shall be
36   perpetually barred from filing claims under this Act, without prejudice to other criminal and
37   civil liabilities.

38   SECTION 6. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial development,
39   implementation, and maintenance of the Electronic Overseas Filipino Worker Claims
40   Portal shall be charged against the current fiscal year’s appropriation of the Department of
41   Migrant Workers. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued operation
42   and enhancement shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

43   SECTION 7. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
44   unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full
45   force and effect.

46   SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
47   issuances, rules and regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions
48   of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
