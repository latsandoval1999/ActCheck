                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

This measure seeks to convert the existing Land Transportation Office (LTO)
Tuguegarao Licensing Center into a regular LTO Licensing Office.

Strategically located in the heart of the 3rd District of Cagayan, and Tuguegarao City
being the regional center of the Cagayan Valley Region, the LTO Tuguegarao Licensing
Center plays a vital role in delivering core LTO services, particularly addressing the
driver licensing needs of residents of Tuguegarao City and the municipalities of
Baggao, Iguig, Amulung, Alcala, Peñablanca, Solana, and Enrile. Its presence
significantly reduces the need for constituents to travel long distances, ensuring faster,
more accessible, and more efficient delivery of public services.

Established through an administrative order, the LTO Tuguegarao Licensing Center
currently operates without a regular budget or plantilla positions. Despite this
limitation, it continues to provide reliable, consistent, and customer-focused service to
the community.

Converting the LTO Tuguegarao Licensing Center into a regular LTO Licensing Office
will enable it to have its own personnel complement and dedicated budget under the
annual General Appropriations Act. This institutional strengthening will result in
improved operational capacity, enhanced service efficiency, and better overall service
delivery for the people of Cagayan Province.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT CONVERTING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE LICENSING
CENTER LOCATED IN TUGUEGARAO CITY, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, INTO A
 REGULAR LICENSING OFFICE AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The existing Land Transportation Office (LTO) Licensing Center located
in Tuguegarao City, Province of Cagayan, is hereby converted into a regular Licensing
Office.

SECTION 2. The Secretary of Transportation shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of a regular LTO Licensing Office in
Tuguegarao City, Province of Cagayan, the funding of which shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
