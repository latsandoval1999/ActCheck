EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to establish a District Office of the Land Transportation Franchising and
Regulatory Board (LTFRB) in the Municipality of Virac, Province of Catanduanes, to bring
franchising and other regulatory services closer to public utility vehicle operators, Certificate
of Public Convenience (CPC) holders, and the commuting public.

As an island province, Catanduanes is dependent on maritime transportation for access
to government services based on the mainland Bicol. At present, the nearest LTFRB office is
Regional Office V in Legazpi City, Albay. Consequently, stakeholders who need to transact
with LTFRB must travel by ferry to mainland Bicol and undertake additional land travel to
Legazpi City. Those who are unable to complete their transactions in time for the return trip
may also incur accommodation, food, and other incidental expenses. This results in significant
financial burden and extended travel time simply to process documents and avail of LTFRB
services. The absence of an LTFRB district office in Catanduanes therefore creates a
substantial barrier to accessible and efficient government service.

The establishment of an LTFRB District Office in Virac will bring essential LTFRB
programs and services closer to the people, improve public service delivery, and reduce the
costs and inconvenience borne by transport operators and stakeholders. Lower transaction and
compliance costs may also help ease operational expenses. Greater accessibility to the LTFRB
will likewise encourage regulatory compliance, discourage the operation of unregulated public
transportation, improve public safety, and enhance the overall efficiency of LTFRB operations
in the province. As the provincial capital and center of government and commerce, Virac is
strategically located to host an office that can efficiently serve stakeholders from all eleven
(11) municipalities of Catanduanes.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this proposed measure is earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL NO. 118: 39

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A LAND TRANSPORTATION FRANCHISING AND
REGULATORY BOARD (LTFRB) DISTRICT OFFICE IN THE MUNICIPALITY
OF VIRAC, PROVINCE OF CATANDUANES, APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. There is hereby established a district office of the Land Transportation
Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) in the Municipality of Virac, Catanduanes.

SEC, 2. The Secretary of Transportation shall immediately include in the Department
of Transportation's programs the establishment, operationalization, and maintenance of the
LTFRB district office in the Municipality of Virac, Province of Catanduaes, the funding of
which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Transportation shall formulate the necessary rules and
regulations to carry out the purpose of this Act.

SEC. 4. All laws, decrees, orders, and rules and regulations contrary to or inconsistent
with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

SEC. 5. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
