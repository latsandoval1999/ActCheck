                              EXPLANATORY NOTE



The Magna Carta of Public Health Workers (Republic Act No. 7305) was signed
into law almost 30 years ago "to promote and improve the social and economic
well-being of the health workers, their living and working conditions and terms
of employment." Yet, in the war against the COVID-19 pandemic, gaps have been
exposed in our health care system, with our workers bearing the brunt of these
shortcomings while serving in the frontlines under poor working conditions,
meager compensation and delayed release of their benefits.

This bill seeks to amend the Magna Carta of Public Health Workers to ensure
that public health workers can serve under better working conditions,
appropriately compensated and provided with their well-deserved incentives
and benefits. Its passage will not only strengthen the delivery of quality
healthcare services but also lessen “brain drain” of our medical professionals.

Thus, the approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

Sec. 13. Free Continuing Professional Development. - The Department of
Health shall provide free Continuing Professional Development training for public
health worker professionals who are required to undertake such program as a
requirement for the renewal of their professional identification cards under
the regulation of Professional Regulatory Commission as mandated by Republic
Act No. 10912 or the "Continuing Professional Development Act of 2016".
Sec. 14. Implementing Rules and Regulations. -The Secretary of Health after
consultation with appropriate agencies of the Government as well as professional
and health workers' organizations or unions, shall formulate and prepare the
revised implementing rules and regulation of the Magna Carta for Public Health
Workers in accordance with this Act.

Sec. 15. Separability Clause. - If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in force and effect.

Sec. 16. Repealing Clause. - All other laws, 6decrees, executive orders and rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.
