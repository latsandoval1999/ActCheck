                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act No. 9513, otherwise known as the Renewable Energy Act
of 2008, declares it a policy of the State to promote the development and
utilization of renewable energy systems through fiscal and non-fiscal
incentives. One of the key mechanisms under the law is the net-metering
program, which allows qualified end-users to install renewable energy
systems, generate their own electricity, and export excess power to the grid.
Through this program, consumers become “prosumers,” enabling them to
reduce electricity costs, protect themselves from energy price volatility, and
contribute to cleaner and more sustainable energy generation.


       Despite its benefits, the net-metering program remains underutilized.
As of 31 December 2024, only 15,605 qualified end-users nationwide
participate in the program, with a significant concentration in Luzon and very
limited participation in Mindanao. This may be attributed to the existing 100-
kilowatt cap, which restricts participation by larger electricity consumers, as
well as the cumbersome and fragmented permitting and application process.


     This measure seeks to strengthen and expand the net-metering program
by removing the rigid 100-kW cap and authorizing the Energy Regulatory
Commission (ERC) to determine appropriate capacity limits based on grid

stability and consumer welfare considerations. The bill likewise mandates the
streamlining and rationalization of permitting and documentary
requirements, including those imposed by local government units, consistent
with the timelines under the Energy Virtual One Stop Shop Act.


      By removing unnecessary barriers and improving access to renewable
energy systems, this measure aims to encourage broader participation in the
net-metering program, accelerate the country’s transition to clean energy, and
promote greater energy security and sustainability.


      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.
