                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to provide data and communication allowances to all
basic education teachers in both public and private schools during public
health emergencies and other analogous cases. It also ensures the continuous
and effective delivery of education through blended and distance learning
modalities.

       Despite their critical role in sustaining the education system and honing
the minds of the future of the nation, teachers continue to bear out-of-pocket
expenses for internet connectivity and communication services to perform
their duties. These additional costs are not commensurate with existing
compensation schemes and place undue financial burden on them, especially
during emergencies when alternative modes of instruction become
indispensable. Especially that the use of online tools, virtual classrooms, and
digital learning materials have since been integrated into the basic education
curriculum1.

      This proposed measure recognizes that effective teaching in a modern
education system requires more than professional competence, but also
adequate digital support. The provision of a communication and data
allowance ensures that teachers are equipped with the minimum resources
necessary to deliver instruction, maintain learner engagement, and access
platforms without interruption.

       Moreover, the provision of such allowances complements ongoing
efforts to improve the country’s digital infrastructure and expand internet

                       AN ACT
PROVIDING COMMUNICATION AND DATA ACCESSIBILITY,
HARDWARE SUPPORT, AND ALLOWANCES TO ALL PRIVATE AND
PUBLIC BASIC EDUCATION TEACHERS DURING PUBLIC HEALTH
EMERGENCIES AND OTHER ANALOGOUS CASES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Digital Resiliency
for Teachers during Health Emergencies Act”

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared as policy of the State,
consistent with the constitutional provisions on education and national
development, to support the unique needs of the education sector in times of
public health emergencies and similar crises. The State recognizes that school
teachers are essential pillars of national development whose duties must
remain uninterrupted despite external disruptions.

      Towards this end, the State shall ensure that all educators are equipped
with the appropriate tools, digital infrastructure, and support systems
necessary for the efficient and effective performance of their duties in any
learning environment.

      SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

         a. Public health emergency refers to an imminent threat of an illness
      or health condition, caused by bio terrorism, epidemics, pandemics, or
      fatal biological agents, that poses substantial risk of mass casualties or
      long-term disability, necessitating the prolonged closure of schools and
      learning institutions;


                                         1
          b. Other analogous cases means any occurrence or imminent threat
      of any calamity or disaster, natural or man-made, having the same
      effect of prolonged closure of schools and other learning institutions.

         c. Blended learning designates the range of learning possibilities in
      an education program in which content and instruction are presented
      by combining internet, digital media, television or radio broadcast with
      established classroom forms that require the physical co-presence of
      teacher and students.

       SEC. 4. Provision of Digital Devices. – To effectively execute the blended
learning implemented during a declared public health emergency and other
analogous cases, the national government through the Department of
Education (DepEd), in coordination with the Department of Information and
Communications Technology (DICT), shall provide the technologically
appropriate, affordable, and functional computers or similar digital devices to
all basic education teachers, both in public and private schools.

       SEC. 5. Mitigation of Technological Obsolescence. – The DICT shall
establish the minimum technical specifications for the devices mentioned in
Section 4 of this Act. These specifications shall undergo periodic review every
three (3) years to mitigate the impact of obsolescence and ensure that the
hardware remains efficient, effective, and compatible with current educational
software and platforms.

      SEC. 6. Provision of Data and Communication Allowance. – During a
declared public health emergency or other analogous case, the DepEd, in
coordination with the DICT, shall provide a monthly data and communication
allowance to all basic education teachers, both in public and in private. The
DepEd and DICT shall determine the appropriate amount based on prevailing
market rates and the volume of data required for full-time online instructional
delivery.

       SEC. 7. Subsidized Data Rates and Fiscal Incentives. – During a declared
public health emergency or other analogous case, access to websites where the
blended learning materials are published or posted shall be subsidized by the
government and may be accessed free of charge. Moreover, the Department of
Finance (DOF) through the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) shall be allowed
to grant fiscal incentives to concerned suppliers or service providers to lessen
cost: Provided, That, such incentives shall not significantly prejudice revenues
for the government.

       SEC. 8. Applicability. – The provisions of this Act shall apply to all
existing and future public health emergencies and analogous cases declared
by the national or local government.

      SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DepEd, the DICT and
the DOF shall promulgate the corresponding implementing rules and
regulations of this Act within sixty (60) days from the date of its effectivity.

      SEC. 10. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s
appropriations of the DepEd and DICT. Thereafter, such sums as may be
necessary for its continued implementation shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

      SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, and rules and regulations
inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended or
modified accordingly.

       SEC. 12. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall remain
valid and subsisting.

      SEC. 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation or in the Official
Gazette.
