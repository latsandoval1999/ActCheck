 AN ACT EXCLUDING SYSTEMS LOSSES FROM VALUE-ADDED TAXES (VAT)
IN THE SALE OF ELECTRICITY TO RESIDENTIAL HOUSEHOLD CONSUMERS

Electricity consumers in the Philippines continue to face persistently high power costs,
with the country ranking among those with the highest electricity tariffs in Southeast
Asia. Recent developments have further underscored the volatility of electricity prices
due to global fuel supply disruptions, prompting government intervention to stabilize
the market.

One contributing factor to high electricity bills is the “system loss” charge, which
represents the difference between the electricity supplied to the distribution system
and the electricity actually delivered to end-users. These losses arise from both
technical inefficiencies and non-technical factors such as pilferage. Despite not being
directly consumed by households, this system loss component is currently included in
electricity billing and remains subject to the Value-Added Tax (VAT).

Recent policy discussions and legislative initiatives have highlighted growing public
concern over electricity pricing structures. Lawmakers have noted instances of “bill
shock,” where consumers experienced significantly higher electricity bills even during
periods of reduced consumption, reflecting deeper structural issues in pricing
mechanisms.

As such, this proposed measure seeks to exempt the system loss component from
VAT in the sale of electricity to residential household consumers. By removing VAT on
system loss charge, we will be able to provide immediate and tangible relief to low-
and middle-income Filipino households.

Furthermore, the bill mandates the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) to ensure
that any reduction resulting from the VAT exemption is not offset through other
charges, such as distribution wheeling charges or similar pass-through costs. This
safeguard ensures that the intended benefits of the measure are fully realized by
residential consumers.
In light of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought to promote
fairness, transparency, and affordability in electricity pricing, and to ease the financial
burden on Filipino households amid continuing energy market uncertainties.
