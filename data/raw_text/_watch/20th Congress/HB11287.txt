                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish at least one (1) Flight Standards Inspectorate Centers
(FSIC) each in Visayas and Mindanao under the Civil Aviation Authority of the
Philippines (CAAP). It amends Republic Act (RA) No. 9497, otherwise known as the
“Civil Aviation Authority Act of 2008”, and strengthens CAAP’s capacity to implement
and enforce civil aviation safety standards.

       CAAP is mandated to regulate, supervise, and oversee civil aviation activities in
the country. The concentration of a single FSIC in just one (1) region constrains the
timely conduct of surveillance, certification, licensing, and other regulatory processes in
distant areas. Thus, establishing additional FSICs shall provide extension services for
these functions in Visayas and Mindanao.

        The proposed measure establishes FSICs which shall abide by International Civil
Aviation Organization (ICAO) Standards and Recommended Practices. They shall also
certify commercial air transport and general aviation operators, approve training and
maintenance organizations, and issue airmen licenses to qualified applicants within
Visayas and Mindanao. This improves regulatory accessibility, strengthens monitoring
capability, and allows the CAAP to deliver aviation safety services more efficiently.

       For an archipelagic nation where distance and geography often determine access
to services and opportunities, the State must continue to improve the capacity of its
aviation institutions to deliver timely and effective regulatory oversight. This vital piece
of legislation brings aviation safety functions closer to the people and industries that
depend on them. It strengthens the foundation of public trust in air transportation,
supports balanced regional development, and safeguards the continued growth of
Philippine civil aviation.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
CREATING FLIGHT STANDARDS INSPECTORATE CENTERS UNDER THE
CIVIL AVIATION AUTHORITY OF THE PHILIPPINES IN VISAYAS AND IN
MINDANAO, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9497,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “CIVIL AVIATION AUTHORITY ACT OF 2008”,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. A new Section 41 is hereby inserted after Section 40 of Republic Act
No. 9497, otherwise known as the “Civil Aviation Authority of 2008”, to read as follows:

            “SEC. 41. FLIGHT STANDARDS INSPECTORATE CENTERS. –
       THE AUTHORITY SHALL BE AUTHORIZED TO ESTABLISH,
       OPERATE, AND MAINTAIN AT LEAST ONE (1) FLIGHT
       STANDARDS INSPECTORATE CENTER (FSIC) EACH IN VISAYAS
       AND IN MINDANAO. THE FSIC SHALL BE HEADED BY A CENTER
       DIRECTOR, WHO MAY BE ASSISTED BY ONE (1) ASSISTANT
       CENTER DIRECTOR.

            “THE FSIC SHALL HAVE, WITHIN THEIR RESPECTIVE
       REGIONS, THE FOLLOWING FUNCTIONS:

                  “(A) MANAGE ALL THE ACTIVITIES AND PERSONNEL
               OF FSIC;

                 “(B) PROMOTE CIVIL AVIATION SAFETY THROUGH
               PROPER IMPLEMENTATION OF REGULATIONS AND
               CONDUCT OF PROMPT, SUSTAINED, AND CONSISTENT
               SURVEILLANCE   IN   ACCORDANCE    WITH   ICAO
               STANDARDS AND RECOMMENDED PRACTICE;

                 “(C) CERTIFY COMMERCIAL AIR TRANSPORT AND
               GENERAL AVIATION OPERATORS AND APPROVE
               TRAINING   ORGANIZATIONS  AND   MAINTENANCE
               ORGANIZATIONS;



                                              1
                 “(D) ISSUE           AIRMEN       LICENSES        TO     QUALIFIED
               APPLICANTS;

                 “(E) HARMONIZE OPERATING REGULATIONS AND
               COORDINATION AMONG THE VARIOUS DEPARTMENTS
               RESPONSIBLE FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF NATIONAL
               AND INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS; AND

                 “(F) PERFORM SUCH OTHER POWERS AND FUNCTIONS
               AS MAY BE PRESCRIBED BY LAW OR ASSIGNED BY THE
               DIRECTOR GENERAL.”

       SEC. 2. All succeeding sections of this Act shall be renumbered accordingly.

       SEC. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – For the effective implementation of
this Act, Department of Transportation, Department of Foreign Affairs, Department of
Tourism, Department of Finance, Department of Labor and Employment, Department of
Interior and Local Government, and other concerned agencies shall, within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, promulgate the implementing rules and
regulations.

      SEC. 4. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act shall be declared
unconstitutional or invalid, such declaration shall not invalidate other parts thereof
which shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with or contrary to the
provisions of this Act are hereby amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general application.
