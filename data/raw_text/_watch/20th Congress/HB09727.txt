                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The energy industry is one of the most highly technical and specialized
sectors, where expertise is developed through extensive knowledge,
experience, and industry exposure. As the principal agency responsible for the
formulation and implementation of the country’s energy policies, the
Department of Energy (DOE) requires leadership that possesses not only
competence and integrity, but also a deep understanding of the complexities
of the energy sector.


       At present, existing law imposes restrictions that may disqualify
otherwise qualified individuals from appointment as Secretary of the DOE
solely on the basis of prior affiliations with energy industry participants.
While safeguards against conflicts of interest remain essential, such
restrictions may unnecessarily limit the pool of competent and experienced
professionals capable of leading the Department.


       This measure seeks to remove the prohibitive provision while
strengthening transparency and accountability mechanisms. To prevent
conflicts of interest, the bill mandates the public disclosure of prior industry
involvement, the divestment of any financial interests in energy-related
entities, and the termination of any employment, consultancy, directorship, or

other connections with energy industry participants upon assumption of
office.


      By allowing highly qualified and experienced professionals to serve in
the DOE, subject to strict conflict-of-interest safeguards, this bill aims to
strengthen the country’s energy governance and policy implementation.


      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
earnestly sought.
