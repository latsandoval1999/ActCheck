                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Value-Added Tax (VAT) is one of the primary sources of revenue of the
national government under the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended.
Currently, most goods and services are subject to a uniform twelve percent (12%) VAT
rate.

       While VAT has proven effective in generating revenues for the government,
the imposition of a uniform tax rate on both essential commodities and luxury items
creates an inequitable burden on low- and middle-income households, thus raising
concerns regarding equity and fairness in the Philippine tax system that undermines
the principle of tax equity and fairness embodied in the 1987 Constitution of the
Philippines, which mandates that taxation shall be equitable and progressive.

       Low-income households allocate a larger portion of their income to necessities
such as food, medicine, and other essential services. The imposition of the same VAT
rate on these goods as on luxury or non-essential items places a disproportionate
burden on economically vulnerable sectors.

       This proposed measure seeks to reform the VAT system by expanding
exemptions for basic goods and services while ensuring that luxury and non-essential
items remain subject to appropriate taxation. Through this approach, the measure
aims to ease the financial burden on Filipino households while maintaining the
government’s capacity to generate revenues necessary for public services and national
development.

       Ultimately, this bill promotes a more equitable tax structure that aligns taxation
with the taxpayer’s ability to pay while protecting the welfare of ordinary Filipino
families.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

1   SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Equitable Value-Added
2   Tax Reform Act.”

3   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
4   establish a fair, equitable, and progressive taxation system that protects low-income
5   households from disproportionate tax burdens while ensuring sustainable
6   government revenues for national development.

7   Towards this end, the objectives of this Act are:

8      a. To reduce the financial burden on Filipino households by expanding VAT
9         exemptions on basic goods and essential services;
 1      b. To promote fairness in taxation by ensuring that luxury and non-essential items
 2         bear appropriate tax burdens;
 3      c. To protect low-income and vulnerable sectors from the disproportionate effects
 4         of consumption taxes; and
 5      d. To maintain a stable and sustainable revenue system for the government.
