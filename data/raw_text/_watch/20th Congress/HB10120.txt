                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

The barangay serves as the basic political unit of the country, according to the Local Government
Code of 1991. As the basic political unit, it deals with the primary planning and implementation of
government policies, plans, programs, projects, and activities in the community and serves as a forum
wherein the collective views of the people may be freely expressed, deliberated upon and considered,
realized, and where disputes are resolved or may be amicably settled. Most, if not all, of the national
government’s efforts requires intervention and support of the barangays as they are the closest form
of government to the people.

The administration of the barangay, composed of Punong Barangay and the Sangguniang Barangay
officials, receive compensation in the form of honorarium, and not a fixed salary within a
compensation system under the Local Government Code. Unfortunately, barangay officials are not
granted corresponding death, disability and accident insurance during the performance of their
duties, despite the number of risks attributed to their positions.

The barangay serves as the first recourse of the people, may it be in terms of conflict resolution,
medical and burial support, financial assistance, disaster and risk response and management, and the
like. Simply put, they serve as the frontliners and are expected to address a number of issues apart
from their regular duties and responsibilities as Barangay Officials, which are already challenging to
begin with. Hence, it is only right that these officials shall be accorded utmost protection in response
to their on-the-ground call of duty.

This bill seeks to amend Republic Act No. 6942 or “An Act Increasing the Insurance Benefits of
Local Government Officials and Providing Funds Therefore” in order to expand the insurance
coverage of barangay officials in times of death, total and permanent incapacity or disability, and
accident, and increasing the benefits to be received therein, thereby recognizing their vital role in
immediately carrying out government’s services to the communities.

Henceforth, in view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                              AN ACT
PROVIDING DEATH, DISABILITY, AND ACCIDENT INSURANCE COVERAGE
TO OTHER BARANGAY OFFICIALS, BARANGAY TANODS, BARANGAY
HEALTH WORKERS, BARANGAY DAY CARE WORKERS AND MEMBERS OF
THE LUPONG TAGAPAMAYAPA, AND INCREASING THE INSURANCE
BENEFITS PROVIDED THEREIN, AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6942 AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted in the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

         SECTION 1. Section 1 of Republic Act No. 6942 is hereby amended to read as follows:

         “Section 1. Coverage of the Act. - Every punong barangay, sangguniang barangay
         member, [chairman of the kabataang barangay] SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN (SK)
         CHAIRMAN. Barangay secretary [and], barangay treasurer, BARANGAY TANOD,
         BARANGAY HEALTH WORKER. BARANGAY DAY CARE WORKER. AND
         MEMBER OF THE LUPONG TAGAPAMAYAPA, and every member of the
         sangguniang panlalawigan, sangguniang panlungsod, and sangguniang bayan, duly elected
         or appointed, and duly qualified, who does not receive a fixed salary during his
         incumbency, shall be automatically covered by this Act: Provided, that the moment said
         official receives a fixed salary whether in the same capacity or by virtue of a permanent
         appointment in any other government office or by election to another public office, his
         coverage under this Act shall automatically cease and shall then be covered under the
         appropriate law. Any of the abovementioned local officials who shall automatically lose
         coverage or cease to be covered by this Act by virtue of termination or resignation from
         office for any lawful cause shall have the option to continue the coverage by paying the
         required premiums thereon.”

         barangay], BARANGAY TANOD, BARANGAY HEALTH WORKER. BARANGAY
         DAY CARE WORKER. OR MEMBER OF THE LUPONG TAGAPAMAYAPA shall
