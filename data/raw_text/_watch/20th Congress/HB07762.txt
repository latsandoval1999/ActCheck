                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to remove gender-based discrimination in the treatment of
death or physical injuries inflicted under exceptional circumstances, as well as in the
prosecution of adultery and concubinage under Act No. 3815, otherwise known as
the “Revised Penal Code”, as amended.

       Article 247 of the Revised Penal Code currently protects a spouse or parent
who kills or injures a spouse or daughter caught in sexual intercourse under specific
circumstances. However, the law excludes sons from this protection and does not
clearly distinguish between the offending spouse and the innocent one. This
proposed measure corrects these gaps by using gender-neutral terms and ensuring
that the law covers all children. It also clarifies the roles of the erring and innocent
spouse.

       For adultery and concubinage, existing provisions impose unequal
consequences on spouses. A married woman faces stricter penalties and a broader
basis for prosecution than a married man, and the crime of adultery is easier to
prove than concubinage. The bill replaces these two offenses with a single,
gender-neutral crime of marital infidelity, ensuring equal accountability for either
spouse and for the third party involved.

       This vital piece of legislation removes longstanding inequities in the law by
adopting one definition and one penalty structure for marital infidelity applicable to
both husbands and wives. It upholds equality between women and men and affirms
the State’s duty under international conventions to eliminate discriminatory laws.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
ELIMINATING GENDER DISCRIMINATION IN CERTAIN CRIMES
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE ACT NO. 3815, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS
“THE REVISED PENAL CODE”, AS AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Article 247 of Act No. 3815, otherwise known as the “Revised Penal
Code”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

                “ARTICLE 247. Death or Physical Injuries Inflicted Under
             Exceptional Circumstances. – Any legally married person who,
             having surprised [his] THE ERRING spouse in the act of
             committing sexual intercourse with another person, shall kill
             any of them or both of them in the act or immediately thereafter,
             or shall inflict upon them any serious physical injury, shall
             suffer the penalty of destierro.

                “If [he] THE INNOCENT SPOUSE shall inflict upon them
             physical injuries or any other kind, [he] THE INNOCENT
             SPOUSE shall be exempt from punishment.

                 “These rules shall be applicable, under the same
             circumstances, to parents with respect to their [daughters]
             CHILDREN under eighteen years of age, and their seducers,
             while the [daughters] CHILDREN are living with their parents.

                “Any person who shall promote or facilitate the prostitution
             of [his wife or daughter] THE SPOUSE OR CHILDREN, or
             shall otherwise have consented to the infidelity of the other
             spouse shall not be entitled to the benefits of this article.”

      SEC. 2. Chapter One under Title Eleven and Article 333 of the same Act are
hereby amended to read as follows:
