                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Republic Act No. 12232 was enacted into law to fix the term of office of
Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) officials at four (4) years, providing greater
stability and continuity in grassroots governance. Under Section 2 of RA 12232, the
next regular Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections (BSKE) are scheduled to
be held in November 2026.

        This bill proposes resetting the next synchronized BSKE to the second Monday
of November 2028 to address acute, compounding global and domestic crises.
Crucially, the country faces severe economic headwinds driven by escalating Middle
East geopolitical tensions. As a heavy net importer of oil, the Philippines is vulnerable
to global fuel price spikes, supply chain disruptions, and heightened domestic
inflation. At the same time, agricultural production and domestic food security are
threatened by the possible devastating impacts of Super El Niño, which has been
predicted to induce droughts, crop damage, and losses in rural livelihoods across the
archipelago.

        Holding a standalone local election in November 2026—just eighteen months
after the May 2025 national polls and right before preparations begin for the May
2028 elections—places unnecessary strain on government resources during a critical
time for our economy. Moving the election date to November 2028 directly serves the
public interest. First, it ensures stability by allowing national and local agencies to
keep their full focus on economic recovery, disaster response, and inflation control
without constant political interruptions. Second, holding the election six months after
the May 2028 national polls gives the Commission on Elections (COMELEC) enough
time to use fully tested and secure voting technology smoothly while preventing voter
fatigue.

     Finally, aligning the election timeline optimizes state resources and
administrative capacity, ensuring that public machinery remains concentrated on
protecting vulnerable households, stabilizing essential prices, and restoring
agricultural resilience. By balancing the duty to hold regular elections with the urgent
need for national economic stability, this bill protects both election integrity and
public welfare.
        In view of these compelling state interests and urgent national economic
priorities, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

  AN ACT POSTPONING THE SYNCHRONIZED BARANGAY AND SANGGUNIANG
  KABATAAN ELECTIONS TO NOVEMBER 2028, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
           REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12232, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


        SECTION 1. Date of Election. — Section 2 of Republic Act No. 12232 is hereby

amended to read as follows:

        "SEC. 2. Date of Election. — The next regular Barangay and Sangguniang

Kabataan elections shall be held on the second Monday of November 2028, and

every four (4) years thereafter."

        SEC. 2. Assumption of Office. — Section 3 of Republic Act No. 12232 is hereby

amended to read as follows:

        "SEC. 3. Assumption of Office. — The term of office of barangay officials and

members of the Sangguniang Kabataan elected after the effectivity of this Act shall

commence at noon on the first day of December next following their election."

        SEC. 3. Transitory Provision. — Section 5 of Republic Act No. 12232 is hereby

amended to read as follows:

        "SEC. 5. Transitory Provision. — Incumbent elective barangay officials serving

their third consecutive term in the same position shall not be eligible to run for the
same position in the November 2028 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan

Elections."

       SEC. 4. Hold-Over Capacity. — The hold-over provision under Section 4 of

Republic Act No. 12232 shall remain in full force and effect. All incumbent barangay

and Sangguniang Kabataan officials shall continue to hold office in a hold-over

capacity until their successors have been duly elected and qualified, unless sooner

removed or suspended for cause in accordance with law.

       SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within thirty (30) days

from the effectivity of this Act, the Commission on Elections (COMELEC) shall

promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 6. Separability Clause. — If any portion or provision of this Act is

declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder of this Act not affected thereby

shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. — All laws, acts, presidential decrees, executive

orders, issuances, presidential proclamations, rules and regulations or parts thereof,

which are contrary to and inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are hereby

repealed, amended or modified accordingly

       SEC. 8. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its

publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
