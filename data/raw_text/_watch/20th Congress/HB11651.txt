AN ACT GRANTING THE ORIENTAL MINDORO- ELECTRIC
COOPERATIVE, INC. (QRMECO) A FRANCHISE TO CONSTRUCT,
INSTALL, ESTABLISH, OPERATE, OWN, MANAGE AND MAINTAIN
DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS FOR THE CONVEYANCE OF ELECTRIC
POWER TO THE END-USERS IN THE CITY OF CALAPAN AND
MUNICIPALITIES OF BACO, BANSUD, BONGABONG, BULALACAO,
GLORIA, MANSALAY, NAUJAN, PINAMALAYAN, POLA, PUERTO
GALERA, ROXAS, SAN TEODORO, SOCORRO, AND VICTORIA IN THE
PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Oriental Mindoro Electric Cooperative, Inc. (ORMECO) plays a critical role in the
electrification, economic development and public welfare of the Province of Oriental
Mindoro. As a distribution utility operating within a defined franchise area, it is imbued with
public interest consistent with Article XII, Section 11 of the 1987 Constitution, which
requires that public utilities operate under a legislative franchise subject to regulation by the
State.

This proposed measure grants ORMECO a franchise to construct, install, establish, operate,
own, manage and maintain a distribution system for the conveyance of electric power to end-
users within the City of Calapan and the municipalities comprising the Province of Oriental
Mindoro.

The bill aligns the operations of the Grantee with the regulatory framework established
under Republic Act No. 9136 (EPIRA), ensuring continued oversight by the Energy
Regulatory Commission, and with Republic Act No. 10531, strengthening the supervisory
role of the National Electrification Administration.

===== [page 2] =====
Recognizing the State policy to accelerate the exploration and development of renewable
energy resources, the measure incorporates provisions consistent with Republic Act No.
9513. It mandates facilitation of renewable energy integration, compliance with Renewable
Portfolio Standards, and implementation of net-metering mechanisms to enable households
and private end-users to generate electricity from renewable sources and supply excess power
to the distribution system under lawful and regulated conditions.

To safeguard the independence of the cooperative and prevent undue political influence over
a public utility, the bill includes a governance provision prohibiting members of the Board of
Directors and the General Manager from having a relationship by consanguinity or affinity up
to the third civil degree with incumbent elective officials within the franchise area. This
measure promotes institutional integrity, policy independence, and public trust in the
management of an essential public service.

The bill likewise strengthens consumer protection, ensures transparency in rate-setting,
preserves regulatory jurisdiction, provides procedural due process in franchise revocation,
and harmonizes the franchise with existing statutory framework.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure ig earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL Nol 1651

Introduced by Representatives Arnan C. Panaligan and Alfonso V. Umali, Jr.

AN ACT GRANTING THE ORIENTAL MINDORO ELECTRIC
COOPERATIVE, INC. (QRMECO) A FRANCHISE TO CONSTRUCT,
INSTALL, ESTABLISH, OPERATE, OWN, MANAGE AND MAINTAIN
DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS FOR THE CONVEYANCE OF ELECTRIC
POWER TO THE END-USERS IN THE CITY OF CALAPAN AND
MUNICIPALITIES OF BACO, BANSUD, BONGABONG,
BULALACAO, GLORIA, MANSALAY, NAUJAN, PINAMALAYAN,
POLA, PUERTO GALERA, ROXAS, SAN TEODORO, SOCORRO,
AND VICTORIA IN THE PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Nature and Scope of Franchise. Subject to the provisions of the
Constitution and applicable laws, rules and regulations, there is hereby granted to the
Oriental Mindoro Electric Cooperative, Inc. (ORMECO) hereunder referred to as the
Grantee, its successors or assigns a franchise to construct, install, establish, operate, own,
manage and maintain in the public interest and for the benefit of its member-consumer-
owners, a distribution system for the conveyance of electric power to the end-users in the
City of Calapan and the municipalities of Baco, Bansud, Bongabong, Bulalacao, Gloria,
Mansalay, Naujan, Pinamalayan, Pola, Puerto Galera, Roxas, San Teodoro, Socorro, and
Victoria in the Province of Oriental Mindoro. As used in this Act, distribution system refers
to the system of wires and associated facilities including sub-transmission lines belonging to
a franchised distribution utility extending between the delivery point on the national
transmission system or generating facility and the metering point or facility of the end-users.

SECTION 2. Manner of Operation of Facilities. All electric distribution facilities,
lines and systems for electric services installed, owned, maintained, operated and managed
by the Grantee, its successors or assigns shall be operated or maintained at all times in a
superior manner, and it shall be the duty of the Grantee, its successors or assigns, whenever

===== [page 4] =====
required to do so by the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), or its legal successor, or the
Department of Energy (DOE), or its legal successor, or any other government agency
concerned to modify, improve and change such facilities or systems in such manner and to
such extent as the progress in science or technology and improvements or innovations in the
electric power services may render reasonable and proper. Whenever practicable, and for
purposes of maintaining order, safety and aesthetics along the highways, roads, streets, alleys
or right-of-way, the Grantee may allow the use of free spaces in its poles, facilities, or right-
of-way by interested parties upon reasonable compensation to the Grantee considering the
costs incurred to accommodate and administer the use of the Grantee's facilities by such
parties. The ERC shall decide in case of dispute or disagreement between the parties.

SECTION 3. Authority of the ERC and National Electrification Administration
(NEA). The Grantee shall secure from the ERC the Certificate of Public Convenience and
Necessity and other license, permit or authority indispensable for the construction and
operation of the electric power distribution system required under Republic Act No. 9136,
without prejudice to the supervisory authority of NEA under Republic Act No. 10531.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish or impair the regulatory authority
of the ERC under Republic Act No. 9136 or the supervisory authority of NEA
under Republic Act No. 10531.

SECTION 4. Excavation and Restoration Works. For the purpose of erecting and
maintaining the poles or other supports for said facilities, wires or other conductors or for the
purpose of laying and maintaining said facilities, wires, cables or other conductors, it shall be
lawful for the Grantee, its successors, or assignees, in coordination with the Department of
Public Works and Highways (DPWH) or the focal government unit (LGU) concerned, as
may be appropriate, to make excavations or lay conduits in any of the public places,
highways, road, streets, lanes, alleys, avenues, sidewalks, or bridges of the province, cities or
municipalities: Provided, however, that public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley,
avenue, sidewalk, or bridge disturbed, altered, or changed by reason of erection of poles or
other supports or the underground laying of wires, other conductors or conduits, shall be
repaired and replaced in workmanlike manner by the Grantee, its successors or assignees, in
accordance with the standards set by the DPWH or the local government unit concerned.
Should the Grantee, its successors or assignees, after ten (10) days from receipt of written
notice from the DPWH or concerned LGU, fail, refuse or neglect to repair or replace any
part of public place, highway, road, street, lane, alley, avenue, sidewalk, or bridge altered,
changed or disturbed by the said Grantee, its successors or assignees, then the DPWH or the
local government unit concerned shall have the right to have the same repaired and replaced
in good order and condition and charge the Grantee, its successors or assignees, double the
amount of the cost and expenses for such repair or replacement.

SECTION 5. Responsibility to the Public. The Grantee shall supply electricity to its
captive market in the least-cost, technically feasible and reliable manner consistent with ERC
and DOE regulations. In the interest of the public good and as far as feasible and whenever
required by the ERC, the Grantee shall modify, improve, or change its facilities, poles, lines,
systems and equipment for the purpose of providing efficient and reliable service and
reduced electricity costs. The Grantee shall charge reasonable and just power rates for its
services to all types of consumers within its franchised area in order that business and
industries shall be able to compete. The Grantee shall have the obligation to provide open
and nondiscriminatory access consistent with Republic Act No. 9136 and applicable ERC

===== [page 5] =====
rules on retail competition and open access. The Grantee shall not engage in any activity that
will constitute an abuse of market power such as unfair trade practices, monopolistic
schemes, and other activities that will hinder competitiveness of business and industries.

SECTION 6. Rates for Services. The retail rates and charges for the distribution of
electric power by the Grantee to its end-users shall be regulated by and subject to the
approval of the ERC or its legal successor. The Grantee shall identify and segregate in its
electricity bill to the end-users the components of the retail rate pursuant to Republic Act No.
9136, unless otherwise amended. Such rates charged by the Grantee to the end-users shall be
made public and transparent. The Grantee shall implement lifeline rate and other subsidized
rate programs mandated by law and ERC regulations.

SECTION 7. Promotion of Consumer Interests. The Grantee herein shall establish a
consumer desk that will handle consumer complaints and ensure adequate promotion of
consumer interests. The Grantee shall act with dispatch on all complaints brought before it.

SECTION 8. Promotion and Integration of Renewable Energy. Consistent
with Republic Act No. 9513, otherwise known as the Renewable Energy Act of 2008, the
Grantee shall actively promote, facilitate and support the development, integration and
utilization of renewable energy resources within its franchise area.

The Grantee shall facilitate the interconnection of renewable energy generation
facilities, subject to technical standards and grid safety requirements prescribed by the ERC,
the Department of Energy, and other competent authorities.

Nothing herein shall authorize the Grantee to refuse or unduly delay the
interconnection of qualified renewable energy facilities that comply with applicable technical
and regulatory requirements, except for valid technical, safety, or system reliability grounds
as determined in accordance with ERC rules.

The Grantee shall comply with Renewable Portfolio Standards and related renewable
energy obligations prescribed by the DOE and ERC.

SECTION 9. Net-Metering and Distributed Energy Resources in Missionary Areas.
End-users within the franchise area may install renewable energy systems for their own
consumption in accordance with Republic Act No. 9513 and applicable DOE and ERC

regulations.

To the extent portions of the franchise area are classified as missionary areas under
applicable DOE or ERC issuances, the implementation of net-metering or other distributed
energy resource (DER) mechanisms shall be subject to:

(a) technical feasibility and system reliability requirements;

(b) consistency with Universal Charge for Missionary Electrification (UCME) policies;
and

(c) prior approval of the Energy Regulatory Commission.

===== [page 6] =====
The Grantee shall allow the interconnection of qualified renewable energy facilities
and shall install appropriate bi-directional metering devices, subject to ERC-approved
technical standards and cost allocation rules.

Compensation for excess electricity generated shall be determined in accordance with
ERC-approved net-metering rules, avoided-cost methodologies, or such other distributed
energy resource compensation mechanisms as may be authorized by the ERC for missionary
areas.

The Grantee shall not impose arbitrary, unreasonable, or discriminatory
interconnection charges beyond those authorized by law or regulation.

SECTION 10. Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructure Protection. The Grantee shall
adopt and implement reasonable and appropriate measures to protect its distribution system,
customer information, operational technology systems, information and communications
technology infrastructure, and other critical facilities from cybersecurity threats, unauthorized
access, disruption, sabotage, and other risks that may affect the reliability, integrity, and
security of electric power distribution services, in accordance with applicable laws,
regulations, and standards issued by the DOE, ERC, NEA, DICT, NPC, and other competent
authorities.

SECTION 11. Independence from Political Influence. In furtherance of the policy
declared under Section 26-A of Presidential Decree No. 269, as amended by Republic Act
No. 10531, and considering the public utility character of the franchise granted herein, no
member of the Board of Directors, President, or General Manager shail be related by
consanguinity or affinity within the third civil degree to an incumbent Governor, Vice
Governor, City Mayor, Municipal Mayor, or Member of the House of Representatives
representing the franchise area.

The NEA shal! promulgate rules governing conflicts of interest, political neutrality,
and independence of management.

The governance of the Grantee and the election of the officers and members of the
Board of Directors shall remain non-partisan in character.

Any violation of this provision shall constitute a ground for disqualification or
removal in accordance with NEA rules and applicable laws.

SECTION 12. Commitment to Provide and Promote the Creation of Employment
Opportunities. The Grantee shall create employment opportunities and shall allow on-the-job
training in its franchise operation: Provided, that priority shall be accorded to the residents
where their principal office is located: Provided, further, That the Grantee shall comply with
the applicable labor standards and allowance entitlement under existing labor laws, rules and
regulations, and similar issuances.

SECTION 13. Right of the Government. A special right is hereby reserved to the
President of the Philippines, in times of war, rebellion, public peril, calamity, emergency,
disaster or disturbance of peace and order: to temporarily take over and operate the stations
or facilities of the Grantee; to temporarily suspend the operation of any station or facility in
the interest of public safety, security, and public welfare; or to authorize the temporary use

===== [page 7] =====
and operation thereof by any agency of the government, upon due compensation to the
Grantee, for the use of the stations or facilities during the period when these shall be so
operated.

SECTION 14. Right of Eminent Domain. Subject to the limitations and procedures
prescribed by law, the Grantee is authorized to exercise the right of eminent domain insofar
as it may be reasonably necessary for the efficient maintenance and operation of services.
The Grantee is authorized to install and maintain its poles, wires, and other facilities over
and across public property and other similar property of the Government of the Philippines,
its branches, or any of its instrumentalities, subject to environmental laws and land
classification laws. The Grantee may acquire such private property as is actually necessary
for the realization of the purposes for which this franchise is granted: Provided, that proper
expropriation proceedings shali have been instituted and just compensation paid, subject to
existing laws governing expropriation and the prior approval of competent authorities.

SECTION 15. Term of Franchise. Unless sooner cancelled, the franchise shall be in
effect for a period of twenty-five (25) years from the date of effectivity of this Act. Failure to
operate continuously for two (2) years, without justifiable cause, shall constitute a ground for
revocation subject to proper administrative determination in accordance with applicable laws
and regulations. Provided, that failure due to force majeure, fortuitous events, or causes
beyond the control of the Grantee shall not constitute ground for revocation.

SECTION 16. Warranty in Favor of the National and Local Governments. The
Grantee shall hold the national, provincial, city, and municipal governments of the
Philippines free from all claims, accounts, demands, or actions arising from accidents
causing injury to persons or damage to properties, during the construction, installation,
operation, and maintenance of the distribution system of the Grantee.

SECTION 17. Liability for Damages. The Grantee shall be liable for any injury to
persons and damage to properties arising from accidents by reason of any defective
construction under this franchise of any neglect or omission to keep its poles and wires in
safe condition.

SECTION 18. Sale, Lease, Transfer, Usufruct, or Assignment of Franchise. The
Grantee shall not sell, lease, transfer, grant the usufruct, or assign this franchise or the rights
and privileges acquired thereunder to any person, firm, company, corporation, or other
commercial or legal entity, or merge with any other corporation, or entity, or shall transfer
the controlling interest of the Grantee, whether as a whole or in parts, and whether
simultaneously or contemporaneously, to any such person, firm, company, corporation, or
entity without the prior approval of the Congress: Provided, That Congress shall be informed
of any sale, lease, transfer, grant of usufruct, sale, or assignment of franchise or the rights
and privileges acquired thereunder, or of the merger, or sale of the controlling interest within
sixty (60) days after the completion of said transaction: Provided, further, That any such
transfer, sale, or assignment is in accordance with the constitutional limitations: Provided,
furthermore, That failure to report to Congress such change of ownership shall constitute a
ground for revocation after due notice and hearing, without prejudice to congressional
action: Provided, finally, That any person or entity to which this franchise is sold,
transferred, or assigned, shall be subject to the same conditions, terms, restrictions, and
limitations of this Act.

SECTION i9. Authority to Enter into Joint Ventures and Management Agreements.
Subject to the Constitution and applicable laws, the Grantee may enter into joint venture
agreements, management contracts, technical assistance agreements, public-private
partnership arrangements, financing agreements, or other similar commercial and operational
arrangements for the rehabilitation, modernization, expansion, improvement, financing,
management, or operation of its distribution system and related facilities.

Such agreements shall not be construed as a sale, lease, transfer, grant of usufruct, or
assignment of this franchise or of any of the rights and privileges granted herein.

No agreement entered into pursuant to this Section shall result in the transfer of
control of the Grantee or of this franchise without prior congressional approval as required
under the Constitution and applicable laws.

The Grantee shall remain fully responsible and accountable for compliance with the
terms and conditions of this franchise and with all applicable laws, rules, regulations, and
orders of the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), the National Electrification
Administration (NEA), and other competent government agencies.

Nothing in this Section shall be construed to authorize the delegation of regulatory
compliance responsibilities or the exercise of powers reserved by law to the Grantee, the
ERC, the NEA, or other competent authorities.

All agreements entered into pursuant to this Section shall be subject to applicable
laws and regulations, including Republic Act No. 9136 and Republic Act No. 10531.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to prohibit management, rehabilitation, or
operational arrangements authorized by the Nationa! Electrification Administration pursuant
to Republic Act No. 10531 and other applicable laws.

SECTION 20. Reportorial Requirement. The Grantee shal] submit an annual report to
Congress, through the Committee on Legislative Franchises of the House of Representatives
and the Committee on Public Services of the Senate, on its compliance with the terms and
conditions of the franchise and on its operations on or before April 30 of every year during
the term of the franchise.

SECTION 21. Applicability of Existing Laws. The Grantee shall comply with and be
subject to the provisions of Commonwealth Act No. 146 or the "Public Service Act”, as
amended, Republic Act No. 9136, Republic Act No. 10531, otherwise known as the
"National Electrification Administration Reform Act of 2013", and Republic Act No. 9513,
otherwise known as the "Renewable Energy Act of 2008".

SECTION 22. Existing Powers. Existing mandates, powers, functions, and privileges
granted to electric cooperatives under Presidential Decree No. 269, as amended, and other
applicable laws shall remain valid and effective unless expressly repealed by subsequent
legislation.

SECTION 23. Repealability and Non-Exclusivity Clause. This franchise shall be
subject to amendment, alteration, or repeal by Congress when the public interest so requires
and shall not be interpreted as an exclusive grant of the privileges herein provided for.

===== [page 9] =====
SECTION 24. Separability Clause. If any of the sections or provisions of this Act is
held invalid, all other provisions not affected thereby shali remain valid.

SECTION 25. Repealing Clause. All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
letters of instruction, administrative rules and regulations or parts thereof which are contrary
to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

All existing permits, certificates, licenses, and authorizations issued by the ERC,
NEA, or any government agency to ORMECO prior to the effectivity of this Act shall remain
valid and effective and shall be deemed issued pursuant to this franchise.

SECTION 26. - Supersession of Existing Franchise and Transition of Authority.
Upon the effectivity of this Act, any prior legislative franchise previously granted to

ORMECO covering substantially the same area and purpose is hereby deemed superseded
by this Act.

The grant of franchise under this Act shall replace and continue the authority of the
Grantee to operate a distribution system within the franchise area, without interruption of
service and without prejudice to the validity of existing permits, licenses, contracts, power
supply agreements, financial obligations, regulatory approvals, and other lawful undertakings
entered into or issued prior to the effectivity of this Act.

Nothing herein shall be construed to impair vested rights, lawful contracts, or existing
obligations of the Grantee, including loans, power supply agreements, and regulatory
approvals previously granted by the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), the National
Electrification Administration (NEA), or any competent government authority, which shall
remain valid and effective subject to existing laws and regulations.

All proceedings, applications, and cases pending before the ERC, NEA, courts, or any
administrative agency at the time of effectivity of this Act shall continue and be resolved as if
filed under this franchise.

The term of the franchise granted herein shall commence from the date of effectivity
of this Act and shall govern the continued operation of the Grantee notwithstanding any
remaining unexpired period under any prior franchise or authority.

SECTION 27. Effectivity Clause. This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days
from its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation,
whichever comes later.

Approved.
