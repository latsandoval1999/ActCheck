                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish the Lingkod Agricultural Pension Fund to provide
a sustainable and dedicated pension system for farmers and fisherfolk who have
long sustained the nation’s food security yet remain among the most economically
vulnerable sectors in society.

       Farmers and fisherfolk form the backbone of the Philippine economy. They
ensure the steady supply of food to Filipino households, contribute significantly to
rural development, and help stabilize national markets. Despite their invaluable role,
many agricultural workers reach old age without adequate savings, social
protection, or access to formal retirement benefits. Unlike employees in the public
and private sectors who are covered by established pension systems, a large number
of small-scale farmers and fisherfolk operate in the informal economy and remain
excluded from meaningful retirement security.

      This measure addresses that gap by creating the Agricultural Pension Fund
(APF), a dedicated fund that will provide pension benefits to qualified agricultural
workers, especially farmers and fisherfolk, upon reaching retirement age.

        By establishing a structured and government-backed pension mechanism, this
bill recognizes the dignity of agricultural labor and affirms the State’s commitment
to inclusive social protection. It provides long-overdue security to farmers and
fisherfolk who have devoted their lives to feeding the nation and deserve stability
and support in their retirement years.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING AN AGRICULTURAL PENSION                         FUND TO PROVIDE
PENSION   BENEFITS  TO   FARMERS AND                          FISHERFOLK, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Lingkod Agricultural
Pension Fund Act”.

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared a policy of the State to,
from time to time, review to upgrade the pensions and other benefits due to retirees
of both the government and private sectors. It acknowledges that farmers and fisher
folk are integral part to the country's development, and therefore shall accord due
recognition to their great contribution by providing them with pension benefits to
assist them with their basic needs when they reach the age of retirement.

       SEC. 3. Creation of the Agricultural Pension Fund. — There is hereby established
an Agricultural Pension Fund (APF) that shall provide pension benefits to
agricultural workers, especially farmers and fisher folk.

      SEC. 4. Implementing Agency. — The Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation
(PCIC), an attached agency of the Department of Agriculture (DA), shall be the
implementing agency of the provisions of this Act. In addition to its existing duties
and responsibilities, it shall:

          a. Create a pension plan for agricultural pensioners;

          b. Manage and invest the APF to ensure its sustainability;

          c. Determine the criteria for eligibility, in coordination with relevant
       government agencies; and

          d.​ Perform other duties and functions towards the efficient and effective
       implementation of this Act.

        SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the PCIC Board of Directors, in consultation with the Secretary
of Agriculture and representatives of recognized farmers and fisherfolks groups,
shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.
​
        SEC. 6. Appropriations. — Such amount necessary to implement the provisions
of this Act shall be included in the annual appropriations of the DA.

       SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
