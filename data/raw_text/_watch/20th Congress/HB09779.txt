                              EXPLANATORY NOTE


       The teaching profession is among the most demanding vocations in the
country. Public school teachers devote long hours to lesson preparation, classroom
instruction, and administrative responsibilities, often at the expense of their own
health and well-being. In recent years, more and more cases have shown that
teachers are experiencing stress, burnout, and illness brought about by heavy
workloads, overcrowded classrooms, and the emotional demands of guiding
students.

      Despite their indispensable role in shaping the nation’s future, teachers are
not sufficiently provided with paid sick leave benefits that would allow them to
recover without financial strain. This proposed measure seeks to grant all public
school teachers fifteen (15) days of sick leave with pay per year. Such provision
recognizes the reality that teachers, like all workers, are vulnerable to illness and
must be afforded adequate time to recuperate.

       By institutionalizing this benefit, the State affirms its commitment to
safeguarding the welfare of educators. Healthy teachers are better equipped to
deliver quality instruction, maintain classroom productivity, and contribute to the
overall advancement of the education system. Ensuring their well-being is not only a
matter of fairness but also an investment in the future of Filipino learners and the
nation.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
PROVIDING SICK LEAVE BENEFITS OF FIFTEEN DAYS WITH PAY PER YEAR
                TO ALL PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Sick Leave with Pay for Public School Teachers. All public
school teachers shall be entitled to sick leave benefits of fifteen (15) days with pay
per year. In the computation of the same, Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays shall be
excluded.


       The leave privilege provided under this Act shall be in addition to the service
credits and the Proportional Vacation Pay already provided under present laws and
rules: Provided, That the same shall be non-cumulative and non-convertible to cash.


       SEC. 2. Implementing Rules and Regulations. The Department of
Education      shall   promulgate   the   rules and regulations necessary        for the
implementation of this Act within a period of thirty (30) days after its effectivity.


       SEC. 3. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, rules and regulations, and
other issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.
