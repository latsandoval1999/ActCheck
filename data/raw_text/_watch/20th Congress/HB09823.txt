                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Access to quality education is a constitutional right and a vital foundation
of national development. Yet many Filipino learners continue to face difficulties
in obtaining timely and affordable access to textbooks and other essential
learning materials. In an increasingly digital world, educational resources must
be made more accessible, inclusive, and responsive to the needs of learners.

      This bill requires that all textbooks and instructional materials submitted
for approval, adoption, or procurement by the Department of Education (DepEd)
be accompanied by a digital copy that may be made available free of charge to
learners, teachers, and schools through DepEd-authorized platforms.
Compliance with this requirement shall be a condition for DepEd approval and
procurement.

      The measure likewise promotes the development and use of Open
Educational Resources (OER), which are freely accessible and openly licensed
educational materials that may be used, adapted, and shared to expand learning
opportunities.

       By ensuring the free digital availability of approved learning resources, this
measure will reduce costs for families, expand access to quality educational
materials, support flexible learning modalities, and help build a more inclusive,
resilient, and future-ready basic education system.

     The measure respects and preserves the intellectual property rights of
authors and publishers while advancing the State's responsibility to make
education more accessible to all.

      In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

                          “AN ACT
     REQUIRING THE FREE DIGITAL PROVISION OF TEXTBOOKS
     USED   IN  BASIC  EDUCATION,   ENCOURAGING   OPEN
     EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

        Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
 Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “E-Textbook Para
 Sa Lahat Act.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to
 promote accessible, inclusive, and equitable education for all. Recognizing the
 transformative potential of digital learning tools, the State shall ensure that
 approved textbooks and learning resources used in basic education are made
 available in digital format at no cost to learners, without prejudice to existing
 policies governing the provision of printed instructional materials. The State
 shall likewise encourage the creation, adaptation, and use of Open Educational
 Resources (OER) to foster collaboration, innovation, and equity in education.

     SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

     a) “Digital Copy” refers to an electronic version of a textbook or
        instructional material that faithfully reproduces the content of
        the approved printed version and is accessible through
        commonly used digital devices;

     b) “Open Educational Resources (OER)” refer to teaching, learning,
        and research materials in any medium that are in the public
        domain or released under an open license that permits free
        access, use, adaptation, and redistribution;

     c) “Instructional Materials” refer to textbooks, workbooks, teacher's
        guides, reference materials, and other learning resources
         intended for use in basic education; and

      d) “DepEd-Authorized Platform” refers to any digital repository,
         learning management system, website, application, or other
         platform officially designated by the Department of Education for
         the distribution of learning resources.

      SECTION 4. Mandatory Free Digital Format and Condition for DepEd
Approval. – All publishers, authors, copyright owners, or other rights holders of
textbooks and instructional materials submitted for evaluation, approval,
adoption, endorsement, or procurement by the Department of Education
(DepEd) shall:

   a) Provide a digital copy of the material in a format accessible on standard
      digital devices, including but not limited to PDF or EPUB;

   b) Submit such digital copy to the DepEd free of charge; and

   c) Authorize the DepEd to make such digital copy available through DepEd-
      authorized platforms or other authorized channels solely for educational
      purposes and without charge to learners, teachers, and schools.

      Compliance with the foregoing requirements shall be a prerequisite for the
approval, adoption, endorsement, procurement, or inclusion of such materials
in the official list of textbooks and learning resources of the DepEd.

       The digital copies provided under this Act shall not be sold, licensed for
profit, or otherwise commercially exploited by any person or entity.

      SECTION 5. Promotion of Open Educational Resources. – To further
enhance access to quality education, the DepEd shall encourage the
development, use, adaptation, and dissemination of Open Educational
Resources (OER), which are freely accessible and openly licensed educational
materials that may be used, shared, adapted, and redistributed.

      The DepEd shall formulate a program for the development, adoption, and
dissemination of OER and may collaborate with state universities and colleges,
local government units, private educational institutions, civil society
organizations, professional associations, and development partners for this
purpose.

      The DepEd shall likewise establish policies providing incentives and
support mechanisms for schools, educators, and authors that develop OER for
use in the basic education curriculum.
      SECTION 6. Non-Compliance. Failure to comply with the requirements
of this Act shall be sufficient ground for the rejection, delisting, or
disqualification of the textbook or instructional material from DepEd approval,
adoption, endorsement, or procurement, without prejudice to other
administrative remedies available under applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

       SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DepEd shall
issue the necessary rules and regulations within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act. Such regulations shall include, but not be limited to:

    a) Technical standards for digital formats;
    b) Guidelines for access and distribution;
    c) Provisions to ensure the digital version is identical in content
       to the approved print version;
    d) Measures to ensure accessibility for learners with disabilities;
    e) Data privacy and copyright safeguards.

      SECTION 8. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected
thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

      SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive and administrative
orders, rules, and regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

       SECTION10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
