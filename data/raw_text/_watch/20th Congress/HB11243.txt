                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Persons with disabilities should not be required to repeatedly renew their
identification cards when their disabilities are permanent and irreversible. The
periodic renewal of PWD identification cards creates unnecessary inconvenience,
administrative burdens, and expenses for persons who are already living with
permanent conditions.

       This measure seeks to amend Republic Act No. 7277, as amended,
otherwise known as the “Magna Carta for Persons with Disability,” by providing
for the lifetime validity of PWD identification cards issued to persons with
permanent disabilities.

        Under the proposed measure, permanent disability shall be medically
certified and appropriately verified, while the PWD identification card shall remain
valid for the lifetime of the cardholder. Replacement shall remain available in
cases of loss, damage, change in material information, or when the card becomes
unusable, without requiring automatic re-certification of the permanent disability.

       The measure likewise provides that existing PWD identification cards
issued to persons with permanent disabilities shall be deemed lifetime-valid,
subject to the applicable rules on replacement and verification.

        By eliminating unnecessary periodic renewal while maintaining appropriate
verification and registration safeguards, this measure seeks to make government
services more accessible, efficient, and responsive to the needs of persons with
permanent disabilities.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                             “AN ACT
      FURTHER AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277, OTHERWISE
      KNOWN AS THE “MAGNA CARTA FOR PERSONS WITH
      DISABILITY,” AS AMENDED, TO PROVIDE FOR THE LIFETIME
      VALIDITY OF IDENTIFICATION CARDS ISSUED TO PERSONS
      WITH PERMANENT DISABILITY”

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Section 4 of Republic Act No. 7277, as amended, otherwise
known as the “Magna Carta for Persons with Disability is hereby amended
to read as follows:

           “SECTION 4. Definition of Terms: For purposes of this Act, these
      terms are defined as follows:

                                     xxx

            (c) Disability shall mean (1) a physical or mental
            impairment that substantially limits one or more
            psychological, physiological, or anatomical functions of an
            individual or activities of such individual; (2) a record of
            such an impairment; or (3) being regarded as having such
            an impairment;

            (c-1) Permanent disability refers to a physical, mental,
            intellectual, or sensory impairment that substantially limits
            one or more psychological, physiological, or anatomical
            functions of an individual or activities of such individual and
            is medically determined to be irreversible, non-transitory, or
            reasonably expected to continue for the lifetime of the
            individual. The permanent nature of the disability shall be
            certified by a licensed physician with appropriate
            competence to determine such disability and verified by the
            appropriate local government office, including the Persons
            with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) or the Local Social
            Welfare and Development Office (LSWDO), in accordance
            with the standards and procedures prescribed by the
            National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA), in
            coordination with the Department of Health (DOH) and the
            Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD). x x
            x”

       SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the NCDA, in coordination with the Department
of Health (DOH), Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD),
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), Persons with Disability
Affairs Offices (PDAOs), Local Social Welfare and Development Offices (LSWDOs),
National Privacy Commission (NPC), and other relevant government agencies, shall
formulate the rules and regulations necessary for the proper implementation of this
Act.

      SECTION        4.      Separability       Clause.       -      If  any part
or provision of this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other
provisions not otherwise affected shall remain in full force and effect.

      SECTION 5. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
