                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Personnel Economic Relief Allowance (PERA) was established to help government
employees cope with the rising cost of living and the effects of inflation. However, its current rate of
Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) per month has remained unchanged since 2009.

        Over the years, increases in the prices of food, transportation, electricity, fuel, and other
essential goods and services have significantly reduced the purchasing power of government
employees. The existing allowance is no longer sufficient to provide the economic relief it was
intended to deliver.

       This measure seeks to increase the monthly PERA of government employees from Two
Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) to Five Thousand Pesos (₱5,000.00), including qualified personnel in
national and local government offices, as well as military and uniformed personnel.

       It likewise provides for an automatic yearly adjustment equivalent to any increase in the
annual inflation rate to help preserve the purchasing power of the allowance and ensure that
government employees continue to receive meaningful economic assistance.

      Recognizing the important role of public servants in delivering essential government services
and implementing programs for the Filipino people, this measure seeks to provide them with fairer
and more responsive economic support.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                              AN ACT
   INCREASING THE PERSONNEL ECONOMIC RELIEF ALLOWANCE (PERA) OF
  GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES TO FIVE THOUSAND PESOS AND APPROPRIATING
                         FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Dagdag PERA Act".

        SEC. 2. Personnel Economic Relief Allowance (PERA). – The Personnel Economic Relief
Allowance (PERA) granted to all government employees in the amount of Two Thousand Pesos
(₱2,000.00) per month is hereby adjusted to Five Thousand Pesos (₱5,000.00) per month, and shall be
subject to an automatic yearly adjustment equivalent to any increase in the annual inflation rate in the
country.

        SEC. 3. Coverage. – The PERA shall be granted to all government personnel, whether
employed by the national or local governments, appointive or elective, and whether occupying regular,
contractual, or casual positions, as provided for by Republic Act No. 6758, or the "Compensation and
Position Classification Act of 1989," as amended, as well as military and uniformed personnel:
Provided, That government personnel stationed abroad already receiving overseas allowances shall
not be entitled to the provisions of this Act.

        SEC. 4. Appropriations. – For the first year of the implementation of this Act, funds necessary
for the PERA of national and local government units shall be charged against savings, which for this
purpose includes unreleased appropriations and other programmed appropriations. Thereafter, the
amount necessary shall be provided in the annual General Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity
of this Act, the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) shall promulgate the necessary rules
and regulations for the proper implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall continue in full force
and effect.

        SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, proclamations, rules and
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
        SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

       Approved.
