                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Over the years, Parañaque City has experienced sustained population growth
driven by residential expansion and urban development. This increase has inevitably
translated into greater demand for public education, particularly at the secondary
level. As enrollment continues to grow, ensuring that students receive quality
instruction and adequate support services requires a more responsive institutional
arrangement.

        This measure seeks to separate the Senior High School Department of Don
Bosco National High School in Barangay Don Bosco Homes, Parañaque City and
convert it into an independent institution to be known as the Don Bosco Senior High
School. With 612 learners utilizing only 10 classrooms, Don Bosco National High
School has experienced increasing classroom congestion, requiring larger class
sizes and limiting the availability of learning spaces. The separation of the senior
high school department will help address these challenges by allowing for more
efficient use of facilities and educational resources. It will likewise allow the school to
focus exclusively on the unique academic, technical-vocational, sports, and arts
education needs of senior high school learners.
        The establishment of the Don Bosco Senior High School is a necessary
investment in the future of Parañaque's youth. As enrollment continues to grow, our
educational institutions must grow with it. Every learner deserves a school system
that is equipped to provide the enabling conditions where dreams of young people
may be nurtured.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Establishment of a Senior High School. — The Senior High School of Don
Bosco National High School in Barangay Don Bosco, City of Paranaque, Metro Manila is
hereby separated from the Don Bosco National High School and converted into an
independent senior high school to be known as the Don Bosco Senior High School.

The Don Bosco Senior High School shall be under the supervision of the Department of
Education (DepEd) and shall offer academic, technical-vocational- livelihood (TVL), sports,
and arts and design tracks as may be determined appropriate by the DepEd, in accordance
with existing laws, rules, and regulations.

SEC. 2. General Supervision. — The Don Bosco Senior High School shall operate under
the general supervision of the DepEd, through the Schools Division Office of Paranaque City,
and in coordination with the Regional Office of the DepEd.

The Secretary of Education shall issue the necessary guidelines for the effective
implementation of this Act, including the organization, staffing, and operational standards of
the school.

SEC. 3. Appropriations. — The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of the Don Bosco Senior High School.
The funding requirement for its initial implementation shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the DepEd, and thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its
continued operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act (GAA).

SEC. 4. Implementation. — The DepEd shall take the necessary steps to ensure that the
required infrastructure, teaching and non-teaching personnel, equipment, and instructional
materials are provided. The Department may enter into partnerships with the local
government unit (LGU) and other stakeholders to facilitate implementation.

The Secretary of Education shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to
carry out the purpose of this Act.

SEC. 5. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SEC. 6. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
