                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish the Benguet Agricultural Logistics and Transport
Support Program to assist highland vegetable farmers in the Province of Benguet by
providing organized logistics, transport support facilities, and market linkages that
will reduce post-harvest losses and improve the efficiency of transporting
agricultural products from farms to major markets across the country.

        The Province of Benguet serves as one of the primary sources of
semi-temperate vegetables in the Philippines, supplying significant volumes of
produce to major urban markets throughout Luzon. Farmers in the province play a
critical role in ensuring the steady supply of fresh vegetables that contribute to the
country’s food security and agricultural economy. However, despite their vital
contribution, many vegetable farmers continue to face serious challenges in bringing
their produce to market.

       One of the major constraints confronting farmers in Benguet is the high cost
and inefficiency of transporting vegetables from remote farming areas to trading
centers and urban markets. Many farmers rely on fragmented and privately
arranged transport systems that often result in delayed deliveries, higher
transportation costs, and increased post-harvest losses due to improper handling
and inadequate logistics infrastructure. These challenges significantly reduce the
income of farmers and limit the profitability and sustainability of vegetable farming
in the province.

        Recognizing these challenges, this measure proposes the establishment of a
government-supported agricultural logistics and transport assistance program that
will provide transport support services, establish agricultural transport hubs, deploy
vegetable transport vehicles, and improve the coordination of vegetable distribution
systems linking Benguet farmers to major markets across the country. The bill also
strengthens collaboration among the Department of Agriculture, the Provincial
Government of Benguet, the Department of Trade and Industry, and the Department
of Public Works and Highways to ensure an efficient and sustainable agricultural
logistics system for the province.
       Improving transport efficiency, reducing post-harvest losses, and
strengthening market access will increase the income of vegetable farmers, enhance
the competitiveness of Benguet’s agricultural sector, and ensure the continuous
supply of highland vegetables to markets nationwide.

      In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is respectfully sought.

                           AN ACT
ESTABLISHING THE BENGUET AGRICULTURAL LOGISTICS AND
TRANSPORT SUPPORT PROGRAM TO ASSIST HIGHLAND VEGETABLE
FARMERS IN THE PROVINCE OF BENGUET, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Benguet Agricultural
Logistics Support Act.”

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State recognizes the vital role of farmers in
ensuring national food security and sustaining the agricultural economy. It shall
adopt policies that improve agricultural productivity, reduce post-harvest losses, and
increase the income of farmers.

        The Province of Benguet is the country’s primary source of semi-temperate
vegetables, supplying major markets across Luzon. However, farmers continue to
face significant challenges in transporting their produce due to high transportation
costs, inadequate logistics support, and the absence of organized vegetable transport
systems.

       Towards this end, the State shall establish a government-supported
agricultural logistics and transport assistance program to reduce post-harvest losses,
improve market access, and increase the profitability of vegetable farming in
Benguet.

       SEC. 3. Establishment of the Benguet Agricultural Logistics and Transport Support
Program. - There is hereby established a Benguet Agricultural Logistics and
Transport Support Program to assist farmers in transporting vegetables and other
agricultural products from farms and trading posts to major markets across the
country.

       The program shall provide logistical support including but not limited to:

                                           1
          a.​ Transport assistance for farmers and farmer cooperatives;

          b.​ Establishment of agricultural transport hubs;

         c.​ Deployment of government-supported vegetable transport vehicles;
      and

         d.​ Improvement of vegetable distribution systems linking Benguet to
      major markets.

​      SEC. 4. Agricultural Transport Support Facilities. – The Department of
Agriculture (DA), in coordination with the Provincial Government of Benguet, shall
establish Agricultural Transport Support Facilities in strategic areas within the
province to facilitate the efficient transport of vegetables.

      These facilities shall include:

          a.​ Vegetable consolidation loading stations;

          b.​ Packaging and handling areas to reduce damage during transport; and

          c.​ Coordinated scheduling of transport vehicles to major trading centers.

       SEC. 5. Priority Access for Farmer Cooperatives. - Duly registered farmers’
cooperatives and associations in the Province of Benguet shall be given priority
access to the logistics and transport support services under this Act. The Department
of Agriculture shall assist farmer organizations in organizing collective transport
systems to reduce transportation costs.

      SEC. 6. Institutional Market Linkages. - The Department of Agriculture and the
Department of Trade and Industry shall assist Benguet farmers in establishing
transport routes and supply agreements with:

          a.​ Major urban markets;

         b.​ Institutional buyers such as hospitals, schools, and government feeding
      programs;

          c.​ Supermarkets and food distribution companies; and

          d.​ Other regional trading centers across the country.

       SEC. 7. Implementing Agencies. - The Department of Agriculture shall serve as
the lead implementing agency in coordination with: The Provincial Government of
Benguet, The Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), The Department of Public
Works and Highways (DPWH), and Farmers’ cooperatives and organizations.

       SEC. 8. Funding. - The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act under the Department of
Agriculture. The Provincial Government of Benguet and partner agencies may also
provide counterpart funding for infrastructure and equipment.

       SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within one hundred eighty (180)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Agriculture, in coordination
with the Provincial Government of Benguet, shall promulgate the necessary
implementing rules and regulations.

      SEC. 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, rules and regulations, or
parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.

      SEC. 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
