                              AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR FAIR TREATMENT OF CERTAIN SENIOR CITIZEN PENSIONERS
BY GRANTING ANNUAL CHRISTMAS CASH GIFT AND PENSION INCREASE TO
PENSIONERS OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY SYSTEM AND GOVERNMENT SERVICE
INSURANCE SYSTEM WHO ARE BENEFICIARIES OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7699, “AN
ACT INSTITUTING LIMITED PORTABILITY SCHEME IN THE SOCIAL SECURITY
INSURANCE SYSTEMS BY TOTALIZING THE WORKERS’ CREDITABLE SERVICES
OF CONTRIBUTIONS IN EACH OF THE SYSTEMS”

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks for a fair treatment to our senior citizen pensioners who are
bene iciaries of Republic Act No. 7699, “An Act Instituting Limited Portability Scheme
in the Social Security Insurance Systems by Totalizing the Workers’ Creditable
Services or Contributions in each of the Systems” but are not deemed quali ied, based
on existing implementing rules and regulations and other issuances of the SSS and
GSIS Boards, to receive some of the bene its given to regular pensioners, speci ically
the annual Christmas cash gift and right to pension increase.

While RA 7699 appears to be a special law, an accommodation to individual workers
who have not “saved enough” in either system, it is necessary to remind the managers
of our social security agencies that the law they are implementing and the funds that
they are zealously guarding were created to ful il the State’s obligation to protect its
people in times of inancial want, especially when they are no longer considered
employable. R.A. No. 7699 provides the formula to enable workers to bene it from the
fruits of their savings or contributions in both systems.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Provisions of any general or special law or rules and regulations to the
contrary notwithstanding, pensioners of the Social Security System (SSS) and the
Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) who are bene iciaries of Republic Act
No. 7699, “An Act Instituting Limited Portability Scheme in the Social Security
Insurance Systems by Totalizing the Workers’ Creditable Services or Contributions in
each of the Systems” shall be entitled to receive a Christmas cash gift from the GSIS,
13th month pension from the SSS, and pension increases similar to old-age pensioners
of both systems.

SECTION 2. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the SSS and the
GSIS, in consultation with the Department of Finance and accredited organizations of
pensioners of the SSS and GSIS shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary
to implement this Act: Provided, That any con lict in the interpretation of the law and
the implementing rules and regulations shall be resolved in favor of the pensioners.

                                                                           3|Page
SECTION 3. Nothing in this Act shall be construed to diminish or reduce the bene its
being enjoyed by pensioners under R.A. No. 7699 arising from existing laws,
issuances, or SSS and GSIS Board Resolutions.

SECTION 4. All laws, presidential decrees or issuances, executive orders, letters of
instruction, administrative orders, SSS and GSIS Board resolutions, and rules and
regulation contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, modi ied, or amended accordingly.

SECTION 5. This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
