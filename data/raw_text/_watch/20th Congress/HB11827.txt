AN ACT

PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Despite the indispensable roles farmers and fisherfolk play in our country’s goal to secure stable and
sufficient food, they remain among the most economically vulnerable sectors in the country, under a
never-ending cycle of poor access to education, limited opportunities for their families, debilitating
poverty, and inadequate social mobility.

This bill therefore seeks to provide full educational scholarships to their children and dependents, ensure
equitable access to quality education, from basic to tertiary level, and establish a dedicated fund to
support this program while promoting inclusive and equitable development in the agricultural sector.

Comprehensive scholarship benefits covering tuition and other school fees, as well as essential support
such as board and lodging, transportation, allowances for books, clothing, and food shail be provided to
ensure that financial constraints will not prevent qualified beneficiaries from pursuing and completing
their education, whether in public or private institutions.

For sustainability of the program, an educational benefits fund shall be established exclusively for the
implementation of the scholarship program and designates the UniFAST Governing Board, in coordination
with the Department of Agriculture and representatives from the sector, to administer and manage the
fund and formulate the guidelines to ensure efficient and transparent implementation.

Recognizing that education is a powerful tool in breaking the cycle of poverty, this proposed legislation
adheres to the State’s policy to promote social justice, improve access to education, ag#l uplift the livingy
conditions of farmers and fisherfolk.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

Section 1. — This Act shall be known as the “Children and Dependents of Farmers and Fisherfolks
Scholarship Act of 2026”.

SEC. 2. Full scholarship for Children and Dependents of Farmers. — Children and dependents of
farmers listed in the Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) shall be entitled to
full scholarship from basic education until they complete tertiary education, subject to rules and
regulations as mandated by this Act. The benefits shall include all expenses for education,
whether in public or private school, such as but not limited to tuition fees, miscellaneous and
other fees, board and lodging, transportation expenses, allowances for books, clothing, food and
other similar expenses.

SEC. 3. Special Fund for Educational Benefits. — There is hereby established a Special Fund, to be
known as the “Educational Benefits for Farmers and Fisherfolks Fund”, hereinafter referred to as
the “Fund”, in the amount of One Hundred Million pesos (P100,000,000.00). This fund shall be
perpetually maintained and used exclusively to provide educational benefits to the children and
dependents of farmers and fisherfolk; Provided, That not more than five percent (5%) of the Fund
shall be allocated for administrative expenses related to the implementation of this Act.

SEC. 4. Administration of the Fund. — The Unified Student Financial Assistance for Tertiary
Education (UniFAST) Governing Board, in consultation with the Department of Agriculture (DA),
and three (3) representatives from the farmers and fisherfolks selected from a verified procedure
of selection as agreed upon by the farmers and fisherfolks, shall have the primary responsibility
of managing and supervising the Fund. It shall also be responsible for formulating the mechanics,

shall serve as the Secretariat providing administrative support to the Board and shall perform

SEC. 5. implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this
Act, the UniFAST Board, in consultation with the DA, shall promulgate the necessary rules and

SEC. 6. Separability Clause. — lf any provision or part hereof is held invalid or unconstitutional, the
remainder of the Jaw or the provision not otherwise affected shall remain valid and subsisting.

SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. — Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive order, letter of
instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation contrary to, or inconsistent with the

provisions of this Act is hereby repealed, modified or amended accordingly.

SEC. 8. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in at least two
(2) newspapers of general circulation.
