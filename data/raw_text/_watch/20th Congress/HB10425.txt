                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

The increasing frequency of floods, landslides, forest degradation, and other environmental
challenges highlights the need to strengthen environmental governance at the local level. Local
government units (LGUs) play a vital role in protecting and managing forests, watersheds, coastal
resources, and other natural assets. However, many LGUs still lack dedicated Environment and
Natural Resources Officers (ENROs), as their appointment under the Local Government Code of
1991 remains optional. This results in many LGUs either having no designated ENRO or
assigning environmental responsibilities to personnel who lack the technical expertise or security
of tenure necessary to ensure continuity in environmental management programs.

Therefore, this measure seeks to amend Section 484 of Republic Act No. 7160 by making the
appointment of Provincial, City, and Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Officers
mandatory and establishing the position as regular and permanent. Institutionalizing these
positions will strengthen the capacity of LGUs to implement environmental protection, watershed
rehabilitation, biodiversity conservation, climate change adaptation, and disaster risk reduction
programs.

By ensuring that every local government has a qualified and permanent Environment and Natural
Resources Officer, this bill seeks to improve policy continuity, strengthen environmental law
enforcement, enhance disaster risk reduction and climate resilience, and promote the responsible
stewardship of the country's natural resources for the benefit of present and future generations.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

         AN ACT MANDATING THE PERMANENT APPOINTMENT OF
  PROVINCIAL/CITY/MUNICIPAL ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL RESOURCES
  OFFICER AMENDING SECTION 484 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160 OTHERWISE
           KNOWN AS THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION. 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be referred to as the "Mandatory Permanent
Appointment of Provincial/City/Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Officer
Act".

SEC. 2. Sec. 484 (a) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government
Code of 1991, is hereby amended to read as follows:

         (a) No person shall be appointed environment and natural resources
             officer unless he is a citizen of the Philippines, of good moral
             character, a holder of a college degree preferably in forestry,
             environment, agriculture, or agroforestry from a recognized college
             or university, with first-grade civil service eligibility or its equivalent,
             and PREFERABLY a resident of the local government unit
             concerned. The Provincial and City ENRO shall at least have a
             masteral degree in forestry, environment, agriculture, or agro-
             forestry and must at least have five (5) years-experience in
             environmental and natural resources management, conservation,
             and utilization and three (3) years-experience in the case of the
              Municipal ENRO. THE PROVINCIAL AND CITY ENRO SHALL
              HAVE A SALARY GRADE OF 24 AND THE MUNICIPAL ENRO
              SHALL HAVE A SALARY GRADE OF 22.

              The appointment of Provincial/City/Municipal Environment and
              Natural Resources Officer (P/C/MENRO) SHALL BE MANDATORY
              AND SHALL BE A REGULAR AND PERMANENT POSITION IN
              THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS.

SEC. 3. Repealing Clause. – All laws, acts, decrees, executive orders, administrative
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SEC. 4. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect upon publication in the Official Gazette or
in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
