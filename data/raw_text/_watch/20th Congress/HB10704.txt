                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to promote the responsible and wider adoption of behind-the-meter
electricity generation and energy storage systems by simplifying regulatory requirements,
removing unnecessary restrictions, and establishing clear and uniform procedures for
their installation and, when necessary, interconnection with the distribution system.


       Reliable, affordable, and sustainable electricity is essential to the daily lives of
Filipino families, the growth of businesses, and the continued development of our
communities. Today, consumers have greater access to technologies such as rooftop solar
panels, battery energy storage systems, and other distributed energy resources that allow
them to generate and store electricity primarily for their own use. These technologies can
reduce electricity expenses, improve the reliability of power supply, and lessen
dependence on the electric grid.


       However, consumers continue to encounter unnecessary procedures, regulatory
requirements, and restrictions before they can install and operate these systems, even
when such systems are intended solely for their own use and do not affect the public
electricity network. Subjecting consumer-owned systems to requirements designed for
commercial power producers may impose disproportionate burdens, discourage
investment, and prevent households and businesses from taking advantage of
technologies that can contribute to greater energy efficiency and resilience.


       Through this measure, consumers who generate or store electricity solely for their
own should not necessarily be subjected to regulations intended for commercial power
producers. Where interconnection with the distribution system is necessary, this measure
seeks to establish clear, transparent, predictable, and uniform procedures that protect the
safety and reliability of the electric power system without creating unreasonable barriers
to adoption.
          The measure is consistent with the energy priorities articulated by President
Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. in his 2026 State of the Nation Address, particularly the need to
ensure reliable and affordable electricity and strengthen the country's energy security.
Enabling consumers to generate and store electricity for their own use can complement
the government's efforts to develop a more resilient, sustainable, and secure energy
sector.


          By removing unnecessary regulatory obstacles while preserving appropriate safety,
technical, and regulatory standards, this bill empowers Filipino consumers to take a more
active role in meeting their own energy needs. It promotes consumer choice, encourages
investment in modern and cleaner energy technologies, supports energy security, and
contributes to a more reliable and resilient electric power sector.


          In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
