                         AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR HEALTH BENEFITS AND INSURANCE COVERAGE FOR

 INFORMAL WORKERS HEALTH INSURANCE PROGRAM, PROVIDING
GOVERNMENT ASSISTANCE FOR PREMIUM CONTRIBUTIONS, AND FOR
                     OTHER PURPOSES

                           EXPLANATORY ROTE

       The Constitution recognizes the right of the people to health and directs
the State to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health
development. Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise known as the "Universal Health
 Care Act," establishes a national health insurance framework covering all
 Filipinos through direct and indirect membership and provides entitlement to
 health benefit packages under the National Health Insurance Program.

        Despite this framework, many workers in the informal economy continue
 to experience difficulty in maintaining continuous health insurance coverage.
 Their income is often irregular, seasonal, or unstable, and they may lack an
  employer who can facilitate registration, premi um payment, or access to health
  services.

       Informal workers include self-employed workers, own-account workers,
  vendors, home-based workers, paid and unpaid family workers, seasonal
  workers, micro-entrepreneurs, platform-based workers, and other workers
  whose livelihood is not covered, or is insufficiently covered, by formal
  employment arrangements.
