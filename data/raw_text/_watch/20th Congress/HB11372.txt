                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Access to appropriate hospital care depends not only on the presence of a health
facility, but also on whether that facility has the capacity, services, equipment, and
personnel required to respond to the needs of the population it serves.

        The 1987 Constitution directs the State to protect and promote the right to health
of the people. Consistent with this mandate, the Universal Health Care Act seeks to ensure
that all Filipinos have equitable access to quality and affordable health care services
through an integrated and responsive health system.

       Enacted in 2022, Republic Act No. 11720 established the Joni Villanueva General
Hospital (JVGH) in the Municipality of Bocaue, Province of Bulacan, as a Level II general
hospital under the direct control and supervision of the Department of Health (DOH).

        In the years since its establishment under R.A. No. 11720, JVGH has experienced
substantial growth in the utilization of its services. Its bed occupancy rate increased from
34.05% in 2023 to 125.97% in 2024 and 119.71% in 2025, and further reached 145.15% as of
June 2026. Admissions rose from 656 in 2023 to 10,494 in 2025, while emergency room
visits increased from 10,638 to 36,352 and outpatient visits from 26,551 to 60,144 during
the same period.

        This increasing utilization has placed corresponding pressure on the hospital's
existing capacity. JVGH currently has a licensed capacity of 100 beds, while its
implementing bed capacity as of 2026 already ranges from 120 to 150 beds. The sustained
rise in admissions, emergency cases, and outpatient consultations supports the continued
expansion not only of bed capacity, but also of hospital facilities, service capability, and
human resources.

       The hospital has already begun developing the institutional capacity required for
a higher level of care. Its Hospital Development Plan identifies the progressive
strengthening of critical care, specialty services, advanced diagnostics, health human
resources, infrastructure, and referral networks as part of its strategic direction toward
Level III capability. The plan likewise envisions JVGH as a stronger frontline referral
facility for critical cases and expressly includes the pursuit of legislation upgrading the
hospital to Level III.

        The need to upgrade JVGH must likewise be considered in relation to the size and
continuing development of the population it serves. Based on the 2024 Census of
Population of the Philippine Statistics Authority, Bulacan had a population of 3,876,806
as of 1 July 2024, while Central Luzon had a total population of 12,989,074. Bulacan was
the most populous province in the region, accounting for about 29.8% of Central Luzon’s
population.

       Central Luzon’s population increased from 12,422,172 in 2020 to 12,989,074 in 2024,
equivalent to an average annual population growth rate of 1.08%. The province’s
substantial population, together with continuing urbanization and economic and
industrial development, reinforces the need for accessible and adequately distributed
hospital facilities capable of providing specialized and subspecialized care.

        This need is reflected in the Philippine Health Facility Development Plan (PHFDP)
2020–2040, which provides the national strategic framework for health-facility
development based on population need, equitable access, and the coordinated expansion
of infrastructure, equipment, and human resources for health. For Central Luzon, the
PHFDP reported a supply of only 2,094 Level III hospital beds in 2019, against a projected
requirement of 8,468 beds by 2025, indicating a gap of 6,374 beds between the reported
baseline supply and projected 2025 need. The PHFDP further projects additional
requirements of 1,417 Level III or apex beds by 2030, 1,596 by 2035, and 1,793 by 2040,
bringing the cumulative Level III bed requirement for Central Luzon for 2025–2040 to
13,274 beds.

        The regional shortfall is particularly relevant to Bulacan. JVGH is the province’s
DOH-retained hospital and presently provides Level II public hospital capability. At the
tertiary level, the province has only one Level III hospital, with an authorized bed capacity
of 300 beds. Based on the current provincial hospital inventory, Bulacan has 3,008
authorized hospital beds, of which 2,031 are in private facilities and 977 are in public
hospitals. Public-sector capacity consists of 577 Level I beds, 100 Level II beds, and 300
Level III beds. The province has 141 intensive care unit beds, only 39 of which are in public
hospitals, including 35 in the public Level III hospital. These figures illustrate the limited
public tertiary and critical care capacity available to a province of nearly 3.9 million
people.

        The proposed upgrade of JVGH would therefore address more than the needs of a
single institution. It would strengthen Bulacan’s public referral network, expand access to
higher-level care, and contribute to addressing the broader deficiency in Level III hospital
capacity identified for Central Luzon. It is likewise consistent with the development
trajectory already set out in the JVGH Hospital Development Plan, which calls for the
progressive expansion of infrastructure, equipment, specialty and critical-care services,
personnel, and teaching and training capability necessary for Level III operations.

       This bill therefore seeks to upgrade the Joni Villanueva General Hospital from a
Level II to a Level III general hospital, provide for the corresponding upgrading of its
professional health care services and facilities, authorize the necessary increase in its
workforce, and allow future increases in bed capacity to be guided by its Hospital
Development Plan, the Philippine Health Facility Development Plan, and applicable DOH
standards.

       Strengthening a public hospital requires both recognition of present demand and
preparation for future need. In the case of JVGH, rising utilization demonstrates the
immediate demand; the population and regional health-facility data establish the broader
need; and the Hospital Development Plan provides a concrete path toward higher service
capability. Upgrading JVGH to Level III will help ensure that the capacity of the public
health system develops alongside the population it is meant to serve.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

        MUNICIPALITY OF BOCAUE, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A LEVEL III
           GENERAL HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

            Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1           Section 1. The Joni Villanueva General Hospital (JVGH), located in the
 2   Municipality of Bocaue, Province of Bulacan, is hereby upgraded from a Level II into a
 3   Level III general hospital. The JVGH shall remain under the direct control and supervision
 4   of the Department of Health (DOH).
 5
 6           Section 2. Any future increase in the authorized bed capacity of JVGH shall be
 7   consistent with its approved Hospital Development Plan and the Philippine Health
 8   Facility Development Plan.
 9
10           Section 3. The existing professional health care services and facilities of the JVGH
11   shall be upgraded and expanded to conform with and be commensurate to its
12   classification and operation as a Level III general hospital and its authorized bed capacity
13   as provided in Section 2 of this Act.
14
15          Section 4. The existing workforce of the JVGH, including medical, nursing, allied
16   health, administrative, technical, and support personnel, shall be increased
17   correspondingly.
18
19          Section 5. The Secretary of Health shall, in consultation with the Medical Center
20   Chief of the JVGH, determine the additional positions necessary for the operation of the
21   hospital as a Level III general hospital, subject to applicable laws, rules, and regulations.
