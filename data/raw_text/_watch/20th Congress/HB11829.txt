AN ACT
DECLARING THE BLUE LAGOON, LOCATED IN BARANGAY MARGUES, MUNICIPALITY OF DATU ODIN
SINSUAT, PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO DEL NORTE, AN ECOTOURISM SITE

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to declare the Blue Lagoon, located in Barangay Margues in the Municipality of Datu
Odin Sinsuat, as an eco-tourism site.

Comparable to the now famous Enchanted River of the Municipality of Hinatuan in Surigao del Sur, the
lagoon has become a much-sought-after destination by visitors from other provinces because of the
mesmerizing coior of the water fed by natural springs.

Its aquamarine waters and serene surroundings have remained largely undiscovered, its unspoiled
charm waiting to be explored.

A 30-minute ride from the City of Cotabato, it is an ideal destination for adventure seekers and nature
enthusiasts looking to experience the unspoiled beauty of the Province of Maguindanao del Norte.

Declaring Blue Lagoon as an ecotourism site will result in its development and improvement as a
tourist destination, and more importantly, contribute to the overall economic progress of the local

community and Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat. _A

tn view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

AN ACT
DECLARING THE BLUE LAGOON, LOCATED IN BARANGAY MARGUES, MUNICIPALITY OF DATU ODIN
SINSUAT, PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO DEL NORTE, AN ECOTOURISM SITE

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
Assembled:

SECTION 1. The Blue Lagoon, located in Barangay Marguez, Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat, Province
of Maguindanao del Norte, is hereby declared an ecotourism site. Pursuant thereto, its development
shall be prioritized and supported by the Department of Tourism (DOT), subject to the rules and
regulations governing the development of ecotourism sites.

SEC. 2. Within one (1) year after the approval of this Act, the local government of the Municipality of
Datu Odin Sinsuat shall, in coordination with the DOT, Department of Environment and Natural
Resources, and concerned agencies of the government, prepare a tourism development pian that shall
include the construction, installation, and maintenance of such appropriate infrastructure and facilities
to enhance tourism in the area: Provided, That said development plan shall ensure the preservation
and conservation of the natural beauty and resources of the area: Provided further, That the
implementation of the tourism development plan shall be included in the budget of the local
government of the Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat.

SEC. 3. The DOT shall assist the local government of the Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat in the
implementation of the tourism development pian by providing technical assistance in tourism capacity
building. it shall also include Mt. Minandar in its national and regional promotion programs.

SEC. 4. The funding for the implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual budget of the
local government of the Municipality of Datu Odin Sinsuat. The DOT may augment such allocation with
internally-generated funds.

SEC. 5. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the local government of the Municipality
of Datu Odin Sinsuat shall, in consultation with the DOT and concerned agencies of the government,
promulgate rules and regulations to implement this Act.

SEC. 6. All laws, rules, regulations, executive orders, proclamations, presidential decrees and other
issuances inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Act are hereby deemed repealed or amended
accordingly.

SEC. 7. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a
newspaper of general circulation.
