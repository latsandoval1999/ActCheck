                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Our teachers play a big role in shaping our children into a responsible citizens
and preparing the next generation to lead and contribute in the development of our
country.


       The global celebration of "World Teachers' Day" during the month of October
is dedicated to honoring the important role of teachers in transforming education.
More importantly, it raises awareness about the systemic support they need to fully
realize their potential as educators. Recent headlines in the Philippines have heavily
underscored this need.


       While Republic Act No. 10743 already legally recognizes the ﬁfth day of
October as National Teachers' Day, it currently designates it only as a special working
holiday. Teachers cannot fully receive the appreciation of their students and
communities if they are still required to report for their regular school responsibilities.
Therefore, this bill seeks to declare October 5 of each year as a non-working holiday
exclusively for all our teachers. This bill seeks to declare the ﬁfth day of October each
year as the National Teachers’ Day, which will be a non-working holiday for all our
teachers.


       Let us give our teachers the genuine recognition they truly deserve. They have
tirelessness and selﬂessly dedicated their time and talents to imparting valuable
lessons and even life skills to our children. They are indeed the unsung heroes of our
society as that are often easily taken for granted.
      Giving them a dedicated, non-working day of rest is a small but meaningful
token of gratitude. After all, it is the least we can do to honor the very people who
spend their lives building the foundation of our country's future.


      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                 AN ACT
         DECLARING OCTOBER 5 OF EVERY YEAR AS NATIONAL TEACHERS’
            DAY, A NON-WORKING HOLIDAY FOR ALL TEACHERS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National Teachers’ Day Act.”

        Section 2. To recognize the rights and the invaluable contributions of all the teachers
in the country, October 5 of every year is hereby declared as National Teachers’ Day, a non-
working holiday for all teachers in the Philippines.

        Section 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations – The Department of Education
(DepEd), Commission on Higher Education (CHED), and the Technical Education Skills and
Development Authority (TESDA) shall jointly formulate and issue the rules and regulations to
fully implement the provisions of this Act.

       Section 4. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
