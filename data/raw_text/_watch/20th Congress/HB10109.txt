                            AN ACT
    MANDATING ALL COMMERCIAL ESTABLISHMENTS AND PUBLIC
   BUILDINGS IN EVERY CITY AND MUNICIPALITY OF THE COUNTRY,
  WHETHER FOR PROFIT OR NOT AND OPERATING PUBLIC TOILETS TO
    INSTALL BIDET SPRAYS ON SUCH PUBLIC TOILETS, IMPOSING
 PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREFORE AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


      The State recognizes that the right of every establishment to a fair return
on investment carries with it a corresponding social responsibility to provide
adequate facilities for the comfort of its clientele. As such, Republic Act 11311
was enacted in 2018 to ensure that high foot-traffic areas such as Land
Transportation Terminals, Stations, Stops, Rest Areas, and Roll-on/Roll-Off
Terminals provide facilities such as internet and clean sanitary facilities.
      Since most commercial operations are typically located in public spaces
with heavy foot traffic, it follows that this is where people would most likely need
bathrooms. Basic restrooms ideally include sufficient lighting, ventilation,
running water that is safe to use, soap, a mirror, and a hand dryer.
       As a part of better public service for Filipinos, who traditionally and
culturally practice the use of water and soap instead of tissue paper in the
restrooms, this measure proposes the installation of bidet sprays in all
commercial establishments, especially in restaurants across the country. By
having a safe, adequate, and running water supply in every available restroom
in public areas, people can be assured that they can be relieved with the same
comfort as they would in their own homes.
      This bill also intends to contribute to the environmental cause of
eliminating toilet paper and paper waste over time.
       While bidet sprays are not the ultimate solution to perfecting restrooms,
this bill further hopes to move the nation one step closer to transforming public
restrooms into spaces that truly give comfort.
      In view of the foregoing, immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
    MANDATING ALL COMMERCIAL ESTABLISHMENTS AND PUBLIC
   BUILDINGS IN EVERY CITY AND MUNICIPALITY OF THE COUNTRY,
 WHETHER FOR PROFIT OR NOT, AND OPERATING PUBLIC TOILETS TO
    INSTALL BIDET SPRAYS ON SUCH PUBLIC TOILETS, IMPOSING
 PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREFORE AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:
SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Bidet in Every Public
Toilet Act”.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the state to promote health
and sanitation, allowing hygienic measures of isolating human contact with
hazardous wastes generated by excreta, urine, wastewater, solid wastes, and
industrial wastes, which will be detrimental to human health if left uncontrolled.

SECTION 3. Coverage. There shall be installed bidets in toilet seats in the public
restrooms of malls, hospitals, cinemas, restaurants, cafes, inns, hotels, and
other similar establishments in every city and municipality across the country.

SECTION 4. Implementation. No business permit and/or license shall be
issued to any of the aforementioned applicants-establishments who failed to
comply with Section 3 of this Act. In the event that the license and/or permit
had already been issued, but upon inspection it was found that the
establishment failed to maintain the operative condition of the bidets in the toilet
seats, charges shall be filed for the cancellation/revocation of the said
license/permit. The City or Municipality Business Permits and Licensing
Department (BPLD) shall administer the implementation and full compliance of
the above-stated establishments.

SECTION 5. Penalties. For violation of this Act, the following penalties shall be
imposed:

First Offense:                             Written Warning/Notice
Second Offense:                            PHP 1,000.00
Third and Offense:                         PHP 3,000.00
Fourth and Subsequent Offense:             PHP 5,000.00 plus cancellation of
                                      Business Permit

The aforementioned fines shall be imposed on the owners, operators and/or
managing officers of the malls, hospitals, cinemas, restaurants, cafes, inns,
hotels, and other similar establishments who failed to comply with section 3 of
this Act, or those who have been issued with license or permit that failed to
maintain the operative condition of bidets in toilets seats of the restrooms of the
afore-stated business establishments.

SECTION 6. Compliance. All existing establishments shall comply with the
provisions of this ordinance within thirty (30) days of approval of this ordinance.
Otherwise, the Business Permits of said establishments shall not be renewed.

SECTION 7. Separability Clause. Any portion or provision of this Act that is
declared unconstitutional shall not have the effect of nullifying other portions or
provisions hereof as long as such remaining portions can still subsist and be
given effect in their entirety.

SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. All laws, ordinances, rules, regulations, other
issuances, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with this Act, are hereby
repealed or modiﬁed accordingly.

SECTION 9. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect ﬁfteen (15) days after its
publication in the Ofﬁcial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
