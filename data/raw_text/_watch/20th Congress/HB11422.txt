20                                                EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   Republic Act No. 10912, or the Continuing Professional Development Act of 2016,
23   recognizes the importance of continuing education for Filipino professionals. However,
24   while CPD remains largely a requirement for maintaining professional credentials, it is
25   without a clear system connecting CPD credits to career advancement, specialization,
26   or higher qualifications. Access to CPD programs may also be difficult when training is
27   costly, unavailable, or burdensome.
28
29   Hence, this proposed measure seeks to make CPD more meaningful by linking it with
30   career progression and specialization, allowing professionals to earn and transfer
31   credits toward recognized qualifications, while requiring government agencies and
32   private employers to provide or support accessible and affordable CPD programs.
33
34   Continuing professional development should not simply be a requirement to maintain a
35   license. It should help professionals improve their skills, advance in their careers, and
36   receive recognition for what they have learned. It is not supposed to be a mandatory
37   requirement professionals should blindly follow just for the sake of compliance.
38
39   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
