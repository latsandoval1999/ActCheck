11                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE
12
13          The Municipality of Tudela, Province of Cebu, traces its foundation on May 20,
14   1892, when the Consejo de Administracion Civil en las Islas Filipinas issued a decree
15   approving its creation as a town separate and independent from the Municipality of
16   Poro.1
17
18          Historically, Tudela was formerly known as Tag-anito, in reference to the
19   practice of the early inhabitants of worshipping anitos or spirits.2 The initiative to
20   establish Tag-anito as an independent municipality began on December 10, 1890,
21   when officials and prominent residents of the Municipality of Poro and the Visita de
22   Tag-anito signed a petition requesting its establishment.3 The petition received broad
23   support from the local principalia and was certified by Fr. Ramon Alegria, the
24   Recollect parish priest of Poro. Among its signatories were Don Felipe Sotto,
25   gobernadorcillo of Poro, and former municipal leaders Pedro Gonzales, Domingo
26   Huerta, Mariano Garciano, Pablo Gutierrez, and Simeon Gonzales.
27
28          Following several deliberations by the Spanish colonial authorities, the petition
29   was approved on May 20, 1892.4 Thereafter, the first election of municipal officials was
30   held on August 4, 1892, at the Casa Tribunal Municipal, then located at what is now
31   the corner of Aguinaldo and Mabini Streets in the present Poblacion.5 Later, during
32   the American period, Tudela was formally recognized as one of the municipalities in
33   the Philippines.6
34
35        This history underscores that the annual celebration of Tudela’s Founding
36   Anniversary is a meaningful occasion to honor its origins, cultural heritage, and the

 7           Thus, this bill seeks to declare May 20 of every year a special non-working

10   and historical activities that strengthen community solidarity and local pride.
11

11                              AN ACT
12       DECLARING MAY 20 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
13     HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TUDELA, PROVINCE OF CEBU, IN
14          COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY
15
16          Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
17   assembled:
18
19         SECTION 1. May 20 of every year is hereby declared a special non-working
20   holiday in the Municipality of Tudela, Province of Cebu, in commemoration of its
21   Founding Anniversary.
22
23         SEC. 2. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
24   publication in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.
