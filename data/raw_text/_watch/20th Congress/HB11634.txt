                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Philippine National Police (PNP), as the country’s primary law
enforcement agency, must be equipped with the infrastructure, technology,
mobility assets, communications systems, investigative capabilities, and other
resources necessary to effectively perform its mandate and respond to evolving
public safety and security challenges.

       While the PNP has undertaken various capability enhancement initiatives,
modernization efforts remain dependent on periodic appropriations and
individual procurement programs. A comprehensive and sustained framework is
therefore necessary to ensure that capability development is systematic,
responsive to operational needs, and supported by appropriate procurement and
institutional mechanisms.

      This measure seeks to institutionalize a PNP Modernization and Capability
Enhancement Program covering infrastructure and facilities development,
equipment acquisition and upgrading, information and communications
technology, investigative and forensic capabilities, and institutional and
professional development. It likewise provides for appropriate procurement
strategies, supply-chain security, technology transfer, coordination with relevant
government agencies, and a defined implementation period and funding
framework.

       Through this measure, the PNP shall be better positioned to strengthen its
operational capabilities, uphold human rights and professional standards, adopt
modern technologies, and deliver more effective and responsive police services to
the Filipino people.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            “AN ACT
      PROVIDING FOR THE MODERNIZATION AND CAPABILITY
      ENHANCEMENT OF THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE AND
      APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR”

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Philippine
National Police Modernization and Capability Enhancement Act.”

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to modernize the Philippine National Police (PNP) to a level where it can
effectively and fully perform its mandate as the primary law enforcement agency
of the government. Towards this end, the State shall enhance the capability of
the PNP with a view of transforming it into a highly efficient, capable and
competent police force with a community and service-oriented culture among its
ranks, and adhering to and internalizing the enduring core values of love of God,
respect for authority, selfless love and service for people, sanctity of marriage,
and family life, responsible dominion and stewardship over material things, and
truthfulness.

      The PNP shall uphold and protect human rights in the performance of its
constitutional and statutory mandates. For this purpose, the State shall pursue
the implementation of a Modernization and Capability Enhancement Program
for the PNP focused on infrastructure and facilities development as well as
equipment acquisition and upgrading. The State shall also ensure that
modernization strengthens not only the operational capability of the PNP but
likewise its institutional culture of professionalism, ethical leadership, integrity,
accountability, and service to the people.

      Section 3. PNP Modernization Thrusts. - As used in this Act, the term
“modernization” shall be understood to mean the state of being enlivened or the
condition of achieving a dynamic level of performing police functions, law
enforcement operations, and frontline public safety services, and pursuing
modern approaches against criminality with the end view of establishing and
maintaining for all people a safe, secure, orderly, peaceful, and prosperous
nation. Towards this aspiration, the PNP shall pursue, among others, the
following modernization thrusts:

      a. Fill-up of basic equipment and construction of standard police stations
         and offices nationwide;

      b. Acquisition of modern equipment;

      c. Adoption of secured, modern, artificial intelligence (AI)-driven, real-time
         information and communication technology (ICT) systems to modernize
         and integrate police administration and operations;

      d. Strengthening of police coordination and cooperation with the citizenry,
         local governments, public safety agencies, other law enforcement
         agencies and pillars of the criminal justice system, and the regional and
         international police communities; and

      e. Enhancement of law enforcement approaches and intensified
         operations against transnational crime and emerging forms of
         criminality, insurgency, terrorism, and other serious threats to national
         security;

      f. Strengthening a culture of professionalism, ethical leadership,
         integrity, accountability, respect for human rights, community-oriented
         policing, and continuous values formation to reinforce public trust and
         confidence in the Philippine National Police.

       Section 4. Components of the Modernization and Capability
Enhancement Program. - The PNP modernization thrusts shall be realized
through the implementation of a Modernization and Capability Enhancement
Program. As used in this Act, the term police “capability enhancement” shall be
understood to mean a measurable improvement in the performance of PNP
mandates that translates into an enduring ability to enforce the law, prevent and
control crimes, maintain peace and order, ensure public safety and internal
security, and protect the country against threats to national security including
terrorism. The Modernization and Capability Enhancement Program shall
consist of the following components:

      a. Infrastructure and Facilities Development – The objectives of this
         component are:

         1. Establishment of Standard Infrastructure and Basic Facilities such
            as:
        i. Construction and upgrading of standard infrastructure like
           buildings for police stations and offices, that are accessible and
           suited to the peculiar topography of or weather conditions in the
           area, with provision for adequate perimeter fencing;

        ii. Construction and upgrading of basic facilities for administrative,
            investigative, and operational functions, such as crime
            laboratory; evidence rooms, storage rooms, records rooms;
            appropriate custodial facilities; communication systems;
            research and development centers; training institutions;
            hospitals and dispensaries for delivery of medical and dental
            services; and quartering and barracks facilities, in accordance
            with approved camp master development plans;

        iii. Construction and upgrading of facilities to provide repair and
            maintenance works, such as regional or provincial motor pool
            and maintenance facilities for other mobility assets.

   2.     Construction and upgrading of facilities to support regulatory
          functions;

   3.     Development, procurement, and management of ICT systems and
          automated systems to ensure integration and effective
          coordination; and

   4.     Real estate acquisition, titling, and management.

b. Equipment Acquisition and Upgrading – The objectives of this
   component are judicious procurement and fill-up of basic equipment,
   and acquisition of modern equipment for enhanced PNP capabilities,
   such as: munitions and ordnance; firepower and weapons;
   transportation and mobility assets; communication, intelligence,
   investigative and forensic equipment, including police training and
   education equipment.

c. Institutional and Professional Development. – The objectives of this
   component are to strengthen the character, competence, and
   institutional culture of the PNP through a continuing, values-based and
   principle-centered professional development program for all uniformed
   and non-uniformed personnel.

   The program shall be developed and periodically updated by the PNP,
   in consultation with the National Police Commission (NAPOLCOM) and
   other relevant institutions, and shall include, at a minimum:
         1. values formation anchored on the enduring core values of the PNP
            and the principles of integrity, justice, humility, moral courage,
            accountability, stewardship, and service above self;

         2. ethical leadership, command responsibility, and principled decision-
            making, particularly in the exercise of police authority;

         3. constitutional policing, respect for human rights and human
            dignity, and the lawful, necessary, and proportionate use of force;

         4. anti-corruption, integrity-building, accountability, and responsible
            stewardship of public funds, property, equipment, and authority;

         5. community-oriented policing, public service ethics, communication,
            empathy, and citizen engagement;

         6. ethical and responsible use of information and communications
            technology, artificial intelligence, surveillance systems, digital
            evidence, social media, and other emerging technologies; and

         7. mental, emotional, spiritual, and psychosocial wellness, resilience,
            and family support, consistent with freedom of religion and belief.

      The foregoing shall be integrated into recruitment and orientation
programs, basic and specialized training, in-service training, supervisory and
command courses, promotion and leadership development programs, and
continuing professional education within the PNP.

       Section 5. Standard of Modernization. - In preparing and implementing
the Modernization and Capability Enhancement Program, the standard test to
indicate modernity is the possession of latest attributes or innovative qualities
of infrastructure, facilities, and equipment, over and above the basic and
standard features. This will enable the PNP to better adapt to the needs of the
present time, add value to services delivered, and achieve increased citizen
satisfaction with the police. In endeavoring to modernize police infrastructure
and facilities, the primary consideration shall be developing capabilities for
command and direction, tactical and strategic control, effective communication,
robust ICT and automated systems networks, upholding human rights and Rule
of Law, and sustained police-community-relations.

       The acquisition of modern equipment shall be based on the results of
operational needs assessment. For purposes of this Act, the term “operational
needs assessment” refers to the mandatory periodic evaluation of materiel and
logistical capabilities with the end view of identifying deficiencies or limitations
in existing resources and addressing significant impediments in achieving
operational tasks and attaining organizational mission, through equipment
acquisition.

       Section 6. Negotiated Procurement. - The Chief of the PNP, upon the
recommendation of the NAPOLCOM and with the approval of the Secretary of the
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), may resort to
negotiated procurement under the applicable provisions of Section 35 of
Republic Act No. 12009, otherwise known as the “New Government Procurement
Act,” and its implementing rules and regulations, when the procurement involves
modern equipment for enhanced PNP capabilities and the applicable conditions
for such procurement are satisfied.

      For procurements involving an agency or instrumentality of another
country, the PNP shall comply with the applicable requirements of Republic Act
No. 12009, its implementing rules and regulations, and relevant guidelines
issued by the Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB).

       Nothing in this Section shall be construed as granting the PNP a
procurement authority beyond that provided under existing procurement laws,
rules, and regulations.

      Section 7. Modernization and Capability Enhancement Program. -
Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Chief of the PNP shall,
in consultation with the Chairperson of the NAPOLCOM and the Secretary of the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM), prepare a Modernization and
Capability Enhancement Program.

      After consultation, the Chief of the PNP shall submit the Modernization
and Capability Enhancement Program to the NAPOLCOM, which shall endorse
the same to Congress, through the Congressional Oversight Committee created
under this Act.

      The program shall indicate the following:

      a.    The police organizational structure and staffing pattern, authorized
            personnel strength, and logistic and facilities inventory during the
            various implementation stages;

      b.    The capability enhancement programs, projects, and activities to be
            undertaken vis-à-vis the particular component and corresponding
            objectives and modernization thrusts they relate to;

      c.    The institutional and professional development programs to be
            implemented, including their curriculum, target personnel,
            implementation schedule, delivery mechanisms, and measurable
            performance indicators;
     d.   The priorities, phases of implementation, and schedules of the
          capability enhancement projects and activities; and

     e.   The estimated average cost of each project or activity to be
          undertaken.

      Section 8. Key Result Areas for Program Implementation. – The PNP
shall adopt a strategic approach in implementing the Modernization and
Capability Enhancement Program, through the rationalization of community-
and-service-oriented police administration and operations, geared towards
achieving optimum outcomes in the following key result areas:

     a.   Beat Patrol - The improved patrol coverage through the acquisition
          and upgrading of communications equipment and mobility equipment
          to improve response time and enhance crime prevention.

     b.   Maritime Patrol - The upgrading of the capability to conduct operations
          against illegal activities in rivers and coastal maritime areas such as
          illegal fishing, piracy, illegal smuggling of contraband and the like. It
          shall include the enhancement of the capability to perform duties on
          maritime environmental protection and ecological security.

     c.   Illegal Drugs Suppression - The development of its capability in the
          enforcement of laws relating to drug abuse and the increased coverage
          in illegal drugs suppression operations.

     d.   Firearms Control - The development and enhancement of a
          comprehensive firearms control regulation mechanism to address the
          proliferation of unlicensed and illegal firearms.

     e.   Intelligence Operations - The development and enhancement of the
          capability for information collection, processing and dissemination.

     f.   Aerial Surveillance and Air Transport - The acquisition and upgrading
          of modest air transport capability to convey its personnel and supplies
          over the entire length and breadth of the archipelago. This will
          necessitate the acquisition of multi-role aircrafts to perform public
          order and safety functions such as observation, surveillance,
          detection, among others. This includes the acquisition of unmanned
          aerial vehicles or drones, which shall serve as force multipliers in
          performing aerial surveillance and support to police operations in
          crime prevention and solution.
      g.   Criminal Investigation - The enhancement of legal and scientific
           criminal investigations utilizing improved crime laboratory
           techniques, methodologies and responsive procedures.

      h.   Cybercrime Investigations - The development of capabilities to
           implement and enforce pertinent laws on cybercrimes and pursue an
           effective anti-cybercrime campaign.

      i.   Moral and Values Formation - The development of police personnel
           guided by integrity, accountability, respect for human dignity, and
           selfless service to the people.

      j.   Anti-Terrorist Operations - The promotion and utilization of the
           multilateral exchange of information with other police organizations,
           to include the improvement of its institutional capabilities to deal with
           terrorist activities as may be defined by law.

      k.   Disaster Response and Rescue Operations - The improvement of the
           institutional capabilities to respond to all types of disasters or
           national emergencies.

      l.   Civil Disturbance Control - The development of civil disturbance
           doctrines consistent with the constitutional and statutory provisions
           of human rights as well as the acquisition of equipment and devices
           to implement these doctrines.

      Section 9. Negotiated Procurement for National Security Equipment.
– Pursuant to Section 35 of Republic Act No. 12009, otherwise known as the
“New Government Procurement Act,” the PNP may resort to the applicable
negotiated procurement modalities authorized under said law in the acquisition
of national security equipment that is mission-critical, highly specialized, or
security-sensitive, necessary for its modernization and capability enhancement
programs, subject to the conditions, procedures, safeguards, and applicable
guidelines prescribed under Republic Act No. 12009 and its implementing rules
and regulations.

     For purposes of this Section, the following terms shall be understood to
mean as follows:

      a. National Security Equipment refers to any equipment, system, or
         technology directly used for the national defense, security, or
         intelligence operations of the State. It includes, but is not limited to,
         communication systems, weapons, surveillance devices, mobility
         assets, protective gear, command-and-control systems, and related
         support technologies and critical infrastructures intended to preserve
         state sovereignty and national security.

      b. Non-National Security Equipment refers to all other property, machinery,
         or technology not falling within the definition of national security
         equipment, including general administrative, office, and productivity
         appliances, as well as facilities, systems, or other equipment used for
         purely police and community-oriented functions that are not inherently
         tied to the nation’s defense or core security operations. These include,
         among others, equipment for community patrol, police-community
         relations, public safety operations, and investigative activities of a non-
         classified or civilian nature. Such items shall be procured through the
         regular procurement modalities under Republic Act No. 12009 (New
         Government Procurement Act) and its implementing rules.

       The PNP may undertake negotiated procurement only in accordance with
the applicable procurement modality and conditions under Republic Act No.
12009 and its implementing rules and regulations, including, where applicable,
procurement involving defense cooperation agreements and inventory-based
items.

     The resort to negotiated procurement shall be justified by the applicable
grounds and conditions prescribed under Republic Act No. 12009 and its
implementing rules and regulations.

      All negotiated procurement under this Section shall be conducted in
accordance with the procedures and safeguards prescribed under Republic Act
No. 12009, its implementing rules and regulations, and applicable GPPB
guidelines.

     Section 10. Pooled Procurement Strategy for National Security
Equipment. - In addition to Negotiated Procurement, the PNP may utilize Pooled
Procurement as a procurement strategy for the acquisition of national security
equipment, pursuant to Section 17 of Republic Act No. 12009 and its
Implementing Rules and Regulations.

      For purposes of this Section:

      a. Pooled Procurement refers to the mechanism whereby the PNP, either
         acting alone or in collaboration with other Procuring Entities,
         consolidates its requirements for national security equipment into a
         single, joint, bulk, or group procurement to leverage economies of scale,
         achieve value for money, and enhance technical capacity, quality,
         availability, and sustainability of supply.
      b. National Security Equipment shall have the same meaning as defined
         under the immediately preceding Section.

      c. Non-National Security Equipment shall likewise refer to all other
         property, machinery, or technology that does not fall within the
         definition of national security equipment and shall be subject to the
         procurement modalities prescribed under Republic Act No. 12009.

       The PNP may initiate, host, or participate in pooled procurement
arrangements with other law enforcement or defense agencies under a
Collaborating Procuring Entities (CPE) framework, designating a Lead Procuring
Entity (LPE) to handle the consolidated procurement process, including
requirement formulation, solicitation, evaluation, awarding, and distribution,
subject to a duly executed Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) among the
participating entities and the applicable requirements of Republic Act No. 12009
and its implementing rules and regulations.

       All pooled procurement activities involving national security equipment
shall ensure continuous coordination with the Department of National Defense
(DND) and the Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB), as may be
applicable, to guarantee alignment with applicable technical, logistical, and
security standards, as well as conformity with the rules governing consolidated
or group procurements under Republic Act No. 12009.

      Section 11. Alignment with the Self-Reliant Defense Posture
Revitalization Act. - In the implementation of modernization and procurement
projects, the PNP shall, whenever practicable, align its acquisition processes with
the Self-Reliant Defense Posture (SRDP) Program under Republic Act No. 12024,
to promote self-sufficiency, local production, and technology transfer in
applicable national security equipment and systems.

      Priority shall be given to qualified in-country enterprises and local
manufacturers capable of producing, assembling, or servicing such national
security equipment, consistent with the national policy of defense and law
enforcement self-reliance and supply chain security. Where foreign procurement
is necessary, preference shall be extended, where permitted under existing
procurement laws and regulations, to suppliers offering countertrade, offset, co-
production, or technology-transfer arrangements that will enhance domestic
capability.

      Section 12. Supply Chain Security and Technology Transfer. - All
modernization projects involving national security equipment shall integrate a
Supply Chain Security and Technology Transfer Component designed to ensure
continuous supportability, maintenance capability, and protection of sensitive
technologies and information. The PNP shall formulate and implement measures
to identify, assess, and protect critical suppliers, components, and systems
essential to police operations and national security, in coordination with the
Department of National Defense (DND) and other relevant national security
agencies.

      For purposes of this Section, “Supply Chain Security” refers to the
systematic process of ensuring the integrity, reliability, and resilience of the end-
to-end chain of production, acquisition, distribution, maintenance, and disposal
of national security equipment and related systems, to prevent, detect, and
mitigate risks of compromise, disruption, sabotage, or unauthorized access that
may endanger public safety, operational readiness, or national security.

      Section 13. Coordination with the Department of National Defense
and the Government Procurement Policy Board. - To promote collaboration
and harmonization in capability development and procurement standards, the
PNP shall coordinate with the Department of National Defense (DND) and the
Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB) in the planning, acquisition, and
implementation of modernization projects, particularly those involving national
security equipment and technologically advanced systems.

      Such coordination shall ensure the alignment of procurement processes
with Sections 17 and 35 of Republic Act No. 12009 and the consistent
application of technical, logistical, and operational standards adopted by the
defense and law enforcement sectors. The implementation of this Section shall
be consistent with Republic Act No. 12009, its implementing rules and
regulations, Republic Act No. 12024, and their respective implementing rules
and applicable GPPB guidelines, particularly in areas concerning negotiated
procurement, pooled procurement, local industry participation, and technology
transfer.

      Section 14. Period of Implementation. - The Modernization and
Capability Enhancement Program under this Act shall be implemented within
ten (10) years: Provided, that payments for amortization of outstanding multi-
year contract obligations incurred under the PNP Modernization and Capability
Enhancement Program may be extended beyond this period.

       Section 15. Appropriations for the Modernization and Capability
Enhancement Program. - The amount necessary to support the modernization
and capability enhancement programs, activities and projects under the Program
in relation to Section 7 shall be appropriated by Congress.

      Under      the  program,    an    amount    of   Forty    Billion  Pesos
(₱40,000,000,000.00) shall be allocated for the first two (2) years of
implementation, for basic equipment fill-up, and establishment of standard
infrastructure and basic facilities: Provided, that, subject to the annual
appropriations process and availability of funds, Eleven Billion, Two Hundred
Fifty Million (₱11,250,000,000.00) shall be appropriated every year thereafter:
and Provided, further, that, the entire Program shall be based on a ceiling of One
Hundred Thirty Billion Pesos (₱130,000,000,000.00) budget requirement, for the
full period of implementation.

      Whenever deemed necessary, in order to expedite full implementation of
the program, the Chief of the PNP may, subject to the approval of the President
and consistent with the provisions of existing laws, including Republic Act No.
12009, Commission on Audit rules and regulations, under such terms and
conditions most favorable to the government, enter into multi-year contracts and
other contractual arrangements.

      For this purpose, Congress shall, upon issuance of a multi-year
contractual authority by the Department of Budget and Management, make the
corresponding appropriations for the ensuing fiscal years, to cover multi-year
obligations.

      Appropriations to support the Modernization and Capability Enhancement
Program shall be treated as distinct and separate budget items from the regular
appropriations for the PNP, subject to applicable budget classification and
General Appropriations Act requirements.

       Section 16. Tax Treatment of the Purchase and Importation of the
PNP. - All internal revenue tax and duty obligations from the purchase and
importation of munitions and ordnance, firepower and weapons, transportation
and mobility assets, communication, intelligence, investigative and forensic
equipment and other equipment by the PNP, under the Modernization and
Capability Enhancement Program, that directly and exclusively relate to the
performance of its police and law enforcement functions, shall be chargeable to
the tax expenditure fund provided for in the General Appropriations Act, subject
to existing tax laws, applicable tax regulations, and budgeting, accounting, and
auditing rules.

        Donations made to the PNP to be used actually, directly, and exclusively
for its Modernization and Capability Enhancement Program shall be exempt from
donor’s tax and the same shall be considered as allowable deduction from the
gross income of the donor for purposes of computing taxable income in
accordance with the provisions of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997,
as amended. Likewise, donations from foreign sources shall be exempt from
value-added tax and customs duties, subject to existing laws and applicable
rules and regulations.

      Section 17. Local Government Support Through Local Development
Plans. – In the formulation of their respective socio-economic local development
programs, all provincial, city, and municipal development councils shall, in
coordination with the Philippine National Police (PNP), and consistent with
applicable laws, regulations, comprehensive land use plans and local
development plans, provide for the allocation of adequate, suitable, and
accessible lands in their respective comprehensive land use plans for the
establishment of basic police infrastructure and support facilities within their
areas of jurisdiction, taking into account the present and projected operational
and infrastructure requirements of the local police forces and other police
support units.

      Regional Development Councils shall ensure that allocation of such lands
for use of local police forces and other police support units, as coordinated with
the PNP, are included in the approved socio-economic local development
programs of provinces and cities prior to integration into their respective
Regional Development Plans and subsequent submission to the National
Economic and Development Authority.

      Within one hundred twenty (120) days from the effectivity of this Act, the
Chief of the PNP shall submit to the NAPOLCOM such guidelines and parameters
governing the coordination with local government units and the identification,
suitability, and allocation of lands for the police, for approval and
implementation.

       In effecting land allocation for local police forces and other police support
units, any acquisition of privately owned property shall be undertaken only in
accordance with the Constitution and existing laws, including applicable
requirements on due process and just compensation. Nothing in this Section
shall be construed as authorizing the compulsory acquisition of private property
except as may be authorized by law.

       Section 18. Congressional Oversight Committee. - There is hereby
created a Congressional Oversight Committee upon the effectivity of this Act to
monitor and oversee the implementation of the provisions of this Act. It shall be
composed of four (4) members from the House of Representatives and four (4)
members from the Senate with the Chairpersons of the Committee on Public
Order and Safety and the Committee on Public Order and Dangerous Drugs of
each Chamber, respectively, as Joint Chairpersons. The three (3) other members
representing the House of Representatives shall be designated by the Speaker of
the House of Representatives while the three (3) other members from the Senate
shall be designated by the Senate President. The Minority shall have at least one
(1) representative from each Chamber.

      Section 19. Program Modification. - In the exigency of public service or
whenever necessary to address unforeseen emergencies to curb transnational
crime, emerging forms of criminality, insurgency, terrorism and other serious
threats to national security, or to satisfy delivery of public safety services, the
Chief of the PNP may modify, through substitution or revision, a component
project in the Modernization and Capability Enhancement Program, subject to
the favorable endorsement of the NAPOLCOM, for the President’s approval.
Within thirty (30) days from such approval, the Chief of the PNP shall report such
program modification to Congress, through the Congressional Oversight
Committee.

      Section 20. Annual Reports. - Within the first quarter of the succeeding
year of implementation, the Chief of the PNP shall submit to the President and
Congress, through the Chairperson of the NAPOLCOM, an annual report on the
progress of the implementation of the Modernization and Capability
Enhancement Program.

      Section 21. Sunset Review. - After five (5) years following its effectivity,
or as the need arises, the Congressional Oversight Committee shall conduct a
sunset review of this Act, to systematically evaluate the accomplishments under
the Modernization and Capability Enhancement Program and its impact on the
PNP organizational structure, institutional mechanisms, and performance of
functions, for purposes of determining and effecting remedial legislation.

       Section 22. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the PNP, in consultation with the NAPOLCOM
and the DBM, shall formulate the implementing rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act.

      Further, the PNP, through the NAPOLCOM and DILG, shall, in
coordination with the DND and the GPPB, promulgate the necessary suppletory
rules and regulations to implement negotiated procurement and pooled
procurement as provided under this Act, consistent with Republic Act No. 12009,
Republic Act No. 12024, their respective implementing rules and regulations,
and applicable GPPB guidelines.

     Section 3. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act shall be held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not otherwise affected shall
remain in full force and effect.

      Section 24. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with or contrary to this Act, are hereby deemed repealed
or modified accordingly.

      Section 25. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of
general circulation.

      Approved,
