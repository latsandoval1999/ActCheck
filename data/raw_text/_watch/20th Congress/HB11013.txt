                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to institutionalize the Alternative Classroom Learning
Experience (ACLE) Program in all public and private secondary and tertiary
educational institutions throughout the Philippines. It complements formal
classroom instruction through student-led academic, civic, and experiential learning
activities that cultivate critical thinking, democratic participation, leadership, and
social responsibility.

       The country continues to confront a learning crisis that extends beyond
academic performance, but also to the development of critical thinking, civic
engagement, and leadership among learners. The Second Congressional
Commission on Education (EDCOM II)1 described the state of Philippine education
as a matter of “national survival” and emphasized that ninety one percent (91%) of
Filipino ten-year-old learners are unable to read and understand age-appropriate
texts, while persistent weaknesses in analytical reasoning, communication, and
problem-solving continue to undermine the country’s human capital. The report
further stressed that learning recovery cannot rely solely on traditional classroom
instruction and called for transformative reforms that develop learners’ higher-order
thinking, collaboration, and real-world competencies.

       Likewise, the Department of Education’s Quality Basic Education
Development Plan (Q-BEDP) 2025–20352 recognizes that improving education
quality requires learner-centered, experiential, and innovative approaches that
prepare students for rapid technological change, artificial intelligence, and the
Fourth Industrial Revolution. Internationally, UNICEF Philippines3 continues to
warn that the country’s learning crisis threatens its demographic dividend, noting

sustainable national development. Despite these realities, opportunities for

initiatives of individual schools rather than on a consistent national policy.

      This measure institutionalizes the Alternative Classroom Learning Experience
(ACLE) Program as a mandatory activity to be conducted at least once every
semester or academic term in all secondary and tertiary educational institutions. It
empowers student councils and student governments to organize educational
discussions, policy fora, workshops, community integration activities, lectures,
symposia, fieldwork, film screenings, and other experiential learning initiatives with
the support of school administrations. It transforms these activities into an integral
component of the educational experience, guaranteeing that learners develop not
only academic competence but also leadership, civic consciousness, social
awareness, innovation, and the capacity to engage constructively with diverse ideas
and contemporary national issues.

        The bill reinforces the policy objectives of Republic Act (RA) No. 9155,
otherwise known as the “Governance of Basic Education Act of 2001”; RA No. 7722,
otherwise known as the “Higher Education Act of 1994”; RA No. 8292, otherwise
known as the “Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997”; and RA No. 9500,
otherwise known as the “University of the Philippines Charter of 2008”, all of which
uphold quality education, academic freedom, institutional autonomy, and the
holistic development of Filipino learners.

      Education must do more than produce graduates who can pass examinations;
it must nurture citizens who can question, innovate, lead, and serve. Every learner
deserves opportunities to experience education beyond the confines of textbooks
and classrooms, where ideas are challenged, perspectives are broadened, and
meaningful engagement with society begins.

       As the nation strives to recover from years of learning loss while preparing its
youth for an increasingly complex and interconnected world, investing in programs
that cultivate critical thinkers and responsible leaders has become an urgent national
imperative. Institutionalizing the Alternative Classroom Learning Experience
Program will help ensure that Filipino students are not merely equipped to earn a
diploma, but are empowered to shape communities, strengthen democratic
institutions, and lead the country's future with competence, integrity, and purpose.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE ALTERNATIVE CLASSROOM LEARNING
EXPERIENCE PROGRAM TO ALL SECONDARY AND TERTIARY
EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS THROUGHOUT THE PHILIPPINES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Alternative Classroom
Learning Experience (ACLE) Act”.

       SEC. 2. Alternative Classroom Learning Experience. – The Alternative Classroom
Learning Experience (ACLE) Program is a student-led and - organized activity that
raises academic freedom inside our nation's campuses. It aims to provide students
with alternative viewpoints, perspectives, ideas, and concepts not usually discussed
through formal learning instruction and not usually found in textbooks and official
educational materials. Activities related to the ACLE Program shall include but not
limited to: workshops, educational discussions, training, community integration,
field-work, fora, symposia, film showing and/or audio-visual presentations,
lectures.

       SEC. 3. Conduct of an ACLE per Term or Semester. – All secondary and tertiary
educational institutions are mandated to conduct an Alternative Classroom
Learning Experience (ACLE) activity at least once every semester or term, as the
case may be. Student councils or student governments shall be primarily in charge
of organizing the said activity. School administrations shall provide the necessary
assistance to the student body to carry out the aim of this activity.

       SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The College Editors Guild of the
Philippines (CEP) as the oldest, broadest and only-existing alliance of tertiary
student publication in the Asia-Pacific region and the National Union of Students of
the    Philippines    (NUSP)     as     a    nation-wide    alliance   of    student
councils/governments/unions committed to the advancement of the students’
democratic rights and welfare, and both are Securities and Exchange Commission
(SEC)-registered national formation of student councils and government, in

        SEC. 5. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared

effect.

       SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
