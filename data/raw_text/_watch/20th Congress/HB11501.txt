                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

Southern Leyte and Bohol are linked by sea, but travel and the movement of
goods between them remain dependent on maritime crossings. A permanent link
would improve access between Eastern and Central Visayas and create new
opportunities for trade, tourism, and investment. It would also provide another
transport option when sea crossings are unavailable.

This bill provides for the construction of a bridge or undersea tunnel following a
proposed alignment from Maasin City, Southern Leyte, through Lapinig Island,
Bohol to Ubay, Bohol. It would establish a continuous land route across the two
water crossings, subject to the results of feasibility and engineering studies.

Lapinig Island serves as an intermediate point in the proposed route. Accordingly,
the project must consider the connections between Maasin City and Lapinig
Island, between Lapinig Island and Ubay, and across the island itself. Examining
these sections together is necessary to ensure that the completed link functions as
one practical transport corridor.

The proposed link would make travel between Southern Leyte and Bohol easier,
provide a more direct route for the movement of goods, and improve access to
essential services. It could also strengthen disaster response by giving emergency
personnel, equipment, and relief supplies another route between the two
provinces when conditions permit.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. There shall be constructed under the supervision of the Department
of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) an undersea tunnel or bridge, from the
Province of Southern Leyte to the Province of Bohol, including access roads
thereto.

SECTION 2. The funding for the feasibility study and engineering design shall
be included in the General Appropriations Act (GAA), and shall be undertaken
by a qualified international consulting firm with proven experience in similar
long-span bridge or undersea tunnel projects. To uphold the quality, credibility,
and global competitiveness of the outputs, the study should be financed through
grant funding or technical assistance from development partners. Thereafter, the
Secretary of the DPWH shall include in the Department’s program the
construction of said undersea tunnel or bridge, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual GAA.

SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
