                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       June 24, 1571 marks the formal establishment of the City of Manila by Miguel
López de Legazpi, laying the foundation for what would become the capital of the
Philippines and the center of the country's political, economic, cultural, and historical
development.

        For more than four centuries, Manila has stood at the forefront of the nation's
history. It has witnessed the country's struggles, triumphs, and transformation. It serves
as the seat of government and the home of countless institutions that have shaped the
Filipino identity.

        Like any great city, Manila's history is full of conflicts, one of both glory and
adversity. Yet, through every trial, Manila has remained steadfast, continually
rebuilding and moving forward. Celebrating its founding is not only an occasion to
honor its past, but also an opportunity to reaffirm hope for its future and recognize the
unwavering spirit of the Manileños.

        Declaring June 24 of every year as a special non-working holiday in the City of
Manila will provide residents the opportunity to commemorate the city's founding,
participate in civic and cultural activities, and foster greater appreciation of Manila's
enduring legacy and invaluable contributions to nation-building.

       Ultimately, this measure recognizes the importance of preserving local history
and strengthening the sense of identity and pride among Manileños, especially the
younger generation.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE 24 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-
WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF MANILA, TO BE KNOWN AS
"MANILA DAY," AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Manila Day
Act."

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to recognize and preserve the historical and cultural heritage of local
government units and to promote pride by commemorating their history.

June 24 marks the founding of the City of Manila under Spanish rule in 1571
through the establishment of its city government. As the nation's capital and one
of the oldest chartered cities in Asia, Manila occupies a unique place in
Philippine history, serving as the country's political, economic, cultural, and
educational center for centuries. Declaring June 24 as a special non-working
holiday in the City of Manila will provide its residents an opportunity to
commemorate the city's rich history, honor its contributions to nation-building,
and participate in activities that strengthen local identity and community
solidarity.

      Section 3. Special Non-Working Holiday. – June 24 of every year is
hereby declared a special non-working holiday in the City of Manila, to be
known as "Manila Day."

       Section 4. Implementing Agencies. – The City Government of Manila,
in coordination with the appropriate national government agencies, shall
organize and promote activities and programs commemorating Manila Day,
highlighting the city's historical, cultural, and civic significance.
       Section 5. Application to Labor. – The appropriate labor rules and
regulations pertaining to a special non-working holiday shall therefore apply.

       Section 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, and
administrative orders or parts thereof inconsistent with any provision of this Act
are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

        Section 7. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
