                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Sports play a fundamental role in nation building by fostering unity, cultivating
national pride, promoting physical and mental health, driving economic development and
promoting social development among the youth. Sports provide opportunities for young
Filipinos to discover and develop their talents while fostering values that contribute to
responsible citizenship and community engagement.

       The Province of Zamboanga Sibugay has produced talented athletes who have
excelled in various local, regional and national competitions. However, the absence of a
dedicated sports academy and training center limits opportunities for systematic athlete
development, coaching enhancement, sports science support and the provision of
adequate training facilities.

       The Municipality of Alicia, strategically located within the First Legislative District
of Zamboanga Sibugay and accessible to neighboring municipalities, is well-positioned to
serve as a hub for sports development in the province. Establishing a sports academy and
training center in Alicia will help identify, nurture and develop promising athletes while
providing a venue for training camps, sports competitions, coaching clinics and youth
development programs.

       The proposed Alicia Sports Academy and Training Center shall serve as a center for
excellence in sports development, training and education. It shall promote grassroots
sports programs, enhance athletic performance through modern training methodologies,
and contribute to the overall development of sports in Zamboanga Sibugay and nearby
provinces.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
         ESTABLISHING THE ALICIA SPORTS ACADEMY AND
  TRAINING CENTER IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ALICIA, PROVINCE OF
   ZAMBOANGA SIBUGAY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

      SECTION 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as "Alicia Sports Academy and
Training Center Act".

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State declares priority to education,
science and technology, arts, culture, and sports to foster patriotism and nationalism,
accelerate social progress, and promote total human liberation and development. The
State likewise promotes physical education and encourage sports programs, league
competitions, and amateur sports, including training for international competitions, to
foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for the development of a healthy and alert
citizenry.

       SEC. 3. Establishment of Alicia Sports Academy and Training Center. –
There shall be established in the Municipality of Alicia, Province of Zamboanga Sibugay,
a sports academy and training center to be known as the "Alicia Sports Academy and
Training Center", which shall be constituted as the primary sports development and
training center for the whole province of Zamboanga Sibugay and the neighboring
provinces as well.

      SEC. 4. Sports Curriculum. – The Philippine Sports Commission (PSC), in
coordination with the Department of Education (DepEd), the Commission on Higher
Education (CHED), the Provincial Government of Zamboanga Sibugay and the
Municipality of Alicia, shall formulate the curricula as well as create the necessary training
programs, appropriate tournaments and competitions to accomplish the objectives and
purpose of this Act.

       SEC. 5. Training Center, Facilities and Equipment. – The construction,
establishment and operation of the Alicia Sports Academy and Training Center shall be
funded by the PSC, the Office of the President, the Department of Education (DepEd), the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), and other government agencies. The
Provincial Government of Zamboanga Sibugay and the Municipality of Alicia may likewise
                                                                                       2
contribute their respective allocations. In addition, grants and endowments intended for
sports development may be obtained from local or foreign sources.

      The PSC, in coordination with the Provincial Government of Zamboanga Sibugay
and the Municipality of Alicia, shall take the necessary steps to upgrade, improve and
modernize existing facilities, equipment and personnel, and integrate them into the
newly-created sports academy and training center. They shall likewise establish the
system by which the facilities shall be maintained and managed.

      SEC. 6. Appropriations. – The Secretary of Education shall immediately
include in the Department's programs the operation of the Alicia Sports Academy and
Training Center, the funding of which may be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

        SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The PSC, in coordination
with the Department of Education (DepEd), the Provincial Government of Zamboanga
Sibugay, the Municipality of Alicia, and other concerned stakeholders, shall promulgate
the necessary rules and regulations for the implementation of this within ninety (90) days
after its enactment into law.

       SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.


       Approved,
