                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Student-athletes embody the ideals of excellence, discipline, teamwork, and
perseverance. As they strive to excel both in the classroom and in athletic competition,
they contribute not only to the development of Philippine sports but also to the formation
of responsible, well-rounded citizens. However, many student-athletes continue to face
challenges in balancing their academic responsibilities with the rigorous demands of
training and competition, while remaining vulnerable to discrimination, exploitation,
inadequate health protection, unsafe training and competition environments, and limited
institutional support.

Although Republic Act No. 10676, otherwise known as the "Student-Athletes Protection
Act," provides safeguards against the commercialization of student-athletes and
regulates residency requirements, there remains a need for a more comprehensive legal
framework that fully recognizes and protects their rights, welfare, safety, and holistic
development.

Therefore, this measure seeks to establish the Magna Carta for Student-Athletes,
guaranteeing their rights to quality education, safe and healthy training and competition
environments, proper medical care, scholarship protection, freedom from discrimination
and abuse, and meaningful participation in decisions affecting their welfare.

It likewise defines the responsibilities of schools, athletic associations, and government
agencies in ensuring that student-athletes receive adequate academic, physical,
emotional, and medical support while pursuing excellence in sports. By requiring access
to qualified healthcare professionals, safe facilities and equipment, emergency medical
assistance, and protection from violence, exploitation, and performance-enhancing
drugs, this bill promotes the safety and well-being of every student-athlete throughout
their athletic journey.
By strengthening institutional support and promoting a balanced approach to education
and athletic development, this bill seeks to empower student-athletes to realize their full
potential without compromising their academic aspirations, health, safety, and well-being.

Ultimately, this bill aims to uphold the rights and welfare of every Filipino student-athlete
and foster an environment where academic achievement and athletic excellence
complement one another in building a healthier, safer, more disciplined, and globally
competitive generation of Filipinos.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

     AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE MAGNA CARTA OF STUDENT ATHLETES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION. 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Magna Carta of Student-
Athletes".

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – Article XIV, Section 1 of the 1987 Constitution recognizes
the role of the State to protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at
all levels, and to take appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all. Further,
Section 19 (1) of the same Article provides that the State shall promote physical
education, sports programs, and competitions alongside training for international
competitions to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for the attainment of a
healthy and alert citizenry. Thus, the State shall recognize and uphold the rights of
Student-Athletes to further hone their skills and abilities in their respective fields of
amateur sports without neglecting their education and general well-being.

It is the intention of this law to protect and promote the rights of the Student-Athlete, who
is, first and foremost, a student. To this end, the rights guaranteed by this law and the
obligations imposed on schools, athletic associations, and their officials and
representatives shall seek to ensure that the Student-Athlete attains quality education
while honing his/her skill and reaching his/her full potential as an athlete in an amateur
sports setting. Ultimately, this law aims to enable the Student-Athlete to enjoy a balanced
life while in school and training or competing for his/her sport.
SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to Student-Athletes of any school that conducts
sports programs and competitions.

SEC. 4. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act, the following terms shall be defined as
follows:

   a. Athletic Association – refers to any organization that is responsible for governing
      inter-school athletic programs and competitions such as, but not limited to, the
      Private Schools Athletic Association (PRISAA), University Athletic Association of
      the Philippines (UAAP), National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA), Women's
      National Collegiate Athletic Association (WNCAA), State Colleges and Universities
      Athletic Association (SCUAA), Cebu Schools Athletic Foundation Inc. (CESAFI),
      and National Capital Region Athletic Association (NCRAA), among others but shall
      not include National Sports Associations (NSAs), as defined under Section 3 (a)
      of Republic Act No. 10676 or the "Student-Athletes Protection Act.";
   b. National Sports Associations (NSAs) – refer to associations organized for their
      respective sports in the Philippines, duly recognized by the Philippine Sports
      Commission (PSC), which is granted autonomy and technical control over the
      promotion and development of its sport, subject to the supervisory and visitorial
      powers of the PSC under Section 11 of Republic Act No. 6847 or "The Philippine
      Sports Commission Act.";
   c. School – refers to an institution recognized by the State which undertakes
      educational operations such as grade school, high school, college, university, or
      technical-vocational education and training institution; and
   d. Student-Athlete – refers to a student currently enrolled in any school who is part of
      any of the school's athletic teams or programs and is representing or has an
      intention to represent the school in an interschool athletic program or competition:
      Provided, That a student shall still be considered a Student-Athlete for purposes
      of certain provisions of this Act if there is a valid reason for his/her non-enrollment,
      such as a long-term illness, severe incapacity, or personal reasons that seriously
      hinder enrollment for that term: Provided, Further, That for purposes of being
      eligible to represent his/her school in an inter-school athletic program or
      competition, the Student-Athlete shall be enrolled in at least a minimum full-time
      program of studies and be in good academic standing.

SEC. 5. Rights of Student-Athletes. – Student-Athletes shall have the right to:

   a. Education
      1. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to have his/her educational needs prioritized
         by the participating school or athletic association. To this extent, the school
         administration will strive to provide tutorials or extra academic support to assist
         the Student-Athlete in maintaining good scholastic standing;
      2. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to have his/her academic performance
         monitored by the participating school to ascertain how his/her involvement in
         athletic programs and competitions affects his/her class performance;
   3. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to be allowed by the participating school to
      be excused from class, which will not be charged against his/her allowable
      absences, due to sports-related activities and circumstances attested to by the
      concerned coach or sports official: Provided, That he/she makes up for the
      missed classes, activities, or tests;
   4. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to continue availing of his/her full or partial
      scholarship for his/her continued education and/or financial support even after
      suffering from injury that resulted from his/her participation in the athletic
      program, which prevents him/her from further training for and competing in
      inter-school competitions; and
   5. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to complete his/her academic requirements
      on time. In no case shall a Student-Athlete in junior or senior high school be
      allowed to repeat a year level and play at the same time.

b. Health and Safety
   1. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to train and compete in safe and healthy
      environments, as provided by the school during practice and training sessions
      or by the athletic association during inter-school athletic programs and
      competitions;
   2. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to have safe, well-maintained, and
      upgraded equipment, and well-fitted uniforms to be provided by the school or
      athletic association;
   3. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to have access to health care professionals
      who monitor his/her physical health condition and nutritional needs, particularly
      during training and competitions and immediately after the competitions, as
      provided by the school, and to medics in emergency situations during athletic
      programs or competitions, as provided by the athletic association;
   4. It is the right of current and former Student-Athletes to receive free or
      subsidized medical expense coverage from the participating schools or athletic
      associations he/she represented or are representing in case of any physical
      injury or sports-related harm suffered by him/her as a result of his/her
      participation in the athletic program or competition;
   5. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to have privacy with regard to any personal
      health information that might cause defamation or insult towards
      himself/herself, such as his/her family's medical history or physical or mental
      condition requiring treatment, among others;
   6. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to file for a temporary Leave of Absence
      (LOA) from his/her participation in school due to valid grounds, such as the
      need to focus on studies, illness/sickness or any personal/family reasons:
      Provided, That an absence beyond a reasonable period may result in the loss
      of scholarship or other privileges: Provided, Further, That it is understood that
      he/she cannot participate in events of the athletic associations during such
      leave; and
   7. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to be protected by the school or athletic
      association against exploitation, inappropriate influences, and other
       circumstances prejudicial to his/her physical, mental, emotional, social, and/or
       moral development, such as public humiliation, among others.

c. Choice of School
   1. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to transfer from his/her current school to
      any school that he/she is accepted into for any reason, such as, but not limited
      to:
      i. Unavailability of his/her desired class/course;
      ii. Failure to pass the class/course leading to dismissal;
      iii. Violation of the school's policy leading to dismissal; or
      iv. Personal reasons/considerations.
   2. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to be free from any act of restriction or
      punishment by the old and/or the new school due to his/her transfer from the
      former to the latter, in accordance with Section 4 of Republic Act No. 10676 or
      the Student-Athletes Protection Act.

d. Other Rights
   1. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to undergo training and workshops for the
      further development of his/her skills related to his/her sport. To this end, the
      school or the athletic association cannot prohibit the Student-Athlete from
      attending such training or workshops: Provided, That the Student-Athlete
      endeavors to schedule the same on a date that does not conflict with the games
      of the school and/or the athletic association;
   2. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to try out for and participate in international
      competitions where he/she will represent the country, and other national
      competitions. This shall not prevent him/her from playing for his/her school
      team or in any competitions of athletic associations: Provided, That he/she
      remains in good academic standing;
   3. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to receive or renew his/her athletic grants
      for the current semester or term: Provided, That he/she complies with the
      requirements of the school where he/she is enrolled in;
   4. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to be coached by skilled sports officials,
      who are trained in sport-specific safety and equipped with the latest information
      about the risks and hazards of their respective sports.

      It is also the right of the Student-Athlete to be trained by a coach other than
      those associated with his/her school: Provided, That the same is coordinated
      with his/her coach and/or director of the school's athletic program or the latter's
      equivalent: Provided, Further, That the Student-Athlete shall not be prevented
      from exercising said right without valid cause;
   5. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to have consistent, open, and inclusive
      dialogue with school officials and athletic associations regarding the
      establishment of rules, policies, and regulations that concern and affect them;
   6. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to be treated with respect and dignity, and
      be free from any form of discrimination on account of age, sex, gender,
      language, ethnicity, religion, ideology, disability, education, or status;
      7. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to be free from abuse or violence, be it
         physical, verbal, sexual, emotional, psychological, spiritual, or cultural in
         nature.

         Student-Athletes are strongly encouraged to promptly report any incident
         involving abuse or violence in order to facilitate a fast and satisfactory
         resolution thereof;
      8. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to have opportunities to engage in other
         safe recreational activities during his/her leisure hours: Provided, That it is not
         inconsistent with his/her sport and will not expose him/her to undue risks; and
      9. It is the right of the Student-Athlete to engage in gainful employment: Provided,
         That it does not interfere with his/her studies, training schedule, and
         competitions and does not prevent him/her from playing for his/her school team
         or in any competitions of the athletic associations: Provided, Further, That
         he/she remains in good academic standing.

SEC. 6. Obligations of Student-Athletes. – For active involvement in the promotion and
protection of their rights:

   a. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to conduct himself/herself in a
      responsible manner at all times, reflective of the values of the school that he/she
      represents, bearing in mind that he/she carries the name and colors of his/her
      school and is a role model to the other students;
   b. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to train regularly and obey the rules
      set by his/her coaches and the school;
   c. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to represent his/her school in
      competitions chosen by his/her coach and/or school. In case of conflict between a
      school-sanctioned competition and other sports events, he/she is duty-bound to
      prioritize the former with the exception of international competitions or pre-
      qualifying events thereof;
   d. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to voluntarily present himself/herself
      for the pre-competition and post-competition general health examinations;
   e. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to attend training and workshops
      designed and conducted for him/her;
   f. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to prioritize his/her academic
      performance to ensure that his/her participation in athletic programs will not disrupt
      or hinder the completion of his/her class/course. He/she shall diligently attend
      classes and comply with the minimum class/course load, passing grade
      requirement, and other requirements set by the school;
   g. A Student-Athlete shall not be allowed to participate in any competition if he/she
      has not attained the minimum academic requirement, unless he/she completes
      his/her missing requirements during summer break;
   h. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete not to use, offer, or purchase any
      performance-enhancing drugs (PEDs) and other prohibited substances;
   i. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to uphold the integrity of the games,
      the name and reputation of his/her school, and the athletic association he/she is
      representing by not being involved in betting, game-fixing, wagering, gambling,
      and other illicit acts or activities, nor shall he/she be affiliated or involved with
      persons associated with gambling and/or game-fixing;
   j. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to practice and maintain a respectful
      and non-violent attitude towards himself/herself, other athletes, coaches, sports
      officials, and any person in general; and
   k. It is the responsibility of the Student-Athlete to preserve and promote the school-
      sanctioned competition in all stages, from recruitment to competition, in which
      he/she participates.

SEC. 7. Obligations of Schools. – Schools have the following obligations:

   a. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to support the Student-Athlete in
      fulfilling his/her responsibilities, first and foremost, as a student and then as an
      athlete;
   b. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to provide counselors, other than
      coaches, who will help the Student-Athlete in his/her academic work, in balancing
      his/her responsibilities, and in reaching his/her full potential in his/her sport;
   c. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to monitor the academic performance
      of the Student-Athlete and certify that he/she complies with the minimum
      class/course load, passing grade requirement, and other conditions set in this Act
      and by the schools;
   d. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to provide the Student-Athletes with
      a safe and healthy training environment;
   e. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to provide the Student-Athlete with
      safe, well-maintained, and upgraded equipment and well-fitted uniforms;
   f. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to provide the Student-Athlete access
      to health care professionals, who will monitor his/her physical health condition and
      nutritional needs, particularly during training and immediately after the
      competitions;
   g. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to provide the Student-Athlete with
      free or subsidized medical expense coverage in case of any physical injury or
      sports-related harm suffered by him/her as a result of his/her participation in the
      athletic program or competition;
   h. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to maintain the Student-Athlete's full
      or partial scholarship for his/her continued education and/or financial support even
      after suffering from an injury that resulted from his/her participation in the athletic
      program, which prevents him/her from further training for and competing in inter-
      school competitions;
   i. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to allow the Student-Athlete to file a
      temporary LOA from the athletic team or program due to valid grounds, such as
      the need to focus on studies, illness/sickness or any personal/family reasons:
      Provided, That an absence beyond a reasonable period may result in the loss of
      scholarship or other privileges: Provided, Further, That it is understood he/she
      cannot participate in events of the athletic associations;
j. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to protect the privacy of any personal
   health information of the Student-Athlete that might cause defamation or insult
   towards him/herself, such as his/her family's medical history or physical or mental
   condition requiring treatment, among others;
k. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to protect the Student-Athlete against
   exploitation, inappropriate influences, and other circumstances prejudicial to
   his/her physical, mental, emotional, social, and moral development, such as public
   humiliation, among others;
l. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to uphold the Constitutional right of
   the Student-Athlete to a quality education, which includes the choice of school or
   the right to transfer to another school for any reason such as, but not limited to,
   those enumerated in Section 5(c)(i) of this Act, subject to the provisions of Republic
   Act No. 10676 or the Student-Athletes Protection Act;
m. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to allow the Student-Athlete to try out
   for and participate in international competitions where he/she will represent the
   country and other national competitions. This shall not prevent him/her from
   playing for his/her school team or in any competitions of athletic associations:
   Provided, That he/she remains in good academic standing;
n. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to grant or renew the Student-Athlete's
   athletic grants for the current semester or term: Provided, That the Student-Athlete
   complies with the requirements of the school;
o. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to allow the Student-Athlete to be
   coached by skilled sports officials who are trained in sport-specific safety and
   equipped with the latest information about the risks and hazards of their respective
   sports. The school and its officials shall likewise respect the right of the Student-
   Athlete to be trained by a coach other than those associated with the school,
   subject to coordination with the coach or athletic director, and shall not prevent the
   exercise of such right without valid cause;
p. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to ensure that no Student-Athlete
   shall, on account of age, sex, gender, language, ethnicity, religion, disability,
   education, or status, be excluded from participation in, denied the benefits of, or
   subjected to discrimination under any athletic program or activity. Schools shall
   also ensure pro rata representation of women in athletic scholarship programs
   based on the percentage of women in the total student population;
q. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to protect the Student-Athlete from
   abuse or violence, whether physical, verbal, sexual, emotional, psychological,
   spiritual, or cultural in nature, and to promptly act upon incidents involving such
   abuse or violence;
r. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to protect the health, safety, and
   welfare of the Student-Athlete from performance-enhancing drugs (PEDs) and
   other prohibited substances. School officials or representatives who offer or
   pressure Student-Athletes to take such substances shall be terminated from the
   school;
s. It is the duty of the schools and their officials not to sponsor, operate, advertise, or
   promote any betting, game-fixing, wagering, or gambling scheme based on
   competitive games involving Student-Athletes or on their performances;
   t. It is the duty of the schools and their officials not to intentionally suspend or
       otherwise delay a junior or senior high school Student-Athlete from graduating in
       order to extend the period of athletic eligibility;
   u. It is the duty of the schools not to offer benefits and incentives that result in the
       commercialization of the Student-Athlete, in accordance with Sections 5 and 6 of
       Republic Act No. 10676 or the Student-Athletes Protection Act;
   v. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to support the Student-Athlete's right
       to undergo training and workshops for the further development of his/her skills,
       provided that the schedule does not conflict with school games;
   w. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to conduct forums informing new
       Student-Athletes and school officials of this Act, their corresponding rights and
       obligations, and to use various forms of communication to keep them updated;
   x. It is also the duty of the schools and their officials to conduct forums educating
       Student-Athletes on topics including discrimination against women, the dangers of
       PEDs, and protection from violence and abuse;
   y. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to allow the Student-Athlete
       opportunities to engage in other safe recreational activities during leisure hours,
       provided these are consistent with the sport and do not expose the Student-Athlete
       to undue risks;
   z. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to allow the Student-Athlete to engage
       in gainful employment, provided it does not interfere with studies, training
       schedules, or competitions; and
   aa. It is the duty of the schools and their officials to preserve and promote school-
       sanctioned competitions in all stages, from recruitment to competition, in which the
       Student-Athlete participates.

SEC. 8. Obligations of Athletic Associations. – Athletic Associations have the following
obligations:

   a. It is the duty of the athletic associations to support the Student-Athlete’s choice of
      school without any restriction or penalty. In the event that the issue is brought to
      the athletic association, the same shall be resolved in favor of honoring the right
      of choice of the Student-Athlete: Provided, That there are no violations of Section
      7(u) of this Act;
   b. It is the duty of the athletic associations to ensure that the Student-Athlete plays in
      a safe and healthy environment during inter-school athletic programs and
      competitions;
   c. It is the duty of the athletic associations to provide the Student-Athlete with safe,
      well-maintained, and upgraded equipment for use during inter-school athletic
      programs and competitions;
   d. It is the duty of the athletic associations to provide the Student-Athlete access to
      medics in case of accidents, injuries, and other emergency situations during
      athletic programs or competitions;
   e. It is the duty of the athletic associations to keep and protect the privacy of any
      personal health information of the Student-Athlete that might cause defamation or
      insult towards himself/herself, such as his/her family’s medical history or physical
      or mental condition requiring treatment, among others;
   f. It is the duty of the athletic associations to protect the Student-Athlete against
      exploitation, inappropriate influences, and other circumstances prejudicial to
      his/her physical, mental, emotional, social, and moral development, such as public
      humiliation, among others;
   g. It is the duty of the athletic associations not to sponsor, operate, advertise, or
      promote any betting, game-fixing, wagering, or gambling scheme on one or more
      competitive games in which the Student-Athlete participates, or intended to
      participate, or on one or more performances of the Student-Athlete in such games;
   h. It is the duty of the athletic associations to support the Student-Athlete’s right to
      undergo training and workshops for the further development of his/her skills. To
      this end, athletic associations cannot prohibit the Student-Athlete from attending
      such training or workshops: Provided, That the Student-Athlete will endeavor to
      schedule the same on a date that does not conflict with the games of the athletic
      association;
   i. It is the duty of the athletic associations to allow the Student-Athlete to try out for
      and participate in international competitions where he/she will represent the
      country, and other national competitions. This shall not prevent him/her from
      playing for his/her school team or in any competitions of athletic associations:
      Provided, That he/she remains in good academic standing;
   j. It is the duty of the athletic associations to uphold the rights of Student-Athletes
      and shall not infringe on their playing years, except for a maximum period of one
      (1) year residency, as provided in Republic Act No. 10676 or the Student-Athletes
      Protection Act;
   k. It is the duty of the athletic associations to sanction member-schools which violate
      the rights of the Student-Athlete or fail to address the same or otherwise allow its
      officials to do so. In this regard, consistent with Republic Act No. 10676 or the
      Student-Athletes Protection Act, athletic associations shall enact rules which shall
      support this Act and provide sanctions for violations thereof; and
   l. It is the duty of the athletic associations to preserve and promote the amateur
      nature of school-sanctioned competitions in all stages, from recruitment to
      competition, that the Student-Athlete participates in.

SEC. 9. Obligations of NSAs and the Philippine Sports Commission. – It is the duty of
NSAs to support the schedule of the various athletic associations to the extent possible,
so as not to schedule tryouts, trainings, or trips abroad during crucial training periods or
competitions. They shall also help strengthen school sports programs by coordinating
with schools and athletic associations, providing technical guidance, facilitating access to
coaches and training resources, and promoting student-athlete participation in grassroots
and age-group competitions.

SEC. 10. Implementation. – The Commission on Higher Education (CHED), the
Department of Education (DepEd), the athletic associations (UAAP, NCAA, WNCAA and
SCUAA, among others), the PSC, and the National Academy of Sports, with the
participation of representatives from sports organizations with proven track records of
involvement in promotion of the rights and welfare of Filipino athletes, shall promulgate
the Implementing Rules and Regulations of this Act within thirty (30) days from its
effectivity. The DepEd and CHED shall penalize schools that disregard a Student-
Athlete’s academic rights and/or that do not sanction their officials for doing so. DepEd
and CHED shall also be responsible for penalizing schools that offer or abet in the offer
of incentives and benefits beyond those allowed by Section 7(u) of this Act. To this end,
the DepEd and the CHED may impose sanctions such as, but not limited to, diminution
or withdrawal of subsidy, recommendation on the downgrading or withdrawal of
accreditation, program termination, or school closure: Provided, That these shall be in
accordance with the provisions of Republic Act No. 10676, or the Student-Athletes
Protection Act, when applicable.

SEC. 11. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in force and effect.

SEC. 12. Effect on Republic Act No. 10676. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed as to
have amended or repealed Republic Act No. 10676, or the Student-Athletes Protection
Act.

SEC. 13. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance, executive order,
letter of instruction, rule or regulation inconsistent with the provisions of this Act is hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 14. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
