                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Artificial Intelligence (AI) is reshaping economies, industries, public institutions,
and everyday life at an unprecedented pace. Across the world, governments are
adopting national policies and regulatory mechanisms to harness the benefits of AI
while safeguarding the public against its potential risks. From healthcare and
education to transportation, finance, agriculture, and public governance, AI
technologies are increasingly influencing how societies function and how decisions are
made.

       The Philippines stands at a crucial point in its digital transformation journey.
While AI presents immense opportunities to improve public services, strengthen
national competitiveness, generate employment, and accelerate innovation, it likewise
raises serious concerns relating to privacy, cybersecurity, labor displacement,
misinformation, algorithmic discrimination, and the misuse of automated systems. The
absence of a comprehensive legal and institutional framework governing AI may
expose the country to technological vulnerabilities and leave Filipino citizens
inadequately protected from harmful and unethical applications of emerging
technologies.
       This proposed measure seeks to establish a balanced, adaptive, and rights-
based framework for the development and regulation of Artificial Intelligence in the
Philippines. It aims to encourage innovation and investment in AI while ensuring that
the deployment and use of such technologies remain transparent, accountable, fair,
secure, and aligned with constitutional rights and national interests.

       The bill provides guiding principles for ethical AI governance, promotes
research and development, strengthens workforce preparedness, protects human
rights and data privacy, and establishes mechanisms for transparency and public
accountability in AI systems. It likewise adopts a risk-based approach to AI regulation,
ensuring that high-risk AI systems are subject to stricter oversight and compliance
standards.

      Central to this proposed legislation is the creation of the Artificial Intelligence
Development Authority (AIDA), which shall serve as the lead government body
responsible for formulating national AI policies, coordinating inter-agency initiatives,
developing regulatory standards, and ensuring compliance with ethical and legal
safeguards in the use of AI technologies.

        Ultimately, this measure seeks to position the Philippines as a responsible and
globally competitive participant in the evolving AI ecosystem while ensuring that
technological advancement remains people-centered, inclusive, and beneficial to all
Filipinos.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 4           SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Artificial
 5   Intelligence Development and Governance Act.”
 6

 7           SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the
 8   State to promote the responsible development, deployment, and utilization of Artificial
 9   Intelligence (AI) technologies in the Philippines consistent with the principles of
10   transparency, accountability, fairness, inclusivity, privacy protection, human dignity,
11   public welfare, and national security. The State recognizes the transformative potential
12   of AI to drive innovation, improve public services, expand economic opportunities, and
13   strengthen national development. Toward this end, the State shall establish an
14   enabling regulatory environment that encourages innovation while safeguarding the
15   rights and welfare of the people.
16
17          SECTION 3. Definition of terms. - For purposes of this Act:
18
 1         (a) “Artificial Intelligence (AI)” refers to machine-based systems capable of
 2   performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, including learning,
 3   reasoning, prediction, decision-making, language processing, and problem-solving;
 4
 5         (b) “AI System” refers to any software, application, algorithm, or computational
 6   model utilizing AI techniques;
 7
 8           (c) “High-Risk AI System” refers to AI systems that significantly affect
 9   fundamental rights, public safety, health, employment, education, law enforcement,
10   or critical infrastructure;
11
12          (d) “AI Developer” refers to any person or entity involved in the design,
13   creation, testing, or deployment of AI systems;
14
15           (e) “AI User” refers to any person, organization, or government agency that
16   utilizes AI systems;
17
18          (f) “Human Oversight” refers to mechanisms ensuring that AI systems remain
19   subject to meaningful human supervision and intervention; and
20
21         (g) “Transparency” refers to the obligation to provide understandable
22   information regarding the operation, purpose, and decision-making processes of AI
23   systems.
24
25         SECTION 4. Creation of the Artificial Intelligence Development
26   Authority (AIDA). - There is hereby created an independent body to be known as
27   the Artificial Intelligence Development Authority (AIDA), which shall serve as the
28   primary agency responsible for the formulation, coordination, implementation, and
29   enforcement of national AI policies and regulations.
30
31        The AIDA shall be attached to the Department of Science and Technology
32   (DOST) for policy and program coordination.
33

35   following powers and functions:

37
38         (b) Develop standards, rules, and ethical guidelines governing AI systems;
39
40          (c) Conduct monitoring, audits, and assessments of AI systems, particularly
41   high-risk applications;
42
43           (d) Coordinate with government agencies, academic institutions, private
44   entities, and international organizations on AI-related initiatives;
45
46         (e) Promote AI research, innovation, and workforce development programs;
47
 1           (f) Establish regulatory sandboxes for emerging AI technologies;
 2
 3          (g) Investigate violations of this Act and impose appropriate administrative
 4   sanctions; and
 5
 6         (h) Submit annual reports to Congress and the Office of the President regarding
 7   the implementation of this Act.

 9           SECTION 6. Principles of Responsible AI Governance. - The
10   development and deployment of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems shall be guided by
11   the principles of respect for human rights and human dignity, fairness and non-
12   discrimination, transparency and explainability, accountability and meaningful human
13   oversight, privacy and data protection, safety, security, and reliability, as well as
14   inclusivity and accessibility. AI systems shall be designed, developed, and utilized in a
15   manner that protects the welfare of individuals and communities, promotes public
16   trust, prevents harm and bias, safeguards personal data, and ensures that the benefits
17   of AI technologies are accessible and responsive to the diverse needs of the Filipino
18   people.
19
20          SECTION 7. Regulation of High-Risk AI Systems. - High-risk AI systems
21   shall be subject to stricter regulatory and compliance requirements to ensure the
22   protection of public welfare, fundamental rights, safety, and national security. Such
23   requirements shall include the conduct of mandatory risk assessments prior to
24   deployment, the establishment of meaningful human oversight mechanisms, the
25   maintenance of adequate technical documentation and audit trails, and the
26   implementation of robust data governance and cybersecurity safeguards. Providers
27   and users of high-risk AI systems shall likewise comply with transparency obligations
28   and undergo periodic compliance reviews, evaluations, or audits as may be prescribed
29   by the Artificial Intelligence Development Authority (AIDA).

30          SECTION 8. Prohibited AI Practices. - The following AI practices are
31   prohibited:

32           (a) AI systems that manipulate human behavior in a manner that causes
33   harm;

34         (b) Social scoring systems that unfairly discriminate against individuals or
35   groups;

36           (c) Unauthorized biometric surveillance or facial recognition systems that
37           violate privacy rights;

38           (d) AI systems designed for unlawful discrimination; and

39           (e) Any AI application declared unlawful by the AIDA pursuant to this Act.
