                             AN ACT
TRANSFERRING THE NATIONAL FIRE TRAINING INSTITUTE (NFTI) FROM THE
PHILIPPINE PUBLIC SAFETY COLLEGE (PPSC) TO THE BUREAU OF
FIRE PROTECTION (BFP), AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE SECTIONS 66, 67,
AND 68 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6975, AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC ACT
NO. 11279, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT ACT OF 1990”

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill is in support of the legislative agenda of the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP),
our partner in securing our public and private infrastructures from all destructive
 ires.

The National Fire Training Institute is established under Republic Act No. 6975 and is
responsible for training BFP personnel.

This measure seeks to transfer the NFTI, which is currently under the administration
of the Philippine Public Safety College, to the BFP to give the BFP the freedom to
manage its programs, craft its policies, and calibrate its programs of instructions in
order to be more attuned to the needs of the BFP and its trainees.

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Section 67 of Republic Act No. 6975 is further amended to read as follows:

     “Sec. 67. In addition, the BFP shall be responsible for the mandatory and
     leadership training of all Fire Non-Commissioned Of icers (FNCOs). The
     supervision and control over the NFTI shall be established in the revised table
     or organization and equipment and staf ing patterns under Section 68-B.

     The NFTI shall be headed by a Fire Brigadier General.”

SECTION 2. Section 66 of R.A. No. 6975, as amended by R.A. No. 11279, otherwise
known as “An Act Transferring the PNPA and the NPTI from the PPSC to the PNP” is
hereby amended to read as follows:

     “Sec. 66. Creation of the Philippine Public Safety College. – There is hereby created
     the Philippine Public Safety College (PPSC), which shall be the premier
     educational institution for the training of all personnel of the Bureau of Jail

     Fire Commissioned Of icers and Police Commissioned Of icers, and specialized

SECTION 3. Section 67 of R.A. No. 6975, as amended by R.A. No. 11279, is hereby
amended to read as follows:

     “Sec. 67. Composition, Powers, and Functions. – The college shall consist of the
     National Police College, the National Forensic Science Training Institute, the
     Bureau of Jail Management and Penology, and other special training centers as
     may be created by the Department, whose functions shall be as follows:

     (a) Formulate and implement training programs for all personnel of the Jail
         Bureaus and other training centers under the Department;
     (b) Formulate and implement select mandatory career courses for police
         commissioned of icers;
     (c) Formulate and implement specialized courses for personnel of the PNP;
     (d) Establish and maintain adequate physical training facilities;
     (e) Develop and implement research and development to support educational
         training programs;
     (f) Conduct an assessment of the training needs of all its clientele; and
     (g) Perform such other related functions as may be prescribed by the Secretary.”

SECTION 4. A new Section 68-A is hereby inserted to read as follows:

     “Sec. 68-A. Transfer of Personnel and Properties. – The personnel and all
     properties, facilities, records, equipment, funds, choices in action,
     appropriation, rights, functions, liabilities, and other assets of the NFTI are
     hereby transferred to the BFP.”

SECTION 5. A new Section 68-B is hereby inserted to read as follows:

     effectivity of this Act, the Chief of the BFP shall submit the revised table of

     of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) and the Department of Budget and
     Management (DBM) for approval. The revised table of organization and
     equipment and staf ing pattern may include the reorganization and renaming of

SECTION 6. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of the NFTI
under the PPSC. Thereafter, such amount as may be necessary for its continued
implementation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the BFP shall, in coordination with the PPSC and DILG shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this
Act.

SECTION 8. Separability Clause. — If any part, section or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such
declaration shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 10. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
