                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

           As the Filipino population ages, more senior citizens are living with
    chronic and age-related conditions that require continuous maintenance
    medications. The rising cost of medicines, vitamins, and other medically
    necessary supplements can place a significant financial burden on elderly
    Filipinos, particularly those who rely solely on pensions or have limited
    sources of income.

          While Republic Act No. 9994, otherwise known as the “Expanded
    Senior Citizens Act of 2010,” provides senior citizens with discounts and tax
    exemptions on medicines and other health-related goods, these benefits do
    not necessarily ensure that maintenance medications are available and
    affordable to all senior citizens. For many elderly Filipinos, even the
    remaining cost of regularly purchased medicines may constitute a
    substantial portion of their limited income.

          This measure seeks to establish a government program that will
    provide free maintenance medications and medically indicated vitamins and
    mineral supplements to qualified senior citizens, subject to an appropriate
    national formulary and prescription requirements. It also provides
    mechanisms for community-based access and delivery, particularly for the
    oldest- old, frail, homebound, and bedridden senior citizens.

          The measure further ensures coordination with the Philippine Health
    Insurance Corporation’s existing medication benefits, including the
    Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment (GAMOT),
    to complement existing programs and prevent duplication of benefits.

          By reducing the financial burden of essential medicines and promoting
    continuity of treatment, this measure seeks to improve medication
    adherence, prevent avoidable complications and hospitalizations, and
    promote healthier and more dignified aging among Filipino senior citizens.
          In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is
    earnestly sought.

                             “AN ACT
          PROVIDING FOR FREE MAINTENANCE MEDICATIONS,
          VITAMINS, AND MINERAL SUPPLEMENTS FOR SENIOR
          CITIZENS”

           Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
    Philippines in Congress assembled:

          Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Lingap Gamot
    at Resistensya Pack Para sa Nakatatandang Pinoy Act."

          Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall adopt an
    integrated and comprehensive approach to health development, which shall
    endeavor to make essential goods, health, and other social services available
    to all the people at affordable cost, giving priority to the needs of the
    underprivileged, sick, elderly, persons with disabilities, women, and
    children.

          It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote and protect the
    health and well-being of senior citizens by ensuring continuous and
    affordable access to essential maintenance medications and medically
    indicated vitamins and mineral supplements, thereby helping prevent
    complications, improve treatment adherence, and enable senior citizens to
    maintain an active and meaningful participation in society.

          Section 3. Free Maintenance Medications, Vitamins, and Mineral
    Supplements. - The government shall provide free maintenance medications
    and medically indicated vitamins and mineral supplements to qualified
    senior citizens, subject to the coverage, formulary, prescription, and other
    conditions provided under this Act and its implementing rules and
    regulations.

           Section 4. Eligibility and Registration. - To qualify for the free
    maintenance medications, vitamins, and mineral supplements, a senior
    citizen must:

          a) be a registered senior citizen with the Office of Senior Citizens
             Affairs (OSCA);
                                                                                     2
      b) be registered with a designated primary healthcare provider,
         government health facility, or authorized dispensing facility, as may
         be prescribed by the Department of Health (DOH);
      c) have a valid prescription or treatment plan issued by a licensed
         physician or other health professional authorized by law to
         prescribe the covered medication, in accordance with applicable
         laws, rules, and regulations; and
      d) comply with such other reasonable requirements as may be
         prescribed by the DOH to establish medical necessity and prevent
         duplication or misuse of benefits.

       Section 5. Access to Maintenance Medications, Vitamins, and
Mineral Supplements. - The free maintenance medications, vitamins, and
mineral supplements shall be accessed through barangay health centers,
government health facilities, Botika ng Bayan, PhilHealth-accredited
dispensing facilities, and/or pharmacies or drug outlets duly accredited or
authorized by the DOH or PhilHealth, as applicable: Provided, that access to
and delivery of these items to the most vulnerable-old, frail, homebound, and
bedridden senior citizens may be facilitated through designated barangay
health workers or other authorized community health personnel, subject to
applicable laws and regulations on the dispensing, handling, storage, and
delivery of medicines.

      Section 6. National Senior Citizens Maintenance Medicine and
Supplement Formulary. – The DOH, in coordination with the Philippine
Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), Food and Drug Administration
(FDA), and other concerned agencies, shall establish and periodically update
a National Senior Citizens Maintenance Medicine and Supplement
Formulary identifying the maintenance medications, vitamins, and mineral
supplements that may be provided under this Act.

       The Formulary shall, as appropriate, consider the prevalence of
chronic diseases among senior citizens, clinical necessity, safety and
efficacy, availability of generic equivalents, cost-effectiveness, and the
recommendations of relevant health experts.

      Section 7. Harmonization with PhilHealth Benefits. – Covered
maintenance medications of qualified senior citizens that are included in the
PhilHealth Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment
(GAMOT) or any successor outpatient medicine benefit shall be provided in
accordance with the applicable PhilHealth benefit package and shall not be
subject to duplicate reimbursement under this Act.

      Medicines, vitamins, and mineral supplements covered by this Act but
not included in the applicable PhilHealth benefit package may be provided
through the program established under this Act, subject to the National
Senior Citizens Maintenance Medicine and Supplement Formulary and the
guidelines prescribed by the DOH.

    Section 8. Special Assistance for Vulnerable Senior Citizens. - The
DOH, in coordination with the National Commission of Senior Citizens
                                                                                 3
(NCSC), Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD),
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), local government
units, and other concerned agencies, shall establish mechanisms to
prioritize and facilitate the delivery of covered maintenance medications,
vitamins, and mineral supplements to most vulnerable-old, frail, indigent,
homebound, and bedridden senior citizens, particularly those who have
difficulty accessing health facilities.

       Section 9. Implementation and Coordination. – The DOH shall be
responsible for the implementation of this Act. It shall coordinate with
relevant agencies, including but not limited to PhilHealth, the NCSC, the
DSWD, the DILG, Food and Drug Administration (FDA), Department of
Budget and Management (DBM), local government units, and recognized
senior citizens associations, to ensure the efficient allocation, procurement,
distribution, and monitoring of the free maintenance medications, vitamins,
and mineral supplements.

       Section 10. Monitoring and Reporting. - The DOH shall establish a
monitoring system to track the allocation, procurement, distribution, and
utilization of the free maintenance medications, vitamins, and mineral
supplements. An annual report shall be submitted to Congress detailing the
program's implementation, expenditures, coverage, availability of medicines,
incidence of stock-outs, and effectiveness in improving the health and well-
being of senior citizens.

      Section 11. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the DOH and other concerned agencies, subject to existing
budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations. Thereafter,
such sums as may be necessary for the continued implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

      Section 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The DOH, in
coordination with PhilHealth, NCSC, DSWD, DILG, FDA, DBM, and other
concerned agencies, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to
implement this Act effectively within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

       Section 13. Separability Clause. - If any part, section, or provision of
this Act is held invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not
affected shall remain in full force and effect.

      Sec. 14. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and
regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed
or amended accordingly.

      Sec. 15. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
