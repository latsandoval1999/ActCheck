                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

Since the enactment of Republic Act No. 6975, otherwise known as the Department of the
Interior and Local Government Act of 1990, the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology
(BJMP) and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), under the Department of the Interior and
Local Government (DILG), have continuously strengthened their roles in maintaining public
safety, promoting public order, and protecting lives and property. Their professionalization was
further reinforced by Republic Act No. 9263, which professionalized both agencies.

Over the years, the BJMP and BFP have expanded their personnel complement and operational
responsibilities, reflecting the growing demands of their respective mandates. This underscores
the need to retain a competent, experienced, and professional workforce.

Currently, Section 39 of Republic Act No. 6975, as amended, sets the compulsory retirement
age of uniformed personnel at fifty-six (56) years. This measure seeks to increase the
compulsory retirement age to fifty-seven (57) years for uniformed personnel of the Philippine
National Police (PNP), BJMP, and BFP, thereby aligning their retirement ages.

The proposed adjustment will allow experienced personnel to further contribute their expertise,
leadership, and institutional knowledge, and provide more time to mentor younger personnel.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACТ INCREASING THE COMPULSORY AGE OF RETIREMENT FOR
 OFFICERS AND NON-OFFICERS OF THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE
(PNP), BUREAU OF JAIL MANAGEMENT AND PENOLOGY (BJMP), AND THE
BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION (BFP) FROM FIFTY-SIX (56) YEARS OLD TO
FIFTY-SEVEN (57) YEARS OLD, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 39
OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6975, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "DEPARTMENT
OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT ACT OF 1990,” AS AMENDED

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:



SECTION 1. Section 39 of Republic Act No. 6975 is hereby amended to read as follows:

“Section 39. Compulsory Retirement. – Compulsory retirement, for officer and non-officer,
INCLUDING THE UNIFORMED PERSONNEL OF THE BUREAU OF JAIL
MANAGEMENT AND PENOLOGY AND THE BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION, shall
be upon the attainment of age [fifty-six (56)] FIFTY-SEVEN (57): Provided, That in the case
of any officer with the rank of Police Brigadier General, Police Major General or Police
Lieutenant General, the Commission may allow his retention in the service for an unextendible
period of one (1) year.”

        SECTION 2. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 3. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

APPROVED.
