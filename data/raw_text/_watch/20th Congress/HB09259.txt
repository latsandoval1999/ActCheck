20                                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   This bill seeks to institutionalize a uniform five-year validity period for all Professional
23   Identification Cards (PICs) issued by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).
24
25   Presently, the validity and renewal cycles of PRC-issued identification cards are tied to
26   varying administrative requirements and continuing professional development
27   compliance, which may create unnecessary administrative burden on licensed
28   professionals and inefficiencies in government service delivery. A standardized five-
29   year validity period will streamline regulatory processes, reduce transaction costs, and
30   provide clarity and predictability for professionals across all regulated fields.
31
32   This measure also mandates the enhancement of the PRC’s Licensure Examination
33   and Registration Information System (LERIS) and related digital infrastructure to ensure
34   efficient processing, monitoring, and renewal of professional licenses. The
35   strengthening of these systems is intended to support modernization efforts within the
36   PRC and improve access to regulatory services, particularly through online platforms.
37
38   By extending and standardizing the validity period of Professional Identification Cards,
39   this bill aims to balance regulatory oversight with administrative efficiency, ensuring that
40   professionals are not unduly burdened while maintaining the integrity and accountability
41   of professional practice in the country.
42
43   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

22                                 AN ACТ
23   PROVIDING FOR A FIVE-YEAR VALIDITY PERIOD OF THE PROFESSIONAL
24   IDENTIFICATION CARDS OF ALL PROFESSIONAL UNDER THE PROFESSIONAL
25   REGULATION COMMISSION
26
27   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
28   Congress assembled:
29
30   SECTION 1. All Professional Identification Cards (PICs) issued by the Professional
31   Regulation Commission (PRC) shall be valid for five (5) years, subject to the compliance
32   with all the legal and procedural requirements for its issuance.
33
34   SEC. 2. To operationalize the provisions of this Act, the Information and Communication
35   Technology Service (ICTS) and other concerned offices or divisions of the PRC shall
36   complete the necessary systems enhancement, particularly the Licensure Examination
37   Registration Information System or the LERIS, and other administrative processes,
38   including the finalization of the revised assessment of annual registration fees within six
39   (6) months from the effectivity of this Act.
40
41   SEC. 3. If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act is declared invalid or
42   unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected are in full force and
43   effect.
44
45   SEC. 4. All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, and rules and regulations
46   contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are amended, repealed or
47   modified accordingly.
48
49   SEC. 5. Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
50   or in a newspaper of general circulation.
