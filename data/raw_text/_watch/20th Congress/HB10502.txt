                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Philippines has long been recognized for producing some of the
world's finest ube (Dioscorea alata). Once regarded as a traditional
backyard crop, ube has evolved into a premium ingredient sought after by
global food manufacturers, bakeries, restaurants, and consumers. From ice
cream and pastries to beverages and confectioneries, demand for
Philippine ube continues to expand in both domestic and international
markets.

        The country has seen the writing on the wall. The global appetite for
ube is no passing trend but a sustained opportunity that can generate jobs,
raise farm incomes, stimulate investments, and position the Philippines as
the undisputed world leader in premium ube products. Yet despite this
growing demand, the industry remains fragmented, constrained by
inconsistent planting materials, limited research, inadequate processing
facilities, weak value chain coordination, and the absence of a dedicated
institution to steer its long-term development.

      This measure recognizes that the Philippine ube industry deserves to
be treated as an anchor industry capable of driving rural development and
agricultural industrialization. By creating the Philippine Ube Industry
Development Office and institutionalizing a comprehensive industry
development program, the bill seeks to provide coherent leadership in
research, seed systems, production, post-harvest facilities, processing,
marketing, export promotion, and investment generation.

       More importantly, the measure embodies a long-term vision of
nurturing the industry from farm to global market. It seeks to empower
farmers with quality planting materials, modern technologies, financing, and
market access, while encouraging private investment in value-added
manufacturing and export-oriented enterprises. Through coordinated
planning and sustained government support, the Philippines can build a
resilient, competitive, and innovation-driven ube industry.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT​
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE PHILIPPINE UBE
      INDUSTRY, CREATING THE PHILIPPINE UBE INDUSTRY
  DEVELOPMENT OFFICE, ESTABLISHING THE PHILIPPINE UBE
      INDUSTRY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM AND FUND, AND
             APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the "Philippine
Ube Industry Development Act."

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to promote a competitive, sustainable, climate-resilient, and
export-oriented Philippine ube industry that enhances food security,
increases farmers' incomes, generates rural employment, and strengthens
the country's position as the global source of premium ube products.
Toward this end, the State shall:

     (a) Increase domestic ube production and productivity;
     (b) Develop an integrated value chain from seed production to export
     marketing;

     (c) Promote research, innovation, and commercialization of improved
     ube varieties;

     (d) Encourage investments in processing, storage, logistics, and
     value-added manufacturing;

     (e) Ensure quality assurance, traceability, and compliance with
     international standards; and

     (f) Strengthen collaboration among government agencies, local
     government units, state universities and colleges, the private sector,
     farmers, and exporters.

     SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. As used in this Act:

     (a) Department refers to the Department of Agriculture.

     (b) Office refers to the Philippine Ube Industry Development Office
     (PUIDO) created under this Act.

     (c) Ube Industry refers to all activities involving the production,
     propagation, research, processing, marketing, distribution, export,
     and utilization of ube (Dioscorea alata).

     (d) Value Chain refers to the interconnected activities from production
     of planting materials to processing, distribution, retail, and export.

     (e) Stakeholders refer to farmers, cooperatives, processors,
     exporters, traders, researchers, academic institutions, financial
     institutions, and other persons engaged in the ube industry.

      SECTION 4. Creation of the Philippine Ube Industry
Development Office. There is hereby created within the Department of
Agriculture the Philippine Ube Industry Development Office (PUIDO).

     The Philippine Ube Industry Development Office (Office) shall be the
primary office of the Department responsible for the formulation,
coordination, integration, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of all
programs, projects, and activities relating to the Philippine ube industry.
      The Office shall serve as the central coordinating body of the
Department for all matters pertaining to ube production, research and
development, extension, processing, marketing, value chain development,
investment promotion, and export development.

      The Office shall be headed by an Executive Director, who shall be
appointed by the Secretary of Agriculture, and shall be assisted by such
technical and administrative personnel as may be necessary.

       The Secretary of Agriculture may reorganize or realign existing units,
personnel, programs, and resources of the Department necessary for the
effective implementation of this Act.

      SECTION 5. Functions of the Philippine Ube Industry
Development Office. The Office shall have the following powers and
functions:

     (a) Serve as the principal office of the Department for the planning,
     implementation, coordination, and monitoring of all national ube
     industry development programs;

     (b) Formulate, update, and oversee the implementation of the
     Philippine Ube Industry Roadmap;

     (c) Harmonize the plans, programs, and projects of all bureaus,
     attached agencies, regional field offices, and other offices of the
     Department relating to the ube industry;

     (d) Coordinate with other national government agencies, local
     government units, state universities and colleges, government-owned
     and controlled corporations, farmers' organizations, cooperatives,
     processors, exporters, development partners, and private sector
     stakeholders in the implementation of industry programs;

     (e) Recommend policies, standards, and regulatory measures
     necessary for the sustainable growth and competitiveness of the
     industry;

     (f) Develop and maintain a comprehensive national database and
     information management system for the Philippine ube industry,
      including production, productivity, market prices, processing capacity,
      exports, investments, and value chain performance;

      (g) Coordinate the development and distribution of quality-assured
      planting materials, certified seed systems, nurseries, and germplasm
      conservation programs;

      (h) Promote research, product development, biotechnology,
      mechanization, climate-resilient production systems, and innovative
      technologies throughout the value chain;

      (i) Develop and implement programs on post-harvest handling,
      storage, processing, logistics, packaging, branding, and value
      addition;

      (j) Facilitate market development, domestic promotion, export
      expansion, international market access, and participation in global
      trade exhibitions;

      (k) Coordinate investment promotion initiatives and facilitate
      public-private partnerships for industry development;

      (l) Assist farmers, cooperatives, and agribusiness enterprises in
      accessing credit, crop insurance, technical assistance, training,
      extension services, and other government support programs;

      (m) Monitor and evaluate the implementation of government
      interventions affecting the industry and recommend appropriate policy
      reforms;

      (n) Prepare annual industry performance reports and submit
      recommendations to the Secretary of Agriculture and Congress; and

      (o) Perform such other powers and functions as may be necessary to
      carry out the objectives of this Act.

       SECTION 6. Consolidation and Coordination of Ube Programs.
All offices, bureaus, attached agencies, corporations, regional field offices,
and operating units of the Department implementing programs relating to
the production, research, propagation, extension, post-harvest
development, processing, marketing, investment promotion, and export of
ube shall coordinate with the Philippine Ube Industry Development Office to
ensure policy coherence, efficient utilization of government resources, and
unified implementation of the Philippine Ube Industry Roadmap.

      Nothing in this Act shall diminish the statutory powers and functions
of the Bureau of Plant Industry, Bureau of Agricultural Research,
Agricultural Training Institute, Bureau of Agricultural and Fisheries
Engineering, Bureau of Agricultural and Fisheries Standards, Agribusiness
and Marketing Assistance Service, and other concerned agencies. The
Office shall exercise coordinative, planning, monitoring, and policy
integration functions to ensure convergence among implementing
agencies.

      SECTION 7. Secretariat to the Ube Industry Development
Council. The Philippine Ube Industry Development Office shall serve as
the permanent Secretariat of the Ube Industry Development Council and
shall provide technical, administrative, research, and monitoring support for
the implementation of the Council's policies and resolutions.

      SECTION 8. Philippine Ube Industry Roadmap. Within one (1) year
from the effectivity of this Act, the Office shall prepare and publish a
ten-year Philippine Ube Industry Roadmap. The Roadmap shall include: (a)
Production targets; (b) Expansion of planting areas; (c) Seed system
development; (d) Research priorities; (e) Processing and value-adding
strategies; (f) Export development initiatives; (g) Climate resilience
measures; (h) Investment priorities; (i) Human resource development; and
(j) Annual performance indicators.

     The Roadmap shall be reviewed every five (5) years.

      SECTION 9. Philippine Ube Industry Development Program. The
Department shall implement a comprehensive development program which
shall include: (a) Distribution of certified planting materials;         (b)
Establishment of nurseries and mother blocks; (c) Farmer training and
extension services; (d) Farm mechanization support; (e) Irrigation and
water management; (f) Soil fertility improvement; (g) Pest and disease
management; (h) Post-harvest facilities; (i) Processing centers; (j) Product
development and innovation; (k) Branding and promotion;(l) Export market
assistance; and (m) Digital traceability systems.
      SECTION 10. Research and Development. The Department, in
coordination with the Department of Science and Technology, State
Universities and Colleges, and research institutions, shall prioritize
research on: (a) Improved varieties; (b) Disease resistance; (c) Tissue
culture and rapid propagation; (d) Climate adaptation; (e) Food processing
technologies; (f) Nutritional and pharmaceutical applications; (g)
Mechanization; and (h) International competitiveness.

       SECTION 11. Quality Standards and Certification. The
Department, in coordination with the Bureau of Agriculture and Fisheries
Standards, the Food and Drug Administration, the Department of Trade and
Industry, and other concerned agencies, shall establish standards for: (a)
Planting materials; (b) Fresh ube; (c) Processed products; (d) Geographic
identification; (e) Product traceability; and (f) Export certification.

      SECTION 12. Investment Promotion. The Board of Investments,
Department of Trade and Industry, and other investment promotion
agencies shall encourage investments in: (a) Processing facilities; (b) Cold
storage; (c) Packaging; (d) Export logistics; (e) Biotechnology; (f) Farm
mechanization; and (g) Agritourism.

      SECTION 13. Ube Industry Development Council. There is hereby
created the Ube Industry Development Council, chaired by the Secretary of
Agriculture, with representatives from:

     (a) Department of Agriculture (DA);

     (b) Department of Trade and Industry (DTI);

     (c) Department of Science and Technology (DOST);

     (d) Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR);

     (e) Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG);

     (f) Department of Economy, Planning and Development (DepDev);

     (g) Cooperative Development Authority (CDA);
     (h) State Universities and Colleges (SUCs);

     (i) Farmers' organizations;

       The Council shall provide policy direction and recommend measures
for the implementation of this Act.

      SECTION 14. Philippine Ube Industry Development Fund. There
is hereby established a Philippine Ube Industry Development Fund to
finance programs under this Act. The Fund shall be sourced from (a)
Annual appropriations; (b) Grants and donations; (c) Official development
assistance; (d) Income from government-supported projects; and (e) Other
lawful sources.

      SECTION 15. Incentives. Qualified farmers, cooperatives,
processors, and investors may receive technical assistance, financing
support, grants, equipment assistance, credit guarantees, and other
incentives consistent with existing laws.

      SECTION 16. Role of Local Government Units. Local government
units shall integrate ube development into their local agriculture and
investment plans and may establish local ube development programs
consistent with the national roadmap.

     SECTION 17. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current
appropriations of the Department of Agriculture. Thereafter, such sums as
may be necessary shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

     SECTION 18. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within one
hundred twenty (120) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department,
in consultation with concerned agencies and stakeholders, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations.

      SECTION 19. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected
thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.
      SECTION 20. Repealing Clause. All laws, executive orders, rules,
regulations, and other issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 21. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
