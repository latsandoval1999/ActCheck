                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to convert the Jct. Bukidnon Cagayan de Oro Iligan Road (BCIR) –
Agusan – Balubal –Sayre Highway in Cagayan de Oro City from a city road to a national road.

        The road is presently in poor condition and in dire need of repair and rehabilitation.
City funds are not adequate for its maintenance and/or upgrading. Hence, the need to tap
other sources. With its improvement, easier access to both points would be ensured and the
potentials for commercial and industrial development would be realized.

       With its conversion into a national road, it would receive the much needed funding and
assistance it deserves.

       Passage of this measure is earnestly requested.

                             AN ACT
CONVERTING THE JCT. BUKIDNON CAGAYAN DE ORO ILIGAN ROAD (BCIR)-
AGUSAN-BALUBAL-SAYRE HIGHWAY IN CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY INTO A NATIONAL
ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress be
assembled:

      Section 1. The Jct. Bukidnon Cagayan de Oro Iligan Road (BCIR) – Agusan – Balubal
–Sayre Highway is hereby converted into a national road.

      Sec 2. The Secretary of Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the
necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

       Sec. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper
of general circulation.
