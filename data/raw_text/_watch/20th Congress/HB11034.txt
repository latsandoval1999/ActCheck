                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE


       This bill seeks to amend Republic Act No. 11282, which converted the Oroquieta
Agro-Industrial School into the Misamis Occidental State College (MOSC).

      To align the legislative and executive composition of the Board of Trustees with the
updated government structures and legislative mandates, this measure updates the
designation of the House representative to the Chairperson of the Committee on Higher and
Technical Education, updates NEDA to the Department of Economy, Planning, and
Development (DEPDev), and explicitly includes a representative of the nonteaching
personnel association as well as federation-level representation for existing sectors.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
AMENDING SECTION 5 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11282, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
    “MISAMIS OCCIDENTAL STATE COLLEGE ACT,” TO REORGANIZE THE
               COMPOSITION OF ITS BOARD OF TRUSTEES


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:
SECTION 1. Section 5 of Republic Act No. 11282 is hereby amended to read as follows:
              “SEC. 5. The Governing Board. – The governing body of the College shall be
      the Board of Trustees, hereinafter referred to as the Board, which shall be composed
      of the following:
      (a) The Chairperson of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), Chairperson;
      (b) The President of the College, Vice-Chairperson;
      (c) The Chairperson of the Committee on HIGHER, TECHNICAL AND VOCATIONAL
      EDUCATION [Education, Arts and Culture] of the Senate, member;
      (d) The Chairperson of the Committee on HIGHER AND TECHNICAL EDUCATION
      [Basic Education and Culture] of the House of Representatives, member;
      (e) The Regional Director of the DEPARTMENT OF ECONOMY, PLANNING, AND
      DEVELOPMENT (DEPDEV) [National Economic and Development Authority
      (NEDA), Region X], member;
      (f) The Regional Director of the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority
      (TESDA), member;
      (g) The Regional Director of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST),
      member;
      (h) The Regional Director of the Department of Agriculture (DA), member;
       (i) The President of the FEDERATION OF faculty ASSOCIATIONS [association] of
       the College, member;
       (j) The President of the FEDERATION OF student COUNCILS [council] of the
       College, member;
       (k) The President of the FEDERATION OF alumni ASSOCIATIONS [association] of
       the College, member;
       (L) THE REPRESENTATIVE OF THE NONTEACHING OR PERSONNEL
       ASSOCIATION OF THE COLLEGE, MEMBER; AND
       (M) [(l)] Two (2) prominent citizens FROM THE PRIVATE SECTOR who have
       distinguished themselves in their PROFESSIONS [profession] or fields of
       specialization, members.


       The Board shall appoint two (2) prominent citizens who shall be selected from a list
       of at least five (5) qualified persons from the Province of Misamis Occidental, as
       recommended by a Search Committee constituted by the President of the MOPSC,
       in consultation with the Chairperson of the CHED and other members of the Board,
       based on the guidelines, qualifications and standards for the position set by the
       Board. They shall serve for a term of two (2) years from the date of appointment.


             The TERMS [term] of office of the PRESIDENTS [president] of the
       FEDERATION OF faculty ASSOCIATIONS, [the president of the] FEDERATION OF
       student COUNCILS, [and the president of the] FEDERATION OF alumni
       ASSOCIATIONS, AND THE REPRESENTATIVE OF THE NONTEACHING
       PERSONNEL shall be coterminous with their respective terms of office as set forth
       in their respective CONSTITUTIONS [constitution] and bylaws.


             In case of vacancy among appointive positions in the Board, the vacancy shall
       be filled by the Board following the guidelines set by it, and the appointee shall hold
       office only for the unexpired portion of the term.”


SEC. 2. Separability Clause. - If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall
remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 3. Repealing Clause. - Republic Act No. 11282 and all laws, presidential decrees,
executive orders, rules and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are repealed or modified accordingly.
SEC. 4. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
