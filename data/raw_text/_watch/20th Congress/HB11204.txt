                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Rice is a staple in the Filipino diet and an important component of daily
meals. However, unnecessary rice wastage remains a concern, particularly
when consumers are served portions that exceed their needs.
       One practical way to address this concern is to give consumers greater
flexibility in the amount of rice they purchase and consume. This measure seeks
to require food establishments to offer half-cup rice servings as an option,
allowing consumers to choose a portion that better corresponds to their needs
and reducing the likelihood of excess rice being left uneaten.
      By promoting appropriate portion sizes and responsible consumption,
this measure seeks to reduce avoidable rice wastage, conserve valuable food
resources, and encourage more sustainable consumption practices. It likewise
provides consumers with a more affordable option when a full cup of rice is
more than they require.
      In light of the foregoing, the passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
       REQUIRING FOOD ESTABLISHMENTS TO SERVE HALF-CUP RICE
      SERVINGS, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF


     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
           Congress assembled:


 1             SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Anti-Rice
 2      Wastage Act.”

 3             SEC. 2. Half-Rice Requirement. – All proprietary establishments serving
 4      food, including restaurants, eateries, hospitals, school canteens and cafeterias,
 5      hotels, inns, and other similar food establishments that serve meals prepared outside
 6      the home, shall hereby be required to offer half-cup rice servings, the selling price
 7      of which shall be adjusted accordingly. Offering half-cup rice servings for the price
 8      of one (1) cup serving shall be considered a violation of this Act.
 9             SEC. 3. Penalty Clause. – Any food establishment that violates any
10      provision of this Act shall be subject to a fine of Fifty Thousand Pesos
11      (Php 50,000.00) for the first offense, One Hundred Thousand Pesos
12      (Php 100,000.00) for the second offense, and Two Hundred Thousand Pesos

 3          SEC. 4. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
 4   declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
 5   force and effect.
 6          SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
 7   regulations which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
 8   modified accordingly.
 9          SEC. 6.      Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days from
