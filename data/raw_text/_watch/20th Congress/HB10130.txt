                                   Explanatory Note


The Social Security System (SSS) provides retirement, disability, death, and survivor
benefits to millions of private sector workers under Republic Act No. 11199. In 2025, the
SSS began implementing a multi-year Pension Reform Program (PRP) that grants annual
increases to pensions. However, this program is based on administrative approval and is
not yet institutionalized by law.

Without legislative backing, future pension increases remain uncertain and subject to
policy changes. This exposes retirees to the risk of stagnant benefits, especially during
periods of high inflation.

This bill seeks to institutionalize the automatic pension escalation mechanism for
SSS retirees by enshrining it in law. It mandates annual increases of ten percent (10%)
for retirement and disability pensions, and five percent (5%) for death and survivor
pensions, or the Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation rate, whichever is higher.

By codifying the pension escalation program, the measure ensures predictability, protects
retirees’ purchasing power, and strengthens the long-term social safety net for private
sector workers.

This legislative proposal ensures that the SSS pension escalation program is no
longer dependent on administrative decisions but is permanently enshrined in law,
giving retirees predictable and inflation-protected benefits.

In view of the foregoing, immediate passage of this bill is earnestly requested.

                                   AN ACT
       INSTITUTIONALIZING THE AUTOMATIC PENSION ESCALATION MECHANISM
      FOR RETIREES OF THE SOCIAL SECURITY SYSTEM (SSS), PROVIDING FUNDS
                      THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

 1   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
 2   assembled:

 3        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “SSS Automatic Pension
 4   Escalation Act.”

 5        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to safeguard the welfare
 6   of private sector retirees by ensuring that their pensions are regularly adjusted to maintain
 7   its purchasing power, protect it against inflation, and guarantee predictability in retirement
 8   income.

 9        SEC. 3. Institutionalization of Pension Escalation. - The automatic pension
10   escalation program of the SSS shall be institutionalized by law. All retirement and
11   disability pensioners shall receive an annual increase in their monthly pension.

12        The annual pension escalation under this Act shall be based on the official
13   Consumer Price Index or inflation data published by the Philippine Statistics Authority,
14   whichever is higher. Those receiving death and survivor pensions shall also receive an
15   annual increase of five percent (5%) or equivalent to the Consumer Price Index (CPI)
16   inflation, whichever is higher.
17        SEC. 4. Coverage. - This provisions of this Act shall apply to all SSS retirement,
18   disability, death, and survivor pensioners except those pensioners who opted for lump-
19   sum benefits.

20        SEC. 5. Funding Source. - The annual pension escalation shall be funded by the
21   Social Service System from the SSS investment earnings and other sources as may be
22   determined by the SSS Board of Trustees.
 1        SEC. 6. Annual Actuarial Review. - The SSS shall conduct an annual actuarial
 2   review to assess and determine the sustainability of the pension fund. The result of the
 3   actuarial report shall be submitted to the Speaker of the House of Representatives and
 4   to the House Committee on Appropriations, the Senate President and the Senate
 5   Committee on Finance, and to the Secretary of the Department of Finance.

 6        SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
 7   effectivity of this Act, the SSS, coordination with the Governance Commission for GOCCs
 8   and other stakeholders shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to
 9   implement the provisions of this Act.

10        SEC. 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
11   unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

12        SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
13   regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

14        SEC. 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
15   in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

16          Approved.
