                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan (BSK) are the fundamental
institutions of local governance, serving as the government's primary link to
communities and the youth. Through the Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections (BSKE), citizens are afforded the opportunity to choose leaders who will
promote grassroots development, maintain peace and order, and advance meaningful
youth participation in governance.

         However, the conduct of nationwide synchronized elections demands
substantial financial, administrative, and logistical resources. As government continues
to address competing national priorities, including the implementation of critical
infrastructure projects, education and health programs, disaster preparedness
initiatives, and fiscal consolidation measures, it has become imperative to reassess the
timing of the 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections.

        Postponing the BSKE to 2027 will enable the government to redirect public
funds toward more urgent and immediate national concerns while allowing the
Commission on Elections (COMELEC) sufficient time to complete procurement
activities, enhance election preparations, improve voter education campaigns, and
ensure the orderly, efficient, and credible conduct of the elections.

       The proposed measure likewise seeks to prevent disruptions in the delivery of
essential public services at the barangay level by allowing incumbent barangay and
Sangguniang Kabataan officials to continue serving in a holdover capacity until their
duly elected successors shall have qualified. Such holdover authority is intended solely
to preserve continuity in governance and maintain uninterrupted delivery of basic
public services during the postponement period.

     This measure does not diminish the people's right to suffrage nor alter the
democratic process. Rather, it merely adjusts the schedule of the elections in
furtherance of the State's compelling interest in ensuring fiscal prudence,
administrative efficiency, and the orderly conduct of elections, while preserving
stability in local governance.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                AN ACT
     POSTPONING THE NOVEMBER 2, 2026 BARANGAY AND SANGGUNIANG
     KABATAAN ELECTIONS TO NOVEMBER 1, 2027, EXTENDING THE TERMS OF
     INCUMBENT BARANGAY AND SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN OFFICIALS IN A
     HOLDOVER CAPACITY, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR
     OTHER PURPOSES

 1   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
 2   Congress assembled:
 3
 4        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Barangay and
 5   Sangguniang Kabataan Elections Postponement Act of 2026."
 6
 7         SECTION 2. Postponement of the 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang
 8   Kabataan Elections. - Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, the
 9   Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections (BSKE) scheduled on 2 November
10   2026 are hereby postponed to 1 November 2027, which shall be the date for the
11   conduct of the next Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections.
12
13
14          SECTION 3. Holdover of Incumbent Officials. - The incumbent Punong
15   Barangays, Members of the Sangguniang Barangay, Sangguniang Kabataan
16   Chairpersons, and Members of the Sangguniang Kabataan shall continue to serve in a
17   holdover capacity until their successors shall have been duly elected and qualified,
18   unless sooner removed or suspended for cause in accordance with law.
19
20           SECTION 4. Term of Office of Officials Elected in the 2027 BSKE. -
21   Officials elected in the Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections to be held on 1
22   November 2027 shall serve the term prescribed under existing laws unless otherwise
23   provided by subsequent legislation.
24
 1           SECTION 5. Authority of the Commission on Elections. - The
 2   Commission on Elections (COMELEC) shall issue the necessary rules, regulations,
 3   resolutions, and calendar of activities for the effective implementation of this Act. It
 4   shall likewise adjust all election-related deadlines, including but not limited to voter
 5   registration, filing of certificates of candidacy, campaign period, appointment of
 6   election personnel, procurement of election materials, and canvassing procedures.
 7
 8          SECTION 6. Use of Funds. - The funds appropriated for the conduct of the
 9   postponed 2026 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections shall remain available
10   for the conduct of the elections on the new date set under this Act, subject to
11   applicable budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.
12
13          SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within thirty (30)
14   days from the effectivity of this Act, the Commission on Elections shall promulgate the
15   implementing rules and regulations necessary for the faithful implementation of this
16   Act.
17
18            SECTION 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
19   unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain
20   in full force and effect.
21
22          SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules
23   and regulations, ordinances, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with
24   the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
25
26          SECTION 10. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
27   its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
