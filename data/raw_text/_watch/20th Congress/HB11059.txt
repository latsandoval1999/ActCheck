                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

         The Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) is an important institution for ensuring the participation of
the youth in local governance. Through the SK, young Filipinos are given the opportunity to represent
their fellow youth and participate in the formulation and implementation of programs and policies in
their communities.

       However, vacancies in the Sangguniang Kabataan continue to occur for various reasons.
These include positions that remain unfilled after elections, as well as vacancies arising from
resignation, death, removal from office, permanent incapacity, failure to qualify, and other
circumstances recognized by law. Under Republic Act No. 10742, as amended by Republic Act No.
11768, vacancies in the SK membership are generally filled through a special Katipunan ng Kabataan
assembly and election.

        The number of unfilled positions after the 2023 Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan
Elections demonstrates that the issue is not merely theoretical. Following the 2023 elections, 692 SK
positions nationwide remained vacant because there were no candidates for those positions—139 SK
Chairperson positions and 553 SK Member positions. The Commission on Elections subsequently
had to provide rules for the conduct of special elections to address these vacancies.

        More importantly, vacancies do not occur only immediately after an election. During the term
of office, an SK position may become vacant because of resignation, death, permanent incapacity,
removal from office, failure to qualify, or other circumstances. These vacancies can leave an SK with
fewer members than required and may affect its ability to meet quorum, deliberate on matters, and
effectively implement programs for the youth.

        The existing requirement of a special election or special Katipunan ng Kabataan assembly
may also result in delays in filling a vacant position. Conducting a separate electoral process for a
single vacant seat requires coordination, administrative preparation, and resources, while the vacant
position may remain unfilled during the process.
     This measure therefore proposes a simpler and more efficient mechanism for filling
permanent vacancies in the membership of the Sangguniang Kabataan.

       Instead of conducting a special election for every vacancy, the remaining members of the
Sangguniang Kabataan shall recommend a qualified member of the Katipunan ng Kabataan to fill the
vacant position. The recommendation shall be submitted through a duly approved SK Resolution to
the concerned City or Municipal Mayor, who shall make the appointment.

       This proposed mechanism is similar to the general approach under the Local Government
Code in filling permanent vacancies in local sanggunian, while still preserving the participation of
the youth sector in identifying the person who will represent them.

       The proposed appointment shall be limited to the unexpired portion of the term, and the
appointee must possess all the qualifications and none of the disqualifications prescribed by law. The
measure also provides a definite period for the SK to submit its recommendation to ensure that
vacancies will not remain unfilled for an extended period.

      The objective of this measure is simple: to ensure that every Sangguniang Kabataan remains
complete, functional, and capable of serving the youth of the barangay.

       By replacing the requirement of a special election with a timely appointment upon the
recommendation of the SK Council and approval of the concerned Mayor, this measure will reduce
administrative delays, avoid unnecessary electoral expenses, and ensure continuity in youth
representation and governance.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

                                AN ACT
     AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10742, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
“SANGGUNIANG KABATAAN REFORM ACT OF 2015,” AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC
    ACT NO. 11768, PROVIDING FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF SANGGUNIANG
  KABATAAN MEMBERS TO FILL PERMANENT VACANCIES, AND FOR OTHER
                               PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress

SECTION 1. Section 19 of Republic Act No. 10742, otherwise known as the “Sangguniang
Kabataan Reform Act of 2015,” as amended by Republic Act No. 11768, is hereby further amended
by deleting the second paragraph thereof and replacing the same with the following:

          “SEC. 19. Sangguniang Kabataan Succession and Filling Up of
          Vacancies. — In case a Sangguniang Kabataan Chairperson refuses to
          assume office, fails to qualify, voluntarily resigns, dies, is permanently
          incapacitated, is removed from office, or otherwise permanently vacates the
          office, the Sangguniang Kabataan member who obtained the highest number
          of votes in the election immediately preceding shall assume the office of the
          Chairperson for the unexpired portion of the term. In case said member
          refuses to assume the position or fails to qualify, the Sangguniang Kabataan
          member obtaining the next highest number of votes shall assume the position
          of the Chairperson for the unexpired portion of the term.

          In case of a permanent vacancy in the membership of the Sangguniang
          Kabataan, the vacancy shall be filled through a special election conducted
          by the Commission on Elections in accordance with existing election
          laws, rules and regulations the vacancy shall be filled by appointment in
          accordance with the following procedure:

          (a) The remaining members of the Sangguniang Kabataan shall, within
          fifteen (15) days from the occurrence of the permanent vacancy, convene
          and, by a majority vote of all incumbent members, recommend a
          qualified nominee to fill the vacant position;

          (b) The recommendation of the Sangguniang Kabataan shall be
          embodied in a duly approved Sangguniang Kabataan Resolution and
          transmitted to the Mayor of the concerned city or municipality;
            (c) Upon receipt of the Sangguniang Kabataan Resolution, the Mayor
            shall appoint the qualified nominee to fill the vacant position for the
            unexpired portion of the term;

            (d) The nominee shall possess all the qualifications and none of the
            disqualifications prescribed under this Act and shall be a member of the
            Katipunan ng Kabataan of the barangay concerned;

            (e) In the event that the Sangguniang Kabataan fails to submit a
            recommendation within the period prescribed in paragraph (a), the
            Mayor shall require the Sangguniang Kabataan to submit its
            recommendation within five (5) days from receipt of such notice. If the
            Sangguniang Kabataan still fails to submit a recommendation within the
            prescribed period, the Mayor may appoint any qualified member of the
            Katipunan ng Kabataan residing in the barangay to fill the vacancy;

            (f) The person appointed under this Section shall serve only for the
            unexpired portion of the term of the vacant position; and

            (g) The Commission on Elections shall no longer conduct a special
            election for the purpose of filling a permanent vacancy in the
            membership of the Sangguniang Kabataan.”

            In case of a temporary vacancy in the office of the Sangguniang
            Kabataan Chairperson, the succession provided under this Section shall
            apply in accordance with existing law.”
