EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bilt seeks to establish a multi-purpose farm for the breeding, spawning, and hatchery of catfish,
tilapia, mudfish, eel, and other freshwater fishes that are viable in mud ponds and concrete tanks in
the Municipality of Kabuntalan, Province of Maguindanao de! Norte.

The people of Kabuntalan generally rely on agriculture for their livelihood, particularly the cultivation
of rice, corn, and assorted vegetables. As such, the supply of fish and other aquatic resources for their
important dietary needs is far from ideal, resulting in their price becoming quite prohibitive for many
of its people.

The establishment of a multi-purpose fishery farm in the Municipality of Kabuntalan, Province of
Maguindanao del Norte, therefore will greatly benefit its people, as this will address the demand for
affordable fish; provide a stable source of fry or fingerlings and reliable technical expertise for the
setting up, management, and operation of small-scale fish farms, empowering many of its residents
and creating secondary income streams from aquaculture.

Moreover, it will empower small-scale fish farmers and strengthen local aquaculture, promote
inclusive rural development, and contribute to food security, in consonance with the spirit and intent
of the provisions of the 1987 Constitution, which mandates the State to protect the rights of
subsistence fishermen, especially of local communities, to the preferential use of local marine and
fishing resources, both inland and offshore.

This proposed bill, although local in its scope and application, will eventually redound to nation-
building, as it will provide a viable source of livelihood for people in the rural areas of the Municipality

of Kabuntalan and contribute to the eradication of poverty, particularly in far-flung communities.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Establishment of a Multi-Purpose Fishery Breeding Farm and Hatchery ~ There
shall be established, under the supervision of the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources
of the Department of Agriculture (BFAR-DA), a multi-purpose farm for the breeding, spawning,
and hatchery of catfish, titapia, mudfish, eel, and other freshwater fishes in the Municipality
of Kabuntalan, Province of Maguindanao del Norte.

SEC 2. Research and Experimentation. — The BFAR-DA shall disseminate its technical
knowledge and expertise on the culture of catfish, tilapia, mudfish, eel, and other freshwater
fishes to the inhabitants of the Municipality of Kabuntalan, Province of Maguindanao del
Norte; and shall continuously conduct research and experimentation on the breeding,
spawning, hatchery, and culture of these fish species to discover new cost-effective methods
and technologies, especially as they apply to local conditions.

SEC 3. Appropriations. — The Secretary of the Department of Agriculture shall include in the
Department’s programs the establishment of the multi-purpose farm for the breeding,
spawning, and hatchery of catfish, tilapia, mudfish, eel, and other freshwater fishes in the
Municipality of Kabuntalan, Province of Maguindanao del Norte, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

Further, the local government unit concerned shall set aside an amount deemed appropriate
for the operation and maintenance of the said multi-purpose farm to be sourced from any of
its available local revenue.

SEC 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The Department of Agriculture (DA) and its
related agencies, including BFAR, shall jointly promulgate the necessary rules and regulations
for the effective implementation of this Act within sixty (60) days from its effectivity.
