                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF CULTURE, ARTS, TOURISM
 AND HERITAGE COUNCILS IN EVERY PROVINCE OF THE COUNTRY

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Nations that fail to learn from the past are bound to repeat their
mistakes. For this reason, civilizations have long valued their art, heritage,
and traditions. Our culture—expressed through our languages, cuisines,
music, handicrafts, museums, and literature—shapes the shared identity of
Filipinos today. When we enrich and celebrate our nation’s culture, we do
more than honor those who came before us; we also equip and inspire the
next generations of Filipinos to build a stronger future.

      The National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA) was created
primarily for the purpose of developing, enhancing, and institutionalizing arts
and culture programs in the country. However, it remains limited by its
organizational nature – an agency based in the country’s capital with no
bureaucratic network to expand its programs, projects, and activities up to
the grassroots level. At best, it could only enter into memoranda of agreement
with local government units to fund particular programs or projects with an
arts and culture component.

      Animating the citizenry with the love and passion for their culture, arts,
tourism, and heritage should be a shared responsibility of both the national
and the local government units. Cultural programs at the provincial and city
level must be complemented with the support of national agencies like the
NCCA, the National Museum, the National Historic Commission of the
Philippines, and stakeholders from the private sector.
      While a number of local government units have taken the initiative of
organizing their own arts, culture, tourism, and heritage councils, a vast
number of our provinces, which have rich untapped potential, remain in need
of such support.

      In order to strengthen and institutionalize this necessary collaboration,
this bill seeks to establish Culture, Arts, Tourism, and Heritage Councils in
every province of the country. These opportunities to preserve, enhance, and
develop local arts, culture, tourism, and heritage shall be a fitting gift for the
next generation to appreciate and enjoy.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Culture, Arts,
Tourism, and Heritage Councils in Every Province Act.”

SECTION 2. Creation of Culture, Arts, Tourism, and Heritage Councils.
– There is hereby created, in every province in the country, a Culture, Arts,
Tourism, and Heritage Council that shall undertake culture, arts, tourism,
and heritage preservation, enhancement, and development.

SECTION 3. Funding. – The Department of the Interior and Local
Government (DILG) shall assist in the creation of these councils and provide
for their initial funding. The DILG shall, in coordination with the National
Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA), and other relevant culture, arts,
tourism and heritage agencies actively promote the creation of these councils.

The subsequent allocation for the operationalization of these councils shall be
borne by their respective local government units.

SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within thirty (30) days
from the approval of this Act, the Department of Interior and Local
Government, the Department of Tourism, and the NCCA, in coordination with
representatives from the Local Government Units, shall promulgate the rules
and regulations implementing the provisions of this Act.

SECTION 5. Non-Impairment Clause. – Nothing in this Act shall be
construed as to diminish, impair, or repeal rights recognized, granted, or
made available to disadvantaged, marginalized, or specific sectors under
existing laws.
SECTION 6. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act shall
be declared or held to be unconstitutional or invalid, other provisions hereof
which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. – All general and special laws, decrees,
executive orders, proclamation and administrative regulation, or any parts
thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended or
modified accordingly.

SECTION 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
publication in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation.
