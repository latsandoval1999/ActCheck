                           AN ACT

   INDUSTRY STAKEHOLDERS AND ESTABLISHING THE NORA AUNOR
                  AWARD FOR FILM EXCELLENCE


                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

        According to Article XIV, Section 14 of the 1987 Constitution, “[t]he State shall
foster the preservation, enrichment, and dynamic evolution of a Filipino national
culture based on the principle of unity in diversity in a climate of free artistic and
intellectual expression.” Therefore, it is crucial to protect Filipino artistic excellence in
filmmaking and production while addressing the emerging difficulties faced by the
industry.

        The Philippine film industry, throughout the years, has created countless
award-winning productions that have earned local and international recognition. While
films have become an essential channel for creative expression, the rising cost of
tickets and film production, declining audience attendance, and increasing competition
from online streaming services pose pressing issues for the continuation and growth
of the film industry. These challenges particularly affect small film outfits and
independent filmmakers' capacity to present Filipino creativity and talent and
showcase their work on both the local and international stage.

       This proposed bill, the "Nora Aunor Act", responds to these challenges by
providing incentives to the Philippine film industry, including exemptions from income
tax, value-added tax, and customs duties, as well as allowing the deduction of film
production expenses from gross income for tax purposes. Pursuant to these
incentives, the bill also institutionalizes an annual award for exceptional scripts and
concepts, the Nora Aunor Award for Film Excellence. This award will empower Filipino
filmmakers to thrive and innovate.

 1   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
 2   Congress assembled:
 3
 4          SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the "Nora Aunor Act"
 5
 6           SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State
 7   to foster the preservation, enrichment, and dynamic evolution of a Filipino national
 8   culture based on the principle of unity in diversity in a climate of free artistic and
 9   intellectual expression. Toward this end, and in recognition of the constitutional
10   mandate that arts and letters shall enjoy the patronage of the State, the Philippine
11   film industry shall be given adequate support to encourage the production of quality
12   films that depict and reinforce genuine Filipino identity, history, culture, and values,
13   and to achieve global recognition and appreciation of Filipino cinematic art.
14
15         SEC. 3. Coverage. This Act shall apply to duly-accredited Philippine film
16   Industry stakeholders as provided for in Section 6 of this Act including, but not limited
17   to:
18         (a) Film production companies;
19         (b) Independent filmmakers;
20         (c) Film distributors;
21         (d) Post-production and editing studios;
22         (e) Film exhibitors and cinemas;
23         (f) Artists, directors, writers, and technical crew;
24         (g) Film education institutions and training centers;
25
26          For purposes of this Act, a Philippine film industry stakeholder shall refer to
27   those falling under items (a) to (g) of this section and such other person, natural or

 1   juridical, whose business is directly related to or has stake in the Philippine film
 2   industry.
 3
 4           SEC. 4. Nora Aunor Award for Film Excellence. To provide support for the
 5   production of films of high artistic value or profound social or historical significance,
 6   there shall be an annual award to be known as Nora Aunor Award for Film Excellence.
 7   The Award shall be extended to exceptional scripts and concept to be selected by a
 8   committee of nine (9) members to be composed of film professionals, scholars, critics,
 9   and other experts in film and cultural studies to be chosen and headed by the
10   Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer of the Film Development Council of the
11   Philippines (FDCP). The selection committee shall determine the award to be extended
12   to the recipients of the Nora Aunor Award for Film Excellence, including but not limited
13   to, cash grant to support film production of winning scripts or concepts, and promotion
14   and distribution activities in national and international film festivals.
15
16          SEC. 5. Tax Incentives. Duly-accredited film industry stakeholders shall be
17   entitled to the following tax incentives:
18
19           (a) Exemption of newly-accredited film production entities from income tax for
20   a period of five (5) years from the date of accreditation;
21           (b) Exemption of the sale or lease of goods and services from value-added tax
22   upon certification by the FDCP that the same shall be used actually, directly, and
23   exclusively in film production, distribution, and exhibition;
24           (c) Exemption of the importation on instruments, equipment, software,
25   supplies, and materials from customs duties upon certification by the FDCP that the
26   same shall be used actually, directly, and exclusively in film production, distribution,
27   and exhibition: Provided, however, That the exemption from customs duties shall only
28   apply when such instruments, equipment, software, supplies, and materials are not
29   available from any merchant, supplier or distributor operating within the Philippines;
30   and
31           (d) Authorization to deduct from gross income, for purposes of computing the
32   taxable income of the accredited film industry stakeholder concerned, an amount
33   equivalent to one hundred and fifty percent (150%) of all expenses actually, directly
34   and exclusively spent for film production: Provided, That for the purpose of this
35   section, film production expenses shall include, but not be limited to, payment of
36   artists' talent fees, construction of film sets, and lease of filming locations.
37
38          In furtherance of the declared policy provided under Section 2 of this Act, the
39   FDCP shall, through the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG),
40   encourage local government units to offer tax or non-tax incentives and other relief
41   and privileges to accredited film industry stakeholders.
42
43          SEC. 6. Accreditation. The FDCP shall, in consultation with the film industry
44   stakeholders, formulate the rules and establish criteria for accreditation of film industry
45   stakeholders. The accreditation shall be anchored on the following principles:
46          (a) The accreditation of film industry stakeholder shall be voluntary in nature;
47          (b) The accreditation shall ensure the observance of equal rights and lawful
48   interest of all parties involved;

                                                 2
 1          (c) The accreditation process shall be transparent and impartial, with clear
 2   guidelines and standards which shall be made available by the FDCP;
 3          (d) Accredited film industry stakeholder shall adhere to ethical and legal
 4   standards for film industry to be formulated by the FDCP; and
 5          (e) The accreditation process shall be grounded on the objective of promoting
 6   the development of the local film industry through adoption of high standards and
 7   innovative practices in film production.
 8
 9          SEC. 7. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial implementation
10   of this Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the FDCP.
11   Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be included in the General
12   Appropriations Act.
13
14          SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days
15   from the effectivity of this Act, the FDCP shall, in coordination with the Department of
16   Finance, Bureau of Internal Revenue, and DILG, promulgate the necessary rules and
17   regulations to effectively implement the provisions of this Act.
18
19          SEC. 9. Separability Clause. If, for any reason, any part or provision of this
20   Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not
21   affected shall remain in full force and effect.
22
23          SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. All laws, acts, decrees, executive orders,
24   issuances, and rules and regulations or parts thereof which are contrary to and

27          SEC. 11. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect immediately after its complete
28   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
