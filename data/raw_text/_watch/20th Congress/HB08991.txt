                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

     Motorcycle taxis have become an integral part of the country’s urban
transportation landscape, providing commuters with a flexible and efficient alternative
in congested cities. As their use continues to expand, so too does the State’s
responsibility to ensure that public convenience does not compromise passenger
safety.

      While existing laws and regulations address general road safety and vehicle
registration, there remains no clear statutory requirement mandating standardized
passenger safety accessories for motorcycles used in passenger-for-hire operations.
As a result, safety practices vary widely among operators and platforms, leaving
passengers exposed to unnecessary risks related to balance, stability, and sudden
movement during travel.

     This bill seeks to address this gap by requiring all motorcycles used for
motorcycle taxi services to be equipped with basic, standardized passenger safety
accessories. These include secure rear handholds or safety rails and back-support
devices such as top boxes with integrated backrests or equivalent attachments
approved by the Land Transportation Office (LTO). These measures are intended to
enhance passenger stability, reduce the likelihood of falls or loss of balance, and
improve overall riding confidence.

      The measure recognizes the operational realities of motorcycle taxis by tasking
the Department of Transportation (DOTr), through the LTO and in coordination with
the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) and other
relevant stakeholders and agencies, to develop technical standards that ensure safety
accessories do not compromise vehicle balance, maneuverability, or compliance with
existing road safety regulations.

     The bill further assigns responsibility to digital platform operators to ensure
compliance among their partner riders, reflecting the role of platforms in regulating
access to motorcycle taxi services. This shared accountability framework promotes
consistent enforcement while avoiding undue regulatory burden on individual riders.

the required accessories, while ensuring that newly accredited units meet safety
standards prior to operation. Lastly, the measure mandates the government to provide
such passenger safety accessories, without prejudice to operators shouldering it, to
ensure that this requirement will not be a burden anymore to the working class of
motorcycle taxi owners or drivers.

      By institutionalizing minimum passenger safety requirements for motorcycle taxis,
this bill promotes safer urban mobility, protects the riding public, improves confidence
of the passengers and ensures that innovation in transportation services is matched
by clear and enforceable safety standards.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                 AN ACT
     REQUIRING THE INSTALLATION OF STANDARDIZED PASSENGER SAFETY
     ACCESSORIES ON MOTORCYCLES USED FOR TRANSPORT NETWORK
     VEHICLES SERVICES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Motorcycle Taxi
 2   Passenger Safety Accessories Act.”
 3
 4        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
 5   promote the safety, security, and welfare of the riding public by ensuring that
 6   motorcycles used for passenger transport are equipped with standardized safety
 7   accessories that enhance passenger stability, protection, and confidence during travel.
 8
 9         The State recognizes the growing role of motorcycle taxis in urban mobility and
10   affirms that public convenience shall not come at the expense of passenger safety.
11
12        SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:
13
14        a. Motorcycle Taxi or Mototaxi refers to a motorcycle used for hire to transport
15           passengers, whether operated independently or through a digital platform.
16
17        b. Transport Network Vehicle Service (TNVS) for Motorcycles refers to a system
18           that uses an online-enabled platform or application to connect passengers
19           with motorcycle riders for compensation.
20
21        c. Passenger Safety Accessories refer to standardized motorcycle attachments
22           designed to enhance passenger safety and stability, including but not limited
23           to rear handholds or safety rails, top boxes with backrests, side handles, or
24           other devices approved by the Land Transportation Office (LTO).
25

 5         SEC. 4. Mandatory Installation of Passenger Safety Accessories. – All
 6   motorcycles used for motorcycle taxi or motorcycle TNVS operations shall be
 7   equipped with passenger safety accessories that meet the standards prescribed under
 8   this Act.
 9
10        At a minimum, such accessories shall include:
11
12             a. A secure rear handhold or safety rail accessible to the passenger; and
13
14             b. A back-support device, such as a top box with an integrated backrest or
15                an equivalent safety attachment approved by the LTO.
16
17             c. No motorcycle shall be accredited or allowed to operate as a motorcycle
18                taxi unless it complies with these requirements.
19
20         SEC. 5. Standards and Technical Specifications. – The Department of
21   Transportation (DOTr), through the Land Transportation Office (LTO), in coordination
22   with the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) and other
23   relevant stakeholders and agencies, shall promulgate the technical standards,
24   specifications, and installation guidelines for passenger safety accessories.
25
26        Such standards shall ensure that accessories:
27
28        a. Do not compromise vehicle balance or maneuverability;
29        b. Are securely installed and regularly maintained;
30        c. Are compatible with existing motorcycle safety regulations; and
31        d. Sensitive to the comfort of passengers of different sexes.
32
33         SEC. 6. Responsibilities of Platform Operators. – Platform operators shall
34   ensure that all partner riders comply with the requirements of this Act as a condition
35   for accreditation and continued access to the platform.
36
37        Platform operators shall not dispatch or allow bookings for motorcycles that fail
38   to meet the prescribed safety accessory standards.
39
40        SEC. 7. Compliance Period. – All existing motorcycle taxi owners, driver and
41   operators shall comply with this Act within one (1) year from its effectivity.
42
43        All newly accredited motorcycle taxi units shall comply prior to commencement
44   of operations.
45
46         SEC. 8. Funding and Cost Responsibility. – The cost of the passenger safety
47   accessory and its installment upon the motorcycle taxi shall not be borne by the owner
48   or driver. The government through the LTO shall provide motorcycle taxi owners or
49   drivers with such passenger safety accessories. However, nothing in this Act shall
