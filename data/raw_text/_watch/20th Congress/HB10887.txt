                                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

      A loan application can now be completed in minutes. Unfortunately, so can the
harassment that follows it.

       Online lending has made credit faster and more accessible to Filipinos who may
otherwise have difficulty obtaining funds through traditional channels. The Bangko
Sentral ng Pilipinas’ 2025 Consumer Finance and Inclusion Survey found that one in four
Filipino adults had outstanding loans and noted the growing reach of online lending
platforms. The same survey found that speed of loan processing and approval is among
the foremost considerations of borrowers. Speed and convenience clearly matter to
borrowers, but convenience should not come at the cost of fairness, privacy, or dignity.

       In March 2026, the Department of Information and Communications Technology,
National Privacy Commission, and Securities and Exchange Commission jointly warned
of reports involving online lending platforms engaged in harassment, intimidation, public
shaming, and unlawful use of personal data.1 The advisory reiterated prohibitions against
unnecessary access to borrowers’ contact lists, contacting persons other than lawful
guarantors for debt collection, and using personal data for threatening or unfair collection
practices.

       Technology has changed not only how quickly people may borrow, but also how
much information a lender may attempt to obtain and how easily pressure may be exerted
on a borrower. A delinquent loan should not turn a borrower’s contact list into a collection
directory, a family group chat into a public notice board, or a mobile phone into an
instrument of humiliation. A debt is an obligation to pay; it is not a surrender of dignity,
privacy, or basic rights.

       This bill establishes durable minimum protections for online borrowers without
attempting to legislate every technical detail of a rapidly evolving industry. It requires
clear disclosure and affirmative acceptance of loan terms; promotes responsible lending;

1   Joint Public Advisory on Online Lending Platforms (OLPs) issued on 18 March 2026
prohibits undisclosed, excessive, fictitious, and circumvention-based charges; protects
borrowers against unnecessary and disproportionate access to personal data; and
prohibits harassment, public shaming, false threats, deceptive collection, and demands
for payment from persons who never assumed the debt.

      This measure likewise ensures that lenders cannot avoid accountability by
outsourcing collection or other functions, and extends the core prohibitions to
unauthorized online lenders.

       Online lending has made borrowing possible with a few taps on a screen. Our laws
must ensure that those few taps do not open the door to hidden charges, harassment, or
misuse of a person’s private life. Credit may be digital, but the rights on the other side of
the screen remain decidedly human.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate enactment of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                 AN ACT
        PROTECTING BORROWERS FROM UNFAIR, DECEPTIVE, ABUSIVE, AND
        PREDATORY ONLINE LENDING PRACTICES, ESTABLISHING MINIMUM
        STANDARDS FOR ONLINE LENDING AND DIGITAL DEBT COLLECTION,
        STRENGTHENING ENFORCEMENT AGAINST UNAUTHORIZED ONLINE
               LENDING PLATFORMS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

            Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1          Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Fair Online Lending Act.”
 2
 3          Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to promote
 4   responsible and inclusive access to credit while protecting borrowers from unfair,
 5   deceptive, abusive, and predatory online lending practices.
 6
 7          Towards this end, the State shall promote transparency, responsible lending and
 8   pricing, data privacy and security, fair debt collection, effective consumer redress, and
 9   accountability of lenders and their service providers, while enabling legitimate innovation
10   and access to credit.
11
12          Section 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:
13
14          (a) Borrower refers to a natural person who applies for or obtains a loan through
15              an Online Lending Platform;
16
17          (b) Collection Agent refers to any natural or juridical person engaged by or acting
18              on behalf of an Online Lender or Unauthorized Online Lender to collect or
19              recover a loan obligation or communicate with a Borrower for such purpose;
20
21          (c) Online Lender refers to a financing company or lending company authorized by
22              law to extend credit and which offers or administers loans through an Online

 2
 3          (d) Online Lending Platform (OLP) refers to a mobile application, website, digital
 4              interface, or other financial technology-enabled platform through which a
 5              Borrower may apply for, obtain, manage, repay, or otherwise transact in
 6              relation to a loan;
 7
 8          (e) Third-Party Service Provider (TPSP) refers to any person or entity engaged by an
 9              Online Lender to perform or facilitate any material activity relating to the
10              offering, processing, servicing, or collection of a loan; and

13              in, or holds itself out as engaging in, the business of extending credit to the
14              public through digital means without the registration, Certificate of Authority,
15              or other authorization required by law.
16
17          Section 4. Scope and Coverage. – This Act shall apply to financing companies and
18   lending companies under the supervision of the Securities and Exchange Commission
19   (SEC), their OLPs, and their TPSPs.
20
21          An Online Lender shall comply with the affirmative regulatory duties under
22   Sections 6 and 7 of this Act. The prohibitions under such Sections shall likewise apply to
23   an Unauthorized Online Lender. The other borrower protections and prohibitions under
24   this Act shall apply to an Unauthorized Online Lender and to persons acting on its behalf
25   whenever expressly so provided.
26
27           This Act shall apply when an online loan is marketed, offered, extended, serviced,
28   or collected in the Philippines, or when the Borrower resides in the Philippines, subject to
29   applicable rules on jurisdiction and enforcement.
30
31          Section 5. Authority to Engage in Online Lending. – No person shall engage in, or
32   hold itself out as engaging in, the business of extending credit to the public through an
33   OLP without the authority required by law.
34
35          No financing company or lending company shall operate or use an OLP unless it
36   possesses a valid Certificate of Authority and complies with applicable SEC requirements
37   governing OLPs.
38
39         Every OLP shall clearly identify the financing company or lending company
40   responsible for the loan and provide sufficient information to enable a prospective
41   Borrower to verify its authority to operate.
42
43          The SEC shall maintain and make publicly accessible an updated registry of duly
44   authorized financing companies and lending companies, together with the OLPs

 4          Section 6. Disclosure and Affirmative Acceptance of Loan Terms. – In addition to
 5   the disclosures required under Republic Act No. 3765, otherwise known as the “Truth in
 6   Lending Act,” and other applicable laws, an Online Lender shall, before a Borrower
 7   becomes bound by an online loan, clearly disclose the material terms of the transaction,
 8   including:
 9          (a) The principal amount of the loan;
10          (b) The amount actually receivable by the Borrower after authorized deductions;
11          (c) The applicable interest rate;
12          (d) All fees, charges, deductions, and other costs;
13          (e) The total amount payable;
14          (f) The payment schedule; and
15          (g) The applicable penalties or charges for late payment or nonpayment.
16
17          The disclosures shall be presented in plain and readily understandable language.
18
19          An Online Lender shall furnish the Borrower a copy of the final loan agreement
20   and applicable disclosure statement in a form that may be retained or subsequently
21   accessed.
22
23          No loan shall be consummated or disbursed without the Borrower’s affirmative
24   acceptance of the final loan amount and material terms. Silence, inactivity, pre-selected
25   consent, continued use of an application, or any other act that does not clearly manifest
26   affirmative acceptance shall not constitute such acceptance.
27
28           Section 7. Responsible Lending and Loan Administration. – An Online Lender
29   shall adopt reasonable and proportionate measures to assess a Borrower’s ability to repay
30   and to prevent irresponsible lending and over-indebtedness, consistent with Republic Act
31   No. 11765, otherwise known as the “Financial Products and Services Consumer Protection
32   Act,” and applicable regulations.
33
34          An Online Lender shall disclose the resulting obligation and its material terms
35   before any renewal, refinancing, rollover, or restructuring of a loan.
36
37          It shall be unlawful to:
38          (a) Increase an approved loan amount or credit limit, or disburse an amount in
39              excess of that affirmatively accepted by the Borrower, without the Borrower’s
40              separate affirmative acceptance;
41          (b) Automatically renew, roll over, refinance, or restructure a loan without the
42              Borrower’s prior, informed, and affirmative consent; or
43          (c) Structure a loan or series of transactions for the purpose of evading an
44              applicable interest-rate, fee, penalty, or total-cost ceiling.

 2          Section 8. Interest, Fees, Charges, and Penalties. – All interest, fees, charges,
 3   penalties, and other amounts imposed in connection with an online loan shall comply
 4   with applicable laws and regulations.
 5
 6         No Online Lender or Unauthorized Online Lender shall demand, collect, or receive
 7   any amount that:
 8         (a) Was not disclosed and agreed upon as required by law or is not otherwise
 9             lawfully imposable;
10         (b) Exceeds an applicable statutory or regulatory ceiling;
11         (c) Is false, fictitious, duplicative, or unrelated to a legitimate cost or service; or
12         (d) Is structured, recharacterized, divided, transferred, or otherwise imposed for
13             the purpose of circumventing an applicable ceiling or consumer protection
14             requirement.
15
16          Pursuant to the Financial Products and Services Consumer Protection Act, the SEC
17   may determine the reasonableness of interest charges, fees, penalties, and other loan costs
18   within its jurisdiction and prescribe appropriate regulatory measures in accordance with
19   law.
20
21          Section 9. Data Privacy and Protection Against Abusive Use of Personal Data. –
22   An Online Lender, Unauthorized Online Lender, TPSP, Collection Agent, or person acting
23   on behalf of any of them shall process personal information and sensitive personal
24   information in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the “Data
25   Privacy Act of 2012,” and applicable rules of the NPC.
26
27           No such person or entity shall require or obtain unnecessary, disproportionate,
28   bulk, or indiscriminate access to a Borrower’s contact list, address book, call logs, private
29   messages, photographs, media files, social media contacts, or similar information stored
30   in or accessible through the Borrower’s device or account.
31
32          Personal information or sensitive personal information obtained from any source
33   shall not be used for harassment, public shaming, reputational pressure, or unlawful
34   disclosure of a Borrower’s indebtedness.
35
36          A person identified merely as a character reference shall not become liable for the
37   Borrower’s debt. No person shall be treated as a guarantor, surety, co-maker, or other
38   person liable for the obligation unless such person has separately and expressly assumed
39   such liability in accordance with law.
40
41          Section 10. Fair Debt Collection. – An Online Lender, Unauthorized Online
42   Lender, TPSP, Collection Agent, or person acting on behalf of any of them shall employ
43   only reasonable and lawful means to collect an obligation and shall respect the dignity,
44   privacy, and lawful rights of the Borrower and other persons contacted in connection with

 1   the debt.
 2
 3          The following acts are prohibited:
 4          (a) Using or threatening violence, physical harm, unlawful restraint, or damage to
 5              property;
 6          (b) Using obscene, insulting, degrading, or abusive language intended or
 7              reasonably likely to harass, humiliate, or intimidate a Borrower or other
 8              person;
 9          (c) Threatening arrest, criminal prosecution, imprisonment, seizure of property,
10              or any other action that has no lawful basis, or falsely representing that such
11              consequence will automatically result from mere nonpayment of a debt;
12          (d) Falsely representing oneself as a court, law enforcement officer, government
13              official, lawyer, or other person possessing authority that the collector does not
14              possess;
15          (e) Creating or using any document or communication falsely made to appear to
16              have been issued by a court, law enforcement agency, government office, or
17              other public authority;
18          (f) Publishing or threatening to publish a Borrower’s identity, personal
19              information, loan information, or alleged delinquency for the purpose of, or
20              with the reasonably foreseeable effect of, shaming, humiliating, harassing,
21              intimidating, or coercing the Borrower;
22          (g) Disclosing the existence or details of a Borrower’s debt to any person who is
23              not legally liable for the obligation, except when required or authorized by law;
24          (h) Demanding payment from a character reference or any person who has not
25              legally assumed liability for the debt;
26          (i) Contacting a Borrower or other person repeatedly, excessively, or at
27              unreasonable hours in a manner intended or reasonably likely to constitute
28              harassment or abuse;
29          (j) Making a false representation or employing a deceptive means to collect or
30              attempt to collect a debt;
31          (k) Collecting or attempting to collect any amount not lawfully due; or
32          (l) Using personal data or device access in violation of this Act or applicable data
33              privacy laws.
34
35          Nothing in this Section shall prevent a lender or its representative from truthfully
36   informing a Borrower of lawful remedies available for the collection of an obligation or
37   from commencing an action or proceeding authorized by law.
38

 7           Section 12. Non-Waiver of Borrower Rights. – Any agreement, consent, waiver,
 8   authorization, acknowledgment, or stipulation that waives, limits, defeats, or circumvents
 9   a right or protection granted to a Borrower under this Act shall be void and unenforceable

11
12         Section 13. Consumer Complaints and Redress. – Every Online Lender shall
13   provide an accessible mechanism for receiving and resolving Borrower complaints.
14
15          Complaints and claims within the jurisdiction of the SEC shall be handled in
16   accordance with the consumer redress, adjudication, reimbursement, disgorgement, and
17   other enforcement powers granted to the SEC under the Financial Products and Services
18   Consumer Protection Act and other applicable laws.
19
20          For purposes of enforcing this Act, the SEC shall have jurisdiction over
21   Unauthorized Online Lenders with respect to administrative violations of this Act and
22   may, after notice and opportunity to be heard, order the refund, restitution, crediting, or
23   disgorgement of amounts unlawfully obtained or collected from Borrowers. Purely civil
24   claims shall remain subject to the applicable monetary and jurisdictional limits prescribed
25   under the Financial Products and Services Consumer Protection Act and other applicable
26   laws.
27
28          The SEC may order the remedies provided under Section 16 of this Act, together
29   with such restitution, disgorgement, or other relief as it may lawfully grant.
30
31         No Borrower shall recover more than once for the same injury or amount
32   unlawfully collected.
33
34          Section 14. Enforcement Against Unauthorized Online Lending. – Upon a finding
35   that a person or entity is engaging in online lending without the authority required by
36   law, the SEC may issue a cease-and-desist order.
37
38           Pursuant to a final or otherwise enforceable order, the SEC may require, or seek
39   the assistance of, application marketplaces, digital distribution platforms, payment
40   service providers, advertising platforms, and other intermediaries to discontinue services
41   that directly enable the operation of an Unauthorized Online Lender, subject to applicable
42   law and the jurisdiction of the appropriate regulator.
43

18          Section 16. Effect of Unlawful Charges and Practices. – Any interest, fee, charge,
19   penalty, or other amount imposed or collected in violation of an applicable ceiling or
20   prohibition shall be void to the extent of the unlawful or excessive amount.
21
22           Any amount unlawfully collected shall, as appropriate, be credited against the
23   Borrower’s outstanding lawful obligation or refunded to the Borrower, without prejudice
24   to restitution, disgorgement, or other relief available under law.
25
26          In the case of a loan lawfully extended by an authorized lender, the invalidity of
27   an unlawful interest, fee, charge, penalty, collection practice, or contractual stipulation
28   shall not, by itself, extinguish the Borrower’s obligation to repay the principal actually
29   received and any other amounts lawfully due.
30
31          Nothing in this Section shall validate an unauthorized lending operation or confer
32   upon an Unauthorized Online Lender a right to recover that it would not otherwise
33   possess under existing law.
34
35           Section 17. Interagency Coordination. – The SEC shall coordinate with the BSP,
36   Department of Information and Communications Technology, NPC, Department of
37   Justice, National Bureau of Investigation, Philippine National Police, Cybercrime
38   Investigation and Coordinating Center, and other concerned agencies for the effective
39   enforcement of this Act, particularly against Unauthorized Online Lenders and cross-
40   border online lending operations.
