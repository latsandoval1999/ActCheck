                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Every day, in classrooms across the country, public school teachers stand at
the frontlines of nation-building. Yet behind their unwavering dedication often lie
silent sacrifices: postponed medical checkups, unaddressed dental care, and the
constant anxiety of how to pay hospital bills in times of illness.

      It is a painful irony that those entrusted with caring for the nation’s children
frequently struggle to care for themselves.

       This Bill seeks to correct this inequity by institutionalizing comprehensive
healthcare benefits for all public school teachers through mandatory enrollment in
an accredited Health Maintenance Organization (HMO), with both medical and
dental coverage. This benefit shall be granted in addition to their existing salaries
and allowances, without diminution, and shall serve as a concrete expression of the
State’s gratitude for their indispensable service.

       The 1987 Constitution declares it a policy of the State to protect and promote
the right to health and to afford full protection to labor. It likewise mandates that
education be given the highest budgetary priority. These constitutional commitments
ring hollow if the very educators who give life to our educational system are left
vulnerable to illness and financial distress.

       Under the present administration, there has been a renewed emphasis on
strengthening the education sector—not only through curriculum reforms and
infrastructure improvements, but also by improving the welfare of teachers. The
expansion of medical allowances and continuing efforts to enhance teachers’
benefits reflect a recognition that quality education begins with supported and
motivated educators. This bill builds upon and institutionalizes these efforts by
ensuring that healthcare coverage for teachers is secured by law.

      In view of the foregoing, approval of this legislative measure is earnestly
sought.

  AN ACT PROVIDING HEALTHCARE BENEFITS TO PUBLIC SCHOOL
       TEACHERS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

    Section 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “HMO for Teacher’s Act”.

    Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
uphold the dignity, rights, and essential contribution of public school teachers to
national progress. The State shall likewise adopt an integrated and comprehensive
approach to health development. To this end, the State shall ensure the welfare of
teachers by providing adequate safeguards, benefits, and support systems that promote
their well-being, enhance their professional growth, and enable them to continue
rendering effective and dedicated service to the public.

     Section 3. Coverage. - This Act shall provide healthcare benefits through
accredited Health Maintenance Organization (HMO) to all public school teachers from
primary to high school, including special and alternative learning schools.

     Section 4. Enrollment of Teachers to HMO. – All public school teachers who
have completed their six-month probationary period shall be enrolled to a Health
Maintenance Organization (HMO), for both medical and dental coverage. The same
shall be sufficient to cover check-ups, emergency hospitalization, and necessary
medical procedures.

     In no way shall this be deducted from their existing salary and benefits or taken as
replacement thereof. It shall be considered as additional benefit for teachers.
     Section 5. Selection of HMO. – The selection of the Health Maintenance
Organization (HMO) shall be undertaken through the appropriate bidding and
procurement process in accordance with existing laws and regulations, ensuring that the
annual benefit limit adequately meets the beneficiaries’ needs and that the HMO is
accredited by hospitals nationwide.

     Section 6. Optional Enrollment of Dependents. – Teacher-beneficiaries may
enroll their dependents at their own expense.

      Section 7. Appropriations. – The funding necessary to carry out the provisions of
this Act is hereby authorized to be appropriated from the General Appropriations Act of
the year following its enactment, and every year thereafter

      Section 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The Department of Education
(DepEd) in coordination with the Civil Service Commission (CSC), the Department of
Budget and Management (DBM) and all other agencies related thereto shall formulate
and promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of
this Act within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

       Section 9. Separability Clause. - If any provision or part of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

       Section 10. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, and administrative
orders or parts thereof inconsistent with any provision of this Act are hereby repealed
or amended accordingly.

       Section 11. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the online version of the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of
general circulation or with an established internet website presence.
