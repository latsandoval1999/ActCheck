                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

The quality of education largely depends on the dedication and competence of our public
school teachers. As the primary movers of learning and nation-building, they deserve fair
compensation and adequate support that recognize the value of their profession and
enable them to perform their duties effectively.

Despite their indispensable role, many public school teachers continue to face financial
and professional challenges brought about by increasing responsibilities and the rising
cost of living. These realities underscore the need to strengthen the government's
commitment to improving their welfare and ensuring that the teaching profession remains
attractive to qualified and dedicated educators.

This measure seeks to enhance the compensation and benefits of public school teachers
as an investment in the country's education system and the future of every Filipino
learner.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT INCREASING THE MINIMUM SALARY OF PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS
  FROM SALARY GRADE ELEVEN (11) TO SALARY GRADE SEVENTEEN (17),
  ENHANCING THEIR COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS, AND APPROPRIATING
                         FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Public School Teachers Salary
Increase Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to proactively
promote, protect, and enhance the social, economic, and professional well-being of public
school teachers as the primary agents of national development and transformation.

This shall be achieved through the continuous improvement of their living conditions, the
modernization of their working environments, and the provision of a living wage that
reflects the dignity of the profession.

In accordance with the 1987 Philippine Constitution, the State shall ensure that education
remains the highest budgetary priority. To this end, the State commits to a system of
competitive remuneration, holistic benefits, and professional career pathways designed
to attract, develop, and retain the nation's best available talents, ensuring that teaching
remains a source of lifelong job satisfaction and a catalyst for social mobility.
SEC. 3. Coverage. - This Act shall cover teaching personnel in all public schools in the
elementary and secondary levels under the Department of Education (DepEd), as defined
by Republic Act No. 4670, otherwise known as the "Magna Carta for Public School
Teachers."

SEC. 4. Increasing the Minimum Salaries of Public School Teachers. - The minimum
salary grade level of public school teachers shall be Salary Grade 17.

Salary grade levels of those occupying higher positions shall likewise be adjusted
accordingly.

SEC. 5. Teaching Supplies Allowance. - Teaching personnel in public schools shall be
entitled to a Teaching Supplies Allowance to cover the purchase of teaching materials,
classroom supplies, instructional resources, and other learning implements necessary for
effective classroom instruction.

The allowance shall be provided in cash and is hereby fixed at Fifteen Thousand Pesos
(₱15,000.00) per teacher per school year, subject to periodic review and adjustment as
may be necessary.

SEC. 6. Medical Examination and Treatment Allowance. - A Medical Examination and
Preventive Healthcare Allowance of Ten Thousand Pesos (₱10,000.00) per teacher per
school year is hereby granted to all public school teachers, subject to periodic review and
adjustment as may be necessary.

The allowance shall cover annual physical examinations, mental health consultations,
laboratory and diagnostic services, preventive healthcare, and other medically necessary
outpatient services, in accordance with guidelines to be issued by the Department of
Education.

The Secretary of Education, in coordination with the Department of Health and the
Department of Budget and Management, shall issue the implementing guidelines for this
Section.

SEC. 7. Adjustment of Salaries. - To preserve the objectives of this Act, the salaries of
public school teachers shall be reviewed every two (2) years by the Secretary of
Education or his or her duly authorized representatives.

The Secretary shall submit recommendations to both Houses of Congress indicating
whether salary adjustments are warranted.

SEC. 8. Periodic Review of Allowances. - The Secretary of Education is hereby mandated
to periodically review the teaching supplies and medical allowances, taking into account
prevailing prices of classroom supplies and medical services, and recommend the
necessary adjustments to Congress.
SEC. 9. Priority in Budget Allocation. - The Government shall appropriate such sums as
may be necessary to carry out the objectives of this Act.

Provided, that the salary increase of teaching personnel in public schools under the
Department of Education shall take priority over non-educational and non-agricultural
budgetary allocations.

SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Education, in coordination with the Department
of Budget and Management, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for its
effective implementation.

SEC. 11. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SEC. 12. Repealing Clause - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, regulations, and
other issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 13. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
