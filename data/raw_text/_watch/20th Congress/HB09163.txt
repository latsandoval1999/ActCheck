                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article XIV, Sections 1 and 2 of the 1987 Constitution mandate the State
to protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels
and to take appropriate steps to make such education accessible to all. The
Constitution likewise directs the State to establish, maintain, and support a
complete, adequate, and integrated system of education responsive to the needs
of the people and society.

       In the modern digital era, access to educational materials and learning
resources has increasingly become dependent on technology and internet
connectivity. However, many Filipino learners, particularly those enrolled in
public schools and those residing in geographically isolated and disadvantaged
areas, continue to face difficulties in accessing updated textbooks, reference
materials, and other educational resources due to financial limitations,
insufficient library facilities, and unequal access to digital infrastructure.

       This proposed measure seeks to institutionalize the Philippine Online
Library as a centralized and accessible digital repository of textbooks,
instructional materials, and other educational resources for elementary and
secondary learners. Through the digitization of prescribed learning materials and
the utilization of information and communications technology, the measure aims
to expand educational access, promote digital inclusion, and strengthen the
delivery of public education throughout the country.

       The bill likewise encourages inter-agency coordination among the
Department of Education (DepEd), Department of Information and
Communications Technology (DICT), and the National Library of the Philippines
(NLP) to ensure the proper development, management, and maintenance of the
digital platform and educational repository, while remaining consistent with
existing laws on intellectual property, cybersecurity, and data privacy.
      By leveraging technology to improve access to quality educational
resources, this measure seeks to help bridge the digital divide, support learners
and educators, and advance a more inclusive, modern, and resilient educational
system for present and future generations.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
      MANDATING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE PHILIPPINE ONLINE
      LIBRARY AND THE DIGITIZATION OF ALL PRESCRIBED
      REFERENCES FOR PUBLIC EDUCATION, PROVIDING FUNDS
      THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Title - This Act shall be known as the "Philippine Online
Library Act."

        SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - Pursuant to Article XIV, Sections 1
and 2 of the 1987 Constitution, the State shall protect and promote the right of
all citizens to quality education at all levels and shall take appropriate steps to
make such education accessible to all. The State shall likewise establish,
maintain, and support a complete, adequate, and integrated system of education
responsive to the needs of the people and society, and shall provide for a system
of free public education in the elementary and secondary levels.

       Towards this end, the State recognizes the vital role of information and
communications technology in improving access to educational resources and
advancing inclusive and equitable learning opportunities. The State shall
therefore endeavor to establish and maintain a Philippine Online Library that
shall provide learners, particularly those enrolled in public schools, with access
to digitized textbooks, instructional materials, and other educational resources,
subject to existing intellectual property laws and other applicable laws, rules,
and regulations.

      The State shall further promote digital literacy, technological innovation,
and equal access to educational materials, especially in underserved and
geographically isolated areas, through coordinated efforts among relevant
government agencies and stakeholders.
      SECTION 3. The Philippine Online Library - The Department of
Education (DepEd), in coordination with the National Library of the Philippines
(NLP) and the Department of Information and Communications Technology
(DICT), shall establish, operate, and maintain the Philippine Online Library.

       The Philippine Online Library shall serve as a centralized digital repository
of digitized textbooks, learning modules, reference materials, open educational
resources, and other instructional materials prescribed or authorized by DepEd
for elementary and secondary education.

       The digitization, reproduction, storage, and distribution of copyrighted
materials under this Act shall be undertaken in accordance with existing
intellectual property laws, licensing agreements, fair use limitations, and other
applicable laws and regulations.

      The DICT shall provide the necessary technological infrastructure,
cybersecurity protection, and systems maintenance for the online platform, while
the National Library of the Philippines shall assist in digital archiving,
preservation, and content management.

      The Philippine Online Library shall be made accessible free of charge to
learners, teachers, and the public through an online platform, subject to
reasonable rules and security protocols established by the implementing
agencies.

       The implementing agencies shall ensure that the platform complies with
existing laws and standards on data privacy, cybersecurity, accessibility for
persons with disabilities, and digital inclusion.

      SECTION 4. Transfer of Serviceable Computer Equipment from
Government Agencies and Instrumentalities. - Subject to existing auditing,
accounting, inventory, cybersecurity, and government property disposal laws,
rules, and regulations, National Government Agencies (NGAs), Government
Owned or Controlled Corporations (GOCCs), Government Financial Institutions
(GFIs), and Local Government Units (LGUs) may transfer serviceable and
unutilized computer equipment and related devices to DepEd schools for
educational use.

      Prior to transfer, the DICT or the authorized information technology
personnel of the transferring agency shall ensure that all devices are serviceable,
properly sanitized of confidential or sensitive data, and compliant with applicable
cybersecurity standards.

      Schools with inadequate access to computer devices and those located in
geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas (GIDAs) shall be prioritized in
the distribution of donated or transferred equipment.
      The DepEd shall maintain an inventory and monitoring system for all
transferred equipment under this Act.

      SECTION 5. Additional Online Educational Materials - The DepEd, in
coordination with the National Library of the Philippines and other relevant
government agencies, educational institutions, and stakeholders, shall
supplement the Philippine Online Library with additional educational materials,
including Filipiniana resources, local literature, indigenous knowledge systems,
historical archives, multimedia educational content, and open educational
resources (OERs), to further enrich the learning experience of Filipino students.

      Priority shall be given to the development and inclusion of culturally
relevant, age appropriate, and curriculum aligned materials.

     The DepEd shall also prioritize the development and use of open
educational resources whenever practicable.

      SECTION 6. Custody of the Digitized Textbooks and Reference
Materials- The DepEd shall serve as the lead implementing agency and shall
exercise primary supervision over the development and utilization of the
Philippine Online Library.

       The National Library of the Philippines shall have joint custody of all
digitized archival and reference materials and shall provide technical assistance
on preservation, cataloguing, and digital archiving standards.

       The DICT shall be responsible for the maintenance, cybersecurity
protection, technological management, and continuous improvement of the
digital infrastructure and online systems of the Philippine Online Library.

       SECTION 7. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations
of the DepEd and the DICT. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the
continued implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

     In the allocation of funds, priority shall be given to public schools in
underserved, remote, and geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas
(GIDAs).
       SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DepEd, in coordination with the DICT,
the National Library of the Philippines, and other relevant agencies, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation
of this Act.

      The implementing agencies shall conduct periodic assessments of the
Philippine Online Library to ensure continued relevance, accessibility,
technological adequacy, and curriculum alignment.

       SECTION 9. Separability Clause. - If any part or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other parts hereof not affected thereby
shall remain valid.

      SECTION 10. Repealing Clause- All laws, acts, presidential decrees,
executive orders, administrative orders, rules and regulations inconsistent with
or contrary to the provisions of this Act are deemed amended, modified or
repealed accordingly.

      SECTION 11. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
