                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish a Modern Hospital Network Licensing System that
regulates hospitals and healthcare facilities as part of integrated networks. It
institutes a comprehensive licensing framework covering governance, quality and
patient safety standards, interoperability and cybersecurity requirements,
emergency preparedness, and continuity of care, while promoting integration
between public and private healthcare systems and providing appropriate
incentives, administrative sanctions, and criminal penalties to ensure compliance.

        Republic Act No. 4226, otherwise known as the “Hospital Licensure Act”, has
governed hospital licensing in the Philippines for over five decades, but it no longer
reflects the realities of modern healthcare delivery. Today, many hospitals operate as
part of integrated networks spanning multiple facilities, employ digital health
systems and telemedicine, and rely on interoperable health information systems.
These are developments that the current facility-based licensing model was never
designed to address.

       This bill ensures that our healthcare regulatory framework keeps pace with
scientific advances and digital transformation, while safeguarding patient safety,
data privacy, and quality of care for all Filipinos.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                         AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A MODERN HOSPITAL NETWORK LICENSING SYSTEM,
REPEALING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 4226, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS THE “HOSPITAL LICENSURE ACT”, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Hospital Network
Licensing Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people, instill health consciousness
among them, and adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health
development. The State recognizes that the delivery of healthcare services must
continuously evolve in response to scientific advances, changing public health
conditions, and the increasing complexity of modern healthcare systems.

       To this end, the State shall establish a modern, responsive, interoperable,
patient-centered, and resilient hospital and healthcare regulatory framework that
ensures accessible, equitable, safe, efficient, and quality healthcare services for all
Filipinos. The State shall promote the integration and coordination of healthcare
delivery systems, strengthen continuity of care, enhance emergency preparedness
and public health response capabilities, and support innovation in healthcare
administration, infrastructure, and service delivery.

       The State further recognizes the transformative role of digital technologies,
health information systems, telemedicine, artificial intelligence, and other emerging
innovations in improving healthcare accessibility, efficiency, accountability, and
patient outcomes. Accordingly, the State shall promote adaptive and
technology-neutral regulatory mechanisms that uphold patient safety, data privacy,
cybersecurity, ethical standards, and public accountability while fostering
innovation and sustainable healthcare modernization.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

           a.​ Digital Health System refers to electronic systems used for healthcare
       delivery, administration, diagnostics, patient management, telemedicine, or
       clinical decision support;

           b.​ Healthcare Facility refers to hospitals, infirmaries, ambulatory surgical
       centers, specialty clinics, laboratories, dialysis centers, rehabilitation facilities,
       telemedicine centers, and similar establishments providing healthcare
       services;​

           c.​ Hospital Network refers to an integrated healthcare delivery system
       composed of one or more hospitals and related healthcare facilities operating
       under unified governance, management, referral coordination, or health
       information systems;​

           d.​ Interoperability refers to the capability of different healthcare
       information systems and applications to exchange, interpret, and securely use
       patient and operational data;

          e.​ Licensing Authority refers to the Department of Health (DOH) or its
       duly authorized regulatory body;​

          f.​ Network License refers to a license issued to an integrated hospital
       network under this Act;​

          g.​ Patient Continuity of Care refers to coordinated healthcare services
       across facilities, providers, and levels of care throughout a patient’s treatment;
       and​

           h.​ Telemedicine refers to the remote delivery of healthcare services using
       telecommunications and digital technologies.

​       SEC. 4. Scope of Regulation. – This Act shall apply to all hospitals, healthcare
facilities, integrated hospital networks, and public and private healthcare delivery
systems operating within the Philippines, including healthcare entities utilizing
telemedicine or digital health platforms as part of clinical operations.

       SEC. 5. Establishment of a Hospital Network Licensing System. – The DOH shall
establish a comprehensive licensing framework for integrated hospital networks.
Licensing shall cover:

          a.​ Governance structures;​

          b.​ Healthcare quality standards;​

          c.​ Referral systems;​




                                             2
          d.​ Patient safety protocols;​

          e.​ Emergency preparedness;​

          f.​ Interoperability standards;​

          g.​ Cybersecurity measures;​

          h.​ Workforce requirements; and​

          i.​ Continuity-of-care systems.

       SEC. 6. Types of Licenses. – The DOH shall classify licenses issued under this
Act into such categories, including but not limited to:

          a.​ Standalone Hospitals;​

          b.​ Integrated Hospital Networks;​

          c.​ Specialty Hospital Networks;​

          d.​ Teaching and Academic Medical Networks;​

          e.​ Telehealth-Integrated Healthcare Systems; and​

          f.​ Such other classifications as may be necessary.

      SEC. 7. Digital Health and Interoperability Standards. – All licensed entities
under this Act shall:

          a.​ Maintain secure electronic health record systems;​

          b.​ Comply with national interoperability standards;​

          c.​ Ensure patient access and portability of medical records;​

          d.​ Implement cybersecurity safeguards;​

          e.​ Establish disaster recovery systems for health data; and​

          f.​ Comply with the Data Privacy Act and related laws.

      The DOH, in coordination with the Department of Information and
Communications Technology, shall promulgate interoperability and cybersecurity
standards.

      SEC. 8. Quality and Patient Safety Standards. – The licensing authority shall
adopt measurable healthcare quality indicators, including:



                                             3
         a.​ Infection prevention and control;​

         b.​ Patient outcomes;​

         c.​ Mortality and morbidity reporting;​

         d.​ Appropriate staffing levels and workforce competency;​

         e.​ Emergency response capability;​

         f.​ Patient rights compliance;​

         g.​ Referral turnaround efficiency; and​

         h.​ Such other standards as may be necessary.

      SEC. 9. Emergency Preparedness and Health System Coordination. – All licensed
hospital networks shall maintain:​

         a.​ Emergency operations plans;​

         b.​ Surge capacity systems;​

         c.​ Coordinated referral mechanisms;​

         d.​ Interoperable emergency communications;​

         e.​ Disaster response protocols; and​

         f.​ Participation in regional healthcare emergency coordination systems.

The DOH may require mandatory coordination during declared public health
emergencies.

       SEC. 10. Public-Private Health System Integration. – The DOH shall promote
integration between public and private healthcare systems through:​

         a.​ Interoperable referral systems;​

         b.​ Emergency bed coordination;​

         c.​ Shared public health reporting systems;​

         d.​ Integrated disaster response protocols; and​

         e.​ Coordination with PhilHealth and Universal Health Care mechanisms.




                                           4
      SEC. 11. Licensing Procedures. – No hospital, healthcare facility, or hospital
network covered under this Act shall operate without a valid license issued by the
DOH. Licenses shall be subject to:

            a.​ Initial inspection;​

            b.​ Periodic renewal;​

            c.​ Performance evaluation;​

            d.​ Compliance audits; and​

            e.​ Emergency inspections when necessary.

        SEC. 12. Transparency and Public Reporting. – The DOH shall require licensed
entities to publicly disclose:

            a.​ Licensing status;​

            b.​ Quality performance indicators;​

            c.​ Accreditation status;​

            d.​ Patient safety incidents, subject to confidentiality laws; and​

            e.​ Emergency capacity information during public health crises.

         SEC. 13. Administrative Sanctions. – The Department of Health may impose:

            a.​ Fines;​

            b.​ Suspension of licenses;​

            c.​ Revocation of licenses;​

            d.​ Temporary operational restrictions; or​

              e.​ Mandatory corrective actions upon entities found violating this Act or
         its implementing rules.

         Administrative fines shall be adjusted periodically to maintain deterrent
value.

        SEC. 14. Criminal Liability. – Any person who knowingly operates a healthcare
facility or hospital network without the required license, falsifies compliance
records, obstructs lawful inspections, or willfully commits acts or omissions
constituting gross negligence resulting in serious injury, death, or substantial risk to
patient safety shall, upon conviction, be punished by imprisonment of not less than



                                             5
one (1) year but not more than six (6) years, or a fine of not less than Five hundred
thousand pesos (Php 500,000.00) but not more than Five million pesos (Php
5,000,000.00), or both, at the discretion of the court.

       SEC. 15. Incentives for Compliance and Innovation. – The State may provide
incentives for:

          a.​ Interoperable healthcare systems;​

          b.​ Regional healthcare integration;​

          c.​ Healthcare innovation;​

          d.​ Telemedicine expansion in underserved areas;​

          e.​ Disaster-resilient healthcare infrastructure; and​

          f.​ Quality improvement initiatives.

        SEC. 16. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the Department of
Health. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

       SEC. 17. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within one hundred eighty
(180) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health, in consultation
with relevant government agencies and stakeholders, shall promulgate the
necessary implementing rules and regulations.

        SEC. 18. Transitory Provision. – Existing hospitals and healthcare facilities
licensed under Republic Act No. 4226, otherwise known as the “Hospital Licensure
Act”, shall continue operating for a transition period of three (3) years from the
effectivity of this Act, during which they shall comply with the requirements herein.

       SEC. 19. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
force and effect.

       SEC. 20. Repealing Clause. – Republic Act No. 4226, otherwise known as the
“Hospital Licensure Act”, is hereby repealed. All laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act
are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 21. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
