                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to appropriate the amount of Two Billion Pesos (Php 2,000,000,000.00)
for the extension of the Cagayan de Oro Coastal By-Pass Road from Barangay Gusa to
Barangay Bugo in Cagayan de Oro City.

       Roads are beneficial to the livelihood of the people in the rural areas. They serve as
convenient links between the barangay and provide direct access to the town proper where
hospital, law enforcement offices and other government agencies are located.

        Cagayan de Oro City is the capital of Misamis Oriental. As one of our fast-growing
tourist destinations, it has attracted a lot of people from the rural areas and its neighboring
provinces. The city plays a pivotal role in our country’s efforts to attain economic growth. The
extension of the CDO Coastal By-Pass Road will greatly stimulate and promote economic
development in the region by increasing commercial activities and easier and faster travel time
for the residents.

       In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
APPROPRIATING THE AMOUNT OF TWO BILLION PESOS (PHP 2,000,000,000.00) FOR
THE EXTENSION OF THE CAGAYAN DE ORO COASTAL BY-PASS ROAD FROM
BARANGAY GUSA UP TO BARANGAY BUGO IN THE CITY OF CAGAYAN DE ORO,
PROVINCE OF MISAMIS ORIENTAL AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. The amount of Two Billion Pesos (Php 2,000,000,000.00) is hereby appropriated
out of any funds in the National Treasury, not otherwise appropriated, for the extension of the
Cagayan de Oro Coastal By-Pass Road from Barangay Gusa up to Barangay Bugo in
Cagayan de Oro City, Province of Misamis Oriental. Thereafter, such sum as may be
necessary for the improvement and maintenance of the coastal road shall be included in the
annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a national newspaper
of general circulation.
