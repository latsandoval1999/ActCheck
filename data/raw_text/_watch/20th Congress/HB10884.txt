AN ACT ESTABLISHING MOBILE REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH CLINICS
FOR   UNDERSERVED     AND   GEOGRAPHICALLY    ISOLATED
COMMUNITIES, PROVIDING ACCESS TO ESSENTIAL WOMEN’S
HEALTH SERVICES, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The Constitution recognizes the right to health of every Filipino and directs
the State to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development
that shall make essential goods, health services, and other social services available
to all. Despite existing efforts to improve healthcare delivery, many women,
particularly those living in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas
(GIDAs), rural communities, island barangays, Indigenous Peoples' communities,
and disaster- or conflict-affected areas, continue to experience barriers in accessing
timely, affordable, and quality reproductive, maternal, and preventive healthcare
services.

       Limited healthcare facilities, geographic distance, inadequate transportation,
socioeconomic challenges, and shortages in health personnel often delay access to
essential services and contribute to preventable maternal and reproductive health
complications. These challenges highlight the need for innovative and community-


                                                                                     1
 based healthcare delivery mechanisms that can bring critical services closer to
 women and families who need them most.

       Thus, this bill seeks to establish the Mobile Reproductive Health Clinic
 Program under the Department of Health (DOH) to provide accessible, equitable,
 and quality reproductive, maternal, preventive, and women's healthcare services
 through mobile healthcare units deployed to underserved and hard-to-reach
 communities. By bringing healthcare services directly to communities, the measure
 aims to reduce disparities in health access, promote early detection and prevention
 of reproductive and maternal health conditions, and strengthen continuity of care
 through coordinated referrals with Rural Health Units, hospitals, and other
 appropriate healthcare facilities.

        The proposed Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics shall provide essential
 services such as prenatal and postnatal care, maternal health monitoring,
 reproductive health counseling and education, family planning information
 consistent with existing laws, breast and cervical health screening, nutrition
 counseling, preventive health services, mental health support, and referral
 assistance.

       Through this measure, the State shall strengthen primary healthcare delivery
 and improve access to essential women's health services. This represents a proactive
 investment in equitable healthcare delivery and advances the State’s commitment to
 ensuring that no woman is deprived of necessary health services because of
 geographic, economic, or social barriers.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Mobile Women's Health
Clinics Act”.


SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State,
consistent with Article II, Section 15 and Article XIII of the 1987 Constitution, to
protect and promote the right to health of the people by ensuring accessible,



                                                                                  1
affordable, equitable, and quality reproductive, maternal, and preventive healthcare
services for all, particularly for women residing in geographically isolated and
disadvantaged areas (GIDAs) and other underserved communities.


Towards this end, the State shall:


   a) Ensure equitable and timely access to reproductive, maternal, and women's
      healthcare services through community-based and mobile healthcare delivery
      systems;
   b) Strengthen the capacity of the public health system to provide preventive,
      promotive, curative, and referral services in underserved and hard-to-reach
      areas;
   c) Reduce maternal morbidity and mortality through early detection, timely
      intervention, and continuity of maternal healthcare services;
   d) Promote preventive healthcare, health education, and informed decision-
      making consistent with existing laws and public health policies;
   e) Enhance collaboration      among national      government agencies,       local
      government units, public health institutions, and other stakeholders in the
      delivery of mobile healthcare services; and
   f) Ensure that all services provided under this Act are delivered in a manner that
      upholds the dignity, privacy, cultural sensitivity, and rights of all patients,
      consistent with existing laws.


SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:




                                                                                    2
(a) Mobile Reproductive Health Clinic refers to a mobile healthcare unit equipped
to provide reproductive, maternal, preventive, and women's health services in
geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas (GIDAs) and other underserved
communities.


(b) Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Areas (GIDAs) refer to
communities identified by the Department of Health as having limited access to
healthcare facilities and services due to geographic, socioeconomic, security, or
environmental barriers.


(c) Essential Women's Health Services refer to a comprehensive range of
reproductive, maternal, preventive, and women's healthcare services, including but
not limited to:


   1. Prenatal, antenatal, and postnatal care;
   2. Maternal health assessment and monitoring;
   3. Reproductive health counseling and education;
   4. Family planning information and counseling consistent with existing laws;
   5. Breast and cervical health screening and other preventive screening services
      authorized by law;
   6. Screening, counseling, and appropriate referral for sexually transmitted
      infections (STIs) and other reproductive tract infections, consistent with
      existing laws and Department of Health guidelines;
   7. Nutrition counseling for women, pregnant individuals, lactating mothers, and
      infants;




                                                                                  3
   8. Mental health screening and psychosocial support related to pregnancy,
      childbirth, and reproductive health, with referral to appropriate healthcare
      providers when necessary;
   9. Health education on adolescent reproductive health, maternal health,
      responsible parenthood, and disease prevention, consistent with existing laws;
      and
   10.Such other preventive, promotive, diagnostic, and referral services as may be
      authorized by law or prescribed by the Department of Health through its
      implementing rules and regulations.


SEC. 4. Establishment of the Mobile Reproductive Health Clinic Program. -
The Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with local government units and
public health institutions, shall establish and implement a nationwide Mobile
Reproductive Health Clinic Program.


The Program shall aim to:


   a) Improve access to reproductive, maternal, and preventive healthcare services
      for women, particularly those residing in geographically isolated and
      disadvantaged areas (GIDAs) and other underserved communities;
   b) Reduce preventable maternal and infant morbidity and mortality through
      timely and accessible healthcare interventions;
   c) Strengthen the delivery of primary healthcare services by providing regular
      mobile outreach, health education, screening, and referral services;




                                                                                   4
   d) Promote early detection, prevention, and management of reproductive and
      women's     health      conditions   through     community-based   healthcare
      interventions;
   e) Enhance continuity of care by facilitating referrals, follow-up consultations,
      and coordination with Rural Health Units, hospitals, and other appropriate
      healthcare facilities; and
   f) Support the achievement of national public health goals by expanding
      equitable access to quality reproductive and maternal healthcare services.


The Program shall prioritize:


   a) Geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas;
   b) Rural municipalities;
   c) Island barangays;
   d) Indigenous Peoples' communities; and
   e) Disaster-affected and conflict-affected areas.


SEC. 5. Services Provided. - Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics may provide the
following services:


   a) Prenatal and postnatal consultations;
   b) Maternal health monitoring;
   c) Family planning information and counseling consistent with existing laws;
   d) Breast and cervical health screening;
   e) Reproductive health education;
   f) Nutrition counseling for mothers and infants;


                                                                                   5
   g) Vaccination and preventive health services; and
   h) Referral services to hospitals and higher-level healthcare facilities.


All services shall be provided in accordance with existing laws, ethical standards,
and public health guidelines.


SEC. 6. Minimum Standards and Equipment of Mobile Reproductive Health
Clinics. - To ensure the safe, effective, and consistent delivery of healthcare
services, each Mobile Reproductive Health Clinic shall, subject to the availability of
funds and in accordance with standards prescribed by the Department of Health
(DOH), be equipped with the necessary medical equipment, medicines, and support
facilities appropriate to the services authorized under this Act.


At a minimum, each Mobile Reproductive Health Clinic shall, whenever practicable,
include:


           a. A private consultation and examination area that ensures patient safety,
              dignity, and confidentiality;
           b. Basic diagnostic equipment, including blood pressure apparatus, fetal
              Doppler or equivalent fetal monitoring device, weighing scales,
              thermometers, glucometers, and other essential medical instruments as
              prescribed by the DOH;
           c. Equipment and supplies for maternal and reproductive healthcare,
              including examination tables, sterile instruments, emergency obstetric
              supplies, and infection prevention and control materials;




                                                                                     6
         d. Essential medicines, vaccines, family planning commodities authorized
            under existing laws, and other medical supplies included in the
            Philippine National Formulary or as prescribed by the DOH;


         e. Cold-chain storage facilities for vaccines and temperature-sensitive
            medicines, whenever applicable;
         f. Basic laboratory or point-of-care diagnostic equipment, where feasible
            and consistent with DOH standards;
         g. Reliable    communication      equipment    to    facilitate    emergency
            coordination, teleconsultation where available, and referrals to
            appropriate healthcare facilities;
         h. Information and educational materials on reproductive health, maternal
            health, nutrition, preventive healthcare, and other health promotion
            programs; and
         i. Such other equipment, supplies, and facilities as the DOH may
            prescribe through the Implementing Rules and Regulations to ensure
            the effective delivery of healthcare services under this Act.


The DOH shall establish and periodically update the minimum operational
standards, equipment specifications, maintenance requirements, and inventory
guidelines for all Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics established under this Act.


SEC. 7. Procurement, Maintenance, and Management of Mobile Clinic Assets.
- The Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with concerned government
agencies and local government units, shall establish appropriate systems for the
procurement, acquisition, deployment, operation, maintenance, and replacement of


                                                                                    7
Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics, medical equipment, supplies, and other
program assets necessary for the implementation of this Act.


All procurement activities undertaken pursuant to this Act shall comply with existing
government procurement laws, rules, and regulations, including applicable
principles of transparency, competitiveness, accountability, efficiency, and value for
money.


The Department of Health shall ensure that:


         a. Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics and related equipment are
             procured according to applicable technical specifications, quality
             standards, and healthcare requirements;
         b. Regular inspection, preventive maintenance, repair, and replacement
             schedules are established to ensure the safe and continuous operation
             of mobile clinics;
         c. An inventory management system is maintained for all vehicles,
             medical equipment, medicines, supplies, and other assets acquired
             under the Program;
         d. Appropriate safeguards are established to prevent misuse, unauthorized
             disposal, diversion, or personal use of government-owned mobile
             clinics and program resources; and
         e. Records relating to procurement, maintenance, utilization, and
             disposition of program assets are properly maintained and made
             available for audit and monitoring in accordance with existing laws.

SEC. 8. Composition of Mobile Health Teams. - Each Mobile Reproductive
Health Clinic shall, whenever practicable, include:


            a. A physician or municipal health officer;
            b. A nurse;
            c. A midwife;
            d. A social worker or counselor; and
            e. Barangay health workers or community health volunteers.


SEC. 9. Training and Capacity Building. - The Department of Health (DOH) shall
develop and implement a continuing training and capacity-building program for
personnel assigned to Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics to ensure the effective,
safe, and culturally responsive delivery of healthcare services under this Act.




                                                                                    9
The training program shall, at a minimum, cover the following:


(a) Maternal, newborn, reproductive, and women's healthcare;
(b) Preventive healthcare and health promotion;
(c) Emergency obstetric care, patient stabilization, and referral protocols;
(d) Infection prevention and control, patient safety, and quality assurance;
(e) Gender sensitivity, patient rights, confidentiality, and ethical standards in
healthcare delivery;
(f) Cultural competence, including the delivery of healthcare services to Indigenous
Peoples and other culturally diverse communities;
(g) Mental health awareness and psychosocial support related to reproductive and
maternal health;
(h) Proper collection, management, and protection of patient information in
accordance with applicable laws on data privacy and confidentiality;
(i) Community engagement, health education, and effective communication
strategies; and
(j) Such other competencies as the Department of Health may determine necessary
through the Implementing Rules and Regulations.


The Department of Health may collaborate with local government units, government
hospitals, state universities and colleges, professional healthcare organizations, and
other qualified institutions in the development and conduct of training programs
under this Act.




                                                                                   10
Personnel assigned to Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics shall participate in
periodic continuing professional development and competency enhancement
activities, subject to existing laws, rules, and regulations.


SEC. 10. Emergency Care and Referral Protocols. - Mobile Reproductive Health
Clinics shall provide immediate assessment and initial stabilization of patients
requiring urgent or emergency reproductive, maternal, or women's healthcare within
the capability of the mobile health team.


In cases requiring a higher level of medical care, the mobile health team shall
promptly facilitate the referral and, whenever practicable, coordinate the safe
transport of the patient to the nearest appropriate Rural Health Unit, municipal or
city health office, government hospital, or other licensed healthcare facility capable
of providing the necessary treatment.


The Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with local government units and
appropriate healthcare facilities, shall establish standardized referral protocols to
ensure the timely transfer, continuity of care, and follow-up of patients served under
this Act.


Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics shall maintain updated directories of referral
facilities and emergency contact information within their respective areas of
operation and shall document all emergency cases and referrals in accordance with
Department of Health guidelines and applicable laws on data privacy and patient
confidentiality.




                                                                                   11
SEC. 11. Coordination and Responsibilities of Local Government Units. - Local
government units (LGUs), through their respective Provincial, City, Municipal, and
Barangay Health Offices, shall support the implementation of the Mobile
Reproductive Health Clinic Program within their respective jurisdictions.


For this purpose, LGUs shall:


         a. Identify and prioritize geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas
            (GIDAs), underserved barangays, Indigenous Peoples' communities,
            and other communities in need of mobile reproductive and maternal
            healthcare services;
         b. Coordinate with the Department of Health (DOH) in the planning,
            scheduling, and deployment of Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics;
         c. Conduct community information and education activities to promote
            awareness of the Program and encourage community participation;
         d. Assist in the registration of beneficiaries, organization of outreach
            activities, and dissemination of schedules for mobile clinic visits;
         e. Designate and coordinate with Rural Health Units, government
            hospitals, or other appropriate healthcare facilities to facilitate referrals,
            follow-up consultations, and continuity of care;
         f. Provide, whenever practicable, logistical and administrative support,
            including suitable deployment sites, utilities, transportation assistance,
            security, and other resources necessary for the efficient operation of
            Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics;
         g. Facilitate coordination with Barangay Health Workers, community
            health volunteers, Indigenous Peoples' representatives, and other local


                                                                                       12
             stakeholders to ensure culturally appropriate and accessible healthcare
             delivery;
         h. Maintain records and submit such reports as may be required by the
             Department of Health for monitoring and evaluation purposes; and
         i. Encourage the integration of the Mobile Reproductive Health Clinic
             Program into local health plans and investment programs and allocate
             available funds from local health appropriations, Gender and
             Development (GAD) funds, and other lawful sources, subject to
             existing laws, rules, and regulations.


SEC. 12. Health Education and Community Awareness. - The DOH and local
government units shall conduct culturally sensitive community education campaigns
regarding:


         a. Maternal health;
         b. Preventive healthcare;
         c. Nutrition and infant care;
         d. Reproductive health awareness; and
         e. Available healthcare services.


SEC. 13. Data Collection and Monitoring. - The DOH shall establish a monitoring
and evaluation system to assess:


         a. Number of beneficiaries served;
         b. Maternal and reproductive health indicators;
         c. Service accessibility gaps; and


                                                                                  13
         d. Program effectiveness.


All personal health information shall be protected in accordance with existing data
privacy laws.


SEC. 14. Digital Health Records and Information Management. - The
Department of Health (DOH), in coordination with local government units and
relevant government agencies, shall establish and maintain an appropriate digital
health records system for services provided through Mobile Reproductive Health
Clinics, whenever practicable and consistent with existing government health
information systems.


The digital health records system shall aim to:


         a. Ensure accurate and timely documentation of healthcare services
             provided to patients;
         b. Facilitate continuity of care, follow-up consultations, and referrals
             between Mobile Reproductive Health Clinics, Rural Health Units,
             hospitals, and other healthcare facilities;
         c. Support monitoring,        evaluation,   health   planning, and   policy
             development through reliable and timely health data;
         d. Reduce duplication of medical records and improve coordination
             among healthcare providers; and
         e. Strengthen the delivery of quality and accessible healthcare services in
             underserved communities.




                                                                                  14
The DOH shall establish standards and protocols for the collection, storage, access,
sharing, retention, and protection of health information generated through the
Program.


All digital health records and personal health information collected, processed, or
maintained under this Act shall be protected in accordance with Republic Act No.
10173, otherwise known as the "Data Privacy Act of 2012," its implementing rules
and regulations, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.


Access to patient information shall be limited to authorized healthcare personnel and
government officials who require such information for legitimate healthcare
delivery, public health monitoring, or other lawful purposes.


The DOH shall ensure that appropriate safeguards, including cybersecurity
measures, access controls, data security protocols, and regular system assessments,
are implemented to protect patient confidentiality and prevent unauthorized access,
disclosure, alteration, or misuse of health information.


SEC. 15. Annual Report to Congress. - The Department of Health (DOH) shall,
not later than March 31 of each year, submit to the President of the Philippines, the
Senate, and the House of Representatives an annual report on the implementation of
this Act during the preceding calendar year.


The annual report shall include, but not be limited to, the following:




                                                                                  15
         a. The number and geographic distribution of Mobile Reproductive
            Health Clinics established and deployed;
         b. The number of communities, barangays, and beneficiaries served,
            disaggregated by sex, age group, and geographic location, where
            practicable and consistent with applicable laws;
         c. The types and volume of healthcare services provided;
         d. Maternal and reproductive health indicators relevant to the objectives
            of this Act, including service coverage and referral outcomes;
         e. Program accomplishments, implementation challenges, and corrective
            measures undertaken;
         f. Budget utilization, including expenditures and other sources of
            financial or technical assistance received for the Program; and
         g. Recommendations for legislative, policy, or administrative measures to
            further strengthen the implementation of this Act.


The Department of Health shall make the annual report publicly available through
its official website, subject to existing laws on data privacy, confidentiality, and
access to information.


SEC. 16. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the Department of
Health (DOH). Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.




                                                                                 16
The Department of Health shall include the funding requirements for the Mobile
Reproductive Health Clinic Program in its annual budget proposal, taking into
consideration the establishment, operation, maintenance, staffing, equipment,
medicines, training, monitoring, and evaluation of the Program.


Local government units are encouraged to allocate funds from their respective local
health budgets, Gender and Development (GAD) funds, and other lawful
appropriations to supplement the implementation of the Program within their
jurisdictions.


The Department of Health may likewise receive grants, donations, technical
assistance, or financial support from government-owned or -controlled corporations,
development partners, private sector entities, and other lawful sources, subject to
existing budgeting, auditing, procurement, and other applicable laws, rules, and
regulations.


SEC. 17. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health, in coordination with the
Department of the Interior and Local Government, Philippine Commission on
Women, and relevant agencies, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary
for the effective implementation of this Act.


SEC. 18. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.
