                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

Our Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs), our “Mga Bagong Bayani,” leave their families and work
far from home to provide a better future for their loved ones. Their remittances help families meet
daily needs, support education and healthcare, and contribute to the country’s economy. However,
the challenges they face often continue when they return home.

Many returning OFWs struggle to find decent employment, make use of the skills they gained
abroad, access capital to start a business, and adjust to family and community life after years of
separation. While the government already provides assistance through the Department of Migrant
Workers (DMW), Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA), National Reintegration
Center for OFWs (NRCO), and other agencies, there is a need for a more coordinated, accessible,
and sustainable approach to reintegration.

This measure seeks to establish a Comprehensive and Sustainable OFW Reintegration Program
that will provide returning OFWs with livelihood and business assistance, employment and skills
training, financial support, and social and psychological services. It will also strengthen the NRCO
and establish or enhance local OFW help desks to bring government assistance closer to returning
workers and their families.

The goal is simple when our OFWs come home, they should have meaningful opportunities to
rebuild their livelihoods, reunite with their families, and build a secure and dignified future in the
Philippines.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT    ESTABLISHING   A COMPREHENSIVE       AND SUSTAINABLE
REINTEGRATION   PROGRAM    FOR   OVERSEAS    FILIPINO WORKERS,
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Comprehensive Overseas Filipino
Workers (OFWs) Reintegration Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. —It is hereby declared the policy of the State to afford full
protection to labor, local and overseas, organized and unorganized, and promote full employment
and equality of employment opportunities for all. The State recognizes the significant contribution
of Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) to the national economy and commits to creating an
environment where migration becomes an option rather than a necessity. Towards this end, the
State shall establish a sustainable, holistic, and institutionalized framework for the economic,
social, and psychological reintegration of returning OFWs and their families into mainstream
Philippine society.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act, the following terms shall be defined
as follows:

   a. Overseas Filipino Worker (OFW) refers to a person who is to be engaged, is engaged, or
      has been engaged in a remunerated activity in a state of which he or she is not a citizen, or
      on board a vessel navigating the foreign seas.
   b. Reintegration refers to a mechanism or process aimed at facilitating the successful re-entry
      of returning OFWs into Philippine society through economic support, skill alignment,
      employment placement, and psychosocial wellness programs.
   c. Sustainable Enterprise refers to micro, small, or medium enterprises (MSMEs) established
      by or employing OFWs that are economically viable, socially responsible, and
      environmentally sound.
   d. NRCO refers to the National Reintegration Center for OFWs created under Republic Act
      No. 10801 and consolidated under Republic Act No. 11641 (Department of Migrant
      Workers Act).

SECTION 4. The Comprehensive OFW Reintegration Program Framework. — There is hereby
established the Comprehensive OFW Reintegration Program Framework (hereinafter referred to
as the "Program"), to be spearheaded by the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) through the
National Reintegration Center for OFWs (NRCO), in coordination with the Overseas Workers
Welfare Administration (OWWA), Technical Education and Skills Development Authority
(TESDA), Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), Department of Labor and Employment
(DOLE), and Local Government Units (LGUs).

The Program shall encompass three main structural components:

   a. Economic and Enterprise Development Component
   b. Employment Re-entry and Upskilling Component
   c. Psychosocial and Family Wellness Component

SECTION 5. Strengthening the National Reintegration Center for OFWs (NRCO). — The NRCO
shall serve as the lead executive agency for the implementation of this Act. The organization and
staffing structure of the NRCO shall be expanded to establish Regional OFW Reintegration Desks
across all administrative regions of the Philippines.

SECTION 6. One-Stop Reintegration Help Desks in LGUs. — All local government units (cities
and municipalities) shall establish a dedicated OFW Help Desk under their respective PESO or
Community Development Offices. The LGUs shall allocate localized resources to assist returning
OFWs in business permitting, local employment matching, and access to provincial social services.

SECTION 7. Inter-Agency Coordinating Council on OFW Reintegration. — An Inter-Agency
Coordinating Council is hereby created to oversee, monitor, and evaluate the execution of this Act.
The Council shall be composed of:

   a. Secretary of the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) – Chairperson
   b. Administrator of the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA) – Co-
      Chairperson
   c. Secretary of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) – Member
   d. Secretary of the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) – Member
   e. Director-General of the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA)
      – Member
   f. Secretary of the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) – Member
   g. Two (2) representatives from accredited non-governmental organizations (NGOs)
      representing OFWs – Members

SECTION 8. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act
shall be charged against the current appropriations of the Department of Migrant Workers and the
Overseas Workers Welfare Administration. Thereafter, such sums as may be needed for its
continued implementation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

SECTION 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Migrant Workers, in consultation with OWWA, DTI,
DOLE, TESDA, DILG, and relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 10. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force
and effect.

SECTION 11. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, rules, and regulations, or parts
thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

SECTION 12. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
