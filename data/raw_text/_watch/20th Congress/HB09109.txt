                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Bill seeks to segregate parcels of land containing an approximate area of
Two Hundred Seventy-One Thousand Four Hundred (271,400 sq.m.) square
meters, located at Pidawan, Ongasan, Barangay Loakan Proper, Baguio City,
from Military Reservation under Proclamation No. 2405, subject to private
rights and to convert them into alienable and disposable public land and award
it directly to actual occupants who are the Heirs of Guesu Dacdacan also known
as Guesec Chacchacan, to promote social justice and support the government's
housing and urban development goals.

The Heirs of Chacchacan had been occupying the said area even before it was
proclaimed as military reservation in 1985. In fact, on July 28, 1961, an
approved survey plan PSU-186219 in the name of Guesu Dacdacan was issued
by the Department of Agriculture and Natural Resources, now, Department of
Environment and Natural Resources (DENR). The approved survey plan was
resurveyed which was logged as PSU-CAR-186219 Amd. Furthermore, as early
as May 1923, the parcels of land were declared under Declaration of Real
Property No. 109 in the name of Guesec Dacdacan which was later changed to
Tax Declaration No. 379 under the Heirs of Guesec Dacdacan.

When DENR Special Order No. 31, series of 1990 was promulgated to identify
and delineate the ancestral lands and domains of indigenous cultural
communities within the City of Baguio and the rest of the Cordilleras, the Heirs
of Chacchacan applied under this provision for the recognition of their ancestral
land claim. An investigation report dated October 15, 1991, was issued by the
Office of the Special Task Force on Ancestral Land (STFAL) of DENR-CAR,
stating that the ancestors of the Chacchacan are long-time residents of Baguio
City and they belong to Ibaloi Tribe. Likewise evidences such as old stonewalls,
trees and improvements were found at said place which shows that their
ancestors occupied the said land.

On January 30, 1992, a resolution was issued by the Office of the Special Task
Force on Ancestral Land (STFAL) of DENR-CAR, declaring that their ancestors
have clearly established that they have valid ancestral land claims. The STFAL
finds that the recognition of ancestral land claim filed by the Heirs of Guesec
Chacchacan to be ancestral land under the concept and guidelines contained in
the DENR Special Project.

With the creation of the Indigenous Peoples Rights Act of 1997 or Republic Act
8371, the application of the Heirs of Guesec Chacchacan was turned over to the
National Commission on Indigenous People (NCIP). Up to the present, the
application of the Heirs of Chacchacan is still pending before the office of the
National Commission on Indigenous People (NCIP).

In view thereof, the immediate passage of this Bill is earnestly sought.

Congress Assembled:

the State to recognized ownership of lands, waters, and natural resources

Certificates of Ancestral Domain Titles (CADT) or Certificates of Ancestral
Land Titles (CALT).

      SECTION 3. Forest and Watershed reserves within the said area shall
absolutely remain as reservation and the DENR and the City of Baguio shall
immediately fence the same.

      SECTION 4. Validation of the Survey. — The Department of
Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) shall validate the survey already
conducted identified as PSU-1862 which was already approved then later on
resurveyed and logged as PSU-CAR-186219 Amd. Covering the area of Two
Hundred Seventy-One Thousand Four Hundred (271,400 sq.m.) square meters.
      SECTION 5. Disposition. — The DENR shall dispose of and award the
segregated land to bona fide occupants or the Heirs of Guesu/Guesec
Dacdacan/Chacchacan pursuant to Republic Act No. 730 and/or Republic Act
No. 10023.

      SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

       SECTION 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
