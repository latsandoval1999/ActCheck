                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Access to healthcare remains a challenge for many Filipinos. While
government programs continue to expand healthcare coverage, many patients
still rely on the generosity and dedication of physicians and dentists who
voluntarily provide their services free of charge.

       Through medical missions, charity clinics, emergency response efforts,
and other pro bono work, these healthcare professionals help bring much-needed
care to those who would otherwise go untreated. Their contributions not only
improve health outcomes but also strengthen the delivery of healthcare services
in communities with limited access to medical and dental care.

       This bill seeks to encourage and recognize such service by granting
reasonable and accountable tax incentives to physicians and dentists who render
qualified pro bono services to indigent and underserved Filipinos. By supporting
healthcare professionals who volunteer their time and expertise, the State can
help expand access to essential healthcare while advancing its constitutional duty
to protect and promote the right to health of the people.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT GRANTING TAX INCENTIVES TO PHYSICIANS AND DENTISTS
WHO RENDER QUALIFIED PRO BONO SERVICES TO INDIGENT AND
UNDERSERVED FILIPINOS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Pro Bono Healthcare
Incentives Act."

        Section 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State,
consistent with Article II, Section 15 of the 1987 Constitution, to protect and promote
the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them. Likewise,
pursuant to Article XIII, Section 11 of the Constitution, the State shall adopt an
integrated and comprehensive approach to health development and endeavor to make
essential goods, health, and other social services available to all the people at affordable
cost.

To this end, the State shall encourage physicians and dentists to voluntarily render
qualified pro bono medical and dental services to indigent, marginalized, and
underserved Filipinos by granting reasonable tax incentives.

       Section 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:

   (a) Physician refers to a duly licensed physician, in good standing, authorized to
       practice medicine in the Philippines;
   (b) Dentist refers to a duly licensed dentist, in good standing, authorized to practice
       dentistry in the Philippines;
   (c) Qualified Pro Bono Service refers to medical or dental services rendered
       voluntarily and without compensation to eligible beneficiaries under this Act;
   (d) Eligible Beneficiaries refer to:
           (1) Indigent patients certified by the Department of Social Welfare and
               Development (DSWD), the Local Social Welfare and Development
               Office (LSWDO), or the barangay;
           (2) Beneficiaries of Department of Health (DOH)-accredited medical or
               dental missions; and

        Section 4. Grant of Tax Credit. — A qualified physician or dentist who renders
at least fifty (50) hours of Qualified Pro Bono Service during a taxable year shall be
entitled to a tax credit equivalent to Five Hundred Pesos (₱500.00) for every verified
hour of service rendered.

The total tax credit claimable under this Act shall not exceed One Hundred Fifty
Thousand Pesos (₱150,000.00) per taxable year.

The tax credit shall be applied only against the income tax liability of the qualified
physician or dentist for the taxable year in which the qualified services were rendered.
Any excess tax credit shall neither be refundable nor carried over to succeeding taxable
years.

        Section 5. Conditions for Availment. — To qualify for the tax credit under this
Act, the following conditions shall be complied with:

   (a) The Qualified Pro Bono Service shall be rendered through any of the following:

         (1) Government hospitals;
         (2) Rural health units or barangay health stations;
         (3) Public schools;
         (4) Charitable institutions accredited by the Department of Health; or
         (5) Medical or dental missions accredited by the Department of Health;

   (b) No professional fee, honorarium, allowance, reimbursement, PhilHealth
       payment, grant, or any other form of compensation shall have been received by
       the physician or dentist for the same services;
   (c) The physician or dentist shall maintain complete records of the services
       rendered, including the dates, location, duration of service, and number of
       patients served; and
   (d) The services rendered shall be certified by the head of the government facility,
       accredited institution, or accredited mission where the services were performed.

       Section 6. Certification and Verification. — The institution or facility where
the Qualified Pro Bono Service was rendered shall issue a certification containing:

    (a) The name and Professional Regulation Commission license number of the
    physician or dentist;

    (b) The dates and duration of the services rendered;

    (c) The number of beneficiaries served;

    (d) A statement that the services were rendered without compensation; and

    (e) Such other information as may be required under the implementing rules and
    regulations.
The Department of Health shall establish and maintain an electronic registry of
accredited institutions and certified Qualified Pro Bono Services for monitoring and
verification purposes.

        Section 7. Annual Reporting. — Every institution authorized to issue
certifications under this Act shall submit an annual report to the Department of Health
and the Bureau of Internal Revenue containing the names of participating physicians
and dentists, the number of verified service hours rendered, and such other information
as may be necessary to facilitate verification.

         Section 8. Fraudulent Claims and Penalties. — Any physician, dentist,
institution, or other person who knowingly submits false certifications, falsifies records,
or fraudulently claims tax incentives under this Act shall:

(a) Refund the full amount of tax credits improperly claimed, together with applicable
interest, surcharges, and penalties under the National Internal Revenue Code, as
amended;

(b) Be subject to appropriate administrative sanctions under applicable laws, including
proceedings before the Professional Regulation Commission, where warranted; and

(c) Upon conviction, suffer a fine of not less than Fifty Thousand Pesos (₱50,000.00)
but not more than Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱500,000.00), without prejudice to
prosecution under other applicable laws.

       Section 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Bureau of Internal Revenue, the Department of
Health, the Professional Regulation Commission, and the Professional Regulatory
Boards of Medicine and Dentistry shall jointly promulgate the rules and regulations
necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

        Section 10. Appropriations. — The amounts necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the
Department of Health. Thereafter, such amounts as may be necessary shall be included
in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       Section 11. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not
affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

       Section 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, and
administrative orders or parts thereof inconsistent with any provision of this Act
are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      Section 13. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of
general circulation.
