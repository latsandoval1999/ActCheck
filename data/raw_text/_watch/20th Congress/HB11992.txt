                           AN ACT
  EXEMPTING COMMON AREAS IN RESIDENTIAL CONDOMINIUM AND

  AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE SECTION 234 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO.
7160, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991"

                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


      Article XIII, Section 9 of the 1987 Constitution mandates the State to make
decent and affordable housing accessible to underprivileged and homeless citizens.
However, for many Filipino families, particularly low- and middle-income earners,
homeownership remains a financial challenge.

       Beyond the cost of acquiring a home, homeowners must also shoulder recurring
expenses such as utilities, maintenance, and association dues, especially in
condominiums and subdivisions. Among these is the real property tax (RPT) imposed
on common areas such as hallways, parks, playgrounds, and shared facilities.
Although these spaces are intended for the common use of residents and do not
necessarily generate income, the taxes imposed on them may be passed on to
homeowners through association dues. These additional costs may also discourage
developers from providing open spaces and community amenities that contribute to
more livable and sustainable residential communities.

       This bill seeks to amend Section 234 of Republic Act No. 7160, or the Local
Government Code of 1991, to exempt duly designated common areas in residential
subdivisions and condominiums from real property tax, provided that these are not
used for commercial or income-generating purposes. By reducing recurring housing
expenses, this measure seeks to ease the financial burden on homeowners while
encouraging the development of greener, safer, and more sustainable residential
communities.
