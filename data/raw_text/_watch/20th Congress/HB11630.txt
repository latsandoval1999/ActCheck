                               EXPLANATORY NOTE


The barangay is the basic political unit in the Philippines. It is the primary
planning and implementing unit of government policies, plans, programs,
andprojects in the community. As the most immediate and accessible political
unit, it is often the very first point of contact for citizens seeking public services,
redress, or clarity about government programs.

Despite this critical role, millions of Filipinos still face significant friction when
processing government transactions. Residents often endure the financial and
temporal burden of traveling to municipal or city halls—or even regional national
agency offices—to secure basic documents, process registrations, or seek
employment assistance. While Republic Act No. 11032, otherwise known as the
"Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018,"
successfully mandated the creation of one-stop business facilitation services at
the city and municipal levels, the grassroots barangay level has largely been left
to rely on fragmented, manual, and localized administrative systems.

This bill seeks to bridge that gap by establishing a standard Barangay One-Stop
Service Center (BOSSC) in every barangay nationwide.

The BOSSC will serve as a unified, digitized frontline desk within the barangay
hall. It will consolidate the issuance of all barangay-level documents (clearances,
certificates of residency and indigency) and act as a digital conduit for national
and municipal services. Furthermore, it will streamline the implementation of
special mandates, such as the free issuance of pre-employment records under
the First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act. By equipping these centers with
standardized technology in partnership with the Department of the Interior and
Local Government (DILG) and the Department of Information and
Communications Technology (DICT), we empower barangays to serve not just as
political boundaries, but as highly efficient service hubs.
To ensure that no Filipino is left behind by bureaucratic red tape, and to bring
the government directly to the doorsteps of the people, the immediate passage of
this bill is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
   ESTABLISHING A STANDARD ONE-STOP SERVICE CENTER IN EVERY
  BARANGAY IN THE PHILIPPINES, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR,
                    AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Barangay One-Stop
Service Center (BOSSC) Act."
SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote a just and
dynamic social order that ensures the prosperity and independence of the nation.
In line with this, the State shall ensure the efficient, transparent, and expedited
delivery of essential public services at the grassroots level. The State recognizes
the barangay as the fundamental unit of governance and shall empower it with
the necessary infrastructure and capacity to serve as the primary service delivery
hub for its constituents.

SEC. 3. Establishment of the Barangay One-Stop Service Center. — There is
hereby established a standard Barangay One-Stop Service Center, hereinafter
referred to as the "BOSSC," in every barangay in the Philippines. The BOSSC shall
be physically located within the barangay hall or any accessible adjacent facility
owned by the barangay.

SEC. 4. Functions and Services of the BOSSC. — The BOSSC shall serve as the
central hub for the processing and issuance of local documents and the
facilitation of select municipal and national government services. Its primary
functions shall include, but are not limited to:
   1. Consolidated Issuance of Barangay Documents: Expedited processing and
      release of the Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, Certificate of
      Indigency, and Barangay Business Clearance.

   2. First-Time Jobseeker Assistance: Facilitation of free barangay certifications
      and clearances for first-time job applicants pursuant to Republic Act No.
      11261.

   3. National Agency E-Services Portal: Provision of a designated digital kiosk
      or workstation with internet access where residents can process online
      applications for the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), National Bureau
      of Investigation (NBI), PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and the Social Security System
      (SSS).

   4. Grievance and Public Information Desk: Serving as the official receiving
      desk for barangay blotter reports (Katarungang Pambarangay) and the
      dissemination of public information during calamities or health
      emergencies.

SEC. 5. Administration and Personnel. — The BOSSC shall be managed by the
Barangay Secretary, who shall act as the BOSSC Administrator. The Barangay
Treasurer shall be responsible for the collection of all authorized local fees and
charges.

To ensure the efficient operation of the center, the Punong Barangay may
designate a BOSSC Information Technology (IT) Officer or equivalent
administrative staff, subject to the availability of local funds and civil service
rules.

SEC. 6. Digitization and Systems Integration. — The Department of
Information and Communications Technology (DICT), in coordination with the
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), is hereby mandated to
develop a standard, secure, and cloud-based Barangay Management Information
System (BaMIS).

   1. The system shall be provided to all BOSSCs free of charge and shall include
      modules for document processing, resident registries, and secure payment
      gateways.
   2. The DICT shall ensure that the system employs end-to-end data encryption
      and complies strictly with Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act
      of 2012.

SEC. 7. Capacity Building. — The DILG, through the Local Government
Academy (LGA), and the DICT shall conduct mandatory and continuous training
programs for barangay officials and BOSSC personnel on system operations,
customer service, and data privacy.
SEC. 8. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act, including the development of the standard BaMIS software by the
DICT and the initial procurement of hardware for fourth, fifth, and sixth-class
municipalities, shall be charged against the current year’s appropriations of the
DILG and the DICT.

Thereafter, the funds necessary for the continuous operation, maintenance, and
supply of the BOSSCs shall be charged to the respective National Tax Allotment
(NTA) and locally generated revenues of the barangays.

SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the DILG and the Secretary of the
DICT, in consultation with the Liga ng mga Barangay sa Pilipinas and other
relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for
the effective implementation of this Act.

SEC. 10. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, presidential decrees,
proclamations, rules, regulations, issuances, and enactments or parts thereof
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 12. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.

Approved
