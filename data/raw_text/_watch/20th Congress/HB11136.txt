                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish an integrated school in Ciudad Kaunlaran, Barangay
Molino 2, City of Bacoor, Province of Cavite to be known as Ciudad Kaunlaran
Integrated School.

       Ciudad Kaunlaran is an in-city vertical socialized housing project of the National
Housing Authority (NHA) located in Molino 2, City of Bacoor, Cavite built on 52,718
square meters of land. It has a total of 1,860 units allocated for informal settler families
(ISFs) residing along the coastal barangays of Bacoor Bay, subject of Manila Bay
continuing mandamus of the Supreme Court.

       The CKIS will be responsible for educating the school-aged children from the
1,860 families in Ciudad Kaunlaran, which is in compliance to Section 21 of Republic
Act No. 7279 or the Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992 which required the
provision of basic services. As an integrated school, CKIS will offer complete basic
education from Kindergarten to Grade 12 with a unified instructional program as
defined under DepEd Order No. 40, series of 2014 ad Republic Act No. 10533 or the
K to 12 Law.

      Considering that there are already residents in Ciudad Kaunlaran, immediate
approval of this bill is hereby sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


       SECTION 1. There is hereby established, under the supervision of the
Department of Education, an integrated school in Ciudad Kaunlaran, Barangay Molino
2, City of Bacoor, Province of Cavite to be known as the Ciudad Kaunlaran Integrated
School.


       SEC. 2. The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of the Ciudad Kaunlaran Integrated
School, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.


       SEC. 3. Within ninety (90) days from the approval of this Act, the Secretary of
Education shall formulate the rules and regulations implementing the provisions of this
Act. Such implementing rules and regulations shall provide for the school’s
organization, administration, operations and management, in accordance with
Republic Act No. 9155, or the “Governance of Basic Education Act of 2001”, Republic
Act No. 10533, or the “Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013”, and other basic
education-related laws.
