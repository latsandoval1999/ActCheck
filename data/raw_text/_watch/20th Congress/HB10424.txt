                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

Barangay officials and community workers serve as the first line of public service, delivering
essential government programs and responding directly to the needs of their constituents. As
frontliners in governance, they perform vital functions in maintaining peace and order, promoting
public health, resolving community disputes, implementing social services, and ensuring the
effective delivery of government programs at the grassroots level. Despite their invaluable
contributions, many of them retire after years of dedicated service without receiving any form of
retirement benefit or financial security.

While the Local Government Code of 1991 provides honoraria, allowances, and other benefits for
barangay officials, it does not grant retirement pay to barangay officials, barangay tanods,
members of the Lupong Tagapamayapa, barangay health workers, and barangay day care
workers. As a result, many individuals who have devoted years of service to their communities
are left without adequate recognition and financial assistance upon retirement.

Therefore, this measure seeks to amend Section 393 of Republic Act No. 7160 by granting
qualified barangay officials and community workers a lump sum retirement pay equivalent to one
(1) year of honorarium, subject to the conditions provided by law. It likewise establishes a
Barangay Retirement Fund to ensure the sustainable funding and efficient administration of
retirement benefits for eligible beneficiaries.

By providing retirement benefits to barangay officials and community workers, this bill recognizes
their invaluable service, promotes their financial security after retirement, and strengthens morale
among those who dedicate themselves to public service at the grassroots level.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

SEC. 2. Barangay Retirement Fund Created. – There is hereby created a Barangay
Retirement Fund (“BRF”) which shall be used to fund the retirement of barangay officials
mentioned in this Act. The amount equivalent to one percent (15) of the share of the
national government under Section 284 of RA No. 7160, as amended, shall be used for
this purpose.

The Department of the Interior and Local Government (“DILG”) shall administer and
ensure the appropriate disbursement of the BRF.

SEC. 3. Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Budget and Management, in
coordination with the DILG, shall issue the necessary rules and regulations to implement
this Act.

SEC. 4. Repealing Clause. – Section 393 of RA 7160, other laws, executive orders,
issuances, and rules and regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby amended,
repealed, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 5. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
