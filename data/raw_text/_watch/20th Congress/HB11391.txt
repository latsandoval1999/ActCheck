AN ACT GRANTING A STUDENT DISCOUNT ON THE PURCHASE OF
EDUCATIONAL ELECTRONIC DEVICES, PRESCRIBING THE GUIDELINES FOR ITS
IMPLEMENTATION, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Student Educational Device
Discount Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote quality, accessible, and inclusive education by ensuring that students have
equitable access to digital learning tools and technologies. The State recognizes that
electronic devices such as laptops, desktop computers, tablets, and similar educational
technologies have become essential learning tools in both traditional and digital learning
environments. It is therefore imperative to lessen the financial burden on Filipino families
by granting qualified students discounts on the purchase of educational electronic
devices.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

(a) Student refers to any Filipino citizen currently enrolled in a recognized public or
private elementary, secondary, technical-vocational, undergraduate, graduate, or post-
graduate educational institution duly recognized by the Department of Education
(DepEd), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), or the Technical Education and
Skills Development Authority (TESDA).

(b) Educational Electronic Devices refer to laptops, desktop computers, tablets, e-book
readers, and other electronic devices prescribed or recognized by educational institutions
as necessary for academic instruction, research, projects, online learning, or educational
activities.

(c) Accredited Retailer refers to any manufacturer, distributor, or retail establishment
duly registered with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) or other appropriate
government agency authorized to sell electronic devices covered by this Act.
SECTION 4. Student Discount. - Qualified students shall be entitled to a twenty percent
(20%) discount on the purchase of one (1) educational electronic device every three (3)
years from accredited retailers.

The discount shall apply to the retail selling price exclusive of any promotional discounts
already granted by the retailer. The student shall receive whichever discount is higher.

SECTION 5. Covered Devices. - The discount shall apply to:

   1. Laptop computers;
   2. Desktop computers;
   3. Tablet computers;
   4. Hybrid laptop-tablet devices;
   5. Educational e-book readers; and
   6. Other electronic devices as may be determined by the DTI, DepEd, CHED, and
      TESDA to be primarily used for educational purposes.

Accessories, gaming consoles, entertainment devices, mobile phones primarily intended
for communication, smartwatches, and other non-essential electronic gadgets shall not
be covered unless expressly determined by the implementing agencies to be necessary
educational tools.

SECTION 6. Eligibility Requirements. - To avail of the discount, the student shall
present:

(a) A valid school identification card or certificate of enrollment;

(b) A valid government-issued identification card or other proof of identity; and

(c) Such additional documents as may be prescribed under the Implementing Rules and
Regulations (IRR).

For minors, the purchase may be made by a parent or legal guardian upon presentation
of the student's documentary requirements.

SECTION 7. Tax Incentives. - Retailers granting the discount under this Act may claim
the amount of the discount as a deductible expense for income tax purposes, subject to
existing tax laws, rules, and regulations and verification by the Bureau of Internal
Revenue (BIR).

SECTION 8. Prevention of Abuse. - Any person who fraudulently avails of the student
discount by using falsified documents or misrepresenting eligibility shall be liable under
existing laws and shall be permanently disqualified from availing of the benefits provided
under this Act.
Retailers found to have knowingly participated in fraudulent transactions shall likewise be
subject to administrative and criminal sanctions under applicable laws.

SECTION 9. Information Campaign. - The Department of Education, Commission on
Higher Education, Technical Education and Skills Development Authority, Department of
Trade and Industry, and Department of Information and Communications Technology
shall jointly conduct nationwide information campaigns to inform students, schools,
retailers, and the general public of the benefits and procedures under this Act.

SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Trade and Industry, in coordination with the
Department of Education, Commission on Higher Education, Technical Education and
Skills Development Authority, Department of Information and Communications
Technology, Department of Finance, and Bureau of Internal Revenue, shall promulgate
the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 11. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the concerned agencies.
Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

SECTION 12. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.

SECTION 13. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, rules, regulations, or parts
thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SECTION 14. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
