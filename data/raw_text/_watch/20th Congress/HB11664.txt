                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Municipality of Santa Maria in the Province of Bulacan is a rapidly growing
urban and commercial hub in Central Luzon. Its strategic location serves as a
primary gateway connecting neighboring municipalities to the North Luzon
Expressway (NLEX) and major economic centers. Due to continuous
industrialization and population growth, local traffic density has increased
significantly.

The Santa Maria - Pandi Bypass Road, spanning approximately three (3)
kilometers within the territorial jurisdiction of Santa Maria, was constructed to
alleviate heavy traffic congestion along the town center and facilitate smoother
transit between Santa Maria and the Municipality of Pandi.

Despite its vital function as an arterial bypass, the maintenance, repair, and
operational upkeep of the Santa Maria - Pandi Bypass Road currently burden the
local government unit (LGU) funds. Converting this local road into a national road
will transfer its supervision and maintenance to the Department of Public Works
and Highways (DPWH). This shift ensures continuous funding for infrastructure
maintenance, improvement, and safety upgrades under the National Expenditure
Program (NEP).

Furthermore, integrating this bypass road into the national road network will
enhance regional connectivity, lower transportation costs for agricultural and
commercial goods, boost local economic activity, and support public safety and
disaster response mobility.

In view of the foregoing considerations, the immediate passage of this bill is
earnestly sought.

 AN ACT CONVERTING THE SANTA MARIA - PANDI BYPASS ROAD IN THE
   MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A
      NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The Santa Maria - Pandi Bypass Road, approximately three (3)
kilometers in length, located in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of
Bulacan, is hereby converted into a national road under the jurisdiction and
supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the
Department’s program the improvement, repair, maintenance, and
operationalization of the Santa Maria - Pandi Bypass Road, the funding of which
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).

SEC. 3. The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary
rules, regulations, and directives to implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved
