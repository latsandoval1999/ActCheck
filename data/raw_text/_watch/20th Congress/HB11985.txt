                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Technical-vocational education plays a vital role in preparing students for
employment, entrepreneurship, and the changing demands of the labor market.
However, strengthening the alignment of Senior High School’s Technical-
Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) Curriculum with industry requirements is essential
to ensure that learners acquire relevant, practical, and marketable skills.

        This bill seeks to strengthen the TVL Curriculum by establishing a TVL
Council to coordinate the efforts of the Department of Education, the Technical
Education and Skills Development Authority, other concerned government
agencies, and the private sector. It aims to promote industry-responsive training,
facilitate access to national certification, strengthen work immersion
partnerships, and improve the employability of TVL graduates.

      Through this measure, the State seeks to provide learners with greater
opportunities for decent employment, fair compensation, and economic
productivity without necessarily requiring a college degree.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                                  AN ACT
               STRENGTHENING THE TECHNICAL, VOCATIONAL AND
              LIVELIHOOD CURRICULUM FOR SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL
              AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “TVL Curriculum
Act.”

       SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall protect and promote
the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels and take appropriate
steps to make such education accessible to all. The right to education shall be
promoted with the end goal of producing graduates who are prepared to become
civically active and economically productive members of society.

      The State shall also ensure that education aligns with the goals of national
development and industrialization. To this end, the State shall ensure access to
affordable and competitive training that produces skilled graduates capable of
meeting the requirements of various industries and accessing decent
employment without necessarily obtaining a college degree.

       SECTION 3. Objectives. - Graduates of the Technical-Vocational-
Livelihood (TVL) Curriculum shall be awarded senior high school diplomas upon
satisfying the applicable graduation requirements and shall be provided
opportunities to obtain pertinent National Certificates (NCs) or Certificates of
Competency (COCs), subject to TESDA assessment and certification
requirements. The TVL Curriculum shall equip learners with the competencies
and skills necessary for employment and enhance industry confidence in the
qualifications of its graduates, thereby promoting their employability and access
to decent work and fair compensation.
      SECTION 4. TVL Council. - The TVL Council, herein referred to as the
“Council,” is hereby created and shall be attached to the Department of
Education (DepEd). It shall be composed of seven (7) members, namely: (1) the
Secretary of the DepEd, as Chairperson; (2) the Director-General of the Technical
Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), as Vice-Chairperson; (3)
the Secretary of the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE); (4) the
Secretary of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI); (5) the Secretary of the
Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT); (6) the
President of the Philippine Chamber of Commerce and Industry (PCCI); and (7)
the President of the Employers Confederation of the Philippines (ECOP).

       Members of the Council shall serve without compensation but shall be
entitled to reimbursement for expenses related to their participation in the
official activities of the Council, in accordance with pertinent laws, rules and
regulations.

      Members may designate representatives who shall be of rank no lower
than that of an Undersecretary. The PCCI and ECOP may designate
representatives with appropriate executive or managerial authority.

      The Council shall meet at least once every quarter. The Chairperson or a
majority of the Council's members may call a special meeting whenever
necessary.

      Meetings may be conducted virtually when meeting in person is impossible
or impractical, provided that all Council members are able to participate and
communicate adequately during the meeting.

      SECTION 5. Duties and Functions of the Council. - The Council shall
have the following duties and functions:

   (a) Periodically consult with various industries to ensure that the programs,
       curriculum, courses, and other activities of the TVL Curriculum respond
       to their skills and human resource requirements, thereby strengthening
       the eligibility and employability of graduates;

   (b) Ensure that the curriculum incorporates the competencies and skills
       required for the relevant TESDA National Certificates (NCs) or Certificates
       of Competency (COCs), and that learners are adequately prepared for the
       corresponding assessments, subject to applicable TESDA standards and
       requirements;

   (c) Ensure that the TVL Curriculum is aligned with the Philippine
       Qualifications Framework (PQF), including its qualification levels and
       descriptors covering knowledge, skills and values; application; and degree
       of independence;
   (d) Encourage various industries to employ TVL graduates by raising
       awareness of their qualifications and employment eligibility through
       information campaigns and programs such as job fairs;

   (e) Call upon local government units (LGUs), their respective local school
       boards, and other agencies, particularly those in localities where schools
       offering the TVL Curriculum are located, for assistance, coordination, and
       collaboration in aligning and harmonizing the plans, resources, and
       efforts of schools and the pertinent LGUs; and

   (f) Undertake other duties and functions that may be necessary or incidental
       to the effective implementation of this Act.

       SECTION 6. Roles of Government Agencies. - To achieve the objectives
of this Act, the following government agencies shall carry out their respective
roles:

      (a) DepEd. - The DepEd shall formulate and implement the academic
          curriculum to ensure that learners under the TVL Curriculum possess
          the knowledge and competencies required to function effectively in the
          workplace and remain eligible for higher education, should they choose
          that pathway. The DepEd shall appoint and designate public school
          teachers to implement the academic curriculum and, in coordination
          with TESDA, ensure the engagement of qualified instructors to deliver
          technical-vocational training in accordance with applicable TESDA
          competency standards.

      (b) TESDA. - TESDA shall formulate and implement the technical-
          vocational training standards, competency requirements, and related
          programs necessary to equip learners with the skills and abilities
          required for employment and to qualify for the relevant National
          Certificates (NCs) or Certificates of Competency (COCs). It shall
          prescribe the qualifications of instructors for technical-vocational
          training within its mandate and oversee the applicable assessment and
          certification processes.

      (c) DOLE. - The DOLE shall, in consultation with the DepEd and TESDA,
          formulate guidelines for Work Immersion to protect learners' rights,
          welfare, and safety during immersion, without prejudice to applicable
          labor laws and standards governing graduates who subsequently enter
          into employment. The DOLE shall apprise the Council of the skills
          needed by various industries to help prevent job and skills mismatch.

      (d) DTI. - The DTI shall promote the employment and entrepreneurship
          opportunities of TVL graduates by facilitating industry partnerships
          and linking enterprises with schools offering the TVL Curriculum for
         Work Immersion and potential employment                opportunities,   in
         coordination with the DepEd, TESDA, and DOLE.

      (e) Other Government Agencies. - Other government agencies are hereby
          authorized, upon the request of the DepEd or TESDA and subject to
          applicable laws, rules and regulations, to lend or transfer such
          apparatus, supplies, and other properties as may be needed by schools
          offering the TVL Curriculum, and to detail employees for duty therein
          when, in the judgment of the head of the bureau or office, they can be
          spared without serious detriment to the public service.

       Employees so detailed shall perform such duties as may be required under
such detail, and the time employed shall be counted as part of their regular
official hours, subject to applicable civil service, budgeting, accounting, and
auditing rules and regulations.

       SECTION 7. Partnerships with the Private Sector. - The PCCI and ECOP
shall inform the Council of the skills and expertise needed by their respective
members. They shall further encourage their members to participate in Work
Immersion as partner institutions willing and able to provide expertise, facilities,
and other resources to learners.

       Schools and participating partner institutions shall establish appropriate
partnership arrangements to ensure the proper supervision, safety, and welfare
of learners during Work Immersion, in accordance with applicable laws, rules
and regulations.

       SECTION 8. Tax Treatment of Donations. - All grants, bequests,
endowments, donations, and contributions made to the Council and actually,
directly, and exclusively used for the effective implementation of this Act shall
be subject to applicable laws and regulations governing donor's tax exemptions
and the deductibility of donations from the gross income of donors, including the
requirements of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended. The
payment of national taxes, including customs duties, by the Council shall be
governed by applicable tax laws and regulations.

      SECTION 9. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be included in the current year's appropriations
of the DepEd, to the extent available, while the funding requirements for its
continued implementation shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act, subject to the usual budgeting, accounting, and auditing
laws, rules and regulations.
      SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the DepEd shall, in
consultation with the Director-General of TESDA, the members of the Council,
and other relevant stakeholders, formulate the rules and regulations necessary
to implement the provisions of this Act.

       SECTION 11. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not so declared shall remain in
force and effect.

      SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof, contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 13. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
