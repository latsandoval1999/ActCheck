                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Land Transportation Ofice (LTO) District Office in Lucena City serves as
the primary transportation regulatory and enforcement hub for Lucena City and the
entire Second District of Quezon Province. However, operating under a restrictive
Class "C" designation has created severe operational bottlenecks, resulting in long
client waiting times due to an insufficient personnel complement, limited physical
 space, and inadequate IT workstations.
        LTO Lucena's actual workload and financial performance have far outgrown its
current Class "C" classification. Under LTO Memorandum Circular No. 2020-2200,
office reclassifications are evaluated based on annual transaction volume weighted at
seventy percent and total revenue collection weighted at thirty percent. From CY 2023
to CY 2025, LTO Lucena handled an average of 114,794.67 total transactions
annually, surpassing the 111,001 national threshold required for a maximum
transaction score. Over the same three-year period, the office generated an average
 annual revenue ofP160,467,548.06, wellabove the P67.77 Million benchmark needed
for a maximum revenue score.

        Official evaluation data from LTO Region IV-A confirms that LTO Lucena fully
qualifies for classification as a Class "A" District Office. Upgrading its status wil
formally expand its permanent staffing, improve its facility and IT capacity, shorten
turnaround times, andstrengthen road safety and enforcement across the province.

          Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

          SECTION 1. The district office of the Land Transportation Office in the City of
Lucena, Province of Quezon, is hereby upgraded from Class "C" to Class "A" district
office.


          SECTION 2. The Department of Transportation (DOTr) shall immediately
include in the Department's program the upgrading of the LTOdistrict office in the
City of Lucena, Province of Quezon, the initial funding of which shall be charged
against the current year's appropriation of the same and existing LTO district office.
Thereafter, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.

          SECTION 3.This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.
