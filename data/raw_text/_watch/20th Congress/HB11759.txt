                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Technical-Vocational Education and Training (TVET) serves as an essential
backbone in transforming Filipino workforce into a competitive, future-ready, and
skills-based economy. The optimization of TVET will ensure that our workforce
remains highly responsive to the ever-changing and rapid evolution of industry
demands. As the Davao Region continues to accelerate as a premier economic and
industrial hub in Mindanao, elevating the existing TVET infrastructure is critical to
sustaining its rapid growth in manufacturing, agriculture, and eco-tourism

       To address this, this bill seeks to establish the Davao Central State Polytechnic
Institute by consolidating and rationalizing nine (9) TESDA Technology Institutions
(TTIs) across the Davao Region under the leadership of the TESDA Regional Training
Center - Korea-Philippines Vocational Training Center (RTC-KPVTC) Davao as its
main campus and cluster convenor.

       This proposed measure directly responds to Memorandum RO-XI No. 07-1583
s. 2026 issued by the TESDA Region XI Regional Office. It is aligned with the national
education reform framework, including the key recommendations of the Second
Congressional Commission on Education (EDCOM II), the National Technical
Education and Skills Development Plan (NTESDP) 2023-2028, the Philippine
Qualifications Framework (PQF) Act (R.A. 10968), and the Philippine Development
Plan.

      Currently, individual training centers face persistent operational constraints,
fragmented resource allocation, and restricted credit-transfer pathways into higher
education. Transforming these isolated centers into a unified, multi-campus State
Polytechnic System elevates TVET delivery from basic skill certifications (NC I to IV)
to advanced three-year diploma programs, applied research, technology incubation,
and Industry 4.0 technical competencies.
        Adopting a strategic "Hub-and-Spoke" model, RTC-KPVTC Davao will serve
as the central hub and Regional Technology Innovation Center (RTIC) for higher-level
technical education. Simultaneously, constituent campuses in Davao City (Mintal),
Davao del Sur, Davao del Norte, Davao de Oro, Davao Oriental, Malita, IGACOS, and
SIMTRAC will capitalize on specialized domain strengths—spanning industrial
automation, smart agriculture, eco-tourism, logistics, heavy equipment, and marine
technologies.

       Further, the bill grants necessary fiscal autonomy through a Special Trust Fund
and mandates ladderized diploma-to-degree pathways, ensuring long-term financial
sustainability and seamless academic progression for students.

      Ultimately, the Davao Central State Polytechnic Institute will serve as a vital
engine for industrial productivity, MSME competitiveness, and inclusive economic
growth throughout Mindanao.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE DAVAO CENTRAL STATE POLYTECHNIC
      INSTITUTE, INTEGRATING THE TESDA REGIONAL TRAINING
  CENTER - KOREA-PHILIPPINES VOCATIONAL TRAINING CENTER (RTC-
  KPVTC) DAVAO, PROVINCIAL TRAINING CENTER - DAVAO, PROVINCIAL
 TRAINING CENTER - DAVAO DEL SUR, PROVINCIAL TRAINING CENTER -
   MALITA, PROVINCIAL TRAINING CENTER - DAVAO DEL NORTE, CITY
TRAINING CENTER - IGACOS, PROVINCIAL TRAINING CENTER - DAVAO DE
     ORO, PROVINCIAL TRAINING CENTER - DAVAO ORIENTAL, AND
 PROVINCIAL TRAINING CENTER - SIMTRAC, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND
FUNCTIONS, PROVIDING FOR ITS ADMINISTRATION, AND APPROPRIATING
                        FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Davao Central State
Polytechnic Institute Act."

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote accessible, inclusive, equitable, industry-responsive, and globally competitive
technical education and skills development that contributes to national productivity,
innovation, and sustainable economic growth.

        To this end, the State shall strengthen higher-level technical education through
establishment of integrated Regional Polytechnic Systems to serve as a regional center of
excellence for advanced technical education, technology transfer, and workforce development.

        Section 3. Establishment of Polytechnic Institute. – There is hereby established
unified Inter-Province Technical and Vocational Education and Training System to be known
as the Davao Central State Polytechnic Institute, under the administration of the Technical
Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA).

       The Institute shall integrate the following TESDA Technology Institutions:

       1. TESDA Regional Training Center - Korea-Philippines Vocational Training Center
          (RTC-KPVTC) - Davao (Buhisan, Tibungco, Davao City);
       2. TESDA Provincial Training Center - Davao (Mintal, Davao City);
       3. TESDA Provincial Training Center - Davao del Sur;
       4. TESDA Provincial Training Center - Malita;
       5. TESDA Provincial Training Center - Davao del Norte;
       6. TESDA City Training Center - IGACOS;
       7. TESDA Provincial Training Center - Davao de Oro;
       8. TESDA Provincial Training Center - Davao Oriental; and
       9. TESDA Provincial Training Center - SIMTRAC.

The RTC-KPVTC Davao shall serve as the Main Campus and Cluster Convenor of the
Institute, while all other integrated training centers shall serve as constituent campuses and
specialized extension sites.

       Section 4. Mandate. – The Institute shall provide competency-based technical and
vocational education, diploma-level technical education, higher national certificate programs,
micro-credentials, applied research, technology development, innovation, entrepreneurship,
community extension services, and industry-responsive workforce development programs.

        It shall likewise:
        a. Develop highly skilled technicians, technologists, engineers, and middle-level
professionals;
        b. Establish seamless learning pathways from National Certificates to Diploma
qualifications and ladderized degree programs consistent with the Philippine Qualifications
Framework;
        c. Strengthen strategic partnerships with industries, local government units, higher
education institutions, and international development partners;
        d. Support technology transfer, commercialization, and productivity enhancement for
MSMEs and industrial sectors; and
        e. Serve as a Regional Technology Innovation Center (RTIC) consistent with TESDA's
innovation agenda.

        Section 5. Campus Specialization. – To maximize institutional capacities and address
local economic demands, the constituent campuses shall specialize in strategic priority sectors:

       a. RTC-KPVTC Davao (Main Campus): Advanced Manufacturing, Industrial
          Automation, Mechatronics, Automotive and Heavy Equipment Technology,
           Information and Communications Technology (ICT), Regional Technology
           Innovation Center (RTIC), and Diploma/Higher Technical Education Programs.

       b. Provincial Training Center - Davao: Construction Technologies, Civil Works,
          Urban TVET, and Enterprise-Based Training.

       c. Provincial Training Center - Davao del Sur: Agriculture, Agri-Processing, Food
          Technology, and Rural Enterprise Development.

       d. Provincial Training Center - Malita: Fisheries, Marine/Aquaculture Technologies,
          and Rural Community-Based TVET.

       e. Provincial Training Center - Davao Del Norte: Logistics, Smart Agriculture, Supply
          Chain Management, and Manufacturing.

       f. City Training Center - IGACOS: Sustainable Tourism, Hospitality Management,
          Eco-Trades, and Marine Transport Services.

       g. Provincial Training Center - Davao de Oro: Mining & Resource Management
          Technologies, Heavy Equipment Maintenance, Safety, and Eco-Tourism.

       h. Provincial Training Center - Davao Oriental: Agro-Forestry, Renewable
           Energy, Coconut and Agricultural Industrialization.

       i. Provincial Training Center – SIMTRAC: Heavy Equipment Operations,
          Construction Technology, Infrastructure Maintenance, and Transport Trades.

        The TESDA Director-General may establish additional campuses or adjust campus
specializations as industry demand arises, subject to existing rules and appropriations

       Section 6. Governance and Administration. – The Institute shall be headed by an
Executive Director with Salary Grade 27, in accordance with the Organization and Staffing
Standards (OSS) of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), under the supervision
of TESDA, and appointed by the TESDA Director-General.

        The Executive Director shall be assisted by Directors with Salary Grade 25, responsible
for the following:
        a. Academic Affairs;
        b. Administration and Finance;
        c. Research, Innovation, and Extension Services;
        d. Industry Partnerships and Resource Generation; an
        e. Planning and Quality Assurance.
        The Executive Director shall have the authority to sign contracts, memorandum of
agreements and understanding. The Executive Director shall have the authority of disposition
of the established Special Trust Fund (STF) such as, grants, donations and internally generated
funds such as tuition fees, charges and auxiliary services which is retained and used for
operations, instruction, research, and extension rather than being remitted to the National
Treasury, as authorized by Republic Act No. 8292.

       The Campus Director with Salary Grade 25 shall render full-time service and shall be
responsible for the administration and operation of the Institute's campus. The duties and
responsibilities of the Campus Director, including the appropriate compensation schedule, shall
follow the position qualification standard of TESDA for such office, in consultation with the
DBM.

        As necessary and practicable, and subject to approval by the TESDA Director General,
the Campus Director herein appointed shall enter into mutual agreements with local-based
counterpart agencies or instrumentalities and persons, both private and government, for such
assistance as may be necessary to effectively implement this Act.

        The academic and administrative staff of the Institute shall be organized following the
staffing pattern of the TESDA and SUCs for such type of school or whichever is applicable
pursuant to existing laws, rules and regulations.

        Section 7. Human Resources and Personnel. – All existing personnel of the
integrated TESDA Technology Institutions (TTIs) shall be absorbed into the Institute
without diminution of rank, salary, benefits, or security of tenure, subject to Civil Service
Commission (CSC), DBM, and TESDA regulations. TESDA shall undertake organizational
restructuring and plantilla rationalization to optimize operational deployment.

        Section 8. Assets, Properties, and Liabilities. – All lands, buildings, facilities,
laboratories, equipment, records, assets, liabilities, and appropriations of the integrated
constituent campuses shall be transferred to the Institute. Existing property rights shall remain
protected and reserved for public technical education purposes.

       Section 9. Finance, Procurement, and Special Trust Fund. – The Institute shall
adopt an integrated financial management, budgeting, procurement, and asset management
system.

        Income generated from training fees, assessment, consultancy, technology
commercialization, auxiliary services, grants, and donations shall accrue to a Special Trust
Fund (Revolving Fund) of the Institute. These funds shall be retained and utilized exclusively
for instruction, applied research, facility modernization, faculty development, student support,
and extension services, pursuant to existing government accounting and auditing laws.
       Section 10. Ladderized Diploma-to-Degree Programs. – Pursuant to Republic Act
No. 10968 (PQF Act) and Republic Act No. 10647 (Ladderized Education Act), the Institute is
mandated to establish ladderized pathways integrating TESDA-registered technical-vocational
diploma programs with baccalaureate degree offerings. Credits earned under technical-
vocational qualifications shall be articulated and credited toward higher credentials.

        Section 11. Transition and Integration. – Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity
of this Act, TESDA shall constitute a Transition Committee composed of representatives from
TESDA, DBM, CSC, and relevant stakeholders to draft the comprehensive Transition Plan
covering organizational, human resource, financial, and academic integration.

       The Committee shall formulate a Transition Plan which shall cover the following:
       a. organizational restructuring;
       b. human resource integration;
       c. finance and procurement integration;
       d. harmonization of academic and training programs;
       e. institutional development planning; and
       f. implementation of the Regional Polytechnic TVET System.

       Section 12. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the integrated TTIs and
Provincial Training Centers (PTCs). Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the
continued operation and capital outlay of the Institute shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act (GAA).

       Section 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within one hundred twenty
(120) days from the effectivity of this Act, TESDA, in consultation with the Department of
Education, the Commission on Higher Education, the Department of Labor and Employment,
the Department of Trade and Industry, the Department of Science and Technology, the National
Economic and Development Authority, the Department of Budget and Management, the Civil
Service Commission, concerned local government units, industry partners, and other relevant
stakeholders, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.

       Section 14. Separability Clause. – If any part or provision of this Act be held invalid
or unconstitutional, the other parts or provisions that are not affected shall remain in full force
and effect.

        Section 15. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended,
or modified accordingly.

       Section 16. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in newspaper of general circulation.
