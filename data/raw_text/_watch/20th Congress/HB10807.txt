                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

           Barangay governments serve as the frontline administrative and public service arm
    of the State. Under Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government
    Code of 1991, they function as the primary planning and implementing unit of government
    policies, plans, programs, projects, and activities in the community. Furthermore, they
    provide a vital public forum where the collective views of the people are expressed and
    considered, and where local disputes are amicably settled.

            In executing these statutory mandates, barangays bear the direct responsibility of
    implementing national and local welfare programs, maintaining public peace and order,
     delivering basic health and social services, and administering community-based disaster
    risk reduction and management efforts. Consequently, the effective and efficient
    execution of these essential public functions depends heavily upon the institutional
    capacity, administrative stability, and continuity of elected local leadership.

            This proposed measure seeks to strengthen governance at the grassroots level by
    establishing a fixed five-year term of office for elected barangay officials and members of
    the Sangguniang Kabataan. The current statutory term limits subject local administration
    to overly frequent electoral cycles, creating systemic disruptions, administrative
    inefficiency, and a constant shift of focus toward campaign activities rather than long-term
    community development.
