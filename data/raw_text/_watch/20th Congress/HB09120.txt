                       AN ACT
DECLARING THE PHILIPPINE MAHARLIKA HIGHWAY AS THE
NATIONAL FOOD HIGHWAY, PROVIDING AN INTEGRATED
SYSTEM FOR THE PROCESSING, STORAGE, TRANSPORT,
DISTRIBUTION, AND MONITORING OF FOOD PRODUCTS, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Section 21, Article II of the 1987 Constitution mandates that the State shall
promote comprehensive rural development and agrarian reform. Furthermore,
Section 9, Article XIV provides that the State shall promote the growth and
generalized spread of technology and ensure that the benefits of science and
technology are used to improve the productivity of the country.

       The Philippine agricultural sector remains the backbone of the national
economy, yet it is burdened by a fragmented and inefficient logistics network. As
of 2025, post-harvest losses in the Philippines remain alarmingly high, reaching
as much as 33% for mangoes and up to 45% for onions. This is primarily due to
inadequate cold storage and the lack of specialized transport corridors. These
inefficiencies do not only result in billions of pesos in lost revenue for our farmers
but also drive up retail food prices, contributing to the 44% food insecurity rate
reported among the population in 2023-2024.

     By declaring the Philippine Maharlika Highway as the National Food
Highway, this bill institutionalizes a strategic, integrated system for the
  processing, storage, and rapid distribution of agricultural goods. This measure
  transitions our primary road network from a mere transit route into a "living"
  logistics ecosystem. It not only prioritizes the movement of perishable goods
  through the establishment of strategic cold-chain hubs in partnership with the
  private sector, which is already projected to reach 100,000 pallet positions by
  2026, but also ensures that food quality is monitored from farm to table. This bill
  also creates the National Food Highway Management Board to oversee and
  implement this Act.

         Furthermore, this bill introduces the "Philippine Food Loop" concept by
  integrating the Maharlika Highway with the Western Philippine Nautical
  Highway. This creates a resilient, multi-modal circuit that ensures the continuous
  flow of food even when specific regions are hit by typhoons. Designating this
  highway as a National Food Highway is a critical structural intervention to
  modernize Filipino agriculture, lower the cost of living, and achieve genuine
  national food sovereignty.

        This measure was also filed during the 19th Congress.

        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National Food
Highway Act”.


      Section 2. Declaration of the Philippine Maharlika Highway as the
National Food Highway. – The Philippine Maharlika Highway, which stretches
from Laoag City in Ilocos Norte to Zamboanga City in Mindanao, is hereby
declared as the National Food Highway.


      Section 3. Definition of the National Food Highway. – The National Food
Highway refers to an integrated system for the processing, storage, transport,
distribution, and monitoring of food products along the Philippine Maharlika
Highway.


      Section 4. Scope and Coverage of the National Food Highway. – The
National Food Highway shall cover the following:


      a) Food processing and storage facilities located along the Philippine
         Maharlika Highway;
      b) Transport and distribution systems for food products along the
         Philippine Maharlika Highway; and,
      c) Food monitoring system to ensure the safety and quality of food
         products transported and distributed along the Philippine Maharlika
         Highway.



      Section 5. Roles and Responsibilities of Government Agencies and
Stakeholders. – The following government agencies and stakeholders shall have
the following roles and responsibilities in the implementation of the National
Food Highway:


      a) Department of Agriculture (DA) shall be responsible for the
         establishment and management of food processing and storage facilities
         along the National Food Highway, in partnership with the Department
         of Trade and Industry (DTl) and the private sector. The DA shall also
         provide technical assistance to local farmers and food producers to
         ensure the quality and safety of food products.
      b) Department of Transportation (DOTr) shall be responsible for the
         development and implementation of a transport and distribution system
         for food products along the National Food Highway, in partnership with
                                                                              2
         the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and the
         private sector. The DOTr shall also ensure the safety and security of
         food products during transport and distribution.
      c) Department of Health        (DOH) shall be responsible for the
         implementation of a food monitoring system to ensure the safety and
         quality of food products transported and distributed along the National
         Food Highway. The DOH shall also provide training and technical
         assistance to food safety inspectors and other relevant personnel to
         ensure compliance with food safety standards.
      d) Local Government Units (LGUs) shall provide support and assistance
         to the implementation of the National Food Highway within their
         respective jurisdictions, including, but not limited to, the provision of
         necessary permits, licenses, and regulatory compliance certificates.


      Section 6. Creation of a Food Processing and Storage System. –The DA,
in partnership with the DTl and the private sector, shall establish and manage
food processing and storage facilities along the National Food Highway. These
facilities shall comply with food safety standards and shall cater to the needs of
local farmers and food producers for the processing, storage, and distribution of
their products.


      Section 7. Development of a Transport and Distribution System. – The
DOTr, in partnership with the DPWH and the private sector, shall develop and
implement a transport and distribution system for food products along the
National Food Highway. This system shall ensure the safe and efficient transport
and distribution of food products and shall comply with relevant laws and
regulations.



                                                                                 3
      Section 8. Implementation of a Food Monitoring System. – The DOH
shall implement a food monitoring system to ensure the safety and quality of food
products transported and distributed along the National Food Highway. This
system shall include the following:


      a) Inspection and monitoring of food processing and storage facilities for
         compliance with food safety standards;
      b) Regular testing and analysis of food products for contaminants and
         other harmful substances;
      c) Monitoring of transport and distribution systems to ensure compliance
         with relevant laws and regulations; and,
      d) Provision of training and technical assistance to food safety inspectors
         and other relevant personnel.


      Section 9. Creation of the National Food Highway Management Board.
– There is hereby created a National Food Highway Management Board, which
shall be responsible for the implementation of this Act.


      Section 10. Composition of the National Food Highway Management
Board. – The National Food Highway Management Board shall be composed of
the following:


      a) Secretary of Agriculture or his/her representative as the Chairperson;
      b) Secretary of Transportation or his/her representative as the Co-
         Chairperson;
      c) Secretary of Public Works and Highways or his/her representative as a
         member;
      d) Secretary of Trade and Industry or his/her representative as a member;

                                                                                  4
     e) Secretary of Health or his/her representative as a member;
     f) Three (3) representatives from the private sector, one each from the
        farmers' groups, transport industry, and health industry, as members;
        and,
     g) One (1) representative from the academe or research institutions, as a
        member.


     Section 11. Powers and Functions of the National Food Highway
Management Board. – The National Food Highway Management Board shall
     have the following powers and functions:


     a) Develop and implement plans, programs, and projects for the efficient
        and timely delivery of fresh produce, livestock, and other agricultural
        products from the farms to the markets and consumers;
     b) Establish and maintain a database of agricultural products, producers,
        markets, and other relevant information along the National Food
        Highway;
     c) Coordinate with relevant government agencies and private sector
        stakeholders for the development and improvement of infrastructure
        and logistics systems along the National Food Highway, including the
        construction of cold storage facilities, processing centers, and logistics
        hubs;
     d) Promote the use of modern technologies and best practices in
        agriculture and logistics systems to improve the efficiency and
        competitiveness of the agriculture sector;
     e) Develop and implement measures to ensure the safety and quality of
        agricultural products transported along the National Food Highway;
     f) Monitor and evaluate the implementation of this Act and recommend

                                                                                 5
         policy and legislative reforms to improve the effectiveness and
         sustainability of the National Food Highway; and,
      g) Perform such other functions as may be necessary to achieve the
         objectives of this Act.


      Section 12. Expansion of the National Food Highway into the Philippine
Food Loop. – The National Food Highway may be expanded to include the
Western Philippine Nautical Highway. Thereafter, the existing National Food
highway and the Western Philippine Nautical Highway shall be integrated to
comprise the Philippine Food Loop.


      This expansion shall take effect upon the completion of the necessary
facilities and infrastructure along the Western Philippine Nautical Highway.


      Section 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DA, in coordination with the DTI, DOTr,
DPWH, DOH, concerned LGUs, and in collaboration with private stakeholders,
shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary to ensure the effective
implementation of this Act.


      Section 14. Appropriations. – The necessary funds for the implementation
of this Act shall be sourced from the annual General Appropriations Act and other
available funds.


      Section 15. Separability Clause. – If any provision, or part hereof, is held
invalid or unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not
otherwise affected shall remain valid and subsisting.
