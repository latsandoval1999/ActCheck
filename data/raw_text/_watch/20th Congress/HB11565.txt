EXPLANATORY NOTE

Filipinos with autism and their families face challenges that extend far beyond obtaining a
diagnosis. Many families struggle to find affordable assessment, therapy, education, health
services, and other forms of support. These difficulties become even greater for families with
limited financial resources or those who do not have ready access to specialists and appropriate
facilities.

For parents and caregivers, raising and supporting a person with autism can be a lifelong
responsibility. During childhood, families must navigate health care, education, therapy. and
social services. As the child grows older, new concerns arise, including further education, skills
development, employment, independent living, and continued care and support.

Government assistance should therefore not end with diagnosis or childhood intervention.

This bill seeks to establish a comprehensive National Plan of Action for the Care, Support. and
Inclusion of Persons with Autism Spectrum Disorder. It brings together the programs and
responsibilities of government agencies involved in health, education, social welfare,
employment, skills development, and local government services so that persons with autism
and their families can receive more coordinated support.

The measure promotes early identification and appropriate intervention, accessible health and
rehabilitation services, inclusive education, skills development and employment opportunities,
support for parents and caregivers, and greater public awareness and acceptance. It likewise

strengthens referral systems so that families can more easily determine where and how to obtain
appropriate government services.

More importantly, the measure recognizes that persons with autism have different needs at
different stages of their lives. Government programs must therefore provide continuity of
support from childhood and education to adulthood, employment, community participation,
and, when necessary, continuing care.

Persons with autism are members of our families and communities who deserve the same
opportunities to learn, work, participate, and live with dignity. A more inclusive society
requires not only awareness and acceptance, but also accessible services and concrete
opportunities that allow every Filipino to reach his or her potential.

AN ACT
INSTITUTING A NATIONAL PLAN OF ACTION FOR THE CARE, SUPPORT,
DEVELOPMENT, AND INCLUSION OF PERSONS WITH AUTISM SPECTRUM
DISORDER, PROVIDING SUPPORT FOR THEIR FAMILIES AND CAREGIVERS,
AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SEC. 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “National Autism Care, Support, and
Inclusion Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. \t is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect and
promote the rights, dignity, welfare, and full participation of persons with autism spectrum
disorder (ASD) in society.

Towards this end, the State shall adopt a comprehensive and life-course approach to autism
that promotes early identification and intervention, accessible health and rehabilitation
services, inclusive education, social protection, skills development, employment, community
participation, and appropriate support for families and caregivers.

The State shall likewise promote public understanding and acceptance of persons with autism
and endeavor to eliminate discrimination, stigma, and barriers that prevent their full
participation in society.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. For purposes of this Act:

(a) Autism Spectrum Disorder or ASD refers to a developmental condition characterized by
differences or difficulties in social communication and interaction, together with restricted or
repetitive patterns of behavior, interests, or activities, as determined in accordance with
recognized clinical standards;

(b) Caregiver refers to a parent, family member, guardian, or other person primarily
responsible for providing care or assistance to a person with ASD;

(c) Early intervention refers to appropriate services and support provided to children who
have been identified as having, or being at risk of having, developmental difficulties associated
with ASD; and

(d) Person with ASD refers to a person who has been appropriately diagnosed with Autism
Spectrum Disorder.

SEC. 4. National Plan of Action for Autism. There is hereby established a National Plan of
Action for the Care, Support, Development, and Inclusion of Persons with Autism Spectrum
Disorder, hereinafter referred to as the National Autism Plan.

The National Autism Plan shall provide an integrated framework for government policies,
programs, and services addressing the needs of persons with ASD throughout the different
stages of their lives.

It shall include, but shall not be limited to:

(a) Public awareness and education;

(b) Early identification, screening, assessment, diagnosis, and appropriate intervention;
(c) Accessible health, rehabilitation, and therapy services;

(d) Inclusive and appropriate education;

(e) Social protection and family support,

(f) Skills development, livelihood, and employment;

(g) Transition programs from childhood to adulthood;

(h) Community participation and independent or supported living, when appropriate;

(i) Training and capacity-building for professionals, teachers, health workers, social workers,
caregivers, and other persons involved in providing services to persons with ASD;

(j) Research and collection of reliable national data on autism; and

(k) Such other programs necessary to promote the welfare and inclusion of persons with ASD.

Such services shall, whenever appropriate, be integrated into health care provider networks and

PhilHealth shall, consistent with applicable laws, actuarial sustainability, clinical standards,
and health technology assessment requirements, periodically review and develop appropriate
benefit packages covering medically necessary assessment, diagnostic, rehabilitation, and other

SEC. 8. Education and Learning Support. The DepEd and CHED shall ensure that persons
with ASD are provided appropriate opportunities to access inclusive and quality education
consistent with their individual needs and abilities.

The DepEd shall strengthen programs for inclusive education, appropriate learning support,
teacher training, reasonable accommodation, and coordination between schools, families,
health professionals, and other service providers.

No learner shall be denied access to education solely on the basis of ASD.

SEC. 9. Transition to Adulthood, Skills Development, and Employment. The National Autism
Plan shall include programs addressing the transition of persons with ASD from childhood and
formal education into adulthood.

TESDA, DOLE, DSWD, and other concerned agencies shall develop and strengthen programs
for appropriate skills assessment, technical and vocational training, livelihood assistance, job
preparation, supported employment, job matching, and other employment opportunities for
persons with ASD who are able and willing to work.

Government agencies shall encourage partnerships with private employers, educational
institutions, civil society organizations, and other stakeholders to expand employment and
livelihood opportunities for persons with ASD.

Section 10. Support for Families and Caregivers. The DSWD, in coordination with the DOH,
LGUs, and other concerned agencies, shall develop and strengthen programs providing
information, counseling, training, referral assistance, psychosocial support, and other
appropriate services for families and caregivers of persons with ASD.

Government programs shall recognize the important role of parents and caregivers in the
continuing care, development, and inclusion of persons with ASD.

Special attention shall be given to low-income families and caregivers who face substantial
financial or social difficulties in accessing necessary services.

SEC. 12. National Autism Resource and Referral Center. The DOH, in coordination with the
DSWD, DepEd, NCDA, and other concerned agencies, shall establish or designate an existing
government facility to serve as the National Autism Resource and Referral Center.

The Center shall serve as a national resource for information, technical assistance, training,
research, referral, and coordination concerning ASD.

The DOH may designate qualified government hospitals or facilities as regional referral centers
where necessary and practicable.

SEC. 13. Autism Information System and Research. The DOH, in coordination with the
Philippine Statistics Authority, DSWD, DepEd, NCDA, academic institutions, and other
concerned agencies, shall develop an appropriate system for collecting reliable information
necessary for planning, research, policy formulation, resource allocation, and evaluation of
autism-related programs.

All collection, processing, storage, sharing, and use of personal and health information under
this Act shall comply with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the “Data Privacy Act
of 2012,” and other applicable laws and regulations.

Data used for statistical, policy, or research purposes shall, whenever appropriate, be
anonymized or de-identified.

SEC. 14. Public Awareness and Acceptance. The Council shall undertake continuing public
information and education programs to improve understanding of ASD, promote early
consultation and intervention, encourage acceptance and inclusion, and address
misinformation, stigma, bullying, and discrimination against persons with ASD.

Government agencies, LGUs, educational institutions, and other stakeholders are encouraged
to participate in such activities.

SEC. 16. Monitoring and Evaluation. The Inter-Agency Council on Autism shall establish
appropriate indicators for monitoring the implementation and effectiveness of the National
Autism Plan.

The National Autism Plan shall be periodically reviewed and updated to respond to
developments in research, service delivery, education, employment, social protection, and the
needs of persons with ASD and their families.

SEC. 17. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity
of this Act, the DOH, in coordination with the members of the Inter-Agency Council on Autism
and after consultation with persons with ASD, families, caregivers, advocacy organizations,
professional organizations, and other stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the implementation of this Act.

SEC. 18. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act
shall be charged against the current appropriations of the concerned agencies.

Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued implementation shall be included
in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 19. Separability Clause. If any provision or part of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions or parts not affected thereby shall remain in full force and
effect.

SEC. 20. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, and
other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended. or
modified accordingly.

SEC. 21. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
