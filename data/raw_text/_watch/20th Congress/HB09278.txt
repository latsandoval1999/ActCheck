                           AN ACT
STRENGTHENING THE ISSUANCE, VALIDITY, AND REGULATION OF
PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES IDENTIFICATION CARDS, PRESCRIBING
PENALTIES FOR ABUSE THEREOF, AND AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO.
7277, AS AMENDED

                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Republic Act No. 7277, as amended by Republic Act No. 9442 and Republic Act No.
10754, affirms the State’s commitment to uphold the rights, dignity, and welfare of persons
with disabilities (PWDs). Central to this framework is the grant of privileges and support
services that enable PWDs to participate fully in society.

However, persistent challenges—particularly the misuse and fraudulent issuance of PWD
identification cards—have undermined the effective implementation of the law. The lack of
standardized issuance, inconsistent validity periods, and weak enforcement mechanisms has
led to abuse, ultimately depriving legitimate PWDs of their rightful benefits.¹

This measure seeks to amend Sections 32 and 46 of Republic Act No. 7277 by establishing
clear and uniform rules on the issuance, validity, and security of PWD IDs. It introduces
differentiated validity periods—ten (10) years for persons with permanent disabilities and one
(1) year for those with temporary disabilities, subject to reassessment—ensuring both
practicality and accountability.

The bill further mandates a uniform ID design prescribed by the National Council on Disability
Affairs (NCDA) and adopted nationwide by local government units, enhancing recognition and
reducing fraud. It also strengthens penal provisions by imposing stricter penalties on
individuals who falsify or misuse PWD IDs, as well as on public officials who facilitate such
acts. Offenses committed through digital platforms shall likewise be prosecuted under Republic
Act No. 10175.

To support effective implementation, the measure provides for a strengthened and unified PWD
database through coordination among key agencies, aligned with the Community-Based
Monitoring System under Republic Act No. 11315, to improve data accuracy, planning, and
service delivery.²
This proposed legislation builds on prior legislative efforts and addresses the burden of frequent
ID renewal, particularly for PWDs with permanent disabilities and limited mobility—an issue
compounded by gaps in accessibility despite the mandate of Batas Pambansa Blg. 344.³

The approval of the measure will strengthen safeguards, improve systems, and reinforce
accountability to protect the integrity of PWD benefits.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Carta for [Disabled] Persons with Disabilities as amended by Republic Act No. 9442, is hereby
further amended to read as follows:

                 “SEC. 32. Persons with [disability] DISABILITIES shall be entitled to the
        following:

                 The abovementioned privileges are available only to persons with [disability]
        PERMANENT OR TEMPORARY DISABILITIES who are Filipino citizens,
        REFUGEES, OR STATELESS PERSONS upon submission of [any of the following
        as proof of his/her entitlement thereto:]

                 [(I)] An identification card issued by the [city or municipal mayor or the
                 barangay captain] PERSONS WITH DISABILITY AFFAIRS OFFICE
                 (PDAO) OR A DESIGNATED FOCAL PERSON PERFORMING PDAO

                 MAYOR of the place where the persons with [disability] DISABILITIES
                 reside[s], AS PROOF OF THEIR ENTITLEMENT THERETO.
                 [(II) The passport of the persons with disability concerned; or
                 (III) Transportation discount fare Identification Card (ID) issued by the
                 National Council for the Welfare of Disabled Persons (NCWDP).]

              THE IDENTIFICATION CARDS (IDs) ISSUED TO PERSONS WITH
        DISABILITIES SHALL CONFORM TO THE UNIVERSAL DESIGN
       PRESCRIBED BY THE NATIONAL COUNCIL ON DISABILITY AFFAIRS
       (NCDA) AND ADOPTED BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS (LGUs).

       SHALL BE VALID FOR A PERIOD OF TEN (10) YEARS. CONVERSELY,
       THE IDs FOR PERSONS WITH TEMPORARY DISABILITIES SHALL BE
       VALID FOR A PERIOD OF ONE (1) YEAR, AND MAY BE RENEWED ONLY
       UPON COMPLETION OF REASSESSMENT CONDUCTED BY AN
       APPROPRIATE MEDICAL PRACTITIONER.

            THE IDs, AS PROVIDED IN THE PRECEDING PARAGRAPH SHALL
       BE FREE OF CHARGE, INCLUDING REISSUANCE DUE TO LOSS OR
       DAMAGE.”

SEC. 3. The NCDA shall, in coordination with the Department of Social Welfare and
Development (DSWD), Philippine Statistics Authority, Department of Health, Department of
the Interior and Local Government (DILG), Department of Information and Communications
Technology, Professional Regulation Commission, National Anti-Poverty – Persons with
Disability Sector, National Privacy Commission, strengthen a unified persons with disabilities
identification card system, including a Community-Based Monitoring System (CBMS).
SEC. 4. Within one hundred eighty (180) days from the effectivity of this Act, the NCDA and
the DSWD shall, in coordination with the DILG, the DOH, other government agencies and
stakeholders, issue the rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 5. If any portion or provision of this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the
remainder provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SEC. 6. All laws, decrees, executive orders, proclamations, rules and regulations, ordinances,
other issuances or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with any provision of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SEC. 7. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation.
