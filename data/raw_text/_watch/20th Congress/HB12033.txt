                                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to expand access to appropriate residential care, rehabilitation,
and reintegration services for children in conflict with the law (CICL). It amends
Republic Act (RA) No. 9344, otherwise known as the “Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act
of 2006”, as amended, to establish additional Bahay Pag-asa facilities in every
province and highly urbanized city (HUC).

       Since the passage of RA No. 9344, as amended, the Juvenile Justice and
Welfare Council (JJWC) reported one hundred and eighteen (118) operational Bahay
Pag-asa facilities nationwide as of November 20251. The Department of Social
Welfare and Development (DSWD) also announced in January 2026 that it would
begin constructing five (5) additional regional facilities with an initial allocation of
Two hundred seventy-five million (Php 275,000,000.00) under the 2026 General
Appropriations Act2. These premises prove the continuing need to expand
residential care infrastructure and strengthen the juvenile justice system’s capacity to
respond to the needs of children requiring structured intervention.

      This proposed measure establishes, operates, and maintains an additional
Bahay Pag-asa in every province and HUC. It addresses the limitations of existing
youth detention facilities and the transfer of CICL to newly established facilities to
decongest existing facilities.

       Children who enter the juvenile justice system require more than temporary
custody. They need appropriate care, education, guidance, and interventions that
address their circumstances and prepare them for responsible reintegration into
society. Inadequate access to suitable facilities places undue pressure on existing
centers and local social welfare services. Accountability and rehabilitation must
always work together, and a child’s encounter with the law should not foreclose the

                           AN ACT
ESTABLISHING ADDITIONAL HOUSES OF HOPE FOR CHILDREN IN
CONFLICT WITH THE LAW, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT
NO. 9344, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “JUVENILE JUSTICE AND WELFARE
ACT OF 2006”, AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10630, AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Section 49 of Republic Act (RA) No. 9344, otherwise known as the
“Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act of 2006”, as amended by RA No. 10630, is hereby
further amended to read as follows:

             “SEC. 49. Establishment of ‘Bahay Pag-Asa’. - Each province and
      highly-urbanized city (the LGUs) shall [be responsible for building,
      funding and operating a] ESTABLISH, OPERATE, AND MAINTAIN
      AT LEAST TWO (2) ‘Bahay Pag-asa’ FACILITIES within their
      jurisdiction [following] IN ACCORDANCE WITH the standards [that
      will be] set by the DSWD and adopted by the JJWC. FOR LGUs THAT
      HAVE NOT YET ESTABLISHED THEIR FIRST ‘BAHAY PAG-ASA’
      FACILITY, THE DSWD AND THE CONCERNED LGU SHALL
      PRIORITIZE THE IMMEDIATE COMPLETION OF THE FIRST
      FACILITY BEFORE CONSTRUCTING THE ADDITIONAL
      FACILITY MANDATED HEREUNDER.

          “THE ‘BAHAY PAG-ASA’ REHABILITATION CENTERS
      MANDATED UNDER THIS SECTION SHALL BE JOINTLY
      MANAGED, OPERATED, AND MAINTAINED BY THE DSWD
      AND THEIR RESPECTIVE LGU.

             “Every ‘Bahay Pag-asa’ [will] SHALL have a special facility
      called the IJISC. This Center [will] SHALL be allocated for children in
      conflict with the law in accordance with Sections 20, 20-A and 20-B
      hereof. These children [will] SHALL be required to undergo a more

      intensive multi-disciplinary intervention program. The JJWC in
      partnership with, but not limited to, the DSWD, the DOH, the DepED
      and the DILG, [will] SHALL develop and set the standards for the
      implementation of the multi-disciplinary intervention program of the
      IJISC. Upon institutionalization of the IJISC program, the JJWC [will]
      SHALL continue to monitor and provide technical assistance to the
      multi-disciplinary teams operating the said centers.

           “WITHIN SIX (6) MONTHS FROM THE EFFECTIVITY OF
      THIS ACT, THE DSWD, IN COORDINATION WITH THE JJWC
      AND CONCERNED LGUs, SHALL FORMULATE AND
      IMPLEMENT A FIVE (5) YEAR INFRASTRUCTURE AND
      MULTIDISCIPLINARY DECONGESTION PLAN. THE PLAN
      SHALL ADDRESS THE PHYSICAL AND OPERATIONAL
      LIMITATIONS OF EXISTING YOUTH DETENTION CENTERS
      AND FACILITATE THE DECONGESTION AND TRANSFER OF
      CHILDREN IN CONFLICT WITH THE LAW TO COMPLIANT
      ‘BAHAY PAG-ASA’ FACILITIES.”

​     SEC. 2. Section 50 of the same Act is hereby further amended to read as

             “SEC. 50. Care and Maintenance of the Child in Conflict with the

        SEC. 4. Periodic Review and Monitoring. – The DSWD, the JJWC, and the
concerned LGUs shall submit an annual report to the Senate Committee on Social
Justice, Welfare and Rural Development and the House Committee on Welfare of
Children detailing the number of Bahay Pag-asa established from the effectivity of
this Act, the number of children in conflict with the law detained in each ‘Bahay
Pag-asa’ facility, and any other relevant information Congress may require, to ensure
that each province and highly urbanized city has established a ‘Bahay Pag-asa’
facility in accordance with this Act.

       SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
approval of this Act, the DSWD and JJWC, in coordination and consultation with
non-government organizations providing short-term residential care for children in
conflict with the law, shall formulate rules and guidelines as may be necessary for
the proper implementation and enforcement of this Act. The implementing rules

      SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
