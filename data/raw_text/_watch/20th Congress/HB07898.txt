                                     EXPLATYATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to declare December 29 of every year as a special non-working holiday in the
Municipality of Leon in honor of Captain Jose Calugas Sr. Its purpose is for the amendment of
Republic Act No. 10805, which simply declares December 29 as a working holiday in the said
municipality.

Captain Jose Calugas Sr., bom on December 29, 1907 in Leon, was a member of the Philippine
Scouts during World War II. He received the Medal of Honor for his bravery and heroism during
the Battle of Bataan. He also. fought for the liberation of the Philippines from the Japanese.

Therefore, declaring December 29 as a special non-working holiday for the Municipality of
Leon, allows the Leonians to further give importance to their history and identity and to give
them ample opportunities to honor their descendants. This is also to allow them to work together
to continue their progress as a municipality and further reflect towards their contribution to
nation building and the betterment of its people.

So as to fully recognize the significance of this day in giving honor and to recognize the lasting
impact and influence of the heroism of Captain Jose Calugas, Sr., December 29 should be
declared as a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Leon amending the previous
Republic Act No. 10805 which declared it merely as a working holiday.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.
