HOUSE BILL wol1964

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the San Miguel By-pass Road, in the Municipality of San
Miguel, Province of Catanduanes, into a national road, placing its supervision, improvement,
repair, and maintenance under the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

The Municipality of San Miguel is an agricultural community where abaca farming and
other agricultural activities contribute significantly to local livelihoods and economic
development. The San Miguel By-pass Road is intended to provide an alternative route for
motorists, improve access to nearby communities, and facilitate the transportation of
agricultural products, goods, and essential services.

Given its importance, the conversion of the road into a national road will facilitate the
allocation of appropriate national funding for its improvement, rehabilitation, and long-term
maintenance, while easing the financial burden on the provincial government. This will help
ensure safer, more accessible, and disaster-resilient road infrastructure, improve transportation
efficiency, and further promote agricultural productivity and socioeconomic development in
San Miguel and the Province of Catanduanes.

This proposal is anchored on DPWH Department Order No. 112, s. 2024, which sets
the standards for road conversion. The road proposed is eligible for conversion as it meets the
standard of connecting communities to bayan and businesses and providing an alternative route
that will reduce traffic congestion within the municipal center, improve transportation
efficiency, and facilitate the movement of agricultural products, goods, and essential services
in the municipality of San Miguel.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this proposed measure is earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL nol1964

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SAN MIGUEL BY-PASS ROAD, IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF SAN MIGUEL, CATANDUANES, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD
AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. It is hereby declared that the San Miguel By-pass Road, in the
Municipality of San Miguel, Catanduanes, is converted into a national road under the
jurisdiction and supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

SECTION 2. The Secretary of the DPWH shall include in its program the expansion,
improvement, rehabilitation, maintenance, and repair of the said road, the funding of which
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
