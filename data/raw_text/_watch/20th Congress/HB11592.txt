EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Curacha, scientifically identified as Ranina ranina (Linnaeus, 1758), is internationally
referred to as the spanner crab or red frog crab and locally as Curacha or Kuracha. This species,
belonging to the family Raninidae, is recognized as an important edible and commercially valuable
crab. Philippine scientific and fisheries literature identify the species as particularly abundant and
economically significant in the coastal waters of southwestern Mindanao, including Zamboanga
and the Sulu area.

Curacha represents more than a seafood delicacy; it is a vital marine resource that supports
the livelihoods of fisherfolk and coastal communities and contributes to the food cultufe and
culinary identity of Zamboanga and neighboring areas. Scientific studies have documented its
economic significance and the presence of both domestic and international markets for the species.

Ranina ranina has a broad Indo-Pacific distribution and is commercially harvested in
several countries. The species is particularly recognized in international seafood markets,
including those in Australia and East Asia. Large specimens command significant value in live
seafood markets, demonstrating the commercial potential of the species when supported by
effective fisheries management, production systems, quality standards, and market infrastructure.

The primary challenge for the Philippines is not solely to increase the volume of Curacha
harvested from the sea. Rather, it is to develop the science and technology necessary to sustainably
provide a stable, adequate, high-quality, and traceable supply of Curacha while safeguarding the
resource that supports coastal communities.

Science and innovation form the foundation for achieving this objective. Research on
reproductive biology, population dynamics, genetics, nutrition, disease management, habitat,
hatchery production, nursery systems, aquaculture, stock enhancement, responsible harvésting,
post-harvest handling, processing, and value addition can enable the country to transition from
reliance on unpredictable wild harvests to a more sustainable and knowledge-based Curacha
industry.

Studies on the reproductive biology of Ranina ranina in southwestern Mindana@ have
already generated important scientific information regarding reproductive activity, egg-bearing
females, maturity, and fecundity. These findings demonstrate that a robust and sustained scientific
program can provide the information required for responsible management and the future
development of production technologies.

This proposed measure establishes the National Curacha Science and Innovation Program,
designating the Department of Science and Technology as the lead agency. It also provides for
Centers for Excellence, a Curacha Innovation Fund, technology transfer and extension sefvices,
market development, and an inter-agency mechanism. The proposed Curacha Innovation Fund will
support research institutions, universities, fisherfolk organizations, cooperatives, entrepreneurs,
startups, processors, and other qualified stakeholders in developing technologies to enhanee the
sustainability and competitiveness of the Curacha industry.

The proposed Centers for Excellence, particularly in Zamboanga City, Sulu, and other
strategic locations, will serve as research and innovation hubs where scientists, fisherfolk, industry,
and government collaborate to develop practical solutions. These centers will build upon existing
scientific capacity in Western Mindanao and the Sulu area, where research on Ranina ranina has
already been conducted.

The measure also recognizes that production alone is insufficient. The country must
consistently deliver safe, high-quality, properly handled, traceable, and competitively priced
products to both domestic and international consumers. Therefore, market development, branding,
product development, food safety, export readiness, cold-chain infrastructure, and international
market compliance are integral components of the proposed program.

The ultimate objective is to establish a sustainable Curacha value chain, encompassing
scientific research, resource management, production, harvesting, post-harvest handling,
processing, technology transfer, market development, and exportation. This comprehensive
approach aims to benefit fisherfolk, coastal communities, researchers, entrepreneurs, consumers,
and the Philippine economy.

Investing in science and innovation today will help ensure that Curacha remains an
abundant and sustainable resource in the future. More importantly, the country can develop the
capacity to adequately and sustainably produce Curacha for a growing domestic and international
market, transforming a treasured seafood resource of Zamboanga and Sulu into a competitive
Philippine marine product while safeguarding the ecosystems and communities that support it.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Curacha Crab Scienee and

Innovation Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to recognize
the economic, scientific, cultural, nutritional, and ecological importance of the spanner crab, red
frog crab, or Curacha, scientifically known as Ranina ranina (Linnaeus, 1758), as a valuable marine

resource and potential high-value seafood commodity of the Philippines.

The State shall promote the responsible development of science, technology, research,
innovation, sustainable fisheries, aquaculture and stock-enhancement technologies, post-larvest
systems, processing, value addition, market development, and export capabilities relating to

Curacha.

The Curacha is a locally important and highly valued crab associated particularly with the
coastal waters of Zamboanga City and the Sulu archipelago, where it forms part of the livelihood

and food culture of coastal communities. The State shall ensure that the development of the

Towards this end, the State shall establish an integrated science and innovation program

lead agency in the implementation of this Act.

The DOST shall formulate and implement the National Curacha Science and Innovation

Resources (DA-BFAR), Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), Department of Environment and

(CHED), Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), relevant local government units
(LGUs), state universities and colleges (SUCs), private higher education institutions, research
institutions, fisherfolk organizations, cooperatives, seafood processors, exporters, and other

relevant stakeholders.

The DOST shall establish the necessary scientific, technological, research, innoyation,

financing, extension, and commercialization mechanisms to achieve the purposes of this Act.

SEC. 8. Market Development and Promotion. — The DTI, in coordination with DOST,
DA-BFAR, DOT, the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA), and other relevant agencies, shall

develop and implement a Curacha Market Development and Promotion Program.
The Program shall:
(a) Establish and promote Curacha as a high-value Philippine seafood product;

(b) Develop a national branding, labeling, traceability, and quality-assurance system for

Philippine Curacha;

(c) Identify and develop domestic and international markets for fresh, chilled, frozen,

processed, and value-added Curacha products;

(d) Assist fisherfolk, cooperatives, processors, exporters, and enterprises in complying
with international food safety, sanitary, phytosanitary, environmental, packaging,

labeling, and traceability requirements;

(e) Facilitate participation in international seafood exhibitions, trade missions, business

matching activities, and other export-promotion activities;

(f) Promote the unique culinary, cultural, and geographie identity of Curacha from

Zamboanga and Sulu;

(g) Encourage investment in sustainable production, processing, cold-chain logisti¢s, and

export facilities; and

(h) Develop market intelligence systems concerning prices, demand, supply, comsumer

preferences, trade requirements, and emerging international markets.

The promotion of Curacha for export shall be undertaken only in a manner consistent with
sustainable fisheries management, conservation measures, food safety requirements, and the long-

term availability of the species.

SEC. 9. Inter-Agency Committee. — There is hereby created an Inter-Agency Committee
on Curacha Science, Innovation, and Sustainable Development, which shall provide policy

direction and ensure coordination in the implementation of this Act.
