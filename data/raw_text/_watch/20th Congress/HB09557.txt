                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Constitution recognizes education as a fundamental right and mandates
the State to make quality education accessible to all.

      Access to educational resources is essential to ensuring quality and inclusive
higher education. However, many students in State Universities and Colleges (SUCs),
particularly those in underserved and remote areas, continue to face difficulties in
obtaining learning materials and reliable internet connectivity necessary for academic
study and research.

      This measure seeks to establish a National Academic Content Repository
(NACR) that will provide SUC students and faculty with free access to digital
textbooks, open educational resources, lecture materials, and other academic
content. It likewise aims to expand campus-wide internet connectivity through the
provision of free Wi-Fi services in SUCs, thereby supporting digital learning, research,
and innovation.

       By harnessing information and communications technology to improve access
to knowledge and educational opportunities, this bill promotes academic excellence,
digital inclusion, and equitable access to higher education for all Filipino students.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                             “AN ACT
      ESTABLISHING    A   NATIONAL    ACADEMIC    CONTENT
      REPOSITORY    AND    PROVIDING   DIGITAL   LEARNING
      RESOURCES AND FREE INTERNET ACCESS IN STATE
      UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
 Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Universal Access to
Learning Resources Act”.

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to promote
quality, accessible, and inclusive higher education through the use of information
and communications technology, digital learning resources, and reliable internet
connectivity. Toward this end, the State shall support the development and
dissemination of academic content and expand access to digital learning
opportunities in State Universities and Colleges.

      SECTION 3. Objectives – This Act aims to:

    a. Provide free and equitable access to academic learning resources for
       students enrolled in State Universities and Colleges (SUCs).

    b. Establish a National Academic Content Repository (NACR) containing
       government-funded, institutionally developed, licensed, and openly
       accessible educational resources.

    c. Ensure Free High-speed Wi-Fi access in all SUC campuses to facilitate
       digital learning and research.

    d. Support the development, digitization, and sharing of locally
       produced academic content.

     SECTION 4. National Academic Content Repository (NACR) – There is
hereby established a National Academic Content Repository (NACR) to be managed
by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) in partnership with the Department
of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) and State Universities and
Colleges (SUCs). The NACR shall:

    a. Store and disseminate digital textbooks, lecture videos, syllabi,
       research papers, and open educational resources (OERs) across
       disciplines.

    b. Provide free online access to all SUC students and faculty nationwide.

    c. Ensure    content is    quality-assured,   accessible,   inclusive,   and
       culturally relevant.

    d. Encourage faculty-generated content,       with appropriate academic
       recognition and attribution.

       The acquisition, digitization, reproduction, storage, dissemination, and use of
educational materials under this Act shall be undertaken in accordance with
applicable intellectual property laws, licensing agreements, and other pertinent laws,
rules, and regulations. The NACR and all related digital platforms shall likewise
comply with existing laws and standards on data privacy, cybersecurity, accessibility
for persons with disabilities, and digital inclusion.

    SECTION 5. Digital Content Development and Acquisition – To populate the
NACR, CHED shall:

    a. Acquire licenses for digital textbooks and resources used in SUC
       courses.

    b. Promote the creation of localized and open educational content through
       grants to SUCs and faculty members.

    c. Enter into agreements with local and international publishers,
       academic institutions, and educational content providers to secure
       affordable licensing, subscription, or open-access arrangements.

    d. Establish content review panels to ensure academic quality and
       accuracy.

    e. Ensure that all content included in the NACR is accessible, inclusive,
       culturally relevant, and, whenever practicable, available in formats
       suitable for persons with disabilities and learners in geographically
       isolated and disadvantaged areas.
       SECTION 6. Free Wi-Fi Access in SUCs – The DICT, in partnership with SUCs,
shall implement campus-wide high-speed Wi-Fi access that is:

 a. Free of charge to all enrolled SUC students and faculty.

 b. Secure and administered in accordance with applicable data privacy and
    cybersecurity laws to protect users and prevent unauthorized access,
    misuse, or security breaches.

 c. Designed to support access to the NACR and other academic services.

       Priority shall be given to rural and under-resourced campuses during
 initial implementation.

     SECTION 7. Responsibilities of SUCs – Each SUC shall:

    a. Designate an existing office, unit, library, or responsible officer to
       coordinate with CHED and DICT in the implementation of this Act.

    b. Encourage faculty to contribute, adapt, and use digital content from
       the NACR.

    c. Support students in digital literacy and responsible use of online
       academic resources.

    d. Integrate NACR content into library services and course delivery.


     SECTION 8. Capacity Building and Incentives – The CHED shall offer:

       a. Training and workshops for faculty on creating digital materials and
          OERs;

       b. Recognition and lawful incentive programs, subject to existing civil
          service, budgeting, compensation, and auditing laws, rules, and
          regulations, for academic content creators whose work is utilized
          nationally;

       c. Technical assistance to SUCs for Wi-Fi installation, server setup,
          and NACR access; and

       d. Capacity-building programs on intellectual property rights, open
          educational resources, digital accessibility, data privacy, and
          cybersecurity for faculty members and relevant personnel.
      SECTION 9. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the
CHED and the DICT. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act, subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and
regulations.

     Implementation of this Act shall be subject to the availability of funds and
existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.


      SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations – Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, CHED and DICT, in coordination with SUCs and other
stakeholders, shall issue the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) to carry out
the provisions of this Act.

       SECTION 11. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act shall be held
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not otherwise affected shall remain
in full force and effect.

      SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders or issuances or
any part thereof, which are inconsistent herewith, are hereby repealed or amended
accordingly.

      SECTION 13. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

 Approved.
