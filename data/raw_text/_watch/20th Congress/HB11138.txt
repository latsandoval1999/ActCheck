                           AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR THE PERPETUAL EXISTENCE OF THE PHILIPPINE
INTERNATIONAL TRADING CORPORATION (PITC), AMENDING FOR THE
PURPOSE SECTION 2 OF PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 1071, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS “THE REVISED CHARTER OF THE PHILIPPINE INTERNATIONAL
TRADING CORPORATION”

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippine International Trading Corporation (PITC) was created through
Presidential Decree No. 252, and its charter was subsequently revised by Presidential
Decree No. 1071, otherwise known as the “Revised Charter of the Philippine
International Trading Corporation.” Under Section 2 of Presidential Decree No. 1071,
the Corporation is authorized to exist for a period of fifty (50) years, with its present
corporate term set to expire on 25 January 2027.

For several decades, PITC has served as a government corporate vehicle in pursuing
the State’s objectives in international trade. Consistent with its charter, the Corporation
has undertaken trade-related activities and provided services intended to facilitate
international trade, promote Philippine products and enterprises, and support the
Government in its foreign procurement and marketing activities.

As the expiration of PITC’s corporate term approaches, there is a need to ensure the
continuity of its juridical personality, operations, and existing commitments. The
expiration of its corporate term without an appropriate legislative measure may create
uncertainty regarding the Corporation’s authority to continue performing its lawful
functions, administering its assets and liabilities, honoring its contractual
commitments, and undertaking transactions necessary for the fulfillment of its
mandate.

The proposed measure seeks to address this concern by providing for the continued
corporate existence of PITC, while ensuring that such continued existence remains
subject to appropriate government oversight and accountability. The measure
provides for the perpetual term of existence of the Corporation, unless otherwise
provided by law, while recognizing the authority of the Governance Commission for
Government-Owned or -Controlled Corporations (GCG) to evaluate the Corporation in
accordance with Republic Act No. 10149, otherwise known as the “GOCC Governance
Act of 2011,” and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

The proposed framework recognizes that the continued existence of a government-
owned or -controlled corporation must be accompanied by mechanisms for periodic
assessment of its relevance, efficiency, financial viability, performance, and
consistency with national development policies and priorities. Accordingly, the GCG
may conduct periodic performance evaluation and oversight of PITC, taking into
account, among others, its financial performance, operational efficiency, governance
and risk management systems, delivery of services, and contribution to the promotion
and development of Philippine international trade.

The measure further recognizes that perpetual corporate existence does not exempt
PITC from continuing government evaluation, oversight, and accountability. The
Corporation shall remain subject to the evaluation, monitoring, and other appropriate
actions authorized under Republic Act No. 10149, otherwise known as the “GOCC
Governance Act of 2011,” and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations. Where
warranted by such evaluation and consistent with applicable law, the appropriate
government authorities may undertake or recommend such lawful measures as may
be necessary to ensure that the Corporation remains relevant, efficient, financially
viable, and responsive to the national interest.

The grant of perpetual existence is not intended to constitute a determination that the
Corporation shall operate indefinitely regardless of its continued relevance,
performance, or economic viability. Rather, PITC shall remain subject to the
performance evaluation, monitoring, and oversight mechanisms applicable to
government-owned or -controlled corporations under Republic Act No. 10149 and
other applicable laws.

At the same time, the proposed measure does not seek to expand the substantive
powers and functions of PITC under Presidential Decree No. 1071. Rather, it seeks to
ensure the continuity of the Corporation’s juridical personality and operations while
strengthening accountability and providing for continuing evaluation by the appropriate
government authorities.

The proposed perpetual corporate term will also eliminate the need for periodic
legislative action solely for the purpose of extending the Corporation’s corporate
existence, while preserving the authority of Congress to amend, repeal, or otherwise
modify the Corporation’s charter or corporate existence through subsequent
legislation. This approach provides greater institutional stability and continuity while
maintaining the necessary safeguards to ensure that PITC remains relevant, efficient,
financially viable, and responsive to the national interest. Perpetual corporate
existence does not preclude the State from undertaking performance evaluation,
monitoring, and oversight of the Corporation's relevance, performance, efficiency, and
financial viability, nor does it prevent the appropriate government authorities from
recommending or undertaking such lawful measures as may be warranted by the
results of such evaluation.
The continued corporate existence of PITC is therefore intended to prevent disruption
in its legal existence and operations and to ensure the orderly continuity of its
contracts, obligations, assets, liabilities, and other lawful commitments. It will likewise
enable the Corporation to continue performing its existing mandate and contributing,
within the scope of its charter, to the promotion and development of Philippine
international trade.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate enactment of this measure is earnestly sought.
