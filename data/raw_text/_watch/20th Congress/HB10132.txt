                                    Explanatory Note
The digital transformation of government and regulated private sectors has become a
cornerstone of national development. Fragmented systems, duplicative databases, and
vendor-dependent technologies have long hampered efficiency, interoperability, and
citizen trust in digital services.
This bill seeks to institutionalize Architectural Domain Governance through the
establishment of the Philippine Reference Architecture Framework (Ph-RAF). The
framework shall serve as the national guide for the governance of business processes,
applications, data, technology infrastructure, and information security.
The measure is aligned with the Department of Information and Communications
Technology’s National ICT Ecosystem Framework (NIEF) and E-Government
Master Plan (EGMP), the National Privacy Commission’s Data Privacy Act of 2012,
and the Philippine Development Plan 2023–2028, which emphasizes digital
transformation, innovation, and inclusive governance.
By mandating technology independence, interoperability, business continuity, and
privacy-by-design, this Act will reduce inefficiencies, strengthen cybersecurity, and
promote citizen-centered digital services. It will also ensure that government ICT projects
and covered private organizations adhere to common standards, thereby safeguarding
public data and enhancing transparency and accountability.
In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                    AN ACT
           INSTITUTIONALIZING ARCHITECTURAL DOMAIN GOVERNANCE IN
     GOVERNMENT AND COVERED PRIVATE ORGANIZATIONS, ESTABLISHING THE
     PHILIPPINE REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE FRAMEWORK, AND PROVIDING FOR
         ITS IMPLEMENTATION IN ALIGNMENT WITH EXISTING DICT POLICIES,
          NATIONAL PRIVACY COMMISSION MANDATES, AND THE PHILIPPINE
                              DEVELOPMENT PLAN


     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:
1
2        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Architectural Domain
3    Governance Act of 2026.”
4         SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to foster
5    a secure, interoperable, citizen-centered, and future-ready digital governance
6    environment. In pursuit of this policy, the State shall establish a national reference
7    architecture framework that shall guide the governance of business processes,
8    applications, data, technology infrastructure, and information security.
 9   The implementation of this Act shall be consistent with the National ICT Ecosystem
10   Framework (NIEF), the E-Government Master Plan (EGMP) of the Department of
11   Information and Communications Technology (DICT), the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and
12   issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC), and the Philippine Development
13   Plan 2023–2028.
14         SEC. 3. Scope and Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all departments, bureaus,
15   offices, agencies, and instrumentalities of the National Government, including
16   government-owned or controlled corporations, government financial institutions, and
17   state universities and colleges, as well as local government units.
18   Covered private organizations that collect, store, process, transmit, or use public data, or
19   provide essential public-facing services, shall likewise be subject to the provisions of this
20   Act, in coordination with their respective regulators and the DICT.
21        SEC. 4. Lead Implementing Agency. - The DICT shall serve as the lead
22   implementing agency of this Act. In the exercise of this mandate, it shall coordinate with
23   the NPC, the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the National Economic and
1    Development Authority (NEDA), the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA), the Cybercrime
2    Investigation and Coordinating Center (CICC), and other relevant agencies to ensure
3    harmonized implementation.
4         SEC. 5. Establishment of the Philippine Reference Architecture Framework. -
5    There is hereby established a Philippine Reference Architecture Framework (Ph-
6    RAF), which shall serve as the national guide for the governance, design, development,
7    procurement, maintenance, and integration of systems involving public data and public-
8    facing digital services.
 9   The Ph-RAF shall prescribe principles and standards for interoperability, portability,
10   technology independence, business continuity, disaster recovery, accessibility, usability,
11   cybersecurity, and privacy-by-design. It shall likewise provide templates and reference
12   models for business, application, data, technology infrastructure, and information security
13   domains.
14     SEC. 6. Governance Principles. - All systems, platforms, and digital services covered
15   by this Act shall adhere to the following principles:

16      1. Technology independence and avoidance of vendor lock-in;
17      2. Business continuity and resilience against disruptions;
18      3. Secure and reliable interoperability;
19      4. Accessibility and inclusivity in public-facing services;
20      5. Security and privacy embedded by design;
21      6. Transparency and accountability in architecture-related decisions; and
22      7. Cohesive yet loosely coupled governance across domains.

23      SEC. 7. Philippine Architecture Governance Council. - There is hereby created a
24   Philippine Architecture Governance Council under the DICT, composed of
25   representatives from relevant agencies, regulators, LGUs, and stakeholders. The Council
26   shall recommend updates to the Ph-RAF, issue governance policies, coordinate
27   implementation, monitor compliance, and submit annual reports to Congress.
28      SEC. 8. Implementation Roadmap. - The DICT, in coordination with the Council, shall
29   prepare a five-year phased roadmap for implementation, aligned with the Philippine
30   Development Plan. The roadmap shall include:

31      ●   Year 1: Design and deployment of the compliance management system;
32      ●   Year 2: Rollout of data architecture standards;
33      ●   Year 3: Rollout of business architecture standards;
34      ●   Year 4: Rollout of application and infrastructure standards;
35      ●   Year 5: Rollout of information security standards.

36      SEC. 9. Compliance and Monitoring. – No government ICT project shall proceed to
37   full implementation unless it has obtained a certification of compliance with the Philippine
38   Reference Architecture Framework (Ph-RAF) standards or an approved exception from
39   the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT). Covered private
40   organizations shall likewise align their governance structures with the Ph-RAF, subject to
41   the principles of proportionality and phased implementation.
42      In determining compliance, the DICT shall consider whether the proposed project
43   avoids unnecessary duplication of existing systems, is capable of securely exchanging
44   information with other government systems were permitted by law, minimizes
1    unnecessary dependence on a single vendor, platform, or proprietary technology, and is
2    capable of maintaining continuity of operations during emergencies, disasters, or system
3    failures.
4       The DICT, in coordination with the appropriate regulatory agencies, shall monitor
5    compliance with this Act and its implementing rules and regulations, require corrective
6    action plans where necessary, and impose appropriate sanctions in accordance with
7    existing laws and due process.

 9          SEC. 11. Use of Common Standards and Reusable Digital Tools. - The DICT
10   shall promote the use of common standards, reusable templates, shared services, and
11   approved digital tools to prevent unnecessary duplication and reduce government
12   spending on separate systems with similar functions.
13   Government agencies shall first determine whether an existing approved system, shared
14   service, template, or reusable digital tool may be used before developing, procuring, or
15   commissioning a new system.
16   Nothing in this section shall prevent an agency from developing or procuring a separate
17   system when justified by law, security, operational requirements, cost, public interest, or
18   the specific needs of the agency.
19
20          SEC. 12. User-Friendly and Inclusive Digital Services. - Public-facing digital
21   services covered by this Act shall be designed and implemented in a manner that is
22   secure, accessible, and understandable.
23   The DICT shall issue guidelines to ensure that public-facing digital services take into
24   account the needs of persons with disabilities, senior citizens, persons with limited digital
25   literacy.
26   Covered agencies shall, whenever practicable, provide clear instructions, simple
27   language, feedback mechanisms, and alternative assistance channels for users who are
28   unable to fully access or use digital services.
29        SEC. 10. Data Privacy and Security. - Implementation of this Act shall be
30   consistent with the Data Privacy Act of 2012, cybersecurity laws, records management
31   rules, freedom of information policies, and other applicable statutes. Nothing herein shall
32   authorize the disclosure of personal, confidential, or proprietary information except as
33   lawfully required.
34         SEC. 11. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial implementation of
35   this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the DICT. Thereafter, such
36   sums as may be necessary shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
37        SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
38   effectivity of this Act, the DICT, in coordination with the Department of Finance, and the
39   Department of Budget and Management, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
40   regulations to implement the provisions of this Act.
41        SEC. 12. Transitory Provisions. - Covered agencies and organizations shall be
42   given a reasonable transition period, as provided in the implementing rules and
43   regulations, to comply with this Act.
1    Existing systems shall not be required to be immediately replaced, but upgrades,
2    integrations, and new projects involving public data shall be aligned with Ph-RAF within

4        SEC. 13. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
5    unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
6         SEC. 14. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
7    regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
8    accordingly.
 9         SEC. 15. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
10   in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
