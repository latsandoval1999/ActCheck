                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Colonel Pedro Pasia was one of Cuenca, Batangas' most
distinguished sons and a revolutionary leader who rendered exemplary
service during the Philippine struggle for independence. His courage,
patriotism, and leadership significantly contributed to the revolutionary
movement and remain an enduring source of pride and inspiration for the
people of Cuenca.

      Recognizing his invaluable contribution to Philippine history, the
National Government previously declared July 7, 1971 a special public
holiday in the Municipality of Cuenca through Proclamation No. 874 (1971)
in commemoration of his heroism. More recently, the Sangguniang Bayan
of Cuenca enacted a local ordinance institutionalizing the annual
observance of "Col. Pedro Pasia Day" and adopted Resolution No.
2026-213 requesting the enactment of a national law declaring July 7 of
every year as a permanent local special holiday.

     It is fitting that the State permanently recognize Colonel Pedro
Pasia's legacy by enacting legislation declaring July 7 of every year a
special working holiday in the Municipality of Cuenca. Such recognition will
strengthen historical awareness, promote patriotism among future
generations, and preserve the municipality's rich revolutionary heritage.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is
earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT​
DECLARING JULY 7 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY
 IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CUENCA, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS, TO
      COMMEMORATE THE LIFE, HEROISM, AND LEGACY OF
REVOLUTIONARY LEADER COLONEL PEDRO PASIA, TO BE KNOWN
                 AS "COL. PEDRO PASIA DAY"

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

     SECTION 1. July 7 of every year is hereby declared a special working
holiday in the Municipality of Cuenca, Province of Batangas, to be known
as "Col. Pedro Pasia Day," in commemoration of the life, heroism,
patriotism, and invaluable contributions of Colonel Pedro Pasia to the
Philippine Revolution and the struggle for Philippine independence.

     SEC. 2. The local government of the Municipality of Cuenca, Province
of Batangas, shall organize appropriate commemorative, educational,
cultural, historical, and civic activities for the annual celebration of Col.
Pedro Pasia Day, including ceremonies, exhibits, lectures, historical
reenactments, and other programs that promote appreciation of Colonel
Pedro Pasia's legacy and the historical heritage of the Municipality of
Cuenca.

      SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
