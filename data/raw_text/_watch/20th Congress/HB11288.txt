                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to increase the number of Associate Justices from sixty-nine
(69) to seventy-eight (78). It amends Batas Pambansa Blg. 129, otherwise known as
the “Judiciary Reorganization Act of 1980”, and improves the accessibility, efficiency,
and responsiveness of the justice system.

        According to the Supreme Court of the Philippines (2024)1, the judiciary
continues to experience substantial appellate caseloads that require sustained
institutional strengthening to preserve the constitutional right to the speedy
disposition of cases. The concentration of appellate divisions in Metro Manila also
imposes additional logistical burdens on litigants, lawyers, witnesses, and
government agencies coming from far-flung regions. This often results in higher
litigation costs and delays in the administration of justice.

       This proposed measure decentralizes appellate services in the country and
establishes permanent regional stations in Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. Every
unresolved appeal represents individuals, families, businesses, and communities
waiting for justice to take its course. Delayed appellate review prolongs uncertainty
and burdens those who seek the protection of the law. Strengthening the Court of
Appeals through expanded judicial capacity and broader regional accessibility shall
bring the administration of justice closer to the Filipino people.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                              AN ACT
CREATING ADDITIONAL DIVISIONS IN THE COURT OF APPEALS,
INCREASING THE NUMBER OF COURT OF APPEALS JUSTICES FROM
SIXTY-NINE (69) TO SEVENTY-EIGHT (78), AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 129, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “JUDICIARY
REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1980”, AS AMENDED, APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Section 3 of Batas Pambansa (BP) Blg. 129, otherwise known as “The
Judiciary Reorganization Act of 1980”, as amended by Executive Order No. 33, s. 1986,
is hereby further amended to read as follows:

             “Sec. 3. Organization. – There is hereby created a Court of
      Appeals which shall consist of a Presiding Justice and [fifty]
      SEVENTY-SEVEN (77) Associate Justices who shall be appointed by
      the President of the Philippines. The Presiding Justice shall be so
      designated in his appointment, and the Associate Justice shall have
      precedence according to the dates of their respective appointments, or
      when the appointments of two (2) or more of them shall bear the same
      date, according to the order in which their appointments were issued
      by the President. Any member who is reappointed to the Court after
      rendering in any other position in the government shall retain the
      precedence to which he was entitled under his original appointment,
      and his service in the Court shall, for all intents and purposes, be
      considered as continuous and uninterrupted.”

       SEC. 2. Section 4 of the same Act is hereby further amended to read as
follows:

             “Sec. 4. Exercise of Powers and Functions. – The Court of Appeals
      shall exercise its powers, functions, and duties through [seventeen
      (17)] TWENTY-SIX (26) divisions, each composed of three (3)

       SEC. 4. Referral of Pending Cases. – Upon the effectivity of this Act, all pending
cases, except those which have been submitted for resolution, shall be referred to the
proper division of the Court of Appeals.

        SEC. 5. Transfer of Assignment. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed to
allow the transfer, except in cases of temporary assignment, of any member of the
Court of Appeals to any place or station without such member’s written consent, or
to undermine the security of tenure of its members as provided in the Constitution,
or alter the seniority in the Court of Appeals in accordance with existing laws.

      SEC. 6. Funding. – The amounts necessary to carry out the provisions of this
Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its
enactment into law and thereafter.

      SEC. 7. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision thereof not affected
thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, letters of instruction,
executive orders, rules, or regulations, or any part thereof inconsistent with the
provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following
its publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
