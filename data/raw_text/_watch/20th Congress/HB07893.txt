                          AN ACT
PROVIDING FREE PSA BIRTH CERTIFICATES FOR STUDENT ATHLETES
PARTICIPATING IN GOVERNMENT-RECOGNIZED SPORTS EVENTS

                                 EXPLANA TORY NOTE

         Article XIV Section 19 of the 1987 Constitution directs the State to promote physical
education, encourage sports programs and foster athletic excellence to develop a healthy and
alert citizenry. At all levels of competition, student athletes are required to present certified
PSA birth certificates as part of eligibility verification. For many families, however, the cost of
securing these documents continues to be a financial burden that can hinder participation in
sanctioned events.

        This measure removes that barrier by granting student athletes one free PSA-issued
birth certificate when participating in government-recognized or nationally sanctioned sports
events, including competitions endorsed by DepEd, CHED, the Philippine Sports Commission,
the Philippine Olympic Committee and accredited National Sports Associations. The bill
covers athletes competing in domestic and international youth tournaments and emerging
disciplines such as e-sports, ensuring that no athlete is excluded due to inability to pay for
required documentation.

        By eliminating this cost, the State strengthens its commitment to youth development,
sports excellence and equitable access to athletic opportunities, consistent with its
constitutional duty to support the physical, moral and social well-being of the youth.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “Free PSA Birth Certificate For
Student Athletes Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the State to promote youth development,
sports excellence and equal opportunity in athletic participation. The State shall remove undue
financial barriers that hinder student athletes from joining government-recognized or nationally
sanctioned sports events and shall ensure that documentary requirements imposed by
government agencies remain accessible and supportive of public purpose.

SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. For purposes of this Act:

   a) Student Athlete refers to a bona fide learner or student enrolled in any public or private
      basic education institution or higher education institution who is officially listed as a
      participant in a sanctioned sports event.

   b) Sanctioned Event refers to any sports competition that is officially recognized, endorsed
      or sanctioned by the Department of Education (DepEd), the Commission on Higher
      Education (CHED), the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC), the Philippine Olympic
      Committee (POC), or any National Sports Association (NSA) duly accredited under the
      POC; and in the case of international competitions, an event where the Philippines is
      formally represented through a delegation endorsed by the school, CHED, DepEd, PSC,
      POC, or the relevant NSA. This definition covers youth international competitions
      tournaments and emerging sports including e-sports, provided they are sanctioned
      through the appropriate Philippine authorities.

   c) Endorsing School Authority refers to the principal, athletics director or sports
      coordinator who issues the endorsement required under this Act.
SEC. 4. Coverage. This Act shall apply to student athletes officially endorsed to participate in
government-recognized or nationally sanctioned sports events during the school year or athletic
season.

SEC. 5. Waiver of PSA Birth Certificate Fees. The Philippine Statistics Authority shall issue
one (1) certified true copy of a PSA birth certificate free of charge to each student athlete
covered by this Act for the purpose of verifying eligibility for participation in sanctioned
events.

The waiver applies only to requests made specifically for participation in such events.

SEC. 6. Verification and Endorsement.
  a) The endorsing school authority shall issue a certification stating that the student athlete
       is an official participant in a sanctioned event.
  b) The endorsement shall accompany the student athlete’s application for the free birth
       certificate.
  c) The PSA may establish additional verification procedures, including digital validation
       systems, to prevent fraud and ensure operational integrity.

SEC. 7. Limitations.
  a) The waiver shall apply only once per sanctioned event per student athlete.
  b) The waiver shall not cover expedited processing fees or requests for multiple copies
       unless required by the sanctioning government agency.
  c) PSA may require digital or electronic submission of endorsements to streamline
       verification.

SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of
this Act, the PSA, in coordination with DepEd, CHED, PSC, POC and the NSAs involved,
shall issue the necessary rules for its effective implementation. Coordination with the POC and
NSAs shall be limited to matters relating to event sanctioning and delegation procedures.

SEC. 9. Appropriations. Funds necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be
charged to the current appropriations of the agencies concerned. Thereafter, such amounts as
may be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 10. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional, the
remainder shall continue in full force and effect.

SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations inconsistent with
this Act are repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 12. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
