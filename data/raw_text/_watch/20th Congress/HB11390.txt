              Introduced by REP. CLAUDINE DIANA D. BAUTISTA-LIM


AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE USE OF PUBLIC SCHOOL MULTI-PURPOSE
INFRASTRUCTURE FOR COMMUNITY HEALTH, WELLNESS, SPORTS,
RECREATIONAL, AND RELATED ACTIVITIES DURING NON-CLASS DAYS,
PROVIDING GUIDELINES FOR ITS IMPLEMENTATION, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Community Access to
School Wellness Facilities Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to promote and protect
the right to health of the people, encourage physical fitness and active lifestyles,
strengthen community participation, and maximize the efficient use of government
infrastructure. The State likewise recognizes that public school facilities are valuable
community assets that may be utilized beyond instructional purposes, provided that such
use does not interfere with the delivery of quality education or compromise the safety and
security of students, teachers, and school property.

SECTION 3. Objectives. - This Act aims to:

(a) Maximize the utilization of existing public school infrastructure during periods when
classes are not being conducted;

(b) Promote healthy lifestyles through accessible community-based health and wellness
activities;

(c) Encourage sports, recreation, fitness, and preventive health programs;

(d) Strengthen partnerships among schools, local government units, and community
organizations; and

(e) Foster community ownership and shared responsibility in the preservation of public
school facilities.
SECTION 4. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

(a) Public School Multi-Purpose Infrastructure refers to school gymnasiums, covered
courts, multipurpose halls, open grounds, sports facilities, jogging paths, playgrounds,
and other facilities suitable for community activities.

(b) Non-Class Days refer to Saturdays, Sundays, legal holidays, semestral breaks,
Christmas vacation, summer vacation, and other days when no regular classes or official
school activities are scheduled.

(c) Health and Wellness Activities include physical fitness programs, exercise classes,
walking and jogging activities, sports clinics, community sports, yoga, aerobics, dance
fitness, wellness seminars, nutrition education, preventive health campaigns, blood
donation drives, vaccination activities, medical and dental missions, mental health
programs, and similar community-based activities.

SECTION 5. Authorized Use. - Subject to the approval of the School Head and in
accordance with guidelines issued by the Department of Education (DepEd), public
school multi-purpose infrastructure may be made available for health, wellness, sports,
recreational, and other community development activities during non-class days.

Priority shall be given to programs organized or endorsed by:

(a) The Department of Education;

(b) The Department of Health;

(c) The Local Government Unit;

(d) Barangays;

(e) Government agencies;

(f) Accredited civic organizations;

(g) Non-government organizations; and

(h) Community associations.

SECTION 6. Conditions for Use. - The use of school facilities shall be subject to the
following conditions:

(a) Educational activities shall at all times take precedence over community use;

(b) Activities shall not damage school property or disrupt school operations;
(c) Users shall maintain cleanliness, sanitation, peace, and order;

(d) No activity shall involve gambling, political campaigning, partisan political meetings,
unlawful assemblies, or activities contrary to law or public morals;

(e) Alcoholic beverages, illegal drugs, firearms, and other dangerous weapons shall be
strictly prohibited within school premises except as otherwise authorized by law; and

(f) Users shall comply with safety, security, and environmental regulations prescribed by
the Department of Education.

SECTION 7. Management and Scheduling. - The School Head shall establish a
transparent scheduling system to regulate the use of school facilities and ensure
equitable access by community groups while preserving the primary educational function
of the school.

SECTION 8. Maintenance and Liability. - Organizations or individuals authorized to use
school facilities shall be responsible for maintaining cleanliness and order during the
activity and shall be liable for any damage caused to school property through negligence
or intentional acts.

The Department of Education may require reasonable security deposits, indemnity
agreements, or proof of insurance for large-scale activities, as may be provided in the
Implementing Rules and Regulations.

SECTION 9. Coordination with Local Government Units. - Local Government Units
may provide logistical, financial, maintenance, medical, security, and emergency
response assistance to schools implementing this Act.

SECTION 10. Accessibility. - Whenever practicable, schools shall ensure that facilities
used under this Act are reasonably accessible to senior citizens, persons with disabilities
(PWDs), children, and other vulnerable sectors.

SECTION 11. Fees. - The Department of Education may authorize the collection of
reasonable maintenance or utility fees solely to cover operational expenses, provided that
no fees shall be charged for activities conducted by government agencies in the exercise
of their official functions or for community health programs sponsored by the government.

SECTION 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Education, in coordination with the Department
of Health, the Department of the Interior and Local Government, the Department of Social
Welfare and Development, the Philippine Sports Commission, and concerned
stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.
SECTION 13. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 14. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 15. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, rules, regulations, and
other issuances inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 16. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
