     AN ACT MANDATING THE ACCREDITATION OF ALL PET CREMATORIUMS
OPERATING IN MUNICIPALITIES AND CITIES IN THE COUNTRY
                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE
       On October 31, 2026, Bacoor City Government ordered the closure of Ahjay Paws
Aftercare Pet Crematorium in connection with allegations that the remains of twenty-six (26) dogs
and three (3) cats, which were supposed to be cremated by the establishment, were instead in a
vacant lot in San Jose Del Monte Bulacan.
       Pets are not just domesticated animals, they become part of the family and are beloved by
their owners. They also provide emotional support to their owners, and it is just right that they
should be accorded proper burial or cremation services once they pass away.
       The reported dumping of the remains of these pets in Bulacan may constitute violations of
applicable sanitation, environmental, animal-welfare, and local ordinances. Presidential Decree
No.856, otherwise known as the Code on Sanitation of the Philippines, provides for the protection
of public health and proper management of matters that may constitute nuisances or sources of
danger to public health.
        Republic Act No. 8485, otherwise known as the Animal Welfare Act of 1998, as amended
by Republic Act No. 10631, already provides for the supervision and regulation of facilities
utilized for the breeding, maintaining, keeping, treating, or training of animals, including
household pets. Existing regulations likewise require covered facilities to register with the
Department of Agriculture – Bureau of Animal Industry (DA-BAI) before operation, and local
government units are directed to require proof of such registration before issuing or renewing
business permits.
       However, there is a need for a specific and uniform regulatory framework for pet
crematoriums, particularly with respect to inspection, operational capability, equipment,
documentation, chain of custody, proof of cremation, and proper disposal or release of animal
remains.
        Hence, this proposed legislation aims to hold responsible the owners and operators of pet
crematoriums for the improper or indiscriminate handling of animal remains and shall mandate the
accreditation of such establishments by the DA-BAI before commencing or continuing operations.
It likewise requires local government units to verify accreditation before issuing or renewing
business permits, without dispensing with other requirements under sanitation, environmental,
fire-safety, building, zoning, animal-welfare, and related laws.
       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

          AN ACT MANDATING THE ACCREDITATION OF ALL PET CREMATORIUMS
     OPERATING IN MUNICIPALITIES AND CITIES IN THE COUNTRY

           Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:


 1           Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as “Pet Crematorium Accreditation
 2   Act.”

 3          Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to adopt a
 4   systematic management and disposal of animal remains in accordance with the Presidential Decree
 5   No. 856, otherwise known as the Code on Sanitation of the Philippines; Republic Act No. 9003,
 6   otherwise known as the Ecological Solid Waste Management of 2000; Republic Act No. 8485,
 7   otherwise known as the Animal Welfare Act of 1998, as amended by Republic Act No. 10631;
 8   and other applicable laws, rules, regulations, and local ordinances.

 9          To this end, the State shall mandate the accreditation of all pet crematoriums operating in
10   municipalities and cities in the country and shall ensure that such establishments are properly
11   inspected, monitored, and required to maintain records concerning the handling and disposition of
12   animal remains.

13          Section 3. Accreditation of Pet Crematoriums. – All pet crematoriums operating or
14   intending to operate in municipalities and cities shall secure accreditation from the Department of
15   Agriculture – Bureau of Animal Industry (DA-BAI).
 1
 2           No city or municipality shall issue or renew business permit to a pet crematorium unless
 3   the applicant presents proof of valid DA-BAI accreditation and all other permits, clearances,
 4   registration, and authorizations required under applicable national and local laws.
 5           Accreditation under this Act shall be without prejudice to, and shall not replace, the
 6   registration requirements under Republic Act No. 8485, as amended by Republic Act No. 10631,
 7   and other applicable laws, rules, regulations, and local ordinances.
 8
 9          For purposes of accreditation, pet crematoriums shall comply with the following
10   requirements:
11
12              1. They must undergo actual inspection by the DA-BAI to verify their existence,
13                 operational capability, facilities, and equipment;
14
15              2. They must present all applicable government permits, clearances, registrations, and
16                 authorizations, and proof of compliance with applicable laws, rules, regulations,
17                 and local ordinances;
18
19              3. They must present a written standard operating procedure for documentation and
20                 handling of animal remains, beginning with collection or receipt and ending with
21                 cremation and the final disposal, release, or delivery of the cremated remains;
22
23              4. They must present proof of cremation for services rendered, particularly for
24                 individual pet cremation services rendered;
25
26              5. They must maintain records sufficient to establish the identity, receipt, handling,
27                 cremation, and final disposition of the animal remains; and
28
29              6. They shall be subjected to annual assessment by the DA-BAI to ensure continuing
30                 compliance with this Act and its implementing rules and regulations.
31

32          The DA-BAI shall maintain a registry of accredited pet crematoriums and shall furnish the
33   concerned local government units with updated information regarding the accreditation status of
34   such establishments.
35
36           Section 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Department of Agriculture through
37   the DA-BAI, in coordination with the Department of Health, the Department of Interior and Local
38   Government, the Department of Environment and Natural Resources, local government units, and
39   other stakeholders, shall within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, promulgate the
40   necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of the provisions of this Act.
