                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Across the Philippines, accidents, natural disasters, and emergencies are
recurring realities. Data from the Philippine Red Cross and the Department of
Health show that thousands of Filipinos suffer injuries annually from road accidents,
workplace hazards, and calamities such as typhoons and earthquakes. Yet, many
victims could have been stabilized or saved if bystanders had basic knowledge of
first aid and survival techniques. For example, the World Health Organization
estimates that timely first aid can reduce preventable deaths by up to 15% in trauma
cases. In disaster-prone countries like the Philippines, where an average of 20
typhoons strike yearly, survival skills such as evacuation readiness, CPR, bleeding
control, and safe sheltering are not optional but essential. Integrating these
competencies into the educational system ensures that every student, from
elementary to tertiary level, acquires life-saving knowledge early on.

        This measure also aligns with the Department of Education’s thrust to
promote holistic learning and disaster risk reduction education. By institutionalizing
first aid and survival training, schools become not only centers of academic learning
but also hubs of resilience and preparedness. The provision of funds guarantees that
institutions can procure training materials, partner with accredited organizations like
the Red Cross, and capacitate teachers to deliver standardized modules.

      Ultimately, this Act seeks to cultivate a generation of citizens who are not only
academically competent but also equipped to respond effectively in emergencies,
thereby reducing fatalities, strengthening community resilience, and supporting
national disaster preparedness goals.

  AN ACT MANDATING THE INTEGRATION OF BASIC FIRST AID AND
SURVIVAL SKILLS TRAINING IN THE CURRICULA OF ALL INSTITUTIONS
 OF LEARNING AND PROVIDING THE APPROPRIATE FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

Sec. 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as “Basic First Aid and Survival Skills
Education Act.”

Sec. 2. Integration of Basic First Aid Training and Survival Skills training. - All
institutions of learning, including but not limited to schools, colleges, universities,
and vocational learning institutions, shall integrate into their respective curricula
and make available to all its students relevant and updated subjects, units, electives,
or courses regarding basic first aid training and survival skills training.

Sec. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations - The Commission on Higher
Education (CHED) and the Department of Education (DepEd), in coordination with
the Department of Health (DOH), Department of the Interior and Local Government
(DILG), and the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council
(NDRRMC), shall promulgate the corresponding implementing rules and
regulations of this Act within sixty (60) days from the date of its effectivity.

Sec. 4. Appropriations. - The funds necessary to implement the provisions of this
Act shall be sourced from the respective budgets of the CHED and the DepEd in the
annual General Appropriations Act.

Sec. 5. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, and rules and regulations inconsistent
with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified
accordingly.

Sec. 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
two (2) newspapers of general circulation or in the Official Gazette.
