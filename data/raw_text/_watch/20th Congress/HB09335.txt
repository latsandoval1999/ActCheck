54   AN ACT ESTABLISHING A DISTRICT-LEVEL BLOOD STATION IN THE FIRST
55   DISTRICT OF COTABATO, STRENGTHENING THE LOCAL BLOOD SERVICES
56   NETWORK, PROVIDING FOR ITS MANDATE, GOVERNANCE, QUALITY
57   STANDARDS, HUMAN RESOURCES, FUNDING MECHANISMS, PENALTIES, AND
58   FOR OTHER PURPOSES

60           SECTION 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “First District of Cotabato Blood
61   Station Act.”
62
63           SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect
64   and promote the right to health of every Filipino, and to ensure the availability of safe, adequate,
65   accessible, and equitable blood supply for all, particularly those in geographically isolated and
66   disadvantaged areas (GIDAs). Consistent with Republic Act No. 7719, as amended,, Executive
67   Order No. 7 series of 2010 on the National Voluntary Blood Services Program, and the Universal
68   Health Care Act (Republic Act No. 11223), the State shall strengthen district-level blood service
69   delivery through complementary facilities that integrate within the national blood services
70   network. Blood services shall at all times be voluntary, non-remunerated, non-commercialized,
71   and governed by the highest standards of safety, quality, transparency, and accountability. The
72   State shall likewise safeguard blood services from political interference, commercial exploitation,
73   and any form of discrimination.
74
75          SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. As used in this Act:
76
77      (a) "Blood Station" refers to a DOH-licensed blood service facility authorized to conduct
78          voluntary donor recruitment, blood collection, storage, and distribution of screened blood
79          and blood components received from a licensed blood center, but not to conduct blood
80          testing or component processing.
81      (b) "District-Level Blood Station (DLBS)" refers to the blood station established under this
82          Act within the First District of Cotabato.
83      (c) "Blood Services Network" refers to the integrated system of blood centers, blood stations,
84          hospital blood banks, and blood collection units operating under the technical supervision
85          and regulatory authority of the DOH.

181           SECTION 7. Donor Eligibility, Screening, and Deferral Standards. The DLBS shall adopt
182   and strictly implement donor eligibility, pre-donation screening, and deferral standards consistent
183   with DOH Administrative Orders, the National Blood Services Program guidelines, and applicable
184   World Health Organization (WHO) standards. At a minimum:
185
186      (a) Prospective donors shall undergo a pre-donation health history interview and physical
187          examination, including hemoglobin level assessment, conducted by qualified personnel
188          prior to each donation;
189      (b) Temporary deferral periods shall be applied in accordance with DOH standards, including
190          deferrals following illness, medication use, recent immunizations, tattooing, surgery, or
191          travel to areas with endemic transfusion-transmissible infections;
192      (c) Permanent deferral criteria shall be applied to donors with a history of transfusion-
193          transmissible infections, including HIV, hepatitis B, hepatitis C, syphilis, and malaria, or
194          other conditions as prescribed by the DOH;
195      (d) All donor screening information shall be recorded in the voluntary donor registry and
196          treated as strictly confidential in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012;
197      (e) No person shall be coerced, induced by material reward, or pressured into donating blood;
198          and
199      (f) The DLBS shall ensure that a licensed medical officer or physician is available or on-call
200          to manage adverse donation reactions and donor-related medical emergencies.

411           SECTION 19. Prohibited Acts. The following acts are hereby prohibited:
412
413       (a) Operating a blood service facility without a valid DOH License to Operate;
414       (b) Selling, buying, commercializing, or profiting from blood, blood components, or any
415           product directly derived therefrom, except for authorized cost-recovery service fees under
416           Section 16 of this Act;
417       (c) Inducing or coercing an individual to donate blood through monetary payment, gifts,
418           favors, undue influence, or any other form of material compensation;
419       (d) Diverting, misappropriating, or misusing blood supplies, DLBS funds, or DLBS resources
420           for purposes other than those authorized under this Act;
421       (e) Directing, influencing, or manipulating blood allocation, donor recruitment, or DLBS
422           operations for partisan political purposes or for the personal or political benefit of any
423           official;
424       (f) Disclosing donor or recipient personal or medical information without lawful authority or
425           the consent of the data subject, in violation of the Data Privacy Act;
426       (g) Falsifying, tampering with, or destroying DLBS records, reports, donor registries, or
427           laboratory data;
428       (h) Conducting blood testing, serological screening, or component processing at the DLBS in
429           violation of the centralized testing mandate; and
430       (i) Any other act constituting a violation of Republic Act No. 7719, Republic Act No. 11223,
431           or any other applicable law, rule, or regulation governing blood services.
