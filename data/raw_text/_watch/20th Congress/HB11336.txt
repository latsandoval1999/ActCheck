                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Republic Act No. 9003, otherwise known as the “Ecological Solid Waste
Management Act of 2000,” established a comprehensive framework for the proper
management of solid wastes, emphasizing source reduction, reuse, segregation,
recycling, composting, resource recovery, and the proper treatment and disposal of
residual wastes.

         However, more than two decades after its enactment, the country continues to
face serious solid waste management challenges. Increasing waste generation, limited
waste diversion and recovery capacity, and inadequate treatment and final disposal
facilities continue to place pressure on local governments and communities.

         Recent incidents underscore the urgency of strengthening the country’s solid
waste management system. In January and February 2026, major trash slides occurred
at sanitary landfills in different parts of the country, resulting in loss of lives and persons
reported missing. These incidents prompted the Department of Environment and Natural
Resources (DENR) to undertake a nationwide review of operating sanitary landfills. More
recently, on August 11, 2026, tons of garbage were reported to have clogged the water
channel linking Parañaque and Pasay, underscoring the continuing challenge of keeping
critical waterways and drainage systems free from solid waste.

       These incidents, although different in nature, highlight the need for a more
comprehensive solid waste management system that addresses not only waste
generation and diversion, but also the safe and proper treatment and final disposal of
residual wastes.
      This proposed measure seeks to amend Republic Act No. 9003 to expressly
recognize Waste Thermal Treatment as an environmentally sound option for the
treatment of residual solid wastes, subject to strict environmental, health and safety
safeguards and compliance with applicable laws, rules and regulations. It defines Waste
Thermal Treatment and Waste Thermal Treatment Facility and establishes a regulatory
framework for their establishment and operation.

       The measure does not intend to make thermal treatment a substitute for waste
prevention, segregation, recycling, composting, or other waste diversion measures.
Rather, it provides an additional treatment option for residual wastes remaining after
feasible source reduction, reuse, segregation, recycling, composting, and other waste
diversion and recovery measures. Waste Thermal Treatment Facilities shall not be used
to undermine or replace mandatory waste diversion programs under Republic Act No.
9003.

       To ensure safe and environmentally sound operations, the measure requires
compliance with applicable emission and environmental quality standards, proper waste
characterization and feedstock management, the use of appropriate and environmentally
sound technology, continuous monitoring and reporting, and proper management and
final disposal of ash and other residues. The necessary environmental permits and
authorizations must also be secured before a facility may operate.

       The establishment of Waste Thermal Treatment Facilities shall not be mandatory
for every province, city, or municipality. Such facilities may be established or utilized only
where warranted by the applicable local solid waste management plan, waste
characterization, technical and economic feasibility, environmental safeguards, and
availability of appropriate feedstock. The measure likewise directs the DENR, in
consultation with concerned agencies, local government units, and other stakeholders, to
establish detailed technical and environmental standards for their operation.

       The proposed amendments seek to strengthen, and not weaken, the ecological
solid waste management framework established under Republic Act No. 9003. By
providing a properly regulated treatment option for residual wastes that cannot feasibly
be diverted, the measure seeks to complement existing waste diversion programs, reduce
pressure on limited treatment and final disposal capacity, and enable local governments
to manage residual wastes more safely, efficiently, and sustainably.

       For the Filipino people, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

         ACT OF 2000,” TO RECOGNIZE WASTE THERMAL TREATMENT
              AS AN ENVIRONMENTALLY SOUND SOLID WASTE
             MANAGEMENT OPTION, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

assembled:

     to adopt a systematic, comprehensive and ecological solid waste management
     program which shall:
     “x x x

     and disposal of solid waste through the formulation and adoption of the BEST

     TREATMENT AND WASTE-TO-ENERGY TECHNOLOGIES THAT

amended to read as follows:
    “SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:
    “x x x
    “(dd) Resource recovery shall refer to the collection, extraction or recovery of
    recyclable materials from the waste stream for the purpose of recycling,
    generating energy, or producing a product suitable for beneficial use,
    INCLUDING THROUGH WASTE-TO-ENERGY OR OTHER WASTE
    THERMAL TREATMENT TECHNOLOGIES THAT COMPLY WITH
    APPLICABLE ENVIRONMENTAL, HEALTH AND SAFETY STANDARDS;
    (dd-1) Waste Thermal Treatment shall refer to a controlled process
    in which solid waste is subjected to elevated temperatures, with or
    without oxygen, to cause physical or chemical changes in the waste,
    including reduction of its volume or mass, destruction or stabilization
    of hazardous constituents, or recovery of energy or other useful
    outputs, through processes such as incineration, gasification,
    pyrolysis or other thermal processes, and in which resulting
    emissions and residues are managed in an environmentally sound
    manner.
    (dd-2) Waste Thermal Treatment Facility shall refer to any facility,
    installation, or combination of equipment designed, constructed, and
    operated primarily for the thermal treatment of solid waste,
    including equipment and systems for the receipt, storage and
    preparation of waste, thermal treatment, and the management of
    resulting emissions and residues.

SECTION 10. National Solid Waste Management Commission. The National Solid
Waste Management Commission shall, in addition to its existing powers and functions,
formulate or approve the standards, criteria and guidelines necessary for the
implementation of waste thermal treatment under this Act, in coordination with the DENR
and other concerned agencies. Nothing in this Section shall be construed as diminishing
the powers and functions of the Commission under Republic Act No. 11898.
SECTION 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) calendar
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DENR, in consultation with the National Solid
Waste Management Commission, Department of Health, Department of Energy,
Department of the Interior and Local Government, concerned LGUs, the private sector,
the academe and other stakeholders, shall promulgate the implementing rules and
regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act. The IRR shall establish,
at a minimum, the technical and environmental requirements for waste thermal treatment
facilities, including feedstock characterization, facility siting and design, emissions
monitoring and disclosure, residue management, emergency response, facility closure
and decommissioning, and procedures for clustering LGUs and establishing common
facilities.
SECTION 12. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the DENR and other
concerned agencies. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the continued
implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
SECTION 13. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and
effect.
SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.


SECTION 15. Effectivity Clause. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following
its complete publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.


       Approved.
