                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to promote safe online spaces for all users of social media
applications and digital platforms, especially children and the youth, by establishing
clear government guidelines on responsible digital engagement and strengthening
parental awareness.

        The rapid expansion of mobile, gaming, and interactive applications with
social interaction capabilities has transformed how Filipinos communicate, learn,
and entertain themselves. However, these platforms also expose users, particularly
minors, to obscene, discriminatory, exploitative, and other harmful content. The
absence of standardized safeguards and coordinated public education efforts has left
many families unprepared to address the risks associated with unrestricted digital
access.

      This measure mandates the Department of Information and Communications
Technology (DICT), in coordination with the National Telecommunications
Commission (NTC) and the National Privacy Commission (NPC), to develop and
implement guidelines promoting responsible online behavior.

       Recognizing the crucial role of parents and guardians, the bill institutionalizes
a Mandatory Parental Awareness and Information Drive. Local Government Units
shall require parents and guardians to complete a program on the dangers of
children’s unrestricted access to digital platforms before enrolling their children in
early education programs.

       By promoting responsible digital use and strengthening parental
involvement, this bill advances the State’s constitutional duty to protect the physical,
moral, intellectual, and social well-being of its citizens in the digital age.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
PROMOTING SAFE ONLINE SPACES FOR ALL MOBILE MULTIMEDIA
APPLICATIONS AND DIGITAL PLATFORMS USERS

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Safe Online Spaces
Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is a constitutionally declared policy of the
State to promote and protect the physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual, and social
well-being of its citizens, especially the youth. To breathe life to this provision, the
State shall promote the responsible use of all social media applications and digital
platforms.

       SEC. 3. Promotion of Safe Online Spaces. — The Department of Information and
Communications       Technology      (DICT), in coordination with National
Telecommunications Commission (NTC) and National Privacy Commission (NPC),
shall develop, adopt and implement guidelines for all social media and digital
platforms, including mobile, gaming and interactive applications with social
interactions capability downloadable in the country, promoting responsible usage,
such as avoidance of language otherwise considered as obscene, improper,
discriminatory, profanity-laced, indecent, intemperate and offensive. This shall
include regulation on the ability to share, within these applications and platforms,
contents which are explicit, exploitative and immoral.

       An indispensable part of such guidelines shall be the mandatory short prompt
notification on all social media and digital platforms, including mobile, gaming and
interactive applications with social interactions capability, forewarning users that
these apps may expose them to conversations, exchanges and interactions containing
obscene, improper and offensive language, lascivious videos and graphic contents.
Such a prompt shall appear on the screen once the user opens the app for the first
time in a day.

       SEC. 4. Mandatory Parental Awareness and Information Drive. — The Local
Government Units (LGUs) through the Rural or City Health Units and the
Department of Social Welfare Development (DSWD) through the Day Care Centers,
are hereby directed to spearhead the implementation of the Mandatory Parental
Awareness and Information Drive for parents and guardians. Parents or guardians
shall be required to attend and complete a program on the dangers of children
having unrestricted access to all social media and digital platforms, including
mobile, gaming and interactive applications with social interactions. This shall be
mandatory before their children be allowed to enroll in any early education
program, both in public and in private sectors.

       The Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), in
close coordination with the Department of Health (DOH) and with the help of
National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) and National Privacy
Commission (NPC) shall create, develop, furnish and distribute materials for this
program on responsible use of social media applications other digital platforms.

      The Department of Education (DepEd) is also directed to include the Parental
Awareness and Information Drive on their agenda during Parents-Teachers
Association meetings for elementary and secondary levels of education.

        SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — The Department of
Information and Communications Technology (DICT), in coordination with the
appropriate government agencies, including the Department of Health (DOH), the
Department of Education (DepEd), the National Telecommunications Commission
(NTC) and National Privacy Commission (NPC), and in consultation with relevant
stakeholders, shall promulgate the implementing rules and regulations within ninety
(90) from the effectivity of this Act.

       SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
