                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to strengthen the disability and survivorship benefits granted
to members of the Philippine National Police who suffer permanent and total
disability in the line of duty. It amends Section 73 of Republic Act (RA) No. 6975,
otherwise known as the “Department of the Interior and Local Government Act of 1990”,
as amended by RA No. 8551. It also provides for an updated computation of
disability pensions to reflect prevailing salary adjustments of active-duty police
personnel and removes the existing limitation on survivorship pension benefits
granted to qualified dependents.

        Police officers continue to face life-threatening risks in the performance of
their duties. In a July 2024 report, the Philippine National Police disclosed that
sixteen (16) police officers died and forty (40) others were injured in the line of duty
from January to July 2024 alone. This highlights the dangerous realities faced daily
by law enforcers1. In another incident reported in January 2024, three (3) police
officers were killed while four (4) others were wounded during a gun battle with an
armed criminal group in Samar while serving warrants of arrest2. Meanwhile,
permanently disabled retirees and their families continue to struggle against rising
living costs. The Philippine Statistics Authority reported that headline inflation rose
to 7.2% in April 2026, driven largely by increases in food, transportation, and utility
costs3.

       Despite these economic realities, existing law does not provide automatic
adjustments in disability pensions corresponding to salary increases granted to
active police personnel, causing many disability pensioners to receive benefits that
gradually lose value over time.

        This proposed measure provides for automatic pension adjustments based on
the prevailing scale of base pay of active police personnel. It likewise removes the
restrictive five-year survivorship limitation and ensures the continued receipt of
pensions by surviving spouses and dependent legitimate children after the death of
a permanently disabled retiree. The bill strengthens the social protection framework
for police personnel who sustain permanent disabilities in the line of duty and
guarantees that benefits remain responsive to prevailing economic conditions and
the long-term needs of affected families.

        This measure responds to a profound obligation owed to those who place
their lives at risk in service to the nation. Police officers who become permanently
disabled often carry the physical, emotional, and financial consequences of duty
long after operations end. Behind every injured officer is a family forced to confront
uncertainty, rising expenses, and the painful reality that sacrifice sometimes comes
with lifelong hardship. The nation cannot allow those who protected communities
and preserved peace and order to face abandonment once disability prevents them
from continuing service. This bill recognizes that genuine public service deserves
enduring protection, meaningful support, and a system that upholds the dignity of
those who sacrificed health and livelihood in defense of the Filipino people.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
STRENGTHENING THE DISABILITY AND SURVIVORSHIP BENEFITS OF
THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6975, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “DEPARTMENT OF
THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT ACT OF 1990”, AS AMENDED
BY REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8551

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Section 73 of Republic Act (RA) No. 6975, otherwise known as the
“Department of the Interior and Local Government Act of 1990”, as amended by RA
No. 8551, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

              “SEC. 73. Permanent Physical Disability. - An officer or
      non-officer who is permanently and totally disabled as a result of
      injuries suffered or sickness contracted in the performance of his duty
      as duly certified by the National Police Commission, upon finding and
      certification by the appropriate medical officer, that the extent of the
      disability or sickness renders such member unfit or unable to further
      perform the duties of his position, shall be entitled to one year's salary
      and to lifetime pension equivalent to eighty percent (80%) of his last
      salary, WHICH SHALL BE SUBJECT TO ADJUSTMENTS BASED
      ON THE PREVAILING SCALE OF BASE PAY OF POLICE
      PERSONNEL IN THE ACTIVE SERVICE, in addition to other
      benefits as provided under existing laws.

             “Should such member who has been retired under permanent
      total disability under this section die [within five (5) years from his
      retirement,] his surviving legal spouse or if there be none, the
      surviving dependent legitimate children shall be entitled to the
      pension [for the remainder of the (5) years guaranteed period].”

      SEC. 2. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The National Police Commission
in coordination with the Philippine National Police and the Department of the
