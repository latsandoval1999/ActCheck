                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

     The Constitution recognizes the right of farmers to receive a just share of the
fruits of their labor. It is unfortunate that in this day and age of trade globalization,
the welfare of the people who grow or raise our food has been neglected. We talk
about raising production, consumer protection, importing shortfalls, raising or
lowering tariffs, mechanization and the use of technology in farming, livestock
growing or aquaculture but never about the plight of the simple farmer, grower,
raiser who are the backbone of our food producing sector.

Our rice farmers have been plagued by losses brought about allegedly by the Rice
Tariffication Law. Whatever the reason may be, the prevailing market price for
their rice produce simply was not enough to cover the time, effort and expenses to
raise their crops. SRPs have been imposed on meat and vegetable products to
protect consumers. But who protects the supply side of the equation?

It is also a fact that our economy has suffered a severe downturn brought about by
the COVID 19 pandemic. Industries will take time to retool and rebuild but while
we wait for our industries to catch up or resume normal production, economic
experts point at the agricultural or food producing sectors as one of the sectors that
could help bring our economy back up. It is however hard to entice farmers and
food producers to work the land if it does not prove to be income generating.

This proposed measure of authorizing the Department of Agriculture to regulate
FARM GATE PRICES of agricultural produce, livestock, fisheries and aquaculture
products will ensure that our farmers, growers, fisherfolk, all the people
responsible for raising, catching or growing our food are assured of their just return
for the time, effort and expense they have incurred.

The approval of this measure is earnestly requested.

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
TO REGULATE FARM GATE PRICES FOR AGRICULTURAL AND
    FISHERIES PRODUCTS AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:

Section 1. Short Title. Farm Gate Price Regulation Act

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. The State shall protect the right of
farmers, the fisherfolk, livestock raisers and other sectors involved in food
production to receive a just share of the fruits of their labor. The State shall
therefore come up with a program that ensures the profitability of the food
production sector.

Section 3. Definition of Terms. For the purposes of this Act, the following
are hereby defined as follows:

   a. Agricultural Products refer to unprocessed food derived from
      cultivated plants or animals to sustain or enhance human life.
      Agricultural produce shall also refer to unprocessed food derived
      from the following activities:
      1. Agriculture: cultivating soil; planting; raising, and harvesting
          crops; rearing, feeding, and managing animals
      2. Aquaculture: raising private aquatic animals (fish)
      3. Horticulture: growing fruits, vegetables, and plants
   b. Fisheries Products refer to unprocessed food derived from the activity
      of catching fish and other living organisms from the wild.
   c. Farm Gate Price refers to the prices received by farmers, fisherfolk,
      livestock growers for their produce at the location of “farm” or first
      point of sale. Transportation and marketing costs are not included in
      farm gate prices.
Section 4. The Department of Agriculture is authorized to regulate
farm gate prices of agricultural and fisheries products, as defined in
Section 3 of this Act and shall therefore establish a Standard Farm Gate
Price index. The Standard Farm Gate Price index shall continuously
undergo seasonal review and appropriate changes made, taking into
account the time, effort and resources, market conditions and other
economic indicators so as to ensure profitability will accrue to the benefit
of our food producers.

Section 5. The Bureau of Agriculture and Fisheries Standards of the
Department of Agriculture shall come up with qualitative, quantitative
and other related standards which shall serve as the baseline for
determining the Standard Farm Gate Price for agricultural and
fisheries produce.

Section 6. The Department of Agriculture shall strengthen its Market
Linkages program in support of this Act to ensure that the farmers,
fisherfolk, livestock raisers and other members of the food producing
sector has an outlet for their products as well as ensure the compatibility
of supply versus demand.

Section 7. Implementing Rules. – Within ninety (90) days from
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Agriculture shall promulgate
the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of
this Act.

Section 8. Repealing Clauses. – All laws, executive orders, letters of
instructions, rules and regulations and other issuances, or provision thereof,
which are inconsistent with the of this Act are hereby repealed, amended or
modified accordingly.

Section 9. Separability Clause. – The provisions of this Act are hereby
declared separable. If any provision or portion thereof shall be held invalid
or unconstitutional, the other provisions will remain in full force and effect.

Section 10. Effectivity – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days
following its publication in two (2) national newspaper of general circulation,
or in the Official Gazette, whichever comes first.

Approved.
