AN ACT
ESTABLISHING THE MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA BARBARA AS THE
OFFICIAL CAPITAL OF THE PROVINCE OF ILOILO

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The 1987 Constitution underscores the vital importance of a responsive and accountable local
government structure anchored on local autonomy and decentralization. In this context, the
designation of a provincial capital should consider not only historical heritage but also
geographic accessibility, institutional capacity, existing infrastructure, economic potential and
its ability to support the long-term development of the province.

The Municipality of Santa Barbara, Province of Iloilo, presents a compelling basis for such
designation. It holds a distinguished place in Philippine history as the Cradle of the Revolution
in the Visayas. On November 17, 1898, it witnessed the historic Cry of Santa Barbara, where
the Philippine flag was raised for the first time outside Luzon. It also served as the military
headquarters of General Martin Teofilo B. Delgado, a revolutionary leader who later became
the first civil governor of Iloilo. Designating Santa Barbara as the official provincial capital
would provide lasting institutional recognition to this important chapter in Iloilo’s history and
its contribution to the country’s struggle for independence.

Beyond its historical significance, Santa Barbara possesses geographic and physical
characteristics that support its role as a provincial administrative center. It is a first-class
municipality comprising sixty (60) barangays and covering more than 13,000 hectares. Located
approximately fifteen (15) kilometers from Hoilo City and near the Hoilo International Airport,
it is connected by national and provincial road networks that link it with neighboring
municipalities and other parts of the province. Its location provides a practical setting for
strengthening access to provincial government services while complementing the existing
functions of Iloilo City.

Santa Barbara also has an established and diversified economic base anchored on agriculture,
commerce, transportation and services. Its agricultural resources, growing commercial activity
and strategic location provide opportunities for further development in agribusiness,
manufacturing, tourism and other investments. Its development as a provincial center may
likewise support a more balanced distribution of economic activity and infrastructure across
Tloilo.

Equally significant is Santa Barbara’s demonstrated institutional and public-service capacity.
The municipality has an established network of health, education, disaster-response and other
public facilities serving its sixty (60) barangays. Its local government has likewise
demonstrated capacity in revenue administration and public-sector management, including
recognition for its performance in real property tax collection and assessment. These existing
capabilities provide a practical foundation for assuming a broader role in provincial
administration.

The municipality is also already becoming a site for provincial government infrastructure. The
development of the Iloilo Government Center in Barangay Bolong Oeste, together with existing
provincial government properties in Santa Barbara, provides a concrete foundation for
strengthening government-related activities and inter-agency coordination. The ongoing
development of this government center demonstrates that the municipality is not being
proposed solely on the basis of future potential, but also on existing institutional and
infrastructural foundations.

By bringing together Santa Barbara’s historical significance, geographic accessibility,
economic base, public-service infrastructure and demonstrated institutional capacity, this
measure provides a practical foundation for recognizing the municipality as the official capital
of Iloilo Province. It seeks to strengthen the province’s administrative and developmental
framework while supporting a more balanced and sustainable distribution of government
activity and economic growth.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Official Capital. - The Municipality of Santa Barbara, in the Province of [loilo,
is hereby designated as the official capital of the Province of Iloilo.

SEC. 2. Implementation and Continuity. — The Provincial Government of Iloilo, in
coordination with relevant national government agencies, is authorized to adopt such
administrative measures as may be appropriate to give effect to this designation, in accordance
with applicable laws, rules, and regulations, and without disruption to ongoing public services
and government operations.

SEC. 3. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 4. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, regulations, or parts
thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SEC. 5. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
