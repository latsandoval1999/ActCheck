                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The State recognizes the right of every Filipino to health and adopts an
integrated and comprehensive approach to health development. Senior citizens,
who are among the most vulnerable sectors of society, are particularly
susceptible to chronic kidney disease and other renal illnesses that require
regular dialysis treatments for survival.

      Despite existing government health programs, many senior citizens
continue to face significant financial burdens arising from the costs of
hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, renal replacement therapies, related laboratory
procedures, medicines, and supplies. For elderly patients who rely mainly on
limited pensions or family support, the continuing expenses associated with
dialysis often result in delayed treatment, financial hardship, and reduced
quality of life.

      This measure seeks to guarantee full coverage of medically necessary
hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and other Department of Health-approved
renal replacement therapies for all senior citizens through PhilHealth-accredited
health facilities, thereby ensuring that no elderly Filipino is deprived of life-
sustaining treatment due to financial constraints.

      By strengthening health protection for senior citizens and promoting
equitable access to essential healthcare services, this bill advances the
constitutional mandate to protect and promote the right to health of the people.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           “AN ACT
     PROVIDING FREE HEMODIALYSIS, PERITONEAL DIALYSIS,
     AND OTHER RENAL REPLACEMENT THERAPIES FOR SENIOR
     CITIZENS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”

            Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
      Philippines in Congress assembled:

            SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the
     "Free Dialysis and Renal Replacement Therapy for Senior Citizens Act."

             SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the
     policy of the State to protect and promote the health and well-being
     of senior citizens by ensuring equitable, affordable, and timely access
     to life-sustaining dialysis and renal replacement therapies, consistent
     with the Constitution, the Universal Health Care Act, and laws
     protecting senior citizens.

           SECTION 3. Full Coverage of Dialysis and Renal
     Replacement Therapy for Senior Citizens. – All senior citizens who
     are medically diagnosed with chronic kidney disease or other renal
     conditions requiring renal replacement therapy shall be entitled to full
     coverage of medically necessary hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and
     other Department of Health (DOH)-approved dialysis or renal
     replacement therapies through PhilHealth-accredited health facilities.

            PhilHealth shall provide full reimbursement for such services,
     including necessary renal replacement therapies, professional fees,
     medicines, laboratory examinations, and medical supplies directly
     related to the treatment, in accordance with standards jointly
     prescribed by the DOH and PhilHealth.
      No qualified senior citizen shall be charged any co-payment,
balance billing, or other out-of-pocket expense for covered services
under this Act.

      SECTION 4. Funding. – The amounts necessary to implement
this Act shall be sourced from PhilHealth funds, appropriations
authorized under the General Appropriations Act, and such other
sources as may be provided by law. The DOH and PhilHealth shall
ensure the availability of funds necessary for the effective
implementation of this Act.

      SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within
ninety (90) days from the approval of this Act, the DOH, PhilHealth,
and the National Commission on Senior Citizens (NCSC), in
coordination with other implementing agencies, shall formulate
rules and guidelines as may be necessary for the proper
implementation and enforcement of this Act.

        SECTION 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this
Act or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions or sections
of this Act and the application of such provision or section to other
persons or circumstances, not affected thereby shall continue to be in
full force and effect.

      SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential
decrees or issuances, executive orders, letters of instruction,
administrative orders, rules or regulations contrary to or inconsistent
with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, modified or
amended accordingly.

     SECTION 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen
(15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a
newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved.
