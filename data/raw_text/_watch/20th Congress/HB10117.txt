                           AN ACT
MANDATING THE IMPOSITION OF LESSER PENALTIES IN CRIMINAL CASES
WHERE THE CORRESPONDING CIVIL ASPECT HAVE BEEN SETTLED
THROUGH ALTERNATIVE DISPUTE RESOLUTION, AMENDING FOR THE
PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9285, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
"ALTERNATIVE DISPUTE RESOLUTION ACT OF 2004"

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) has steadily evolved into an invaluable
and highly effective legal mechanism within our judicial system, offering an efficient
paradigm for the settlement of complex controversies and significantly mitigating the
protracted nature of traditional courtroom litigation.

       In order to incentivize the adoption of ADR mechanisms and systematically
strengthen its practice across the country, this measure introduces a restorative and
mitigating benefit favorable to the accused through a grant and reduction of the
imposable principal penalty in the event of a conviction in criminal proceedings,
provided that the civil liability arising from the offense has been conclusively settled
with finality through authorized ADR processes. This is also aimed to significantly
expedite the disposition of criminal dockets and optimize judicial economy.

         By accelerating the determination, execution, and full satisfaction of civil
liabilities which are legally deemed impliedly instituted alongside the criminal action
this measure ensures a more swift, harmonious, and equitable administration of
justice for both the aggrieved parties and the state.

       In view of the foregoing, the approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:


 1         SECTION 1. A new Section 7 is hereby inserted under Chapter 1 of Republic
 2   Act No. 9285 which shall read as follows:
 3
 4         “SEC. 7. EFFECTS OF ADR ON CRIMINAL CASES. — SUBJECT TO
 5         THE IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING SECTION OF THIS ACT, IN
 6         CRIMINAL CASES WHERE THE CIVIL ASPECT OF WHICH IS
 7         SETTLED WITH FINALITY THROUGH ADR, THE PENALTY TO BE
 8         IMPOSED SHALL BE AT LEAST TWO (2) DEGREES LOWER THAN
 9         THAT PRESCRIBED BY LAW. IF THE SUBJECT OFFENSE CANNOT
10         BE LOWERED BY TWO (2) DEGREES PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 61
11         OF THE REVISED PENAL CODE, THEN THE PENALTY TO BE
12         IMPOSED SHALL BE FROM THE MINIMUM TO THE NEXT LOWER
13         PENALTY PRESCRIBED BY LAW.”
14
15         The succeeding sections are hereby renumbered accordingly.
