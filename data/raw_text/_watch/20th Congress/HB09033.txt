                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Price Act (Republic Act No. 7581) was enacted to protect consumers
against hoarding, profiteering, and cartels, particularly during periods of
calamity or emergency. It classifies essential goods into two categories: Basic
Necessities and Prime Commodities.

While the current law covers a wide range of goods—from rice and salt to
plywood and school supplies—it notably excludes one of the most significant
drivers of inflation, which is fuel. Gasoline and diesel are the lifeblood of the
economy. When fuel prices spike, a domino effect ensues, raising the cost of
transporting food, the operation of public utilities, and the manufacturing of
nearly every consumer good.

Currently, petroleum products are regulated primarily under the Downstream
Oil Industry Deregulation Act of 1998 (RA 8479), which favors market-driven
pricing. However, this leaves the government with limited tools to intervene
when fuel prices become unconscionable or when the public is subjected to
"price shocks."

By classifying gasoline and diesel as Prime Commodities, this bill grants the
government the authority to include fuel in the monitoring list of the Price
Coordinating Council, impose price ceilings during emergencies or periods of
abnormal price fluctuations, and strictly penalize price manipulation
specifically related to fuel products.

This amendment is a proactive step toward economic stability and the
protection of the Filipino people's purchasing power.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT INCLUDING GASOLINE AND DIESEL AS PRIME COMMODITIES,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7581, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS THE "PRICE ACT," AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC ACT NO.
10623, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:
      SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Fuel Price
Protection Act."
      SEC. 2. Amendment. - Section 3, Paragraph (8) of Republic Act No. 7581,
otherwise known as the "Price Act," as amended by Republic Act No. 10623, is hereby
amended to read as follows:

      "(8) ‘Prime Commodities’ are goods not considered as basic necessities
      but are essential to consumers in times of any of the cases provided
      under Section 7 of this Act such as, but not limited to, flour; dried,
      processed or canned pork, beef and poultry meat; dairy products not
      falling under basic necessities; onions, garlic, vinegar, patis, soy sauce;
      toilet soap; fertilizer, pesticides and herbicides; poultry, livestock and
      fishery feeds and veterinary products; paper; school supplies; nipa
      shingles; sawali; cement; clinker; GI sheets; hollow blocks; plywood;
      plyboard; construction nails; batteries; electrical supplies; light bulbs;
      steel wire; GASOLINE; DIESEL; and all drugs not classified as essential
      drugs by the Department of Health and such other goods as may be
      included under Section 4 of this Act."
      SEC. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). - Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Trade and Industry
(DTI), in coordination with the Department of Energy (DOE) and the
Department of Agriculture (DA), shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.
     SEC. 4. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.
      SEC. 5. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
