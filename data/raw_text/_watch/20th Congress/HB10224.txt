                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

Public school teachers are the backbone of our nation’s educational system. They
are tasked not only with imparting knowledge but also with shaping the character
and future of Filipino youth. Despite their invaluable contribution to society,
many public school teachers continue to face economic hardships, particularly
regarding affordable and decent housing.

Due to modest salaries and the rising costs of real estate, a significant number of
our teachers are forced to rent substandard housing, live in areas far from their
designated schools, or rely on high-interest loans that further deplete their take-
home pay. Long commutes and poor living conditions contribute to physical
exhaustion and mental stress, which ultimately impact the quality of education
delivered to our students.

This bill, otherwise known as the "Public School Teachers’ Housing Act," seeks to
address this pressing issue by institutionalizing a comprehensive, accessible, and
affordable housing program specifically designed for active public school
teachers. By providing low-interest housing loans, subsidized amortization, and
dedicated on-site housing projects near public schools, this measure aims to
uplift the living standards of our educators, ensure their financial security, and
motivate them to remain in the noble profession of teaching.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT INSTITUTING A COMPREHENSIVE HOUSING PROGRAM FOR
 PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS, PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR
                              OTHER PURPOSES
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:


      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Safe Schools and
Learning Environments Act."

      Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right of all citizens to quality education, and to recognize the vital
role of teachers in national development. Towards this end, the State shall
implement measures that promote the welfare, economic security, and dignity of
public school teachers, including the provision of decent, affordable, and
accessible housing.

      Sec. 3. Establishment of the Public School Teachers’ Housing Program. -
There is hereby established a Comprehensive Housing Program for Public School
Teachers. The Program shall be jointly implemented by the Department of
Education (DepEd), the Department of Human Settlements and Urban
Development (DHSUD), and the National Housing Authority (NHA), in
coordination with key shelter agencies such as the Home Development Mutual
Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund).

      Sec. 4. Scope and Coverage – This Act shall apply to all regular public
school teachers in public basic education institutions, including elementary,
junior high, and senior high schools under the jurisdiction of the Department of
Education (DepEd).
      Sec. 5. Components of the Program - The Program shall include, but not be
limited to, the following components:

   1. On-Site and Near-Site Housing Projects: The NHA and DHSUD shall
      identify and develop idle government lands for the construction of
      residential units, medium-rise buildings, or subdivisions dedicated
      exclusively to public school teachers. Priority shall be given to sites
      accessible to public schools.

   2. Subsidized Housing Loans: The Pag-IBIG Fund shall allocate a special
      window for public school teachers, providing housing loans with lower
      interest rates (not exceeding three percent per annum) and extended
      repayment periods of up to thirty (30) years.

   3. Rent-to-Own Schemes: For teachers who cannot immediately afford down
      payments, a rent-to-own mechanism shall be developed for government-
      built housing units.

      Sec. 6. Incentives for Private Developers. – Private real estate developers
who partner with the government to construct housing units specifically allocated
for the Program shall be entitled to tax incentives, including exemption from
project-related value-added tax (VAT) and expedited processing of development
permits.

     Sec. 7. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year's
appropriations of the DepEd and DHSUD. Thereafter, such sums as may be
necessary for its continued implementation shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act (GAA).

       Sec. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DepEd, DHSUD, NHA, and Pag-IBIG Fund, in
consultation with recognized teachers' unions and organizations, shall
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation
of this Act.

       Sec. 9. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not
affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

      Sec. 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders,
presidential issuances, and other administrative rules and regulations or parts
thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or
modified accordingly.
      Sec. 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
