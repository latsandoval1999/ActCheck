                            EXPLANATORY NOTE
      This proposed measure seeks the statutory recognition and creation of
Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo in the Municipality of Tupi, Province of South
Cotabato, and to ensure that it is entitled to its lawful share in the National Tax
Allotment, formerly referred to as the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA).
       Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo was created pursuant to Sangguniang
Panlalawigan Resolution No. 158, Series of 2023, which enacted Provincial
Ordinance No. 14, Series of 2023. Its creation was ratified in a plebiscite conducted
by the Commission on Elections on 13 July 2024.
       Section 285 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local
Government Code of 1991, provides that the financial requirements of barangays
created by local government units after the effectivity of the Code shall be the
responsibility of the local government unit concerned. Statutory recognition is
therefore necessary to place Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo on the same fiscal
footing as other barangays and to enable it to receive its corresponding share from
national taxes.
       Barangays are the basic political units through which essential public services
are delivered directly to communities. Providing Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo with
a regular and predictable share in national revenues will strengthen its capacity to
finance basic services, local development programs, public infrastructure, disaster
preparedness, and other responsibilities entrusted to barangay governments.
      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this Bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:
       SECTION 1. Creation of Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo. - There is
hereby created a barangay to be known as Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo in the
Municipality of Tupi, Province of South Cotabato. The barangay shall retain the
territorial boundaries established under Provincial Ordinance No. 14, Series of 2023,
and as ratified in the plebiscite for its creation.
       SEC. 2. Plebiscite. - The plebiscite conducted and supervised by the
Commission on Elections (COMELEC) on 13 July 2024, pursuant to Sangguniang
Panlalawigan Resolution No. 158, Series of 2023, and Provincial Ordinance No. 14,
Series of 2023, which ratified the creation of Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo, shall
serve as substantial compliance with the plebiscite requirement under Section 10 of
Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, otherwise known as the Local Government
Code of 1991.
        SEC. 3. Incumbent Barangay Officials. - The incumbent elected barangay
officials of Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo shall continue to hold office until such
time that their successors have been duly elected and qualified.
       SEC. 4. Public Infrastructure, Assets, and Facilities. - All existing public
infrastructure, assets, and facilities belonging to or administered by Barangay Juan-
Loreto Tamayo at the time of the approval of this Act shall be retained by and
administered by the barangay.
       SEC. 5. National Tax Allotment Share. - Barangay Juan-Loreto Tamayo
shall be entitled to its corresponding share in the National Tax Allotment, formerly
referred to as the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA), in accordance with Section 285
of Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, and other applicable laws, rules, and
regulations.
      SEC. 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
      Approved.
