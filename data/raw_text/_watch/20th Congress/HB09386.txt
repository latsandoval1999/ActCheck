                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippine Racing Commission was created under Presidential Decree No.
420 in 1974 to supervise and regulate horse racing in the Philippines. After
several decades, however, the legal framework governing the racing industry
needs to be updated to respond to present regulatory, developmental, fiscal,
and social protection concerns.

This bill seeks to reconstitute the Philippine Racing Commission into the
Philippine Racing Corporation, a government-owned or controlled corporation
with original charter. The proposed reconstitution will allow the agency to
exercise its regulatory mandate with greater institutional capacity, corporate
flexibility, and financial accountability. As a GOCC, the Corporation will be
better equipped to manage revenues, administer industry development
programs, support the growth and modernization of the racing industry, and
professionalize its governance subject to the oversight mechanisms applicable
to GOCCs. A stronger and more competitive racing industry may also expand
economic activity, generate employment, and increase potential government
revenues from racing-related operations.

The bill strengthens the Corporation as the primary regulatory and
developmental authority for horse racing and other authorized animal racing
in the Philippines. It rationalizes its powers and functions, expands its
jurisdiction to other forms of animal racing, and provides a clearer framework
for licensing, regulation, enforcement, and revenue monitoring.
The measure also enhances the welfare of jockeys and horse trainers by
institutionalizing and strengthening the Jockeys and Horse Trainers Injury,
Disability and Death Compensation Fund. This recognizes the occupational
risks faced by racing personnel and provides a mechanism for injury,
disability, death, medical, and other welfare benefits.

The bill likewise places the National Stud Farm under the supervision and
control of the Philippine Racing Corporation to better align horse breeding,
genetic improvement, racehorse development, and animal health programs
with the broader modernization of the racing industry.

Finally, the bill provides for the transfer of assets, personnel, records, pending
matters, licenses, permits, and issuances from the existing Commission to
the new Corporation to ensure continuity of operations. It repeals Presidential
Decree No. 420, as amended, and amends or repeals Republic Act No. 6115
insofar as inconsistent with the proposed Act.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                               AN ACT
     RECONSTITUTING THE PHILIPPINE RACING COMMISSION INTO THE
     PHILIPPINE RACING CORPORATION, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Republic
     of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

1         SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Philippine
2    Racing Corporation Modernization and Social Protection Act.”

3          SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the
4    State to:

5       a. Promote, develop, regulate, and modernize horse racing and other
6          authorized animal racing as sports, recreation, tourism-related
7          activities, and legitimate sources of government revenue, consistent
8          with integrity, fairness, animal welfare, transparency, and public trust;

 9      b. Strengthen the Philippine Racing Corporation as the primary regulatory
10         and developmental authority for horse racing and other authorized
11         animal racing in the Philippines, ensuring a level playing field among
12         racing clubs, horse owners, breeders, horse trainers, jockeys, and other
13         stakeholders in the racing industry;
14      c. Ensure that the government’s share and all other revenues derived from
15         racing activities are collected, managed, allocated, and utilized in a
16         transparent, accountable, efficient, and development-oriented manner;
1       d. Institutionalize clear and sustainable funding allocations for the Horse
2          Racing Regulatory Program, Horse Racing Industry Development and
3          Incentive Program, the National Stud Farm, and the Jockeys and Horse
4          Trainers Injury, Disability and Death Compensation Fund;

5       e. Enhance the welfare and social protection of jockeys, horse trainers,
6          and other qualified racing personnel by strengthening and
7          institutionalizing mechanisms for injury, disability, death, medical, and
8          other welfare benefits, including the periodic review and adjustment of
9          compensation and assistance;

10      f. Strengthen and institutionalize the National Stud Farm created under
11         Republic Act No. 4618 as the primary government facility for horse
12         breeding, genetic improvement, racehorse development, and the
13         prevention of illegal importation of race horses, under the supervision
14         and control of the Philippine Racing Corporation; and

15      g. Regulate and supervise other forms of authorized animal racing as
16         sports, recreation, tourism-related activities, and legitimate sources of
17         government revenue, consistent with animal welfare, public order,
18         transparency, and responsible industry development.

19          SEC. 3. Creation of the Philippine Racing Corporation. - The
20   Philippine Racing Commission created under Presidential Decree No. 420, as
21   amended, is hereby reorganized and reconstituted into a government-owned
22   or controlled corporation with original charter, organized as a non-stock
23   corporation wholly owned and controlled by the National Government, to be
24   known as the Philippine Racing Corporation (PHILRACOR), hereinafter
25   referred to as the Corporation.

26         The Corporation shall be attached to the Office of the President for
27   policy and program coordination and shall be subject to Republic Act No.
28   10149, otherwise known as the “GOCC Governance Act of 2011,” and other
29   applicable laws, rules, and regulations governing government-owned or
30   controlled corporations.

31         The Corporation shall possess juridical personality, perpetual
32   succession, and corporate powers, including the power to sue and be sued,
33   adopt and use a corporate seal, enter into contracts, acquire, own, hold, lease,
34   encumber, and dispose of real and personal property, receive and administer
35   funds, grants, donations, revenues, fees, charges, and other lawful income,
36   maintain corporate funds and accounts, and exercise such other corporate
37   powers as may be necessary or incidental to the performance of its mandate
38   under this Act.
1          The Corporation shall serve as the primary regulatory and
2    developmental authority for horse racing and other authorized animal racing
3    in the Philippines.

4           SEC. 4. Board of Directors. - The Corporation shall be governed and
5    its activities be directed, controlled, and managed by a Board of Directors,
6    hereinafter referred to as the Board, composed of a Chairperson and six (6)
7    Directors to be appointed by the President of the Philippines in accordance
8    with Republic Act No. 10149, otherwise known as the “GOCC Governance Act
9    of 2011,” and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

10        The Chairperson and Directors shall be persons of proven integrity and
11   competence in law, finance, racing, sports administration, or related fields.

12          Provided, That of the six (6) Directors, there shall be one (1)
13   representative of the Racehorse Owners' Association, one (1) representative of
14   the Jockeys' Association, and one (1) representative of the Horse Trainers'
15   Association, none of whom shall have any substantial interest in any racing
16   club, track betting corporation, or allied entities regulated by the Corporation.

17          SEC. 5. Term of Office. - The Chairperson and Directors shall hold
18   office in accordance with Republic Act No. 10149, otherwise known as the
19   “GOCC Governance Act of 2011,” and other applicable laws, rules, and
20   regulations.

21         Each appointive Director shall hold office for a term of one (1) year,
22   unless sooner removed for cause: Provided, That the appointive Director shall
23   continue to hold office until a successor has been appointed and qualified.

24         An appointive Director may be nominated by the Governance
25   Commission for GOCCs for reappointment by the President only if such
26   Director obtains a performance score of above average or its equivalent or
27   higher in the immediately preceding year of tenure, based on the applicable
28   performance criteria for appointive directors of government-owned or
29   controlled corporations.

30          Any appointment to a vacancy shall be only for the unexpired portion
31   of the term of the predecessor and shall be made in accordance with Republic
32   Act No. 10149 and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

33         SEC. 6. Powers and Functions of the Corporation. - The Corporation
34   shall exercise, among others, the following powers and functions, without
35   prejudice to those already vested under existing law, insofar as not
36   inconsistent with this Act:
 1         a. Supervise, regulate, and control horse racing in the Philippines,
 2            including racing clubs, and all persons and entities engaged in or
 3            connected with horse racing;
 4         b. Issue, renew, suspend, or revoke licenses of jockeys, horse trainers,
 5            grooms, horse owners, racing clubs, racing officials, and other
 6            licensees;
 7         c. Formulate and enforce rules and regulations on horse racing,
 8            including the conduct of races, handicapping, licensing, and
 9            discipline of all racing participants;
10         d. Approve and monitor the racing calendar and race schedules of all
11            racing clubs;
12         e. Investigate, hear, and decide cases involving violations of racing
13            laws, rules, and regulations, and impose appropriate sanctions,
14            including fines, suspensions, and disqualifications;
15         f. Supervise, regulate, and monitor the compliance of racing clubs with
16            the franchise and tax obligations and revenue remittance
17            requirements;
18         g. Exercise the allocation of revenues from racing in accordance with
19            the legislative franchises and relevant laws;
20         h. Coordinate with relevant government agencies, such as the
21            Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), Games and
22            Amusements Board (GAB), and the Department of Agriculture (DA),
23            among others, to ensure harmonized policies in gaming, sports, and
24            revenue allocations;
25         i. Formulate and implement rules and regulations governing the
26            conduct of other animal racing to promote transparency, animal
27            welfare, and sustain public interest;
28         j. Promulgate rules and regulations necessary to carry out the
29            objectives of this Act and periodically review and revise the same in
30            consultation with stakeholders; and
31         k. Perform such other duties and exercise all other powers incidental
32            or necessary to the accomplishment of the aims and objectives of
33            this Act.

34         SEC. 7. Executive Director as Head of Office. - The Corporation shall
35   have an Executive Director who shall serve as the Head of Office and principal
36   operating officer of the Corporation, under the policy direction, control, and
37   supervision of the Board of Directors. The Executive Director shall be
38   appointed by the Board of Directors, subject to applicable laws, rules, and
39   regulations.

40       The Executive Director shall be responsible for the day-to-day
41   management, administration, and operations of the Corporation, and for the
1    implementation of the policies, programs, and directives approved by the
2    Board.

3           The Executive Director shall exercise supervision and control over the
4    officers and employees of the Corporation and shall have authority, upon
5    approval of the Board, to appoint, promote, discipline, and remove personnel,
6    and to create, reclassify, reorganize, or abolish positions, subject to civil
7    service laws, the rules and regulations of the Governance Commission for
8    GOCCs, the Department of Budget and Management, the Commission on
9    Audit, and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

4                            CHAPTER III
5       JOCKEYS AND HORSE TRAINERS INJURY, DISABILITY AND DEATH
6                        COMPENSATION FUND

 7          SEC. 11. Jockeys and Horse Trainers Injury, Disability and Death
 8   Compensation Fund. - There is hereby institutionalized the Jockeys and
 9   Horse Trainers Injury, Disability and Death Compensation Fund, hereinafter
10   referred to as the Compensation Fund, which shall provide financial and
11   related benefits for work-connected injury, disability, or death of licensed
12   jockeys and horse trainers. The Compensation Fund may also extend
13   financial aid or welfare assistance to such licensees, subject to the limits and
14   conditions prescribed by the Corporation under this Act.

15         SEC. 12. Coverage. - The Compensation Fund shall cover all duly
16   licensed jockeys and horse trainers licensed by the Corporation who are in
17   good standing at the time of the injury, disability, or death, or at the time of
18   application for eligible financial assistance.

19         SEC. 13. Sources of the Fund. - The Compensation Fund shall be
20   sourced from:

21      a. Statutory shares or earmarked percentages of racing revenues, as may
22         be provided by law;
23      b. Donations, grants, endowments, and such other sources as may be
24         allowed by law; and
25      c. Such other amounts as may be lawfully allocated by the Corporation,
26         subject to applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

27          All such amounts shall be maintained in a separate and distinct
28   account and shall be used exclusively for the purposes set forth in this
29   Chapter. Amounts accruing to the Compensation Fund shall be treated as
30   special or trust funds held for the benefit of qualified beneficiaries and shall
31   not form part of the unrestricted corporate income or net earnings of the
32   Corporation available for dividends, surplus distribution, or general corporate
33   expenses, subject to applicable accounting, auditing, and public finance
34   rules.

35        SEC. 14. Management and Administration of the Fund. - The
36   Corporation shall administer and manage the Compensation Fund for this
37   purpose, shall have the authority to:
 1         a. Promulgate rules and regulations governing eligibility, documentary
 2            requirements, procedures, and the computation of benefits;
 3         b. Receive, evaluate, process, and investigate applications for claims
 4            and benefits under the Fund;
 5         c. Grant financial aid, subject to clear limitations and guidelines, to
 6            qualified jockeys and horse trainers in accordance with this Act and
 7            the rules issued hereunder;
 8         d. Periodically review and, when necessary, revise the schedule of
 9            benefits and compensation in light of actuarial projections, the
10            financial viability of the Fund, the financial condition of the
11            Corporation, and prevailing socio-economic conditions, after
12            consultation with industry stakeholders and appropriate
13            government agencies;
14         e. Monitor and evaluate the status and utilization of the Compensation
15            Fund; and
16         f. Exercise such other powers as may be necessary or incidental to
17            effectively manage and protect the Compensation Fund.

18          SEC. 15. Schedule of Compensation and Benefits. - Subject to
19   periodic review and adjustment by the Corporation, the benefits that may be
20   granted under the Compensation Fund shall include, but not be limited to,
21   the following:

22         a. Death Benefit – In case of death of a covered jockey or horse trainer
23            arising from or related to the performance of his or her licensed
24            duties, a lump sum benefit of not less than Thirty Thousand Pesos
25            (P30,000.00) shall be paid to his or her beneficiaries;
26         b. Permanent Disability Benefit – In case of work-related permanent
27            disability, the licensed jockey or trainer shall receive a benefit of not
28            less than Twenty Thousand Pesos (P20,000.00);
29         c. Hospitalization and Medical Benefit – For confinement, treatment, or
30            care arising from illness or accident directly connected with racing
31            activities or official duties, the Fund may reimburse up to an amount
32            not exceeding Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) per year per covered
33            person;
34         d. Housing or Shelter Assistance – The Fund may extend financial
35            assistance for the acquisition of modestly priced housing or lots by
36            eligible jockeys or horse trainers, in an amount not exceeding Fifty
37            Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00) per beneficiary.

38   The Corporation shall review and may increase the foregoing amounts after
39   five (5) years from the effectivity of this Act and every five (5) years thereafter:
40   Provided, That any increase shall be based on the financial condition and
1    projected life of the Fund, after appropriate consultation with stakeholders
2    and appropriate government agencies.

3    All benefits granted hereunder shall be released in accordance with the rules
4    and regulations promulgated by the Corporation governing eligibility,
5    documentary requirements, procedures, and the computation and release of
6    benefits.

 7         SEC. 16. Coordination with Other Social Protection Systems. -
 8   Benefits from the Compensation Fund shall be without prejudice to any other
 9   benefits that the covered person may receive from the Social Security System
10   (SSS), Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), PhilHealth, Employees’
11   Compensation Commission, or private insurance and benefit schemes,
12   provided that there shall be no double recovery for the same component of
13   loss where prohibited by law.

14         SEC. 17. Audit and Transparency. - The Compensation Fund shall
15   be subject to annual audit by the COA. The Corporation shall promote
16   transparency and accountability in managing the Compensation Fund
17   through publication on its website or in such medium as it may determine,
18   an annual report on the status of the Fund.

19                                   CHAPTER IV
20                               NATIONAL STUD FARM

21         SEC. 18. National Stud Farm. - The National Stud Farm, created
22   under Republic Act No. 4618 to improve the breed of Philippine horses and to
23   prevent the illegal importation of race horses, is hereby placed under the
24   supervision and control of the Philippine Racing Corporation.

25          All functions, programs, assets, records, equipment, facilities, and
26   resources of the National Stud Farm which are, at the time of effectivity of this
27   Act, being administered by the Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI) and other
28   units of the Department of Agriculture (DA), are hereby transferred to the
29   Corporation, consistent with the goal of streamlining the bureaucracy to align
30   efficiency, economy, and effectiveness in operations.

31          SEC. 19. Mandate and Functions of the National Stud Farm. -
32   Under the supervision and control of the Corporation, the National Stud Farm
33   shall:

34         a. Implement horse-breeding and genetic improvement programs to
35            upgrade the Philippine horse breed and ensure a sustainable supply
36            of quality horses for racing and other purposes;
 1         b. Establish and maintain stud books, registries, and other records
 2            necessary for the identification, traceability, and breeding history of
 3            horses;
 4         c. Promote and supervise the importation, quarantine, and use of
 5            suitable breeding stock in a manner consistent with animal health,
 6            biosecurity, and customs law, and prevent illegal importation of race
 7            horses;
 8         d. Provide technical assistance, training, and advisory services to
 9            breeders and horse owners;
10         e. Coordinate with relevant government agencies, such as the
11            Department of Agriculture, the Bureau of Animal Industry, and local
12            government units, on programs and regulations affecting horse
13            breeding and animal health; and
14         f. Perform such other functions as may be prescribed by the
15            Corporation consistent with its mandate under this Act and
16            Republic Act No. 4618.

17         SEC. 20. Integration with the Compensation Fund and Industry
18   Programs. - The Corporation shall ensure that the programs of the National
19   Stud Farm are harmonized with:

20         a. The Jockeys and Horse Trainers Injury, Disability and Death
21            Compensation Fund, through programs that enhance the safety of
22            horses, jockeys, horse trainers, and other racing participants
23            through improved breeding, conditioning, veterinary standards, and
24            animal health practices; and
25         b. Industry development initiatives, including incentives for local
26            breeders, programs to widen access to quality breeding stock, and
27            measures to improve the competitiveness, sustainability, and
28            professionalism of the Philippine racing and breeding industry.

29   The Corporation shall adopt measures to ensure that revenues generated by
30   horse racing and earmarked for horse-breeding and National Stud Farm
31   activities are efficiently utilized and directly contribute to the long-term
32   development of the racing and breeding industry.

36   regulate and supervise other forms of animal racing conducted as a sport,
