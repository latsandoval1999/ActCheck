                            AN ACT
     PROVIDING FOR THE LIFETIME VALIDITY OF PERSONS WITH
    DISABILITY IDENTIFICATION CARDS ISSUED TO PERSONS WITH
  PERMANENT DISABILITY, FURTHER AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
 REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “MAGNA CARTA
              FOR DISABLED PERSONS”, AS AMENDED

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       In the Philippines, persons with disabilities (PWDs) represent at least
12% of the adult population. According to the Department of Health and the
UN Convention on the Rights of Persons With Disabilities, PWDs include those
who have long-term physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory impairments
which, in interaction with various barriers, may hinder their full and effective
participation in society.1

      The State’s commitment to removing barriers and assisting persons
with disabilities in their reintegration to mainstream society is enshrined in
Republic Act No. 7277 or the Magna Carta for Disabled Persons. However,
presently, PWDs are required to renew their IDs every three (3) years for the
re-evaluation of their entitlement to the benefits pertaining to their status as
a person with disability. This additional bureaucracy creates unwanted
hurdles, especially for persons with total permanent disability.

       A permanent disability is a mental or physical illness or a condition that
affects a major life function over the long term. A permanent disability could




1 https://doh.gov.ph/persons-with-disabilities
be as severe as the loss of an eye or as moderate as a broken leg leaving the
inability to walk on grossly uneven surfaces.

      After dialogues and consultations with advocates, this Representation
echoes the sentiments that the amendment of the relevant provisions of
Republic Act No. 7277, as amended, so as to provide lifetime validity of PWD
IDs, would be a welcome relief for PWDs with permanent disabilities such as
pre-existing birth defects, permanent blindness or deafness, or loss of limb or
body parts, sparing them the burden and inconvenience of renewing their
PWD IDs every three years.

       This bill recognizes the important contributions of persons with
disabilities and their well-deserved reintegration to society. Thus, this
proposed legislation allows for the issuance of PWD cards with lifetime validity
for persons with permanent disabilities, duly certified by the Municipal or City
Health Office where the person with disability resides.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
     PROVIDING FOR THE LIFETIME VALIDITY OF PERSONS WITH
    DISABILITY IDENTIFICATION CARDS ISSUED TO PERSONS WITH
  PERMANENT DISABILITY, FURTHER AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
 REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “MAGNA CARTA
              FOR DISABLED PERSONS”, AS AMENDED

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Lifetime Validity
of Persons with Disability Cards for Persons with Permanent Disability Act”.

SECTION 2. Amendatory Provision. – Section 32 of Republic Act No.
7277, otherwise known as the “Magna Carta for Disabled Persons”, as
amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

      “SEC. 32. Persons with disabilities shall be entitled to the
      following:

      XXX.

      The above-mentioned privileges are available only to persons
      with disability who are Filipino citizens upon submission of
      any of the following as proof of his/her entitlement thereto:

      (I)    An identification card issued by the city or municipal
             mayor or the barangay captain of the place where the
             person with disability resides;
      (II)   The passport of the persons with disabilities concerned;
             or
      (III)   Transportation discount fare Identification Card (ID)
              issued by the National Council for the Welfare of
              Disabled Persons (NCWDP) ON DISABILITY AFFAIRS
              (NCDA).

      THE IDENTIFICATION CARDS ISSUED TO PERSONS WITH
      PERMANENT DISABILITY AS PROVIDED IN (I) AND (III) OF
      THE PRECEDING PARAGRAPH SHALL BE VALID DURING
      THE LIFETIME OF THE HOLDER, AND MAY BE RENEWED
      IN CASE OF LOSS OR DAMAGE: PROVIDED, THAT SUCH
      PERMANENT    DISABILITY   IS  CERTIFIED   BY    THE
      MUNICIPAL OR CITY HEALTH OFFICE WHERE THE
      PERSON    WITH   DISABILITY   RESIDES;   PROVIDED
      FURTHER,   THAT   THE    ISSUING  OFFICES    SHALL
      IMPLEMENT MEASURES TO ENSURE THAT THE LIFETIME
      VALIDITY OF THE IDENTIFICATION CARDS IS NOT
      ABUSED, ESPECIALLY WHEN THE HOLDER OF THE ID
      DIES.”

SECTION 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the National Council on Disability
Affairs shall, in coordination with the Department of Interior and Local
Government, Department of Health, Department of Social Welfare and
Development, and other relevant government agencies and stakeholders,
issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

SECTION 4. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any provision of this
Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, parts or provisions of this Act
which are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees, Implementing Rules and Regulations inconsistent with this Act are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
