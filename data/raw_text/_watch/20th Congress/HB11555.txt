EXPLANATORY NOTE

Violence against women and their children (VAWC), including trafficking in persons, online
sexual abuse and exploitation of children (OSAEC), child sexual abuse or exploitation materials
(CSAEM), neglect, and discrimination, remains pervasive and complex. Section 9(d) of Republic
Act No. 9710 (Magna Carta of Women) mandates a VAWC Desk in every barangay. Yet frontline
practice is hampered by politically driven turnover, uneven training, and limited welfare support
for the designated personnel.

This measure institutionalizes the designation, qualifications, tenure protection, functions, and
welfare support of Barangay VAWC Desk Officers nationwide; establishes Regional and
Provincial/City VAWC Desk Unit Training and Resource Centers; and strengthens national-to-
local governance, data protection, and financing. It advances State obligations under:

RA 9208, as amended by RA 10364 and RA 11862 (Anti-Trafficking in Persons Acts),
which require vigilant community identification, immediate referral, and robust
protection for trafficked or at-risk women and children;

RA 11930 (Anti-OSAEC and CSAEM Act), mandating prevention, reporting, and
referral of online sexual abuse and exploitation of children and child sexual abuse or
exploitation materials, with clear protocols for frontline responders;

RA 7610 (Special Protection of Children from Abuse, Neglect, and Discrimination Act),
emphasizing LGUs’ duties as first responders and child- rights duty-bearers;

RA 9262 (Anti-VAWC Act of 2004), which this bill operationalizes at the barangay
level;

RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), to ensure confidentiality and safe data
processing; and

RA 11036 (Mental Health Act), to guarantee access to psychosocial care for survivors
and frontline workers.

ARTICLE I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. Short title. — This Act shall be known as the “VAW Desk Institutionalization,
Training, and Support Act.”

SECTION. 2. Declaration of policy and objectives. — It is the policy of the State to:

(a) Protect the rights and welfare of women and children through survivor, centered,
child-friendly, trauma-informed, and gender-responsive services;

(b) Institutionalize functioning VAWC Desks in all barangays, with trained, accountable,
and adequately supported personnel;

(c) Establish Regional and Provincial/City Training and Resource Centers (TRCs) to
standardize training, supervision, and quality assurance;

(d) Integrate community responses to trafficking in persons, OSAEC/CSAEM, and other
child protection concerns consistent with RA 9208, as amended; RA 11930; and RA
7610;

(e) Ensure sustainable financing, information security, and inter-agency coordination
consistent with RA 9262, RA 9710, RA 10173, RA 11036, and related laws.

SECTION. 3. Coverage. — This Act applies to all barangays, municipalities, cities, provinces,
highly urbanized and independent component cities, and the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region

in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM), without prejudice to higher standards adopted by LGUs or
BARMM ministries.

SECTION. 4. Definition of terms. — For purposes of this Act:

(a) VAWC Desk — The barangay service point mandated to receive, assist, and refer
survivors of violence against women and their children, including trafficking and
OSAEC/CSAEM-related cases, consistent with RA 9710 and relevant issuances.

(b) VAWC Desk Officer — The trained and designated barangay personnel who perform
the functions of the VAWC Desk, including coordination, documentation, basic case
management support, and referral.

===== [page 4] =====
(c) Violence Against Women and their Children (VAWC) — Acts defined in RA 9262 and
related laws, including physical, sexual, psychological, and economic abuse.

(d) Trafficking in Persons (TIP) — Acts defined under RA 9208, as amended by RA 10364
and RA 11862, including attempted trafficking and trafficking in children.

(e) Online Sexual Abuse and Exploitation of Children (OSAEC) — Offenses defined in RA
11930, including CSAEM and related online-facilitated crimes.

(f) Child — A person below 18 years of age, or over 18 but unable to fully care for or
protect themselves from abuse, neglect, cruelty, exploitation, or discrimination as
defined under applicable laws including RA 7610 and RA11930.

(g) Survivor-centered approach — Ensuring safety, confidentiality, respect, informed
consent, and autonomy of survivors in all interventions.

(h) Child-friendly procedures — Measures that uphold the best interests of the child,
with developmentally appropriate communication and safeguards.

(i) Qualified dependents — The legitimate, legitimated, acknowledged illegitimate, or
legally adopted child or children of a VAWC Desk Officer who are primarily dependent
on the officer for support and who meet age or school-enrollment criteria in the IRR.

(j) Support programs — Welfare, educational, health, mental health, capacity- building,
equipment, recognition, and professional development benefits under this Act,
subject to DBM and COA rules.

(k) Training and Resource Center (TRC) — Regional and Provincial/City facilities
established under this Act to deliver training, supervision, technical assistance,
knowledge management, surge support, and quality assurance.

(1) Referral pathway — A documented sequence of service providers and authorities for
protection, investigation, medical, psychosocial, legal, shelter, and reintegration
services, including agencies designated under RA 9208, RA 11930, and RA 7610.

(m) Implementing agencies — DILG as lead, in coordination with DSWD, DOH,
DepEd, DOJ, PNP, PCW, DICT, and other agencies; and the counterpart BARMM
ministries.

ARTICLE II

INSTITUTIONALIZATION OF BARANGAY VAWC DESKS AND OFFICERS

SECTION. 5. Establishment of VAWC Desks. — Every barangay shall maintain a functioning
VAWC Desk accessible to the public during barangay operating hours, with 24/7 emergency
referral protocols. At least one (1) trained VAWC Desk Officer shall be designated; larger or high-
incidence barangays may designate additional officers pursuant to DBM and COA rules.

SECTION. 6. Designation, qualifications, and disqualifications. —

(a) Designation shall be by Sangguniang Barangay Resolution upon recommendation of
the Punong Barangay, recorded with the DILG Local Government Operations Officer
(LGOO) and the City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (C/MSWDO).

(b) Minimum qualifications: completion of core training modules prescribed by the
National Program: demonstrated gender sensitivity; adherence to survivor- centered

===== [page 5] =====
and child-friendly principles; and familiarity with RA 9262, RA 9208 (as amended),
RA 11930, RA 7610, and data privacy and evidence preservation basics.

(c) Disqualifications: immediate family members of the incumbent Punong Barangay;
persons with pending administrative or criminal cases involving gender-based
violence, trafficking, or child abuse; or those who violated confidentiality or ethical
standards, as defined in the IRR.

SECTION. 7. Tenure, due process, and continuity. — The VAWC Desk Officer shall serve
coterminous with the appointing Punong Barangay unless removed for just cause after due
process per the IRR. Arbitrary or politically motivated removal undermining service continuity
is prohibited. Vacancies shall be filled only after orientation or training to avoid service
disruption. Preference shall be given to incumbent trained officers with satisfactory
performance.

SECTION. 8. Functions of VAWC Desk Officers. — The Officer shall:
(a) Receive, document, and manage referrals for VAWC-related incidents with strict
confidentiality and informed consent;

(b) Identify trafficking indicators and OSAEC/CSAEM red flags and immediately activate
referral pathways to appropriate authorities and services under RA 9208 (as
amended) and RA 11930;

(c) Coordinate with PNP Women and Children Protection Desks (WCPDs), Women and
Children Protection Units (WCPUs), LSWDOs, health offices, DepEd Child Protection
Committees, prosecutors, and accredited service providers for protection, medical,
psychosocial, legal, and shelter services;

(d) Assist in applications for Barangay or Temporary Protection Orders and ensure safety
planning consistent with RA 9262 and Supreme Court rules;

(e) Undertake mandatory reporting for child abuse and exploitation cases and ensure
child-sensitive interviewing consistent with RA 7610 and applicable protocols;

(f) Preserve and transmit records and potential evidence securely, including digital
evidence in OSAEC/CSAEM cases, consistent with RA 10173, chain-of- custody
standards, and the IRR;

(g) Conduct community awareness and prevention activities aligned with GAD plans; and
(h) Perform other related duties as may be provided in the IRR.
SECTION. 9. Performance standards and evaluation. — DILG, in coordination with DSWD,
DOH, DepEd, DOJ, PNP, PCW, DICT, and other agencies, shall issue a performance framework and

tools for VAWC Desks, covering timeliness, quality, privacy compliance, and survivor and child-
safeguarding outcomes, to guide capacity-building, recognition, and continued designation.

ARTICLE Ill
NATIONAL GOVERNANCE, COORDINATION, AND STANDARDS

SECTION. 10. Lead agency and National Program. — DILG shall lead the National VAWC Desk
Program in coordination with DSWD, DOH, DepEd, DOJ, PNP, PCW, DICT, and other agencies, with
counterpart engagement of BARMM ministries. A National Technical Working Group (NTWG)
shall be convened within ninety (90) days from effectivity to issue standards, curricula, and
protocols.

SECTION. 11. Training, certification, and accreditation. — The NTWG shall prescribe tiered
training and certification for VAWC Desk Officers, supervisors, and trainers, including modules
on RA 9262, RA 9208 (as amended), RA 11930, RA 7610, trauma-informed care, data privacy,
digital safety, and evidence handling; recognize prior learning; and require periodic
recertification. Agencies, CSOs, and academic institutions may be accredited to deliver modules.

SECTION. 12. National registry and information systems. — DILG, with DICT and PCW, shall
maintain a secure national registry of trained and active VAWC Desk Officers and a harmonized
reporting system enabling lawful data sharing, case tracking, and analytics, with privacy-by-
design features pursuant to RA 10173 and the IRR.

SECTION. 13. Protocols and safeguards. — The NTWG shall adopt national protocols on:
(a) Survivor-centered and child-friendly case handling and interviewing;

(b) Informed consent, confidentiality, safe and minimal data collection, and privacy-
preserving data sharing;

(c) Referral pathways for VAWC, TIP, and OSAEC/CSAEM, including coordination with
national and local bodies designated under RA 9208 (as amended) and RA 11930;

(d) Emergency response, psychosocial first aid, and safety planning; and

(e) Interoperability with WCPUs, WCPDs, social protection programs, and school-based
child protection mechanisms.

ARTICLE IV
REGIONAL AND PROVINCIAL/CITY VAWC DESK UNIT TRAINING AND RESOURCE
CENTERS

SECTION. 14. Establishment. — There are hereby established:
(a) A VAWC Desk Unit Training and Resource Center (TRC) in every DILG Regional
Office; and

(b) A Provincial /City VAWC Desk Unit TRC in every province and in highly urbanized and
independent component cities, to be hosted by the LGU’s GAD Office or equivalent, in
coordination with the DILG Provincial/City Office and the PCW, with linkage to
BARMM counterparts where applicable.

===== [page 7] =====
SECTION. 15. Mandate and functions of TRCs. — TRCs shall:
(a) Deliver standardized basic and advanced training, coaching, and supervision for
VAWC Desk Officers and LGU focal persons, including specialized modules on TIP
and OSAEC/CSAEM;

(b) Provide technical assistance on protocols, forms, privacy, safe digital practices,
and quality improvement;

(c) Maintain a knowledge hub of laws, jurisprudence, tools, and IEC materials;

(d) Offer surge support and mentoring to LGUs during emergencies, complex or high-
profile cases, or disasters, in coordination with DRRM offices;

(e) Facilitate confidential case review conferences and learning exchanges;

(f) Conduct routine quality assurance, supportive supervision, and performance
audits; and

(g) Coordinate with universities, CSOs, faith-based and professional organizations
for training, research, and innovation partnerships.

SECTION. 16. Governance and staffing. — Each Regional TRC shall be chaired by the DILG
Regional Director and co-chaired by the DSWD Regional Director, with members from DOH,
DepEd, DOJ, PNP, PCW, DICT, RDC GAD Committee, and CSO representatives. Provincial/City
TRCs shall be chaired by the Governor/City Mayor or authorized representative, with the DILG
Provincial/City Director as co-chair. Subject to existing rules, DBM shall determine necessary
plantilla or contract-of-service positions to operationalize TRCs.

SECTION. 17. Minimum standards. — The NTWG shall set minimum TRC standards on
facilities, staffing ratios, trainer qualifications, curricula, supervision frequency, QA tools, and
data protection measures.

ARTICLE V
WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT SUPPORT

SECTION. 18. Honoraria and benefits. — Barangay VAWC Desk Officers shall receive monthly
honoraria and allowable benefits consistent with DBM Local Budget Circulars and other
applicable rules, without prejudice to higher support from LGUs. No double compensation shall
be allowed.

SECTION. 19. Capacity building and professional development. — VAWC Desk Officers shall
be entitled to training, recertification, and professional development opportunities delivered or
endorsed by TRCs, including travel and training allowances per DBM rules.

SECTION. 20. Health and mental health support. — Officers shall have access to health
services, including mental health and psychosocial support, debriefing, and counseling,
consistent with RA 11036 and DOH guidelines.

SECTION. 21. Work tools and safety. — Officers shall be provided basic tools (secure logbooks
or digital systems, IEC materials, communication support, and PPE when needed) subject to COA
and procurement rules. Digital tools shall conform to privacy and cybersecurity standards under
RA 10173 and the IRR.

===== [page 8] =====
SECTION. 22. Recognition and incentives. — Performance-based recognition and modest
incentives may be granted by LGUs or national agencies consistent with DBM and COA rules and
the performance framework.

SECTION. 23. Limited support for qualified dependents. — Subject to performance criteria
and means-testing in the IRR, dependents of VAWC Desk Officers may receive:

(a) Educational assistance or scholarships;
(b) School supplies or nutrition packs; and

(c) Access to health and immunization programs and LGU-led reading or psychosocial
activities.

Benefits shall be non-transferable, transparently documented, and subject to available funds and
auditing rules.

ARTICLE VI
FINANCING AND APPROPRIATIONS

SECTION. 24. National appropriations. — The amount necessary to implement the National
Program and Regional TRCs shall be charged against current appropriations of DILG and
concerned agencies. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act (GAA) of DILG, with corresponding support to DSWD, DOH, DepEd,
DOJ, PNP, PCW, DICT, and other concerned agencies for their components.

SECTION. 25. Local funding sources. — LGUs may fund implementation through:
(a) GAD Budget — At least five percent (5%) of total annual appropriations for gender
and development programs pursuant to PCW-NEDA-DBM issuances;

(b) 20% Local Development Fund (LDF) — For human development and social
protection projects aligned with the AIP;

(c) Special Education Fund (SEF) — For educational assistance consistent with RA
7160, Section 272, and related issuances;

(d) Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund (LDRRMF) — For
psychosocial support, emergency response training, and capacity-building
consistent with RA 10121; and

(e) Barangay and LGU General Funds and allowable income sources.

===== [page 9] =====
SECTION. 26. Grants and donations. — National agencies and LGUs may receive grants,
donations, and technical assistance from development partners, NGOs, people’s organizations,
academic institutions, and the private sector for programs under this Act, subject to RA 7160,
COA rules, and conflict-of- interest safeguards.

SECTION. 27. Fiscal safeguards. — All support and benefits shall be performance-based, legally
authorized, time-bound where appropriate, and compliant with DBM and COA rules. Lawful
existing benefits shall not be diminished, and duplication is prohibited.

ARTICLE VII
MONITORING, ACCOUNTABILITY, AND REMEDIES

SECTION. 28. Monitoring and evaluation. — The NTWG shall establish a results framework
with indicators on access, quality, timeliness, privacy compliance, and survivor- and child-
safeguarding outcomes, with annual public reporting in aggregate form preserving
confidentiality.

SECTION. 29. Compliance reviews. — DILG shall conduct periodic compliance reviews of LGUs.
Findings shall inform capacity-building and may trigger show-cause directives for non-
compliance, without prejudice to other remedies under existing laws.

SECTION. 30. Administrative liability. — Public officials who knowingly and unjustifiably
obstruct VAWC Desk operations, violate confidentiality, fail to undertake mandatory reporting,
or arbitrarily remove trained officers without due process may be subject to administrative
sanctions under existing laws, rules, and regulations, without prejudice to criminal and civil
liabilities under RA 9262, RA 9208 (as amended), RA 11930, RA 7610, and related statutes.

SECTION. 31. Grievance redress. — The IRR shall establish clear grievance mechanisms for
survivors and officers, including whistleblower protections consistent with existing laws.

ARTICLE VII
IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS; TRANSITORY

SECTION. 32. IRR. — Within ninety (90) days from effectivity, DILG, in coordination with DSWD,
DOH, DepEd, DOJ, PNP, PCW, DBM, DICT, COA, LGU leagues, CSOs, and BARMM counterparts, shall
promulgate the IRR.

SECTION. 33. Transitory provisions. — Within one hundred eighty (180) days from effectivity:

(a) All barangays shall confirm or designate VAWC Desk Officers and submit
documentation to DILG and the C/MSWDO;

(b) Incumbent trained officers shall continue in a holdover capacity subject to performance
standards;

(c) Regional and Provincial/City TRCs shall be organized and minimally staffed; and

(d) Initial training and certification schedules shall be published.

ARTICLE IX
FINAT. PROVISIONS

===== [page 10] =====
SECTION. 34. Separability clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions shall not be affected.

SECTION. 35. Repealing clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations inconsistent
with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION. 36. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved.
