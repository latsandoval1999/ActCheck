                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Section 5, Article XIII of the 1987 Constitution mandates the State to
support agriculture through appropriate technology, research, and adequate
financial, production, and marketing services. In line with this policy, the State
must establish mechanisms that preserve perishable agricultural goods, reduce
post-harvest losses, and ensure a stable and sufficient food supply for the
population.

       Food security remains a fundamental national concern, closely linked to
economic stability, public welfare, and national resilience. As an agricultural
country, the Philippines continues to face persistent challenges in ensuring a
stable and sufficient food supply, particularly in the context of post-harvest
losses, price volatility, and inefficiencies in the distribution of perishable goods.

       A major contributor to these challenges is the lack of adequate cold storage
infrastructure nationwide. Perishable agricultural products, such as fruits,
vegetables, fish, and meat, are highly vulnerable to spoilage due to insufficient
storage and handling facilities. The limited availability of accessible and
strategically located cold storage facilities results in significant post-harvest
losses, reducing farmers’ incomes and contributing to supply constraints and
  price volatility that ultimately burden consumers. Access to existing cold chain
  systems also remains uneven, particularly in geographically isolated and
  disadvantaged areas and in provinces with high agricultural production. As a
  result, many farmers, fisherfolk, and small-scale food processors are compelled
  to sell their produce immediately at lower prices or risk spoilage, weakening the
  agricultural value chain and undermining overall food security.

          This proposed measure seeks to address these challenges by establishing
  food security cold storage facilities in every province. By ensuring the availability
  of temperature-controlled storage infrastructure, the State aims to reduce post-
  harvest losses, extend the shelf life of perishable goods, and enable more efficient
  distribution and market timing of agricultural products. The measure further
  promotes accessibility by requiring strategic placement of facilities and
  prioritizing underserved and high-producing areas through a phased
  implementation approach. It also designates the Department of Agriculture as the
  lead implementing agency and mandates coordination with local government
  units to ensure the effective establishment, operation, and maintenance of the
  facilities.

         Ultimately, the establishment of food security cold storage facilities is
  expected to strengthen the agricultural value chain, improve farmers’ incomes,
  stabilize food prices, and enhance the country’s capacity to respond to supply
  disruptions. By investing in critical post-harvest infrastructure, the State affirms
  its commitment to achieving long-term food security and building a more resilient
  and efficient food system.

         Measures seeking the establishment of cold storage facilities have been
  filed as early as the 8th Congress, although most were limited to specific local
  government units. Recognizing their growing importance, similar nationwide
  measures were also filed during the 19th Congress.

        In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Food Security
Cold Storage Facilities Act.”

       Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes food security and
price stability as national priorities essential to promoting the welfare of its
citizens. Toward this end, the State shall promote the establishment of cold
storage facilities to reduce post-harvest losses, minimize food wastage, and
preserve agricultural and food products.
       The State shall establish food security cold storage facilities in every
province and ensure their efficient, accessible, and sustainable operation.

      Section 3. Definition of Food Security Cold Storage Facilities. – Food
Security Cold Storage Facilities refer to temperature-controlled infrastructure

                                                                               3
designed to preserve perishable agricultural and food products and maintain their
quality and safety.
       Such facilities shall be equipped with appropriate refrigeration systems and
storage equipment in accordance with standards to be prescribed by the
Department of Agriculture (DA).
       Where practicable, these facilities shall adopt energy-efficient and
environmentally sustainable technologies.
       Subject to the guidelines of the Department of Health, and if necessary,
such facilities may be utilized for the storage of temperature-sensitive health
commodities during emergencies.

       Section 4. Establishment of Food Security Cold Storage Facilities in
Every Province. – The Department of Agriculture (DA), as the lead implementing
agency, in coordination with the local government units (LGUs), shall establish
at least one (1) food security cold storage facility in every province.
       The establishment of such facilities shall be implemented in phases,
prioritizing underserved provinces, geographically isolated and disadvantaged
areas (GIDAs), and major food-producing regions.
       Facilities shall be strategically located to ensure accessibility to farmers,
fisherfolk, food processors, and other stakeholders, taking into account proximity
to production areas and transportation networks.
       Existing government or private structures that meet the minimum standards
or may be upgraded to comply with the requirements of this Act may be
designated as food security cold storage facilities, subject to guidelines issued by
the DA.

       Section 5. Roles of the Department of Agriculture and local government
units. –
       (a) Department of Agriculture (DA). – The DA shall serve as the lead
           implementing agency and shall:
             1. Formulate and implement a National Cold Storage Development
                Plan, including standards, site selection criteria, and operational
                guidelines for facilities established under this Act;
             2. Provide technical and financial assistance to LGUs for the
                establishment and upgrading of cold storage facilities;
             3. Set and enforce standards for the operation, maintenance, and
                utilization of the facilities;
                                                                                  4
            4. Monitor and evaluate the performance and utilization of the
               facilities; and,
            5. Ensure alignment of this Act with existing national policies,
               programs, and industry roadmaps on cold chain development.
      (b) Local Government Units (LGUs). – The LGUs shall:
            1. Provide appropriate sites for the establishment of the facilities;
            2. Oversee the day-to-day operation, management, and
               maintenance of the facilities; and,
            3. Facilitate access of farmers, fisherfolk, cooperatives, and food
               processors to the facilities.

      The DA and LGUs may enter into partnerships with private entities,
cooperatives, and other stakeholders for the establishment, operation, and
management of cold storage facilities, subject to applicable laws, rules, and
regulations.

       Section 6. Incentives. – The State shall provide incentives to private
entities and LGUs that will establish and operate cold storage facilities in
accordance with this Act. The incentives may include tax exemptions, subsidies,
and other forms of financial support pertinent to such an establishment.

      Section 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the Department of Agriculture shall, in
consultation with the LGUs and other concerned government and private
agencies, promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to carry out the
provisions of this Act.

        Section 8. Appropriation. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of
the Department of Agriculture. Thereafter, such amounts as may be necessary for
its continued implementation shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act.
        The LGUs shall ensure the continued operation and maintenance of the
facilities, subject to technical and financial assistance from the national
government.

      Section 9. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any part or provision

      Section 10. Repealing Clause. – Any law, presidential decree or issuance,
executive order, letter of instruction, administrative order, rule or regulation

      Section 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days

general circulation, whichever comes first.
