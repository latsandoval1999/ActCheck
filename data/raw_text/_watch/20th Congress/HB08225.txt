                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to declare December 11 of every year as a special nonworking
holiday in the Municipality of Tuba, Province of Benguet, to be known as “Tuba Day,” in
commemoration of its founding anniversary. The measure aims to honor the
municipality’s historical establishment, celebrate its cultural heritage, and recognize its
enduring contributions to provincial and regional development.

        December 11, 1911 marks a defining moment in Tuba’s history, as this date
signifies the creation of its township under an executive issuance during the American
colonial period. The establishment of Tuba involved the reorganization of earlier
administrative divisions, leading to the formation of a new township composed of
several original barrios situated south of the Baguio Townsite Reservation. Over the
years, these communities grew and evolved into thirteen (13) barangays in total, namely:
Ansagan, Camp 1, Camp 3, Camp 4, Nangalisan, Poblacion, San Pascual, Tabaan Norte,
Tabaan Sur, Tadiangan, Taloy Norte, Taloy Sur, and Twin Peaks, which now form the
municipality’s political and cultural backbone. Known as the “Gateway to the South and
the Cordillera,” Tuba has long served as a point of convergence for diverse groups and
cultures. Its strategic location, rich historical roots, and vibrant communities have made
it a dynamic municipality where various traditions coexist and contribute to a shared
local identity.

       Commemorating its founding anniversary through an officially declared
nonworking holiday provides an avenue for residents to celebrate the origin of their civil
government and to honor the unity, resilience, and cultural diversity that define Tuba
today. Moreover, Institutionalizing “Tuba Day” supports the promotion of heritage
awareness, civic pride, and local tourism by allowing the municipality to highlight its
unique history and role as a vital gateway to the Cordillera highlands.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
DECLARING DECEMBER 11 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TUBA, PROVINCE OF BENGUET IN
COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY, TO BE KNOWN AS
“TUBA DAY”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. December 11 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking
holiday in the Municipality of Tuba, Province of Benguet, to be known as “Tuba
Day” in commemoration of its Founding Anniversary.

       SEC. 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
