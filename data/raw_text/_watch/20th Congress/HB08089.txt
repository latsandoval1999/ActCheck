                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

This measure seeks to convert the existing Land Transportation Office (LTO)
Tuguegarao District Office from its current Class “C” classification to a Class “A” district
office.

Strategically located in the heart of the 3rd District of Cagayan, the LTO Tuguegarao
District Office plays a vital role in delivering essential LTO services to residents of
Tuguegarao City and the nearby municipalities of Baggao, Iguig, Peñablanca, Solana,
and Enrile. Its presence ensures faster, more accessible, and more efficient delivery
of public services to a growing number of constituents.

Pursuant to LTO Memorandum Circular No. 2020-2200, a District Office may be
classified as Class “A” if it records at least 111,001 transactions and collects a minimum
revenue of Php 67,774,619. The LTO Tuguegarao District Office has surpassed these
benchmarks. In 2024 alone, it processed 336,428 motor vehicle registrations and
generated Php 84,416,774.36 in collected revenue—figures that continue to increase
annually.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT CONVERTING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE TUGUEGARAO
      DISTRICT OFFICE INTO A CLASS “A” DISTRICT OFFICE AND
                APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The existing Land Transportation Office (LTO) District Office in
Tuguegarao City, Cagayan (LTO Tuguegarao District Office) is hereby converted into
a Class “A” LTO district office.

SECTION 2. The Secretary of Transportation shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of the LTO Tuguegarao District Office.
Thereafter, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act.

SECTION. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
