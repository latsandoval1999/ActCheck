                             AN ACT
 PROVIDING FOR A FISCALLY RESPONSIBLE AND TARGETED AUTOMATIC
 SUSPENSION OR REDUCTION OF EXCISE TAXES ON REGULAR GASOLINE,
  UNLEADED PREMIUM GASOLINE, AND DIESEL DURING EXTRAORDINARY
 GLOBAL OIL PRICE SURGES, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 148
   OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED

                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Global oil markets remain highly volatile and susceptible to geopolitical
tensions, supply disruptions, and speculative pressures. As a net oil-importing country,
the Philippines is particularly vulnerable to abrupt increases in crude prices. When
global oil prices surge, the effects cascade immediately through the domestic
economy: transport fares rise, food production and distribution costs increase,
electricity generation becomes more expensive, and overall inflationary pressure
intensifies.

       These global price dynamics have been magnified by the ongoing US-Israel
war with Iran, which began with coordinated military strikes against Iranian targets in
late February 2026 and has since escalated into a regional conflict involving retaliatory
strikes, widening engagements, and closure or disruption of shipping routes such as
the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil supply. The conflict has already
contributed to sharp increases in global oil prices and logistical disruptions that affect
energy markets worldwide.
       Under Republic Act No. 10963 or the TRAIN Law, excise taxes on petroleum
products are fixed at ₱10.00 per liter for gasoline and ₱6.00 per liter for diesel. These
are specific taxes imposed per liter regardless of prevailing global oil price levels.
Bagama’t mahalaga ang excise tax bilang pinagkukunan ng pondo ng pamahalaan,
nananatili itong buo kahit na may matinding pagtaas sa pandaigdigang presyo ng
krudo. During extraordinary oil price surges, this fixed structure compounds already
elevated pump prices.
       Diesel fuel is especially critical. It powers public utility vehicles, cargo trucks,
agricultural machinery, and fishing vessels. Ang pagtaas ng presyo ng diesel ay
direktang nakaaapekto sa presyo ng pagkain at pamasahe. Gasoline increases
likewise burden workers and small enterprises dependent on daily mobility. When
global crude prices spike sharply, the combined effect of high base prices and full
excise taxes can aggravate inflation and strain livelihoods.

        This measure introduces a calibrated, rules-based mechanism for excise tax
relief during extraordinary oil price surges. It establishes an automatic trigger based
on the average Dubai crude oil price using the Mean of Platts Singapore. When the
average reaches or exceeds USD 80 per barrel for at least one month, diesel excise
taxes are reduced by fifty percent (50%) and gasoline by thirty percent (30%). When
the average reaches or exceeds USD 100 per barrel, diesel excise taxes are fully
suspended and gasoline excise taxes reduced by fifty percent (50%).

       Hindi ito blanket suspension. Graduated ang tugon upang maging proporsyonal
sa tindi ng pagtaas ng presyo ng langis. By prioritizing diesel, the bill recognizes its
broader inflationary pass-through effect on food and transport.

        The measure also provides targeted relief. During activation periods, public
utility vehicles, accredited transport network vehicle services, registered farmers,
fisherfolk, and food producers are entitled to full diesel excise relief through refund,
credit, or digital subsidy mechanisms. Layunin nitong protektahan ang mga sektor na
may direktang epekto sa pang-araw-araw na pangangailangan ng mamamayan.

        To preserve fiscal stability, the bill includes a safeguard clause. If the projected
national government fiscal deficit exceeds the programmed ceiling under the General
Appropriations Act by more than one percent (1%) of gross domestic product, only the
partial suspension applies unless Congress authorizes broader relief. This ensures
that consumer protection is balanced with fiscal discipline.

        The bill provides automatic resumption of full excise rates once Dubai crude
prices fall below USD 80 per barrel for two consecutive months, preventing prolonged
revenue losses when conditions normalize. A five-year sunset provision mandates
congressional review. Transparency is strengthened through required monthly
certification of crude prices and quarterly fiscal impact reporting to Congress.

       Sa harap ng pabagu-bagong pandaigdigang merkado ng langis, kailangan ng
malinaw at awtomatikong mekanismo ng proteksiyon. This measure institutionalizes a
predictable and data-driven response to extraordinary oil price surges—timely,
targeted, and fiscally responsible.

       In view of the foregoing, the swift passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

27         Section 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Fuel Price Stabilization and
28   Targeted Excise Relief Act.”
29
30           Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to protect consumers,
31   workers, and small enterprises from extraordinary fuel price volatility while maintaining
32   fiscal sustainability and revenue stability. The State recognizes that excise taxation on
33   petroleum products is a significant revenue source; thus, any suspension or reduction
34   mechanism must be predictable, time-bound, data-driven, and fiscally responsible.
