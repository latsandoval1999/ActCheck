                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Barangay is the primary planning and implementing unit of government
policies, programs, projects, and activities in the community. As the
grassroots level of our democratic institution, Barangay Officials—including
the Punong Barangay, Sangguniang Barangay members, the Sangguniang
Kabataan Chairperson, the Barangay Secretary, and the Barangay
Treasurer—serve as the frontliners of public service.

These individuals are often on call 24/7, mediating disputes, maintaining
peace and order, and implementing national health and social programs.
However, upon completion of their terms of office, many of these dedicated
public servants find themselves without stable employment or a clear career
path, despite years of invaluable experience in public administration,
community leadership, and crisis management.

This bill seeks to recognize the service of former Barangay Officials by
providing them with preferential consideration for employment in Local
Government Units (LGUs) and National Government Agencies (NGAs). To
maintain the integrity of the bureaucracy and ensure the quality of public
service, this preference is strictly subject to the requirements of the Civil
Service Commission (CSC) and the specific qualifications of the positions.

By facilitating their entry into the professional civil service, the state not only
rewards faithful community service but also retains experienced individuals
within the government workforce who have a proven track record of dealing
with the needs of the Filipino people.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 AN ACT PROVIDING PRIORITY EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR
  BARANGAY OFFICIALS WHO HAVE COMPLETED THEIR TERMS OF
OFFICE IN LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS AND NATIONAL GOVERNMENT
   AGENCIES, SUBJECT TO CIVIL SERVICE ELIGIBILITY, AND FOR
                      OTHER PURPOSES
Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:
       SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Barangay
Officials’ Career Transition Act."
      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy - It is the policy of the State to recognize the
roles of barangay officials as frontliners of government service and to provide them
with opportunities for career advancement and economic stability after their tenure,
consistent with the principle of merit and fitness in the civil service.

        SEC. 3. Coverage - This Act shall apply to all Punong Barangays, members
of the Sangguniang Barangay, Sangguniang Kabataan Chairpersons, Barangay
Secretaries, and Barangay Treasurers who have completed at least one (1) full term
of office and have been honorably discharged from their positions.

       SEC. 4. Priority Employment - All Local Government Units (LGUs) and
National Government Agencies (NGAs) shall grant preferential consideration to
qualified former barangay officials in the hiring of personnel for vacant regular,
contractual, or casual positions, provided that:
   1. The applicant meets the minimum qualification standards for the position,
      including education, experience, and training;
   2. The applicant possesses the required Civil Service Eligibility for the position;
      and
   3. The applicant has obtained a "Very Satisfactory" performance rating (or its
      community equivalent) during their last term of office.
       SEC. 5. Integration to Human Resource System - The Civil Service
Commission (CSC), in coordination with the Department of the Interior and
Local Government (DILG), shall develop a database of former barangay
officials who are civil service eligible to assist LGUs and NGAs in identifying
potential candidates for vacant positions.
       SEC. 6. Civil Service Eligibility. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed
as a waiver of the requirement for civil service eligibility. Former barangay
officials who do not yet possess the required eligibility are encouraged to take
the career service examinations or apply for the Barangay Official Eligibility
(BOE) as provided under existing CSC rules for those who meet the specific
tenure requirements.
      SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). - Within sixty (60)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Civil Service Commission (CSC), the
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), and the Department
of Budget and Management (DBM) shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.
     SEC. 8. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.
      SEC. 9. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
