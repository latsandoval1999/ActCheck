28   AN ACT ESTABLISHING AN EXTENSION CAMPUS OF THE UNIVERSITY OF
29   SOUTHERN MINDANAO (USM) IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ALEOSAN, PROVINCE
30   OF COTABATO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHERN MINDANAO–
31   ALEOSAN CAMPUS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

32   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
33   assembled:

34         Section 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “University of Southern Mindanao–
35   Aleosan Campus Act.”
36          Section 2. Establishment of Aleosan Extension Campus. It is hereby established an
37   extension campus of the University of Southern Mindanao (USM) in the Municipality of Aleosan,
38   Province of Cotabato, to be known as the University of Southern Mindanao–Aleosan Campus
39   (USM–Aleosan Campus).
40           Section 3. Mandate. The USM–Aleosan Campus shall offer graduate and undergraduate
41   programs within fields determined by the Board of Regents (BOR) of USM, and other priority
42   areas aligned with local and regional development needs, particularly of Cotabato Province. The
43   campus shall also undertake research and extension services and provide progressive leadership in
44   its areas of specialization.
45           Section 4. Campus Administration. The USM–Aleosan Campus shall be headed by a
46   Campus Administrator who shall render full-time service and shall be appointed or designated by
47   the BOR of USM upon the recommendation of a search committee and the University President,
48   subject to the guidelines, qualifications, and standards set by the BOR.
49          Section 5. Compliance with CHED Requirements. The establishment of the USM–Aleosan
50   Campus shall ensure that the minimum requirements for its operation and program offerings,
51   including faculty qualifications, facilities, and academic standards, are complied with as prescribed
52   by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED).
53         Section 6. Post-Enactment Institutional Requirements. In coordination with the main
54   campus of USM, and within a period of one year after the approval of this Act, the USM–Aleosan
55   Campus shall:
