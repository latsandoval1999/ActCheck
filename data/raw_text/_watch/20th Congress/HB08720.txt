                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to institutionalize the direct participation of coconut farmers in
the management and utilization of the Coconut Farmers and Industry Trust Fund
(CFITF). It amends Republic Act No. 11524, otherwise known as the “Coconut
Farmers And Industry Trust Fund Act”.

       The coconut industry remains an important sector of agriculture in the
Philippines. It provides employment to approximately 2.5 - 3.5 million farmers in
sixty-nine (69) provinces in the Philippines1 and has a total production area of 3.62
million hectares. Coconut products continue to rank among the country’s top export
commodities as reported by the Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA)2 and yet
coconut farmers continue to face challenges that limit their economic stability. Many
farmers work with aging coconut trees that produce lower yields, while limited
access to technology constraints their productivity. They also bear the direct impact
of pests and diseases such as coconut scale insects and lethal yellowing, as well as
increasingly severe weather disturbances that damage farms and disrupt harvests.

       This measure introduces the Coconut Farmers Advisory Board (COCOFAB), a
dedicated body composed of representatives from registered farmers’ federations
and cooperatives, that will directly engage in the review of development priorities,
budget allocations, and program implementation. It mandates consultation prior to
key fund decisions and strengthens transparency through the public disclosure of
investment reports, fund releases, and project performance. This ensures that
financial decisions align with the actual needs of the community.

       This vital piece of legislation ensures that coconut farmers are not merely
beneficiaries but active partners in shaping the future of the industry. It advances a
more responsive and accountable Trust Fund that delivers timely and equitable
