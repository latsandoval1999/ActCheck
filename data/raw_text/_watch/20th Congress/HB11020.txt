                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The maritime transport sector remains a critical backbone of inter-island
connectivity in the Philippines, particularly in regions such as Bicol where
mobility, trade, tourism, and access to essential services rely heavily on domestic
shipping. Despite its importance, many shipping operations in the country
continue to rely on manual ticketing systems and cash-based transactions,
which are prone to inefficiencies, long queues, revenue leakages, and limited
transparency.

       The continued reliance on manual and cash-based systems also poses
challenges to passenger convenience, financial inclusion, and public safety,
especially in the context of increasing demand for contactless and technology-
driven services. Moreover, the absence of standardized digital systems limits the
ability of government agencies to maintain accurate passenger manifests,
monitor compliance, and implement data-driven policies in the maritime sector.

       The proposed measure seeks to address these gaps by requiring all
domestic shipping lines and maritime passenger carriers to adopt electronic
ticketing (e-ticketing) systems and cashless payment options. This initiative is
aligned with the State policy to promote the development of information and
communications technology, digital innovation, and efficient delivery of public
services, consistent with Article II of the 1987 Constitution.

      This measure complements existing laws such as the E-Commerce Act of
2000 (Republic Act No. 8792), which recognizes the legal validity of electronic
transactions, and the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), which
ensures the protection of personal data in digital systems. It likewise aligns with
the national government’s thrust toward digital transformation and financial
inclusion, as supported by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and the Department
of Information and Communications Technology.

      Importantly, the bill does not eliminate cash transactions entirely but
instead requires the availability of cashless alternatives, thereby ensuring
inclusivity for passengers in areas with limited digital access while gradually
transitioning the sector toward modernization.

       The measure also strengthens inter-agency coordination by designating
the Department of Transportation, the Maritime Industry Authority, the
Philippine Ports Authority, the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, and the Department
of Information and Communications Technology as implementing agencies. This
ensures that existing mandates are respected and harmonized, thereby avoiding
duplication of functions and potential conflicts in authority.

      For the Bicol Region, the adoption of e-ticketing and cashless systems is
expected to significantly improve port efficiency, passenger experience, tourism
flow, and regional economic activity, while enhancing transparency and
accountability in maritime operations.

       By institutionalizing digital ticketing and payment systems in the maritime
sector, this bill aims to promote a modern, efficient, and inclusive transportation
system that is consistent with national development goals and the evolving needs
of the Filipino people.

     It is for the foregoing premises that the approval of this Bill is earnestly
sought.

                             AN ACT
      REQUIRING ALL SHIPPING LINES OPERATING IN THE PHILIPPINES
   TO ADOPT ELECTRONIC TICKETING (E-TICKETING) AND CASHLESS
          PAYMENT SYSTEMS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

       Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:


     SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “E-Ticketing and
Cashless Maritime Transactions Act.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to promote the use of digital technologies to improve efficiency,
transparency, and accessibility in public transportation and ensure safe,
convenient, and contactless transactions for passengers, consistent with public
welfare and consumer protection. Toward this end, the State shall support the
development of a digital economy and financial inclusion, particularly in the
transport sector and enhance regulatory oversight, accountability, and data-
driven governance in maritime transport.

       SECTION 3. Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all domestic shipping lines,
ferry operators, roll-on/roll-off (RORO) operators, and other maritime passenger
carriers operating within Philippine jurisdiction.

       SECTION 4. Mandatory Adoption of E-Ticketing Systems. - All covered
entities shall adopt and implement an electronic ticketing (e-ticketing) system
that shall:

      a) Allow passengers to book, purchase, and receive tickets electronically;
      b) Generate unique, verifiable digital tickets;
      c) Maintain a secure passenger manifest database accessible to
         regulators;
      d) Integrate with port and terminal systems where applicable; and
      e) Comply with standards prescribed by the appropriate regulatory
         agencies.

      SECTION 5. Mandatory Adoption of Cashless Payment Systems. - All
covered entities shall provide cashless payment options, which may include debit
and credit cards, mobile wallets (e.g., E-money platforms), online banking and
QR-based payments, and other digital payment systems accredited by the
Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) provided, that cash payment options shall not
be entirely eliminated, especially in areas with limited digital infrastructure, in
order to ensure inclusivity.

      SECTION 6. Data Privacy and Cybersecurity – All e-ticketing and payment
systems shall comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)
and Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10175).
      Operators shall ensure data security, confidentiality, and protection
against unauthorized access.
      SECTION 7. Interoperability and Standards. - The DICT, in coordination
with DOTr and BSP, shall establish interoperable standards to ensure
compatibility across different shipping lines and ports, integration with national
transport and payment systems, and ease of use for passengers across regions,
including the Bicol Region.

      SECTION 8. Incentives and Support Mechanisms. – To encourage
compliance, the government may provide technical assistance through DICT,
access to financing or credit facilities, and tax incentives for early adopters,
subject to existing laws.

       SECTION 9. Penalties. – Any shipping line that fails to comply within the
prescribed period shall be subject to administrative fines, suspension or non-
renewal of permits/licenses by MARINA, and other sanctions as may be provided
in the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).

     SECTION 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90)
days from effectivity, DENR, in coordination with relevant agencies, shall
promulgate the Implementing Rules and Regulations.

      SECTION 11. Separability Clause. – If for any reason, any part or provision
of this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or
provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.
      SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, presidential
decrees or issuances, letters of instruction, administrative orders, rules, and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 13. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
