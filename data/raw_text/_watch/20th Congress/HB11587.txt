EXPLANATORY NOTE

Innovation fuels economic growth, sparks technological breakthroughs, creates jobs, and
paves the way for sustainable progress. When ideas, research, technology, and entrepreneurship
come together, innovation empowers communities and industries to tackle new challenges, boost
productivity, and unlock fresh opportunities for prosperity.

The Zamboanga Peninsula boasts a vibrant and expanding economy, a wealth of talented
people, and immense promise for research, entrepreneurship, and technological progress. Blessed
with abundant raw materials, rich agricultural and marine resources, and unique local products,
the region stands poised to add value, drive innovation, and compete on both national and global
stages. By harnessing these assets through innovation, local ideas and products can become high-
value goods, cutting-edge technologies, and thriving enterprises.

Zamboanga City, especially its First Legislative District, stands ready to become the home
of a vibrant innovation hub—a dynamic meeting ground for innovators, researchers, entrepreneurs,
industries, government agencies, academic institutions, and all who share a vision for progress.

Consistent with and in furtherance of Republic Act No. 11293, otherwise known as the
“Philippine Innovation Act,” this measure seeks to establish the Zamboanga City Innovation Hub
(ZCIH) as the main innovation hub serving Zamboanga City and the entire Zamboanga Peninsula.
The Hub shall serve as a center for research, innovation, entrepreneurship, technology
development, incubation, prototyping, and commercialization, while providing technical,
institutional, and business support to innovators, startups, researchers, students, and micro, small,
and medium enterprises.

Creating the ZCIH will help bring the national innovation agenda to life by energizing the
region’s innovation ecosystem, championing research and technology, elevating the value and
competitiveness of local resources, nurturing technology-driven enterprises, and opening doors to
inclusive and sustainable growth across the Zamboanga Peninsula.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly soughit.

NA REIKO CHUA-TAI

AN ACT
CREATING THE ZAMBOANGA CITY INNOVATION HUB, PROVIDING FUNDS
THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga City Innoyation
Hub Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote s¢ience,
technology, research, entrepreneurship, innovation, and technology commercialization as
instruments of inclusive and sustainable economic development, consistent with Republic Act No.

11293, or the Philippine Innovation Act.

SEC. 3. Creation of the Zamboanga City Innovation Hub. — There is hereby created the
Zamboanga City Innovation Hub (ZCIH), which shall serve as a collaborative center for
innovation, research, technology development, entrepreneurship, incubation, prototyping, and

commercialization.

The Hub shall establish linkages among government agencies, local government units,

universities, research institutions, industries, startups, MSMEs, investors, and civil society.

SEC. 4. Functions. — The ZCIH shall:

a. Provide incubation, mentoring, training, prototyping, and technical assistance to

innovators, startups, and MSMEs;

b. Facilitate research, technology transfer, intellectual property development, and

c. Promote collaboration among academic institutions, government, industry, and

d. Support innovations addressing local and regional concerns, including agriculture,
fisheries, manufacturing, digital technology, climate resilience, and sustainable

e. Develop programs that strengthen Zamboanga City as an innovation and technology

hub in Western Mindanao.

SEC. 5. Management and Administration. — The ZCIH shall be attached to the City
Government of Zamboanga and shall be managed by a governing council composed of
representatives from the City Government, DOST, DTI, DICT, CHED, relevant higher edueation
and research institutions, private sector, and other stakeholders as may be determined by its

implementing rules and regulations.

SEC. 6. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of the participating agencies and the City
Government of Zamboanga, subject to existing budgeting laws and regulations. Thereafter, such
sums as may be necessary shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act and the

annual budget of the City Government.

The Hub may also receive grants, donations, contributions, and other lawful sources of

funds.

SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the appropriate government agencies and the City Government of

Zamboanga shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for its implementation.
