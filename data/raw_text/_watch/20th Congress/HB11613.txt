EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 15, Article II of the Constitution states that "The State shall protect and promote
the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them." Furthermore, it
provides that the State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health
development which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health and other social services

available to all the people at affordable cost.

The Philippines is composed of 82 provinces, each serving as an important geographic
and administrative unit in the delivery of basic services. Among these essential services is
healthcare, making it vital to ensure that adequate and appropriate health services are available

within these geographic areas and accessible to communities across the country.

However, access to hospital care remains uneven to date. While several provinces
already have hospitals capable of providing Level II services, such facilities are not uniformly
available throughout the country. The Department of Health has recognized this disparity, with
former Health Secretary Teodoro J. Herbosa reporting that 40 of the country’s 82 provinces
did not have a DOH hospital, with residents in some areas having to travel three to four hours
to another city or province to access a DOH facility and government-supported medical
services'. Such barrier can make it more difficult for patients to benefit from government
programs such as the Zero Balance Billing policy, particularly when access to govermment

hospitals require considerable travel.

This challenge is particularly significant and visible for communities outside highly
urbanized cities. Patients requiring healthcare services beyond those available at primary or
Level I facilities may have to travel to another province or highly urbanized city, or seek
treatment from private hospitals, resulting in additional costs that can be particularly
burdensome for those with limited financial means. Strengthening hospital capacity at the
provincial level is therefore necessary to bring more comprehensive medical care closer to
communities and reduce the geographic and financial barriers to accessing appropriate hospital

services.

This proposed measure seeks to institutionalize the establishment of at least one (1)
Level II government hospital in every province of the Philippines. Its objective is to establish
a uniform national framework that ensures every province has access to enhanced and
comprehensive hospital care. It likewise seeks to advance the policy of the Department of
Health pursuant to the Republic Act No. 11223, or the Universal Health Care (UHC) Act,
whereby all provinces should have at least one Level II hospital, with the vision of establishing
an equitable health system where every Filipino can access appropriate and quality healthcare
where they reside. The measure further aims to upgrade, expand, modernize, or, where feasible,
convert existing provincial DOH primary care facilities and Level I hospitals into Level II

hospitals.

By ensuring that every province has access to at least one (1) Level II government
hospital, the State can strengthen the country’s healthcare system, facilitate patients’ access to
government health programs such as the Zero Balance Billing policy, and advanee its

constitutional mandate to make essential health services accessible and affordable to all.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

AN ACT
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF AT LEAST ONE (1) LEVEL II
HOSPITAL IN EVERY PROVINCE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress

assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Level II Hospital for Every

Province Act.”

Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. —\t is hereby declared as the policy of the State to adopt
an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development, which shall be available and
accessible to all the people at affordable cost. Towards this end, the Philippine health delivery
system must be reformed and modernized in order for it to be available, accessible and

affordable pursuant to the common goal of bringing universal health care for Filipinos.

Sec. 3. Establishment of Level II Hospitals in All Provinces. — Within five (5) years
from the effectivity of this Act, every province shall have at least one (1) Level Il hospital
operating within its territorial jurisdiction. The Department of Health (DOH) must formulate a
prioritization plan for the purpose of implementing this Act, subject to the following
considerations:

a) Top priority shall be given to provinces which do not have any Level II hospital within

their territorial jurisdiction;

===== [page 4] =====
b) Provinces which are geographically isolated or separated by significant distance or
physical barriers from the nearest Level II hospital, including island provinces, shall
likewise be given priority for the establishment of a Level II hospital; and

c) Existing provincial, district, or other government hospitals, as identified by the DOH,
shall be considered for upgrading, modernization, expansion, and conversion into Level

II hospitals.

Sec. 4. Audit and Inventory of Existing Government Hospitals. — Within six (6)
months from the promulgation of the implementing rules and regulations of this Act, an audit
and inventory of the physical facilities, equipment and instruments, as well as the personnel,
of all government hospitals in each province shall be conducted to determine their present
classification, capacity, and suitability for upgrading, expansion, and modernization into a
Level II hospital. The results of the audit and inventory shall serve as the basis for identifying
the government hospital to be upgraded, expanded, and modernized into a Level II hospital in

accordance with the requirements and standards prescribed by the Department of Health.

Sec. 5. Provision and Protection of Healthcare Personnel. — The Department of
Health, in coordination with the Department of Budget and Management and other relevant
government agencies, shall ensure the provision of the necessary plantilla positions of Level II
hospitals established, upgraded, expanded, or modernized pursuant to Section 3 of this Act, in
accordance with the staffing requirements and standards prescribed by the Department of
Health.

Provided, that officials and employees of a hospital converted pursuant to this Act shall
be given preference and priority in the filling of plantilla positions in the resulting Level II
hospital, subject to the applicable qualification standards and eligibility requirements
prescribed by the Civil Service Commission. Provided, further, that no official or employee so
absorbed shall suffer any diminution in rank, salary, tenure, or benefits by reason of such

conversion.

Sec. 6. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be included in the annual General Appropriations of the Department of Health. The
Department may likewise accept donations, grants, bequests, and official development

assistance for the purpose of this Act, subject to existing laws, rules and regulations.

===== [page 5] =====
Sec. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health, in coordination with other relevant
government agencies, shall issue the necessary rules and regulations to implement the

provisions of this Act.

Sec. 8. Repealing Clause. — All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
proclamations, rules and regulations, or any part thereof, which are inconsistent with the

provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
Sec. 9. Separability Clause. — \f any provision or part of this Act, or the application
thereof to any person or circumstance, is held unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder of this

Act shall not be affected thereby.

Sec. 10. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in

the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation in the Philippines.

Approved,
