                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish a segregated Shari’ah-compliant services and
financial facility under the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), amending
for the purpose Republic Act (RA) No. 8291, otherwise known as “The Government
Service Insurance System Act of 1997.”

       Section 5, Article III (Bill of Rights) of the 1987 Constitution states in part
that: “The free exercise and enjoyment of religious profession and worship, without
discrimination or preference, shall forever be allowed xxx.” Consistent with this
constitutional guarantee, the State has a legitimate interest in ensuring that
government financial and social protection institutions can accommodate lawful and
voluntary financial arrangements that are consistent with the religious convictions
of their members. Such accommodation affirms both neutrality and inclusivity, while
strengthening the constitutional promise of equal respect for diverse faith tradition.

       Under the current framework of RA 8291, Muslim Filipino workers face a
structural barrier. Membership in the GSIS is mandatory, and its operations are
interest-based. Although Muslim employees contribute to GSIS in the same way as
every other member, they may avoid its loans, policies, or investment-linked benefits
because of the prohibitions on riba (interest), gharar (excessive uncertainty), and
maysir (gambling-like speculation). This creates a gap in access to benefits, in which
Muslim members effectively subsidize a system they cannot fully use and avail.

      This bill, therefore, addresses the issue by mandating a segregated ledger
within GSIS to prevent the co-mingling of conventional and Shari’ah-compliant
funds. It replaces conventional interest-bearing loans with asset-backed Islamic
contracts, such as Murabahah (cost-plus sale) and Ijarah (leasing). Thus, a
Shari’ah-compliant services and financial facility would give them an equal
opportunity to benefit from the same social protection that other members enjoy.
However, this bill shall not mandate the segregation of contributions of Muslim
employees and employers from that of non-Muslim employees and employers.

      The proposed measure also creates a Shari’ah Advisory Council (SAC) within
the GSIS to provide independent advice concerning the Shari’ah compliance of
products, contracts, investments, and transactions. The SAC intends to provide
specialized Shari’ah expertise while leaving the administration and management of
the GSIS with its duly constituted Board and management. Significantly, the bill
does not alter the existing compulsory GSIS membership system or diminish any
benefit, right, privilege, or obligation under RA 8291. Participation in the Shari’ah-
compliant services and financial facility shall be voluntary and available to qualified
GSIS Muslim members. Likewise, no member shall be discriminated against on the
basis of religion in connection with access to other GSIS services not exclusively
designed for Muslim members.

       The establishment of the Shari’ah-compliant services and financial facility
will provide an additional lawful financial accommodation within the GSIS while
preserving its fundamental mandate and fiduciary responsibility.

      In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
