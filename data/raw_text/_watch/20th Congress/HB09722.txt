                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Filipino culture has long upheld the value of caring for the elderly
within the family. However, cases of abandonment and neglect of senior
citizens are increasing due to financial constraints, family conflicts, and the
inability of caregivers to provide adequate support.

      Some local government units have taken steps to address this gap. In
Valenzuela City, facilities such as Bahay Kalinga and Bahay Kanlungan Tahanan
nina Lolo at Lola provide shelter, healthcare, and daily support for homeless
and abandoned senior citizens. Despite these efforts, such facilities remain
limited, leaving many elderly Filipinos without proper care and protection.

       Aligned with the State’s duty to protect the welfare of senior citizens,
this bill seeks to institutionalize a nationwide framework for the establishment
of nursing homes for homeless and abandoned elderly in every city and
municipality. These facilities shall be constructed by local government units
and operated and maintained by the Department of Social Welfare and
Development (DSWD) in coordination with LGUs.

      This measure complements Republic Act No. 7876, or the “Senior
Citizens Center Act,” by ensuring that vulnerable elderly individuals not only
have access to community programs, but also to residential care when family
support is no longer available.

                               AN ACT
          ESTABLISHING A NURSING HOME FOR HOMELESS AND
     ABANDONED SENIOR CITIZENS IN EVERY CITY OR MUNICIPALITY IN
          THE PHILIPPINES, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR,
                      AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
           Congress assembled:


 1             SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Homes for
 2      Abandoned Seniors Act".

 3             SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the
 4      State to value the dignity of every human person, guarantee full respect for human
 5      rights, extend assistance to its elderly citizens through programs of social security,
 6      and promote the right to health and safety of senior citizens. Towards this end, the
 7      State shall extend assistance to abandoned and homeless senior citizens through a
 8      social care and security program.
 9             SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act, the following terms
10      shall mean:




                                                                                            1
 1           (a) Abandoned Senior Citizen refers to a senior citizen who has no means of
 2              meeting basic needs, or whose family had deserted them and as, such,
 3              appropriate care, protection and support are not met;
 4           (b) Homeless Senior Citizen refers to a senior citizen who lacks a fixed,
 5              regular, and adequate residence, or who resides in a place unfit for
 6              human habitation, or who lacks the means and resources to obtain
 7              secure, safe, and proper permanent housing;
 8           (c) Nursing Home refers to a home for the elderly persons, established and
 9              maintained under this Act by a city or municipality, which provides
10              living accommodations and support services. These include food and
11              clothing, medical and health care, as well as recreational and social
12              interaction activities. It primarily serves abandoned senior citizens who
13              lack family support or homeless senior citizens;
14           (d) Senior Citizen refers to any resident citizen of the Philippines at least
15              sixty (60) years of age.
16           SEC. 4. Establishment of Nursing Homes. – A nursing home shall be
17   established in every municipality and city to provide care for abandoned and
18   homeless senior citizens. The facilities shall be constructed by the respective local
19   government units (LGUs) and shall be operated and maintained by the Department
20   of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), in coordination with the concerned
21   LGUs.
22           SEC. 5. Functions of Nursing Homes. – The nursing homes established
23   under this Act shall provide institutionalized residential care programs and services
24   to homeless or abandoned senior citizens within their territorial jurisdiction. These
25   services shall include but not be limited to the following:
26           (a) Comfortable and safe living accommodation;
27           (b) Adequate food and appropriate clothing;
28           (c) Access to medical consultation and or necessary treatment;




                                                                                        2
 1          (d) Health care services;
 2          (e) Occupational opportunities;
 3          (f)   Recreational and social interaction activities;
 4          (g) Counseling services;
 5          (h) Case conference;
 6          (i)   Facilitation of family dialogue sessions;
 7          (j)   Outreach to existing family members for reintegration;
 8          (k) Literacy and continuing education program;
 9          (l)   Skills and productivity development activity; and
10          (m) Religious, cultural and vocational enrichment program/activities.
11          SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The DSWD, in
12   coordination with the Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG),
13   Department of Health (DOH), the Federation of Senior Citizens Associations of
14   the Philippines (FSCAP), and the Confederation of Older Persons Associations of
15   the Philippines (COPAP), shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations to
16   effectively implement the provisions this Act, not later than ninety (90) days from
17   its effectivity.
18          SEC. 7. Exemptions of the Nursing Home. – The established nursing
19   home shall be exempt from the payment of customs duties, taxes, and tariffs on the
20   importation of equipment and supplies that are actually, directly, and exclusively
21   used for the operation of the nursing home pursuant to this Act, including items
22   donated to the nursing home for such purpose.
23          SEC. 8. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the
24   provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the
25   year following its enactment into law and every year thereafter.
26          SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
27   invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full
28   force and effect.
