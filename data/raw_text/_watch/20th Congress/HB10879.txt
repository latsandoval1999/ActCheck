21                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
22
23   The dignity of persons with disabilities should include the right to be recognized without
24   having to repeatedly prove their condition. In other words, a permanent disability does
25   not become temporary simply because an identification card has an expiration date.
26
27   This proposed measure therefore seeks to grant lifetime validity to PWD identification
28   cards issued to persons with permanent disabilities. This proposal recognizes that once
29   the permanence of a disability has been properly established, repeated renewal only
30   adds a burden to our brothers and sisters with disabilities.
31
32   Likewise, this proposed measure also distinguishes between permanent and temporary
33   disabilities to ensure that PWD benefits remain available to those who qualify, while
34   preventing abuse of PWD privileges.
35
36   Ultimately, this bill seeks to make the recognition of disability simpler, more accessible,
37   and more humane. Behind every PWD identification card is a person who may already
38   face challenges that require physical, mental, and emotional strength every day. It is
39   now the responsibility of the State to ensure that its laws and systems do not add to
40   these burdens but instead provide an effective support to our brothers and sisters with
41   disabilities.
42
43   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

19                                  AN ACT
20           GRANTING LIFETIME VALIDITY OF IDENTIFICATION CARDS FOR
21         PERSONS WITH PERMANENT DISABILITY AND RATIONALIZING THE
22          VALIDITY PERIOD FOR TEMPORARY DISABILITY IDENTIFICATION
23        CARDS, FURTHER AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO.
24         7277, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE “MAGNA CARTA FOR DISABLED
25                           PERSONS”, AS AMENDED
26
27     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
28     Congress assembled:

29
30     SECTION 1. Section 32 of Republic Act No. 7277, otherwise known as the
31     “Magna Carta for Disabled Persons”, as amended by Republic Act No. 9442, is
32     hereby further amended to read as follows:

33               SEC. 32. Persons with disability shall be entitled to the following: xxx

34               The abovementioned privileges are available only to persons with
35               PERMANENT OR TEMPORARY disability who are Filipino citizens upon
36               submission of AN IDENTIFICATION CARD ISSUED BY THE PERSONS
37               WITH DISABILITY AFFAIRS OFFICE (PDAO) OR A DESIGNATED
38               FOCAL PERSON PERFORMING PDAO FUNCTIONS, DULY SIGNED
39               BY THE CITY OR MUNICIPAL MAYOR OF THE PLACE WHERE THE
40               PERSONS WITH
 1           DISABILITY RESIDE, AS PROOF OF THEIR ENTITLEMENT THERETO.

 2           THE IDENTIFICATION CARDS (IDs) ISSUED TO PERSONS WITH
 3           DISABILITY SHALL CONFORM TO THE UNIVERSAL DESIGN
 4           PRESCRIBED BY THE NATIONAL COUNCIL ON DISABILITY AFFAIRS
 5           (NCDA) AND ADOPTED BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS.

 6           THE IDs ISSUED TO PERSONS WITH PERMANENT DISABILITIES
 7           SHALL HAVE LIFETIME VALIDITY. CONVERSELY, THE IDs FOR
 8           PERSONS WITH TEMPORARY DISABILITIES SHALL BE VALID FOR
 9           A PERIOD OF FIVE (5) YEARS, AND MAY BE RENEWED ONLY UPON
10           COMPLETION OF REASSESSMENT CONDUCTED BY AN
11           APPROPRIATE MEDICAL PRACTITIONER.

12           THE IDs, AS PROVIDED IN THE PRECEDING PARAGRAPH SHALL BE
13           FREE OF CHARGE, INCLUDING REISSUANCE DUE TO LOSS OR
14           DAMAGE.

15
16    SECTION 2. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within one hundred eighty (180)
17    days from the effectivity of this Act, the NCDA and the Department of Social Welfare
18    and Development (DSWD) shall, in coordination with the Department of Interior and
19    Local Government (DILG), the Department of Health (DOH), and other government
20    agencies and stakeholders, issue the rules and regulations to implement the provisions
21    of this Act.

22
23    SECTION 3. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
24    unconstitutional or invalid, the remainder of this Act or any provision not affected
25    thereby shall remain in force and effect.

26
27    SECTION 4. Repealing Clause. – All other laws, presidential decrees, executive
28    orders, rules and regulations and other issuances, or any part thereof, inconsistent with
29    this Act are hereby repealed, modified, or amended accordingly.

30
31    SECTION 5. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
32    publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
