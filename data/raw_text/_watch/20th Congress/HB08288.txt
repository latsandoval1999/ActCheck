                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Access to reliable and affordable electricity is indispensable to
economic growth, social development, and the delivery of essential public
services. Electric distribution utilities, particularly electric cooperatives,
play a crucial role in ensuring that communities-urban and rural alike­
receive stable and continuous power supply.

       The Cebu II Electric Cooperative, Inc. (CEBECO II) has been serving
consumers in the northern portion of the Province of Cebu and remains a
vital institution in sustaining local development. Its service area includes
the cities of Danao and Bogo, and the municipalities of Medellin,
Daanbantayan, San Remigio, Tabogon, Borbon, Sogod, Tabuelan,
Tuburan, Catmon, Carmen, and Compostela-areas that include growing
urban centers, tourism hubs, agricultural communities, and expanding
commercial districts.

an electric distribution utility imbued with public interest, CEBECO II
