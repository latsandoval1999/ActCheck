10
11   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Philippine Civil Registration
12   and Vital Statistics Act”
13
14   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State shall promote and maintain an efficient,
15   responsive, inclusive, and accessible civil registration and vital statistics system. Towards
16   this end, standardization and modernization of registration procedures is necessary to
17   establish the identity of an individual for administrative and legal purposes. Moreover,
18   efficient and simplified procedures in recording vital events must be applied in civil
19   registration in order to establish the most precise identity of an individual for the benefit
20   of the general public.
21
22   The State declares the registration of all births, deaths, and marriages without
23   discrimination as a national State policy.
24
25   In the implementation Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Act, institutionalizes and
26   hereby maintains a legal of the policy stated above, the State, in enacting this framework
27   related to the Civil Registration and Vital Statistics (CRVS), ensuring a system that
28   provides equal access that is affordable, available, and protects the rights of all citizens,
29   especially children. Such system will also ensure the generation of statistics and easier
30   retrieval and recording of statistics.
