                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The 1987 Constitution, under Section 5(5), Article XIV, mandates the State to
ensure that the teaching profession attracts and retains its rightful share of the best
available talents through adequate remuneration and other means of professional
satisfaction and fulfillment. This constitutional provision underscores the importance of
recognizing, protecting, and promoting the welfare of teachers as essential partners in
nation-building.

      Teachers are among the country's most valuable human resources. Their
responsibilities extend beyond classroom instruction and may include administrative
and ancillary duties, school-related activities, and other assignments necessary to
support the delivery of public education.

       The physical, mental, and emotional demands associated with the teaching
profession make access to comprehensive and affordable healthcare an essential
component of teachers' welfare. Regular medical consultations, preventive healthcare,
timely diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and mental health services are necessary not
only to safeguard their well-being but also to enable them to continue performing their
responsibilities effectively. However, access to specialized and affordable medical
services remains an important concern, particularly for teachers and their families who
must rely on existing healthcare facilities and services that may not adequately address
their specific needs. Recognizing that the welfare of teachers extends to the protection of
their families, the State must adopt measures that provide them with greater access to
quality healthcare.

       This proposed measure therefore seeks to promote preventive medicine, early
detection and treatment of illnesses, physical rehabilitation, and the overall health and
wellness of teachers and their families.
       Investing in the health and welfare of teachers is an investment in the quality and
sustainability of Philippine education. A healthy and well-supported teaching
workforce is better equipped to fulfill its vital role in shaping future generations and
advancing national development. Providing teachers and their dependents with a
dedicated healthcare institution is a concrete expression of the State's commitment to
recognize their invaluable service and uphold their dignity and well-being.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A FIVE HUNDRED (500) BED CAPACITY HOSPITAL FOR
TEACHERS AND THEIR DEPENDENTS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as “Philippine Teachers’ Hospital
Act.”

       Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
recognize the role of Filipino teachers in national progress and development, protect
their interests and well-being, and provide them and their dependents access to
affordable health care and development through an integrated and comprehensive
approach. Towards the end, the State shall create a Teachers hospital which will make
available to the health and wellness of teachers, whether in active duty or retired, and
their dependents.

       Sec. 3. Creation. - Special hospital for teachers shall be the primary facility for the
health and wellness of teachers and their dependents. It is hereby established one (1)
Teachers’ Hospital in every major city in the country.

       Sec. 4. Qualified beneficiaries. - The following persons shall be entitled to the
benefits of this Act, subject to the following qualifications:
   1. Any person qualifying as a teacher as defined by law, incumbent or retired;
   2. Any person teaching any subject, including practical and vocational arts, at any
       level of schooling in all public schools;
   3. Any person employed as school administrative support employees;
   4. Physicians, dentists, and nurses employed exclusively by public schools;
   5. Officials and employees of the Department of Education (DepEd) and the
       Commission on Higher Education (CHED);
   6. Other public school employees, who have rendered a minimum of ten (10) years
       of continuous service prior to their retirement; and
   7.​ Any of the following teaching dependents:
           a.​ Legitimate spouse;
           b.​ Minor children or children incapable of self-support because of mental or
               physical defect, regardless of age;
           c.​ Parents dependent upon the teacher.

Sec. 5. Objectives. - To ensure the availability, accessibility and affordability of quality
health care, the Philippine Teachers’ Hospital shall:
   a)​ Provide comprehensive health care services from all qualified beneficiaries and
       their legal dependents;
   b)​ Complement the existing package of services under the National Health
       Insurance Program so as to include preventive, diagnostic, curative and
       rehabilitative programs;
   c)​ Conduct medical examination to ensure the physical and mental capability of all
       teacher applicants;
   d)​ Effectively monitor the condition of patients; and
   e)​ Generate relevant information or data in aid of policy formulation.

Sec. 6. Governing Board. - The Philippine Teachers' Hospital shall be governed by the
Board of Directors composed of the following:

a) The Secretary of the Department of Education as ex-officio Chairman;

b) The Secretary of the Department of Health as ex-officio Vice Chairman;

c) The Secretary of the Department of Social Welfare and Development as ex-officio
member;

d) Four (4) representatives from the teachers sector: one (1) from the National Capital
Region, one (1) from Luzon, one (1) from Visayas, and one (1) from Mindanao as
Members, who shall be appointed by the President of the Philippines from a list of
nominees recommended by the Chairperson in consultation with the Party-List
representing the teacher's sector. Each shall serve for a term of three (3) years without
reappointment.



Sec. 7. Powers and Responsibilities of the Board. - The Board of Directors of the Philippine
Teachers' Hospital shall have the following powers and responsibilities:
      a) To formulate and implement measures and programs to attain the objectives
      and purposes as enunciated in Section 5 of this Act;

      b) To organize the structure of the Philippine Teachers' Hospital in accordance
      with the staffing pattern prepared by it in coordination with the Department of
      Health (DOH) and the Civil Service Commission (CSC), and approved by the
      Department of Budget and Management (DBM);

      c) To prescribe the duties and fix the compensation subject to the provision of
      Republic Act No. 6758, as amended, or the "Compensation and Position
      Classification Act of 1989" and other relevant laws;

      d) To enter into agreements and contracts in furtherance of its establishment,
      maintenance, operations, and objectives;

      e) To receive donations, contributions, bequests, subsidies or financial aid;

      f) To issue rules and regulations to carry out the objectives and purposes of this
      Act; and

      g) To perform such other duties and functions as may be provided by law.

Sec. 8. Meetings and Quorum. - The Board shall meet at least once a month or as often as
may be necessary at the office of the Philippine Teachers' Hospital. Special meetings
shall be governed at the call of the Chairman or by a majority of the members of the
Board. Presence of majority of the members of the Board shall constitute a quorum.

Sec. 9. Allowances and per diems. - The members of the Board shall receive a per diem for
every meeting actually attended subject to the pertinent budgetary law, rules and
regulations on compensation, honoraria and allowances.

Sec. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - The Board shall adopt rules and regulations
to implement the provisions of this Act within ninety (90) days from the date of its
effectivity.

Sec. 11. Appropriations. - The Secretary of Health shall immediately include on the
Department's program the implementation of this Act. The necessary funding to carry
out the provisions of this Act, for its operation, and maintenance shall be included in
the Annual General Appropriations Act.
Sec. 12. Tax Exemption. - All donations, contributions, bequests, subsidies or financial
aids to the Philippine Teachers' Hospital shall be exempt from payment of taxes under
the National Internal Revenue Code and the Local Government Code.

Sec. 13. Annual Report. - The Philippine Teachers' Hospital shall render to the President
of the Republic of the Philippines and to each House of Congress an annual operational
and financial report within thirty (30) days from the day of regular session of Congress
the next calendar year.

Sec. 14. Separability Clause. - If any provision or portion of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act or any provision not thereby affected shall
remain in full force and effect.

Sec. 15. Repealing Clause. - All laws, decrees, executive orders, ordinances, rules,
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with any
provision of this Act, are hereby repealed, amended, and/or modified accordingly.

Sec. 16. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
