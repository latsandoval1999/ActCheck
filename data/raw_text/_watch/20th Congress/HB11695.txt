EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines’ ability to achieve further economic and social development will largely
rely on its capacity to produce a workforce that is highly skilled, innovative, scientifically literate,
and technologically capable. Since science, technology, engineering, mathematics, digital
technology, and research are continually transforming industries and public services, expanding
access to good and specialized Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM)
education, especially at the secondary level, is essential.

Zamboanga City is a major educational and economic centre in western Mindanao, and the
size of its student population shows that there is still a need to extend educational opportunities,
especially in specialized areas which prepare young Filipinos for higher education, professional
careers, research, innovation, and meeting the demands of a rapidly changing economy.

The Philippine Statistics Authority, on the basis of data from the Department of Education's
Learner Information System and as of January 30, 2025, stated that Zamboanga City had 238,760
learners in all grades during the 2024-2025 school year. Of these, 120,888 were male learners and
117,872 were female learners. It should be noted that Zamboanga City had the highest number of
enrolled students among the school divisions in the Zamboanga Peninsula. Given the large number
of learners, there is a clear need for the city to keep on expanding and diversifying its educational
infrastructure and its range of specialized learning opportunities.

The fact that Zamboanga City already operates the Regional Science High School for
Region IX shows that the city has already taken on the role of offering specialized science
education. Although the proposed Zamboanga City Science High School is being established, this
is not intended to repeat or reduce the role of the present Regional Science High School; instead,
it aims at complementing the existing science-oriented institutions by increasing the city's overall
ability to provide specialized STEM education.

Just because there is one institution with a scientific focus does not mean that the
educational needs of all qualified students are properly fulfilled. Admission to the specialist
science schools is necessarily selective, and at the same time the City's large number of learners is
still increasing and creating greater demand for high-quality educational opportunities. Having a
second institution would offer more chances to students who have the ability and interest in
science, mathematics, research, technology, engineering and related subjects.

The new Zamboanga City Science High Schoo! will be located in the first legislative
district of Zamboanga City so as to give residents of that district and the adjacent communities

better access to specialized STEM education. Setting up the school will help improve geographic
accessibility, allow the public education system to accommodate more academically capable
students, and offer another opportunity for the development of young scientists, researchers,
engineers, technologists, innovators and other professionals.

The School suggested will have a curriculum that is both enriched and specialized, with a
focus on science and mathematics, technology and engineering, computer science, research and
scientific investigation, innovation, environmental science, and other emerging areas. It will give
students the chance to carry out research, take part in scientific and academic competitions, interact
with universities and research institutions, and work on solutions to real-world problems which
affect Zamboanga City and the broader Zamboanga Peninsula.

The fact that the School has been set up also meets the need to strengthen the country's
STEM pipeline, since students who have early experience of laboratory work, scientific inquiry,
research methods, technology, and innovation are in a better position to go on to higher education
and careers in medicine, engineering, information technology, environmental science, agriculture,
education, research, and in other areas which are important for national and regional development.

A science high school in the first legislative district would also help to ensure equitable
access to specialized public education. It would not be the case that students are prevented from
reaching their full academic potential just because the existing specialized institutions have limited
capacity or are not conveniently accessible to their communities. By increasing the number of
public science schools, more qualified students will have the opportunity to receive an education
which is specifically designed to develop their scientific and analytical skills.

The School in question could also function as a centre for scientific research and innovation
in Zamboanga City. By forming partnerships with universities, colleges, research institutes,
government agencies, industries, and other organisations, the School will be able to offer its
students mentoring, research opportunities, internships, laboratory experience, and exposure to
emerging technologies.

The measure also acknowledges that spending on STEM education is an investment in the
future of Zamboanga City, since the city’s large number of learners offers a solid basis for creating
a bigger group of young people who are well trained and scientifically capable and who can thus
contribute to the city's economic development and to the progress of the Zamboanga Peninsula.

The legislation in question is therefore aiming not only at setting up another secondary
school but at creating an institution whose purpose is to extend access to STEM education, foster
scientific talent, promote research and innovation, and prepare the young people of Zamboanga
City for the opportunities and challenges ahead.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Pol

HOUSE BILL NO. 11695

Introduced by REPRESENTATIVE KATRINA REIKO CHUA-TAI

AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL IN THE FIRST LEGISLATIVE
DISTRICT OF ZAMBOANGA CITY TO BE KNOWN AS THE “ZAMBOANGA CITY
SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL,” AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. There is hereby established, under the supervision of the Department of
Education, a science high school in Barangay Calarian, First Legislative District of Zamboanga

City, to be known as the Zamboanga City Science High School.

SEC. 2. The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Department’s
program the operationalization of the Zamboanga City Science High School, the funding of which

shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC 3. Within one hundred twenty (120) days of the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary
of Education shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary for the implementation of

this Act.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official

Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
