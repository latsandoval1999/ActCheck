13                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE
14
15   Republic Act No. 1340, enacted in 1955, governs the establishment and statutory
16   framework of the National Children’s Hospital. While the institution has since
17   evolved in function, patient load, and clinical complexity, its enabling law has not kept
18   pace with present-day pediatric health demands, referral patterns, and service
19   standards.
20
21   Over the decades, the National Children’s Hospital has effectively operated beyond
22   the scope of a basic specialty hospital and now functions in substance as a tertiary
23   pediatric referral institution handling complex, multidisciplinary, and subspecialty
24   cases. Patient congestion, long waiting times, and high bed occupancy rates indicate
25   a persistent capacity gap relative to national pediatric healthcare needs.
26
27   This measure amends Republic Act No. 1340 to formally rename the institution as the
28   National Children’s Hospital and Medical Center, thereby aligning its
29   statutory identity with its actual and intended role as a tertiary pediatric specialty and
30   training center. The designation “Medical Center” reflects expanded mandates in
31   advanced clinical care, residency and fellowship training, research, and nationwide
32   pediatric referral support.
33
34   The bill further increases the authorized bed capacity to four hundred (400) in
35   order to address sustained patient demand, reduce overcrowding, and enable the
36   phased expansion of intensive care, neonatal, oncology, surgical, and other high-
37   dependency pediatric services. The capacity increase is paired with authority for
38   phased implementation to ensure fiscal and operational prudence.
39
40   The proposed amendments preserve the original public service mandate of the
41   hospital while modernizing its legal framework, institutional classification, and
42   service scale. Strengthening pediatric referral infrastructure is a direct investment in
43   child health outcomes, equity in access to specialized care, and long-term human
44   capital development.

14   AN ACT AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 1340, ENTITLED “AN ACT
15   AMENDING SECTIONS ONE, TWO, THREE, FOUR AND FIVE OF
16   REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED SEVEN HUNDRED THIRTY-ONE
17   PROVIDING FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE NATIONAL
18   CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS FOR THE
19   PURPOSE,” RENAMING THE NATIONAL CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL AS
20   THE NATIONAL CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL AND MEDICAL CENTER AND
21   INCREASING ITS AUTHORIZED BED CAPACITY TO FOUR HUNDRED
22   (400)
23
24   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
25   Congress assembled:
26
27
28   SECTION 1. Amendment of Section 1 of Republic Act No. 1340. Section 1 of
29   Republic Act No. 1340 is hereby amended to read as follows:
30
31         “SECTION 1. THERE IS HEREBY ESTABLISHED UNDER THE
32         DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH THE NATIONAL CHILDREN’S
33         HOSPITAL     AND   MEDICAL    CENTER,    HEREINAFTER
34         REFERRED TO AS THE NCHMC, WHICH SHALL BE A TERTIARY
35         PEDIATRIC SPECIALTY REFERRAL HOSPITAL AND MEDICAL
36         CENTER UNDER THE MANAGEMENT AND SUPERVISION OF A
37         CHIEF OF HOSPITAL OR MEDICAL CENTER DIRECTOR, AS THE
38         CASE MAY BE, POSSESSING THE QUALIFICATIONS PRESCRIBED
39         BY THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH UNDER EXISTING LAWS,
40         RULES, AND REGULATIONS.
41
42         THE NCHMC SHALL HAVE ALL THE POWERS AND FUNCTIONS OF
43         A GOVERNMENT MEDICAL CENTER AND SHALL SERVE AS A
44         NATIONAL REFERRAL CENTER FOR PEDIATRIC CARE, TRAINING,
45         AND RESEARCH.”
46
47

 1   SEC. 2. Authorized Bed Capacity. A new paragraph is hereby inserted in Section
 2   1 of Republic Act No. 1340, as amended, to read as follows:
 3
 4         “THE AUTHORIZED BED CAPACITY OF THE NATIONAL
 5         CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL AND MEDICAL CENTER IS HEREBY
 6         INCREASED TO FOUR HUNDRED (400) BEDS. THE DEPARTMENT
 7         OF HEALTH SHALL IMPLEMENT THE BED CAPACITY INCREASE IN
 8         PHASES, CONSISTENT WITH SERVICE DEMAND, STAFFING
 9         STANDARDS, AND AVAILABLE APPROPRIATIONS.”
10
11   SEC. 3. All provisions of Republic Act No. 1340 and other laws, executive issuances,
12   rules, and regulations referring to the National Children’s Hospital are hereby
13   amended to read as National Children’s Hospital and Medical Center.
14
15   SEC. 4. Consistent with its designation as a medical center, the National Children’s
16   Hospital and Medical Center shall be upgraded to support expanded tertiary and
17   subspecialty pediatric services, training programs, and research functions, and the
18   Department of Health is hereby authorized to create additional plantilla positions and
19   upgrade existing ones as may be necessary.
20
21   SEC. 5. Appropriations. The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this
22   Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act under the budget of
23   the Department of Health.
24
25   SEC. 6. Separability Clause. If any provision of this Act is declared invalid, the
26   remainder shall not be affected.
27
28   SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations
29   inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
30
31   SEC. 8. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after publication in
32   the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
33
34   Approved,
