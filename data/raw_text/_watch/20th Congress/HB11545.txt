                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippines is recognized as one of the countries most vulnerable to the
adverse impacts of climate change. Severe weather events, accelerated
deforestation, watershed degradation, and loss of biodiversity threaten rural
livelihoods, compromise food and water security, and trigger catastrophic
disasters like flash floods and landslides.

While national environmental frameworks such as the Revised Forestry Code
(P.D. 705), the Climate Change Act of 2009 (R.A. 9729), and the National
Greening Program (NGP) exist, their implementation often lacks localized,
community-anchored execution and sustainable follow-through.

The Barangay is the primary planning and implementing unit of government
policies, plans, programs, projects, and activities in the community. It stands
at the frontline of disaster risk reduction and environmental management.
However, many barangays lack both explicit mandates and dedicated, localized
financial support to sustain forestland rehabilitation, seedling propagation,
and continuous protection activities within their territorial jurisdictions.

This bill seeks to institutionalize community-driven climate action by
mandating a nationwide Community-Based Greening and Forest
Rehabilitation Program at the barangay level. Specifically, this measure
mandates every barangay with degraded forestlands to establish community
nurseries, initiate localized reforestation, and conduct regular maintenance
and protection activities.

By decentralizing reforestation and empowering local communities as primary
stewards of their natural environment, we build climate-resilient barangays
and safeguard our ecosystems for future generations.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                          AN ACT
    PROMOTING GREENING, FORESTLAND REHABILITATION, AND
 PROTECTION AT THE BARANGAY LEVEL, SPURRING LOCAL CLIMATE
   CHANGE ACTION, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR
                      OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
Philippines in Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the "Barangay Forest
Rehabilitation and Green Local Climate Action Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect and
advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord
with the rhythm and harmony of nature. Recognizing the critical role of local
government units as partners in environmental management, the State shall
promote decentralized, community-driven greening, forestland rehabilitation,
and micro-scale climate action to build resilient communities, restore
ecosystem services, and secure rural livelihoods.

SEC. 3. Scope and Coverage. — This Act shall apply to all barangays across
the Philippines, with priority given to barangays situated within, adjacent to,
or encompassing:

   1. Public forestlands, timberlands, and protected areas;

   2. Watershed areas and critical headwaters;



   3. Coastal areas vulnerable to storm surges and erosion (for mangrove
      rehabilitation); and

   4. Degraded, ancestral, or disaster-prone community lands.
SEC. 4. Barangay Greening and Forest Rehabilitation Program (BGFRP). —
There is hereby established the Barangay Greening and Forest Rehabilitation
Program, to be implemented by all covered barangays in coordination with the
Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), Department of
the Interior and Local Government (DILG), and respective Municipal or City
Environment and Natural Resources Offices (C/MENRO).

The Program shall encompass:

   1. Establishment of Barangay Nurseries: Setting up community-managed
      tree nurseries producing native, endemic, fruit-bearing, and bamboo
      species;

   2. Targeted Reforestation & Agroforestry: Conducting systematically
      planned planting in identified rehabilitation sites, emphasizing
      indigenous species for watershed protection and agroforestry for local
      livelihood;

   3. Protection and Maintenance: Continuous monitoring, fire prevention,
      patrol, and maintenance of established green areas; and

   4. Urban & Coastal Greening: In urban barangays, the program shall focus
      on linear parks, riverbanks, pocket forests, vertical greening, and
      mangrove/coastal greenbelts where applicable.

SEC. 5. Barangay Green Corps (BGC). — Each participating barangay shall
organize a Barangay Green Corps (BGC) under the supervision of the Barangay
Development Council (BDC) and the Committee on Environment. The BGC shall
be composed of local volunteers, youth, indigenous peoples, and local
residents.

Members of the BGC shall receive appropriate training from the DENR and
LGU-MENRO/CENRO and shall be provided daily stipends or honoraria
through local Cash-for-Work programs or allocated project funds during active
rehabilitation and maintenance cycles.

SEC. 6. Integration into Barangay Plans. — Every barangay shall integrate its
localized greening, climate adaptation, and forest protection strategies into its
Barangay Development Plan (BDP) and Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Plan (BDRRMP).

SEC. 7. Institutional Support and Inter-Agency Roles. —

   1. DENR: Shall provide technical assistance, spatial mapping, soil
      assessment, seed/seedling stock, and verify site viability;

   2. DILG: Shall oversee LGU compliance, issue operational guidelines, and
      monitor program integration into local governance metrics;
   3. DA: Shall provide high-value agricultural trees, bamboo planting
      materials, and technical advice for integrated agroforestry projects.

SEC. 8. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations
of the DENR and DILG. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its
continued operation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
Act (GAA).

Additionally, Local Government Units (LGUs) are authorized and encouraged
to allocate a portion of their National Tax Allotment (NTA), Local Disaster Risk
Reduction and Management Fund (LDRRMF), and Special Development Funds
to support barangay-level greening initiatives.

SEC. 9. Incentive System. — Barangays demonstrating outstanding
performance in forest protection, survival rates of planted species, and
innovative local climate actions shall be eligible for financial grants, technical
support, and recognition under the annual "Outstanding Green Barangay
Awards" to be administered by the DILG and DENR.

SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DENR, in consultation with the DILG,
Department of Agriculture (DA), Climate Change Commission (CCC), and
representatives from local government associations, shall formulate and
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

SEC. 11. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in full force and effect.

SEC. 12. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, and
regulations inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 13. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.

Approved
