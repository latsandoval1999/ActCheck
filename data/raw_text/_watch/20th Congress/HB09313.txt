                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish an Environmental Service Premium (ESP) for local
government units (LGUs) hosting critical watersheds, recognize ecological services
as a basis for fiscal equity, and include critical watershed areas as a factor in the
computation of the National Tax Allotment under Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise
known as “The Local Government Code of 1991”. It advances a policy framework that
compensates environmental stewardship, strengthens climate resilience, and
supports sustainable development in communities that bear the responsibility of
protecting the country’s vital water resources.

       Across the country, watershed-hosting LGUs carry the burden of protecting
forests, regulating land use, and preventing environmental degradation despite
limited fiscal capacity. Watershed areas cover about seventy percent (70%) of the
Philippines’ land area and serve as critical sources of water for domestic,
agricultural, and industrial use1. At the same time, more than half of key river basins
remain unprotected, while only about twenty two percent (22%) of land area enjoys
formal protection status, exposing vast watershed areas to deforestation, erosion,
flooding, and biodiversity loss2. The Department of Environment and Natural
Resources has identified millions of hectares of watershed areas as highly vulnerable
to degradation, with consequences that include water shortages, reduced
agricultural productivity, and increased disaster risks. Real-world impacts already
manifest in major water systems such as the Ipo watershed, which supplies around
ninety six percent (96%) of Metro Manila’s water yet has experienced a significant
decline in forest cover, weakening its ecological stability3. Despite these, the current
fiscal framework under the Local Government Code does not account for

        This proposed measure institutionalizes an Environmental Service Premium
and the integration of watershed areas into the National Tax Allotment formula. It
establishes a more equitable fiscal mechanism that recognizes ecological
contributions as a measurable basis for resource distribution. It also provides clear
criteria for allocation and ensures that funds support watershed rehabilitation,
climate adaptation, disaster risk reduction, and sustainable livelihoods. These
reforms align fiscal policy with environmental governance and create stronger
incentives for LGUs to actively protect and manage critical ecosystems.

        This measure responds to an urgent national reality. Watersheds sustain cities,
irrigate farms, generate power, and shield communities from floods and droughts,
yet the LGUs that protect them often struggle with limited resources and competing
demands for development. Every degraded watershed places millions at risk of
water scarcity and disaster. Every neglected forest weakens the country’s resilience
against climate change. This bill asserts that environmental stewardship deserves
recognition, not neglect. It declares that those who protect the nation’s lifelines must
no longer stand alone. The passage of this measure affirms a simple but powerful
principle: the survival of communities, the security of water, and the future of the
nation depend on how decisively we act today.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING AN ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICE PREMIUM FOR LOCAL
GOVERNMENT UNITS HOSTING CRITICAL WATERSHEDS, PROVIDING
FOR THE INCLUSION OF CRITICAL WATERSHED AREAS IN THE
COMPUTATION OF THE NATIONAL TAX ALLOTMENT, AMENDING FOR
THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “THE
LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991”, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Environmental Service
Premium and Watershed Equity Act.”

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote equitable fiscal distribution among local government units (LGUs) by
recognizing not only population and land area, but also the ecological burdens and
responsibilities brought by certain jurisdictions. Further, the State affirms the need to
recognize and compensate for the ecosystem services provided by LGUs hosting
critical watersheds, which play a vital role in water security, biodiversity
conservation, climate regulation, and disaster risk reduction.

       Toward this end, the State shall strengthen environmental protection and
climate resilience by incentivizing sustainable land use and watershed conservation.
It also supports participatory and inclusive governance that recognizes the role of
Indigenous Peoples and local communities as primary stewards of these ecosystems.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

           a. Critical Watershed refers to a drainage area of a river system supporting
       existing and proposed hydro-electric power, irrigation works or domestic
       water facilities needing immediate rehabilitation as it is being subjected to a
       fast rate of denudation causing accelerated erosion and destructive floods.
       This shall include areas declared as such by law, executive issuance, or
       certified by the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR),

       Updating all Forestry Laws and For Other Purposes”, as amended;

          b. Environmental Service Premium (ESP) refers to the additional share
       granted to LGUs hosting critical watersheds in recognition of ecological
       services and stewardship obligations; and

           c. Host LGU refers to any province, city, municipality, or barangay with
       territorial jurisdiction over all or part of a certified critical watershed.

       SEC. 4. Inclusion in National Tax Allotment Formula. – In addition to the factors
currently provided under existing laws, certified critical watershed areas shall be
considered as an additional factor in the computation of the National Tax Allotment
of host LGUs.

      Congress shall determine the percentage weight of such factor upon the
recommendation of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), DENR,
Department of Economy, Planning and Development (DEPDev), and Department of
the Interior and Local Government (DILG) following technical studies and
stakeholder consultations.

       SEC. 5. Environmental Service Premium. – There is hereby established an
Environmental Service Premium to be granted annually to qualified host LGUs,
sourced from appropriations authorized by Congress or from a designated portion
of the National Tax Allotment pool as may be provided by law. The ESP shall be
distributed based on:

          a.​ Size of certified watershed area;

          b.​ Ecological significance and vulnerability;

          c.​ Performance in conservation and environmental compliance;

          d.​ Presence of indigenous stewardship systems; and

          e.​ Other criteria to be determined under the Implementing Rules and
       Regulations.

       SEC. 6. Use of Funds. – Amounts received under this Act shall be used
exclusively for:

          a.​ Watershed rehabilitation and forest protection;

          b.​ Climate adaptation and disaster risk reduction projects;

          c.​ Sustainable livelihood programs for affected communities;

          d.​ Indigenous Peoples’ environmental stewardship support;

          e.​ Water resource management and monitoring systems; and

          f.​ Other related environmental programs.

       SEC. 7. Multidisciplinary Feasibility Study and Technical Review. – Within one (1)
year from the effectivity of this Act, the DBM, DENR, DILG, and DEPDev shall
jointly conduct a multidisciplinary technical feasibility study and fiscal impact
assessment for the phased implementation of the Environmental Service Premium
and revised NTA formula.

       SEC 8. Transparency and Accountability. – All funds released under this Act
shall be subject to accounting, auditing, and public disclosure requirements under
existing laws. LGUs shall submit annual utilization and performance reports.

        SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within one hundred twenty (120)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the DBM, DENR, DILG, DEPDev, and the
Department of Finance shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the
effective implementation of this Act.

      SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette, or in a newspaper of general circulation.
