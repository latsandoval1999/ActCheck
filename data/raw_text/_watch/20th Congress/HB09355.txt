                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The State recognizes that infrastructure is a critical instrument for
regional integration and equitable access to opportunities. Road networks
that provide alternative corridors between major urban centers are
essential in strengthening connectivity, improving mobility, and ensuring
the efficient movement of goods and services across regions.

      The Iligan City–Cagayan de Oro City Alternate Road, particularly the
Digkilaan–Bulua Section, serves as a vital link between Iligan City and the
Municipality of Opol in Misamis Oriental. A feasibility study has already been
conducted, affirming its viability and strategic importance. Beginning at the
Barangay Hall of Digkilaan and extending toward Limuda Integrated School
in Barangay Mainit, Opol, the road establishes a strategic inter-city and inter-
provincial connection.

      Road opening and concreting works are being undertaken by both the
regional office of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH)
and the Iligan City District Engineering Office. However, key segments
remain incomplete, limiting continuity and preventing the road from fully
functioning as a reliable alternate corridor.

      Once completed, the road will enhance mobility between Iligan City
and Cagayan de Oro City, decongest existing routes, and strengthen
transport resilience across Northern Mindanao. It will also improve access
to essential services and facilitate trade and logistics.
      Declaring the Iligan City–Cagayan de Oro City Alternate Road a
national road and ensuring its full construction and completion require
sustained national support. This will enable the DPWH to implement
comprehensive infrastructure works in accordance with national standards.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
 DECLARING THE ILIGAN CITY–CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY ALTERNATE
ROAD (DIGKILAAN–BULUA SECTION) A NATIONAL ROAD, PROVIDING
    FOR ITS CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT AND MAINTENANCE,
            AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Iligan
City–Cagayan de Oro City Alternate National Road (Digkilaan–Bulua
Section) Act.”

      SECTION 2. Declaration. — The Iligan City–Cagayan de Oro City
Alternate Road (Digkilaan–Bulua Section) in Barangay Digkilaan, Iligan City,
traversing from the Barangay Hall of Digkilaan to Limuda Integrated School
in Barangay Mainit, Opol, Misamis Oriental, shall be declared a national road
under the supervision and administration of the Department of Public
Works and Highways (DPWH).

      SECTION 3. Development and Maintenance. — The DPWH shall
undertake the construction, completion, improvement, and maintenance of
the road in accordance with national standards and specifications.

      SECTION 4. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the
continued construction, improvement, and maintenance of the road, as well
as other activities necessary for its development as a national road, shall be
included in the General Appropriations Act (GAA). Thereafter, the Secretary
of Public Works and Highways shall include the funding requirements
therefor in the Department’s program for inclusion in the annual GAA.
       SECTION 5. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
