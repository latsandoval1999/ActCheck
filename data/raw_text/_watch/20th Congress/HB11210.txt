                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Personnel Economic Relief Allowance (PERA) was first granted in 1991 at ₱500 per month. In
2009, through Joint Resolution No. 4 of the Senate and the House of Representatives, the ₱1,500
Additional Compensation and the ₱500 PERA were consolidated into a fixed monthly allowance of
₱2,000 for all government employees. Since then, no further increase has been legislated.

For more than sixteen years, this allowance has remained stagnant despite the undeniable rise in the
cost of living. PERA was intended to provide modest relief to public servants against inflation and
the increasing prices of basic goods and services. However, its current level has long ceased to fulfill
its purpose. The erosion of purchasing power has left government employees—particularly those in
the lower and middle income brackets—struggling to meet the daily needs of their families.

The situation has grown heavier because of the lasting effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, the
unstable rise and fall of fuel and petroleum prices, the pressure brought by tax reforms like the
TRAIN Law, and the unrest in West Asia. Together, these forces have placed a greater burden on
ordinary families, including those of our civil servants, making daily life harder and more uncertain.

It is a fundamental duty of the State to ensure that the men and women who keep the machinery of
government running are afforded fair and reasonable compensation. Public service requires
sacrifice, but it should not demand that our employees endure economic hardship without adequate
support. By increasing the PERA to ₱5,000 per month, this measure seeks to restore fairness, provide
genuine relief, and recognize the indispensable role of government employees in nation-building

For the foregoing reasons, the passage of this bill is sought.

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 1   SECTION 1. Increase in the Personnel Economic ReliefAllowance. The Personnel Economic
 2   Relief Allowance (PERA) is hereby increased from the present Two Thousand Pesos (P2,000) per
 3   month to Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000) per month.
 4   The PERA shall likewise be subject to automatic yearly adjustments equivalent to any increase of
 5   the annual inflation rate in the country.
 6   SEC. 2. Coverage. The PERA shall be granted to civilian government personnel whether employed
 7   by the national or local governments, appointive or elective, and whether occupying regular,
 8   contractual, or casual positions, whose positions are covered by Republic Act 6758, otherwise
 9   known as the "Compensation and Position Classification Act of 1989," as amended, as well as
10   military and uniformed personnel, Provided, that the government personnel stationed abroad already
11   receiving overseas allowances shall not be entitled to PERA.
12   SEC. 3. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be
13   taken from current appropriations. Thereafter, funds needed in subsequent years shall be included
14   in the General Appropriations Act.
15   For local government units, the PERA shall be charged against their respective local funds. Any
16   deficiency shall be charged against the balances of the National Tax Allotments, which are hereby
17   authorized to be realigned for the purpose. Thereafter. the amount necessary shall be provided in the
18   respective local budgets.
19   SEC. 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. The Department of Budget and Management shall
20   issue implementing rules and regulations within fifteen (15) days from the effectivity of this Act.
21   SEC. 5. Separability Clause. If, for any reason, any provision of this Act is held unconstitutional,
22   all other provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.
23   SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, rules and regulations, and other issuances inconsistent
24   with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
25   SEC. 7. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
26   Gazette or one (1) newspaper of general circulation.
