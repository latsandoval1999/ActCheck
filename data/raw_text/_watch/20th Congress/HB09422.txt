                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to simplify and standardize the documentary
requirements for availing government financial assistance in order to ensure
the timely, efficient, and dignified delivery of aid to individuals and families
in need.

       Despite the State’s mandate to promote social justice and provide
responsive social protection, existing systems for availing government
assistance remain inconsistent across implementing agencies. Programs under
the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of
Health (DOH), Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), and the
Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) often impose overlapping
requirements which result in delays that undermine the urgency of assistance,
particularly in medical emergencies, burial needs, and other crisis situations.

      The need for reform is underscored by recent developments illustrating
the human cost of administrative inefficiencies in aid distribution. A reported
incident on 19 April 2026 in Quezon City, involving a motorcycle taxi rider
who died while waiting in line for a fuel subsidy payout, highlights the
urgency of streamlining government assistance systems to prevent avoidable
hardship and ensure that support reaches beneficiaries in a timely manner.[1]

      While Republic Act No. 9485, otherwise known as the “Anti-Red Tape
Act of 2007”, as amended, mandates the simplification of procedure and
reduction of bureaucratic inefficiencies in government services, the absence of
a uniform standard for documentary requirements allow administrative
discretion to persist, creating barriers for indigent and vulnerable
beneficiaries who are least equipped to comply with complex processes.

                     AN ACT
SIMPLIFYING  THE DOCUMENTARY   REQUIREMENTS   FOR
ACCESSING GOVERNMENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Simplified
Government Aid Access Act.”

      SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to promote the general welfare of Filipino people by ensuring that individuals
and families experiencing difficult situations are provided with timely and
accessible financial assistance. Towards this end, the government shall adopt
measures to remove excessive documentary requirements and streamline
procedures for availing aid from government agencies.

       SEC. 3. Scope and Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all national and
local government agencies and instrumentalities, including but not limited to
the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of
Health (DOH), and Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth),
which provide medical, burial, and similar forms of financial assistance.

      SEC. 4. Standardized Minimum Requirements. – To avail of immediate
financial assistance, beneficiaries in need shall only be required to submit the
following documents:

          a. Valid government-issued identification card (ID);




                                         1
         b.​ Billing statement from the hospital, clinic, funeral parlor, or
      related entities; and

         c.​ Referral or endorsement letter issued by the City or Municipal
      Social Welfare and Development Office or duly authorized local social
      worker.

       In cases where the request of assistance is made through a
representative, such representative shall present an authorization letter duly
signed by the beneficiary, along with a valid government-issued ID of both
the beneficiary and the representative. The authorized representative must be
a relative of the beneficiary within the fourth degree of consanguinity or
affinity, or a legal guardian. Provided, That no individual shall act as a
representative for more than two (2) beneficiaries to whom they are not
related.

      SEC. 5. Streamlined Processing. – All government agencies concerned
shall process requests for assistance within three (3) working days upon
submission of complete requirements under Section 4 of this Act.

      SEC. 6. Coordination with the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA). – The
DSWD and all implementing agencies shall coordinate with the Anti-Red
Tape Authority (ARTA) to ensure full compliance with Republic Act No. 9485,
as amended also known as the Anti-Red Tape Act. They shall likewise work
with ARTA to address complaints involving undue delays, excessive
documentary requirements, or any form of non-compliance with the
provisions of this Act.

       SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within thirty (30) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DSWD, in coordination with ARTA, DOH,
PhilHealth, Department of the Interior and Local Government, and the
Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office shall promulgate the implementing
rules and regulations.

      SEC. 8. Separability Clause. – Should any provision or part of this Act be
declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions and parts hereof,
insofar as they are separable from the invalid ones, shall remain in full force
and effect.

       SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, proclamations,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
