HOUSE BILL No. 11827

Introduced by Representative EDGAR R. ERICE

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Government hospitals must be able to respond to the actual health needs of the communities
they serve. As populations grow and demand for medical services increases, some Department
of Health hospitals find themselves operating beyond their authorized bed capacity or requiring
additional services, personnel, and facilities.

At present, increasing the authorized bed capacity of a government hospital generally requires
legislation. While congressional oversight remains important, requiring a separate law every
time a DOH hospital needs to adjust its bed capacity can delay improvements that are already
justified by actual patient demand.

Hospital capacity should be able to respond more efficiently to changing conditions.

This bill seeks to authorize the Department of Health to determine and approve the bed capacity
and service capability of hospitals under its direct supervision and control. Such decisions shall
be based on objective considerations, including actual hospital utilization, population and
health needs of the area served, availability of other health facilities, demand for specialized
services, and the capacity of the hospital to safely provide additional services.

The measure also recognizes that increasing the number of authorized beds alone does not
necessarily improve health care. Additional beds must be supported by sufficient doctors,
nurses and other health personnel, as well as the necessary equipment, facilities, medicines,
and operating resources. Any expansion in hospital capacity must therefore be accompanied
by corresponding improvements in staffing and service capability.

By allowing the DOH to make evidence-based adjustments while retaining appropriate
safeguards and congressional oversight, government hospitals can respond more quickly to
increasing patient demand without sacrificing the quality and safety of health services.

Ultimately, hospital capacity should reflect the number of Filipinos who actually need care
rather than remain limited by figures that may no longer reflect present conditions.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT
AUTHORIZING THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH TO DETERMINE AND
APPROVE THE BED CAPACITY AND SERVICE CAPABILITY OF HOSPITALS
UNDER ITS DIRECT SUPERVISION AND CONTROL, PROVIDING STANDARDS
AND SAFEGUARDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SEC. 1. Short Title. This Act shall be known as the “DOH Hospital Bed Capacity and
Service Capability Rationalization Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect and
promote the right to health of the people by ensuring the availability, accessibility, efficiency,
and responsiveness of government hospital services.

Towards this end, the State shall provide the Department of Health (DOH) with sufficient
flexibility to adjust the bed capacity and service capability of hospitals under its direct
supervision and control according to actual and projected health needs, while ensuring that
such adjustments are supported by adequate health personnel. facilities, equipment, and
financial resources.

SEC. 3. Authority of the Department of Health. Notwithstanding any provision of law to the
contrary, the DOH is hereby authorized to determine, set, and approve the authorized bed
capacity and service capability of all existing and newly established hospitals under its direct
supervision and control.

The DOH may increase, decrease, or otherwise adjust the authorized bed capacity and service
capability of a DOH hospital whenever such adjustment is justified by the health needs of the
population and the operational requirements of the hospital, subject to the standards and
safeguards provided under this Act.

No adjustment shall diminish the existing capacity or level of service of a hospital when such
reduction would adversely affect access to essential health services in the area it serves.

SEC. 4. Criteria for Determining Bed Capacity and Service Capability. In determining or
adjusting the authorized bed capacity and service capability of a DOH hospital, the DOH shall
consider, among others:

a) Actual and projected hospital occupancy and utilization rates;

b) Population, demographic trends, and projected population growth within the hospital's
catchment area;

c) Disease burden and prevailing health needs of the population served;

d) Patient volume, including emergency room consultations, admissions, outpatient
services, and referrals;

e) Availability and accessibility of other public and private hospitals and health facilities
within the area;

f) Demand for specialty and subspecialty services;

g) Geographic accessibility and the hospital's role within the applicable health care
provider network;

h) Availability of qualified health personnel;

i) Existing and required hospital infrastructure, equipment, and support services;

j) Capacity of the hospital to safely and effectively accommodate the proposed
adjustment; and

k) Such other relevant factors as may be determined by the DOH.

The DOH shall establish uniform standards and procedures for the assessment of hospitals
under this Section.

SEC. 5. Corresponding Adjustment in Service Capability. An increase in authorized bed
capacity shall, whenever necessary, be accompanied by a corresponding improvement in the
hospital's service capability, including appropriate facilities, equipment, diagnostic services,
pharmacy services, emergency services, and other support systems necessary for the safe and
effective operation of the additional capacity.

The DOH shail ensure that increases in bed capacity are not implemented solely as numerical
adjustments without the resources necessary to provide appropriate patient care.

SEC. 6. Health Human Resources. Any increase in the authorized bed capacity or service
capability of a DOH hospital shall be accompanied by an assessment of the additional health
human resources necessary to safely and effectively operate such increased capacity.

The DOH shall determine the appropriate staffing pattern for the hospital consistent with
applicable standards and shall coordinate with the Department of Budget and Management
(DBM) for the creation, upgrading, or filling of positions that may be necessary, subject to
applicable civil service, budgeting, and compensation laws, rules, and regulations.

SEC. 7. Hospital Development Plan. Each DOH hospital seeking or requiring a substantial
adjustment in bed capacity or service capability shall prepare or update its hospital
development plan in accordance with standards prescribed by the DOH.

The plan shall identify the hospital's existing and projected capacity, health human resource
requirements, infrastructure and equipment needs, service capability, projected patient
demand, and estimated financial requirements.

The DOH may require phased implementation where immediate full implementation is not
operationally or financially practicable.

SEC. 8. Funding Requirements. The financial requirements necessary to implement approved
adjustments in the bed capacity and service capability of DOH hospitals, including
corresponding requirements for health personnel, facilities, equipment, medicines, supplies,
and operating expenses, shall be included in the DOH's annual budget proposal.

Implementation of approved adjustments shall be subject to the availability of appropriations
and applicable budgeting, accounting, auditing, and procurement laws, rules, and regulations.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed as authorizing the operation of additional hospital beds
or services without adequate resources necessary to ensure patient safety and quality of care.

SEC. 9. Transparency and Reporting. The DOH shall maintain and make publicly available
an updated list of DOH hospitals indicating their respective authorized bed capacities and
service capabilities.

The DOH shall submit to the Committees on Health and Appropriations of the Senate and the
House of Representatives an annual report on the implementation of this Act.

The report shall include:

Section 10. Non-Diminution of Congressional Authority. Nothing in this Act shall be
construed to authorize the DOH to establish a new government hospital where the creation or
establishment of such hospital requires an Act of Congress.

The authority granted under this Act shall apply to the determination and adjustment of the bed
capacity and service capability of hospitals lawfully established and placed under the direct
supervision and control of the DOH.

SEC. 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity
of this Act, the DOH, in coordination with the DBM and after consultation with DOH hospitals
and other relevant stakeholders, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for its
effective implementation.

The implementing rules shall prescribe uniform standards, procedures, documentary
requirements, assessment criteria, and monitoring mechanisms for adjustments in hospital bed
capacity and service capability.

SEC. 12. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall
be charged against the current appropriations of the DOH.

Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued implementation shall be included
in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SEC. 13. Separability Clause. \f any provision or part of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions or parts not affected thereby shall remain in full force and
effect.

SEC. 14. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, and
other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended. or
modified accordingly.

SEC. 15. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
