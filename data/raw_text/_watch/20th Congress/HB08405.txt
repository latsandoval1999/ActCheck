                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to establish a vegetable processing plant in the province of
Benguet to mitigate the perennial oversupply problem of farmers. This plant will be
equipped with technology for processing, preservation, and packaging of vegetables
to enhance their market value and shelf life. The plant will provide farmers with the
means to process and add value to their produce, enabling them to access new
markets and reduce the impact of oversupply.

       The oversupply of vegetables is a recurring problem for Benguet farmers
annually, leading to significant economic challenges. Reports from news agencies
frequently highlight instances where unsold vegetables are discarded, reflecting a
severe mismatch between production and market demand1. This surplus often
results in vegetables being dumped, causing food waste and financial losses to
farmers. To mitigate further losses, some farmers opt to leave their crops to wither in
the fields rather than harvest them, as the costs associated with harvesting,
transporting, and marketing these vegetables can outweigh potential revenue. This
practice not only increases food waste but also represents a missed opportunity to
use these resources effectively.

       The occurrence of vegetable oversupply may be inevitable but processing
these surplus vegetables into new products can significantly mitigate the problem.
For instance, processing Wombok (Chinese cabbage) into Kimchi, potatoes into
ready-to-cook fries, preserved carrots and peas, and other crops into dehydrated
snacks and condiments offers a viable solution. Unfortunately, Benguet lacks the
necessary processing plant to address this.

       The development and implementation of market promotion campaigns will
further increase awareness and demand for processed Benguet vegetables and other
crops in both domestic and international markets. This effort will focus on branding
Benguet vegetables as premium products known for their quality, freshness, and
nutritional value, thereby enhancing their competitiveness in the global market.

                           AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A VEGETABLE PROCESSING PLANT IN THE PROVINCE OF
BENGUET, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREOF

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as "The Benguet Vegetable
Processing Plant Act".

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote sustainable agricultural practices, reduce food waste, and enhance the
profitability of farming through the establishment of a processing plant. This Act
aims to address the issue of vegetable oversupply in Benguet by providing the
necessary infrastructure to enable farmers to process and add value to their farm
produce.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

          a. Vegetable Processing Plant refers to a specialized infrastructure
       equipped with machinery, technology, and plants necessary for training and
       processing, preservation, and packaging of vegetables into various products;

           b. Farmers Organization refers to any association or cooperative
       composed primarily of farmers engaged in vegetable production and related
       activities within Benguet; and

           c. Market Promotion Campaigns refer to strategic initiatives and
       activities aimed at raising awareness, enhancing visibility, and increasing
       demand for processed Benguet vegetables in both domestic and international
       markets. These campaigns include branding, marketing, and promotional
       efforts to highlight the quality, freshness, and nutritional value of Benguet
       vegetables.

       SEC. 4. Establishment of the Benguet Vegetable Processing Plant. – The
Department of Agriculture (DA) is hereby mandated to establish a vegetable
processing plant in a strategic location within the province of Benguet. This plant
shall be equipped with machineries and technology for training, processing,
preservation, and packaging of vegetables to enhance their market value and shelf
life. The establishment of the vegetable processing plant shall prioritize
environmentally sustainable practices, including energy-efficient technologies and
waste management systems to minimize environmental impact. The DA, in
coordination with relevant agencies, shall oversee the planning, construction, and
operation of the vegetable processing plant, ensuring alignment with national
agricultural policies and standards.

        SEC. 5. Benguet Vegetable Processing Plant Board. – The Benguet Vegetable
Processing Plant Board (BVPPB) is hereby established to oversee the implementation
of this Act. The council shall be composed of the following:

         a.​ Provincial Agriculturist of Benguet-PLGU as Chairperson;

         b.​ A representative from the DA Regional Office;

         c.​ A representative from the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI);

         d.​ Benguet Vegetable Processing Center as Manager;

        e.​ One (1) expert in        agricultural processing appointed by the
      Chairperson, if applicable;

         f.​ One (1) expert in marketing appointed by the Chairperson, if
      applicable;

         g.​ One (1) representative from private sector partner, if applicable;

         h.​ Five (5) representatives from the Provincial Federation of Farmers
      Organizations in Benguet, one (1) representative from the following:

               i.​   Head, Heart, Hands, Health Club (4-H Club);

              ii.​   Rural Improvement Club (RIC);

              iii.​ Pambansang Mannalon, Maguuma, Magbabaul, Magsasaka ng
             Pilipinas (P4MP);

              iv.​   Federation of Benguet Farmers’ Cooperative; and

              v.​ Accredited Non-Government             Organization     relative   to
             Agriculture and Food Processing.

       The members of the board shall receive emoluments as determined by the
Board and in accordance with existing budget and accounting rules and regulations.
The representatives from farmers' organizations shall be endorsed by their respective
organization through a resolution duly approved by their officers for a term of three
(3) years, which can be renewed by the respective organization.

     SEC. 7. Roles and Responsibilities Benguet Vegetable Processing Plant Board
(BVPPB). – The Board shall be responsible for:

          a.​ Policy Development and Implementation

               i.​ Develop comprehensive policies and guidelines for the efficient
             and effective operation of the vegetable processing plant;

              ii.​ Ensure that the policies and guidelines align with national
             agricultural goals and sustainable practices; and

              iii.​ Regularly review and update the policies to adapt to changing
             market conditions, technological advancements, and feedback from
             stakeholders.

          b.​ Address Operational Issues and Concerns

               i.​ Serve as the primary body for addressing any issues or concerns
             that arise in the day-to-day operations of the plant;

              ii.​ Implement mechanisms for receiving and resolving complaints,
             suggestions, and feedback from farmers, staff, and other stakeholders;
             and

              iii.​ Facilitate dialogue and mediation in case of disputes or conflicts
             related to the use of the plant.

          c.​ Coordination of Market Promotion Campaigns and Market Linkages

               i.​ Develop and coordinate comprehensive market promotion
             campaigns to increase awareness and demand for processed Benguet
             vegetables in both domestic and international markets; and

              ii.​ Establish and strengthen market linkages between farmers,
             processors, distributors, and retailers.

          d.​ Monitoring and Evaluation

               i.​ Implement a strong monitoring and evaluation framework to
             continuously assess the effectiveness of the plant and programs;

      The roles and responsibilities of the BVPPB may be refined or expanded

       SEC. 8. Management Staff. – The BVPPB shall appoint a Manager to oversee the
overall operations of the vegetable processing plant. The Manager shall be
supported by a dedicated team of technical and administrative staff. The
organizational structure, along with its duties and responsibilities, shall be crafted in
the Implementing Rules and Regulations based on the needs of the operation.

       SEC. 9. Marketing and Distribution of Processed Products. – The processed
vegetable products produced by the Benguet Vegetable Processing Plant shall be
marketed, distributed, and sold by the Plant through its management, or in
coordination with the Provincial Local Government Unit of Benguet, DTI, DA, and
other relevant agencies.

       The Plant, or with its partner agencies, as applicable, shall:

          a.​ Formulate and implement marketing strategies to promote the
       processed products in local, national, and international markets;

          b.​ Establish partnerships with wholesalers, retailers, institutional buyers,
       and online marketplaces to widen distribution channels;

          c.​ Ensure that marketing and sales activities are aligned with the market
       promotion campaigns developed under this Act;

          d.​ Maintain transparent accounting of revenues generated from sales,
       subject to existing auditing and government accounting rules and regulations;
       and

           e.​ Reinvest a portion of the revenues into the improvement, maintenance,
       and expansion of the plant and its services, consistent with BVPPB-approved
       financial management policies.

      The BVPPB shall issue guidelines on pricing, branding, labeling, and
revenue-sharing arrangements to ensure fairness, sustainability, and compliance
with national quality and food safety standards.

      SEC. 10. Market Development and Promotion. – The DA, in collaboration with
the Department of Tourism, DTI, and other relevant agencies, shall develop and

implement market promotion campaigns and market linkages to increase awareness

       SEC 11. Funding. – The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act
shall be appropriated from the national budget to finance the establishment of the
vegetable processing plant. The operation and maintenance cost of the plant shall be
allocated in the annual appropriations of the DA. Additional funding may be
sourced from public-private partnerships, international grants, and other lawful
means to supplement the budget allocated.

       SEC 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within one hundred twenty
(120) days from the effectivity of this Act, the DA, in consultation with relevant
stakeholders, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act.

      SEC. 13. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, letters of instructions,
rules and regulations and other issuances, or provision thereof, which are
inconsistent with the of this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified
accordingly.

       SEC. 14. Separability Clause. – If any provision or portion of this Act are
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected, shall remain
valid and subsisting.

      SEC. 15. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
