                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

A significant number of government workers serve the State under non-career appointments,
including those on contractual, co-terminus, project-based, or fixed-term arrangements. While
these personnel perform essential functions across government agencies, their employment status
often prevents them from accumulating the continuous length of service required to qualify for
retirement benefits under existing laws. As a result, many non-career government workers reach
the end of their working years without adequate retirement security, despite years of meaningful
public service.

Republic Act No. 8291, otherwise known as the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS)
Act of 1997, requires a minimum of fifteen (15) years of credited service for entitlement to
retirement benefits. This requirement, while appropriate for career personnel, has had the
unintended effect of excluding non-career employees who, due to the nature of their appointments,
are unable to complete the required period of service even though they have consistently
contributed to government operations and programs.

This bill seeks to address this structural inequity by expressly extending GSIS retirement coverage
to qualified members in the non-career service. By amending Sections 13(b) and 13-A of Republic
Act No. 8291, the measure clarifies that non-career personnel may avail of retirement and other
applicable benefits under the GSIS, subject to the conditions provided by law.

Central to this reform is the insertion of a new Section 13-B, which provides non-career members
who have not completed the minimum fifteen (15) years of contributions with the option to
voluntarily continue paying GSIS premiums. Under this provision, qualified members may pay
both the personal and agency share of contributions until they meet the minimum requirement for
retirement benefits. To promote affordability and prevent undue financial burden, the bill
authorizes the GSIS to allow contributions to be based on a lower salary grade, under reasonable
terms and conditions.

This voluntary contribution mechanism recognizes the realities faced by non-career government
workers, many of whom experience intermittent employment and income insecurity. It affirms the
principle that years of public service—regardless of employment classification—should not result
in exclusion from social protection in old age.

This proposed measure is a refiled bill that was approved on Third Reading during the Nineteenth
Congress, demonstrating prior legislative acknowledgment of the need to make the government
retirement system more inclusive and responsive to changing labor arrangements in the public
sector.

Ultimately, this bill advances fairness, dignity, and social justice by ensuring that non-career
government workers are afforded a viable pathway to retirement benefits. By strengthening the
inclusiveness of the GSIS, the measure reinforces the State’s commitment to protecting all
government workers who have devoted their labor to public service. The passage of this bill is
earnestly sought.

Be it enacted in the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Republic of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

       SECTION 1. Section 13 (b) of Republic Act (R.A.) No. 8291, otherwise known as
       “The Government Service Insurance System Act of 1997” is hereby amended to
       read as follows:

              "SEC. 13. Retirement Benefits. -

              x x x…

              (b) Unless the service is extended by appropriate authorities,
              retirement shall be compulsory for an employee at sixty-five (65)
              years of age, with at least fifteen (15) years of service: Provided, That
              [if he] A MEMBER WHO has less than fifteen (15) years of service

                                                                                             3
      [, he] may be allowed to continue in the service in accordance with
      existing civil service rules and regulations[.]: PROVIDED FURTHER,
      THAT MEMBERS IN THE NON-CAREER SERVICE, AS DEFINED
      IN SECTION 9, CHAPTER 2, SUBTITLE A, TITLE I, BOOK V, OF
      EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 292, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
      “ADMINISTRATIVE CODE OF 1987,” MAY AVAIL OF THE
      RETIREMENT AND OTHER APPLICABLE BENEFITS UNDER
      THIS ACT, AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 13-B HEREIN.”

SEC. 2. Section 13-A of RA 8291 is hereby amended to read as follows:

      "SEC. 13-A. Conditions for Entitlement. - A member who retires
      from the service shall be entitled to the retirement benefits in
      paragraph (a) of Section 13 hereof: Provided, That THE MEMBER:

      (1) [he] has rendered at least fifteen (15) years of service;

      (2) [he] is at least sixty (60) years of age at the time of
           retirement; and

      (3) [he] is not receiving a monthly pension benefit from
           permanent total disability”

SEC. 3. A new Section 13-B is hereby inserted to RA 8291, to read as follows:

      "SEC. 13-B. OPTION FOR MEMBERS IN THE NON-CAREER
      SERVICE. - A MEMBER IN THE NON-CAREER SERVICE WHO
      HAS NOT CONTRIBUTED AT LEAST FIFTEEN (15) YEARS OF
      PREMIUM SHALL BE ALLOWED TO CONTINUE TO PAY THE
      FULL PREMIUMS, REPRESENTING THE PERSONAL AND
      AGENCY SHARE, UNTIL SUCH MEMBER SHALL HAVE
      COMPLETED THE MINIMUM NUMBER OF YEARS TO QUALIFY
      FOR RETIREMENT BENEFIT: PROVIDED, HOWEVER, THAT
      THE GSIS SHALL ALLOW A MEMBER TO PAY THE PREMIUM
      FOR A LOWER SALARY GRADE, UNDER REASONABLE
      TERMS AND CONDITIONS, TO PROVIDE FLEXIBILITY AND
      AFFORDABILITY ON THE PART OF THE MEMBER.”
SEC. 4. Nothing herein shall diminish the retirement and separation benefits
already enjoyed under the existing laws at the time of retirement.

SEC. 5. Within sixty (60) days after the effectivity of this Act, the GSIS shall
promulgate the rules and regulations to implement this Act.
