                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to institutionalize a comprehensive and accessible
financial education program for Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and their
families. This promotes financial security, informed decision-making, and the
productive use of remittances.

      OFWs continue to play a vital role in the Philippine economy through
the remittances they send to support their families and contribute to national
development. Despite these contributions, many OFWs and their households
remain vulnerable to excessive debt, fraudulent investment schemes, and
financial mismanagement.

       Financial literacy is essential to ensure that the earnings of OFWs
translate into sustainable economic gains for themselves and their families.
Equipping them with practical knowledge on budgeting, savings,
investments, credit management, insurance, and consumer protection
strengthens their capacity to build financial resilience and prepare for future
uncertainties.

       This measure integrates mandatory and regularly updated financial
education into the migration cycle by incorporating such training into
Pre-Departure Orientation Seminars (PDOS) and Post-Arrival Training
Seminars (PATS). It likewise extends financial education services to the
families of OFWs through online and community-based platforms,
recognizing their invaluable role in managing remittances and household
finances. The bill further strengthens public awareness against online scams,
fraudulent schemes, predatory lending practices, and other financial threats
that target OFWs and their dependents.
      Consistent with the State’s constitutional duty to protect labor and
promote the welfare of Filipino workers overseas, this measure empowers
OFWs and their families with the knowledge and skills necessary to achieve
long-term financial stability and maximize the benefits of overseas
employment.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                        AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR A FREE FINANCIAL EDUCATION TO OVERSEAS
FILIPINO WORKERS AND THEIR FAMILIES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Free OFW
Financial Education Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State
to afford full protection to local and overseas labor and promote full
employment and equality of employment opportunities for all. Toward this
end, the State shall have the continuous duty to acknowledge the valuable
contribution of Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) to our economy through
their remittances. In doing so, the State shall adopt measures that will take
care of the welfare and benefits of OFWs and their families like the provision
for a free financial education or literacy training to equip them with enough
knowledge for a proper and wise use of their hard-earned money.

      SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall cover all OFWs, either land-based or
sea-based, documented or otherwise, and their families.

       SEC. 4. Mandatory Financial Education Seminar. – All OFWs shall
undergo mandatory and continuously updated financial education or literacy
training seminars which shall become an integral part of the Pre-Departure
Orientation Seminars (PDOS) of departing OFWs. Moreover, Post-Arrival
Training Seminars (PATS) on financial education or literacy shall likewise be
required of OFWs within a reasonable time upon arrival in their respective
countries of destination on their first employment contract, or those who
transfer from one place of work to another without stopping by the

Philippines. Overseas Filipino Seafarers shall have the option of attending
these continuously updated financial education or literacy training seminars
at their respective points-of-hire, or within a reasonable time, after their return
to the Philippines.

      Families of OFWs throughout the Philippines shall likewise be
equipped with financial education and knowledge through on-line seminars
and other feasible and effective means.

      SEC. 5. Participating Agencies. – The PDOS and PATS shall be primarily
conducted by the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW), as the lead agency,
in coordination with the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA),
the Department of Finance (DOF), the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), the
Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) and all the
barangays under it, and the accredited PDOS providers across the country,
including the embassies or consulates, whichever may be available, in the
countries of destination.

      As the lead agency, the DMW is tasked to formulate training modules
on financial education or literacy, initiate and conduct training programs on
financial management and responsibility; apprise OFWs on financial
opportunities and pitfalls, among others; ensure the accessibility of qualified
online financial and economic advisors and/or counselors; and disseminate
knowledge-expanding activities that promote financial education or literacy
among OFWs and their dependents.

       SEC. 6. Free Financial Education Programs. – Financial education or
literacy training programs undertaken under this Act shall be free-of-charge.

      The DMW shall, in coordination with the OWWA, the DOF, and the
BSP, educate the OFWs and their families on the following financial subjects,
issues or programs: Consumer Protection, Protection on Mortgaged or
Collateralized Properties, Avoidance of Exorbitant Interests on Loans or
Debts, and Credit Information on Micro-and Small-Scale Enterprises to
prospective lenders.

     Financial education shall also include information on Obligations and
Contracts, Credit Transactions, Interests, Pledges, Mortgages, Guarantees, and
Knowledge on Financial Products such as stocks, bonds, insurance, and
mutual funds, which are available in the market.

       The DMW shall regularly coordinate with the Department of
Information and Communications Technology and social media companies to
disallow posts and messages that are meant to confuse, mislead, victimize,

defraud or entice OFWs and their families to engage in gambling and other
similar activities, including on-line scams, pyramiding schemes, and the like.

       SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days
from the approval of this Act, the Secretary of the DMW shall, in consultation
with the Administrator of the OWWA, the Secretary of the DOF, the Governor
of the BSP, and the Secretary of the DILG, promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, issuances,
rules and regulations which may be inconsistent with any of the provisions of
this Act are hereby deemed repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

       SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held
unconstitutional or invalid, such holding shall not affect other provisions not
affected thereby.

      SEC. 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
