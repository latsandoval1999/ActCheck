                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Panaon Island, home to the municipalities of Liloan, San Francisco, San Ricardo, and
Pintuyan in the Province of Southern Leyte, has immense potential for economic
growth, tourism, agriculture, and isheries. Yet, despite its strategic location along the
Maharlika Highway, many communities on the island continue to struggle with poor
road connectivity. Families, farmers, isherfolk, students, workers, and patients often
have to travel long and dif icult routes just to reach neighboring towns or access
markets, schools, hospitals, and other essential services. This lack of reliable
transportation slows economic progress, increases the cost of doing business, and
limits opportunities for thousands of residents.

This bill seeks to address these longstanding challenges by directing the Department
of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) to plan, construct, and maintain the Panaon
Island Circumferential Road. The project will connect the municipalities of Liloan, San
Francisco, San Ricardo, and Pintuyan through a safer and more ef icient road network,
making travel faster, reducing transportation costs, improving the delivery of goods
and services, strengthening disaster response, and opening more opportunities for
trade, tourism, and investment. The road alignment will be determined only after the
necessary engineering, environmental, and right-of-way studies and consultations
have been completed, ensuring that the project is safe, sustainable, and responsive to
the needs of local communities.

                              AN ACT
MANDATING THE PLANNING, DEVELOPMENT, CONSTRUCTION AND

PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Panaon Island
Circumferential Road Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote equitable
infrastructure development, improve mobility and connectivity among communities,
reduce transport costs, facilitate access to markets and essential services, and support
inclusive economic growth in geographically isolated and underserved areas.

The State recognizes the strategic location of Panaon Island along the Maharlika
Highway and the need to improve connectivity among the municipalities of Liloan,
San Francisco, San Ricardo, and Pintuyan in the Province of Southern Leyte.

SECTION 3. Construction of the Panaon Island Circumferential Road. — The
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) shall plan, develop, construct,
upgrade, and maintain a road network to be known as the Panaon Island
Circumferential Road.

The road shall establish or improve an all-weather transport connection among the
municipalities of Liloan, San Francisco, San Ricardo, and Pintuyan, and shall connect
                                                                            3|Page
with the existing national-road network and other appropriate transport facilities in
the Province of Southern Leyte.

The inal alignment, length, width, classi ication, design standards, bridges, slope-
protection works, drainage systems, and other necessary components shall be
determined by the DPWH on the basis of the feasibility study, detailed engineering
design, geotechnical and geohazard assessments, environmental requirements, and
consultations with affected local government units and communities.

SECTION 4. Components of the Project. —

The project shall include, as may be necessary:

   (a) Road opening, construction, widening, upgrading, paving, and improvement;

   (b) Bridges, causeways, culverts, drainage systems, retaining walls, slope-
       protection works, and other ancillary structures;

   (c) Road safety facilities, including signs, pavement markings, barriers, lighting
       where necessary, and other traf ic-control devices;

   (d) Climate- and disaster-resilient design features appropriate to the conditions of
       Panaon Island;

   (e) Acquisition, donation, or other lawful arrangements for the required right-of-
       way;

   (f) Relocation assistance and other lawful compensation for affected persons,
       subject to existing laws and regulations; and

   (g) Other works necessary to make the road safe, accessible, and operational
       throughout the year.

SECTION 5. Feasibility Study and Detailed Engineering. — Within one hundred
eighty (180) days from the effectivity of this Act, or within such period as may be
reasonably necessary due to the technical complexity of the project, the DPWH shall
undertake or complete the feasibility study and detailed engineering design of the
project.

The feasibility study and detailed engineering design shall consider:

                                                                           4|Page
   (a) Traf ic demand, population, economic activity, tourism potential, and access to
       public services;

   (b) Geotechnical, geological, seismic, hydrological, and geohazard conditions;

   (c) Climate change, looding, storm surge, landslide, coastal erosion, and other
       disaster risks;

   (d) Environmental and social impacts;

   (e) The availability and status of the right-of-way;

   (f) The protection of private property, ancestral domains, protected areas, forests,
       coastal resources, and other environmentally sensitive areas; and

   (g) The most cost-effective and technically appropriate alignment and
       construction method.

No procurement for the principal civil-works components shall commence until the
detailed engineering, right-of-way assessment, and other documentary requirements
prescribed by law and applicable DPWH rules have been suf iciently completed.

SECTION 6. Implementation. — The DPWH shall be the lead implementing agency
for the project and shall coordinate with the Provincial Government of Southern
Leyte, the municipalities of Liloan, San Francisco, San Ricardo, and Pintuyan, the
Southern Leyte Second District Engineering Of ice, and other concerned national
government agencies.

The DPWH shall prepare a multi-year implementation program identifying the
project’s phases, estimated cost, target completion dates, funding requirements, and
priority sections.

The project may be implemented by phases, taking into account engineering
feasibility, right-of-way availability, connectivity bene its, urgency, and the availability
of appropriations.



SECTION 7. Environmental, Social, and Disaster-Resilience Requirements. —
The project shall comply with all applicable environmental, social-safeguard,
occupational-safety, disaster-risk-reduction, and heritage-protection laws, rules, and
regulations.
                                                                               5|Page
The DPWH shall conduct meaningful consultations with affected local government
units, communities, landowners, indigenous peoples, and other stakeholders,
whenever required by law.

The project shall adopt designs and construction methods that minimize
environmental damage, protect public safety, and promote resilience to natural
hazards.

SECTION 8. Right-of-Way. — The acquisition, donation, negotiation, expropriation,
valuation, payment, and other arrangements concerning the right-of-way shall be
undertaken in accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

No person shall be deprived of property without due process and just compensation
as required by the Constitution and applicable law.

SECTION 9. Funding. — The amount necessary for the feasibility study, detailed
engineering design, environmental and social studies, right-of-way acquisition,
construction, and other activities necessary for the implementation of this Act shall
be included in the General Appropriations Act.

Thereafter, the Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include the project in
the Department’s annual and multi-year infrastructure programs, subject to the usual
budgetary, procurement, accounting, auditing, and other applicable laws, rules, and
regulations.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed as an automatic or immediate appropriation of
an amount not provided in the General Appropriations Act or in a duly enacted
appropriations law.

SECTION 10. Maintenance and Classi ication. — Upon completion of any section of
the project and its acceptance by the DPWH, such section shall be classi ied and
maintained in accordance with applicable national-road standards and laws.

The DPWH shall include the completed road and its appurtenant structures in its
regular annual maintenance, repair, rehabilitation, and improvement programs.

SECTION 11. Transparency and Reporting. — The DPWH shall submit to the Senate
and the House of Representatives, through their respective committees on public
works, an annual report on the implementation of this Act.

                                                                         6|Page
The report shall include:

   (a) The status of the feasibility study and detailed engineering;

   (b) The approved alignment and project scope;

   (c) The status of right-of-way acquisition;

   (d) The amount appropriated, obligated, and disbursed;

   (e) The physical and inancial accomplishment of each project phase;

   (f) Major implementation issues and proposed solutions; and

   (g) The target dates for the commencement and completion of each phase.

The DPWH shall make the report available to the public in accordance with applicable
laws on transparency and access to information.

SECTION 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DPWH, in coordination with the concerned local
government units and national government agencies, shall promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 13. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in
full force and effect.

SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 15. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
