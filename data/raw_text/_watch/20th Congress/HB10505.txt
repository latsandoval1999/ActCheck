                             AN ACT
INCREASING THE INCOME TAX EXEMPTION THRESHOLD FOR INDIVIDUAL
TAXPAYERS TO FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (₱500,000.00), AMENDING
FOR THIS PURPOSE SECTION 24(A)(2)(a) AND SECTION 24(A)(2)(b) OF THE
NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

The present income tax schedule under Section 24(A)(2)(a) of the National Internal
Revenue Code of 1997, as amended, imposes a zero percent (0%) income tax rate only
on the irst Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Pesos (₱250,000.00) of taxable income. The
 irst ₱250,000.00 of taxable income is treated as exempt from income tax under the
tax reforms introduced by Republic Act No. 10963, otherwise known as the “Tax
Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion (TRAIN) Act.”

The cost of basic goods and services, housing, transportation, education, health care,
and other necessities has substantially increased since the current threshold was
established. Increasing the exemption threshold to Five Hundred Thousand Pesos
(₱500,000.00) will provide individual taxpayers with greater disposable income and
help ensure that income needed for basic living expenses is not subject to income tax.
The Supreme Court has recognized that income tax exemptions and adjustments may
be intended to limit the amount of income subject to tax, thereby enabling individuals
to meet basic necessities and retain more disposable income.

This measure therefore proposes that the irst ₱500,000.00 of the taxable income of
individual taxpayers be subject to a zero percent (0%) income tax rate. It likewise

SECTION 3. Amendment of Section 24(A)(2)(b) of the National Internal Revenue
Code of 1997, as amended. —

For purposes of the eight percent (8%) income tax option under Section 24(A)(2)(b)
of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended, the amount of taxable
income exempt from income tax shall be increased from Two Hundred Fifty Thousand
Pesos (₱250,000.00) to Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱500,000.00).

Accordingly, Section 24(A)(2)(b) of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as
amended, shall be read as follows:

     (b) Rate of Tax on Income of Purely Self-Employed Individuals and/or
     Professionals Whose Gross Sales or Gross Receipts and Other Non-
     Operating Income Does Not Exceed the Value-Added Tax Threshold as
     Provided in Section 109(BB). — Self-employed individuals and/or
     professionals shall have the option to avail of an eight percent (8%) tax on
     gross sales or gross receipts and other non-operating income in excess of Five
     Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱500,000.00) in lieu of the graduated income tax
     rates under Subsection (A)(2)(a) of this Section and the percentage tax under
     Section 116 of this Code.

SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). — Within ninety (90)
days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Finance shall, upon the
recommendation of the Commissioner of Internal Revenue, promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary for its effective implementation.

The implementing rules and regulations shall include the necessary adjustments to
withholding-tax tables, income tax return forms, tax-computation procedures, and
other administrative requirements affected by this Act.

SECTION 5. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force
and effect.

SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation. It shall
apply to taxable income earned beginning January 1 of the calendar year following its
effectivity.
