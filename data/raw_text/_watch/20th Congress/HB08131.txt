                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE
Malnutrition is not just a health issue… it is a national concern that affects our children’s future
and our country’s progress.

A World Bank study estimates that childhood undernutrition cost the Philippines approximately
US$4.4 billion, or about 1.5% of GDP, in 2015 alone.¹ This significant economic loss reflects the
long-term impact of poor nutrition on productivity, employability, and human capital formation.
The same study emphasizes that investments in nutrition are among the most cost-effective
strategies for national growth.²

Recent findings of the Food and Nutrition Research Institute (FNRI) under its Expanded National
Nutrition Survey show that stunting remains prevalent among Filipino adolescents, particularly
those from rural areas and from the poorest households.³ Adolescence is a critical period of rapid
growth and brain development. Proper nutrition at this stage directly affects concentration,
cognitive performance, school attendance, and overall academic achievement.

However, many secondary school learners from low-income families continue to face limited
access to adequate and nutritious meals. While Republic Act No. 11037 or the “Masustansyang
Pagkain para sa Batang Pilipino Act” institutionalized the National Feeding Program for
undernourished children in public day care centers and elementary schools,⁴ coverage does not
extend to learners in secondary education.

This measure therefore seeks to expand the National Feeding Program to include qualified
secondary school students, ensuring that our adolescents receive the nutritional support
necessary for healthy growth and improved learning outcomes.

As NANAY, the undersigned firmly believes that when we nourish our children, we strengthen
the very foundation of our nation.

The immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

    AN ACT EXPANDING THE COVERAGE OF THE NATIONAL FEEDING
   PROGRAM TO INCLUDE SECONDARY SCHOOLS, AMENDING FOR THE
  PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11037 OR THE "MASUSTANSYANG PAGKAIN
                  PARA SA BATANG PILIPINO ACT"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title – “This Act shall be known as the Expanded National Feeding
Program Act.”

SEC. 2. Section 4(b) of Republic Act No. 11037, otherwise known as the “Masustansyang
Pagkain para sa Batang Pilipino Act”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

      “Section 4(b). School-Based Feeding Program. — The DepEd shall
      implement a school-based feeding program for undernourished public school
      children from kindergarten to Grade Twelve (12): Provided, That the Program
      shall include the provision of at least one (1) fortified meal to all
      undernourished public kindergarten, elementary, and secondary school
      children for a period of not less than one hundred twenty (120) days in a year:
      Provided, further, That the cost of the aforementioned meals shall ensure that
      school children receive the appropriate amount of nutrition, which may be
      adjusted every year to account for inflation and for nutritional adjustments as
      recommended by the Food and Nutrition Research Institute (FNRI): Provided,
      furthermore, That for each school division of the DepEd, meal distribution and
      other overhead costs of the program shall not be more than ten percent (10%)
      of the total meal cost: Provided, finally, That in the preparation of fortified
      meals, the DepEd shall collaborate with recognized parents’ organizations, as
      well as the parents of the beneficiaries of the Program, whenever applicable.”

SEC. 3. Separability Clause. — Should any provision of this Act be declared
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 4. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations, or other
issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC 5. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
