EXPLANATORY NOTE

Filipinos shine for their creativity, ingenuity, and resourcefulness, qualities that pulse
through our music, dance, literature, theater, visual arts, crafts, and architecture. Art is the heartbeat
of our history and cultural identity. Through art, generations have kept traditions alive, shared
stories, voiced dreams, and handed down our vibrant heritage to those who come after.

The 1987 Constitution recognizes the importance of both education and the arts. Article II,
Section 17 provides that the State shall give priority to education, science and technology, arts,
culture, and sports. Article XIV, Section 1 further mandates the State to protect and promote the
right of all citizens to quality education and to make such education accessible to all. The
Constitution likewise calls for the preservation and enrichment of Filipino culture and equal access
to cultural opportunities through the educational system.

Yet, despite these mandates, countless young Filipinos with remarkable artistic gifts remain
without access to the specialized instruction, facilities, and opportunities they need to flourish. Just
as scholars and athletes thrive with guidance and training, artists deserve a nurturing environment
where their talents can vibrantly blossom.

This proposal seeks to establish the Zamboanga City School for the Arts, a specialized
public secondary school for students with exceptional aptitude and potential in Arts and Design.
The School shall provide quality general education alongside specialized training in various artistic
disciplines, in accordance with the standards and programs of the Department of Education.

Zamboanga City stands ready and eager to become the first in Region IX to host a high
school devoted to the arts. With its vibrant tapestry of traditions, languages, music, dance,
literature, crafts, and countless cultural expressions, the City is poised to become a beacon for arts
education in the Zamboanga Peninsula. The School will not only safeguard Mindanao’s multitude
of traditions but also empower young artists to explore, adopt. and invent new forms of cfeative
expression.

The School aims to welcome not only the youth of Zamboanga City but also gifted students
from every corner of the Zamboanga Peninsula. With fair and inclusive admission standards, those
with artistic promise can pursue specialized arts education, no matter their background or means.
The School will offer inspiring studios, rehearsal and performance spaces, state-of-the-art
facilities, technology, and resources essential for vibrant arts education. It may also forge
partnerships with government agencies, local communities, cultural institutions, artists,
universities, and other organizations to enrich its programs.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the “Zamboanga City Sche@ol for

the Arts Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect
and promote the right of all citizens to quality education at all levels and to take appropriaté steps

to make such education accessible to all.

Pursuant to Article II, Section 17 of the 1987 Constitution, the State shall give priority to
education, science and technology, arts, culture, and sports to foster patriotism and nationalism,

accelerate social progress, and promote total human liberation and development.

Pursuant to Article XIV, Sections 14, 15, and 18 of the Constitution, the State shal! foster
the preservation, enrichment, and dynamic evolution of Filipino national culture; extend patfonage
to arts and letters; conserve, promote, and popularize the nation's historical and cultural heritage

system, scholarships, grants, incentives, cultural centers, and other public venues.

SEC. 3. Creation of the Zamboanga City High School for the Arts. - There is hereby
created a high school for the arts in the City of Zamboanga to be known as the “Zamboanga City

High School for the Arts,” hereinafter referred to as the “School.”

The school shall be administered and supervised by the Department of Education (DepEd),
in coordination with the National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA) and the Cultural

Center of the Philippines (CCP).

SEC 4. Mandate. - The School shall implement a general secondary curriculum preseribed
by Republic Act NO. 10533, OR THE “Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013,” with special
focus on the arts that shall aid in recognizing early talent and exceptional giftedness of young
children, thereby ensuring the nurturance of artists of excellence and the preservation and

promotion of Filipino heritage.

SEC. 7. Meetings. - The Advisory Board shall convene at least once every quarter.
However, the Chairperson of the Board may, upon three (3) days prior written notice, call a

maximum of two (2) meetings in a quarter whenever necessary.

A quorum of the Board shall consist of majority of all its members holding office at the
time of the meeting: Provided, That the Chairperson of the Board or the School Director shall be

among those present in the meeting.

In the absence of the Chairperson of the Board, a duly authorized representative may attend
with all the rights and responsibilities of a regular member; Provided, That during such méeting,

the School Director, as Vice-Chairperson, shall be the presiding office.

The members of the Advisory Board shall not receive any salary but shall be entitled to
reimbursements for actual and necessary expenses incurred, either in their attendance to meetings
of the Board or in connection with other official business authorized by the DepEd Regional
Director, in case of the other Board members, and by the appropriate DepEd official, in case of the

Chairperson of the Board, subject to existing laws and regulations.

SEC. 8 School Director. - There shall be a School Director who shall serve as the chief
academic officer, head of the school faculty and the chief executive officer of the School. The
functions, powers and duties, in addition to those specifically described in this Act, shall be those
usually pertaining to the office of the principal of any high school in the Philippine education

system. And those which are related or necessary to the School Direetor’s functions.

The School Director shall coordinate the implementation of policies and guidelines with
the Advisory Board, and shall articulate to the Board all concerns of the School. The School
Director shall serve a term of five (5) years, render full-time work, and shall be eligible for

appointment to another term.

The School Director shall be appointed by the DepEd Regional Director, subject to the
guidelines and qualifications set forth in the implementing rules and regulations, upon the

recommendation of a duly constituted Search Committee.
