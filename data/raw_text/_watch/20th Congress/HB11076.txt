                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Agriculture and fisheries remain vital sectors of the Philippine economy,
sustaining millions of livelihoods and ensuring food security. However, small
farmers and fisherfolk continue to face significant barriers in accessing affordable
credit, including high interest rates, stringent collateral requirements, and limited
financial inclusion.

      Despite existing policies such as the Agriculture and Fisheries Modernization
Act (Republic Act No. 8435) and the Agri-Agra Reform Credit Act of 2009
(Republic Act No. 10000), access to financing for small-scale producers remains
inadequate. Many financial institutions continue to fall short of meaningful support,
and compliance mechanisms have not fully addressed the structural constraints faced
by the sector.


                                                                                      1
       To address these gaps, this bill mandates financial institutions to allocate a
 portion of their loan portfolio specifically for agri-fishery borrowers, ensures
 affordable interest rates, removes collateral barriers through a government-backed
 guarantee mechanism, and strengthens institutional support for inclusive agricultural
 financing.

       By establishing an Agri Credit Guarantee Fund and promoting accessible,
 low-interest, and collateral-free loans, this measure seeks to empower small farmers
 and fisherfolk, increase productivity, reduce rural poverty, and enhance national
 food security.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


      SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Small Farmers
and Fisherfolk Credit Access Act”


      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. -- It is hereby declared the policy of the
State to promote inclusive rural development, ensure equitable access to financial



                                                                                1
resources for small farmers and fisherfolk, and strengthen agricultural productivity
and food security. The State shall facilitate affordable, accessible, and responsive
credit systems that address the unique needs and vulnerabilities of the agri-fishery
sector, and shall remove structural barriers to financing, including high interest rates
and collateral requirements.


      SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:
      (a) Small Farmers refers to an individuals engaged in agriculture with
      landholdings of not more than five (5) hectares;
      (b) Fisherfolk refers to an individual engaged in fishing, aquaculture, or fish
      processing, including municipal fisherfolk;
      (c) Agri-Fishery Loans refers to the loans extended for agricultural and
      fisheries production, processing, marketing, and related activities;
      (d) Agri Credit Guarantee Fund (ACGF) refers to a government-backed fund
      that guarantees loans extended to eligible borrowers under this Act.


      SECTION 4. Mandatory Credit Allocation. -- All covered financial
institutions shall allocate at least ten percent (10%) of their total loan portfolio for
agri-fishery loans to small farmers and fisherfolk. Compliance with this requirement
shall be direct and shall not be substituted by alternative compliance mechanisms
unless expressly authorized under this Act. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)
shall issue guidelines to ensure effective implementation and monitoring.


      SECTION 5. Preferential Interest Rates. – The loans granted under this Act
shall be extended at concessional interest rates ranging from three percent (3%) to
five percent (5%) per annum, inclusive of all charges directly related to the extension


                                                                                      2
of credit, excluding only reasonable service fees as may be determined by the
Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP).


      The Department of Finance (DOF), in coordination with the Bangko Sentral
ng Pilipinas (BSP) and the Department of Agriculture (DA), shall determine
mechanism for an interest rate support mechanism, including interest subsidies,
rediscounting windows, credit enhancements, or other fiscal and monetary
instruments.



      SECTION 6. Collateral-Free Lending and Risk Mitigation. -- Loans
extended to qualified small farmers and fisherfolk under this Act shall, as far as
practicable, be granted without the requirement of traditional collateral, particularly
for loans below a threshold amount to be determined by the BSP in coordination
with the Department of Agriculture (DA).

      In lieu of conventional collateral, covered financial institutions are hereby
encouraged to adopt alternative credit assessment and risk management
mechanisms, including but not limited to cash flow-based lending, character-based
lending, group or cooperative guarantees, warehouse receipts, crop liens, value chain
financing, and digital or data-driven credit scoring systems. To mitigate risks and
encourage lending, all eligible loans shall be covered by the Agri Credit Guarantee
Fund established under this Act. Guarantee coverage shall be designed to reduce
credit exposure while ensuring prudent lending practices.




                                                                                     3
             The BSP, in coordination with the DOF and DA, shall issue guidelines
on risk-sharing arrangements, eligibility for collateral-free loans, and safeguards to
prevent moral hazard and ensure responsible lending.


      SECTION 7. Establishment of the Agri Credit Guarantee Fund – Thereby
established an Agri Credit Guarantee Fund (ACGF), which shall provide guarantee
coverage for loans extended to qualified small farmers and fisherfolk under this Act.
The Fund shall be administered by a designated government financial institution
under the policy guidance of the Department of Finance and in coordination with the
Department of Agriculture.


      The Agri Credit Guarantee Fund shall have the following powers and
functions:


      (a). Provide partial or full credit guarantees, covering a portion of the principal
      and/or interest of eligible loans, subject to risk-sharing parameters to be
      determined in the implementing rules and regulations;
      (b) Establish guarantee ceilings, coverage ratios, and claim procedures to
      ensure fiscal sustainability and operational efficiency;
      (c) Reduce the credit risks borne by participating financial institutions and
      incentivize increased lending to the agri-fishery sector;
      (d) Support innovative and inclusive financing mechanisms, including
      climate-resilient and value chain financing models;
      (e) Establish streamlined claims processing mechanisms to ensure timely
      settlement of guarantee claims; and




                                                                                       4
      (f) Maintain actuarial soundness and financial sustainability through
      appropriate fund management policies.


      SECTION 8. Eligibility and Priority Beneficiaries. — The benefits under
this Act shall be made available to qualified small farmers and fisherfolk, as defined
under this Act, who are engaged in agricultural and fisheries activities, whether
individually or as part of organized groups. Priority shall be given to marginalized
and vulnerable sectors, including but not limited to subsistence farmers and
fisherfolk, agrarian reform beneficiaries, women and youth engaged in agriculture
and fisheries, indigenous peoples, and duly registered cooperatives and associations
of farmers and fisherfolk.


      The Department of Agriculture, in coordination with Local Government Units
and relevant agencies, shall establish a system for the identification, validation, and
prioritization of eligible beneficiaries, including the use of existing registries.


      SECTION 9: Incentives for Compliance. – Covered financial institutions
that exceed the mandatory credit allocation under this Act shall be entitled to
incentives, subject to applicable laws, rules, and regulations.


      Such incentives may include, but shall not be limited to, preferential access to
government guarantee facilities, eligibility for rediscounting or refinancing
programs, and regulatory incentives as may be determined by the Bangko Sentral ng
Pilipinas (BSP), including possible adjustments in reserve requirements or capital
treatment, consistent with prudential standards.




                                                                                      5
      SECTION 10. Penalties for Non-Compliance. — Failure of covered
financial institutions to comply with the mandatory credit allocation requirement
under this Act shall subject them to appropriate penalties to be imposed by the
Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP).


      Such penalties may include, but shall not be limited to, monetary fines
proportionate to the degree of non-compliance, imposition of corrective action plans,
restrictions on certain banking privileges, or other regulatory sanctions consistent
with the supervisory powers of the BSP.


      SECTION 11. Capacity Building and Support Services. The Department of
Agriculture (DA) , in coordination with the Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR),
Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (TESDA), and Local Government Units (LGUs), shall
provide comprehensive support services to beneficiaries under this Act.


      Support services shall include technical assistance on farm and fishery
productivity, financial literacy and loan management, enterprise development, value
chain integration, climate-resilient practices, and digital financial inclusion.The DA
shall likewise facilitate linkages between beneficiaries and markets, cooperatives,
and support institutions to enhance the productive use of credit and improve
repayment capacity.


      Financial institutions are encouraged to complement these efforts by
providing borrower education, advisory services, and monitoring support to ensure
responsible credit utilization and reduce default risks.


                                                                                    6
      SECTION 12. Monitoring and Evaluation. – The BSP, in coordination with
the DA and DOF, shall establish a monitoring and evaluation system to track
compliance, loan performance, and impact on beneficiaries. An annual report shall
be submitted to Congress.


      SECTION 13. Funding. – The initial funding for the Agri Credit Guarantee
Fund shall be sourced from the General Appropriations Act of Department of
Agriculture (DA) and/or the Department of Finance (DOF).


      SECTION 14. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within ninety
(90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Bangko Sentral of Philippines (BSP),
in coordination with Department of Finance (DOF) and Department of Agriculture
(DA), shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations.


      SECTION 15. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.


      SECTION 16. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, and regulations
inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


      SECTION 17. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
