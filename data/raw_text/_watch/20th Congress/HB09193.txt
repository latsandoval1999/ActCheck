Importantly, the bill upholds breastfeeding as the primary and preferred method of infant
feeding, positioning donor human milk strictly as a medically indicated alternative when a
mother's own milk is unavailable or insufficient. It emphasizes that all human milk donation
must remain voluntary, altruistic, and non-commercial, with safeguards against financial
inducement and undue influence, consistent with the Philippine Milk Code.

To ensure safety and quality, the measure provides for standardized donor screening, milk
processing, storage, and distribution systems aligned with Department of Health regulations
and internationally recognized practices. It also introduces innovations such as an Online Live
Milk Map and Reserve System to improve real-time coordination, equitable allocation, and
emergency response.

By strengthening breastfeeding support systems and establishing a safe, ethical, and accessible
human milk banking network, this measure aims to reduce infant morbidity and mortality,
improve neonatal outcomes, and uphold the fundamental rights of every child to optimal
nutrition and every mother to adequate breastfeeding support.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
