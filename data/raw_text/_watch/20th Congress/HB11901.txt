HOUSE BILL nol190 1

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Gogon-Palnab-Pajo-Igang-Talisoy-Magnesia-Mamangal-
Lictin Provincial Road, traversing the Municipalities of Virac and San Andres in the Province
of Catanduanes, into a national road, placing its supervision, improvement, repair, and
maintenance under the Department of Public Works and Highways.

Virac serves as the hub of government services, business, trade, and other economic
activities in the province. The subject road provides a vital link for the communities within
Virac and its neighboring municipalities such as San Andres, facilitating efficient and seamless
transportation of agricultural products and goods, access to essential government and public
services, and other local economic activities.

Given its importance, the conversion of the road into a national road will facilitate the
allocation of appropriate national funding for its improvement, rehabilitation, and long-term
maintenance, while easing the financial burden on the provincial government. This will help
ensure safer, more accessible, and disaster-resilient road infrastructure, further promoting
connectivity and socioeconomic development in the Province of Catanduanes.

This proposal is anchored on DPWH Department Order No. 112, s. 2024, which sets
the standards for road conversion. The road proposed is eligible for conversion as it meets the
standard of connecting to provincial capitals and provide link between Virac, the provincial
capital, and communities in San Andres, supporting access to government services, commercial
activities, and other essential services.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this proposed measure is earnestly sought.

EULOG MODRIGUEZ

HOUSE BILL NO:

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

AN ACT CONVERTING THE GOGON-PALNAB-PAJO-IGANG-TALISOY-
MAGNESIA-MAMANGAL-LICTIN PROVINCIAL ROAD, TRAVERSING THE
MUNICIPALITIES OF VIRAC AND SAN ANDRES IN THE PROVINCE OF
CATANDUANES, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. It is hereby declared that the Gogon-Palnab-Pajo-Igang-Talisoy-

Magnesia-Mamangal-Lictin Provincial Road, traversing the Municipalities of Virac and San
Andres in the Province of Catanduanes, is converted into a national road under the jurisdiction

and supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DP WH).

SECTION 2. The Secretary of the DPWH shall include in its program the expansion,

improvement, rehabilitation, maintenance, and repair of the said road, the funding of which

shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the

Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
