                                       Explanatory Note

        Any country aiming to stimulate growth and development must certainly always rely on
building infrastructures to encourage investors and increase government spending on infrastructure
projects. “Building” infrastructures towards sustainable growth and development, however, does
not rely on a single industry or profession alone.

        A country that builds a resilient and sustainable community needs to and must rely on the
collective action and competence of several professionals who work in the field of built
environment, such as (1) architecture, (2) interior design, (3) landscape architecture, (4)
environmental planning, (5) geodetic engineering, (6) civil engineering, (7) electrical engineering,
(8) mechanical engineering, (9) sanitary engineering, (10) master plumber, (11) electronics
engineering, and (12) real estate services.

        Under this Bill, “[t]he State recognizes the importance and contributions of the Built
Environment Professions in nation-building and development. Hence, the State shall ensure that
all practicing Built Environment Professionals meet the minimum requirements to safeguard the
life, health, property, and welfare of the public; foster their continuing professional growth and
advancement; encourage and enhance collaboration; and continually review and improve the
standards of regulation, professional service, and practice towards Filipino Built Environment
Professionals becoming socially and environmentally responsible and globally competitive.”

       In view of this recognition, it is, thus, imperative to establish a council that shall have
administrative supervision over these Built Environment Professions. This is the objective of this
Bill.

        This proposed legislation aims to create a council for the built environment (“Council”) to
(1) advise on policy matters concerning the interests of the public in the built environment; and
(2) oversee the regulation and facilitate collaboration and cooperation of Built Environment
Professions. In relation to these objectives, the Council shall have the powers and functions
pertaining to policy advisory, oversight, and collaborative platform.

       Among others, it shall:

       (a)     act in an advisory capacity on any and all matters concerning the built environment;

                                           AN ACT
         CREATING THE “COUNCIL FOR THE BUILT ENVIRONMENT,”
       APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES


       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:


                                             ARTICLE I
                                     GENERAL PROVISIONS


       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Council for the Built
Environment Act."
       Section 2. Statement of Policy. - The State recognizes the importance and contributions
of the Built Environment Professions in nation-building and development. Hence, the State
shall ensure that all practicing Built Environment Professionals meet the minimum
requirements to safeguard life, health, and property, foster their continuing professional growth
and advancement, encourage and enhance collaboration, and continually review and improve
the standards of regulation, professional service, and practice towards Filipino Built
Environment Professionals becoming socially and environmentally responsible and globally
competitive.
                                                                                                    3
       Section 3. Definition of Terms. - As used in this Act, unless the context otherwise
requires, the following terms shall be defined as follows:
           a. Built Environment means the field within which the registered professionals
               practice;
           b. Built Environment Professions means the following professions -
                  i.   Architecture;
                 ii.   Interior Design;
                iii.   Landscape Architecture;
                iv.    Environmental Planning;
                 v.    Geodetic Engineering;
                vi.    Civil Engineering;
               vii.    Electrical Engineering;
               viii.   Mechanical Engineering;
                ix.    Sanitary Engineering;
                 x.    Master Plumber;
                xi.    Electronics Engineering; and
               xii.    Real Estate Services.
           c. Council means the Council for the Built Environment created under Section 8
               of this Act;
           d. Professional means a person who is registered as such under any of the
               Professional Regulatory Laws;
           e. Professional Regulatory Law refers to the legal framework governing the
               practice of specific profession; the legal charter creating and governing the
               various Boards, defining their functions and providing for the rules and policies
               that govern the practice of a specific profession;
           f. Accredited and Integrated Professional Organization (AIPO) refers to the
               concerned Board and PRC Accredited Integrated Professional Organization for
               a given profession which is specifically mandated by the provision of the PRC
               to integrate the professionals into one national organization and where
               membership therein by professionals is automatic and mandatory;
           g. Accredited Professional Organization (APO) refers to the PRC Accredited
               Professional Organization where membership therein by professionals is only
               voluntary;
           h. CHED refers to the Commission on Higher Education;

           i. MRA refers to Mutual Recognition Agreements between or among states;
           j. PRC refers to the Professional Regulation Commission;
           k. PRB refers to the Professional Regulatory Board.


                                        ARTICLE II
    ORGANIZATION OF MULTIPARTITE COUNCILS FOR BUILT ENVIRONMENT
                                       PROFESSIONS


        Section 4. Creation of Multipartite Councils for the Built Environment Professions.
-                               A Multipartite Council for each of the Built Environment
Professions (herein referred to as “MC”) is hereby created to be attached to the PRC.
        Section 5. Objectives of the MC. -The objectives of the MC are to –
           a. Provide a platform to discuss policy concerns in the education, internship,
               professional regulation, and continuing professional development; and
           b. Promote and encourage collaboration between the different stakeholders in the
               regulation and practice of the Built Environment Professions.
        Section 6. Functions, Powers and Duties of the MC. The MC shall perform the
following functions:
           a. Monitor local and international benchmarks in professional education,
               internship, regulation and practice;
           b. Study and evaluate educational innovations that shall lead to curriculum
               development;
           c. Regularly review the Subjects for Examination;
           d. Develop and regularly review the Table of Specifications for the Licensure
               Examination;
           e. Develop and regularly review Internship/Guidelines in the areas such as
               diversification of experience, duties and responsibilities of Mentors and
               collaborative work;
           f. Develop and regularly review Continuing Professional Development
               Guidelines;
           g. Develop and regularly review Standards of Professional Practice in areas such
               as Scope of Services, Schedule of Fees, and inter-professional collaboration;
               and



                                                                                               5
           h. Develop and regularly review professional education, internship, regulation and
               practice standards in reference to the Philippine Qualifications Framework Act.
       Section 7. Composition of the MC. The MC shall be composed of the following –
           a. Chairperson of the Professional Regulatory Board;
           b. President of the AIPO;
           c. President of the Academic Organization or Deans and Heads of Schools duly
               accredited by the PRC upon the recommendation of the CHED;
           d. One (1) representative from Private Practice; and
           e. One (1) representative from Government Practice.


                                        ARTICLE III
       ORGANIZATION OFTHE COUNCIL FOR THE BUILT ENVIRONMENT


       Section 8. Creation of the Council for the Built Environment. –The Council for the
Built Environment (herein referred to as the “Council”) is hereby created, to be attached to the
PRC.
       Section 9. Objectives of the Council. - The Council is an overarching body that has
the following objectives -
           a. Advise on policy matters concerning the interests of the public in the built
               environment, including but not limited to:
                  i.   sustainable development;
                 ii.   human resource development; and
                iii.   health, safety and environmental protection standards.
           b. Oversee the regulation of Built Environment Professions, providing guidance
               on:
                  i.   Required qualifications;
                 ii.   Standards of education;
                iii.   Training and competence;
                iv.    Promotion of professional status; and
                 v.    Uniform application of standards and guidelines set by the PRC.
           c. Facilitate collaboration and cooperation of the Built Environment Professions
               with:
                  i.   Other Built Environment Professions;



                                                                                                   6
                ii.      Foreign Professionals; and
               iii.      Non-professional creatives and artists.
       Section 10. Mandate, Functions, and Powers of the Council. The Council shall
exercise the following powers and functions:
       POLICY ADVISORY
           a. Act in an advisory capacity on any matter concerning the built environment,
              including, but not limited to: resource utilization, socio-economic development,
              public health and safety, and the environment;
           b. Provide advice and consultation on national policy that could impact on the built
              environment, human resource development in relation to the built environment
              professions, and the recognition of new professions;
           c. Comment, if necessary, on all proposed legislation impacting on health and
              safety in the built environment;
       OVERSIGHT
           d. Ensure the consistent application of policy for the Built Environment
              Professions with regard to -
                      i. Registration requirements of registered professionals;
                      ii. Key elements of competence testing of registered professionals;
                  iii. Codes of conduct;
                  iv. Delineation of scope of services;
                      v. The principles upon which to base the Standards of Professional Practice
                         and Schedule of Fees;
                  vi. Standards of health, safety and environmental protection within the built
                         environment;
                 vii. The accreditation of APOs/AIPOS for the built environment
                         professions, by approving the accreditation framework giving due
                         recognition of the characteristics of each Built Environment Profession;
                viii. Establishment of education and training standards in terms of the
                         Philippine Qualifications Framework Act;
           e. Act as an appeal body with regard to matters referred to it in terms of the PRLs
              regulating the Built Environment Professions;
           f. Receive and assimilate the annual reports of the PRBs, AIPOs and Academic
              Sectors and submit a summary to the Commissioner.

       COLLABORATION PLATFORM
           g. Promote coordination between the Commission on Higher Education and the
               Academic Sectors for the Built Environment Professions in relation to the
               accreditation of education institutions;
           h. Coordinate the establishment of MRAs for Built Environment Professionals,
               providing guidelines on the practice of Filipino Professionals abroad as well as
               Foreigner Professionals in the Philippines;
           i. Foster and encourage collaboration and cooperation between Built Environment
               Professionals by setting up guidelines, terms of reference and standards;
           j. Establish terms of engagement to facilitate the efficient and mutually beneficent
               collaboration of Built Environment Professional with Non-Professional
               creatives and artists.
       Section 11. Composition of Council. The Council shall be composed of ex officio
members and regular members.
       The ex officio members of the Council are:
           a. Chairperson of the PRC;
           b. Chairperson of the CHED;
           c. Chairpersons of the PRBs of all Built Environment Professions;
       The regular members of the Council are:
           a. One (1) representative from each of the Multipartite Councils (non-PRB) of all
               Built Environment Professions;
       Section 12. Appointment of Members of the Council. The ex officio members of the
Council, except the Chairperson of the PRC, who shall serve as Chairperson of the Council,
may designate their respective next-in-rank officials as permanent alternates whose acts are
considered as the acts of their principals.
       Every regular member shall be appointed by the President from a list submitted by the
Chairperson of the PRC upon consultation with the other ex officio members of the Council,
drawn from a nomination process resulting from public consultations with stakeholders
engaged in each Built Environment Profession, nominating at least three (3) nominees for every
regular member seat: Provided, That each of the nominees is not a holder of any government
position and must have at least ten (10) years of experience in the profession which the nominee
belongs to; Provided, further, That each of the nominees shall have been officially endorsed
by the AIPO and the duly recognized association of academics of their profession; Provided,
finally, That the list of nominees shall be vetted by a joint task force constituted by the
Professional Regulation Commission and the Commission on Higher Education representing
                                                                                                   8
the professional and academic interests of the Filipino people, respectively.
        Section 13. Removal from Office. The Chairperson of the Council may remove a
member of the Council from office on account of misconduct or inability to perform his or her
duties after due process.
        Section 14. Disqualification, Vacation and Filling of Vacancies. A person may not be
appointed as a member of the Council if that person -
             a. Is not a Filipino citizen and resident in the Philippines;
             b. Has been convicted of a crime involving moral turpitude;
             c. Has, in terms of this Act, been found guilty by the Council of improper conduct;
                or
             d. Is not a registered professional.
        A member of the Council must vacate his or her office if he or she -
             a. Becomes disqualified from being appointed as a member of the Council;
             b. Resigns by written notice addressed to the Chairperson;
             c. Has, without leave of the Council, been absent for more than two consecutive
                meetings of the Council;
             d. Ceases to be permanent resident in the Philippines; or
             e. Is removed from office under Section 13 of this Act.
        If a member of the Council dies or vacates his or her office before the expiration of his
or her term of office, the Chairperson must, within sixty (60) days from the date on which the
vacancy occurred, appoint the person nominated by the Council, to fill the vacancy for the
unexpired portion of the period for which that member was appointed.
        Section 15. Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson. The Council shall be chaired by
the Chairperson of the PRC.
        The Council shall elect a deputy chairperson among the regular members.
        The regular members of the Council shall serve for a term of six (6) years, unless sooner
separated from the service due to death, voluntary resignation, or removal for cause. In case of
death, resignation, or removal for cause, their replacements shall serve only the unexpired terms
of the replaced members. Of the first set of regular members, eleven (11) shall hold office for
a term of three (3) years and the remaining eleven (11) for a term of six
(6) years.




                                                                                                    9
       The regular members of the Council shall receive per diem every meeting in accordance
with existing rules and regulations.
       Section 16. Meetings of the Council. The Council shall meet at least once every six
(6) months, or as often as necessary at the call of the Chairperson or majority of the members.
Members of the Council who cannot physically attend or vote at Council meetings can
participate and vote through remote communication technologies or other electronic means
such as videoconferencing, teleconferencing, or other alternative modes of communication that
allow them reasonable opportunities to participate. A majority of the incumbent members of
the Council shall constitute a quorum to do business.
       Section 17. Executive Committee. The Council shall have an Executive Committee
consisting of the Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson of the Council and not more than three
other members elected by the Council. The Council may appoint alternate members to the
Executive Committee.
       A member of the Executive Committee -
           a. Serves on the executive committee for a period of one year;
           b. May be re-elected; and
           c. Remains in office until his or her successor is elected.
       No two members of the Executive Committee shall come from the same Built
Environment Profession.
       The Executive Committee must hold a meeting as and when necessary, but at least
four (4) times per year.
       Section 18. Powers of the Executive Committee. The Executive Committee shall
exercise the following powers:
           a. Ensure that the resolutions of the Council are carried out;
           b. Consider any matter delegated to it by the Council in terms of any law, and to
               advise the Council in connection therewith;
           c. Exercise or perform any power, duty or function delegated to it by the
               Council; and
           d. Report at every ordinary meeting of the Council on the activities of the
               Executive Committee.
       Section 19. Committees of Council. The Council may establish any Committee to -
           a. Assist it in the performance of its functions; or
           b. Investigate and make recommendations on matters relating to any of its
               functions.


                                                                                               10
       The Council shall designate a Chairperson for any such Committee, and determine the
quorum for meetings. The Council shall also determine the terms of reference of the
Committees.
       Section 20. Secretariat and Staff. The Council -
           a. Shall establish a Secretariat; and
           b. May employ such staff, as it may deem necessary, for the carrying out of its
               functions, on such conditions, including the payment of remuneration and
               allowances, as it may determine, and may dismiss any person so appointed.
       Section 21. Delegation of Powers. The Council may, delegate any power, duty or
function conferred or imposed on it by this Act to -
           a. A Committee established under Section 19;
           b. The Executive Committee elected under Section 17;
           c. The Chairperson of the Council or any member of the Council; or
           d. Any officer of the Council.
       Section 22. Reporting. The Council shall furnish the Commissioner an Annual Report
with regard to the functions and affairs of the Council including, but not limited to:
           a. a report regarding the execution of the functions of the Council;
           b. a report regarding the envisaged strategies of the Council; and
            c. such other information as the Commissioner may from time to time determine.
          Section 23. Delineation of Scope of Work and Terms of Reference for
Collaboration. The Council must, after receipt of the recommendations submitted to it by the
MCs in terms of the Professional Regulatory Laws, -
           a. Determine policy with regard to the identification of work for the different Built
               Environment Professions;
           b. Consult with any person, body or industry that may be affected by the
               identification of work in terms of this section;
           c. Identify the scope of work for every Built Environment Profession; and
           d. Establish terms of reference for collaboration between Built Environmental
               Professionals, Foreign Professionals and Non-Professional creatives and artists.

                                          ARTICLE IV
                                      FINAL PROVISIONS


        Section 24. Appropriations. The amount necessary for the immediate implementation
of this Act shall be included in the budget of the Professional Regulation Commission in the
annual General Appropriations Act.
        Section 25. Implementing Rules and Regulations. The Professional Regulation
Commission, the Commission on Higher Education, together with the Professional Regulatory
Boards and AIPOs of the Built Environment Professions are tasked to prepare the
Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) required for the implementation of this Act. The
IRR shall be promulgated within ninety (90) days upon approval of this Act.
        Section 26. Separability Clause. If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Act
is declared unconstitutional, or invalid, the other sections and provisions, which are not affected
by it, shall continue to be in full force and effect.
        Section 27. Repealing Clause. All laws, decrees, executive orders, agreements, rules,
and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed
or modified accordingly.
        Section 28. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after is complete
publication in either the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.


        Approved.
