                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

Cebu and Negros Island are linked by sea, but travel and the movement of goods
between them remain dependent on maritime crossings. A permanent link would
improve connectivity within Central Visayas, facilitate the movement of people
and goods, and create new opportunities for trade, tourism, investment, and
regional development. It would also provide an additional transport option when
sea crossings are unavailable or disrupted.

This bill provides for the construction of a bridge or undersea tunnel connecting
the Municipality of Santander, Cebu, and the Municipality of Sibulan, Negros
Oriental. The proposed project would establish a direct and continuous land
transport connection between Cebu Island and Negros Island, subject to the
results of comprehensive feasibility, engineering, environmental, and other
necessary technical studies.

The project must consider not only the bridge or tunnel crossing itself, but also
the necessary approach roads, interchanges, and connections to the existing road
networks of Cebu and Negros Oriental. These components must be planned and
developed as an integrated transport corridor to ensure that the completed link
provides safe, efficient, and convenient movement between the two islands.
The proposed link would make travel between Cebu and Negros Oriental easier
and more efficient, provide a more direct route for the movement of goods, and
improve access to essential services, employment centers, and economic
opportunities. It could also strengthen disaster response and emergency mobility
by providing another route for personnel, equipment, relief supplies, and other
essential resources between the two islands.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. There shall be constructed under the supervision of the Department
of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) an undersea tunnel or bridge, from the
Province of Cebu to the Province of Negros Oriental, including access roads
thereto.

SEC 2. The funding for the feasibility study and engineering design shall be
included in the General Appropriations Act (GAA), and shall be undertaken by a
qualified international consulting firm with proven experience in similar long-
span bridge or undersea tunnel projects. To uphold the quality, credibility, and
global competitiveness of the outputs, the study should be financed through grant
funding or technical assistance from development partners. Thereafter, the
Secretary of the DPWH shall include in the Department’s program the
construction of said undersea tunnel or bridge, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual GAA.

SEC 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
