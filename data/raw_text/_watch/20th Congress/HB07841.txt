GOVERNANCE ACT OF 2011”


                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Republic Act No. 10149, otherwise known as the GOCC Governance Act of 2011, was
enacted to institutionalize reforms that promote transparency, accountability, and fiscal
prudence among government-owned or -controlled corporations (GOCCs). However,
more than a decade since its enactment, implementation experience has revealed
critical gaps, ambiguities, and structural limitations that must be addressed to meet
evolving governance standards and the State’s continuing public sector rationalization
efforts.

This proposed measure introduces targeted amendments to strengthen and
modernize the mandate of the Governance Commission for GOCCs (GCG). It clarifies
key definitions, most notably that of “GOCC”, to ensure uniform application of the law
and eliminate inconsistencies across statutes. It expressly establishes restructuring
mechanisms such as merger, consolidation, and regularization, thereby providing a
definitive legal framework for streamlining entities whose corporate structures are no
longer aligned with their mandates or whose functions are better suited to regular
agency status.

The bill further enhances the regulatory and quasi-judicial authority of the GCG by
empowering it to issue subpoenas, conduct investigations, impose administrative
sanctions, and enforce compliance with governance standards. It strengthens
accountability   through   improved    reporting   requirements,    performance-based

                                                                                         1
compensation standards, and clearer rules on the terms of appointive directors.
Institutional stability and independence are likewise reinforced through fixed terms,
security of tenure, qualified immunity, and indemnification provisions, balanced by
safeguards against abuse.

Notably, substantially similar reforms embodied in this measure were previously
deliberated and approved by the Committee on Government Enterprises and
Privatization during the previous Congress, reflecting institutional recognition of the
urgent need to update and reinforce the governance framework for GOCCs. The
present bill builds upon those prior efforts and incorporates further refinements to
ensure responsiveness to current governance challenges.

In sum, this proposed legislation represents a decisive reform to ensure a disciplined,
coherent, and accountable GOCC sector. By reinforcing oversight, rationalizing
structures, and aligning corporate governance with contemporary public sector
reforms, it safeguards public assets and strengthens fiscal responsibility. Its passage
will affirm the State’s unwavering commitment to good governance and ensure that
GOCCs operate efficiently, transparently, and strictly in the service of the national
interest.
