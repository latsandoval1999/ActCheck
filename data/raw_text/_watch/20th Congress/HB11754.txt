14                                         EXPLANATORY NOTE
15
16   Bedridden individuals—whether due to severe illness, advanced age, neurological disorders, or
17   major physical disabilities—face profound vulnerabilities. Beyond the emotional and physical toll
18   on both the individuals and their families, the financial burden of caring for a bedridden family
19   member is immense. Daily expenses for specialized medical supplies, adult diapers, catheter care,
20   bedsore management, prescription medication, and home-based caregiving place extreme strain on
21   low- to middle-income households.
22
23   While current social welfare legislation provides general subsidies to indigent senior citizens and
24   persons with disabilities (PWDs), these existing social safety nets were not designed to cover the
25   extraordinary and continuous cost of full home-based care for bedridden individuals.
26
27   This bill establishes a direct base financial subsidy of Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) per month
28   to qualified bedridden individuals across the country. Crucially, this measure explicitly provides
29   that this subsidy shall be over and above any benefit, social pension, or assistance granted under
30   existing laws for Senior Citizens and PWDs, ensuring that receiving this support will not reduce or
31   disqualify a beneficiary from other statutory benefits. Furthermore, to safeguard the purchasing
32   power of this support against economic fluctuations and rising costs, the bill mandates an automatic
33   annual inflation adjustment based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) published by the Philippine
34   Statistics Authority (PSA).
35
36   In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

 74         AN ACT PROVIDING A MONTHLY CASH ASSISTANCE FOR BEDRIDDEN
 75               INDIVIDUALS AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR
 76
 77         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
 78   assembled:
 79
 80   SECTION 1. Short Title.
 81
 82          This Act shall be known as the "Bedridden Individuals Cash Assistance Act."
 83
 84   SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.
 85
 86           It is the policy of the State to promote a just and dynamic social order that will ensure the
 87   prosperity and independence of the nation and free the people from poverty through policies that
 88   provide adequate social services, promote full employment, a rising standard of living, and an
 89   improved quality of life for all. The State further recognizes its obligation to protect and provide
 90   special support to vulnerable sectors, including persons with severe medical impairments and
 91   disabilities, ensuring their right to health, purchasing power stability, and human dignity.
 92
 93   SECTION 3. Definition of Terms.
 94
 95          For purposes of this Act, the following terms shall mean:
 96
 97              a. Bedridden Individual — refers to any Filipino citizen who is continuously
 98                 confined to bed or chair due to a permanent or long-term severe physical disability,
 99                 chronic terminal illness, advanced age, neurological impairment, or injury that
100                 completely prevents them from performing primary activities of daily living (ADLs)
101                 without full assistance from a caregiver.
102
103              b. Authorized Representative — refers to a primary caregiver, legal guardian, or
104                 immediate family member within the fourth civil degree of consanguinity or affinity
105                 designated by or on behalf of the bedridden individual to apply for and receive the
106                 cash assistance.
107
108              c. Consumer Price Index (CPI) — refers to the indicator generated by the Philippine
109                 Statistics Authority (PSA) that measures the average changes in the retail prices of a
110                 fixed basket of goods and services usually purchased by households.
111
112              d. C/MSWDO — refers to the City or Municipal Social Welfare and Development
113                 Office.
114
115   SECTION 4. Cash Assistance Benefit, Inflation Adjustment, and Non-Exclusion.
116
117              a. Base Benefit: Every qualified bedridden individual shall be entitled to receive a
118                 direct cash assistance in the initial base amount of Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00)
119                 per month. The cash benefit shall be disbursed on a monthly or quarterly basis
120                 directly to the beneficiary or through their designated Authorized Representative.
121
122              b. Supplemental Nature / Non-Exclusion Clause: The financial assistance provided
123                 under this Act shall be over and above, and independent of, any social pension,
124                 cash incentive, discount, or financial assistance due or granted to the beneficiary
125                 under Republic Act No. 7432, as amended (Expanded Senior Citizens Act),
126                 Republic Act No. 7277, as amended (Magna Carta for Persons with Disabilities),
127                 Republic Act No. 11916, or any other existing law, ordinance, or local government
128                 assistance program.
129
130              c. Mandatory Periodic Inflation Adjustment: The monthly cash assistance
131                 prescribed under this Section shall be subject to an automatic review and adjustment
132                 every calendar year to account for inflation. The Department of Social Welfare and
133                 Development (DSWD), in coordination with the National Economic and
134                 Development Authority (NEDA) and the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), shall
135                 adjust the cash benefit amount using the prevailing Consumer Price Index (CPI) for
136                 the target fiscal year.
137
138              d. Non-Diminution of Benefits: In no case shall the adjusted monthly cash assistance
139                 be lower than the initial base amount of Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00).
140
141   SECTION 5. Qualifications and Eligibility.
142            To qualify for the benefit provided under this Act, an applicant must satisfy the following
143   criteria:
144
145
146              a. Must be a Filipino citizen residing in the Philippines;
147
148              b. Must be certified as bedridden by a licensed municipal, city, or provincial health
149                 officer, or an accredited government physician;
150
151              c. Must not be currently residing in or admitted to a fully state-funded long-term
152                 institutional care facility where medical and daily living expenses are completely
153                 subsidized by the government.
154
155          Provided, That eligibility for or receipt of Senior Citizen or PWD benefits shall not
156   disqualify an applicant from receiving the assistance under this Act.
157
158
159   SECTION 6. Appropriations.
160
161           The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against the
162   current appropriations of the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD). Thereafter,
163   such sums as may be necessary for its continued implementation, including the adjusted amount
164   reflecting inflation, shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA).
165
166   SECTION 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).
167
168            Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Social Welfare and
169   Development, in consultation with the Secretary of Finance, NEDA, PSA, National Council on
170   Disability Affairs (NCDA), National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC), DOH, DILG, and
171   relevant non-governmental organizations, shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for
172   its effective implementation.
173
174   SECTION 8. Separability Clause.
175
176            If any provision or part of this Act is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining
177   parts or provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.
178
179
180
181   SECTION 9. Repealing Clause.
182
183          All laws, executive orders, rules, regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the
184   provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
185
186   SECTION 10. Effectivity.
187
188         This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a
189   newspaper of general circulation.
