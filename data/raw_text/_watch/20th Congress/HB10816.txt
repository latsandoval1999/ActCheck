                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


       The barangay is the basic political unit of the country and serves as the
primary venue for the delivery of government services and the implementation of
programs at the community level. Barangay officials and other barangay
frontliners are often the first to respond to emergencies, maintain peace and
order, assist in disaster response, facilitate community programs, and address
the immediate needs and concerns of their constituents.

      These responsibilities expose them to various risks, including death,
permanent disability, and injuries arising from accidents in the course of
performing their duties. While Republic Act No. 6942, otherwise known as the
“Act Increasing the Insurance Benefits of Local Government Officials and
Providing Funds Therefor,” provides insurance coverage to certain local officials,
its coverage does not adequately extend to other barangay personnel who
likewise perform essential frontline functions in their communities. The benefits
provided under the law likewise require enhancement in view of present
economic conditions and the increasing costs associated with death, disability,
hospitalization, and medical care.

      This measure therefore seeks to expand the coverage of Republic Act No.
6942 to include barangay tanods, barangay health workers, barangay day care
workers, and members of the Lupong Tagapamayapa, and to increase the death,
disability, and accident insurance benefits available to covered personnel.

      The measure further provides for the corresponding payment of insurance
premiums based on the actual number of covered personnel, subject to annual
appropriations and periodic actuarial assessment by the Government Service
Insurance System to ensure the sustainability and adequacy of the insurance
program.
       Through this measure, the State recognizes the invaluable contribution of
barangay officials and other barangay frontliners and seeks to provide them and
their families with greater financial protection against the risks attendant to the
performance of their public service.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            “AN ACT
      PROVIDING DEATH, DISABILITY, AND ACCIDENT INSURANCE
      COVERAGE TO OTHER BARANGAY OFFICIALS, BARANGAY
      TANODS, BARANGAY HEALTH WORKERS, BARANGAY DAY
      CARE WORKERS AND MEMBERS OF THE LUPONG
      TAGAPAMAYAPA,   AND    INCREASING   THE   INSURANCE
      BENEFITS PROVIDED THEREIN, AMENDING FOR THIS
      PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6942 AND APPROPRIATING
      FUNDS THEREFOR”

      Be it enacted in the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Section 1 of Republic Act No. 6942, otherwise known as the
“Act Increasing the Insurance Benefits of Local Government Officials and
Providing Funds Therefor,” is hereby amended to read as follows:

            “SECTION 1. Coverage of the Act. – Every Punong
      Barangay, Sangguniang Barangay member, Sangguniang Kabataan
      Chairman, barangay secretary, barangay treasurer, barangay
      tanod, barangay health worker, barangay day care worker, and
      member of the Lupong Tagapamayapa, and every member of the
      Sangguniang Panlalawigan, Sangguniang Panlungsod, and
      Sangguniang Bayan, duly elected, appointed, designated, or
      otherwise qualified under existing laws, shall be automatically
      covered by this Act during his or her incumbency, appointment,
      designation, or period of authorized service, as the case may be,
      subject to the provisions herein.

             “Provided, That the coverage of an elected or appointed official
      shall cease upon the expiration, termination, or lawful cessation of
      his or her term or appointment, or upon receipt of a fixed salary in
      the same capacity or by virtue of a permanent appointment in
      another government office or election or appointment to another
      public office, in which case he or she shall thereafter be covered
      under the appropriate law.
            “Provided, further, That barangay tanods, barangay health
      workers, barangay day care workers, and members of the Lupong
      Tagapamayapa shall be covered during the period of their duly
      recognized appointment, designation, or service in the barangay.

            “Any covered person whose coverage ceases by reason of
      lawful termination, resignation, expiration of term, or cessation of
      appointment or designation may, subject to such conditions as may
      be prescribed by the Government Service Insurance System,
      continue such coverage by paying the required premiums therefor.”

       SECTION 2. Section 2 of Republic Act No. 6942 is hereby amended to read
as follows:

            “SEC. 2. Death and Burial Benefits. – In the event of death
      of an insured person during his or her period of coverage, the
      beneficiaries of a Punong Barangay or a member of the Sangguniang
      Panlalawigan, Sangguniang Panlungsod, Sangguniang Bayan,
      Sangguniang Barangay, or Sangguniang Kabataan shall be entitled
      to Forty Thousand Pesos (₱40,000.00), while the beneficiaries of a
      barangay secretary, barangay treasurer, barangay tanod, barangay
      health worker, barangay day care worker, or member of the Lupong
      Tagapamayapa shall be entitled to Twenty Thousand Pesos
      (₱20,000.00).

             “In addition to the death benefits herein provided, a burial
      benefit of Four Thousand Pesos (₱4,000.00) shall be paid to the
      beneficiaries within twenty-four (24) hours after written notice and
      satisfactory proof of death.

            “Should any covered person be assassinated or killed while in
      the performance of official duties or on the occasion thereof, his or
      her beneficiaries shall be entitled to double the applicable amount
      of indemnity provided under this Section.”

       SECTION 3. Section 3 of Republic Act No. 6942 is hereby amended to read
as follows:

             “SEC. 3. Total and Permanent Incapacity or Disability. –
      In the event of total and permanent incapacity or disability, as
      determined by the Government Service Insurance System,
      hereinafter referred to as the System, a Punong Barangay or a
      member of the Sangguniang Panlalawigan, Sangguniang
      Panlungsod, Sangguniang Bayan, Sangguniang Barangay, or
      Sangguniang Kabataan shall be entitled to Twenty-Four Thousand
      Pesos (₱24,000.00), while a barangay secretary, barangay
      treasurer, barangay tanod, barangay health worker, barangay day
      care worker, or member of the Lupong Tagapamayapa shall be
      entitled to Ten Thousand Pesos (₱10,000.00):
           “Provided, That the disability occurred during the period of
     coverage, whether work-connected or not.

           “In addition to the disability benefits herein provided, a burial
     benefit of Four Thousand Pesos (₱4,000.00) shall be paid to the
     beneficiaries in the event that death occurs within two (2) years from
     the onset of total and permanent incapacity or disability.

            “Anyone availing of the benefits under this Section shall not
     be entitled to the benefits under Sections Two, Four, and Five hereof
     for the same contingency.”

    SECTION 4. Paragraph 1 of Section 4 of Republic Act No. 6942 is hereby
amended to read as follows:

              “SEC. 4. Injuries Resulting from Accidents. – For injuries
       resulting from an accident occurring during the period of coverage,
       whether work-connected or not, a Punong Barangay or a member
       of the Sangguniang Panlalawigan, Sangguniang Panlungsod,
       Sangguniang Bayan, Sangguniang Barangay, or Sangguniang
       Kabataan shall be entitled to the following benefits, which in no
       case shall exceed the maximum benefit of Twenty Thousand
       Pesos (₱20,000.00) per covered year:

       “(a) For an injury to the brain resulting in the loss of sanity, Twenty
       Thousand Pesos (₱20,000.00);

       “(b) For permanent loss of sight in both eyes, Twenty Thousand
       Pesos (₱20,000.00);

       “(c) For permanent loss of sense of hearing in both ears, Sixteen
       Thousand Pesos (₱16,000.00);

       “(d) For permanent loss of sight in one eye, Nine Thousand Pesos
       (₱9,000.00);

       “(e) For permanent loss of sense of smell and sense of taste, and of
       hearing in one ear, Eight Thousand Pesos (₱8,000.00);

       “(f) For the loss or destruction of two or more limbs, Twenty
       Thousand Pesos (₱20,000.00);

       “(g) For the loss of one limb, Ten Thousand Pesos (₱10,000.00);

       “(h) For the loss or destruction of both hands at or above the wrists,
       Sixteen Thousand Pesos (₱16,000.00);

       “(i) For the loss or destruction of both feet at or above the ankles,
       Sixteen Thousand Pesos (₱16,000.00);
       “(j) For the loss or destruction of one hand at or above the wrist,
       Eight Thousand Pesos (₱8,000.00);

       “(k) For the loss or destruction of one foot at or above the ankle,
       Eight Thousand Pesos (₱8,000.00);

       “(l) For the loss or destruction of every thumb or great toe, Two
       Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00);

       “(m) For the loss or destruction of every finger or toe, One
       Thousand Pesos (₱1,000.00);

       “(n) For any other injury requiring hospitalization of at least forty
       (40) days, Four Thousand Pesos (₱4,000.00), and in cases where
       hospitalization is less than forty (40) days, at such amount not
       exceeding Two Thousand Pesos (₱2,000.00) as determined by the
       System and prescribed in its rules and regulations; and

       “(o) For any other injury not requiring hospitalization but requiring
       medical attention for at least fifteen (15) days, Two Thousand Pesos
       (₱2,000.00), and in cases where the medical attention extended is
       less than fifteen (15) days, at such amount not exceeding One
       Thousand Pesos (₱1,000.00) as determined by the System and
       prescribed in its rules and regulations.”

       SECTION 6. Section 6 of Republic Act No. 6942 is hereby amended to read
as follows:

            “SEC. 6. Responsibility for Payment of Premiums. – For all
      insurance benefits provided under this Act, the National
      Government shall appropriate annually and include in the General
      Appropriations Act the total annual premiums based on the actual
      number of covered Punong Barangays, members of the Sangguniang
      Panlalawigan, Sangguniang Panlungsod, Sangguniang Bayan,
      Sangguniang Barangay, and Sangguniang Kabataan, as certified by
      the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG).
            “For barangay secretaries, barangay treasurers, barangay
      tanods, barangay health workers, barangay day care workers, and
      members of the Lupong Tagapamayapa, the total annual premiums
      shall be included in the annual budget of the barangay concerned,
      subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules
      and regulations and to the availability of funds.

            “The annual premiums shall be Two Hundred Forty-Seven
      Pesos (₱247.00) for every Punong Barangay and member of the
      Sangguniang      Panlalawigan,     Sangguniang     Panlungsod,
      Sangguniang Bayan, Sangguniang Barangay, and Sangguniang
      Kabataan, and Sixty-Two Pesos (₱62.00) for every barangay
      secretary, barangay treasurer, barangay tanod, barangay health
      worker, barangay day care worker, and member of the Lupong
      Tagapamayapa.

            “The System shall conduct periodic actuarial studies to
      determine the adequacy of the premium rates in relation to the
      benefits and coverage provided under this Act. Based on such
      actuarial studies, the premium rates may be adjusted, subject to the
      approval of the appropriate authorities and the availability of
      appropriations.

            “The total annual premiums shall be released to the System
      in accordance with the General Appropriations Act and the
      applicable annual budgets of the barangays concerned. Any failure
      or delay in the payment of premiums shall not prejudice the right of
      a covered person or his or her beneficiaries to avail of the benefits
      under this Act, subject to the implementing rules and regulations.”

       SECTION 7. Section 8 of Republic Act No. 6942 is hereby amended to read
as follows:

             “SEC. 8. Processing of Claims. – All claims for payment
      pursuant to the provisions of this Act shall, with the assistance of
      the DILG, the concerned local government unit, or other appropriate
      government office, be processed and settled by the System within
      thirty (30) days from submission of all required documents, at the
      nearest System branch or through such other mechanisms as may
      be authorized by the System.

             “The System shall adopt simplified documentary and
      processing requirements, particularly for beneficiaries of deceased
      covered persons and for covered personnel residing in geographically
      isolated and disadvantaged areas.”
       SECTION 8. Section 9 of Republic Act No. 6942 is hereby amended to read
as follows:

                  “SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. –
            Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act, the
            System, in coordination with the DILG, Department of Budget
            and Management (DBM), Department of Finance (DOF), and
            other concerned agencies, shall promulgate the rules and
            regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this
            Act.

                   “The implementing rules and regulations shall, among
            others, prescribe the documentary requirements, procedures
            for enrollment and certification of covered personnel, payment
            and remittance of premiums, processing of claims,
            determination of disability, and coordination between the
            System, the DILG, and the concerned local government units.”


      SECTION 9. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the
implementation of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act
of the year following its effectivity and thereafter. The corresponding
appropriations of local government units shall be included in their annual
budgets, subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing laws, rules and
regulations.

       SECTION 10. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall
continue to be in full force and effect.

       SECTION 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders,
rules, regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.


      Approved.
