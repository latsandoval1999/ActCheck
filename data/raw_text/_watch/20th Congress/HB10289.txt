                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

This legislative measure proposes the establishment of a uniform excise tax rate for all
vapor products, irrespective of nicotine formulation. The initiative seeks to rectify
structural deficiencies in the current excise tax regime under Section 144(c) of the National
Internal Revenue Code (NIRC), which imposes different rates—₱60.20 per milliliter for
nicotine salt solutions and ₱69.46 per 10 milliliters (equivalent to ₱6.95 per milliliter) for
freebase nicotine solutions. Such disparity has created regulatory loopholes and
incentivized tax avoidance, contributing to substantial revenue loss.

Illicit trade in vapor products is likewise rampant due to misdeclaration and smuggling.
Euromonitor estimates illicit trade of vapor products at 84%.

Cognizant of the above issues, the proposed bill introduces a simplified and harmonized
excise tax structure. By adopting a single rate across all vapor product types, the measure
eliminates classification ambiguities, enhances compliance, and fosters equitable treatment
among industry stakeholders.

In support of standardization and regulatory clarity, the bill also prescribes specific
container or packaging capacities for vapor products: 1ml, 2 ml, 3ml, 5 ml, and 10 ml. This
provision aims to streamline tax administration, prevent misdeclaration of product
volumes, and facilitate accurate assessment and monitoring. By limiting packaging to
defined sizes, the measure reduces opportunities for manipulation and underreporting,
thereby reinforcing compliance and enabling more effective enforcement of excise tax
laws.

The reform is expected to improve collection efficiency and enhance revenues. Moreover,
it reinforces the constitutional principles of equality and uniformity in taxation by ensuring
that articles of the same kind or class are taxed at consistent rates.
In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.
