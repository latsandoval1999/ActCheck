EXPLANATORY NOTE

The conversion from Extension Office to District Office of the Land Transportation
Office in the Municipality of Binangonan, Rizal, represents a significant step towards
enhancing accessibility and efficiency in providing essential services in the 1st District of Rizal,
consisting of four municipalities. This strategic move will bridge the gap between the
government’s services and the underserved communities, especially that the Municipality of
Binangonan is situated in the farthest area of the District.

As the Region continues to develop and progress, the number of registrants,
transactions, and work services is expected to rise significantly. By the Conversion of LTO
Binangonan Extension Office to LTO Binangonan District Office, we are ensuring that the
needs of the people are met permanently and effectively, while also contributing to the overall
development and progress of the region.

Given these circumstances, the passage of this Bill is not just advisable but indeed an
imperative action to meet the evolving requirements of the local populace, especially the people
of the Municipalities of Angono and Binangonan and promote the efficiency and effectiveness
of government services in the region.

It reflects the commitment to improving the lives of the people and the fostering of
development in the Ist District of Rizal, making this cause earnestly sought by all stakeholders
involved.

AN ACT CONVERTING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO)
EXTENSION OFFICE LOCATED IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BINANGONAN,
PROVINCE OF RIZAL INTO A REGULAR LTO DISTRICT OFFICE AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it in enacted by Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

Section 1. The existing Land Transportation Office (LTO) Extension Office located in the
Municipality of Binangonan in the 1* District of Rizal is hereby converted into a regular LTO
District Office.

Section 2. The Secretary of the Department of Transportation shall immediately include in the
Department’s program the operationalization of the LTO District Office in the Municipality of
Binangonan, Province of Rizal; the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current
year’s appropriations of the LTO-Binangonan Extension Office. Thereafter, such sums, as may
be necessary for the continued implementation of this Act, shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act.

Section 3. The Secretary of the Department of Transportation shall issue such rules and
regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

Section 4. The Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette
or in a newspaper of general circulation
