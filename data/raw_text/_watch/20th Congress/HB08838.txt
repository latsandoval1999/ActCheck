14                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE
15
16           The increase in global oil prices has significantly affected the domestic economy and
17   cost of living. As petroleum products are critical inputs across multiple sectors, increases in
18   fuel prices have a cascading effect on the prices of basic goods and services.
19
20           At present, petroleum products are subject to a twelve percent (12%) Value-Added Tax
21   (VAT) under the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended. While VAT remains a
22   vital source of government revenue, its continued imposition on fuel during periods of elevated
23   global oil prices exacerbates inflationary pressures and weakens the purchasing power of
24   Filipino households.
25
26            Republic Act No. 12316 authorizes the President to suspend or reduce the excise tax on
27   petroleum products, but this relief would be a far from sufficient response to the effects of the
28   oil crisis, which has the potential to worsen or continue for an extended period of time.
29
30           This measure seeks to authorize the temporary suspension or reduction of VAT on
31   petroleum products. In doing so, it intends to ease the burden of high fuel prices and mitigate
32   inflationary pressures on basic goods and services. In view of the foregoing, the passage of this
33   measure is earnestly sought.

14    AN ACT TEMPORARILY SUSPENDING OR REDUCING THE IMPOSITION OF
15     VALUE-ADDED TAX (VAT) ON PETROLEUM PRODUCTS, AMENDING FOR
16      THE PURPOSE THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS
17                  AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
18
19        Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
20   assembled:
21
22          SECTION 1. Authority to Suspend or Reduce VAT on Petroleum Products. –
23   Notwithstanding the provisions of Sections 106, 108, and 109 of the National Internal Revenue
24   Code of 1997, as amended, the President of the Philippines may, upon the recommendation of
25   the Development Budget Coordination Committee (DBCC), in coordination with the Secretary
26   of Energy, suspend the imposition of, or reduce the rate of, the Value-Added Tax (VAT) on
27   petroleum products, subject to the following conditions:
28
29          (a) The average Dubai crude oil price based on Mean of Platts Singapore (MOPS) has
30          reached or exceeded Eighty United States Dollars (USD 80) per barrel for one (1) month
31          immediately preceding the issuance of the suspension or reduction order; or
32
33          (b) A state of national emergency or calamity has been declared by the President, and
34          such condition has resulted in extraordinary increases in domestic pump prices of
35          petroleum products, as certified by the Secretary of Energy.
36
37   The suspension or reduction may be applied to specific petroleum products, which include, but
38   is not limited to, (1) gasoline; (2) diesel fuel; (3) kerosene; (4) liquefied petroleum gas (LPG);
39   (5) aviation fuel; (6) fuel oil and bunker fuel, and may be implemented either as a full
40   suspension or partial reduction of the applicable VAT rate, as may be warranted by prevailing
41   economic conditions.
42
43           SECTION 2. Reporting Requirements. – Any suspension or reduction authorized under
44   this Act shall be effective for a period not exceeding six (6) months, unless otherwise extended
45   or terminated earlier by Congress through a joint resolution:
46
47          Provided, That the aggregate period of such suspension or reduction shall not exceed
48          one (1) calendar year;
49


                                                     2
 1          Provided, further, That any suspension or reduction shall be lifted when the conditions
 2          that warranted its issuance no longer exist;
 3
 4          Provided, furthermore, That upon expiration of the suspension or reduction period, the
 5          twelve percent (12%) VAT rate shall be automatically reinstated without need of further
 6          legislative or executive action;
 7
 8          Provided, finally, That the authority granted to the President under this Act shall be
 9          exercised only until December 31, 2028.
10
11           SECTION 3. Duration and Limitation. – Within fifteen (15) days from the issuance of
12   any suspension or reduction order, and every month thereafter, the President shall, through the
13   Secretary of Finance, submit to the Senate and the House of Representatives a report containing
14   the following:
15
16      1. The factual basis for the suspension or reduction of VAT;
17
18      2. The estimated foregone revenues; and
19
20      3. The expected impact on inflation, fuel prices, and overall economic activity.
21
22         SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within fifteen (15) days from the
23   effectivity of this Act, the Department of Finance (DOF), the Department of Budget and
24   Management (DBM), the National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA), the
25   Department of Energy (DOE), and the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), in coordination with
26   the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) and the Bureau of Customs (BOC), shall jointly issue
27   the necessary rules and regulations for the implementation of this Act.
28
29        SECTION 5. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held invalid or
30   unconstitutional, the other provisions not so declared shall remain in force and effect.
31
32         SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders and rules and regulations
33   contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or amended
34   accordingly.
35
36         SECTION 7. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
37   in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
