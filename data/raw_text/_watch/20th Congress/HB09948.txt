                             AN ACT
MANDATING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF REGIONAL K-9 ADOPTION SHELTERS
FOR RETIRED POLICE DOGS, PROVIDING FOR ADOPTION, VETERINARY CARE,
AND POST-ADOPTION MONITORING, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

                                          EXPLANATORY NOTE


         In June 2026, nine (9) retired Narcotic Detection Dogs (NDDs) from the Philippine Drug
Enforcement Agency (PDEA) that have served for years in the anti-illegal drug operations across
the Philippines were put up for adoption. These dogs have an average age of 12 years and have
retired from the service. While in the service, these dogs played key roles in anti-drug operations,
including inspections in jail facilities, search-and-seizure operations, interdiction activities, and
checkpoints operations in airports, seaports, and transport terminals nationwide.1
        In 2022, three (3) retired canines in Cordillera were also put up for adoption. These dogs
assisted in locating a missing Korean National who was hiking in Barlig, Mountain Province in
2017. They also aided in the search and rescue /retrieval operations after a landslide in Sitio
Sakrang, Barangay Banawel, Natonin, Mountain Province in 2018.2
        The Philippine National Police (PNP) Explosive Ordnance Disposal and Canine Group
manages hundreds of trained dogs nationwide, while PDEA operates a specialized K-9 facility for
narcotics detections dogs. These service canines are reliable partners of law enforcement agencies.
Upon retirement, however, many are either retained by handlers or placed for adoption without a
uniform, dedicated shelter-and-adoption framework that ensures standardized veterinary care,
adopter screening and post-adoption monitoring.

                                  AN ACT

     AND POST-ADOPTION MONITORING, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

           Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

 1           Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Regional K-9 Adoption Shelter
 2   Act”.
 3
 4          Section 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to protect
 5   and promote animal welfare, ensure humane treatment, prevent cruelty, neglect and abandonment,
 6   and promote responsible pet ownership, consistent with Republic Act No. 8485 otherwise known
 7   as the Animal Welfare Act of 1998, as amended by Republic Act No. 10631, and Republic Act
 8   No. 9482 otherwise known as the Anti-Rabies Act of 2007.
 9
10           To carry out this policy, the State shall endeavor to provide appropriate shelter and
11   adoption mechanisms for retired police canines, in recognition of their invaluable service and to
12   ensure their continued welfare after retirement.
13
 1           Section 3. Establishment of Regional K-9 Adoption Shelter. – There shall be established at
 2   least one (1) Regional K-9 Adoption Shelter in each administrative region. The Shelter shall be
 3   established and operated by the host provincial government or local government unit (LGU) as
 4   may be identified in the implementing rules and regulations, in coordination with the PNP and
 5   PDEA, and in accordance with animal welfare standards.
 6
 7           The shelter shall keep records of retired canines including their adoptive owners for
 8   monitoring to ensure that they are not being abused or are being used for illegal practices. All
 9   shelters shall comply with the registration and regulatory requirements of the Department of
10   Agriculture (DA) through the Bureau of Animal Industry, consistent with Animal Welfare Act and
11   such other rules and regulations.
12
13          Section 4. Turnover, Custody, and Coordination. – The PNP and PDEA shall coordinate
14   with the host LGU / Shelter for the turnover of retired K-9s including the submission of the K9s
15   medical and vaccination records and other relevant information necessary for proper care and
16   placement.
17
18          Section 5. Appropriations. – To carry out the provisions of this Act, the amounts necessary
19   for the implementation of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
20   Act. LGUs operating the Shelters may allocate funds from their local budgets, subject to applicable
21   budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules. The DA, PNP, and PDEA shall provide such technical
22   and program support as may be provided in the General Appropriations Act and in the
23   implementing rules and regulations.
24
25           Section 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
26   effectivity of this Act, the Department of Agriculture, PNP, PDEA, in coordination with relevant
27   agencies and stakeholders, shall promulgate the implementing rules and regulations to effectively
28   carry out the provisions of this Act.
29
30           Section 7. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional
31   or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.
32
33           Section 8. Repealing clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations, or parts
34   therefor, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified
35   accordingly.
36
37          Section 9. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
38   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
