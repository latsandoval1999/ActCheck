                            EXPLANATORY NOTE
      This Bill seeks the official statutory creation and recognition of Barangay
Santa Cruz in the Municipality of Tampakan, Province of South Cotabato, so that it
may receive its lawful share in the National Tax Allotment, formerly referred to as
the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA). Similar measures were filed in previous
Congresses.
        Barangay Santa Cruz came into existence through Sangguniang Bayan
Resolution No. 122, Series of 2001, and succeeding Sangguniang Panlalawigan
Resolution Nos. 13 and 170, both Series of 2001. Its creation was subsequently
ratified in a plebiscite conducted by the Commission on Elections on 30 June 2003.
Since its creation, Barangay Santa Cruz has functioned as a regular local government
unit, serving its constituents, electing its officials, implementing public service
projects and programs, and administering its assets and facilities.
       Because Barangay Santa Cruz was created by a local government unit after
the effectivity of the Local Government Code of 1991 and was not created by
statute, its financial requirements have historically been borne by the local
government units concerned pursuant to the final proviso of Section 285 of
Republic Act No. 7160.
        Its official recognition by Congress will allow Barangay Santa Cruz to receive
its just share of national revenues and strengthen its ability to finance essential
services and development programs. Fiscal autonomy at the barangay level translates
directly into better and more responsive services for the people.
      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this Bill is earnestly sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:
        SECTION 1. Official Creation of Barangay Santa Cruz. - Barangay Santa
Cruz in the Municipality of Tampakan, Province of South Cotabato, is hereby
officially created as a barangay and shall retain its identity and existing territorial
boundaries as established by the local measures creating it and as ratified in the
plebiscite for its creation.
       SEC. 2. Public Infrastructure, Assets, and Facilities. - All public
infrastructure, assets, and facilities owned by or belonging to Barangay Santa Cruz,
Municipality of Tampakan, Province of South Cotabato, shall be retained by it.
      SEC. 3. Incumbent Barangay Officials. - The incumbent barangay officials
of Barangay Santa Cruz shall continue to hold office until such time that their
successors have been duly elected and qualified.
       SEC. 4. National Tax Allotment Share. - Barangay Santa Cruz shall be
entitled to its corresponding share in the National Tax Allotment, formerly referred
to as the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA), in accordance with Section 285 of
Republic Act No. 7160, as amended, and other applicable laws, rules, and
regulations.
    SEC. 5. Plebiscite. - The plebiscite conducted and supervised by the
Commission on Elections (COMELEC) on 30 June 2003, pursuant to the local
measures creating Barangay Santa Cruz, which ratified its creation, shall serve as
substantial compliance with the plebiscite requirement required by law.
      SEC. 6. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
      Approved.
