                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to amend Section 3 of Republic Act No. 11210 to provide
additional maternity leave benefits to women diagnosed with postpartum depression,
thereby expanding the scope of existing maternity protection to include maternal
mental health.

       Postpartum depression is a serious and often underrecognized public health
concern that affects a significant number of women during and after pregnancy.
According to the World Health Organization (WHO), approximately 10% of pregnant
women and 13% of women who have just given birth experience a mental disorder,
primarily depression1. In developing countries, the prevalence is even higher, reaching
15.6% during pregnancy and 19.8% after childbirth2. In severe cases, maternal
suffering may lead to suicide, and affected mothers may be unable to properly care
for themselves and their children, thereby negatively impacting child growth and
development

       Emerging evidence further shows that postpartum depression does not always
manifest immediately after childbirth. Studies indicate that depressive symptoms may
develop or persist months after delivery, with approximately 7.2% of women
experiencing postpartum depressive symptoms as late as nine to ten months
postpartum3. Notably, more than half of these women did not exhibit symptoms during
the earlier postpartum period, highlighting the delayed and evolving nature of
maternal mental health conditions4.

       Despite the enactment of Republic Act No. 11210, which significantly expanded
maternity leave benefits in the Philippines, the current framework primarily addresses
physical recovery and immediate postnatal needs. It does not sufficiently account for
maternal mental health conditions such as postpartum depression, which may require
extended periods of care, treatment, and psychosocial support beyond the standard
leave period.

      This piece of legislation seeks to address these gaps by granting additional
maternity leave benefits to women diagnosed with postpartum depression, thereby
expanding the scope of existing maternity leave entitlements. Ultimately, this measure
ensures that mothers are afforded adequate time and support to recover both
physically and mentally, contributing to healthier families and stronger communities.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                 AN ACT
     AMENDING SECTION 3 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11210, OTHERWISE KNOWN
     AS THE “EXPANDED MATERNITY LEAVE LAW”, BY PROVIDING ADDITIONAL
     MATERNITY LEAVE BENEFITS TO WOMEN DIAGNOSED WITH POSTPARTUM
     DEPRESSION, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

1          Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
2    in Congress assembled:

3           SECTION 1. Section 3 of Republic Act No. 11210 is hereby amended to read as
4    follows:

 5         “Sec. 3. Grant of Maternity Leave. — All covered female workers in
 6         government and the private sector, including those in the informal
 7         economy, regardless of civil status or the legitimacy of her child, shall be
 8         granted one hundred five (105) days maternity leave with full pay and an
 9         option to extend for an additional thirty (30) days without pay: Provided,
10         That in case the worker qualifies as a solo parent under Republic Act No.
11         8972, or the “Solo Parents’ Welfare Act”, the worker shall be granted an
12         additional fifteen (15) days maternity leave with full pay.

13         IN ADDITION TO THE BENEFITS PROVIDED HEREIN, ANY
14         COVERED FEMALE WORKER WHO IS DIAGNOSED WITH
15         POSTPARTUM DEPRESSION BY A LICENSED HEALTH
16         PROFESSIONAL SHALL BE GRANTED AN ADDITIONAL FIFTEEN
17         (15) DAYS MATERNITY LEAVE WITH FULL PAY.

18         Enjoyment of maternity leave cannot be deferred but should be availed of
19         either before or after the actual period of delivery in a continuous and
20         uninterrupted manner, not exceeding one hundred five (105) days, as the
21         case may be.

22         Maternity leave shall be granted to female workers in every instance of
23         pregnancy, miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy,

                                                                                          1
1           regardless of frequency: Provided, That for cases of miscarriage or
2           emergency termination of pregnancy, sixty (60) days maternity leave with
3           full pay shall be granted.”

 4          Sec. 2. Non-Diminution of Benefits. – Nothing in this Act shall be construed to
 5   diminish existing maternity benefits currently enjoyed, whether these are granted
 6   under Collective Bargaining Agreements (CBA) or present laws, if the same are more
 7   beneficial to the female worker. Furthermore, any other working arrangement that the
 8   female worker shall agree to during the additional maternity leave period shall be
 9   allowed: Provided, That in case the woman qualifies as a solo parent under Republic
10   Act No. 8972 or the “Solo Parents' Welfare Act,” the availment of additional fifteen
11   (15) days maternity leave with full pay shall not be diminished.

12          Sec. 3. Penalties. – Whoever fails or refuses to comply with the provisions of
13   this Act shall be punished by a fine of not less than Twenty thousand pesos
14   (P20,000.00) nor more than Two hundred thousand pesos (P200,000.00), and
15   imprisonment of not less than six (6) years and one (1) day nor more than twelve (12)
16   years or both. Furthermore, if the act or omission penalized by this Act shall be
17   committed by an association, partnership, corporation, or any other institution, its
18   managing head, directors, or partners shall be liable to the penalties provided in this
19   Act for the offense.

20   Failure on the part of any association, partnership, corporation, or private enterprise
21   to comply with the provisions of this Act shall be a ground for non- renewal of business
22   permits.

23         Sec. 4. Funding Source. – The amount necessary to fund this additional
24   maternity benefit shall be taken from revenues from excise taxes on sweetened
25   beverages, alcohol, tobacco products, heated tobacco products, and vapor products.
26   It may be augmented under the General Appropriations Act.

27         Sec. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Civil Service Commission
28   (CSC), the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), and the Social Security
29   System (SSS) shall issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
30   implementation of this Act within sixty (60) days from the effectivity thereof.

31         Sec. 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held invalid or
32   unconstitutional, the other provisions not so declared shall remain in force and effect.

33          Sec. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations
34   contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or
35   amended accordingly.

36          Sec. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
37   publication in the Official Gazette or a newspaper of general circulation.
