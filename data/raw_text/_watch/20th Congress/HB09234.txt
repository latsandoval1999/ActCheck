                                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Article II, Section 15 of 1987 Constitution provides that the State shall protect
the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them. Also,
the State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health
development which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health, and other social
services available to all the people at affordable cost. There shall be priority for the
needs of the underprivileged sick, elderly, disabled, women, and children.1

       Recent studies have highlighted a significant gap in mental health services
within the Philippines. According to the World Health Organization, mental health
disorders affect one in four people globally, yet access to mental health professionals
remains limited, particularly in public healthcare settings. Research by the Department
of Health indicates that the shortage of psychiatrists and mental health professionals
contributes to inadequate mental health support, exacerbating conditions and leading
to poorer health outcomes for those affected. 2

       This bill proposes to establish mental health units in all government hospitals
across the country and deploy psychiatrists and mental health practitioners in these
units. By ensuring that each government hospital has three (3) dedicated psychiatric
professionals, the bill aims to enhance the quality and accessibility of mental health
services, providing timely and effective care to individuals in need. The presence of
psychiatrists in these facilities will facilitate comprehensive mental health assessments,
diagnosis, and treatment, thereby addressing mental health issues with the same
urgency and importance as physical health conditions. Improved mental health

         The need for this bill stems from the growing recognition of mental health as a
critical aspect of public health. Institutionalizing psychiatrists in government hospitals
will not only bridge the existing gaps in mental health services in the country but also
promote a more integrated approach to healthcare that considers mental and physical
health as interlinked. For people and communities, improved mental health services
can lead to a higher economic productivity. By improving access to psychiatric care,
this bill seeks to advance the nation's overall health standards, reduce the stigma
associated with mental health issues, and ensure that every individual has the

 1       SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Mental Health Units
 2   in Government Hospitals Act.”
 3
 4       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby the policy of the State to address
 5   concerns on the availability and accessibility of mental health services in government
 6   hospitals. By ensuring the availability of psychiatrists in all government hospitals, this
 7   policy aims to improve access to mental health services for individuals in need. The
 8   inclusion of psychiatrists in government hospitals will lead to improved access, timely
 9   diagnosis and treatment, and facilitate integrated care for better patient outcomes.
10
11      SEC. 3. Mental Health Units in Government Hospitals. – All government
12   hospitals shall establish Mental Health Units in the Philippines and shall have at least
13   three (3) psychiatrists on full-time basis to ensure that individuals seeking mental
14   health services in government hospitals have access to qualified psychiatrists for
15   diagnosis, treatment, and care.
16
