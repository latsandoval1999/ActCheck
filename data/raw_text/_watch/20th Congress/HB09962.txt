                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to promote gender equality and uphold the sanctity of
marriage. It amends Articles 333 and 334 of Republic Act No. 3815, otherwise known
as “The Revised Penal Code” and establishes equal standards of criminal liability for
marital infidelity regardless of sex.

        The existing provisions on adultery and concubinage impose different
standards of liability on husbands and wives. A married woman may be prosecuted
for a single act of sexual intercourse outside marriage, while a married man becomes
liable only under more limited circumstances. These distinctions reflect outdated
gender norms that no longer accord with the constitutional guarantee of equal
protection under the law and the State’s commitment to ensuring the equality of
women and men.

       The 1987 Constitution recognizes marriage as an inviolable social institution
and affirms the fundamental equality of all persons before the law. Consistent with
these principles, this measure removes discriminatory distinctions in the treatment
of marital infidelity while preserving legal protections that safeguard the integrity of
marriage and family life.

      In holding spouses to the same standard of fidelity and accountability, this bill
advances fairness, strengthens respect for marriage, and aligns existing penal laws
with contemporary constitutional values.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
ESTABLISHING EQUAL CRIMINAL LIABILITY AND PENALTIES FOR
MARITAL INFIDELITY, PROMOTING GENDER EQUALITY AND
UPHOLDING THE SANCTITY OF MARRIAGE, AMENDING FOR THE
PURPOSE ACT NO. 3815, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “THE REVISED PENAL
CODE”, AS AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     Section 1. Title Eleven, Chapter One, Article 333 of the Revised Penal Code, as
amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

                   “Article 333. Who are guilty of adultery. – Adultery is
          committed by:

                 a. ANY married PERSON who shall have sexual
             intercourse with ANOTHER PERSON not HIS/HER SPOUSE
             and by the OTHER PERSON who has carnal knowledge of
             HIM/HER knowing HIM/HER to be married, even if the
             marriage be subsequently declared void;​

               b. ANY MARRIED MAN WHO SHALL COMMIT A
             SEXUAL ACT BY INSERTING HIS PENIS INTO ANOTHER
             PERSON’S ORAL OR ANAL ORIFICE AND BY THE OTHER
             PERSON WHO SHALL COMMIT A SEXUAL ACT WITH
             HIM KNOWING HIM TO BE MARRIED, EVEN IF THE
             MARRIAGE BE SUBSEQUENTLY DECLARED VOID; AND​

               c. ANY MARRIED PERSON WHO SHALL COMMIT AN
             ACT BY USING ANY BODY PART, INSTRUMENT OR
             OBJECT, IN A SEXUAL MANNER, WITH ANOTHER
             PERSON AND BY THE OTHER PERSON WHO SHALL
             COMMIT A SEXUAL ACT WITH HIM/HER KNOWING

              SUBSEQUENTLY DECLARED VOID.

                   x x x”

     SEC. 2. Title Eleven, Chapter One, Article 334 of the Revised Penal Code, as
amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

              “Article 334. Concubinage. – Any SPOUSE who shall keep a
           mistress OR PARAMOUR in the conjugal dwelling, or shall have
           sexual intercourse, under scandalous circumstances, with a
           PERSON who is not HIS/HER SPOUSE, or shall cohabit with
           HIM/HER in any other place, shall be punished by PRISON
           MAYOR in its minimum and medium periods.

              “The concubine OR PARAMOUR KNOWING HIM/HER TO
           BE MARRIED shall suffer the penalty of PRISON MAYOR IN ITS
           MINIMUM AND MEDIUM PERIODS.”

       SEC. 3. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and effect.

       SEC. 4. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 5. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
