                             EXPLANATORY NOTE
       While recent reforms such as Republic Act No. 11976 or the Ease of Paying
Taxes Act have simplified tax compliance, there remains a significant imbalance
between the powers of tax authorities and the protection of taxpayer rights. Reports
of harassment, abuse of discretion, and lack of accessible remedies continue to
undermine public trust in revenue agencies such as the Bureau of Internal Revenue
(BIR) and the Bureau of Customs (BOC). Existing laws, including Republic Act No.
6713, provide general standards for public officials but do not sufficiently guarantee
enforceable rights and remedies for taxpayers.

       This bill seeks to institutionalize a Taxpayer Bill of Rights and Obligations
(TBORO) that clearly defines the fundamental rights and duties of taxpayers in their
interactions with tax authorities. It aims to ensure transparency, fairness, due
process, and accountability in tax administration, while also promoting voluntary
compliance through better taxpayer awareness and protection.

        The measure likewise proposes the creation of an independent Office of the
National Taxpayer Advocate, mandated to assist taxpayers in resolving disputes,
address complaints, and safeguard taxpayer rights against abuse, inefficiency, or
inaction by revenue agencies. By strengthening taxpayer protection mechanisms, this
bill seeks to foster greater public trust, improve compliance, and ultimately enhance
revenue collection.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

 1             SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as "The Taxpayer’s
 2      Bill of Rights and Obligations Act."

 3             SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the
 4      State to provide increased protection of taxpayers’ rights in complying with their
 5      obligation to pay national and local taxes to the government, to the end that abuses
 6      committed against taxpayers are prevented, if not completely eradicated, and taxes
 7      are collected responsibly. In turn, taxpayers are expected to faithfully comply with
 8      their tax obligations to the government.

26   amend, modify, or repeal the provisions of the abovementioned laws: Provided,

 3          SEC. 5. Taxpayer’s Basic Rights. — The following are the taxpayer’s
 4   basic rights:
 5          (a) Right to be informed. – The taxpayer shall have the right to know in a
 6               simplified manner how one can comply with laws, rules and
 7               regulations implemented by revenue authorities. The taxpayer is
 8               entitled to clear, complete, accurate, and simplified explanation of tax
 9               laws, rules, regulations, procedures, and other issuances, as well as be
10               apprised of taxpayer’s rights and obligations in all tax forms,
11               publications, notices, correspondences, assessments, rulings, and
12               decisions.
13                    In case of any deficiency tax findings, alleged violations or any
14               decisions or rulings rendered by the revenue authorities, the taxpayer
15               shall be informed in the written decision or ruling, clearly and
16               sufficiently of the facts and laws thereon, including any supporting
17               computation and documentation upon which they are based.
18                    The taxpayer shall have the right to be informed of any institution
19               by revenue authorities of a summary collection action, which requires
20               sale or seizure of property or the freezing of assets.
21                    The service of the notices and decisions to the taxpayers shall be
22               made in accordance with laws, rules, and regulations.
23          (b) Right to fair, uniform, and faithful application of laws. - The taxpayer
24               shall have the right to a fair, uniform and faithful application of laws,
25               rules, regulations, and other issuances of revenue authorities in all
26               proceedings involving the taxpayer.
27          (c) Right to challenge the position of and be heard by Revenue
28               Authorities. – The taxpayer shall have the right to challenge and raise

 1         objections against the findings, assessments, rulings, or decisions of
 2         the revenue authorities.
 3              The taxpayer shall have the right to be heard and that the
 4         evidence presented by the taxpayer be duly considered in deciding any
 5         matter involving the taxpayer.
 6   (d) Right to fair and impartial proceedings before the Revenue
 7         Authorities. – The taxpayer shall at all times be entitled to fair and
 8         impartial dispositions of the assessment cases, deficiency findings,
 9         requests for rulings, decisions, and other dispositions made by the
10         revenue authorities concerning the taxpayer.
11   (e) Right to present evidence and witnesses. – The taxpayer shall have the
12         right to be given ample and adequate opportunity to present evidence
13         in the possession of the taxpayer or any third party, and call on
14         witnesses to testify, as the case may be, that would assist the taxpayer
15         in the pursuit of any assessment or matter involving it.
16   (f)   Right to be assisted or represented by a qualified professional. – The
17         taxpayer shall have the right to be assisted or represented by a
18         competent and independent qualified professional, such as a legal
19         counsel, accountant, or customs broker, of the Taxpayer’s own choice,
20         in all administrative, civil, or criminal tax proceedings: Provided, That
21         any communication, correspondence and/or documents submitted or
22         exchanged between the taxpayer and the qualified professional that
23         they engage shall be considered strictly confidential, except as
24         otherwise provided under existing laws, rules and regulations.
25              In case the taxpayer experiences financial difficulty in engaging
26         the services of a private counsel or qualified professional, the Office of
27         the National Taxpayer Advocate (ONTA) shall provide free and
28         effective representation to the taxpayer, upon the latter’s request.

 1   (g) Right to timely and expeditious completion and finality of tax audits. –
 2         The taxpayer shall have the right to have revenue authorities begin and
 3         complete tax audits and to address the requests and other matters
 4         pending before the revenue authorities in a timely and expeditious
 5         manner.
 6              The taxpayer shall have the right to know when the revenue
 7         authorities have completed an audit or examination of the taxpayer.
 8   (h) Right against baseless assessment. – The tax authorities are prohibited
 9         from issuing assessments against taxpayers without the benefit of a
10         proper audit conducted by authorized revenue authorities against
11         taxpayers. Otherwise, such assessments shall be void.
12   (i)   Right to be given an opportunity for amicable settlement. – Unless
13         contrary to laws, the taxpayer shall have the opportunity to amicably
14         settle the taxpayer’s deficiencies and delinquencies, at any stage of the
15         proceedings: Provided, That said settlement must be in writing and
16         duly signed by the parties or their agent.
17   (j)   Right to avail of installment payment. – In so far as may be permitted
18         by law, the taxpayer shall have the right to settle tax liabilities through
19         installment payments.
20   (k) Right to pay no more than the correct amount of tax. – The taxpayer
21         shall have the right to pay only the amount of tax legally due,
22         including interest, surcharges, fines, and penalties.
23   (l)   Right to relief from paying interests, surcharges and penalties. – The
24         taxpayer shall be entitled to a reduction of interests, surcharges and
25         penalties levied on their tax liabilities in case of any excessive
26         assessment or undue delay caused by the revenue authorities,
27         including instances of loss of records, personnel transfers, extended

18          SEC. 6. Taxpayer’s Basic Obligations. – Complementary to the above
19   taxpayer’s rights are the following duties and responsibilities of the taxpayer:
20          (a)   Obligation to Comply with Tax Laws. – The taxpayer shall observe
21                and comply with tax laws, rules, and regulations with reasonable care
22                and diligence;
23          (b)   Obligation to Truthfully and Timely Disclose Information. – The
24                taxpayer shall truthfully and timely disclose complete and accurate
25                information as required by tax laws, rules, and regulations, including
26                but not limited to, registered address, official email address,
27                registered activities, tax type details, and accounting period;




                                                                                        6
 1          (c)   Obligation to Lodge and Submit Required Documents Completely and
 2                on Time. – The taxpayer shall lodge and submit, either personally or
 3                through electronic means, in a timely manner all relevant information,
 4                books of accounts, reports, records, returns, and other documents to
 5                revenue authorities on or before the dates set by tax laws, rules, and
 6                regulations;
 7          (d)   Obligation to Keep and Make Tax Records Available for
 8                Examination. – The taxpayer shall keep accurate records of the
 9                required books of accounts and other tax information for the duration
10                required by law, rules, and regulations, and make them available for
11                examination by revenue officers following proper procedures;
12          (e)   Obligation to Cooperate with Revenue Authorities in Tax Audit. – The
13                taxpayer shall cooperate with revenue authorities in the latter’s
14                conduct of tax audits in accordance with laws, rules, and regulations;
15                and
16          (f)   Obligation to Timely Pay Taxes. – The taxpayer shall timely pay the
17                taxes due to the government through any available payment channels.
18          SEC. 7. Creation of the Office of the National Taxpayer Advocate. — In
19   order to protect the rights of taxpayers, both substantive and procedural, the ONTA
20   is hereby created to promote tax compliance and assist taxpayers. The ONTA shall
21   be an independent and autonomous office attached to the Department of Justice
22   (DOJ) for purposes of policy and program coordination.
23          The ONTA shall have the power to provide for the structural organization
24   and staff of its offices, create divisions and units, including regional or provincial
25   offices, as may be necessary, and designate and appoint officers and employees of
26   the Office, subject to the approval of the Department of Budget and Management
27   (DBM) and in accordance with civil service laws, rules, and regulations: Provided,

 3          SEC. 8. Powers, Functions and Responsibilities of the Office of the
 4   National Taxpayer Advocate. — The ONTA shall have the following functions,
 5   powers, and responsibilities:
 6          (a) Assist the public in their queries and concerns relative to taxes by
 7               providing legal and procedural advice, including guidance on remedial
 8               measures that can be availed of, which may be in writing upon request
 9               of the taxpayer;
10          (b) Represent low income taxpayers, and assist, initiate, and intervene, as
11               may be applicable, in tax cases, complaints, and proceedings, after due
12               evaluation: Provided, That the ONTA shall have the power to set
13               qualifications of taxpayers who shall be given priority in the ONTA’s
14               representation services pursuant to Section 5(f) of this Act. The ONTA
15               may collaborate with qualified professionals to assist in the
16               representation of low income taxpayers who have a dispute with
17               revenue authorities and to provide education and outreach program to
18               low income taxpayers;
19          (c) Engage in regular information, education, and communication
20               programs for all taxpayers on tax laws, rules, regulations, circulars,
21               including those that implement Supreme Court decisions, and other
22               issuances that affect them, including their rights and remedies under
23               the law;
24          (d) Recommend remedial administrative measures to revenue authorities
25               by way of continuing policy review, and to the Anti-Red Tape
26               Authority (ARTA) for purposes of reducing regulatory burden to
27               taxpayers;

 9          SEC. 9. Qualifications of the National Taxpayer Advocate and Deputy
10   National Taxpayer Advocate. –        The ONTA shall be headed by the National
11   Taxpayer Advocate, who shall have the primary authority and responsibility for
12   the effective exercise of its mandate and the discharge of its powers and functions.
13   The National Taxpayer Advocate shall be assisted by Deputy National Taxpayer
14   Advocates, one (1) each for the National Capital Region, Luzon, Visayas, and
15   Mindanao.
16          The National Taxpayer Advocate, who is a member of the Philippine Bar in
17   good standing, and deputy national taxpayer advocate, who is a member of the
18   Philippine Bar in good standing or a Certified Public Accountant, shall be Filipino
19   citizens, with at least ten (10) years of practice in the field of taxation with proven
20   integrity and competence and must not have been convicted of any crime involving
21   a violation of any tax laws and rules, and of any crime involving moral turpitude.
22          SEC. 10. Appointment, Term, and Rank. – The National Taxpayer
23   Advocate and Deputy National Taxpayer Advocates shall be appointed by the
24   President. The National Taxpayer Advocate may recommend the Deputy National
25   Taxpayer Advocates who shall be appointed by the President: Provided, That
26   preferably at least two (2) of the Deputy National Taxpayer Advocates are women.
27   The National Taxpayer Advocate and the four (4) Deputy National Taxpayer
28   Advocates shall, subject to the mandatory retirement age provided under the civil

 2   appointment.
 3          In case of vacancy arising from retirement, resignation, removal, permanent
 4   incapacity, or death of the National Taxpayer Advocate or Deputy National
 5   Taxpayer Advocate, the President shall appoint a successor to serve a fresh term of

10          SEC. 11. Disqualification. – An individual cannot be appointed as the
11   National Taxpayer Advocate or Deputy National Taxpayer Advocate if such
12   individual has been an employee or officer of, or has rendered service as
13   consultant or in any other capacity to any of the revenue authorities for two (2)
14   years prior to such appointment.
15          SEC. 12. Submission of Annual Reports. – The Congressional Oversight
16   Committee on the Comprehensive Tax Reform Program (COCCTRP) shall

25   personally or through electronic means, to the COCCTRP and shall include
26   appropriate remedial action, if any, or proposed legislation, and other
27   recommendations on the proper implementation of this Act.

 1          SEC. 13. Penalties for the Violation of the Bill of Rights of Taxpayers. —
 2   Notwithstanding the administrative remedies provided in the National Internal
 3   Revenue Code (NIRC) of 1997, as amended; the Customs Modernization and
 4   Tariff Act of 2016 (CMTA); R.A. No. 1125 creating the Court of Tax Appeals

11          Violations penalized by this act shall prescribe after twelve (12) years.

13   the law, and if the same be not known at the time, from the discovery thereof and
14   the institution of judicial proceeding for its investigation and punishment:
15   Provided, That the prescription shall be interrupted when proceedings are
16   instituted against the guilty person, and shall begin to run again if the proceedings
17   are dismissed for reasons not constituting jeopardy.
