20                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   Businesses have the resources to make a significant difference in the communities that
23   sustain them. Corporate Social Responsibility allows businesses to use those resources
24   not only for growth, but also to support education, health, environmental protection,
25   disaster relief, poverty alleviation, community development or any other charitable
26   purposes they prefer.
27
28   Existing laws already provide tax deductions and other incentives for donations and
29   charitable activities that serve the public. While these measures encourage businesses
30   to contribute to society, there is also a need to give corporations greater flexibility in
31   allocating their resources to better plan these activities.
32
33   Under existing law, stock corporations are generally prohibited from retaining surplus
34   profits in excess of one hundred percent (100%) of their paid in capital stock, subject to
35   the lawful provided exceptions. This proposed measure seeks to recognize Corporate
36   Social Responsibility as an additional legitimate purpose for which surplus profits may
37   be retained, provided that the CSR projects or programs are duly approved by the board
38   of directors.
39
40   By giving corporations greater leeway to retain and devote a portion of their profits to
41   CSR, the measure seeks to encourage businesses to undertake programs that create
42   lasting benefits for their employees, communities, and the environment.
43
44   The measure does not compel businesses to give away their profits. Rather, it gives
45   corporations that are willing to do more the flexibility to use their resources for purposes
46   that extend beyond the interests of their shareholders and contribute to the welfare of
47   the communities in which they operate.

20                             AN ACT
21        ENCOURAGING CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY, PROVIDING
22                        INCENTIVES THEREFOR
23
24   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
25   Congress assembled:
26
27        SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Corporate Social
28   Responsibility Act".
29
30          SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the vital role of the
31   private sector in nation-building and shall 5 encourage its active participation in
32   fostering sustainable economic 6 development and environment protection in the
33   Philippines.
34
35          Towards this end, the government shall mobilize its various agencies, in
36   coordination with nongovernment and people's organizations, to work hand-in-hand
37   for the integration, promotion, and strengthening of corporate social responsibility in
38   all business organizations.
39
40          SEC. 3. Corporate Social Responsibility. - All business organizations
41   established and operating under Philippine laws, whether domestic or foreign, are
42   hereby encouraged to observe corporate social responsibility in the operation of their
43   businesses in the country.
44
45          As used in this Act, corporate social responsibility (CSR) refers to the
46   commitment of business to contribute on a voluntary basis to sustainable economic
47   development I working with relevant stakeholders to improve their lives in ways that
48   are good for business, sustainable development agenda and society at large. CSR-
49   related activities shall include the following:
50
51           (a) Charitable programs and projects;
52           (b) Scientific research;
53           (c) Youth and sports development;
 1          (d) Cultural or educational promotion;
 2          (e) Services to veterans and senior citizens;
 3          (f) Social welfare;
 4          (g) Environmental sustainability;
 5          (h) Health development;
 6          (i) Disaster relief and assistance;
 7          (j) Socialized and low-cost housing; and
 8          (k) Employee and worker welfare related CSR activities.
 9
10          All business organizations are allowed to donate products and services under
11   their CSR-related activities for disaster relief and assistance, in accordance with the
12   regulations to be issued by the appropriate government agency. All existing laws and
13   regulations restricting or prohibiting the right of local government units under a state
14   of calamity and/or during a national emergency to solicit or accept any donation of
15   products and services under the CSR-related activities for disaster relief and
16   assistance of a business organization are hereby amended.
17
18          SEC. 4. Deduction from Unrestricted Retained Barnings. – To encourage
19   companies to engage in CSR, Section 42 of Republic 9 Act No. 11232, otherwise
20   known as the "Revised Corporation Code 10 of the Philippines" is hereby amended
21   to read as follows:
22
23          "SEC. 42. Power to Declare Dividends. – xxx
24
25          "Stock corporations are prohibited from retaining surplus profits in excess of
26   one hundred percent (100%) of their paid-in capital stock, except: (a) when justified
27   by definite corporate expansion OR FOR CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY
28   (CSR) projects or programs approved by the board of directors; or (b) when the
29   corporation is prohibited under any loan agreement with financial institutions or
30   creditors, whether local or foreign, from declaring dividends without their consent, and
31   such consent has not yet been secured; or (c) when it can be clearly shown that such
32   retention is necessary under special circumstances obtaining in the corporation, such
33   24 as when there is need for special reserve for probable contingencies."
34
35           SEC. 5. Awards and Recognition. – The Department of Trade and Industry
36   (DTI) shall recognize and reward all business organizations for outstanding,
37   innovative and world-class CSR-related services, projects and programs. It shall
38   likewise extend endorsement and encouragements to domestic and foreign
39   corporations doing business in the Philippines which are candidates for recognition
40   in international award-giving bodies for their CSR-related activities.
41
42          SEC. 6. Local Government Units. – All local government units where CSR-
43   related activities are conducted shall extend whatever assistance is necessary for
44   business organizations to accomplish their CSR programs and projects.
45
46          SEC. 7. Periodic Report. – All business organizations shall submit the list of
47   their CSR activities as part of their annual or regular report to the Securities and
48   Exchange Commission (SEC), DTI, or the Department of Finance, as the case may
49   be.
 1          SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days after
 2   the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of Finance shall, in coordination with DTI and
 3   the SEC, promulgate rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this
 4   Act.
 5
 6          SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid
 7   or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue in full
 8   force and effect.
 9
10          SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. – Section 42 of Republic Act No. 11232 is hereby
11   amended. All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations, and other issuances, or
12   parts thereof, inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
13
14          SEC. 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
15   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
