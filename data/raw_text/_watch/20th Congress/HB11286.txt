                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to establish clear and objective conditions for the grant of Good
Conduct Time Allowance (GCTA). It further amends Article 97 of Act No. 3815,
otherwise known as the “Revised Penal Code”, as amended by Republic Act (RA) No.
10592, to strengthen the administration of sentence credits while preserving GCTA
as an incentive for genuine rehabilitation.

       RA 10592 recognizes good conduct as a basis for reducing the period of
imprisonment and encourages persons deprived of liberty to reform while serving
their sentences. However, the lack of uniform standards to determine whether an
inmate has earned sentence credits often lead to inconsistent assessments, which
undermines the confidence in the fair and accountable administration of the GCTA.

       The proposed measure establishes the standard for determining good
conduct based on an inmate’s demonstrated conduct and participation in
rehabilitation, productive activities, restorative efforts, and institutional discipline. It
recognizes that sentence reduction must be administered with due regard to the
gravity of the offense, public safety, and the interests of victims and their families.
Thus, it retains the GCTA for offenders who demonstrate meaningful compliance
toward rehabilitation; and excludes persons convicted of heinous crimes.

        A credible correctional system must encourage reform without weakening
accountability. This vital piece of legislation shall ensure that sentence credits are
granted on the basis of demonstrable conduct rather than inconsistent standards. It
therefore reinforces the integrity of the correctional system, recognizes genuine
rehabilitation, and protects public confidence in the administration of criminal
justice.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
DEFINING GOOD CONDUCT AND PROVIDING CONDITIONS FOR THE
GRANT OF GOOD CONDUCT TIME ALLOWANCE, FURTHER AMENDING
FOR THE PURPOSE ARTICLE 97 OF ACT NO. 3815, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS
THE “REVISED PENAL CODE”, AS AMENDED BY REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10592

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Article 97 of Act No. 3815, otherwise known as the “Revised Penal
Code”, as amended by Republic Act No. 10592, is hereby further amended to read as
follows:

           “ART. 97. Allowance for good conduct. – GOOD CONDUCT
      REFERS TO THE CONSPICIOUS AND SATISFACTORY
      BEHAVIOR OF A DETAINEE OR CONVICTED PRISONER,
      CONSISTING OF ACTIVE INVOLVEMENT IN REHABILITATION
      PROGRAMS WHICH INCLUDE ADOPTION OF RESTORATIVE
      JUSTICE AMONG OTHERS, PRODUCTIVE PARTICIPATION IN
      AUTHORIZED WORK ACTIVITIES OR ACCOMPLISHMENT OF
      EXEMPLARY DEEDS, OWNERSHIP OF THE CRIME AND
      EARNEST EFFORTS TO SEEK FORGIVENESS FROM THE
      VICTIMS AND THEIR FAMILIES, AND                  EXEMPLARY
      COMPLIANCE WITH INSTITUTIONAL DISCIPLINARY RULES
      AND REGULATIONS.

           “IN THE EVALUATION AND CREDIT OF GOOD
      CONDUCT     TIME    ALLOWANCE,      OBJECTIVE   AND
      QUANTITATIVE CRITERIA SHALL BE FORMULATED BY THE
      SECRETARY OF JUSTICE WITHIN SIXTY (60) DAYS FROM THE
      APPROVAL OF THIS ACT. THE CRITERIA SHALL BE USED BY
      THE DIRECTOR OF THE BUREAU OF CORRECTIONS, THE
      CHIEF OF THE BUREAU OF JAIL MANAGEMENT AND
      PENOLOGY AND/OR THE WARDEN OF A PROVINCIAL,
      DISTRICT, MUNICIPAL OR CITY JAIL IN GRANTING

      ALLOWANCES FOR GOOD CONDUCT.

            “The good conduct of any offender qualified for credit for
      preventive imprisonment pursuant to Article 29 of this Code, or of any
      convicted prisoner in any penal institution, rehabilitation or detention
      center or any other local jail shall entitle him to the following
      deductions from the period of his sentence:

                “1. During the first two (2) years of imprisonment, he shall
             be allowed a deduction of twenty (20) days for each month of
             good behavior during detention;

                 “2. During the third to the fifth year, inclusive, of his
             imprisonment, he shall be allowed a deduction of twenty-three
             (23) days for each month of good behavior during detention;

                “3. During the following years until the tenth year, inclusive,
             of his imprisonment, he shall be allowed a deduction of
             twenty-five (25) days for each month of good behavior during
             detention;

                “4. During the eleventh and successive years of his
             imprisonment, he shall be allowed a deduction of thirty (30)
             days for each month of good behavior during detention; and

                “5. At any time during the period of imprisonment, he shall
             be allowed another deduction of fifteen (15) days, in addition to
             numbers one (1) to four (4) hereof, for each month of study,
             teaching or mentoring services time rendered.

             “An appeal by the accused shall not deprive him of entitlement
      to the above allowances for good conduct.”

       SEC. 2. Exemption. – Persons convicted of heinous crimes as defined in
Republic Act No. 7659, entitled “An Act to Impose the Death Penalty on Certain Heinous
Crimes, Amending for that Purpose the Revised Penal Laws, As Amended, Other Special
Penal Laws, And For Other Purposes”, as amended, are not entitled to the benefits of
this Act.

      SEC. 3. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Secretary of Justice shall,
within sixty (60) days from the approval of this Act, promulgate the necessary rules
and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

       SEC. 4. Separability Clause. – If any part, section, or provision of this Act is
held invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions not affected shall remain in full
force and effect.

      SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations or
