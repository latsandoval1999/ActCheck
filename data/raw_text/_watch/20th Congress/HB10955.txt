                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

The contiguous corridor comprising the City of Marikina and the Municipalities of San
Mateo and Rodriguez (Montalban) in the Province of Rizal serves as a critical strategic
gateway bridging Metro Manila and the Sierra Madre mountain range. This
interconnected geographic area shares vital natural resources, such as the Upper
Marikina River Basin, while facing common socio-economic, environmental, and
infrastructure challenges.

Despite rapid urban expansion, this contiguous territory possesses great potential for
sustainable economic growth through ecotourism, low-impact industrial development,
modern infrastructure integration, and unified watershed protection.

To attract catalytic investments, encourage green industry adoption, and ensure
sustainable development, this bill establishes the Marikina-San Mateo-Rodriguez
(MARISARO) Growth Area, creates the MARISARO Growth Area Development
Authority (MGADA), and provides a targeted framework of fiscal, non-fiscal, and
administrative incentives for eligible projects and enterprises.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly requested.

                            AN ACT
ESTABLISHING THE MARIKINA-SAN MATEO-RODRIGUEZ GROWTH AREA,
CREATING THE MARIKINA-SAN MATEO-RODRIGUEZ GROWTH AREA
DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY, PROVIDING INCENTIVES THEREFOR, DEFINING ITS
POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "MARISARO Growth Area
Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote
balanced, sustainable, and inclusive regional development. To this end, the State shall
foster inter-local government coordination to accelerate economic growth through:

      a) The development of ecotourism hubs;

      b) The promotion of environmentally sustainable industrial sectors;

      c) The convergence of resilient infrastructure networks;

      d) The perpetual protection and management of shared river basins and
      watersheds; and

      e) The provision of targeted fiscal and administrative incentives to registered
      enterprises operating within designated priority sectors.

SEC. 3. Coverage of the Growth Area. - The Marikina-San Mateo-Rodriguez Growth
Area, hereinafter referred to as the MARISARO Growth Area, shall cover the entire


                                           2
administrative territories of the City of Marikina, the Municipality of San Mateo, and the
Municipality of Rodriguez, Province of Rizal.

SEC. 4. Creation of the MARISARO Growth Area Development Authority. - There is
hereby created a body corporate to be known as the MARISARO Growth Area
Development Authority, hereinafter referred to as the Authority. The Authority shall be
attached to the Department of Economy, Planning and Development (DEPDev) for policy
coordination.

SEC. 5. Powers and Functions of the Authority. - The Authority shall exercise the
following powers and functions:

      a) Strategic Planning: Formulate and implement the MARISARO Integrated Master
      Development Plan, incorporating eco-zoning, land-use harmonization, and
      sustainable growth targets;

      b) Ecotourism Development: Identify, conserve, and develop ecotourism zones,
      biodiversity reserves, and heritage sites within the Growth Area;

      c) Sustainable Industrial Development: Delineate eco-industrial zones for low-
      emission, tech-driven, and green manufacturing activities;

      d) Infrastructure Integration: Plan, coordinate, and monitor inter-jurisdictional
      infrastructure projects, including flood management, mass transportation, road
      networks, and renewable energy integration.;

      e) Environmental Protection: Formulate and enforce strict environmental
      guidelines for the preservation of the Upper Marikina River Basin and surrounding
      ecosystems in coordination with the Department of Environment and Natural
      Resources (DENR);

      f) Incentive Administration: Process, review, and evaluate applications for local
      administrative benefits, fast-track processing, and qualification for national tax
      incentives under Title XIII of the National Internal Revenue Code, as amended.

SEC. 6. Board of Directors. - The corporate powers and functions of the Authority shall
be vested in a Board of Directors, composed of:

   1. Secretary of DEPDev (or representative), as Ex-Officio Chairman;
   2. City Mayor of Marikina, as Ex-Officio Member;
   3. Municipal Mayor of San Mateo, Rizal, as Ex-Officio Member;
   4. Municipal Mayor of Rodriguez, Rizal, as Ex-Officio Member;
   5. Governor of the Province of Rizal (or representative), as Ex-Officio Member;
   6. Secretary of DENR (or representative), as Ex-Officio Member; and
   7. Two (2) representatives from private sector/CSOs in the area, appointed by the
      President.

                                            3
SEC. 7. Executive Director. - The Authority shall be headed by an Executive Director
with the rank of Assistant Secretary, appointed by the President upon recommendation
of the Board, to manage day-to-day operations.

SEC. 8. Priority Sectors for Growth Area Incentives. - Enterprises registered with the
Authority may qualify for incentives if engaged in any of the following priority sectors:

      a) Ecotourism and Cultural Heritage: Eco-resorts, community-based nature trails,
      sustainable adventure facilities, and heritage conservation projects.

      b) Green and Eco-Industrial Enterprises: Zero-waste manufacturing, circular
      economy projects, electric vehicle (EV) assembly, recycling, and low-carbon tech
      industries.

      c) Sustainable Infrastructure and Utilities: Renewable energy generation, water
      purification, flood prevention systems, integrated mass transport, and green
      building developments.

      d) Watershed and Environmental Services: Reforestation, river rehabilitation, and
      waste management services operating within the Upper Marikina River Basin.

SEC. 9. Applicable Incentives. – Registered enterprises can avail of the following
national and local tax and other incentives:

      (a) National Tax Incentives

      Registered enterprises operating within the Growth Area that meet the Strategic
      Investment Priority Plan (SIPP) criteria under Title XIII of the National Internal
      Revenue Code (Corporate Recovery and Tax Incentives for Enterprises Act /
      CREATE Law), as amended, shall be eligible to apply for:

          1. Income Tax Holidays (ITH);
          2. Enhanced Deductions or Special Corporate Income Tax (SCIT) rates;
          3. Duty exemptions on imported capital equipment and raw materials used
             directly in registered activities; and
          4. Value-Added Tax (VAT) zero-rating on local purchases directly linked to
             registered activities.

      (b) Local Tax and Fee Discounts

      Subject to the approval of their respective Sanggunians, the constituent LGUs
      (Marikina, San Mateo, Rodriguez) may grant registered enterprises:

          1. Local Business Tax (LBT) Relief: A discount of up to fifty percent (50%) on
             LBT for the first five (5) years of operations;

          2. Real Property Tax (RPT) Relief: Exemption or reduction on RPT levied on
             newly constructed eco-industrial facilities or ecotourism infrastructure for up
             to three (3) years.

       (c) Green Infrastructure Incentive

       Enterprises that incorporate verified green building designs, rainwater harvesting
       systems, or renewable power generation (such as behind-the-meter solar
       systems) shall receive an additional 10% rebate on local permit fees and priority
       processing.

       (d) Administrative Incentives and One-Stop Action Center

       The Authority shall establish a One-Stop Action Center (OSAC) to facilitate and
       streamline the issuance of all local and national permits, licenses, and clearances
       for registered enterprises within thirty (30) days from submission of complete
       requirements.

SEC. 10. Inter-LGU Cooperation and Local Autonomy. - Nothing in this Act shall
diminish the constitutional autonomy of constituent LGUs. The Authority shall work in
close consultation with the local Sanggunians to align local comprehensive land use plans
(CLUPs) and local incentive codes with the MARISARO Master Plan.

SEC. 11. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this
Act shall be charged against the current year’s unallocated funds of the National
Government. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary, shall be included in the annual
General Appropriations Act (GAA).

SEC. 12. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). - Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, DEPDev, in consultation with the Department of Finance (DOF),
Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), DENR, Department of Tourism (DOT), and the
constituent LGUs, shall issue the necessary rules and regulations.

SEC. 13. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 14. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, proclamations, issuances,
rules, and regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SEC. 15. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
