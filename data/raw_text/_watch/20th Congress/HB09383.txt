                             EXPLANATORY NOTE


       The 1987 Philippine Constitution mandates the State to evolve a progressive
system of taxation. In 2018, Republic Act No. 10963, or the Tax Reform for
Acceleration and Inclusion (TRAIN) Law, took a monumental step towards this goal
by restructuring the personal income tax brackets and establishing a PHP 250,000
annual tax exemption threshold. This effectively shielded minimum wage earners
and the lower-middle class from the burden of income taxation.

      However, since the passage of the TRAIN Law, the Philippine economy has
experienced compounding inflation, significantly eroding the purchasing power of
the peso. The Consumer Price Index (CPI) has consistently risen, meaning that the
PHP 250,000 threshold established eight years ago no longer holds the same real-
world value today.

       We are once again facing the threat of "bracket creep"—a phenomenon where
workers receive nominal wage increases simply to keep up with the cost of living,
only to be pushed into higher tax brackets without any real increase in their wealth
or purchasing power.

       This bill seeks to immediately correct this injustice by increasing the base
personal income tax exemption from PHP 250,000 to PHP 350,000, mathematically
adjusting for the cumulative inflation from 2018 to 2026. This adjustment will
instantly restore the intended financial relief for Filipino workers, giving them
greater take-home pay to afford basic necessities.

       Furthermore, to prevent the recurrence of "bracket creep" and to avoid the
need for Congress to pass continuous remedial tax legislation, this bill
institutionalizes an automatic indexation mechanism. By mandating the Department
of Finance to adjust the income tax brackets every three (3) years based on the
prevailing Consumer Price Index, we ensure that our tax system remains
permanently fair, equitable, and economically sound.

       In view of the foregoing, immediate passage of the bills is earnestly sought
for.

  AN ACT INCREASING THE BASE EXEMPTION FOR PERSONAL INCOME
   TAX, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 24 OF THE NATIONAL
  INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED, AND INSTITUTING
   AUTOMATIC INDEXATION BASED ON THE CONSUMER PRICE INDEX

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the Future-Proofed
Adjustment of Income Rates (FAIR) Tax Act.

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the declared policy of the State to protect the
purchasing power of the working class and promote a genuinely progressive and
equitable tax system. Recognizing the erosive effect of inflation on real income, the
State shall mandate the adjustment of the personal income tax exemptions based on
the prevailing Consumer Price Index (CPI) to correct "bracket creep" and ensure that
minimum wage earners and the middle class are fairly taxed.
