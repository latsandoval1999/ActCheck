                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to require government officials and employees to execute a
written waiver authorizing lawful examination of their bank deposits and
investments, in connection with duly verified investigations. Grounded on the
principle that public office is a public trust, it also seeks to strengthen standards of
integrity in government, and to reinforce the obligation of public servants to uphold
openness in their financial dealings.

        The measure is consistent with Republic Act No. 3019, otherwise known as
the “Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act”, which seeks to deter graft or corrupt
practices committed by public officers and private persons alike. It likewise
addresses the legal barriers that may hinder the investigation of unexplained wealth,
while strengthening the accountability mechanisms available to the Office of the
Ombudsman, subject to appropriate safeguards ensuring confidentiality, due
process, and the protection of individual rights. It further recognizes that the
effective deterrence of corruption requires not only punitive enforcement, but also
structural reforms that institutionalize transparency and reinforce the integrity and
capacity of public institutions.

      Ultimately, the bill advances a meaningful reform that helps foster ethical
governance, prevent abuse of public office, and restore public confidence in
government. This measure offers a concrete and principled step toward cleaner,
more trustworthy governance especially at a time of declining public trust in
government officials, public institutions, and the Philippine government as a whole.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
MANDATING GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS AND EMPLOYEES TO EXECUTE
AND SUBMIT A WRITTEN PERMISSION TO EXAMINE, INQUIRE OR LOOK
INTO ALL THEIR DEPOSITS AND INVESTMENTS THEREBY WAIVING THE
BANK SECRECY LAW AS PROVIDED FOR UNDER REPUBLIC ACT
NUMBERS 1405 AND 6426

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

        Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bank Secrecy Waiver
Act”.

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
promote transparency, accountability, and integrity in public service. All public
officials and employees shall be open and transparent in all their financial
transactions and dealings.

      SEC. 3. Coverage. – This Act shall apply to all public officials and employees as
defined under Section 3(a) and (b) of Republic Act No. 6713, otherwise known as the
“Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees”, except those
who serve in an honorary capacity.

       SEC. 4. Submission of Written Waiver. – All public officials and employees
covered by this Act shall, within thirty (30) days from assumption of office, submit
to the Office of the Ombudsman a written waiver expressly authorizing the
Ombudsman to examine, inquire into, and look into all their deposits and
investments, including foreign currency deposits, in banks and other financial
institutions, both within and outside the Philippines, as well as investment bonds
issued by the government of the Philippines, its political subdivisions, and
instrumentalities.

        Incumbent public officials and employees shall submit their written waiver
within thirty (30) days from the effectivity of the implementing rules and regulations
of this Act.


                                            1
       SEC. 5. Failure or Refusal to Submit Bank Secrecy Waiver. – Any public official or
employee who fails or refuses to submit the required written waiver as mandated by
this Act shall not be allowed to enter upon, or continue exercising the functions of,
his or her office, and shall be subject to administrative and/or criminal liability as
may be provided by law.

        SEC. 6. Use and Confidentiality of Information. – The written waiver and any
information or data obtained by virtue thereof shall be exclusively used by the
Office of the Ombudsman for the purpose of investigating a duly verified complaint
or prosecuting a case before the courts. The Ombudsman shall ensure the
confidentiality of such information, except as may be necessary for the performance
of its official functions.

       SEC. 7. Lead Agency. – The Office of the Ombudsman shall have the primary
responsibility to implement this Act: Provided, That it shall enlist the advice of the
Department of Justice and the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas in the drafting of the
written permission or waiver as provided in this Act.

       SEC. 8. Penalties. – Any person who unlawfully discloses or misused
information obtained under this Act shall be penalized in accordance with existing
laws, without prejudice to administrative or criminal liability.

      SEC. 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Office of the Ombudsman
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective
implementation of this Act within ninety (90) days from its effectivity.

       SEC. 10. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
force and effect.

       SEC. 11. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
