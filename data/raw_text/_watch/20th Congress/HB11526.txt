20                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   There are moments in a young person's life that mark the beginning of something new.
23   Graduation is one of them. It is the day a student closes one chapter of life and begins
24   to build a future of his or her own.
25
26   But while graduation marks the beginning of a new chapter for the student, it should
27   also mark the beginning of something new for the country.
28
29   This proposed Graduation Legacy for Reforestation Act seeks to make tree planting a
30   meaningful part of a student’s life. By requiring every graduating senior high school and
31   college student to plant two trees, graduation becomes not only the beginning of a new
32   chapter in a student's life, but also the beginning of a new life in the form of a tree. Years
33   from now, the graduate may no longer remember the exact details of the ceremony, but
34   the tree can remain as a living reminder of the day he or she began a new journey.
35
36   More than a graduation requirement, this measure seeks to give every graduating
37   Filipino an opportunity to leave a living legacy.
38
39   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

20                                AN ACT
21       REQUIRING ALL GRADUATING SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL AND COLLEGE
22        STUDENTS TO PLANT TWO (2) TREES EACH AS A CIVIC DUTY FOR
23     ENVIRONMENT PROTECTION AND PRESERVATION, AND APPROPRIATING
24                           FUNDS THEREFOR
25
26
27   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
28   Congress assembled:

29        SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Graduation Legacy
30   for Reforestation Act.”

31         SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the declared policy of the State to
32   protect and advance the right of the Filipino people to a balanced and healthful
33   ecology in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature. Towards this end, the State
34   shall promote and pursue reforestation programs and encourage tree planting as a
35   means of involving the youth in public and civic affairs.

36        SECTION 3. Tree Planting as a Mandatory Requisite for Graduation. - As an
37   act of public service before they join the country’s labor force, all graduating
38   students in senior high school and college, except physically handicapped and
39   students with special needs, shall plant two (2) trees each as a prerequisite for
40   graduation.

41        SECTION 4. Tree Planting Sites – The Department of Environment and Natural
42   Resources (DENR) and the concerned local government units (LGUs) shall
43   determine the appropriate tree planting sites within the LGU territories, in
44   coordination with schools, colleges and universities.

45         SECTION 5. Preference for Indigenous Species. - The species of trees to
46   be planted should be appropriate to the location, climate and topography of the area,
47   with preference for the planting of indigenous species.

48         SECTION 6. Partner Agencies and Stakeholders and their responsibilities. The
 1   Department of Education (DepEd) and the Commission on Higher Education (CHED)
 2   shall implement the provisions of this Act, in partnership with the following
 3   agencies:

 4        (a) DENR;

 5        (b) Department of Agriculture (DA);

 6        (c) Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG);

 7        (e) Department of Science and Technology (DOST); and

 8        (f) National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP).

 9        The responsibilities of these agencies shall include:

10        (1) Establishment of nursery and seedling preparation;

11        (2) Site identification and preparation;

12        (3) Technical support and extension services;

13        (4) Monitoring and evaluation.

14        SECTION 7. Provision of Seedlings. – The Community Environment and
15   Natural Resources Office (CENRO) of the DENR and the DA, in partnership with LGUs
16   throughout the Philippines shall maintain a seedling bank that will serve as a ready
17   source of seedlings to be used in the implementation of this Act.

18         SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations – Within sixty (60) days
19   after the effectivity of this Act, the DepEd, and CHED shall, in coordination with DENR
20   and DILG, and in consultation with other agencies, promulgate and issue the
21   necessary rules and regulations to implement this Act.

22        SECTION         9.     Appropriations.   -      The amount          necessary
23   for   the   initial implementation      of    this Act     shall be      charged
24   against     the      current      year’s appropriations of the departments and
25   agencies concerned. Thereafter, the funding of which shall be included in the annual
26   General Appropriations Act. The LGU concerned may set aside funds from any local
27   revenue an amount deemed appropriate for the implementation of this Act.

28       SECTION 10. Separability Clause. - Should any portion or provision of this Act
29   be declared unconstitutional, the remainder thereof not otherwise affected shall
30   remain in full force and effect.

31         SECTION 11. Repealing Clause. -            All   laws,  presidential    decrees,
32   executive orders, proclamations or administrative regulations that are inconsistent
33   with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

34        SECTION 12. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
35   publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
