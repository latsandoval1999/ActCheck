EXPLANATORY NOTE

Schools are meant to be safe spaces where children can learn, develop, and thrive.
However, the growing threats of bullying, violence, abuse, mental health concerns,
cyberbullying, and other emerging digital risks, compounded by natural disasters and security-
related incidents, underscore the urgent need for a comprehensive and coordinated approach to

school safety.

While existing laws and policies provide safeguards against specific threats, there
remains a need to institutionalize an integrated framework that addresses the physical, mental,
emotional, psychosocial, and digital safety and well-being of learners and school personnel.
School safety must go beyond responding to incidents and instead emphasize prevention,

preparedness, timely intervention, and recovery.

This proposed Comprehensive School Safety Act seeks to establish a National
Comprehensive School Safety Framework covering all public and private basic education
institutions. It mandates the adoption of School Safety Plans, appropriate security and
emergency preparedness measures, strengthened anti-bullying and child protection
mechanisms, mental health and psychosocial support services, responsible technology use, and

effective incident reporting, response, and recovery systems.

CHAPTER II. SCHOOL SAFETY POLICIES

SEC. 7. Policy on Safe School Culture. — Every school shall foster a safe, inclusive,
respectful, and supportive school culture that cultivates socioemotional learning, empathy, peer
connectedness, and the preservation of human dignity, in accordance with applicable DepEd
and DOH standards on the social and physical environment of schools. Schools shall promote
positive relationships, mutual respect, responsibility, resilience, and meaningful participation
among all members of the school community through preventive, developmentally appropriate,
and evidence-based policies, programs, and practices that strengthen positive behavior,
respectful conflict resolution, and a shared commitment to maintaining a safe learning

environment.

SEC. 8. Policy Against Violence and Harm. ~ Every school shall adopt and enforce a
policy against all forms of violence and harm, including physical violence, child abuse,
exploitation, bullying, discrimination, harassment, sexual and gender-based violence, corporal
punishment, retaliation, and other acts that threaten the safety, dignity, or well-being of learners
and school personnel, whether occurring in person or through digital or other means. Violence
or harmful conduct perpetrated by school personnel against learners shall be subject to
applicable child protection laws and appropriate administrative, disciplinary, and legal
measures. For incidents involving learners, schools shall ensure that violence and harmful
conduct are promptly addressed and shall implement interventions that are fair, proportionate,
developmentally appropriate, trauma-informed, and restorative, where appropriate, with due

regard to the safety and rights of all persons involved.

SEC. 9. Anti-Bullying and Learner Protection Policy. — Pursuant to Republic Act No.
10627, or the Anti-Bullying Act of 2013, every school shall adopt and implement a
comprehensive Anti-Bullying and Learner Protection Policy that promotes the safety, dignity,

SEC. 10. School Code of Conduct. — The School Safety Handbook shall include a
School Code of Conduct that promotes a culture of respect, responsibility, integrity, inclusivity,
and accountability among members of the school community. The Code shall clearly define
unacceptable acts, corresponding disciplinary and restorative interventions, and the rights and
responsibilities of all members of the school community. In implementing the Code, schools
shall ensure that disciplinary measures are fair, proportionate, developmentally appropriate,
non-discriminatory, and restorative in nature, with due regard for the best interests of the child,

due process, and the rights of school personnel.

SEC. 11. Learner Participation and Voice. — Every school shall recognize and promote
the right of learners to participate meaningfully in matters affecting their safety, well-being,

and school life, consistent with their evolving capacities and the best interests of the child.

Schools shall establish age-appropriate, inclusive, and learner-responsive mechanisms
through which learners may express their views, report safety concerns, contribute to school
safety initiatives, and participate in the development, implementation, monitoring, and
evaluation of school safety policies and programs. Schools shall provide opportunities for the
student council and other recognized learner organizations to participate in school safety

initiatives and such processes.

Learner participation shall be safe, respectful, and free from discrimination, retaliation,

or intimidation.

school safety governance system to ensure the effective implementation of this Act and to

Every school shall organize or designate an appropriate school safety governance
mechanism composed of school personnel responsible for learner protection, counseling and
psychosocial support, school safety and security, health services, disaster risk reduction and

management, and other functions as may be prescribed by the DepEd.

existing school personnel.

CHAPTER IL. Safety During Off-Campus Activities

SEC, 22. Safety During Off-Campus Activities. — Schools conducting authorized off-
campus activities shall adopt and implement measures to ensure the safety, security, health,
and well-being of learners and school personnel participating in such activities. Schools shall
take reasonable and appropriate measures to prevent foreseeable harm to learners and school

personnel throughout the activity.

Prior to any authorized off-campus activity, the school shall conduct an appropriate risk
assessment and prepare an activity-specific safety plan, which shall include learner supervision
arrangements and designated personnel-in-charge, transportation safety measures, emergency
communication and response procedures, emergency contact information for participating

learners, and such other safety measures as may be prescribed by DepEd.

Schools shall also ensure full disclosure to parents or guardians of information
regarding the activity, including the objectives of the itinerary, transport arrangements,
accommodations, identified risks, emergency procedures, and contact information of
responsible personnel. Schools shall likewise ensure that reasonable safety equipment is made
available during the activity, and that appropriate informed consent is secured in accordance

with applicable laws and DepEd policies.
The DepEd shall prescribe minimum operational standards governing educational

tours, work immersion, athletic competitions, cultural activities, community engagement

activities, and other authorized off-campus activities.

CHAPTER III. Digital Safety and Responsible Technology Use

SEC. 23. Digital Safety and Responsible Technology Use. — Schools shall adopt and
implement comprehensive policies and programs to ensure that the use of digital technologies
in teaching, learning, communication, and school operations is safe, responsible, ethical, age-

appropriate, and consistent with the best interests and developmental needs of learners.

Such policies and programs shall promote digital wellness and responsible technology
use, including developmentally appropriate use of digital devices, social media, gaming,

artificial intelligence, and other emerging technologies.

Schools shall establish measures to protect learners and school personnel from
cyberbullying, online abuse, cybersecurity risks, and other forms of digital harm. Such
measures shall include, as appropriate, the prevention and early identification of digital-related
harm and harmful or unsafe technology use, appropriate intervention and referral mechanisms,
media and information literacy programs, orientation programs for parents and guardians,
procedures for reporting and responding to digital safety incidents, and other digital safety
measures as may be prescribed by the DepEd.

Schools shall prohibit the use of mobile devices and other electronic gadgets by learners
within school premises during class hours, except for learning-related activities, health and
well-being-related activities, and purposes related to safety, emergency response, or risk
management, including in response to a perceived threat or danger, and during field trips and

other school-related activities conducted outside school premises.

Implementation of this Section shall be consistent with applicable laws on child

protection, data privacy, cybersecurity, and online safety.

SEC. 36. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part hereof is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the remainder of the law or the provision not otherwise affected shall remain

valid and subsisting.

SEC. 37. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations or other
issuances or parts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or modified

accordingly.
SEC, 38. Effectivity. — Notwithstanding the non-issuance of the implementing rules
and regulations, this Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its publication in the

Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
