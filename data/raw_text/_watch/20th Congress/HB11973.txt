                                 EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to grant full tuition scholarships for qualified public elementary
and high school teachers in the country who are pursuing, or planning to pursue their
master’s or doctoral degrees in state universities and colleges (SUCs) nationwide. It
seeks to cover the full cost of tuition and requires grantees to render four (4) years of
service in the Department of Education (DepEd) upon completion of each program.

        Postgraduate education sharpens teachers’ content mastery and teaching
practice, and it is increasingly required for promotion and career progression. Yet the
cost of graduate study falls largely on teachers themselves. Many shoulder tuition out of
modest salaries or through loans, while existing scholarship opportunities remain
limited in number and reach. As a result, many deserving teachers delay or give up
further studies, to the detriment of both their professional growth and the quality of
instruction in public schools.

        This measure establishes clear eligibility requirements to ensure that scholarship
grants benefit qualified and deserving teachers. Applicants must meet the prescribed
service, employment, academic, performance, and character standards, as well as the
admission requirements of their chosen SUC. The program shall cover the full cost of
tuition, while the four (4)-year service obligation ensures that public schools and their
learners benefit from the knowledge and competencies acquired by scholarship
recipients. Grantees who fail to fulfill their obligation shall be required to reimburse the
government in accordance with the terms prescribed herein.

        Investing in teachers is an investment in the quality of Philippine education.
Providing qualified educators with greater access to advanced studies strengthens their
capacity to teach and helps ensure that Filipino learners receive better instruction. The
proposed scholarship program therefore advances both teacher development and the
State’s commitment to quality education.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
GRANTING PUBLIC ELEMENTARY AND HIGH SCHOOL TEACHERS
SCHOLARSHIP FOR GRADUATE AND POST-GRADUATE STUDIES IN
LOCAL AND STATE UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES IN THE COUNTRY

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Public School Educators
Scholarship Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
give priority to education to foster patriotism and nationalism, accelerate social
progress and promote total human liberation and development. Toward this end,
the State shall enhance the right of teachers to professional development through
making the availment of further studies more accessible.

       SEC. 3. Coverage. – Public school teachers in elementary and high school levels
shall be eligible to avail of the scholarship grants for master’s degree or a doctorate
degree.

        SEC. 4. Scope of the Scholarship Grant. – The scholarship grant shall cover the
full cost of tuition fee for postgraduate education.

      SEC. 5. Qualifications. – In order to qualify for the scholarship program, the
teacher applicant must have the following qualifications:

          a. ﻿Public elementary and high school teachers who are in active and
       continuous service for at least three (3) years;​

          b. ﻿Must hold a permanent position;

          c. ﻿Must have no pending administrative and/or criminal case;
          d.​ ﻿Must have obtained at least a Very Satisfactory performance rating for
       two (2) consecutive periods immediately preceding the nomination;​

          e.​ Physically and mentally fit;​

          f.​ ﻿Must pass the Graduate Mental Ability Test (GMAT) of the state
       universities and colleges (SUCs) of his/her choice;​

          g.​ ﻿Must have an average of at least 2.25, or its equivalent, in his/her
       college transcript of records;​

          h.​ ﻿Must have good character and demonstrated qualities of leadership
       and positive attitudes in keeping with the mission and vision of the
       Department of Education (DepEd);​

          i.​ ﻿Such other qualifications as the DepEd may determine including, but
       not limited to, regular qualifying examinations.

      SEC. 6. Place of Study. – The scholarship grants shall be applicable in all SUCs
throughout the country.

       SEC. 7. Service Obligation. – Immediately upon completion of each graduate
and post-graduate program, a grantee shall serve the DepEd for at least four (4)
years.

       For this purpose, the grantee shall execute a contract with the DepEd
incorporating the details of the said service. Any grantee who violates the service
obligation imposed under this Act shall be liable to reimburse the government on
the fund assistance received under this Act in full or pro tanto as the case may be.

      SEC. 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from the
approval of this Act, DepEd and the Commission on Higher Education shall
promulgate the rules and regulations necessary to implement the provisions of this
Act.

        SEC. 9. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
