                        AN ACT
 PERIODICALLY INCREASING THE HONORARIUM FOR TEACHER-
POLL WATCHERS AND EXEMPTING FROM INCOME TAXATION THE
HONORARIA, ALLOWANCES, AND OTHER FINANCIAL BENEFITS OF
TEACHERS UPON RENDERING SERVICE EVERY ELECTION PERIOD


                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill proposes a periodic increase of honoraria granted to teachers serving during
elections and the exemption from income taxation of all honoraria, allowances, and
other financial benefits they receive upon delivery of their electoral duties.

Every election period, thousands of public school teachers render their service as
poll workers. Their role is indispensable to ensure clean, honest, orderly, and
credible elections. In performing these duties, some teachers endure long hours of
travel, hazardous conditions, and, in some instances, threats of violence in conflict
areas. Many even traverse long distances across rivers, forests, and remote areas to
safeguard the integrity of the electoral process.

For their invaluable service, teachers receive honoraria, allowances, and other
financial benefits. However, these amounts are often modest and not fully
commensurate with the effort they put to ensure that ballots are transported to polling

 precincts untampered. Worse, such compensation remains subject to income
 taxation, further reducing the already limited financial benefits accorded to them.

 Through this proposed measure, teacher-poll workers are justly compensated and
 allowed to fully enjoy the benefits of their labor in recognition of their dedication
 and sacrifice in protecting electoral processes and, in extension, preserving
 democracy.

 In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Poll Workers Protection
and Welfare Act.”



SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – Article II, Section 18 of the 1987 Philippine
Constitution stipulates the State’s policy to “protect the rights of workers and
promote their welfare.” Moreover, under the Magna Carta for Public School


                                                                                1
Teachers, it is the State’s policy “to promote and uplift the economic status of public
school teachers.” Thus, all honoraria, allowances, and financial benefits that public
school teachers receive as compensation for being poll watchers during national and
local elections shall be exempted from income tax, in recognition of the public
service they render during such time. The compensation for their service shall
likewise periodically increase upon every period of elections.



SECTION 3. Exemption from Income Tax. – All honoraria, allowances, and other
financial benefits granted to teachers rendering service during an election period
shall not be included in the computation of gross income and shall be exempt from
income tax.



SECTION 4. Periodic Increase in Honorarium. – The price of honorarium
received by teachers serving as poll workers shall increase upon every election
period by no less than Five hundred pesos (₱500.00), subject to the discretion of the
Commission on Elections (COMELEC) and in consideration of the consumer price
index at the time of elections.



SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Commissioners of the
COMELEC and Bureau of Internal Revenue shall promulgate such rules and
regulations as may be necessary to implement the provisions of this Act within sixty
(60) days from its effectivity.

SECTION 6. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall remain in full force and
effect.



SECTION 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive issuances, rules and
regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed and/or modified
accordingly.

SECTION 8. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its
complete publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
