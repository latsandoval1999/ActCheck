                          AN ACT
ESTABLISHING    THE   NATIONAL    AUTISM   SUPPORT    AND
DEVELOPMENT CENTER, CREATING REGIONAL AUTISM RESOURCE
CENTERS, STRENGTHENING EARLY INTERVENTION, HEALTH,
EDUCATIONAL, THERAPEUTIC, TRANSITION, AND FAMILY SUPPORT
SERVICES FOR PERSONS WITH AUTISM, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “National Autism
Support and Development Center Act”.

       SEC 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the state to
protect and promote the rights and welfare of persons with autism and persons
with other developmental disabilities, recognizing their dignity, autonomy, and
full participation in society.

       Consistent with Article II, Section 11 and Article XIII on social justice and
human development of the Constitution, Republic Act (RA) No. 7277 otherwise
known as the “Magna Carta for Disabled Persons”, as amended, RA 11650 or the
“Inclusive Education Act”, and the United Nations Convention on the Rights of
Persons with Disabilities, the State shall ensure accessible, inclusive, and
responsive, and person-centered systems of support, including early identification
and intervention, health and habilitative services, inclusive education, family
support, skills development, independent living support, and pathways toward
social and economic participation.

      SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

         a. Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) refers to a neurodevelopmental
      condition characterized by differences in communication, social interaction,
      sensory processing, and patterns of behavior, with varying support needs.

          b. Developmental disability refers to a severe and chronic condition
      attributable to mental and/or physical impairments manifested during the
      developmental period that may substantially affect independent
      functioning and may require lifelong or extended support;

         c. Early Intervention Services refer to developmental, therapeutic,
      educational, family support, and related services designed to address
      developmental needs of infants and young children with autism or
      developmental                                              disabilities;

         d. Habilitative Services refer to services intended to help persons with
      autism attain, maintain, or improve functional, adaptive, social,
      educational,               or                vocational               skills;

         e. Family Support Services refer to counseling, parent education,
      caregiver training, respite support, referral assistance, and related services
      designed           to          strengthen            family          capacity;

         f. Indigent Person with Autism refers to a person with autism whose
      family income falls below the poverty threshold as determined by the
      Philippine Statistics Authority or those identified as poor by the National
      Household Targeting System for Poverty Reduction (NHTS-PR) of the
      DSWD;

         g. Regional Autism Resource Center refers to a regional service and
      support center established or designated under this Act;

        h. Person-Centered Care refers to services designed and delivered in a
      manner responsive to the needs, preferences, rights, and goals of the

      individual.

         i. Supported Employment refers to individualized assistance,
      workplace accommodations, job coaching, and related services enabling
      persons with autism to obtain, maintain, and advance in competitive or
      appropriate employment.

      SEC. 4. Establishment of the National Autism Support and Developmental Center.
– There is hereby established under the Department of Health (DOH) a specialized
national center to be known as the National Autism Support and Development
Center (NASDC) which shall serve as the principal coordinating, technical, and
specialized support institution for programs and services under this Act.

       The NASDC shall operate in coordination with the Department of
Education (DepEd), Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD),
Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), National Council on Disability
Affairs (NCDA), local government units (LGUs) and relevant public and private
stakeholders.

       SEC. 5. Functions of the NASDC. – The NASDC shall have the following
functions:

         a. Formulate and implement a comprehensive national autism support
      and                     development                        framework;

         b. Develop standards, protocols, and evidence-based guidelines for
      screening, assessment, intervention, habilitation, referral, and support
      services;

         c. Establish and operate diagnostic, therapeutic, developmental, and
      support                        service                       programs;

          d. Provide, directly or through coordinated referral networks,
      subsidized or free diagnostic, therapeutic, habilitative, and support services
      for           indigent             persons             with            autism;

          e. Develop and support early identification and intervention programs
      integrated into primary health care and community-based systems;

         f. Provide training and continuing capability-building programs for
      teachers, therapists, physicians, allied professionals, social workers,

      caregivers,              and               community                workers;

         g. Support inclusive education transitions, independent living
      preparation, and employment pathways for adolescents and adults with
      autism;

         h. Provide family support services, counseling, caregiver education,
      and                        referral                          assistance;

         i. Conduct or support research, innovation, and pilot programs related
      to     autism     supports      and      developmental        disabilities;

         j. Maintain, subject to data privacy laws and ethical safeguards, a
      national autism information and research registry for policy and planning
      purposes;

         k. Undertake      public    awareness    and   anti-stigma    campaigns;

         l. Provide technical assistance to regional centers and LGUs; and

         m. Perform such other related functions necessary to carry out the
      purposes of this Act.

         n. Develop standards and model programs for transition-to-adulthood
      and supported employment services;

         o. Provide technical support for implementation of inclusive education
      and community-based habilitation models;

         p. Develop a national monitoring and evaluation framework for
      autism-related programs and services.

        SEC. 6. Regional Autism Resource Centers. – DOH, in coordination with
relevant agencies, shall establish or designate Regional Autism Resource Centers
in all administrative regions, subject to phased implementation and availability of
funds.

      These centers shall serve as hubs for:

         1. diagnostic               and             referral             services;

      SEC. 7. National Autism Advisory Council. – There is hereby created a
National Autism Advisory Council to provide policy advice, inter-agency
coordination, and stakeholder participation in the implementation of this Act.

      The Council shall be composed of:
        1. One (1) representative from the DOH, who shall sit as Chairperson;

        2. One (1) representative from the DepEd who shall sit as the Vice
      Chairperson;

         3. One      (1)   representative         from     the   DSWD       as    Member;

         4. One      (1)   representative         from     the    DOLE      as    Member;

         5. Executive       Director         of     the      NCDAs,        as     Member;

        6. Two (2) representatives from the Autism Society of the Philippines as
      Members;

        7. Two (2) representatives from the Parents of Children and Adults with
      Autism                 as                 Members;                   and

         8. One (1) representative from the allied therapy professions.

      Representatives from the private sector and civil society shall serve for a
term of three (3) years, renewable once.

     The Council shall meet at least quarterly and may constitute technical
working groups as necessary.
The Council shall assist in policy coordination, implementation review, and
monitoring of this Act.

      SEC. 8. Executive Director and Secretariat. – The NASDC shall be headed by
an Executive Director appointed in accordance with civil service laws, rules, and
merit-based standards.

       No person shall be appointed Executive Director unless such person
possesses recognized competence and at least five (5) years of relevant experience
in developmental disability, public health, education, rehabilitation, social
services, or related fields.

      The                  Executive                   Director                    shall:

          a. Administer and implement the programs and policies of the NASDC;

          b. Supervise               personnel              and            operations;

          c. Prepare     strategic    plans,     budgets,    and     annual      reports;

         d. Coordinate     with government         agencies,      LGUs,   and    partner
      institutions;

          e. Oversee      implementation          of    regional      centers;       and

          f. Exercise such other powers necessary to carry out the purposes of
      this Act.

      A Secretariat and such technical and administrative personnel as may be
necessary shall support the NASDC.

       SEC. 9. Early Identification and Intervention. – DOH, through primary health
care facilities, government hospitals, and community health systems, shall
strengthen developmental surveillance, screening, referral pathways, and timely
intervention services for infants and children at risk of developmental disabilities.
Protocols developed under this section shall, where feasible, be integrated into
existing child health and primary care systems.

       SEC. 10. Inclusive Education and Transition Support. – DepEd, in coordination
with the NASDC and relevant agencies, shall strengthen educational support,
transition planning, and appropriate accommodations for learners with autism
consistent with existing inclusive education laws and policies.



                                          5
Programs supporting post-secondary transition, vocational preparation, and
supported employment pathways, and adult life planning may likewise be
developed in coordination with appropriate agencies.

       SEC. 11. Local Government Participation. – LGUs shall endeavor, consistent
with local autonomy and available resources, to establish or support local autism
programs, resource hubs, and community-based services consistent with this Act.
The NASDC shall provide technical assistance to LGUs to integrate autism-specific
services into their respective Persons with Disability Affairs Offices.

      SEC. 12. Employment and Adult Support Services. – DOLE, DSWD, the
Technical Education And Skills Development Authority, and other relevant
agencies, in coordination with the NASDC, shall support the development of
programs promoting skills development, supported employment, livelihood
opportunities, and independent living assistance for adolescents and adults with
autism.

      SEC. 13. Coordination with Existing Laws and Programs. – Implementation of
this Act shall be harmonized, where applicable, with RA 7277, as amended; RA
11650; RA 11228; RA 11036 where relevant; RA 7610, where child protection
concerns arise; and other related laws, policies, and issuances on disability rights,
health, education, and social protection.

      SEC. 14. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial
implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current appropriations of
the DOH. Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued operation shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       The NASDC may receive grants, donations, endowments, and other lawful
contributions from domestic or foreign sources, subject to existing auditing,
budgeting, and accountability laws.

       SEC. 15. Transitory Provisions. – Within the period of one hundred eighty
(180) days after the approval of this Act, the Advisory Council shall be organized
and the initial implementation framework shall be adopted.

       Existing mandates of agencies shall remain, and functions under this Act
shall be exercised in coordination therewith.

      SEC. 16. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within ninety (90) days
from effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health, in coordination with the

      SEC. 17. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations
and other issuances, or parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act,
are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

       SEC. 18. Separability Clause. – Should any provision of this Act be declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions of this Act which are not affected
hereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

       SEC. 19. Effectivity. – This act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in any two (2) newspapers of general
circulation in the Philippines.
