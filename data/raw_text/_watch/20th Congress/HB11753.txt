                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The Constitution recognizes the vital role of education in nation-building
and mandates the State to protect and promote the right of all citizens to quality
education at all levels. State universities and colleges give concrete expression to
this mandate by bringing affordable and responsive higher education closer to
communities, particularly to students whose circumstances would otherwise limit
their access to tertiary education.
      President Ramon Magsaysay State University (PRMSU) has long served
as a principal center of higher learning, research, innovation, and extension in the
Province of Zambales and Central Luzon. Since its establishment and subsequent
conversion and renaming under Republic Act Nos. 8498 and 11015, the
University has expanded its reach and academic responsibilities in response to
the changing educational, economic, and development needs of the Province, the
region, and the country.

      Notwithstanding this growth, the University’s existing charter no longer
fully reflects the scope of its present operations. In particular, the PRMSU
campuses in the Municipalities of Santa Cruz, Masinloc, Botolan, and Castillejos
require express statutory recognition and integration to place them on a firm
institutional footing and ensure their sustained development. Formal recognition
will enable these campuses to receive appropriate funding, infrastructure, faculty
and non-teaching personnel, and other resources necessary to deliver quality
instruction, research, extension, and community services.

      This bill therefore seeks to strengthen and modernize the charter of
PRMSU by recognizing the satellite campuses in Santa Cruz, Masinloc, Botolan,
and Castillejos as constituent campuses of the University, while affirming the
continued operation of its existing campuses in Iba, San Marcelino, and
Candelaria. It likewise broadens the University’s curricular mandate to include
emerging and high-impact fields such as artificial intelligence, information and
communications technology, maritime education, medicine and health-related
programs, law and legal management, the creative industries, and peace and
security studies, subject to the standards of the Commission on Higher Education
and the capabilities of the University.

      The measure also updates the composition, powers, and duties of the Board
of Regents; strengthens participatory, transparent, and accountable governance;
and clarifies the roles of the University President, the Administrative Council,
and the Academic Council. It further safeguards academic freedom and
institutional autonomy, promotes equitable access through scholarships and
affirmative action programs, and provides for the orderly transfer and
administration of the assets, liabilities, personnel, and lands of the University and
its campuses.

      By providing a unified and responsive charter, this measure will allow
PRMSU to plan and develop as one University system, allocate resources more
equitably among its campuses, and offer programs aligned with local and national
development priorities. It will also strengthen the University’s capacity to
produce competent professionals, undertake relevant research, support industry
and local government, and create opportunities for learners throughout Zambales
and neighboring communities.

      In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly requested.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:
SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Revised President
Ramon Magsaysay State University Charter.”

SEC. 2. General Mandate. – The President Ramon Magsaysay State University,
hereinafter referred to as “PRMSU” or the “University,” shall primarily provide
advanced and higher professional, technical, and special instructions in education

                                                                                4
and technology, engineering and architecture, agriculture, forestry and fisheries,
public administration and management, business and accountancy, economics
and finance, tourism, hospitality and culinary management, creative industries,
humanities, arts and sciences, information and communications technology,
artificial intelligence, law and legal management, medicine and health-related
programs, peace and security programs, and other relevant fields of study. It shall
also undertake research and extension, as well as income generation programs,
for the sustainable development of the Province of Zambales, the region, and the
country1.

SECTION 3. Curricular Offerings. – The University shall offer undergraduate
and graduate programs in the fields of education, industrial technology,
engineering, and architecture, agriculture, forestry and fisheries, public
administration and management, business and accountancy, economics and
finance, tourism, hospitality and culinary management, creative industries,
humanities, arts and sciences, information and communications technology,
artificial intelligence, maritime education, law, and legal management, medicine,
nursing, and health-related programs, criminology, and peace and security
programs, and other relevant programs within its areas of specialization and
according to its capabilities, as the Board of Regents may deem necessary to carry
out its objectives, in order to meet the needs of the Province of Zambales, the
region and the country.

SECTION 4. Satellite Campuses. – The satellite campuses of the President
Ramon Magsaysay State University located in the Municipalities of Santa Cruz,
Masinloc, Botolan, and Castillejos, all in the Province of Zambales, are hereby
officially recognized, integrated, and given legal standing as constituent

   The existing campuses of PRMSU in Iba (Main Campus), San Marcelino, and

shall likewise continue to operate as integral campuses of the University2.

      Act No. 7722, and the TESDA under Republic Act No. 7796;

      drawn by the Administrative and Academic Councils as herein provided;
   c. Appoint, upon the recommendation of the President, the vice presidents,
      deans, directors, campus administrators, faculty members, and other
      officials and employees of the University;
   d. Fix and adjust the salaries of faculty members, administrative officials and
      employees, subject to the Revised Compensation and Position
      Classification System; grant leaves of absence; and remove them for cause
      in accordance with the requirements of due process;
   e. Fix tuition fees and other necessary school charges after due consultation
      with concerned sectors. Such fees and charges shall constitute special trust
      funds deposited in any authorized government depository bank. Any
      income generated by the University from tuition fees, auxiliary services,

SEC. 7. Meetings of the Board. – The Board shall meet regularly at least once
every quarter and may convene for special meetings as may be necessary. A
quorum shall consist of a majority of all members holding office at the time of
the meeting: Provided, however, that the Chairperson of the Board or the
President of the PRMSU is among those present. In the absence of the CHED
Chairperson, a duly designated CHED Commissioner shall represent the
Chairperson with all the rights and responsibilities of a regular member; in such
case, the President of the PRMSU as Co-Chairperson shall be the presiding
officer. Members shall serve without compensation but shall be reimbursed for
necessary expenses incurred in attendance of Board meetings or in connection
with official Board business.

SEC. 8. The University President. – The PRMSU shall be headed by a President
appointed by the Board, subject to its guidelines, qualifications and standards,
upon the recommendation of a duly constituted Search Committee, who shall
render full-time service. The President shall hold office for a term of four (4)
years, renewable for another term.

        Within six (6) months before the expiration of the term of the incumbent
President, the Board shall constitute the Search Committee for the Presidency.

        The salary of the President of the PRMSU shall be in accordance with
Republic Act No. 11466, the “Salary Standardization Law of 2019,” as amended,
which shall be comparable to that of presidents of similar state educational
institutions.

        In case of vacancy in the Office of the President, the Board shall designate
an Officer-in-Charge, who shall serve only during the unexpired portion of the
term.


                                                                                 11
SEC. 9. The Secretary of the University. – The Board shall appoint a Secretary
who shall serve for both the Board and the PRMSU, keep all records and
proceedings of the Board, and communicate to each member notice of meetings.

SEC. 10. The Treasurer of the University. The Treasurer of the Philippines shall
be the ex officio Treasurer of the PRMSU.

SEC. 11. The Administrative Council. There shall be an Administrative Council
consisting of the President of the PRMSU as Chairperson, and the vice presidents,
campus administrators, university directors, deans, and other officials of equal
rank as members, whose duty is to implement the policies governing the
administration, management and development planning of the PRMSU, including
its satellite campuses, as approved by the Board.

SEC. 12. The Academic Council. – There shall be an Academic Council with the
President of the PRMSU as Chairperson and all academic staff with the rank of
not lower than Assistant Professor as members. The Academic Council shall
prescribe curricular offerings, fix requirements for admission, graduation and
conferment of degrees, exercise disciplinary power over students, and
recommend rules for student discipline — all subject to review and approval by
the Board through the President.

SEC. 13. The Faculty. – No political belief, gender preference, cultural or
community affiliation, ethnic origin, nor religious opinion or affiliation shall be
a matter of inquiry in the appointment of faculty members: Provided, That no
member of the faculty shall teach for or against any particular church or religious
sect.




                                                                                12
SEC. 14. Academic Freedom and Institutional Autonomy. – The PRMSU shall
enjoy academic freedom and institutional autonomy pursuant to Paragraph 2,
Section 5 of Article XIV of the Constitution.

SEC. 15. Scholarship Programs and Admission. – The PRMSU shall provide
scholarship and other affirmative action programs to assist poor but deserving
students. No student shall be denied admission to the PRMSU or any of its
satellite campuses by reason of sex, gender, religion, cultural or community
affiliation, physical disability or ethnic origin.

SEC. 16. Authority to Loan or Transfer Apparatus, Equipment, Supplies and
Detail of Personnel. – Upon the request of the President of the PRMSU, the heads
of national government bureaus and offices may loan or transfer to the PRMSU
apparatus, equipment, supplies, or personnel. Employees so detailed shall
perform duties as required by the President and the time served shall count as part
of their regular service.

SEC. 17. Assets, Liabilities and Personnel. – All assets, real and personal,
personnel, and records of the PRMSU, including those pertaining to the satellite
campuses in Santa Cruz, Masinloc, Botolan, and Castillejos, as well as all
liabilities or obligations, are hereby transferred to and shall be under the
jurisdiction of the PRMSU: Provided, That the positions, rights, and security of
tenure of faculty members and personnel employed under existing laws shall be
respected: Provided, further, That incumbents shall remain in the same status until
otherwise provided by the Board: Provided, finally, That the PRMSU shall
accordingly upgrade or increase its human resource components as the Board may
deem appropriate.

      All parcels of land occupied by the PRMSU and its satellite campuses are
hereby declared to be the property of the PRMSU and shall be titled under its
                                                                                13
name: Provided, That should the PRMSU cease to exist or be abolished, the same
shall revert to the concerned LGU or to the Republic of the Philippines, as the
case may be.

SEC. 18. Tax Exemption on Importation and Donations. – The importation by
the PRMSU of economic, technical and cultural books or publications for
educational purposes shall, upon certification by the CHED, be exempt from
customs duties under Republic Act No. 10863, the “Customs Modernization and
Tariff Act (CMTA).” All grants, bequests, endowments, donations and
contributions made to and used by the PRMSU, including its satellite campuses,
shall be exempt from donor's tax and shall be deductible from the gross income
of the donor under the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended.

SEC. 19. Filing of Report. – On or before the fifteenth (15th) day of the second
(2nd) month after the opening of the regular classes each year, the Board shall
file a detailed report on the progress, condition, and needs of the PRMSU and its
satellite campuses with the Office of the President of the Philippines, through the
CHED Chairperson, and with both Houses of Congress.

SEC. 20. Suppletory Application. – The provisions of Republic Act No. 8292,
otherwise known as the “Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997,” and its
Implementing Rules and Regulations as updated by CHED Memorandum Order
No. 7, Series of 2022, shall form an integral part of this Act and shall serve as
part of the governing charter of the University.

SEC. 21. Transitory Provisions. Upon the effectivity of this Act, all employees
of the PRMSU and its satellite campuses shall be retained in their present
positions and no one shall be adversely affected by the approval of the
amendments of the original charter.


                                                                                14
      The satellite campuses in Santa Cruz, Masinloc, Botolan, and Castillejos
shall be formally integrated within one hundred eighty (180) days from the
effectivity of this Act. During the transition period, the operations of said
campuses shall continue uninterrupted, and funding for their operations shall be
charged against the existing budget of the PRMSU pending the release of
additional appropriations provided herein.

      To ensure the smooth transition of the operations of the University, the
incumbent President of the PRMSU shall serve as the transitory President for one
(1) term upon the effectivity of this Act.

SEC. 22. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of
this Act, including the recognition, integration, and operationalization of the
satellite campuses in Santa Cruz, Masinloc, Botolan, and Castillejos, shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA) following the
effectivity of this Act.

SEC. 23. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any part or provision of this
Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not
affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 24. Repealing Clause. – Republic Act No. 11015, otherwise known as the
“Act Renaming the Ramon Magsaysay Technological University (RMTU) as the
President Ramon Magsaysay State University (PRMSU),” Republic Act No.
8498, and all other laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, and rules and
regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 25. Effectivity. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
