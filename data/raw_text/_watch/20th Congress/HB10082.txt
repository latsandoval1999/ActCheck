                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to expand the statutory benefits and incentives granted to
public health workers. It amends Republic Act (RA) No. 7305, otherwise known as
the “Magna Carta of Public Health Workers”, to guarantee increased compensation for
overtime work, and work performed during rest days and special holidays.

       Public health workers continuously provide their indispensable services
despite long working hours, hazardous working conditions, and public health
emergencies. Despite the enactment of RA 7305, the benefits provided fail to
adequately match the demands and risks inherent in public health service.

       This measure requires overtime, rest day, and special holiday work to be
compensated exclusively in cash. It also increases the night shift differential, hazard,
subsistence, and laundry allowances to accurately reflect the amount needed by our
public health workers on the ground.

        The provision of adequate compensation is essential to protect the welfare of
public health workers, improve workforce retention, and sustain an efficient and
resilient public healthcare system. Ensuring that those who safeguard the nation’s
health receive benefits that reflect their current needs is a necessary investment in
the quality and continuity of public health services.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.
