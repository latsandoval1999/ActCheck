                           EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The 1987 Constitution mandates the State to adopt an integrated and
comprehensive approach to health development and to give priority to the
needs of the elderly through programs on social security and social
assistance.

      Republic Act No. 7432, as amended by Republic Act No. 9994 or the
“Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010,” recognizes the invaluable
contribution of senior citizens to nation-building by granting them various
benefits and privileges aimed at promoting their welfare and improving their
quality of life. Despite these measures, many elderly Filipinos continue to face
economic hardship brought about by rising costs of living, inflation, and
limited sources of income.

       In several local government units, birthday cash incentives are already
being granted to senior citizens as a form of recognition and support. However,
the amount and availability of these benefits vary widely across the country,
resulting in unequal treatment among senior citizens.

      This bill seeks to institutionalize a minimum birthday cash benefit of
Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00) for all qualified senior citizens nationwide. The
proposed measure likewise ensures that such benefit shall be released in a
timely and accessible manner, free from transaction or service charges, and
without prejudice to other benefits already granted under existing laws.

      While several local government units already provide birthday
incentives to senior citizens through local ordinances, there is presently no
national law institutionalizing a uniform minimum birthday cash benefit for
senior citizens nationwide.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

        Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
 Philippines in Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. Section 4 of Republic Act No. 7432, as amended, is
 hereby further amended to read as follows:

     "SEC. 4. Privileges for the Senior Citizens. -

     The senior citizens shall be entitled to the following:

"(a) the grant of twenty percent (20%) discount and exemption from the value
       added tax (VAT), if applicable, on the sale of the following goods and
       services from all establishments, for the exclusive use and enjoyment
       or availment of the senior citizen "
                                       xxx
(M) BIRTHDAY CASH BENEFIT OF A MINIMUM AMOUNT OF FIVE
       HUNDRED PESOS (₱500.00), TO BE RELEASED WITHIN A PERIOD
       NOT EXCEEDING SIXTY (60) DAYS FROM THE SENIOR CITIZEN'S
       DATE OF BIRTH. SUCH AMOUNT SHALL BE ADJUSTED FOR
       INFLATION EVERY THREE (3) YEARS AFTER THE EFFECTIVITY
       OF THIS ACT, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE GUIDELINES TO BE
       ISSUED BY THE NATIONAL COMMISSION OF SENIOR CITIZENS."

        SECTION 2. The birthday cash benefit provided under this Act shall
 be granted to senior citizens regardless of any other privilege or government
 assistance they may receive under existing laws, including the cash gift
 provided under Republic Act No. 10868 or the "Centenarians Act of 2016,"
 as amended by Republic Act No. 11982: Provided, That local government
 units (LGUs) which, prior to the effectivity of this Act, grant a birthday cash
benefit to senior citizens in an amount less than Five Hundred Pesos
(₱500.00) shall adjust such benefit to conform with the minimum amount
prescribed herein: Provided, further, That no diminution thereof shall be
allowed in cases where the existing birthday cash benefit granted by the
LGUs exceeds Five Hundred Pesos (₱500.00).

      SECTION 3. The birthday cash benefit granted under this Act shall
be released either through direct cash payout, direct remittance through
accredited or duly authorized payment service providers supervised by the
Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, electronic transfer, or other modes of
disbursement, whichever is more practical and acceptable to the
beneficiary, ensuring its release in the most expeditious and efficient
manner.

       A birthday cash benefit not claimed within one (1) year from the date
it became due shall remain available for payment upon validation of
entitlement in accordance with the implementing rules and regulations

      SECTION 4. Any transaction or service fee arising from the
engagement of a service provider, electronic transfer, or other modes of
disbursement with service fees for the release ofthe birthday cash benefit to
senior citizens shall not be charged against the senior citizen beneficiary.

       SECTION 5. The National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC),
Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), Philippine Statistics
Authority (PSA), local government units, and concerned agencies may share
relevant information, subject to Republic Act No. 10173 or the Data Privacy
Act of 2012, for purposes of validating eligibility and preventing duplication
of benefits.

      SECTION 6. Within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act, the
NCSC, in coordination with the DILG, the Department of Budget and Management,
(DBM) and other concerned agencies shall formulate the necessary rules and
regulations for the proper implementation of this Act.

      SECTION 7. The funds necessary for the initial implementation of
this Act shall be charged against the annual appropriations of the local
government unit where the senior citizen is registered.

       Only senior citizens duly registered with the Office for Senior Citizens
Affairs (OSCA) of their city or municipality shall be entitled to the birthday
cash benefit under this Act.

       SECTION 8. If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the parts or provisions not affected shall remain
in full force or effect.

      SECTION 9. All laws, decrees, executive orders, proclamations, rules
and regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof that are inconsistent
with the provisions ofthis Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SECTION 10. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
