14                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE
15

16   The City of Iloilo is one of the oldest and most historically significant urban centers in the
17   Philippines and has played an important role in the political, economic, cultural, and social
18   development of the country.

19   The legal foundation for the establishment and organization of the City of Iloilo is found in
20   Commonwealth Act No. 57, otherwise known as the Charter of the City of Iloilo, as
21   subsequently amended by Commonwealth Act No. 158 and other amendatory laws.
22   Commonwealth Act No. 158, approved on November 9, 1936, amended the Charter and
23   provided for the organization and administration of the City of Iloilo.

24   The inauguration of the city was originally scheduled by the President for July 16, 1937.
25   On January 23, 1937, President Manuel L. Quezon issued Proclamation No. 133, entitled
26   “Setting Aside July 16, 1937, as a Day for the Inauguration of the City of Iloilo, and
27   Declaring It a Special Public Holiday for the Province of Iloilo.”

28   The inauguration date was subsequently changed. On August 20, 1937, President Quezon
29   issued Proclamation No. 172, expressly amending Proclamation No. 133 and setting aside
30   August 25, 1937 as the day for the inauguration of the City of Iloilo. The same
31   proclamation declared August 25, 1937 a special public holiday for the Province of Iloilo.
32   Project - Proclamations Series of

33   Thus, August 25, 1937 represents the date officially designated by the national government
34   for the formal inauguration of the City of Iloilo. The date consequently acquired historical
35   significance as the city's Charter Day.

36   The establishment of the City of Iloilo was likewise part of the transformation of several
37   historic communities into a unified urban political jurisdiction. The Charter was
38   subsequently amended to further define the territorial jurisdiction of the city. Among these
39   later developments, Commonwealth Act No. 604, approved on August 22, 1940, provided
40   for the inclusion of the territory of the municipality of Jaro into the City of Iloilo.

41   The national government has, on various occasions, continued to recognize August 25 as a
42   significant date for the City of Iloilo through presidential proclamations declaring the date a
43   special non-working day in the city.
44   A permanent declaration by Congress will ensure that the historical significance of August
45   25 is recognized every year and will provide the people of Iloilo City with an opportunity to
46   commemorate the city's beginnings, celebrate its heritage, and reflect upon its continuing
47   contribution to the development of Western Visayas and the Philippines.

48
49   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly sought.

113     AN ACT DECLARING AUGUST 25 OF EVERY YEAR AS ILOILO CITY CHARTER
114     DAY AND DECLARING THE SAME AS A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN
115                           THE CITY OF ILOILO

116         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
117   assembled:

118   SECTION 1. Iloilo City Charter Day.
119         August 25 of every year is hereby declared as Iloilo City Charter Day in the City of Iloilo,
120   in commemoration of the formal inauguration of the City of Iloilo on August 25, 1937.
121
122   SECTION 2. Historical Basis.
123           The declaration of August 25 as Iloilo City Charter Day is based on Proclamation No. 133,
124   dated January 23, 1937, which initially fixed July 16, 1937 as the date of the inauguration of the
125   City of Iloilo, and Proclamation No. 172, dated August 20, 1937, which amended the date of
126   inauguration to August 25, 1937 and declared the same a special public holiday in the Province of
127   Iloilo.
128
129           The observance shall recognize the establishment and formal inauguration of the City of
130   Iloilo as a chartered city and the beginning of its organized city government.
131
132   SECTION 3. Charter of the City of Iloilo.
133          For purposes of this Act, the Charter of the City of Iloilo shall refer to Commonwealth Act
134   No. 57, as amended by Commonwealth Act No. 158 and subsequent amendatory laws, which
135   provided for the organization, powers, territorial jurisdiction and administration of the City of Iloilo.
136
137          Nothing in this Act shall be construed as amending, repealing or modifying the substantive
138   provisions of the Charter of the City of Iloilo, as amended.
139
140   SECTION 4. Special Non-Working Holiday.
141            August 25 of every year shall be observed as a special non-working holiday in the City of
142   Iloilo, subject to applicable laws, rules and regulations.
143
144   SECTION 5. Observance.
145           The City Government of Iloilo, in coordination with appropriate national government
146   agencies, educational institutions, cultural organizations and other stakeholders, may undertake
147   suitable programs and activities to commemorate Iloilo City Charter Day and promote awareness of
148   the city's historical, cultural and civic heritage.
149
150   SECTION 6. Government Support.
151          National and local government offices and agencies are encouraged to extend appropriate
152   support and cooperation to activities for the observance of Iloilo City Charter Day.
153
154
155
156   SECTION 7. Repealing Clause.
157           All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules, regulations, proclamations and other issuances, or
158   parts thereof, inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified
159   accordingly.
160
161   SECTION 8. Effectivity.
162         This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a
163   newspaper of general circulation, pursuant to applicable law.
