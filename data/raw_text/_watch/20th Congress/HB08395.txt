                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to declare September 13 of every year as a special nonworking
holiday in the Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR) in commemoration of the
historic “Sipat” or Mount Data Peace Accord. Signed on September 13, 1986 at the
Mount Data Hotel in Bauko, Mountain Province is the peace agreement between the
Philippine Government and the Cordillera People’s Liberation Army (CPLA), led by
Fr. Conrado “Ka Ambo” Balweg, marked a pivotal moment in the history of the
Cordillera. This accord laid the groundwork for the establishment of the Cordillera
Administrative Region through Executive Order No. 220 in 1987.

       The Sipat, meaning “peace agreement” or “cessation of hostilities”, remains a
foundation in the continuing pursuit of peace, cultural preservation, and
self-determination for the people of the Cordillera. As the region sustains its
longstanding call for autonomy, the Sipat stands as a defining moment that
catalyzed decades of policy initiatives for regional recognition and Indigenous
Peoples’ rights. These include various executive issuances from 1987 onward, the
CBA–CPLA peace process commitments, the 2011 Memorandum of Agreement, the
institutionalization of the “Whole-of-Nation Approach” under Executive Order No.
70, series of 2018, the CBA–CPLA Normalization Roadmap, the Cordillera Regional
Development Plans, and the Philippine Development Plans from 2017–2022 and
2023–2028, efforts reiterated by His Excellency President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. in
his State of the Nation Address as part of the comprehensive peace agenda.

        Declaring September 13 as a special nonworking holiday affirms the
importance of remembering the Sipat as a living legacy. This observance allows the
people of CAR to reflect on the lasting impact of the agreement, strengthen their
collective commitment to peace, and advance the continuing aspiration for
meaningful regional autonomy.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
DECLARING SEPTEMBER 13 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE CORDILLERA ADMINISTRATIVE REGION IN
COMMEMORATION OF THE SIPAT OR MOUNT DATA PEACE ACCORD
ANNIVERSARY, TO BE KNOWN AS “SIPAT DAY”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. September 13 of every year is hereby declared a special non-
working holiday in the Cordillera Administrative Region, to be known as “Sipat
Day” in commemoration of the historic “Sipat” or Mount Data Peace Accord signed
on September 13, 1986 between the Philippine government and the Cordillera
People’s Liberation Army (CPLA) led by Fr. Conrado “Ka Ambo” Balweg.

       SEC. 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
