                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to establish a regular campus of the Cagayan State University (CSU) in
the Municipality of Baggao, Province of Cagayan.

Access to quality and affordable higher education remains a vital instrument for social
mobility, economic development, and poverty reduction. In the Province of Cagayan,
particularly in the Municipality of Baggao and its neighboring localities, many deserving
students continue to face challenges in pursuing tertiary education due to
geographical distance, transportation costs, and the limited availability of higher
educational institutions within their reach.

As the premier state university in the province, the Cagayan State University (CSU)
has played a significant role in providing accessible and quality education to the people
of Cagayan. However, the growing demand for higher education opportunities
necessitates the expansion of CSU's presence in underserved areas to ensure that
more students can benefit from its academic programs, research initiatives, and
extension services.

The Municipality of Baggao is one of the largest municipalities in the province in terms
of both land area and population. The establishment of a regular CSU campus in the
municipality will bring higher education closer to students and their families, reduce
educational expenses, promote greater enrollment and completion rates, and
contribute to local socioeconomic development. It will likewise help produce a highly
skilled, productive, and globally competitive workforce capable of supporting regional
and national development goals.

The proposed campus shall offer degree programs, technical and professional courses,
research activities, and extension services responsive to the educational, economic,
and developmental needs of the municipality and the province. It shall further support
the government's commitment to inclusive growth and the equitable distribution of
educational opportunities, particularly in rural and geographically isolated
communities.
Moreover, CSU has already conducted a favorable feasibility study on the
establishment of a CSU campus in Baggao, Cagayan, and initial funding has been
allocated under the 2025 General Appropriations Act for the proposed campus
infrastructure on the property donated to CSU for the establishment of CSU – Baggao
Campus. These developments demonstrate the viability of the said establishment and
the strong support it has garnered from stakeholders and the local community.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
 ESTABLISHING A REGULAR CAMPUS OF THE CAGAYAN STATE UNIVERSITY
IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BAGGAO, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Cagayan State University
– Baggao Act."

SECTION 2. Establishment of the Cagayan State University – Baggao. –
There is hereby established a regular campus of the Cagayan State University (CSU),
to be located in the Municipality of Baggao, Province of Cagayan, which shall be known
as “Cagayan State University – Baggao.”

SECTION 3. General Mandate. – the CSU – Baggao shall primarily provide short-
term technical-vocational, undergraduate, and graduate courses within its areas of
competency and specialization, pursuant to the mandate of the CSU, and according
to its capability, as the Board of Regents, hereinafter referred to as the Board, may
deem necessary to respond to the human resource development needs of the Province
of Cagayan and the entire Region II. It shall be mandated to undertake research and
extension services and to provide progressive leadership in these areas.

SECTION 4. Supervision and Management of the CSU – Baggao. – The CSU –
Baggao shall be headed by a Campus Administrator who shall be appointed by the
Board, subject to its guidelines on qualifications and standards, and based on the
recommendation of a search committee duly constituted for the purpose of selecting
the Campus Administrator.

SECTION 5. Compliance with the Requirements of the Commission on
Higher Education (CHED). – The CSU shall ensure the compliance of the CSU –
Baggao with the policies, standards, and guidelines of the CHED for the operation of
the Campus, including those related to the offering of courses and the introduction of
new or revised courses and programs prior to its establishment.

SECTION 6. Management of Personnel, Assets, and Records of the Campus.
– All assets, both real and personal, personnel, and records of the CSU - Baggao, as
well as its liabilities or obligations, shall be titled under or named after the CSU.

All parcels of land belonging to the government that shall be occupied by the CSU –
Baggao shall be the property of the CSU and shall be titled under its name: Provided,
That should the CSU – Baggao or the CSU cease to exist or be abolished, or should
such aforementioned parcels of land be no longer needed by the CSU, the same shall
revert to the local government unit concerned or to the Republic of the Philippines, as
the case may be.

SECTION 7. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to carry out the provisions
of this Act, including those required to fund the Plantilla positions of teaching and non-
teaching personnel of the CSU – Baggao, shall be included in the annual General
Appropriations Act (GAA).

SECTION 8. Suppletory Application. – The provisions of Republic Act No. 8292,
otherwise known as the “Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997” shall form part
of this Act.

SECTION 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the Board shall, in consultation with the CHED and the
Department of Budget and Management, issue rules and regulations to implement its
provisions.

SECTION 10. Separability Clause. – If, for any reason, any part or provision of
this Act is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not
affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 11. Repealing Clause. – All other laws, presidential decrees, executive
orders, and rules and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 12. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
