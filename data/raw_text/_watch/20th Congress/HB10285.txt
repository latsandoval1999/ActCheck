 AN ACT PROVIDING FUEL SUBSIDY AND LIVELIHOOD SUPPORT TO
 MUNICIPAL AND SMALL-SCALE FISHERFOLK DURING PERIODS OF
  FISHING BAN OR CLOSED SEASON AND HIGH FUEL PRICES, AND
              APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

Municipal and small-scale fisherfolk are among the poorest sectors of Philippine
society, and their income is doubly threatened by rising fuel costs, which eat into
the profitability of every fishing trip, and by closed fishing seasons imposed for
conservation purposes, which halt their income entirely without adequate
substitute support.

This bill provides a standing fuel subsidy for registered municipal fisherfolk and
a temporary livelihood allowance during legally mandated closed seasons,
recognizing that conservation policy should not be borne disproportionately by
the poorest fishing families.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      SECTION 1. This Act shall be known as the "Fisherfolk Livelihood and Fuel
Subsidy Act."
      SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — The State shall protect the welfare of
municipal and small-scale fisherfolk and ensure that conservation measures do
not unduly burden them without corresponding support.
       SECTION 3. Fuel Subsidy for Registered Fisherfolk. — Fisherfolk registered
under the FishR system shall be entitled to a fuel subsidy or fuel discount card
whenever prevailing diesel or gasoline prices increase by fifteen percent (15%) or
more within a ninety (90)-day period, in the same manner provided for public
utility vehicle drivers under existing law.
       SECTION 4. Closed Season Livelihood Allowance. — During any legally
declared closed fishing season or fishing ban, registered municipal fisherfolk
affected thereby shall receive a livelihood allowance equivalent to the prevailing
minimum wage in their region, for the duration of the closure, not to exceed four
(4) months per year.
      SECTION 5. Alternative Livelihood Support. — The Bureau of Fisheries
and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), in coordination with local government units, shall
provide alternative livelihood training and starter kits, such as aquaculture or
handicraft-making, to affected fisherfolk during closed seasons.
     SECTION 6. Appropriations. — The amount necessary shall be sourced
from the annual budget of the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources,
augmented as necessary through the General Appropriations Act.

      SECTION 8. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid, the other provisions not affected shall remain in force.
      SECTION 9. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, and

      SECTION 10. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
