                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to recognize the importance of the Municipality of Enrile’s founding
anniversary by declaring September 9 of every year a special nonworking holiday in
the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan to remind the residents of their
Municipality’s heritage and origin and enable all its residents to acknowledge and
celebrate it annually.

Enrile lies on the southernmost part of Cagayan, bounded by Santa Maria, Isabela to
the south, by Cagayan River to the east, by Solana and Tuguegarao to the north, and
Rizal, Kalinga to the west. It has a land area of 18,451.3 hectares and is politically
subdivided into 22 barangays.

The Municipality of Enrile was originally named Cabug when it was still a barrio
of Tuguegarao City. It was established as a separate town in September 1849 and
named in honor of Spanish Governor-General Pascual Enrile y Alcedo.

In lieu of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is sought.

                            AN ACT
DECLARING SEPTEMBER 9 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ENRILE, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, IN
COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. September 9 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking
holiday in the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan, in commemoration of its
founding anniversary

SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
