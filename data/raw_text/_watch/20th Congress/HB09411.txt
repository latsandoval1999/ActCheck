20                                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE
21
22   With the increasing use of digital platforms, online banking, mobile communications,
23   and social media, elderly Filipinos have become more exposed to fraud, scams, and
24   misinformation. Deceptive schemes such as phishing, identity theft, and financial fraud
25   continue to threaten the security, financial stability, and overall well-being of senior
26   citizens.
27
28   Recognizing the need to uphold the rights and dignity of older persons, the State, under
29   Republic Act No. 7432, as amended, otherwise known as the “Senior Citizens Act,”
30   affirms its commitment to provide support and protection to senior citizens and promote
31   their general welfare.
32
33   This bill seeks to establish stronger safeguards against fraud and misinformation
34   targeting senior citizens through public education campaigns, accessible information
35   systems, and enhanced coordination among concerned government agencies, financial
36   institutions, and telecommunications providers. It also aims to improve awareness
37   among senior citizens on identifying and preventing fraudulent activities.
38
39   Through this measure, the government seeks to strengthen the protection of senior
40   citizens from abuse and exploitation while promoting their safety, empowerment, and
41   well-being in an increasingly digital society.
42
43   In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

21                                      AN ACТ
22                 PROTECTING SENIOR CITIZENS FROM VARIOUS FORMS OF
23                   FRAUD THROUGH INFORMATION DISSEMINATION AND
24                          PROVIDING MECHANISMS THEREFOR
25
26   Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
27   Congress assembled:
28
29   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Senior Citizens Protection
30   Against Fraud Act.”.
31
32   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to care for the elderly
33   through just programs of social security. To this end, the State shall establish
34   mechanisms to educate and keep the senior citizens informed in order to protect them
35   from various forms of fraud using mail, telecommunications and other modern means
36   of communication and ensure that they are protected from the ills and evils of modern
37   technology.
38
39   SEC. 3. Education on Mail, Telemarketing, and Internet Fraud. - The Department of
40   the Interior and Local Government (DILG) shall, through the local government units,
41   after consultation and in coordination with the different government agencies, educate
42   the senior citizens and their families and caregivers on the following:
43          a. General information on mail, telemarketing, and internet fraud targeting
44              senior citizens, including descriptions of the most common fraud schemes;
45              and
46          b. Information on how and where to report fraud, and how to prevent and avoid
47              various forms of fraud targeting senior citizens.
 1   SEC. 4. Information Gathering. - The DILG shall gather information for dissemination
 2   to senior citizens from the following government agencies:
 3          a. Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) - with respect to fraud and scams related
 4              to products and services of BSP-regulated financial institutions;
 5          b. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and Insurance Commission (IC)
 6              - with respect to fraud and scams related to lending and investment
 7              instruments and insurance products;
 8          c. National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) - with respect to text
 9              scams and subscriber identification module card fraud;
10          d. Philippine Postal Corporation (PHLPost) - with respect to fraud and scams
11              committed through mail;
12          e. Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) - with respect to any reported fraud
13              such as marketing scams; and
14          f. National Bureau of Investigation (NBI), Philippine National Police (PNP), and
15              Cybercrime Investigation and Coordinating Center (CICC) - with respect to
16              other fraudulent activities and cybercrimes.

17
18   SEC. 5. Role of the Department of Information and Communications Technology
19   (DICT). - The DICT shall provide the necessary technical assistance to the DILG,
20   National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC) and the concerned government
21   agencies mentioned in the preceding section, particularly on the development of a
22   website and an online platform to facilitate coordination and sharing of information.
23
24   The DICT shall also provide information on internet and telecommunications fraud
25   targeting senior citizens, including information on how to prevent and avoid these types
26   of fraud.
27
28   SEC. 6. Other Mechanisms. - The DILG shall, in coordination with the NCSC 27 and
29   the DICT, formulate and establish other mechanisms to fully protect senior 28 citizens
30   from any fraud. These mechanisms include:
31         a. Referral of complaints to the PNP or NBI; and
32         b. Establishment of free hotline numbers and website for purposes of reporting
33             mail, telemarketing, and internet fraud.

34   SEC. 7. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within sixty (60) days from the
35   effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior and Local Government shall, in
36   coordination with the Chairperson of the NCSC, promulgate the necessary rules and
37   regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.
38
39   SEC. 8. Separability Clause. - In case any provisions of this Act or any portion thereof
40   is declared unenforceable, illegal, or unconstitutional by a competent court, other
41   provisions shall not be affected thereby.
1   SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. - All laws, presidential decrees, issuances, executive
2   orders, letters of instruction, rules or regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with or
3   contrary to the provisions of this Act are repealed, amended or modified accordingly.
4
5   Sec. 10. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
6   publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
