SPORTS HEROES, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Sports has long served as a powerful platform for national pride, unity,
discipline, and excellence. Filipino athletes, through perseverance and sacrifice,
have consistently elevated the country’s honor in the international arena despite
limited resources and immense challenges. Their achievements inspire generations
of Filipinos and embody the values of patriotism, resilience, sportsmanship, and
service to the nation.

      The historic victories of Filipino athletes in international competitions, most
notably in the Olympic Games and other world-class sporting events, have
demonstrated the need for a more institutionalized and enduring system of
recognition, support, and protection for the country’s sports heroes. While existing
laws provide incentives for medal-winning athletes, there remains no comprehensive
framework that formally recognizes their lifelong contributions to Philippine sports
and nation-building.



                                                                                      1
        This proposed “Bayaning Atletang Pilipino Act” seeks to fill this gap by
 establishing the Order of National Athletes (Orden ng mga Pambansang Atleta) as
 the highest state recognition for Filipino sports heroes who have brought immense
 honor and prestige to the country. The measure likewise institutionalizes additional
 honors such as the National Medal of Excellence in Sports, the Balangay Medal for
 Sportsmanship, and the Coaching Excellence Medal to recognize exemplary
 achievements, heroism, and mentorship in sports.

        Furthermore, the bill creates the Hidilyn Diaz Endowment Fund for Sports
 Heroes to ensure sustainable support for deserving athletes through pensions,
 medical assistance, insurance coverage, scholarships, and continued international
 training opportunities. By naming the endowment fund after Olympic gold medalist
 Hidilyn Diaz, the measure honors her groundbreaking achievement and symbolizes
 the aspirations of future generations of Filipino athletes.

        Through this Act, the State affirms its commitment to honoring their
 sacrifices, securing their welfare, and promoting a culture of excellence in Philippine
 sports.

         Finally, we recognize Rep. Gus Tambunting of the 2nd District of Parañaque
 for filing a similar bill in the Nineteenth Congresses.

       In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:


SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Bayaning Atletang
Pilipino Act”.


SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the declared policy of the State to
promote excellence in the field of sports and foster the values of sportsmanship,
patriotism, and nationalism to support nation-building and development.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, the following terms
are hereby defined as follows:


   a. International sports competition      shall refer to international     sports
      competitions under the following categories:
              1. Regular major competitions, such as but not limited to, the
                 Summer Olympic Games, Winter Olympic Games, Asian Games,
                 Asian Winter Games, Southeast Asian (SEA) Games, Youth
                 Olympic Games, Paralympic Games, Asian Park Games, Asian
                 Indoor and Martial Arts Games, Asian Beach Games, and
                 Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) Para Games;
              2. World-level championships held at least every two (2) years with
                 at least forty-five
              3. (45) countries participating by sport;
              4. Asian-level competitions held at least every two (2) years with at

              5. (25) countries participating by sport; and
              6. Qualifying competitions for World-level games with at least ten

   b. National Academy of Sports or NAS shall refer to the NAS System
      established under Republic Act No. 11470 or any of its existing or future
      campuses which aims to provide quality and enhanced secondary education
      program integrated with a special curriculum on sports.


SECTION 4. The Order of National Athletes. – There is hereby established the
Order of National Athletes (Orden ng mga Pambansang Atleta) which shall be the

SECTION 5. The National Medal of Excellence in Sports. – There is hereby
established the National Medal of Excellence in Sports (Pambansang Medalya ng
Katangi-tanging Husay sa Isports) for outstanding individual and team achievements
in international sports competitions. The President of the Philippines shall confer the
National Medal of Excellence in Sports upon recommendation by the Committee.


SECTION 6. The Balangay Medal for Sportsmanship. – There is hereby
established the Balangay Medal for Sportsmanship (Gawad Balangay) for heroic
sportsmanship shown by athletes during international and local competitions
exemplifying the values of honesty, fairness, selflessness, sacrifice, amity, and
goodwill. The President of the Philippines shall confer the Balangay Medal for
Sportsmanship upon recommendation by the Committee.


SECTION 7. Coaching Excellence Medal. – There is hereby established the
Coaching Excellence Medal (Gabay ng Atletang Pilipino) for coaches who have
gone above and beyond in nurturing athletes to reach their full potential and lead the
team to victory in international sports competitions. The President of the Philippines
shall confer the Coaching Excellence Medal upon recommendation by the
Committee.


SECTION 8. Priority of Awardees and/or their Dependents in the National
Academy of Sports Scholarship. – Awardees under this Act shall have the option

and priority to enroll, on a full- scholarship basis, in the National Academy of Sports
System Main Campus or any regional nationally- funded high schools for sports. In
case the awardee has completed his/her secondary education, or in the case of a
posthumous awardee, his/her legal heir may be prioritized in the entry into the
National Academy of Sports or any regional nationally-funded high school for
sports, subject to the requirements and qualifications as may be set by the NAS or

SECTION 9. Komite ng Bayaning Atletang Filipino. – The Committee shall be
under the Office of the President. The Committee shall be composed of the
following:


          a. Chairperson of the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC), as Chairman;
          b. Secretary of the Department of Education or an authorized
             representative, as Member;
          c. Chairperson of the Commission on Higher Education or an authorized
             representative, as Member;
          d. President of the Philippine Olympic Committee or an authorized
             representative, as Member;
          e. Chairperson     of the Athletes'     Commission      or an    authorized
             representative, as Member;
          f. Chairperson of the National Youth Commission or an authorized
             representative, as Member; and,
          g. Chairperson of the National Historical Commission of the Philippines
             or an authorized representative, as Member.

SECTION 10. Role and Function of the Komite ng Bayaning Atletang Pilipino.
– The Committee shall approve the criteria, standards, nomination and selection
processes, and benefits and privileges of the recipients of the awards under this Act.


The Committee shall likewise be the administrator of the Hidilyn Diaz Endowment
Fund for Sports Heroes.


SECTION 11. Hidilyn Diaz Endowment Fund for Sports Heroes. – There is
hereby established a Hidilyn Diaz Endowment Fund for Sports Heroes to exclusively
fund the benefits provided in this Act and support the continuous training of all the
awardees and their participation or exposure in international sports competitions.
         a. The amount of One Billion Pesos (P1,000,000,000.00) is hereby
             appropriated as seed money of the Endowment Fund; Provided, that no
             grant shall be awarded by the Committee except the interest drawn from
             the Fund; Provided, further, that no grant shall be awarded until after
             one (1) year from the organization of the Fund;
         b. The private portion of the Fund shall be raised from the donations and
             other conveyances by gratuitous title, including funds, materials,
             property, and services,
         c. Contribution to the Fund shall be deductible for income tax purposes
             under Section 34 (h)(2)(a) of the National Internal Revenue Code;
         d. For the sound and judicious management of the Fund, the Committee
             shall appoint a reputable government-accredited investment institution
             as Fund Manager, subject to guidelines promulgated by the Committee
             and applicable government auditing and accounting rules;




                                                                                     6
          e. The Committee shall be the administrator of the Fund and shall prepare
             the implementing guidelines and decision-making mechanisms, subject
             to the following:
               1. Unless otherwise stipulated by the private donor, only earnings of
                  private contributions shall be used;
               2. No part of the seed capital of the Fund, including earnings thereof,
                  shall be used to underwrite overhead expenses for administration;
               3. The Committee shall organize a separate staff to be headed by a
                  comptroller appointed by and directly responsible and accountable
                  to the Committee; and
               4. The Fund shall be audited annually by an independent external
                  auditor or the Commission on Audit, as may be appropriate.


SECTION 12. Existing Incentives, Benefits, and Privileges. – The incentives,
benefits, and privileges provided under this Act shall not diminish or affect existing
incentives, benefits, and privileges provided in existing laws.


SECTION 13. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – The Committee shall
promulgate the implementing rules and regulations (IRR) within ninety (90) days
from the effectivity of this Act.


SECTION 14. Appropriations. – The amount of One Billion                         Pesos
(P1,000,000,000.00) is hereby appropriated to serve as seed money for the Hidilyn
Diaz Endowment Fund for Sports Heroes as provided in Section 11 of this Act. The
amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against
the current year's appropriations of the PSC. Thereafter, such appropriations

SECTION 15. Separability. – If any provision or part of this Act is declared invalid
or unconstitutional, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall continue to
be in full force and effect.


SECTION 16. Repealing Clause. – All laws, executive orders, and administrative
orders, or parts thereof that are inconsistent with any provision of this Act are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.


SECTION 17. Effectivity Clause. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after
its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
