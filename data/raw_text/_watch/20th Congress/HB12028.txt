HOUSE BILL NOL2023

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Republic Act No. 7307, also known as the “Philippine Carabao Act of 1992,” created
the Philippine Carabao Center (PCC) to strengthen the carabao industry as a source of
livelihood and nutrition for farmers and their families. The law initially identified thirteen (13)
carabao centers across the country and mandated the, to conserve, propagate, and promote the
Philippine carabao, as well as to undertake relevant programs for the development of the
carabao industry.

In 2025, Republic Act No. 12308, or the “Animal Industry Development and
Competitiveness Act,” further strengthened the mandate of the PCC by expanding its research
and development functions to cover other animals. It also converted its existing regional centers
into research and development stations, including a station in Region V at the Central Bicol
State University of Agriculture.

The expanded mandate of the PCC provides an opportunity to support the broader
development of the livestock and poultry sector, particularly in areas where production remains
limited and greater technical intervention is needed. Provided this, and consistent with the State
policy of expanding productivity, improving the quality of life, and giving all sectors of the
economy and regions of the country optimum opportunity to develop, this bill seeks to
establish a PCC Research and Development Station at Catanduanes State University
(CatSU) in the province of Catanduanes.

Catanduanes continues to lag behind the other provinces in Region V in terms of
livestock and poultry inventory. As of July 2026, Philippine Statistics Authority data show that
the province recorded only 4,001 carabaos, compared with 99,842 in Camarines Sur, the
highest in the region, and a regional total of 322,458. The province likewise posted the lowest
inventories in the region for cattle, goats, swine, chickens, and ducks, among others. These
figures reflect the limited scale of the province’s animal industry and underscore the need for
stronger and more sustained interventions to improve production, technical capacity, and
farmer support.

A local PCC Research and Development Station in Catanduanes can help address these
gaps by bringing breeding and genetic improvement, animal health and nutrition, research and
technology transfer, and farmer training directly to the province. These interventions can
improve productivity, help revitalize the local livestock and poultry sector, and create more
sustainable livelihood opportunities for farmers and animal raisers.

===== [page 2] =====
Although a PCC Research and Development Station is identified in Bicol, Catanduanes
is an island province geographically separated from the mainland by the Maqueda Channel.
This has levied additional travel, transportation costs, and logistical constraints that make it
more difficult for farmers and raisers to access specialized services and technical support from
mainland-based facilities. Establishing a station within the province would bring services,
research and technology, training, and other animal-industry development support closer to
local farmers and raisers.

Furthermore, CatSU is well-positioned to host the proposed station. It offers agriculture
programs in Animal Husbandry and Animal Science and has demonstrated competencies in
research and extension relevant to the proposed facility — having partnered with national
government agencies in implementing research-based training and extension programs for
various stakeholders. Hosting the PCC station at CatSU can further strengthen collaborative
research, provide practical learning opportunities for students and faculty, and extend technical
services directly to farmers and animal raisers.

While RA 12308 already authorizes the PCC to establish additional research and
development centers, this measure seeks to expressly establish the CatSU station by law to
institutionalize it within the PCC network, ensure continuity beyond changing administrative
priorities, and provide a clear basis for the necessary funding under the annual General
Appropriations Act.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this proposed measure is earnestly sought.

HOUSE BILL nol202 8

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

AN ACT ESTABLISHING PHILIPPINE CARABAO CENTER RESEARCH AND
DEVELOPMENT STATION AT THE CATANDUANES STATE UNIVERSITY IN
THE PROVINCE OF CATANDUANES, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE
SECTION 7 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12308, ENTITLED “AN ACT ENHANCING
ANIMAL INDUSTRY DEVELOPMENT AND COMPETITIVENESS,
STRENGTHENING THE BUREAU OF ANIMAL INDUSTRY, AND CREATING
FOR THE PURPOSE THE ANIMAL COMPETITIVENESS ENHANCEMENT
FUND”

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. The third paragraph of Section 7 of Republic Act No. 12308 is hereby
amended to read as follows:

“SEC. 7. Philippine Carabao Center. - The PCC shall continue to perform its
original mandates under Republic Act No. 7307 or the "Philippine Carabao Act of 1992", and
implement its carabao milk production program. The PCC shall also conduct research and
development for other animals. It may also be referred to as the PCC-National Institute for
Animal Sciences.

“In addition to its existing mandates, the PCC shall develop and implement
strategies for other animals as specified under Section 6 of Republic Act No. 7307, WHICH
INCLUDE, BUT ARE NOT LIMITED TO, THE FOLLOWING:

A. INCREASE IN CARABAO POPULATION AND PRODUCTIVITY;

B. DEVELOPMENT OF FEEDING SYSTEMS FOR BACKYARD AND SEMI-
COMMERCIAL PRODUCTION, IMPROVING REPRODUCTIVE
EFFICIENCY, PHYSIOLOGY AND SCHEMES TO REDUCE LOSSES
CAUSED BY PARASITES AND DISEASES;

C. REPRODUCTION, BREEDING, NUTRITION AND ANIMAL HEALTH;

D. SOCIOECONOMIC RESEARCHES TO ENSURE ECONOMIC
VIABILITY AND ACCEPTANCE OF TECHNOLOGY BY FARMERS;
AND

E. DEVELOPMENT OF APPROPRIATE TECHNOLOGIES THAT ARE
COST-EFFECTIVE, SIMPLE AND PRACTICAL.

===== [page 4] =====
The PCC may also undertake animal vaccine and biologics research and
development.”

“(The existing PCC regional centers] THE RESEARCH AND
DEVELOPMENT STATIONS OF THE PCC SHALL BE (INCLUDE THOSE) located
at the: University of the Philippines Los Bafios (UPLB) in Laguna; Central Luzon State
University (CLSU) in Nueva Ecija; Cagayan State University (CSU) in Cagayan; Mariano
Marcos State University (MMSU) in Ilocos Norte; Don Mariano Marcos Memorial State
University (DMMMSU) in La Union; Central Bicol State University of Agriculture (CBSUA)
in Camarines Sur; CATANDUANES STATE UNIVERSITY (CATSU) IN
CATANDUANES; La Carlota Stock Farm (LCSF) in Negros Occidental; Ubat Stock Farm
(USF) in Bohol; Visayas State University (VSU) in Leyte; West Visayas State University
(WVSU) in Iloilo; Caraga State University (CARSU) in Butuan City; Central Mindanao
University (CMU) in Bukidnon; University of Southern Mindanao (USM) in North Cotabato;
and Mindanao Livestock Production Center (MLPC) in Zamboanga del Norte [shall be
converted to research and development stations of the PCC]. The existing regional centers
SHALL BE CONVERTED TO RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT STATION OF
THE PCC AND may [include] UNDERTAKE research and development on other animals.”

SEC. 2. The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included
in the annual General Appropriations Act;

SEC. 3. All laws, decrees, executive orders, presidential issuances and other
administrative rules and regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions

of this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,
