                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to establish a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve for the Republic of
the Philippines as a proactive measure to safeguard national economic
sovereignty and diversify our foreign reserves away from excessive
dependence on the US Dollar.

The Philippines faces a structural dilemma rooted in its heavy reliance on
the US Dollar. More than 80 percent of our international trade settlements,
overseas Filipino worker (OFW) remittances (which contribute roughly 9-
10 percent of GDP annually), and foreign exchange reserves are
denominated in USD. While the dollar has historically served as the global
reserve currency, the United States’ monetary and fiscal policies have
repeatedly demonstrated a willingness to print money out of thin air
through successive rounds of quantitative easing, massive deficit
spending, and unchecked expansion of the Federal Reserve’s balance
sheet. This practice has fueled persistent global inflation, eroded the real
purchasing power of the dollar, and exported monetary instability to
dollar-dependent economies like ours.

Every time the US prints trillions to finance its domestic priorities, the
value of our hard-earned dollar reserves declines in real terms. This
translates into higher costs for imported fuel, food, and raw materials,
reduced purchasing power for OFW families, and diminished returns on
the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas’ (BSP) reserve holdings. The Philippines is
essentially subsidizing American fiscal profligacy while bearing the
inflationary consequences without any say in US policy decisions.

Bitcoin, by contrast, is the only major asset with a verifiably fixed supply
of 21 million coins — a monetary policy enforced by unbreakable code

rather than political discretion. Its decentralized nature removes any
single government’s ability to debase it. Establishing a Strategic Bitcoin
Reserve will allow the Philippines to hedge against dollar depreciation,
capture long-term appreciation potential, and position the country as a
forward-looking digital-economy leader in Southeast Asia.

The proposed reserve will be managed transparently by the BSP under
strict custodial, security, and reporting standards. Initial funding will
come from a modest reallocation of BSP profits and future appropriations,
ensuring no immediate burden on the national budget. This is not
speculation; it is strategic national insurance against the known risks of
fiat-currency dependency.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly
requested.

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE STRATEGIC BITCOIN RESERVE OF THE
PHILIPPINES, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES


Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Strategic
Bitcoin Reserve Act of 2026.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to protect
the economic sovereignty and financial resilience of the Philippines by
diversifying its reserve assets beyond traditional fiat currencies. The State
recognizes the inherent dilemma of over-dependence on the US Dollar:
while the dollar dominates our trade, remittances, and foreign exchange
reserves, the United States’ repeated practice of expanding its money
supply through quantitative easing and deficit monetization has caused
chronic inflation that erodes the real value of Philippine holdings. This
external monetary risk threatens price stability, import affordability, and
the welfare of millions of Filipino families.

To mitigate this vulnerability, the State shall establish a Strategic Bitcoin
Reserve that leverages Bitcoin’s immutable 21-million-coin supply cap and
decentralized architecture as a hedge against fiat debasement and as a
long-term store of value for the nation.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. — For purposes of this Act:




                                  Page 3 of 4
(a) “Bitcoin” refers to the decentralized digital asset with the ticker symbol
BTC, operating on the Bitcoin blockchain protocol.
(b) “Strategic Bitcoin Reserve” or “Reserve” refers to the national stockpile
of Bitcoin established under this Act.
(c) “BSP” refers to the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas.

SECTION 4. Creation of the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve. — There is
hereby established the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve of the Philippines, to be
administered and managed by the BSP as a distinct component of the
country’s official reserves. The Reserve shall be held in cold-storage wallets
under BSP custody with multi-signature security protocols and regular
third-party audits.

SECTION 5. Acquisition and Funding. —
(a) The BSP is authorized to acquire Bitcoin using:
   (i) an initial appropriation of Ten Billion Pesos (₱10,000,000,000.00)
from the General Appropriations Act of 2027 and succeeding years;
   (ii) a portion of the BSP’s annual net profits as determined by the
Monetary Board; and
   (iii) such other funds as Congress may appropriate.
(b) The BSP shall adopt a disciplined acquisition strategy, including dollar-
cost averaging and opportunistic purchases during market corrections,
with the long-term objective of accumulating a minimum of ten thousand
(10,000) BTC within the first five years, subject to market conditions and
fiscal prudence.

SECTION 6. Management and Custody. — The BSP shall:
     (a) ensure the Reserve is stored offline in geographically diversified,
     high-security facilities;
     (b) prohibit lending, staking, or any leveraged use of Reserve
holdings;
     (c) publish quarterly holdings reports (wallet addresses, total BTC,
     and USD-equivalent valuation) to Congress and the public via the
     BSP website; and
     (d) conduct annual independent security and compliance audits.

SECTION 7. Restrictions on Disposal. — Bitcoin held in the Strategic
Reserve shall not be sold, transferred, or used as collateral except in a
declared national emergency and only upon prior approval by a joint
resolution of Congress. Any proceeds from authorized sales shall be
reinvested in the Reserve or used solely to stabilize the national economy.

SECTION 8. Oversight and Reporting. — The BSP shall submit an
annual report to the President, the Senate, and the House of




                                   Page 4 of 4
Representatives detailing the performance, risk assessment, and strategic
value of the Reserve. A joint Congressional Oversight Committee on the
Strategic Bitcoin Reserve shall be formed to monitor implementation.

SECTION 9. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The BSP, in
consultation with the Department of Finance and the Securities and
Exchange Commission, shall promulgate the necessary rules and
regulations within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.

SECTION 10. Appropriations. — The sum of Ten Billion Pesos
(₱10,000,000,000.00) is hereby appropriated from the National Treasury
for the initial capitalization of the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve. Additional
funding shall be included in subsequent General Appropriations Acts.

SECTION 11. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act is
declared unconstitutional, the remainder shall not be affected.

SECTION 12. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, and
issuances inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.

SECTION 13. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its complete publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2)
newspapers of general circulation.
