                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The Universal Financial Account Act seeks to dramatically expand financial inclusion
for Filipinos by establishing a zero‑balance Universal Financial Account (UFA) automatically
linked to each citizen’s PhilSys ID from birth, with privacy, security, and interoperability at
the core of its design. The measure is especially urgent given that around 44 percent of the
adult population—about 34.3 million Filipinos—remains unbanked, despite the share of adults
owning a financial‑account rising from 29 percent in 2019 to 56 percent in 2021, largely driven
by the COVID‑19‑induced shift toward digital payments.

        The policy explicitly targets these unbanked and underbanked segments by creating a
UFA for every Filipino at birth, designed to be interoperable across accredited banks and
e‑wallets and to support offline channels such as USSD so that even remote and
low‑connectivity areas can participate. By embedding the UFA within the national digital
infrastructure, the State aims to streamline the delivery of social protection programs,
healthcare subsidies, and cash transfers, thereby reducing the substantial leakage that plagues
many social assistance schemes; international and local studies on social protection suggest
that better targeting and digital‑account delivery can cut leakage and improve cost‑benefit
ratios, which aligns with the bill’s projected annual savings of ₱15–20 billion in reduced
leakage from social programs and an estimated rise in tax compliance yielding ₱25–35 billion
in additional government revenue per year.

         The Act likewise reinforces data privacy and cybersecurity, echoing broader regulatory
and policy concerns given the increasing number of financial‑account scams and
digital‑payment frauds in the Philippines. Under the measure, the newly created Universal
Financial Account Authority (UFAA), placed under the Department of Finance, will oversee
standards for interoperability, ensure that adequate cybersecurity and data‑protection controls
are in place, supervise the Financial Inclusion Subsidy Fund (FISF), and coordinate with the
Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and PhilSys. The FISF, funded through the General Appropriations
Act, donor grants, and other lawful sources, is intended to compensate banks and financial
institutions for the costs of maintaining zero‑balance accounts, which is critical because the
current unbanked population—if brought into the formal system—represents a large, untapped
segment of the “bankable” population whose inclusion could help triple the country’s banking
revenue pools by 2030.

      Implementation will proceed in phases over five to seven years, beginning with
newborns and prioritizing regions with the highest unbanked populations, which is consistent

      AN ACT ESTABLISHING A UNIVERSAL FINANCIAL ACCOUNT SYSTEM FOR
         ALL FILIPINOS, CREATING THE UNIVERSAL FINANCIAL ACCOUNT
           AUTHORITY, AND PROVIDING FUNDS AND MECHANISMS FOR
                              IMPLEMENTATION

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled:

1            SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Universal Financial Account
2    Act.”

 3            SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to financial
 4   inclusion by providing accessible banking services to all Filipinos, ensuring that every citizen, from
 5   birth, has the opportunity to participate in the formal financial system. The State shall facilitate the
 6   efficient delivery of social protection programs, healthcare services, and government subsidies
 7   through secure and interoperable digital financial accounts. Furthermore, the State recognizes the
 8   importance of strengthening the digital economy and enabling safe, convenient, and reliable digital
 9   transactions for all citizens. In implementing these measures, the State shall uphold the highest
10   standards of data privacy and cybersecurity to protect the financial information of its people.

11           SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

12               a) Universal Financial Account (UFA) - refers to a zero-balance digital account
13                  automatically created for every Filipino at birth, linked to their PhilSys ID;
14               b) PhilSys ID - The official national identification number issued under Republic Act
15                  No. 11055;
16               c) Universal Financial Account Authority (UFAA) - refers to the government body
17                  responsible for implementing, regulating, and supervising the UFA system;
18               d) Financial Inclusion Subsidy Fund (FISF) - refers to the fund to support banks and
19                  financial institutions for costs associated with maintaining zero-balance accounts;
20               e) Interoperability - refers to the ability of the UFA to conduct transactions across
21                  different banks, e-wallets, and financial platforms.

22            SEC. 4. Creation of Universal Financial Accounts. - Every Filipino shall be automatically
23   provided a zero-balance Universal Financial Account linked to the PhilSys ID, with optional opt-
24   out, as follows:
                                                                                                           1
1               a) Every Filipino shall be automatically provided a Universal Financial Account at
2                  birth.
3               b) The UFA shall be linked to the PhilSys ID and shall be interoperable across
4                  accredited banks and e-wallets.
5               c) Accounts shall have zero balance upon creation and shall include offline transaction
6                  capabilities (e.g., USSD-based services) for unbanked or remote areas.
7               d) Citizens shall have the option to opt-out of the UFA system, following guidelines
8                  set by the UFAA.

 9           SEC. 5. Universal Financial Account Authority (UFAA). - The Universal Financial
10   Account Authority under the Department of Finance is created to oversee UFAs and related
11   functions, as follows:

12              a) There is hereby created the Universal Financial Account Authority, under the
13                 Department of Finance, which shall oversee the implementation, security, and
14                 regulation of all UFAs.
15              b) The UFAA shall:
16                    i)   Establish standards for interoperability across financial institutions;
17                   ii)   Ensure data privacy and cybersecurity measures are in place;
18                  iii)   Supervise the disbursement and management of the Financial Inclusion
19                         Subsidy Fund;
20                  iv)    Coordinate with the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), PhilSys, and other
21                         relevant agencies.

22          SEC. 6. Financial Inclusion Subsidy Fund (FISF). - The Financial Inclusion Subsidy
23   Fund is established to support banks maintaining zero-balance accounts and shall be sourced as
24   follows:

25              a) The FISF is hereby established to provide subsidies to banks and financial
26                 institutions maintaining zero-balance accounts under this Act.
27              b) The Fund shall be sourced from the General Appropriations Act, donor grants, and
28                 any other lawful sources.

29           SEC. 7. Phased Implementation. - The UFAA shall implement the UFA system in phases
30   over five to seven years, prioritizing high-unbanked regions, as follows:

31              a) The UFAA shall implement the UFA system in a phased approach over five (5) to
32                 seven (7) years, starting with newborns.
33              b) The UFAA shall prioritize regions with the highest unbanked populations.

34          SEC. 8. Financial Literacy and Public Awareness. - The UFAA shall implement
35   nationwide financial literacy programs to educate citizens on the use, benefits, and security
36   measures of their Universal Financial Accounts.

37           SEC. 9. Appropriations. - The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act,

39   (₱23,000,000,000.00), shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act for the purpose
