                                               AN ACT
AUTHORIZING STATE UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES TO APPOINT
DISTINGUISHED FOREIGN NATIONALS TO TEACHING AND RESEARCH
POSITIONS AND GRANT THEM ACADEMIC TENURE UNDER EXCEPTIONAL
CONDITIONS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
                                     EXPLANATORY NOTE

        The proposed bill seeks to strengthen the quality and global competitiveness of Philippine
public higher education while preserving the State’s fundamental commitment to the development
of Filipino scholars, researchers, and academic leaders.

        State Universities and Colleges (SUCs) play a vital role in advancing national development
through education, research, innovation, and the formation of highly skilled professionals. As the
demands of science, technology, and emerging academic disciplines continue to evolve, there may
be instances when institutions require expertise that is highly specialized, newly developing, or not
yet sufficiently available within the country. In such circumstances, carefully structured engagement
with distinguished foreign academics may provide opportunities for collaboration, knowledge
exchange, research advancement, and exposure to international standards and practices.

        The measure is therefore anchored on the principle that the participation of foreign academic
experts should be exceptional, complementary, and directed toward strengthening Philippine
academic capacity. It does not seek to diminish the role of Filipino faculty. Rather, it recognizes that
international academic collaboration can serve as a strategic means of accelerating the development
of Filipino scholars and researchers, particularly in fields where expertise is rapidly advancing or
where domestic capacity remains in the process of being established.

         At the same time, the proposed legislation recognizes the constitutional and institutional
importance of Filipino participation in government service and public higher education. The
engagement of foreign nationals in the academic sector must consequently remain consistent with
national sovereignty, public accountability, and the primacy of Filipino academic development. The
measure adopts this approach by treating foreign academic expertise not as a substitute for Filipino
talent, but as a potential catalyst for building stronger and more sustainable domestic capabilities.

         The proposed framework is also intended to promote a culture of academic excellence and
internationalization within SUCs. Greater interaction with internationally recognized scholars can
facilitate meaningful research partnerships, strengthen academic programs, expand opportunities for
Filipino faculty and students, and contribute to the development of globally competitive institutions.
Such engagement may likewise help Philippine public universities participate more effectively in
international research networks and respond to emerging areas of knowledge that are increasingly
important to national development.

        Ultimately, the measure seeks to establish a balanced policy: one that welcomes exceptional
international academic expertise when genuinely beneficial to the country, while ensuring that the
long-term objective remains the cultivation of world-class Filipino faculty, researchers, and
academic leaders. The State’s investment in Filipino higher education must remain central, and any
international engagement should contribute to, rather than compete with, that national priority.

        The proposed Act thus represents an effort to reconcile the constitutional character of public
service with the realities of an increasingly interconnected global academic community. By creating
a principled framework for exceptional international academic participation, the measure aims to
enhance the capacity of Philippine public higher education to generate knowledge, foster innovation,
develop Filipino talent, and ultimately contribute to the country's social, scientific, and economic
progress.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

                                                    AN ACT
     AUTHORIZING STATE UNIVERSITIES AND COLLEGES TO APPOINT
     DISTINGUISHED FOREIGN NATIONALS TO TEACHING AND RESEARCH
     POSITIONS AND GRANT THEM ACADEMIC TENURE UNDER EXCEPTIONAL

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:
 1   SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Foreign Academic Faculty Tenure Act."
 2   SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to:
 3      (a) Uphold the constitutional principle that government service is primarily reserved for Filipino
 4          citizens and governed by merit and fitness, as essential to national sovereignty and
 5          democratic accountability, and to permit exceptions thereto only upon a showing of
 6          compelling State interest and under exceptional and confined circumstances;
 7      (b) Promote the highest standards of academic excellence in public higher education as an
 8          engine of national development, scientific progress, and social advancement, and to support
 9          State Universities and Colleges (SUCs) in attaining and sustaining world-class research and
10          teaching quality;
11      (c) Affirm that Filipino scholars, researchers, and academics are the foundation and future of
12          Philippine higher education, and that the foremost obligation of SUCs is to educate Filipino
13          students, strengthen nationally-rooted research capacity, and invest in the growth and
14          development of Filipino faculty and academic leadership;
15      (d) Authorize the engagement of distinguished foreign nationals in SUCs, in a structured and
16          accountable manner, where their expertise in frontier, emerging, or highly specialized fields
17          would materially advance the institution's academic mission, accelerate the development of
18          Filipino scholars and researchers, and contribute to national development goals that the
19          institution is actively and ambitiously pursuing; and
20      (e) Ensure that all engagement of foreign nationals in SUCs is genuinely complementary to, and
21          actively reinforces, the State's sustained commitment to Filipino faculty development,
22          research investment, and the cultivation of world-class Filipino academic leadership.
23   SEC. 3. Authority to Appoint Foreign Nationals to Tenured Positions. – An SUC is hereby
24   authorized to appoint a qualified foreign national to a tenured teaching or research position. Such
25   authority is exceptional in character and may be exercised only upon satisfaction of the
26   qualifications under Section 6, the necessity test under Section 7, the non-displacement safeguards
27   under Section 8, and all other conditions prescribed in this Act. The authority to appoint shall be
28   subject to prior approval by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) as a condition to the
29   effectivity of any such appointment.
30   Such appointment shall be confined exclusively to teaching, research, mentoring, and scholarly
31   functions and shall not confer eligibility for administrative, governance, or policy-making roles, nor
32   shall it constitute civil service tenure or a permanent appointment within the meaning of local civil
33   service laws. Foreign faculty appointments under this Act are intended to complement and
34   strengthen the SUC's Filipino academic workforce. The appointing institution shall bear the
35   responsibility of establishing, at each stage of the process, that the conditions prescribed in this Act
36   are met.
37   SEC. 4. Scope and Application. – This Act shall apply exclusively to:
38      (a) SUCs as defined under Republic Act No. 8292, otherwise known as the Higher Education
39          Modernization Act of 1997, meaning institutions created by law, funded wholly or
40          substantially by the national government, and vested with the mandate to provide public
41          higher education; and
42      (b) Teaching and research faculty positions whose primary functions consist of classroom
43          instruction, scholarly research, curriculum development, or academic mentoring, and which
44          involve no administrative authority, budgetary control, personnel supervision, or policy-
45          making power.
46   All administrative, executive, managerial, governance, and policy-determining positions are
47   expressly excluded from coverage and shall remain reserved exclusively to Filipino citizens.
48   SEC. 5. Nature and Limits of Academic Tenure. – Teaching and research positions covered by
49   this Act are academic and professional in character, confined to instruction, scholarly inquiry,
50   research, curriculum development, and the dissemination of knowledge. They do not involve the
51   exercise of governmental authority, the formulation of public policy, or the control and
52   administration of the institution, which remain vested in Filipino citizens at all times. This
53   characterization applies solely for purposes of this Act and does not relax any other legal,
54   immigration, labor, or regulatory requirement applicable to foreign nationals under existing laws.
55   Academic tenure under this Act shall mean the conferment of security of academic employment
56   based on merit, demonstrated competence, sustained performance, and the protection of academic
57   freedom essential to teaching and research. Such tenure shall not confer civil service eligibility,
58   plantilla permanence, or any vested right to appointment, promotion, or designation to any
59   administrative or executive position. Academic tenure granted hereunder shall remain subject to
60   periodic evaluation, compliance with institutional and CHED performance standards, and adherence
61   to professional and ethical norms.
62   SEC. 6. Qualifications for Appointment. – A foreign national may be appointed under this Act only
63   upon a clear, convincing, and documented showing of exceptional merit and only if all of the
64   following qualifications are satisfied:
65      (a) The applicant holds a terminal degree from a globally recognized institution of higher
66          learning, or an equivalent professional standing duly recognized in the relevant discipline,
67          supported by evidence of international distinction, peer recognition, or acknowledged
68          leadership in the field;
69      (b) The applicant demonstrates a sustained record of exceptional scholarly, scientific, or
70          professional achievement of international caliber, including high-impact peer-reviewed
71          publications, patents, significant innovations, or other contributions recognized by reputable
72          international academic or professional bodies;
73      (c) The appointment is in a discipline, specialization, or field of study where domestic academic
74          expertise is not yet fully established, is rapidly expanding beyond current institutional
75          capacity, or where the field is newly emerging, highly technical, or evolving at a pace that
76          creates a genuine opportunity for international collaboration to accelerate Philippine
77          academic development in that area; and
78      (d) The applicant makes a contractual commitment, as a condition of appointment and continued
79          tenure, to meaningful and structured academic collaboration, including mentoring of Filipino
80          faculty and graduate students, joint research, curriculum development, and the active
81          nurturing of the next generation of Filipino scholars in the relevant field.
82   SEC. 7. Test of Exceptional Necessity. – No appointment under this Act shall be approved unless
83   the appointing SUC establishes, through clear and contemporaneous documentation, all of the
84   following:
85      (a) The specific academic or research need sought to be addressed cannot be fully met through
86          currently available domestic expertise, notwithstanding the institution's genuine and
87          continuing efforts to develop, recruit, and retain Filipino faculty through available faculty
88          development programs, scholarships, and fellowships;
 89      (b) The foreign national's expertise, credentials, or scholarly record is of a caliber that would
 90          materially advance the institution's academic or research mission in ways that would
 91          significantly accelerate the institution's development in the relevant field; and
 92      (c) The appointment is reasonably expected to produce measurable, verifiable, and time-bound
 93          advances in academic quality, research productivity, or the development of Filipino faculty
 94          and scholars, including enhanced curricula, increased research output, or demonstrable
 95          acceleration of institutional capacity in the relevant area.
 96   SEC. 8. Non-Displacement of Filipino Faculty. – No appointment under this Act shall be approved
 97   without a formal written certification by the appointing SUC, concurred in by CHED, affirming that:
 98      (a) No qualified Filipino faculty member is or will be displaced, demoted, or denied
 99          appointment, promotion, or assignment as a result of the engagement of a foreign national,
100          and that the institution has made and continues to make good-faith efforts to recruit, develop,
101          and retain Filipino faculty for positions within its academic programs;
102      (b) The institution's investments in Filipino faculty development, including scholarships,
103          fellowships, training programs, research funding, and career advancement pathways, are
104          maintained or expanded, and that the engagement of a foreign national does not redirect
105          resources that would otherwise support the professional growth of Filipino academic
106          personnel; and
107      (c) The engagement is structured and expected to make a genuine contribution to the
108          development of Filipino academic leaders and researchers, including through collaborative
109          research, mentoring, curriculum development, and the active growth of Filipino scholarly
110          capacity in the relevant field.
111   SEC. 9. Prohibition on Administrative and Governance Functions. – Foreign nationals appointed
112   under this Act are prohibited from engaging in, assuming, or exercising any administrative,
113   executive, or governance function in a SUC. No such foreign national shall:
114      (a) Hold or be designated to any position of institutional leadership or management, including
115          university president, vice-president, chancellor, dean, director, department chair, program
116          head, or any equivalent post, whether permanent, acting, interim, or concurrent;
117      (b) Exercise authority over institutional budgets, financial planning, procurement, personnel
118          appointments, promotions, discipline, or other matters of institutional governance or
119          administration;
120      (c) Vote or participate as a decision-maker in governing boards, councils, or committees vested
121          with policy-making or institutional decision-making authority; or
122      (d) Represent the SUC in official dealings with government agencies, constitutional bodies,
123          foreign governments, or international organizations.
124   Any act in violation of this section shall be void. Officials responsible for acts of designation or
125   appointment in contravention of this section shall be subject to administrative and disciplinary
126   sanctions in accordance with applicable laws.
127   SEC. 10. Periodic Review of Continued Necessity. – Academic tenure granted under this Act shall
128   be subject to a periodic review of continued necessity as a condition for its continuation. CHED
129   shall prescribe the intervals for such review by regulation, but in no case less frequently than once
130   every five (5) years. Each review shall require documented evidence that:
131      (a) The foreign national continues to satisfy the qualifications and conditions prescribed under
132          this Act and has fulfilled the commitments undertaken upon appointment, including
133          collaborative and mentoring obligations;
134      (b) The academic or research circumstances that warranted the original appointment remain
135          present, and the appointment continues to serve the institution's academic mission and
136          national development objectives; and
137      (c) Measurable progress has been achieved in advancing Filipino scholarly capacity, faculty
138          development, and institutional academic strength in the relevant field as a result of the
139          engagement.
140   Where CHED or the appointing institution determines that the circumstances that warranted the
141   original appointment no longer apply, or that the institution's academic needs in the relevant area
142   can now be fully served by Filipino faculty, the continuation of academic tenure shall be denied or
143   withdrawn in accordance with due process. The appointing institution and the foreign national shall
144   bear the responsibility of demonstrating the continuing basis for the appointment.
145   SEC. 11. Oversight, Regulation, and Enforcement. – CHED shall exercise primary regulatory and
146   oversight authority over the implementation of this Act. CHED shall:
147      (a) Issue comprehensive guidelines governing eligibility, appointment, tenure standards,
148          performance evaluation, periodic review, and termination, including uniform criteria,
149          documentary requirements, and timelines applicable across all SUCs;
150      (b) Evaluate and act on institutional applications for the appointment of foreign faculty, based
151          on a rigorous and consistent assessment of academic necessity, distinguished merit, and
152          compliance with all requirements under this Act;
153      (c) Establish and maintain a centralized national registry of foreign nationals appointed under
154          this Act, containing information on qualifications, fields of specialization, appointment
155          duration, renewal status, and compliance with collaborative and knowledge-transfer
156          commitments, subject to applicable data privacy laws;
157      (d) Monitor and evaluate compliance by SUCs with all requirements and conditions imposed
158          under this Act, including authority to conduct reviews, require documentary submissions,
159          issue corrective guidance, withdraw approval for non-compliant appointments, and
160          recommend administrative action against erring officials; and
161      (e) Submit to Congress an annual report on the implementation of this Act, including the number
162          and distribution of foreign faculty appointments, disciplines and institutions covered,
163          academic outcomes achieved, and contributions to Filipino faculty development and
164          institutional research capacity.
165   In the exercise of its oversight functions, CHED may impose graduated sanctions proportionate to
166   the nature and gravity of any violation, including: issuance of notices of non-compliance and
167   directives for corrective action; suspension or revocation of appointment approvals; suspension of
168   authority to seek further appointments for a specified period; recommendation of administrative
169   sanctions against responsible officials; and referral of matters involving fraud or misrepresentation
170   to the appropriate authorities. Due process, including notice and a reasonable opportunity to be
171   heard, shall be observed in all enforcement proceedings.
172   SEC. 12. Coordination with the Civil Service Commission. – CHED shall coordinate with the Civil
173   Service Commission (CSC) in implementing this Act, including: the joint development of guidelines
174   to clearly distinguish academic teaching and research positions covered by this Act from civil
175   service positions that require Filipino citizenship; the exchange of relevant information on
176   appointments and personnel actions involving foreign nationals in SUCs; and the conduct of
177   coordinated reviews where questions of position classification arise. In case of ambiguity or conflict
178   in the classification of a position, the applicable rules shall be determined in accordance with the
179   constitutional mandate, with the civil service framework governing unless the position clearly and
180   unequivocally falls within the academic exception established under this Act. Nothing herein shall
181   diminish the constitutional authority of the CSC over the civil service.
182   SEC. 14. Sunset Review. – Five (5) years after effectivity, CHED shall conduct a comprehensive
183   evidence-based review of the implementation and impact of this Act. The review shall assess
184   academic and institutional outcomes, including contributions to Filipino faculty development,
185   research capacity, and the overall advancement of Philippine public higher education. The review
186   shall also examine the effectiveness of safeguards, oversight mechanisms, and agency coordination
187   established under this Act. CHED shall thereafter submit to Congress a report containing its findings
188   and specific recommendations on whether the authority granted under this Act should be continued,
189   modified, or allowed to expire.
190   SEC. 14. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from effectivity, CHED,
191   in consultation with SUCs, the CSC, the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), the Bureau
192   of Immigration (BI), and such other agencies as may be relevant, shall promulgate the implementing
193   rules and regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.
194   SEC. 15. Separability Clause. - If any part or provision of this Act which shall be held to be invalid
195   or unconstitutional, other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to
196   be in force and effect.
197   SEC. 16. Repealing Clause. – All other laws, decrees, ordinances, administrative order, rules and
198   regulations, or any part thereof, which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or amended
199   accordingly.
200   SEC. 17. Construction. - In the interpretation of this law, the best interest of the child shall prevail.
