        Global oil price increases directly affect domestic fuel prices and impose additional
costs on transportation, electricity, and basic goods. Because the Philippines relies heavily on
imported petroleum, mechanisms that allow timely relief during periods of unusually high oil
prices are necessary.

        At present, excise taxes on petroleum products under the National Internal Revenue
Code remain fixed regardless of prevailing global oil prices. During periods of unusually high
crude prices, a temporary suspension or reduction of these taxes may help mitigate the burden
on the public.

        This bill amends Section 148 of the National Internal Revenue Code to allow the
President, upon the recommendation of the Development Budget Coordination Committee, to
suspend or reduce excise taxes on fuel when the average Dubai crude oil price reaches or
exceeds eighty dollars per barrel. The suspension shall automatically be lifted once the price
falls below the threshold or after one year.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought

           AMENDED, ALLOWING THE AUTOMATIC SUSPENSION OF EXCISE TAX OF FUELS

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
     assembled.

 3   SEC. 1. Section 148 of the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC), as amended, is hereby
 4   further amended to read as follows:

 9         RECOMMENDATION OF THE DEVELOPMENT BUDGET COORDINATION COMMITTEE,

14         REDUCTION OF EXCISE TAXES SHALL BE AUTOMATICALLY LIFTED WHEN THE ABOVE
