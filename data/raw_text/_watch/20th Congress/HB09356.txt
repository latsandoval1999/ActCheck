                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

     The State recognizes that road networks that connect provincial
boundaries to key urban corridors are essential to regional integration,
mobility, and economic activity. Such roads facilitate the continuous
movement of goods and people across jurisdictions and strengthen linkages
between neighboring provinces and cities.

       The Misamis Oriental–Maria Cristina Boundary Road to Iligan City
Diversion Road serves as a vital connector between the boundary of Misamis
Oriental and the urban road network of Iligan City. Extending from the
vicinity of El Centro along Quezon Avenue in Barangay Poblacion to Pala-o
Bridge, the road provides a direct linkage to a major diversion route,
supporting both local traffic and inter-provincial movement.

      The road has been previously validated for conversion into a national
road, recognizing its functional importance within the transport network.
However, it remains under local jurisdiction, limiting its integration into the
broader national system and constraining sustained development and
maintenance.

       Given its role in linking provincial and city road networks and
supporting traffic flow toward a major diversion corridor, the conversion of
this road into a national road is necessary. This will enable the Department
of Public Works and Highways to ensure its continued improvement,
maintenance, and compliance with national standards.
     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
CONVERTING THE MISAMIS ORIENTAL–MARIA CRISTINA BOUNDARY
 ROAD TO ILIGAN CITY DIVERSION ROAD, FROM EL CENTRO ALONG
  QUEZON AVENUE IN BARANGAY POBLACION TO PALA-O BRIDGE
  IN ILIGAN CITY, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING
                        FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Misamis
Oriental–Maria Cristina Boundary to Iligan City Diversion National
Road Act.”

     SECTION 2. Conversion. — The Misamis Oriental–Maria Cristina
Boundary Road to Iligan City Diversion Road, from El Centro along Quezon
Avenue in Barangay Poblacion to Pala-o Bridge in Iligan City, is hereby
converted into a national road under the supervision and control of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

      SECTION 3. Appropriations. —The Secretary of Public Works and
Highways shall include in the Department’s program the improvement,
repair, and maintenance of the said road, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 4. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
