                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to define and institutionalize the classification, registration,
and operation of all types of electric motor vehicles (EMVs) in the Philippines in
order to establish a clear, comprehensive, and responsive regulatory framework that
promotes innovation, ensures road safety, and supports sustainable transportation.

       The rapid emergence of electric vehicles—including e-bikes, e-tricycles,
e-scooters, and other electric mobility devices—has transformed the country’s
transport landscape. However, the absence of a unified legal and regulatory
framework has resulted in inconsistent policies, confusion among users and
regulators, and gaps in enforcement. This measure addresses these issues by
standardizing vehicle classifications, prescribing appropriate operational guidelines,
and streamlining registration processes under the Land Transportation Office (LTO).

       The proposed measure establishes corresponding rules on where these
vehicles may operate, the requirements for driver licensing and registration, and
safety standards such as the mandatory use of protective helmets. By doing so, the
bill ensures that electric vehicles are properly integrated into the existing transport
system while safeguarding all road users.

        By encouraging the adoption of electric mobility, this measure contributes to
lowering transportation costs, enhancing energy security, and mitigating
environmental impacts. This bill advances the State’s commitment to a modern,
efficient, and sustainable transportation system that is adaptive to technological
developments while remaining anchored on safety, accountability, and public
welfare.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
DEFINING THE CLASSIFICATION, REGISTRATION, AND OPERATION OF
ALL TYPES OF ELECTRIC MOTOR VEHICLES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Electric Motor Vehicle
Registration Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State that
its goals of national economy comprise equitable distribution of opportunities,
income, and wealth; a sustained increase in the amount of goods and services
produced by the nation for the benefit of the people; and an expanding productivity
as the key to raising the quality of life for all. It is in this regard that pursuant to
Republic Act (RA) No. 4136, otherwise known as the “Land Transportation and Traffic
Code”, the Land Transportation Office (LTO) is mandated to register roadworthy
motor vehicles prior to its use or operation on or upon any highway of the
Philippines to contribute to infrastructure development for the benefit of all road
users as well as to increase regulation for road safety.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

           a. Accreditation refers to the authority granted by LTO for Manufacturer,
       Assembler, Importer, Rebuilder and/or Dealer to transact with former
       relative to stock reporting, sales reporting and registration of motor vehicles
       and/or its components;

          b. Category refers to vehicle category as specified in the Philippine
       National Standards (PNS) on Road Vehicles-Classification and Definition PNS
       1891:2008 (UN ECE Published 2017) ICS 43.040.01;

           c. Curb Weight refers to the total unloaded mass of a vehicle with
       standard equipment and all necessary operating consumables such as fluids,
       oils, coolant, refrigerant and batteries in the case of electric vehicles;

      wear a protective helmet similar to those designed for bicycle riders. No

        SEC. 6. Requirements for Classification and Initial Registration of New Model
Electric Vehicle. – To ensure compliance to the standards prescribed above and for
proper classification pursuant to Section 5 of Republic Act No. 8794, entitled “An
Act Imposing a Motor Vehicle User’s Charge on Owners of All Types of Motor Vehicles and
For Other Purposes”, as amended, all manufacturers, assemblers and/or importers of
electric vehicles shall, not later than three (3) months prior to the introduction of any
new model in the market, submit the specifications of such model to the LTO which
shall determine the classification and the rate of MVUC which the new model shall
fall.

        The requirements for the classification of new models of electric vehicles and
its initial registration shall be in accordance with the requirements per existing
policies, rules, and regulations on the registration of motor vehicles except for
emission requisites.

          a.​ Requirements for Classification

                 1.​ Letter-request for classification;

                 2.​ Specification of the electric vehicle;

                 3.​ Photos of the vehicle submitted for classification; and

                 4.​ Motor Vehicle Inspection Report from LTO Motor Vehicle
              Inspection Center (MVIC).

       SEC. 7. Plate and Special Lane for Registration and Recording. – Plates and
stickers of electric vehicles shall follow the specifications to be adopted by the LTO.

       The LTO shall designate special lanes for the registration of electric vehicles
from the effectivity of this Act. In offices where designation of special lanes is not
feasible, electric vehicle transactions shall be assigned at Senior Citizen/Persons
with Disability (PWD) lanes.

        SEC. 8. Manufacturers, Assemblers, Importers, and Dealers (MAIDs) of Electric
Vehicles. – All MAIDs of electric vehicles shall apply and comply with the
accreditation requirements of the LTO per existing rules and regulations. MAIDs of
electric vehicles which do not require registration shall not be accredited by the
LTO.

        SEC. 9. Penalties. – Road violations of Electric Motor Vehicle shall be punished
as follows:

          a.​ For not wearing helmet (driver/rider/backrider), a fine of One
       thousand and five hundred pesos (P1,500.00);

          b.​ For operating in restricted roads (Obstruction), a fine of One thousand
       pesos (P1,000.00);

           c.​ Failure to carry official receipt and certificate of registration (OR/CR),
       a fine of One thousand pesos (P1,000.00);

          d.​ Unregistered/Expired Vehicle Registration, a fine of Ten thousand
       pesos (P10,000.00); and

           e.​ Other applicable penalties under Department of Transportation and
       Communications Joint Administrative Order (DOTC JAO 2014-01) which fine
       is discretionary to the administrative agency taking into consideration all the
       attending circumstances.
