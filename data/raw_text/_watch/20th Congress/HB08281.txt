                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to recognize the historical significance of the founding of the Municipality of
Capoocan by declaring November 23 of every year a special non-working holiday in the
Municipality of Capoocan, Province of Leyte.

Capoocan, Leyte derives its name from the local dialect term mapu-uk, meaning “obstructed,”
which reflects its geographic setting, being hemmed in by Mount Minoro and historically shielded
from severe typhoons. The municipality traces its official creation to November 23, 1917, when
Executive Order No. 87 was issued by Governor-General Francis Burton Harrison, separating
Capoocan from the Municipality of Carigara. The order took effect on January 1, 1918, granting
Capoocan full municipal status.

Today, with a population of 33,721 as recorded in the 2020 Census of Population, the municipality
continues to thrive, shaped by its maritime and agricultural heritage, drawing sustenance from
Carigara Bay and its fertile inland lands.

Declaring November 23 of every year as a special non-working holiday in Capoocan will
appropriately commemorate its foundation, honor its rich history and cultural identity, and foster
greater pride and unity among its residents.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT DECLARING NOVEMBER 23 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAPOOCAN, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, IN
COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. November 23 of every year is hereby declared a special non-working holiday
in the Municipality of Capoocan, Province of Leyte, in commemoration of its Foundation Day,
marking the issuance on November 23, 1917 of Executive Order No. 87 by Governor-General
Francis Burton Harrison, which ordered its separation from the Municipality of Carigara,
effective January 1, 1918;

SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
