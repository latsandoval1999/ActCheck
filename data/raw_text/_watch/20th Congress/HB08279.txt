                                   EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to recognize the importance of the Municipality’s founding anniversary by
declaring May 12 of every year a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Julita,
Province of Leyte.

The Municipality of Julita, located in the Province of Leyte, owes its formal establishment to
Executive Order No. 278, series of 1949, which created the municipality in response to the
growing need for a more focused and effective system of local governance in the area. This
milestone not only recognized Julita as an independent administrative unit but also enabled its
residents to directly manage their local affairs, resources, and development priorities. Since its
founding, Julita has evolved into a steadily progressing municipality, marked by improvements
in public services, agricultural productivity, community cooperation, and socio-economic
initiatives. The annual commemoration of its founding anniversary is an important opportunity
for the local government and its people to reflect on the historical events that shaped Julita and
to acknowledge the contributions of the leaders and citizens who worked to build a strong and
cohesive community.

Declaring May 12 of every year as a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of
Julita will allow residents to more fully engage in civic and cultural activities that highlight the
municipality’s heritage, achievements, and aspirations. This observance will help
institutionalize historical awareness among the younger generation, foster pride in Julita’s
identity, and strengthen the community’s sense of continuity and shared purpose.

In lieu of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is sought.

AN ACT DECLARING MAY 12 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING
HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF JULITA, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, IN
COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. May 12 of every year is hereby declared as a special non-working holiday in
the Municipality of Julita, Province of Leyte.

SECTION 2. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
