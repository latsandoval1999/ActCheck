                                    EXPLANATORY NOTE

Sports bring Filipinos together, inspire national pride, and showcase the country's talent and
determination on the world stage. Every time a Filipino athlete competes internationally, whether
in the Olympic Games, World Championships, or regional sporting events, they carry not only our
flag but also the hopes and aspirations of the nation. Through their discipline, perseverance, and
sacrifices, they become symbols of Filipino excellence and resilience.

Yet behind every medal and victory is a story of years of relentless training, physical hardship,
and personal sacrifice. While these athletes dedicate the best years of their lives to representing
the country, many face uncertainty once their competitive careers come to an end. After retirement,
some struggle with financial difficulties, lingering injuries, limited employment opportunities, and
inadequate access to healthcare. Despite the recognition they receive during their athletic careers,
many are left without sufficient long-term support.

Although Republic Act No. 10699, otherwise known as the National Athletes and Coaches
Benefits and Incentives Act, provides cash incentives and benefits for outstanding athletic
achievements, it does not establish a comprehensive system that ensures the continued welfare,
health, and dignity of our national athletes after retirement.

By passing this measure, the State affirms that the service and sacrifices of our athletes do not end
when they leave the playing field. Those who have devoted their lives to bringing honor and glory
to the Philippines deserve more than applause and medals they deserve lasting security, dignity,
and the assurance that the nation they proudly represented will continue to stand by them for the
rest of their lives.

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.



REP. ELISA T. KHO, M.D., FPCCP

AN ACT GRANTING DISTINCTIVE HONORS AND PRIVILEGES TO NATIONAL
ATHLETES, CREATING A NATIONAL ENDOWMENT FUND FOR SPORTS HEROES,
AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the "Bayaning Atletang Pilipino Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is the policy of the State to promote physical education
and encourage sports programs to foster self-discipline, teamwork, and excellence for the
development of a healthy and alert citizenry. The State further recognizes that athletes who achieve
international distinction serve as inspirations to the youth and ambassadors of goodwill, and are
therefore entitled to distinctive state honors, social security protection, and lifetime privileges
commensurate with their contributions to national prestige.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act:

       (a) National Athlete refers to any Filipino citizen, athlete, or sports icon who is a member
       of the national training pool and has officially represented the Philippines in international
       sports competitions sanctioned by the International Olympic Committee (IOC), the
       Philippine Olympic Committee (POC), or the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC).

       (b) Sports Heroes refers to national athletes who have won gold, silver, or bronze medals
       in the Olympic Games, World Championships, Asian Games, Southeast Asian (SEA)
       Games, or equivalent world-class elite sports events.

       (c) Distinctive honors refers to state recognized awards, medals of honor, and ceremonial
       titles bestowed upon elite sports figures.

SECTION 4. Distinctive Honors and the Order of National Athletes. – There is hereby created the
Order of National Athletes (Orden ng mga Bayaning Atleta), which shall serve as the highest state
recognition bestowed upon retired or active Filipino sports heroes. The award shall be jointly
administered by the PSC, the POC, and the National Commission for Culture and the Arts
(NCCA), conferred annually by the President of the Philippines.

SECTION 5. Lifetime Privileges and Benefits. – In addition to existing incentives under Republic
Act No. 10699, all accredited sports heroes shall be entitled to the following lifetime privileges:

       (a) Comprehensive Healthcare- Automatic enrollment in a premium, fully subsidized
       National Health Insurance Program (PhilHealth) bracket, alongside a supplemental lifetime
       medical care allowance to cover sport-related injuries and chronic illnesses.

       (b) Lifelong Pension- A monthly, tax-free retirement pension scheme starting at the age of
       forty-five (45) or upon formal retirement from the national pool, the baseline amount of
       which shall be determined by the PSC based on medals won.

       (c) Dependent Education Grants- Full tertiary education tuition scholarships in any state
       university or college (SUC) for up to two (2) legitimate or recognized dependents of the
       sports hero.

       (d) Livelihood and Employment Assistance- Mandatory technical-vocational or
       entrepreneurial training via TESDA, and priority hiring in government sports development
       agencies and public academic institutions as coaches, consultants, or athletic directors.

SECTION 6. Creation of the National Endowment Fund for Sports Heroes. – There is hereby
established a permanent trust fund to be known as the National Endowment Fund for Sports Heroes
(the "Fund"), which shall be used exclusively to finance the retirement benefits, pensions, medical
emergency grants, and death benefits of accredited national sports heroes.

The Fund shall be managed by a Board of Trustees chaired by the Chairperson of the PSC, with
representatives from the Department of Finance (DOF), GSIS, POC, and two (2) retired national
athletes.

SECTION 7. Funding Sources. – The Fund shall be constituted from the following sources:

       (a) An initial seed capital of Five Hundred Million Pesos (₱500,000,000.00) to be
       appropriated from the National Treasury;

       (b) Ten percent (10%) of the gross annual income share of the national government from
       the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR), which shall be
       automatically remitted directly to the Fund;

       (c) Donations, endowments, and grants from local or international private entities, which
       shall be declared tax-deductible under the National Internal Revenue Code.

SECTION 8. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within sixty (60) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the PSC, in coordination with the Games and Amusements Board (GAB),
the POC, the DBM, and the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), shall promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary to implement this Act.

SECTION 9. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 10. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed,
amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
