                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

Many Filipinos delay or forego medical consultations and diagnostic examinations due
to the cost of healthcare services. This often results in the late detection of illnesses,
higher medical expenses, and increased risk of complications that could have been
prevented through regular check-ups and timely medical intervention.

Preventive healthcare is essential in building a healthier nation. Regular medical
check-ups allow early identification of health risks, promote healthier lifestyles, and
enable appropriate treatment before illnesses progress into more serious conditions.
Strengthening preventive health services will not only improve individual health
outcomes but also reduce the long-term burden on families and the country’s
healthcare system. More importantly, early detection and proper medical intervention
can help save lives by identifying serious health conditions at a stage when treatment
is more effective and recovery is more attainable.

Therefore, this bill seeks to institutionalize the Libreng Check-Up Program by providing
every Filipino with access to a free annual medical check-up through the Philippine
Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth). The program shall cover general physical
examinations and basic laboratory and diagnostic tests through accredited healthcare
providers and institutions, consistent with existing health programs and subject to
expansion based on the health needs of Filipinos.

Fundamentally, this bill aims to promote preventive healthcare, encourage early
detection of diseases, and ensure equitable access to essential health services so that
every Filipino may receive timely care before medical conditions become severe or
life-threatening

In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR FREE ANNUAL MEDICAL CHECK-UP FOR FILIPINOS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:


SEC. 1. Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Libreng Check-Up Act”.
SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes its duty to protect and promote
the right to health of the people. Pursuant to its duty, the State shall provide the
services needed to ensure the health and wellness of every Filipino.
Aside from enhancing programs that focus on curative medicine, there should be
emphasis on improving the country’s health care system in terms of preventive
interventions and strengthening the health-seeking behavior of citizens.
In line with this, Filipinos shall be entitled to an annual medical check-up, through the
programs of the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), which is free of
charge in any government hospital or institution.
SEC. 3. Population Coverage. – Every Filipino citizen shall be covered by the free
annual medical check-up under this Act, pursuant to Section 5 of Republic Act No.
11223, otherwise known as the “Universal Health Care Act.”
SEC. 4. Medical Check-Up Coverage. – PhilHealth, in consultation with the
Department of Health (DOH), shall establish a program to provide Filipinos with a free
annual medical check-up, which shall include, but not be limited to, a general physical
examination and the following basic laboratory and diagnostic tests:
   a. Blood tests (such as complete blood count, fasting blood sugar, lipid profile);
   b. Urine and stool examinations;
   c. Chest x-ray and other imaging procedures;
   d. Electrocardiogram (ECG);
   e. Cancer screening tests based on age and risk factors (such as Pap smear, PSA
      test);
   f. Other screening or diagnostic procedures as may be necessary based on the
      patient’s medical evaluation.
Provided, That prior to the availment of any laboratory and/or diagnostic examinations,
the patient must first be screened by a PhilHealth-accredited primary care provider, as
stated under Section 6(d) of R.A. No. 11223, to evaluate and determine the
appropriate examinations to be administered:
Provided, further, That such examinations shall be conducted through PhilHealth-
accredited health care institutions (HCIs):
Provided, furthermore, That the coverage of laboratory and/or diagnostic examinations
may be expanded, subject to the availability of PhilHealth funds and based on the
current and emerging health needs of Filipinos:
Provided, finally, That this program may be implemented and harmonized with existing
and future programs of PhilHealth, including but not limited to the Konsultasyong Sulit
at Tama (Konsulta) package, without prejudice to the development or expansion of
other programs aligned with this Act.
SEC. 5. Schedule of Free Medical Check-Up. – The duration of availing the free
medical check-up shall be for the whole calendar year. PhilHealth shall establish a
system to secure that every Filipino will be accommodated and get the services that
they are entitled to.
SEC. 6. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – PhilHealth shall be primarily
responsible for the implementation of this Act. Within thirty (30) days from the
effectivity of this Act, PhilHealth, in consultation with the Department of Health (DOH),
shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for its effective implementation.
SEC. 7. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to implement this Act shall be
charged against the total revenue of PhilHealth, composed of premium contributions
from direct contributors and premium subsidies of the government for the indirect
contributors, of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.
SEC. 8. Separability Clause. – If any provision or part of this Act is declared invalid or
unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full
force and effect.
SEC. 9. Repealing Clause. – All other laws, decrees, orders, other issuances, rules
and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.
SEC. 10. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in
the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
