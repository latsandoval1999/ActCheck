              Introduced by REP. CLAUDINE DIANA D. BAUTISTA-LIM


AN ACT PROHIBITING HOSPITALS FROM IMPOSING HOSPITAL ROOM, BED, AND
RELATED CHARGES ON PATIENTS WHO HAVE BEEN MEDICALLY CLEARED FOR
DISCHARGE BUT REMAIN CONFINED SOLELY DUE TO ADMINISTRATIVE OR
BILLING DELAYS, PRESCRIBING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Fair Hospital Discharge
Act."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is the policy of the State to protect and promote
the right to health of the people and ensure that all patients are treated with dignity,
fairness, and compassion. The State recognizes that patients who have already been
medically cleared for discharge should not bear additional financial burdens resulting
solely from delays attributable to hospital administrative processes, including billing
preparation, clearance procedures, insurance verification, or other internal requirements.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. - For purposes of this Act:

(a) Medically Cleared for Discharge refers to the written determination of the attending
physician that the patient no longer requires hospital confinement and may safely leave
the hospital.

(b) Administrative Delay refers to any delay occurring after medical clearance that is
attributable to the hospital's internal processes, including but not limited to:

   1. Preparation of the final hospital bill;
   2. Processing of PhilHealth claims;
   3. Verification of HMO or insurance coverage;
   4. Internal clearance procedures;
   5. Pharmacy, cashier, or records processing;
   6. Encoding, documentation, or information system delays; and
   7. Other non-medical administrative requirements.
(c) Hospital refers to any government or private hospital, infirmary, medical center, or
similar health facility licensed by the Department of Health (DOH).

SECTION 4. Prohibited Acts. - After a patient has been medically cleared for discharge,
no hospital shall impose or collect:

(a) Additional room charges;

(b) Additional bed charges;

(c) Additional nursing service charges attributable solely to the continued stay caused by
administrative delays;

(d) Additional accommodation fees; or

(e) Any similar confinement charges resulting exclusively from the hospital's inability to
complete its internal discharge procedures.

SECTION 5. Charges That May Still Be Collected. - Nothing in this Act shall prevent
hospitals from collecting:

(a) Charges for medical services, medicines, laboratory examinations, imaging
procedures, treatments, or professional services actually rendered before medical
clearance;

(b) Charges arising from medical complications requiring renewed confinement after the
initial medical clearance;

(c) Charges resulting from the patient's voluntary request to remain confined after all
discharge documents and billing have been completed; or

(d) Charges arising from delays directly attributable to the patient or the patient's
authorized representative, including the failure to submit required documents or settle
valid financial obligations after the hospital has completed all discharge requirements.

SECTION 6. Hospital Discharge Timeline. - Every hospital shall establish an efficient
discharge system designed to complete all billing and administrative procedures within a
reasonable period after medical clearance, as may be prescribed by the Department of
Health through the Implementing Rules and Regulations.

SECTION 7. Duty to Inform. - Hospitals shall inform patients and their authorized
representatives in writing of:

(a) The date and time the patient was medically cleared for discharge;

(b) The status of discharge processing;
(c) Any administrative delay encountered; and

(d) The patient's rights under this Act.

SECTION 8. Complaint Mechanism. - The Department of Health shall establish an
accessible complaint mechanism through which patients may report violations of this Act.
Hospitals shall likewise designate a Patient Discharge Assistance Officer responsible for
facilitating the prompt resolution of discharge-related concerns.

SECTION 9. Penalties. - Any hospital found to have violated this Act shall be subject to
the following administrative penalties, without prejudice to civil or criminal liability under
existing laws:

(a) First Offense: Written warning and mandatory corrective action;

(b) Second Offense: Administrative fine of not less than Fifty Thousand Pesos
(₱50,000.00) but not more than Two Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱200,000.00);

(c) Third and Subsequent Offenses: Fine of not less than Two Hundred Thousand
Pesos (₱200,000.00) but not more than Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱500,000.00),
suspension of the hospital's license for an appropriate period as determined by the
Department of Health, or other administrative sanctions authorized by law.

The Department of Health may also order the refund of any charges unlawfully collected
under this Act.

SECTION 10. Protection of Patient Rights. - No patient shall be denied discharge,
harassed, intimidated, or subjected to discriminatory treatment for asserting rights under
this Act.

SECTION 11. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the Department of Health, in coordination with the Philippine Health
Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), the Department of Finance, representatives of
government and private hospitals, and other concerned stakeholders, shall promulgate
the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 12. Appropriations. - The amount necessary for the implementation of this
Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 13. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 14. Repealing Clause. - All laws, executive orders, rules, regulations, or parts
thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.
SECTION 15. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
