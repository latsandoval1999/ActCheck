                              AN ACT
ESTABLISHING A FIRE STATION IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ANAHAWAN,
PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREOF

                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

Every Filipino community deserves timely and reliable protection during ires,
disasters, accidents, and other emergencies. The Constitution recognizes the State’s
duty to provide adequate public services and improve the quality of life of its people,
while Republic Act No. 6975 requires at least one ire station in every municipality.
Yet the Municipality of Anahawan, Southern Leyte, still does not have a dedicated ire
station and is currently served by the ire station in neighboring San Juan.

Anahawan is home to thousands of residents across its fourteen barangays, including
families, schools, businesses, government of ices, and other establishments that
require dependable emergency services. When every second can mean the difference
between saving a life and losing one, distance matters. The absence of a local ire
station may result in longer response times, especially during ires, natural disasters,
vehicular accidents, and other emergencies requiring immediate rescue and
assistance.

This bill seeks to establish a dedicated Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) ire station in
Anahawan. Bringing ire ighters, rescue personnel, equipment, and emergency
services closer to the people will allow faster response, strengthen ire prevention
and preparedness, and provide greater security to families and businesses. The
Municipality of Anahawan shall provide the necessary land or site, while the BFP shall
operate the station in accordance with applicable standards and regulations.

The establishment of a ire station in Anahawan is more than the construction of a
government facility, it is an investment in lives, property, and community safety. It will
give families greater peace of mind and ensure that help is closer when they need it
most. By bringing essential emergency services closer to the people of Anahawan, this
measure advances the State’s duty to protect every community and provide genuine,
responsive public service

In view of the foregoing, ug sa ngalan sa SERBISYONG TINUORAY, the undersigned
respectfully requests the immediate passage of this measure.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Establishment. — There shall be established a ire station in the
Municipality of Anahawan, Province of Southern Leyte. The ire station shall be under
the administrative and operational supervision of the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP),
an agency under the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), and
shall be organized, staffed, equipped, and operated in accordance with Republic Act
No. 6975, Republic Act No. 11589, Republic Act No. 9514, and other applicable laws,
rules, regulations, and standards.

The ire station shall provide ire prevention and suppression services, rescue and
evacuation assistance, emergency response, and other related services within the
Municipality of Anahawan and, when necessary, in neighboring localities pursuant to
applicable BFP policies and operational arrangements.

SECTION 2. Site of the Fire Station. — The Municipality of Anahawan shall identify
and provide, without cost to the National Government, a suitable parcel of land for the
establishment and operation of the ire station.

The site shall comply with the location, accessibility, safety, zoning, technical, and
other standards prescribed by the BFP and other competent government agencies.

                                                                           3|Page
SECTION 3. Appropriations. — The amount necessary for the implementation of
this Act shall be included in the appropriations of the BFP under the annual General
Appropriations Act.

The appropriations shall cover, as may be necessary, the construction, acquisition, or
improvement of the ire station; the procurement of ire trucks, ire ighting and rescue
equipment, communications equipment, and other facilities; and the recruitment,
assignment, training, maintenance, and operation of the personnel and services of the
station.

Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued maintenance and operation of the
 ire station shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act, subject to the
usual budgetary, accounting, and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.

SECTION 4. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — Within ninety (90) days from
the effectivity of this Act, the DILG, through the BFP, shall promulgate the rules and
regulations necessary for its effective implementation.

The implementing rules and regulations shall include the standards and procedures
for the selection and approval of the site, the establishment and construction of the
station, the allocation and deployment of personnel, the procurement and
maintenance of equipment, and the coordination of the ire station with the
Municipality of Anahawan and neighboring local government units.

SECTION 5. Separability Clause. — If any part, section or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected by such
declaration shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules,
regulations, issuances, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modi ied accordingly.

SECTION 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect ifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Of icial Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
