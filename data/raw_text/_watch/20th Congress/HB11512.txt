                                      EXPLANATORY NOTE

The early years of a child's life are critical to their physical, emotional, social, and cognitive
development. During this period, Child Development Workers (CDWs) play a vital role in providing
care, early education, protection, and developmental support to young children, particularly those
in vulnerable and underserved communities.

Despite the importance of their work, many CDWs continue to face challenges related to
employment security, compensation, benefits, professional development, and access to adequate
support and resources. These conditions may affect not only their welfare but also the quality and
continuity of early childhood development services provided to children.

CDWs perform responsibilities that require patience, dedication, specialized knowledge, and a
deep commitment to the welfare of children. It is therefore necessary for the State to recognize
their indispensable contribution to nation-building and ensure that they are provided with
appropriate employment protection, fair compensation, benefits, training opportunities, and other
forms of support.

This measure seeks to establish a comprehensive framework for the protection and promotion of
the rights and welfare of CDWs. It aims to strengthen their employment security, provide
appropriate plantilla positions and compensation, promote continuing education and skills
development, and ensure access to benefits and other privileges commensurate with the
importance of their duties.

By investing in the welfare and professional development of CDWs, the State likewise invests in
the future of Filipino children. A well-supported and adequately protected workforce is essential
to ensuring that every child receives quality care, early learning, and developmental support
during the most formative years of life.

In view of the foregoing, the passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

     AN ACT INSTITUTING THE MAGNA CARTA FOR CHILD DEVELOPMENT
               WORKERS AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "Magna Carta for Child
Development Workers."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. - The State recognizes the vital role of children and youth
in nation-building and shall promote and protect their physical, moral, spiritual,
intellectual, and social well-being. Towards this end, the State shall adopt policies that
will improve the social and economic welfare of CDWs who provide pre-school education
and social development services to children.

SEC. 3. Coverage. - This Act shall cover all CDWs under Republic Act No. 10410,
otherwise known as the "Early Years Act of 2013," herein defined as persons primarily
engaged in the provision of early child development services and programs such as care,
social development, education, protection, and other needs of children aged four (4) years
old and below in all government-sponsored care centers.

SEC. 4. Plantilla Positions and Security of Tenure in Child Development Centers. – At
least one (1) CDW I and one (1) CDW II plantilla positions shall be created in all child
development centers nationwide. The duly appointed CDWs shall enjoy security of tenure
and shall be protected and governed by civil service rules and regulations. No CDW shall
be removed or transferred from office without just cause, due process, or prior
consultation with the concerned CDW.

SEC. 5. Creation of Special Personnel Selection Board. - A Special Personnel Selection
Board (SPSB) for CDW positions shall be created to screen candidates and create a pool
of at least five (5) qualified applicants for possible appointment to the subject positions.
The SPSB shall be composed of the local social welfare and development officer
(LSDWO) as chairperson, and the local health officer, human resource management
officer, and local government unit (LGU) first level employees' representative as
members.

SEC. 6. Recruitment and Selection. - Upon publication and posting by the local Human
Resource Management Office (HRMO) of vacant permanent CDW positions in particular
child development centers, all qualified applicants shall submit their application, together
with complete requirements, to the HRMO for preliminary evaluation; Provided, That all
qualified incumbent CDWs hired under casual, contractual, and job order employment
status shall automatically be considered candidates and given priority for appointment to
permanent CDW positions.

The local chief executive, as the appointing authority, shall exercise sound discretion in
appointing, from the SPSB-recommended pool pursuant to Sec. 5 of this Act, a CDW in
particular child development center. Under no circumstances shall an applicant be
discriminated against on the basis of gender, religion, age, status, race, or political
affiliation.

SEC. 7. Qualifications, Positions and Salary Grade. - A CDW possessing the following
qualifications shall be hired to the position of:

(a) CDW I, with Salary Grade 6, must possess the following qualifications:

   1. Must be of legal age;
   2. Must have completed at least the K-12 Program or two (2) years of college
      education or its equivalent;
   3. Must be certified by either the ECCDC council or the deputized local Social Welfare
      and Development Office (LSWDO) to have complied with existing requisites and
      trainings in early childhood care and development;
   4. Must not have been convicted by final judgment of any crime; and
   5. Must not have a pending case in relation to Republic Act No. 7610, otherwise
      known as the "Special Protection of Children Against Abuse, Exploitation and
      Discrimination Act" and other similar laws protecting children.

CDWs legitimately serving as such upon the approval of this Act who are above
seventeen (17) years old but below eighteen (18) years old and do not possess any of
the above-stated disqualifications, shall continue in service to serve as such and are
considered qualified to be covered under this Act; Provided, that their retention has been
recommended by their direct supervisor on the basis of their performance.

CDWs who have not completed the K-12 Program and have no college education upon
the approval of this Act shall be given two (2) years within which to complete equivalent
training to be able to continue in the service, in accordance with Section 12 of this Act.
The necessary training courses shall be prescribed by the ECCDC Council.
(b) CDW II, with Salary Grade 8, must possess the same qualifications mentioned in
paragraph (a) of this section and must have five (5) years of work experience and at least
twelve (12) hours of relevant training.

Qualifications and exceptions applicable to this section shall be subject to conditions
imposed by the Civil Service Commission (CSC).

SEC. 8. Prohibition on Discrimination. - A CDW shall have the right to be protected from
discrimination based on age, sexual orientation, gender identity, ethnic grouping, civil
status, disability, creed, religious or political affiliation. There shall be no discrimination
whatsoever in the entrance to the occupation, or during its exercise, or in the termination
of services based on any ground other than professional consideration.

SEC. 9. Working Hours. - The normal hours of work of any CDWs shall not exceed eight
(8) working hours a day or forty (40) working hours a week; Provided, that the LSWDO
may require CDWs to render service beyond their working hours during emergency
situations such as natural disasters and man-made calamities: Provided, that emergency
response and survival kits shall be provided and that working hours should be conducted
in child-friendly spaces.

SEC. 10. Code of Conduct of CDWs. - Within six (6) months from the approval of this Act,
the Early Childhood Care and Development Council (ECCDC), in consultation with a
national organization of CDW, shall formulate a Code of Conduct for CDWs in line with
the Civil Service Commission (CSC) Rules and Regulations and adhere to the principles
of the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child. Each CDW worker shall be provided
with a copy of the Code at the expense of the government.

SEC. 11. Additional Compensation and Allowances. – CDWs shall be provided with the
following additional compensation, whenever applicable:

(a) Overtime Pay - for services rendered beyond the required working hours as prescribed
in Section 8 hereof;

(b) Hazard Allowance - CDWs in rural and urban areas, exposed to situations, conditions,
or factors in the work environment or place where foreseeable but unavoidable danger or
risks exist which adversely endanger their health or life and/or increase the risk of
producing adverse effect on their person in the exercise of their duties, to be validated by
the proper authorities, shall be entitled to hazard allowance in an amount to be determined
by the LSDWO of the local government unit concerned; and

(c) Subsistence Allowance – CDWs who render service within the premise of an isolated
Child Development Center shall be entitled to subsistence allowance equivalent to the
meals they take in the course of their duty, which shall be computed in accordance with
prevailing circumstances as determined by the local government unit concerned.
SEC. 12. Right to Self-Organization. - CDWs shall have the right to freely form, join or
assist organizations in order to defend and protect their mutual interests and to obtain
redress of their grievances.

SEC. 13. Other Benefits and Privileges. - CDWs shall be entitled to the following
additional benefits and privileges:

(a) Married CDWs - Whenever possible, married couples who are both CDWs workers
shall be assigned in the same municipality or city;

(b) Free Legal Services - Legal representation and consultation services for CDWs shall
be immediately provided by the Public Attorney's Office in civil and criminal cases filed by
or against CDWs arising out of or in connection with the performance of their duties as
such;

(c) Free Medical Examination and Treatment - Annual medical examinations shall be
provided by government hospitals, free of charge, to all CDWs. CDWs workers suffering
from work-related ailments shall be treated without cost in government hospitals;

(d) Access to Livelihood, Loans, Grants, and Skills Enhancement - The DSWD and the
LGUs, in coordination with other concerned government agencies, shall provide
organized CDWs with a mechanism for accessing livelihood, loans, grants, and skills
enhancement services; and

(e) Other Benefits. - All CDWs shall automatically become members of the Government
Service Insurance System (GSIS), the Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG), and
the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), subject to premium payments
by both employers and employees, and shall be entitled to maternity/paternity, sick, and
vacation leaves as provided for by existing laws.

SEC. 14. Training, Education and Skills Enhancement. - The ECCDC, in coordination
with the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Technical Education and Skills
Development Authority (TESDA), shall provide for a system of continuing education, skills
training, and knowledge enhancement programs for CDWs. The CHED and TESDA shall
adopt an equivalency system of education that shall recognize and duly credit the actual
work experiences and prior learning of CDWs as formal or academic training units.

SEC. 15. Support from Non-government Organizations (NGOs), Private Volunteer
Organizations and Other Private Institutions. - Non-governmental organizations or private
volunteer organizations, and other private institutions are hereby encouraged to assist or
support the government in the implementation of programs and projects for CDWs.
Pursuant thereto, all Child Development Centers may accept such assistance or support
from NGOs.

SEC. 16. Appropriations. - The amount necessary to cover the salaries and benefits of
CDWs shall be charged from the Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA), Special Education
Fund (SEF), and Gender and Development (GAD) Fund of LGUs concerned; Provided,
That the national government shall provide subsidy for at least one (1) CDW per center
in the fourth, fifth, and sixth class municipalities. A portion of the contribution from the
Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR), as provided under Republic
Act No. 10410, as well as additional funds generated from donations, fees, and
contributions collected by the ECCDC from whatever source, shall be set aside for the
purpose.

SEC. 17. Implementing Rules and Regulations. - Within six (6) months from the approval
of this Act, the ECCDC, DSWD, and DILG, in consultation with the CSC and national
organization of CDWs, shall formulate the necessary rules and regulations to implement
the provisions of this Act.

SEC. 18. Penal Provisions. - It shall be unlawful to violate the right of any CDW to security
of tenure, to deprive them of any rights as provided under this law, or condition the same
on political support for any public official. Any person who violates the provisions of this
Act shall be punished with a fine of not less than Twenty Thousand Pesos (P20,000.00)
or imprisonment of not less than two (2) months but not more than one (1) year, or both
at the discretion of the Court. In addition to the penalties stated above, the offender shall
be dismissed from government service if the offender is a public official.

SEC. 19. Separability Clause. - If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, other parts or provisions hereof not affected thereby shall continue to be in full
force and effect.

SEC. 20. Repealing Clause. - Section 6 of Republic Act No. 6972, otherwise known as
the "Barangay-level Total Development and Protection of Children Act," and all laws,
executive orders, presidential decrees, presidential proclamations, letters of instructions,
rules and regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SEC. 21. Effectivity Clause. - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication
in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
