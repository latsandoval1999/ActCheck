                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to ensure that Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous
Peoples (ICCs/IPs) have immediate access to legal assistance. It amends Republic
Act (RA) No. 8371, otherwise known as the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997, and
provides tax incentives to lawyers who render pro bono legal services to ICC/IPs.

        Although RA No. 8371 recognizes and protects the rights of ICCs/IPs, other
members of these communities do not have immediate access to legal representation
when the situation calls for it. Mainly due to poverty, geographic isolation, and the
complexity of legal proceedings involving ancestral domains, it prevents them from
effectively asserting and protecting their rights guaranteed under the Philippine
constitution.

       This bill empowers the National Commission on Indigenous People (NCIP) to
appoint counsels de oficio for indigent ICC/IPs with pending cases. It also encourages
lawyers to provide pro bono services through tax incentives. The call for justice for
one of the country’s most vulnerable sectors is of utmost importance and thus shall
be prioritized. This measure reinforces the State’s constitutional duty to recognize,
protect, and promote the rights of indigenous people.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR INCENTIVES TO MEMBERS OF THE LEGAL PROFESSION
ENGAGED IN THE DEFENSE OF THE RIGHTS OF INDIGENT INDIGENOUS
PEOPLES, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8371,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “THE INDIGENOUS PEOPLES’ RIGHTS ACTS OF
1997”

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. A new section to be denominated as Section 78-A is hereby inserted
after Section 78 of Republic Act No. 8371, otherwise known as “The Indigenous
Peoples’ Rights Act of 1997”, to read as follows:

           “SECTION 17-A. PRO BONO LEGAL SERVICES FOR
      INDIGENT ICCS/IPS. – THE NCIP IS HEREBY EMPOWERED TO
      APPOINT COUNSELS DE OFICIO FOR INDIGENT ICCS/IPS
      WITH PENDING CASES BEFORE IT. APPOINTED COUNSELS DE
      OFICIO SHALL BE ENTITLED TO TAX FREE HONORARIA OR
      PER DIEMS AS THE NCIP MAY PROVIDE UNDER ITS RULES.

           “ANY MEMBER OF THE PHILIPPINE BAR WHO HAS
      RENDERED FREE LEGAL SERVICES TO INDIGENT ICCS OF AT
      LEAST ONE HUNDRED (100) HOURS WITHIN ONE (1) YEAR
      SHALL BE ENTITLED TO DIRECT DEDUCTION FROM HIS
      INCOME TAX DUE IN THE AMOUNT OF ONE HUNDRED
      THOUSAND PESOS (P100,000.00). FOR THE AVOIDANCE OF
      DOUBT, THIS PROVISION SHALL APPLY TO LAWYERS
      RENDERING DIRECT LEGAL SERVICES TO THE ICCS/IPS OR
      LEGAL SERVICES RENDERED OUTSIDE OF THE NCIP SUCH AS
      BUT NOT LIMITED TO LEGAL ADVICE ON CONTRACTS, OR
      PROVIDING LEGAL ADVICE.

          “THE   BUREAU OF   INTERNAL  REVENUE IN
      CONSULTATION WITH THE INTEGRATED BAR OF THE
