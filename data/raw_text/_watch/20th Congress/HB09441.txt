                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The enactment of Batas Pambansa Blg. 838 on 27 April 1984 changed the name of the
Opon District Hospital in Lapu-Lapu City to Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital. More than four
decades after, it is but timely and necessary to rename the Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital to
Lapu-Lapu City Hospital to reflect its present role as the City’s primary public hospital.

       The continued use of the term “District Hospital” no longer reflects the hospital’s actual
city-wide service coverage and referral role. Omitting this term will also help eliminate
confusion in emergency referrals, patient transfers, procurement documentation, and inter-
agency records, such as the Department of Health (DOH) licensing and PhilHealth contracting.

         Renaming the Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital to Lapu-Lapu City Hospital will more
accurately reflect its present function, mandate, and level of service as the principal public
hospital of the city, consistent with its existing and/or targeted DOH hospital licensing
classification, bed capacity, service capability, and role as a city-wide referral and service
facility. The hospital has continuously expanded its scope of services, facilities, and
capabilities, and now serving not only a district but the entire City of Lapu-Lapu.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

                            AN ACT
RENAMING THE LAPU-LAPU CITY DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE CITY OF
LAPU-LAPU TO LAPU-LAPU CITY HOSPITAL (LLCH)

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

      SECTION 1. The Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital located at Barangay Gun-ob, Lapu-
Lapu City, is hereby renamed to Lapu-Lapu City Hospital.

        SEC. 2. Section 1 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 838, entitled “An Act Changing the Name
of the Opon District Hospital in the City of Lapu-Lapu to Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital” is
hereby amended accordingly.

       SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official
Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
