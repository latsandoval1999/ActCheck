                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      Education remains one of the most powerful tools for breaking the cycle of
poverty and empowering communities. Yet for many Filipino children living in
Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Areas (GIDA)—including remote upland
communities, island barangays, and conflict-affected regions—access to quality basic
education remains a persistent challenge. Long travel distances, lack of transportation,
difficult terrain, inadequate school infrastructure, limited electricity and connectivity,
and shortages of teachers often prevent learners from fully exercising their
constitutional right to education.

     Despite the efforts of the Department of Education (DepEd) and local
government units, existing education planning frameworks do not sufficiently address
the unique geographic and socio-economic realities of GIDA communities. Many
schools operate with inadequate facilities, multigrade classrooms, and minimal
teaching personnel, while some communities remain without reasonable access to a
school within a safe and practical distance.

     This proposed “GIDA Schools Act” seeks to institutionalize a comprehensive and
targeted framework for improving access to basic education in geographically isolated
and disadvantaged areas. The measure formally recognizes GIDA Schools within the
public education system and establishes clear indicators for their identification based
on conditions such as remoteness, limited infrastructure, small student populations,
multigrade classes, and the presence of Indigenous Peoples communities.

      The bill further mandates the development of a National GIDA Schools Mapping
System to guide evidence-based education planning and resource allocation. Through
this system, the government can more effectively identify underserved areas, prioritize
infrastructure investments, and ensure that learners have reasonable geographic
access to basic education facilities.

     Recognizing that education access challenges extend beyond school buildings
alone, the measure promotes inter-agency collaboration involving key institutions such
as the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) for access roads, the

                                            1
Department of Energy (DOE) and National Electrification Administration (NEA) for
electrification, and the Department of Information and Communications Technology
(DICT) for connectivity. Such coordinated efforts are essential to addressing the
broader structural barriers faced by learners in remote communities.

      The bill also provides targeted support for teachers and non-teaching personnel
assigned to GIDA Schools, including hardship allowances, mobility support, housing
assistance, professional development opportunities, and localized recruitment
strategies that prioritize community-based educators and Indigenous Knowledge
holders.

     Importantly, the measure affirms the rights of Indigenous Cultural Communities
and Indigenous Peoples, ensuring that educational initiatives within ancestral domains
comply with the Indigenous Peoples’ Rights Act (IPRA) and incorporate Indigenous
Knowledge Systems and Practices (IKSP) into the learning process where appropriate.

    By institutionalizing sustained government support for GIDA Schools, this
measure seeks to ensure that no child is deprived of the opportunity to learn simply
because of where they were born or where their community is located.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                                  AN ACT
     INSTITUTIONALIZING SUPPORT FOR PUBLIC BASIC EDUCATION SCHOOLS IN
     GEOGRAPHICALLY ISOLATED AND DISADVANTAGED AREAS (GIDA) AND
     APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

         Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1        SECTION 1. Short Title. - This Act shall be known as the "GIDA Schools Act."
 2
 3          SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. -The State shall protect and promote the right of
 4   all citizens to quality education at all levels, and take appropriate steps to make
 5   education accessible to all.
 6
 7        The State also recognizes that accessibility to school sites is an essential
 8   component of the right to basic education. Education planning, school mapping, and
 9   school infrastructure development shall be guided by the principle that learners must
10   have reasonable geographic access to a basic education school that is compliant with
11   national education standards.
12
13        Towards this end, the State shall improve the quality and access to basic
14   education for children in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas, by
15   investing in school infrastructures, access roads, and programs responsive to their
16   unique contexts.
17
18        In implementing these measures, the State recognizes and promotes the rights
19   of Indigenous Cultural Communities within the framework of national unity and
20   development. To this end, the State shall recognize the right of indigenous families
21   and communities to retain shared responsibility for the upbringing, training, education,
22   and well-being of their children, consistent with the rights of the child.
23
24        SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. - As used in this Act:

29         SEC. 4. Establishment of GIDA Schools. - Public basic education schools
30   located in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas are hereby established as
31   GIDA Schools, under the administration of the DepEd, to cater to learners who face
32   significant geographic, infrastructural, or access-related barriers to basic education.
33
34        For purposes of this Act, a school may be identified as a GIDA School if it has
35   any or a combination of the following indicators:
36
37        a) Less than four (4) classrooms;
38        b) Makeshift or nonstandard rooms;
39        c) Irregular or no electricity;
40        d) No allocation of funds for repairs or new construction projects in the last four
41           (4) years;
42        e) Access travel time of more than one (1) hour from the center of a city or
43           municipality, or with difficulty of terrain or of such nature as to be considered
44           having difficulty of access to existing educational institutions;
45        f) Multigrade classes or rooms;
46        g) Less than five (5) teachers;
47        h) A student population of less than one hundred (100) learners;
48        i) More than seventy-five percent (75%) IP learners;
49        j) Location in communities affected by conflict, insecurity, disaster vulnerability,
50           or other conditions that significantly impede safe access to education; and


                                                 4
 1        k) Such other analogous circumstances identified by the DepEd, which make it
 2           demonstrably difficult for learners to access school facilities.

 4         The DepEd, is hereby vested with the authority, through appropriate guidelines,
 5   to supplement, refine, operationalize, and update these indicators affecting
 6   geographic isolation, resource deprivation, or community vulnerability based on
 7   evidence and measurable criteria, and consistent with the policy and objectives of this
 8   Act.
 9         The establishment of GIDA Schools within ancestral domains shall be
10   undertaken in accordance with Republic Act No. 8371, otherwise known as the
11   "Indigenous Peoples Rights Act of 1997," including compliance with Free Prior and
12   Informed Consent (FPIC) requirements.

14         SEC. 5. Accessibility of Basic Education School Sites. - The DepEd shall
15   adopt and implement a national school accessibility standard to guide school siting,
16   school mapping, and infrastructure programming for public basic education schools,
17   including GIDA Schools.
18
19        The accessibility standard for these school sites must ensure that:
20        a) Students shall have access to a basic education school within a walkable
21           distance of three (3) kilometers from their place of residence; or
22        b) Where walkable access is not feasible due to terrain, geography, or safety
23           considerations, no reasonable access and safe transportation arrangements.
24
25        This standard shall be periodically reviewed and integrated into school planning
26   systems and inter-agency coordination, subject to considerations of safety and
27   feasibility.
28
29         SEC. 6. Establishment of the National GIDA Schools Mapping System. –
30   Within one (1) year from effectivity of this Act, DepEd shall develop and publish a
31   National GIDA Schools Map which shall serve as basis for planning, resource
32   allocation, and program implementation under this Act. The DepEd shall review and
33   update the GIDA Schools Map at least once every three (3) years to ensure
34   responsiveness to changing conditions and educational needs.
35

37   collaborate with concerned national government agencies (NGAs), local government

40

42           support of GIDA Schools;
43        b) Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), for the construction of
44           access roads leading to GIDA schools;
45        c) Department of Energy (DOE) and National Electrification Administration
46           (NEA), for the provision of electricity;
47        d) Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), for the
48           installation of information and communications technology and connectivity;

19         SEC. 8. Construction Standards for GIDA Schools. - School buildings and
20   related facilities under this Act shall comply with the minimum standards and
21   specifications prescribed by the DepEd for public school infrastructure; Provided, That,
22   where full compliance with these minimum standards and specifications is not feasible,
23   the DepEd shall prescribe appropriate and context-responsive standards specifically
24   for GIDA Schools. Such standards shall ensure that all school facilities remain safe,
25   durable, accessible, climate- and disaster-resilient, and suitable to the geographic,
26   cultural, and environmental conditions of the communities they serve.
27
28         The DepEd shall, when practicable, integrate the use of bamboo, engineered
29   bamboo, and other sustainable, locally sourced materials in the design, construction,
30   repair, and rehabilitation of public school buildings and facilities: Provided, That the
31   use of such materials shall strictly comply with the building codes, safety metrics,
32   structural standards and guidelines set forth by relevant regulatory bodies to ensure
33   the structural integrity and disaster-resilience of the school infrastructure.
34
35         The construction of GIDA Schools shall be subject to applicable environmental
36   laws, including Republic Act No. 11038, or the "Expanded National Integrated
37   Protected Areas System (E-NIPAS) Act of 2018", and Presidential Decree No. 705, or
38   the "Revised Forestry Code of the Philippines," in coordination with the Department of
39   Environment and Natural Resources (DENR).

26         b) Housing and Accommodation Support - DepEd, in coordination with
27            concerned LGUs, shall provide accommodation support, which may include
28            the construction of teacher quarters where feasible, rental or housing
29            subsidies, or partnerships with LGUs and communities for safe local
30            housing;
31         c) Professional Development and Teacher Pipeline - DepEd shall strengthen
32            teacher preparation and retention through:
33             (1)    Relevant in-service training and bridging programs through the
34                    National Educators' Academy of the Philippines (NEAP) in

36             (2)    Scholarships and teacher education support programs for aspiring
37                    teachers from GIDA and ICC/IP communities, linked to return-to-
38                    service obligations in GIDA Schools; and

44            promote community-based and context-responsive staffing:
45             (1)    Prioritizing the hiring of qualified teachers and essential non-

19         SEC. 12. Implementation. - The DepEd shall plan and implement programs
20   under this Act in coordination with relevant national government agencies (NGAs),
21   local government units (LGUs), and partner CSOs.

23         SEC. 13. Monitoring and Reporting on Learner Outcomes. - The DepEd shall
24   regularly assess and report on learner participation, retention, and learning outcomes
25   in GIDA Schools, which shall be the basis for the allocation of national funds and guide
26   the participation of LGUs in supporting these schools. Learner outcomes shall be
27   assessed and reported separately for students enrolled in GIDA Schools, to ensure
28   that progress and gaps are systematically monitored and addressed. The DepEd shall
29   submit an annual report to Congress on the status and implementation of this Act.
