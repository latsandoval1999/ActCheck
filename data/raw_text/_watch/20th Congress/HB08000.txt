                                  EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to convert A. Velez St. in Cagayan de Oro City to Macasandig-Indahag-
Bukidnon Road from a city and provincial road to a national road and an alternate highway
from Cagayan de Oro City to Bukidnon and vice versa.

       The road is presently in poor condition and in dire need of repair and rehabilitation.
City and provincial funds are not available for its maintenance and/or upgrading. Hence, the
need to tap other sources.

       The A. Velez St., Cagayan de Oro City to Macasandig-Indahag-Bukidnon Road
connects the urbanized city of Cagayan de Oro to the last municipality of the neighboring
Province of Bukidnon. With its improvement, easier access to both points would be ensured
and the potentials for commercial and industrial development would be realized.

       With its conversion into a national road and an alternate highway from Cagayan de
Oro City to Bukidnon the road would receive much needed funding and assistance it deserves.

       Passage of this measure is earnestly requested.

                               AN ACT
CONVERTING THE A. VELEZ ST. IN CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY TO MACASANDIG-
INDAHAG-BUKIDNON ROAD INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. The A. Velez St. in Cagayan de Oro City to Macasandig-Indahag-Bukidnon
Road is hereby converted into a national road.

       Section 2. The Secretary of Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue
the necessary rules, orders, and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

      Section 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a
newspaper of general circulation.
