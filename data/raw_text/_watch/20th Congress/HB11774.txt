                          AN ACT
   MANDATING LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS TO ALLOCATE ONE
  PERCENT (1%) OF THEIR NATIONAL TAX ALLOTMENT (NTA) FOR
 PROGRAMS AND PROJECTS FOR SENIOR CITIZENS AND 1% OF NTA
 FOR PROGRAMS AND PROJECTS FOR PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES

                            EXPLANATORY NOTE

      The National Government directs all local government units to allocate
at least one percent (1%) of their Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA) to
programs, projects, and activities (PPAs) proportionately divided among senior
citizens and persons with disabilities (PWDs).

     While there are some LGUs in the country that have already begun
implementing the 1% allocation for the senior and PWD sectors, many LGUs
have not yet established this ruling for their allocation, possibly due to limited
resources.

      With the increase of financial resources in every local government unit
brought about by the Mandanas-Garcia Ruling of the Supreme Court in 2018
(and confirmed in 2019), which increases the share of national government
tax revenue transferred to local governments, LGUs are now capable of
institutionalizing the increase in allocation for the sectors of senior citizens
and persons with disabilities.

     Under the Mandanas-Garcia Ruling, the National Tax Allotment (NTA),
formerly the Internal Revenue Allotment, of local government units reached
PhP1.035 trillion in 2025, an 18.73-percent increase from PhP871.4 billion in
2024.
      Recognizing the role of senior citizens and persons with disabilities in
developing a strong Filipino nation, this bill mandates LGUs to earmark 1%
of their IRA shares for the PPAs for the elderly and another separate 1% for
the persons with disabilities. These PPAs shall include medical and dental
services, fitness and wellness, social services, livelihood and employment,
legal assistance, and recreation.

      Through this, the State will be able to provide sufficient monetary
support for the PPAs that shall address the necessities of millions of senior
citizens and persons with disabilities.

     In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines
in Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Local Government
Allocation for Senior Citizens and Persons with Disabilities Act.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. – The State recognizes the role of
senior citizens and persons with disabilities in nation-building and
community development. Thus, it is hereby declared the policy of the State
to provide senior citizens and PWDs with programs, projects, and activities
that will effectively address their needs.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms. – For purposes of this Act, these terms are
defined as follows:

   a) ‘Senior citizens’ or ‘elderly’ refers to any resident of the Philippines who
      are at least sixty (60) years old.

   b) ‘Persons with disabilities’ refers to those suffering from restrictions or
      different abilities, as a result of mental, physical, or sensory
      impairment, to perform an activity in the manner or within the range
      considered for a normal human being.

SECTION 4. Funding. – All local government units (LGUs) are hereby
mandated to allocate one percent (1%) of their Internal Revenue Allotment
(IRA) for programs, projects, and activities that will benefit senior citizens.
Likewise, all LGUs are directed to appropriate another one percent (1%) of
their IRA for programs, projects, and activities for persons with disabilities.
SECTION 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within sixty (60)
days after the effectivity of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior and Local
Government, in coordination with the Secretary of Social Welfare and
Development, the National Commission of Senior Citizens, and the National
Council on Disability Affairs, shall issue such rules and regulations
necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.

SECTION 6. Non-Impairment Clause. – Nothing in this Act shall be
construed as to diminish, impair, or repeal rights recognized, granted, or
made available to disadvantaged, marginalized, or specific sectors under
existing laws.

SECTION 7. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, the provisions not affected thereby shall continue
to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 8. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules
and regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Act are
hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) newspapers of general
circulation.
