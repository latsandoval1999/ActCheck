                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to mandate the appointment of an Environment and Natural
Resources Officer (ENRO) in every province, city, and municipality across the
country. It further amends Section 484 (a) of Republic Act No. 7160, or the “Local
Government Code of 1991”, as amended.

      Currently, the appointment of ENROs in local government units (LGUs)
remains optional. This has resulted in several LGUs encountering problems with the
coordination and implementation of programs on environmental protection, natural
resource management, solid waste management, and climate change adaptation and
mitigation.

       This proposed measure recognizes that LGUs serve as the frontliners in
addressing environmental degradation and biodiversity loss. It ensures that each
LGU establishes a competent focal person to develop policies, enforce environmental
laws, coordinate with national government agencies, and implement science-based
and community-responsive interventions.

       The mandatory appointment of an ENRO strengthens local environmental
governance and enhances accountability in the stewardship of the environment and
the country’s natural resources. It also aligns with the government’s efforts to ensure
environmental protection and disaster resilience.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                            AN ACT
MAKING THE APPOINTMENT OF AN ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL
RESOURCES OFFICER MANDATORY IN ALL PROVINCES, CITIES AND
MUNICIPALITIES, FURTHER AMENDING SECTION 484 (A) OF REPUBLIC
ACT NO. 7160, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE
OF 1991”, AS AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Section 484 (a) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the
“Local Government Code of 1991”, as amended, is hereby further amended to read as
follows:

             “SEC. 484. Qualifications, Powers and Duties. - (a) xxx

              “The appointment of the environment and natural resources
      officer is [optional] MANDATORY for provincial, city, and municipal
      governments.”

      SEC. 2. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in any newspaper of general circulation.
