                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

       Tuberculosis (TB) remains a significant public health challenge globally, with
the Philippines being no exception. Despite efforts to control the disease, TB cases
continue to increase annually in the Philippines, posing a threat to public health and
socioeconomic development. In light of this escalating crisis, the implementation of a
Comprehensive Plan of Action to Eliminate Tuberculosis is imperative to address the
growing burden of TB and prevent further spread of the disease.

       The rationale for filing the bill stems from several key factors. Firstly, the
increasing number of TB cases in the Philippines is a clear indication of the urgency
to take decisive action. TB does not only cause significant morbidity and mortality but
also places a heavy burden on healthcare systems and the economy. Filing this bill
demonstrates the government’s commitment to prioritizing public health and
safeguarding the well-being of its citizens.

       Moreover, a comprehensive plan of action is essential to coordinate efforts
across multiple sectors and stakeholders involved in TB control. By formalizing a
structured approach through legislation, the government can ensure that resources
are effectively allocated, programs are implemented efficiently, and progress is
monitored systematically.

       Establishing a timeframe of six years for the Comprehensive Plan of Action
provides a clear roadmap for achieving measurable outcomes within a specified
period. This time-bound strategy promotes accountability, fosters a sense of urgency,
and mobilizes stakeholders to work collaboratively towards the common goal of TB
elimination.

       Furthermore, the provision for periodic review every three years enables the
government to assess the effectiveness of the plan, identify gaps or challenges, and
recalibrate strategies as needed. This iterative process of evaluation and adjustment
is crucial for ensuring that the plan remains responsive to evolving epidemiological
trends, emerging challenges, and best practices in TB control.

         By enacting this legislation, the Philippines demonstrates its commitment to
fulfilling international obligations and meeting targets set forth in global TB elimination
strategies, such as the Sustainable Development Goals and the End TB Strategy of the
World Health Organization. This alignment enhances the country's credibility in the
global health community and strengthens its partnerships for TB control efforts.

        The filing of the Comprehensive Plan of Action to Eliminate Tuberculosis Act is
a critical step towards addressing the escalating TB crisis in the Philippines. By
institutionalizing a time- bound, coordinated, and periodically reviewed strategy, the
government can enhance its capacity to combat TB effectively, protect public health,
and ultimately achieve the goal of TB elimination. This legislative initiative underscores
the government's commitment to prioritizing health as a fundamental human right and
promoting the well-being of all its citizens.

      In light of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this measure is earnestly
sought.
