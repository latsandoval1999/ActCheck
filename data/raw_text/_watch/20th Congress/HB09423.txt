                                EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to provide all Filipinos access to a free annual medical check-up as
a fundamental preventive health intervention that promotes early detection, timely
management of disease, and improved overall health outcomes.

       In the Philippines, many illnesses go undiagnosed until they reach advanced and
more costly stages, often due to financial barriers and inadequate access to routine
screening. This measure advances a more preventive, accessible, and people-centered
approach to health care. It covers annual medical check-ups, including essential
laboratory tests such as blood sugar and cholesterol screening, and provides for the
expansion of diagnostic services subject to available resources. It strengthens access to
basic preventive services, enables early identification of health risks and chronic
conditions, reduces avoidable complications, lowers long-term treatment costs, and
eases pressure on the health system.

        The bill gives stronger effect to the preventive and promotive principles of
Republic Act No. 11223, otherwise known as the “Universal Health Care Act”, by
institutionalizing regular health monitoring and prioritizing early intervention over
costly, late-stage treatment. It reflects a policy direction grounded in evidence-based
governance and the long-standing consensus among health professionals that
prevention is both more humane and more efficient than cure.

        Countless families continue to face financial catastrophe due to medical
expenses, while preventable diseases persist as a major public health concern. Ensuring
universal access to routine, preventive care is therefore a necessary intervention that
strengthens individual well-being, promotes health equity, and enhances the overall
resilience of the health system.

       In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

                           AN ACT
PROVIDING FOR A FREE ANNUAL MEDICAL CHECK-UP FOR FILIPINOS, AND
APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress
assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Free Annual Medical
Check-Up Act”.

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
support and promote the right to health by enhancing programs which focus on
preventive and curative medicine that advance the health and well-being of Filipinos
and prevent disease, disability, and death. Pursuant thereto, Filipinos shall be entitled to
an annual medical check-up that is free of charge in any government hospital and
institution.

       SEC. 3. Coverage. – All Filipino Citizens, by virtue of their membership in the
Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) mandated under Republic Act
No. 11223, otherwise known as the "Universal Health Care Act", shall be entitled to the
applicable benefits under the Philippine Health Insurance Program.

       Members shall be entitled to a free annual medical check-up that shall include
blood sugar and cholesterol tests. Laboratory and diagnostic tests shall be expanded,
subject to the availability of PhilHealth funds.

       SEC. 4. Schedule of Free Medical Check-Up. – To promote health, identify risks, and
ensure early diagnosis, PhilHealth shall establish a system that allows access to a free
annual medical check-up and ensure that Filipinos shall be accorded the quality health
care services that they deserve.

      SEC. 5. Appropriations. – The amount necessary to implement this Act shall be
charged against the PhilHealth funds.
