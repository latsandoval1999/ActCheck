                          EXPLANATORY NOTE

       The State recognizes that an efficient and integrated road network is
essential to urban mobility, economic activity, and the overall functionality
of growing cities. Circumferential roads, in particular, play a critical role in
distributing traffic, decongesting primary corridors, and improving intra-
city connectivity.

       The Iligan City Circumferential Road (C3) Phase II serves as a key
component of the City’s transport network. Connecting Barangay Sta. Elena
along the Iligan City Diversion Road to Barangay Maria Cristina along the
Overton–Abaga Road, the road provides an alternate route that supports
traffic circulation and access across major urban nodes.

       A feasibility study has already been conducted, affirming the road’s
viability and importance to the city’s long-term transport and infrastructure
development. The road has likewise been validated for conversion into a
national road, recognizing its significance not only to local mobility but also
to the broader road network. Despite this, it remains under local jurisdiction,
limiting the capacity for sustained development, maintenance, and
integration into the national infrastructure system.

      Converting the Iligan City Circumferential Road (C3) Phase II into a
national road will enable the Department of Public Works and Highways to
assume full responsibility for its improvement and maintenance. This will
ensure adherence to national standards and support its role as a vital
circumferential link within Iligan City.

                            AN ACT
    CONVERTING THE ILIGAN CITY CIRCUMFERENTIAL ROAD (C3)
   PHASE II, FROM BARANGAY STA. ELENA ALONG THE ILIGAN CITY
      DIVERSION ROAD TO BARANGAY MARIA CRISTINA ALONG
       THE OVERTON–ABAGA ROAD, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD
               AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the House of Representatives and the Senate of the Philippines
in session assembled:

     SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Iligan City
Circumferential Road (C3), Phase II National Road Act.”

      SECTION 2. Conversion. — The Iligan City Circumferential Road (C3),
Phase II, from Barangay Sta. Elena along the Iligan City Diversion Road to
Barangay Maria Cristina along the Overton–Abaga Road, is hereby converted
into a national road under the supervision and administration of the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH).

      SECTION 3. Appropriations. — The Secretary of Public Works and
Highways shall include in the Department’s program the improvement,
repair, and maintenance of the said road, the funding of which shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SECTION 4. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general
circulation.
