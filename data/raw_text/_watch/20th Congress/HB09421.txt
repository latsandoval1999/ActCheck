                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to rename the Lanipew Barrio School located in Barangay
Tacadang, Municipality of Kibungan, Province of Benguet, into “Dionisio Aligang
Elementary School”. This aims to recognize the invaluable contribution of the late
Dionisio Aligang to the establishment of the school and the advancement of
education in the community. The measure operationalizes the authority granted
under Section 13(e), Chapter II, Book I of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known
as the “Local Government Code of 1991”, and complies with the Revised Guidelines on
the Naming and Renaming of Schools under Department of Education (DepEd)
Order No. 29, s. 2011, as further reinforced under DepEd Order No. 40, s. 2014.

        Across many rural and geographically isolated communities in the country,
public schools continue to exist because of the sacrifices and generosity of ordinary
citizens who voluntarily donated land and resources for the education of future
generations. In Barangay Tacadang, Kibungan, Benguet, the late Dionisio Aligang
donated a ten-thousand-square-meter (10,000 sqm) school site through a notarized
Affidavit of Quitclaim and Waiver of Rights dated July 23, 1993. This has paved the
way for the establishment and continued operation of Lanipew Barrio School.
Despite such contributions, many local benefactors remain historically unrecognized
in official public institutions which results in the gradual loss of community memory
and local heritage. Recognizing this, the DepEd reiterated through DepEd Order No.
29, s. 2011 that naming and renaming public schools must undergo a formal and
lawful process involving the Local School Board and local legislative bodies to
preserve historical and cultural significance in educational institutions.

       The bill gives due recognition to an individual whose contribution directly
benefited generations of learners while complying with the standards prescribed by
the National Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP) and the Department
of Education. It also strengthens community participation in educational
governance, considering that the proposal already carries the endorsement of the
Parents-Teachers and Community Association (PTCA), the Sangguniang Barangay
of Tacadang, the Municipal School Board of Kibungan, and the Sangguniang Bayan
through Resolution No. 20-2025.
       This proposed measure preserves the story of a man whose generosity
opened the doors of education to countless children in a remote community in
Benguet. Every classroom standing on the land he donated stands as a silent
testimony to sacrifice, public spirit, and belief in the power of education. Allowing
his name to fade into obscurity would diminish the very values communities strive
to teach their children: service, compassion, and love for future generations. At a
time when many young Filipinos search for examples of genuine heroism, this bill
ensures that the legacy of Dionisio Aligang remains alive not only in memory, but in
the very institution that his selflessness helped build.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate passage of this bill is earnestly
sought.

                          AN ACT
RENAMING LANIPEW BARRIO SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TACADANG,
MUNICIPALITY OF KIBUNGAN, PROVINCE OF BENGUET, TO DIONISIO
ALIGANG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

      Section 1. Renaming of School. – The Lanipew Barrio School located in
Barangay Tacadang, Municipality of Kibungan, Province of Benguet, is hereby
renamed as “Dionisio Aligang Elementary School”.

      SEC. 2. Implementation. – The Department of Education, particularly the
Schools Division Office of Benguet, shall take the necessary steps for the
implementation of this Act, including the updating of all official records, school
signages, and pertinent documents to reflect the new name of the school.

       SEC. 3. Compliance with Existing Guidelines. – The renaming of the school shall
be consistent with the Revised Guidelines on the Naming and Renaming of Streets,
Public Schools, Plazas, Buildings, Bridges, and other Public Structures issued by the
National Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP).

       SEC. 4. Separability Clause. – In case any provision of this Act or any portion
thereof is declared unconstitutional by a competent court, other provisions shall not
be affected thereby.

       SEC. 5. Repealing Clause. – All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and
regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of
this Act are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

      SEC. 6. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

      Approved,
