                                       EXPLANATORY NOTE

       This bill seeks to institutionalize Continuing Professional Development (CPD)
support for Filipino nurses through employer-assisted learning programs,
government-funded training opportunities, accessible online CPD resources, and
incentives for healthcare institutions that invest in nursing workforce development.
The measure recognizes that continuous professional growth is essential in
maintaining competent nursing practice, improving healthcare delivery, and
strengthening the country’s capacity to respond to evolving health challenges.

        The implementation of mandatory CPD requirements under Republic Act
No. 10912, otherwise known as the “Continuing Professional Development Act of 2016”,
ensures that professionals continuously update their knowledge and skills.
However, many nurses continue to face barriers that limit their ability to participate
in CPD activities. According to Lim-Saco, F. and Guino-o, T., 20241 in their study
published in the Silliman Journal identified registration costs, work schedules, and
transportation constraints as among the major challenges affecting nurses’
participation in professional development programs. Similarly, the study of Palma,
J., et. al., 20202 among hospital nurses in the Philippines found that while nurses
recognize the importance of CPD, financial constraints, limited availability of
programs, and time-related factors remain significant barriers to participation. These
challenges create a gap between the expectation for continuous professional
advancement and the actual capacity of nurses to access relevant learning
opportunities.

      The proposed measure addresses these policy gaps through a comprehensive
support framework for nursing professional development. It addresses the financial
burden of CPD compliance through mandatory employer assistance, including paid

trainers. It also responds to geographic and accessibility barriers through free online
CPD platforms that allow nurses, including those in underserved areas, to
participate in accredited learning activities. Furthermore, it encourages private
sector participation through tax incentives and streamlines hospital-based CPD
initiatives to increase the availability of affordable and relevant professional
development opportunities.

       Nurses remain the backbone of the Philippine healthcare system, carrying the
responsibility of caring for patients in hospitals, communities, and emergency
situations while continuously adapting to new medical technologies and changing
health needs. However, a healthcare system cannot demand excellence from its
workforce without providing meaningful opportunities for growth and
advancement. Supporting nurses in their pursuit of lifelong learning is not merely a
professional obligation; it is an investment in safer patient care, stronger healthcare
institutions, and a more resilient nation. The country’s nurses have long served as a
source of pride and strength, and this measure ensures that they receive the support
necessary to continue serving with competence, confidence, and dedication.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                           AN ACT
INSTITUTIONALIZING CONTINUING PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
SUPPORT FOR NURSES, PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Nursing Continuing
Professional Development (CPD) Support Act”.

       SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people. The State recognizes that
nurses are essential to the delivery of quality health services and shall ensure that
professional development is accessible, affordable, and supported by institutional
mechanisms to alleviate the financial and personal burden on healthcare workers.

       SEC. 3. Definition of Terms. – As used in this Act:

          a. Continuing Professional Development (CPD) refers to the
       post-licensure intellectual or emotional enrichment of professionals as
       defined under Republic Act No. 10912, otherwise known as the “Continuing
       Professional Development Act of 2016”;

          b. CPD Providers refer to entities accredited by the Professional
       Regulation Commission (PRC) to offer CPD programs; and

          c. Nurses refer to all registered nurses in the Philippines, whether
       employed in public or private healthcare institutions, academe, or
       community settings.

      SEC. 4. Mandatory Employer Support. – All healthcare institutions shall,
whether public or private, be mandated to provide the following support for their
nursing staff:

           a.​ Nurses shall be entitled to at least five (5) days of paid professional
       development leave per year to attend accredited CPD seminars, workshops,
       or conventions; and

          b.​ ﻿Employers shall cover at least fifty percent (50%) of the registration
       fees for mandatory CPD courses required for license renewal; Provided, That
       the training is relevant to the nurse’s current area of practice.

      SEC. 5. National Nursing Scholarship and Grant Program. – The Department of
Health (DOH) shall, in coordination with the PRC, establish a Nursing Excellence
Fund. This fund shall provide:

          a.​ Grants for nurses pursuing specialized certifications (e.g., Critical
       Care, Oncology, Dialysis); and

           b.​ ﻿Full subsidies for “Train-the-Trainer” programs to increase the number
       of in-house CPD providers within regional hospitals.

       SEC. 6. Free Online CPD Portals. – The PRC and DOH shall develop a
centralized online platform offering free, high-quality CPD modules for nurses.
These modules shall be accessible to all registered nurses and shall automatically
credit units to their professional profiles upon successful completion.

       SEC. 7. Tax Incentives for Private Institutions. – Private healthcare facilities that
provide full funding for the CPD requirements of their nursing staff shall be entitled
to an additional tax deduction from their gross income, equivalent to one hundred
twenty percent (120%) of the total cost of the CPD training provided.

      SEC. 8. Role of the Professional Regulatory Board of Nursing. – The Board of
Nursing shall simplify the accreditation process for in-hospital training programs to
allow hospitals to conduct their own CPD-earning activities without excessive
administrative fees.

       SEC. 9. Appropriations. – The amount necessary for the initial implementation
of this Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the DOH.
Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its continued implementation shall be
included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

       SEC. 10. Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). – Within sixty (60) days
from the effectivity of this Act, the DOH, PRC, and Department of Budget and
Management shall, in consultation with nursing professional organizations,
promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of
this Act.

       SEC. 11. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full
force and effect.
