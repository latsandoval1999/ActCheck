                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

The defense of the State and the preservation of its territorial integrity are
paramount duties of the government. In an era marked by complex and rapidly
evolving regional security challenges—particularly the need to secure our
maritime domains, protect our sovereign rights, and enhance our disaster
response capabilities—the urgency to accelerate the Armed Forces of the
Philippines Modernization Program (AFPMP) cannot be overstated.

Alongside this, revitalizing the Self-Reliant Defense Posture (SRDP) Program is
vital to gradually reduce our reliance on foreign defense suppliers, foster
technology transfer, and build a robust domestic defense manufacturing sector.

However, the national government's flexibility to finance these capital-intensive,
multi-year defense acquisitions is currently hampered by antiquated legislation.
Presidential Decree No. 415, promulgated in 1974, authorized the President to
contract loans for the defense program but explicitly set a statutory borrowing
ceiling of Three Hundred Million United States Dollars (US$ 300,000,000.00)
under its Section 3.

Over five decades later, this ceiling is severely disconnected from the economic
realities and costs of modern military procurement. Today, the acquisition of a
single modern frigate, a squadron of multi-role fighter aircraft, or a
comprehensive radar network can easily exhaust or exceed this limit. Maintaining
a 1974 borrowing cap in the 21st century effectively cripples the military's ability
to secure comprehensive financing packages for large-scale, big-ticket
modernizations.

This bill seeks to repeal Section 3 of Presidential Decree No. 415 to remove the
obsolete US$ 300 million cap. In its place, this measure establishes a more
dynamic, responsive, and fiscally responsible funding mechanism. It authorizes
the President of the Philippines to contract local or foreign loans, credits, and
indebtedness specifically to fund the AFPMP and the SRDP Program.
To ensure absolute fiscal prudence and macroeconomic stability, the bill
mandates that the exact amounts to be loaned shall no longer be restricted by a
rigid statutory cap, but shall instead be jointly determined by the Department of
National Defense (DND), Department of Finance (DOF), and the Department of
Budget and Management (DBM). This ensures that all defense financing
corresponds directly to the actual requirements of the military, while remaining
strictly aligned with the national government's absorptive capacity, debt
sustainability framework, and fiscal ceilings. By removing an arbitrary relic of the
past and institutionalizing sound fiscal oversight in defense financing, this
measure will guarantee an uninterrupted and adequately scaled funding stream
to finally achieve a credible defense posture for the Republic of the Philippines.

In view of the foregoing, the swift passage of this bill is earnestly sought.

   AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES TO
 CONTRACT LOANS, CREDIT AND INDEBTEDNESS WITH ANY LOCAL OR
 FOREIGN SOURCE TO FUND THE ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES
MODERNIZATION PROGRAM AND THE SELF-RELIANT DEFENSE POSTURE
PROGRAM, REPEALING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION 3 OF PRESIDENTIAL
           DECREE NO. 415, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in
                              Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. Short Title - This Act shall be known as the "Armed Forces of the
Philippines (AFP) Defense Financing Act."

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to secure its
sovereignty and the integrity of the national territory. To this end, the State shall
ensure the continuous, uninterrupted, and adequate funding of the Armed forces
of the Philippines Modernization Program (AFPMP) and the Self-Reliant Defense
Posture (SRDP) Program to attain a credible defense posture.

SEC. 3. Authority to Contract Loans and Indebtedness. — The President of the
Philippines is hereby authorized to negotiate and contract loans, credits, and
indebtedness with any local or foreign financial institution, foreign government,
or multilateral agency, in accordance with the provisions of the 1987 Constitution
and existing laws, specifically for the funding requirements of the AFPMP and the
SRDP.
SEC. 4. Determination of Loan Amount — The exact amount of the loans, credits,
or indebtedness to be contracted under this Act shall be determined jointly by the
Secretary of the Department of National Defense, Secretary of the Department of
Finance, and the Secretary of the Department of Budget and Management.

SEC. 5. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part of this Act is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions not affected thereby
shall remain in full force and effect.

SEC. 6. Repealing Clause. — All laws, executive orders, administrative orders,
rules, and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this
Act are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SEC. 7. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
