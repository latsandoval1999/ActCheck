HOUSE BILL not 1902

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This bill seeks to convert the Bato By-pass Road, in the Municipality of Bato, Province
of Catanduanes, into a national road, placing its supervision, improvement, repair, and
maintenance under the Department of Public Works and Highways (DP WH).

The Municipality of Bato serves as an important link between the provincial capital of
Virac and neighboring municipalities, particularly Baras. With its agricultural, commercial,
and tourism activities, efficient road connectivity is essential to the movement of residents,
goods, and services. The Bato By-pass Road provides an alternative route intended to ease
traffic congestion within the town proper, improve accessibility to nearby communities, and
facilitate smoother transportation.

Given its importance, the conversion of the road into a national road will facilitate the
allocation of appropriate national funding for its improvement, rehabilitation, and long-term
maintenance, while easing the financial burden on the provincial government. This will help
ensure safer, more accessible, and disaster-resilient road infrastructure, improve traffic flow
and connectivity, and further promote socioeconomic development in Bato and the Province
of Catanduanes.

This proposal is anchored on DPWH Department Order No. 112, s. 2024, which sets
the standards for road conversion. The road proposed is eligible for conversion as it meets the
standard of connecting communities to bayans and businesses and providing an alternative
route around the town center, ease traffic congestion, and improve the movement of people,
goods, and services between Bato and neighboring municipalities.

In view of the foregoing, approval of this proposed measure is earnestly sought.

EU - RODRIGUEZ

Republic of the Philippines

TWENTIETH CONGRESS

HOUSE BILL nol 190, 2

Introduced by Honorable EULOGIO “LEO” R. RODRIGUEZ

AN ACT CONVERTING THE BATO BY-PASS ROAD, IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF
BATO, CATANDUANES, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD AND APPROPRIATING
FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in the
Congress assembled:

SECTION 1. It is hereby declared that the Bato By-pass Road, in the Municipality of
Bato, Catanduanes, is converted into a national road under the jurisdiction and supervision of
the Department of Public Works and Highways (DP WH).

SECTION 2. The Secretary of the DPWH shall include in its program the expansion,
improvement, rehabilitation, maintenance, and repair of the said road, the funding of which

shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

SECTION 3. This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the
Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
