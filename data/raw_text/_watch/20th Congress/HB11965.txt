                               EXPLANATORY NOTE

        This bill seeks to provide free glaucoma screening to senior citizens as part of
the State’s responsibility to protect and promote the health of the elderly. It aims to
facilitate early detection of glaucoma and help preserve the eyesight, independence,
and quality of life of senior citizens.

      Glaucoma is a progressive eye condition that can cause irreversible vision loss
when left undetected and untreated. For many Filipino senior citizens, regular eye
examinations may be limited by the cost of medical services, geographic barriers,
and limited access to specialized eye care, particularly in rural and underserved
communities. These barriers may delay the detection and management of glaucoma.
Strengthening access to screening through existing public health facilities can help
address these gaps and encourage timely referral and treatment.

       This bill requires local government units (LGUs), in coordination with the
Department of Health (DOH), to provide free glaucoma screening in DOH-retained
hospitals, LGU hospitals, rural health units, and barangay health stations. It also
provides for DOH technical assistance, screening standards, and coordination with
the Office for Senior Citizens Affairs and local social welfare offices to facilitate
access. Through these mechanisms, the bill seeks to make basic glaucoma screening
more accessible, particularly to elderly Filipinos who face financial or geographic
barriers to eye care.

      In view of the foregoing, the immediate approval of this measure is earnestly
sought.

                         AN ACT
    GRANTING FREE GLAUCOMA SCREENING FOR SENIOR CITIZENS

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

       Section 1. Short Title. – This Act shall be known as the “Glaucoma Screening for
the Elderly Act”.

        SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. – It is hereby declared the policy of the State to
protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness
among them. Toward this end, the State shall grant and provide free glaucoma
screening for senior citizens consistent with the constitutional mandate to give
priority to the needs of the elderly. The State recognizes that early detection through
regular screening is essential to preserving the eyesight, independence, and quality
of life of senior citizens.

       SEC. 3. Provision of Free Glaucoma Screening for Senior Citizens. – Local
government units (LGUs), in coordination with the Department of Health (DOH),
shall provide free glaucoma screening to senior citizens, which shall be conducted in
a DOH-retained hospital, LGU hospital, rural health unit or barangay health station,
subject to such standards as may be issued by the DOH.

        Senior citizens availing of this service must submit proof of eligibility as a
senior citizen, as may be prescribed in the implementing rules and regulations,
provided that access to the service, as far as practicable, be facilitated through the
Office for the Senior Citizens Affairs and/or Local Social Welfare and Development
Office.

       SEC. 4. Appropriations. – To carry out the provision of this Act, LGUs shall
appropriate in their annual budget such sums as may be necessary for each fiscal
year. The DOH may include in its annual budget the amounts necessary for technical
assistance, capacity-building, and the development of screening standards, subject to
existing budgeting rules and regulations.
        SEC. 5. Implementing Rules and Regulations. – Within ninety (90) days from the
effectivity of this Act, the DOH and National Commission of Senior Citizens, in
coordination with LGUs, relevant government agencies, and stakeholders, shall
promulgate the implementing rules and regulations to effectively carry out the
provisions of this Act.

        SEC. 6. Separability Clause. – If any portion or provision of this Act is declared
invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall remain in full force and
effect.

       SEC. 7. Repealing Clause. – All laws, presidential decrees, executive orders,
rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are
hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

      SEC. 8. Effectivity. – This Act shall take effect fifteen days (15) after its
publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.
