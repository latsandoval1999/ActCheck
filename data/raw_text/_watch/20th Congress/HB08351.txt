                                        EXPLANATORY NOTE

      This bill seeks to grant the President of the Philippines temporary
emergency powers to immediately suspend or reduce the imposition of excise
taxes on mineral oils, motor fuels, and other petroleum products. This
ensures that the government can provide timely economic relief to the public
during periods of extreme global market volatility.

       The urgency of this proposed measure is highlighted by the ongoing
geopolitical crisis involving the United States of America, Israel, and Iran,
which has severely disrupted global energy supply chains. The effective
closure of the Strait of Hormuz, a maritime artery responsible for
approximately twenty percent (20%) of global oil and liquefied natural gas
(LNG) consumption1, has led to a sustained surge in international crude
prices. As the Philippines relies heavily on imported petroleum, these
translate into consecutive weekly domestic fuel price hikes2. Such increases
threaten to destabilize the national economy by inflating costs in the
transportation, agriculture, and manufacturing sectors, which results in the
erosion of the purchasing power of Filipinos.

      To address these risks, this measure authorizes the President, upon the
recommendation of the Development Budget Coordination Committee
(DBCC), to temporarily suspend or reduce excise taxes once global oil prices
reach Eighty US Dollars (USD 80) per barrel. This reflects a swift response to
external price shocks, stabilizes domestic fuel prices, and mitigate inflationary
pressures.

                         AN ACT
ALLOWING THE AUTOMATIC SUSPENSION OR REDUCTION OF
EXCISE TAX ON FUELS, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE ARTICLE 148
OF THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS
AMENDED

      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
Congress assembled:

     Section 1. Section 148 of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997
(NIRC), as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

             “SEC. 148. Manufactured Oils and Other Fuels. – x x x

            “NOTWITHSTANDING THE PROVISION ABOVE,
      THE PRESIDENT, UPON THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE
      DEVELOPMENT BUDGET COORDINATION COMMITTEE,
      MAY SUSPEND OR REDUCE THE EXCISE TAXES ON FUEL
      THROUGH AN EXECUTIVE ORDER: PROVIDED, THAT
      THE AVERAGE DUBAI CRUDE OIL PRICE BASED ON
      MEAN OF PLATTS SINGAPORE (MOPS) FOR ONE (1)
      MONTH REACHES OR EXCEEDS EIGHTY DOLLARS (USD
      80) PER BARREL: PROVIDED, FURTHER, THAT THE SAID
      SUSPENSION OR REDUCTION OF EXCISE TAXES SHALL
      BE AUTOMATICALLY LIFTED WHEN THE ABOVE
      CONDITION IS NO LONGER PRESENT.”

      SEC. 2. Separability Clause. – If any provision of this Act is held invalid
or unconstitutional, the other provisions not so declared shall remain in force
and effect.
