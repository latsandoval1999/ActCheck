                                              EXPLANATORY NOTE

      “We, the sovereign Filipino people, imploring the aid of Almighty God, in
order to build a just and humane society and establish a Government that shall
embody our ideals and aspirations, promote the common good, conserve and
develop our patrimony, and secure to ourselves and our posterity the blessings of
independence and democracy under the rule of law and a regime of truth, justice,
freedom, love, equality, and peace, do ordain and promulgate this Constitution.”

       The national vision embodied in the 1987 Constitution, through its Preamble,
requires a citizenry with a strong foundation of discipline, teamwork, commitment,
and resilience. Sports is a powerful, primary instrument for the formation of these
values. It teaches us to strive, to collaborate, and to maintain not just physical
fitness, but total mental, emotional, and even spiritual formation and health.

        Beyond character formation, which is invaluable, sports and sports tourism
represent a huge opportunity for the Philippines. Globally, sports tourism is
emerging as one of the fastest-growing sectors in the tourism industry. The global
sports tourism market is projected to grow at a compound annual growth rate of
16.43% between 2024 and 2032, from USD 544.38 billion in 2023 to USD 2,089.58
billion by 2032.1

(SEA) Games generated over Php 500 million in revenue.2 On a larger scale, co-

        Yet, despite our proven capability to host world-class events and earning
praise for our people’s renowned hospitality, we fail to consistently translate this
potential into lasting national success. For too many Filipinos, sports remains a
dream rather than a viable and honored profession. It is a calling we deny to our
own people because we lack the right systems, support, and structures. This
systemic weakness is most evident in the Olympics. The long wait between the
1996 boxing silver medal by Mansueto Velasco and Hidilyn Diaz's historic gold in
weightlifting in 2020 highlights the challenges faced by Filipino athletes in reaching
their full potential on the world’s biggest sporting stage.

       The Philippine Sports Commission (PSC), established in 1990 through
Republic Act No. 6847, is mandated to, among others, “provide the leadership,
formulate the policies and set the priorities and direction of all national amateur
sports promotion and development, particularly giving emphasis on grassroots
participation.” However, its focus on grassroots participation may not fully address
the needs of elite athletes to compete at the highest levels of sports competitions.

       In 2001, Republic Act No. 9155 or the “Governance of Basic Education Act
of 2001” abolished the Bureau of Physical Education and School Sports (BPESS)
and transferred “[a]ll functions, programs and activities of the Department of
Education related to sports competition” to the Philippine Sports Commission
(PSC). While physical education remains in the curriculum, this shift has weakened
overall sports development.

       This measure will address the perennial challenges that hinder the growth
of Philippine sports. This bill proposes the creation of a Department of Sports, a
cabinet-level agency that will provide focused leadership for national sports
promotion and development, and address the needs of athletes, coaches, trainers,
and sports officials across all levels. With a dedicated department, we can find
comprehensive solutions to the problems besetting Philippine sports, particularly
the lack of a coherent national sports program, the need to enhance the
partnership with various sectors involved in sports, obsolete training methods, lack
of modern and high-quality training facilities, equipment, and sports venues.

          Hence, the approval of this Bill is earnestly requested.

                               AN ACT
      CREATING THE DEPARTMENT OF SPORTS, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND
       FUNCTIONS, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER
                              PURPOSES

     Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in
     Congress assembled:

 1         Section 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Department of
 2   Sports Act of 2025. ”

 3            Sec. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is hereby declared the policy of the
 4   State:

 5      (a) To recognize the role of citizens, especially the youth, in nation-building,
 6          promote and ensure equal access to sports and other physical recreational
 7          activities regardless of sex, gender, race, or socioeconomic status, and
 8          protect and preserve desirable social, cultural, moral, and spiritual values in
 9          a changing world.

10      (b)To promote physical education and encourage sports programs, league
11         competitions, and amateur sports at the grassroots level to foster self-
12         discipline, teamwork, and the development of a healthy and alert citizenry;

13      (c)To institute a focused and comprehensive program for the training of
14         national athletes for international competitions, with the goal of achieving
15         excellence on the world stage, thereby inspiring patriotism and fostering
16         national pride;

17      (d)To recognize the importance of promoting excellence in sports to foster
18         patriotism, nationalism, gallantry, accelerate social progress, and contribute
19         to total human development;

20      (e)To insulate the national sports development program from partisan politics,
21         ensuring that programs are implemented based on merit and not used as
22         instruments for political patronage or personal aggrandizement of any public
23         official;
1       (f) To ensure that the delivery of services, funding, and support to athletes,
2           coaches, and sports organizations is efficient, timely, and free from
3           unnecessary bureaucratic red tape;

4       (g)To create the infrastructure and promote capacity building for broad-based
5          sports and to achieve excellence in various competitive events at the
6          national and international levels.

 7         Sec. 3. Creation of the Department of Sports. — There is hereby created
 8   the Department of Sports, hereinafter referred to as the “Department”, which shall
 9   carry out the above-declared policy and assume the promotion and development
10   of sports and physical fitness in the country.

11         Sec. 4. Mandate. — The Department shall be the primary policy, planning,
12   coordinating, implementing, regulating, and administrative entity of the executive
13   branch of the Government that will formulate, promote, and develop the national
14   sports development agenda for the country.

15        Sec. 5. Powers and Functions. — To carry out its mandate, the
16   Department shall;

17      (a) Formulate, adopt, and implement a comprehensive national sports
18          development plan, as well as other policies, programs, and guidelines that
19          will promote the development and harness the potential of all Filipino
20          athletes, coaches, and trainers, with due consideration to their welfare and
21          potential for success as national athletes competing locally and
22          internationally;

23      (b)Plan, implement, and oversee an integrated sports promotion and
24         development program for the country, in coordination with the various
25         sectors involved in sports, including but not limited to the national sports
26         association, sports organization, and private stakeholders;

27      (c) Oversee and support the participation of athletes in domestic and
28          international sports competitions;

29      (d) Establish and maintain linkages with international sports federations,
30          national sports organizations of other countries, and international non-
31          governmental organizations;

32      (e) Establish, develop, and maintain world-class, fully-equipped sports
33          facilities and centers in strategic places in the country and, as far as
34          practicable, include modern sports complexes adequate for hosting major
35          international competitions;

36      (f) Acquire, use, and control any land, buildings, facilities, equipment,
37          instruments, tolls, and rights required or otherwise necessary for the
38          accomplishment of the objective of the department;

39      (g) Supervise and improve the standard of sports coaches and coaching staff;
 1      (h) Ensure a sustainable funding mechanism for the implementation of sports
 2          policies, plans, programs, projects, and other activities.

 3          In recognition of the autonomy of the Philippine Olympic Committee (POC)
 4   as the representative of the International Olympic Committee (IOC), and the
 5   respective National Sports Associations (NSAs), the authority to select the
 6   athletes and coaches who will form the official national team for IOC-sanctioned
 7   competitions such as the Olympic Games, Asian Games, and Southeast Asian
 8   Games, shall remain the prerogative of the relevant NSAs as recognized by the
 9   POC.

10          Sec. 6. The National Sports Development Agenda. - The Department
11   shall formulate and implement a unified National Sports Development Agenda,
12   which shall serve as the blueprint for the country’s short, medium, and long-term
13   sports development. The Agenda shall be comprehensive, athlete-centered, and
14   inclusive. It shall have, at a minimum, the following mandatory components:

15            (a) Grassroots Sports Promotion - Programs to ensure broad-based
16                public involvement in sports and physical recreational activities,
17                especially among the youth;

18            (b) Elite Sports Development-A focused program for the identification,
19                training, and comprehensive support of national athletes and teams
20                for success in international competitions, including but not limited to
21                the Olympic Games, Paralympic Games, Asian Games, and
22                Southeast Asian Games;

23            (c) Sports Science and Education - Integration of modern sports
24                science, medicine, nutrition, and technology into athlete training and
25                development programs;

26            (d)Sports Infrastructure - A master plan for the development,
27               maintenance, and modernization of sports facilities across the
28               country, ensuring they meet national and international standards;

29            (e) Coaching and Human Resource Development - Programs for the
30                continuous education, certification, and professionalization of
31                coaches, trainers, and sports officials;

32            (f) Public-Private Sector Partnership - A framework to encourage and
33                manage collaboration with the private sector, civil society, and other
34                stakeholders to generate resources and support for sports;

35            (g) Sports for All - Programs that ensure equal access for persons with
36                disabilities, indigenous peoples, and marginalized communities.

37         Sec. 7. Organization. —

38            A. Secretary — The authority and responsibility for the exercise of the
39               mandate of the Department and for the discharge of its powers and
40               functions shall be vested in the Secretary of Sports, who shall be
 1      appointed by the President of the Republic of the Philippines. The
 2      Secretary shall have the following functions:

 3         (1) Provide executive direction and supervision over the entire
 4             operations of the Department and its attached agencies;

 5         (2) Establish policies and standards for the effective, efficient,
 6             and economical operation of the Department, in accordance
 7             with the programs of government;

 8         (3) Review and approve requests for financial and manpower
 9             resources of all operating offices of the Department;

10         (4) Designate and appoint officers and employees of the
11             Department,       excluding    undersecretaries,       assistant
12             secretaries, regional executive directors, and assistant
13             regional directors, in accordance with all relevant civil service
14             rules;

15         (5) Exercise disciplinary powers over officers and employees of
16             the Department in accordance with law, including their
17             investigation and the designation of a committee or officer to
18             conduct investigations;

19         (6) Coordinate with other agencies, as well as public and private
20             interest groups, including non-government organizations, on
21             the Department’s policies and initiatives;

22         (7) Prepare and submit to the President through the Department
23             of Budget and Management an estimate of the necessary
24             expenditures of the Department during the next fiscal year, on
25             the basis of the reports submitted by the bureaus and offices
26             under his/her jurisdiction;

27         (8) Advise the President on the promulgation of executive and
28             administrative orders, and formulation of regulatory and
29             legislative proposals on matters pertaining to sports
30             programs;
31
32         (9) Formulate such rules and regulations and exercise such other
33             powers as may be required to implement the objectives of this
34             Act; and

35         (10)     Perform such other tasks as may be provided by law or
36            assigned by the President of the Republic of the Philippines.

37   B. Undersecretaries. — There shall be five (5) undersecretaries who
38      shall be appointed by the President of the Republic of the
39      Philippines upon the recommendation of the Secretary and the
40      Career Service Executive Board, which shall consist of the following
41      positions:
42         (a) Undersecretary for Administration and Finance;
 1                   (b) Undersecretary for Legal Affairs;
 2                   (c) Undersecretary for Policy and Planning:
 3                   (d) Undersecretary for Operations; and
 4                   (e) Undersecretary for Public Information and Domestic and
 5                       International Relations.

 6            C. Assistant Secretaries. — Each Undersecretary shall be assisted
 7               by at least one (1) Assistant Secretary who shall be appointed by
 8               the President of the Republic of the Philippines upon the
 9               recommendation of the Secretary. Each Assistant Secretary shall
10               be assigned to head a key service, bureau, or functional cluster of
11               the Department, and shall perform such other duties and functions
12               as may be provided by law or delegated by the Secretary.

13            D. Regionai Executive Directors. — There shall be a Regional
14               Executive Director in each region who shall be responsible for
15               efficiently and effectively carrying out the duties and responsibilities
16               of his office.

17            E. Structure and Staffing Pattern. — The organizational framework
18               and staffing pattern of the Department shall be prescribed and
19               approved by the Secretary within ninety (90) days after the approval
20               of this Act, and the authorized position created therein shall be
21               approved by the Department of Budget and Management, which
22               shall be filled through the job opportunity list of the Civil Service
23               Commission Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human
24               Resource Actions.

25          Sec. 8. Qualifications. — No person shall be appointed Secretary,
26   Undersecretary, or Assistant Secretary of the Department unless he or she is a
27   citizen and resident of the Philippines, of good moral character, of proven integrity
28   and possesses at least ten (10) years of proven experience and recognized
29   competence in sports administration, sports management, physical education, or
30   a related field.

31          Sec. 9. Reorganization of Relevant Agencies. — The Philippine Sports
32   Commission is hereby abolished, and its powers and functions, applicable funds
33   and appropriations, records, equipment, property, and personnel are transferred
34   to the Department.

35          The Games and Amusement Board and the National Academy of Sports
36   shall retain their powers and functions but be attached to the Department, which
37   shall have overall supervision over them.

38          Sec. 10. Incentives for Private Sector Support. — (a) All donations,
39   contributions, bequests, and grants to the Department shall be exempt from
40   donor's tax and shall be considered as allowable deductions from the gross income
41   of the donor for purposes of computing the taxable income of the donor in
42   accordance with the provisions of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as
43   amended.
1           (b) The importation of sports equipment, instruments, and capital equipment
2    shall be exempt from customs duties, taxes, and tariffs: Provided, That such sports
3    equipment, instruments, and capital equipment are not available locally in the
4    same quality and for the same price.

5           (c) The Department may, in recognition of significant contributions, authorize
6    the naming of sports facilities, programs, or events after a donor or any person of
7    their choosing, subject to the rules and regulations which shall be promulgated by
8    the Secretary. Such recognition shall be granted in a manner that upholds the
9    public interest and the integrity of the national sports program.

10          (d) All delegations or representatives to any international competition,
11   conventions, meetings, and events shall be exempt from the payment of travel tax,
12   airport tax, and other related taxes or fees now or hereafter imposed by laws or
13   regulations.

14         Sec. 11. Sports Facilities. — The administration, supervision, and full
15   control of all existing sports facilities, including land, buildings, and equipment,
16   owned by the national government as well as those owned by a government-
17   funded foundation, organization, association, and entity shall be transferred to the
18   Department.

19         Within one (1) year from the effectivity of this Act, the Department shall
20   conduct a comprehensive inventory and assessment of all sports facilities, land,
21   and properties transferred to it. This assessment shall determine their current
22   physical condition, operational status, and potential for modernization or
23   redevelopment. The findings shall be used as the basis for the formulation of a
24   National Sports Infrastructure Master Plan, which shall guide future investments
25   and ensure the optimal use of government assets for sports development.

26         Sec. 12. Reportorial Requirements. — The Department together with the
27   Attached Agencies shall, within ninety (90) days after the end of every fiscal year,
28   submit to the Congress an annual report on the status of the implementation of this
29   Act including but not limited to the organization and its operation, application,
30   administration, execution, the effectiveness of its programs, and public
31   expenditures as may be determined by the Congressional Oversight Committee.

32         The annual report should also include the Department’s accomplishments
33   under this Act, together with its plans and recommendations to improve and
34   develop its capability to enforce its provisions, and a complete accounting of
35   transactions with respect to any funds received under this Act.

36         Sec. 13. Appropriations. — The amount needed for the initial
37   implementation of this Act shall be taken from the current year’s appropriations of
38   the PSC by virtue of this Act. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its
39   continued implementation shall be included in the annual General Appropriations
40   Act.

41         Sec. 14. Separability Clause. — If any provision of this Act or the
42   application of such provision to any person or circumstance is held invalid for any
43   reason, the remainder of this Act or the application of such provision to other
44   persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
1         Sec. 15. Repealing Clause. — The provisions of any law, whether general
2   or special, rules and regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof which are
3   inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

4         Sec. 16. Effectivity. — This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
5   publication in the Official Gazette or in at least two (2) national newspapers of
6   general circulation, whichever comes earlier.
