AN ACT
CREATING THE DEPARTMENT OF SPORTS, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND

AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Philippine Constitution mandates the State to promote physical education and
support sports programs that build discipline, resilience, and excellence. Yet decades later,
Filipino athletes continue to train under fragmented systems, with limited support and

inconsistent long-term planning.

In 2021, the world watched as Hidilyn Diaz lifted not only a barbell but an entire nation,
bringing home the Philippines’ first Olympic gold medal. In 2024, Carlos Yulo soared to
double gold at the Paris Olympics. These victories are historic. But they were achieved despite

systemic limitations, not because of institutional strength.

The reality is clear: our athletes have the talent. What they need is a nation that fully

supports them.

This bill seeks to create a Department of Sports that will lead the development of
grassroots programs, unify national and local initiatives, and provide sustained support to both

amateur and elite athletes. By elevating sports to a full department, the government can ensure

consistent funding, strategic planning, and long-term vision. It will allow athletes to focus on

their training, knowing their country stands firmly behind them.

In view of the foregoing, the approval of this measure is earnestly sought.

ARTICLE I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. Short Title. — This Act shall be known as the “Department of Sports
Act.”

SEC. 2. Declaration of Policy. — It is the policy of the State to promote and sustain
fitness, physical education, and sports development in the country. As such, it shall maintain
and provide equal access to resources, services, and facilities related to sports to all Filipinos
to foster and promote health and physical fitness, self-discipline, teamwork, excellence, peace
and camaraderie, youth development, and people empowerment through sports.

It shall also encourage wider participation of all sectors, including the government and
private sector, in amateur sports promotion and development and shall be led by a single,

unified, and integrated national sports policy-making body.

SEC. 3. Creation of the Department of Sports. — There is hereby created the
Department of Sports, hereinafter referred to as the Department.

SEC. 4. Mandate. — The Department shall be the primary policy-making, planning,
coordinating, implementing, and administrative entity of the Executive Branch of the
government that will conceptualize, develop, and promote the national sports program.

SEC. 5. Powers and Functions. — The Department shall exercise the following powers
and functions:

(a) Formulate, recommend, and implement policies and programs for integrated
national sports and sports for Athletes with Disabilities in coordination with the
Department of Health (DOH), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), and
the Department of Education (DepEd), and local government units;

(b) Plan and conduct national sports competitions and establish and continually
strengthen a nationwide grassroots-based sports development program;

(c) Implement sports for early education based on functionality or practicality;

(d) Determine the sports to be prioritized by the government in regional and
international sports competitions taking into consideration the funds needed,
competitive opportunities for emerging and developing athlete, and chances of
winning;

(e) Oversee and support the participation of athletes in regional and international sports
competitions;

(f) Supervise and improve the standards of sports coaches and coaching staff;

(g) Facilitate research and development on sports science and sports medicine and
exercise the outputs therefrom in national sports programs;

(h) Prescribe criteria and standards for the recognition of all national sports associations
and exercise supervisory and visitorial powers over the same;

(i) Supplement government appropriations for sports promotion and development by
encouraging the private sector to contribute to the funding of the various programs
in the Department;

(j) Provide and supervise the completion of adequate and fully-equipped sports
facilities, centers, and complexes in strategic places across the country and regularly
revisit, inspect, and ensure the proper management and maintenance thereof,
excluding school or college-owned sports complexes;

(k) Enter into contracts pursuant to its functions and powers;

SEC. 9. Assistant Secretaries. — The Secretary shall be assisted by three (3) Assistant
Secretaries who shall be career officers appointed by the President upon recommendation of
the Secretary.

SEC. 10. Regional Offices. — The Department shall be authorized to establish, operate,
and maintain a Regional Office in each of the administrative regions of the country as the need
arises. The regional office shall be headed by a Regional Director, who may be assisted by one
(1) Assistant Regional Director. The Regional Offices shall coordinate with regional offices of
other departments, other agencies, and local government units with respect to their local sports
programs.

SEC. 11. Structure and Staffing Pattern. — The Department shall determine its
organizational structure and create new divisions or units as it may deem necessary and shall
appoint officers and employees of the Department in accordance with the Civil Service Law,
rules and regulations.

SEC. 12. Regular Bureaus, Divisions, and Offices. — The Department shall organize
bureaus, divisions, and offices in such manner as would best carry out the declared policies and
objectives enunciated herein and consistent with the provisions of civil service laws, rules, and
regulations. There shall be an administrative services bureau, which shall be primarily
responsible for providing services related to financial management, personnel, records,
supplies, and equipment, and a support services bureau, which shall be primarily responsible
for plans and programs formulation, research, electronic data processing (EDP) and system
management, public information and production, transportation, communication, and such
other functions and responsibilities as may be necessary and required by the Department.

SEC. 13. Specialized Bureaus. — There are hereby created two (2) specialized bureaus
under the Department: (a) the Amateur Sports Development Bureau; and (b) the International
Sports Development Bureau, both of which shall be headed by Deputy Executive Directors.

SEC. 14. Tax Exemption of Earnings from the Use of Sports Facilities. — To
encourage popular participation in amateur sports, no tax, charge, or fee of whatever nature or
any kind shall be levied on the use of sports facilities by the general public, individuals, or
public agencies or offices, or the earnings from the use thereof whether by public or private
entities, whether the tax or fee be on the user, owner, or operator of the sports facility.

SEC, 15. Tax Deduction or Exemption of Donations and Contributions. — All
donations and contributions to the Department in connection with its fund-raising projects and
its continuing sports development programs shall be exempt from the donor’s taxes and shall

be deductible in full in the computation of the taxable net income of the donor. Donations and

SEC. 16. Sports Delegations. — The Department and its delegation or representatives
to any international sports competitions, convention, conference, meeting, and events, and
athletes, coaches, and other officials to any international competition shall be exempt from the
payment of travel tax, airport tax, and any other related taxes or fees now or hereafter imposed
by law or regulation.

SEC. 17. Presidential Land Grant. — The provisions of any existing law to the
contrary notwithstanding the President may, upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), grant by donation, sale, lease,
usufruct, or otherwise to the Department portions of the land of the public domain as may be
necessary for the establishment of regional training centers in all the regions for the country
and the accomplishment of any of its purposes.

SEC. 18. Sports Facilities. — The ownership, administration, and full control of all
existing sports facilities, including land, buildings, and equipment, owned by the national
government as well as those owned by government-funded foundations, associations, and
entities except liabilities, shall be transferred to the Department. Provided, That, the sports
facilities owned by the local government units, state colleges and universities, or public schools
shall remain with the said institutions or entities. The Department shall establish, organize,
manage, and maintain the National Sports Training Center as may be authorized by law.

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 14 of this Act, in the event that sports
equipment and facilities of the Department are contracted out for use by private entities, all
rental income from the sports equipment and facilities owned and operated by the Department
shall be remitted directly to the Department to be used exclusively for the operation and
maintenance of said equipment and facilities.

SEC. 19. Assistance by the Government Entities. - The Department may call upon
any government entity for assistance in the performance of its functions and duties. All heads
of departments, agencies, corporations, and offices of the government are hereby enjoined to
render full assistance and cooperation to the Department to ensure the attainment of its
objectives and the success of the national sports development program.

SEC. 23. Implementing Rules and Regulations. — The Department shall, within
ninety (90) days from the approval of this Act, issue the necessary rules and regulations to
implement the provisions hereof.

SEC. 24. Annual Report. — The Department shall, within ninety (90) days after the
end of every fiscal year, submit to Congress an annual report on its compliance with, or
accomplishments under this Act, together with its plans and recommendations to improve and
develop its capability to enforce the provisions of this Act, and a complete accounting of
transactions with respect to any funds received under this Act.

SEC. 25. Separability Clause. — If any provision or part hereof is held invalid or
unconstitutional, the reminder of the law or the provision not otherwise affected shall remain
valid and subsisting.

SEC. 26. Repealing Clause. — All laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations or parts
thereof inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

SEC. 27. Effectivity. - This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its
publication in the Official Gazette or in any major newspaper of general circulation in the

Philippines.
