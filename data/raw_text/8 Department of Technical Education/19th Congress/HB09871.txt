                                        EXPLANATORY NOTH

       The Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) was established on
August 25, 1994 by virtue of Republic Act No. 7796 or the "Technical Education and Skills
Development Act of 1994". Since its creation, TESDA has remained an essential contributor to the
National Government and its mission to provide Filipinos with quality-assured technical education
and skills development programs, and to mobilize technical-vocational institutions in the Philippines.

        Over two decades after its establishment, TESDA is now the leading government agency that
sets the direction and ensures the efficient delivery of technical education and skills development
programs across various sectors all over the country. However, TESDA has also experienced its share
of challenges and limitations, especially within the context of today's ever-changing local and global
environment. To address concerns such as the strengthening of TESDA' s organizational structure and
the modemization of skills training facilities, the overall system of the Authority must be advanced
through an augmented budget along with adequate personnel, and resources.

       Hence, this proposed measure seeks to repeal Republic Act No. 7796 or the "Technical
Education and Skills Development Act of 1994" and to strengthen the powers and organization
functions of TESDA by elevating it into a department. The department shall become the primary
policy formulation, planning, coordinating, implementing, regulating, and administrative entity of the
government for technical education and training and certification of policies, plans, and programs. It
shall be headed by a Secretary, four (4) Undersecretaries, and four (4) Assistant Secretaries.

       Such change will be necessary in addressing concerns and implementing reforms fitting with
the Fourth Industrial Revolution and other advancements. With this proposed legislative measure,
TESDA will be strengthened as an implementing and regulatory institution of the Technical and
Vocational Sector of the country's trifocal education system. Most importantly, this measure is filed
to benefit the interest and needs of the hard working and skilled Filipino workforce.

       In view of the foregoing, approval of this bill is earnestly soucht.
