                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

It is imperative that the State shall provide relevant, accessible, high quality and
efficient technical education and skills development in support of the
development of high quality Filipino middle-level manpower responsive to and in
accordance with Philippine development goals and priorities.

Article XIV, Section 2 of the 1987 Constitution highlights the government's
duty to ensure a complete and suitable education system that meets the needs
of the people in an ever-changing environment. It also calls for support for
students through scholarships and other aid, especially for those from
underprivileged backgrounds.

Furthermore, it promotes alternative learning approaches, including informal
and community-based programs.

This House Bill seeks to encourage active participation of various concerned
sectors, particularly private enterprises, being direct participants in and
immediate beneficiaries of a trained and skilled work force, in providing technical
education and skills development opportunities.
     Thus the creation of the Department of Technical Education Skills
Development (DTESD) is long overdue.



Thus, the early passage of this bill is earnestly requested.

                           AN ACT
           CREATING THE DEPARTMENT OF TECHNICAL
       EDUCATION AND SKILLS DEVELOPMENT (DTESD), TO
             STREAMLINE GOVERNANCE, ENHANCE
         COORDINATION, ENSURE THE CONTINUITY AND
        EFFECTIVENESS OF PROGRAMS AT DEVELOPING A
       SKILLED AND GLOBALLY COMPETITIVE WORKFORCE,
       APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER
                         PURPOSES


      Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines
in congress assembled:

       SECTION 1.         SHORT TITLE. — This Act shall be known as the
 Department of Technical Education and Skills Development (DTESD)
 Act of 2024 "



            SECTION 2.                DECLARATION OF POLICY – it is hereby
      a declared the policy of the state to:
                  i. Promote and strengthen the quality of technical education and
                    skills    development     programs   to     attain     international
                    competitiveness.
                 ii. Focus technical education and skills development on meeting
                    the changing demands for quality middle-level manpower;
              iii. Encourage critical and creative thinking by disseminating the
                    scientific and technical knowledge base of middle-level
                    manpower development programs;
                 iv. Recognize and encourage the complementary roles of public
                    and private institutions in technical education and skills
                    development and training systems; and
                  v. Instill desirable values through the development of moral
                    character with emphasis on work ethic, self-discipline,
                    self-reliance and nationalism.

       The state shall provide relevant, accessible, high quality and efficient
 technical education and skills development in support of the development
 of high-quality Filipino manpower responsive to and in accordance with
 Philippine development goals and priorities.

      The state shall encourage active participation of various concerned
sectors, public as well as private technical-vocational institutions (TVIs) being
direct participants in and immediate beneficiaries of a technically educated and
trained workforce, in providing technical education services and training
opportunities.

       SECTION 3.            Creation   of   the   Department     of     Technical
 Education and Skills Development. — There is hereby created a
 Department of Technical Education and Skills Development (DTESD),
 which shall be the primary policy formulation, planning, coordinating,
 implementing, regulating and administrative entity of the government for
 technical education and skills development (TESD) agenda.
      SECTION 4.             MANDATE - The Department shall
exercise the following:

     a) Formulate a national technical education and skills
        development program, plans, policies, strategies to promote
        scientific and technical knowledge base of manpower
        development programs, in coordination and in consultation
        with national and local stakeholders, local government units
        (LGUs), and other government agencies;
     b) To ensure permanence and continuity of technical education
        and skills development program at par with international
        standards to attain global competitiveness;
     c) To recommend to the executive and legislative branches,
        priorities and grants on technical education and skills
        development and research;
     d) Assess, review, harmonize and coordinate all TESD policies
        and programs to ensure overall consistency and
        implementation of the national policies;
     e) Organize and constitute various standing committees,
        subsidiary groups, or technical working groups for efficient
        integration, coordination and monitoring of TESD programs
        at the national, regional, and local levels;
     f) Enter into, make, execute, perform and carry-out domestic
        and foreign contracts subject to existing laws, rules and
        regulations;
     g) Restructure the entire sub-sector consisting of all
        institutions and programs involved in the promotion and
        development of middle-level manpower through upgrading,
        merger and/or phase-out following a user-led strategy;
     h) Approve training standards and assessment packages as
        established and conducted by private industries;
     i) To instill necessary values through the development of
        ethical character with emphasis on work ethics, self-
        discipline, self-reliance and nationalism;
     j) To support the implementation of the Philippine
        Qualifications Framework or the PQF Act (Republic Act No.
        10968), the ladderized Education Act (Republic Act No.
        10647) and the Dual Training System Act (Republic Act No.
        7686); and other laws, decrees and orders on education;
           k) To maximize returns to the country's investment in technical
              education and skills development through an efficient
              graduates' employment facilitation program within the
              framework of the national employment service network
              system; and
           l) To support in the poverty alleviation efforts by providing the
              marginalized and vulnerable segments of the country's
              population access to income generating technical education
              and skills development opportunities.
           m) To promote and strengthen the quality of technical education
              and skills development programs to attain global
              competitiveness and inclusive growth;
           n) To focus technical education and skills development on
              meeting the changing demands for quality manpower
              particularly in support of the development thrust of the
              country;
           o) To encourage critical and creative thinking by disseminating
              the scientific and technical knowledge base of manpower
              development programs;
           p) To recognize and encourage the complementary roles of
              public and private institutions, enterprises, local
              government units (LGUs) and civil society organizations
              (CSOs) in technical education and skills development;
           q) Promulgate such rules and regulations and exercise such
              other powers and functions as may be necessary to carry out
              effectively the purpose and objectives of this Act;
           r) Perform such other functions as may be necessary for its
              effective operations and tor the continued enhancement,
              growth or development of higher education.

     SECTION 5.         COMPOSITION.       -    The   Department   shall   be
composed of the Office of the Secretary, and the various bureaus, services
and regional and provincial/district offices.

     The Office of the Secretary shall house the Office of the Department
Secretary, the Offices of the Undersecretaries, the Offices of the Assistant
Secretaries, and their immediate support staff.
     SECTION 6.            QUALIFICATIONS AND APPOINTMENT. - No
person shall be appointed Secretary, Undersecretary and Assistant
Secretary of the Department unless he or she is a citizen and resident of
the Philippines, of good moral character, of proven integrity, and with
relevant competence and expertise.

     SECTION 7.            BUREAUS, SERVICES, AND STAFFING. — The
bureaus under the Office of the Secretary of DTESD shall be headed by an
Executive     Director    to   be    appointed     by   the   President   upon   the
recommendation of the Secretary.

     The Executive Directors shall have the rank and emoluments of
Director V.

      SECTION 8.           TECHNICAL          EDUCATION          AND      TRAINING
CENTERS. - The DTESD shall absorb and enhance existing Technical
Education and Training Centers and establish a network of national,
regional and local technical education and skills development centers for
the purpose of promoting technical education and skills development. This
network shall include leading training technology centers, training centers
in technical vocational schools, including TESDA- administered and
supervised     TVIs,     including    arts   and    trades    schools/colleges   and
polytechnic institutes, and all other duly accredited public and private dual
system educational institutions.

      SECTION 9.           STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN. - Subject to
the approval of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the
Secretary shall determine the organizational structure and create new divisions
or units as may be necessary, and appoint officers and employees of the
Department and the Commission hereinafter created in accordance with the
civil service laws, rules and regulations. The remuneration structure of the
position in the staffing pattern shall strictly conform to Republic Act No. 6758,
otherwise known as the Salary Standardization Law, as amended. SEC. 16.
Formulation of a Comprehensive Development Plan for Middle Level Manpower.

      The DTESD shall formulate a comprehensive development plan for
middle manpower based on a national employment plan or policies for the
optimum allocation, development and utilization of skilled workers for
employment entrepreneurship and technology development for economic and
social growth. This plan shall be updated periodically and submitted to the
President for approval.

     Thereafter, it shall be the plan for the technical education, training, and
certification for the entire country within the framework of the National
Development Plan.

     The DTESD shall direct through its offices to call on public agencies, the
private sector and academe to assist in this effort. The comprehensive plan
shall provide for a reformed industry-based training program including
apprenticeship, dual training system and other similar schemes intended to:

     SECTION 10.          Technical Education and Skills Development
Board. The DTESD shall establish Technical Education and Skills
Development board at the regional and local levels to coordinate and
monitor the delivery of all training activities by the public and private
sectors, and recommend policy, program and project.

     This board shall likewise serve as the Technical Education and Skills
Development Committees of the regional and local development councils.

     The composition of the Technical Education and Skills Development
board shall be determined by the Secretary.

     SECTION 11.          Enterprise-Based    Training   Program.   -   The
DTESD shall promote Enterprise-Based Training as a major mode of
training rather than an employment arrangement and shall see to it that
it is not subject to abuse by unprincipled entities.

     SECTION 12.           Establishment     and    Administration    of    National
Training Standards Policies & Guidelines - There shall be a national training
standard to be established by DTESD-accredited industry board. The DTESD
shall develop and implement a certification and accreditation program in which
private industry groups and trade associations are accredited to conduct
approved assessment packages, and the local government units to promote
such assessment in their respective areas in accordance with the guidelines set
by the DTESD.
      SECTION 13.          Administration of Training Programs. - The
DTESD shall design and administer training programs and schemes that
will develop the capabilities of public and private institutions to provide
quality and cost-effective technical education and skills development and
related opportunities.

      This      training    programs       and     schemes   shall    include
teachers'/trainers' training in occupational trades and related fields of
employment, and value development as an integral component of all
technical education and training programs.

      SECTION 14.          Assistance to Establishments - The DTESD shall
assist any employer or organization engaged in technical education and
training schemes design to attain its objectives under rules and
regulations established by the DTESD.

     SECTION 15.           Accreditation     and    Regulation   of        TESD
Programs. - All TESD shall be duly accredited by the DTESD before it is
offered to the public by any public, private entity or non- government
organization.
       Non-compliance with the accreditation requirements of the DTESD
 shall form part of the bases for the possible suspension or cancellation of
 the entities' license to operate issued by competent authorities.

      SECTION       16.          Enterprise-Based     Connections.     -   The
DTESD shall establish effective and efficient institutional arrangements
with industry bodies and such other organizations or associations to
ensure direct participation of employers and workers in the design and
implementation of technical education and skills development schemes,
competency standardization and certification; and such other functions in
the fulfillment of the DTESD's objectives.




              SECTION 17.        DTESD DEVELOPMENT FUND - A DTESDA
      Development Fund is hereby established, to be managed/administered by
      the Secretary, the income from which shall be utilized exclusively in
      awarding of grants and providing assistance to training institutions,
      industries, local government units for upgrading their capabilities and to
      develop and implement training and training-related activities.
              The contribution to the fund shall be the following:


                    a. one-time lump sum appropriation from the National
                    Government;
                    b. annual contribution from the Overseas Workers Welfare
                    Administration Fund, the amount of which should be part of
                    the study on financing in conjunction with letter (D) of Section
                    34 of RA7796;
                    c. donations, grants, endowments, and other bequests or
                    gifts, and
                    d. any other income generated by the Agency.

              The Secretary shall be the administrator of the fund, and as such,
      shall    formulate   the   necessary   implementing     guidelines   for   the
      management of the fund.
           SECTION 18.        DTESD BUDGET - The amount necessary to
     finance the initial implementation of this Act shall be charged against the
     existing appropriations of the National Manpower and Youth Council
     (NMYC) and the Bureau of Technical and Vocational Education (BTVE).

      SECTION 19.       TRANSITORY       PROVISION.     —    The   DTESD
Secretariat created under Sections IO- 15 of R.A. 7796 shall comprise the
initial manpower complement of the DTESD, Provided, that, no employee
shall be terminated by virtue of this transfer.

      The laws and rules on government reorganization as provided under
Republic Act No. 6656, otherwise known as the Reorganization Law, shall
govern the transfer and reorganization process of the DTESD.

           The Personnel of the DTESD shall, in a holdover capacity,
     continue to perform their respective duties and responsibilities and
     receive their corresponding salaries and benefits until such time
     when the organizational structure pattern of the DTESD shall have
     been approved.

      Provided, That the preparation and approval of the said new
organizational structure and staffing pattern shall, as far as practicable,
respect and ensure the security of tenure and seniority rights of affected
government employees. Those personnel whose positions are not included
in the new staffing pattern or who are not reappointed or who choose to be
separated as a result of the reorganization shall be paid their separation
or retirement benefits under existing laws.

     SECTION 20.        APPROPRIATIONS. - The amount needed for the
initial implementation of this Act shall be taken from the current fiscal
year's appropriation of TESDA. Thereafter, the amount needed for the
operation and maintenance of the DTESD shall be included in the General
Appropriations Act. (GAA).

           The amount of two billion pesos (₱2,000,000,000.00), or so much
     thereof as may be necessary, is there hereby appropriated as funding, out
     of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to carry
     into effect the provisions of this Act.

           The State shall hereby provide a separate authorize operational and
     organizational funding of five hundred million pesos (₱500,000,000.00)
     to implement and to maintain the continuity of its mandate.

           The amount necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act shall be
     included in the annual budget of implementing agencies in the General
     Appropriations Act (GAA) of the year following its enactment into law and
     every year thereafter.

           The state shall hereby authorize the Department of Technical
     Education and Skills Development to re-align its appropriations, in the
     current year of the date of the effectivity of this Act to conform with its
     mandate and requirements.




           SECTION 21.         PERIODIC REPORTS - The TESDA shall submit
     periodic reports on its findings and make recommendations on actions to
     be taken by Congress and Senate and the appropriate department, and in
     order to carry out the objectives and mandates of this Act.

           SECTION 22.         AUTOMATIC REVIEW - Every three (3) years
     after the effectivity of this Act, an independent review panel composed of
     experts to be appointed by the President shall review the policies and
programs in the DTESD and shall make recommendations, based on its
findings, to the President and to both Houses of Congress.

      SECTION 23.          REPEALING CLAUSE - All laws, executive orders,
issuances, rules and regulations inconsistent with this Act are hereby
amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

      SECTION 24.          SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason or
reasons, any portion or provision of this Act shall be held unconstitutional
or invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to
be in full force and effect.

      SECTION 25.          EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Act shall take
effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in any newspaper of general
circulation.




Approved,
